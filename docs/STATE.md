## Current Block

- Jeok Cheongang plans to leave for Mount Jiuhua to calm his body and mind, fearing that his infirmities of old age will make him a burden and endanger Jin Taekyung. Mungyeong asks whether he has heard of the Wa Kingdom and recounts capturing its finest ninja, who revealed that he once abandoned his elderly mother in the mountains.
- The ninja's mother had told him not to hurry and to be careful on the mountain path. Jeok initially mistakes Mungyeong's story for tacit agreement with his departure, but learns that the mother eventually lived out her natural lifespan and was brought home by her repentant son. He realizes that abandoning Taekyung is not selfless protection but the proud, mistaken choice of an old man who refuses to accept his Disciple's support.
- Remembering Taekyung's promises to carry and support him, Jeok understands that Master and Disciple have always walked side by side. His decades-old Heart Demon and buried memories surge up without causing their usual pain, then disperse as memories of Taekyung's voice bring light and peace. Jeok breaks free of his inner chains and rises as the Fire King, sending a powerful wind sweeping outward.

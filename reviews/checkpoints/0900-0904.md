# Review

The reading copies, summary, and active state are broadly consistent. One line in Chapter 904 incorrectly describes the announcement of an heir as a birth.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 904, opening paragraph
- **Current:** the endless cries of “Ten thousand years!” celebrating the heir’s birth had turned into screams and shouts.
- **Defect:** Prince Shangshan is already a child; the banquet celebrates his designation as heir, not his birth.
- **Replacement:** the endless cries of “Ten thousand years!” celebrating the naming of the heir had turned into screams and shouts.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 902 shows the Emperor announcing his heir and Prince Shangshan arriving at the banquet.

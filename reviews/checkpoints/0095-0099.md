# Review

The packet is coherent overall, but the active state preserves one already-resolved question while dropping the new hook about Taekyung’s mother. There are also repeated System-panel formatting inconsistencies, two awkward uses of “reeks to high heaven,” and minor wording drift around real-estate transactions.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity—Unresolved
- **Current:** Unresolved: why Im Chunsoo issued the special warning about Taekyung; who Taekyung called; which apartment is the surveillance base; whether the black Familiar and Kim Gwondong share immediate instructions; and whether Woojin’s investigation and Sangdong’s operation have the same commissioning chain.
- **Defect:** The question about the black Familiar and Kim Gwondong is already resolved: Chapters 96–97 place both within the same Security Team surveillance operation under the Team Leader’s instructions. Meanwhile, the unexplained change in Taekyung’s mother’s behavior and her frequent appointments is a newly introduced hook that the summary drops.
- **Replacement:** Unresolved: why Im Chunsoo issued the special warning about Taekyung; who Taekyung called; which apartment is the surveillance base; why Taekyung’s mother has been leaving the house frequently for important appointments; and whether Woojin’s investigation and Sangdong’s operation have the same commissioning chain.
- **Rationale:** This removes a false open question and preserves the household subplot introduced in Chapter 98 for future continuity.

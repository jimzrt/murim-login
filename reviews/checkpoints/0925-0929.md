# Review

The packet is largely consistent in voice and continuity. Four localized reading-copy issues are actionable.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 927, Taekyung’s description of the System
- **Current:** A supernatural ability no human could even begin to imagine, one that couldn’t be contained by the four characters of *supernatural powers*.
- **Defect:** “Four characters” is a Korean-language calque that does not work for the English phrase.
- **Replacement:** A supernatural ability no human could even begin to imagine. Calling it *supernatural* didn’t begin to cover it.
- **Rationale:** Keeps Taekyung’s point without asking an English reader to count Korean characters.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 928, Taekyung’s plea to the Bow Saint
- **Current:** But the Bow Saint’s reply held not even a glimmer of light as it reached me, shouting in desperation.
- **Defect:** The closing modifier makes the reply sound as though it is shouting.
- **Replacement:** But as I shouted in desperation, the Bow Saint’s reply held not even a glimmer of light.
- **Rationale:** Makes the speaker clear while preserving the desperate plea and bleak answer.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 929, restoration army joins the Embroidered Uniform Guard
- **Current:** With the light of dawn growing clear in the far west,
- **Defect:** Dawn is placed in the west, contradicting the chapter’s earlier eastward dawn imagery.
- **Replacement:** With the light of dawn growing clear in the far east,
- **Rationale:** Restores the chapter’s established direction without changing the scene.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.85
- **Source:** Chapter 929, final sentence
- **Current:** one person opened his eyes.
- **Defect:** The male pronoun narrows the unidentified person’s identity beyond what the packet’s summary and active state disclose.
- **Replacement:** someone opened their eyes.
- **Rationale:** Preserves the awakening without adding an early identity cue.

# Review

The packet preserves its major hooks, tone, and continuity without spoilers, but contains one broken insult-based joke, two instances of title drift, and one awkward description of the Miao forces.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 631, confrontation inside Yohi’s tent
- **Current:** “You’re saying pointless things in front of a Han Chinese man, Yohi.”
- **Defect:** The neutral phrase “Han Chinese man” does not support Jin’s immediate complaint that Baeksang keeps calling him a “bastard,” breaking the exchange and the ensuing Bastard Family joke.
- **Replacement:** “You’re saying pointless things in front of a Han Chinese bastard, Yohi.”
- **Rationale:** Restoring the insult makes Jin’s response logically coherent and preserves the setup for Baeksang’s next line and Jin’s deadpan wordplay.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 633, Namho preparing to return to the Fire Dragon Pavilion
- **Current:** “That the Pavilion Master is looking for you.”
- **Defect:** Jin’s established title in this packet is “Pavilion Head,” but Namho calls him “Pavilion Master” here.
- **Replacement:** “That the Pavilion Head is looking for you.”
- **Rationale:** Using the established title prevents terminology drift within the same chapter sequence.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 633, Namho’s final warning to Jin
- **Current:** “Pavilion Master, there’s one last thing I want to say.”
- **Defect:** The form of address repeats the chapter’s drift from the established title “Pavilion Head.”
- **Replacement:** “Pavilion Head, there’s one last thing I want to say.”
- **Rationale:** This keeps Namho’s address consistent with Jin’s established Murim Alliance title throughout the packet.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 633, the Beast Miao King ordering Yayul Mok to mobilize
- **Current:** “Go with them. Gather the warriors under the Miao people and raise a force in case something happens.”
- **Defect:** “The warriors under the Miao people” is an unnatural and unclear description of warriors belonging to the Miao tribe.
- **Replacement:** “Go with them. Gather the Miao warriors and raise a force in case something happens.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement is natural US English and matches the packet’s established term “Miao warriors” without changing the order’s meaning.

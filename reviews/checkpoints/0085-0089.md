Three minor presentation issues are actionable: inconsistent capitalization of the ordinary noun “fame” in Chapter 86 and a System-generated item window in Chapter 89 that is not formatted as a System panel.

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 86, explanation of Im Chunsoo's reputation

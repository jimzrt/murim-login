# Review

The reading copies are largely consistent, but one drinking-scene term conflicts with the checkpoint decision, and the summary and continuity notes conflate two groups of scouts. A Picasso footnote also confuses Cubism with Impressionism.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1009, Ma Junggeol’s invitation
- **Current:** Just so you know, of all the teas, my brothers and I favor grain tea most.
- **Defect:** “Grain tea” conflicts with the checkpoint’s explicit decision to render the drink as grain liquor and obscures why Sima Gong calls the men renowned drinkers.
- **Replacement:** Just so you know, my brothers and I are especially fond of grain liquor.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding scene has the brothers drinking liquor, and the recorded terminology decision specifies “grain liquor.”

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Checkpoint plot summary, scout arrival
- **Current:** The scouts return early and signal suspicious intruders at the wall.
- **Defect:** The wording implies that Sima Gong’s scouts sent beyond the desert are the riders returning to the wall, which the reading copies do not establish.

- **Rationale:** Chapter 1007 identifies the returning thirty as scouts who left half a shichen earlier and were due to patrol until sunset; Chapter 1006 gives no count for the scouts sent beyond the desert.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Checkpoint continuity, scout deployment
- **Current:** Sima Gong sent thirty scouts beyond the desert after ominous signs. They returned early and signaled the approach of suspicious riders.
- **Defect:** The note assigns the wall patrol’s count and return to the separately mentioned expedition beyond the desert.
- **Replacement:** Sima Gong sent trusted scouts beyond the desert after ominous signs. Separately, thirty mounted scouts returned early from a patrol near the city wall and signaled the approach of suspicious riders.
- **Rationale:** Keeping the groups distinct avoids turning an unconfirmed connection into durable continuity.

# Review

The packet is largely consistent. One count of who knows the Emperor’s condition conflicts with the preceding chapter; three prose choices also stand out as awkward or overly literal.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 934, Emperor’s account of the Divine Physician’s visit
- **Current:** Now that I think about it, there were three of you, not two. Three people who knew about my condition.
- **Defect:** The count includes Taekyung but omits the Divine Physician. Chapter 933 names Baek Yeon and the Bow Saint as the two who knew before Taekyung; the Divine Physician makes a fourth.
- **Replacement:** Now that I think about it, there were three others, not two. Three people who knew about my condition before today.
- **Rationale:** This accounts for Baek Yeon, the Bow Saint, and the Divine Physician without excluding Taekyung, who learned the truth today.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 934, narration after the Emperor predicts war
- **Current:** Four characters that described the current situation more accurately than anything else.
- **Defect:** “Four characters” is a literal idiom gloss that does not fit the English phrase “the calm before the storm.”
- **Replacement:** Nothing described the current situation better.
- **Rationale:** Preserves the judgment without making an inaccurate claim about the English wording.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 932, Taekyung considers his Inventory
- **Current:** They said many a little made a mickle.
- **Defect:** The archaic proverb jars with Taekyung’s contemporary narration.
- **Replacement:** Little things add up.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the point about accumulating junk in his usual voice.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Chapter 932, opening System messages
- **Current:** At that brief command, the dam blocking the floodgates came crashing down.
- **Defect:** A dam blocking floodgates mixes two mechanisms in one image.
- **Replacement:** At that brief command, the floodgates burst open.
- **Rationale:** Conveys the sudden rush of messages with a clear image.

# Review

The packet is cohesive in voice, terminology, formatting, and active continuity. One localized quantity mismatch makes Leviathan’s previously consumed Magic Gem plural despite the surrounding chapters consistently establishing a single gem.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 752, Leviathan’s reflection after feeding
- **Current:** Over the course of roughly a day, Leviathan had completely absorbed the masses of magical power that humans called S-rank Magic Gems.
- **Defect:** This makes the previously consumed S-rank Magic Gem plural, contradicting Chapters 751–752, which establish that Leviathan obtained and absorbed one unrefined S-rank Magic Gem.
- **Replacement:** Over the course of roughly a day, Leviathan had completely absorbed the mass of magical power that humans called an S-rank Magic Gem.
- **Rationale:** The singular preserves the established quantity and keeps the comparison with the later two-gem bait internally consistent.

# Review

Five actionable issues: one inverted joke, one premature continuity claim, one identity/role drift, and two System terminology inconsistencies.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 490, Taekyung questioning a guard about Jeok Cheongang
- **Current:** If I didn’t want to get beaten to death, I’d make up things that never happened and tell you.
- **Defect:** The conditional is logically inverted: inventing information would increase the speaker’s risk of being beaten rather than prevent it.
- **Replacement:** If I wanted to get beaten to death, I’d make up things that never happened and tell you.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the intended sarcastic logic without changing the exchange’s voice or timing.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity bullet for the Water God Dragon’s corpse
- **Current:** The Water God Dragon’s spirit has departed, but its enormous corpse is being dismantled and distributed under Taekyung’s direction.
- **Defect:** The packet shows the corpse being dismantled and preliminary shares being negotiated, but no completed or ongoing distribution. Recording distribution as current fact advances the state beyond Chapter 494.
- **Replacement:** The Water God Dragon’s spirit has departed, but its enormous corpse is being dismantled under Taekyung’s direction; distribution has not yet been completed.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the completed events while preventing the checkpoint state from introducing an unsupported development.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 494, Mungyeong reflecting on his forty years away from Murim
- **Current:** For all that time—long enough for the mountains and rivers to change four times—he had lived as a medical apprentice.
- **Defect:** Calling Mungyeong a medical apprentice conflicts with the established Divine Physician identity and with the immediately following account of independently examining and treating thousands of patients.
- **Replacement:** For all that time—long enough for the mountains and rivers to change four times—he had lived as a physician.
- **Rationale:** “Physician” maintains the established role and fits the medical experience described in the next sentence.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 494, Mungyeong’s poisoned waterskin
- **Current:** Of course, along with cool water, it contained a deadly poison called Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder—an extreme poison that killed its victim before they could take seven steps.
- **Defect:** The narration names the poison as Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder, but the immediately following System panel identifies this dose as Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder, the distinct established term used in the checkpoint state.
- **Replacement:** Of course, along with cool water, it contained a deadly poison called Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder—an extreme poison that killed its victim before they could take seven steps.
- **Rationale:** Using the full established name keeps the narration, System notification, summary, and durable state consistent.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** Chapter 494, System panel after detoxification
- **Current:** > - Status abnormality: **Severe Stomachache**!
> - Status abnormalities will return to normal once you have fully recovered, but decreased abilities cannot be restored!
- **Defect:** The System switches from the established term “Status condition,” used in Chapter 490, to “Status abnormality” for the same category of effect.
- **Replacement:** > - Status condition: **Severe Stomachache**!
> - Status conditions will return to normal once you have fully recovered, but decreased abilities cannot be restored!

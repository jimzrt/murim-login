# Review

The reading copies are broadly coherent and preserve the packet’s major hooks, but the checkpoint drops Lee Jungryong’s takeover of the northern command and the durable state omits Hero’s Soul’s current wielder. There is also one direct contradiction about the Skeleton Warlord’s rejected refund, several English-copy formatting issues, and two minor prose/tense drifts.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** checkpoint summary, Plot, first paragraph
- **Current:** The allied forces launch mobile warfare across five fronts and advance rapidly toward Suining City, where the Arch Lich is believed to be waiting. Jin Taekyung uses Hero’s Soul and the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist to help rout the monsters, but the Arch Lich’s absence and the sudden decline in the enemy army make the victories suspicious.
- **Defect:** The summary drops the consequential Chapter 405 hook that Lee Jungryong and Go Jun covertly removed the northern front’s commander and installed their bribed contact as his replacement.
- **Replacement:** The allied forces launch mobile warfare across five fronts and advance rapidly toward Suining City, where the Arch Lich is believed to be waiting. Jin Taekyung uses Hero’s Soul and the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist to help rout the monsters, but the Arch Lich’s absence and the sudden decline in the enemy army make the victories suspicious. Before the advance, Lee Jungryong and Go Jun covertly engineer the death of the northern front’s commander and install their bribed contact, Lieutenant General Wang Ochun, as his replacement.
- **Rationale:** Lee Jungryong’s covert control of a front-line command is an unresolved antagonist advantage with clear future continuity value, not incidental detail.

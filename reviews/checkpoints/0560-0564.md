# Review

One internal access-list contradiction and one dropped unresolved hook require correction. Three isolated phrases also disrupt the otherwise consistent contemporary English voice.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 564, secured training room description
- **Current:** It was an area permitted only to Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong.
- **Defect:** The access list omits Taekyung, contradicting both his immediately following statement that the room is for founding members “including me” and the active continuity.
- **Replacement:** It was an area permitted only to Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, Im Kkeokjeong, and me.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung is explicitly one of the room’s authorized users and is training the listed members in the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, final paragraph
- **Current:** The Peace Guild expands beyond its original Guild House, buying surrounding properties and constructing a larger facility. Magic Johnson discusses the Guild’s growing influence, gives Taekyung a chip for Team Leader Choi, and confirms that a secured training room is reserved for Taekyung and selected founding members learning the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique.
- **Defect:** The summary drops the deliberate unresolved hook in which seeing Im Kkeokjeong’s desperate training prompts Taekyung to consider beginning an unspecified plan soon.
- **Replacement:** The Peace Guild expands beyond its original Guild House, buying surrounding properties and constructing a larger facility. Seeing Im Kkeokjeong train with desperate persistence prompts Taekyung to consider beginning an unspecified plan soon. Magic Johnson discusses the Guild’s growing influence, gives Taekyung a chip for Team Leader Choi, and confirms that a secured training room is reserved for Taekyung and selected founding members learning the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung’s thought—“Should I start that soon…?”—is an intentional open hook. Recording it without identifying the plan preserves both the ambiguity and the chapter’s forward continuity.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 562, Orc Lord’s charge
- **Current:** At the roar of the powerful mutated individual said to be born only once in a million chances, more than a thousand orcs let out shrieks and struck their weapons together.
- **Defect:** “Born only once in a million chances” is an awkward collocation and reads like untranslated source syntax.

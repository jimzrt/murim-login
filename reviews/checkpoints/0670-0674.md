# Review

The packet is broadly coherent and preserves the established voices, but it contains one broken dialogue callback, one terminology drift, one conspicuous Korean-syntax calque, and two unresolved hooks omitted from the durable state.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 671, Gung Gibang tracking-scent recollection
- **Current:** No mistaking you for a fucking beggar. You can’t get through a sentence without working “filthy” in somewhere.
- **Defect:** The punchline refers to “filthy,” although Gung Gibang repeatedly said “disgustingly,” breaking the lexical callback and weakening Jin’s teasing.
- **Replacement:** No mistaking you for a fucking beggar. You can’t get through a sentence without calling something “disgusting.”
- **Rationale:** This restores the repeated word that motivates Jin’s joke and connects naturally to Gung Gibang’s following reply, “No matter how disgusting I am.”

## C02 — Minor

- **Current:** the tragedy at Yoseo Manor
- **Defect:** The location name drifts from the packet’s established and binding term, “Western Yao Estate,” for the same massacre.
- **Replacement:** the tragedy at the Western Yao Estate
- **Rationale:** The chapter itself calls the incident the Western Yao Estate massacre, and the checkpoint explicitly requires retaining that established rendering.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 673, Baeksang’s memory of Hwi and Hyang
- **Current:** Though they called each other dog-and-monkey enemies despite having grown up together, they would sometimes walk together while avoiding the eyes of others.
- **Defect:** “Dog-and-monkey enemies” is an unnatural Korean-syntax calque that disrupts the otherwise polished narrative voice.
- **Replacement:** Though they had grown up together, they fought like cats and dogs, yet sometimes they slipped away from everyone else to walk alone together.
- **Rationale:** The replacement conveys their outwardly antagonistic childhood relationship and secret intimacy in natural contemporary English without changing the action.

# Review

The reading copies remain coherent overall, but the checkpoint summary and durable state contain repeated factual errors: Sama Pyo’s sobriquet is treated as an object, Taekyung’s sleepy joke about Jung Ho is recorded as a genuine recognition, and Jeok Cheongang’s duel is misreported as an actual Dark Heaven assault. Two conspicuous Korean-style or dated expressions also break Taekyung’s established English voice.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint Plot, opening sentence
- **Current:** At Xixia, Sama Pyo confronts Shaolin’s young Martial Uncle, the scarred and hoarse-voiced Unnamed, over the Black Dragon Saber.
- **Defect:** The summary treats Black Dragon Saber, Sama Pyo’s sobriquet, as the disputed weapon and places the encounter at Xixia despite the chapter locating it in Henan.
- **Replacement:** At a ferry landing in Henan, Sama Pyo confronts Shaolin’s young Martial Uncle, the scarred and hoarse-voiced Unnamed, after taking a merchant’s sword.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 515 identifies Sama Pyo as Black Dragon Saber, has Unnamed order him to return the merchant’s sword, and explicitly states that they are in Henan.

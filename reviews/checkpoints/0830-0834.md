# Review

The packet largely maintains its voice, terminology, and unresolved hooks. Two sentences create avoidable confusion about who is acting and what is happening physically.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 830, after the Doppelganger predicts Asmodeus’s return
- **Current:** I stared blankly at the Doppelganger, laughing aloud.
- **Defect:** The dangling phrase makes Jin appear to be laughing, though the surrounding passage identifies the Doppelganger as the one laughing.
- **Replacement:** I stared blankly at the Doppelganger as it laughed aloud.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the laughter with its established subject without changing the beat.

# Review

The packet’s continuity and terminology are largely consistent. Three English-prose issues are actionable.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 957, Dongting Fisherman’s backstory
- **Current:** The older they were, and the more Fame they had built, the more so.
- **Defect:** The unexplained capitalization makes an ordinary reference to reputation look like a named System attribute.
- **Replacement:** The older they were, and the more fame they had earned, the more so.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the point about age and reputation without introducing an apparent term.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Chapter 959, Demon Bird’s return to his original appearance
- **Current:** The man who had been called Chinggen moments before ran his hands over his own face, returned to him after so long, and his flesh, which seemed ready to burst at any moment.
- **Defect:** The modifiers make the sentence cumbersome and briefly leave it unclear what has returned.

- **Rationale:** Clarifies the physical action and the restored face without changing either detail.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** Chapter 959, closing battle transition
- **Current:** A signal that threw the stopped time of the battlefield, and the wick that had barely remained, into a blaze of blood-red flame.
- **Defect:** The sentence combines stopped time and a remaining wick into a metaphor that is difficult to parse.
- **Replacement:** The battlefield, stilled for only a moment, erupted once more in a blaze of blood-red flame.
- **Rationale:** Preserves the brief pause and violent resumption while making the closing beat readable.

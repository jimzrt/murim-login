There are also minor System-panel, terminology, and prose consistency issues.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity
- **Current:** The identities of the figures behind the split human curtain at the Grand Training Ground remain unknown.
- **Defect:** This hook was resolved at the beginning of Chapter 187, where Jin Wikyung is explicitly identified as the person entering through the divided ranks.
- **Replacement:** Jin Wikyung entered along the path opened when the martial artists surrounding the Grand Training Ground split their human curtain.
- **Rationale:** Keeping the entrance unidentified contradicts Chapter 187 and falsely presents a completed reveal as unresolved.

# Review

The packet is largely consistent, but two lines overstate how completely the conflict ended, one Murong passage resolves a still-conditional outcome, and the proclamation needs quotation formatting.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 980, Jin Taekyung reporting the victory
- **Current:** The nomads who were left completely surrendered to us, and everyone in the Murong Family was either killed or captured.
- **Defect:** The claim that everyone was killed or captured contradicts the later account of Murong Yeonghwi and several dozen others escaping.
- **Replacement:** The nomads who were left completely surrendered to us, and the Murong Family’s forces were defeated.
- **Rationale:** Preserves the reported victory without ruling out the fugitives established in Chapter 982.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** Chapter 981, account of the battle’s end
- **Current:** The battle—and the war—ended that day.
- **Defect:** An unqualified end to the war conflicts with Chapter 984’s announcement that the wider war has begun.
- **Replacement:** The battle—and the war against the invaders—ended that day.
- **Rationale:** Distinguishes the concluded northern campaign from the newly declared war against Dark Heaven.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 983, Namho’s offer to the survivors
- **Current:** If your innocence is proven in the future, the Murong family will have another chance to prove itself under a new Family Head.
- **Defect:** The lowercase “Murong family” blurs the passage’s explicit distinction between the dissolved Murong Family and the surviving Murong household.
- **Replacement:** If your innocence is proven in the future, the Murong household will have another chance to prove itself under a new Family Head.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the conditional offer aligned with the terminology established immediately before it.

## C04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** Chapter 983, closing description of Murong Su
- **Current:** The man they would soon call their Family Head.
- **Defect:** Treats Murong Su’s appointment as certain before the survivors’ innocence has been established.
- **Replacement:** The man they hoped to call their Family Head.
- **Rationale:** Retains the retainers’ expectation without prematurely resolving the condition Namho just imposed.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 984, opening line of the imperial proclamation
- **Current:** Let it be known to all under Heaven, to all the people:
- **Defect:** The displayed proclamation is left as unmarked prose rather than formatted as an ordinary quotation.
- **Replacement:** > Let it be known to all under Heaven, to all the people:
- **Rationale:** Marks the document’s quoted opening without mislabeling it as a System panel.

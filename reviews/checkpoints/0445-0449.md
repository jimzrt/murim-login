# Review

The packet preserves the established voices, terminology, hooks, and uncertainty around Dark Heaven, but contains two contradictory timeline references and five ellipsis-formatting errors in Chapter 449.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 446, narration after the Quest window
- **Current:** According to what Zhuge Feng had said, the Sea Serpent Society Head and a thousand of his men had been buried beneath the waters of Red Cliffs a fortnight ago.
- **Defect:** The stated interval contradicts Zhuge Feng's account that he received news of the massacre one month ago and his later statement that a full month has passed.
- **Replacement:** According to what Zhuge Feng had said, the Sea Serpent Society Head and a thousand of his men had been buried beneath the waters of Red Cliffs a month ago.
- **Rationale:** Using one month maintains the explicit chronology established in Chapters 445 and 446.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 448, arrival at Donghu Stronghold
- **Current:** If Donghu Stronghold really was responsible for the two incidents that had occurred over the past fifteen days, just as the circumstances revealed so far suggested… then a clash of martial force might take place, in accordance with the laws of Murim.
- **Defect:** The fifteen-day interval contradicts the established month-long chronology of the Sea Serpent Society massacre.
- **Replacement:** If Donghu Stronghold really was responsible for the two incidents that had occurred over the past month, just as the circumstances revealed so far suggested… then a clash of martial force might take place, in accordance with the laws of Murim.
- **Rationale:** The Sea Serpent Society was destroyed approximately one month earlier, while the Dongting Fisherman disappeared only three days earlier.

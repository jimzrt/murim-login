# Review

The packet is broadly consistent in voice, terminology, formatting, and active continuity. One summary statement turns an unresolved disappearance into a confirmed abduction, and Chapter 655 contains three localized prose defects.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot paragraph 1
- **Current:** Heugung’s guards are found dead, and a severed wrist believed to be his is recovered, but footprints indicate that a third party intervened and abducted Yohi and Heugung rather than staging their deaths.
- **Defect:** The summary presents the abduction of Yohi and Heugung as established, although the footprints establish only that a third party was present; both characters' fates remain unresolved.
- **Replacement:** Heugung’s guards are found dead, and a severed wrist believed to be his is recovered, but footprints indicate that a third party intervened; what happened to Yohi and Heugung remains unknown.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the revealed evidence without converting Jin’s inference into a settled continuity fact.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 655, description of the Western Yao Estate
- **Current:** The splendid estate, so thoroughly filled with Yohi’s tastes, was no longer beautiful as it had once been.
- **Defect:** The comparative construction is grammatically incomplete.
- **Replacement:** The splendid estate, so thoroughly filled with Yohi’s tastes, was no longer as beautiful as it had once been.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the required “as … as” construction without changing the meaning or narrative voice.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 655, discovery inside Yohi’s room
- **Current:** And something whose use was more valuable than the pouch’s, and which gave off a terrible smell.
- **Defect:** The description treats the comparative value of an object's “use” as though the pouch itself possessed a comparable use, producing an unnatural and distracting construction.
- **Replacement:** And something far more significant than the pouch, which gave off a terrible smell.
- **Rationale:** The revised sentence naturally conveys that the severed wrist is the more important clue while preserving the delayed reveal.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 655, examination of the severed wrist
- **Current:** The cold sensation of flesh gone cold met my fingers, and sticky blood dampened them.
- **Defect:** The repeated use of “cold” creates an accidental tautology and makes the tactile description sound unpolished.
- **Replacement:** The chill of dead flesh met my fingers, and sticky blood dampened them.
- **Rationale:** The replacement removes the repetition while retaining the physical sensation and grim tone.

# Review

The packet is broadly coherent, but it contains one summary misidentification, two material continuity contradictions, an untranslated form of address, a deadline mismatch, and two minor prose-consistency issues.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** A young Shaolin monk named Unnamed then arrives, defeats Heukgeol without killing him, and leads them to Mount Song.
- **Defect:** The summary misidentifies Unnamed’s opponent. Heukgeol is the one-eyed officer whom Jeok Cheongang already knocked into a wall; Unnamed defeats the later hulking Dongcheon Sect master.
- **Replacement:** A young Shaolin monk named Unnamed then arrives, defeats a hulking Dongcheon Sect master without killing him, and leads them to Mount Song.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the sequence and prevents two distinct Dongcheon Sect figures from being conflated.

## C03 — Major

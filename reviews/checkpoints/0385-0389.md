# Review

The packet preserves voice, terminology, hooks, and spoiler boundaries overall. Actionable issues are a duplicated continuity reversal about the western-front breakthrough, one grammatical fragment in Chapter 386, and inconsistent telepathic-dialogue formatting in Chapters 385–386.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, final paragraph
- **Current:** Four days into the full-scale battle, the monster army has exceeded 100,000, communications and satellite surveillance remain disrupted, and the western front is breached by a rapid monster advance.
- **Defect:** The sentence reverses the breakthrough: it implies that the monsters breached Taekyung’s western front, whereas Chapter 389 reveals that Taekyung’s rapid advance breached the monsters’ line.
- **Replacement:** Four days into the full-scale battle, the monster army has exceeded 100,000, communications and satellite surveillance remain disrupted, and Taekyung’s rapid advance breaches the monsters’ line on the western front.
- **Rationale:** This distinction is the chapter’s central reveal and is explicitly preserved elsewhere in the checkpoint continuity.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity, final entry
- **Current:** The monster army has exceeded 100,000, and the western front has been breached by a rapid monster advance while Jin Taekyung's force remains engaged.
- **Defect:** The active-state wording repeats the same subject reversal, recording the western front as breached by advancing monsters rather than recording Taekyung’s breach of the monsters’ line.
- **Replacement:** The monster army has exceeded 100,000, and Jin Taekyung's force has rapidly breached the monsters' line on the western front while remaining engaged.
- **Rationale:** Active continuity must preserve which side was breached so later chapters do not treat Taekyung’s position as having collapsed.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 386, Lee Jungryong introduction
- **Current:** He was also the real leader of that Ares Guild.

A powerful Guild counted among the strongest in the world. And a man ranked among the top three strongest S-rank Hunters.
- **Defect:** The description breaks into two ungrammatical sentence fragments and inconsistently capitalizes the generic noun “guild.”
- **Replacement:** He was also the true leader of Ares Guild, one of the strongest guilds in the world, and one of the world’s three strongest S-rank Hunters.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves all three facts while restoring natural commercial prose and established capitalization.

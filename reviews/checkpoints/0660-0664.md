# Review

The packet is broadly consistent in voice, continuity, hooks, and System formatting. The actionable issues are an internal army-name contradiction in Chapter 662 and two isolated capitalization errors.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 662, Baeksang's account of the Great Snow Mountain battle
- **Current:** At the time, the Western Army we belonged to had Venerable Wusang, a Supreme Peak master, as well as the Palace Lord, who had only just begun to be called the Beast Miao King.
- **Defect:** The force is called the Southern Army immediately before this passage in both Chapters 661 and 662, creating an unexplained internal contradiction.
- **Replacement:** At the time, the Southern Army we belonged to had Venerable Wusang, a Supreme Peak master, as well as the Palace Lord, who had only just begun to be called the Beast Miao King.
- **Rationale:** Using Southern Army preserves the established name throughout Baeksang's continuous account and matches the checkpoint summary.

## C02 — Major

- **Defect:** This repeats the unexplained Western Army designation despite the same force being established as the Southern Army earlier in the account.
- **Replacement:** The dozens of large and small sects that had been assigned to the Southern Army alongside us were no different.
- **Rationale:** The replacement keeps the army designation internally consistent across the scene.

## C03 — Minor

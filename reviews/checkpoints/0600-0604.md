# Review

The packet is coherent overall and preserves the major hooks, but the checkpoint summary and continuity list contain two factual-scope errors. There are also localized prose and ellipsis-formatting inconsistencies in Chapters 601–602.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint Continuity, second bullet
- **Current:** Cheon Taemin is Choi Minwoo’s maternal grandfather and only living blood descendant.
- **Defect:** The sentence incorrectly makes Cheon Taemin the only living blood descendant of Choi Minwoo, reversing the established relationship.
- **Replacement:** Cheon Taemin is Choi Minwoo’s maternal grandfather, and Choi is his only living blood descendant.
- **Rationale:** The reading copies and active continuity establish that Choi Minwoo is Cheon Taemin’s only living blood descendant.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint Plot, second paragraph
- **Current:** Choi is unanimously elected Guild Master of the Peace Guild and Vice Guild Master of Ares Guild.
- **Defect:** The sentence applies “unanimously elected” to both appointments, although the reading copy specifies a unanimous board vote only for the Ares Guild position.
- **Replacement:** Choi is confirmed as Guild Master of the Peace Guild and unanimously elected Vice Guild Master of Ares Guild.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 603 separately reports confirmation of his Peace Guild position and a unanimous Ares Guild board vote.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 601, introduction of Vice President Park Daewon
- **Current:** The elderly middle-aged man seated at the head of the table.
- **Defect:** “Elderly middle-aged” is internally contradictory and reads like an unresolved literal gloss.
- **Replacement:** The older man seated at the head of the table.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves his relative age without assigning him to two conflicting age categories.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 601, executives reacting to Park Daewon

- **Replacement:** “Vice President Park. What did you just say…?”
- **Rationale:** This matches the packet’s binding punctuation rules and the sentence’s questioning delivery.

## C05 — Minor

# Review

The reading copies and active state largely agree on the reveal, the westward pursuit, and the unresolved questions. One line in Chapter 811 contradicts the order Jin has just given.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Chapter 811, Jin’s reply to Yamamoto Genji after assigning the westward party
- **Current:** “No. I’m not leaving you out. I have no intention of leaving you out. Go back.”
- **Defect:** “Go back” reverses the instruction to accompany Jin and conflicts with Jin’s repeated refusal to leave Genji behind.
- **Replacement:** “No. I’m not leaving you out. I have no intention of leaving you out. You’re coming with us.”
- **Rationale:** Keeps Jin’s emphatic repetition while making the order consistent with the party that sets out west.

# Review

The packet is coherent in voice, terminology, and continuity overall. Actionable issues are a systematic doubled-ellipsis formatting drift, one recurring literal wuxia calque, two awkward or incorrect sentences, and an ending that states purification as certain despite the active state preserving it as unresolved.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapters 715–719 throughout; representative occurrence in Chapter 715, Jin and Jeok Cheongang’s exchange
- **Current:** “……You’re joking, right?”
- **Defect:** The reading copies repeatedly use a doubled ellipsis glyph instead of the required single ellipsis character.
- **Replacement:** “…You’re joking, right?”
- **Rationale:** The packet’s dialogue and narration should consistently use a single English ellipsis character.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 717, Hyuk Mujin explaining the proposed search
- **Current:** “Uh, I’m sorry, but I don’t actually know that much. If I had to add anything, it would be what I mentioned earlier—the Nanman leadership is in an uproar, and they were even discussing deploying a net over heaven and earth to search for Great Hero Yayul.”
- **Defect:** “Deploying a net over heaven and earth” is a stiff, literal wuxia calque that does not sound natural in Hyuk Mujin’s casual dialogue.
- **Replacement:** “Uh, I’m sorry, but I don’t actually know that much. If I had to add anything, it would be what I mentioned earlier—the Nanman leadership is in an uproar, and they were even discussing launching an all-out search for Great Hero Yayul.”
- **Rationale:** “Launching an all-out search” preserves the scale and intent while matching contemporary spoken English.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 717, Hyuk Mujin recalling the proposed search
- **Current:** “Oh. The net over heaven and earth.”
- **Defect:** The literal idiom remains unnatural and does not match the revised contemporary phrasing required by the surrounding dialogue.
- **Replacement:** “Oh. The all-out search.”
- **Rationale:** This keeps the reference consistent with Hyuk Mujin’s preceding description.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 717, Namho explaining why the leadership stopped searching
- **Current:** “Under normal circumstances, the leadership would have done something. Whether that meant deploying a net over heaven and earth or secretly sending out warriors to search.”
- **Defect:** The repeated literal idiom is stiff, and the second sentence is a fragment.
- **Replacement:** “Under normal circumstances, the leadership would have done something, whether that meant organizing a full-scale search or quietly sending warriors out to look for him.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement removes the calque, completes the sentence, and preserves the distinction between an open search and a discreet one.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 718, Jin answering the Beast Miao King in the shrine
- **Current:** His answer came without the slightest hesitation, and the broad back shifted.
- **Defect:** The possessive pronouns assign Jin’s answer to the Beast Miao King and describe the Beast Miao King’s back without a natural determiner.
- **Replacement:** My answer came without the slightest hesitation, and his broad back shifted.
- **Rationale:** Jin is the speaker, while the shifting back belongs to the Beast Miao King.

## C06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 719, arrival at the burned Ailao Mountain

- **Defect:** “At the end of the direction” is an unnatural English collocation.

- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the visual information while removing the awkward spatial construction.

## C07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 719, final line
- **Current:** Today was the day a new sacred stone would be born.
- **Defect:** The narration presents the creation of a new sacred stone as certain, contradicting the checkpoint summary and active state, which leave purification and its result unresolved.
- **Replacement:** If this worked, a new sacred stone would be born today.
- **Rationale:** The conditional preserves Jin’s intended goal without prematurely confirming that purification will succeed or revealing its outcome.

# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves its major hooks, character voices, and System formatting. Three actionable issues remain: one established-title drift, one contradictory first-person reference, and one capitalization inconsistency.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 322, Heukgeol Beggar's introduction
- **Current:** “I’m the Black Beggar, head of the Beggars’ Sect’s Xi’an branch.”
- **Defect:** The title shifts to a translated form despite the packet's binding decision to use “Heukgeol Beggar.”
- **Replacement:** “I’m Heukgeol Beggar, head of the Beggars’ Sect’s Xi’an branch.”
- **Rationale:** Using the established title avoids terminology drift and matches every subsequent reference to the character in the chapter.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 323, immediately before Taekyung challenges Hwangbo Eom
- **Current:** The price for saying what I should not have said would be paid from this moment onward.
- **Defect:** The first-person pronoun incorrectly says Taekyung committed the offense, contradicting the preceding lines in which Hwangbo Eom insults and threatens Jeok Cheongang.
- **Replacement:** From this moment on, he would pay the price for saying what he should never have said.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the intended agent and makes Taekyung's retaliatory resolve unambiguous.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 324, palm-strike exchange
- **Current:** Hwangbo Eom’s face had gone pale from his Internal Injury.
- **Defect:** “Internal Injury” is capitalized like a formal System status even though it appears in ordinary narration and is rendered in lowercase elsewhere in the packet.
- **Replacement:** Hwangbo Eom’s face had gone pale from his internal injury.
- **Rationale:** Lowercase matches the term's ordinary narrative use and removes an isolated formatting inconsistency.

# Review

The packet is broadly coherent and spoiler-safe, with consistent character voice and core terminology. Actionable issues are one troop-count contradiction, a dropped Lee Jungryong hook in durable state, a mismatched recalled quotation, noncompliant dialogue punctuation in Chapter 397, and one unnatural injury description.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Durable state, open_questions after Chapter 399
- **Current:** "What happened to the Second Fiend assigned to the Qingcheng attack?"
- **Defect:** The durable state drops Jin's unresolved suspicion that Lee Jungryong and Ares Guild deliberately allowed the northern front to fall, along with his interrupted question about the Death Knights.
- **Replacement:** "What happened to the Second Fiend assigned to the Qingcheng attack?",
    "Why did Lee Jungryong and Ares Guild apparently allow the northern front to fall, and what was Jin about to ask Lee Jungryong about the Death Knights?"
- **Rationale:** This is a prominent unresolved hook from Chapter 395 and should remain available to later chapters without turning Jin's suspicion into a confirmed fact.

# Review

The reading copies remain coherent in voice, terminology, and plot continuity, but Chapters 125–126 contain ellipsis-format drift, Chapter 127 inconsistently restates the Temporary Strength Pill panel, Chapter 128 uses stray UI brackets, and Chapter 129 duplicates an article in a System notice. The checkpoint state also treats the Status Window’s Fame snapshot as final despite two later +40 increases and drops the possible rumor-based Title hook.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapters 125–126, dialogue and thought ellipses throughout; representative instance in Chapter 125
- **Current:** “But you’re a great senior of the Murim, renowned throughout the martial world...”
- **Defect:** These chapters repeatedly use three periods for ellipses, unlike the required ellipsis character and the later chapters’ formatting.
- **Replacement:** “But you’re a great senior of the Murim, renowned throughout the martial world…”
- **Rationale:** Normalize every three-dot ellipsis in Chapters 125–126 to the required single ellipsis character.

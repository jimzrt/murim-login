# Review

The reading copies are broadly coherent and preserve the packet’s major developments, but Chapter 605 has a sustained dialogue-formatting drift, Chapter 608 briefly drifts from the established presidential name, and the durable state drops several unresolved hooks concerning Cheon Taemin, Area A, and Jin’s undisclosed plan.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 605, Magic Johnson’s dialogue after the move to Cheon Taemin’s mansion
- **Current:** 「Space distortion and barriers. Defensive magic is a given, and I’ve even set up humidity and temperature controls. And…」
- **Defect:** Magic Johnson’s ordinary spoken dialogue switches from curly quotation marks to corner brackets here and remains in that format through the end of the chapter, despite using curly quotation marks earlier in Chapter 605 and again in the uninterrupted conversation in Chapter 606.
- **Replacement:** “Space distortion and barriers. Defensive magic is a given, and I’ve even set up humidity and temperature controls. And…”
- **Rationale:** The reading-copy rules require direct speech in curly double quotation marks. All of Magic Johnson’s corner-bracketed dialogue in the remainder of Chapter 605 should be normalized the same way.

## C02 — Minor

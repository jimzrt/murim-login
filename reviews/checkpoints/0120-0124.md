# Review

The packet is coherent overall, but Chapter 124 incorrectly upgrades three Peak martial arts to Supreme Peak, the checkpoint metadata drifts from the established Chief Branch Leader title, and Chapters 120 and 123 contain several ellipses that violate the required punctuation format.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 124, opening narration
- **Current:** They were Supreme Peak martial arts, no less.
- **Defect:** The three manuals offered by Lee Seowol are established as Peak martial arts, not Supreme Peak martial arts. Only the Flame Divine Palm is classified as Supreme Peak.
- **Replacement:** They were Peak martial arts, no less.
- **Rationale:** This contradicts Chapters 121 and 123, the checkpoint terminology decisions, and the later contrast between the three Peak arts and the Supreme Peak Flame Divine Palm.

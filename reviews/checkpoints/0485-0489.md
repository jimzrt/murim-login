# Review

Two continuity errors are actionable: the checkpoint incorrectly marks the new Quest complete, and Chapter 488 plus the continuity records contradict Chapter 487 about how much mana the Water God Dragon absorbed. Chapters 485–488 also use ASCII three-dot ellipses instead of the required ellipsis character.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, final sentence
- **Current:** Taekyung accepts, triggering and immediately completing the Fake Murim Martial Artist Quest through its bizarre “Agh” option.
- **Defect:** The Quest is accepted, not completed. The chapter ends immediately after the acceptance notification, and the durable state correctly records only acceptance.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung accepts the Fake Murim Martial Artist Quest through its bizarre “Agh” option.
- **Rationale:** Marking the Quest complete prematurely erases the unresolved training objective and creates false continuity for subsequent chapters.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 488, opening paragraph
- **Current:** But it possessed power far beyond that of ordinary spirit beasts, which was probably why it had been able to absorb all the mana from the Gate.
- **Defect:** The absolute claim contradicts Chapter 487, which says the Water God Dragon absorbed most of the Gate’s mana while residue remained and continued leaking.
- **Replacement:** But it possessed power far beyond that of ordinary spirit beasts, which was probably why it had been able to absorb most of the mana leaking from the Gate.
- **Rationale:** Using “most” preserves the residual mana responsible for the Mutated Minnows and the Gate’s continuing threat.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity, second bullet
- **Current:** The Water God Dragon absorbed all the Gate’s mana. Its mutated rampage nearly overturned Dongting Lake, which is now calm again.
- **Defect:** The summary repeats Chapter 488’s contradictory absolute quantity even though the Gate retains and leaks residual mana.

- **Rationale:** The continuity record must retain the distinction between the mana absorbed by the Water God Dragon and the residue that continues mutating local life.

## C04 — Major

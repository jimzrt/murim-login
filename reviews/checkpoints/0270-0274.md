# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice, terminology, hooks, and chronology. The major issue is that the checkpoint summary and active state conflate Jihoon’s apparent operation against Jung Hyunwoo with the separate discussion of Taekyung. The remaining findings are localized terminology, prose, grammar, and ellipsis-formatting issues.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** checkpoint summary, Plot paragraph 2
- **Current:** Jihoon learns that the Guild’s higher-ups find Taekyung troublesome and begins arranging an A-Rank raid that appears intended to kill him under cover of a Gate accident.
- **Defect:** This conflates two separate developments. Jihoon orders the A-rank raid immediately after hearing that Jung Hyunwoo has caught the scent; only afterward does the conversation turn separately to Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** Jihoon orders an A-rank raid after learning that Jung Hyunwoo has caught the scent, apparently arranging Jung’s death under cover of a Gate accident. He then reveals that the higher-ups find Taekyung troublesome.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the chapter’s sequence and prevents the checkpoint summary from assigning the apparent assassination plan to the wrong target.

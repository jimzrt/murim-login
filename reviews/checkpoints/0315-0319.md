# Review

The reading copies remain tonally consistent and preserve the packet’s main hooks, but there are two factual contradictions, one omitted party member in active continuity, one under-recorded Zhongnan conflict, and three minor formatting inconsistencies.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** Gung Gibang learns that the Yongbong Escort Bureau’s decline began with the generosity and later qi deviation of its former Bureau Head, Ju Hogun.
- **Defect:** Ju Hogun is identified as the former Bureau Head, contradicting Chapter 316, which calls him the current Bureau Head, and Chapter 317, which says Ju Hwaran leads in her incapacitated father’s place.
- **Replacement:** Gung Gibang learns that the Yongbong Escort Bureau’s decline began with the generosity and later qi deviation of its current Bureau Head, Ju Hogun.
- **Rationale:** Ju Hogun remains the titular Bureau Head despite his qi deviation; Ju Hwaran is acting in his place rather than succeeding him.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 316, explanation of the Yongbong Escort Bureau’s decline
- **Current:** The reason the Escort King had managed to catch two rabbits at once and build the Yongbong Escort Bureau into one of the five biggest in the industry was his fame.
- **Defect:** This contradicts the same chapter’s earlier statement that the Yongbong Escort Bureau had ranked among the top three in the world.
- **Replacement:** The reason the Escort King had managed to catch two rabbits at once and build the Yongbong Escort Bureau into one of the three biggest in the industry was his fame.
- **Rationale:** The bureau’s historical standing should remain numerically consistent within the chapter.

## C03 — Major

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** At Tengwang Pavilion, Hwangbo Eom reprimands Hyuk Sopyung and declares that he is trying to restore Zhongnan’s standing.
- **Defect:** The summary drops the chapter’s internal Zhongnan conflict: Hyuk Sopyung defends the Sect Leader, the Taeeul Sword Unit implicitly sides with him, and Hwangbo Eom curbs his pressure after noticing their reaction.
- **Replacement:** At Tengwang Pavilion, Hwangbo Eom reprimands Hyuk Sopyung and disparages the Sect Leader, but Hyuk Sopyung defends his Master, and the Taeeul Sword Unit’s reaction makes Hwangbo Eom back down before he declares that he is trying to restore Zhongnan’s standing.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the newly established tension between Hwangbo Eom, the Sect Leader’s disciple, and the Taeeul Sword Unit rather than reducing the exchange to a routine reprimand.

# Review

The packet is largely consistent, but one exchange treats an order as a question, and two direct thoughts depart from the established formatting.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 863, Prince Shangshan’s exchange with Baek Yeon
- **Current:** “I asked you a question. Why aren’t you answering?”
- **Defect:** The prince has given Baek Yeon an order, not asked him a question.
- **Replacement:** “I gave you an order. Why haven’t you acknowledged it?”
- **Rationale:** This preserves the prince’s demand for a response without contradicting his preceding line.

## C02 — Minor

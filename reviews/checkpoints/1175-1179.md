# Review

The reading copies and active state are broadly consistent. Two established formatting and terminology choices drift in the English.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1176, Taekyung recalling the pocket watch
- **Current:** The cold ticking of the [Broken Pocket Watch] I’d tucked deep in my Inventory just before logging in seemed to ring in my ears like a phantom sound.
- **Defect:** Square brackets enclose an item name in prose.
- **Replacement:** The cold ticking of the Broken Pocket Watch I’d tucked deep in my Inventory just before logging in seemed to ring in my ears like a phantom sound.
- **Rationale:** The binding Markdown convention does not use square brackets around UI terms.

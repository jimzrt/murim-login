# Review

One stale continuity entry contradicts chapter 558, one technique name drifts within chapter 557, and the reading copies contain several localized wording and formatting inconsistencies.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint continuity, second bullet
- **Current:** The twenty rescued Hunters remain unconscious but alive inside the Skeleton King’s reinforced bone barrier; the Mutated Gate’s final status is unresolved.
- **Defect:** This continuity entry stops at chapter 557 and contradicts chapter 558, where the Hunters have been evacuated, Lee Seungyeop awakens in an ambulance, and Song Song confirms that every member of his team survived.
- **Replacement:** The Forest of Giants encounter has ended, and all twenty rescued Hunters survived and were evacuated; Team Leader Lee Seungyeop later awakened in an ambulance and learned that every member of his team was safe.
- **Rationale:** Active continuity must reflect the latest chapter in the checkpoint rather than preserve an already resolved cliffhanger.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 557, Heavenly Strike introduction
- **Current:** The second and final form of the Blazing Flame Divine Spear, which concentrated three hundred years of the Fire Gate Clan’s macho spirit—the time-honored clan with absolutely no concept of retreat.
- **Defect:** The parent technique changes from Fire Dragon Divine Spear earlier in the same chapter to Blazing Flame Divine Spear when its second and final form is introduced.
- **Replacement:** The second and final form of the Fire Dragon Divine Spear, which concentrated three hundred years of the Fire Gate Clan’s macho spirit—the time-honored clan with absolutely no concept of retreat.
- **Rationale:** Calling Heavenly Strike the second and final form establishes that it belongs to the same technique whose first form, Fire Dragon’s Single Tail, was identified as part of the Fire Dragon Divine Spear.

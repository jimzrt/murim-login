# Review

The packet is broadly coherent and preserves its major hooks, but three continuity statements need correction: the Empress is described using two hands after losing one, the active state incorrectly places the guardian spirit with Jin, and the state overstates how far White Flame penetrated her chest. There are also minor summary, terminology, and punctuation drifts.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 707, Southern Heaven Demon Empress's rapid attack on the Beast Miao King
- **Current:** In the span of an instant, her hands and feet struck every part of the Beast Miao King’s body like flashes of lightning, making his massive frame jolt.
- **Defect:** The Empress has already lost one arm, so the plural “hands” contradicts her physical state.
- **Replacement:** In the span of an instant, her remaining hand and both feet struck every part of the Beast Miao King’s body like flashes of lightning, making his massive frame jolt.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the attack while maintaining the repeatedly established loss of one arm.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint Continuity, guardian spirit bullet
- **Current:** The guardian spirit urged Jin to flee but remains with him; the future of the guardian spirit and sacred stone is unresolved.
- **Defect:** The guardian spirit does not remain with Jin. It lies gravely wounded beside the unconscious Yayul Cheok while Jin takes the sacred stone and runs back into battle.
- **Replacement:** The guardian spirit urged Jin to flee and remains gravely wounded beside the unconscious Yayul Cheok; Jin took the diminished sacred stone with him, and both of their futures are unresolved.
- **Rationale:** The corrected state preserves the characters’ locations and records that the sacred stone, not the guardian spirit, accompanied Jin.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung has driven White Flame through the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's chest.
- **Defect:** “Through” overstates the reading copy, which says the spearhead was driven into the vicinity of her chest and later describes it as driven into her chest, without establishing that it passed completely through.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung has driven White Flame into the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's chest.
- **Rationale:** The active state should not add a degree of penetration absent from the chapter. The same “through” wording in the Plot and Continuity sections should be normalized to “into.”

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** Checkpoint Plot, third paragraph
- **Current:** The Beast Miao King overwhelms the exhausted Empress, but she awakens the innate qi she has carried since birth through the rift.
- **Defect:** The placement of “through the rift” makes it sound as though she carried the innate qi through the rift, rather than hearing the rift as the qi awakens.
- **Replacement:** The Beast Miao King overwhelms the exhausted Empress, but then she hears the rift as the innate qi she has carried since birth awakens.
- **Rationale:** This removes the modifier ambiguity without adding a causal claim beyond the reading copy.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 705, Southern Heaven Demon Empress realizing she may die
- **Current:** The two characters filling the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s mind now were death.
- **Defect:** “Two characters” is a source-language counting calque that clashes with the single English word presented to the reader.
- **Replacement:** Only one word filled the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s mind now: death.
- **Rationale:** The replacement conveys the same mental fixation in natural English and avoids an internally awkward character count.

## C06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapters 705–709, ellipsis punctuation; representative instance in Chapter 705
- **Current:** “……Baekcheon. The Baekcheon Unit.”
- **Defect:** The packet repeatedly uses the six-dot East Asian ellipsis instead of the required single ellipsis character.
- **Replacement:** “…Baekcheon. The Baekcheon Unit.”
- **Rationale:** The binding format requires “…” throughout. All other six-dot ellipses in the packet should be normalized the same way.

## C07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Chapters 706–709, internal-injury terminology; representative instance in Chapter 706
- **Current:** With the Beast Miao King’s help, I had partially recovered from my Internal Injury, and I had also learned that allies were already swarming outside the Inner Palace.
- **Defect:** The generic condition is capitalized in Chapters 706 and 707 but appears naturally lowercased as “internal injuries” in Chapter 709.
- **Replacement:** With the Beast Miao King’s help, I had partially recovered from my internal injury, and I had also learned that allies were already swarming outside the Inner Palace.
- **Rationale:** Lowercasing maintains the packet’s later usage and avoids presenting an ordinary injury description as a formal named term. The matching instance in Chapter 707 should be normalized as well.

# Review

The packet preserves the established voice, terminology, hooks, and active continuity well. One speaker-attribution error creates an internal contradiction, while several smaller issues involve unnatural English and inconsistent ellipsis formatting.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 73, Jin Mukyung’s internal assessment during the final spar
- **Current:** The Jin Family’s Spear Technique Jin Mukyung was using now was no different.
- **Defect:** The sentence attributes the spear technique to Jin Mukyung even though Jin Taekyung is wielding the spear while Mukyung defends with a sword.
- **Replacement:** The Jin Family’s Spear Technique Jin Taekyung was using now was no different.
- **Rationale:** Correcting the name restores the combatants’ established weapons and the paragraph’s focus on how Taekyung has made the technique his own.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 71, System panel after Jin Mukyung strikes Taekyung’s acupoints
- **Current:** - The **Paralysis Acupoint** has been subdued. You will be paralyzed for two hours!
>
> - The **Mute Acupoint** has been subdued. You will be unable to make a sound for two hours!
- **Defect:** “Acupoint has been subdued” is an unnatural collocation that reads like an untranslated system message.
- **Replacement:** - The **Paralysis Acupoint** has been sealed. You will be paralyzed for two hours!
>
> - The **Mute Acupoint** has been sealed. You will be unable to make a sound for two hours!
- **Rationale:** “Sealed” is natural wuxia terminology for disabling someone through an acupoint and remains consistent with the later notices that the effects have been released.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 73, Jin Mukyung’s assessment after ending the final spar
- **Current:** “To think this is all you managed to keep up with me after ten days. What a waste of my ti—”
- **Defect:** The first sentence is grammatically malformed and obscures the intended taunt.
- **Replacement:** “To think this is all you managed after ten days of trying to keep up with me. What a waste of my ti—”
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Mukyung’s dismissive pose while making the sentence natural and immediately comprehensible.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97

- **Current:** To cut to the end of the story, Jin Mukyung agreed to go to the Mount Heng Sword Sect as well.

- **Replacement:** Long story short, Jin Mukyung agreed to go to the Mount Heng Sword Sect as well.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 73, Jin Mukyung’s reply after using Sword Energy
- **Current:** “……Only an idiot would take that at face value.”
- **Defect:** The line uses two consecutive ellipsis characters, inconsistent with the packet’s prescribed single ellipsis character and the formatting used elsewhere.
- **Replacement:** “…Only an idiot would take that at face value.”
- **Rationale:** A single ellipsis preserves the pause while following the binding punctuation standard.

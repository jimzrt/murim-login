# Review

One clear pronoun error and one arrival claim that conflicts with the reading copy and active state.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 1115, Slaughter Saint’s recollection of the fight with the Black Ghosts
- **Current:** If the greatest archer under Heaven hadn’t helped him—a man who could tear apart a target from hundreds of jang away—even the Slaughter Saint would never have made it back in one piece.
- **Defect:** The appositive calls the Bow Saint a man, contradicting the established she/her decision and her pronouns later in the chapter.
- **Replacement:** If the greatest archer under Heaven hadn’t helped him—a woman who could tear apart a target from hundreds of jang away—even the Slaughter Saint would never have made it back in one piece.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the action and description intact while correcting the character reference.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Chapter 1118, defenders hearing the horn east of the gate

# Review

The packet largely maintains its voice and continuity. The actionable issues are an age contradiction, a name drift, a missing Sound Transmission marker, and two localized prose errors.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 873, Taekyung’s assessment of Prince Shangshan
- **Current:** The thirteen-year-old Prince Shangshan had already proved on his own that he could do what even adults who’d done nothing but age with the time handed to them could not.
- **Defect:** Shangshan says he is twelve in Chapter 872, and both speakers refer to him as twelve earlier in Chapter 873.
- **Replacement:** The twelve-year-old Prince Shangshan had already proved on his own that he could do what even adults who’d done nothing but age with the time handed to them could not.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the narration consistent with his stated age.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 873, immediately after Third Shadow’s execution
- **Current:** After wiping Samyeong’s blood from my face, I stepped in front of Prince Shangshan, who was breathing in short, shallow gasps.
- **Defect:** Samyeong is an unmapped romanization of the assassin called Third Shadow throughout the scene.
- **Replacement:** After wiping Third Shadow’s blood from my face, I stepped in front of Prince Shangshan, who was breathing in short, shallow gasps.
- **Rationale:** Uses the established rendering at a moment when the assassin’s identity matters.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 871, Taekyung’s exchange with Jeong Hogun
- **Current:** Are you saying you really don’t know, or that you know but won’t tell me?
- **Defect:** The line lacks the dash marking Sound Transmission, although Jeong immediately answers it through Sound Transmission.
- **Replacement:** —Are you saying you really don’t know, or that you know but won’t tell me?
- **Rationale:** Makes the exchange legible without implying Jeong answered an unspoken thought.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 871, entrance to the Emperor’s bedchamber
- **Current:** He threw open the door before them, decorated with gold, silver, and jewels, without the slightest hesitation, then spoke.
- **Defect:** The first-person narration slips into “them,” and the modifier makes it unclear whether the door or the people are decorated.
- **Replacement:** Without the slightest hesitation, he threw open the door before us, decorated with gold, silver, and jewels, then spoke.
- **Rationale:** Restores Taekyung’s viewpoint and makes the decoration attach clearly to the door.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 870, Taekyung’s Ministry of Punishments wordplay
- **Current:** Was this the same “brother-in-law” as the one who’d married your wife’s older sister?
- **Defect:** The kinship explanation does not support the following joke about Jeong Hogun being transgender: it describes the wife’s sister rather than the speaker’s older sister.
- **Replacement:** Was this the same “brother-in-law” a woman would call her older sister’s husband?
- **Rationale:** Makes the English setup lead into Taekyung’s next thought.

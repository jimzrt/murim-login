# Review

The packet is coherent in voice, plot continuity, and unresolved hooks, with no evident accidental spoilers. Actionable issues are concentrated in Chapter 298’s Markdown/System formatting drift and two incorrect surname references for Go Jun in Chapter 299.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 298, first Skeleton Warlord dialogue after the opening System panel
- **Current:** > - Ah, my soldiers. Forgive your disloyal commander. To be caught in the schemes of a wicked human and erased without even getting to demonstrate your true strength…
- **Defect:** The Skeleton Warlord’s dialogue is formatted as blockquoted material throughout this chapter, unlike the same speaker’s established plain hyphen-led dialogue in Chapters 295–297. It also visually conflates ordinary speech with framed notices.
- **Replacement:** - Ah, my soldiers. Forgive your disloyal commander. To be caught in the schemes of a wicked human and erased without even getting to demonstrate your true strength…
- **Rationale:** Restore the established dialogue presentation for the Warlord and reserve Markdown blockquotes for System panels or genuinely quoted material. The same formatting correction should be applied to the Warlord’s remaining dialogue in Chapter 298.

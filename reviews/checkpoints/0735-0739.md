# Review

Continuity, terminology, unresolved hooks, and spoiler boundaries remain consistent across Chapters 735–739. Three minor prose issues are actionable: one awkward quantifier, one ambiguous relative clause, and one stiff double negative.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 735, holographic meeting after Michael dismisses concern about Cheon Taemin
- **Current:** But this time, every one of them could not help turning the question over in their minds.
- **Defect:** The quantifier and negation combine awkwardly and can momentarily suggest that not everyone considered the question.
- **Replacement:** But this time, none of them could help turning the question over in their minds.
- **Rationale:** The replacement expresses the intended universal reaction clearly and in natural contemporary English without changing the meaning.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 736, first breaking-news report about the Paris Monster Wave
- **Current:** —A Monster Wave has occurred in Paris, France. The incident took place at a skyscraper in the 6th arrondissement of Paris, which has been identified as the Paris branch of Ares Guild, causing tremendous shock…
- **Defect:** The relative clause is grammatically ambiguous because “which” appears to modify Paris or the arrondissement rather than the skyscraper, while “causing tremendous shock” also lacks a clear referent.
- **Replacement:** —A Monster Wave has occurred in Paris, France. The incident took place at a skyscraper in Paris’s 6th arrondissement. The building has been identified as Ares Guild’s Paris branch, a revelation that has caused tremendous shock…
- **Rationale:** The replacement makes the building identification and the cause of the public shock unambiguous while preserving the report’s information and broadcast tone.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 739, narration before discussion of the Middle Eastern terrorist factions
- **Current:** No one was unaware of the bloodbath that had swept through the Middle East and Africa.
- **Defect:** The double negative is unnecessarily stiff and slows otherwise brisk expository narration.
- **Replacement:** Everyone knew about the bloodbath that had swept through the Middle East and Africa.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the universal meaning while matching the reading copy’s contemporary, direct narrative voice.

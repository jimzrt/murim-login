# Review

Continuity and formatting are strong overall. Actionable issues are one council seat-count contradiction, one malformed dialogue sentence, and two instances of drift from the established epithet “Ailao Mountain’s Wraith.”

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 642, tribal grand council seating
- **Current:** Only thirty-two seats were prepared for the grand council. That had been true a hundred years ago, and it was still true now.
- **Defect:** This contradicts the surrounding seat count: thirty-one seats are occupied and two are empty, so thirty-three seats have been prepared today.
- **Replacement:** For the past hundred years, only thirty-two seats had been prepared for the grand council.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the traditional count while allowing today’s unexplained extra seat to produce the stated total of two empty seats.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 640, Jin explaining why he killed the Thousand-Year Spider
- **Current:** “For the Nanman warriors who fell victim to the hands……no, the slime and webs of those bastards I could tear to pieces and still not feel satisfied.”
- **Defect:** The self-correction never closes grammatically, leaving “those bastards” attached to both the victims and the following clause.
- **Replacement:** “For the Nanman warriors who fell victim to the hands… no, the slime and webs of those bastards. Even tearing them to pieces wouldn’t satisfy me.”
- **Rationale:** This completes the hand-to-slime-and-webs joke and restores a clear, natural declaration of vengeance without changing the meaning.

# Review

The reading copies preserve the packet’s voice, hooks, and chronology overall. Actionable issues are a stale active-state entry that contradicts Chapter 239, several uses of “Namgung” for the formally established names Nangong Cheon and Nangong Ok, and three clear prose-level awkwardnesses.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** durable state, active_continuity entry 2
- **Current:** Taekyung completed a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern and remains bound to chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls whose key Jeok holds.
- **Defect:** The active state says Taekyung remains bound and Jeok still holds the relevant key, contradicting the end of Chapter 239, where Taekyung tears the shackles apart and drops the chains and iron balls.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung completed a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern while bound to chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls, but tore the shackles apart and dropped them before facing Nangong Cheon.
- **Rationale:** Active continuity must reflect the latest revealed state rather than the condition that existed during training.

# Review

The reading copies preserve the packet’s hooks, chronology, character voices, and unresolved questions without evident spoilers. Actionable issues are one energy-term error, one established-name drift, repeated nonstandard ellipsis formatting in Chapters 770 and 772, and two continuity-state sentences that incorrectly imply Jin is inside the Skeleton King’s concealed space.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 770, Skeleton King’s opening reflection
- **Current:** Even if he wore a human appearance now, once the Magic wrapped around his entire body was lifted, all that would be revealed was the skeleton of a dead man who had defied the fate of mortality.
- **Defect:** The capitalized term “Magic” conflicts with the established distinction between modern mana and Murim qi, and the surrounding phrasing is unnaturally literal.
- **Replacement:** Even if he looked human now, once the mana shrouding his entire body was dispelled, all that would remain was the skeleton of a dead man who had defied mortality.
- **Rationale:** This restores the binding modern-world energy term while preserving the disguise being removed and the Skeleton King’s underlying form.

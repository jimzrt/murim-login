There is also a recap mismatch between Chapters 173 and 174, several noncompliant ASCII ellipses in Chapter 173, two awkward cultivation-style collocations, and one System-term capitalization drift.

- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung killed Jin Baekyang, the Blade of Flowers, and was rendered unconscious with an Internal Injury by Jeok Cheongang's Flame Divine Palm.

# Review

The packet preserves the established action-comedy voice and most continuity, but it contains two binding terminology errors, one character-name substitution, inconsistent repeated dialogue, several formatting defects, and four state/summary entries that overlook the death immediately before the final charge and therefore overcount the last-stand participants.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 116, Pung Yang’s recollection of the tomb legacy
- **Current:** The Crimson Blood Twelve Swords. The Crimson Blood Cultivation Technique. And a wooden case containing five Temporary Strength Pills.
- **Defect:** The martial art is called Crimson Blood Twelve Swords here, contradicting the binding Crimson Blood Twelve Sabers terminology used later.
- **Replacement:** The Crimson Blood Twelve Sabers. The Crimson Blood Cultivation Technique. And a wooden case containing five Temporary Strength Pills.
- **Rationale:** The packet’s terminology decisions explicitly establish Crimson Blood Twelve Sabers, and Chapter 118 uses that rendering for the same martial art.

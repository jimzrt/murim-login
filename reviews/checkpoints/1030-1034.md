# Review

The main continuity error is a Black Ghost count of five where the surrounding account establishes six. The remaining actionable issues are a terminology mismatch and inconsistent capitalization of “disciple.”

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 1033, Jin Taekyung counting the Black Ghosts
- **Current:** “Then that makes five, counting the ones who’ve just appeared.”
- **Defect:** The count contradicts the two Black Ghosts that passed them and the four now approaching.
- **Replacement:** “Then that makes six, counting the ones who’ve just appeared.”
- **Rationale:** The next paragraph explicitly counts two plus four, leaving one of seven unaccounted for.

## C02 — Minor

- **Defect:** The weapon differs from the checkpoint’s established rendering.

- **Rationale:** The translation decision specifies “chain sickle” for this weapon.

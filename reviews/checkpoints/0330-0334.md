# Review

The packet is broadly coherent, with continuity and active state aligned. Two major destination errors in Chapter 333 contradict the established journey to Chengdu; several minor prose issues introduce awkward cultivation phrasing, an unclear joke, incorrect ship anatomy, or imprecise geography.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 330, opening of the Water Dragon Stronghold scene
- **Current:** With taut rigging and sterns slicing through the current, the fast ships surged forward like the wind.
- **Defect:** The stern is the rear of a ship and would not be the part slicing through the current as the ships surge forward.
- **Replacement:** With taut rigging and bows slicing through the current, the fast ships surged forward like the wind.
- **Rationale:** Using “bows” corrects the physical description without changing the action or rhythm.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 331, Mu Song's introduction
- **Current:** His bulging temples revealed that he was a Peak master who had reached a considerable realm stage.
- **Defect:** “Reached a considerable realm stage” is awkward cultivation-MTL phrasing and does not read naturally in contemporary English.
- **Replacement:** His bulging temples revealed that he was a highly accomplished Peak master.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves his demonstrated level of attainment while removing the unnatural collocation.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 333, Mu Song preparing to resume the journey
- **Current:** The current is rough, but the wind is favorable, so we should reach Xi’an soon.
- **Defect:** The party is traveling by ship from Guang’an to Chengdu, not Xi’an. This contradicts Chapters 331 and 334 as well as the checkpoint summary and active continuity.
- **Replacement:** The current is rough, but the wind is favorable, so we should reach Chengdu soon.
- **Rationale:** Chengdu is the established destination and the city where the party disembarks three days later.

## C04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 333, Mungyeong requesting passage
- **Current:** The thing is, I happen to be on my way to Xi’an too. If you would give me a ride just this once, I’ll repay this kindness even beyond the grave, remember it to my very bones, and even if this body dies and dies, dies a thousand times over and turns to white bones, I will never forget—
- **Defect:** Mungyeong names Xi’an as his destination even though he boards the ship bound for Chengdu and later continues toward the Sichuan Tang Clan. This repeats the preceding destination error and breaks journey continuity.
- **Replacement:** The thing is, I happen to be on my way to Chengdu too. If you would give me a ride just this once, I’ll repay this kindness even beyond the grave, remember it to my very bones, and even if this body dies and dies, dies a thousand times over and turns to white bones, I will never forget—

## C06 — Minor

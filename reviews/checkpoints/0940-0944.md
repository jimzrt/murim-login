# Review

The packet’s voice, terminology, hooks, and active state are largely consistent. Two English passages warrant correction for clarity and internal consistency.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 940, Baek Yeon’s account of the Great Faction War
- **Current:** The late Emperor had seriously considered ordering a campaign, but Baek Yeon had pleaded with him so earnestly—more than he had ever seen him do before—that the Emperor finally gave up the idea.
- **Defect:** The nested pronouns make it unclear who had seen whom plead before.
- **Replacement:** The late Emperor had seriously considered ordering a campaign, but Baek Yeon had pleaded with him more earnestly than ever before, until the Emperor finally gave up the idea.
- **Rationale:** Preserves Baek Yeon’s unusually forceful opposition without the confusing point of comparison.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 943, Taekyung’s assessment of potential reinforcements
- **Current:** There wasn’t a single martial artist to be found in Zhejiang Province, where the imperial capital stood—not even a sect.
- **Defect:** The absolute claim contradicts the martial artists already traveling with Taekyung in Zhejiang.
- **Replacement:** There wasn’t a single Murim sect to call on in Zhejiang Province, where the imperial capital stood.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the point that Zhejiang offers no sect-based reinforcements without denying the presence of the party’s martial artists.

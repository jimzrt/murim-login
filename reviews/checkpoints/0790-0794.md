# Review

The reading copies are largely consistent, but one camera description has an unclear referent, one comment breaks the ellipsis convention, and the checkpoint summary overstates what Jin and the Skeleton King learned.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 790, National Assembly footage
- **Current:** Of all the many cameras installed throughout the National Assembly to capture a moment in history, it was the only one to survive the aftermath of the tremendous battle.
- **Defect:** “It” refers grammatically to the microchip in Choi Minwoo’s hand, not a camera.
- **Replacement:** The chip held footage from the only camera to survive the aftermath of the tremendous battle, out of the many installed throughout the National Assembly to capture a moment in history.
- **Rationale:** Clarifies which object survived without changing the account of the footage.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** the Skeleton King reports that an unidentified presence appeared and vanished without a trace.
- **Defect:** “Appeared” presents the unidentified thing as an observed arrival; the Skeleton King says he could not find it and that it had vanished.
- **Replacement:** the Skeleton King reports that whatever killed them had vanished without a trace.
- **Rationale:** Preserves the uncertainty around the killer without inventing an appearance.

## C04 — Major

- **Replacement:** Whatever killed them disappeared without leaving a trace; the Skeleton King did not see it.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the unresolved presence unidentified and distinguishes his inference from direct sight.

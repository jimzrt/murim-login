# Review

One major continuity-state defect incorrectly leaves Ju Hwaran at Gowolru after her departure. Three minor prose defects create localized voice or idiom drift.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Ju Hwaran is with Taekyung at Gowolru, Song Ilseom remains her direct escort, and her former political engagement to Sama Pyo was accepted for her father's sake.
- **Defect:** The active state is stale: Chapter 534 explicitly has Ju Hwaran leave Gowolru with Song Ilseom to handle Escort Bureau business.

- **Rationale:** Active continuity must reflect the end-of-checkpoint character positions or later chapters may incorrectly place Ju Hwaran with Taekyung.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 530, Taekyung reflecting on Hyuk Mujin's concern
- **Current:** Hyuk Mujin usually didn’t show it very well, but he was a tender spot for me.
- **Defect:** The idiom is malformed and reverses the apparent relationship: a person has a soft spot for someone; he is not a tender spot for them.
- **Replacement:** Hyuk Mujin usually didn’t show it very well, but he had a soft spot for me.
- **Rationale:** The replacement matches Taekyung's belief that Hyuk Mujin is worried about him and restores natural contemporary English.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 531, Taekyung explaining the principles governing Murim
- **Current:** And now, he had realized once again that the Murim of the world operated according to two things:
- **Defect:** “The Murim of the world” is an unnatural noun order, while “operated according to” introduces formal, mechanical diction into Taekyung's conversational narration.

- **Rationale:** The revision preserves the setup for the following two-item list while restoring Taekyung's brisk narrative voice.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** Chapter 532, Taekyung reacting to Ju Hwaran's laugh
- **Current:** A small laugh drifted over from beside me.

I turned toward the owner of that laugh, whose sound made my ears itch just by reaching them.
- **Defect:** “Whose sound made my ears itch just by reaching them” is a conspicuous syntactic calque and awkwardly attaches the relative clause to the laugh's owner.
- **Replacement:** A small laugh drifted over from beside me.

The sound alone made my ears itch, and I turned toward its owner.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Taekyung's flustered physical reaction without the strained relative construction.

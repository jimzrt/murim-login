# Review

The reading copies are broadly coherent and retain Jin’s established voice, but the checkpoint summary overstates two causal conclusions, Chapter 593 has two instances of terminology drift, and the durable state drops established Peace Guild casualties. A few localized English constructions also need correction.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot paragraph 1
- **Current:** Jin spares Se-won after learning that Go Jun, rather than Se-won’s own choices, made him believe he was abandoning his family.
- **Defect:** The summary invents a causal conclusion about Go Jun making Se-won believe he was abandoning his family. Jin instead discovers that Se-won intended to resign, sees the incoming call and family photograph, and hears Se-won condemn his boss.
- **Replacement:** Jin spares Se-won after discovering that he intended to resign and seeing his wife’s incoming call over their family photograph.
- **Rationale:** This states the revealed reasons for Jin’s decision without attributing an unsupported belief or action to Go Jun.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot paragraph 2
- **Current:** Go Jun remains in Area A to face Jin. He remembers how Lee Jungryong found him among the war orphans and made him his Disciple, while the black jewel in his treasured necklace mysteriously allows Jin to breach Area A’s barriers.
- **Defect:** The summary presents the jewel as the mechanism that allows Jin to breach the barriers, although the chapter establishes only that it emits a strange light as the intrusion begins. Its function remains an explicit open question.
- **Replacement:** Go Jun remains in Area A to face Jin. He remembers how Lee Jungryong found him among the war orphans and made him his Disciple; as Jin breaches Area A’s barriers, the black jewel in his treasured necklace emits a strange light for reasons still unknown.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the observed timing without prematurely resolving the jewel’s causal role.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 593, explanation of Striking Second, Hitting First
- **Current:** “Striking Second, Hitting First. Someone who gained strength from a Magic Gem like you would never understand it, even if you died and came back to life.”
- **Defect:** The placement of “like you” momentarily makes it modify “Magic Gem,” producing an awkward and potentially comic misreading.
- **Replacement:** “Striking Second, Hitting First. Someone like you, who gained strength from a Magic Gem, would never understand it, even if you died and came back to life.”
- **Rationale:** The revision removes the grammatical ambiguity while preserving Jin’s contemptuous explanation.

## C06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 594, realization that Go Jun survived
- **Current:** Even if he had soaked in a half-body bath of top-grade potion, reviving him should have been impossible.

# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice and formatting, but Chapter 69 contains one quantity error and one established-term drift. The checkpoint summary and durable state repeatedly treat the deliberately fabricated assassin story as a genuine unresolved attack, and the durable state retains the superseded fifteen-day cohabitation period instead of the final ten days.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary — Plot, first paragraph
- **Current:** Taekyung and the badly injured Hyuk Mujin are taken to the Medicine King Hall, while the Jin Family mistakes the incident for an assassin’s attack and begins a pursuit for the Head Elder’s possible hidden disciple.
- **Defect:** This attributes the misunderstanding and pursuit to the Jin Family as a whole, obscuring that Jin Wikyung knowingly turns the household's misunderstanding into a fabricated cover story.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung and the badly injured Hyuk Mujin are taken to the Medicine King Hall. While the wider household mistakes the incident for an assassin’s attack, Jin Wikyung deliberately uses that misunderstanding to create a pursuit for the Head Elder’s supposed hidden disciple.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 66 explicitly establishes that Jin Wikyung and Wipeng know there was no assassin and deliberately exploit the story for Wipeng’s real mission.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary — Continuity, second bullet
- **Current:** The Jin Family is pursuing an unidentified assassin believed possibly to be the Head Elder’s hidden disciple. The assassin’s identity, sponsor, and possible connection to Song Sword Sect remain unknown.
- **Defect:** The continuity note converts a fabricated cover story and Wipeng's deliberate intimidation of Song Sword Sect into genuine unresolved mysteries.
- **Replacement:** The Jin Family is publicly pursuing a nonexistent assassin alleged to be the Head Elder’s hidden disciple. Jin Wikyung created the cover story for Wipeng’s real mission, and Wipeng’s insinuation at Song Sword Sect was a warning rather than evidence of its involvement.
- **Rationale:** Readers are shown both the invention of the pursuit and Wipeng's strategic use of the alleged trail; neither the assassin nor a connection to Song Sword Sect is an unresolved factual hook.

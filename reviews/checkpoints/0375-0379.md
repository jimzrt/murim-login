# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice and terminology, but the checkpoint materials incorrectly preserve two resolved identity mysteries: the boarding boy is Mungyeong, and the burning aircraft is Taekyung’s private jet. The durable state also lags the final scene and omits its immediate cliffhanger. Minor English-copy issues include two awkward collocations, inconsistent monster-name capitalization, and an untranslated Korean-style sound effect.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** Taekyung’s party leaves Chengdu aboard Water Dragon Stronghold ships, while an unidentified boy asks for passage.
- **Defect:** The summary treats the boy as unidentified even though Chapter 377 reveals that the boy aboard the ship is Mungyeong in disguise.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung’s party leaves Chengdu aboard Water Dragon Stronghold ships after allowing a boy—Mungyeong in disguise—to board.
- **Rationale:** The packet summary should include the identity revealed within the same reviewed range rather than preserve a resolved hook.

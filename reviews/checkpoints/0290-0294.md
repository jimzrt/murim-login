# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves its active hooks without accidental spoilers or internal contradictions. Eight minor corrections are actionable: two US-English spelling inconsistencies, one capitalization drift, one terminology-capitalization drift, and four awkward or calqued constructions.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 290, opening internal monologue
- **Current:** Was it when I achieved Great Completion in the Jin Family's Manoeuvre Technique? Or the Jin Family's Spear Technique?
- **Defect:** “Manoeuvre” uses British spelling despite the requirement for contemporary US English.
- **Replacement:** Was it when I achieved Great Completion in the Jin Family's Maneuver Technique? Or the Jin Family's Spear Technique?
- **Rationale:** The established technique name should use consistent US-English spelling throughout the reading copy.

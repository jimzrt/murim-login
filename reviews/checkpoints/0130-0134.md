# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves its hooks, tone, and active continuity. One established character name drifts, Chapter 132 unnecessarily repeats a footnote from the preceding chapter, and one sentence in Chapter 134 has an awkward possessive construction.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 134, Woo Jintae’s beating
- **Current:** I may be the first man ever to treat you so casually, but please don’t fall in love with me. I’m busy enough loving one Song-i.
- **Defect:** The established name Song Song is rendered as Song-i, creating terminology drift and making the line appear to refer to a different character.
- **Replacement:** I may be the first man ever to treat you so casually, but please don’t fall in love with me. I’m busy enough loving my one and only Song Song.
- **Rationale:** The checkpoint summary and durable state consistently identify Taekyung’s beloved as Song Song. The replacement restores that established name while preserving the exclusivity expressed by “one.”

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 132, opening
- **Current:** “If it wouldn’t be too much trouble, may I have just one candied hawthorn skewer?[^1]”

[^1]: Candied hawthorn skewers are a traditional Chinese snack made by coating skewered fruit in hardened sugar.
- **Defect:** The candied hawthorn footnote is repeated immediately after the term was already defined in Chapter 131.
- **Replacement:** “If it wouldn’t be too much trouble, may I have just one candied hawthorn skewer?”
- **Rationale:** The binding footnote rules prohibit repeating a definition unnecessarily, and consecutive reading copies require no second explanation here.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 134, Woo Jintae refusing to apologize
- **Current:** Born the sole male heir the Seongun Escort Bureau’s family had produced in three generations, he had been pampered and revered his entire life.
- **Defect:** The possessive phrase “the Seongun Escort Bureau’s family” is awkward and suggests that the organization itself possesses a family.
- **Replacement:** As the only male heir born into the family behind the Seongun Escort Bureau in three generations, he had been pampered and revered his entire life.
- **Rationale:** The replacement expresses the same lineage fact in natural English without changing Woo Jintae’s status or upbringing.

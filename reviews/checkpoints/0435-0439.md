# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice, terminology, formatting, and spoiler control. The main issue is that the durable active state drops several unresolved Murim-side developments from chapters 435–438 and cites only chapter 439 as its continuity source. A few minor prose edits are also warranted for unidiomatic or opaque English.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, end of active_continuity
- **Current:** "Team Leader Choi manages Taekyung's media and official arrangements but must consult him before making political commitments."
- **Defect:** The active state ends with the modern-world developments and drops the still-active Murim-side continuity established in chapters 435–438: Mungyeong's concealed identity and continued presence, Taekyung's newly opened Middle Dantian and fabricated explanation, Jeok Cheongang's supervision and warning, and the ongoing journey to Hubei.
- **Replacement:** "Team Leader Choi manages Taekyung's media and official arrangements but must consult him before making political commitments.",
    "Mungyeong remains with Jin Taekyung's group under the identity of a young medical apprentice, while his identity as the former Divine Physician and Slaughter Saint remains concealed.",

    "Jeok Cheongang has begun supervising Taekyung's training, rejects the suspicion that Taekyung used demonic martial arts, and has ordered him to avoid Mungyeong.",
    "The group is traveling toward Hubei and expects to remain in transit for more than a week, allowing Taekyung to alternate between Murim and the modern world."
- **Rationale:** These developments remain operational at the checkpoint boundary and are needed for a reliable handoff into chapter 440.

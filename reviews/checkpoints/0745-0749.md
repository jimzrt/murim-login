# Review

The reading copies remain coherent and preserve the packet’s major hooks, but the checkpoint summary overstates two actions and misstates two active mechanics. The reading copies also contain one ambiguous sentence and several formatting violations involving Korean-style ellipses and a System continuation marker outside its panel.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** checkpoint Plot, first paragraph
- **Current:** The Prophet meets the hidden Hasasin in a desert, using an undetectable transparent barrier to conceal their base and displaying a hoard of Magic Gems and enchanted weapons. He absorbs ten unrefined S-rank Magic Gems and orders Al-Nizar and the Hasasin to prepare another judgment against apostates and Western heretics.
- **Defect:** The summary introduces two unsupported certainties: the concealed area is not established as the Hasasin’s base, and the Prophet draws the Magic Gems into the robe rather than explicitly absorbing them.
- **Replacement:** The Prophet meets the hidden Hasasin in a desert, surrounds the meeting area with a transparent barrier undetectable by science or Magic, and reveals a hoard of Magic Gems and enchanted weapons. He draws ten unrefined S-rank Magic Gems into his robe and orders Al-Nizar and the Hasasin to prepare another judgment against apostates and Western heretics.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the actions shown in Chapter 745 without assigning an unconfirmed function to the location or an unconfirmed mechanism to the Magic Gems.

- **Defect:** Broken Body is not identified as potentially permanent; the possible permanent consequence is attribute loss during recovery. The combat attributes are currently reduced by 50 points rather than already lost.

- **Rationale:** Chapter 749 distinguishes the active debuff, its temporary 50-point penalty, and the separate risk of permanent attribute loss during recovery.

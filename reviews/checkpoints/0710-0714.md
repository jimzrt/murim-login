# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice, terminology, and formatting. Actionable issues are concentrated in the durable state, which drops Jeok Cheongang’s discovery of the System and overstates Baekhwi’s present condition; the plot summary also mistakes Baeksang’s final performance for genuine loyalty. Three minor prose defects remain.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung was unconscious for seven days after Baeksang's death and has now awakened.
- **Defect:** The durable state drops the unresolved hook that Jeok Cheongang witnessed Jin’s five System level-ups and was promised an explanation.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung was unconscious for seven days after Baeksang's death and has now awakened; Jeok Cheongang witnessed his five System level-ups, and Jin promised to explain what happened later.
- **Rationale:** Jeok’s discovery materially changes what he knows about Jin and remains unresolved at the checkpoint boundary.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Dark Heaven has kept Baekhwi alive in a deep sleep, and Baeksang accepted the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's bargain to recover him.
- **Defect:** The present-perfect wording treats Baekhwi’s continued survival as established, contradicting the open question and Baeksang’s final thought that his son may already be awaiting him in the afterlife.
- **Replacement:** Dark Heaven's sorcerers had kept Baekhwi alive in a deep sleep as of Baeksang's last visit, and Baeksang accepted the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's bargain in hopes of recovering him; Baekhwi's current condition remains unresolved.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the last confirmed state without prematurely resolving whether Baekhwi survived the collapse of Dark Heaven’s plan.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, fourth paragraph
- **Current:** After warning that Dark Heaven will eventually devour Nanman and declaring his loyalty to the Lord of Heaven, Baeksang asks the Beast Miao King to execute him.
- **Defect:** The summary reverses the sequence and presents Baeksang’s deliberately staged final performance as genuine loyalty.
- **Replacement:** After asking the Beast Miao King to execute him, Baeksang publicly poses as a loyal servant of the Lord of Heaven and warns that Dark Heaven will eventually devour Nanman, ensuring that he dies as a traitor without public sympathy.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 711, treatment of Wang Ho
- **Current:** I grasped his wrist pulse as he had charged at the Southern Heaven Demon Empress until the final moment, then sent internal energy through his back and along his spine with my other hand.
- **Defect:** The temporal clause attaches to the act of checking Wang Ho’s pulse, making it sound as though Jin grasped him while Wang Ho was still charging.
- **Replacement:** I grasped Wang Ho’s wrist to check his pulse. He had charged at the Southern Heaven Demon Empress until the final moment. Then I placed my other hand against his back and sent internal energy along his spine.
- **Rationale:** Separating the past action from the present treatment removes the malformed attachment without changing either event.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 712, Jeok Cheongang’s interruption
- **Current:** It was Jeok Cheongang’s shout. He had been tending to the Beast Miao King’s Internal Injury.
- **Defect:** “Internal Injury” is unnecessarily singular and capitalized, drifting from the established lowercase prose rendering used elsewhere in the packet.

- **Rationale:** This is ordinary narration rather than a named technique or System term, and Chapter 711 already describes the Beast Miao King’s “severe internal injuries.”

## C06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 710, Jeok Cheongang examining Jin’s injuries
- **Current:** An armor shattered so thoroughly that almost none of its original shape remained. Limbs twisted at unnatural angles.
- **Defect:** “Armor” is being treated as a count noun, and the two fragments read like translation notes rather than polished narrative observation.
- **Replacement:** His armor had been shattered so thoroughly that almost none of its original shape remained, and his limbs were twisted at unnatural angles.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores idiomatic English and keeps the same visual details and grave tone.

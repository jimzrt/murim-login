# Review

The packet is broadly consistent in voice, continuity, and established terminology. Two substantive contradictions remain: the Sichuan City Lord’s name changes within Chapter 350, and Chapter 354 relabels the enemy’s distinct black artifacts as ordinary poison-warding pearls. The durable state also contains one premature outcome and one minor title-grammar inconsistency, while Chapter 352 has a small em-dash spacing error.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 350, discussion of the government search
- **Current:** Sichuan City Lord Heo Gyun looked exactly like a corrupt official, but it seemed he wasn’t completely incompetent.
- **Defect:** The official identified earlier in the same passage as Won Gyun is called Heo Gyun here, creating an internal name contradiction.
- **Replacement:** Sichuan City Lord Won Gyun looked exactly like a corrupt official, but it seemed he wasn’t completely incompetent.
- **Rationale:** The chapter explicitly introduces Won Gyun as the City Lord of Sichuan Province shortly before this sentence, so the later name should remain consistent.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 354, Tang Jinhu battle sequence
- **Current:** The black-clad men were more skilled than the Sichuan Tang Clan martial artists, and the armor and bizarre poison-warding pearls they carried rendered the Tang Clan’s famed poisons and hidden weapons useless.
- **Defect:** The narration calls the black beads poison-warding pearls immediately after establishing that they are artifacts fundamentally different from poison-warding pearls.
- **Replacement:** The black-clad men were more skilled than the Sichuan Tang Clan martial artists, and the armor and bizarre black artifacts they carried rendered the Tang Clan’s famed poisons and hidden weapons useless.
- **Rationale:** Tang Jinhu has just distinguished the ominous black beads from ordinary clear poison-warding pearls; preserving that distinction avoids contradicting the revealed mechanism.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Taekyung remains with Jeok while Cheongpung aids the Tang Clan and returns.

- **Rationale:** Removing the unfulfilled return preserves the current endpoint and avoids prematurely resolving Taekyung’s request that Cheongpung come back.

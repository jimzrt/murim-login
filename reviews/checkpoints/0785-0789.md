# Review

The packet is largely consistent. One name drifts, and a news report contradicts the established capture of Michael Silbert’s followers.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 787, Hunters carrying Jin Taekyung
- **Current:** The unusually small, heavily callused hand belonged to Faye Chen.
- **Defect:** The character is called Pai Chen earlier in the packet.
- **Replacement:** The unusually small, heavily callused hand belonged to Pai Chen.
- **Rationale:** Restores the name used in Chapters 785 and 786.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** Chapter 788, breaking-news announcement
- **Current:** World Hunter Federation’s first official announcement: “Two hours ago, we summarily executed Michael Silbert and his followers, who betrayed humanity. The operation is still ongoing.”
- **Defect:** The announcement says all his followers were executed, but Chapter 786 establishes that some were captured, including Huginn.
- **Replacement:** World Hunter Federation’s first official announcement: “Two hours ago, we summarily executed Michael Silbert and the traitors who fought alongside him at the National Assembly. The operation is still ongoing.”
- **Rationale:** Limits the execution claim to the battle at the National Assembly without erasing the captives or the continuing operation.

# Review

The reading copies are generally coherent and maintain the established action-comedy voice. Actionable issues include one incorrect form of address, an unrevealed Mapo Bridge detail appearing twice in the checkpoint material, two small continuity overstatements, and a few localized prose or terminology slips.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 286, nurse announcing Im Hyeokjun's recovery
- **Current:** “Mr. Lee! Patient Im Hyeokjun is conscious!”
- **Defect:** The nurse addresses Taekyung as Mr. Lee even though his surname is Jin, creating a direct name-continuity error.
- **Replacement:** “Mr. Jin! Patient Im Hyeokjun is conscious!”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung immediately responds to the announcement, and no relevant Mr. Lee is present in the scene.

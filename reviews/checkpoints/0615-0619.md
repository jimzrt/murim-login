# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice, terminology, hooks, and System formatting. Three minor prose calques remain, and the checkpoint summary and active state overstate the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group’s headcount by treating the nearly thirty people as nearly thirty men plus one woman.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 616, Song Ilseom conversation by the water
- **Current:** At my certain accusation, Song Ilseom, who had been sitting by the water, awkwardly looked around.
- **Defect:** “At my certain accusation” is an unnatural English collocation and reads like a Korean syntax calque.
- **Replacement:** Faced with my accusation, Song Ilseom, who had been sitting by the water, looked around awkwardly.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the action and tone while using natural contemporary English.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 617, opening arrival of the Water Dragon Stronghold ships
- **Current:** For a martial artist who had reached a realm stage, it went without saying.
- **Defect:** “Reached a realm stage” is generic cultivation-MTL phrasing and does not sound natural in the established commercial prose.
- **Replacement:** Any martial artist who had reached a certain level could spot them with ease.
- **Rationale:** This states the same observation directly without the stiff terminology or dangling implication.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 617, activities aboard the ship
- **Current:** Sama Pyo and Song Ilseom devoted themselves to their own martial arts training after the first day without a single clash between them.
- **Defect:** The placement of “after the first day” is ambiguous: it can modify either their training or the absence of clashes.
- **Replacement:** From the second day onward, Sama Pyo and Song Ilseom devoted themselves to their own martial arts training without clashing even once.
- **Rationale:** The replacement makes both the timeline and their continued restraint immediately clear.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint Plot summary, Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group
- **Current:** The group’s nearly thirty men and one woman were later found dead from deadly venom.
- **Defect:** Chapter 619 describes nearly thirty people in total—one woman and the remaining members men—not nearly thirty men plus one woman.
- **Replacement:** All members of the group—one woman and nearly thirty people in total—were later found dead after being poisoned with deadly venom.
- **Rationale:** This removes the one-person headcount inflation and matches the chapter’s account.

# Review

The reading copies carry Qinghai Lake through the crossing but call it a river in four places. Chapter 1079 also repeats a reply.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** Chapter 1075, closing paragraph
- **Current:** A gentle ripple stirred in the wide, blue river beyond the thick veil of fog.
- **Defect:** Calls the water a river immediately before the scene identifies it as Qinghai Lake.
- **Replacement:** A gentle ripple stirred on the wide, blue lake beyond the thick veil of fog.
- **Rationale:** The next chapter identifies the blue water as Qinghai Lake.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 1076, opening
- **Current:** The river there was blue, and as wide as the sea.
- **Defect:** Calls Qinghai Lake a river in the passage that names it.
- **Replacement:** The water there was blue, and as wide as the sea.
- **Rationale:** The following sentences identify the body of water as a lake.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1078, ship crossing
- **Current:** I was silently watching the river waters of Qinghai Lake, which had a thin layer of ice in places from the bitter cold, when someone’s voice drifted to my ears.
- **Defect:** Describes the lake’s waters as river waters.
- **Replacement:** I was silently watching the waters of Qinghai Lake, which had a thin layer of ice in places from the bitter cold, when someone’s voice drifted to my ears.
- **Rationale:** The party is crossing Qinghai Lake by ship.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** Chapter 1079, chime before arrival
- **Current:** A clear chime rang out above the rushing river.
- **Defect:** Calls the water beneath the crossing ship a river.
- **Replacement:** A clear chime rang out above the water rushing past the ship.
- **Rationale:** The ship is still crossing Qinghai Lake; the replacement retains the sense of moving water without changing the location.

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1079, Taekyung addressing Cheongpung
- **Current:** “Yes?”

“Yes?”
- **Defect:** Duplicates Cheongpung’s reply with no intervening action or speaker change.
- **Replacement:** “Yes?”
- **Rationale:** One reply completes the exchange; the second reads as an accidental duplicate.

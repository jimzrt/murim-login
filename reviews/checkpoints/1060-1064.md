# Review

Three actionable continuity and wording defects: the Great Sir’s chosen name lacks its dialogue cue, the Qinghai messenger changes species between chapters, and a self-correction repeats the same title.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** Chapter 1061, Jeok Cheongang’s interruption
- **Current:** “You bastard, you’re the biggest piece of shit under heaven!”
- **Defect:** The Great Sir immediately adopts “Madman” from what Jeok supposedly said, but Jeok never says it.
- **Replacement:** “You bastard, you’re the biggest madman under heaven!”
- **Rationale:** Restores the audible cue for the Great Sir’s response while retaining Jeok’s insult and its superlative.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 1063, transition to the Qinghai message
- **Current:** Until a peregrine falcon, having flown from Qinghai on the distant western frontier, completed its weary journey.
- **Defect:** The messenger is called a peregrine falcon here but a messenger eagle throughout the immediate Chapter 1064 continuation.
- **Replacement:** Until a messenger eagle, having flown from Qinghai on the distant western frontier, completed its weary journey.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the same bird identifiable across the chapter break; the later description of its message and interception consistently calls it an eagle.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 1063, final sentence
- **Current:** A missive, wet with blood, was tied to the leg of a falcon gliding through the air with its great wings spread wide.
- **Defect:** The bird is again called a falcon immediately before Chapter 1064 identifies it as a messenger eagle.
- **Replacement:** A missive, wet with blood, was tied to the leg of an eagle gliding through the air with its great wings spread wide.
- **Rationale:** Maintains continuity at the precise chapter handoff.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 1064, Taekyung recalls the Grand Mage
- **Current:** The Grand Mage.

No—the Grand Mage.
- **Defect:** The apparent self-correction repeats the same title, leaving no distinction for the reader to follow.
- **Replacement:** The Grand Mage.
- **Rationale:** Removes a nonfunctional correction without inventing an earlier name or identity.

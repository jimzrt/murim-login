# Review

The reading copies largely maintain the intended voice and uncertainty. A few lines misidentify a speaker’s referent or state an unconfirmed rumor as fact; the summary and state also overlook the opening line revealed from Taekyung’s letter.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 877, Taekyung’s recollection after Hong Jin describes the late Emperor
- **Current:** At the words *Dark Heaven*, I remembered that cursed creature I’d seen only about two weeks ago.
- **Defect:** No one says “Dark Heaven” in the preceding exchange.
- **Replacement:** I thought of Dark Heaven—and remembered that cursed creature I’d seen only about two weeks ago.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the connection in Taekyung’s thoughts without attributing words to the dialogue.

## C02 — Minor

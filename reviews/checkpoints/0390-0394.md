# Review

The packet is generally coherent in voice and continuity, but it contains two character/affiliation errors, a chapter-wide dialogue-formatting inconsistency, one item-term drift, and an unresolved child-related hook omitted from the active state.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 390, explanation of the western front's division of labor
- **Current:** When the Hunters of the Public Security Armed Forces Department, myself included, defeated the monsters at the front, the military forces following behind would ‘clean up’ the area and the monster corpses.
- **Defect:** The parenthetical incorrectly identifies Jin Taekyung as a member of the Public Security Armed Forces Department rather than an allied Hunter fighting alongside it.
- **Replacement:** When the Hunters of the Public Security Armed Forces Department and I defeated the monsters at the front, the military forces following behind would ‘clean up’ the area and the monster corpses.
- **Rationale:** Jin is a Korean ally operating with the department, not one of its Hunters; the current wording creates an affiliation contradiction.

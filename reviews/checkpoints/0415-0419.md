# Review

The packet is coherent overall, but it contains two substantive continuity defects: Lee is described as unwounded despite being injured in the preceding fight, and the active state's open question still refers to three Hunters after Wu and Lee have died. The chapter-boundary reprise between 416 and 417 also drifts in wording, while Chapter 418 introduces systematic typography inconsistencies.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 419, opening confrontation
- **Current:** Though he had not suffered a single wound, he felt as if he had been cut by a blade.
- **Defect:** This contradicts Chapter 417, where Jin's spear cuts Lee Jungryong and draws blood.
- **Replacement:** Though he had suffered only a slight wound, he felt as if he had been cut by a blade.
- **Rationale:** The revision preserves the intended contrast between Lee's limited physical injury and his sudden sense of danger without erasing the wound already established on-page.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, open_questions
- **Current:** Can the three Hunters stop the city's Gate transformation?
- **Defect:** The question is stale after Jin kills both Wu Heixing and Lee Jungryong; only Jin remains to confront the Arch Lich.
- **Replacement:** Can Jin Taekyung stop the city's Gate transformation?
- **Rationale:** Active state must reflect the end-of-packet situation rather than the three-person strike force that entered the city.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 416, final paragraph
- **Current:** “These fucking bastards are more interested in the back of my head than the Arch Lich. That’s what I’m certain of.”
- **Defect:** “Interested in the back of my head” is an unnatural Korean-idiom calque and disagrees with the natural rendering used when the line is reprised in Chapter 417.
- **Replacement:** “These fucking bastards are more interested in stabbing me in the back than fighting the Arch Lich. That’s what I’m certain of.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the betrayal metaphor, reads naturally in Jin's voice, and makes the chapter-boundary reprise consistent.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 417, opening reprise
- **Current:** “Rather… certainty.”

“Certainty?”

“Yes.”

The corners of my mouth rose before I could stop them. I threw out the one thing I had pressed down and held back time and time again.

“I’m certain these fucking bastards are more interested in stabbing me in the back than fighting the Arch Lich. Something like that.”
- **Defect:** The reprise is malformed and drifts from the exchange at the end of Chapter 416. “Rather… certainty” is not idiomatic dialogue, and “Something like that” weakens what the narration presents as an unequivocal accusation.
- **Replacement:** “If anything, I’m certain.”

“Certain?”

“Yes.”

The corners of my mouth rose before I could stop them. I threw out the one thing I had pressed down and held back time and time again.

“These fucking bastards are more interested in stabbing me in the back than fighting the Arch Lich. That’s what I’m certain of.”
- **Rationale:** This restores natural English and makes the overlapping material match the preceding chapter without changing Jin's meaning or tone.

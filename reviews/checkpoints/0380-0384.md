# Review

The reading copies and state are broadly consistent in voice, formatting, and continuity. Four actionable issues remain: one character-name typo, two instances that incorrectly elevate the incomplete Liches to Arch Liches, and one summary quantity that conflicts with the chapter.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 384, Magic Johnson introduction
- **Current:** I felt even more nervous than I had when facing Chairman Shao Yang.
- **Defect:** Xiao Yang is incorrectly called Shao Yang, conflating his name with Shao Shen's.
- **Replacement:** I felt even more nervous than I had when facing Chairman Xiao Yang.
- **Rationale:** The chapter and binding terminology establish the state chairman's name as Xiao Yang.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity
- **Current:** The Chengdu International Airport’s monster army was controlled by three incomplete Arch Liches, all destroyed by Taekyung and the Skeleton Warlord.
- **Defect:** The three controllers are incorrectly identified as incomplete Arch Liches. They were incomplete Liches serving a separate Arch Lich.
- **Replacement:** The Chengdu International Airport’s monster army was controlled by three incomplete Liches serving an Arch Lich, all destroyed by Taekyung and the Skeleton Warlord.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 381 explicitly states that they had not yet completed their transformation into Liches and served the Arch Lich; calling them Arch Liches collapses the established hierarchy.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** The Chengdu International Airport attack involved an undead army controlled by three incomplete Arch Liches, all of whom Taekyung destroyed.
- **Defect:** The active state incorrectly records the three controllers as incomplete Arch Liches rather than incomplete Liches serving an Arch Lich.
- **Replacement:** The Chengdu International Airport attack involved an undead army controlled by three incomplete Liches serving an Arch Lich, all of whom Taekyung destroyed.
- **Rationale:** This terminology error would propagate a false equivalence between the three subordinate necromancers and their Arch Lich master into later continuity.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** Taekyung discovers that nearly half the army consists of undead controlled by an unidentified force.
- **Defect:** “Nearly half” means slightly less than half, while Chapter 380 states that roughly half or more of the army is undead.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung discovers that roughly half or more of the army consists of undead controlled by an unidentified force.

# Review

The reading copies maintain a consistent action-comedy voice and established terminology overall. One continuity gap obscures two transfers of Yeti’s Necklace, the checkpoint continuity incorrectly says Taekyung remains alone, and two minor terminology-capitalization inconsistencies should be normalized.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 216, after Won Myunghoon asks about Yeti’s Necklace
- **Current:** “By the way, where’s the necklace? I don’t see it.”

Was the necklace really the issue right now?

Overcome by an inexplicable sense of emptiness, I answered carelessly.

“…I put it in my pants pocket.”

“Why?”

“Just because. It was getting in the way. Why?”

“No, it doesn’t matter. Just make sure you keep it safe.”

Won Myunghoon’s gaze flicked toward my lower body. After confirming that the pocket of my tight leather pants was bulging, he added,

“Don’t lose it. Got it?”

“Yes.”

There was no way I could lose it. No—if I did lose it, there would be no reason for me to keep living.

*My stuff is precious.*

It was a good thing my body in the Murim and my body in reality were the same.

When I thought of the black anaconda coiled inside my pants, my gloomy mood improved a little.
- **Defect:** Chapter 215 ends with Im Kkeokjeong wearing the necklace, but this passage places it in Taekyung’s pocket without showing its return. Chapter 217 then has Kkeokjeong wearing it again, also without a transfer. The two omitted handoffs create a visible continuity break.
- **Replacement:** “By the way, where’s the necklace? I don’t see it.”

Was the necklace really the issue right now?

Overcome by an inexplicable sense of emptiness, I answered carelessly.

“…Kkeokjeong handed it back after the fight, so I put it in my pants pocket.”

“Why?”

“Just because. It was getting in the way. Why?”

“No, it doesn’t matter. Just make sure you keep it safe.”

Won Myunghoon’s gaze flicked toward my lower body. After confirming that the pocket of my tight leather pants was bulging, he added,

“Don’t lose it. Got it?”

“Yes.”

There was no way I could lose it. No—if I did lose it, there would be no reason for me to keep living.

*My stuff is precious.*

It was a good thing my body in the Murim and my body in reality were the same.

When I thought of the black anaconda coiled inside my pants, my gloomy mood improved a little.

Before we set out again, I returned the necklace to Kkeokjeong. Unlike me, he still needed it.
- **Rationale:** Explicitly accounting for both transfers preserves the existing joke while making the necklace’s location consistent across Chapters 215–217.

## C02 — Major

- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity

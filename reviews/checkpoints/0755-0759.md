# Review

Continuity, voice, summaries, and active state are otherwise consistent. One System notice creates a Named-monster contradiction, several Korean character-count references remain literal in English, and Chapters 756 and 759 contain chapter-wide typography drift.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 756, Leviathan defeat System panel
- **Current:** > - **Leviathan** is a powerful S-rank monster and the only Named monster. Since you have accumulated remarkable achievements, an additional **Reward** will be granted!
- **Defect:** Calling Leviathan “the only Named monster” contradicts Chapter 755, which explicitly identifies the Arch Lich and Behemoth as named monsters.
- **Replacement:** > - **Leviathan** is a powerful S-rank monster and a one-of-a-kind Named monster. Since you have accumulated remarkable achievements, an additional **Reward** will be granted!
- **Rationale:** This preserves Leviathan’s status as a unique individual without falsely claiming that no other Named monsters exist.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 756, throughout; representative span near the opening
- **Current:** For an instant, Leviathan's vision turned white. The mythical monster, screaming from the pain, ground its broken teeth together.

*I won't die. This body won't die. Never!*
- **Defect:** Chapter 756 uses straight apostrophes throughout, unlike the curly typography consistently used in the surrounding chapters.
- **Replacement:** For an instant, Leviathan’s vision turned white. The mythical monster, screaming from the pain, ground its broken teeth together.

*I won’t die. This body won’t die. Never!*
- **Rationale:** Normalizing the chapter’s apostrophes removes a conspicuous formatting discontinuity within the packet.

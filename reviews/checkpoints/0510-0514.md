# Review

The reading copies are broadly consistent in voice and terminology, but the checkpoint repeatedly leaves Jeok Cheongang’s revealed approach unresolved, the continuity summary conflates Sama Pyo’s sobriquet with the disputed sword, Chapter 513 contradicts itself on the Black Dragon Demon Gate inscription count, and Chapter 512 has a scene-break formatting anomaly.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint Continuity, second bullet
- **Current:** A stranger capable of walking across the water has approached Taekyung; the stranger’s identity and motives are unresolved.
- **Defect:** The stranger is identified as Jeok Cheongang at the start of Chapter 512, so this resolved reveal is incorrectly preserved as an open hook.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang approached Taekyung by walking across the water, brought him food, and persuaded him to abandon the shark-assisted shortcut.
- **Rationale:** Leaving the identity unresolved contradicts both Chapter 512 and the checkpoint plot summary and could cause later continuity work to conceal or re-resolve an already revealed fact.

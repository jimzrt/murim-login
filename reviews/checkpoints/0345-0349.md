# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves its major hooks, terminology, and active continuity without apparent spoilers. Three minor issues are actionable: an isolated tense shift, a redundant phrase, and an unexplained time unit that disrupts the English reading copy.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 348, opening of Taekyung’s martial-arts discussion with Cheongpung
- **Current:** Right now, my eyes are closed.

I focus my mind in the quiet darkness. As a voice pierces my ears, someone’s figure takes shape in the darkness.
- **Defect:** The narration abruptly shifts into present tense for two sentences before returning to the packet’s established past-tense narration.
- **Replacement:** At that moment, my eyes were closed.

I focused my mind in the quiet darkness. As a voice pierced my ears, someone’s figure took shape in the darkness.
- **Rationale:** Keeping the passage in past tense removes an isolated viewpoint-tense drift without changing the action or imagery.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 347, preparation of the treatment room
- **Current:** In the space of two shichen, the prison cell visible behind him had been transformed into a fairly convincing treatment room.
- **Defect:** The culturally specific time unit “shichen” is left unexplained even though it has a direct, natural English equivalent here.
- **Replacement:** In the space of four hours, the prison cell visible behind him had been transformed into a fairly convincing treatment room.

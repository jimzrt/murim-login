# Review

The reading copies remain tonally consistent, but the checkpoint summary and durable state contain material continuity drift: Taekyung’s sixth-stage breakthrough is placed in the Fire Gate Cavern, his killing of Heuk Jongpil is treated as an unresolved mystery, and the party is incorrectly described as traveling from Taiyuan. The durable state also drops unresolved hooks, while Chapters 240 and 242 inconsistently pluralize Taekyung’s single iron ball.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, fourth paragraph
- **Current:** After another year in the Fire Gate Cavern, Taekyung reaches the sixth stage of the Scorching Sun Divine Arts and uses the spear, White Flame, to break the shackles and chains that have bound him for a year.
- **Defect:** This places the sixth-stage breakthrough after an additional year in the Fire Gate Cavern, contradicting Chapter 244, which establishes that Taekyung trained there for roughly one year, reached the cavern-assisted limit of the fifth stage, and later reached the sixth stage at the Jin Family’s rented estate.
- **Replacement:** After roughly a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern, Taekyung reaches the sixth stage of the Scorching Sun Divine Arts at the Jin Family’s rented estate and uses White Flame to break the shackles and chains that have bound him for a year.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the established duration and correctly locates the sixth-stage breakthrough outside the cavern.

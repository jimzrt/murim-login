# Review

The reading copies and state are largely consistent. Four actionable issues remain: a wrong name, a misattributed death, an overstatement of who carried out the executions, and a broken sentence.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1026, Taekyung’s memory of his father
- **Current:** Sima Gong had no idea.
- **Defect:** Names Sima Gong instead of Sama Pyo, the person Taekyung is speaking with.
- **Replacement:** Sama Pyo had no idea.
- **Rationale:** Sima Gong stayed with the defenders; Sama Pyo is walking beside Taekyung and has just mentioned his father.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity
- **Current:** He killed Goyangcheon, the last survivor of the Goyang Family and former Spear King, and recalls killing the Heaven-Poison Demon Lord and the Hainan Sect Leader.
- **Defect:** Attributes the Heaven-Poison Demon Lord’s death to the Blood-Sword Demon Lord.
- **Replacement:** He killed Goyangcheon, the last survivor of the Goyang Family and former Spear King, and the Hainan Sect Leader. Mae Jonghak killed the Heaven-Poison Demon Lord.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 1027 says the Heaven-Poison Demon Lord died at Mae Jonghak’s hands; the Blood-Sword Demon Lord says he killed the other two.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** The Blood-Sword Demon Lord killed a thousand people with a gesture and admires Jeok Cheongang for burning his attackers alive at Mount Jiuhua.
- **Defect:** States that his gesture itself killed the captives, rather than signaling their execution.
- **Replacement:** The Blood-Sword Demon Lord signaled the execution of roughly a thousand people and admires Jeok Cheongang for burning his attackers alive at Mount Jiuhua.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 1027 shows his hand coming down with the command “Slash!” before the heads fall. The reading copy does not establish that he personally struck them.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 1029, reaction to the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s admiration
- **Current:** At the Blood-Sword Demon Lord, his eyes shining like morning stars, Jeok Cheongang, I, and even Sama Pyo—all of us had been watching the situation calmly—seemed at a loss for words.
- **Defect:** The opening phrase is ungrammatical and obscures what prompts their reaction.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang, Sama Pyo, and I had all been watching calmly, but at the sight of the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s eyes shining like morning stars, we seemed at a loss for words.
- **Rationale:** Restores a clear subject and reaction while retaining the contrast between their composure and his unsettling delight.

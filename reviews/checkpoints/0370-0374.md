# Review

Two major internal contradictions disrupt otherwise consistent voice and continuity: the reunion dialogue names the wrong form of address, and Tang Sadok is called dead despite being established as comatose. Two System-wide ellipsis-style violations are also actionable.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 374, Jin brothers’ reunion
- **Current:** “Hold on, hyung. Let’s talk after you let go. Let go.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung already calls Jin Wikyung “hyung,” so Wikyung’s immediate complaint that he said “hyungnim” and should use “hyung” has no logical trigger.
- **Replacement:** “Hold on, hyungnim. Let’s talk after you let go. Let go.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the intended contrast between the stiff “hyungnim” and the familiar “hyung,” preserving Wikyung’s complaint and the brothers’ established comic dynamic.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 374, Tang Horyong and Jin Wikyung’s meeting
- **Current:** “To be precise, when I first left Henan, my purpose was to find the murderers who killed the late Poison King Tang Sadok and the Heaven-Shaking Divine Nun. But the situation changed considerably along the way.”
- **Defect:** Calling Tang Sadok “the late Poison King” directly contradicts the surrounding chapter, which establishes that he is unconscious and ends with his awakening.
- **Replacement:** “To be precise, when I first left Henan, my purpose was to find the murderers believed to have killed Poison King Tang Sadok and the Heaven-Shaking Divine Nun. But the situation changed considerably along the way.”
- **Rationale:** This preserves Jin Wikyung’s original understanding when he departed Henan while making clear that Tang Sadok’s reported death was mistaken, consistent with both the earlier coma reference and the chapter-ending awakening.

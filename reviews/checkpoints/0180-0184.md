# Review

The reading copies remain coherent and preserve the packet’s hooks, but the durable state contains a wrong character attribution and an obsolete contradiction about Taekyung’s absence. There is also one family-title drift, one stale open-question formulation, a punctuation-formatting error, and two conspicuous literal calques.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** The Heavenly Wind Band was annihilated near Datong by an unknown Supreme Peak master; Jin Taekyung privately suspects Mae Jonghak.
- **Defect:** The suspicion is attributed to Jin Taekyung, but Chapter 180 establishes that Jin Wikyung is the person who privately suspects Mae Jonghak.
- **Replacement:** The Heavenly Wind Band was annihilated near Datong by an unknown Supreme Peak master; Jin Wikyung privately suspects Mae Jonghak.
- **Rationale:** The incorrect subject creates a material continuity error in the active state and conflicts with both Chapter 180 and the checkpoint summary.

## C02 — Major

# Review

Four actionable continuity and clarity issues: an inconsistent title, a mistaken arrival, an ambiguous pronoun, and a duplicated identification.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 1090, office description
- **Current:** The office of the Qinghai City Lord, now a headless corpse, was as impossibly spacious and luxurious as ever.
- **Defect:** The officeholder is called the Qinghai Governor immediately afterward.
- **Replacement:** The office of the Qinghai Governor, now a headless corpse, was as impossibly spacious and luxurious as ever.
- **Rationale:** Uses one title for the same deceased officeholder within the scene.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1090, account of the Beggars’ Sect disciples
- **Current:** Only one young, nameless disciple had made it back to Xining.

And even he had died along the way.
- **Defect:** The disciple died after meeting scouts a hundred li west of Xining, not after reaching the city.
- **Replacement:** Only one young, nameless disciple had made it back toward Xining.

And even he had died along the way.
- **Rationale:** Preserves his journey and death without contradicting the opening scene.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** Chapter 1094, arrival of the allied fighters
- **Current:** The qi of the Zaha Divine Technique enveloped his entire body.
- **Defect:** After the introduction of two additional speakers, “his” has an unclear antecedent.
- **Replacement:** The qi of the Zaha Divine Technique enveloped Cheongpung’s entire body.
- **Rationale:** Identifies whose qi is being described without changing the action.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 1094, Taekyung senses the encirclement
- **Current:** *The Grand Mage. No—the Grand Mage.*
- **Defect:** The supposed correction repeats the same identification.
- **Replacement:** *The Grand Mage.*
- **Rationale:** Removes a contradictory self-correction while retaining Taekyung’s recognition.

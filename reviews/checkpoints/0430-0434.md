# Review

The reading copies largely maintain the established voice, terminology, uncertainty around the magic circle, and required formatting. Actionable issues are one cross-chapter continuity contradiction, one name-rendering inconsistency that disrupts a reveal, two durable-state defects, and two awkward English constructions.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 431, Go Jun confrontation after Jin fires Finger Qi at the concealed Hunters
- **Current:** Three unconscious black shadows fell from the air in the distance, accompanied by faint impacts.
- **Defect:** The three Hunters are called unconscious here, but Chapter 432 establishes that they remained conscious while paralyzed and watched Jin mutilate Go Jun.
- **Replacement:** Three black shadows fell from the air in the distance, accompanied by faint impacts.
- **Rationale:** Removing “unconscious” preserves the action while making their condition consistent with the next chapter.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 434, revelation that Chairman Shao Yang is Xiao Shen’s grandfather
- **Current:** *Shao Yang. Shao Shen. Shao, Shao?*

I had dismissed it because Xiao was a common Chinese surname, like Kim or Park in Korea, but now that I thought about it, their faces looked strangely similar, too.
- **Defect:** The internal thought changes established “Xiao Shen” to “Shao Shen,” while the following sentence uses “Xiao.” The inconsistent romanization violates the terminology decision and makes the shared-surname realization incoherent on the page.
- **Replacement:** *Shao Yang. Xiao Shen. Shao… Xiao?*

Their surnames sounded almost identical, but I had dismissed it because Xiao was as common in China as Kim or Park was in Korea. Now that I thought about it, their faces looked strangely similar, too.
- **Rationale:** This retains both binding English names and makes Jin’s delayed realization understandable despite their differing established romanizations.

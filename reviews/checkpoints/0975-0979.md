# Review

The reading copies largely maintain a consistent voice and format. Three passages need clarification: one prematurely suggests Temur’s surrender is complete, and two state an unconfirmed family relationship as fact.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** Chapter 979, Temur’s response to Jin Wikyung
- **Current:** And Temur had submitted.
- **Defect:** The unqualified past tense makes Temur’s surrender to Jin Wikyung appear complete before he decides whether to accept the offer.
- **Replacement:** And Temur had already surrendered once.
- **Rationale:** The later reference to surrendering to survive establishes an earlier surrender; this wording distinguishes it from the decision still before him.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot
- **Current:** In a memory, Jin Mukyung recalls his childhood and his father apologizing after Mukyung’s mother died in childbirth.
- **Defect:** The reading copy identifies the deceased as the Family Head’s wife, not explicitly as Mukyung’s mother.
- **Replacement:** In a memory, Jin Mukyung recalls his childhood and his father apologizing after the Family Head’s wife died in childbirth.
- **Rationale:** This retains the revealed relationship without resolving one the reading copy leaves unstated.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Jin Mukyung recalled his childhood and his father’s apology following his mother’s death in childbirth.
- **Defect:** The active state repeats the summary’s unconfirmed identification of the deceased as Mukyung’s mother.
- **Replacement:** Jin Mukyung recalled his childhood and his father’s apology following the death of the Family Head’s wife in childbirth.
- **Rationale:** Durable continuity should preserve the reading copy’s stated relationship rather than promote an inference to fact.

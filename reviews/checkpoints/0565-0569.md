# Review

The packet is coherent overall, but it contains one timeline contradiction, two untranslated Korean-dependent constructions, a reversed dialogue response, and recurring ellipsis-format drift beginning in Chapter 566.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 565, first Mutated Gate identification
- **Current:** Those five syllables naturally flashed through my mind.
- **Defect:** The syllable count reflects the Korean term rather than the established English rendering “Mutated Gate,” making the narration factually wrong in English.
- **Replacement:** Those two words naturally flashed through my mind.
- **Rationale:** “Mutated Gate” consists of two words, and counting words avoids carrying a Korean-specific syllable count into the English reading copy.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 566, comparison between Song Cheonwoo’s online profile and Choi’s file
- **Current:** Unlike the online profile, whose updates had stopped five years ago, the final page of the file dealt with recent events.
- **Defect:** This contradicts the earlier statement in the same chapter that the profile’s last update was three years ago.
- **Replacement:** Unlike the online profile, whose updates had stopped three years ago, the final page of the file dealt with recent events.
- **Rationale:** The chapter explicitly establishes the three-year interval before this passage; changing it to five creates an unnecessary continuity conflict with no intervening clarification.

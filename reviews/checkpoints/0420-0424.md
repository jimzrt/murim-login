# Review

The packet preserves the major hooks, terminology, and active continuity without accidental spoilers or substantive contradiction. Actionable issues are a recurring direct-speech formatting drift in Chapters 421, 423, and 424, one category-capitalization drift, several awkward English constructions, and an unclear continuity-summary description of White Flame’s return.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 421, dialogue beginning after Magic Johnson’s warning
- **Current:** —Human, hurry!
- **Defect:** This begins a chapter-wide shift to script-style em-dash dialogue, contrary to the established curly-quotation format used in Chapter 420 and required for direct speech.
- **Replacement:** “Human, hurry!”
- **Rationale:** The Skeleton Warlord’s speech from inside the inventory is formatted with quotation marks elsewhere, so its location does not justify a different dialogue convention. All corresponding spoken lines in this chapter should follow the established format.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 423, opening exchange with the Skeleton Warlord
- **Current:** —You did it, human! Finish it quickly!
- **Defect:** Chapter 423 resumes the script-style em-dash dialogue drift instead of using curly quotation marks for direct speech.
- **Replacement:** “You did it, human! Finish it quickly!”
- **Rationale:** This is ordinary direct speech and should match the packet’s established prose-dialogue formatting. The same correction applies throughout the chapter.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 424, Arch Lich dialogue after Jin collapses
- **Current:** —You, human. How does it feel to pay the price for your rash arrogance?
- **Defect:** Chapter 424 continues the script-style em-dash dialogue drift rather than the required curly-quotation format.
- **Replacement:** “You, human. How does it feel to pay the price for your rash arrogance?”
- **Rationale:** The Arch Lich’s spoken dialogue uses quotation marks in Chapters 420 and 422. Its punctuation should not alternate between chapters without an in-world distinction. The same correction applies throughout Chapter 424.

## C04 — Minor

## C05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 420, Jin’s anger before attacking the Arch Lich
- **Current:** It surged like lava in a volcanic region.
- **Defect:** The comparison is tautological and sounds like a literal dictionary gloss rather than Jin’s brisk narration.
- **Replacement:** It surged like lava from an active volcano.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the volcanic image and intensity while removing the awkwardly generic phrase “in a volcanic region.”

## C06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 424, Jin’s Indomitable recovery
- **Current:** The Arch Lich had been watching me like a monkey at the zoo, but its eye-lights opened wide.
- **Defect:** The current comparison grammatically makes the Arch Lich the monkey rather than the observer treating Jin as an exhibit.
- **Replacement:** The Arch Lich had been watching me as though I were a monkey at the zoo, but now its eye-lights widened.
- **Rationale:** This restores the intended observer-subject relationship and smooths the description without changing the comic humiliation or reaction.

## C08 — Minor

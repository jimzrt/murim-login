# Review

The reading copies maintain strong continuity, terminology, and character voice overall. The durable state drops Taekyung’s transferred internal energy and ongoing injuries, misstates the battle as finished, and omits the Divine Physician’s broken wrist. The prose also contains one awkward housing description, one grammatical number mismatch, and repeated use of six-dot CJK ellipses instead of the mandated ellipsis character.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung has reached the Supreme Peak realm through enlightenment.
- **Defect:** This drops the still-active consequences of the Heavenly Power Demon’s intervention: the three jiazi transfer, Taekyung’s promise to kill the Western Heaven Demon Lord, and his severe injuries despite partial restoration.
- **Replacement:** After the Heavenly Power Demon escaped and transferred three jiazi of internal energy to him, Jin Taekyung promised to kill the Western Heaven Demon Lord; three subsequent Level Ups partially restored his severe injuries, and enlightenment carried him into the Supreme Peak realm.
- **Rationale:** These facts remain directly relevant to Taekyung’s current power, physical condition, and objective in the unresolved confrontation.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** The Divine Physician remains alive but unconscious after the battle and has lost his dantian and martial arts.
- **Defect:** The confrontation is explicitly unresolved, so “after the battle” contradicts the active state; the entry also drops the Divine Physician’s broken wrist.
- **Replacement:** The Divine Physician remains alive but unconscious after shielding Jeok Cheongang; he has lost his dantian and martial arts and has a broken wrist.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves his complete current condition without implying that the ongoing battle has ended.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** Chapter 360, Taekyung imagining waking in the modern world
- **Current:** So would the one-room at Hope Goshiwon, with its lingering smell of a stale bachelor.[^1]
- **Defect:** “The one-room at Hope Goshiwon” is an awkward housing calque, and “the smell of a stale bachelor” sounds unintentionally literal rather than like Taekyung’s conversational narration.
- **Replacement:** So would my room at Hope Goshiwon, with its lingering stale-bachelor smell.[^1]
- **Rationale:** This retains the self-deprecating image and the specific location while making the sentence natural in contemporary US English.

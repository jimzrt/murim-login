# Review

The packet is largely consistent in voice and continuity. Four English-copy issues are actionable.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 820, Fire Dragon’s Single Tail
- **Current:** My relaxed muscles loosened, and the flames coiling around my spearhead flickered.
- **Defect:** The description repeats the same physical action.
- **Replacement:** My muscles relaxed, and the flames coiling around my spearhead flickered.
- **Rationale:** Removes the awkward repetition without changing the action.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 822, Skeleton King confronting Hamid Shah Masoud
- **Current:** The Skeleton King held the [Hero’s Sword], shining with a brilliant light reserved for the chosen, and stared at him.
- **Defect:** Square brackets around the item name conflict with the packet’s English-formatting rule.
- **Replacement:** The Skeleton King held the Hero’s Sword, shining with a brilliant light reserved for the chosen, and stared at him.
- **Rationale:** Retains the established item name while removing UI-style brackets from prose.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** Chapter 823, narration immediately after the Level Up panel
- **Current:** His physical wounds and fatigue had recovered, but his Middle Dantian was beyond the System’s power to heal.
- **Defect:** The categorical claim that his wounds recovered contradicts the panel’s partial healing and the Broken Body debuff rejecting healing.
- **Replacement:** The Level Up had eased his wounds and fatigue, but it could not heal his Broken Body debuff, depleted mental strength, or overtaxed Middle Dantian.
- **Rationale:** Keeps the narration consistent with the immediately preceding System notices and Jin’s continuing condition.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** Chapter 823, Magic Johnson considering Jin’s request
- **Current:** He was ashamed of himself for having no choice but to make it.
- **Defect:** “Make it” has no clear antecedent, obscuring the decision Magic Johnson dreads.
- **Replacement:** He was ashamed of the decision he knew he had to make.
- **Rationale:** Makes the reference intelligible without stating the decision before he reaches it.

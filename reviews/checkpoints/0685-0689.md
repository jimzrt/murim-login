# Review

The reading copies preserve the packet’s major hooks and terminology, but the checkpoint continuity misstates Muyaho’s return, and both the continuity summary and durable state incorrectly leave Yohi in the stone chamber after she has entered the bright realm. Three sentences also show actionable prose drift.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary — Continuity
- **Current:** Muyaho returned with Jin and Yohi after Hanbaek’s death; the White Tiger collapsed but remains alive.
- **Defect:** This incorrectly says Muyaho returned with Jin. Jin was already at the Poisonblood Grounds; Muyaho returned to him carrying Yohi and Heugung.
- **Replacement:** Muyaho returned to Jin with Yohi and Heugung after Hanbaek’s death; the White Tiger later collapsed but remains alive.
- **Rationale:** The current wording contradicts the sequence established at the end of Chapter 685 and obscures Heugung’s presence during Muyaho’s return.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary — Continuity

- **Defect:** The location is stale by the end of Chapter 689: Yohi has already left the stone chamber on Muyaho’s back and entered the bright realm.

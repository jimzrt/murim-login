# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves the major hooks, character voices, and unresolved suspicions. Five actionable issues remain: two terminology inconsistencies, one sentence that assigns the quarterfinal loss to the wrong person, one capitalization that accidentally implies Jongni Chu knows about the System, and one awkward English construction.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 251, Taekyung describing the disguised Cheongpung
- **Current:** but the side profile of the way he kept shoving dumplings into his mouth looked somehow familiar.
- **Defect:** The construction incorrectly assigns a side profile to an action and reads like an unpolished syntax calque.
- **Replacement:** but something about his profile as he kept shoving dumplings into his mouth looked familiar.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the visual recognition cue while making the sentence natural contemporary English.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 252, Jeok Cheongang taunting the Thunderbolt Saber King
- **Current:** You’re the Grand Family Head of the Hebei Peng Family who was eliminated in the quarterfinals, after all.
- **Defect:** The relative clause grammatically says that the Thunderbolt Saber King himself was eliminated, contradicting the surrounding exchange in which his eldest grandson lost to Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** You’re the Grand Family Head of the Hebei Peng Family whose eldest grandson was eliminated in the quarterfinals, after all.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the intended target of the taunt without changing its substance or tone.

## C04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 254, Jongni Chu reflecting on Taekyung’s behavior
- **Current:** Besides, what kind of lunatic would take the Dog-Beating Staff Technique head-on just to raise his Toughness?

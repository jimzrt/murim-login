# Review

The packet is broadly consistent in voice and terminology, but the 627–628 transition rephrases a repeated hook, one animal sound breaks the packet’s established formatting, and the active state prematurely asserts that Yohi recognized Taekyung despite his mask.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 628, opening line repeated from the end of Chapter 627
- **Current:** “Only a few decades ago, we united and risked our lives fighting for the Han Chinese. But now, you’ve turned your swords on us.”
- **Defect:** The repeated transition hook does not match Chapter 627’s closing line and changes Yayul Mok’s direct accusation that Han Chinese killed Nanman people into the more figurative claim that they turned their swords on them.
- **Replacement:** “Only a few decades ago, we united and risked our lives fighting for the Han Chinese. And now, you killed some of us.”
- **Rationale:** Matching the repeated line preserves a seamless chapter transition and avoids altering the force and specificity of the accusation.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity final entry
- **Current:** Yohi has noticed Jin Taekyung in the crowd and her eyes lit up when she recognized him.
- **Defect:** The state asserts that Yohi recognized Jin Taekyung, but the chapter establishes only that her eyes found the tiger-masked figure and lit up. Confirmed recognition is neither stated nor explained and may prematurely resolve the closing hook.
- **Replacement:** Yohi noticed the tiger-masked Jin Taekyung in the crowd, and her eyes lit up when they found him.
- **Rationale:** The replacement records the observable event without adding certainty about recognition or spoiling the unresolved reason for Yohi’s reaction.

# Review

The packet’s continuity and terminology are largely consistent. Three reading-copy phrases need correction; one mistakenly implies that Taekyung breaks the Myriad-Poison Ring.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 936, Taekyung notices the Divine Physician holding the ring
- **Current:** I stared at the very familiar-looking ring in silence, then broke it.
- **Defect:** “Broke it” reads as though Taekyung physically breaks the ring, contradicting the ensuing exchange.
- **Replacement:** I stared at the very familiar-looking ring in silence, then broke the silence.
- **Rationale:** The next line is Taekyung speaking, not damaging the ring.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 938, Jeok Cheongang recalls the Thunderbolt Saber King
- **Current:** he’s been an idiot then and now.
- **Defect:** The tense does not work with “then and now.”
- **Replacement:** he was an idiot then, and he still is.
- **Rationale:** This keeps Jeok’s blunt judgment while making the time contrast natural English.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 939, Temur reflects on his former life
- **Current:** mare’s-milk wine made by milking and boiling horses alongside his followers
- **Defect:** The phrasing says the horses, rather than their milk, were boiled.
- **Replacement:** mare’s-milk wine made by milking mares and boiling the milk alongside his followers
- **Rationale:** This preserves the stated preparation steps without the unintended physical action.

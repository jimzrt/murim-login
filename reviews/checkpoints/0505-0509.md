# Review

The packet is coherent in voice, terminology, formatting, hooks, and active state, with one explicit lineage contradiction in Chapter 507.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 507, Jeok Cheongang explaining the Fire Gate Clan’s connection to the Nanman Beast Palace
- **Current:** More than two hundred years ago, the Fifth Sect Leader of our sect destroyed the Five Poisons Sect of Nanman.
- **Defect:** The identified Sect Leader contradicts the record quoted earlier in the same chapter, where Gu Jincheon, the Ninth Sect Leader, states that he destroyed the Five Poisons Sect.
- **Replacement:** More than two hundred years ago, the Ninth Sect Leader of our sect destroyed the Five Poisons Sect of Nanman.
- **Rationale:** Using “Ninth Sect Leader” restores internal continuity with Gu Jincheon’s explicit account and preserves the historical basis for the Nanman Beast Palace’s goodwill toward the Fire Gate Clan.

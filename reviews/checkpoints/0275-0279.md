# Review

The reading copies maintain a consistent brisk, darkly comic voice, but one martial-art name contradicts the preceding action, the continuity state misstates or reopens established facts about Team 11, and several minor Markdown or capitalization inconsistencies remain.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 278, opening paragraph
- **Current:** If the dozens of layers of defensive barriers hadn’t absorbed part of the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist’s impact, the entire place would have been reduced to ash.
- **Defect:** The technique name contradicts Chapter 277, where the barrier-destroying attack is explicitly the Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist.
- **Replacement:** If the dozens of layers of defensive barriers hadn’t absorbed part of the Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist’s impact, the entire place would have been reduced to ash.
- **Rationale:** The two techniques are intentionally distinct, and the established action immediately before this chapter identifies the full-strength strike as the Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Continuity
- **Current:** Taekyung is the apparent intruder and reported killer of twenty-eight members.
- **Defect:** This says Taekyung killed all twenty-eight members even though Chapter 278 establishes that ten survived.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung is the apparent intruder who defeated all twenty-eight Team 11 members, killing eighteen and sparing ten.
- **Rationale:** The mansion initially contained twenty-eight Black Hunters, and the subsequent survivor count is ten; the continuity summary must distinguish defeating the entire force from killing every member.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Durable state, open_questions
- **Current:** What happened to Team 11 after it reported the intruder, and who was the intruder?
- **Defect:** The state treats Team 11’s fate and the intruder’s identity as unresolved even though the packet establishes that Team 11 was the force at Im Yeongjun’s mansion and Taekyung was the intruder who defeated it.
- **Replacement:** How will Myeongdong Guild respond after losing Team 11?
- **Rationale:** The matching remote base, twenty-eight-member force, emergency message, timing, and Taekyung’s assault resolve the existing question; only the Guild’s response remains open.

# Review

The packet is largely consistent, but one action is assigned to the wrong person, one title creates a character contradiction, and the assassin’s epithet drifts twice.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 890, Ma Sanbao and Jeong Hogun leaving the guard post
- **Current:** Having lost control of the conversation in an instant, he could only leave with his face rigid as a rock, following Jeong Hogun.
- **Defect:** Jeong Hogun appears to follow himself; Ma Sanbao is the one leading him away.
- **Replacement:** Having lost control of the conversation in an instant, Jeong Hogun could only follow Ma Sanbao, his face rigid as a rock.
- **Rationale:** The preceding exchange establishes that Ma Sanbao has seized the initiative and Jeong Hogun is reluctantly going with him.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 893, Jin reflecting on the Slaughter Saint
- **Current:** And so the greatest assassin of all time became the Divine Physician.
- **Defect:** This makes the Slaughter Saint and his disciple, the Divine Physician, the same person.
- **Replacement:** And so the greatest assassin of all time became a physician.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding passage identifies the former assassin as the Divine Physician’s Master.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 893, Namho’s question about the old assassin
- **Current:** Are you certain the old man you saw was the One-Legged Killing Ghost?
- **Defect:** The epithet differs from the packet’s established “One-Legged Ghost Killer.”
- **Replacement:** Are you certain the old man you saw was the One-Legged Ghost Killer?
- **Rationale:** The translation decision explicitly maps both variants of the epithet to “One-Legged Ghost Killer.”

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 893, Namho describing the assassin’s prosthetic leg
- **Current:** According to the information the Hidden Shadow Pavilion obtained, the One-Legged Killing Ghost had only one leg.
- **Defect:** The same assassin’s epithet changes again within the conversation.
- **Replacement:** According to the information the Hidden Shadow Pavilion obtained, the One-Legged Ghost Killer had only one leg.
- **Rationale:** Keeping the established epithet makes clear that Namho is discussing the person Sama Pyo just named.

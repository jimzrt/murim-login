# Review

The reading copies, checkpoint summary, and active state are otherwise coherent, with consistent terminology, voice, formatting, and unresolved hooks. One durable-state entry materially understates the attacking force by conflating the final three survivors with the roughly thirty original attackers.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity entry 5
- **Current:** Three masked Peak masters attacked the pavilion; they were Nanman infiltrators with tongues severed long ago and died after severing their own heart meridians.
- **Defect:** This incorrectly states that only three masked Peak masters attacked the pavilion. Roughly thirty masked warriors launched the assault; three Peak masters were merely the final attackers left alive when Jin attempted to interrogate them.
- **Replacement:** Roughly thirty masked warriors between upper First Rate and Peak attacked the pavilion. The final three attackers left alive were Peak masters and Nanman infiltrators with tongues severed long ago; they died after severing their own heart meridians.
- **Rationale:** Chapters 652–654 consistently establish an initial force of roughly thirty attackers. Preserving the distinction between the full assault force and the final three prevents a major continuity error in the active state.

# Review

The packet is broadly coherent, with hooks and active state carried forward accurately. One rank contradiction and three localized English/terminology issues are actionable.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 247, discussion of the Hunroe Leg
- **Current:** That Supreme Peak master who was said to have no equal in kicks throughout Guangxi?
- **Defect:** The Hunroe Leg is called a Supreme Peak master here, contradicting both the checkpoint continuity and durable state, which identify him as a Peak master. Supreme Peak is the substantially higher realm associated here with figures such as the Ten Kings.
- **Replacement:** That Peak master who was said to have no equal in kicks throughout Guangxi?
- **Rationale:** This restores the established power classification and avoids materially overstating the Hunroe Leg's strength.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 245, explanation of Dogons
- **Current:** A gambler who had reached the Peak of his craft was called a Dogon.
- **Defect:** Capitalizing “Peak” makes an ordinary metaphor look like the formal Murim cultivation rank, even though the sentence concerns gambling expertise.
- **Replacement:** A gambler who had reached the peak of his craft was called a Dogon.
- **Rationale:** Lowercase distinguishes the ordinary expression from the established Peak martial realm.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 248, Jeok Cheongang's reflection on meeting Taekyung
- **Current:** When Jeok Cheongang was about to let go of the word *life*, a hand named Jin Taekyung reached out and caught it.
- **Defect:** The phrases “let go of the word life” and “a hand named Jin Taekyung” read as conspicuous Korean-syntax calques and interrupt the otherwise natural emotional narration.
- **Replacement:** When Jeok Cheongang was about to let go of life itself, Jin Taekyung reached out and caught hold of him.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the rescue metaphor and emotional meaning while making the sentence natural in contemporary English.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** Chapter 249, Baek Woo reacting to Taekyung's counter
- **Current:** M-Moving second, yet taking control first?
- **Defect:** The line is a stiff martial-arts calque rather than spontaneous dialogue, particularly in the phrase “taking control first.”
- **Replacement:** Y-You moved after me, but still got there first?
- **Rationale:** The replacement naturally expresses the same observation and aligns with the narration immediately afterward, which explains that Taekyung started later but reached his target first.

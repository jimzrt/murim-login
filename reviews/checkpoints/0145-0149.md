# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves its major hooks, but the active state contains one temporal contradiction and the reading copies have several minor terminology, capitalization, and punctuation inconsistencies.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Mae Jonghak remained in deep seclusion at a hidden Huashan residence and had been there at least ten years before the luncheon; Cheongpung came to Huashan at about age three or four rather than being born there.
- **Defect:** The present-tense claim that Mae Jonghak remains in seclusion contradicts the subsequent active-continuity entry stating that he left Huashan and disappeared.
- **Replacement:** Before disappearing, Mae Jonghak had remained in deep seclusion at a hidden Huashan residence for at least ten years; Cheongpung came to Huashan at about age three or four rather than being born there.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the established duration and Cheongpung hook while making Mae Jonghak's former seclusion consistent with his current disappearance.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 146, departure from Prince Shangshan's residence
- **Current:** Having obtained the royal guard gear set he had longed for so desperately, he decided to stay at the Jin Family of Taiyuan for the time being.
- **Defect:** The established System item name drifts from “Royal Guard Armor Set” to the generic and differently capitalized “royal guard gear set.”
- **Replacement:** Having obtained the Royal Guard Armor Set he had longed for so desperately, he decided to stay at the Jin Family of Taiyuan for the time being.
- **Rationale:** Using the binding item name maintains terminology continuity with the checkpoint decisions and System reward.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 145, Li Feng confronts the Five Gates' heirs
- **Current:** Putting aside the fact that he was a Peak master, he was the Disciple of the Sword Saint and Huashan’s direct Disciple.
- **Defect:** Common-noun uses of “disciple” are unnecessarily capitalized, and the missing article before “Huashan’s direct disciple” makes the sentence sound translated rather than natural.
- **Replacement:** Putting aside the fact that he was a Peak master, he was a disciple of the Sword Saint and a direct disciple of Huashan.
- **Rationale:** The revision retains both affiliations while restoring natural contemporary English and consistent capitalization.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 148, Jin Wikyung praises Taekyung
- **Current:** “Ehem. It feels strange to say this myself, but Taekyung really is an extraordinary talent.
- **Defect:** “Ehem” is a nonstandard rendering of the English throat-clearing interjection and clashes with the otherwise contemporary prose.
- **Replacement:** “Ahem. It feels strange to say this myself, but Taekyung really is an extraordinary talent.
- **Rationale:** “Ahem” is the standard English interjection and preserves Jin Wikyung's self-conscious throat-clearing.

## C05 — Minor

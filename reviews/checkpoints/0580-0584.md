# Review

The packet is broadly coherent, but the active state retains obsolete Busan combat status, the checkpoint summary misstates Behemoth’s leg injury, and several terminology, geography, and formatting inconsistencies need correction.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Busan's Kraken has been eliminated, but more than one thousand Mermen remain across Haeundae and Gwangalli while the Peace Guild and other forces contain the disaster.
- **Defect:** The active state preserves an obsolete threat status after Chapter 582 establishes that the remaining Mermen have been eliminated.
- **Replacement:** Busan's Kraken and the remaining Mermen across Haeundae and Gwangalli have been eliminated; Jin Taekyung is continuing to search for survivors.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 582 explicitly identifies that group as the last Mermen and confirms that everything has been taken care of. Leaving the old state active would create false continuity in later chapters.

# Review

The reading copies preserve the main hooks and voice, but the active state drops the party split, Chapter 450 drifts from the established First Rate terminology, Chapter 454 renders the same imperial relationship three inconsistent ways, and Chapters 453–454 repeatedly use doubled ellipsis glyphs.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Durable state, active_continuity
- **Current:** Taekyung's party has escaped Tianling Falls, reached Zaoyang, and is traveling by land toward the Zhuge Clan.
- **Defect:** The active state obscures the consequential party split and can incorrectly place Jin Wikyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Mungyeong with the traveling group.
- **Replacement:** Taekyung, Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, and Hyuk Mujin crossed Tianling Falls, reached Zaoyang with a Zhuge Clan retainer, and are traveling by land toward the Zhuge Clan; Jin Wikyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Mungyeong remained at Donghu Stronghold.
- **Rationale:** Chapter 450 explicitly leaves Jin Wikyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Mungyeong at the massacre site while Taekyung's smaller group departs. Their locations and access to the evidence are active continuity.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 454, Taekyung discussing Ju Wongong's imperial relationship
- **Current:** “Imperial relatives come in all grades. If he’s an eighth-degree relative, doesn’t that mean he isn’t really related by blood, but merely has a few drops of blood somewhere in common? If I spit into the Yangtze right now, the Yangtze and I would probably be about eighth-degree relatives.”
- **Defect:** The dialogue replaces Chapter 453's clear “third cousin” relationship with the opaque “eighth-degree relative,” creating terminology drift and making an actual blood relationship sound internally contradictory.
- **Replacement:** “Imperial relatives come in all grades. If he’s only the Emperor’s third cousin, doesn’t that mean they merely have a few drops of blood in common? If I spit into the Yangtze right now, the Yangtze and I would probably be third cousins too.”
- **Rationale:** Chapter 453 already establishes Ju Wongong as the Emperor's third cousin. Retaining that natural English relationship preserves both the hierarchy and Taekyung's joke.

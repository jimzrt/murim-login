# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves its major hooks without apparent spoilers. Two major continuity/terminology errors are actionable: the Fire Gate Clan is misnamed once, and Jin Wikyung is mistakenly identified as Jin Taekyung. Chapter 197 also contains a systematic formatting drift to three-dot ellipses, and Chapter 196 has one awkwardly redundant phrase.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 195, Taekyung's reflection after Jeok Cheongang names his Master
- **Current:** That’s the Volcano Gate Clan for you. Ruthless.
- **Defect:** The established organization name changes from Fire Gate Clan to the nonexistent Volcano Gate Clan.
- **Replacement:** That’s the Fire Gate Clan for you. Ruthless.
- **Rationale:** Fire Gate Clan is the binding rendering of 열화문 and is used throughout the packet.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 196, textile merchant's reaction to the emptied shop
- **Current:** He already operated several textile shops, but Taiyuan was his largest main branch—and the symbolic shop passed down to him from his grandfather.
- **Defect:** “Largest main branch” is redundant and makes Taiyuan sound like the branch rather than the location of the shop.
- **Replacement:** He already operated several textile shops, but the Taiyuan shop was his largest branch—and the symbolic shop passed down to him from his grandfather.
- **Rationale:** The revision preserves every fact while making the sentence natural and unambiguous.

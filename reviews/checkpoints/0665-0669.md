# Review

The packet is broadly coherent, but it contains one tribal-name drift, one misleading statement about Jin’s sealed internal energy, one inverted summary claim, and two smaller clarity issues.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 668, after the Bai captain crashes into the corridor
- **Current:** Soon, the warriors of the Maek people realized the identity of the monster that had crashed into them and stared with their mouths hanging open.
- **Defect:** The guards are consistently identified as Bai warriors throughout the encounter; the unexplained switch to Maek is terminology drift.
- **Replacement:** Soon, the Bai warriors realized the identity of the monster that had crashed into them and stared with their mouths hanging open.
- **Rationale:** Maintains the established tribal identity of the warriors in this scene and avoids introducing an unexplained group.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 668, the Bai captain’s final thoughts after Jin punches him
- **Current:** It was a powerful punch that could only mean Jin Taekyung had recovered his internal energy.
- **Defect:** Presented as objective narration, this contradicts Chapters 668–669, which establish that Jin’s internal energy remains sealed and that he is using the Middle Dantian instead.
- **Replacement:** To the captain, the powerful punch felt like proof that Jin Taekyung had recovered his internal energy.
- **Rationale:** Preserves the captain’s mistaken conclusion while making the viewpoint explicit and maintaining the ongoing Middle Dantian mystery.

# Review

The reading copies retain a consistent action-comedy voice and established terminology. Two continuity entries incorrectly attribute the Three Hands of Zhongnan’s loss of consciousness to Song Il rather than Jeok Cheongang; Chapter 192 also has one unclear action antecedent, Chapter 193 has an anomalous extra heading, and the state omits Taekyung’s confirmed Agility above 300.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 192, Jeok Cheongang’s arrival
- **Current:** Something firm and hot had seized his wrist as he tried to unleash his second form.

His hand clamped down.
- **Defect:** The second sentence makes “His hand” appear to refer to Song Il, obscuring that the unseen attacker has seized and tightened his grip on Song Il’s wrist.
- **Replacement:** A firm, hot hand seized his wrist as he tried to unleash his second form.

Its grip tightened.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the concealed arrival while making the physical action and ownership of the gripping hand unambiguous.

# Review

One major continuity error is repeated in Chapter 456 and the checkpoint summary: Taekyung orders a warning sent to Donghu Stronghold after its annihilation, where context requires Dongting Lake. Chapter 457 also gives the searchers fifteen minutes despite a displayed limit of under ten minutes and immediately states that searching past that limit is pointless.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 456, preparations before departure
- **Current:** Inform Donghu Stronghold, too. The fast boats from Water Dragon Stronghold should be waiting at the Zaoyang ferry, so you can entrust the letter to our household retainers.
- **Defect:** Donghu Stronghold has already been annihilated, so Taekyung cannot warn it about the impending operation. His destination and the threatened location under discussion are Dongting Lake.
- **Replacement:** Send word to Dongting Lake, too. The fast boats from Water Dragon Stronghold should be waiting at the Zaoyang ferry, so you can entrust the letter to our household retainers.
- **Rationale:** The chapter repeatedly establishes Donghu Stronghold as one of the three destroyed strongholds. Retaining it here creates a direct continuity contradiction and obscures the warning that leads into the Dongting Lake disaster.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, second paragraph
- **Current:** Zhuge Gyun coordinates the Zhuge Clan’s intelligence with the Beggars’ Sect and Lower District Sect, while Taekyung warns Wudang and Donghu Stronghold.
- **Defect:** The summary repeats the impossible claim that Taekyung warns the already-destroyed Donghu Stronghold.
- **Replacement:** Zhuge Gyun coordinates the Zhuge Clan’s intelligence with the Beggars’ Sect and Lower District Sect, while Taekyung warns Wudang and Dongting Lake.
- **Rationale:** The summary should preserve the intended warning destination without propagating Chapter 456’s continuity error into later state.

## C03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 457, search across Dongting Lake
- **Current:** “You have fifteen minutes from now. Both of you search as far as you can within that time. If you don’t find any survivors, come back.”
- **Defect:** The visible Quest limit is 9 minutes 43 seconds, and the next paragraph explicitly says that searching beyond that limit would be meaningless. Giving the searchers fifteen minutes contradicts both facts.
- **Replacement:** “You have nine minutes from now. Both of you search as far as you can within that time. If you don’t find any survivors, come back.”
- **Rationale:** Nine minutes fits the displayed countdown and the subsequent rescue at 1 minute 12 seconds. The later line “Fifteen minutes. I’ll remember, Benefactor.” must likewise read “Nine minutes. I’ll remember, Benefactor.”

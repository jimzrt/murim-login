# Review

The packet is broadly coherent, but it contains two binding terminology mismatches, one leadership-title contradiction, a duck/chicken continuity error, and several formatting or prose-level consistency issues.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 160, first post-breakthrough reward panel
- **Current:** - *Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron* has been added to your Inventory.
- **Defect:** The System notice drops “Unprocessed” from the established full item name later shown in the Item Window and required by the terminology ledger.
- **Replacement:** - *Unprocessed Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron* has been added to your Inventory.
- **Rationale:** System-generated item names should remain exact and consistent across acquisition notices, Inventory references, and Item Windows.

## C02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 160, first post-breakthrough reward panel
- **Current:** - Quest *Find the Craftsman* has been created.
- **Defect:** The Quest name conflicts with the binding established rendering “Find the Master Artisan,” which is used in Chapter 164 and the checkpoint state.
- **Replacement:** - Quest *Find the Master Artisan* has been created.
- **Rationale:** This is the formal name of the same System Quest and must not vary between its creation notice and Quest Window.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 161, contents of Black Sand’s letter
- **Current:** *If you want to become a khan, come.*
- **Defect:** The written message is formatted as italicized direct thought rather than as quoted written material.
- **Replacement:** > If you want to become a khan, come.
- **Rationale:** Italics are reserved for direct thoughts; a blockquote clearly distinguishes the letter’s text from narration without mislabeling it as a System panel.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 162, the old man blocks the Human Butcher’s sword
- **Current:** “…A chicken bone?”

“There’s no part of a chicken that goes to waste. The meat is delicious, the bones make a fine broth when simmered down, and sometimes they’re even good for wielding Sword Energy.”
- **Defect:** The bone abruptly changes from duck to chicken even though the old man has been eating the explicitly identified roasted duck throughout the scene.
- **Replacement:** “…A duck bone?”

“There’s no part of a duck that goes to waste. The meat is delicious, the bones make a fine broth when simmered down, and sometimes they’re even good for wielding Sword Energy.”
- **Rationale:** Keeping the animal consistent preserves the physical continuity of the prop used to cut the sword.

# Review

Continuity, terminology, hooks, and state remain coherent across Chapters 525–529. Two minor dialogue-level issues disrupt the otherwise consistent contemporary English voice.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 527, Hyuk Mujin announcing the first visitor
- **Current:** “Why do you say ‘I hear he’s come to visit’ instead of just ‘he came to visit’?”
- **Defect:** Taekyung purports to quote Hyuk Mujin’s wording, but Hyuk Mujin actually said, “I hear there’s a visitor downstairs.” The mismatched quotation blurs the joke about his conspicuously indirect phrasing.
- **Replacement:** “Why do you say ‘I hear there’s a visitor downstairs’ instead of just ‘there’s a visitor downstairs’?”
- **Rationale:** Echoing the preceding line exactly makes Taekyung’s objection and the setup for Cheongpung’s secondhand message immediately clear.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 527, Taekyung reacting to the Kongtong Sect Leader
- **Current:** *No, seriously—why is hyung there?*
- **Defect:** The unexplained Korean address “hyung” breaks the English-only reading copy and reads as a residual Korean meme calque rather than Taekyung’s otherwise natural contemporary narration.
- **Replacement:** *No, seriously—what was he doing there?*
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Taekyung’s incredulous comic reaction without introducing an unglossed Korean kinship term or changing the event.

# Review

The packet is largely consistent. One reversed meaning and two ellipsis-formatting slips are actionable.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 949, assessment of the Mount Heng Sword Sect
- **Current:** But no matter how much the sect had grown, there was no question it couldn’t weather the crisis right on its doorstep.
- **Defect:** “No question it couldn’t” implies the sect could survive the crisis, contradicting Jin Wikyung’s reason for ordering a retreat.
- **Replacement:** But no matter how much the sect had grown, there was no way it could weather the crisis right on its doorstep.
- **Rationale:** Restores the intended threat without changing the surrounding assessment.

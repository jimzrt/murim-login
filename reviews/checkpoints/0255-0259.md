# Review

The packet is internally consistent, preserves the active hooks, and shows no accidental spoilers or material voice drift. Four minor prose and formatting issues are actionable.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** Chapter 257, final-duel exchange
- **Current:** I could see Jongni Chu’s eyes widened in astonishment.
- **Defect:** The verb form is ungrammatical after “could see.”
- **Replacement:** I could see Jongni Chu’s eyes widen in astonishment.
- **Rationale:** The bare infinitive “widen” is required in this construction and preserves the action and viewpoint.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Chapter 258, Taekyung pursuing Jongni Chu
- **Current:** Even with my seventh-tier grades, I knew the law of universal gravitation.
- **Defect:** “Seventh-tier grades” is an unnatural and unclear English collocation.
- **Replacement:** Even though my school grades were in the seventh tier, I knew the law of universal gravitation.
- **Rationale:** The replacement clarifies that Taekyung means his academic performance while preserving the specified tier and the self-deprecating joke.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 258, pursuit parties separating
- **Current:** The Sect Leaders of the Beggars’ Sect and Kunlun Sect—two sects renowned for their movement techniques—disappeared with their disciples in tow.
- **Defect:** The appositive is grammatically attached to the Sect Leaders but describes the sects, producing a momentary category mismatch.
- **Replacement:** The leaders of the Beggars’ Sect and Kunlun Sect—both sects renowned for their movement techniques—disappeared with their disciples in tow.
- **Rationale:** This makes the appositive’s referent explicit without changing who departs or why those groups lead the pursuit.

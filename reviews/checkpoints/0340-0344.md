# Review

The packet preserves the established voices, plot hooks, and active continuity without accidental spoilers. Four actionable issues remain: one internally contradictory System counter, one Quest classification mismatch across the chapter break, one awkward sentence in the poison explanation, and one terminology inconsistency for the Gate Guard Pavilion.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 343, Cold-Ice Herb System panel
- **Current:** - Remaining quantity: **100 / 100**.
- **Defect:** The field says all one hundred roots remain while the following notice says gathering is complete.
- **Replacement:** - Progress: **100 / 100**.
- **Rationale:** A completed total of 100/100 is progress, not a remaining quantity; the current wording makes the System panel internally contradictory.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 343, Formless Ultimate Poison explanation
- **Current:** Whether it was a blessing or a misfortune, if the Scorching Yang Qi had not happened to wrap around the poison while it was running wild and played the role of using poison to control poison, he would have melted into a handful of blood long ago.
- **Defect:** The conditional construction is grammatically miscoordinated: “had not happened to wrap” does not agree with “played,” and “played the role of using poison to control poison” is stiff.
- **Replacement:** Whether it was a blessing or a misfortune, if the rampaging Scorching Yang Qi had not happened to envelop the poison and keep it in check—using poison to control poison—he would have melted into a handful of blood long ago.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the mechanism and consequence while making the explanation grammatical and natural.

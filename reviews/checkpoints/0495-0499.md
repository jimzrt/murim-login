# Review

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 497, Mungyeong’s account of his former disciples and Salcheonmun; durable state active_continuity
- **Current:** Mungyeong is the Slaughter Saint, a Returned to Youth Supreme Peak master and the greatest assassin in history; before becoming the Slaughter Saint, he was called Killing Ghost, and he saved countless people as the Divine Physician.
- **Defect:** The durable state omits Mungyeong’s newly revealed membership in the annihilated Salcheonmun and his history of teaching its assassins.
- **Replacement:** Mungyeong is the Slaughter Saint, a Returned to Youth Supreme Peak master and the greatest assassin in history; he was formerly called Killing Ghost, belonged to the now-vanished Salcheonmun, and taught several of its assassins; he also saved countless people as the Divine Physician.
- **Rationale:** Salcheonmun is a named organization tied directly to Mungyeong’s past and future instruction of Taekyung, making the revelation necessary for continuity beyond the checkpoint summary.

# Review

The packet preserves its major hooks, terminology, and System formatting, but contains one cross-chapter identity contradiction, two spider-pronoun/continuity slips, and one conspicuous prose calque.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Chapter 636, Beast Miao King’s declaration before entering the swamp
- **Current:** I am the great chieftain of the Miao people, but I am also the Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace.
- **Defect:** This identifies Yayul Cheok as the Miao people’s great chieftain, contradicting Chapter 639 and the checkpoint’s active continuity, both of which identify him as the Bai people’s great chieftain.
- **Replacement:** I am the great chieftain of the Bai people, but I am also the Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace.
- **Rationale:** The tribal identity must remain consistent across the reading copies, summary, and durable state.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 639, Beast Miao King’s recollection immediately after One Annihilation
- **Current:** When those things are gone, kill him immediately. No. Just don’t let him stop breathing.
- **Defect:** The remembered instruction changes the spider from “it” to “him,” replaces the established em-dash correction with separate sentences, and drops the specific instruction to leave it barely breathing.
- **Replacement:** When those things are gone, kill it immediately. No—just hurt it enough to leave it barely breathing.
- **Rationale:** This is presented as Jin Taekyung’s earlier instruction flashing through Yayul Cheok’s mind, so it should preserve the wording, target, and qualification established only moments earlier.

## C03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** Chapter 639, Jin Taekyung instructing the Beast Miao King before One Annihilation
- **Current:** We need to deal with him now, while he’s lowered his height without suspecting anything.
- **Defect:** The narration abruptly assigns a male human pronoun to the Thousand-Year Spider after consistently referring to it as “it,” and “lowered his height” is an awkward collocation.
- **Replacement:** We need to deal with it now, while it’s lowered itself without suspecting anything.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the established nonhuman pronoun and expresses the spider’s change in position in natural English.

## C04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** Chapter 638, Hope goshiwon reminiscence
- **Current:** I had spent the flower of my youth there among countless spiders, and eventually, I had lived alongside them like neighbors.
- **Defect:** “The flower of my youth” is a conspicuous literal calque, and the repeated past-perfect construction makes Jin Taekyung’s otherwise conversational reminiscence sound stiff.
- **Replacement:** I’d spent my youth there among countless spiders, and eventually learned to live alongside them like neighbors.
- **Rationale:** The revision preserves the duration and adaptation beat while matching Jin Taekyung’s contemporary, conversational narrative voice.

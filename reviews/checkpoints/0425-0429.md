# Review

The packet is coherent and preserves the major hooks, character voices, and established terminology. Actionable issues are a dropped active Quest constraint, a compressed chronology, one name inconsistency, several Chapter 426 dialogue-formatting artifacts, and two awkward English constructions.

## C01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** Checkpoint durable state, active_continuity after the final listed item
- **Current:** "Lee Jungryong is publicly presumed dead without a surviving body, while Wu Heixing's corpse was recovered after the battle."
- **Defect:** The durable active state omits the still-active Quest that prevents Jin from using Login, even though the checkpoint continuity section explicitly preserves that constraint.
- **Replacement:** "Lee Jungryong is publicly presumed dead without a surviving body, while Wu Heixing's corpse was recovered after the battle.",
    "The Quest **One Who Returned from Death** remains active, keeping Login unavailable until the Quest ends."
- **Rationale:** This is an unresolved mechanical restriction and must survive in the active state for subsequent chapters.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** Checkpoint summary, Plot, second paragraph
- **Current:** After four days of staged unconsciousness and a week of recovery and examinations, Jin publicly accepts responsibility for his hostility toward Wu but claims the Arch Lich killed him and that Wu’s potion saved Jin.
- **Defect:** The chronology compresses two distinct intervals: Jin privately stops pretending after four days, his recovery is publicly announced one week after the battle, and another week of examinations follows.
- **Replacement:** After four days of pretending to be unconscious, three more days before his recovery is publicly announced, and another week of examinations, Jin publicly accepts responsibility for his hostility toward Wu but claims the Arch Lich killed him and that Wu’s potion saved Jin.
- **Rationale:** The replacement matches the explicit sequence in Chapter 428 without implying that the four-day pretense and subsequent week account for the entire elapsed period.

# Review

Continuity, terminology, hooks, formatting, and spoiler boundaries remain consistent. Two minor prose constructions create avoidable ambiguity.

## C01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** Chapter 504, outside Jeok Cheongang’s cave
- **Current:** Mungyeong silently gazed at the tall grass and flowers growing thickly around the cave—and the wild animals that had gathered there before suddenly speaking.
- **Defect:** The closing modifier grammatically suggests that the wild animals gathered before speaking, briefly obscuring Mungyeong as the speaker.
- **Replacement:** After silently gazing at the tall grass and flowers growing thickly around the cave and at the wild animals gathered there, Mungyeong suddenly spoke.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the sequence and contemplative pause while attaching the act of speaking unambiguously to Mungyeong.

## C02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** Chapter 504, Zhuge Feng assessing Jin Wikyung
- **Current:** Jin Wikyung’s martial arts were in no way inferior, but even his appearance alone made him little different from a human weapon.
- **Defect:** The comparative “inferior” has no stated point of comparison, leaving the sentence incomplete and weakening Zhuge Feng’s assessment.
- **Replacement:** Jin Wikyung’s martial arts were formidable enough, but even his appearance alone made him little different from a human weapon.
- **Rationale:** This completes the thought naturally without changing the point that both Jin Wikyung’s martial prowess and imposing appearance make him threatening.

# Chapter 691

Sometimes a dream is so wonderful, so hard to leave behind, that you don’t want to wake up. Even after you do, you keep your eyes closed a while longer, going over everything that happened in it.

But as always, things didn’t go the way I wanted.

“Jin Taekyung!”

—Grrr!

Two figures, one large and one small, rushed at me with cries that sounded almost like screams. It wasn’t hard to guess what was about to happen.

Or to prevent it.

Thwack. Splash!

I tripped Yohi and shoved away Muyaho’s drooling muzzle. Then I closed my eyes again.

“Three seconds. Just leave me alone for three seconds.”

I counted to three in my head. Very slowly. I recalled everything I’d seen in the dream, held it in my mind, and opened my eyes again.

Above me was an unfamiliar sky. I couldn’t even tell whether it was day or night.

*An unfamiliar sky, huh?*

That was a new one. How could the sky I saw whenever I looked up feel unfamiliar? But there it was, right in front of my eyes.

I pushed myself upright, my body throbbing.

“First, a question. You’re not about to tell me Dark Heaven took over Nanman while I was unconscious, are you?”

Soaked from her fall into the pond, Yohi stared at me in disbelief.

“That’s what you say after tripping me the moment you wake up?”

“You rushed at me the moment I opened my eyes. And look at the sky. I’d believe you if you told me this was hell.”

“What would you do if it was?”

“Who knows?”

I looked around.

A pond full of clear water. Lush trees and plants. Mountain birds chirped in the thickets surrounding us, where wild animals watched with curious eyes.

*The scenery’s a little too nice for hell.*

I shrugged.

“I’d already be dead. Might as well go back to sleep.”

“…Sleep? Are you out of your mind?”

“Yeah. Everything’s still spinning.”

It was true. Just sitting up made my whole body ache and left me dizzy.

Still…

“I’m alive. Pretty sure of that.”

Yes. I’d survived again.

The pain washing over me and the System notifications ringing in my ears were proof enough.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> You have unchecked notifications.
>
> Would you like to check the new notifications?
>
> Y / N

I was about to open the System window when I remembered something and turned my head.

Blue-white eyes shone as they watched me warily. Behind them, a tail spun like a propeller. I let out a quiet laugh.

“Yeah. I’m alive thanks to you.”

—Kraaang!

Muyaho hurled his enormous body at me as though he’d been waiting for those words.

My not-quite-healed body screamed beneath his weight, but that was all right. It was a good kind of pain. It reminded me I was alive.

Crunch.

“…Ah.”

It was starting to feel less good.

* * *

Fortunately, Muyaho’s assassination attempt—which had me wondering if he worked for Dark Heaven—stopped short of killing me. Once I’d confirmed my ribs were still where they belonged, I asked the question on my mind.

“Where’s Heugung? Why don’t I see him?”

Yohi’s face stiffened the instant I said his name.

“Don’t even mention that bastard.”

“Wait. Don’t tell me…”

“Yes. It’s what you’re thinking.”

“He proposed to you?”

“…!”

“What a lunatic. No matter how much he likes you, how could he propose *then*?”

Yohi stared at me as though I were the lunatic.

“He wasn’t the Heugung we knew. He fooled all of us.”

“I’ll say. Once he used the Bone-Shrinking Technique, he turned out to be a pretty good-looking guy.”

“…The Bone-Shrinking Technique? You knew?”

“Of course. He sent me a secret letter, and we met.”

“Then you knew Dark Heaven had planted him as a spy?”

“Of course I knew th—what?”

What had I just heard?

I thought for a moment before asking, “Is that true?”

“Do I look like I’m lying?”

*Fuck. It’s true.*

Only then did I remember the Quest to follow the tracking scent. Now I understood why the System had singled out Yohi by name.

“Okay. I take back what I said about Heugung being good-looking. My apologies.”

I shouldn’t have said it at all. To hell with his looks—the mood had gone cold long ago. Yohi answered with a sigh.

“It doesn’t matter. I’m alive thanks to you.”

“Oh, and I’m sorry about the proposal thing.”

“That’s fine. He did propose.”

“What?”

“He did. Though it was less a proposal than a threat from someone who had my life in his hands.”

“Did you… accept?”

“I’ve been meaning to ask. Are you actually fucking insane?”

“Uh, sorry. My head’s a mess. I keep saying stupid things.”

I still felt blindsided. I’d suspected Heugung once, but after we ran into those two old monsters in the Poisonblood Grounds, I’d let even that small suspicion go.

*And Heugung, that bastard, was working for Dark Heaven.*

He’d been the knife in the sleeve. One last move the Southern Heaven Demon Empress had kept in reserve.

Which raised another question: how had Yohi and Muyaho escaped him? From what Yohi had said, things had been bad.

“What happened to Heugung?”

“He’s dead.”

After a moment, she added, “Probably.”

“Probably?”

“We don’t know exactly what happened. That White Tiger was at death’s door, and I passed out because of the pill he gave me. When I woke up, I was here.”

The rest of her account was brief. They didn’t know where they were or how much time had passed outside. Muyaho had been moments from death, and I’d been severely injured. Both of us had survived thanks to this mysterious pond.

“I know. It must sound impossible. But really…”

“I get it.”

“What?”

“I get it. All of it.”

What was so hard to believe about a pond that healed wounds?

Maybe someone from this world would struggle to make sense of it. I came from a modern world with potions, Magic, monsters, and Gates. If a few Orcs crawled out of those bushes right now, I wouldn’t bat an eye.

“…”

On second thought, I might raise an eyebrow. Orcs appearing in Murim wouldn’t exactly be normal.

*Unless an actual Gate opened here, like back home.*

I reached out and skimmed the still surface of the pond.

Its effects were much like a potion’s. My father making an entrance like General Kim Jwa-jin couldn’t have been the only reason I’d shaken off the exhaustion in my mind and body so quickly.[^1]

*Item Appraisal.*

Beep.

> **System**
>
> —A mysterious power rejects the System!
>
> —The target cannot be identified!

There it was.

I’d expected as much. Everything about this place was a mystery, from one thing to the next.

And Yohi was about to give me one more.

“I saw a Black Tiger. A black tiger that felt stronger and more extraordinary than any beast I’ve ever encountered.”

“…!”

Splash.

My hand trembled, stirring the water as it began to settle. I watched the ripples spread and swallowed a groan.

*The apparition of Ailao Mountain…*

The enormous Black Tiger flashed through my mind. I’d seen it only once, but I could never forget it.

It had appeared like a ghost, true to its name, then vanished the same way. I could still see it clearly.

*And that was the first and last time.*

That night, the Beast Miao King and I had discovered the Poisonblood Grounds while pursuing it, but the tiger itself had been nowhere to be found. The Nanman Beast Palace investigators sent out after the fight with the Thousand-Year Spider had found no trace of it either. Then one crisis after another had pushed it to the back of my mind.

Now it had appeared here.

*And from the looks of it, it saved us.*

I didn’t know what it was or why it had done that. Frowning, I gave the System a command in my head.

*Check notifications.*

Ding.

A clear bell sounded, audible only to me, and the holographic windows I’d put off checking sprang into the air.

> **System**
>
> —Level Up!
>
> —Some injuries have healed due to the effects of Level Up!
>
> —Some Status effects have been removed due to the effects of Level Up!
>
> —You have achieved the exceptional achievement **The Legend of One Against Two**!
>
> —The achievement Reward has been granted!
>
> —You acquired bonus points!
>
> —You acquired a large amount of EXP and Fame!
>
> —A sudden Quest, **The Knife in the Sleeve**, has been created!
>
> —The sudden Quest has been forcibly canceled!
>
> —You discovered **???**!
>
> —A sudden Quest, **???**, is pending. It will begin once all activation conditions have been met!

I was only seeing them now. The System had kept doing its job while I was unconscious.

The problem was that even a System as all-knowing as a god couldn’t give me a straight answer.

*Those question marks are supposed to bother the hell out of me, right?*

I was staring at the last two notifications when Muyaho, who’d been circling me for a while, nudged me with his enormous head.

—Nnngh. Ngh.

“Need to poop? Go over there. Hyung’s busy.”

—Whiiine.

“I said I’m bu—”

I stopped and looked up.

Something stood atop the cliffs encircling the pond, watching us without giving off the slightest sign of its presence.

*That’s…*

Before I could finish the thought, a voice sounded in my head.

—You’re awake, human.

“…!”

—Come up. We need to talk.

At once, I remembered what had happened a few months ago in a broad, deep river, when an imugi that had failed to become a dragon sent its thoughts into my mind.

* * *

I climbed the cliff alone, leaving Yohi and Muyaho behind.

My body was still in poor shape, but I made it to the moss-covered top. There, at last, I faced a massive Black Tiger dark as the shadows.

The apparition of Ailao Mountain. It was the same one.

“What… are you?”

The Black Tiger held my gaze with eyes whose depths I couldn’t fathom.

—I don’t know.

“What?”

—Even I don’t know what I am. Too many years have passed for me to remember everything, and I’ve been called by many names.

“…The apparition of Ailao Mountain.”

—I haven’t heard that one in a long time. Has it been two hundred years?

It sent the thought calmly and took a step. It clearly had a physical body, yet it moved like a ghost. I heard nothing and sensed no movement at all.

Every hair on my body seemed to stand on end. Then another thought reached me.

—But you know what kind of being I am, human.

“…”

—There’s no use denying it. I’ve already confirmed his existence through you.

The Black Tiger slowly turned and opened its mouth. Between its jaws lay something brilliantly blue and clear.

The Water God Dragon’s Origin Essence.

“When the hell did you steal that, you bastard?”

[^1]: Kim Jwa-jin (1889–1930) was a Korean independence activist and military commander.

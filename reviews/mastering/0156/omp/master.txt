# Chapter 156

*Thud!*

Timing, speed, strength. Even the point of impact.

That blow had landed perfectly. There was just one unfortunate detail: it had landed squarely in my solar plexus.

“Guh!”

Agonizing pain stole my breath. Through my blurred vision, I saw Cheongpung raise his fist.

“W-wait!”

“Why?”

“You hit me in the solar plexus. The solar plexus.”

“Grandfather said that once a fight begins, you have to beat your opponent to a pulp.”

“This is a spar!”

“Grandfather also said you have to treat spars like real battles if you want to survive in the harsh martial world.”

I had nothing to say to that. This wasn’t some old man who ran the neighborhood supermarket. The Sword Saint had taught him that. What could I possibly say? Besides, treating every fight like a real battle wasn’t exactly bad advice.

Once I gave up, a sense of peace washed over me.

“…Fine, fuck it. Hit me.”

“Yes!”

*Smack!*

> **System**
>
> Powerful hit! **Toughness** increased by 2.

My vision flashed white, and my legs gave out. My body slowly toppled backward against my will.

*Can’t crack the back of my head.*

Fortunately, that didn’t happen. Hyuk Mujin had already passed out on the ground, and his butt cushioned the back of my head.

*It’s filthy. Filthy, but soft. Filthy, but firm.*

This bastard had an apple-shaped ass, minimum.

It was enough to make me wonder whether he’d been doing Pilates or yoga between shifts at the Gate Guard Pavilion.

Using Hyuk Mujin’s butt as a pillow, I looked up at the sky. Maybe it was because I’d just been beaten senseless, but everything looked yellow.

*Check Quest.*

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> **Quest**
>
> **Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2**
>
> Cheongpung, exhilarated by your outstanding results, has proposed a second training challenge.
>
> Knock him down at least once before New Year’s Day!
>
> **Grade:** Peak
>
> **Restriction:** Those who have completed the prerequisite Quest
>
> **Mission:** Defeat Cheongpung in a spar (Incomplete)
>
> **Reward:** ???
>
> **Cheongpung** is very pleased.
>
> **Failure:** ???
>
> **Cheongpung** is very saddened.

Any hope that I might win at least once had vanished the moment the spar began.

My spar with Cheongpung was even more one-sided than my duel with Jin Mukyung—and every bit as brutal.

*How does he know so many martial arts?*

In just two days, I’d seen him use more than a dozen.

Just when I thought I was getting used to the Taeeul Miri Palm, he would bring out the Falling Flower Chasing Shadow Palm. Just as that began to look familiar, out came the Crouching Tiger Fist.

Countless other supreme techniques—the very arts that had raised Huashan into the ranks of the Nine Sects and One Gang—had been ingrained into every part of Cheongpung’s body.

*His martial arts are rooted in Huashan. His teacher was the Sword Saint.*

Didn’t this guy need a balance patch?

“Heh heh. Hehehehe.”

As hollow laughter spilled from my lips, Cheongpung approached me.

“Benefactor, are you all right?”

“If you’re going to ask that, you could at least go easy on me.”

“But then it wouldn’t be training. Doing something half-heartedly is worse than not doing it at all…”

“Your grandfather said that, didn’t he?”

“Gasp! How did you know? Did you ever live on Lotus Peak?”

“…No.”

I was born and raised in Gyeonggi Province, you punk.

I heaved a sigh and pulled myself upright. Hyuk Mujin, who had been unconscious for quite some time, still hadn’t moved an inch.

“What did you do to him? Isn’t he going to die at this rate?”

“I did try to control my strength, but I suppose I’m still a little inexperienced.”

“Control your strength?”

“Yes. I’ve only ever had one sparring partner in my entire life.”

Cheongpung had spent his life sparring almost daily with the Sword Saint, a Supreme Peak master. Naturally, he would only have learned how to go all out.

His eyes filled with sadness.

“Now that I’m out in the world, it’s much harder than I expected. I was so clumsy that I injured the Senior from the Zhongnan Sect, and now even Warrior Hyuk…”

It seemed this mountain hermit was having a rough time adjusting to Murim.

I scratched the back of my head.

“Don’t worry about it. The Zhongnan guy deserved what he got, and Mujin won’t hold it against you. This is training, after all.”

“Really?”

“Yes. You’re getting better, so don’t worry too much.”

Cheongpung instantly brightened as if he’d never been sad at all.

“Benefactor, did you know?”

“How would I know?”

“Of all the people I’ve met in Murim, I like you best, Benefactor!”

I drew up my internal energy.

“Back off right now. If you lay so much as a fingertip on me, I’ll bite my tongue and kill myself.”

“Because no matter how hard I hit you, you always get back up!”

“Oh.”

“And you’re the most satisfying to hit!”

Listen to this guy.

Relief and irritation surged through me at the same time. I’d wondered why he seemed so enthusiastic about beating the crap out of me. Apparently, I’d become a punching-power test bag without even realizing it.

*No wonder my Toughness keeps shooting up.*

Toughness was the last stat I’d acquired, and I’d barely invested anything in it. Even so, it had already caught up with the combat stats I’d poured points into.

*Should I be grateful for this?*

It had only been a few days of intensive training, but the results were undeniable.

Scaling the cliff had earned me the Wall Lizard Technique and a considerable boost to my stats. Now I was experiencing Huashan’s martial arts firsthand while raising my Toughness like crazy.

“Please continue to take good care of me. I’m learning a great deal by hitting you, Benefactor.”

“…Ah. Sure.”

I wanted to smack Cheongpung on the back of the head as he bowed politely, but I restrained myself. I knew there wasn’t a trace of malice behind his words.

Part of me was relieved, too.

*If he were any stronger than this, I’d be completely screwed.*

Cheongpung was a mountain I had to overcome.

If he’d been holding back even while facing me, I would’ve found that more insulting than anything else.

Consideration from someone you wanted to defeat never felt like consideration.

I spoke in a solemn voice.

“Let me ask you one thing.”

“Anything, Benefactor.”

“When you fight me, give it everything you’ve got.”

“Everything I’ve got?”

“Yes. Your best. That’s all I ask.”

Cheongpung stared at me for a moment, then nodded.

“Understood.”

“Thank you—”

*Ssssss.*

Before I could finish, the heat of the Zaha Divine Technique rose from Cheongpung’s entire body like wildfire, burning through the frigid winter air.

His eyes, now tinged with a faint violet hue, fixed on me.

“I’ll truly give it my best, Benefactor.”

“…Oh. So this is your best?”

Damn it. I’d forgotten about that.

At my vacant stare, Cheongpung smacked himself on the forehead.

“Oh, right. I’m sorry. I can be a little slow on the uptake.”

“It’s all right. As long as you understand now—”

*Shing.*

This time, Sword Energy shot from the blade in Cheongpung’s hand.

“Now I’m ready to give it my best.”

“Ah…”

“Benefactor, I’ll work really hard! Even if I fight with all my strength, you’ll always get back up!”

I wasn’t so sure. I had a feeling I wouldn’t be getting back up this time.

I stared wordlessly at the surging qi of the Zaha Divine Technique and the blazing Sword Energy before finally managing to speak.

“L-let’s put away the Sword Energy.”

Let me live, you bastard.

* * *

*Whoosh!*

The spearhead tore through the air. A fast, razor-sharp strike. Cheongpung rolled his shoulder aside, and the second and third attacks followed like a storm.

“Hah!”

The spearhead rained down with his shout. The martial art itself was simple and heavy.

But the movements of the man wielding it were light and fast, with unpredictable attacks hidden among his efficient motions.

*Swish! Swish-swish-swish!*

A smile tugged at Cheongpung’s lips as he narrowly evaded the spearhead.

*Wow, this is fun. Was Benefactor always this good?*

He beamed at the young man pressing him relentlessly.

Jin Taekyung. They hadn’t known each other long, but he was a Benefactor who had helped Cheongpung in many ways.

When Jin Taekyung had given him every last candied hawthorn skewer[^1] at their first meeting, Cheongpung had nearly cried.

[^1]: Traditional fruit skewers coated in hardened sugar.

*He’s a good person. He gave me something so precious.*

Grandfather had been wrong. He’d said Murim was crawling with frightening people who were vicious and cruel, but everyone Cheongpung had met was gentle and kind.

He didn’t even dislike the Senior from the Zhongnan Sect whom he’d accidentally injured not long ago.

*He’s my Senior, so he can’t be a bad person.*

As far as Cheongpung knew, the bond between Seniors and Juniors was no ordinary relationship. It was a close tie forged by something thicker than blood.

Grandfather had also always told him to protect the weak.

The Senior from the Zhongnan Sect was weak in martial arts. Hurting someone so weak had pained Cheongpung deeply.

*But…*

It was nice that he didn’t have to feel bad about Jin Mukyung or Jin Taekyung.

They were the strongest people Cheongpung had met since leaving Huashan. Even now, exchanging blows with Jin Taekyung was immensely enjoyable.

*Whoosh!*

Cheongpung easily dodged the spear thrusting toward his shoulder, then burst into laughter despite himself.

“Hehe.”

“You laughing?”

“Because I’m happy.”

“You really need to watch what you say. That’s a dangerous thing to say.”

*Swish-swish-swish!*

Six killing strikes and twice as many feints poured in from every direction. But Cheongpung was already gone.

Jin Taekyung’s spear pierced a pale afterimage. He stared like he’d seen a ghost.

“What the hell was that?”

“Dark Fragrance Drift. Hehe.”

“That’s cheating!”

“Would you like me to teach you?”

“Great Hero Cheongpung, I am but a humble man barely scraping by in this harsh world with the paltry Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique…”

“Oh, right. Grandfather told me not to teach it to anyone.”

“You little bastard!”

*Whoosh!*

Jin Taekyung changed direction and thrust low.

Just before the fiercely spinning spearhead pierced his instep, Cheongpung raised his foot with dazzling speed and stamped the blade down against the ground.

*Scrape!*

“Wow, that was a little dangerous.”

But Jin Taekyung wasn’t listening. With a short shout, he heaved up the spear shaft beneath Cheongpung’s foot.

“Hah!”

“Benefactor, it’s no use. This is the Thousand-Catty Drop[^2]—”

[^2]: A catty is a traditional East Asian unit of weight; the technique’s name evokes immense downward force.

*Whoosh!*

The next instant, Cheongpung felt himself become weightless.

Jin Taekyung’s tremendous strength whipped the spear shaft skyward, flinging Cheongpung’s feet clear and leaving them treading empty air.

Thrown off balance, Cheongpung hurriedly drew up his internal energy.

*Boom! Boom! Swish-swish!*

The force of the Falling Flower Chasing Shadow Palm struck the air and propelled him backward. A split second later, Jin Taekyung’s spear stabbed and slashed through the space he had just occupied.

*Wow.*

Jin Taekyung’s strength was beyond anything Cheongpung had imagined. It still fell short of Grandfather’s, but even so…

No. It wasn’t just his strength.

He had incredible stamina, enough to fall countless times and keep getting back up. Agility that shouldn’t have been possible for someone with his build. And tremendous Toughness.

*Even the way he uses martial arts is different from everyone else.*

Cheongpung was a Peak master. He had been raised by the Sword Saint and had grown up watching the Sword Saint’s martial arts.

A single move—half a form—was enough for him to gauge an opponent’s level.

*Young Hero Jin Mukyung was sharp.*

Jin Mukyung, whom he’d dueled a few days earlier, possessed an aura that seemed sharp enough to cut anyone who touched it. His martial arts were the same.

But despite sharing the same blood, Jin Taekyung’s disposition and martial arts were the complete opposite of his brother’s.

*What should I call it?*

His martial arts were rough, unrefined, and otherwise unremarkable, yet there was a strange tension in his movements. An aura that seeped from him naturally…

“Whew. You coming? If not, I’m coming to you.”

As Jin Taekyung strode toward him, Cheongpung finally found the word.

*Wildness.*

A savage beast determined to tear out its prey’s throat.

Jin Taekyung’s claws had yet to be honed, and that only made them seem larger.

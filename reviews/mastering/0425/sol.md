# Chapter 425

*Clang-clatter!*

White Flame, having lost its target, rolled across the ground.

“Ah.”

The Arch Lich snapped its fingers at me as I stood frozen like a statue. A pair of black hands materialized in midair and constricted around my entire body.

It no longer needed an incantation to cast its magic.

*Step. Step.*

With every step the Arch Lich took, darkness seeped into its mistlike body.

The left arm that had vanished in One Annihilation slowly regenerated, while its shattered, fractured bones knit back together, sturdier than before and gleaming with an even darker hue.

The closer the Gate came to completion, the stronger the Arch Lich became.

—So there was a reason I smelled death upon you. Friendship between a human and a monster… How touching.

*Crack.*

I heard bones shifting out of place throughout my body, but I stared blankly over the Arch Lich’s shoulder.

More precisely, at the sword buried in the ground—and the hand of someone who had held on to its hilt until the very end.

*The Skeleton Warlord.*

Humans and monsters were sworn enemies, as ordained by God.

The same had been true of me and the Skeleton Warlord. At least, it had been at first.

But… now I finally understood. At some point, the Skeleton Warlord had stopped being a monster to me.

*If I’d still thought of it as a monster, I would’ve erased it with my own hands.*

If I had killed the Skeleton Warlord myself, the resulting level-up might have given me one last hope.

But I had hesitated, and ultimately given up.

Not because it had saved my life. Because in my heart, I had already accepted it as a friend.

A hollow laugh escaped me.

*Damn it. I never should’ve gotten attached.*

The Arch Lich’s eye-lights narrowed at the sight of me.

—Are you laughing? In a situation like this?

“My mother always said good fortune comes to those who smile.”

—You have lost your mind.

“Why the fuck do you care if I laugh? What are you, my senior in the army?”

I spat in the Arch Lich’s face. A thick glob of something—whether blood or phlegm, I could not tell—trickled down between its brows.

The red eye-lights blazing where its eyes should have been flared violently.

—Your answer has been heard.

“Go eat a dick.”

It would have been a lie to say I had no regrets about the life I had lived, but none of that mattered now. Regret always came too late, and I had fought until the bitter end.

I had done enough.

Once I let go, everything felt like one long dream. The Arch Lich’s eye-lights reflected my peaceful smile.

—I warned you. Your flesh shall be torn to pieces, and your soul shall wander the River of Death for all eternity.

I readily nodded.

“I guess so. Just like that Demon King bastard you serve.”

—……!

“If I run into him, I’ll give him your regards. What’s his address?”

—……We shall see how long you can continue spouting such nonsense.

At the Arch Lich’s gesture, the enormous hands of mana constricting me seized my limbs. Then they began pulling my body apart with tremendous force.

Slowly.

Little by little.

*Crack. Craaack.*

Something ruptured inside me, and the pain I had momentarily forgotten came roaring back.

I thought I no longer had the strength even to scream, but a cry tore itself from between my lips.

“Gnh… graaaaagh!”

—That is much better.

Pain bleached my vision white. I let out all the screams I had been holding back and waited for death to come.

But when even the pain began to fade, what came for me was not death.

It was a System notification.

*Beep.*

> **System**
>
> - **High-grade Potion** has been used!
>
> - The amount of potion used is too small. Your injuries have been healed slightly!
>
> - Your injuries are extremely severe. You require a greater amount of higher-quality healing than this!

…What?

A cool sensation spread through me, and the pain surged back to life. My eyes flew open.

The Arch Lich was staring at me with mockery gleaming in its eye-lights.

And in its hand was a glass bottle.

*That’s…*

It was one of the potions from Lee Jungryong’s subspace pocket—the one the Arch Lich had stolen earlier.

And now it was using a potion made for healing as an instrument of torture.

Just enough healing to keep me poised at the threshold of death.

Just enough to make me feel the greatest possible pain.

—Foolish human. Did you truly think this body would grant you such an easy, peaceful death?

“……!”

—Continue to howl. Weep like a coward and writhe in agony. Only once you have realized your folly shall you be permitted to cross into the River of Death.

The Arch Lich shook the glass bottle, its voice thick with mockery.

Not even a tenth of the potion was gone. The amount that remained represented all the pain I had yet to endure.

Time would crawl by without end, each moment stretching into an eternity.

Weak healing spread through my body, bringing pain crashing down over every inch of me. My breath came ragged, but I forced myself to smile.

“If I give three cheers for the Demon King, will you finish me off sooner?”

—Perhaps. If you do so with sufficient passion and sincerity, I may consider it.

“No thanks, you son of a bitch.”

—Very well. We have plenty of time, after all. There is no harm in considering it at your leisure—

*Crunch!*

The Arch Lich’s eye-lights trembled.

So did I.

Neither of us had expected what happened next.

No one had.

—……What is this?

Confusion in its voice, the Arch Lich looked down at its chest.

Light.

It was light. A dazzling radiance bright enough to illuminate the entire world had burst through its chest.

*No. That isn’t light.*

I stared, dumbfounded.

It was a sword, shining more brilliantly than ever before.

I knew the name of that all-too-familiar sword.

*Hero’s Soul.*

And I knew the owner of the hand wrapped tightly around its hilt beyond the Arch Lich’s skeletal chest.

“……!”

It was as though the entire world had stopped.

In that impossibly slowed world, I watched hundreds—thousands—of bone fragments gather.

They had been nothing more than the last traces someone had left behind, but now they pulled toward one another and began taking shape.

*Click. Clack!*

Bones fused together.

Two arms and two legs formed first, followed by a chest and abdomen.

The black sheen washed from the bones beneath the radiance spilling from Hero’s Soul. Before long, they had taken on a soft golden hue, growing longer and sturdier.

*Ah.*

It was an astonishing sight.

Countless fragments of bone gathered to form a body, as though time itself were flowing backward. The light streaming from the sword drove the darkness from every joint, heralding the birth of a new being.

At last, a skull settled atop the completed body.

*Flash!*

Golden eye-lights blazed to life in the empty sockets.

The presence was utterly familiar, yet completely strange.

A voice rang out, and time began to flow again.

—You asked me why, did you not?

“……!”

My body trembled as if struck by lightning. A memory from less than an hour ago flashed through my mind.

*Then why? Why the hell did you do it?*

Yes. That was what I had asked.

Why had it saved me? Why had it chosen to become my shield despite knowing it would face Erasure?

It had said it did not know. It did not know why it had done such a thing. Perhaps the sword had bewitched it.

And now, the answer I had not heard then pierced my ears.

—Because though you are devious, you are a pretty decent human. That was all.

*A pretty decent guy.*

Words I had once said to someone.

I stared at it in disbelief.

Its faintly golden body was larger and sturdier than before. An unknown silver pattern was engraved across the forehead of its skull, resembling a crown.

No.

It was unmistakably a crown.

> **System**
>
> **Lv. 160 Skeleton King**

The moment I saw the System window hovering above its head, an incredulous laugh escaped me.

“Look how much you’ve grown, Bones.”

—No wonder I have felt itchy all over for so long.

The small black caterpillar had finally shed its skin and spread its wings.

The Skeleton Warlord—or rather, the Skeleton King’s eye-lights curved like crescent moons.

It was smiling.

So was I.

And there was one being who could not smile.

—Graaaagh! Graaaaaaaaaagh!

The Arch Lich.

The unprecedented named monster that commanded hundreds of thousands of undead and wielded seemingly inexhaustible mana writhed in agony.

A sword had pierced straight through the center of its chest.

The golden radiance flowing from Hero’s Soul devoured its mana and melted its body.

Perhaps even its soul.

—Human.

At the Skeleton King’s quiet call, I realized what I had to do.

*Now… let’s finish this.*

*Beep.*

> **System**
>
> - **Hero’s Soul** grants you the light of healing!
>
> - All status effects have been removed!
>
> - All injuries have been healed, and all attributes restored!
>
> - Someone who is not here smiles at you.

*Fwoooooosh!*

A pillar of light burst from Hero’s Soul and enveloped me.

Gentle warmth flooded my body. Shattered bones knit together, torn flesh healed, and my damaged organs and meridians recovered. At the same time, heat seethed through me like lava, ready to erupt at any moment.

*I can do this.*

The certainty was so absolute that it surprised even me.

And it was true.

With this strength, there was nothing I could not do.

*Come.*

*Fwoooooosh! Clack!*

White Flame flew from where it had been rolling across the ground and landed in my hand.

*Fwoosh!*

A sense of weightlessness swept over me as I stepped forward.

At the end of that step stood the Arch Lich, writhing in agony as streams of tremendous mana poured from its body.

—What in the world! What magic have you used?!

The Arch Lich spotted me and shrieked in a frenzy.

Its red eye-lights shook wildly, swelling larger and larger.

That alone told me how much pain it was in.

—Damn you! Damn you, human! Graaaaagh!

Instead of answering, I slowly raised White Flame.

I aimed its transparent spearhead at the Arch Lich, whose limbs were trembling violently from the pain, and at the enormous Gate looming behind it.

*Whoosh.*

The fire dragon spread its wings and soared from my dantian. Becoming a ball of flame, it surged through every inch of my body, flowed along my fingertips, and kindled layer upon layer of blue fire over White Flame’s spearhead.

—You cur! How dare you—

The Arch Lich flailed its arms and legs in a frenzy, but the spells it hurled vanished before they could reach me, colliding with the barrier-like heat surrounding my body.

Unlike the weakened Arch Lich, however, the darkness around the Gate churned with ever greater violence.

And then I realized.

*Now!*

White Flame trembled as rotational force surged up from my legs, through my waist and shoulder, and into my wrist.

An unprecedented torrent of qi rose from my entire body, kindling flames larger than ever before.

Yes.

This was the moment.

*Fwoosh.*

One step.

All the space separating the Arch Lich and me vanished, and time slowed.

Before its slowly moving lips could finish the incantation, I unleashed a single strike containing every ounce of my will and strength.

—Blink…!

*One Annihilation.*

There would be no second mistake.

The blue fire dragon bursting from White Flame’s spearhead swallowed both the Arch Lich and the Gate.

*Kwooooooooooong!*

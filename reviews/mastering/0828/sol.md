# Chapter 828

Fear comes in different kinds and degrees.

Some people spend their whole lives afraid of water because they fell into a stream as children. Those who’ve lived through an earthquake carry the trauma like a brand.

But the greatest, deepest fear is fear of the unknown.

A realm no one has ever seen—or even imagined.

That is the unknown.

The universe. The deep sea. Places even humanity, with all its science and magic, has yet to fully reach.

And now, a single streak of fire tearing everything around it to shreds.

*BOOOOM!*

It was like a storm.

A storm of fire, so sharp it made your skin crawl, turning everything it touched to ash.

The Doppelganger stared, stunned.

Blue-white flames poured out suffocating heat, tinting its pupils red.

*SHWAAK!*

Tear. Cleave. Cut.

The wind shrieked. The Golems it had spent three years painstakingly shaping burst apart like fireworks.

Limbs made of massive, solid rock were no use. Neither were weapons brought down hard enough to split the earth.

*Fwoosh. KRRRACK!*

They melted. They broke apart.

In an instant, all seventy-two Golems became heaps of rubble—before the Doppelganger could order them to retreat.

No. It hadn’t even had time to give the order.

The flames hadn’t destroyed only the Golems.

*CRASH!*

An immense force shook the air.

The magic circles, which had been scattering dazzling light, burst apart as if they’d exploded.

The Doppelganger watched in horror as the translucent barrier around it melted like candle wax.

“What the…”

Before it could finish, something hot surged up from deep inside its body and spilled through its throat into its mouth.

*Hack.*

The Doppelganger coughed up a clump of dark red blood. Its pupils trembled.

Knowledge from Siegfried Bassman’s memories struck its empty mind.

Mana backlash.

Its connection to countless magic circles had been forcibly severed, sending the energy throughout its body into turmoil.

If it had cast the magic directly instead of preparing the circles through various conduits and spells, the backlash would have been severe enough to kill it several times over.

*How? How?*

The Doppelganger raised its head, gasping for breath.

No trace of composure remained in its shocked eyes. Reflected in them was a human who seemed as unknowable as the unknown itself.

*Jin Taekyung.*

There he stood, gripping a silver spear that shone white as the Golems’ remains rained down over his head.

Through the lingering light of the magic circles melted by the flames, the Doppelganger saw him staring back.

A predator.

“Say that again.”

His voice sounded ready to give out, yet it seethed like lava as it reached the Doppelganger’s ears.

He staggered toward it as if he might collapse at any moment.

*Step.*

It was strange.

The temple was filled with deafening noise. Deprived of its pillars, it was slowly collapsing. Rocks that had once been Golems flew in every direction…

*Step.*

Yet that small human’s footsteps sounded like thunder. His next words made every hair on the Doppelganger’s body stand on end.

“Who exactly is the master here?”

“……!”

The Doppelganger swallowed a scream before it could escape its lips.

Fear showed plainly in its eyes as it stared at Jin Taekyung.

A monster. That human was a monster.

One who exceeded every expectation, every limit, by an impossible margin.

After dying countless times on the battlefield, the Doppelganger had abandoned its contempt for Jin Taekyung. It had set everything else aside and judged the situation coldly. This time, it had been certain.

Once it reached its destination, everything would go smoothly.

Jin Taekyung would never catch it.

But it had been wrong.

The Doppelganger had been the first to leave the battlefield. It hadn’t known that, in that brief time, Jin Taekyung had reached a higher realm.

Now that it understood, it was too late.

*If I waste even a little more time here… I’m finished.*

Erasure. Forever.

Cold fear crawled up its spine. What it did next came from instinct rather than reason.

The Doppelganger burned through its remaining souls without restraint. It healed the body ravaged by mana backlash, then gathered the energy that filled it once more into both hands and unleashed it.

At the inhuman monster approaching it.

At Jin Taekyung.

*FWOOSH!*

A blinding flash erupted.

“No!”

A step ahead of Jin Taekyung, the Skeleton King had been charging toward the Doppelganger. He twisted sharply and threw himself in front of the flash.

*POP!*

Ordinarily, the Skeleton King wouldn’t even have snorted at a threat to Jin Taekyung.

For all his bluster, he acknowledged that Jin Taekyung was stronger than he was.

But…

*Right now, I’m the only one who can protect that devious human.*

He would protect him, even if it put him in danger.

At some point, that had become the most natural thing in the world to the Skeleton King.

Why?

He’d stopped asking himself. Friends didn’t need a reason.

*SHWAA!*

The Skeleton King brought down the Hero’s Sword.

Golden magical power, bright as sunlight and utterly unlike the magical power he knew, split the flash.

No.

Just as he thought he’d cut through it, the flash passed through the blade like mist and swelled like a balloon.

*WHUM.*

*What the—*

Before he could finish the thought, a light far brighter than the first filled his vision.

*FWOOSH!*

The whole world turned white.

An ordinary human would have lost their sight instantly in that blinding flash. Inside it, the Skeleton King belatedly found the answer to his question.

*Damn it.*

There was no doubt.

This was Light magic, a spell even lower- and mid-level mages could cast with ease.

A simple convenience spell. Its light was faint and it had little killing power, so it was almost never used in combat.

At least, that was what the Skeleton King knew. It was accepted as fact among Hunters, too.

If he’d known that Siegfried Bassman, a scholarly Grand Mage, had shut himself away in his hideout to study countless spells—and had improved Light magic’s power and usefulness by several levels—he could have dealt with it.

But it was too late. The mistake made the Skeleton King’s heart sink.

*I can’t see. Anything.*

He hadn’t lost his sight completely.

The Skeleton King was undead. His vitality far exceeded that of a human made of flesh and blood.

But even a moment without sight was a mistake he couldn’t take back when he was fighting an enemy as dangerous as the Doppelganger and trying to protect someone else.

*It planned this from the start. It knew I’d step in…*

In the blank white world, the Skeleton King raised his sword. With his sight gone, his hearing took in everything around him.

The roar of rubble falling a moment later.

An unfamiliar cracking sound from high above.

And then…

A low voice no one else could hear reached his ear.

—Don’t let it get away.

“……!”

At Jin Taekyung’s Sound Transmission, the Skeleton King understood.

Why no spell had struck him, though he was an easy target.

And what the energy moving stealthily through the air was.

*Teleport!*

He was right.

The Doppelganger was pouring all its strength into drawing a Teleport magic circle.

It felt both relief and regret that it had taken the soul of such an accomplished Grand Mage.

*Shit!*

The Doppelganger might be a monster naturally familiar with magical power, but right now it inhabited a human body.

If it cast Teleport without a magic circle to put as much distance as possible between itself and Jin Taekyung, its body would crumble to dust. It wouldn’t even have a chance to resurrect.

*Fwoosh.*

Mana flowed from the hand it swept through the air, gradually wrapping around its body.

The magic circle would take a little time to complete, but it would reduce the danger by more than half.

Instead of disintegrating without a trace, it might manage to save its upper body.

That was enough for the Doppelganger to survive. The ancient monster of the abyss had no intention of letting anyone interfere with a matter on which its continued existence depended.

*Whoosh. CLANG!*

It happened in an instant.

A bone fragment whistled toward it, only to bounce off the defensive magic it hastily cast.

*SH-SH-SH-SH! CLANG!*

With the magic circle nearly complete, the Doppelganger grimaced at the Skeleton King, who kept firing bone fragments without pause.

*What? The effect should last much longer.*

According to the Grand Mage’s memories, the spell had been perfect. It had struck precisely at a weakness and robbed the Skeleton King of his sight.

Losing your sight in battle was like losing the use of both legs.

That was why the Doppelganger hadn’t tried to disable him any further. The Skeleton King was a formidable opponent, and getting far away from Jin Taekyung—who might do anything next—was more urgent.

Yet the Skeleton King’s movements went far beyond anything it had expected.

*CRASH!*

Fired with great speed and force, and aimed with extraordinary precision, the bone fragments finally shattered its defensive magic.

The Doppelganger escaped through the opening with a Blink spell, confusion filling its eyes.

*How…?*

It could tell from every move the Skeleton King made. Those attacks required certainty.

He had taken the full force of the Light magic, yet he kept attacking without a moment’s hesitation.

*POP.*

His blurred form shot forward. Golden magical power coated his sword as it traced a graceful arc.

As its wielder intended.

And as the voice at his ear directed.

—One step left.

At that moment—

*SHWAAK!*

The Skeleton King’s headlong charge shifted.

An ice spear grazed his streaming golden hair and struck a heap of rubble.

*BOOM! CRACK-CRACK!*

The air turned cold. The Skeleton King moved by his own senses and the Sound Transmissions reaching his ear.

—Three steps right. Slash across.

*SHHK!*

Binding magic summoned vines to restrain its target. Stems as thick and tough as chains were severed in a single stroke.

—Duck.

Dozens of Magic Missiles tore through empty air.

—Ten steps forward. Run it through.

*BOOM-BOOM-BOOM!*

As expected of spells cast with a Grand Mage’s power, they struck hard and exploded spectacularly.

But none touched the Skeleton King. The Doppelganger caught its breath when it saw he had closed to within ten steps.

*Already…!*

It felt as though a ghost had bewitched it.

The Skeleton King kept firing bone fragments to disrupt the magic circle. He dodged or cut through every attack spell it cast.

*Was that mutant always this strong?*

The question vanished the moment the Doppelganger saw his unfocused eyes.

A shock like a bucket of cold water took its place.

*It was him. From the start.*

What had Jin Taekyung done?

He could barely stand, yet somehow he had made the Doppelganger this helpless.

Confusion and fear gripped it as it gathered its mana.

Blink magic.

That instant flicker put distance between it and the Skeleton King once more.

Or so it thought.

It would have, if a voice hadn’t reached the Skeleton King before the spell took effect—before several meters of space vanished.

—Five o’clock. Advance and…

Before the Doppelganger’s form could blur, the Skeleton King changed direction and charged, lifting his sword.

*Fwooooosh.*

In a world gone slow, brilliant golden magical power rose like a wave.

The voice came again, and the sword cut through space.

—From the sky to the ends of the earth. Bring it down.

*SHWAAK!*

A golden flash split the monster of the abyss where, a moment earlier, there had been nothing but empty air.

*SHHK. FWOOSH!*

A red line ran from the crown of its head to its crotch.

Blood burst out. Then a Sound Transmission reached the Skeleton King’s ear, sounding almost like a groan.

—Ah, shit. Right. My EXP.

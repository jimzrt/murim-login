# Chapter 1167

It wasn’t a simple thrust.

More precisely, it was something beyond attack or defense.

*Why should it be either?*

The question surfaced through Jin Taekyung’s hazy consciousness. He tilted the White Flame’s spearhead, wreathed in blue-black fire, upward.

Toward the Dragon’s Breath pouring down across the sky.

Like a fool trying to stop a waterfall with an umbrella that had no ribs.

*Shh.*

A movement so simple it could hardly be called a form.

But what that tiny movement did next was beyond even an Ancient Dragon of unprecedented power and knowledge to predict.

*Rrrrrumble!*

Morgoth’s pupils widened.

As time resumed its rapid flow, his eyes flew wide before he even realized it.

*What is this…?!*

It was splitting apart.

The massive pillar of magical power, several meters across, split into dozens of streams the moment it touched the spearhead.

Even now, it continued to split.

*How?*

Morgoth had lived for thousands of years.

Sometimes as a human, an elf, or a dwarf. Sometimes wearing the hide of a monster. He had enjoyed the pleasures only the strong could afford and amassed countless experiences and knowledge.

That only made this harder to understand.

How was such a thing possible?

How could a human smaller than his claw read the flow of energy within Dragon Breath?

But Morgoth’s questions were no more than empty echoes.

Even Jin Taekyung, lost in Trance, couldn’t have answered them.

No—his consciousness had retreated somewhere so deep and distant that he didn’t even know what he was doing.

He simply followed what he saw.

*Rrrrrumble!*

Dark. Pitch-black in every direction.

A muffled roar filled his ears. The magical power diverted up, down, left, and right by the spearhead had unwittingly formed a rounded barrier around Jin Taekyung alone.

At least, that was how it looked from the outside.

*Fwoooosh.*

Why?

It was bright. Radiant.

Though darkness seemed to have swallowed the world, Jin Taekyung’s blue-black eyes saw countless lights glittering before him.

*Beautiful.*

He gazed at the strands of color filling his vision.

Some were red, others blue.

Some shone a dazzling white. Others held a darkness so pure he couldn’t fathom its depth.

The dozens of spells arriving a step behind the Breath were among them.

Water, fire, ice, gusts of wind, lightning.

Each was powerful enough to kill thousands. All had been cast for one purpose: to erase a single person from this world. Jin Taekyung was grateful that person was him.

Had those spells been aimed at his allies instead, they would have died horribly.

*Inventory open. Summon.*

It all happened in an instant.

An old iron spear appeared in midair, and at the same moment, Jin Taekyung instinctively drew up the energy in his Middle Dantian.

*Fwoosh!*

It was swift and powerful.

More so than ever before since he had opened his Middle Dantian.

Wreathed in flame, the iron spear streaked through space like a bolt of lightning. It pierced the core of every spell in its path before finally losing all its power.

*KABOOOOOM!*

Sky and earth shook.

The spells exploded before reaching their target, painting the air with bursts of color. At the unbelievable sight, the Black Dragon opened his enormous jaws wide.

He drew still more of the vast magical power lying within his Dragonheart.

*Rrrrrumble!*

Immense pressure bore down through the spearhead.

And then, beneath the Dragon’s Breath pouring down harder and harder without pause, Jin Taekyung raised the knee buried deep in the ground.

*I have to get up.*

One thought filled his mind.

He had to go. There was no other choice.

And for now, he could forget everything else.

The pain of his bones slipping out of place.

The horrifying mass of magical power that threatened to swallow his body and spearhead at any moment.

The thorny vines constricting his entire body, and the immense gravity pressing down on both shoulders.

*Crack!*

His foot came forward, as unsteady as a child taking its first step.

But as the deep footprint showed, Jin Taekyung’s strength and Will carried more weight than ever before.

More than mere Magic could hold back.

*Crack!*

The thorny vines wrapped around him like chains tore apart, and the gravity that had weighed on him like a massive boulder began to weaken.

*Crack.*

One step.

*Crack.*

Another.

Slowly, without hesitation, Jin Taekyung advanced.

Toward the enormous shadow in his dream-blurred vision, with the spearhead shining alone at the center of the pitch-black Breath to light his way like a torch.

And behind him stood a comrade willing to risk danger for his sake.

“May the flames of hell consume our enemies—”

The Grand Mage’s cry rang through the din like a roar.

*Fwoooosh!*

The sky flushed red. A dozen or so fireballs emerged from between the storm clouds and took aim at the Black Dragon’s head.

“Hell Fire!”

*Rrrrrumble!*

The completed spell rushed toward him, its fierce heat scorching the sky.

But Morgoth wasn’t the least bit startled.

No—when he saw that it was Magic, not a blade, coming for him, he sneered in anger.

He was a Dragon.

Born of wonder, one of the species that ruled Magic—and once the Dragon Lord who had led all his kind from the highest place.

*Disappear.*

He didn’t even need to speak.

Morgoth possessed a powerful will and magical power greater still. His Magic existed in a realm beyond the reach of any other species, humans included.

*PAAAM!*

Anti Magic.

The Dragon’s magical power devoured the human’s mana.

Ash and embers scattered through the air. Far away, Magic Johnson—who had risked intervening while fighting the Guardians—coughed up blood and fell to his knees.

Mana Backlash.

The deadliest injury a mage could suffer.

Yet a gentle smile had formed on the Grand Mage’s blood-soaked lips.

The instant of distraction he had created had freed someone else from the gravity, if only for a moment.

“Go, Jin.”

His lips barely moved around the faint words.

*Fwoosh.*

Flames surged up around Jin Taekyung’s foot as he stepped forward.

Their heat gave him enough power to overcome the briefly weakened gravity and escape the spell’s range, which covered dozens of meters around him.

*KABOOM!*

Energy compressed, then exploded.

And then—

A charge.

*Whooooosh!*

Jin Taekyung raced forward, cutting through the Dragon Breath that stretched ahead in a single line.

The closer he came—and the more danger Morgoth sensed—the fiercer the Breath pouring toward the ground became. It didn’t matter.

The rougher it grew, the more clearly he could follow the flow of its power.

He left the countless roars and screams behind him.

Without a moment’s hesitation, he pressed on. Forward, and farther forward.

At last, as the Dragon’s enormous shadow loomed over him like a mountain, Jin Taekyung instinctively knew what he had to do.

Fire Dragon Divine Spear, Second Form.

Heavenly Strike.

*Rrrrrumble!*

No one there had ever seen anything like it.

The spearhead, wreathed in blue-black flame, rose through the colossal pillar of darkness, cleaving it in two.

Like a dragon soaring into the sky.

Becoming the fire dragon itself.

* * *

It all began and ended in an instant.

*Fwoooosh!*

The darkness scattered. The Dragon’s Breath had finally spent its power.

And a human had done it.

As Morgoth watched the unbelievable sight, he wondered where it had all gone wrong.

Had the arrogance and complacency deep in his heart grown into a snowball large enough to come crashing down on him like this?

Or—

Had the strength and will of a mortal with barely a hundred years to live truly surpassed his own?

*No. That can’t be.*

His name was Morgoth.

Master of the lofty Silver Mountain. Archduke of the Demon Realm.

The ancient Black Dragon muttered to himself, alone in the slowed world, and spread his enormous wings.

Then he dove toward the small figure rising into the sky once more.

*PAAAM!*

Compressed air exploded.

From sky to ground.

From ground to sky.

Two shadows shot toward each other.

The Dragon’s claw, wrapped in pitch-black magical power, and the spearhead filled with blue-black fire tore through space.

As if they had lived for this moment alone.

“I—I…!”

Beyond the howling wind, Morgoth cried out and brought his massive claw down with everything he had.

At that same instant, he realized something.

Someone else had already cut the foreleg he was swinging.

And the wound remained even now, after he had returned to his true form.

*The Skeleton King.*

A shallow wound, but one that had never healed.

To Morgoth, the Skeleton King had been nothing more than a trophy. To someone else, he had been a friend. Perhaps it was instinct that made Morgoth remember that final trace of his resolve now.

Perhaps.

Just perhaps.

The long game he had played for thousands of years was finally coming to an end.

And ominous premonitions never missed their mark.

*Shhk.*

A low, sharp slicing sound.

The spearhead, carrying its wielder’s Will, cut into the last trace his friend had left behind.

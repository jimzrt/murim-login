# Chapter 46

Sizzle.

The meat hit the grill. Thick and red, with white veins of marbling running through it like snowflakes—it was the finest Hanwoo beef.

The sight, the sound, the smell. All of it was intoxicating. The only thing I didn’t like was the price…

“Will this be enough? We can order more after we finish. Some special cuts, too.”

A rich C-rank Hunter was paying, so whatever.

*How long had it been since I’d last had Hanwoo?*

Food in Murim was either too spicy, too salty, or completely bland. And more often than not, I hadn’t even gotten to eat properly. For thirty days, I’d abused my stomach with beef jerky, bigu pills,[^1] and rice balls.

Chomp. Chomp-chomp.

The best thing about beef was how quickly you could eat it. The moment it looked more or less done, it went straight into my mouth.

Every bite felt like walking on clouds. This piece, that piece, the one over there—every last one tasted like heaven.

“Hnnngh.”

Team Leader Choi watched me with that peculiar look of his.

“Would you like some more?”

“No. I should avoid overeating.”

“We’re on our twenty-fifth serving…”

“Twelve servings each isn’t that much.”

“I’ve only eaten three.”

“Oh, can we order some yukhoe?[^2]”

“…Yes.”

“Rice, too.”

“…”

And so the whirlwind of a meal came to an end. At last, Team Leader Choi spoke.

“I told them I killed both of them. The Priest and the Great Warrior.”

“Oh. Thank you.”

“Don’t mention it. Let’s call it a fair deal. If anything, I have something to thank you for as well.”

A fair deal.

He wasn’t wrong. I’d bought myself time to think, and he would gain a reputation. After all, he had led five low-rank Hunters and killed two mid-grade Rare Monsters.

The fact that no one had died in the process would be a huge boost to the Guild’s publicity, too.

*What if Synchronization hadn’t restored my strength?*

Who knew? Someone probably would have died. Maybe none of us would have made it out alive.

“You’d already thought of that.”

He wasn’t just some eccentric. Not with instincts that sharp.

I smiled awkwardly.

“It works out well for both of us.”

“Both of us. Both…”

Team Leader Choi muttered to himself, then abruptly asked,

“Would you like to join the Guild?”

“Pffft!”

Team Leader Choi lifted the tablecloth to block the spray of water, then smoothly produced a business card.

> **Peace Guild, Team 1 Leader Choi Minwoo**

What the hell was this? For a moment, I was completely thrown.

“Y-you’re trying to recruit me? Right now?”

“That’s right. We always need talent.”

I accepted the card and, for some reason, felt strangely moved.

It seemed like only yesterday that I’d been going to interviews with my back bent like a shrimp…

*Live long enough and you really do see everything.*

An actual recruitment offer—and he was treating me like talent. F-rank Hunter Jin Taekyung, you’d come a long way.

“Our Guild is still new, and we don’t have many members yet, but we’re excellent where it counts. For example…”

Team Leader Choi elegantly swirled his wineglass. When had he even ordered that?

“Our finances are extremely solid.”

“Oh, finances!”

“Which means our employee benefits are excellent.”

“Oh, benefits backed by solid finances!”

“Our Guild Master is a B-rank Hunter.”

“Oh, a high-ranking Hunter—the source of those solid finances!”

“And there’s no need to worry about restructuring.”

“Oh, a stable workplace!”

Team Leader Choi asked with an affluent smile,

“Will you join us?”

I scratched my head.

“No. That’s a little…”

“…Pardon?”

“I have circumstances that make it difficult right now. I need some time.”

If I followed my heart, I’d sign the contract right away. Signature, stamp, thumbprint, even a lip print.

*But what if the System disappears tomorrow?*

I’d be completely screwed.

Overnight, I’d go from being called talent to being called a human disaster.[^3]

“Is it about money?”

Money was always a problem.

But this was more important. I couldn’t let the wad of cash in front of me blind me into making a snap decision.

“It’s difficult to explain. I’m sorry, but this isn’t something I can decide right now…”

“One hundred million won.”

“One hundred million?”

“That’s just the signing bonus. We’ll match the rest to the minimum terms for a C-rank Hunter.”

That was dangerous. This time, it was seriously dangerous.

But I resisted with superhuman patience. Hadn’t I learned long ago that life was never that easy?

I might end up choking on that money later.

“I’m sorry.”

Team Leader Choi studied me for a moment, then nodded.

“I’ll wait for your call.”

* * *

One person left, and one person stayed.

Team Leader Choi—no, Choi Minwoo—silently stared at the seat Jin Taekyung had vacated, then took out his phone.

Beep. Beep. Click.

“You bastard—are you psychic? I was just about to call you.”

“What happened with that thing I asked about?”

“I looked into it because you asked, but… is there something about this Jin Taekyung guy?”

“That’s what I called you to find out. So? What did you learn?”

“Dime-a-dozen case. He awakened at twenty, seven years ago, and was assessed as F-rank. There’s a record of him graduating at the top of his class from the Hunter training center…”

The story of Jin Taekyung’s past seven years came through the phone. Then, at one point, Choi Minwoo’s eyebrows twitched.

“What? The Sangdong Station Mutated Gate?”

“Yeah. You know about that incident, right?”

How could he not? It had happened only two years ago, and Choi Minwoo remembered it clearly.

“He was the only survivor. That part surprised me when I checked it, too.”

Choi Minwoo raised his glass of water. His mouth had gone dry at the thought that he might have found a clue to how an F-rank Hunter had killed a mid-grade Rare Monster alone.

“And?”

“He took six months off. The Hunter Administration kept sending investigators, and I guess he spent the time trying to get himself back together. You know how serious the incident was.”

“And then?”

“That’s it. He went back to his Guild, ran Gates until his ass fell off for a year and a half, then got fired. That was exactly three days ago.”

“Why was he fired?”

“Technically, restructuring, but come on. What kind of restructuring does a booger-sized Guild like that need? The incident probably had a lot to do with it. They kept looking over their shoulders at the Administration, then finally pushed him out. From their perspective, he must’ve been awkward to keep around.”

“That’s all?”

“As far as I can tell. Want me to send you the file separately?”

“Send it now. I’m hanging up.”

“Hey, hey!”

Click.

Choi Minwoo tapped the table with his long fingers.

Jin Taekyung. F-rank Hunter. The sole survivor of the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate.

And…

*At least a C-rank Hunter.*

That was the absolute minimum. The image of Taekyung overwhelming a C-rank Rare Monster alone with tremendous strength and skill lingered before his eyes.

*And yet he’s F-rank.*

There were only two possibilities. Either he had been hiding his strength, or he had recently reawakened.

Choi Minwoo suspected the latter, but even that defied common sense.

Countless Hunters retired without ever moving up a single rank.

Reawakening from F-rank to C-rank was, without question, extraordinarily rare.

*This isn’t some kind of game. What the hell is he?*

Choi Minwoo shook his head. He felt as though he’d been bewitched.

*I’ll have to look into this further.*

As he rose from his seat, the restaurant owner approached and held out the bill.

“That’ll be 1,937,000 won.”

“…”

Now he really felt as though he’d been bewitched.

* * *

“Fuck. I’m screwed.”

I sank to the ground in the filthy recycling area. The one thing that should have been there was nowhere to be seen.

“It’s gone. It’s gone. My capsule is gone.”

I’d been anxious ever since parting with Team Leader Choi. But I hadn’t expected someone to take it in less than half a day. I shouted into the empty air.

“Who the fuck was it?!”

And an answer came.

“Me, you son of a bitch.”

Jinho hyung was smoking on the roof of the goshiwon building,[^4] in the same spot where I’d seen him that morning. He exhaled a plume of smoke with a wistful look, then continued.

“I spent ten years crying, regretting, and vowing…”

“You want me to make you really cry and regret it?”

“You’re no fun. You haven’t seen this movie, have you?”

“Quit joking around. This is serious.”

“What, did today’s job come up empty?”

“No.”

My voice went limp.

“The capsule.”

“…Huh?”

“Some bastard took my capsule.”

“Cough! Cough-cough!”

Maybe he’d inhaled the smoke wrong. Jinho hyung coughed like a maniac before finally managing to speak.

“D-didn’t you throw it away because you didn’t need it?”

“I did.”

Until the System came back.

I hadn’t expected everything to change this much in just a few hours.

*I should’ve kept it for one more day.*

Where was I even supposed to start looking? I let out a heavy sigh.

“Hyung, you didn’t happen to see who took it, did you?”

“Uh… well.”

Jinho hyung scratched his head.

“If I saw them, I saw them. If I didn’t, I didn’t.”

Was that supposed to be an answer, or was he just talking shit?

When I glared at him, he smiled sheepishly.

“Look, it’s not like I’m asking for a finder’s fee or anything…”

He was definitely asking for a finder’s fee.

Anyway, that wasn’t the point. I shot to my feet.

“You saw them? You’re sure?”

“If I had to choose, I’d say I saw them.”

“Who? Where did they go?”

“I’m not asking for a finder’s fee, but roughly how much would it be?”

“…One hundred thousand won?”

“Oh, dear. Maybe I’m getting old. My memory’s a little hazy.”

“For fuck’s sake.”

“Right. Well, it’s hot out. Good luck.”

“The finder’s fee will be one hundred and eighty thousand won.”[^5]

Apparently satisfied with the amount, Jinho hyung broke into a bright smile.

“Your capsule. I picked it up.”

“…?”

It took me exactly three seconds to understand.

*What a goddamn highway robber.*

The sheer audacity left me speechless. How could he pull something like this on me?

“But I couldn’t find anywhere to put it. My room’s too small, you know.”

“So?”

“I put it back in your room. I did good, right?”

How was I supposed to hit this guy so thoroughly that word of it spread?

My fists trembled.

* * *

“It really is here.”

The capsule sat there taking up half the studio as if nothing had happened. All I could do was let out a hollow laugh.

*Should I call this lucky?*

Of all the people who could have taken it, it had been Jinho hyung.

I opened the capsule lid, picked up the user manual tossed onto the worn seat, and flipped to the last page.

> **Main Features**
>
> - A customized capsule for one person! Once a user is registered, the capsule becomes permanently bound to that user and remains so until their death.

…Come on. No way.

*It has to be a coincidence.*

But I couldn’t shake the unease. I glared at the capsule, the source of everything that had happened.

*What even is this thing?*

I’d thrown the capsule into the recycling area that morning because I wanted to forget. Life was already hard enough. I wanted to write the whole thing off as a nightmare and go back to living my life.

But now the situation had changed.

*Because the System is here.*

The System…

Then something occurred to me. I placed my hand on the capsule and murmured,

“Item check.”

Ding.

Just as I thought. The corners of my mouth had begun to rise when—

> **System**
>
> This Item cannot be read.

“…It can’t be read?”

This had never happened before.

*Is it because I’m not in Murim?*

Flustered, I checked everything in the room I could get my hands on. The television, a ballpoint pen, even my pillow. Every time, the System displayed accurate information.

There was only one exception.

It couldn’t read the capsule.

“Wow. This is driving me nuts.”

Just in case, I picked up the user manual. The result was the same.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> This Item cannot be read.

I flopped onto the bed and stared blankly at the old, yellow-stained ceiling.

*What’s going on?*

For now, none of it made sense. But one thing was certain.

*I’ve become stronger. Incomparably stronger.*

Power permeated every fiber of my body. Internal energy stirred in my dantian.

Synchronization had given me strength. Strength far beyond that of an F-rank Hunter. Enough strength to defeat a C-rank Rare Monster alone.

*Would you like to join the Guild?*

It was the first recruitment offer I’d ever received. But I had turned it down. The fact that someone had finally recognized me brought fear before joy.

*Can’t blame me.*

I’d endured seven years with the label of F-rank Hunter. A caterpillar that had spent its life crawling through the dirt had suddenly sprouted wings called the System.

Fear was only natural.

*But what if I can keep using this power—keep using the System?*

My heart pounded at the mere thought.

At the same time, the past seven years flashed through my mind. The name F-rank, stamped on me like a brand despite all the insane effort I’d put in. The memories from two years ago, when I’d trembled beneath other people’s contempt, my own guilt, and utter helplessness.

“Fuck…”

I couldn’t take it anymore. I shot to my feet, burst out of the goshiwon, and flagged down a passing taxi.

“Where would you like to go?”

I already knew my destination.

“Take me to the Bucheon Branch of the Hunter Association.”

A Hunter rank reassessment.

I’d start by breaking the loathsome shackle of F-rank Hunter that had tormented me for so long.

*Let’s do this.*

As I clenched my fist, the taxi driver said,

“This is a Seoul taxi.”

“Oh.”

[^1]: Bigu pills are traditional fasting pills said to sustain the body without ordinary food.

[^2]: Yukhoe is seasoned Korean raw beef.

[^3]: The Korean words for “talent” and “human disaster” share the same pronunciation, though they use different characters.

[^4]: A goshiwon is a very small, inexpensive room-for-rent housing arrangement.

[^5]: In Korean, *sip-pal* (“eighteen”) echoes the swear he just used, and he switches abruptly to stiff politeness.

# Chapter 817

The Doppelganger’s new face was all too familiar to Jin Taekyung.

He’d seen it in person only once, shriveled like a mummy. But while investigating Michael Silbert, he’d seen that same face in photographs so many times he was sick of it.

And then there was…

*Whoooosh.*

That immense magical power, so palpable he could almost reach out and touch it.

He didn’t need to check the Level window to know the answer.

“Siegfried Bassman?”

At Jin Taekyung’s muttered question, the Doppelganger snapped its fingers instead of answering.

*Crackle.*

White light flashed.

The faint current running along its finger became dozens of lightning bolts and came crashing down. It took only an instant.

*Zzap!*

Blinding light filled Jin Taekyung’s vision.

His eyes closed before he knew it, but he could still feel the tremendous current through the rock surrounding him, the air, and the ground. Through everything.

*Boom!*

The hard rock and earth beneath him split in a spiderweb of cracks.

Jin Taekyung kicked off the ground and shot upward, thrusting out a palm. The heat of Flame Divine Palm burned through the lightning as it surged forward.

*KABOOOOOM!*

A tremendous blast shook the canyon.

But Jin Taekyung sharpened his sight and caught everything that happened in that brief moment.

The rising flames. The acrid smoke.

And the Doppelganger vanishing like a ghost just before it all erupted.

*Blink.*

Teleport and Warp were spells for crossing long distances. Blink was short-range movement: disappearing for an instant, just as its name suggested.

Its range was limited. Jin Taekyung’s senses and vision, on the other hand, covered the entire canyon.

*There.*

He couldn’t see it. But he could feel it.

Jin Taekyung drew a breath. He trusted his senses and his abilities.

Channeling internal energy through his toes, he stepped on empty air and shot toward the Doppelganger beyond the drifting embers and acrid smoke.

*Whoosh! Boom!*

The air burst around him, unable to withstand his speed. Smoke and embers scattered as if blown apart, and his vision cleared.

At the end of his path, the Doppelganger was fleeing toward the followers who could help it.

“……!”

A chill prickled down its back. The Doppelganger turned instinctively, its eyes widening.

In a world that seemed to slow around it, Jin Taekyung was already right in front of it, filling its vision.

*How the hell?*

Less than a second had passed.

Yet Jin Taekyung had found where Blink had taken it and crossed the dozen or so meters between them.

*You’ve got to be fucking kidding me…!*

The Doppelganger swallowed the curse rising to its lips.

No. It hadn’t even had time to curse.

Waste a moment, and it could lose the Grand Mage’s power forever—an asset far more precious than all the other lives it had discarded.

*Shwaa!*

A reddish-hot spearhead tore through the wind. The killing intent that accompanied it was cold enough to freeze the Doppelganger in place.

But the mana and abilities it possessed at that moment belonged to one of only three Grand Mages in the world.

*Blin—k!*

*Fwoosh, boom!*

Flames erupted along the spearhead and devoured the empty air.

The Doppelganger appeared a dozen or so meters away and felt a chill run down its spine.

Because it had barely escaped?

No.

Because a pair of eyes was staring straight at it.

*Jin Taekyung.*

Their gazes had only met, but its heart sank. An instinct honed over centuries of struggle whispered to it.

Run.

If it didn’t want that beast to tear out the back of its neck, it couldn’t let its guard down for a moment.

The Doppelganger cast Blink again without pausing. In the next instant, it knew its instinct had been right.

*Whoosh! Slice!*

It opened its eyes in a new space and let out the breath it had been holding.

The back of its armor, long since stripped of its original shape and purpose, was hot from the heat that had grazed it.

*He cut me. He’s fast enough to keep up with Blink.*

It had escaped by a hair.

A shiver ran through it. Of all the humans it had absorbed or encountered in passing, none had been a monster like this.

Except for the one person it feared—and had deliberately avoided for that reason.

*Cheon Taemin.*

The savior of humanity.

The only Adversary to face the Demon King Asmodeus with the body and mind of a frail human. A divine man.

The Doppelganger neither denied its fear of Cheon Taemin nor felt ashamed of it.

He was no being it could call “a mere human.”

That was why, even after absorbing Siegfried Bassman years ago and learning of Cheon Taemin’s condition, it had never dared to go after him.

But the Doppelganger had never imagined it could feel this much fear of any human other than Cheon Taemin.

*Blink, Blink, Blink!*

Hesitate, and it might face permanent Erasure.

Desperate, the Doppelganger leaped through space again and again.

Each time its form vanished as if rubbed away by an eraser, it drew closer to the canyon exit, where a fierce battle was underway.

The hunter pursuing it drew closer, too.

*Whsssh!*

A sharp whistle cut through the air.

A dozen or so daggers flashed toward the Doppelganger’s entire body. The attack had been timed and aimed so precisely that it had no chance to cast Blink.

With no other choice, the Doppelganger gritted its teeth and spread both hands.

*Vrrrrm, crack!*

Its mana shield shattered before it had fully formed. The daggers ricocheted away and buried themselves in the cliffs and ground on either side.

But in the brief time it took to block them, Jin Taekyung had reached it.

“Got you.”

“……!”

*Crack!*

The Doppelganger had no time to react.

Its mouth fell open as tremendous pain shot through its wrist.

Siegfried Bassman had been a great Grand Mage in life, but Jin Taekyung’s terrifying strength couldn’t be stopped by any magic in his memories.

*Crreeeak!*

“Gyaaaah!”

The scream tore from the Doppelganger’s lips.

Jin Taekyung’s grip alone crushed flesh and muscle and splintered bone.

Its vision went white. The sky and ground tilted.

*I’m being flipped over. No—I’m going to die.*

The Doppelganger knew it instinctively.

After this terrifying feeling of weightlessness would come the impact. Its whole body would be crushed against the ground.

*No!*

Siegfried Bassman was the most precious of all the lives it had absorbed.

Hunting a publicly known S-rank Hunter was difficult enough. A Grand Mage was an especially valuable prize, granting it the versatile power of magic.

*If I lose magic, I’m finished. I’d rather…!*

Fear sent its thoughts racing. Its decision was immediate.

*Whoooom!*

As it hurtled toward the ground, the Doppelganger drew up mana with all its strength.

*Blink!*

*Flash.*

The world slowed.

Space warped by mana swallowed its body. But this time, the amount of mana was different—and so was the spell’s stability.

The Doppelganger fixed its gaze on the battlefield hundreds of meters away, far beyond Blink’s normal range.

*I have to make it.*

This crossed a forbidden limit of spatial magic.

But it was better than dying here. The fanatics who worshiped it like a god were there, followers it had spent decades cultivating.

*Crreeeak!*

Bone and flesh twisted along with space.

The pain felt as though an invisible hand were squeezing its heart until it burst. None of that mattered.

Jin Taekyung.

If it could get out of this monstrous human’s grasp…

If it could escape under its followers’ protection and carry out the plan it had prepared for so long…

*Goodbye. See you next time.*

The Doppelganger grinned, baring blood-soaked teeth.

*Grrk, whoooosh!*

Mana whipped around it like a storm, and warped space swallowed it whole.

Its vision went black. Then new light, noise, and faces washed over it in waves.

*Boom! KABOOOOOM!*

*Shing! Krrrrk!*

“Gah!”

“Warriors of God, do not fear death!”

“Go fuck yourselves, you insane fanatics!”

Blasts and screams erupted on every side. Shouts from both sides battered its ruptured eardrums.

But all that mattered to the Doppelganger was that the faces in its blurred vision belonged to its followers.

“W-wait!”

“Prophet!”

Fanatics in turbans spotted it and gathered around. The Doppelganger spat up blood mixed with bits of its entrails, then laughed aloud.

“Cough. Heh-heh…”

Pain?

That was fine. It would recover soon.

Bones throughout its body were shattered, and the left arm Jin Taekyung had grabbed had been torn off at the shoulder. What mattered was that Blink had worked beyond its limits.

*An ordinary human would have died.*

It had been lucky.

The spell had succeeded because Siegfried Bassman was a Grand Mage. It had survived the injuries because it was a Doppelganger.

*Slide. Crack.*

Pale flesh, soft as a child’s, slowly grew from the stump of its severed arm. Torn muscles joined, and white bone grew back.

Its recovery was incomparably slower than before. The Doppelganger clicked its tongue bitterly.

*Damn it.*

The countless lives it had absorbed were running low.

By the time it escaped this place entirely, it might have only a handful left.

*But that doesn’t matter. I have others willing to die for me.*

A sea of fanatics surrounded it.

Smiling to itself, the Doppelganger staggered to its feet.

Fierce fighting raged around the canyon exit, but the rear position it had reached with Blink was peaceful. Plenty of troops remained there.

Including the core force it had taken such care to raise.

“Amir.”

At the Doppelganger’s low voice, the surrounding fanatics stepped aside.

An old man carrying a staff approached and knelt before it.

“I have been waiting for you, great Prophet.”

Wonder filled his face and voice.

The Doppelganger looked down at him with all the authority befitting a prophet.

“God has led me elsewhere, so I must leave ahead of you. Hold back Jin Taekyung and the other heretics with all your strength.”

God and Prophet were names that commanded absolute obedience.

But Amir asked again, looking bewildered.

“Jin Taekyung?”

“Why? Are you afraid of him?”

“No. But… hadn’t you already subdued him, Prophet?”

The Doppelganger frowned.

“What do you mean? You couldn’t have seen what happened.”

“Yes. But you personally subdued him and brought him here.”

“What?”

The Doppelganger blinked, unable to make sense of it.

Behind it, Jin Taekyung lay facedown as if dead. His eyes slowly opened.

“Uh, fuck. Where the hell am I now?”

“……!”

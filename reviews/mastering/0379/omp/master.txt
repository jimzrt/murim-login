# Chapter 379

Rrrrrumble!

A tremendous roar shook the air, followed by vibrations surging through the ground.

Twenty-year-old Shao Shen stared toward the horizon, unable to believe what he was seeing.

*How can monsters that should be thousands of kilometers away be here…?*

He was not the only one asking that question.

More than a thousand Hunters from the Public Security Armed Forces and five thousand soldiers of the Chinese People’s Liberation Army, dispatched to maintain public order, were stationed at Chengdu International Airport. Every one of them wondered the same thing as they stared in horror at the reality bearing down on them.

- Squeeeal!

- Grrrrr!

From low-level monsters like goblins and orcs to higher-level creatures like trolls, ogres, and lycanthropes, a vast army of monsters filled the horizon and charged forward with hideous roars.

The one-kilometer gap was shrinking by the second. Shouts bordering on screams erupted across the airport.

“Form ranks by unit! Assemble! Assembllllle!”

“Fire! Fire! I said fire!”

Rat-a-tat-tat! Boom!

The People’s Liberation Army hurriedly formed ranks and poured on the firepower at their commanders’ orders, but the effect was pitiful.

They had been caught off guard by the monster army’s sudden attack.

The soldiers were ordinary people without any special abilities. Terror froze their bodies, and their firearms were barely effective against even the low-level monsters.

“There are too many monsters! There are too many!”

“Pilots!”

“Get the fighters into the air! Bomb them from above—!”

The commanders’ desperate shouts were drowned out by a savage cry that rang across the sky.

- Kyaaaauuu!

“W-What is that?”

“A wyvern! It’s a wyvern!”

A massive silhouette came flying out of the setting sun.

A wyvern, known as the terror of the skies, led dozens of griffons and gargoyles behind it.

The A-rank monsters angled their wings and plunged toward the fighters that had yet to take off.

Krrrunch! Boom!

Powerful wingbeats rocked the several-ton masses of metal, while claws infused with magic tore through their fuselages like paper.

The explosions hurled metal fragments across the tarmac, mowing down the pilots running frantically toward their aircraft.

Bang-bang! Crunch!

They died instantly, without even the chance to scream.

Once the commander regained his senses, the guns opened fire at his orders. But even hundreds—thousands—of bullets could do little more than scratch flesh and hide saturated with powerful magic.

- Kikikikit.

The monsters laughed at the helpless humans.

The sound sent a shock of terror through the crowd, making every hair on their bodies stand on end.

“H-How can this be?”

A monster army filled both the ground and the sky. Creatures that could barely be harmed by firearms were monsters in the truest sense of the word.

“M-Monsters…”

“I-I have to survive. I don’t want to die like a dog in a place like this!”

Fear of death spread faster than any epidemic.

As the soldiers of the People’s Liberation Army began backing away one by one, a lone figure stepped forward instead.

“Don’t retreat!”

Shao Shen shouted, his boyish features not yet fully faded and his eyes blazing.

The Five-Star Red Flag of the People’s Republic of China was emblazoned on the chest of his armor.

“Who are we?”

At the young man’s question, those about to flee stopped in their tracks.

Shao Shen glared at the monster army charging from several hundred meters away. From beneath his low-pulled helmet, his thunderous voice erupted once more.

“Who are we?”

The shout set their blood boiling.

With everyone’s eyes upon him, Shao Shen raised his spear point straight into the air.

“We are the descendants of Zhonghua! We are brothers of the People’s Liberation Army and the Public Security Armed Forces!”

Aura the color of the setting sun surged from the spear point thrust high enough to pierce the sky.

Ssssss!

“Forward! Let’s wipe out every last one of those monsters!”

“Waaaaah!”

A deafening roar shook the earth.

Led by Shao Shen, the Hunters of the Public Security Armed Forces gripped their weapons and charged the monster army like fierce tigers.

“Don’t retreat! Show them the power of Zhonghua!”

“Uraaaaaah!”

- Grrrrr!

- Awooooo!

The cries of humans prepared to die mingled with the monsters’ roars. The two forces converged into a single mass and crashed into each other.

Kra-kra-kra-boom!

A collision that shook heaven and earth.

Death rained down from every direction.

“Gaaaaah!”

- Kweeeek!

Thud! Crunch!

Screams and thunderous impacts erupted across the battlefield.

The aura-coated blade of an A-rank Hunter cleaved through a lycanthrope’s neck, while an ogre’s iron club sent three or four Hunters flying as bloody pulp.

Two Hunters brought down a monster together and turned their weapons toward the next enemy.

A massive shadow fell over them.

- Kiiiiit!

Slash!

The talons of a diving griffon shredded their bodies along with their armor.

As the griffon searched for its next prey, a huge ball of fire hurtled toward it.

“Fireball!”

Bang!

Acrid smoke billowed as the griffon’s body reeled in midair.

The ranged units waiting on the ground did not miss their chance.

“Now!”

Boom! Bang-bang-bang!

Spells of every kind and arrows brimming with mana pierced the griffon.

As it plummeted with a final shriek, the other flying monsters let out savage cries.

- Kyaaaauuu!

They dove toward the ranged units, only to be met by modern weapons of lead and steel.

“Commence mass fire!”

Rat-a-tat-tat-tat! Boom!

Countless rifles and heavy weapons opened fire. Dozens of tanks unleashed their volleys at once.

Modern weapons could not compare to mana, the opposing force to the monsters’ magic. But with so much firepower concentrated on them at once, even the flying monsters were forced to falter.

- Kiiit!

“It’s working!”

“Don’t waste your shots anywhere else! Aim for the eyes!”

Monsters were born steeped in magic, and their flesh shrugged off most physical force.

Their eyes, however, were the sole exception.

Protected by only a thin membrane, their eyeballs were vulnerable enough to be damaged by heavy weapons.

At the sight of the monsters hesitating, the division commander overseeing the battle waved his baton excitedly.

“More! Pour it on! Don’t let those monsters move an inch—”

Whoooooom!

His voice cut off.

Poison Breath from a green wyvern engulfed an area roughly a hundred meters across. The division commander and his command staff were drenched in potent acidic poison and melted away.

“D-Division Commander!”

“The command staff…!”

The People’s Liberation Army descended into panic after losing hundreds of soldiers and senior commanders in the blink of an eye.

Officers, noncommissioned officers, and enlisted soldiers alike stared at the horrific scene before them and cried out in shock.

“This can’t be happening…”

“N-No. This isn’t right. This can’t be happening! This isn’t my mission!”

The scream spoke for everyone.

They had brought this much firepower as a precaution, but their primary mission was to rendezvous with and escort the foreign Hunters arriving soon at Chengdu International Airport, then proceed according to orders from above.

No one had told them that a monster army supposedly thousands of kilometers away might invade the airport.

“What the hell is going on…?”

“We’re going to die. We’re all going to die.”

The fear they had briefly forgotten descended upon the People’s Liberation Army once more.

Unlike the Hunters of the Public Security Armed Forces fighting at the front, they were ordinary people armed with modern weapons.

Their ominous premonition soon became reality.

A nightmare far worse than they had imagined.

- Om. Ne. Ha. So. Yu.

A voice spoke in broken, disconnected syllables.

An eerie sound like static from a radio with a failing signal echoed across the battlefield.

Black fog rolled in from nowhere and spread above the people’s heads.

- Yen. Wi. Ga. Ji. Ke!

Then the horrifying transformation began.

Swoooooosh!

Dark magic as black as storm clouds spread through the blood and corpses like a web.

It breathed new strength and souls into the bodies growing cold, then bound and enslaved them with chains.

Snap. Snap-snap.

An army of skeletons rose slowly from the pools of death, granted new life.

*Those beings* had linked countless dead with invisible chains and made them their servants.

They laughed in satisfaction.

- Kik, kikikik.

- Grrk. Kihihihi.

* * *

- Grrrrr.

With a wet, bubbling growl, a man rose to his feet.

Clad in armor bearing the Five-Star Red Flag and carrying a massive ax, he looked exactly like the A-rank Hunter Shao Shen remembered.

*…Mr. Yao Wei.*

But Shao Shen could not say the man’s name aloud.

He could not bring himself to.

He knew the figure before him was no longer the person he had known.

*Ah… ahhh.*

If Shao Shen had not witnessed his beheading a little over ten minutes ago—if the man had not risen with his severed head tucked beneath one arm—he would still have seen him as a comrade and friend.

But Yao Wei no longer existed.

His new name slipped from Shao Shen’s lips.

“Dullahan…”

A headless knight.

A Dullahan.

Shao Shen bit his lip at the sight of his comrade reborn as a higher undead monster. Something hot ran down his cheek.

“I’m sorry. I truly am.”

- Grrrrraaaah!

The Dullahan charged with a roar, and Shao Shen shot forward like the wind.

The two of them had often sparred this way in the past.

What began from simple competitive pride had become a daily routine, and after every bout, Shao Shen had to endure Yao Wei’s complaints.

*You little brat. No respect for your elders, none at all. Would it kill you to let me win once?*

*Ha-ha. Let’s go get something to eat. The loser pays, so I guess you’re buying again today, Mr. Yao Wei.*

*Your family’s loaded, but you’re still so damn cheap. One of these days, I’m going to make you buy me a meal.*

But it had never happened, and now it never would.

Shao Shen had always won.

*Goodbye. Thank you for everything.*

Whoosh! Slash!

The ax cut through empty air. Aura surged from Shao Shen’s spear point and cleaved through the Dullahan’s upper body.

A line appeared across its waist, and the headless knight’s body slowly collapsed.

Thud. Slump.

Shao Shen stared down at the face of the fallen Dullahan—no, of Yao Wei.

His eyes burned.

“How dare you… How dare you do this…?”

Friends and comrades who had been laughing and joking with him only half a day ago had become undead monsters.

The Hunters of the Public Security Armed Forces were renowned for their strict discipline, but they were not cold-blooded machines devoid of human feeling.

They had entered battle prepared to die. Now, for the first time, they faced a different kind of fear—the fear born of human attachment.

“Wake up! It’s me, Ryu Inchin! Ryu Inchin!”

“H-Hyung…!”

- Grrrrr!

Crunch! Boom!

Screams and death rained down from every direction.

The Public Security Armed Forces had lost nearly half their number. The monster army, meanwhile, had only grown larger and continued surging forward without end.

*Am I going to die here, like this?*

For the first time in his life, Shao Shen contemplated his own death.

The situation was desperate enough to make even someone as bright and cheerful as him think that way.

*We never received a warning signal, so communications must be down. There won’t be any reinforcements, either… This really is the end.*

Slash!

Shao Shen cut down one charging undead monster after another, then laughed hollowly and looked up at the sky.

The sunset was surprisingly beautiful.

Once the sun went down and darkness fell, he would never see anything like it again.

*It’s not bad for the last sky I’ll ever see…*

Huh?

Shao Shen blinked, unable to finish the thought.

Something unimaginably enormous was hurtling down from the sky toward the battlefield.

*An airplane?*

Whoooooom!

A massive aircraft wreathed in flames.

Then someone’s shout rang across the vast sky.

“Hey! Monsters!”

“…?”

- …?

*Am I hearing things?*

Shao Shen was not the only one looking up.

Everyone on the battlefield raised their eyes toward the sky.

A voice tinged with madness thundered overhead.

“I’m putting it in!”

*Putting what in?*

Shao Shen soon realized what the voice meant.

Rrrrrumble!

The airplane’s massive fuselage swept straight across the battlefield.

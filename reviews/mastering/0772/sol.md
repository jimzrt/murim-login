# Chapter 772

The incitement, the speech, the declaration—it was over.

The roar went on.

Reporters’ cameras caught the crowds pouring into the streets, while their microphones picked up cheers shouted in half-hoarse voices. Everywhere felt like a festival.

Only days ago, disaster had descended on this city. Now an inexplicable hope and joy filled it. Those feelings gathered and flowed toward one man.

“Michael! Michael!”

They chanted his name and applauded with all they had.

For a long time. With all their strength.

They poured out their gratitude and respect for a true hero who had stepped forward for their sake, without the slightest desire for the immense power of the World Hunter Federation.

Their hero stood on the stage and gazed at them, his face full of emotion. Then, unable to hold back his tears, he turned and hurried away.

The retreating figure of a hero who had always seemed so strong was beautiful.

From behind.

“Welcome back.”

Huginn was waiting below the stage. He gave a slight bow and gestured, and the Odin Guild’s Hunters hurried to block the reporters following Michael.

“Please step back.”

“Just a moment. It’ll only take—”

“I’m sorry, but not today.”

As Michael walked on, the bright lights and the cheers gradually faded behind him. He wiped the tears from the corners of his eyes and spoke in a dry voice.

“They responded well. Better than I expected.”

“They needed hope that badly. And those tears at the end—you were magnificent.”

“Did you think I was acting?”

Huginn slowed at the unexpected question.

“You… meant them?”

“I did. Though I’m not sure I felt what they did.”

Tears were strange things. They came with joy, sadness, anger—even emotions the person shedding them could not easily name.

His tears had been closest to that last kind.

*What was it?*

Joy at finally reaching his grand objective?

Or… a few of the fragments of feeling he had left?

Michael Silbert considered the reason for his tears, a reason even he did not know. Then he dismissed the thought.

He did not always need to find the right answer.

If he had tried to find the right answer to every problem he had faced, he would not be standing here now.

No. He would have died decades ago.

On that day.

“Guild Master?”

Huginn’s voice broke into his thoughts.

“Go on.”

“I have something to report. Just before you began your speech…”

“The small disturbance on the outskirts of the city?”

“You’ve already received a report?”

“No. I sensed it, faintly.”

Huginn stopped short behind him. Michael smiled.

“Surprised?”

“To be honest, yes.”

“I thought you might be.”

The disturbance had been considerable, but it had happened several kilometers away. Michael recalled the sensation that had swept over him.

“It surprised me too. Heat reached me from all that way off, so suddenly it felt as though every hair on my body might stand on end. For an instant, it was as if I could look down over the entire city.”

“…Guild Master, could it be—”

“I know what you’re thinking, but I wouldn’t worry. I only felt it that intensely for a moment.”

Michael muttered, almost to himself, “Perhaps I’ve been training too hard.”

“You should stop training for a while.”

“I’ve already been holding back. I did push myself too hard this past year. Perhaps I grew impatient without realizing it.”

He already knew what had made him impatient.

And he knew who was behind the fierce heat he had felt in the distance just before his speech.

“What happened to Jin Taekyung? If it had been an emergency, you would have interrupted me in the middle of the speech. It can’t have been too serious.”

“According to the watchers, the Stone King left the hotel first. Not long afterward, the area shook.”

“The top floor where they’re staying must have been the source.”

“Yes. Then everything went quiet, as if nothing had happened.”

“The monster left first. What else?”

“They got as close as they could and tried various spells, but they couldn’t see inside or hear anything…”

“That’s enough. I’d expect as much.”

Michael fell into thought. Then he murmured, “It seems they’ve had a disagreement.”

“A disagreement?”

“They must differ on what to do. And judging by what Jin Taekyung has shown us, he may do something even more reckless than we expect.”

“If you mean *that* reckless, surely…”

“What else? He doesn’t have many options in this situation.”

Huginn’s eyes widened. Michael continued.

“Increase security and tighten surveillance until the inaugural ceremony. Above all, don’t take your eyes off that monster for a moment. He’s the most important piece in this affair. We can’t afford to lose him.”

“Understood.”

Huginn answered grimly, then asked, “Wouldn’t it be better to use this opportunity to capture the monster alive?”

“Capture him?”

“Yes. If they hide him, or reveal his identity and sacrifice him before we can…”

“Huginn.”

Michael cut him off gently and looked at his right-hand man.

“I told you before that Jin Taekyung has a more fatal weakness than anyone else. Do you remember what it is?”

“His emotions…?”

“Exactly. And that is why he has no choice but to hold on to the monster until the very end.”

“You mean…”

“Yes. Somewhere along the way, Jin Taekyung began to think of the monster as a friend.”

“…!”

“Funny, isn’t it?”

Michael could not suppress a quiet laugh.

Even though he was remembered as one of the heroes of the Great Cataclysm, there had been plenty of walls in his way. Back then, he had not been overwhelmingly powerful, and the Odin Guild could hardly have been called a major guild.

So he had become stronger. He had brought down the walls and rivals before him by any means necessary.

And now? He had built a force and fighting strength no one could ignore, only to find that the obstacle in his path was the softest-hearted bastard he had ever faced.

*Idiot.*

Michael gave a hollow laugh and kept walking.

By then, his security team—no, his personal guard—had surrounded him on every side, each Hunter giving off a sharp aura.

Step after step, dozens of feet sounded as one.

When they reached the building where he was staying, the escorting Hunters spread out like a fan to secure the area. Their cordon was so tight that no one could break through.

Huginn stood at their head and gave a slight bow. Michael nodded to him without a word, then stepped into a space reserved for him alone.

Click. Whooom.

The moment he entered the room, the lock engaged. Dozens of defensive and security spells activated in succession.

The distant cheers he had still been able to hear seconds ago cut off.

At last, perfect silence.

Instead of settling onto the sofa, Michael crossed the broad carpet to a large full-length mirror covered in black cloth. He pulled the cloth away and channeled his energy into the clear glass.

Hummm.

A fine resonance filled the room. The mirror’s flawless surface rippled, distorting everything within it.

Whoosh.

The spacious room changed. So did the furniture and fixtures scattered around it. Finally, the person changed too.

In an instant, the scene in the mirror became one of utter darkness. A figure wrapped in a thick robe waited there for Michael Silbert.

—A very moving speech.

The voice gave away neither age nor gender.

Beneath the robe, The Prophet’s mouth curled into a faint smile. Michael Silbert’s eyes darkened.

* * *

Winter nights are long.

That night felt especially long because most of the world could not sleep.

—The World Hunter Federation will protect humanity!

Michael Silbert.

The declaration of a hero who had proven himself over decades set the public’s hearts ablaze again, just as his words had three days earlier.

People rushed into the streets, cheering and shouting. Fireworks burst over cities, and countless people sang as they marched through them.

All they had needed was a little hope.

Hope that someone would protect them and their loved ones from those monsters. Hope that humanity would win this time, as it had before.

Michael Silbert’s declaration washed away the fear that had gripped them for three days, then spread to every corner of the world.

> **Nigerian Civil War Ends!** “We only wanted answers.”
>
> **Congo: Protesters Halt March on Presidential Palace**
>
> **French President:** “The protesters have dispersed. The peace we have now was possible because a hero gave the UN an answer.”

Strictly speaking, Michael Silbert’s words had started all of it. The public saw things differently.

Michael did it all. He’s a true hero.

Hope. Joy. Questions. Anticipation.

Those feelings mingled as the World Hunter Federation’s inaugural ceremony drew closer by the hour. Prominent Hunters and Guild Masters from around the world boarded private planes bound for Korea.

Of course, I was one of them.

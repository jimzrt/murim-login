# Chapter 375

There was an order to visiting the sick.

When word spread that Tang Sadok—the Family Head and an elder of the clan—had awakened, the members of the Tang Clan dropped everything and came running.

But not all hundred-plus of them could see him.

“Benefactor, when can we go see Grandpa Tang?”

“Hard to say. The people ahead of us have been in there for a while, so they should be coming out soon.”

“Oh, I see.”

Cheongpung nodded at my answer.

Wait. Cheongpung?

“What are you doing here? When did you get here?”

“Just now.”

He had joined the conversation so naturally that I hadn’t even noticed him. But why had he come here?

As if he had read my mind, Cheongpung pointed at his chest.

“Mimi said she wanted to see him.”

“…?”

What the hell had I just heard?

Now he could even communicate with a snake. Was this guy from Slytherin instead of Huashan?

Cheongpung tilted his head at my suspicious stare.

“Why are you looking at my forehead all of a sudden? Is there something on it?”

“I was just checking whether you had a lightning-shaped scar.”

“What?”

“It’s a thing.”

The instant I finished speaking—

Clunk.

The tightly shut door of the infirmary opened, and about a dozen people emerged.

They were among the few surviving direct descendants of the Sichuan Tang Clan. Tang Horyong, whom I had met before, was among them.

“Whew…”

His eyes bloodshot, he looked up at the sky before walking toward us.

“The Family Head wishes to see you.”

“We’ve been waiting.”

Jin Wikyung nodded and led the way. Cheongpung and I followed him into the infirmary.

We walked through the heavy smell of medicinal decoctions until a physician with a white cloth covering his nose and mouth led us into a treatment room.

At last, we came face-to-face with several familiar people.

“Cough… You came.”

Tang Sadok’s condition was serious at a glance. His limbs were broken, and the qi within him was unstable from his internal injuries.

He tried to raise his upper body, but the Divine Physician, who had exchanged a brief nod with us, stopped him.

“Family Head, did I not tell you not to move?”

“This old man is a sinner. I committed a crime deserving of death, so it is only right that I ask for punishment.”

Tang Sadok shook his pale head, stared directly at me, and continued.

“I will make no pathetic excuses. The Western Heaven Demon Lord went to the underground prison because I told him about it.”

I folded my arms.

“Ah. No wonder.”

“…?”

“What?”

Tang Sadok asked with a flustered expression.

“You already knew?”

“Of course I didn’t know at first. Things were too hectic back then. But when I thought about it later, I started wondering how the hell that bastard knew where the Myriad-Poison Ring was.”

Only a tiny number of people knew its location.

Cheongpung might have looked as light as a flower petal, but he was as steadfast as the roots of a tree. That left Tang Sadok as the only person who could have revealed it.

“Why did you do it?”

“He said that if I told him where the Myriad-Poison Ring was, he would preserve our family’s bloodline.”

“And you believed him?”

“This old man was foolish. My judgment was clouded for a moment, and I did something I never should have done.”

“At least you realize that.”

Tang Sadok’s eyes trembled as he looked at me.

“The reason this old man survived must have been Heaven’s will. I was spared so that I might apologize to you and receive my punishment.”

“Then what punishment do you seek, Family Head?”

The cold voice belonged to Jin Wikyung, who had been silently listening to our conversation.

“Are you the Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan?”

“Yes. I am also the elder brother who raised his two younger brothers as if they were his own sons.”

Unmistakable anger smoldered in Jin Wikyung’s dark eyes.

“What you did was unthinkable for anyone who walks the righteous path.”

“I know. No… I am well aware. That is why I ask to be punished.”

“What if I order you to take your own life?”

“…!”

Everyone, myself included, stared at Jin Wikyung in shock.

Everyone except Tang Sadok.

His expression perfectly calm, he answered.

“I betrayed the principles of the Way, yet because all of you risked your lives to fight for us, our family’s bloodline will endure. If this worthless old man’s life can serve as atonement, I will gladly offer it.”

A brief silence followed. Then Jin Wikyung sighed.

“Whew…”

He regarded Tang Sadok with a complicated look before turning to me.

“What do you want to do?”

“…About what? Have him kill himself?”

“Anything.”

Having the hilt of a sword suddenly thrust into my hand made my heart clench.

Especially when the life hanging from the other end belonged to the Family Head of the Sichuan Tang Clan.

*Well, that killed the mood.*

Of course, this wasn’t something I could just laugh off. I wasn’t some perfectly impartial, broad-minded Great Hero of Benevolence and Righteousness. When I figured out the whole story, anger had quietly begun simmering inside me.

Everyone’s lives had been on the line back then, not just mine.

But…

“Forget it. I don’t want to take it that far.”

The truth was, part of me understood Tang Sadok’s position.

If I had to weigh an outsider I had met only a few times against my own blood—people I was duty-bound to protect as the head of my family—I might have made the same choice.

*Besides, I owed him.*

Tang Sadok’s help had played a major part in Jeok Cheongang’s awakening.

There might have been a deal involved, but Tang Sadok was still the one who had lent us his clan’s sacred treasure without even telling his own blood relatives.

“So let’s call it even. No, that’s going too far. Let’s just say the Sichuan Tang Clan owes us a huge debt for what happened.”

When I finished, Cheongpung and the Divine Physician spoke up.

“It was definitely Grandpa Tang’s fault that Benefactor was put in danger… but I’ll respect Benefactor’s wishes too.”

“I have already put it behind me. As a physician, my only wish is for the Family Head to recover as soon as possible. He still has surviving clan members who need him.”

Jin Wikyung spoke last. Unlike before, there was no longer any anger in his voice.

No—perhaps he had known what my answer would be from the start.

“You have heard them. What do you think, Family Head?”

“…!”

Tang Sadok’s eyes shook with emotion as he looked at us.

After a brief silence, a hoarse voice escaped his lips.

“This old man… The Sichuan Tang Clan owes all of you a tremendous debt of gratitude.”

The instant Tang Sadok bowed his head in heartfelt thanks—

Ding. Ding. Ding.

> You have acquired the **Title: Tang Clan’s Benefactor**!
>
> You have received a tremendous amount of **EXP** and **Fame** as a reward for completing the Hidden Quest!
>
> **Level Up!**

What the hell? A Hidden Quest out of nowhere?

While I stood dazed by the unexpected System notifications, something cold brushed between my legs and slipped past.

Sssrikk, sssriririk.

“Mimi, you little rascal.”

Watching Mimi reunite with Tang Sadok reminded me of something I had briefly forgotten.

“Oh, right. About the Myriad-Poison Ring… Luckily, I’ve kept it safe all this time—”

“Is that so?”

Tang Sadok cut in before I could finish.

“Then keep it.”

“Yes, then I’ll keep it… Wait, what?”

“I entrust our family’s sacred treasure to you. Consider it a token of our gratitude to our Benefactor, and please do not refuse.”

Ding.

> **System**
>
> By the owner’s will, **Myriad-Poison Ring** has been transferred to you!
>
> A new Item has become bound to you!
>
> Currently bound Items: **White Flame**, **Myriad-Poison Ring**, **???**

> One bound Item has not yet been given a name. Please give it a new name.

Was today some kind of special occasion?

I was starting to get nervous. What kind of fucked-up shit was waiting for me if the System was showering me with rewards like this?

Seeing me open and close my mouth like a goldfish, Tang Sadok smiled faintly.

“If there is anything you desire, tell me. So long as it lies within our family’s power, I will grant it.”

The Divine Physician smiled and answered.

“All I desire is for the patients to recover as soon as possible.”

“Oh…”

An answer worthy of the Divine Physician. Or should I call him Dong Feng now?

One thing was certain: he too was a Divine Physician in every sense of the title.

“What do you desire?”

Cheongpung jumped at the sudden question.

“M-Me?”

Tang Sadok nodded. Cheongpung twisted his hands and feet awkwardly as he answered.

“I… Well… Hmm. Nothing.”

“Truly?”

“Yes. I don’t think there’s anything.”

“…”

“…”

*Hey, you little bastard. Take your eyes off Mimi-chan when you say that.*

I wanted to show him a mirror. His eyes were filled with aching longing for Mimi-chan.

*At this rate, he’s going to burn a hole through her scales.*

Just then, Tang Sadok spoke.

“This little one is an old friend of mine. For the past several decades, she has been the only one with whom I could share the joy, anger, sorrow, and delight I could never reveal to anyone else.”

Cheongpung looked at Tang Sadok with pity.

“Grandpa Tang, you don’t have any other friends.”

“I chose not to make any. That was the burden of serving as the Tang Clan’s Family Head.”

“So you don’t have any friends.”

“It is not that I had none. I could have made them, but…”

“You didn’t have a single friend. How sad.”

“…”

The Divine Physician hurriedly grabbed Tang Sadok by the shoulder.

“Family Head, calm yourself. You’re breathing far too quickly!”

“Huff… Hoo, huff…”

“Take deep, slow breaths. Now, follow me. One, two…”

“Whoooosh…”

After narrowly surviving a bout of high blood pressure, Tang Sadok looked at Cheongpung and spoke.

“But I could entrust Mimi to you…”

Cheongpung covered his mouth with both hands.

“No, Grandpa Tang. I can’t take your only friend away from you.”

“…I haven’t said I would entrust her to you yet.”

“Oh. Ohhh.”

Tang Sadok heaved a deep sigh. For a moment, he had undoubtedly wondered whether it was really safe to entrust Mimi-chan to someone like him.

“Yes, you guessed correctly. Since we do not know what will become of our family, I wish to entrust Mimi to you. Temporarily, of course.”

“Yaaay!”

“Did you hear that last part? Temporarily.”

“Yaaay!”

*I’ll bet Hyuk Mujin’s right wrist he didn’t hear it.*

Cheongpung was beside himself with joy at becoming Mimi’s temporary guardian.

“Don’t worry. I’ll take good care of her!”

“From what I saw last time, Mimi does seem quite fond of you. But she is naturally temperamental and very wary of strangers, so…”

“Mimi! Whirlwind, then spin around and say hello!”

Sssriririk!

“Holy shit.”

She busted out a new move right here.

Jin Wikyung, who had been staring at the spectacle in a daze, muttered blankly,

“It seems you have nothing to worry about, Family Head.”

Tang Sadok’s eyes shook like an earthquake had struck them.

Tang Sadok asked Jin Wikyung to remain behind for a private conversation, so Cheongpung and I left the room first.

No—one more person had just joined us.

“Young Hero Jin, could you spare this old man a moment?”

“Me?”

The Divine Physician nodded with a gentle smile.

“There is something I must ask of you before you leave.”

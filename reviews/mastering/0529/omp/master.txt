# Chapter 529

Jingle.

“Welcome!”

The inn attendant’s hearty cry followed the ring of the bell above the door.

Ju Hwaran sprang to her feet, hope rising in her chest. But disappointment quickly filled her eyes.

“Ah…”

It wasn’t him. From up here, she couldn’t see the newcomer’s face properly, but she could tell that much.

No—if it had been him, she might have known him by his shadow alone.

*It wasn’t him. It wasn’t that person.*

Hwaran sighed softly and looked down at the first floor once more, reluctant to look away. Staring wouldn’t change anything, yet she couldn’t quite give up.

The men seated with her cleared their throats.

“Ahem.”

“Ahem.”

They had been watching Hwaran’s every move from the start. They weren’t foolish enough to miss what her reaction meant.

Three men sat across the table from her. Two of them exchanged a glance—but their eyes weren’t the only things communicating. Their lips barely moved as they spoke through Sound Transmission.

—Fellow Daoist Hwangbo. Who do you think Young Lady Ju is waiting for?

The question came from Baek Woo, the Kunlun Cloud Dragon. Handsome, clear-skinned, and dressed in spotless white, he was widely acknowledged as the Kunlun Sect’s finest young prodigy and was one of the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes.

The handsome young man with the sharp nose furrowed his brow slightly.

—No idea.

—I can think of only one person. If I’m right, it’s the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, Jin Tae—

—Drop it.

The young man who had cut him off so coldly was Hwangbo Ak, Lesser Family Head of the Hwangbo Family, the Shandong Fist Dragon, and another of the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes.

He was in a foul mood.

*To think I, Hwangbo Ak, would be treated as less than some other bastard.*

It was unthinkable. For someone as full of himself as Hwangbo Ak, it was especially hard to accept.

The Hwangbo Family might not be one of the Five Great Families, but its name carried considerable weight throughout Murim. No one could deny that it was an old martial family that had ruled Shandong for generations.

And Hwangbo Ak was its Lesser Family Head.

He had the martial talent to earn a place among the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes, too, and the looks to stand out anywhere. A fine family, immense wealth and prestige, a handsome face—it was only natural that women flocked to him.

The trouble was that, for the first time in nearly thirty years of life, everything he had enjoyed seemed worthless.

*The Dagger Hidden Flower, Ju Hwaran.*

The moment he saw her, he had realized that all the beautiful women he’d met before were fireflies before the sun.

She wore no makeup, unlike the others, yet her face held a beauty he couldn’t describe. Even her plain-colored martial uniform looked more elegant than an ornate court dress.

He had fallen for her at first sight.

*Why couldn’t I have met a woman like her sooner?*

Hwangbo Ak gazed at Hwaran as though entranced. Every small movement she made, every glimmer in her eyes—bright as the morning star—set his heart fluttering and stirred a desire buried deep within him.

But then…

“Haa.”

Hwaran sighed again, and his brow tightened.

He knew what lay behind that sigh. He knew who it was for.

*The Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, Jin Taekyung.*

He repeated the epithet and name silently. Beneath the table, his hand clenched of its own accord.

*What’s so special about that bastard?*

He had already heard about Hwaran’s connection to Jin Taekyung.

The details of what had happened between the Yongbong Escort Bureau and the Zhongnan Sect two months ago had never become widely known. But word had swept through Murim that the Taeeul Merciless Sword, one of Zhongnan’s three greatest masters, had knelt before a young prodigy barely past twenty.

*Impossible.*

It couldn’t have happened. It *shouldn’t* have happened. Hwangbo Ak had dismissed the story as an absurd rumor.

More troubling, though, was his suspicion that the incident had left Hwaran with feelings for Jin Taekyung.

—Are you certain?

Baek Woo perked up at the sudden Sound Transmission.

—Hm?

—I asked if you’re sure about what you said. The Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, Jin Taekyung. Is he really the one Young Lady Ju is waiting for?

—Do you think she’s waiting for the Successor Beggar?

—…

Even Hwangbo Ak had to admit she wasn’t.

Whatever peculiar tastes Hwaran might have, she couldn’t possibly like Gung Gibang. The man gave off the sour reek of stale dog food just by sitting nearby.

—So it really is that bastard? Are you saying this Young Master is less than a worm that crawled out of some backwater corner of Shanxi Province?

—A Divine Dragon, not a worm. And Shanxi Province is no longer a backwater. It’s where the dragon Jin Taekyung rose from obscurity, and it’s the center of the trade routes through the northern plateau.

Having lightly corrected him, Baek Woo continued.

—Fellow Daoist Hwangbo, this isn’t the Jin Family of Taiyuan you used to know. Now that I think about it, I could tell the moment I met the Heaven Shaking Sword.

—The Heaven Shaking Sword? Jin Mukyung?

—Is there another Heaven Shaking Sword we both know?

Hwangbo Ak’s eyebrows twitched.

If Hwaran hadn’t been sitting across from him, sighing over and over, and if that young escort who’d crawled in from who knew where hadn’t been watching, he would have scowled openly.

—Don’t bring up that name. Do you think I asked because I don’t know who he is?

—Ah, that’s right. You and the Heaven Shaking Sword were at Heaven’s Gate Temple together—

—Enough. Stop!

—You startled me. All right. Why are you shouting?

Hwangbo Ak’s clenched fist had turned red.

Jin Mukyung. That was a name he would remember until the day he died.

Hwangbo Ak had thought he possessed everything. Jin Mukyung had shown him a wall he could never climb—and humiliated him in the process.

*Damn it.*

Now the younger brother was standing in his way, too.

Seeing the anger on Hwangbo Ak’s face, Baek Woo moved his lips cautiously.

—Fellow Daoist Hwangbo. May I offer you some advice?

—What now?

—What you said to me just now? If the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon comes, don’t say it out loud. Better yet, don’t open your mouth at all.

—Hmph. Worried we might come to blows?

—No. I’m worried I’ll get dragged into it.

—…What?

Baek Woo regarded him with a look full of concern and pity.

—If you want to die, die alone.

—…!

—You were in closed-door training at your family’s estate during the Star-Array Grand Banquet, so you wouldn’t know. But even now, just thinking about him makes me… Ugh. Anyway, remember what I said. Understood?

What the hell was that supposed to mean? *Die alone?*

Hwangbo Ak stared at him in disbelief for a moment before sending another Sound Transmission.

—Are you… serious?

—Infinite Life Buddha. I swear before the Primordial Heavenly Venerable, I’m completely serious.

—Have you no pride as a martial artist?

—I lost that at the Star-Array Grand Banquet. Do you know what it’s like to have the top of your head stepped on after using the Cloud-Dragon Eight Forms?

—Even so!

—Even so, no. That man is a monster.

—W-What happened to the Kunlun Cloud Dragon, that proud, lofty crane? Where’s the Baek Woo I knew?

Baek Woo looked into the distance, his eyes full of nostalgia.

—Come to think of it, the Successor Beggar said something similar to me back then. In the very next test, Jin Taekyung beat him like a dog. You heard about that, didn’t you? Gung Gibang fell behind in movement technique, lost his head, and tried to grab Taekyung by the hair. He nearly got his own head knocked off.

—…!

—Infinite Life Buddha. Let’s be honest. Do you think everything the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon has done is just a rumor?

—Of course!

Baek Woo let out a quiet sigh at the firm reply.

—Why?

—How could any man do those things? His master, the Fire King, must have handled everything and given his Disciple the credit!

—True. By any ordinary measure, it’s absurd. Reaching Supreme Peak when he’s barely past twenty? How could that be possible?

At last, a sensible answer. Hwangbo Ak nodded with conviction.

—Exactly. The Fire King Jeok Cheongang must have stepped in and handed his Disciple the credit.

—I’m telling you again because I’m worried: don’t say that in front of the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon. I don’t want to be lumped in with you and get my face beaten in.

—…What are you playing at?

—Infinite Life Buddha. It’s simple. Don’t judge him by common sense. Don’t try to understand him. Just accept it.

For a moment, Baek Woo’s almost enlightened tone left Hwangbo Ak speechless. Then he tried another angle.

—W-Wait. You have feelings for Young Lady Ju too, don’t you?

—Young Lady Ju? Of course I like her. What man wouldn’t?

—And she has feelings for Jin Taekyung. You’re still going to defend your rival?

—My life matters more to me than Young Lady Ju. So does my hair.

—What?

Baek Woo checked that no one was looking, then casually dipped his head.

A strangely bare patch showed between the neatly arranged strands at his crown.

—Infinite Life Buddha. See?

—What on earth happened to your—

Hwangbo Ak couldn’t finish. Baek Woo gave him a mournful smile.

—Ever since he stepped on my head at the Star-Array Grand Banquet, the hair there hasn’t grown back…

—…!

—Give up. You’ll feel better. But if you absolutely can’t, please wait until I’m not around. I’d like to live.

What kind of insane nonsense was this?

Hwangbo Ak swallowed hard.

He was still furious, but a chill ran down his spine. Baek Woo was an old friend and a proud martial artist. If even *he* was talking like this…

*Is this for real?*

Hwangbo Ak had never believed all the rumors about the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.

But if—if there wasn’t a shred of falsehood in them…

*A monster beyond common sense.*

Yes. An undeniable monster.

At the same time, unbearable humiliation swept through him.

*Damn it all.*

His fist tightened. A groan escaped through his parted teeth, and Hwaran, sensing that something was wrong, spoke.

“Young Hero Hwangbo. Is something wrong?”

“Ah, no. Nothing.”

At his strained reply, the young escort who had done nothing but occupy a seat at their table gave a quiet laugh.

“It certainly looks like nothing.”

“…I said it’s nothing.”

“If you say so. The food here is good, by the way.”

Hwangbo Ak narrowed his eyes.

He had allowed the man to join them only because he was Hwaran’s escort. From his behavior to the way he spoke, everything about him grated on Hwangbo Ak.

A mutter slipped through his twisted lips.

“This is the trouble with people who have no pedigree…”

Hwaran was no fool. The troubled look on her face gave way to a cold one.

“Young Hero Hwangbo.”

“Ah, I wouldn’t want Young Lady Ju to misunderstand. I wasn’t talking about that fellow.”

Hwangbo Ak put on a false smile and pointed to a table some distance away.

“I meant those demonic, heterodox bastards over there.”

His voice carried through the inn, neither loud nor soft.

Scrape.

Someone rose from their seat.

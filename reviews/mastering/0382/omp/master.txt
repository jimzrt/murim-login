# Chapter 382

Strike the head, and the body falls.

Once I captured the three who appeared to be in charge, the undead under their control stopped moving like clockwork dolls. Deprived of its commanders, the monster army fell apart, its members either dying or fleeing in every direction.

“Arch Lich?”

The three creatures kneeling before me nodded.

I wasn’t sure whether I could call things that were already dead and reduced to bones “creatures,” but whatever.

I stroked each of their skulls in turn.

“When someone asks you a question, you’re supposed to answer. Are you throwing your weight around just because you’re dead?”

They answered before I had even finished speaking.

- Yes. Yes. It was the Arch Lich.

- What the great human heard is correct.

- Indeed.

“Hmm. An Arch Lich. Never heard of that monster before… By the way, which one of you just spoke to me informally?”

- That one!

- How dare he address the great human that way!

Swish! Swish!

Two skeletal fingers pointed at the third creature.

His comrades’ lightning-fast betrayal made the accused skull tremble.

- No! This is vile slander!

“…Doesn’t sound like slander to me.”

If he was going to lie, he could at least change the way he spoke.

After a quick look around, I pulled *it* out of my inventory.

“Boney. Snack time.”

It was a skull with a strange black sheen, flames flickering inside its empty eye sockets.

- …Boney? You might as well go back to calling me Warlordmon.

“Why? You’re all bones, so Boney. It fits perfectly.”

- How dare you insult this commander so!

“If you don’t like it, forget it.”

I was about to put him back into my inventory when the Skeleton Warlord shouted furiously.

- I will gladly eat it!

“What an honest little thing.”

An honest little thing deserved a reward. A bad liar deserved punishment.

“Here. Have a taste.”

- Thank you, slightly less wicked human. But how much may I eat…?

“Just a little, like before.”

- Hmm. I would like more, but very well.

Too many snacks would be overdoing it. With a hint of disappointment, the Skeleton Warlord opened his mouth wide toward the trembling creature.

- Come here.

- E-Eek! No!

- Yes!

The change began the moment the Skeleton Warlord gave his firm reply.

Whoooooosh!

It was fascinating no matter how many times I saw it.

Black mist streamed from the kneeling creature’s body and was sucked into the Skeleton Warlord like dust into a vacuum cleaner.

- Gaaaaaah!

And that wasn’t the only change.

As more of the black mist—the substance the Skeleton Warlord called death energy—flowed out, the creature’s complexion… or rather, its bones, gradually turned white.

Meanwhile, the Skeleton Warlord’s black sheen grew deeper and darker.

- S-Stop!

- Hehehe. Such delicious death energy.

- Noooo!

- This commander shall take it all. Death energyyyy!

Crack!

- Hk!

“Enough with the ‘death energyyyy.’ Where did you learn that weird crap?”

- …Are you really one to talk?

“Anyway, stop eating.”

- Why?!

“You’ll get fat.”

The Skeleton Warlord was struck speechless. I tucked him against my chest and examined the creature.

Its bones had been dark and grimy when I first saw it. Now they were halfway bleached white. Perhaps because so much death energy had been drained from it, the green flames in its eye sockets wavered dangerously.

- Sob… sob…

The other two creatures anxiously clacked their teeth at the sight of their weakened comrade, sucked dry to the bone.

- Ask us anything, great ruler of flame.

- I humbly beseech you. Please accept the loyalty of this lowly being, Orpheus von Maximus Valencia Bayern.

“…Are you undead, or are you bidets?”

They clearly didn’t want to lose their power. Their cooperation was convenient for me, though.

“All right. If there’s anything you haven’t told me, scrape the bottom clean and spill every last detail. But if you start spewing lies…”

- We will tell you everything!

- I humbly beseech you! Please permit this false and lowly being to speak the truth!

- I-I’ll talk.

Apparently, none of the three wanted to end up as bone broth. They threw themselves into the interrogation with enthusiasm.

I heard every last detail of what had happened over the past week, from the Arch Lich’s first appearance to the present. Then I deliberately furrowed my brow.

“You’re sure?”

- Yes, we are!

- We swear upon the River of Death!

- Th-There is not a single lie in our words.

As the three creatures nodded their skulls vigorously, the Skeleton Warlord cut in.

- It is true.

“Did they slip you something under the table? How can you guarantee that?”

- Because they swore upon the River of Death. To beings like us, that is an absolute oath—one that can never be broken.

“Hmm.”

The Skeleton Warlord normally treated everything with all the gravity of a speck of dust. If even he was being this solemn, they probably weren’t lying.

Besides, they had no reason to try anything under the circumstances.

“Okay. I’ll believe you.”

- Thank you! Thank you so much!

- Sob, sob! I shall serve you with all my heart, my king!

- I-I will serve the great human as well. I swear upon the River of Death that I will never again commit such an act—

Crack!

The last creature faltered before he could finish.

The trembling green flames in his eye sockets shifted between me and the fist buried in his chest.

- Wh-Why?

“Why do you think?”

- I-I swear my loyalty. Upon the River of Death, I swear—

“Too late.”

A voice so cold it seemed to belong to someone else slipped through my lips.

“Too late to undo what you’ve done.”

Countless people had died here today.

Even regular troops armed with firearms and Hunters had been unable to stop them. There was no telling how many civilians had died at the monster army’s hands over the past week.

“I don’t need your loyalty. Especially not from pieces of shit like you.”

I released the Scorching Yang Qi I had drawn up from my dantian.

Fwoosh!

Extreme Yang qi erupted from the fist lodged between the creature’s shattered ribs. Blue-white qi blazing with tremendous heat coiled around its entire body.

Kaaaa-boom!

I saw the green flames in his eyes, flickering like candles in the wind, go out.

I also saw the other two creatures spring to their feet and begin chanting.

- Jajeuchawa Eumbado…!

- Bareugan Mahra…!

Whoooong.

A wind of mana swirled around them. Just as their sinister spells were about to be completed, I tossed out a command.

“Devour them. All of them.”

As though he had been waiting for those exact words, the Skeleton Warlord leaped from inside my clothes and opened his mouth wide.

- Gladly.

- Barsaba… Eeeek!

- N-No!

The monsters’ final screams were filled with their desperate desire to live—or rather, to remain undead.

But their wishes meant nothing. The Skeleton Warlord’s suction was stronger and faster than ever.

Whoooooosh! Gulp!

He swallowed an enormous amount of death energy in a single bite, then shuddered.

The next moment, the two skeletons, drained of every last trace of energy, collapsed into piles of bone.

Ding. Ding. Ding.

> **System**
>
> - The unexpected Quest, **Unexpected Assault**, has been successfully completed!
>
> - You have routed the monster army! This is truly an outstanding achievement!
>
> - As a Quest Reward, you have acquired the Title **Undead Hunter**!
>
> - You have acquired a considerable amount of EXP and Fame!
>
> - Level Up!

Only once?

In the past, that would easily have earned me several levels. Now that I was Level 120, the EXP requirement must have gotten much higher.

*It’s not like I did this for the EXP.*

I had only done what needed to be done, but I couldn’t help feeling a little disappointed.

The stronger I became, the more useful I would be in the battles ahead.

*The same goes for him.*

I looked at the Skeleton Warlord.

Thanks to the tremendous amount of death energy he had absorbed, the power emanating from him was on a completely different level from when we first met.

- Hmm. Hoooooo…

Black mist billowed from the holes in his skull—his nose, ears, eyes, and more.

Purple flames blazed in his eye sockets like torches, and his skull gleamed with a smooth, inky luster. Then his booming laughter rang through my head.

- Krah, hahahahaha!

“Turn down the volume. You’re loud.”

- You vile human. This time, this commander must offer you his profound gratitude.

“You should. Who fed you?”

The Skeleton Warlord bristled at my indifferent reply.

- Fed me?! Are you suggesting this body has become a pet?!

“Close enough. Isn’t it?”

- Do not spout nonsense!

“Really? Boney, come.”

I held out my hand.

The skull sprang up and landed neatly on my palm.

As a reward, I gently scratched the spot between his eyes.

“Good boy, Boney. Who’s a pretty boy?”

- …!

The skull trembled violently.

- H-How can this be?! How can this commander respond so to a mere human?!

“Your mouth says no, but your body is honest.”

- I am the master of the Black Forest and commander of the great undead legion! Do not humiliate this body!

“A commander who’s only a head?”

- What?! This paltry body means nothing! I can restore it whenever I wish by expending death energy!

“Really? Then why haven’t you restored it yet?”

- …Because some lunatic human would only smash it again.

“Oh, correct.”

Grinding his bone joints in place of teeth, the Skeleton Warlord narrowed the flames in his eye sockets.

- Why, vile human?

“What?”

- You must have some ulterior motive for feeding me—or rather, granting me such tremendous power. You clearly harbor sinister intentions. Tell me the truth!

I thought for a moment before answering.

“Hmm. Because you’re a fucking weakling.”

- What?

“You can’t beat me anyway. And if I’m going to drag a weakling around, it’ll be easier to make use of one who’s stronger and more useful. Right?”

- …!

“Now get back inside. People are coming.”

I returned the creature, frozen with shock, to my inventory and stood.

The airport duty-free corridor would normally have been packed with employees and passengers. Now it was empty and dark, and three people were walking toward me through the gloom.

Two of them were familiar.

“Mr. Jin Taekyung.”

“Teacher Jin.”

They were Team Leader Choi, who looked relatively presentable, and Shao Shen, the A-rank Hunter from China’s Public Security Armed Forces.

A considerable amount of time had passed since the battle ended, but Shao Shen was still covered in blood and dust. Exhaustion weighed heavily on his face.

“So you were here.”

I greeted Team Leader Choi with a glance and offered an excuse.

“Yes. I had something to take care of.”

“Please, speak casually to me. Sibeol—no, Teacher Jin, you are a hero who saved me and my comrades. More than that, you saved the people of Zhonghua.”

“……”

Was it my imagination, or had he just been about to call me Sibeol-jwa?

Oblivious to my thoughts—or perhaps not—Shao Shen continued with the utmost respect.

“Fortunately, with the help of the two gentlemen from Peace Guild, we were able to repel the monsters. Allow me to thank you once again.”

“Ah, sure. It was nothing. I only did what needed to be done.”

I waved it off, then glanced at Team Leader Choi.

I had been worried that the Integrated Language Pack might malfunction and make him notice something strange. But since I was speaking with Shao Shen, it seemed my words sounded like Chinese even to Team Leader Choi.

“But who is the gentleman beside you…?”

The only unfamiliar face among them belonged to a middle-aged man with graying hair.

He had listened to our conversation in silence. Now he held out his hand.

“I am Wei Penghu, Minister of Defense at the Central Military Commission. It is a pleasure to meet you, Teacher Jin.”

“Minister of Defense…?”

“My rank is Senior General.”

“Oh.”

I could tell he was an important man, but I had no idea what a Senior General was.

As if he had read my mind, Team Leader Choi leaned over and whispered in a voice as tiny as an ant.

“Four-star. Four-star.”

“Oh. Ohhh! You’re a four-star general! Nice to meet you!”

I had been a four-star once, too. The game I played as a kid had been a lot of fun. Its sequel, though? What a spectacular flop.

Wei Penghu smiled faintly at my reaction and clasped my hand.

“Perhaps it is because you are young, but you are certainly full of vigor. I have much to ask you, Teacher Jin… Shall we talk on the way?”

“Sure, why not?”

I started after Wei Penghu, then stopped.

“But where are we going?”

“To the operations headquarters. I have a jet standing by.”

“What? Headquarters? A jet?”

“That is correct. Everyone is waiting for you there.”

Everyone?

Who?

# Chapter 144

He wanted my autograph. A king, no less.

*What the hell is this?*

I hadn’t seen this scenario coming.

As I stood there speechless with bewilderment, a pair of large eyes gazed steadily at me.

“Have I asked too much of you?”

“No, it’s not that… What are you planning to do with my signature?”

“Hmm. If you don’t want to, you don’t have to.”

Beneath the archaic speech straight out of a historical drama lurked an eagerness he couldn’t quite hide.

The way his feet scuffed the training ground like a puppy’s paws and his hands kept fidgeting gave him away.

*The little guy is pretty cute.*

He was still a child. King or not, he couldn’t hide his age.

His expression was full of dignity, but his body was painfully honest. A quiet laugh escaped me.

“Why are you laughing?”

“It’s nothing. So I just carve my name into this wooden tablet?”

The young king’s lips twitched.

“Preferably with your alias as well.”

Why was he taking this so seriously? Holding back another laugh, I picked up the dagger.

*Scritch, scritch, scritch.*

Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Jin Taekyung. I rested the wooden tablet on my knee and carefully carved the seven characters into it.

That was when Zhu Bao suddenly asked, “Wouldn’t it be easier if you used Sword Energy?”

“It would.”

“Then why aren’t you using it?”

“It’s not that I won’t. I can’t.”

“You can’t use Sword Energy?”

“I mean exactly what I said. I’m not a Peak master yet, so I can’t use Sword Energy.”

“You’re… not a Peak master?”

I glanced up to find Zhu Bao staring at me in shock.

“Aren’t you the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi?”

“Yes, that’s me.”

“And yet you can’t use Sword Energy? You aren’t a Peak master?”

“…It happens.”

“No, it doesn’t!”

Wow. He actually sounded hurt.

I’d already been feeling inadequate with all the Sword Energy I’d been seeing lately. Now some kid I’d never met before was twisting the knife.

*Is it a crime not to know how to use Sword Energy?*

My feelings were hurt, and the young prince’s admiration for me had taken a hit.

I was rubbing my nose in wounded frustration when he exclaimed, “How can you be so strong when you aren’t even a Peak master?”

“What?”

“One Question, One Kill Jopil, Blade of Flowers Jin Baekyang, and most recently Pung Yang, the Red Wind Band Leader. Weren’t all the enemies you defeated powerful Peak masters?”

Jopil was the only one I could honestly claim to have defeated entirely through my own strength, but I nodded anyway.

“That’s right.”

“How was such a thing possible?”

“Well…”

*The Inventory is fucking amazing. And nobody can withstand a group beating.*

*Whenever things look hopeless, try digging through your Inventory. You might find an elixir worth half a jiazi or a weapon made of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.*

*…I can’t exactly tell him that.*

Your image was something you had to build for yourself.

I smiled serenely and said, “Because I was stronger, of course.”

“Wow!”

“There’s a saying in Murim. The strong do not survive. Those who survive are strong.”

“Wow!”

“I’ve fought three Peak masters so far. I was once even attacked by dozens of First Rate masters.”

“Good heavens!”

Zhu Bao clenched his tiny fists and gasped in admiration.

His reactions were so gratifying that I couldn’t help wanting to continue. Recalling all the crises I’d overcome, I went on.

“But each time, I fought for my life and survived. Peak masters? Sword Energy? None of that matters.”

“Sword Energy doesn’t matter? Do you truly mean that?”

“Of course.”

*It matters like fucking hell.*

Every bastard I met could reel off Sword Energy like endless strands of rice cake, while I was the only one who couldn’t use it.

Every time I fought, I ended up wondering whether my life was worth any more than a fly’s.

*Make sure you keep yourself loaded with elixirs. Without Sword Energy, it’s your body that suffers.*

With outrageous disrespect, I placed a hand on the young king’s shoulder and whispered, “The desire to win. The unbreakable will to never give up. That is what made the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi who he is today.”

“An unbreakable will…!”

Zhu Bao’s small body trembled. After releasing a heated breath, he asked, “Can I become like you?”

“You can. From what I saw of your training just now, you’ll become a master in no time.”

“D-Do you truly mean that?”

*It’ll be tough without the System.*

*But a growing sprout needs water.*

I nodded at his enormous, sparkling eyes, carved a few more characters into the finished tablet, and handed it to him.

“Whenever things get hard, look at this and take heart.”

“What is this…?”

The young king examined the tablet and broke into a radiant smile.

“Thank you. I shall have this tablet enlarged and hang it on the signboard of the Shanxi Provincial Office.”

“…That?”

“Of course. Everyone who enters or leaves this place shall read your celebrated words.”

As Zhu Bao gazed fondly at the wooden tablet, I thought,

*He’s going to hang that on the signboard of the Shanxi Provincial Office?*

*Dreams☆come true.*

—Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Jin Taekyung—

*…That?*

* * *

By the time we returned to the grand hall, it had been restored to pristine condition as though nothing had ever happened.

The servants filled the newly installed tables with delicacies from land and sea. Once they were done, Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao spoke from the seat of honor.

“I thank you all for willingly answering my summons. Now, please eat your fill.”

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> Quest condition, **Attend the Luncheon Hosted by the City Lord**, has been fulfilled!
>
> The Quest Reward will be issued after the luncheon ends.

The mood remained warm and cheerful. With the young king, both host and master of the gathering, grinning from ear to ear, how could it not?

“I have heard that One Question, One Kill Jopil was an exceptionally vicious man. Could you tell me what he was like?”

“Oh, that bastard was absolutely brutal. You see…”

“I’ve heard that Blade of Flowers Jin Baekyang was a Peak master whose name was known even in the Central Plains. How strong was he?”

“Fucking strong. The man was insane.”

“Young Hero Jin, His Highness is listening. Please mind your language.”

“Ah, sorry. Anyway, what happened was…”

After regaling him with stories for some time, I was exhausted. I tossed Cheongpung to Zhu Bao, who wouldn’t stop asking questions, and quietly began edging away.

“Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, where are you going?”

“You know the Sword Saint, Mae Jonghak, right? This guy is his disciple.”

“The Sword Saint!”

“And he’s a Peak master too. Ask him to teach you Sword Energy.”

Leaving Zhu Bao as delighted as a child meeting Santa Claus, I slipped off to one side.

I spotted the young prodigies of the Five Gates of Shanxi silently cramming food into their mouths and two men engaged in a rather serious discussion.

Neither group was particularly inviting company.

*I’ll just eat.*

But before I could manage more than a few pieces of meat, a syrupy voice wormed into my ear.

“Young Hero Jiiin.”

“…What?”

Hearing that voice killed my appetite on the spot.

“What are you doing over there all by yourself? Come talk with us.”

“No, thank you. I’m hungry.”

“It concerns the Jin Family of Taiyuan.”

“I don’t get involved in family affairs. Speak to my eldest brother separately.”

“What a shame. Then I suppose we’ll have no choice but to entrust it to the Seongun Escort Bureau.”

The Seongun Escort Bureau? The name sounded familiar. Then I remembered—it belonged to the family of the guy I’d beaten black and blue at Honghwa Inn yesterday.

Hadn’t that guy been the Seongun Escort Bureau’s Young Bureau Head?

“What about the Seongun Escort Bureau?”

The corners of Hong Jin’s mouth curled upward.

“Oh, nothing. Please, finish your meal. They say even a dog is left alone while it eats. I could hardly disturb the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi.”

“……”

“Hee-hee. It was only a joke. Don’t look so serious. Come and sit.”

Hong Jin pulled out the chair beside him, and I reluctantly sat down.

Beside Li Feng, of course.

I’ll say it again: my ass is precious.

“Let’s hear what this is about. I don’t know much about these things, so I may not be any help.”

Hong Jin had been pouting in feigned disappointment. Now he spoke.

“Young Hero Jin needn’t make a decision here. You only have to relay the matter to the Lesser Family Head. Assistant Commissioner Li?”

Li Feng took over.

“We would like to borrow the strength of the Jin Family of Taiyuan for this undertaking.”

“This undertaking being…?”

“I believe you’re aware of our plans to connect Shaanxi and Shanxi.”

“The project you originally planned to pursue with the Zhongnan Sect?”

Hong Jin nodded.

“To be honest, the Zhongnan Sect isn’t a bad partner. It is a great sect belonging to the Nine Sects and One Gang, and its leadership—including its Sect Leader, the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord—is pragmatic. They differ from the more insular Murim sects in that regard.”

“Then was there really any need to switch partners? If you hadn’t intended to work with Huashan from the start, leaving things as they were might have been better.”

“It was a decision we reached after careful consideration, though we did overturn it today.”

Hong Jin smiled faintly and continued.

“I may not be a martial artist, but I’m well aware of the Sword Saint’s standing in Murim.”

“……”

“However, the Sword Saint disappeared more than thirty years ago. Had we known sooner that he had remained at Huashan to train a successor, we would never have chosen the Zhongnan Sect.”

Hong Jin finished and shot Li Feng a reproachful look.

Apparently, Li Feng had known about the Sword Saint and Cheongpung for the past ten years without breathing a word of it.

“Deputy Military Commissioner, as I have said before, that was confidential information belonging to our sect. Now that Martial Uncle Cheongpung has descended the mountain, there is simply no longer any reason to conceal it.”

Li Feng replied calmly, then turned to me.

“I’ll get straight to the point. We need an Escort Bureau capable of expanding into the Central Plains, starting with Shaanxi.”

*Ah.*

I had a rough idea of what they wanted.

They were asking the Jin Family of Taiyuan to contribute material resources, manpower, or both.

“Are you planning to establish an Escort Bureau?”

“Something similar. However, we would like to borrow the name of the Jin Family of Taiyuan. In exchange, we will provide half the funding and every possible convenience.”

They were openly offering to bankroll us. At that point, wouldn’t it be better for them to establish the Escort Bureau themselves?

As I wondered why they wouldn’t, I suddenly recalled something Jin Mukyung had told me a few days ago.

*He said the current Emperor assassinated his older brother, the Crown Prince, before ascending the throne. I’m sure of it.*

It was only an unconfirmed rumor, but judging by how cautiously these two were proceeding, it didn’t seem entirely baseless.

Could the Emperor’s suspicion be why he had sent his only younger brother to Shanxi Province, a region treated like the frontier?

*Hmm. Something about this smells fishy too.*

As I wondered whether I’d gotten mixed up in something dangerous, the two men spoke.

“We’ll arrange a meeting regarding this matter in the coming days. Please put in a good word with the Lesser Family Head.”

“Young Master Jin, you know this is a good offer, don’t you?”

“I know. I do, but…”

Looked at one way, this was someone else’s family feud. Ordinary brothers might fight over who got an extra ice cream, give each other bloody noses, and call it a day.

But they weren’t fighting over ice cream. They were fighting over the imperial throne.

A bloody nose wouldn’t be the end of it.

“I’ll make sure to pass the offer along to my eldest brother.”

I deliberately kept my answer noncommittal. Jin Wikyung would be the one making the decision, so there was no point in my agonizing over it—unless he asked for my opinion first.

“That will be enough. He won’t simply dismiss something Young Master Jin tells him. We’ve heard plenty about how much the Lesser Family Head cherishes his younger brothers.”

*So the whole neighborhood knows.*

I drained my cup with an embarrassed look, only to meet the eyes of the four men seated at the far end of a table some distance away. They all looked ready to suffer indigestion.

Oh, right. I’d almost forgotten something.

“Excuse me, Comrade Chairman. No, Deputy Military Commissioner.”

“Yes?”

“About that Escort Bureau. Wouldn’t changing the sign be enough?”

Li Feng looked baffled, but Hong Jin grinned.

“Do you have somewhere in mind?”

“The place you two mentioned earlier.”

“The Seongun Escort Bureau? Don’t underestimate them. They are the foremost Escort Bureau in Shanxi, after all. They won’t be easy to swallow in one bite.”

“That’s why we’ll chew thoroughly.”

One look at their Young Bureau Head had told me exactly what we were dealing with. With Hong Jin and Li Feng backing the Jin Family of Taiyuan as it now stood, we could chew them up bones and all—and still digest them.

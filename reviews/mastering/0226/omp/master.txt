# Chapter 226

Click, click!

A barrage of camera flashes blinded me. You’d think I would have gotten used to it by now, but apparently I was still a pine caterpillar who belonged among pine needles.

“Mr. Jin Taekyung! Did you clear the Gate?”

“If not, why did you come out so quickly?”

Questions rained down from the reporters. If the Gate security guards hadn’t been there, they would have shoved their microphones straight into my mouth.

But the initial frenzy soon died down, and confused voices rose to fill the void.

“But… why is Jin Taekyung covered in blood?”

“The numbers don’t add up, either. The people behind him are Star Guild members.”

“And they’re tied up.”

“Where’s Won Myunghoon? I don’t see him!”

I could more or less imagine how we looked on camera.

There was me, drenched in the blood of monsters and Won Myunghoon. A dozen or so Star Guild members tied together in a line. And Butler Kim holding the rope as though he were walking a dog.

*I can’t blame them for reacting like that.*

I licked my dry lips, then shook my head at Butler Kim as he started to step forward on my behalf.

He had tried to stop me, and I had refused. This had happened because of my choice, so it was only right that I deal with the aftermath myself.

“Mr. Jin Taekyung! What happened?”

“Please, just give us one comment!”

The confused murmuring didn’t last long. The moment the reporters caught the scent of a scoop, they went wild.

I watched the frenzied press corps for a moment, then addressed the young reporter at the very front. He wore thick, horn-rimmed glasses.

“Who do you work for?”

The young man flinched.

“Pardon? M-Me?”

“Yes. You.”

“Uh, well, I…”

He had been shouting louder than anyone, but apparently he hadn’t expected to be singled out.

After a brief panic, he finally managed to answer.

“H-HunterTV.”

“Oh. Them.”

HunterTV was a channel devoted to Hunter-related news and information.

It was a cable channel, but it was also a major broadcaster whose ratings surpassed those of terrestrial networks.

As luck would have it, that made it a good fit for what I was about to say.

“I’ll take your questions.”

“Pardon?”

“There are too many microphones. I’d rather focus on one.”

“So… an exclusive?”

“Call it whatever you want. Shall we begin?”

He looked like a greenhorn, but a reporter was still a reporter.

The bewilderment vanished from his face, and his eyes gleamed behind his glasses.

“How about broadcasting it live?”

“Live?”

“Yes. Like at the Association last time. We can stream it in real time.”

A live stream.

After a moment’s thought, I nodded.

“Let’s do that.”

* * *

iTube was the number-one video-sharing website.

Countless channels, belonging to everyone from individuals to major organizations, were registered on the platform. Among them, HunterTV was a massive channel with several million subscribers in Korea alone.

At the same moment, the phones of HunterTV subscribers going about their daily lives all buzzed with the same notification.

Bzzzz.

[HunterTV has started a live stream.]

[“Jin Taekyung Exclusive Live Broadcast.”]

The view count hit 100,000 in no time.

The sudden influx of viewers had left the screen black, but the chat was already blazing like a furnace packed with firewood.

What is this? Why is there suddenly an exclusive Jin Taekyung live broadcast?

No idea. Feels like it hasn’t even been a day since Lord Fuck entered the Gate.

It’s an A-Rank Gate. He’s already out?

This buffering is absolute garbage.

Everyone shut up and welcome Lord Fuck with the proper reverence.

It’s Lord Fuck!

Jin Taekyung’s popularity was through the roof.

He had saved countless lives by suppressing a B-Rank Gate. His approachable, down-to-earth demeanor, unexpected from an A-Rank Hunter, and even the bizarre spectacle of him accidentally swearing during an official press conference had kept him at the center of attention day after day.

But the excitement didn’t last long. The viewers soon noticed that something was wrong.

Why does he look like that?

Did Lord Fuck get hurt? He’s covered in blood.

Half the people are missing, too. And the ones there are tied up;;

What the hell is this? Something definitely happened.

What’s HunterTV doing? If you started a broadcast, at least ask some questions.

Perhaps mindful of the chaos in the chat, the HunterTV reporter finally spoke.

“I’ll get straight to the point. What happened inside the Gate?”

“It would take too long to explain everything from beginning to end… Is it all right if I summarize?”

“Of course.”

Vrrrrr.

The camera zoomed in until Jin Taekyung’s face filled the screen.

His face was caked with blood and dust, but his eyes were calm and steady. A composed voice emerged from between his parted lips.

“There was a Named Monster.”

“……!”

“It was a Mutated Gate.”

In an instant, the noise at the scene vanished as though someone had pressed the mute button.

The chat froze for a moment, then began racing upward.

They must all be dead. No wonder so many people are missing;;

Is Won Myunghoon dead, too? I haven’t seen him this whole time;

Holy shit…

*A major incident.*

The same words flashed through everyone’s minds.

Even the reporter holding the microphone out to Jin Taekyung forgot his job and stood there with his mouth hanging open.

*A Named Monster.*

Everyone knew the kind of destruction one could cause.

Five years earlier, a Named Monster that appeared in Central and South America had possessed enough power to destroy a small city by itself.

Although it had finally been defeated after several days of fighting, the damage had been enormous.

Hundreds of Hunters had died, along with ten times as many civilians.

The incident had drawn the attention of the entire world. There wasn’t a reporter or viewer who hadn’t heard of it.

The reporters who had been staring back and forth between Jin Taekyung and the chat as if hypnotized suddenly snapped out of it.

“A Named Monster!”

“Hey, get out of here! Grab the cameras!”

No scoop was worth more than their lives.

Just as the reporters, sensing the threat of death, began descending into chaos, the man who had been watching it all finally spoke.

“Ah, I already took down the raid monster, so you don’t have to worry too much.”

His clear voice rang in everyone’s ears.

The reporters who had been about to flee and the viewers swallowing nervously as they stared at their phone screens all wondered if they had heard him correctly.

“Pardon?”

“……What did he say?”

The camera, which had been shaking as though caught in an earthquake, steadied and refocused. Dozens of cameras turned toward the young man’s perfectly calm face.

The reporter in horn-rimmed glasses asked Jin Taekyung in a trembling voice,

“C-Could you say that one more time?”

“It’s just as I said.”

“Then you really killed the Named Monster…”

“Yes. I killed it.”

He killed it? A Named Monster?

Everyone’s jaws dropped at the unbelievable words that had come from Jin Taekyung’s mouth. One reporter finally recovered enough to blurt out a question.

“T-Then did the others die fighting it?”

“Seven people died. The rest are safe…”

His voice trailed off. After thinking for a moment, he continued in the same unwavering tone.

“Oh, I should add Won Myunghoon to the death toll. That makes eight.”

“W-Won Myunghoon? Why did he…”

“Uh, well…”

Jin Taekyung hesitated briefly before adding,

“I got him.”

“……Pardon?”

“Ah, not got. I killed—no, that sounds strange, too. Give me a moment.”

Before the aftershock of the Named Monster bomb had even faded, a second bomb went off.

The dozens of reporters at the scene and the 100,000 viewers watching the stream were left half dazed.

*What the hell is he talking about?*

At the exact moment the same thought crossed everyone’s mind, Jin Taekyung finally found what he considered the most appropriate expression.

“This is probably the best way to put it. I ‘disposed of him.’”

“…….”

“…….”

At the end of the heavy silence, a single new line appeared in the frozen chat.

Did this guy just come back from sorting recyclables at home?

* * *

Everything moved swiftly from there. The emergency investigation team dug into the truth of the incident with speed and precision.

Countless eyes were on the case. If they missed even a single detail, they might receive a lifetime’s worth of abuse.

“Handle this properly. Miss one thing and your head rolls, got it?”

“This has already been reported all the way to the Blue House. If we slip up, we’re all going over the cliff together.”

Both the government and the Hunter Association’s leadership were on edge.

Contrary to their fears, however, it didn’t take long for the whole truth to come to light.

“Holy shit… He killed this thing by himself?”

“He actually killed it.”

The investigators were stunned when they confirmed that the enormous Named Monster lay gruesomely dead.

By then, every Star Guild member who had participated in the raid was tied to a cold metal chair in an interrogation room.

“I don’t know anything.”

“Look, Hunter. The truth will come out soon enough. Why make this difficult?”

“I’m telling you, I don’t know anything.”

“Ha, you bastard… One mental spell would have you spilling everything, but you still have to make this annoying.”

“Did you just swear at me? You think being a prosecutor means you can do whatever you want? And don’t you know mental magic is illegal?”

Their lives were on the line. They couldn’t give up the comfortable lives they had enjoyed as B-Rank Hunters and rot in prison for the rest of their days.

The prosecutors were racking their brains over the Star Guild members’ stubborn denials when an unexpected savior appeared.

“I-I’ll tell you everything.”

Kim Jonghun, Team 1 Leader of the Star Guild, was still mentally shaken when he confessed to everything that had happened.

His testimony was comprehensive. On top of that, an anonymous informant sent in a USB drive and a note.

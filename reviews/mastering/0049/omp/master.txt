# Chapter 49

Early morning.

I slowly opened my eyes.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> Exiting Sleep Mode.

The System notification drew a sigh of relief from me.

“Phew.”

Thank goodness. It hadn’t all been a dream.

It had only been one day, but yesterday had changed my life. If I’d woken up as Jin Taekyung, F-rank Hunter, reality would have felt like a nightmare.

I was sitting up when—

> **System**
>
> Afflicted with the Status Effect **Hangover**.

“Urgh.”

Last night’s aftermath was no joke. I clutched my spinning head and sat cross-legged on the mattress.

According to the System, circulating my qi could detoxify me, too.

> **System**
>
> Beginning Qi Circulation.

Following the formula of the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, I guided my internal energy. The moment I began circulating my qi, the headache started to fade. About ten minutes later, the message I’d been waiting for appeared.

> **System**
>
> **Hangover** has disappeared.

But I didn’t stop. I knew that ending the circulation abruptly midway would make it less effective.

*Something I learned in Murim.*

A full hour passed before I finally uncrossed my legs. My head was clear, and my body brimmed with energy.

The problem was…

*Why am I taking in so little qi?*

To increase my internal energy, I had to draw in qi from outside, store it within myself, and circulate it. But during my first attempt in reality, strangely little qi had entered my body.

*And it’s murky, too.*

It was like a tiny serving of bland food that took forever to arrive. I frowned.

*Maybe it’s the pollution?*

Since internal energy was based on pure natural qi, that was possible. Compared to Murim, the natural environment of modern society was in dire shape.

*Or maybe it’s the location.*

A single room in a decades-old goshiwon was hardly a nature-friendly place. It stank, and the facilities were run-down.

Despite being the goshiwon manager, Jinho was always questioning what this place had originally been.

*Maybe it used to be a torture chamber. During the Great Cataclysm, they probably dragged monsters in, tapped them on the balls with a knife, and asked where the Demon King was. They would’ve given up the location of the Demon King’s parents, too.*

…You had to give him credit for his imagination.

*He should be asleep right now.*

He considered waking up in the morning a disgrace, so there was no need to check. I was wondering whether to go grab breakfast when—

Bzzzt.

A text arrived on my phone.

〈 Designer-Brand Junkie

Designer-Brand Junkie

Do you have some time?

The sender was Designer-Brand Junkie—or rather, Team Leader Choi.

* * *

A glittering chandelier. Stylishly dressed people. Soft classical music playing in the background.

I couldn’t tell whether our meeting place was a café or a high-end restaurant.

“May I take your order?”

*Shit. Even the waiters here look like celebrities.*

He had a model’s proportions and the face of a handsome Greek god.

*Is this how everyone turns gay?*

While I was experiencing an identity crisis, Team Leader Choi calmly placed his order.

“One Black Ivory, please. What about you, Mr. Taekyung?”

The Greek god turned toward me.

Something about the atmosphere made me feel like I shouldn’t order a caramel macchiato.

“I’ll have the same.”

The coffee that arrived a little later was decent enough.

“It’s good. This is Black… what was it?”

“Black Ivory.”

Even after hearing the name again, I still had no idea what it meant. I just accepted it and moved on.

I gave my honest opinion.

“It looks expensive. Do they use good beans?”

“It’s elephant dung.”

“Oh.”

I quietly set down my cup. Team Leader Choi let out a short laugh, then spoke.

“Congratulations.”

The congratulations came out of nowhere, but I immediately understood what he meant.

He was talking about yesterday’s rank reassessment.

“You work fast. It’s personal information, so the Association wouldn’t have disclosed all the details.”

“C-rank reawakened Hunters are rare. Besides, I saw what happened yesterday with my own eyes. How could I not know?”

Fair enough.

As I nodded, Team Leader Choi held something out to me. A single envelope lay neatly on the table.

“What is this?”

“You’ll know when you look.”

*Don’t tell me—money?*

I checked the contents. Instead of a check, I found pages packed with tiny print.

“It’s a contract.”

“These are the best terms our Guild can offer. Give it a read.”

I was already reading it, from the first line to the last. Every clause brought another surprise.

“This doesn’t look like a C-rank contract.”

After seven years in this business, I’d seen plenty and heard even more.

Yet even I had never seen a contract this generous.

“They’re good terms even for a B-rank Hunter.”

“Then why offer them to me…?”

“Because I trust myself.”

“What?”

“I trust my instincts and my eye for people. That’s why I want to sign you, Mr. Jin Taekyung.”

Team Leader Choi set down his empty cup.

“Will you sign?”

Honestly, I was tempted. Very tempted.

Even apart from the terms, someone had recognized my worth and wanted me this badly. It made me want to nod on the spot.

That was why I hesitated longer today than I had yesterday. By the time I finally made my decision, the coffee had gone cold.

“I’m sorry.”

My reason was the same as yesterday.

Under the contract, I would have to work as a Hunter affiliated with the Guild for at least one year.

I appreciated the offer, but… I couldn’t afford to rush into this.

“Have you already signed with someone else? Or are you planning to?”

“No. I just need more time.”

“Time…”

Team Leader Choi sighed.

“I suppose it can’t be helped.”

I was about to apologize again when he said,

“Here’s my second offer.”

“Pardon?”

Team Leader Choi pulled another envelope from his jacket. Still dazed, I took it and checked the contents.

“A provisional contract?”

“You have a rough idea what that is, right?”

Of course I did. I knew it well.

If the Hunter Manpower Office was day labor, a provisional contract with a Guild was temp work. You joined the Guild for a short period and worked as a kind of mercenary.

“You won’t turn this one down too, will you?”

It wasn’t quite as generous as the formal contract, but the terms were still excellent. More importantly, the contract period had been left blank.

“Write down whatever period you want.”

“Oh. Right.”

I took the pen he offered, still somewhat dazed.

After thinking it over, I wrote down seven days. A week should be long enough to see whether the System remained.

Once I’d signed, Team Leader Choi held out his hand.

“I look forward to working with you.”

“That’s my line.”

As we shook hands firmly, it finally sank in that I’d taken my first step as a C-rank Hunter.

*Even if it’s only a provisional contract.*

My chest swelled with emotion.

“Should I come in tomorrow?”

“No.”

Team Leader Choi tapped his watch.

“You start now.”

* * *

Vroom.

Team Leader Choi drove a large military vehicle. He looked like the type to collect expensive supercars, so the choice surprised me. I didn’t understand it until we arrived at the Gate.

“Pick something.”

“Pick what?”

“Equipment.”

Team Leader Choi pressed a small button, revealing a trunk large enough for five grown men to lie down inside.

“I had it modified for work. It seemed wasteful to leave all this at home.”

I stared into the trunk with my mouth hanging open.

*Holy crap.*

Equipment worth at least several million won was neatly stacked and sorted inside. Armor and weapons were only the beginning. There were all kinds of potions and even disposable magic scrolls that most people considered too expensive to use. He had everything.

“…Is all of this yours, Team Leader?”

“For now. Some were gifts, and I bought some because they looked nice.”

Ah. So he bought equipment because it looked nice.

*How rich do you have to be to think like that?*

C-rank Hunters made good money, but Team Leader Choi’s spending was already far beyond that. He must have been rich enough that money had never been a concern.

Gulp.

“I think I’d rather rent something. If I borrow yours and it gets damaged, it’d be a little…”

“They’re out of fashion, so it doesn’t matter.”

Ah. So he even cared whether equipment was fashionable.

At that point, I gave up trying to understand and started choosing my gear.

The System made the process easy.

*Check Item.*

Ding.

> **System**
>
> Item Window
>
> Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set
>
> Type: Armor
>
> **Grade:** First Rate
>
> Restriction: None
>
> Description: Made by skinning a lizardman and using its hide whole.
>
> **Scale Armor** activates when the full set is equipped.

*This looks pretty good.*

It was light, and the leather was both tough and hard.

As soon as I put on the entire set, the **Scale Armor** effect activated.

“Oh.”

Green scales rose from the leather, forming a dense layer over my entire body. Team Leader Choi nodded when he saw me.

“You picked a good one. You’ve got a good eye.”

*The System’s the one with a good eye.*

I smiled awkwardly and rummaged through the weapons. Come to think of it, I’d only ever seen Team Leader Choi use a sword, but there were more than five types of weapons in the trunk alone.

*They’ve all been used, too.*

I could see traces of the effort he’d put into finding the weapon that suited him.

A little later, I found myself holding a spear.

> **System**
>
> Item Window
>
> Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon
>
> Type: Weapon
>
> **Grade:** First Rate
>
> Restriction: None
>
> Description: On a successful attack, **Bleeding** has a high chance of activating.

Anyone who saw me would think I had a lizardman fetish.

The spear completed my equipment selection. Team Leader Choi let out a short laugh.

“What’s so funny?”

“I was just thinking that things are going well.”

“Pardon?”

“You’ll find out soon enough.”

I had no idea what he meant, but I followed him to the Gate. Instead of an Administration employee, an unfamiliar man was waiting there.

“You’ve arrived.”

He bowed at a perfect ninety-degree angle. Even more surprising was how naturally Team Leader Choi accepted it.

“How’s the Gate?”

“I received your call yesterday and restricted access.”

“Thank you for your hard work.”

“Not at all, Young Master.”

*Young Master?*

That term could be used by a woman to address her husband’s unmarried younger brother, but there was no way this man was Team Leader Choi’s sister-in-law…

*So Team Leader Choi was the young master of some rich family.*

No wonder.

I should have realized it the moment I saw him decked out in luxury equipment from head to toe.

And being fashion-conscious enough to care about equipment design? You couldn’t pull that off unless you’d been born with one hell of a gold spoon.

*That guy has everything.*

The man who had everything turned to me.

“All right. Let’s go in.”

“Right now?”

“The equipment is taken care of. Is there a problem?”

His tone made it sound so obvious that I looked around.

There was me, Team Leader Choi, and the unfamiliar middle-aged man. That was everyone.

“What about the other team members?”

“They’re right here. The team members.”

“Oh, I see. He’s going in too, right?”

The middle-aged man cut in, his voice deep.

“I’m not.”

“Then…?”

“It’s just the two of us. There’s no one else.”

Team Leader Choi’s answer left me dumbfounded.

“No one else?”

“No.”

“Not even one person?”

“We’re not bringing so much as a dog.”

Listen to how firm he was. Who did he think he was, Pocheongcheon?[^2]

“So the two of us are clearing the Gate alone?”

“Why not? It’s only a D-rank Gate.”

“This is my first D-rank Gate.”

“I’ve been through plenty.”

No, fuck…

A D-rank Gate wasn’t some neighborhood discount store.

“If it’s just the two of us, I’m backing out.”

To raid a D-rank Gate safely, you needed a team of ten D-rank Hunters. I could use the System, and I’d learned martial arts, but that didn’t eliminate every danger.

“I know what you’re thinking, Mr. Jin Taekyung. But let me tell you one thing.”

Team Leader Choi continued in a relaxed voice.

“I’m a reawakened Hunter, too.”

“Reawakened? I thought you were C-rank, Team Leader…”

“C-rank was what I received when I was first measured. My reawakening happened afterward.”

Meaning his abilities were at least B-rank.

The odds were slim, but he could be even higher.

*A B-rank Hunter.*

If that was true, it changed everything. And with only two of us, my share of the payout would be that much larger.

“If you want to go back, I won’t stop you. I can go in alone. It wouldn’t be the first or second time.”

He regularly went into D-rank Gates alone?

“Then take care. Starting tomorrow, we’ll look for E-rank Gates.”

That sealed it.

I grabbed his shoulder as he turned away.

“Team Leader Choi.”

“Yes?”

“I want to… raid.”

Team Leader Choi smiled warmly.

“Let’s do our best.”

[^2]: Pocheongcheon is a famously incorruptible judge in Chinese legend and popular storytelling.

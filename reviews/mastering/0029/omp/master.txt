# Chapter 29

Some people never hesitate once they’ve made a decision. Jopil was one of them.

“Let’s see what you’ve got.”

With that curt remark, Jopil kicked off the ground. Ten-odd zhang of distance vanished in two steps.[^1] A flash burst from his waist.

*Whoosh!*

*What the…?*

My body moved before I could think. The moment my outstretched spear shaft met the flash—

*Boom!*

The thunderous impact hurled me backward. I shot toward the reconnaissance squad like a cannonball. Shields be damned—the three or four men in front went down in a heap.

“Argh!”

“Squad Leader! Are you all right?”

…Did I look all right?

*Holy shit.*

I tightened my grip on the spear shaft. It still hadn’t stopped shaking.

The flash had been Jopil’s sword. In all twenty-seven years of my life, it was the fastest, strongest attack I had ever faced.

*It hit that hard even though I blocked it?*

If my reaction had been even a moment slower, I would have been hiking up Mount Beimang by now.[^2] I calmed my pounding heart and got back to my feet.

Jopil was smiling.

“I’m disappointed. Is that all you’ve got?”

I had only managed to block because I’d gotten this strong. If I hadn’t leveled up after mowing down those twenty men, I might already be dead.

*Fuck. I picked the wrong opponent.*

My realm was only second rate. Even so, I had easily defeated two first-rate masters—with the martial arts and stats I’d gained through the System, and the combat experience I had built up.

But that single attack told me everything.

*This guy is different.*

I was outmatched in every way. Jopil was in a different league entirely from Lee Seogeun and Black Mountain Blade.

Different enough to bring the word *death* to mind.

“You’re afraid.”

Afraid? Me?

I looked at my hand. The spear had stopped vibrating, but the hand gripping it was shaking like an aspen leaf. My heartbeat was so loud it felt as though everyone could hear it.

“How do you expect to survive in the martial world with a coward’s heart?”

Jopil clicked his tongue.

“This won’t do. I’ll give you a little boost.”

“What?”

“Anger always beats fear.”

Before I could understand what he meant, Jopil flicked his sleeve. Several streaks of light shot out at once.

*Danger!*

The instant a red alert went off in my head, I swung my spear.

*Clang!*

Several throwing knives ricocheted away with a sharp ring.

But I couldn’t stop them all.

“Guh.”

One of the reconnaissance squad members clutched his throat. Blood spurted between his fingers around the throwing knife buried there.

“Grrk. Squad… Leader. Grrk.”

He sank to his knees, gurgling on blood. His still-boyish face was twisted with terror.

“P-please, save…”

*Whoosh. Thunk!*

No one could survive throwing knives to the throat and between the eyes. He was no exception.

*Splat.*

He fell face-first onto the ground, which had only just begun to thaw.

He never got up again.

It had all happened in an instant.

“I chose him because he looked the youngest… Was he a subordinate you cherished?”

I shook my head.

“No.”

“That’s a shame.”

“I don’t even know his name.”

“You called him your subordinate.”

“He was an NPC. I didn’t need to know his name or his age.”

“What?”

I rolled his body onto its back. After brushing the dirt and snow from his face, I closed his staring eyes.

Then, like I was making a confession, I told the still-bewildered Jopil the truth.

“We’ve only known each other for three days.”

“Is that so?”

“That’s right.”

“Then…”

Jopil smiled and continued.

“Why are you angry?”

He was right. Something was bubbling up from the pit of my stomach. My head and chest burned so hot that I had to say something.

*What was that kid’s name again?*

He was the one we called Number Seven in the reconnaissance squad. I had forced him to carry a shield when he didn’t want one and trained him whenever I had the chance. He was young, so a few words of praise would make him break into a goofy grin.

*What was his name?*

In the end, I couldn’t remember.

I didn’t even know all my goshiwon neighbors’ names.[^3] Why would I remember some NPC I had only known for a few days? There was no reason to. No need.

*But…*

I was annoyed. I was angry.

I had thought things would be different in a game, but even here, I had failed to protect my teammate.

Everything I had trained him for, all the effort I had put in over the past few days—gone like foam. I opened my mouth and let out a hot breath.

“You son of a bitch. You’re dead.”

I chewed and swallowed my last fasting pill.

As a System message announced that my energy had recovered—

*Boom.*

I kicked off the ground and launched myself at him.

* * *

Jopil knocked away the spearhead that surged up from his blind spot.

*Clang! Skrrratch.*

Fast and powerful. His fundamentals were solid, and his combat sense was impressive.

He was more than either that brat from the Mount Heng Sword Sect or some rootless wandering martial artist like Black Mountain Blade could handle.

*The Jin Family of Taiyuan… A junchi is still a junchi, even rotten. Is that it?*[^4]

Jopil leisurely slipped past the spearhead as he thought.

The decline of the Jin Family of Taiyuan was nothing new. The prestige that had once held all of Shanxi in its sway was long gone. All that remained was the faded glory of the past and the rare talent that appeared once in a blue moon.

A talent like Jin Taekyung.

*They’ve raised an interesting one.*

This much skill at twenty. He still couldn’t compare to the Heaven Shaking Sword, already hailed as the greatest master in Shanxi, but he was an intriguing young man.

Even setting aside his age, his internal energy and martial arts were both middling… but he knew how to fight. He knew exactly when to retreat and when to advance, and when an opportunity came, he charged like a demon of battle.

Just like now.

“Ha!”

*Whoosh—Boom!*

The spearhead flashed toward his face and punched through empty air. With a bursting crack, Jopil’s hair whipped around him.

*Well, look at this.*

The entire sequence, from raising the spear to thrusting it, was compact and flowed like water. And that movement—smoothly twisting his whole body before unleashing explosive force—was silk-reeling force.[^5] He was still clumsy with it, but there was no mistaking it.

*Silk-reeling force at twenty?*

Innate instinct, the combat experience of a seasoned veteran of Murim, and talent.

Frightening potential.

That was what Jopil was thinking when Jin Taekyung muttered through a stiff expression,

“What the fuck is this?”

“…”

Jopil’s feet tangled.

By the time he realized his mistake, Jin Taekyung’s spear was already snaking in. He leaped backward to open the distance, but the internal energy packed into that spear was no joke.

*Riiip!*

It was the first attack Jopil had allowed through. His outer robe split open in a long gash, baring his chest.

“Ah, so close. I could’ve ended it.”

“…”

*So close? Ended it? Against me—Jopil, One Question, One Kill?*

Watching Jin Taekyung smack his lips, Jopil felt a violent surge of rage.

“Youuu bastard!”

His shout, laden with internal energy, shook the forest. His eyes rolled back as he charged at Jin Taekyung.

* * *

The moment I cut a long gash through Jopil’s clothes, the System notification rang.

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> **Lv.??? Jopil** enters **Berserk**!
>
> Strength and Agility increase for the duration!

*Damn it. Even more?*

“Graaaaah!”

*Boom! Boom!*

Strike after strike crashed down with a roar. The ground was torn apart, and trees ripped free by the roots.

With his eyes rolled completely white, Jopil rampaged like a madman.

*The problem is, he’s no ordinary madman.*

He was a Peak master on top of being insane. If I hadn’t had the **Gambler** Title, which increased my combat stats by 10% in a one-on-one fight, I wouldn’t have lasted this long.

*Boom!*

That wasn’t martial arts.

It was a bombardment.

But Jopil had lost all precision. His movements were compact and fast, but the extra strength from **Berserk** made them too wide and left him full of openings.

*I just need him to show me one opening…*

The problem was, I couldn’t see one.

He had completely lost it and was turning everything around him into a wasteland. All I could do was dodge. I didn’t even dare try to block.

If I stepped into his range, I would be torn to shreds.

“Squad Leader!”

“We’re coming!”

I hurriedly waved off the reconnaissance squad members charging toward me.

“Hey, don’t come! Don’t come! Fall back!”

Had they lost their minds? What were they doing coming anywhere near this fight?

We had already lost one man for nothing. I didn’t want the entire reconnaissance squad wiped out.

And besides—

*If those guys come over here, the Gambler Title’s effect disappears!*

**Gambler** only worked in a one-on-one fight.

I was barely hanging on as it was. If I lost the Title’s effect too, I had no idea how much longer I could last.

“Get back, you bastards!”

I shouted and flung myself sideways. Sure enough, Jopil’s sword smashed the ground apart.

*Crack!*

“Squad Leader!”

Hyuk Mujin’s shout came a beat late. Despite my warning, he was already charging toward us. Behind him, I saw Han Yeop with a determined look on his face.

“Hey, don’t—”

“Jopil, you vile bastard!”

“Get away from the Squad Leader!”

But it was already too late. Hyuk Mujin and Han Yeop, who had sprinted over with all their strength, thrust their weapons at Jopil while he was preoccupied with me.

“Die!”

Sword and spear. Spear and sword.

Perfect timing, perfect attacks, as if they had practiced them beforehand.

There was just one problem.

They had chosen the wrong opponent.

“How dare you, you rats!”

Jopil responded instantly. He drove his sword into the ground, spun around, and struck both weapons with his bare hands.

Facing a sword and spear empty-handed was suicide.

But in Murim, things were different.

More precisely, Jopil was different. He was a Peak master.

*Crack—Crunch!*

His palm had barely struck the side of Hyuk Mujin’s blade, yet the sword shattered before it could touch him. Han Yeop stared in horror at his spear, its head sliced clean off.

That was the result of Jopil’s straightened knife-hand.

“What the…”

“That’s impossi—”

Before they could finish, Jopil slammed both hands into their chests.

They sprayed fountains of blood as they crashed into the trees.

“I’ll kill you.”

A chilling smile spread across Jopil’s face. His eyes were no longer rolled back.

**Berserk** had worn off. He was back in his right mind.

“Fuck…”

From bad to worse. Trouble on top of trouble. Surrounded on all sides.

Jopil, you son of a bitch.

The situation was hurtling toward the worst possible outcome. If anyone was going to stop him, it had to be me.

“Jopil—!”

I drew up my internal energy with all my strength, and it raced through my entire body. I kicked off the ground and shot toward him.

Jopil grinned.

“Right. I’ll kill you first.”

But I had something to count on.

*He’s bare-handed.*

*It was a blunder he’d made while Berserk. And I was sure I could finish this before he yanked the sword out of the ground behind him and swung it.*

The next instant, I hauled up every last bit of internal energy in my dantian. I focused all of it on a single point—the tip of my spear—and thrust.

“Die!”

The final form of the Jin Family’s Spear Technique, Sky-Piercing Strike.

If it could pierce the heavens, why couldn’t it pierce Jopil’s heart?

I was certain.

*This is it… the end.*

The world slowed, and I saw Jopil’s face.

He was smiling.

The moment I saw that smile, I knew.

Something was wrong.

*Skrrrng.*

Jopil’s sword was faster than my spear.

The blood-wet crimson blade shoved my spear shaft aside and drove inward. The instant my spearhead, laden with internal energy, stabbed into empty air—

*Slash.*

A chilling sound, and my chest suddenly felt cool. Then came the searing pain and a burst of blood.

Fortunately, I had jerked back at the last moment and avoided a fatal wound.

*Damn it.*

What the hell had just happened?

As I staggered back, Jopil charged.

*Whoosh-whoosh-whoosh!*

Crimson sword-light poured down. Every flash was so fast and powerful I could barely see it.

I gritted my teeth and swung my spear, but Jopil had the advantage in both momentum and martial arts.

*Slash. Thunk. Splurt.*

Lightning streaked through my entire body. The blade stabbed into my shoulder, slashed my knee, and punched through my side before coming free in a spray of blood.

“Guh.”

“You didn’t drop your weapon. I’ll give you that.”

Jopil added,

“If you can take this too.”

The next instant, his hand slammed into my chest.

[^1]: A zhang is a traditional Chinese unit of distance, roughly 3.3 meters.

[^2]: Mount Beimang is historically associated with burial grounds; hiking it here means being dead.

[^3]: A goshiwon is a very small, inexpensive room-for-rent housing arrangement.

[^4]: Junchi is a fish traditionally prized in Korea; the saying means that something of quality retains its worth even in decline.

[^5]: Silk-reeling force is a method of twisting the entire body in a continuous, coiling motion to release power.

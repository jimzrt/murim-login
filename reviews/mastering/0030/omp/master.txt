# Chapter 30

*Whoosh.*

The instant Jopil’s red-hot palm struck my chest, energy as hot as lava swept through me. My breath caught, and my internal energy surged backward.

*What the hell is this?*

The question flashed through my mind as I went flying. I bounced and rolled for more than ten zhang before finally coming to a stop.[^1]

“Cough.”

Blood trickled down my chin. The occasional unidentifiable chunks mixed in with it were… fuck. Pieces of my organs.

*He got me good.*

Through my blurred vision, I saw Jopil walking toward me. His leisurely stride had the ease of a victor.

*I have to get up.*

I groped beside me until my hand closed around the cool shaft of my spear. Using it as a cane, I barely managed to stand. Jopil narrowed his eyes.

“You can still stand after taking a Flame Divine Palm at seven-tenths power… You’re quite the troublesome junior.”

*Wham!*

Another palm strike slammed into me.

The Flame Divine Palm was every bit as powerful as its name. My shirt burned away, and my flesh melted and sloughed off. The heat burrowing into me severed my blood channels strand by strand.

“Guh.”

Horrific pain crashed over me. But instead of writhing in agony, I drove my spear at Jopil’s vital point.

*Thunk.*

“You’re like a fighting dog.”

Jopil caught the spear shaft and clicked his tongue.

“Someone at your level can’t do anything to me.”

“Go… fuck yourself!”

The next instant, I released the spear and threw myself into his guard.

This was my last chance. I focused every scrap of internal energy into a single point. If I could strike one of his vital points, I might turn the tables.

But…

“Gasp.”

The result was disastrous.

I had forced up my internal energy without regard for my internal injuries. I collapsed on the spot, trembling as if I’d been struck by lightning.

Drool ran from the corner of my mouth.

“Pfft. Puhahaha! What kind of idiot does that?”

Jopil roared with laughter and snapped off the spearhead.

“Well? What will you do now? Huh, youngest young master of the Jin Family of Taiyuan?”

Now I didn’t even have a weapon. Despair darkened my vision.

*Am I going to die like this?*

In the past seven years, death had never felt so close. I had fought more than a thousand battles and survived every one of them. Was I really going to die here?

Just like that?

*No. I can’t die like this…*

I reached out, but that was as far as I got.

—Squad Leader!

A shout crackling with static was the last thing I heard before every light went out.

* * *

When I opened my eyes again, I was in neither heaven nor hell. I was drenched in sweat and gasping for breath. My whole body felt as heavy as waterlogged cotton.

*A dream? Or my life flashing before my eyes?*

Whatever it was, one thing was certain. This was the past—a memory from seven years ago.

The familiar face of my training instructor was proof enough.

“You… You did…”

The instructor couldn’t finish his sentence. His eyes were fixed on a steel training dummy modeled after a monster.

It was little more than scrap metal now, bent and split from head to toe.

“Was that a Skill just now?”

My younger self answered in an exhausted voice.

“Thrust with All My Might.”

“Thrust with All My Might?”

“Yes. I just thrust as hard as I could.”

“You don’t know how you’re doing it, but ever since you Awakened, you’ve been able to use it naturally?”

“Uh, yes. That’s right.”

“That’s a Skill, you idiot.”

Looking dumbfounded, the instructor lowered his gaze to the file in his hand.

“Jin Taekyung. Twenty years old. Lives in Goyang, Gyeonggi Province. F-rank Awakened. This is you?”

“Yes. So, my full-power thrust—no, my Skill. It’s good, right?”

“You’re asking if it’s good?”

The instructor gave a disbelieving laugh.

“I’ve been at this training center for fifteen years, and I’ve never seen anything like it. Destructive power like that should require at least D-rank.”

“Fuck, I knew it. Instructor, I’m leaving.”

“What?”

“I’m going to get reevaluated. Honestly, if I can do that much, I should at least be E-rank.”

“No, I think they evaluated you correctly.”

“What?”

“The Skill itself is incredible. It’s far beyond anything an F-rank should be capable of. But…”

The instructor scratched the back of his head.

“You can’t use it.”

“Why? Why not?”

“Your body can’t keep up. That Skill is more than F-rank mana and physical ability can handle. That’s why you collapse after using it once.”

“But I’m fine.”

“Are you?”

Before I could even nod, the instructor’s hand shot out like lightning and tapped me on the chest.

It really was just a tap. But I had already exhausted all my strength, so I toppled flat on my back.

My entire body ached. I could barely move a finger.

“You’re already forcing your body past its limits. Eventually, it won’t end with a little soreness.”

“…Is there any other way?”

“Solid fundamentals. Excellent technique. And your own combat instincts. If you want to live a long life, don’t use that Skill. Ah, except under special circumstances.”

“Special circumstances?”

The instructor grinned.

“When your life is in danger. That Skill might save you once.”

The next instant, I opened my eyes on the cold snowfield.

* * *

“Squad Leader!”

The last shout I’d heard now rang out clearly. So did the sharp pain from my mangled body.

*I’m still alive.*

My life flashing before my eyes had lasted only an instant. Jopil had turned to look behind him, close enough for me to touch if I reached out.

“They’re certainly loyal. Too bad their skills are pathetic.”

At the end of his gaze, the reconnaissance squad members were charging toward us in a cluster.

“I’m thinking of tearing those bastards apart. What do you say?”

“…dly… fought.”

“What?”

“Stupid… fought.”

I mumbled again. Jopil frowned and bent closer.

“Can’t you even speak properly?”

“I said I’ve been fighting stupidly this whole time.”

*Ptoo!*

The moment I’d been waiting for.

I spat the blood I’d collected into his face. Half a beat faster, my hand was already plunging toward his foot.

*Inventory Open.*

Jopil didn’t know.

I was a Hunter, a man of Murim, and a player who could use the System!

*Equip Dagger.*

A solid weight filled my empty hand. His face splattered with blood, Jopil hurriedly tried to retreat, but the dagger had already punched through the top of his foot.

*Thnk!*

“Guh.”

Even a Peak master was still human.

His body froze at the sudden pain. I released the dagger and reached for his ankle.

*Equip Dagger.*

My Inventory was packed with weapons taken from the enemies I’d defeated since the Tutorial. Swords, spears, axes, daggers… more than twenty in all.

Jopil’s men had been walking armories.

*Slash.*

“Gaaah!”

His Achilles tendon.

The instant I severed the tendon at his ankle, a scream burst from his throat. As his stance crumpled, I rolled aside, slashing and stabbing until his other leg was drenched in blood as well.

*Slash. Stab. Stab. Stab-stab-stab!*

Jopil’s mouth opened wide with rage and pain.

“Gaaaaah! You son of a bitch!”

He had lost the use of both legs, but Jopil was still a Peak master. He twisted around like lightning, evaded my dagger, caught my wrist, and wrenched it.

*Crack.*

“Gah!”

His attack didn’t stop there.

A red-hot palm.

The Flame Divine Palm.

*Boom.*

My chest caved in. My vision went white. Powerful fire qi tore through my body.

*Boom.*

The strength drained out of me.

I heard the bones in my neck shift out of place beneath Jopil’s grip. His chilling voice slid into my ear.

“Now you understand, don’t you? You understand who you dared to provoke.”

“…Cough.”

“If King Yama asks, tell him I sent you.”

I hung limp in his grasp like a corpse. With rapture written across his face, Jopil declared,

“Die.”

The final Flame Divine Palm shot toward my chest. Lava capable of swallowing my life whole burned within his blazing palm.

And at last…

*Tap.*

The world stopped.

There was no lava. No heat at all.

Only his wounded, callused palm resting against my chest.

A ripple passed through Jopil’s eyes.

“You…”

The strength slowly drained from the hand around my throat. As Jopil staggered backward, I saw a dagger buried below his navel.

It had pierced his dantian.

“This… What the hell is this?”

The next instant, blood ran down his chin.

That was only the beginning.

Blood began pouring from his eyes, nose, and ears. A waterfall streamed from all seven of his orifices.

The reconnaissance squad stopped dead at the gruesome sight. Someone muttered with a groan,

“Qi deviation…”

Internal energy was a double-edged sword.

I had waited for the instant Jopil drew his internal energy to its peak, then driven a dagger summoned from my Inventory into his dantian.

The result was a reversal of his internal energy.

Qi deviation.

“You were definitely empty-handed.”

I answered wearily.

“You live long enough, you see all kinds of things.”

“I can’t die like this. This makes no sense.”

Jopil muttered like a man who had lost his mind and stumbled forward one step at a time.

Pools of blood formed wherever he passed.

“I am Jopil. Jopil, One Question, One Kill! The nineteenth-generation successor of the Fire Gate Clan! I am not someone who should die at the hands of a nobody like you!”

Covered in blood and screaming, Jopil radiated a chilling, ghostly aura. Even the reconnaissance squad members who had charged in ready to die trembled with fear.

“Then why? Why am I dying to the likes of you?!”

That was when the ember I’d thought extinguished flared back to life.

The change began in Jopil’s body. The bleeding stopped, and blue veins bulged across his entire body. He seemed incapable of feeling either the pain of his severed tendons or the effects of qi deviation.

Terrible heat poured from every breath he exhaled.

*What is this?*

A literal resurrection?

No. This was Jopil’s final, desperate struggle.

Everyone screamed in terror, but I could see it clearly. His hair was turning white by the second. His skin was shriveling.

Right now, he was…

*Burning away his life.*

*He means to kill me before he goes.*

He had thrown away his most precious possession in exchange for power, and every bit of that power was aimed at me.

Instinct told me I couldn’t dodge.

*Can I do this?*

I picked up the fallen spear.

It was the *Sharp Spear* I’d used ever since the Tutorial.

With its spearhead broken off, it was nothing more than a pointed iron rod.

*I’ll probably end up looking just like this.*

There was only one option left.

Even if I succeeded, there was no guarantee I would survive. But I didn’t have time to hesitate.

“Jin Taekyung!”

“Yeah. Let’s finish this.”

Me and Jopil.

Jopil and me.

We shot toward each other. The heat pouring from him melted the piled snow and dried my lips. I drew a long breath.

*Sss. Hoo.*

The noise around me faded into the distance. My heartbeat and breathing thundered in my ears.

*Thump. Thump-thump. Thump-thump-thump.*

My heartbeat reached its peak.

My breathing fell into place with mechanical precision.

I knew.

*Now.*

At that instant, I awakened the internal energy in my dantian. Inside my ruined body, where my qi and blood channels lay twisted and torn, only the third internal energy remained, hardened like rock.

My sole remaining option.

The final piece needed to complete my Skill.

*Run wild all you want.*

The awakened internal energy went berserk. It forced its way through my ruined qi and blood channels and surged throughout my body. Mind-numbing pain engulfed me.

But the new strength coursing through me soon drowned it out.

*Go!*

Every muscle in my body pulled taut. From my calves and thighs, through my waist and along my thrusting arm, every ounce of strength and internal energy in me surged forward.

Condensed air exploded from the broken spear tip.

At the same time, Jopil thrust out his Flame Divine Palm.

“Dieeeee!”

*Roar!*

Snow erupted into the air with the blast of wind. Through the flakes drifting gently back down, Jopil came into view.

His right arm—the one that had unleashed the Flame Divine Palm—was gone.

So were his shoulder and his side.

Half of his upper body had vanished.

“What kind of martial art…?”

I let the spear fall and answered.

“Thrust with All My Might.”

The next instant, heaven and earth flipped upside down.

Beyond my fading vision, Jopil’s already lifeless head flew high into the sky.

Socheon stood there with a sword as tall as he was, sobbing aloud.

[^1]: A zhang is a traditional Chinese unit of distance, roughly 3.3 meters.

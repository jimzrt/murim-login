# Chapter 95

*Heh heh heh. Success.*

Hong Woojin smiled smugly. He was a veteran at monitoring his targets’ every move and ferreting out every last detail about them.

The information that Jin Taekyung’s younger sister was crazy about animals—cats in particular—had proved especially useful.

“Kitty, answer me. You’re happy to be inside, aren’t you?”

The infiltration had been smooth and successful. From inside the kitten’s body, Hong Woojin let out a triumphant roar.

“Miaowww.”

“Ahh, so cute! Oppa, did you hear that? You heard it, right?”

“Yeah. I heard it.”

“How can you be so indifferent to such a cute little creature? Are you even human?”

“Then what am I, an animal?”

The problem was Jin Taekyung.

Even the most emotionally barren person tended to soften in front of a cute animal, especially a tiny kitten, but…

“Move over. You’re blocking the TV.”

This guy had none of that. Faced with a heart drier than the Sahara Desert, his younger sister, Jin Hayeon, grumbled.

“Good grief, there’s a limit to how cold a person can be. Right, Yeoreum?”

“Yeoreum?”

“Yeah. She was born in summer, so Midsummer. Pretty, right?”

“Midsummer, my ass. Judging by her size, she looks about three months old. That means she was born in late spring, so call her Late Spring or something.”

“…Is that supposed to make sense? Anyway, her name is Yeoreum from today onward. Right, Yeoreum?”

“Mrowww.”

As far as Hong Woojin was concerned, Jin Hayeon deserved most of the credit. Thanks to her, everything was going smoothly.

“Ahh! She answered! Yeoreum, you just answered your big sister, didn’t you? Didn’t you?”

“Miaow.”

“Ugh, my heart!”

*Heh. What an easy mark.* A few meows were all it took to make her lose her mind.

*This is why high-school girls are… No, wait. It’s because I chose the perfect Familiar.*

Hong Woojin was grinning contentedly when Jin Taekyung, who had been staring blankly at the TV, casually tossed out a question.

“Isn’t that one male?”

“Huh? Well…”

“We don’t know yet, do we?”

“Come to think of it, I haven’t checked.”

“Let’s take a look and find out.”

Huh?

Things were taking a strange turn. His body might currently belong to a cat, but Hong Woojin was still a vigorous young man. As Jin Taekyung’s large hand approached, a wave of shame suddenly washed over him.

*That filthy bastard is going to look at my junk?*

Strictly speaking, it wasn’t Hong Woojin’s body. They were different species, so naturally their anatomy was different as well.

However, Familiar magic made the caster and Familiar share everything. He couldn’t help feeling disgusted.

*Absolutely not!*

Hong Woojin hurriedly burrowed into Jin Hayeon’s arms.

“Mrowww.”

“Oh my, I don’t think she likes that.”

“That’s how the world works. Who gets to go through life doing only what they want?”

“But Yeoreum’s a cat.”

“Same goes for cats. If she wants a warm meal and the occasional can of food as a treat, she has to put up with this much.”

What a lunatic. He was spouting utter nonsense just because he wanted to check a cat’s sex. Grinding his teeth, Hong Woojin clung to his only hope, Jin Hayeon.

He rubbed his entire body against her arm and gave her a pitiful look. Her eyes immediately softened.

“What am I going to do? Yeoreum’s so cute I could die.”

“Yeah, she’s cute. So let’s take a look.”

“Do it next time. You’re scaring her.”

“That’s just your imagination.”

“I’m telling you, Yeoreum doesn’t like you, Oppa.”

The trick was to let out a whimper at exactly the right moment.

“Mnyaa. Mngh.”

“See? I’m right, aren’t I?”

“…Then I guess it can’t be helped.”

“Don’t you dare force yourself on her. Kittens are sensitive, so you have to be careful with them.”

Good. He had survived the immediate crisis. Jin Taekyung might seem like a lunatic who lived in his own little world, but he had a soft spot for his family.

Having thoroughly studied all the background information Sangdong Guild had given him, Hong Woojin believed he was one step ahead.

*This is why information matters. You’ve already fallen right into my trap.*

But there was one thing Hong Woojin had never imagined.

“Hey, Sis.”

“Yeah?”

“Do you need more spending money?”

“…Are you trying to bribe me so you can have your way with our Yeoreum?”

“Yeah. A hundred thousand won.”

“Deal. But be gentle so she doesn’t hate you too much, okay?”

“My wallet’s on the desk in my room. Go get it.”

“Eek!”

“Miaow?”

Jin Hayeon vanished like a bullet. Hong Woojin cried out in disbelief.

*You said she was so cute you could die! You called her our Yeoreum!*

*What a shameless little brat.*

One minute she had acted ready to give Yeoreum her liver and gallbladder, and now she was abandoning “our Yeoreum” for a mere hundred thousand won?

But he was given no time to lament the realities of capitalist society.

“Come on. Let’s play.”

Grab.

Jin Taekyung seized all four of his limbs with lightning speed, a revolting smile on his face.

Hong Woojin screamed desperately.

*Let go! Let go, you son of a bitch!*

“Hiss! Hissss!”

The kitten’s fur stood on end as he hissed, catching the attention of his only hope as she rummaged through Jin Taekyung’s wallet.

“Oppa!”

“Yeah, take another hundred thousand won.”

“Thanks!”

*Hey! Hey!*

His only hope had fallen and become a slave to capitalism!

Before the shock had even worn off, Jin Taekyung’s hot breath washed over him.

“Our little Yeoreum, shall we see what you’ve got down there?”

At that desperate, life-or-death moment—

*Sever Link!*

“Miaowww!”

With a mournful cry, all the strength drained from the kitten’s body.

Then Hong Woojin opened his eyes somewhere dark and gasped for breath.

“Haaah!”

Since starting work as a Hunter, he had handled more than a hundred assignments, large and small, but this was the first time he had ever felt his life was in danger.

He looked down at the goose bumps covering his forearms and began to gag.

“Urgh.”

His stomach churned, and his head throbbed.

It was a side effect of the sudden Link severance. Only after gulping down the potion he had prepared in advance as if it were cold water could Hong Woojin finally catch his breath.

“Jin Taekyung, that fucking bastard…”

For the first time, he regretted accepting the assignment.

Hayeon spotted us as she came out of my room.

“You didn’t do anything mean to our Yeoreum, did you?”

“That’s not what I meant… Never mind. Forget it.”

The whole story was too long and complicated to explain piece by piece. Besides, I had no intention of telling her.

Imagine telling her that some shady bastard had crawled inside a cat and was using it to watch us. It was obvious how anxious she would become. My family couldn’t find out what was happening.

*Besides, I was the one who let it happen.*

I hadn’t allowed a Familiar into the house because Hayeon had begged me or because Mom had given her permission.

*I let it in because I wanted to.*

I didn’t know who had hired them, but even if I drove them off now, they would keep trying.

It didn’t matter whether the Familiar took the form of an insect like last time or a cat like today.

What mattered was that I already knew what the Familiar was and could drive it away whenever I wanted.

*They’re definitely nearby.*

Within five hundred meters of the house. Somewhere inside that radius was the mage controlling the Familiar. If I shook him down, I was bound to find a connection.

*First I’ll punch him in the mouth. Then I’ll ask questions.*

He was illegally spying on civilians, so there was no way he could report me even if I beat him up. Just thinking about giving him a proper thrashing made my fists itch.

*How dare they snoop around my house?*

They had interrupted my first vacation in ages, and thanks to these bastards, I had already spent well over three hundred million won. No matter how I looked at it, I was getting screwed.

I was watching TV with my face twisted into a scowl when Hayeon cautiously studied my expression and spoke.

“Oppa, are you mad?”

“No. What would I be mad about?”

“I’m sorry.”

“…What’s gotten into you? You’re starting to scare me.”

I wasn’t joking. I was more startled than when I had first discovered the Familiar. Since when could she say things like that?

I asked seriously.

“Are you sick?”

“No, it’s just…”

“Then are you hungry?”

“That’s not it…”

Just as Hayeon hesitated, about to say something, a stomach growled from somewhere.

It wasn’t mine, and Mom had stepped out to run an errand.

“You’re hungry.”

“Mm, a little?”

“Right. You’re so hungry you’re starting to talk nonsense. You know where my wallet is, right? Order anything you want.”

“Really?”

“Yeah. Up to a hundred thousand won.”

“Wow, look at my big brother throwing money around now that he’s earning some.”

I never thought I’d live to hear her call me her big brother like that. It was the first time since she was in middle school, and it gave me goose bumps.

“You’re the Familiar, you little bastard!”

“What are you talking about? Anyway, I can order whatever I want as long as it’s under a hundred thousand won?”

“Yeah. No, wait. Fine. Order everything you want.”

“What about the money left over?”

“…Have you been depositing money with me or something?”

“The more, the better.”

Her shamelessness left me dumbfounded, but at the same time, I was happy.

She had never once asked me for spending money. Judging by the clothes she wore and how she usually looked, she was far more modest than the kids her age I occasionally saw on the street.

*What an old soul.*

Come to think of it, Hayeon had always been like that. She rarely cried as a child and seldom expressed her feelings honestly. She had only developed her current personality after starting high school.

*It wouldn’t hurt her to act spoiled once in a while… She grew up too soon.*

*Maybe even sooner than I did.*

Was that why? Nothing she did or said bothered me in the slightest. If anything, I was proud of her. Happy, even.

“Fine. Take it all. Take everything.”

“Really?”

“Yeah.”

Hayeon beamed at me.

“That’s a relief. I almost felt bad for nothing.”

“What’s there to feel bad about?”

“I took three hundred thousand won from your wallet earlier.”

“…What?”

“But now that you’ve said I can keep whatever’s left after spending a hundred thousand won on food, I feel much better.”

“Hold on. Didn’t I tell you to take two hundred thousand won?”

“It was an impulsive accident.”

“…Don’t you mean an impulsive crime?”

I take back everything I just said.

The sight of her humming as she walked back into her room couldn’t have been more irritating.

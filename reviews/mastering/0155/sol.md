# Chapter 155

The room was silent. Six or seven scholars worked frantically within it.

Haggard-faced, they dealt with the mountain of bamboo slips one by one while reporting various matters to the master of the office.

“There’s been a dispute between the Yellow River Gang and the Sogong Sect. They’ve asked our family to mediate…”

“Tell them we’ll set aside time on New Year’s Day to discuss it. Inform the Inner Hall Master in advance.”

“What should we do about the Five Gates of Shanxi?”

“Ah, is this about my third brother?”

“Yes. The hall masters of the Five Gates of Shanxi are waiting to offer their apologies.”

“Send them back. If they were truly sorry, they should have come themselves instead of sending their hands and feet. Inform the Inner Hall Master about that as well.”

“Next is a matter from the Southern Merchant Guild…”

Even as he received their reports, Jin Wikyung, the master of the office, never looked up from the bamboo slips.

But the next report made even him raise his head.

“Lesser Family Head, mounted-bandit groups from the northern plateau are making suspicious movements near Datong.”

“Mounted bandits? Is the information from the Lower District Sect?”

“Yes. If left unchecked, they’ll launch a large-scale raid against the common people.”

“How many?”

“Five groups have formed an alliance, and nearly five hundred men are gathering.”

“Mounted bandits. A constant nuisance.”

Jin Wikyung rubbed between his brows, his face weary.

The Jin Family of Taiyuan was undeniably powerful, but it still lacked the strength to cover all of Shanxi Province.

That was why the mounted bandits continued watching for an opportunity despite knowing that the Red Wind Band had been annihilated.

“Should we deploy our martial artists?”

Jin Wikyung immediately shook his head.

“No.”

“We’ve had more martial artists join our family recently than we can count. We have men to spare.”

“We’ve already shed too much blood. Besides, the people we accepted this time are still inexperienced. We could send hundreds and lose hundreds.”

The Mount Heng Sword Sect.

One leg of the tripod supporting Shanxi Province had broken, and the water within was beginning to spill.

They needed a plan before the boiling water scalded them.

After a moment’s thought, Jin Wikyung spoke.

“For now, focus on establishing and stabilizing branches in every prefecture and county. That takes priority.”

“Lesser Family Head!”

The scholar cried out in alarm. The others, who had been concentrating on their own work, also looked up.

Hundreds of mounted bandits were about to descend upon the region, yet establishing branches took priority? Did he mean to ignore the common people and let them die?

Disappointment began to spread across the scholars’ faces, but Jin Wikyung continued.

“Instead, ask the Five Gates of Shanxi for support. Two hundred men should be about right… What do you think?”

“That won’t be nearly enough.”

Few people could speak so bluntly to the Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, and none of them were among the scholars in this office.

Jin Wikyung grinned at Wipeng as he came through the door.

“Is that so? I thought it would be plenty.”

“What are the Five Gates of Shanxi like these days? They’re too busy fighting among themselves. They’ll keep their carefully trained elites tucked safely behind their walls and fill the ranks with clueless Second Rate and Third Rate martial artists.”

“That sounds plausible.”

“More than plausible. It’s what they’ll do nine times out of ten. The mounted bandits will grin from ear to ear when they see all those peach-fuzzed youngsters.”

“Haha. So you’re saying we should step in?”

“What choice do we have? Give me some capable men, and I’ll go myself. Then the Five Gates of Shanxi won’t dare try anything underhanded.”

“True. Isn’t the Ghost Sword more frightening than any ghost?”

Wipeng shook his head at Jin Wikyung’s teasing tone.

“That’s enough. Tell me.”

“Tell you what?”

“You already have a plan, don’t you?”

“What plan? Your idea sounded good to me.”

“Good grief. Since when have you listened so closely to anything I say?”

“Every word from your mouth is a golden rule to me.”

Wipeng heaved a sigh and turned to the scholar standing blankly nearby.

“What do you think?”

“P-pardon?”

Thin and pallid, the man was the very picture of a sheltered scholar who had spent his life buried in books.

Caught off guard by Wipeng’s question, he stammered out an answer.

“I-I don’t think it will be enough.”

“Is that all?”

“We should send more men…”

Jin Wikyung watched him for a moment, then cut in with a laugh.

“That’s enough. And you.”

Already cowed by Wipeng’s sharp aura, the scholar flinched.

“Yes.”

“Send word to the Five Gates of Shanxi and the Shanxi Provincial Office. Tell them mounted bandits are swarming near Datong and that we request their assistance.”

“The Shanxi Provincial Office?”

“The people are in danger. The government ought to roll up its sleeves and help. Oh, and make sure the Five Gates of Shanxi hear about that as well.”

“Considering how passive the government has been over the past several years, there’s little chance they’ll agree.”

“Then make them agree.”

The smile vanished from Jin Wikyung’s face.

“Isn’t that your job?”

“Ah.”

The scholar came sharply to his senses. Exhausted though he was after several sleepless nights, he had approached the matter far too simply.

Ask the Shanxi Provincial Office for aid? Compared to that, the proposal from Wipeng, a martial artist to his very bones, sounded far more realistic.

“My apologies.”

“You’re still inexperienced, so I understand. But our family doesn’t need scholars. It needs wise men who can devise the best course of action for our family. Remember that.”

There was nothing he could say. Seeing that not only the scholar before him but all the others had flushed with shame, Jin Wikyung spoke again.

“You must all be tired. Go get some rest for today.”

No one would refuse an order to rest, least of all men who had spent the last three days surviving on brief naps with bamboo slips for pillows.

Once the scholars had dragged themselves out, Wipeng pulled over an empty chair.

“Are those the new recruits?”

“I couldn’t manage everything alone. They’re still better than nothing.”

“I’m not so sure. They all seem rather unimpressive.”

“How many of them do you think took up their studies hoping to join our family? It’s only natural.”

Jin Wikyung stretched. A loud series of cracks echoed through the room.

“It’s only been a few days. We’ll have to separate the jade from the stones and let the rest go.”

“Is that also why you didn’t tell them about the secret agreement with the Shanxi Provincial Office?”

“Why do you think it’s called a secret agreement? The fewer people who know, the better.”

In truth, securing the Shanxi Provincial Office’s assistance would not be particularly difficult. Hadn’t Jin Wikyung and Deputy Military Commissioner Hong Jin already exchanged a great deal during their conversation five days ago?

“Our family hasn’t fully established itself yet. It would be troublesome if rumors began circulating that we’d made a secret agreement with the government.”

“True. Especially when the rumors happen to be facts.”

Jin Wikyung’s conversation with Hong Jin had touched upon several matters that Shanxi Province’s small and medium-sized sects—particularly the Five Gates of Shanxi—would not appreciate.

If the Seongun Escort Bureau ever learned what the two men had discussed, its people might well collapse frothing at the mouth.

“So, how are the other preparations coming along?”

“I’ve completed the personnel changes as you ordered. The heads of the Inner and Outer Halls remain in place, and we’ve created three new squads…”

Wipeng’s report concerned the first matter Jin Wikyung had addressed after the war ended.

The Jin Family of Taiyuan was like the rising sun. To the hot-blooded youths of Shanxi Province, it was the finest choice available.

If the family meant to embrace the endless stream of newcomers, it would need to widen its arms.

“…That’s how I handled it, but with our numbers expected to keep growing, we’ll likely need to reorganize again later.”

“Let’s hope so.”

Jin Wikyung tried to appear calm as he listened.

The Jin Family of Taiyuan had declined slowly but steadily since the Great Faction War. Now, however, it was rapidly regaining its former glory.

*No. Perhaps we’ll become even stronger than we were before the Great Faction War.*

If that happened…

Only then would they truly deserve to be called a great family.

They would cease to be a martial family on the frontier and stand shoulder to shoulder with the towering powers of the realm.

*A great family. A great family…*

The mere thought set his heart racing.

Just as every martial artist dreamed of becoming the Martial God, Jin Wikyung had long dreamed of elevating his family into the ranks of the great families.

*New Year’s Day is almost here.*

On that day, before every sect in Shanxi Murim, the Jin Family of Taiyuan would be recognized as the undisputed hegemon of Shanxi Province.

Jin Wikyung had no doubt that the first day of the coming year would mark the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s first step toward becoming a great family.

Just as he secretly clenched his fist—

“Um… May I come in?”

“Hm? Of course.”

The voice belonged to the scholar who had just left.

“What is it? Did you forget something?”

“It’s not that…”

Under Jin Wikyung and Wipeng’s puzzled gazes, the scholar continued cautiously.

“There’s some news I failed to report.”

“You’re too conscientious for your own good. You’ve worked for days without proper sleep. Don’t worry about it. Go get some rest.”

“No. I should have told you earlier, but it slipped my mind…”

“Now, now. It’s fine. I told you to rest.”

Wipeng chimed in.

“My lord is right. You’re not a jiangshi. You need enough rest if you’re going to work hard again tomorrow—”

“Huashan has sent the Three Plum Blossom Elites.”

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng shot to their feet simultaneously.

“What?”

“What did you say?”

Who were the Three Plum Blossom Elites?

They were the most exceptional talents among the Plum Blossom Swordsmen, Huashan’s finest.

In particular, Huashan’s Lone Crane, Baek Museong, was a major figure expected to become the next Sect Leader and carry Huashan’s future upon his shoulders.

After hearing such news, how could the two men’s eyes not nearly pop from their sockets?

“Is that true?”

“Yes. I meant to mention it last, but I forgot. And there’s one more thing.”

“One more?”

“Again? Hurry up and tell us!”

The visit from the Three Plum Blossom Elites was astonishing enough, yet there was still more.

Frowning, the scholar continued.

“They said their Grandmaster had disappeared. They think he may have come to our family, so they asked us to send word if we see him… Who is this Grandmaster?”

Still unfamiliar with the affairs of Murim, the scholar had no idea what the fuss was about. Jin Wikyung and Wipeng, however, could only gape.

“If Huashan’s Grandmaster is…”

“Th-th-that…”

Sword Saint Mae Jonghak.

Unable to bring themselves to say the name aloud, the two men merely mouthed the words and swallowed.

Huashan clearly wished to keep the Sword Saint’s whereabouts within the sect for now. At a time like this, the less said, the better.

“Th-that… What did you say?”

“Th-that thing. You know how it is.”

“Pardon?”

“You may leave now. Erase everything that happened here from your mind. Understood?”

The bewildered scholar bowed and left. Only then did the words they had been holding back burst free.

“The Sword Saint is coming!”

“Shh! Keep your voice down. We don’t know that for certain yet.”

Despite his words, Wipeng’s face was flushed bright red. To a swordsman like him, Sword Saint Mae Jonghak stood above even the Jade Emperor.

To think he might meet such a man in person! Perhaps he might even receive a pointer from him.

“B-but why would the Sword Saint come to our family?”

“What else could bring him here?”

“Ah.”

In his excitement, Wipeng had forgotten who was currently staying with the Jin Family of Taiyuan.

“The Sword Saint broke his seclusion to find his beloved disciple.”

“It’s only speculation, but that seems highly likely. I hear he raised the boy like his own grandson. One can only imagine how deeply he cares for him.”

Jin Wikyung grinned from ear to ear.

Whatever the reason, the Sword Saint’s visit was cause for celebration, both for Jin Wikyung as a martial artist and as the Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan.

“His name was Cheongpung, wasn’t it? Where is he now? I seem to recall hearing some time ago that he was training my youngest brother in the Wall Lizard Technique.”

“The Wall Lizard Technique training ended two days ago. Now he’s…”

“He’s what?”

“He’s beating the crap out of the Third Young Master.”

“Whaaat?”

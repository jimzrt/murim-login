# Chapter 45

Slash.

The red eyes blinked meekly. So even a C-rank Rare Monster—the terrifying Hobgoblin Great Warrior—could make a face like that.

*How had I never noticed?*

With that passing thought, I flicked the blood from my spearhead. At the same time—

Thud.

Something heavy hit the ground. The Great Warrior saw what it was and instinctively shuffled backward.

Well, fair enough. Its right arm had been severed clean below the elbow.

*I thought you’d be a little different.*

The Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique. That was how I’d slipped inside its reach. The Great Warrior reflexively swung its right arm, but there was nothing left there.

“Forgot already?”

I pressed the spearhead to the Great Warrior’s side and swept it upward. Charged with internal energy, the blade split its tough hide and muscle.

Slash—

Its left arm came off in a spray of green blood.

Both arms gone in an instant, the Hobgoblin Great Warrior roared in rage and agony.

—Kuwaaaaaargh!

But I still couldn’t let my guard down. Even without its arms, it was more than dangerous enough with just its body.

“Yeah. Come on.”

Before I’d even finished speaking, its massive frame charged.

Clang! Clang! Clang!

As expected, this thing’s entire body was a weapon. Like the monster it was, it possessed primal senses and strength far beyond any human’s.

Swish. Slash.

Its hooked claws grazed my forearm. A chunk of flesh tore away, and blood poured out. Without flinching, I blocked the heel coming down toward the crown of my head.

Thud. Crunch.

The ground beneath my feet began to sink.

Incredible strength. At this rate, I might get buried standing up.

*If I didn’t have internal energy, that is.*

I drew up every last bit of internal energy in my dantian. Power flooded through my entire body. As my spear shaft slowly rose, the Great Warrior’s eyes wavered.

*Too late.*

If it had been whole from the start, maybe things would have been different. But after losing both arms, the Hobgoblin Great Warrior was no longer a match for me.

—Grrr.

In the end, it was the first to retreat. By then, the fight was as good as over.

I pressed forward and thrust my spear.

*Jin Family’s Spear Technique. First form.*

The movements engraved into my body and mind unfolded in a rush.

Thrust. Cut. Block and strike with the shaft. Each movement was simple by itself, but changing the order and position created countless combinations.

That was my definition of martial arts.

Clang! Clang!

Swish. Swish-swish-swish!

Second form. Third form. Fourth…

The hooked claws, hard as steel, were sliced away. With every step it retreated, another wound opened and more blood spilled.

Before long, the Hobgoblin Great Warrior’s back struck the wall.

“Grrrk…”

A C-rank Rare Monster. This beast had once seemed impossible to defeat, but the will to fight had long since faded from its red eyes.

“Time to go.”

Shunk.

Ghk. With a final death rattle, the hulking green body slid down the wall.

And then—

Ding.

> **System**
>
> You have defeated Lv. 45 Hobgoblin Great Warrior!
>
> Level Up!

The System notification rang out. I’d thought I would never hear or see one again…

*The System, out of nowhere?*

But I wasn’t the only one confused.

“…Mr. Jin Taekyung?”

Team Leader Choi had already dealt with the Priest. His pupils shook as if an earthquake had struck them while his gaze darted between me and the Great Warrior’s corpse.

“What… are you?”

Good question. I’d like to know that myself.

Heh heh. Heh heh heh.

* * *

“A Rare Monster?”

The cigarette dropped from the government official’s mouth.

He looked like a born civil servant, so this was anything but good news for him. Sure enough, he eyed us with naked unease.

“W-what rank?”

Team Leader Choi wearily shook the Priest’s staff.

Clank.

“C-rank Rare. Hobgoblin Priest.”

“C-rank?! For fuck’s—”

Hey, save the swearing. There’s one more.

I kicked the Hobgoblin Great Warrior’s greatsword, which I’d been using as a walking stick. The official turned at the dull clang.

“And that?”

“Buy one, get one free. C-rank Rare. Hobgoblin Great Warrior.”

“Two mid-grade Rare Monsters? In an E-rank Gate?”

I understood his reaction. None of this made any damn sense.

The official stared back and forth between us and the equipment for a long moment before finally nodding with a miserable expression.

“I’ll contact my superiors first.”

“Hey, mister. Hold on.”

Im Kkeokjeong had regained consciousness at some point. I was holding him upright, and he had suffered serious injuries, including at least five broken bones.

“Call us a healer. A pretty one.”

The E-rank trio collapsed onto the ground as well.

“And potions! Potions! Bring out the good stuff!”

“Ah, for fuck’s sake! How did you people manage this Gate?”

“All our gear’s smashed too! Huh? What are you going to do about that?”

What did they think? The government would compensate them for everything.

There was an actual law covering situations like this. Every item consumed inside the Gate, along with medical bills and additional compensation, would be paid for.

In other words, this was all an act to squeeze out a little more.

*Tsk, tsk. Even so, people are watching.*

Just then, I felt a piercing gaze from beside me.

“…Mr. Taekyung.”

It was Team Leader Choi.

“What are you doing?”

He stared at me in confusion. More precisely, at the hand holding my dagger.

Scrape.

Charged with internal energy, the dagger carved long gashes through my seven-year-old leather armor, ruining it beyond repair.

“…”

“…”

“Team Leader Choi.”

I sighed heavily enough to sink the ground.

“That Hobgoblin Great Warrior was incredibly strong. I escaped with my life, but every piece of equipment I owned was destroyed.”

“…”

“The armor that protected me for seven years—and now even my spear.”

“The spear looks perfectly—”

At that moment, I planted one foot on the middle of the spear and slowly pressed down.

Before the majesty of my three-digit Strength stat, the iron spear bent like taffy.

“Perfectly fine? This does?”

“…”

Team Leader Choi closed his mouth.

* * *

Team Leader Choi was called away by the officials dispatched to take charge, while the other four were loaded into an ambulance for treatment.

Instead of the musty break room, I was left alone in a spacious, comfortable office. Now that the moment had finally arrived, my heart began to pound.

*St-Status Window?*

The System answered my doubtful call.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> **Status Window**
>
> **Lv. 33 Jin Taekyung**
>
> **Job:** First Rate Martial Artist
>
> **Fame:** 0
>
> **Titles:** 2 (Title effects active)
>
> - Novice Trainee (Training speed +10%)
> - Gambler (Combat-related stats +10% in one-on-one matches)
>
> **Strength:** 120  **Stamina:** 125
>
> **Agility:** 121  **Intelligence:** 20
>
> **Charm:** 20  **Internal Energy:** 15 years
>
> **Remaining Points:** 30
>
> Synchronization is complete. Changes have occurred to Titles and Fame.

“Fuck. It really showed up.”

I stared blankly at the Status Window for a moment before noticing something strange.

*The Status Window… changed?*

The first things that stood out were my Fame resetting to zero and two missing Titles. The final line gave me a pretty good idea why.

*Because this isn’t Murim?*

All five hundred points of Fame I’d accumulated had been earned as a martial artist in Murim. The missing Title *Scion of a Prestigious Family* made sense for the same reason.

*Well, I’m not the son of some prestigious family here. And I’m definitely no Sleeping Dragon.*

If I earned a Title here, it would probably be something like *Scion of the Common Folk*. Or *Earthworm* instead of Sleeping Dragon.

“What else changed?”

For the next ten minutes or so, I opened, closed, and tested every System function one by one. The Status Window was the only thing that had changed.

No—there was one more.

The Quest Window.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> **Quest**
>
> **Traitor**
>
> You have discovered the traitor’s identity. Rejoin the main force, inform them that there is a traitor, and lead the battle to victory!
>
> **Grade:** Peak
>
> **Restriction:** Jin Taekyung
>
> **Mission:** Punish the traitor (Incomplete)
>
> Battle victory (Incomplete)
>
> **Reward:** Vast EXP and Fame
>
> Valuable iron chest
>
> **Failure:** Death

It was the Chain Quest I’d received after killing Gwak Jun and the assassins. My thoughts grew tangled.

*Does it want me to go back? To Murim?*

I knew now. The Murim I’d experienced wasn’t an illusion or a game. It sounded utterly impossible, but Murim was probably…

Creak.

I turned at the sudden sound. Team Leader Choi stood in the doorway.

“Let’s go, Mr. Taekyung.”

It looked like the discussion had wrapped up. I put the thought aside and rose.

“The others…?”

“They should be on their way to the hospital by now. They’ll need to stay for about a week. You don’t need to worry.”

I wasn’t particularly worried. They had the sturdy bodies of combat-type Hunters, not to mention potions and healing magic. They would be back on their feet soon enough.

And…

“How did it go?”

“They’ll contact us again in a few days. An investigator will probably visit you as well.”

An investigator.

Not the sort of person I wanted to deal with again. But that wasn’t the answer I wanted, so I asked once more.

“What about the other thing?”

“Hmm.”

Team Leader Choi regarded me with an odd expression, as though he were studying some fascinating animal.

*Did he tell them the truth?*

I didn’t want my strength becoming public knowledge. Especially now, when I still had no idea why I could see the System in reality or how it was connected to Murim.

An F-rank Hunter defeating a C-rank Rare Monster alone was unprecedented. An awl hidden in a sack would always poke through, but I’d spent my whole life being the exact opposite. I couldn’t help being cautious.

So I’d asked him:

“Could you keep what happened with me a secret?”

It must have been a difficult request for Team Leader Choi. I was asking him to give a false statement for someone he’d only just met.

“Mr. Jin Taekyung.”

“Yes.”

His grave voice made my heart sink.

Then—

“Let’s get something to eat.”

“What?”

“Look.”

Team Leader Choi held out his wrist. A glittering Magic Gem digital watch showed two in the afternoon.

“It’s an N Company model carved from a whole C-rank Magic Gem. It’s enchanted with enhancement magic, so in an emergency, you can even use it as a shield—”

*So that’s where he was going.*

“…Let’s go.”

The man was impossible to read.

“Let’s get some meat. Do you like Hanwoo?[^1]”

“Hanwoo?”

“Yes. Highest grade.”

And he was rich.

“I can never get enough of it.”

This wasn’t just any beef. It was Hanwoo. Top-grade Hanwoo at that.

System or whatever—to hell with it. First, I needed to fill my empty stomach.

[^1]: Hanwoo is beef from Korean native cattle, prized as premium meat.

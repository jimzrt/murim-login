Countless flashes erupted without pause, bright enough to make people forget the sun was already setting. Despite the midwinter cold, hundreds of thousands packed the surrounding area and greeted the returning hero with thunderous cheers.

And at the center of it all stood one man, staring blankly at the welcome that had far exceeded anything he’d imagined.

“…What the hell is all this, goddammit?”

“Was meint sie damit?”

At first, Jin Taekyung watched the scene with an awkwardly stiff expression. Before long, however, he smiled and waved at the crowd.

*They’re people who like me.*

Six months ago, Jin Taekyung had been no more than a grain of sand on a beach.

An F-rank Hunter who could be found anywhere, ignored by everyone and swept away whenever a wave rolled in.

He had never once imagined that a day like this would come.

*All I ever dreamed of was retiring without dying…*

He had gained so much. He’d been forced to walk a thorny path with death on every side, but the fruit hanging along the way had been sweet. Power beyond his wildest imagination, enormous wealth, and even fame.

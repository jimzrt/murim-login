# Chapter 78

Hunters are all hard drinkers.

Even an F-rank Hunter—the lowest classification—has physical abilities and a metabolism far beyond those of an ordinary person.

There’s a reason people say some Hunters don’t drink, but no Hunter can’t drink.

“Hic. One more glass.”

Well, apparently there was one.

Miss Song’s eyes were already half-glazed as she shook her empty glass furiously.

“One more glaaass!”

*She’s pretty even when she’s drunk… No, that’s not the point. Isn’t this getting a little dangerous?*

I watched her with concern.

*She must’ve drunk too fast.*

The moment the drinking started in earnest, she’d chugged an entire bottle of soju and had been like this ever since. Every now and then, she slurred something incomprehensible about someone having no damn tact and being cursed with rotten luck.

*Did something bad happen to her?*

While Im Kkeokjeong filled her glass, I leaned toward Team Leader Choi and whispered.

“Team Leader. Did something happen to Miss Song?”

Team Leader Choi answered with an awkward expression.

“……Something did.”

“I knew it.”

“Very recently, too.”

“Oh. Ah.”

Miss Song’s misfortune was my misfortune. Just sitting there watching her broke my heart.

“Whew. I hope things work out for her.”

“……”

“……”

Both Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim, who was sitting beside him, stared at me strangely.

This was starting to feel weird.

“What?”

“Nothing.”

“People can be like that when they’re young.”

Their answers were oddly lukewarm, but that wasn’t important right now.

Crack.

“Drink! We’re drinking ourselves to death tonight!”

Miss Song had opened her third bottle of soju and was going wild.

“Ha-ha-ha! This is why I like Miss Song so much!”

Like a fish in water—no, like a bandit who’d found booze—Im Kkeokjeong egged her on from beside her.

“Shouldn’t we stop her?”

“Ah, Miss Song?”

“Yes.”

Team Leader Choi shrugged.

“She’ll be fine. It’s not like I’ve only known her for a day or two. That’s just how Miss Song gets when she drinks.”

“Even so… Wait a second.”

I fixed Team Leader Choi with a penetrating stare.

He’d seemed suspicious for a while, and now I’d finally caught him.

“How do you know what Miss Song is like when she drinks?”

“Because I’ve drunk with her.”

“……”

Was this bastard making fun of me? Did he think I was asking because I couldn’t figure that out?

“That’s not what I mean.”

“Then what do you mean?”

“I mean…”

Now that he’d put it that way, I had nothing to say. When I thought about it, who was I to question the relationship between them?

Just as I found myself at a loss for words, Team Leader Choi spoke.

“You’ve heard of Ares, right?”

“Of course.”

Ares, the god of war.

The name of a god from ancient Greek and Roman mythology. These days, though, it was famous for something else.

“Who in Korea hasn’t heard of the Ares Guild?”

The pride and joy of Korea’s Hunters.

Hundreds of Guilds existed in Korea, but only one stood at the top: the Ares Guild. From the early days of the Great Cataclysm to the present, their achievements had been too numerous to count.

*They’re legends. Plain and simple.*

They appeared in educational comics, animated programs, movies, novels—you name it. They’d even made it into textbooks.

The Ares Guild’s standing in Korea was comparable to that of a living King Sejong or an active-duty Admiral Yi Sun-sin. No, it might have been even higher.

*They’re famous all over the world, after all.*

If you asked most foreigners, *Do you know King Sejong? King-God-General Yi Sun-sin?* they’d probably think, *What the hell is this Asian guy talking about?* But the Ares Guild was different.

*Do you know Ares?*

*Oh, yes!*

Even a tough-as-nails Texas grandpa would slap his twin pistols and know what you meant. That was the accepted academic consensus.

“Why are you asking about the Ares Guild?”

Team Leader Choi took a sip of beer before answering.

“Because I used to be with them.”

“Oh. I see… Huh?”

What had I just heard?

I sat there blinking for a while before finally speaking.

“You belonged to the Ares Guild?”

“I was a Team Leader. Though I was pretty far down the ladder.”

The Ares Guild had high standards. They selected only the best and trained them to become even better. Team Leader Choi might have called himself low-ranking, but the fact that he’d made Team Leader there was already incredible.

Though right now, he just looked like a lunatic to me.

“Then why did you leave?”

Money, honor, status.

It was the best job any Hunter—any man—could ever want. And he’d thrown it all away!

“Were you some kind of outcast at work?”

Team Leader Choi considered the question for a moment before answering.

“That might have been it. Miss Song was the only person who treated me normally.”

“……Then was Miss Song in the Ares Guild too?”

“She was on my team. I learned about her drinking habits at a team dinner.”

I swallowed hard.

*These people are total elites.*

My gaze moved between Team Leader Choi, who was sipping his beer, and Miss Song, who was drinking straight from the bottle, before settling on one person.

“Could it be that Butler Kim also…?”

“Me?”

Butler Kim smiled kindly and waved a hand.

“I retired a long time ago. Ha-ha-ha.”

“What?”

So he was a former Hunter.

I suddenly remembered the odd feeling I’d always gotten from Butler Kim—and the fact that I’d never once tried to assess him with my Qi Sense.

*Who is this man, really?*

Just as I was about to raise my Qi Sense, Im Kkeokjeong spoke up. He’d been busily inhaling meat and liquor without paying any attention to our conversation.

“Oh, the burner went out. Miss Song, do we have another gas canister?”

“Hic. That was the last one.”

“Damn, we can’t let this kill the momentum. Should we just eat it?”

Im Kkeokjeong grumbled as he flipped a piece of meat that was still mostly raw. Butler Kim gave him a gentle smile.

“That won’t do.”

Then two things happened at once.

Snap!

Butler Kim snapped his fingers.

Fwoosh!

A blast of scorching heat erupted. Blue flames surged directly over the grill, heating the plate and cooking the meat in an instant before vanishing.

“This is…”

Im Kkeokjeong and I shouted simultaneously.

“A mage!”

“It’s cooked perfectly!”

“……”

“What? Taekyung, hurry up and eat.”

*Forget it, old man.*

I shook my head.

More importantly, who would’ve guessed Butler Kim was a mage? No wonder something about him had always felt strange.

“You really had me fooled.”

Butler Kim picked up a perfectly cooked piece of meat.

“I never meant to deceive you. As I said, I’m just a retired has-been.”

*Has-been, my ass.*

If Butler Kim was a has-been, half the mages still active today ought to plant their heads on the floor.

*At least B-rank.*

He could summon flames with a single snap of his fingers, and his control was precise enough to cook the meat perfectly without burning it or leaving it underdone. Judging by the circumstances, he’d probably belonged to the Ares Guild before retiring.

And if he’d been active during the Great Cataclysm…

*……This guy’s a big shot.*

On top of that, he was an incredibly senior one.

I asked cautiously, “Um, which Hunter training center did you go through?”

“Nonsan.[^1] What about you, Mr. Taekyung?”

“Gasp. Me too. The 28th Regiment, 1st Battalion.”

“Really? What a coincidence. I was in the 28th Regiment, 1st Battalion too. Which company?”

“Second Company.”

“Then perhaps it isn’t a coincidence but fate. Ha-ha.”

There was nothing more to discuss. I rose from my seat and bowed deeply at the waist.

“It’s an honor to meet you, Senior.”

[^1]: Nonsan is home to Korea’s main Army recruit training center.

There’s a saying in Korea about school ties, regional ties, and blood ties.[^2] Hunters were no different.

The chance of awakening was 0.1 percent—one in a thousand. With odds that slim, it was rare for anyone you knew from ordinary life to awaken. The Hunter training center might not seem like much, but it was where a Hunter’s network began.

“You don’t have to go this far. Please, sit down.”

“You can speak casually with me.”

“I don’t really stand on ceremony…”

Just as Butler Kim and I were basking in the warm camaraderie between Senior and junior, Team Leader Choi suddenly cut in.

“Butler Kim. Why don’t you do as Mr. Jin asks?”

*What an ill-mannered bastard. How dare he tell such a distinguished Senior what to do…*

*Hmm. I guess he can.*

Come to think of it, Team Leader Choi was the bigger shot. He employed a former Ares Guild mage as his butler.

*What kind of family does he come from?*

Was his grandfather the president and his father the prime minister?

As my curiosity grew, Team Leader Choi continued.

“I think it’s time we settled how everyone should address one another. You’re the face of our Guild, after all. We can’t keep calling you Butler Kim or Uncle forever, can we?”

Butler Kim considered this for a moment before answering.

“I’ll follow the Young Master’s wishes.”

Team Leader Choi nodded and swept a stern gaze over everyone present.

“Then from now on, we’ll all address Butler Kim as Guild Master. No objections, correct?”

Im Kkeokjeong and Miss Song answered.

“Damn, this meat is incredible. Is it because it was grilled with magic?”

“The booze goes down! Booze! Down it goes, down it goes!”

“……”

Team Leader Choi gazed at them with regret before turning to me. I had conspicuously raised one arm.

“What does that mean?”

“I have a question.”

*At least this guy is a little better.*

Team Leader Choi’s expression seemed to say exactly that.

“Go ahead.”

“Wasn’t Team Leader Choi the Guild Master?”

“……”

Looking utterly betrayed, Team Leader Choi pulled something from inside his coat and handed it to me. I took it and examined it.

It was a business card.

“I have this.”

“What does it say?”

“Choi Minwoo, Team Leader of Team 1, Peace Guild.”

“Yes. I’m the Team Leader.”

“Oh.”

“Butler Kim is the Guild Master. I’m the Team Leader. The other three are team members. Do you understand now?”

I didn’t know whether Butler Kim was merely a boss in name or a public figurehead, but I nodded anyway.

If I didn’t, Team Leader Choi looked like he might cry.

“Did everyone else understand?”

At Team Leader Choi’s question, Im Kkeokjeong and Miss Song answered.

“Wow, even the liquor tastes amazing. Is it because we’ve got magically grilled meat for an appetizer?”

“How long are you going to make my shoulders dance? They’re dislocated! Dislocated! Dislocated!”

“……”

*Hey, are you crying?*

[^2]: School ties, regional ties, and blood ties are traditionally regarded in Korea as major sources of social connections and influence.

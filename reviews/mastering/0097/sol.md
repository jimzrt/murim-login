# Chapter 97

“Oh my, welcome!”

An ajumma who looked to be in her forties greeted me in a nasal singsong.

Maybe it was because the real-estate office was so close to home, but she looked vaguely familiar, like someone I’d passed once or twice on my way back.

“A young man! What would you like to drink? Coffee? Yulmu tea?[^1] Cola?”

“Coffee, please.”

“Black, with creamer, or…”

“Black.”

“Well, look at you. A young bachelor who knows how to drink his coffee.”

I let her rapid-fire chatter go in one ear and out the other as I took a seat. There was something else I needed to pay more attention to than the talkative real-estate ajumma.

*This is…*

A familiar energy flowed through the office.

Mana.

*Eavesdropping magic?*

There was almost no chance the owner had installed security magic. Who would put an expensive magic device in a real-estate office rather than their home?

The people watching me had clearly prepared it in advance.

*Well, I expected as much.*

They were using Familiars, after all. Eavesdropping magic was nothing by comparison.

The question was how many of them there were and where they were hiding…

“Here you go. One coffee.”

I accepted the cup with a polite smile.

“Ah, thank you.”

I meant it sincerely.

After all, she was about to tell me where they were based.

* * *

—So, what brings our handsome young man here?

—I’m looking for a place.

The eavesdropping magic transmitted their voices with perfect clarity. Kim Junsu, who had briefly deactivated his Familiar magic, exchanged looks with another team member and the Security Team Leader.

“Didn’t that bastard visit a real-estate office recently?”

“Yes. He went to one in Goyang. That was before we were assigned to him, so Team 1 Leader got the information from Hong Woojin and passed it on to us.”

“Junsu’s right. We visited the office afterward and asked around. Apparently, he even put down a deposit.”

“How much does he have in his account right now?”

The Security Team already knew Jin Taekyung’s finances inside and out.

At the Security Team Leader’s question, one of the team members quickly pulled out a tablet and opened the report.

“About 3.7 billion won. Three billion of that will go toward purchasing the new house.”

“Are you sure he’s buying it?”

“We even heard that he plans to set a date with the owner and sign the contract soon, so there’s no question about that. We looked into it, and apparently, the target lived in that neighborhood as a child. The place seems to have special meaning to him.”

“I see…”

The Security Team Leader frowned.

The man was about to sink most of his fortune into a new house. So why was he looking for another place in this neighborhood now?

*His Guild is in Bucheon, too.*

He had no idea what the bastard was thinking, but something about it bothered him.

“Hey, turn up the volume a little.”

“Yes, sir.”

The conversation continued in the three men’s ears.

—What kind of place are you looking for?

—Either monthly rent or a jeonse lease.

—I do have a few, but… as you know, this neighborhood overlaps with a safety zone, so it’s a little expensive.

—That’s fine. I’m a Hunter.

—Oh my, you’re a Hunter? No wonder you’re so fit. What rank are you? Ah, is it nosy of me to ask?

—Nothing special. It’s not very high. C-rank.

—Oh my, oh my. You must make good money. Can I feel your arm? Oh-ho-ho!

—Ha-ha. Show me some good listings and I’ll think about it. Actually, show me everything you’ve got. Jeonse, places for sale, all of it. If I find something I like, I might just buy it.

The three men listening were dumbfounded.

“That bastard’s having the time of his life.”

“Can you blame him? He lived as an F-rank Hunter, then started raking in money after his reawakening. Of course his ego’s through the roof.”

“Hmm. True. That’s the age for it.”

They all knew the feeling from experience—the thrill of stepping into a whole new world.

Luxury goods that had once been too expensive even to look at suddenly seemed like nothing, and people began looking at you differently.

“That bastard’s right in the middle of it.”

“‘If I find something I like, I might just buy it,’ my ass. Once you pay the balance on the house you already contracted for, you’ll barely have enough left for a jeonse deposit, you idiot.”

“Still, I’m jealous. What does he eat to have hair that thick?”

Jin Taekyung’s immature posturing was pathetic, yet quiet laughter escaped them all the same.

Before they knew it, the three men had relaxed. They were still listening, but it felt more like having the radio on.

—How about this place? Around five hundred million won for a jeonse lease. Considering it’s in a safety zone, it’s listed well below market price.

—Not bad. Are there any others?

—Of course there are. There’s another listing two buildings over from the one I just showed you… Oh, this one was taken recently. It was monthly rent, but the terms were exceptionally good.

—Oh, really?

—Yeah. If you’d come a few days earlier, you could’ve snagged it. The place wasn’t well maintained, but the rent was cheap. And if you have money, you can always fix that with some remodeling.

—That’s a shame.

—I’m disappointed too. Some scary-looking man came in and started ordering me around. Did he think he’d left a house in my care or something? I’d much rather give it to a young, handsome bachelor. You know what I mean?

—Ugh, sounds like a total boomer.

—I thought he might be a gangster, so I didn’t dare make a peep. And the stench of lonely bachelor was practically pouring off him. I thought I was going to die. Ho-ho-ho.

Grrrind.

Teeth ground beside them. Kim Junsu and the other team member fought back the laughter threatening to burst out.

*It’s the Team Leader.*

*Definitely the Team Leader.*

A gangster’s face and the stench of a lonely bachelor. That alone was enough to identify the Security Team Leader.

His face was so intimidating that rumor had it the interviewer who met him when he first applied to Sangdong Guild had been too frightened to ask any more questions and hired him on the spot.

“Has that ajumma lost her mind…?”

The Team Leader ground his teeth and whipped around. The other two men, red-faced from holding back their laughter, hurriedly lowered their heads.

“Looks like you’re having a hard time keeping it in.”

“N-No, sir.”

“Nothing of the sort, sir.”

They tried desperately to deny it, but the Team Leader was already thoroughly offended. He rose from his seat.

For a single man in his mid-forties, being called a lonely bachelor touched a nerve no one should ever have touched.

“I’m going to the sauna to wash off this lonely-bachelor stench. Have a transcript ready for me to review the moment I get back.”

“What?”

Kim Junsu and the other team member stared at him in disbelief.

A C-rank Hunter showing off in a real-estate office and a scatterbrained ajumma. Why would they need to transcribe such a completely unremarkable conversation?

“Team Leader, this is all being recorded automatically…”

“Prepare a full page of your individual assessments, too. He’s an important target, so we ought to gather the team’s opinions.”

“…”

“…”

Since when had he ever cared about his team’s opinions? And how could he justify going to a sauna while they were monitoring such an important target?

Their faces twisted at their petty superior’s retaliation.

“Didn’t you hear me? Repeat the order. Execute!”

“…Yes, sir.”

“…Execute.”

“You bastards have gotten way too lax. You treat your Team Leader like dog shit.”

The Security Team Leader glared at his subordinates and stormed out of the room.

Bang!

The apartment’s front door slammed shut. The two men left behind immediately spat out everything they had been holding in.

“Ah, fuck.”

“Isn’t this taking things way too far?”

“Why is he taking it out on us because he has an ugly face and can’t get married?”

“Is his face the only problem? The ajumma said he ordered her around. His personality’s rotten too.”

“For fuck’s sake. I can’t stand this job anymore.”

“Ah, I really can’t afford this kind of stress. It’ll make even more of my hair fall out.”

Kim Junsu muttered tearfully and felt the top of his head. He couldn’t be sure, but it seemed like he’d lost at least ten hairs in the last few minutes.

“What do we do about the transcript and the assessments?”

“What do you think? If you don’t want to watch the Team Leader throw another fit, you write them. Or do you want to go to the hospital and get a doctor’s note?”

“…”

“Just slap something together. I’ll cover for you and say you couldn’t write yours because you were using Familiar magic.”

After heaving deep sighs, the two men began cursing the Team Leader in earnest.

All the while, the conversation continued through the transmitter.

—It’s nice. It faces south, so it gets plenty of sunlight. What about the building next to it? Don’t tell me that one’s gone too.

—Huh? No, it’s still available. Business has been slow lately, so the only places that have gone recently are… Wait, young man.

—Yes?

—Your arm is really firm. Goodness, just look at those muscles and veins.

—…

* * *

“Young man, come again! Come twice!”

I left the real-estate office with the ajumma’s regretful farewell behind me.

Goose bumps covered the arm she had just touched.

*Whether it’s an ajumma or an ajusshi, people who grow older without growing up are all the same when it comes to hitting on someone younger.*

I’d practically fled from her lingering gaze, but I had already accomplished what I’d gone there to do. I had no regrets about leaving.

*Confirm the properties rented or sold recently.*

Today marked exactly five days since the raid with Im Changsoo.

In other words, the surveillance team couldn’t have been assigned to me more than five days ago.

*I did my best to play it off and ask indirectly without making it obvious…*

Since I knew about the eavesdropping magic, every word and action had been deliberate. I wanted them to see me as an unremarkable C-rank Hunter consumed by vanity and extravagance.

*Whether they bought it is another question.*

My conversation with the real-estate ajumma had given me an important clue. I silently recited the addresses I had memorized.

*Building 5, Unit 901. Building 4, Unit 302. Building 3, Unit 202.*

Those were the three properties rented or sold within the past five days.

Using our house as the center, I had limited the search to a five-hundred-meter radius—the maximum range of Familiar magic.

The watchers had to be somewhere inside it.

*The problem is finding them.*

I didn’t just want to drive them away. I wanted to catch them, beat the hell out of them, and find out who was behind all this.

If I went after the wrong place too soon, they might catch on and run.

*They could be hiding in a car, so I should check the parking lot too.*

Searching the nearby homes would tip them off, but I could search the parking lot without looking suspicious.

All I had to do was pretend to take a walk while sweeping the area with Qi Sense.

Game over.

“Oh, we meet again.”

Just like this guy.

I greeted Kim Gwondong when he acted as though he recognized me.

“Looks like it.”

“You said you were going to the real-estate office. Finished already?”

“I was just making inquiries. Once I got there and saw the prices, though, I ran straight back out.”

“That’s this neighborhood for you. Still, you’re doing well for yourself, young man. At your age, all I did was sit at home and eat my parents’ food.”

“Doing well? Ha-ha.”

If he knew what I was really capable of, he’d pass out.

Kim Gwondong laughed along, oblivious to my thoughts, then spoke.

“Well, I should get going. I need to walk to the park over there and back.”

“You must enjoy taking walks.”

“Huh? I don’t do it because I enjoy it. I do it because I have to. You’ll have a hard time too when you reach my age.”

He conspicuously waved his thin arms and legs.

To all appearances, he was just a scrawny, potbellied middle-aged man.

*He certainly looks like a civilian.*

Anyone else would have fallen for it completely.

But there was no such thing as a Level 42 civilian.

*Probably a C-rank Hunter. Judging by his build, he likely specializes in stealth and pursuit.*

Once you knew someone was a Hunter, there was plenty you could infer.

I said goodbye to Kim Gwondong.

“Then I’ll see you around.”

“Maybe you will, maybe you won’t. Ha-ha.”

Well, I definitely wanted to see him again.

Of course, next time we wouldn’t be parting with friendly smiles and laughter. I wanted to knock him flat right then and there, but the time wasn’t right yet.

I gave him a slight bow and turned to leave when his voice came from behind me.

“Oh, right. That cat seemed awfully smart. I saw it on my way over, and it was still sitting there.”

He had even given me a helpful reminder not to forget my Familiar.

Just as he’d said, the cat was waiting for me in the same spot as before.

“Meow.”

Yeah. Hyung’s back, you little punk.

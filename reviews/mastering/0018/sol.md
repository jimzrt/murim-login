# Chapter 18

The atmosphere went from bad to worse.

“The important thing is that the duel was fair!”

“Fair? Since when is using poison considered fair? What is this, the Sichuan Tang Clan?”

“He says he didn’t do it! And as the Medicine King Hall Leader said earlier, Lee Seogeun was perfectly fine—”

“Of course the Third Young Master would deny it. And how can you trust that quack?”

“A quack? You bastard!”

Then one particularly agitated voice pierced my ears.

“If necessary, we should prevent the war even if it means offering up the Third Young Master’s head!”

…What?

“What outrageous nonsense!”

“Am I wrong? It’s time we admitted the truth. The Mount Heng Sword Sect is stronger than our family. If war breaks out, hundreds will be killed or injured. At worst, our family could be destroyed. All this would end if we handed over the Third Young Master, the cause of the entire crisis!”

Was that supposed to be an argument, or a fart? It had been a while since I’d heard such spectacular bullshit. The back of my neck tightened, and my chest began to ache.

But someone else stepped forward before I could.

“What did you just say?”

The voice was quiet, but it carried power. If anything, its quietness made every word clearer.

It was Jin Wikyung. He surveyed the room, his face expressionless.

“Whose. Head. Did you say we should offer?”

Each syllable fell separately, his voice rimed with frost. The air turned so chilling that even I shivered.

The White Tiger Hall Leader immediately opened his mouth.

“Why, obviously the Third Young Master, the main culprit behind this—ah.”

The bastard had no hair and no tact. The White Tiger Hall Leader trailed off, but it was already too late.

“So you intend to hand the Third Young Master’s head over to the Mount Heng Sword Sect because of an unconfirmed accusation? Is that something a hall leader of this family should say?”

“No, that isn’t what I meant…”

Overwhelmed by Jin Wikyung’s aura, the White Tiger Hall Leader avoided his gaze. Jin Wikyung silently glared at him.

A man who rarely got angry was all the more frightening when he did.

The sight of the White Tiger Hall Leader’s deathly pale face felt like a ten-year bout of indigestion finally clearing up. Jin Wikyung rose and looked coldly down at everyone present.

“You already know the truth.”

He swept his gaze across the room and spoke firmly.

“You know the duel was fair and that Lee Seogeun’s poisoning was a conspiracy. Yet despite knowing that, you would have us submit to them out of fear?”

The members of the Elder Council faction avoided his gaze. Jin Wikyung’s sneer sharpened.

“I don’t know who placed it in their hands, but the Mount Heng Sword Sect now holds the hilt of a sword called justification. Whether they draw it today or tomorrow—or whether it has already been drawn—it is too late for us to turn things back. Only one path remains. We fight.”

“…”

“Do you want to live? Do you want to protect the family? If you truly do, ready the martial artists and prepare for war. Otherwise, cut off my younger brother’s head and mine and offer them up. If wealth and glory are all you desire, I suppose that would not be a bad choice.”

A suffocating silence seized the main assembly hall.

If one man had not spoken the next moment, that silence might have crushed us for hours.

“Excellent.”

It was the man who had watched the entire affair without saying a word.

The Head Elder.

* * *

The Head Elder.

Someone I needed to watch. The family’s most senior elder and the leader of the Elder Council.

I recalled Jin Wikyung’s earlier Sound Transmission.

*He told me to be careful.*

Among all the NPCs, Jin Wikyung was my greatest ally and adviser.

I hadn’t taken his warning lightly. I’d watched the Head Elder whenever I had the chance.

And I’d reached one conclusion.

*Fuck, I can’t figure him out at all.*

If someone asked how many NPCs I’d encountered since starting this godforsaken game, the number would easily exceed a hundred.

Each of them had their own expressions and personalities. The servant I’d met at the pleasure house wore a world-weary customer-service smile. The coachman who brought me here had a goofy streak. The family’s senior members were surprisingly simple-minded and aggressive.

But the Head Elder…

*I can’t read him.*

He simply watched everything with that strange smile.

When the interrogation began. When the senior members of each faction shouted at one another. Even now.

Clap. Clap. Clap.

The Head Elder’s vigorous applause echoed through the hall.

“I thought you still a mere youngster, but before I knew it, the Lesser Family Head had become such a dignified martial artist. Excellent. That is how the Lesser Family Head of our family should conduct himself.”

“I can only apologize for showing you such an unseemly side.”

“Excessive humility can appear arrogant. You have nothing to apologize for.”

Despite the Head Elder’s praise, Jin Wikyung’s face remained stiff.

“May this old man add a few words? What do you say, Lesser Family Head?”

“I will heed them.”

As the Head Elder slowly rose, dozens of pairs of eyes fixed on him.

He was the family’s most senior elder. In terms of authority and standing within the family, he might even surpass Jin Wikyung.

The biggest problem was that he led the opposing faction—the Elder Council.

*Fuck. I’m screwed.*

Preparing for the worst, I began edging toward the door. Then the Head Elder’s first words reached my ears.

“You’re rotten to the core.”

Huh?

I whipped my head around. The Head Elder still wore that same strange smile.

*Did I hear him wrong?*

I hadn’t.

“Even beasts join forces when an enemy enters their den. Yet men who call themselves senior members of this family propose offering up the head of a direct-line member to prevent a war. Heh. To think men like you sit on our family council.”

“H-Head Elder, you misunderstand. We merely…”

“White Tiger Hall Leader.”

The White Tiger Hall Leader stiffened at the Head Elder’s chilly call.

“Was I away for too long? Or did this happen because the Family Head was absent?”

“N-no, Head Elder.”

Judging by his expression alone, he looked like some muscular old guy at the gym casually asking whether you’d eaten. The words coming out of his mouth, however, were downright vicious.

*What is this?*

What the hell was going on? Why was he taking our side?

I looked around frantically, but everyone else seemed just as confused as I was. Both factions were visibly flustered.

“What do you think, Lesser Family Head?”

“I’m not sure what you mean.”

“If war is now inevitable, our first priority should be putting our own house in order. Should we not begin by cutting off the heads of those men who are little better than traitors?”

In the frozen silence, Jin Wikyung stared at the Head Elder for a long moment before forcing out an answer like a sigh.

“That cannot be done.”

Whew.

The White Tiger Hall Leader sighed in relief.

Considering that he had just suggested offering up my head, I was a little disappointed.

*Should I suggest killing just him?*

“Those men were not content merely to frame a direct-line member of the family. They even argued that we should surrender him to the enemy. Do you not think your response is too lenient?”

“They have served our family faithfully for many years. I will consider their words an ill-considered remark made in the heat of the moment.”

The Head Elder laughed heartily.

“An ill-considered remark. An ill-considered remark, indeed. Yes, the Lesser Family Head’s capacity is greater than I imagined. You are truly worthy of your position. In that case, I shall not hold these men responsible. I thank the Lesser Family Head for magnanimously forgiving the thoughtless words of a few old men.”

The Head Elder bowed, and the others hurriedly waved their hands and bent at the waist in return.

“Oh, Head Elder, please. We were the ones who failed to think matters through.”

“Please don’t do this.”

“You’re only making us more ashamed. Please…”

“Head Elder!”

Jin Wikyung was the one who had spared them, yet they were causing an uproar around the Head Elder.

What a performance. Go on, keep it up.

I watched the spectacle, clicking my tongue inwardly, when—

*Wait. A performance?*

An inexplicable sense of wrongness swept over me. I hurriedly examined the people gathered around the Head Elder.

And then I saw them.

The faint smiles passing over the lips of the Elder Council members who had cornered me earlier.

*No way…*

Had all of this been planned? Every last bit of it?

Why? What were they trying to accomplish? Countless questions rose and vanished. My thoughts were a complete mess.

*Does Jin Wikyung know something?*

I didn’t even need to turn and look for him. The Head Elder was already raising Jin Wikyung’s hand high and shouting.

“With the Family Head absent, the Lesser Family Head is effectively our Family Head! I support him in this matter. If we unite around him, then no matter how formidable our enemies may be, they will not dare challenge the Jin Family!”

The feverish atmosphere. The cheers and chants.

*Where had I seen this before?*

Something about it felt strangely familiar.

And then—

“Thank you.”

The instant I saw the corners of Jin Wikyung’s mouth trembling and the Head Elder’s strange smile, I realized what it reminded me of.

*A campaign rally.*

The Head Elder’s figure overlapped with the politicians I had seen on television.

* * *

It left a bad taste in my mouth, but once the Head Elder raised Jin Wikyung’s hand, the meeting proceeded swiftly.

The Elder Council faction stopped harping on the duel, and the discussion shifted entirely toward preparing for war.

“How many men are currently available?”

“If we recall the martial artists stationed elsewhere… roughly two hundred.”

Two hundred?

The unexpectedly large number impressed me, but the next question shut me up.

“If we select only elites of First Rate or higher?”

“Fewer than twenty. Of course, the number would be different if we included everyone present.”

Including the senior members, the family had around thirty or forty First Rate masters.

I didn’t know much about how things worked here, but that seemed like a decent number.

As expected of a prestigious martial family with deep roots. Two hundred years of accumulated strength didn’t simply vanish.

“What about the Mount Heng Sword Sect?”

“We have confirmed at least three hundred martial artists so far.”

Three hundred. And that was only the minimum, putting the difference at more than a hundred men.

But that was fine. It wasn’t as if fights were decided by numbers—

“And they have more than fifty First Rate martial artists.”

This war was going to be difficult.

Still, with masters like Jin Wikyung and Wipeng, it ought to be winnable. They were high-level NPCs whose strength even my Qi Sense couldn’t read. The Head Elder probably belonged in that category as well.

“Our family has three Peak masters in total: the Lesser Family Head, the Head Elder, and Sir Wipeng. The Mount Heng Sword Sect has five.”

…Fuck this. I couldn’t do it. Seriously.

*What? A prestigious martial family with deep roots? Two hundred years of history?*

What the hell had these bastards been doing for two hundred years? I’d heard the Mount Heng Sword Sect was less than thirty years old, yet we weren’t merely outmatched in military strength.

We were being crushed. Crushed.

*Did the Black Death sweep through here last year or something?*

Plague or no plague, I felt like I was about to lose my mind. I found myself staring at the meeting-hall ceiling through a yellow haze when I made a sudden decision.

*I need to run.*

Around four in the morning should do. Once everyone was asleep, I’d sneak over the wall and run as far away as possible.

At my current level, I could easily deal with five or six martial artists at the Heavenly Axe’s level.

I could scour every mountain I came across and rack up EXP and Fame—

“M-Mount Heng! A messenger pigeon has arrived from the Mount Heng Sword Sect!”

A martial artist rushed into the hall with the announcement.

Someone took the tightly rolled sheet of paper and unfurled it. Red characters, as though written in blood, appeared before my eyes.

Even without the System translating them, I could understand what they said.

> **Enemies Who Cannot Live Beneath the Same Sky**

*Enemies who could not coexist beneath heaven.*

Jin Wikyung spoke, his expression grim.

“From this moment onward, our family enters a state of war. Without my seal, no one may enter or leave the family grounds, regardless of status. Maintain the strictest vigilance. Do not let so much as a single ant through. Understood?”

“Yes!”

“…”

As I stood there half dazed, a System notification rang in my ears.

Ding.

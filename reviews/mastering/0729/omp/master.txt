# Chapter 729

I’d never gone fishing in my life, and I didn’t know how.

But I did know how to catch more fish.

*Bait.*

That was what we needed. Scatter hints about a major announcement far and wide, then draw as much attention as possible from as many fish—or rather, people—as we could.

Of course, we’d need to do a little persuading along the way.

Team Leader Choi and I weren’t trying to fill a bucket. We wanted a boat so full the catch spilled over the sides. And for that, we needed a good crew. The first person Team Leader Choi called was President Baek Hanseong.

“Pardon?”

He must have been through plenty to make it into the Blue House just past forty. The words *Mana Cultivation Method* made his eyes widen. The name that followed made him spill his coffee.

“I-Is that true? Did he really…?”

“Yes. My maternal grandfather created it himself. He wants to share the Mana Cultivation Method with everyone.”

“I-I can’t believe it.”

Who was Cheon Taemin?

A living savior. A hero who had accomplished something no one else in human history ever had.

He was the Slayer who had defeated the Demon King Asmodeus and brought the Great Cataclysm to an end. Everyone respected his name.

“But why is the Mana Cultivation Method called *Smiling*…?”

“…I brought out the wrong one. Please forget what you saw.”

Honestly, if people knew I’d created it, I wouldn’t care whether it was called *The Smiling Mana Cultivation Method* or *As-Salamu Alaykum*.

But with Cheon Taemin’s name attached, Team Leader Choi and I agreed that *The Smiling Mana Cultivation Method* was a problem.

“This is the Mana Cultivation Method my maternal grandfather created.”

Team Leader Choi placed a neatly bound booklet on the table. A new name was printed on the cover.

“*Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique*…”

“The Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique is versatile enough for even the lowest-ranking Hunter to learn, and exceptionally safe. It won’t produce tremendous results the moment someone learns it, but it will undoubtedly be a great help to Hunters.”

Of course it would. That was what I’d made it for.

The name had changed for grown-up reasons, but I’d come up with the new one too. Feeling a little proud, I added, “In short, it’s a fucking incredible Mana Cultivation Method.”

Team Leader Choi gave me a pointed cough. President Baek didn’t notice—or, more likely, he was too distracted to notice.

He stared at the booklet containing the technique’s principles as though entranced. It was a long while before he came to his senses.

“But why bring something this important to me…?”

“We need an ally.”

President Baek hadn’t bought the presidential chair secondhand on Oi Market. He understood at once why Team Leader Choi and I had come.

“You want a breakwater.”

“We hope we can support each other.”

“But even if a major Guild tried to interfere, it would have a hard time while your grandfather is around.”

A sound assessment.

If it were true.

*Unfortunately, it isn’t.*

We wanted to avoid as much outside interference as possible. That was why we were using the unconscious Cheon Taemin’s name to release the Smiling—no, the Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique. Team Leader Choi had no intention of telling the truth even to President Baek, who had always been friendly to us.

He was a politician, after all.

“Neither my grandfather nor I want any conflict or controversy. That’s why we need the government’s cooperation.”

“Does that mean the Blue House can announce the existence of this Mana Cultivation Method itself?”

Team Leader Choi shook his head.

“It should be enough to say we’ve been working together and that there’ll be a major announcement in two days. That alone will get the public’s attention.”

“So you want me to drum up interest…”

“The release will be presented as a national project. Korea’s standing will rise as a result.”

“Hmm.”

The first public release of a Mana Cultivation Method in history. Korea’s name would forever be tied to it—and so would the President’s achievement.

A faint smile appeared on President Baek’s lips.

“The Korean flag looks especially fine today.”

Someone once said the most powerful drug in the world was a patriotic high.

*Flap—*

* * *

Cheon Taemin. The public release of a Mana Cultivation Method. A national project.

Those three phrases brought everything to a boil like a cauldron over charcoal. The fire was lit in Korea.

> “My beloved and respected fellow citizens. I stand before you today to make an important announcement…”

President Baek made good on his promise to cooperate by calling an emergency press conference at the Blue House.

He spoke passionately before a crowd of reporters and cameras, including the foreign press. The conference went out live on television, spread across the world, and caused an uproar.

> **Mana Cultivation Method to Be Made Public—A Historic First!**
>
> **Savior of Humanity Saves the World Once Again!**
>
> **Will the Living Legend Finally Show Himself?**

Cheon Taemin’s name alone was newsworthy after he’d stayed out of sight for so long. Now the Mana Cultivation Method he’d created was going to be released to the entire world.

The response was explosive.

No—the media had already exploded.

“Hey! Reporter Park! The Peace Guild! Call the Peace Guild!”

“I-I already did. I can’t get a thing out of them.”

“What about Ares? Don’t you have a contact there? We’ve gotten tips from them before!”

“He’s in a detention center right now. He got caught up in that Go Jun incident a while back…”

“You’re killing me. What about Choi Minwoo?”

“Don’t even ask. It’s nearly impossible to get through to him, and you couldn’t draw a drop of blood from his people if you tried.”

“Then Jin Taekyung?”

“…I’m the one who’d bleed if I tried him, Chief. Do you want to watch me get beaten to death?”

Tips were surfacing everywhere, with no way to tell who was leaking them. Unless a source approached a reporter first, there was no way to reach them.

The media, at their wits’ end, could only churn out clickbait. Online, things were even worse.

Netizens could usually find two sides to anything and argue about it without end. But beneath articles about the Mana Cultivation Method’s release, they were united in praise.

Someone was giving away a secret technique of the kind others fought to keep hidden, with no restrictions at all.

Simply because they wanted to help everyone.

And the release wouldn’t benefit only Hunters. Stronger Hunters could deal more quickly with the disturbing incidents that had been occurring one after another. Ordinary people would be safer too.

Of course, some people were disappointed for an entirely different reason.

“Hmm.”

“It would have been nice to have some advance notice.”

Ares Guild.

Choi Minwoo dipped his head at the cautious complaints from executives who had survived the upheaval within the Guild.

“I’m sorry I couldn’t tell you beforehand. But I thought it best to act quickly, before the information leaked.”

The executives exchanged glances at the young, capable new Vice Guild Master’s apology.

He’d admitted fault first. That gave them grounds to press him—just enough to make sure he never acted on his own again.

The problem was the formidable legitimacy of a Vice Guild Master so young he was practically green.

*He stepped forward personally.*

*I’ve never even seen his face.*

*Does he care about his grandson that much?*

Cheon Taemin hadn’t appeared before them in a very long time.

Some executives recruited from outside had never met their Guild Master, even after joining Ares. Even those who had been there for years had begun to harbor vague suspicions after Lee Jungryong’s funeral.

*Something’s happened to the Guild Master.*

It was a reasonable suspicion.

Cheon Taemin hadn’t appeared at the funeral of Lee Jungryong, a man who had been practically his sworn brother. Nor had he intervened when Lee Jungryong sent his grandson from one overseas branch to another, all but exiling him.

But…things were different now.

Even if doubts still lingered, showing them would be incredibly foolish. Cheon Taemin’s name still commanded their deepest respect. And his grandfather wasn’t the young Vice Guild Master’s only winning card.

“You’re all awfully quiet.”

A low voice broke the silence. Jin Taekyung looked up from the webtoon on his smartphone.

“When someone apologizes, you’re supposed to answer them.”

“…!”

“Aren’t you?”

Dozens of eyes quivered. Throats bobbed.

The executives exchanged glances, then bowed their heads with forced smiles.

That was when it happened.

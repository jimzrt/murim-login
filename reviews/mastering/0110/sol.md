# Chapter 110

*Boom!*

The thunderous crash heralded the arrival of a powerfully built middle-aged man.

Beneath his thick, bristling brows, tigerish eyes swept the hall with the fury of an enraged beast.

“Which one of you was spouting that nonsense just now?”

Everyone gathered there was a senior figure of the Mount Heng Sword Sect.

Though they might not have measured up to their predecessors, they all possessed First Rate martial arts and considerable experience. Yet even they could only shrink back and avoid his gaze.

The middle-aged man before them had more than earned that kind of fear.

*Damn it. Of all people, it had to be the Tiger of Mount Heng…*

People said two beasts lived on Mount Heng.

The Blood Wolf Sword and the Tiger of Mount Heng. The closest of friends, yet each a wall the other had to overcome.

Cheol Mubaek had been known as the Tiger of Mount Heng, a Peak master, for decades.

“I asked which one of you it was!”

His roar made every hair on the senior figures’ bodies stand on end.

They said that whenever Cheol Mubaek truly lost his temper, even his close friend Lee Cheonbaek stayed out of his way. These men lagged far behind him in both martial arts and age. What more needed to be said?

“We should be standing together and driving out those mounted-bandit bastards, yet you dare defy Cheonbaek’s dying wishes and harbor thoughts of treason?”

His gaze seemed ready to spew flames. The senior figures flinched as though they had been burned.

“G-Great Hero Cheol, you misunderstand.”

“How could we possibly harbor thoughts of treason?”

“Then have I grown so old that my ears no longer work?”

At that moment, everyone in the main hall felt parched. It was no illusion, but the effect of the terrifying Scorching Yang Qi pouring from Cheol Mubaek.

*This is insane.*

*What the hell did he eat to amass such monstrous internal energy…?*

Simply standing near him made it difficult to breathe, and sweat streamed down their bodies. This was the true power of the Tiger of Mount Heng, the Peak master said to have trained alone somewhere in the vast mountain range since his early twenties.

“Huff… Haaah…”

“Great Hero, please calm yourself… Hah.”

The senior figures panted for breath while the Peak master glared at them without the slightest hint of mercy.

Just as the air in the hall threatened to boil like lava—

“Uncle Cheol, it’s hot.”

A voice as clear and refreshing as a stream spoke up, and slender white fingers tugged at Cheol Mubaek’s sleeve. The angry furrows in his brow instantly smoothed away, as though someone had pulled them flat.

“W-Was it very hot?”

“Yes. I can’t even breathe.”

“Oh dear. I wasn’t thinking of you. How is it now?”

“Much better. Thank you, Uncle Cheol.”

“Don’t thank me. Protecting you is my duty, Seowol.”

Cheol Mubaek’s mighty Scorching Yang Qi subsided.

Only then did held breaths burst out across the hall. Once the sweat-drenched men had recovered their senses, they realized that Cheol Mubaek had not come alone.

“Y-Young Lady.”

“We greet the Young Lady.”

The senior figures hurriedly rose to pay their respects, making Cheol Mubaek’s brows rise. But the “Young Lady” spoke first.

“I’ll tell you both one last time, Iron Sword Squad Leader, Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion.”

The woman who had been hidden behind Cheol Mubaek’s massive frame stepped into view. She was slender and graceful, and the hem of her blue gown rustled as it brushed the floor.

“Change how you address me. Not Young Lady—Sect Leader.”

Meeting her frost-covered gaze, the men remembered something they had momentarily forgotten.

*Ah. That’s right.*

The Blood Wolf Sword, Lee Cheonbaek.

Of all his children, Lee Seowol had inherited his blood most strongly.

* * *

I knew next to nothing about horseback riding. I had only ridden a few times since coming to Murim. In the modern world, it was practically an aristocratic sport reserved for the rich, so I’d never had the chance to try it.

Still, my physical abilities were excellent, and I was riding a well-trained horse. Even at a furious gallop, I had enough breathing room to check the System Window.

*Open Quest Window.*

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> **Quest**
>
> **Yesterday’s Enemy, Today’s Ally**
>
> Now that the whole truth has come to light, the Mount Heng Sword Sect is no longer your enemy, but an ally with whom you must join forces. Invite them to the Jin Family of Taiyuan for the upcoming New Year’s Day.
>
> **Grade:** Peak
>
> **Restriction:** Jin Taekyung
>
> **Objective:** Deliver the invitation (Incomplete)
>
> **Reward:** ???
>
> **Failure:** None

Quests were fluid. Depending on the circumstances, sudden Quests could appear or existing Quests could be updated, as this one had been.

*The Grade went up.*

The Quest Grade changing from First Rate to Peak meant the road to the Mount Heng Sword Sect had become anything but easy.

For example, there was the Red Wind Band. Or the Red Wind Band. Probably the Red Wind Band…

Never mind. Thinking about it any more would only make me miserable.

*Every day in this place is like walking on thin ice.*

I thought I’d finally received an easy Quest for once, only for trouble to erupt again.

I wasn’t as anxious as I would have been before. That was partly thanks to Jin Mukyung’s reassuring presence, but it was also because I had grown stronger myself.

*Open Status Window.*

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> **Status Window**
>
> **Level:** 55 — Jin Taekyung
>
> **Class:** First Rate Martial Artist
>
> **Fame:** 1,300 (+150)
>
> **Titles:** 4 (Title effects active)
>
> — Returnee (All stats +10)
>
> — Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi (All stats +10, Fame +100)
>
> — Scion of a Prestigious Family (All stats +5, Fame +50)
>
> — Gambler (Combat-related stats +10% in one-on-one combat)
>
> **Strength:** 196 (+25)  
> **Stamina:** 195 (+25)
>
> **Agility:** 192 (+25)  
> **Intelligence:** 35 (+25)
>
> **Charm:** 35 (+25)  
> **Internal Energy:** 15 years
>
> **Toughness:** 155 (+25)
>
> **Remaining Points:** 0

*Ahh, barkeep.*

*Look how well I’ve grown. All by myself.*

Strength, Agility, and Stamina were all closing in on 200. Just looking at them made me feel full, and the Toughness I had gained from Jin Mukyung beating the hell out of me was coming along nicely too.

*The Title bonuses are hefty, too. This should be enough.*

Until now, I had raised my stats to survive. Every time I made progress on a Quest, crises came crashing in from every direction. How could I possibly have afforded to invest points in Charm or Intelligence?

*Even if I raised my Intelligence until I had an IQ of 180, it’s not like I’d start thrusting my spear scientifically.*

The same went for Charm. Jopil and the Head Elder weren’t going to spare me just because I was handsome.

Of course, raising those stats would probably help me somehow in the future. But while my life was hanging by a thread, I hadn’t had the nerve to invest in noncombat stats.

*I can more or less keep myself alive now.*

Once this Quest was over, I planned to devote some attention to my internal energy and noncombat stats.

The items I had obtained after defeating Jopil—including the Blazing Flame Divine Pill—were already sitting untouched in my Inventory.

*Of course, taking it wrong could send me straight to the grave.*

Just then, Wolhwa, who had been riding at the front, spotted a stream and pulled to a stop.

“We’ll rest for a little while. The horses are exhausted.”

How much time had passed?

We had ridden without stopping since leaving the shrine. Dawn had broken, and now the sun hung high overhead. System messages announcing increases to my Strength and Stamina had even appeared twice, so this really had been a forced march.

“Whew. My ass is killing me. If I’d known this would happen, I should’ve been born a coachman’s son instead of a farmer’s.”

While the horses rested, Hyuk Mujin dropped heavily to the ground. As the lowest-level member of our group, he was visibly more exhausted than the rest of us.

“Having a hard time?”

Hyuk Mujin wiped the sweat from his forehead with his sleeve.

“Honestly, yes… But strangely enough, it’s much better than last time.”

“Last time?”

“You’ve forgotten already? The scouting mission.”

“Ah, right.”

I had nearly died after running into Jopil during a scouting mission for White Tiger Hall. We had taken horses with us then too.

*Though we ended up abandoning them once the blizzard hit.*

The memory drew a quiet laugh from me.

“What’s so funny?”

“I was thinking about you. You mouthed off to me without knowing any better and got the crap beaten out of you.”

“……Do you really have to dredge up the past to feel better?”

“You’re the one who asked, punk.”

“Anyway, I’m saying this feels much easier than it did back then.”

“Really?”

“Yes. We’ve ridden much farther than we did during the scouting mission, but I’m not even that tired. Maybe I’m getting used to riding?”

“Maybe… Wait a second.”

Something occurred to me, and I heightened my Qi Sense.

*Ding.*

With the familiar System notification, a Level Window appeared over Hyuk Mujin’s bewildered face.

> **System**
>
> **Level:** 38 — Hyuk Mujin

“……Huh?”

A breathy sound escaped my open mouth. When had Hyuk Mujin’s Level gotten this high?

*Strictly speaking, it wasn’t that high.*

But considering he had been only Level 20 when we first met, even calling it astonishing progress didn’t do it justice. At this point, he was practically a new man.

*Come to think of it, his Level has been rising steadily ever since we met.*

As I searched my memory, the details came back more clearly.

It had been the same when we reunited in the scouting party. Every time I heightened my Qi Sense, Hyuk Mujin’s Level seemed to have risen by one or two.

Now he was Level 38. In terms of time spent in Murim, he had nearly doubled his Level in just over two months.

*Then maybe…?*

The possibility made me stare intently at Hyuk Mujin.

What if he received stat points like I did? Could I distribute them for him?

*It’s possible.*

Considering how much stronger I was than Hunters or martial artists around the same Level, the System did seem to provide some kind of enhancement…

*It’s worth a try.*

“Why are you staring at me like that? Is there something on my face?”

“No. You’re just ugly.”

“……Seriously.”

I grabbed Hyuk Mujin by the shoulder and shouted inwardly.

*Open Status Window!*

At that exact moment—

“What are you doing? You’re hurting my shoulder.”

“Oh. Right.”

Nothing happened. I’d thought something might at least pop up.

Then again, my main character was still nowhere near the Level cap, so what business did I have raising an alt? It was still disappointing, though.

*Should I try saying it out loud?*

Everyone would definitely think I was a lunatic, but that would be better than moving on with the nagging feeling of leaving the bathroom without washing my hands.

I stealthily laid a hand against Hyuk Mujin’s back—lightly, ever so lightly—and muttered under my breath.

“Open Status Window.”

“Seriously, what has gotten into you today?”

I ignored him and shot to my feet.

The notification I had been waiting for chimed, and a System Window appeared.

“Yes! There it is!”

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> The Quest condition **Time Limit** has been added.
>
> Arrive at the Mount Heng Sword Sect within **22:00:00**. If you are late, there will be no turning back.

“Yes…”

My voice died away. My eyes trembled.

*A time limit? Why the hell is there a time limit?*

*Why are you doing this to me? Seriously.*

I let out a deep sigh. Wolhwa’s eyes widened.

“Young Master Jin, are you ill?”

“No, it’s not that. Do you know roughly when we’ll arrive?”

“Hmm. At this pace, sometime before tomorrow evening?”

“Ah.”

It was a little past noon now. That meant we still had more than a full day’s ride ahead of us.

Judging by the change to the Quest Window, the Red Wind Band bastards would attack the Mount Heng Sword Sect within that time limit…

What the hell was I supposed to do?

“Shouldn’t we get going?”

“The horses are exhausted. They need to rest for an hour.”

“Horses, you’re fine, aren’t you? You heard them, right? They said they’re fine.”

“……”

Yeah. I knew you’d look at me like that.

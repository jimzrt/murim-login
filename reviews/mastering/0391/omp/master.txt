# Chapter 391

Zhang Wei, commander of the 2nd Company of the Sichuan Province Public Security Armed Forces, had felt uneasy about this operation from the very beginning.

*Advance ahead of the others with the army and seize the city? That’s not what Regimental Commander Shao ordered.*

His doubts only deepened when they went out of their way to bypass the friendly forces fighting up ahead.

“Senior Colonel Wang, are you certain our regimental commander approved this operation?”

The middle-aged senior officer frowned at Zhang Wei’s question.

“Why do you ask?”

“No matter how I look at it, something seems wrong. Our regimental commander clearly ordered us to conserve our strength in the rear and guard headquarters…”

“Ah, you mean that little regimental commander.”

Zhang Wei’s expression hardened at the senior officer’s mocking tone toward Shao Shen.

“Regimental Commander Shao Shen holds the same rank as you, Senior Colonel Wang. And he’ll soon be promoted to major general.”

“That ignorant child simply got lucky. As if awakening as an A-rank Hunter weren’t enough, that Korean bastard’s performance landed him on the promotion list too. Considering how quickly he’s risen through the ranks, perhaps he has some decent guanxi in political circles.”[^1]

“He may be young, but he’s earned his position. He’s also highly respected and treats us like brothers.”

The senior officer ran a hand along the baton he was holding.

“I’m well aware that you Hunters don’t care much for rank or seniority. But this is wartime. The order came from Senior General Liao, our commander.”

“From the commander himself…?”

“That’s right. So if you don’t want to obey, turn back now. Just understand that doing so will constitute disobeying orders.”

“…!”

“All we’re trying to do is speed up our advance. It seems unlikely under the circumstances, but if any monsters remain, we’ll wipe them out and rescue any civilians in danger. That’s all. Understood?”

Zhang Wei remained silent for a long moment before nodding and stepping away.

The Public Security Armed Forces were normally treated as an organization separate from the military, but this was wartime. Defying Senior General Liao, commander of the western front, would not be a wise choice.

“What did he say?”

Zhang Wei answered his subordinate’s hushed question.

“He says it’s an easy operation, so we should shut up and follow him. If we refuse, it’s disobeying orders.”

“Do you believe him?”

“If you mean the first part, of course not. But the second part is probably true. What do you think?”

“Of course I don’t believe him. That bastard Wang is incompetent. He only rose this far because of a single connection to the Princelings. Everyone knows he’s Senior General Liao’s lapdog.”

“And this order came from that very Senior General Liao. For now, we’ll have to endure the stench, however rotten it smells.”

“Damn it.”

“But Wang might be telling the truth. Most of the monsters in the area should already be engaged with our forces. With our current strength, we can handle a few stragglers.”

Zhang Wei looked around. In addition to the hundred Hunters under his command, there were around twenty tanks and five hundred infantrymen. Three combat helicopters flew overhead, scouting the way ahead.

*Please, let us get through this without any serious losses.*

Perhaps Zhang Wei’s wish reached the heavens, because what he feared did not come to pass.

The small town near Suining City had been devastated by monsters, but when they entered with every sense on alert, the ruined streets were deathly silent.

The occasional goblin or orc sprang out at them, but they posed no threat whatsoever.

—Kieeeek!

Slice!

After cutting down a dozen or so monsters at the head of the column, Zhang Wei relaxed slightly.

“Different species are appearing on their own. They must be stragglers that broke away from the main horde.”

“Just as expected. What did I tell you?”

The officer’s smug response grated on him, but this was still better than the alternative.

Zhang Wei silently nodded—then froze at the senior officer’s next words.

“You want us to split our forces?”

“That’s right. Under these circumstances, finding survivors as quickly as possible should be our priority.”

“But Senior Colonel Wang, we haven’t even been inside the city for an hour. We should advance farther toward the center before—”

“Impertinent.”

“Pardon?”

“You Hunters may be better in a fight, but I’m several steps ahead of you when it comes to tactics. I currently hold command authority, so shut your mouth and follow my orders.”

“…!”

“Didn’t you hear me? Or should I give the order directly to your subordinates?”

“…I’ll do it.”

“You have a detailed map, I assume. We’ll meet in the central square in two hours.”

The officer gave Zhang Wei a spiteful smile and turned to climb into an armored vehicle.

At that exact moment—

Whoooosh—boom!

Zhang Wei blinked, dazed. He wiped his face with one palm, and it came away covered in sticky blood.

The vivid crimson blood was unmistakably human. Beside him, the officer’s body—its entire upper half gone without a trace—rolled out of the armored vehicle.

Thud.

A suffocating silence descended.

Zhang Wei was the first to regain his senses.

“Everyone, prepare for battle!”

“Monsters! Monsters have appeared!”

“Tanks!”

“Th-The regimental commander has been killed!”

The Public Security Armed Forces Hunters were the first to respond to the shouts. The soldiers grasped the situation a moment later.

Already thrown into utter chaos by their commander’s sudden death, what awaited them next was an even more gruesome end.

Whoooosh!

A single shriek tore through the air.

A streak of light, moving too fast to see, swept through the soldiers marching in orderly ranks.

Boom-boom-boom!

It burst dozens of people like balloons before finally piercing an armored vehicle moving under infantry escort.

The streak punched through its tough outer shell and buried itself deep inside. The sight made Zhang Wei bellow like thunder.

“Everyone, get clear!”

But before his warning could reach them, a deafening explosion swallowed everything.

KABOOM!

The armored vehicle erupted in a red flash and a cloud of black smoke. Hundreds—thousands—of fragments became blades that tore through everything around it.

Soldiers and lower-ranking Hunters who had failed to react in time collapsed like sheaves of straw. Too many to count fell where they stood.

Their lifeless bodies did not so much as twitch.

“…!”

“W-What the hell is this…?”

Zhang Wei roared at the people standing frozen like statues.

“Spread out! Everyone, spread out! Get as far away from the tanks and armored vehicles as possible!”

But the soldiers were experiencing real combat for the first time in their lives, and their bodies refused to move. Their still-unseen opponent did not let the confusion go to waste.

Whoooosh!

The sound of death rang out again.

This time, however, there was more than one streak of light.

Boom! Boom-boom-boom!

“Aaaaargh!”

Flames surged upward, and screams flooded the air.

The roughly twenty tanks and armored vehicles were disabled in the blink of an eye.

The three combat helicopters circling overhead also shattered into countless fragments and crashed to the ground.

Boom! Clatter, clatter.

Amid the horrific scene—a veritable mountain of corpses and sea of blood—Zhang Wei felt a chill run down his spine.

*At least A-rank monsters. And there’s more than one.*

Ironically, that was the most accurate judgment Zhang Wei made all day.

Ssssss.

Between dark alleys and collapsed buildings. Atop a building that was still burning.

Ten figures appeared like black mist, staring down at the hundreds of surviving humans with blazing violet eyes.

A groan escaped Zhang Wei’s lips when he spotted the figures mounted on warhorses stripped down to the bone.

“…Death Knights.”

Once noble, they were knights of death corrupted by dark magic.

The Death Knights that had vanished after the Great Cataclysm along with the Lich had reappeared.

Ten of them.

The first word that came to Zhang Wei’s mind was *death*.

*Is this where it ends?*

Death Knights were beings that surpassed A-rank monsters.

A hundred Hunters and part of the military force still remained, but Zhang Wei already knew.

There would be no escaping the net they had cast.

But…

“I have no intention of dying quietly.”

Zhang Wei was not alone in that thought.

Unlike the soldiers, who were so terrified they could barely hold their guns, the Public Security Armed Forces Hunters raised their weapons, their eyes filled with resolve.

“You don’t have to do this.”

One of the platoon commanders answered Zhang Wei’s quiet words with a blunt question.

“But you do?”

“I’m sorry. I never should have brought you here.”

“You didn’t bring us. We followed you. You’ve saved our lives more than ten times, Company Commander. I’d say it’s about time we faced something like this for you.”

He spoke as though it were nothing, but he could not hide the tremor in his voice.

Zhang Wei licked his parched lips as he stared at the ten motionless Death Knights.

“Fight with everything you have, and make sure at least one of you survives. That’s all I have to say.”

A thunderous cheer erupted in place of an answer. With their fear driven away, the Hunters’ eyes shone like morning stars.

If they had wanted safe, prosperous lives, they could have chosen another path.

But instead of becoming bodyguards for wealthy entrepreneurs or working as mercenaries, they had joined the Public Security Armed Forces out of honor and a sense of responsibility as Hunters.

This was a battle they could never retreat from.

“Forward! Sons and daughters of the people! Descendants of Zhonghua!”

Zhang Wei roared with all his strength and was about to charge the nearest Death Knight when—

Rumble-rumble-rumble!

It was pure instinct.

A tremendous force rolled over them with the violent tremors. Every hair on their bodies standing on end with terror, the surviving humans turned their heads.

Countless eyes fixed on a black knight slowly crossing the ruined downtown district.

Red eyes flashed beneath his low-set helmet. The breath of death poured from between his bloodless lips.

—Sar, Garosh.

Words in a language of the demon realm that none of them could understand.

And yet, in the next moment, Zhang Wei realized what they meant.

*Kill them all.*

The ten Death Knights charging from every direction were reflected in Zhang Wei’s wide eyes.

Slice!

The massacre began.

* * *

Splash.

A horse’s bony hoof stepped into a pool of blood.

The Death Knights approached the black knight as he gazed down at the corpse of Zhang Wei, the middle-aged Hunter who had resisted to the very end, and dropped to one knee.

—Lord. Your next command.

The next command.

After a moment of silence, the black knight suddenly extended a hand.

Whoosh—slice!

A black streak of light lashed out like a whip, cutting through concrete and rebar.

The building collapsed at an angle. Within the newly exposed interior, several humans crouched with their hands clamped tightly over their mouths.

“Jinjin. Honey. It’s okay. It’s okay…”

“Sniff… sob…”

“Dada?”

A human male and female.

And…

A being so infinitely small and light that it was impossible to distinguish whether it was male or female.

Perhaps that was what they called a child.

*Child. Child?*

Where had he heard that word before?

The black knight pushed the sudden question aside and extended his hand. Every human within sight had to be annihilated.

Those were the orders he had received.

But—

—…?

For some reason, his hand would not move.

They were weak creatures, so fragile that a snap of his fingers would reduce them to a handful of blood. Yet it was as though an invisible barrier surrounded them.

—What are you?

At the black knight’s eerie voice, the child squirming in its parents’ arms suddenly burst into a wail.

—This is…

The black knight abruptly raised his head and stared into the distance, then wheeled his horse around.

The Death Knights questioned their leader’s sudden action.

—Lord?

—We’re returning. Now. At once.

—Then we will take care of the humans—

—We’re returning. Now. At once.

That was all.

The ten Death Knights knelt in salute, then spurred their horses after their leader.

Just before they vanished like mist, the black knight’s red eyes settled on the three humans who had survived by a miracle, then moved away.

Whoooooosh.

A wind heavy with the smell of blood swept through the corpse-filled ruins.

[^1]: *Guanxi* refers to a network of personal connections and favors, especially one useful in politics or business.

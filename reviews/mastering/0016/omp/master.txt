# Chapter 16

“Whew. Finally done.”

Jin Wikyung set down his brush. Wipeng, seated beside the door as always, raised his head.

“You’re getting faster at handling your work. You’ve worked hard toda—”

Wipeng trailed off. He had just noticed the mountain of documents still piled on the table.

Come to think of it, it was only around noon. There was no way anyone could have finished that much work already.

*Did I hear him wrong?*

“Good. This should do…”

This time, it wasn’t a hallucination. Jin Wikyung’s face popped up above the pile of documents, proving he had heard correctly.

“Wipeng, come here. This is very important.”

His voice was so serious that Wipeng grew concerned.

*Has something happened?*

Perhaps the documents had revealed serious corruption. Or maybe the Elder Council, which was always watching for an opportunity, had caused some major incident.

*This is bad. We haven’t even finished dealing with the Mount Heng Sword Sect yet.*

A moment later, Wipeng accepted the document Jin Wikyung held out. His face twisted strangely.

“…What is this?”

“Can’t you tell? It’s a drawing.”

The alarming document Wipeng had imagined was nowhere to be seen. Instead, Jin Wikyung had handed him a single sheet of rice paper with a drawing on it.

“No, that’s not what I meant. Why would you suddenly…”

Jin Wikyung cut him off with a secretive smile.

“Look closely. It isn’t an ordinary drawing.”

*It isn’t an ordinary drawing?*

Wipeng’s eyes widened.

All manner of legends from across Murim flashed through his mind.

A Daoist who ascended to immortality after gazing upon a single painting. An absolute master who attained enlightenment after seeing a mural in an ancient cave!

Wipeng’s gaze roamed across the rice paper for a long while. Then his eyes suddenly trembled as if he had been struck by lightning.

“C-could this be…?”

“You noticed. That’s right.”

Jin Wikyung chuckled.

“I drew yesterday’s duel.”

“……”

“Lee Seogeun lying defeated, and Taekyung standing proud! The sky stretching above the young hero who will one day become the greatest under heaven!”

“……”

“I deliberately drew it from a low angle. What do you think? It’s good, isn’t it? Isn’t it?”

Wipeng’s hands trembled around the rice paper. He wanted to crumple it, tear it apart, cover it with a week’s worth of piss and shit, dry it thoroughly, and then set it on fire.

But—

“…It’s very well drawn.”

Wipeng was a rational man. He summoned the tremendous mental fortitude of a martial artist at the Peak realm.

Of course, he also possessed enough courage to reprimand a lord whose sanity was in serious doubt.

“With this much work piled up, how could you spend the entire morning drawing a single picture?”

“Of course I couldn’t.”

“And yet you—”

“Did you think I spent three hours working on just one?”

“Pardon?”

“Of course I drew another. I was going to show it to you later, but you really are quick on the uptake.”

Wipeng accepted the second sheet of rice paper with trembling hands. Grinning from ear to ear, Jin Wikyung began explaining the drawing.

“This one depicts the scene right after the duel. The young hero declares that he refuses to rest on his laurels and will return to the training hall! And the people gaze up at him in admiration!”

“Well, I do agree with much of that. The Third Young Master truly has changed a great deal.”

“Hasn’t he? I was surprised myself.”

After defeating Lee Seogeun in the duel, Jin Taekyung had defied everyone’s expectations and returned to the training hall. Jin Wikyung’s eyes grew distant as he recalled the previous day.

“I always knew this day would come. The youngest is a heavenly eagle. Wipeng, can’t you hear it? The mighty beating of Taekyung’s wings…”

“I can’t hear any wings, but I can hear you spouting nonsense.”

Wipeng lowered the rice paper with a sigh of complete resignation.

Flap.

“Gasp.”

“See? You heard it!”

Wipeng hurriedly turned toward the window. A hawk had just landed there and was preening its feathers. A small container was fastened to its ankle.

“It’s a messenger hawk.”

These hawks were rigorously trained from the time they were fledglings before being put into service.

The Jin Family of Taiyuan possessed only two, and they were dispatched solely for matters of extreme urgency.

“We have a problem.”

Jin Wikyung’s voice sank.

It soon became clear that he was right.

* * *

“My name is Han Yeop.”

“Pardon?”

“It’s an honor to meet you.”

The introduction came out of nowhere. Of course, I recognized his face.

I had seen him more often than anyone else since entering the training hall.

*Should I call him the training hall guard?*

A guard. A martial guard. Whatever the proper term was, the NPC in front of me was a Jin Family of Taiyuan martial artist assigned to the training hall. Bringing me meals and herbal decoctions was part of his job.

*But why is he suddenly introducing himself?*

I had never exchanged a single word with this NPC. He didn’t seem to harbor any ill will toward me, but he had never been particularly friendly, either.

“Ah, yes. Nice to meet you too.”

Despite my lukewarm response, the guard’s—or rather, Han Yeop’s—face lit up.

*What the hell? Why is he suddenly acting like this?*

“I was there yesterday as well.”

“There? Oh.”

He meant the duel. So many people had gathered that there was nothing surprising about Han Yeop being among them.

“I watched from beginning to end. I saw how heroically you fought against that vicious Lee Seogeun of the Mount Heng Sword Sect!”

*Vicious? Heroically?*

*Is that really how it looked?*

Honestly, from a modern man’s perspective, they were both pieces of shit.

No, I almost wanted to take Lee Seogeun’s side. As an older brother myself, no matter how fucking awful my little sister’s personality was, I’d lose my mind if she told me she was dating a bastard like Jin Taekyung.

Of course, the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s attitude and demands had been absurd. That was why I’d had no choice but to fight.

“My heart pounded the entire time. I’m sure everyone else there felt the same way.”

Han Yeop continued, his face flushed with excitement. He seemed to be under a serious misunderstanding, but I had no idea when I was supposed to stop him.

“I once misunderstood you too, Young Master. But now everyone in the family knows the truth.”

This time, I couldn’t help asking.

“The truth? What truth?”

“Well…”

Han Yeop lowered his voice to a breathless whisper. Never mind the hot breath against my ear—the actual words sent goose bumps down my skin.

In short—

“I’m the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s secret weapon?”

“Yes! Exactly!”

Han Yeop nodded furiously.

“Everything you’ve shown us until now was a disguise. Since childhood, you’ve endured grueling training and mastered both the literary and martial arts, all while cultivating virtue and righteousness. You are the Sl-Sleeping…”

I couldn’t bring myself to say the rest. Han Yeop stepped in before my hands and feet could curl up from embarrassment.

“The Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi! Everyone in the family knows now! You are Shanxi’s Sleeping Dragon, still concealed beneath the waters, waiting to ascend to the heavens!”

*Oh, please. Somebody save me. And stop shouting.*

The Sleeping Dragon? Everyone in the family knew?

*If I die, the cause of death will be embarrassment.*

As I writhed in agony, Han Yeop gazed at me with shining eyes.

“It’s true, isn’t it? I know it’s rude to ask, but just between us…”

This wouldn’t do. I resolved to stamp out this ridiculous, cringe-inducing rumor, no matter who had started it.

“I have no idea who spread such an absurd rumor, but…”

That was when it happened.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> - A rumor about the **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi** is spreading throughout the **Jin Family of Taiyuan**.
>
> - Fame has risen by 10 due to the influence of the rumor.
>
> - The more people believe the rumor, the more Fame will rise.

I continued with a solemn expression.

“Every word of it is true.”

“I knew it! I never doubted you!”

I watched Han Yeop walk away with rapture written across his face and shed a single tear.

*Fuck…*

*Ah, I miss my mom.*

* * *

My duel with Lee Seogeun had taught me several things.

First—

*I’m strong.*

Early in the game, I had knocked out the Heavenly Axe, the tutorial NPC, with a single blow. What had been mere suspicion back then was now a certainty.

I was strong. Strong enough to defeat a Level 30 like Lee Seogeun without much trouble. My greater combat experience had certainly played a part, but my basic stats were superior across the board.

*Strength, Stamina, Agility. We were either evenly matched, or I had a slight edge.*

Stats. The difference lay in our attributes. I was a player in this game, and I had continued to grow through the System.

Learning martial arts, training, and completing various Quests had rapidly increased my stats. The results had been plain to see during the duel.

Second—

*I don’t have enough internal energy.*

In terms of internal energy alone, Lee Seogeun had been ahead of me. Far ahead.

What the hell had he eaten growing up? I had smashed him dozens of times with the shaft of my spear and pounded him one-sidedly from the mount, yet he had endured it all. He had even counterattacked and drawn on more internal energy at the very end.

*I currently have ten years of internal energy.*

Lee Seogeun probably had twenty—twice as much as I did. That led me to a third realization.

*The difference in internal energy determines the stage of one’s martial arts.*

I had defeated Lee Seogeun, a First Rate martial artist. Yet the realm displayed by the System still listed me as Second Rate.

I suspected internal energy was the reason. Once I raised the one attribute I lacked, wouldn’t my realm finally advance too?

Once I had sorted out my thoughts, something else occurred to me.

*This is basically the same as Hunter rank classification.*

Every newly awakened Hunter had to visit a designated center to have their abilities, suitable profession, and mana capacity assessed. No matter how high their physical abilities were, anyone with insufficient mana received a frosty reception during their rank evaluation.

That was also why mages started at D-rank at the lowest. Thanks to the nature of their profession, mages began with plenty of mana.

*Still, the game is better than reality.*

At least I could grow here. Reality didn’t work that way. Even after busting my ass for seven years, all I had accomplished was getting lumped in with the E-ranks. I was still an F-rank Hunter.

Anyway, I had a rough plan now.

*My stats are high enough. I’ll cycle the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique whenever I have time and prioritize Quests that reward EXP.*

It had been over a week since I was trapped in this godforsaken game. I had no idea what the people in the real world were doing, but clearly, rescue wasn’t coming.

I had to prepare properly and see this through myself.

“Open Quest Window.”

Ding.

> **System**
>
> **Quest**
>
> **Logout**
>
> You must now make your way through this harsh Murim.
>
> Become stronger and more famous.
>
> For the day that will someday come…
>
> **Grade:** Main Quest  
> **Restriction:** Jin Taekyung  
> **Mission:** Achieve the **First Rate** realm (Incomplete)
>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Reach Level 30 (17 / 30)
>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Reach Fame 500 (70 / 500)
>
> **Reward:** **Logout**

“Damn, that’s brutal.”

I sat cross-legged. Two days remained before my seclusion ended. I intended to accumulate as much internal energy as possible.

> **System**
>
> - Fame has risen by 3 due to the influence of the **Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi** rumor.

The Fame-increase messages that chimed from time to time offered a small measure of comfort.

* * *

Late that night, the lights came on in the main assembly hall. At the Lesser Family Head’s request, a secret meeting of the family council had been convened.

The hour was late and the summons abrupt, leaving several senior members with sour expressions.

“A sudden summons? What could this be about?”

“Exactly. Calling us here at this hour without even telling us why…”

“The Lesser Family Head is still young. He has no understanding of procedure or etiquette.”

“He must be feeling rather triumphant after what happened yesterday. Though I suppose his only weakness has disappeared. No one expected the Third Young Master to be so capable.”

“That must have taken the wind out of the Elder Council’s sails. They were probably preparing to make a major move over the Third Young Master.”

“Hmph. Even now, the Head Elder ought to step forward and set the family straight.”

“Careful. Watch your tongue…”

At that moment, every voice fell silent.

The doors to the meeting hall opened, and Jin Wikyung entered.

Opinions of the Lesser Family Head varied among the senior members, but the silence that settled over the room the instant he appeared proved that he possessed the makings of a leader.

“Thank you all for answering my summons at this late hour.”

Jin Wikyung took the seat of honor and began to speak, but the next words refused to come.

*What should he say? Where—and how—should he begin?*

His head throbbed. But this was something they had to know.

“The reason I called everyone here tonight is…”

That was when a strange voice interrupted him.

“It must be because of the Mount Heng Sword Sect.”

The voice was uncanny. It sounded old, yet young at the same time. Rough and gentle, with an inexplicable resonance beneath it all.

*Could it be…?*

Jin Wikyung’s face tightened.

The doors to the meeting hall, which had seemed as though they would never open again, began to part.

Step. Step. Step.

Five old men glided into the room. The moment everyone recognized the man at the front, they hurriedly rose and bowed their heads.

“We pay our respects, Head Elder!”

The Head Elder.

After years of seclusion, he had finally appeared.

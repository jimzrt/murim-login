# Chapter 457

“Dongting Lake! A ship has sunk in Dongting Lake!”

“P-please, help us! People have fallen into the water!”

*Clang, clang—!*

Screams rang out in the distance, and the sharp peal of warning bells echoed through the darkness.

But another sound had reached my ears a moment earlier.

*Ding.*

*This is…*

A familiar notification.

A chill of foreboding ran down my spine. I stared at the translucent System window floating before my eyes.

> **System**
>
> - An unexpected Quest, **The Tragedy of Dongting Lake**, has been generated!
> - You cannot refuse the Quest!
>
> **Quest**
>
> **The Tragedy of Dongting Lake**
>
> Dongting Lake is one of the most celebrated scenic destinations under heaven.
>
> Poets and men of letters climb Yiyang Tower to enjoy its beautiful vistas and compose verses about nature, while those blessed with power and wealth launch large, splendid pleasure boats and revel for days and nights.
>
> But today, this clear, blue-green lake is filled with shattered wreckage and corpses.
>
> Unless someone steps forward, those still alive will meet the same fate!
>
> **Grade:** Peak
>
> **Restriction:** Jin Taekyung
>
> **Mission:** Rescue People (Incomplete)
>
> **Reward:** ???
>
> **Failure:** The deaths of the people
>
> *A considerable amount of time has already passed. You must rescue the people before it is too late!*

As I finished reading the final line of the Quest window, a small holographic window appeared in the air.

> **System**
>
> **Time Limit:** 45 minutes 36 seconds

Damn it. Less than an hour.

*If we waste even a moment… everyone will die.*

There wasn’t even time to wonder how such a catastrophe had happened. Someone’s life might depend on every minute—maybe even every second.

I drew as much internal energy as possible from my dantian and opened my mouth.

“From this moment on, run to Dongting Lake as if your lives depend on it. We save the people first.”

*Whoosh!*

No one answered. Their faces simply hardened as they accelerated again.

Using the distant cries and warning bells as our compass, we ran and ran.

By the time the first digit of the time limit changed from four to two, we could finally see it.

*Fwoom, roar!*

Dozens of vessels, large and small, burned across the broad waters, engulfed in flames as countless shouts rang out from every direction.

“Bring boats! Even a ferryboat will do, so hurry!”

“We don’t have enough boatmen!”

“More! Bring more people! Things have come to this, so where are the military vessels? What are they doing?”

“Ahhh! Ahhh!”

With every step, the scene came into sharper focus.

More than a hundred people raced frantically along the shore of Dongting Lake, while several small ferryboats moved slowly across the water.

*That won’t be enough.*

They were doing everything they could, but their efforts fell woefully short of what was needed to rescue so many people.

Even I, with martial arts that more than qualified me as a superhuman, felt my breath catch at the sight.

*How are we supposed to save them?*

I had heard plenty about the vastness of Dongting Lake, but seeing it for myself, I realized it was far larger than I had imagined.

There was no way the vessels would be packed close together on such a vast lake. Judging from the distances between them, every ship must have been out enjoying itself at least several hundred zhang from the others.[^1]

And this was only what I could see immediately. How many more boats and people were submerged beyond my sight?

*Damn it.*

I bit down hard on my lip and accelerated.

The murmurs grew louder as people spotted us racing toward them like the wind.

A military officer who appeared to belong to the local authorities cried out urgently.

“A-are you martial artists? Please, help—”

“No time for explanations. We’re borrowing some boats.”

“Pardon?”

Leaving the dumbfounded officer behind, I thundered an order.

“Gung Gibang, Hyuk Mujin! Smash the ferryboats!”

*Whoosh!*

There was no hesitation or doubt in either man’s movements.

Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin shot past me with forceful shouts, unleashing palm force and sword strikes.

*Slash, boom!*

The two ferries being hurriedly moved by the people were transformed into hundreds of pieces of wood in an instant.

The military officer, momentarily stunned by what had happened before he could even say anything, screamed,

“What in the world are you doing!”

Shocked cries erupted from every direction.

“T-the ferryboats…!”

“Those bastards are the villains who caused this disaster! They must be trying to keep us from rescuing the people!”

Shock and anger spread rapidly. All manner of curses poured in from every side, along with gazes brimming with killing intent.

But I didn’t care.

No—it would be more accurate to say I had no time to care.

> **System**
>
> **Time Limit:** 28 minutes 52 seconds

Barely thirty minutes remained.

Explaining myself now would only cost more lives.

Fortunately, there were three martial artists here who could understand my intentions and act on them.

“Cheongpung, Gung Gibang!”

“Got it!”

“Yes, Benefactor!”

Realizing what I meant, Gung Gibang and Cheongpung scooped up the ferryboat fragments and hurled them with all their strength toward the dark waters of Dongting Lake.

*Whoosh, splash!*

Dozens of wooden fragments sliced through the darkness and scattered across the lake.

“W-what!”

“Could this be…?”

A few people finally realized what we were doing, their eyes widening. But there still weren’t enough fragments.

Some might even sink before our feet reached them.

In that case…

*We’ll have to make the path as we go.*

The instant I reached that decision, I stamped down hard.

*Boom!*

The sand and dust piled along the shore surged into the air all at once, making the unprepared people cough.

Taking advantage of the moment when everyone’s vision was obscured, I reached out.

As my hand met damp, solid wood, I spoke the command only I could use.

*Store in Inventory.*

*Ding. Ding. Ding.*

> **System**
>
> - **Ferryboat Fragment** has been stored in your Inventory!
> - **Ferryboat Fragment** has been stored in your Inventory…

Notifications rang in my ears as the wooden fragments dissolved into thin air.

I left behind only enough fragments to avoid arousing suspicion, then shouted forcefully,

“Cheongpung and Gung Gibang, come with me! Hyuk Mujin, stay here and gather boats and people!”

“Y-yes, sir!”

Hyuk Mujin had been gasping for breath for some time now, and he barely managed to squeeze out the words. By then, Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, and I were already shooting toward the dark waters of Dongting Lake.

*Whoosh!*

Heat surged from my lower abdomen and flowed down into my legs.

Formidable internal energy flooded a body whose muscles and elasticity were so perfect they could be mistaken for a Heavenly Martial Physique.

*Now!*

*Boom!*

A massive shock wave burst from my toes, pressed down against the ground, and shoved back the rippling water. Sand and gravel scattered as dust, while a cool wind swept across my entire body.

If I reached out, I felt as though I could pluck the stars scattered across the night sky from the heavens.

But only for a moment.

Every leap was followed by a fall.

*Whoooosh!*

The black water, surging violently, rushed toward us.

My eyes, as keen as a wild animal’s, easily picked out a half-submerged piece of wood.

*Tap. Thump!*

My landing was light, but the second leap that followed was heavy.

Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, and I launched ourselves forward again.

Using the scattered ferryboat fragments as stepping stones, we leaped once, twice, three times…

Only after repeating the process several times did the vessel that had seemed so far away draw close enough to make out clearly.

But as the distance shrank, the foreboding in my heart grew.

*I can’t feel any life force.*

There were no survivors’ screams, no movement—nothing that should have been there if anyone was still alive.

And it did not take long for the foreboding I felt to become reality.

*Tap.*

Most of the vessel’s hull was already underwater.

On the vessel, most of whose hull was already submerged, Gung Gibang and Cheongpung landed on the bow and muttered like men groaning in pain.

“This is…”

“…Benefactor.”

Their voices sounded faint, as if they were coming from hundreds of zhang away.

In that suffocating silence, I stared blankly around me.

Everywhere I looked, countless corpses floated among the wreckage of shattered ships.

They were dead.

All of them.

A potbellied man who appeared to be a merchant, dressed in splendid silk. A gaunt, shabby-looking boatman. Musicians and courtesans who seemed to have been hired to liven up the festivities.

There were hundreds within sight alone.

No lively music drifted across the black water. No laughter.

All that remained was destruction and death—and the fear plainly etched into the faces of the dead.

*How could this have happened?*

For a moment, my mind went completely blank.

As if possessed, I raced across the water from fragment to fragment, using Qi Sense without restraint in search of even a single survivor.

But…

*Beep.*

> **System**
>
> - **Qi Sense** has failed.
> - The qi of the desired target could not be detected within range.

The System was more merciless than ever.

The translucent holographic window and grating failure notification told me that there was not a single survivor nearby.

“What in the world…”

I muttered in despair—and then a thought flashed through my mind like lightning.

*Wait. The System?*

I hurriedly raised my head and stared into the air.

The translucent holographic window was still there.

> **System**
>
> **Time Limit:** 9 minutes 43 seconds

“……!”

It felt as if someone had struck me hard in the back of the head.

If there truly were no survivors, the Quest should have been canceled or a failure notification should have appeared. The System was faster and more direct than anything.

But the Quest was still in progress, and the System window displaying the time limit hadn’t disappeared.

That could only mean one thing.

As I stood there, frozen stiff, Gung Gibang spoke to me in a cautious voice.

“Unfortunately, it seems pointless to keep searching for survivors…”

“There are survivors.”

“What?”

“We just can’t see them. There are survivors somewhere much farther away. I’m sure of it.”

Gung Gibang had been about to say something, but his expression hardened when he saw the certainty on my face.

Cheongpung spoke in a voice more serious than usual.

“If what you say is true, Benefactor…”

“Then we find them and save them. No matter what it takes.”

Gung Gibang stared at me for a moment, then nodded.

“We’ll have to split up. If we tear apart the sunken boats around here and use the pieces, we might be able to keep going for another half a shichen.[^2]”

“You have fifteen minutes. Search as far as you can before then. If you don’t find any survivors, come back.”

The time limit showed how much time the survivors had left. Searching beyond that would be meaningless.

My unusually firm tone puzzled them, but they soon accepted the order without another word.

“We’ll do that.”

“Fifteen minutes. I’ll remember, Benefactor.”

“Then we split up and each take a different direction. See you soon.”

*Thump, whoosh!*

With that, I kicked off the bow and leaped.

Watching the time limit dwindle by the second, I pushed my internal energy and senses to their limits and raced on and on across Dongting Lake, vast as the open sea.

> **System**
>
> **Time Limit:** 1 minute 12 seconds

*Am I too late?*

And just as I was about to give up on everything—

“P-please, save me…”

A tiny, faint voice reached my ears.

[^1]: A **zhang** is a traditional Chinese unit of distance, roughly 3.3 meters.

[^2]: A **shichen** is a traditional time unit of roughly two hours.

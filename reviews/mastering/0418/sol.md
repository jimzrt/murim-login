# Chapter 418

Sometimes, when I took someone’s life—when I faced the desperate voices and pleading eyes of enemies begging to be spared—I found myself wondering.

*Why? Why on earth?*

Why didn’t they stop to think about what they themselves had done? Why did they always look to others for the cause of everything that befell them?

*Too late.*

If you tried to kill someone, you had to consider the possibility that you might be killed instead.

The seeds had already been sown. At last, it was time to reap the harvest.

I stared into Wu Heixing’s bloodshot eyes and whispered,

“Why should I take that kind of risk?”

“…!”

I hadn’t asked because I wanted an answer.

There was nothing more to hear or see. Without hesitation, I grabbed Wu Heixing by the neck and twisted.

*Crunch.*

Flesh crumpled and bone splintered beneath a grip strong enough to tear steel like paper.

The last light faded from his wide, frozen eyes.

The instant his struggling body went limp, a System notification rang out.

> **System**
>
> - Defeated **Lv. 135 Wu Heixing**!
> - Gained a substantial amount of **EXP**!
> - Gained no **Fame**. If it becomes known that you killed this person, significant repercussions are expected!

That was when Lee Jungryong rose to his feet more than twenty meters away and spoke.

“Not even a moment’s hesitation.”

I tossed Wu Heixing’s lifeless body aside.

“He needed to die.”

“Do you know who that boy’s father is?”

“I’ve heard of him. Apparently, he was one of the men who took a sledgehammer to Confucius’s tomb decades ago.”[^1]

“And that young Red Guard grew up to become the most powerful man in Chinese politics and the leader of the Crown Prince Party. Even Chairman Xiao Yang does not dare touch him.”

“Damn. What a fucking amazing continent. In our country, that’d be like the arsonist who burned down Sungnyemun becoming President. Don’t you think?”

“There is no need to understand it. But there is one fact you must know. With your own hands, you killed the only son that the most powerful man among one billion people had after turning fifty.”

“Me? No, I didn’t.”

I smiled shamelessly.

“Wu Heixing died after a fierce battle with the Arch Lich.”

“…!”

“I told you, I already picked out his epitaph. *Here lies a bad chink who became a good Chinese man.* Of course, your gravestone will be right beside his.”

Lee Jungryong stared at me with an inscrutable expression, then let out a quiet laugh.

“You’re a crafty one.”

“I liked the scenario you two came up with. Let me borrow it.”

“Do as you please. It will not go the way you wish regardless.”

“Maybe…”

I gestured toward Wu Heixing’s sprawled corpse with my chin.

“So far, things seem to have gone more or less my way.”

“Yes. In that respect, you and I are alike.”

“What?”

As I frowned, Lee Jungryong gave me a faint smile.

“Wu Heixing was easily swayed and loose-lipped. A terrible man to conspire with, but even someone like him had his uses.”

“…What the fuck are you talking about?”

“This is what I mean.”

Lee Jungryong pressed something in his hand. A beam pierced the thick fog and struck a chunk of concrete, projecting a hologram above it.

The short video, barely ten seconds long, showed Wu Heixing begging for his life—and me breaking his neck without a moment’s hesitation.

“Huh.”

I let out a hollow laugh.

“Nice image quality. Perfect for giving people the wrong idea.”

“Indeed. Especially an old father who has lost his only son, born late in life.”

“Planning to show him the unedited version?”

“Perhaps due to magical interference, a malfunction will cause the beginning of the recording to disappear.”

“That’s one hell of a coincidence.”

“Anyone who sees it will be appalled. Not only those who delight in tearing others down, but even those who worshipped you will turn against you. And the people you cherish so dearly will slowly crumble beneath the public’s condemnation.”

The Peace Guild.

Mom and Hayeon.

Their names and faces flashed before my eyes the moment I heard Lee Jungryong’s words.

I stepped forward, planting my foot on Wu Heixing’s corpse.

*Step.*

“Was this your plan from the beginning?”

“Wu Heixing was merely a tool from the start. He could never have been anything more.”

*Step.*

Lee Jungryong began walking toward me as well.

But the qi pouring from his entire body was on a completely different level from anything he had shown before.

That was why Wu Heixing’s death had failed to disturb his composure.

The cunning beast had achieved its objective. Now, at last, it had bared the claws it had kept hidden.

“Damn it. No wonder it seemed so easy.”

Lee Jungryong gave a dry laugh at my complaint.

“I will praise you for one thing. You far exceeded my expectations.”

“Let me ask you one last time. Why are you going this far?”

“Could a young pup like you possibly understand?”

*Step.*

“Were you really that afraid of Team Leader Choi—no, Choi Minwoo?”

“Will you not shut that mouth of yours?”

*Kuwaaaaaaaang!*

A massive wave of qi surged outward, driving back the fog and seizing control of the space around us.

The razor-sharp killing intent made my skin prickle. Anger unlike anything I had ever seen rose in Lee Jungryong’s once-placid eyes.

“I won my place entirely through my own strength. It is my empire, built over the course of my entire life!”

It was anger he had kept buried, an emotion he had not allowed himself to show in a very long time.

Lee Jungryong glared at me with fire blazing in his eyes and ground out each syllable.

“No one can bring it down. Not you, not that boy Minwoo, and… not even my hyung!”

*Hyung?*

I was given no time to question the unexpected word.

*Step.*

A single step rang out, unusually heavy and deep.

At the same time, Lee Jungryong’s form rippled like water.

The beast vanished, leaving behind an afterimage, and appeared right in front of me.

*Fweeeeeeeeng!*

A mass of light filled my vision. I twisted at the waist and swung White Flame.

The instant light and flame collided, a thunderous roar erupted as though the sky itself had split apart.

*Kwaaang!*

* * *

The explosion was enormous.

A high-rise that had barely remained standing collapsed as though it had burst apart. Glass and concrete shattered into countless tiny fragments that shot in every direction.

The impact of the clash could likely be felt from dozens of kilometers away.

Amid the successive collapses and thunderous booms, I launched myself forward.

*Fwish!*

Beyond the dust cloud splitting apart before me, a man stood poised in midair as though on solid ground.

His clear eyes, always impossible to read, now blazed with fury.

“Jin Taekyung!”

Neither of us waited for the other.

I shattered the ground as I sprang upward, while he stepped on empty air and plunged toward me.

The sword and spear we swung at each other collided dead-on, and an immense shock wave engulfed us both.

Hurled backward at the same instant as though we had planned it, we kicked off the structures of the already-ruined city and shot toward each other again.

*Shweeeeeek—Kwang!*

With a thunderous boom, Lee Jungryong’s sword deflected my spearhead and thrust straight toward my throat.

*Boom!*

I jerked my head aside. Air shrieked past, and severed strands of hair scattered behind me.

But it wasn’t over.

*Shwish-shwish-shwish!*

Lee Jungryong’s sword shot forward as a streak of pure white light.

A net of Force streamed from the blade.

Faced with that spectacle of light filling my vision, I awakened all the internal energy sleeping within my dantian.

Three jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi rose like wildfire and surged through every limb and meridian. Blue hellfire blazed along the transparent spearhead, rippling smoothly like a dragon’s tail.

*Fire Dragon’s Single Tail.*

*Whoooooosh!*

A single swing.

The fire dragon’s tail fanned outward, erasing part of the onrushing net of Force.

Now it was time to tear the loosened net apart with sharpened claws.

*Heavenly Strike.*

The Fire Dragon Divine Spear consisted of only two forms.

Yet the ancient Sect Leader of the Fire Gate Clan who created it had earned the title of the greatest spearman under heaven. In the final moments of his life, he left behind a brief testament concerning his martial art.

> “A martial art I devoted my entire life to, yet failed to complete. Even so, a spear art worthy of being called the greatest under heaven.”

His words were true.

That was the Fire Dragon Divine Spear. A spear art that could proudly call itself the greatest under heaven with only two forms, and…

*Kuwaaaaaang!*

Heavenly Strike, unleashed with all my power, tore through the net of Force and swept across everything ahead of it.

The flames were upon Lee Jungryong before he had any chance to react. Watching them rush toward him, he brought his sword down with a forceful shout.

“Ha!”

*Fwish!*

The flames split at the tip of his sword.

His form shot forward like an arrow through the paths of fire opening to either side, unleashing countless sword strikes.

*Shu-shu-shu-shu-shuk!*

Chest, throat, shoulder, arms, legs…

Within a span of time short enough to be called an instant—

Faced with arcs of swordlight slashing toward me like rays of light, I was suddenly seized by wonder.

*How? How is this possible?*

I was a Hunter. At the same time, I was a martial artist.

No. Perhaps *Player* was the name that suited me best.

On a sweltering summer day, I had picked up a discarded VR capsule at a recycling station and gained a new power called the System. I had become the only person to stand astride both the modern world and Murim.

But still…

*Boom!*

Not a single wasted movement. Near-perfect control over his strength. Even his manipulation of qi was flawless.

After parrying Lee Jungryong’s attack, I found myself admiring him. It had nothing to do with what kind of person he was. I was simply stunned by his skill.

*So he was this strong.*

After reaching the Supreme Peak realm, I had thought there could not be many people in the modern world capable of matching me.

After coming to Sichuan and meeting several S-rank Hunters, that belief had only grown stronger.

No matter how much energy each of them possessed, martial arts did not exist here.

Hunters might fight efficiently, but when it came to using qi efficiently, they were practically illiterate.

Lee Jungryong? I knew he had learned what the modern world called a mana cultivation method, but I had assumed he would not be much different.

Now I realized the truth.

My arrogance had led me to the wrong conclusion.

*Slash!*

My thigh suddenly felt cool. A heartbeat later, pain as hot as fire followed.

But why did my mind clear and my vision sharpen, as though I had just awakened from a long sleep?

*Slash, slash, slash!*

My arm. My calf. The back of my neck.

Blood sprayed out, and the qi carried through his Force disrupted the flow of my internal energy.

As I staggered, I looked at Lee Jungryong’s face while he continued raining sword strikes down upon me.

His forehead glistened with sweat. His eyes held the certainty of victory, and a smile hung at the corners of his mouth.

*Ah. I don’t like this.*

I didn’t like that smile. I didn’t like the fact that a man like him possessed this much power.

Something surged inside me, and I swung White Flame.

*Kwaang!*

The first exchange.

Blood flowed from the cuts inflicted earlier, and pain spread through me. Without a word, I thrust my spear forward again.

Lee Jungryong, who had retreated a step, met me with a calm smile.

*Boom!*

The second exchange.

Lee Jungryong retreated two steps and stared at me in surprise.

*He still had that much strength left?*

The thought was written plainly across his face.

*Of course I do.*

I muttered inwardly, adjusted my grip on the spear, ignored the faint pain spreading through me, and stepped forward.

*Boom! Boom! Kwaang!*

Three times. Four times. Five times.

Each time spear and sword collided and thunderous roars erupted, Lee Jungryong’s expression hardened further.

The smile at the corners of his mouth had vanished long ago.

“You bastard…!”

Hearing that growling shout instead of his usual voice, soft as a cat’s paw, made me laugh for some reason.

Intoxicated by my own strength, I had underestimated his skill and made a foolish mistake.

But Lee Jungryong had done the same.

The claws he had bared were certainly strong and sharp, but they were not enough to kill me.

“Jungryong.”

I drew a deep breath, and stale air and a foul stench flooded my lungs.

Even so, I felt refreshed, as though something inside me had finally opened wide.

Yes. Now I understood.

“Let’s clip those claws.”

Lee Jungryong would die here today.

[^1]: The reference is to the Red Guards’ attack on Confucius’s tomb during the Cultural Revolution.

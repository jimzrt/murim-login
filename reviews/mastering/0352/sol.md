# Chapter 352

*They’re coming.*

The Green Shadow Squad martial artist’s disjointed words told Tang Sadok everything he needed to know.

Insight struck like a flash of lightning. The words gave shape to the unease that had been lurking in his heart, confirming the conclusion drawn from all his years of experience.

*The murderer isn’t alone!*

The one-armed man who had killed his father and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun was coming to the Sichuan Tang Clan with his subordinates.

Who the true mastermind was, how many men they had, and how they had managed to move through Sichuan as freely as their own backyard—none of that mattered now.

The next moment, Tang Sadok’s voice erupted like a thunderbolt.

“One Origin, Three Divisions, and Five Squads—prepare to meet the enemy!”

Rumble!

The Family Head’s Hall trembled beneath the tremendous shout, laden with powerful internal energy.

The first to react to the sudden disturbance were Tang Sadok’s guards, stationed around the hall.

“Family Head!”

Bang!

By the time they came crashing inside, clan members who had heard Tang Sadok’s shout were already carrying his orders throughout the compound.

Within fifteen minutes, hundreds of Tang Family members, united by venomous resolve and resentment, would assemble.

And then…

*I’ll show you what the Tang Family’s revenge looks like.*

Despite the fury boiling in his chest, Tang Sadok’s eyes were as cold as a bitter northern wind.

“Family Head, what in the world is—”

“The men who deserve to die are coming. That is all.”

He waved away the bewildered guards and fixed his gaze on the Green Shadow Squad martial artist.

The man’s uniform was torn and soaked in blood, unmistakable evidence of battle.

“What happened? Tell me everything you saw and heard. Leave nothing out.”

The Green Shadow Squad martial artist finally caught his breath and began his report in an exhausted voice.

“It began when we discovered a suspicious group while searching near the Golden Hall.”

“A suspicious group?”

“Yes.”

“The Golden Hall is barely half a day from our family. The Beggars’ Sect and the government troops have already completed every inspection and search in the area. How could…”

Tang Sadok trailed off, his gaze turning grim.

“…Government troops. They were dressed as government troops.”

The Green Shadow Squad martial artist stared wide-eyed at the Family Head, then nodded.

“Y-yes! They were definitely wearing government uniforms.”

“A disguise.”

Tap. Tap.

Tang Sadok drummed his bony fingers against the table.

They had been caught completely off guard.

The Beggars’ Sect and the government troops had confirmed Chengdu’s safety several times, and it had been only two days since they expanded their search area.

The enemy had slipped into Chengdu through the gap left when the net loosened.

*It wouldn’t have been difficult.*

The elite were always deployed to the front lines. The most experienced and sharp-eyed members of the Beggars’ Sect and government troops had already left Chengdu.

Besides, several thousand soldiers had been roaming the city for nearly ten days. Anyone dressed in their uniforms could have blended in with ease.

“How many were there?”

“Perhaps to avoid suspicion, they were traveling in several groups. But from what we could determine, there were at least three hundred.”

“Three hundred?”

“Yes. I’m certain.”

The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion, who had been listening with an anxious expression, suddenly spoke in a triumphant tone.

“Ha! Only three hundred? They must have a death wish. Isn’t that right, Uncle?”

“…”

“Our family alone has more than three hundred martial artists ready to fight at a moment’s notice. And that isn’t all. Isn’t this exactly why we built an impregnable fortress? They won’t even make it over the walls before our poison and hidden weapons kill them.”

Tang Sadok finally opened his mouth.

“Be quiet.”

“Pardon?”

“I said shut up.”

“U-Uncle, why are you acting like this? It’s only three hundred. We could wipe out that many in the blink of an eye…”

“Only three hundred?”

The fingers drumming against the table stopped dead.

Tang Sadok stared at his only nephew with utter contempt.

“You haven’t forgotten the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath, have you?”

“…How could I?”

The Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath was practically taboo within the Tang Clan.

Fifty years ago, the Sichuan Tang Clan had been forced to abandon the land it had occupied for centuries and flee in disgrace.

That day, Tang Sadok had lost his only older brother, and the Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion had lost his father before he was even born.

“But things are different now!”

Tang Sadok answered his nephew’s impassioned shout in a cold voice.

“Yes. Exactly what is different?”

“The Demonic Cult’s forces numbered two thousand, while our family had only three hundred martial artists!”

“You’re more or less correct so far. Go on.”

“Although we were defeated and forced to retreat, our family suffered barely more than a hundred casualties, while the Demonic Cult lost over a third of its forces.”

“That is also true. Those losses delayed the Demonic Cult’s conquest of Sichuan. Our family lost its home, but gained even greater fame in exchange.”

“Then why?”

Bang!

The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion slammed his fist down, denting the iron table. He glared at his uncle with blazing eyes.

“Why are you afraid of a mere three hundred enemies? Fifty years ago, even the Demonic Cult’s vicious fiends—several times more numerous than this—had to shed untold blood to overcome our family!”

His ringing shout shook the air inside the hall.

Tang Sadok’s answer to his furious nephew was short and simple.

“So? Is that all?”

“…What?”

“You pathetic fool.”

Tang Sadok sighed as his nephew’s eyes wavered.

“The Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath is a humiliating chapter of our history that must never be forgotten. But it is also the day our family proved its strength to the entire world.”

“Y-yes. That is why martial artists throughout the world still praise our family’s martial might and righteous spirit.”

“Ha… Hahahaha!”

Ignorance this profound was practically a disease. Tang Sadok let out a hollow laugh as he looked at his nephew, who still failed to understand.

“Yes. The entire world knows. Do you think our enemies don’t?”

“……!”

“After the Great Faction War, our family built an impregnable fortress that no one could breach. We increased our numbers and armed every clan member. Is there anyone under heaven who doesn’t know that? Is there anyone who doesn’t know the strength and determination our family displayed during the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath?”

The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion turned pale beneath the storm of Tang Sadok’s fury.

Only now did he understand. Anyone who knew the strength of the Sichuan Tang Clan and still dared to invade with three hundred men could not be dismissed as *only* three hundred.

The old Family Head had reached that conclusion without hesitation.

“Sound the Tang War Drum.”

A stir passed through the guards, who had been standing frozen like statues.

“Y-you mean the Tang War Drum?”

“Distribute poison and hidden weapons to everyone, regardless of age or sex. Retired elders, craftsmen who have never learned martial arts, women—no one is exempt. Our family will commit its full strength to this battle.”

“A-as you command!”

The blade-sharp order wiped the color from the guards’ faces. They hurried into motion.

When the Tang War Drum thundered through the Sichuan Tang Clan, everyone would understand.

Their family stood on the brink of destruction.

And no one felt that danger more keenly than Tang Sadok.

*This is dangerous.*

The experience and instincts he had honed over years of leading the family whispered a warning.

A sea of corpses and blood surpassing even the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath might be waiting for the Sichuan Tang Clan.

Then came the sound that announced the beginning of everything.

Boom. Boom. Boom!

The majestic sound alone was enough to set hearts pounding. The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion murmured,

“So this is the Tang Clan drum…”

The Tang Clan drum had not sounded since before he was born. He was still struggling to contain his emotions upon hearing it for the first time when Tang Sadok spoke.

“They beat us to it.”

“U-Uncle?”

The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion spun toward him and saw the fathomless depths of Tang Sadok’s eyes.

At the same moment, flickering figures raced into the hall with swift movement techniques.

“Family Head!”

“It’s the enemy! They’re coming while beating a war drum!”

“They launch an attack while sounding their own war drum. Impressive bastards. Truly impressive.”

Tang Sadok burst into laughter.

The ten people staring at him looked dumbfounded.

“This is no time to laugh!”

“Family Head, please give us your orders!”

They varied widely in appearance. One was a young man who seemed to be in his thirties, another was a graying middle-aged man, and there was even an old man around Tang Sadok’s age.

Whatever their ages, each had proven their martial prowess among the clan’s hundreds of members.

That was why Tang Sadok had given them another name.

“The Tang Clan’s Ten Wonders. Perfect timing. I was waiting for you.”

“Family Head, what in the world is happening? We should attack them at once—”

“You can ask that fellow over there for the details.”

The gazes of the Tang Clan’s Ten Wonders followed Tang Sadok’s.

Finding himself under the scrutiny of so many powerful figures, the Green Shadow Squad martial artist swallowed hard.

“I-I’ll tell you anything I know!”

“That is good to hear. Then let this old man ask you…”

Tang Sadok continued in a gentle voice.

“Weren’t you one-armed?”

“……!”

“……!”

At that moment, time seemed to stop.

The majestic thunder of the Tang War Drum, which had finally begun to sound. The frantic movements and shouts of the clan members.

Time continued to flow outside, but everything within the hall had frozen in place.

Amid the silence and shock, only one man moved backward through time.

Crack. Crrrck.

Bones lengthened and contracted. Muscles swelled.

The blood-soaked uniform tore apart, splitting the two characters embroidered across its chest—*Tang Family*—in half.

The Green Shadow Squad martial artist slowly straightened.

He was no longer a young man in his twenties.

Nor had he ever been a member of the Green Shadow Squad.

“You have a sharp eye. The Poison King raised his son well.”

The middle-aged man smiled and rubbed his left arm. The skin around his elbow was a subtly different color, as though the arm had been torn off and reattached.

“And to answer your question… I honestly don’t know myself. Let’s just call it demonic martial arts for convenience.”

A green light flickered in Tang Sadok’s eyes as he stared at the middle-aged man.

“So it was you. You killed my father.”

“Poison King Tang Taesang truly lived up to his reputation. I was honestly impressed. That was when I lost my arm, too.”

The middle-aged man clenched and unclenched his left fist, making a show of frowning.

“The sensation still isn’t quite what it used to be. I wonder if I’ll ever fully recover…”

“Do not worry. This old man will personally cut off your head so you never have to concern yourself with it again.”

“We’ll lend a hand as well.”

Rumble!

The tremendous qi surging from Tang Sadok and the Tang Clan’s Ten Wonders shook the hall.

Yet even before a deadly poison capable of killing with a single breath, the middle-aged man—the Western Heaven Demon Lord—smiled calmly.

“If you can, by all means.”

Roar!

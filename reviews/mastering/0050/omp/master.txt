# Chapter 50

My mouth fell open at the sight before me.

Beyond the dense forest, wetlands stretched endlessly beneath the hot, humid air.

“This is a D-rank Gate…”

I knew higher-ranked Gates contained larger spaces. I just hadn’t realized the difference would be this dramatic.

“Big, isn’t it?”

I forgot all about being angry.

“Yeah. Big enough to be daunting.”

“This is about average. Starting with B-rank Gates, they’re so large you have to hire a guide.”

“…Feels like we need one here, too.”

“We’ll be fine today.”

Team Leader Choi pulled out a laminated sheet and showed it to me.

“What’s that?”

“A map. They gave it to me when the rights were transferred.”

A map. Then we wouldn’t have to worry about getting lost—

“Wait. What did you just say?”

“I said it was a map.”

“No, not that. The part after that.”

“You mean the transfer of rights?”

Yeah, that, you bastard.

“What does that mean?”

“Exactly what it sounds like.”

Team Leader Choi spoke as if it were nothing.

“This Gate is mine.”

“What?”

“More precisely, I hold the exclusive rights to it.”

Maybe it was the wetlands. Sweat beaded on my palms, and my throat went bone-dry.

I swallowed.

*Exclusive rights to a Gate?*

In Korea, Gates were national property.

Since the Great Cataclysm, Magic Gems had become a core energy source, and Gates were essentially diamond mines that never ran dry. Yet this guy held the exclusive rights to one.

*Who the hell is he?*

I knew he was a rich family’s young master, but I hadn’t realized he was this rich.

“Let’s get moving.”

Team Leader Choi strode ahead. Even the sight of his back was dazzling.

Ah, that golden radiance. That untouchable scent of wealth.

*I’ll bury my bones in your Guild.*

I swore it then and there.

* * *

The wetlands were a maze.

Without a capable guide like Team Leader Choi, I would have wandered around for ages. I had no idea how long we’d been walking when—

- Keeiik.

The cry became even clearer as I drew up my internal energy.

Monsters.

*At least twenty.*

According to Team Leader Choi’s explanation on the way here, the monsters inhabiting this Gate were Swamp Lizardmen.

They lived in several colonies under a single tribe, and this appeared to be one of them.

“This is the small colony closest to the entrance. There should be about twenty, so let’s take them out quickly and move on.”

Team Leader Choi advanced, hacking through the vines. He was usually a pretty-faced young master, but once actual combat began, he was no joke. He plowed forward like an eight-ton truck with a broken steering wheel, practically radiating machismo.

*He’s fucking cool.*

I felt like Team Leader Choi and I could sweep aside not just thirty Lizardmen, but a hundred.

Now I understood why people got high-level players to carry them in online games.

“Keiik!”

Maybe that was why my first face-to-face encounter with Lizardmen didn’t make me particularly nervous.

I was more fascinated by finally seeing monsters I’d only ever seen in photos and videos.

“Whoa, they’re big.”

Even the smallest of the twenty stood a head taller than me.

They were at least two meters tall, with lean muscles and thick tails that made them look even larger.

*Still not as big as the Hobgoblin Great Warrior I fought yesterday, though.*

These hideous basketball prospects didn’t seem too pleased to see us.

- Keiik!

- Keet, keet!

The harpoons in their hands flashed. I watched them approach from the front, spreading out to surround us.

“No archers.”

“Swamp Lizardmen mostly use harpoons. Be careful in case they throw them.”

“Yep.”

He really was a lizard expert. He even stood there with his arms casually folded.

“Keiik!”

They were barely twenty meters away now. I grinned at Team Leader Choi.

“These guys have no fear.”

“That’s a Lizardman trait. They’re aggressive and prefer a head-on fight.”

“Aha.”

In the meantime, the distance had narrowed to ten meters. Team Leader Choi’s folded arms were beginning to bother me.

“I think it’s about time we started fighting.”

“I suppose it is.”

“…Right. We should.”

Whoosh!

Three or four harpoons tore through the air. If I’d still been an F-rank Hunter, my life would have flashed before my eyes. Now, I simply swung my spear and knocked them aside.

Clang, clang, clang!

Only then did I notice something strange.

- Kieeeik!

- Keet! Keiik!

*What’s wrong with them?*

Their eyes rolled back until only the whites showed, and they shrieked wildly. This went beyond hostility. It was practically hatred.

As I stared in confusion, Team Leader Choi casually supplied the answer.

“It’s because of your equipment.”

Equipment? What about my—

Ah.

*Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set. Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon.*

*Shit. Just look at those names.*

If I were a Lizardman, I’d throw a harpoon at me too.

*Come to think of it, they only threw them at me.*

With the enemy of their entire race standing right in front of them, they were treating Team Leader Choi like he was invisible.

At this rate, I was going to be the only one getting attacked—

No, wait.

“Did you plan this?”

“Plan what?”

“You dressed me in this so I’d pull aggro while you mopped them up. That was your strategy all along, wasn’t it?”

“I swear it wasn’t.”

Team Leader Choi’s expression turned solemn.

“I’m going to stand back and do nothing.”

“What?”

“You’ll pull the aggro, and you’ll handle the raid. Don’t get the wrong idea.”

*What the hell is this bullshit?*

“So… you’re telling me to fight them alone?”

“Yes.”

“While you stand there with your arms folded and watch?”

“I’ll unfold my arms.”

“No, for fuck’s sake…”

“Harpoons incoming.”

Whoosh!

Harpoons flew as green scales flashed from every direction. The Lizardmen charged.

Team Leader Choi sprang back and shouted gravely,

“You got this!”

Was this guy completely insane?

I wanted to run over and grab him by the collar, but harpoons were already flying in from every direction.

Clang!

- Keiik!

“You bastards.”

Grinding my teeth, I drew up my Qi Sense. Twenty windows popped into view above their heads.

> **System**
>
> **Lv. 40 Lizardman Tribesman**

Level 40, huh?

I tightened my grip on my spear.

“You’re all dead.”

Ding.

> **System**
>
> - Damage dealt to this monster increases by 10% due to the effect of **Lizardman Hunter’s Leather Set**.
>
> - Damage dealt to this monster increases by 20% due to the effect of **Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon**.
>
> - All **Lizardmen** in this Gate are hostile toward you. They will never stop until they have avenged this grudge!

At the same time, the Lizardman Slayer’s Harpoon swept through a heavy arc. The forms of the Jin Family’s Spear Technique, now at Seven Stars, poured down upon them.

Crunch!

* * *

- Keiik…

The yellow reptilian eyes slowly closed.

The green wetlands were awash with corpses and blood. Not a single Lizardman remained standing.

Ding.

> **System**
>
> - Level Up!
>
> - Level Up!

Leaving the cheerful System notifications behind, I turned around.

Team Leader Choi was leaning against a tree. He’d said he would unfold his arms, yet there he stood with them folded again. That made me twice as pissed.

“What the hell was that?”

Team Leader Choi silently studied me, then tossed something over.

It was a small drink bottle filled with red liquid—a potion that restored depleted Stamina.

“So you give me the disease, then offer me the medicine?”

“You look remarkably healthy for a sick man. You don’t have so much as a scratch.”

…That actually made some sense. I almost let him convince me.

While I hesitated, Team Leader Choi spoke.

“I’m sorry.”

He didn’t stop there. He bowed at the waist.

The apology was so formal that it left me with nothing to say. My anger eased, but I still wanted an explanation.

“Why did you do it?”

“I needed to see what you could do.”

“Just for that?”

“It’s important to me. I need to know how you move, how much you can handle, and how far we can go together.”

“So you sat back while I was surrounded by monsters?”

“If I hadn’t trusted you, I wouldn’t have done it.”

I had to admit, being praised by someone as capable as him felt good.

*More importantly, I wasn’t hurt, and I leveled up twice.*

Trying not to sound too eager, I asked,

“So what’s the verdict?”

“Hmm.”

Team Leader Choi studied me with that peculiar look of his. At last, he spoke.

“I still don’t know.”

“What?”

“Which is why…”

Team Leader Choi pulled something out and tossed it to me.

It was a long, opaque cylinder. When I shook it, liquid sloshed inside.

“What is this, a potion?”

“There’s a button on the bottom. Press it.”

*Does that open it?*

I tilted my head and pressed the button.

Click.

The front of the cylinder sprang open.

The problem was—

Fwoooosh—

Boom!

Whatever had been inside shot upward like a firework and burst in midair. Pink liquid sprayed across a wide area.

“Huh?”

*What was that?*

As I stared blankly, Team Leader Choi’s voice reached my ear.

“Pheromones.”

“Pheromones? Like perfume?”

“Something like that.”

“…?”

“They were extracted from a female Lizardman.”

The instant he finished speaking, I felt a tremor.

It was the ground.

The ground was rumbling.

Team Leader Choi helpfully added,

“They’re extremely potent.”

*You bastard…*

* * *

“Team Leader Three.”

Those were the first words the half-gray-haired, middle-aged man had spoken in an hour. Kim Sangshik, who had been waiting endlessly while the Guild Master chain-smoked, answered at once.

“Yes, Guild Master.”

“You’re curious, aren’t you? Wondering what kind of shit I called you in first thing this morning to raise hell about?”

“Ah, no, sir.”

The Sopung Guild Master smiled good-naturedly.

“You’re right.”

“What?”

“I called you in to raise hell.”

“…”

“Do you know where I went today?”

The Guild Master exhaled a plume of cigarette smoke.

“The Guild Alliance breakfast meeting.”

Small and midsize Guilds were businesses too. In every region, Guild Masters met to build alliances and socialize. Today had been one of those meetings.

“I hadn’t eaten a thing and was just about to take my first bite when that bastard from Sangdong Guild told me something interesting. He said a newly awakened C-rank had been one of our Guild members until a few days ago.”

“…Guild Master, that’s…”

The Guild Master raised a hand, cutting Kim Sangshik off.

“I thought he was talking out of his ass. The Sangdong Guild Master might be a rude bastard, but he’s not the kind of man to make up a story out of nowhere. Especially not at an alliance meeting.”

“I-I’ll look into it again!”

“You will, Team Leader Three? No. That won’t be necessary.”

The Guild Master flung a crumpled bundle of papers across the room.

“I already looked into it.”

Kim Sangshik immediately recognized the papers as someone’s personal file.

He recognized the name, too.

“Jin Taekyung. You know that name, don’t you, Team Leader Three?”

*Fuck.*

Kim Sangshik squeezed his eyes shut. The Guild Master tapped the ash from his cigarette.

“I understand, Team Leader Three. You’re a founding member of the Guild. You figured you could fire one bottom-tier Hunter you didn’t like, then stick your beloved son in the empty spot. Right?”

“Yes. Yes, sir.”

“But now the F-rank loach you treated like dirt and kicked out has returned as a dragon. I specifically told you to recruit him, but if you admitted the truth, you knew I’d raise hell. And you were afraid people would say you had shit for eyes and couldn’t recognize talent when you saw it. So you filed a false report. Right?”

Sizzle.

The Guild Master crushed out his cigarette. The embers scattered, and the last of his patience vanished with them.

“Team Leader Three. No, Sangshik. How long has it been? About twenty years?”

Kim Sangshik answered uneasily.

“Twenty-one years, Guild Master.”

“That’s not what I meant.”

“…Yes, hyung.”

“Good. That sounds better. Keep calling me that from now on.”

“What?”

“I’ve dragged you along for twenty-one years. That’s more than enough. I’ll speak to the members of Team Three separately, so as of today, you’re out.”

“H-hyung!”

“Keep your mouth shut and leave quietly, and I won’t stand in your way. Once you’re gone, you can do whatever you want.”

The Guild Master’s voice was thick with suppressed fury.

Kim Sangshik realized he had no choice.

*Leave? Leave the Guild?*

He had spent more than twenty years at this job. Now he was being told to leave over something like this, cast aside without a second thought.

*Fucking hell…*

He clenched his teeth and walked out of the office.

One final blow followed him out.

“Hey, HR. Process two departures today. Kim Sangshik and Kim Sangho.”

For the next few days, Sopung Guild was in an uproar over the rare spectacle of a father and son being kicked out together.

At the same time, the Guild’s hottest topic became the recent whereabouts of the bottom-tier Hunter who had quit not long before.

“They say he’s C-rank now. Reawakened.”

“Oh my, Mr. Taekyung? The Taekyung I know?”

“That’s what I’m saying. He hit the lottery. Apparently Kim Sangshik went to recruit him without even realizing who he was and got shut down hard.”

“He found every excuse he could to fire him, and now he’s reaping what he sowed. I heard Team Leader Kim’s been going around calling it wrongful dismissal and badmouthing the Guild Master.”

“He still hasn’t come to his senses. Still, I’m jealous. When do I get to live a life like that?”

“Honestly, Taekyung deserves it. He worked so hard that eventually, luck found him.”

“Deserves it, my ass. What, does that mean the rest of us have been living it up? It was luck. Nothing but luck.”

Half envy and half jealousy, the conversations always ended with the same question.

“Where is Mr. Taekyung now, and what’s he doing?”

# Chapter 371

“We pay our respects to Great Hero Jin Taekyung, the Blazing Fire Divine Dragon!”

When that cry of awe rang throughout the grounds of the Sichuan Tang Clan, Jin Taekyung was not the only one who felt a shiver.

Watching from afar, his old master had to struggle to contain the emotions welling up inside him.

*You rascal…*

Jeok Cheongang remembered the day he first met Jin Taekyung with perfect clarity.

The Third Young Master of a martial family on the frontier. The young man who had only just begun to make a name for himself in Shanxi had become a hidden dragon, and now, at last, he was spreading his wings and soaring into the blue sky.

*Yes. You are the Divine Dragon. You are the Great Hero.*

Their eyes met, and Jin Taekyung grinned. Leaning halfway out the window, he waved both hands like a madman.

“What’s my name?”

“Jin Taekyung!”

“What’s my epithet?”

“Blazing Fire Divine Dragon!”

“Shout it louder—!”

“Waaaaaaah!”

The solemn courtyard erupted into a frenzy. Jeok Cheongang finally burst into the laughter he had been holding back.

“Ha! Hahaha!”

His hearty laughter mingled with the cheers. The breeze was cool, and the sky was clear.

It marked the beginning of an age of chaos, but the day a new hero arose was warm with spring.

* * *

Jeok Cheongang appeared just after the frenzy died down.

“Quite the crowd you’ve packed into this millet-sized room.”

“Huh? It isn’t cramped at all.”

“Great Hero Jeok, there’s an empty seat over there.”

Unlike Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin, who wouldn’t know how to read the room if they died and came back to life, Gung Gibang—a beggar of the purest pedigree—moved at once.

“Oh, now that you mention it, we’re practically bursting at the seams. I’ll wait outside.”

Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin waved him off.

“Goodbye, Young Hero Gung.”

“That guy’s finally leaving. Squad Leader, haven’t you been smelling a mutt’s ass around here?”

I cautiously nodded.

“Yeah, it was pretty bad…”

“Cut the bullshit and get out, both of you!”

“No, I’m telling you, it really did smell…”

“All right. Don’t get so angry, Young Hero Gung.”

At Gung Gibang’s outburst, Cheongpung bowed his head, looking dejected.

“Mimi. Say goodbye with a whirlwind spin.”

Sssrik. Sssrrrikkk!

“…What the fuck?”

She’d picked up another trick while I wasn’t looking.

After delivering that flashy performance, Cheongpung finally left the room. Jeok Cheongang spoke.

“You wild brat. You really caused a commotion.”

His face was stern, his voice low. Same as ever. He seemed so thoroughly himself that I couldn’t help smiling.

“You’re laughing?”

“Why wouldn’t I? Should I cry?”

“Look at this rascal. You made a spectacle of yourself in front of all those people and tarnished our sect’s reputation, yet you still have the nerve to say that?”

“You were laughing awfully loudly for someone who thought that.”

“……!”

“I saw everything.”

The stern expression he had fought so hard to maintain crumbled. Jeok Cheongang slowly turned his head toward the window.

“Ahem. What do you mean, you saw something?”

“About fifty other people probably saw it too. I thought the corners of your mouth were going to start bleeding.”

He’d looked practically like the Chinese Joker.

That final blow left him no avenue of escape. Jeok Cheongang hesitated for a long time before finally muttering a single word.

“…Did.”

“Pardon?”

“I said you did well!”

His face blazing red, Jeok Cheongang shouted the words and then grumbled under his breath.

My smile deepened. Yes. That was all I needed to hear.

“Thank you. It was all thanks to you, Old Man.”

“……”

What was this? Something about his expression seemed strange.

His eyes almost looked hurt. Dumbfounded, I asked,

“What is it this time?”

“It’s nothing. Nothing at all.”

“For something that’s nothing, your expression looks a little…”

“I said it’s nothing!”

“Why are you shouting? We were having such a nice, heartwarming moment for once.”

“If I say it’s nothing, take it as nothing! You’re the one making me act like this by interrogating me over every little thing!”

“Whoa. It’s getting worse.”

What was wrong with him all of a sudden? Had I made some kind of mistake?

I was tilting my head in confusion when I sensed it.

Swish.

Two presences were coming up the stairs.

Their footsteps were so light and elusive that I would never have noticed them if my senses hadn’t grown even sharper after crossing the wall.

*They’re both incredible masters.*

Two Supreme Peak masters out of nowhere?

After all the danger I had faced recently, my body moved on its own.

But as I clenched my fists, Jeok Cheongang stopped me with a glance and waved his hand. A gust of hot wind gently pushed open the closed door.

“You’re all so impatient. Couldn’t even wait a quarter of an hour before swarming up here?”

An old nun and a Daoist answered Jeok Cheongang’s gruff complaint in turn.

“Impatient, you say? Coming from you, Benefactor Jeok, that gives me a rather strange feeling.”

“This junior apologizes for the discourtesy. However, given the matter at hand…”

The old nun, her face covered in deep wrinkles, was a stranger to me. The Daoist, however, was easy enough to recognize.

I gave the old Daoist a fist-and-palm salute.

“Greetings, Perfected One.”

“Good to see you again, Fellow Daoist Jin.”

The old Daoist was the Sect Leader of the Qingcheng Sect, the Clear Wind Ancient Sword. I had once visited him to ask for help finding the Divine Physician.

*Then this nun must be the Sect Leader of the Emei Sect… but who is she?*

I had been in the Murim long enough to know a fair amount about its famous masters, but not enough to keep track of every detail of other sects’ affairs.

Especially when it came to the new Sect Leader appointed after the death of the Heaven-Shaking Divine Nun.

As if reading my thoughts, Jeok Cheongang quietly sent me a Sound Transmission.

—That old hag is the Emei Sect’s Extinction Divine Nun. She may look kind, but once she sees red, she turns into a full-blown rakshasa. Watch your words and actions around her. Above all, don’t say a single word about her age.

I cautiously replied through Sound Transmission.

—How old is she, exactly?

—Older than this old man. She was also the Heaven-Shaking Divine Nun’s martial aunt.

—Ah.

I didn’t know Jeok Cheongang’s exact age, but I knew he was over a hundred. That, combined with the sheer menace of her epithet…

I bent deeply at the waist before the ancient granny who looked like the chairwoman of a bossam restaurant.[^1]

“Greetings! My name is Jin Taekyung!”

“Good to meet you, Benefactor Jin.”

The Extinction Divine Nun studied me with an odd look, then nodded.

“Just as the rumors say. No—even more impressive.”

The Clear Wind Ancient Sword gave a faint smile.

“Isn’t he truly astonishing?”

“The waves behind the Yangtze push the waves ahead. It seems the younger generation means to push even us old-timers aside. To cross the wall at such a young age…”

“And that is not all, Divine Nun. There is also a young man named Cheongpung.”

“Ah, the one said to be the Sword Saint’s heir?”

“Yes. This poor Daoist’s cultivation is too lacking to judge which of them is superior, but both young men are unquestionably blessed by Heaven. They are a great blessing to the Murim.”

“Oh…”

Interest and astonishment flashed across the two Sect Leaders’ weary faces.

I was growing embarrassed and shifting my eyes around when Jeok Cheongang stepped in front of me.

Given the difference in our heights, it didn’t make much difference.

“Keep staring and you’ll wear the boy’s face away. Say what you came to say and be off.”

The two Sect Leaders looked awkward at his brusque words, then finally got to the point.

“A problem has arisen. Could the two of you accompany us for a while?”

“It will not take long. I promise.”

“Accompany you? Right now?”

Jeok Cheongang asked in a displeased tone, then turned to me.

“What will you do?”

“……”

Two Sect Leaders of the Nine Sects and One Gang were asking me for a favor. What exactly was I supposed to do?

“I’ll go.”

My body felt light, and going with them would give me a chance to hear everything that had happened. I had no particular reason to refuse.

At my ready answer, the two Sect Leaders turned and began leading the way. Then Jeok Cheongang suddenly spoke.

“But why don’t I see one of them?”

“He is waiting downstairs.”

“He said he did not want the others to notice him.”

*One of them? If he’s important enough to be part of this group, could it be Tang Sadok, the Family Head of the Tang Clan?*

*Well, whoever it is, I’ll see him soon enough.*

My curiosity vanished without a trace the moment I stepped out of the pavilion and saw the person waiting there.

At the sight of that familiar face, a delighted shout burst from me.

“Hey, you bastard! Mungyeong!”

Mungyeong looked exactly as he had before leaving the Sichuan Tang Clan.

Dark Heaven had invaded soon after he left, so I had been worried about him. Our paths must have crossed somewhere, because he had somehow escaped unhurt.

“When did you get here? Damn, you look even taller than before. Are you going through a growth spurt?”

“……”

“Why are you so quiet? In a bad mood? Or maybe…”

I mussed Mungyeong’s hair, then lowered my voice to a whisper.

“Did you have a wet dream this morning?”

“……!”

“You did. You definitely did.”

Look at the brat, too embarrassed to say anything.

I smiled in satisfaction.

That kind of thing happened from time to time at his age. Maybe I should take this opportunity to spread proper sex education throughout this stale old Murim.

“This hyung of yours will show you a whole new world. From now on, call me Teacher Jin Seong-ae.”

Teacher Gu Seong-ae, are you watching? Your knowledge is crossing space and time.

I was patting Mungyeong’s shoulder, swelling with pride, when it happened.

“Uh…”

“Well…”

“Good grief… We forgot to tell him.”

Forgot to tell me what?

Three long sighs sounded behind me. At the same time, a dry voice slipped between Mungyeong’s lips.

“Take your hand off.”

“…Huh?”

“And my wet dreams ended a long time ago.”

“That’s a little concerning, because you still haven’t completely finished your secondary sexual development—no, that’s not what I mean.”

I swallowed hard.

A horrifying possibility had suddenly flashed through my mind.

“Who… are you?”

*Mom, I’m scared.*

* * *

“…and that is how he ended up like this.”

“A truly bizarre affair.”

Even as they raced along at tremendous speed using their movement techniques, the two Sect Leaders’ voices did not waver.

They looked at me as though waiting for an answer, and I managed to part my lips.

“Ah, yes. So the place we’re heading to has some kind of strange formation, right?”

“That is what we learned after capturing and interrogating Samgoe. But the formation itself is so strange…”

Jeok Cheongang answered gruffly.

“This old man and this brat are both hopeless when it comes to formations. Even if we look at it, we won’t understand a thing.”

I agreed. Mount Jiuhua’s Blazing Fire Cave contained an extraordinary arcane formation, but that didn’t make either of us an expert.

*We’d be better off calling someone from the Zhuge Clan.*

But it would take the Zhuge Clan a considerable amount of time to arrive.

For some reason, the two Sect Leaders seemed to have high expectations of us. Especially me.

“Fellow Daoist Jin, did you notice anything else strange about that man? Anything he said or did?”

“What I told you is everything. And from what I could tell… there wasn’t much room for him to get any stranger.”

Dark Heaven’s people were bizarre simply by virtue of existing. They were fanatics who practically—no, outright—worshiped a being called the Heavenly Lord as a god.

The powers and bizarre abilities displayed by the Blood Lord and the Western Heaven Demon Lord were no different.

*Regenerating like a human troll, detaching and reattaching an arm as if it had double-sided tape on it—and to top it all off, even possession.*

The more I thought about it, the more miraculous it seemed that we had fought those bastards and won.

“Hm. Before you awoke, I heard a general account of the situation from Benefactor Jeok. Once the message hawk we sent to Henan returns with a reply, we should know how to proceed.”

“Just in case, if you notice anything strange when you see the formation, let us know.”

“Yes.”

As I answered, I glanced at the figure far ahead of us.

Mungyeong. No, the man known by the epithet Slaughter Saint.

*Fuck. Why is the Slaughter Saint here?*

Honestly, I had nearly pissed myself.

Even if I had spent half a day splashing around in the Sanzu River and grilling meat on its banks, I wouldn’t have felt this close to death.

*Mungyeong was the Slaughter Saint. And I’d asked the Slaughter Saint if he’d had a wet dream!*

Teacher Gu Seong-ae. I nearly fucking died because of you.

Just as I secretly breathed a sigh of relief, the dense brush around us vanished and towering cliffs began to come into view.

[^1]: *Bossam* is boiled pork commonly served with napa cabbage or other greens for wrapping.

[^2]: The Sanzu River is a Buddhist river said to separate the living world from the afterlife.

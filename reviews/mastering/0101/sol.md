# Chapter 101

*What the hell is going on?*

I was dumbfounded. Butler Kim’s appearance was unexpected, but that was nothing compared to how he had addressed Im Chunsoo.

*Chunsoo? You?*

*Did these two know each other?*

Come to think of it, Butler Kim and Im Chunsoo had a lot in common. They were around the same age, and both had long careers as Hunters.

*Now that I think about it, Butler Kim was active as a Hunter during the Great Cataclysm, too.*

He had never told me himself, but I had suspected as much. I glanced back and forth between them before cautiously asking,

“Are you two close?”

Im Chunsoo’s eyes quivered.

* * *

When Im Chunsoo first received the report on the Peace Guild, he had doubted his own eyes.

There, in one of the photographs, was a face from his hazy memories—a face he had been certain he would never see again.

*Wh-who did you say this man was?*

*Peace Guild Master Kim Hwajong. He’s fifty-five years old and a B-rank Hunter.*

*Kim Hwajong? A B-rank Hunter?*

*Yes. Is something wrong?*

*No, no. He looks like someone I used to know. I mistook him for that person.*

*Of course.*

Im Chunsoo breathed a sigh of relief.

The man looked somewhat similar, but that was all. More importantly, hadn’t that person died over thirty years ago?

No matter how bizarre the world had become, the dead couldn’t come back to life.

*Even if that bastard did come back, he wouldn’t be rotting away in a place like this. I just mistook some washed-up old B-rank Hunter for him.*

That day, Im Chunsoo had treated himself to a glass of soju for the first time in ages, trying to shake off his unease—and the terrible memories that still haunted him even past fifty.

*Damn. I’m this old, and this still gets to me.*

Later, when he heard that Kim Hwajong’s information was under airtight security, a chill had run down his spine.

*Could it be? No. There’s no way.*

But then… why were bad feelings never wrong?

“Rise up. Fire Wall.”

The quiet incantation was followed by a wall of fire surging into the air.

Fwoosh!

Blazing blue flames vaporized the ice spikes, each harder than steel, without leaving a trace.

And then—

“I’m glad I’m not too late. You too, Chunsoo.”

The moment Im Chunsoo heard the voice he could never forget, even in his dreams, it all came flooding back.

The terror of being under that man’s thumb.

The humiliation of rolling around on the ground just to survive.

*Fuck… I’m screwed.*

As he stood frozen like a statue, Jin Taekyung asked him,

“Are you two close?”

What? *Close?*

Im Chunsoo swallowed the torrent of curses rising from deep in his lungs and turned around.

There stood the man he had believed dead for decades.

“I-Instructor.”

Butler Kim smiled gently. It was the smile of a demon, seared deep into Im Chunsoo’s mind even after all these years.

“Twenty-eighth Regiment, First Battalion, Second Company. Im Chunsoo. Yes, I recognized you the moment I saw you.”

The words resonated in his very soul.

Im Chunsoo’s hunched back snapped straight. His heels came together at a forty-five-degree angle, and his eyes fixed fifteen degrees above the horizon.

The movements came as swiftly as lightning, followed by a thunderous roar.

“Trainee Number One! Im! Chun! Soo!”

The mountain shook as he bellowed his military identification for the first time in thirty years.

* * *

When Team Leader 1 reached the top of the mountain, he found grass splattered with blood and the Security Team trussed up in magical ropes.

*What a disgraceful sight.*

Clicking his tongue inwardly, he drew his sword and cut them loose.

They had all shed some blood, but none appeared seriously injured. Apparently, Jin Taekyung had shown at least a bare minimum of restraint.

*That puts him at the very top of B-rank… or possibly A-rank.*

That was Team Leader 1’s assessment of Jin Taekyung’s strength.

For one man, the walk back down the mountain was sheer hell.

“Why did you do it? We might have been able to smooth over the surveillance, but what happened today could amount to attempted murder. Did you even consider the consequences before resorting to violence?”

“…I’m sorry.”

The Security Team Leader hung his head.

He had pushed ahead with an absurdly reckless show of force, only to have Jin Taekyung wipe the floor with them. He wouldn’t have had an excuse even if he had ten mouths.

“The Guild Master is deeply disappointed.”

“Th-then…?”

“A written apology and a pay cut are a given. Be prepared for worse.”

“Do you mean… dismissal?”

“That’s up to the Guild Master.”

“Team Leader, perhaps you could…”

“Let me make this clear: don’t ask me for favors. I have no interest in defending someone who dragged my workplace’s name through the mud, and I have no intention of opposing the Guild Master’s decision.”

“…”

Team Leader 1 let out an irritated sigh.

That was when a deafening shout echoed from far away.

—Trainee Number One! Im! Chun! Soo!

“……”

“……”

*What was that? Were they hearing things?*

Team Leader 1 and even the grim-faced Security Team members jumped in surprise. The Security Team Leader was the first to regain his composure.

“Team Leader 1, I’m sorry to say this under the circumstances, but I think I just heard the Guild Master’s voice.”

Team Leader 1, who had been digging a finger into his ear, stared at him.

“…You heard it too?”

“We heard it as well.”

“We couldn’t tell whether it was the Guild Master’s voice, though… I think we heard his name.”

“That was someone’s name? I thought it was just screaming.”

“Really? It sounded like someone reporting his rank and name to me.”

Team Leader 1’s expression hardened as he listened to the Security Team whisper among themselves.

“Who said that? What was that about reporting his rank and name?”

To him, Im Chunsoo was a respected Senior as well as his superior.

He was a peerless Hunter who had fought since the Great Cataclysm—a war hero. And they were claiming that he had suddenly reported his military identification?

Team Leader 1 had never imagined such a thing. He *couldn’t* imagine it.

“You still have enough energy to spout nonsense? Is that something anyone in their right mind would say?”

“S-sorry.”

“We must have misheard.”

“Get a grip, all of you. Understood?”

Team Leader 1 had just resumed walking after delivering the warning when it happened again.

—No, sirrrrr!

“……”

—I’ll correct it, sirrrr!

“……”

It was the soul-deep roar of a private second class.

A moment passed before Team Leader 1’s tightly sealed lips finally opened.

“From this moment on, we run at full speed. Move!”

“M-move!”

Everyone there was at least a C-rank Hunter—a superhuman who had long since surpassed the limits of an ordinary person.

They thundered downhill like runaway locomotives and reached the hiking-trail entrance in under five minutes.

“Slow as hell. You’re only getting here now?”

“Guild Master!”

“Keep it down. You’ll burst my eardrums.”

Team Leader 1 sighed in relief when he saw Im Chunsoo looking the same as ever.

“I thought something might have happened…”

“Something? Did anything seem strange?”

“N-no, sir. But where did that Jin Taekyung bastard go?”

“I gave him a talking-to and sent him home. Once we spoke, he turned out to be a more decent fellow than I expected. Why?”

“I thought he might have caused some kind of disturbance.”

“Oh, you mean the guy who was shouting earlier?”

“Yes, sir. But the voice sounded exactly…”

He couldn’t bring himself to finish.

*Exactly like yours, Guild Master.*

Im Chunsoo glared at him.

“Exactly like what?”

“N-nothing, sir.”

“What a strange thing to say. Some greenhorns were playing army down there, so I chased them off. Do colleges still have that sort of hazing culture these days?”

“Ah, I see.”

“What do they call it again? I put them through a few PT exercises, and they acted like they were dying.”

Team Leader 1 felt his suspicions melt away completely.

*I must have lost my mind. How could I even think such a thing?*

While he reflected deeply on his mistake, Im Chunsoo laid into the Security Team.

“Security Team Leader.”

“Y-yes, sir!”

“Well, look at that. You certainly know how to answer. You caused this mess, yet you still think you deserve to be called Team Leader?”

“I’m sorry, Guild Master!”

“Are the rest of you keeping quiet because you did such a fine job? Do you want me to make my sword dance today?”

“We’re sorry, Guild Master!”

Team Leader 1 watched with a satisfied smile.

Some people occasionally spread vicious rumors that Im Chunsoo had a godawful personality. But Team Leader 1, who had observed the man up close, knew the truth. Im Chunsoo was a charismatic superior and an exemplary Senior in life.

*Guild Master, I’ll follow you forever.*

As he gazed at Im Chunsoo’s back with boundless respect, Team Leader 1 suddenly cocked his head.

*…Why is there dirt on the Guild Master’s back?*

He must have scolded those kids rather vigorously.

* * *

“We’ve arrived.”

Butler Kim’s voice snapped me out of my daze. I looked around and saw the entrance to my apartment complex through the window.

*When did we get here?*

“Th-thank you.”

“Don’t mention it.”

A smile spread across his handsomely weathered face. He looked like a middle-aged actor who had once ruled an era.

*No, this man really did rule an era.*

Until now, I had thought of him as little more than a Hunter from a much older generation. Clearly, I hadn’t understood Butler Kim at all.

*He worked an A-rank mage like a dog—and Im Chunsoo, no less.*

First, he had made the old war hero do a hundred sets of PT Exercise Number Eight. Then he had kicked him in the shin with a dress shoe. Even now, remembering how he had calmly torn into Im Chunsoo in that gentle voice sent chills down my spine.

*Trainee, who told you to use mana during PT exercises?*

Whack!

*Trainee Number One Im Chunsoo. S-sorry, sir.*

*Does it hurt? Has your voice gotten weaker with age, too?*

*No, sirrrrr!*

*Attention. At ease. Attention. At ease.*

Snap-snap-snap-snap!

*On your backs. On your fronts. On your backs. On your backs.*

*Gasp.*

*Trainee, did you not hear this Instructor order you onto your back? Get your head straight.*

*I’ll correct it, sirrrr!*

*And why are you bullying an innocent junior? Hasn’t this Instructor told you time and again that Seniors and juniors should help each other?*

*S-sorry, sir.*

*Repeat after me. Cherish your junior on the way down. Cherish your junior on the way up. One. Two.*

*Cherish my junior!*

*Trainee, you were Class 25, if I remember correctly. Is that right?*

*Trainee Number One Im Chunsoo. Yes, sir.*

*This Instructor was Class 3. If word of today’s incident gets out or anything like it happens again, Classes 4 through 24 will assemble without exception.*

*…*

*Why aren’t you answering? Prepare for squat jumps.*

*P-prepare, sir…*

He worked him over, then worked him over some more.

It was a sight money couldn’t buy. If the Sangdong Guild members had witnessed it, the Guild might have been forced to shut down that very day.

*Who the hell is Butler Kim, really?*

As a member of Class 3 at the Hunter Training Center, he had enough seniority to line up every Guild Master in the country on a parade ground and give them all a beating.

And he had been an Instructor on top of that.

It wouldn’t be an exaggeration to say that nearly every mage from the early days of the Great Cataclysm had passed through his hands.

*He’s at least A-rank as a mage, too.*

That much was obvious from the way Im Chunsoo had taken his punishment without daring to make a peep. Butler Kim outranked him in both seniority and skill.

Fire probably had an elemental advantage over ice, but Butler Kim was also the man who had trained Im Chunsoo and helped him reach his current position.

*But…*

Why was someone that accomplished working as a butler?

I stole a sidelong glance at Butler Kim, only for our eyes to meet.

“You seem to have a great deal you want to ask me.”

“To be honest, I do.”

My curiosity was killing me.

Seeing every thought written plainly across my face, Butler Kim’s lips curled into a smile.

“It’s a long story.”

“That’s fine. I’m on vacation, so I have plenty of time.”

“Ah, then perhaps I can hear your story as well, Mr. Jin. I have quite a few questions of my own.”

“Come to think of it, it’s already dinnertime. Tomorrow is the last day of my vacation, and I have a real-estate contract to sign. Hahaha.”

“……”

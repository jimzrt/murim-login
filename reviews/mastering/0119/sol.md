# Chapter 119

*Shnk!*

A sharp tingle ran through my fingertips—a familiar sensation I’d felt thousands, even tens of thousands of times before.

*It went in.*

Certain I’d landed the blow, I twisted the dagger buried in Pung Yang’s side. He screamed.

“Graaaargh!”

The dagger itself must have hurt like hell, but the strike had also inflicted a serious internal injury. His Body-Protecting Qi scattered like smoke before it could fully form, proving as much.

*That was close.*

Pung Yang’s instincts were no joke. If the dagger hadn’t pierced him in that split second before his Body-Protecting Qi formed, I would have been the one in trouble.

“Guh!”

Pung Yang spat out dark blood and thrust out an arm. A flash burst from the darkness of his sleeve.

*Shweeeeeek!*

I instantly stepped back and jerked my head away. A throwing knife shot from Pung Yang’s sleeve and narrowly grazed the bridge of my nose.

*That was dangerous.*

If I hadn’t moved, it would have struck me in the throat instead.

I’d only managed to dodge because I’d already seen Pung Yang use throwing knives. I stuck out my tongue and licked up the blood trickling down my nose.

“Thanks. I was thirsty anyway.”

“You rat bastard…!”

Pung Yang backed away and ground his teeth.

“Was this your plan from the beginning?”

“Yeah. Hurts, doesn’t it?”

“Just wait. I’ll pay you back a hundredfold.”

“There’s no need to go that far between friends. You can keep it.”

“Grrrrrgh!”

Teasing him was surprisingly satisfying. It would have been nice if he dropped dead from rage, but I doubted I’d get that lucky.

*It was a good opportunity.*

I’d succeeded in drawing Pung Yang in with Taunt, but I’d failed to finish him off. Even after taking the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, there was still a huge gap between us.

And more importantly—

*This is getting rough.*

My entire body burned like a furnace. I hadn’t been able to control the thirty years of Scorching Yang Qi from the start, and now it was running completely wild.

Suppressing it with my existing internal energy would have been difficult enough, but I’d let it run free while fighting Pung Yang at close quarters. My condition was steadily worsening.

*Damn it. I wish it had been a Temporary Strength Pill instead of the Blazing Flame Divine Pill.*

The two pills had been created for entirely different purposes.

The Temporary Strength Pill was something like a stimulant that temporarily awakened a person’s latent power and pushed it to the limit. The difference between the two was like the difference between traditional herbal medicine and a stimulant.

*Does that bastard not have any side effects? He’s still disgustingly lively.*

I was eyeing Pung Yang with a hint of envy when—

“Cough.”

Oh? What was this?

As Pung Yang coughed, the whites of his eyes showed for an instant.

His eyes had been blood-red this whole time. There was only one possible reason for that sudden change.

*The Temporary Strength Pill is wearing off.*

* * *

Pung Yang was alarmed. He immediately recognized the warning signs from his body.

*Already?*

A Temporary Strength Pill’s effects lasted a little over one shichen. Yet barely half a shichen had passed, and the power surging through his body was already beginning to fade.

The fatigue he hadn’t felt before settled heavily onto his shoulders, while pain that had seemed far away began to gnaw at his nerves.

“Cough.”

And now he had suffered a considerable internal injury as well.

Pung Yang bit his lip at the sight of the dark-red blood mixed into his cough.

*I’ve expended too much strength.*

The Temporary Strength Pill was a wondrous item that could draw out twice a person’s strength—or even more—but that power came at a price.

It granted tremendous strength for a short time, but the user’s physical abilities declined afterward. If the user’s body could no longer withstand the strain, the pill’s effects would end.

*Taking Temporary Strength Pills back-to-back was the problem.*

First the Tiger of Mount Heng, Cheol Mubaek, and then these young brats from the Jin Family of Taiyuan. Without the pills, Pung Yang would have died long ago, but he knew their price and feared the backlash.

*At this rate, the effects will last no more than one meal’s time…*

He had to finish the battle before then.

He could not use his final Temporary Strength Pill. Taking three in succession might put his life at risk.

“One meal’s time…”

Pung Yang muttered under his breath and glared at the remaining prey.

“That’s more than enough.”

The flash of white vanished as his eyes filled with blood-red light once more.

* * *

Had I imagined it?

When Pung Yang raised his head again, his eyes were red, and an overwhelming aura poured from his body.

“I’ll kill you with a single stroke.”

I kept my eyes on his curved saber.

“I thought you needed the martial arts formulas.”

“It doesn’t have to be you. Why do you think I kept Jin Mukyung alive?”

“Because you love him.”

“I swear by the gods of heaven and earth… I will cut out your tongue.”

Pung Yang raised his curved saber.

*Shweeeeeek! Clang!*

He caught the iron arrow hurtling toward him and frowned.

“There’s no shortage of bastards eager to die. No, is it a bitch this time?”

The archer slowly approached and stopped beside me. Her footsteps were light as down, and the top of her head barely reached my shoulder. Even in this desperate situation, her beauty stole my attention for an instant.

Lee Seowol, Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect, whispered to me.

“We’ll buy you some time.”

By *we*, she meant herself and the surviving martial artists of the Mount Heng Sword Sect.

There were only ten of them. Every one was a First Rate master, as one might expect of those who had survived such a brutal battle to the bitter end. But against Pung Yang, the outcome was obvious.

*They’ll be wiped out.*

How much time could they buy even if they stepped in? They were more likely to get in my way. It was cold, but that was the reality.

I shook my head.

“That won’t change anything.”

“I’m not asking you to fight alongside us.”

“Then?”

“Take your brother and run.”

Pung Yang stood only a little over ten zhang—about thirty meters—away. No matter how quietly she spoke, there was no chance a Peak master like him had failed to hear.

“What? Run? Hahahaha!”

Lee Seowol ignored his booming laughter and continued.

“It may only be for a moment, but we’ll buy you what time we can. Run as far as possible.”

I looked toward the fortress gate. It was about one hundred zhang—three hundred meters—from here. Just beyond it were Wolhwa, Hyuk Mujin, and the horses waiting to carry us away.

*It’s worth a try.*

The Blazing Flame Divine Pill was already causing internal injuries, but I could withstand them for a while longer. If I squeezed out every last ounce of strength, I might be able to escape even with Jin Mukyung on my back.

But…

*They’ll die.*

Every last one of them. Not a single exception.

Lee Seowol herself was included in that *we*. She had stepped forward prepared to die as well.

*Why?*

The Mount Heng Sword Sect and the Jin Family of Taiyuan were bitter enemies. Even if both sides had been manipulated by the Head Elder, we had killed one another and shed a great deal of blood.

And yet Lee Seowol was trying to save me.

Even if it cost her life and the lives of her subordinates.

“You don’t need to look at us like that. I reached this decision after careful consideration. In return, may I ask you for one thing?”

“What is it?”

“Avenge us.”

Lee Seowol stared coldly at Pung Yang.

“Kill him. Make it as cruel as possible.”

Ten people were willing to sacrifice their lives for one man’s death.

I could hear Lee Seowol’s hatred in every word. As I stood there speechless, Pung Yang watched us with a mocking smile.

“You’ve come up with quite the bold plan, but it won’t happen. All of you will leave your bones here.”

One of the Mount Heng martial artists shouted, “Shut up, you vicious bastard!”

*Thwack!*

The next instant, the martial artist’s head snapped back. The iron arrow Pung Yang had been holding was embedded in his forehead.

“Senior Brother!”

As the belated cry rang out, Pung Yang smiled.

“I was just about out of throwing knives… Thank you, Sect Leader.”

Lee Seowol bit her lip.

“Go! Hurry!”

I took a deep breath. I had already mapped out every step in my head.

Jin Mukyung lay only about twenty zhang—sixty meters—away. If I drew up as much internal energy as possible and ran with him on my back, enduring the damage, I could reach the fortress gate in no time.

Pung Yang might catch up by then, but if they bought me just a little more time, I could survive.

*I can go back.*

Back to the Jin Family of Taiyuan, where Jin Wikyung was. Back home, where my mother and Hayeon were waiting.

If I survived and waited for another chance, I knew I could return far stronger—strong enough to make Pung Yang look insignificant.

*That’s enough.*

I drew another deep breath and turned toward Lee Seowol.

“I’ll avenge you. I swear.”

For the briefest instant, I thought she smiled.

Perhaps I imagined it. The moment passed too quickly, and when I looked again, only grim resolve remained on her face.

“Go. Hurry.”

Her words were the signal. The Mount Heng martial artists charged with furious shouts, Lee Seowol leading them.

*Right. Time to go.*

I drew up every last bit of internal energy in my body. The damage caused by the Scorching Yang Qi sent sharp pain tearing through me and dark-red blood trickling from my nose, but I endured it.

It only had to last a moment.

Just one moment.

*Inventory open. Equip spear.*

The cool shaft settled into my grip.

I pushed off the ground.

*Boom.*

I shot forward like an arrow—no, faster than one.

Not toward Jin Mukyung.

Toward Pung Yang.

His face split into a broad grin.

“That’s more like it!”

“Shut up, you son of a bitch.”

“Ha-ha-ha-ha!”

Pung Yang’s curved saber seemed larger than ever. Brimming with red saber qi, it swept toward me.

*Whoooosh!*

The wind exploded, and even the air seemed to vanish.

I gripped the iron spear hard enough to crush it.

*Please. Just this once.*

I poured all fifteen years of my internal energy into the spear. Drawing my shoulder and waist as far back as they would go, I hurled everything I had forward.

*One Annihilation.*

*Rumble-rumble-rumble!*

The immense saber qi collided with the vortex.

A thunderous roar filled the world as if the sky itself were splitting apart. Through the raging wind, I saw it clearly.

*Kaboom!*

The white vortex shattered against the red saber qi. That immense concentration of qi completely pulverized One Annihilation.

Pung Yang’s face shone with ecstasy.

“This is as far as you go.”

The next instant, Pung Yang’s internal energy surged along the spear and slammed into me.

*Boom!*

Distant pain and ringing ears crashed over me in waves.

*Ding.*

> **System**
> - You have suffered **Internal Injury**! Your condition is extremely serious!
> - **Scorching Yang Qi** is running wild!
> - You have suffered a **Severe Injury**! All stats have dropped significantly!

The System notifications rang out one after another, mingling with the Mount Heng martial artists’ shouts, Lee Seowol’s scream, and Pung Yang’s laughter.

*It’s over.*

The instant my legs gave out beneath me, a powerful hand clamped around my throat.

“Guh. Guhh.”

“That was fairly impressive. If I’d been even a moment slower, you might have won.”

Pung Yang bared his teeth in a grin. The red light in his eyes was slowly fading.

*Damn it. So fucking close.*

I wanted to say it aloud, but my mouth was full of blood.

“I swore by the gods of heaven and earth, didn’t I? I swore I’d pull out your tongue.”

Had he said that? By now, I could barely remember who I was.

Pung Yang pried open my mouth with his other hand. His rough fingers seized my blood-soaked tongue and pulled.

“Ghh…”

“When you were running that mouth, didn’t you know this would happen? Huh?”

“Ghaa… hahahaa.”

“Hahaha! What the hell are you babbling about? Shall I let you say your last words?”

Pung Yang roared with laughter and released my tongue. At last, I could speak. I swallowed the blood filling my mouth.

“Salty.”

“What?”

“Fucking salty.”

“What kind of bullshit is that?”

*What do you think?*

*I’m saying the taste of your fingers brought me back to my senses.*

Half-conscious, I muttered,

*Inventory open. Summon anything.*

*Ding.*

> **System**
> - No item named **Anything** can be found. The oldest item stored in your inventory will be summoned instead.
> - The **Unnamed Sword** has been summoned.

*The Unnamed Sword? What was that again?*

*Whatever.*

I gathered the last of my strength and thrust the sword in my hand at Pung Yang’s chest, shrouded in Body-Protecting Qi.

It was a futile last-ditch attack with an obvious outcome.

*Damn Body-Protecting Qi.*

But this was enough.

I had no regrets left.

Just as my head began to droop—

*Shnk!*

*Ding.*

> **System**
> - The **Unnamed Sword** has satisfied a specific condition.
> - **Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron** has destroyed **Body-Protecting Qi**.

…Huh?

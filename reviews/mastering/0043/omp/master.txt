# Chapter 43

Swish.

A razor-sharp dagger dug into a Hobgoblin corpse.

Fluids splattered and a foul stench filled the air, but I didn’t stop. I discarded the heads, neatly cut the hides from their backs and bellies, and stacked them in a pile.

“You’re quite skilled.”

“…Thank you.”

It was Team Leader Choi. The kindness in his voice made me feel guilty. Getting jealous of someone like him—how childish was that?

Especially after dragging up an illusion I’d already decided to forget.

*What am I, a teenager?*

I finished the job with my face burning red.

A little over ten minutes, so about twenty seconds per corpse? Im Kkeokjeong came over to watch and gaped at how fast I was.

“Damn, do you do nothing but this all day?”

“Plenty of people can do this much.”

“Plenty, my ass. You’re a full-blown professional.”

Hmm. That made me a little proud.

I’d played modest a second ago, but Im Kkeokjeong was right. There weren’t many Hunters who could butcher this cleanly at this speed.

“I’m just quick with my hands.”

I’d worked factory jobs back in school. I’d even stuck eyes on dolls. In a way, I’d started doing this before I ever worked as a Hunter.

“I kept it up during raids, too.”

“You switched over to this completely?”

“No. I did both. It was a side job.”

“A side job?”

Team Leader Choi, who’d been listening quietly, cut in.

“Oh, yes.”

“What exactly did you do?”

“I carved out time during breaks after combat and did this.”

“Did you receive additional pay?”

“Of course. It’s work too.”

“How long have you been doing it?”

“The whole time. From the beginning until now.”

Im Kkeokjeong shook his head in disbelief.

“You relentless bastard. I couldn’t do that.”

“You get used to it. It helps build stamina, too.”

“Your life’s on the line, and you’re using it to build stamina? Hahaha! Team Leader Choi, didn’t I tell you? Nobody works as hard as this kid.”

*You told him that?*

“Hm.”

Instead of answering, Team Leader Choi studied me for a moment, then turned away.

“The work’s done, so shall we get moving again?”

What was that reaction supposed to mean? While I stood there bewildered, Im Kkeokjeong gave me an unreadable smile.

“Keep it up.”

*No, seriously, what?*

* * *

The raid went smoothly. The E-rank Hunters had worked together for so long that they moved as one. Once they thinned out the monsters, Team Leader Choi took over.

Slash—

Every swing of his sword sent up a fountain of blood.

I took in every second of the fight unfolding right in front of me.

*He really can fight.*

Of course, I couldn’t help thinking about Murim.

Not the dance-like routines people practiced in city parks at dawn, but real martial arts. I’d seen them with my own eyes and even learned them myself.

*…Huh?*

*No way. Still, maybe?*

It was ridiculous. There was no way it would work, but I could at least try once.

*It won’t work. It won’t… But it’d be nice if it did.*

Just as I was about to take a cautious step—

“Mr. Taekyung. Please handle the cleanup.”

Team Leader Choi’s voice snapped me back to my senses.

*I’ve completely lost it.*

I’d gotten distracted in the middle of a raid.

*Get a grip.*

Whatever I wanted to do later, the raid came first.

That was only fair to my teammates—and it was the principle that would keep me alive.

“Yes. Coming!”

I raced over like the wind and started taking the corpses apart.

Team Leader Choi and Im Kkeokjeong hovered nearby throughout the break as if they weren’t tired at all.

*They’re seriously getting on my nerves.*

* * *

Im Kkeokjeong came over again at the next break, without fail.

“Taekyung. How long have you been using a spear?”

“Seven years.”

“Wow. At that point, you’re practically a master. A real master.”

“…?”

Then again at the break after that.

“Taekyung. You said you were top of your training class, right?”

“Yes. But I was only first among the F-ranks, so it doesn’t mean much.”

“You punk. That’s what makes it impressive. I finished dead last.”

“You’re impressive too, hyung. But I need to get back to work.”

“Oh, right.”

And then the next break. And the one after that. And the one after that.

“Taekyung. Taekyung. Taekyung.”

“Hyung. I think my cochlea’s ruptured.”

“You said you worked at your previous Guild for several years, right?”

“Ah.”

I wanted to switch to a mage right then and there, just so I could cast a silence spell on that man’s mouth.

*This is driving me crazy.*

There was only one way out: finish the work before blood really started pouring from my ears. I clenched my teeth and kept my hands moving.

Slice. Slice. Slice.

“Taekyung.”

*Save me.*

I sent a distress signal to Team Leader Choi, who was sitting nearby, but he answered by quietly slipping away.

* * *

“The work’s done.”

I stood with the bag on my back, heavier than before. Hobgoblins were the monsters that kept on giving. Poison-resistant hides, a few usable weapons—and they’d even dropped two E-grade Magic Gems.

*The hides are in good condition, and there are two Magic Gems. This haul should be worth a decent amount.*

Team Leader Choi had contributed the most, so he’d probably take about half. But with so few people, the other team members would still turn a profit.

Not me, of course. According to the contract, the 300,000-won base pay was all I would get.

*If he’s in a good mood, maybe he’ll throw me a little extra.*

Team Leader Choi was a fairly generous employer. He was a C-rank Hunter far above me, yet he always spoke politely to me. His personality was a little unusual, but by the standards of this industry, he was practically a saint.

*And he’s a skilled Hunter I can trust.*

Thanks to him, we’d made it this far quickly and without any trouble.

All that remained was the final part of the Gate.

The Boss Zone.

If we opened the stone gate in front of us, the Gate’s boss monster and the magic field that would send us back outside should be waiting beyond it.

“Man, all that moving around made me hungry.”

“Let’s finish this quick and grab some gukbap.[^1] Taekyung, you’re coming too, right? You too, Team Leader Choi.”

“I’m fine. I’ll order sushi separately.”

“…”

Talking about food with the Boss Zone right in front of us. And one guy was even planning to order sushi by himself.

*Is it really okay to be this relaxed?*

Of course, I had no right to say that. Just portering and butchering between fights had already earned me a day’s pay.

Until I landed a job at a new Guild, I couldn’t ask for anything more than Team Leader Choi calling me regularly. A cushy gig like this? I could do it a hundred times over.

“Well, shall we head in?”

“Yes.”

At Team Leader Choi’s answer, Im Kkeokjeong slammed his tower shield into the stone gate.

Boom!

The gate exploded, showering the ground with powdered stone and dust. I followed the others into the Boss Zone.

And there…

—Keuruk.

It stood.

*A shaman?*

That was the first word that came to mind when I saw it.

An old Hobgoblin in robes covered with unreadable patterns. A withered staff in one hand, a sharp dagger in the other.

—Karruk. Chwi. Akto.

Despite the deafening crash of the stone gate breaking, it kept muttering, completely unfazed. Each time it finished a syllable, black energy rose from the corpses scattered around it.

*Wait. Corpses?*

I’d seen it right. Nearly a hundred dead Hobgoblins lay atop the altar. Looking closer, they were the monsters we should have been fighting in the Boss Zone.

*What the hell is going on?*

While all of us froze at the unprecedented sight, Team Leader Choi shouted like a thunderclap.

“Get outside. Now!”

That was when the mysterious old Hobgoblin turned its head.

Clink.

Its staff shook lightly.

—Mita. Allo.

The air shuddered.

The spell activated.

“Behind the shields!”

The tanks hurriedly raised their tower shields. But that was the wrong call. What it had cast wasn’t an attack spell.

Boom. Boom. Boom!

“Behind us! The passage is being blocked!”

“Run! Hurry!”

*Damn it. Too late.*

As if time had been reversed, the shattered stone gate stood intact again.

And the changes didn’t stop there.

The rubble on the ground stacked itself into two, then three layers of walls, and vines from the cave walls wound tightly around them.

“Everyone, out of the way!”

It was Im Kkeokjeong. His muscles had swollen as if they were about to burst—he must have used a Strength Enhancement Skill. Tower shield in hand, he charged the stone gate.

“Haaah!”

Bang! Crack!

“…Goddamn it.”

Im Kkeokjeong dropped his tower shield, his face blank with disbelief. An E-rank Hunter had used a Skill and still hadn’t broken through. All he’d managed to do was wreck his shield.

*Enhancement magic?*

Whatever it was, one thing was certain.

That old Hobgoblin could wield magic powerful enough to make it a monster at least one or two stages above us.

And…

Swish!

There was someone among us capable of facing it.

*When did he—?*

Team Leader Choi was already charging the creature, as if he’d known we wouldn’t be able to break through the blocked passage.

“The wind takes hold. Haste.”

He shot forward. More than a hundred meters vanished in an instant. With only five paces left, Team Leader Choi drew the sword at his waist.

Whoosh!

A full-power attack from a C-rank Hunter.

But the old Hobgoblin merely gave him a nasty grin.

—Karruk. Chwi. Akto.

The change happened in an instant.

Whoosh—

The hundred-odd corpses scattered across the altar shriveled, then crumbled like sand. The black energy drawn from them gathered into a single mass and slammed into Team Leader Choi’s side.

Thud!

“Ghk.”

Team Leader Choi went flying. His face contorted as he said,

“We have to stop it. Right now.”

“…That?”

It was already too late.

Only a few seconds ago, it had been nothing more than a tangible mass of energy. Now it was rapidly taking shape.

A hulking body nearly three meters tall. Bulging muscles and a monstrously huge greatsword.

“What the hell is that…?”

Someone muttered in a daze. Like me, they had clearly never seen anything like it before.

Only Team Leader Choi knew what the monsters were.

“A Hobgoblin Great Warrior. A C-rank Rare Monster.”

The fact that something that size was a Hobgoblin was shocking enough, but it was nothing compared to what came next.

*A C-rank Rare Monster?*

*Fuck, why is that thing showing up here?*

A Rare Monster was a special creature with only a slim chance of appearing in a given Gate. And that Great Warrior was C-rank—something neither I nor the other team members should ever have encountered in our lives.

…Of course, that was no longer true.

“You want us to fight that thing?”

“Those things.”

Team Leader Choi pointed at the old Hobgoblin.

“A Hobgoblin Priest. Also a C-rank Rare Monster.”

“Ah, fuck…”

The curse slipped out before I could stop it. Im Kkeokjeong asked with a grim expression,

“What are our chances? Give it to me straight.”

“If we take out the Priest first, there’s hope. But…”

Boom. Boom.

Team Leader Choi’s words cut off.

The Great Warrior was coming toward us.

One step. Then another.

The cave floor shook.

“We need to hold that thing off for a little while.”

*Who does?*

“Us?”

“No. All of you.”

“Kuwooooh!”

The Great Warrior’s roar sent a stalactite crashing down from the cave ceiling.

Team Leader Choi turned away with a resolute look I’d never seen on him before.

“The wind takes hold. Haste.”

*Hey, you bastard.*

[^1]: Gukbap is a Korean dish of soup served with rice.

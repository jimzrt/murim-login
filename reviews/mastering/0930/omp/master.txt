# Chapter 930

Strange.

The unfamiliar ceiling that should have been waiting for me, just like always, somehow felt familiar.

*What is this?*

As my vision slowly sharpened, I looked around.

The space was far too large to call a mere bedroom. Thick pillars stood between the curtains surrounding it, while braziers placed here and there and night-shining pearls set into the ceiling like stars bathed the room in a soft glow.

*Wait. Night-shining pearls?*

Then I realized where the déjà vu was coming from—and why the place felt familiar.

*This is…*

That’s right.

This was a vast pavilion built to serve as both living quarters and a place to conduct state affairs. A fortress packed with guards.

Qianqing Palace.

*No wonder it felt familiar.*

Without the night-shining pearls, it might have taken me longer to figure it out. Only an emperor could plaster his ceiling with pearls big enough to fetch hundreds of silver nyang on Bok Choy Market as if they were glitter stickers.

*But…why am I here?*

The question surfaced in my strangely empty head.

*Whoosh.*

A faint breeze stirred the curtains around the bedroom. At the same moment, a man in black appeared like a ghost and bowed his head slightly.

“Have you awakened?”

His voice was muffled, almost a mumble.

I stared blankly at the unexpected visitor, then calmly assessed the situation.

The Emperor’s quarters. The Emperor’s blanket. The Emperor’s bed. The Emperor’s pillow.

My clothes were golden from head to toe, and when I lifted my pants for a peek, even my underwear was gleaming golden silk.

The full King-God-Emperor package.

And No Shadow—the Emperor’s hidden guard, an assassin and Supreme Peak master—was speaking to me with such courtesy.

*Then could this be…*

Through clear-headed reasoning, I reached a conclusion. A shudder ran through me.

“Possession…!”

It had to be. There was no other explanation.

I’d gone to sleep and woken up to find that my life had changed genres from fusion Murim to a possession story. As I reeled from the shock, No Shadow spoke in his usual muffled voice.

“Are you all right?”

*Would you be all right?* nearly burst out of me, but figuring out what had happened came first.

“What time is it? No, how many days have passed since the grand banquet?”

“……?”

Apparently, that was a strange question from an emperor who’d just woken up. No Shadow stared at me before answering.

“Three days.”

“Three days?”

“Yes.”

“……Then how long was I asleep?”

“Three days as well.”

I squeezed my eyes shut.

Three days. In just three days, everything had gone to shit.

To hell with being an emperor. I’d gone from a handsome guy in his early twenties with his whole life ahead of him to a middle-aged man who looked ancient, and everything I’d achieved had gone up in smoke.

*System window…open.*

I silently tried the command, just in case.

Nothing. No clear chime, no translucent holographic window.

*What the hell is going on? How did things get like this?*

I couldn’t breathe.

The levels I’d busted my ass to gain, my stats—I couldn’t even access the System window now. I might never make it back to the real world.

*Wait. Then what happened to the original me?*

My eyes flew open. I shouted at No Shadow in a panic.

“Then what happened to me…? No, what happened to Jin Taekyung?”

No Shadow blinked above his pitch-black mask.

“You mean Jin Taekyung of the Jin Family of Taiyuan?”

“Yes, the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, Jin Taekyung! The handsome, tall guy with perfect muscles who somehow isn’t popular with women!”

His answer brought my thoughts to a halt.

“He has yet to awaken.”

“What?”

“For reasons unknown, he has not regained consciousness. I regret to report that, according to the examination of the one called the Divine Physician, he is as good as dead.”

“……!”

“As a result, His Highness the Crown Prince has refused food and drink for three days. He said there was something he must return to a friend with whom he shared a bond…”

No Shadow trailed off and carefully held something out. I accepted it in a daze.

Rough, cold metal.

Gleaming like obsidian, it was the divine artifact of the Sichuan Tang Clan—the one I’d given Zhu Bao in case the worst happened.

*The Myriad-Poison Ring.*

I could see the boy who’d promised to survive and return it to me before long.

But…

*Damn it.*

I didn’t know how it had happened, but that shitty fate had twisted my life into knots once again.

Jin Taekyung of the Jin Family of Taiyuan was gone.

And perhaps Jin Taekyung the Hunter’s life in Korea had ended here too.

*Crack.*

At some point, I’d clenched my fist so hard my knuckles had gone white. Then I saw the wrinkles covering the back of my hand, and the strength left me.

*Slip.*

My fingers loosened. The Myriad-Poison Ring rolled over my rough skin and fell onto the bluestone floor.

*Clang. Clang.*

The cold sound echoed alone through the vast room.

I watched the ring spin, the hope gone from my eyes. Every time its black gem caught the light of the night-shining pearls, faces I would never see again flashed before me.

My family, more precious to me than my life. Team Leader Choi and so many other comrades.

All the people I trusted, and who trusted me.

And someone else I’d only just learned had been born to the same fate.

*The Martial God.*

Another System user. Another Player.

Someone chosen by this cruel fate long before I was.

Where was he now?

Had he gone through the same thing?

Or had the System chosen me as a new Player because he really was dead?

I didn’t know.

Not right now. Perhaps not ever.

I was no longer the chosen one or a Player. The System knew the truth, but it wouldn’t answer my questions.

No. I wouldn’t be able to hear its answer.

That was how the System worked. Its power was meant for one person alone.

*And so…another Player will appear.*

The hollow thought hovered on the tip of my tongue without becoming words.

I stared at the Myriad-Poison Ring, still spinning on the blue stone.

Still spinning. And spinning.

“……?”

Wait.

Something was wrong.

“H-how long is that thing going to keep spinning?”

No Shadow answered my cautious question.

“This is why I hate people who catch on too fast.”

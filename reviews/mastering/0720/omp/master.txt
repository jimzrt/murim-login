# Chapter 720

Whew.

A cool breeze brushed past me. I took a small, deep breath and looked around.

The Sacred Land.

Could any place deserve that name more?

But where I stood was only its body.

*Even this Sacred Land has a heart of its own.*

A place filled with the energy of life and Purification. The place that had brought my dying body back to life.

I walked slowly toward the distant pond.

There was no sun, yet the light falling from above was warm. Flowers and grass bloomed lushly in every direction.

Jeok Cheongang and the Beast Miao King stared at the strange space, stunned. Their lips moved as though they wanted to say something, but they closed their mouths and followed me.

Their patience didn’t last long.

*Splash.*

I waded straight into the pond. Cold water soon rose to my waist, and Jeok Cheongang cried out in alarm.

“Th-that bastard…!”

Apparently he thought I meant to kill myself.

He looked ready to grab me by the collar and drag me out. Instead of explaining, I bit my pinky.

*Crunch.*

A faint sting. The skin split, and blood flowed.

I grinned at the wide-eyed Jeok Cheongang and dipped my hand into the clear water.

*One. Two. Three…*

I counted slowly in my head before pulling it out. In barely thirty seconds, my pinky had healed completely.

Jeok Cheongang, who had looked ready to jump in after me, and the Beast Miao King, who had been trying to stop him, muttered in disbelief.

“What in the world…”

“…Everything Yohi said was true.”

There was a reason people came up with the saying that seeing once was better than hearing a hundred times. I could talk until my mouth went dry, but showing them was faster.

*A little performance.*

It worked. And I’d confirmed something for myself, too.

*The pond still works.*

I’d been a little worried. Like a Golem collapsing when its core was destroyed, the Sacred Land might have vanished once demonic qi consumed the sacred stone.

But that worry disappeared as cleanly as the wound on my pinky. In its place came something close to certainty.

*This is it. The answer.*

The System had never lied to me. It presented the facts as they were; finding a new path in its maddeningly plain text was up to me.

Just like now.

*The Quest information said “by any means.” I’m sure of it.*

That was right. My mission had been to *dispose of* the Corrupted Divine Artifact, not *destroy* it.

I’d caught on rather late, but my memories of the guardian spirit had led me back to this place. To the pond that had healed me when I was near death and cleansed the impurities from my body.

*Healing and Purification.*

I didn’t know where its mysterious power came from. I only knew I had no better option.

I walked toward the center of the pond.

*Swish.*

Gentle ripples spread around me. The pond wasn’t deep, and a face tight with tension wavered on its clear surface.

*Gulp.*

I swallowed and looked at my clenched fist. I felt the hard surface of the Corrupted Divine Artifact—and the powerful demonic qi pulsing up between my fingers.

*…If this backfires, we’re in for a real shitshow.*

I wasn’t kidding. There was enough demonic qi here to wreck the pond and the entire Sacred Land besides. This place would become the real, actual Poisonblood Grounds. The guardian spirit, fresh from filing his change-of-address paperwork in the afterlife, would turn up in my dreams every night to hiss at me.

But…

*What else am I supposed to do? There’s no other option.*

Fuck it.

I squeezed my eyes shut and plunged the Corrupted Sacred Stone into the water.

*Splash! Fwoooosh!*

The calm surface broke. Spray shot up and soaked me from head to toe.

And then…everything went quiet.

“…?”

What the hell? Why was it so quiet?

I hesitated through a moment that felt like an eternity, then opened my eyes.

A little. Just a little.

The instant I peered through them—

*Blub blub.*

Bubbles rose to the surface.

*Whoooooosh.*

Water streaked with murky darkness began to spiral up from the center of the pond.

Which meant…

*Fuck. I’m standing right on it.*

The realization had barely struck when an enormous pillar of water surged up beneath my feet.

* * *

*Kwaaaaaaah!*

I had no time to react. Water and darkness filled everything around me.

I fought to move against the chaos that had swallowed me without warning. Or I tried to.

I would have struggled with all my strength if I hadn’t realized the truth a moment later.

“…!”

I could see it. Feel it.

Water and darkness spun fiercely around me. But so did fresh air and wind—things I couldn’t have felt underwater.

*Air? Wind?*

I blinked and looked around.

I wasn’t floating beneath the surface. The water that had risen to my chest was gone. Solid ground lay beneath my feet, while water and darkness swirled endlessly on every side.

*It…wrapped around me?*

There was no doubt. Rather than sweeping me away, the enormous pillar of water had enclosed me. Beyond the waves and darkness walling me in, impossible scenes flashed past.

*Fwoooosh!*

*This is…*

I understood at once. I wasn’t hallucinating from shock. I was seeing the history held within this pond.

Or perhaps the memories of the divine artifact that had shared this land’s fate since its birth.

“…The sacred stone.”

The words escaped me in a murmur. I stared at the pillar of water surrounding me.

Time flowed through its ceaseless waves.

A sprout pushed up from barren ground and grew into a towering tree. Grass and flowers spread, and animals appeared one by one.

*So fast.*

The scenes reflected in the water changed in less than an instant. Day and night traded places dozens of times in the blink of an eye. On one day, thunder and lightning filled the world. On another, a mountain collapsed and erased a river.

Hundreds of years. Perhaps thousands.

Through it all, the sacred stone remained where it was. And others stayed beside it.

Horses. Monkeys. Leopards. Bears…

Beasts of different kinds and shapes, though none could truly be called a mere beast. Countless among them shone like stars in the night sky, then faded. At last, a tiger as white as snow took its place beside the sacred stone.

*The guardian spirit.*

Then darkness came. A darkness dreadful enough to make me shudder. Sticky, wicked qi surged through the waves.

After all those years, the memories had reached that day.

But the final guardian spirit charged forward, its white mane streaming behind it. With a roar, it bit and clawed at the darkness.

Until demonic qi seeped into the wounds covering its body. Until its blue-white eyes turned black.

There, the sacred stone’s memories ended.

No. Perhaps this was a new beginning.

*Swish.*

The water rippled again. The darkness vanished, and a face appeared in the pillar surrounding me.

A young man stood with his eyes squeezed shut, plunging something he held deep into the pond. I knew him too well to mistake him.

The sacred stone’s newest memory began with me.

And that could mean only one thing.

*Ding.*

A clear chime rang in my ears. The darkness writhing in the pillar of water faded without a trace.

As I stared, a translucent holographic window appeared before me.

> **System**
>
> - The Pond of Life purifies all evil and impure energy.
>
> - The demonic qi that had seeped into the Corrupted Divine Artifact has completely disappeared!
>
> - **Mission:** Dispose of the Corrupted Divine Artifact (Complete)
>
> - You have successfully completed the Quest, Corrupted Divine Artifact!
>
> - You have obtained a vast amount of EXP and Fame as a Quest completion Reward!
>
> - Level Up!
>
> - Level Up!
>
> - You have accomplished an unbelievable achievement!
>
> …
>
> …
>
> …

*Ding. Ding. Ding.*

System notifications rang without pause, and holographic windows filled the air. But all of it felt distant. Through the slowly dispersing pillar of water, a single object shone.

*Swish.*

As if entranced, I reached out. The water parted before my hand.

It was smaller and lighter than a child’s fist, yet impossibly warm. The moment my fingertips touched it—

*Fwoooooosh!*

Dazzling light swelled outward. The pillar that had surrounded me shot into the sky and burst apart.

*Fwoooooosh!*

The power of life spread beyond the hidden Sacred Land, on toward somewhere far away.

With the new sacred stone in my hand, I tipped my head back and looked up. Another clear chime rang out among the falling droplets.

*Ding.*

> **System**
>
> - The energy held within the Pond of Life becomes rain and seeps into every part of this land.
>
> - The earth will grow more fertile, the plants will stay green, and the wounded will be healed.
>
> - The Pond of Life has lost all its energy and dried up. But do not worry. Time has the power to change many things.
>
> - The sacred stone will remain in this land, as it always has. Together with a new guardian spirit.

*A new guardian spirit?*

Before I could grasp what that meant, I heard footsteps.

*Thud.*

A large forepaw came down on the dry ground where the pond had been.

Snow-white fur stirred in a breeze from somewhere, and its blue-white eyes, clearer and brighter now, looked just like someone’s in my memories.

“…So it’s you.”

*Grrrr.*

Muyaho—no, the new guardian spirit—came closer and rubbed his head against me.

I stroked the back of his neck without a word. Jeok Cheongang, who had watched it all in a daze, finally managed to speak.

“…Can’t this old man take him with me?”

The Beast Miao King’s face went stern.

“Do you think I’d let you?”

Neither of them noticed the green leaf that had just poked up beneath the rain falling around them.

Nor did they know that the rain would soak all of Nanman for three days.

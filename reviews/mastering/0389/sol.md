Students at last freed from the college entrance exam were busy either preparing to retake it or enjoying themselves, while office workers with dark circles beneath their eyes packed onto public transportation.

Amid that peaceful, ordinary routine, a bombshell no one had expected dropped.

**Breaking News—United Nations Security Council Makes Major Announcement**

The roughly thirty-minute video began with Chairman Xiao Yang staring gravely into the camera.

“I stand before you as the ninth state chairman of the People’s Republic of China and a member of the United Nations Security Council to address the massive monster wave that has erupted in Sichuan Province.”

It was a bombshell that seized the attention of the entire world and shook all of Asia.

The battlefield where fierce combat would soon erupt was packed. An endless wasteland stretched before us, with countless monsters filling the horizon.

The wind carried the creatures’ thick killing intent and stench toward us.

“Fucking hell. They sure gathered a lot of them.”

Team Leader Choi, standing beside me, answered my mutter.

“No matter how many we kill, there’s no end to them.”

It was the fourth day since we had been deployed to the western front and the fighting had begun in earnest. There was no trace left of the Team Leader Choi who had always looked immaculate.

Covered in blood and dust, he regarded me with calm, somber eyes.

“When do we begin?”

“Who knows? We should ask what our little commander thinks first. Right?”

That last question wasn’t directed at Team Leader Choi.

Our twenty-one-year-old “little commander,” who had barely left my side, answered.

“I will follow Teacher Jin’s orders!”

The sparkle in Shao Shen’s eyes made me snort with laughter.

“You’re still going on about calling me Teacher. You were the one who told me to speak casually to you first. I said you might as well call me hyung.”

“C-can I really do that?”

“I told you it was fine as long as you were okay with it. But can you really act like this in front of your men? I heard you’re about to be promoted to major general.”

Shao Shen shook his head at lightning speed.

“There’s no problem at all! H-h-hyung-nim!”

He was frighteningly calm in battle, so I had no idea why he stammered so much the rest of the time.

I looked at the roughly one thousand Hunters from the Public Security Armed Forces lined up behind him.

Their heated eyes shone with admiration and awe for the strong.

Of course, no one’s burned brighter than Shao Shen’s.

“Give us your orders, H-hyung-nim.”

“Orders, huh?”

I suddenly looked up at the sky.

A huge eagle circled overhead with its wings spread wide.

Today felt good too.

“Follow me. Just like you have until now.”

“…!”

“Right now.”

Crack.

The force of ten thousand geun concentrated in the tip of my foot split the ground like a spiderweb and drove it inward.

Boom!

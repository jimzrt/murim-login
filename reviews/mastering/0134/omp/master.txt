# Chapter 134

*Smack!*

Woo Jintae’s head snapped to the side with a satisfying crack. He stared blankly as he touched his cheek.

*What just happened?*

He was dumbfounded. Had he just been slapped? By a punk like this?

Woo Jintae was nearly thirty. He had married young and already had a family of his own.

Yet some kid who barely looked twenty had subjected him to such humiliation.

“Eek! Young Hero Woo!”

“H-Hyung, are you all right?”

“Isn’t that the Young Bureau Head of the Seongun Escort Bureau?”

“That’s him. My goodness, what kind of reckless bastard would… Wait. Doesn’t he look familiar?”

The young prodigies’ belated reactions and the stares of the inn’s guests doubled Woo Jintae’s shame.

He glared at the little bastard before him, his eyes brimming with killing intent.

“How dare you…”

“That’s not what you should be saying.”

“What?”

“Not *how dare you*. You should be asking *how did you do that?* If you’re a martial artist.”

Woo Jintae froze.

Since childhood, he had enjoyed the full support of the Seongun Escort Bureau, consuming all manner of elixirs and learning First Rate martial arts. Even if he hadn’t been born with extraordinary martial talent, it was only natural that he prided himself on being a move or two ahead of the other young prodigies.

*How did someone like me get caught so easily?*

He hadn’t even seen when or how the bastard approached. The humiliation of being disgraced in front of everyone had overwhelmed everything else.

Only then did Woo Jintae carefully study his opponent.

“Look at those eyes darting around. What, want me to give you my measurements?”

“Who are you, and what sect are you from?”

“I’m Tien Shinhan of the Dodong Sect, you son of a bitch.”

Woo Jintae endured the young bastard’s profanity only because he had finally regained his reason.

Anything could happen in the Murim. If his opponent was a disciple of some great sect, Woo Jintae would have to back down without making a fuss.

*The Dodong Sect? Tien Shinhan of the Dodong Sect?*

Both the sect and the man’s name were utterly unfamiliar. Woo Jintae glanced behind him and saw the same bewilderment on the other young prodigies’ faces.

His mind raced.

*As far as I know, there’s no Dodong Sect in Shanxi Province.*

Establishing a new sect in Shanxi Province, where the existing sects had already staked out their territory, was nearly impossible.

Even setting aside interference from the other sects, word of it would spread immediately.

*If they raised a young prodigy of this level at his age, the sect must possess considerable power… Where could it be? Shaanxi? Henan?*

As Woo Jintae hesitated, his eyes darting around, the young martial artist who had been standing by and watching as if this had nothing to do with him suddenly asked,

“What’s the Dodong Sect? Is it a real sect?”

“Of course not. Tell a lie convincingly enough, and sooner or later it becomes the truth.”

“Ah. I’ll engrave those golden words upon my heart.”

“You little punk. You’re talking pretty for once.”

The young man dressed in beggar-like rags added,

“Tell a lie convincingly enough, and it becomes the truth… Those are wise words. I can sense profound mysteries within them.”

“Ah… right. Thanks for the compliment, I guess.”

Sparks flew from Woo Jintae’s eyes as he listened to the three of them.

“You bastard! Reveal your identity!”

“Why? So you can hear who I am and where I’m from, pick a fight if I sound like an easy target, then tuck your tail between your legs and apologize if my status is too high?”

“…”

Crude as it was, the remark hit the mark. When Woo Jintae couldn’t answer, the young bastard clicked his tongue.

“Punk, you started with the wrong question. Talk about the guilty party getting angry. If you wanted an answer from me, you should’ve apologized first.”

Woo Jintae bit his lip.

Apologize?

Born the sole male heir the Seongun Escort Bureau’s family had produced in three generations, he had spent his entire life being pampered and revered. He wasn’t flexible enough to bow his head to some nobody of unknown origin.

Having made up his mind, Woo Jintae subtly signaled the other young prodigies of the Five Gates of Shanxi.

*He can’t be that strong.*

There were only three of them. Woo Jintae’s side had five First Rate masters, each counted among Shanxi Province’s foremost young prodigies.

The sight of the other four discreetly gripping their weapons at his signal reassured him.

“Do you know who I am—or rather, who we are?”

“I happen to know your names, at least. But is that an apology?”

“An apology? You’ve got some nerve.”

Woo Jintae sneered.

“We are the heirs of the Five Gates of Shanxi. Surely you won’t claim you’ve never heard of them.”

“Fine, I’ll say I have. But are you really not going to apologize? This is your final warning.”

“I’ll warn you, too. I don’t know where you came from, but you picked the wrong person to mess with—”

*Smack!*

Woo Jintae’s vision flashed white.

He stumbled backward instinctively as lightning-fast slaps struck him one after another.

*Smack! Smack! Smack-smack!*

One slap for every step.

After taking five slaps in all, Woo Jintae staggered.

The relentless blows had struck only one side of his face, leaving his cheek grotesquely swollen and blood pooling inside his split mouth.

*Even my father has never hit me.*

It was the first humiliation of his life, and it had happened in front of everyone.

His eyes rolled wildly.

“You fucking—!”

“I told you it was your final warning. Do you not know what *final* means, what *warning* means, or neither?”

“How dare you hit me?”

“I might be the first man who’s ever treated you this way, but please don’t fall in love with me. I’m busy enough loving Miss Song-i.”

“I’ll kill you!”

“I’ll beat you!”

*Smack-smack-smack!*

“Guh!”

Woo Jintae couldn’t regain his senses.

The blows carried no internal energy, nor did his opponent seem to be using any mysterious manoeuvre technique. Yet he had no way to block the storm of slaps raining down on both cheeks.

*How is this possible?*

As he suffered helplessly, a crazed shout rang in his ears.

“Slap with the left hand, slap with the right!”

*Smack-smack!*

“One chi, two chi, three chi, four chi—ppukku cheek! Ppukku cheek!”

*Smack-smack-smack!*

His attacker kept shouting incomprehensible nonsense as he slapped him over and over. He looked like a madman born for the sole purpose of smacking people across the face.

Just like now.

“Raise your left hand and lower your right. Lower your right and lower your left. Lower your left, and don’t raise your right.”

“Hurk!”

“That’s right. Good job. As a reward, your teacher is going to give little Woo Jintae’s cheeks a thorough beating.”

*Smack-smack-smack-smack!*

How many times had Woo Jintae been struck in that brief instant?

Thirty? Fifty?

The nonstop blows left his head ringing and tears welling in his eyes.

In the end, all he could do was cry for help.

“P-please! Somebody help me!”

* * *

The Young Bureau Head of the Seongun Escort Bureau—the very bureau whose wealth alone was said to rival that of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, the richest family in Shanxi—cried out desperately.

“P-please help me!”

Yet even after Woo Jintae cast aside all pride and begged, the young prodigies of the Five Gates of Shanxi couldn’t bring themselves to draw their weapons.

“How are we supposed to beat that…?”

The words slipped from someone like a groan, speaking for them all.

They were right. There was no need to experience it firsthand.

Just watching was enough to make their knees buckle and their legs go weak. Their opponent might have been around their age, but he was unquestionably a master who far exceeded their imagination.

“To reach that realm at his age…”

“He’s a complete monster.”

*Smack-smack!*

“P-please spare my life!”

Woo Jintae’s voice grew even more desperate. At some point, his plea for help had subtly changed.

If this went on much longer, he might start begging them to kill him instead.

“W-we should help him, right?”

“At the very least, we should try to stop him… Young Master Wang, do something.”

“Me? Why me all of a sudden?”

“Why do you think? Every time we meet, you brag about your family’s martial arts. You said your sword arts were nothing to be ashamed of anywhere in the Central Plains.”

The heir of the Wang Family Estate, who never missed an opportunity to boast about the greatness of his family’s martial arts, answered with a solemn expression.

“I use a saber. I never learned sword arts.”

“What?”

“And why are you pushing this onto me? If Young Lady Shin wants to stop him so badly, she can step in herself.”

“My goodness. Unbelievable.”

As the young prodigies of the Five Gates passed the responsibility around, Hyuk Mujin, who had been silently watching, suddenly cut in.

“You’re all wasting your time worrying. If it were me, I’d hurry over and lend a hand.”

“You—no, sir. Who are you?”

With such a terrifying scene unfolding before them, even an unremarkable martial artist warranted a degree of politeness.

Young Lady Hwang, who had received Shu brocade from Woo Jintae only moments earlier, quickly stepped forward.

“Let me make this clear. We bear no ill will toward any of you. You understand, don’t you?”

“Really? If you insist, then let’s say that’s true.”

“I’m not asking you to pretend. I’m telling you it’s the truth.”

“What good will telling me do?”

Hyuk Mujin grinned and jerked his chin toward Jin Taekyung, who was enthusiastically beating Woo Jintae.

“Our Captain has quite a temper. Once the gentleman getting beaten over there collapses, who do you think will be next? You, Young Master? Or the Young Lady beside you?”

“…”

“Oh, and just so you know, that man hits men and women alike.”

“I-I didn’t laugh!”

“Neither did I!”

“Do I have to repeat myself? Telling me won’t do you any good. Although there is one thing that might work.”

“One thing?”

The young prodigies’ ears perked up.

They hadn’t trained in martial arts for nothing. They knew they had no chance against that monstrous young man.

“S-somebody save me…”

Woo Jintae’s dying voice drifted into their ears at that moment, further inflaming their survival instincts.

“W-what exactly do we have to do?”

Hyuk Mujin stroked his chin, putting on a grave expression.

“Well, you’re all scions of powerful families, so I’m not sure you’ll be able to manage it.”

“I will!”

“I’ll do it! Whatever it is!”

“Good. At least you understand the basics.”

Hyuk Mujin nodded in satisfaction and pointed at Cheongpung, who was watching the one-sided beating with his mouth hanging open.

“First, offer this gentleman a sincere apology.”

Before he had even finished speaking, the young men and women of the Five Gates bent deeply at the waist.

“We sincerely apologize.”

“We’re truly sorry for judging you so rashly based on your appearance.”

Cheongpung scratched the back of his head.

“I’m fine, so please stand up.”

Considering how they had mocked him in front of so many people, Cheongpung forgave them so readily that even Hyuk Mujin was taken aback.

His face, which should rightfully have been flushed with anger, was as bright and innocent as ever.

“You’re forgiving them that easily?”

“Yes. Is there a problem?”

“Not exactly, but… Weren’t you angry earlier? They humiliated you in public.”

“Why would I be angry? I just found it fascinating.”

“What? What do you mean?”

“That was the first time so many people had paid attention to me. It was also the first time someone who looked down on me apologized afterward. I found it fascinating and fun.”

“…”

“Ah, I really am glad I came down from the mountain.”

*This guy’s a little nuts, too.*

Hyuk Mujin stared at Cheongpung as if he were some strange creature, then shook his head and turned to the young prodigies.

“You heard him. Since the Young Hero here has accepted your apology, we’ll consider that matter settled.”

“Oh!”

“We’re saved!”

But Hyuk Mujin wasn’t finished.

“All right, then. That leaves the second and final condition.”

“…The second?”

“There’s more?”

“Yes. This is the most important one.”

Then he solemnly declared to the four men and women staring at him,

“All of you, get down and plant your heads on the floor.”

“…!”

“…!”

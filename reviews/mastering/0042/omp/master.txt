# Chapter 42

A Gate.

The greatest scar left by the Great Cataclysm.

More than thirty years ago, Demon King Asmodeus had used Gates as invasion routes. Long since reduced to a means of earning a living for Hunters, they were now excavation sites for Magic Gems, a higher-dimensional energy resource.

“You’re with the Peace Guild, right?”

A middle-aged man in work clothes approached us from the entrance.

He was a civil servant from the Gate Management Office, one of whom was stationed at every Gate.

“Yes.”

The civil servant nodded at Team Leader Choi’s curt reply.

“You’re right on time. When will you be entering?”

“We’ll gear up and go in immediately.”

“There’s a waiting room on the second floor. Come back down when you’re ready. See you then.”

We followed Team Leader Choi inside. The two-story building housing the Management Office was as run-down as they came, and the waiting room wasn’t much better.

*Damn. That smell.*

The stench of sweat hit me the moment we opened the door. Rusty lockers and an overflowing trash can stood out too.

“This place is awful. Don’t they ever air it out?”

“That’s Gate officials for you. It’s not called a cushy post for nothing.”

Leaving the grumbling behind, I changed into my raid gear: leather armor and lightweight combat boots. Finally, I drew a spear from its long case and gripped it.

*It’s been a while.*

The way it fit perfectly in my hand felt both familiar and strange.

It was definitely different from the weapon I’d used over the past month… No. I shouldn’t think about that anymore.

*I have to forget all of it now.*

As I needlessly tightened the perfectly good laces on my boots, Im Kkeokjeong came over.

In full-body armor and carrying a massive tower shield, he looked every inch the main tank.

“You ready?”

“Yes. Pretty much.”

Im Kkeokjeong looked me up and down, then clicked his tongue.

“Would you look at this guy. How old is that gear?”

“Who knows? I bought it when I first started, so at least seven years?”

Back when I was a rookie, I’d splurged on it at an underground shop in Dongdaemun.[^1] I still remembered the exact price.

1.98 million won.

*I could barely sleep for days after buying it.*

Being a Hunter was a job where the expenses ran almost as high as the income. Most of those expenses went toward equipment, all because of the unique nature of Gates.

*Gear that isn’t imbued with mana falls apart in no time.*

That was why the most common approach was to make equipment using Magic Gems harvested from Gate monsters.

The higher the Grade of Magic Gem used, the more the price shot through the roof.

“Seven years? Good grief. I know you like money, but you have to spend it when it counts. You really want to pinch pennies when your life’s on the line?”

“Hmm. I had my reasons.”

Reasons I didn’t particularly want to discuss. Im Kkeokjeong spoke in a worried voice.

“You’re in a noncombat position today, so you should be fine… but be careful.”

“Yes.”

I felt strange even as I answered. His words reminded me of someone who, until recently, had constantly been telling me to be careful.

But the sentiment didn’t last long.

“Is everyone ready?”

When Team Leader Choi appeared, my jaw dropped.

*No way.*

“Is that a Red Drake leather set?”

“Hmm.”

Team Leader Choi’s face hardened. I’d only ever seen equipment like that in luxury-store catalogs, and the words had slipped out before I could stop them.

“Ah, sorry…”

“It’s nothing. It was merely made using five types of magic and B-grade Magic Gems.”

“…”

“Not that I’m emphasizing this, but the materials are five types of magic and B-grade Magic Gems.”

“Ah. Right.”

“Red Drake leather is popular because of its distinctive sheen, but that’s all for show. It isn’t actually very useful.”

Team Leader Choi moved his rigid body, and a faint red glow flashed across the armor.

“But it is very beautiful.”

“…”

“Let’s head downstairs.”

Team Leader Choi left the waiting room first. Im Kkeokjeong came over to me.

“That guy loves it when people recognize his equipment.”

“I mean, I get it, but… what’s with him?”

“No idea. He’s an equipment nut. There’s even a rumor he blew his entire fortune on that set and sleeps in it every night.”

*So he’s a nutjob too.*

I sighed inwardly and prayed that today’s raid would end safely.

* * *

“This isn’t the number of people you registered.”

The civil servant looked displeased. Fair enough. We were supposed to enter now, but the entire party hadn’t arrived.

*How long are we going to have to wait?*

Six people had gathered in front of the Gate. Excluding me, the porter, that left only five combatants.

Since this was an E-rank Gate, we needed at least five more E-rank Hunters… and with those Peace Guild bastards nowhere in sight, the civil servant had every right to be annoyed.

“If you add personnel, you need to inform us in advance.”

*Huh? What did he just say?*

Additional personnel?

“I’m sorry.”

At Team Leader Choi’s curt apology, the civil servant picked up his pen and struck several lines through the paperwork.

“We can amend it, so it’s not a major problem… but please be more careful next time.”

“Yes.”

*Not a major problem, my ass. We’re missing people!*

I poked Im Kkeokjeong in the ribs.

“Hyung. When are the others getting here?”

“What others?”

“The Peace Guild.”

“Team Leader Choi’s right there.”

“What?”

“Oh, did I not tell you? The Peace Guild is new, so it doesn’t have many members. There are only three in total, including the Guild Master. Hahaha.”

…You’re laughing?

“Come on, hyung.”

“I know, punk. But you don’t need to worry.”

Im Kkeokjeong patted my shoulder and pointed at Team Leader Choi, who was signing the paperwork.

“That man’s a C-rank Hunter.”

“Oh. C-rank…”

I shut my mouth.

If Team Leader Choi was a C-rank Hunter, there was no problem. No, he was far better than ten E-rank Hunters. He was a mid-rank Hunter, in a completely different league from a low-rank Hunter like me.

*I should’ve realized the moment I saw him wearing that luxury gear.*

The man was walking around with a high-end apartment wrapped around his body. Most low-rank Hunters couldn’t afford equipment like that even if they scraped together their entire fortunes ten times over.

A faint halo seemed to shine behind Team Leader Choi.

“Please verify the signature.”

Even the way he handed the pen back to the civil servant was elegant.

Every little movement seemed to drip with wealth.

*So this is the dignity of a C-rank Hunter.*

*That’s fucking cool.*

I wanted to become sworn brothers with him. I wanted to swear a gay oath beneath the Gate—no, a Gate oath.

“All right, then. Let’s go in… What is it?”

Team Leader Choi flinched and looked at me.

“Nothing. I just thought you looked cool.”

“Excuse me?”

“The equipment. I said it looks cool.”

At that moment, the corner of Team Leader Choi’s mouth twitched.

“It’s nothing. It was merely made using five types of magic and B-grade Magic Gems…”

*A nutjob I wanted to befriend.*

* * *

I took a step toward the Gate.

Whoosh—

A familiar sensation enveloped me. The cool, sticky energy unique to mana.

Then the scenery changed.

“It’s a cave.”

Just as Team Leader Choi said, this raid was taking place in a cave. The moisture-laden walls were damp, and everything around us lay submerged in faint darkness.

“Mr. Taekyung. Take out a flashlight.”

“Yes.”

I quickly set down my backpack. It was a porter’s bag Team Leader Choi had handed me just before we entered the Gate. It looked ordinary, but it was an expensive item enchanted with both space-expansion and weight-reduction magic.

“Here’s the flashlight.”

I handed it over since he’d asked, but in my experience, using one wasn’t the best approach. It was convenient, but if the light went out, your eyes wouldn’t be able to adjust to the sudden darkness.

It was much better to wait a little and let your eyes adjust first…

Click.

“Let there be light. Light.”

Whoosh. A ball of light the size of a soccer ball shot from the flashlight and smacked against the cave ceiling.

“…Magic?”

“It has a built-in spell that activates as soon as you recite the incantation. It lasts quite a while, so we shouldn’t have any problems for the next three or four hours. It’s a limited edition I bought from Company M…”

Im Kkeokjeong, who had been watching, summed it up in three words.

“It’s crazy expensive.”

Was Team Leader Choi’s face darkening slightly, or was that just the shadows? Either way, the raid had become much easier thanks to him.

*Money really is the best.*

Capital ruled the world, and Gates were no exception. People with money fought comfortably with magic equipment. People without it carried torches.

“Take your positions.”

Everyone responded immediately to Team Leader Choi’s command. Im Kkeokjeong and the other E-rank tank took the lead, with Team Leader Choi behind them. I, the porter, stood at the very back with the two ranged damage dealers.

“Move.”

We began advancing slowly.

The bright magic light illuminated the path ahead.

“…”

*I want one.*

* * *

“Kiiiieet!”

The creatures crouching in the darkness emerged with shrill cries. Green skin. Short torsos. Hideous limbs.

Hobgoblins, a more powerful species of ordinary goblin.

*About thirty of them.*

They would be difficult for only five people to handle.

*But having a C-rank Hunter changes everything.*

On top of that, the other four were veteran E-rank Hunters with at least a decade of experience each.

I hung well back and watched the battle.

“Ranged!”

Arrows flew the instant Team Leader Choi gave the order. One shot, one kill. The arrows buried themselves in vital points, dropping the Hobgoblins without shields one after another.

“Kiit!”

“Well, look at that. Shield-bearers. What do we do?”

“Hold.”

Team Leader Choi followed with another order.

“Tanks, advance.”

The tanks moved forward in thick full-body armor, tower shields raised. Im Kkeokjeong and the other man both stood over 190 centimeters tall, making them intimidating as hell.

“Kiiiieet!”

But monsters were called monsters for a reason. Trusting in their numbers, the Hobgoblins swarmed forward like bees.

But…

“Well, now.”

Bam! Bam! Bam!

They struck the big, beautiful tower shields and went flying, reduced to bloody pulp. Seizing the momentum, Im Kkeokjeong swung his mace like a flyswatter. Limbs shattered and skulls burst with every blow.

“Take that! And that!”

…What was this, whack-a-mole?

*Veterans, all right.*

All four of them stuck to their roles without overextending, steadily whittling down the enemy’s numbers.

Knowing when to retreat and when to advance was the kind of combat intelligence that came only from extensive experience.

“Everyone, hold your positions.”

Then one man moved.

The man who had been controlling the flow of battle until now.

“I’ll handle the rest.”

A red streak flashed across his armor as he plunged among the remaining twenty Hobgoblins.

Slash—

A head soared into the air along a clean arc. It happened so quickly that the monsters couldn’t react.

“Kiiie?”

Slash. Slash. Slash.

The blade never stopped. Merciless and efficient, it pierced and sliced through the monsters’ vital points. This was no longer a battle.

It was a massacre. One man slaughtering some twenty monsters.

*This is a C-rank Hunter.*

I could see his speed and strength. All of it.

And I knew.

*He really is strong.*

As a mere F-rank, I couldn’t even dream of matching him. He was a mid-rank Hunter three full levels above me—a wide river and a high wall that effort alone could never bridge.

But at the same time, a question surfaced.

*What if this were Murim?*

Team Leader Choi was undeniably strong. He had the abilities befitting a C-rank Hunter and plenty of experience.

But if this were Murim… if he were a martial artist of Murim…

*I’d be stronger.*

His movements were fast and powerful, but that was all. He hadn’t learned martial arts, nor did he use mana efficiently.

At best, he was first-rate. In Murim, that was as far as he would go.

*But this is reality.*

I bit my lip without realizing it.

A mid-rank Hunter and a bottom-rank Hunter.

While the mid-rank Hunter, clad in expensive equipment, slaughtered monsters, the bottom-rank Hunter could only stand and watch.

As a porter.

“Kiiieet…”

The last Hobgoblin collapsed. Team Leader Choi pulled his sword from its chest, and our eyes met.

“Mr. Taekyung. Please handle the byproducts.”

“…”

“Mr. Taekyung?”

I wanted to tell him that I was stronger than he was.

But in the end, I couldn’t.

Because this was my reality.

“…Thank you for your hard work.”

I *used to be* stronger than you.

[^1]: Dongdaemun is Seoul’s major wholesale-market district.

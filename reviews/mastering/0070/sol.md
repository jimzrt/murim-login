# Chapter 70

Outside, dawn was still gray. The winter air was cold as ice, and frost crunched underfoot with every step across the training ground.

Jin Mukyung had already warmed up. He grinned when he saw me.

“Well? Are you mentally prepared?”

Of course not. But I hid my churning stomach and nodded.

Someone once said that if you couldn’t avoid something, you should enjoy it. Thinking of this as just another step toward becoming stronger made me feel a little more at ease…

Shing.

“Then let’s see those impressive skills of yours.”

*Is this bastard Korean? Why is he so damn impatient?*

There was no time to dodge. Jin Mukyung closed the distance in an instant, and I hurriedly swung my spear at him.

Clang! Screeech!

Jin Mukyung’s breath drifted between us over our locked weapons.

“Your stance isn’t bad.”

Of course it wasn’t. I had survived seven years in Gates with nothing but a spear while my life hung in the balance. The experience I had built up with it was on a completely different level from my fists and kicks.

I glared into his eyes and answered.

“This time, it won’t be easy.”

One word at a time. Clear and deliberate.

“You…”

Had he sensed my unusual aura? Jin Mukyung’s eyes trembled.

“You’d better watch how you look at me.”

“Oh.”

* * *

Swoosh. Thud!

Jin Taekyung collapsed.

It was hardly surprising. This was already his fifth knockout.

The real surprise lay elsewhere.

*I can’t believe he lasted two hundred exchanges against me.*

Five spars over two hours.

Anyone would have been exhausted. But Jin Taekyung was different. He kept springing back up like a roly-poly toy, growing stronger each time. By the final spar, even Jin Mukyung had been forced to take him seriously.

*What the hell is this guy?*

Unarmed, his movements had been as clumsy as those of an eighty-year-old man. But the moment he picked up a spear, everything changed.

The coward who had fawned over Jin Mukyung to avoid a beating vanished. In his place stood a seasoned spearman.

*He was like a wandering martial artist. Someone who had survived countless brushes with death.*

His movements were so simple they seemed crude, while his irregular attacks and evasions relied entirely on instinct. A wandering martial artist’s fighting style followed no fixed pattern.

That was exactly how Jin Taekyung had looked to Jin Mukyung.

Which only made the question more baffling.

*How?*

Even among the heirs of prestigious families who picked up a sword as soon as they learned to walk, countless people spent their lives hovering around the threshold of First Rate. Yet this man had reached the wall of the Peak realm in only three years.

And instead of reeking of courtesans’ face powder, he carried the unmistakable air of a seasoned wandering martial artist. The more Jin Mukyung thought about it, the more absurd it seemed.

*Is that even possible?*

Technically, yes.

First, a Supreme Peak master would have to cleanse his tendons and marrow. Then elixirs would have to be used to improve his constitution. After that, he would need to swing a spear until he shit blood while building real combat experience.

Every single day for three years!

“…That’s ridiculous.”

Jin Mukyung muttered hollowly and scratched his head.

Then only one answer remained.

*A genius.*

That one word resolved every question.

Why?

Because he was a genius. Someone blessed by heaven, just as the word implied. Someone born with extraordinary talent.

Geniuses were ahead in every way. They started from a completely different place.

“Zzz… Hoo…”

“…”

But of all people, this guy was a genius?

Impossible. It couldn’t be true!

Anger suddenly surged within Jin Mukyung, and he kicked his younger brother in the rear.

“Prrrblblbl.”

Jin Taekyung rolled across the ground and shuddered.

“W-Wolhwa noona. Not there.”

“…!”

“If you suddenly do that… Ah. Aah!”

Snap.

The last thread of Jin Mukyung’s patience broke.

* * *

Beep!

> **System**
>
> Sleep Mode has been forcibly terminated!

*So it can be forcibly terminated?*

My joy at discovering a new function lasted only a moment. Why had Sleep Mode been forcibly terminated?

Because someone had woken me up, obviously.

“On the sacred training ground, you say what? ‘Not there’? ‘Not there’?”

Whack! Whack! Whack!

Curled up like a shrimp, I pleaded in a dying voice.

“Please spare me…”

“No!”

Thwack-thwack-thwack!

I had no idea how many times he hit me. I lost consciousness twice in the middle of it.

My last memory was of using the last of my strength to leave a dying message on the training ground before collapsing. When I opened my eyes, it was already night.

*What did I even do for it to be night already?*

I felt like a time traveler. Ever since I met Jin Mukyung, entire days had been flying by.

Pain swept through my body as I muttered, “Jin Mukyung really is strong.”

Aside from Jin Mukyung, I had faced two Peak masters so far.

The Head Elder and Jopil.

*The Head Elder doesn’t count.*

In his case, I had been blessed with incredible luck. Without the sacrifices of the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s martial artists and Lee Cheonbaek’s surprise attack, I could never have defeated him, even if I died and came back to life.

*Then how does Jopil compare to Jin Mukyung?*

I did not have to think for long.

I had fought both of them, so the choice was easy.

*Jin Mukyung is stronger.*

Jopil was unquestionably a monster. Even now, I got goose bumps whenever I remembered the savage heat pouring from his Flame Divine Palm.

But how should I put it? He had the strength expected of a Peak master and wielded a deadly martial art in the Flame Divine Palm, but that was all.

*More precisely, it’s the difference in how they use their martial arts.*

Jin Mukyung was different. Even during our spars, he had used at least ten different martial arts to shut down every one of my attacks.

To a martial artist, each martial art was another weapon. Jin Mukyung knew exactly which weapon to draw in any given situation.

*And compared to him, what do I have?*

Setting aside the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, which I had possessed from the beginning, the only martial arts I had learned in Murim were the Jin Family’s Spear Technique and the Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique.

Of course, both were unquestionably First Rate martial arts.

But put another way, they were only useful at the First Rate level.

The limitations of my martial arts. There was no way Jin Wikyung had failed to notice what I had felt.

*That must be why he put me with Jin Mukyung. Ugh.*

The pain made me wince. I had barely raised my upper body, yet every joint throbbed and my skin stung.

Apparently, even the rest provided by Sleep Mode had its limits.

*Well, I did get beaten half to death.*

To complete the Quest by earning Jin Mukyung’s recognition, I would have to endure more days like today—perhaps even worse ones.

I might simply get beaten senseless and fail the Quest anyway.

*One mountain after another.*

And that made me happy.

Back when I was an F-rank Hunter, I had not even been given the right to climb a mountain. But everything had changed.

Now there was a mountain for me to overcome, and I had been given the chance to climb it.

Faster than anyone else!

*This isn’t the time to lie around.*

Forgetting the pain, I rose. I could not bear to waste another minute or second.

* * *

Jin Mukyung was in the underground training ground.

I descended the stairs as quietly as possible and spotted him below.

“Hah!”

With a short shout, his sword moved.

Whoosh! Shh-shh-shhk!

The blade split the air.

Jin Mukyung moved without hesitation, thrusting and slashing through empty space at the speed of light.

About fifteen minutes passed before he lowered his sword and released a long breath.

“Hoo.”

He wiped away his sweat with his sleeve, then turned toward me.

Apparently, he had noticed my presence some time ago.

“Tell me.”

The question came out of nowhere.

Caught off guard, I asked, “Tell you what?”

“What did you think of the martial art I just performed?”

“Uh, well, first of all, it was really fast…”

“For the record, if you spout pointless bullshit like ‘It was fast’ or ‘It was strong,’ I’ll kill you.”

*Is he a ghost or what?*

I racked my brain for an answer that would keep me alive.

What had Jin Mukyung’s martial art been like?

As I thought back on it, a vague impression began to surface.

“Rough?”

Jin Mukyung’s eyebrow twitched.

*Was that the right answer?*

“Did you just speak casually to me?”

“…It seemed rough.”

“Even a little kid could tell me that. Be more specific.”

The image in my head gradually grew clearer.

I remembered the blade pouring down upon an imaginary enemy and the movements that accompanied it. Fast and unrestrained. And the aura pressing down from every direction.

It was like…

“A waterfall?”

“…”

“Huh?”

*What? Was that the right answer?*

After a long silence, Jin Mukyung abruptly swung his scabbard.

Smack!

“Ow! Why did you hit me?”

“Just because.”

He stared at me with a strange look in his eyes.

“How did someone like you ever come about?”

Was that an insult or a compliment?

I had no idea what he meant, but if only to soothe my wounded pride, I was going to hear the answer. Rubbing my throbbing forehead, I asked, “So, was I right?”

“There are thousands of martial arts manuals in Heaven’s Gate Temple. They were left there by our predecessors to cultivate the younger generation of the orthodox Murim.”

“And?”

“The Falling Flow Sword you just saw is one of them. I found it buried deep in the archives.”

Falling flow. In other words, falling water.

A waterfall.

I had simply blurted out the first thing that came to mind, but it had actually been the right answer.

“Oh, ooh.”

*Am I really a genius?*

If I could recognize something like that after learning martial arts for only two months, even I was frightened by how strong I might become in the future.

“Surely you’re not having some embarrassing thought about being a genius just because you managed that much?”

“…”

*He really is a ghost.*

Still, I seemed to have at least a little talent.

Unable to let go of the thought, I cautiously asked, “Can everyone normally do this much?”

Jin Mukyung flinched.

“O-Of course. Anyone with eyes should be able to figure out that much.”

“Come on.”

“‘Come on’? Want me to pluck out one of your eyes?”

“…That might be a bit much.”

*Why is this bastard so touchy today? Did something piss him off?*

Even after I backed down, Jin Mukyung continued to huff with anger.

“It’s basic. Basic. Anyone can do it.”

“I said I get it. Why do you keep getting angry? You’re scaring me.”

“Are you rebelling against me? Going through your tempestuous phase? Want a taste of the Twelve Gale Fists?”

I did not know what the Twelve Gale Fists were, but getting hit by them sounded painful.

I shook my head furiously, but Jin Mukyung’s anger showed no sign of fading.

“You think you know martial arts? Huh?”

“N-No, sir.”

“How long have you been learning martial arts?”

The answer slipped out reflexively.

“Two months. Two months.”

“Right, a bastard who’s only been at it for two months… What? Two months?”

Jin Mukyung glared at me with bloodshot eyes.

“Not three years, but two months?”

This was an emergency.

It was time for the instincts I had developed over seven years in the workforce to shine. I answered quickly, making sure to emphasize one particular part.

“Three years! Plus two months!”

Jin Mukyung’s fist had been trembling as if he were having a stroke, but it gradually steadied. For some reason, even his voice seemed a little gentler.

“You little bastard. You startled me.”

*You startled me even more, you son of a bitch.*

*Does he have anger-management issues?*

If he asked Jin Wikyung, my lie would be exposed immediately. But at least I would avoid the misfortune of experiencing the Twelve Gale Fists right now.

In any case, Jin Mukyung’s anger subsided.

“I’ll say this only once. Listen carefully.”

“I’ll engrave it on my heart.”

I bowed deeply, and he declared in a domineering tone, “I teach. You obey.”

“…”

*Is this a dog-training school or what?*

“There will be no objections. Why? Because I’m stronger than you.”

It was true, so I had no desire to argue.

Money, power, force. Their forms might differ, but the world always revolved around the strong.

I wanted to stand at its center.

“What will you do?”

My answer had been decided a long time ago.

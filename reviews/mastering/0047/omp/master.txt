# Chapter 47

The Hunter Association.

The name had appeared some thirty years ago, with the end of the Great Cataclysm.

The first-generation Hunters who survived that blood-soaked catastrophe raised its banner, and over the years, the Hunter Association grew into an organization of tremendous stature.

Gulp.

I swallowed as I stared up at the Association building. Even in this dense forest of skyscrapers, it stood out for its sheer size and height. Though it was an ordinary weekday, countless people streamed in and out.

*How many years has it been?*

Every Awakened had their rank assessed under the Association’s supervision.

I was no exception. Thinking back to twenty-year-old Jin Taekyung walking in here full of excitement almost made me laugh—

Like hell it did.

*Now that I’m actually here, I’m nervous as fuck.*

I walked into the lobby on stiff legs. The place was the size of a sports field and packed with people. Following the electronic signs posted throughout the building, I found the place I was looking for.

**Rank Assessment Waiting Room**

At the clerk’s instructions, I filled out the paperwork and went inside. Dozens of people were waiting to have their ranks assessed.

Thud.

The door shut behind me, and every eye in the room shot toward me like an arrow.

*I can’t breathe in here.*

Even the air felt different. Tension hung over the entire waiting room, taut enough to snap.

*One assessment can decide your entire career as a Hunter.*

I’d been the same way. I’d once gotten so nervous that I farted in front of an examiner. That pretty much said it all.

I was waiting my turn, thinking about this and that, when—

“Jin Taekyung?”

A familiar voice came from behind me. I slowly turned my head.

There he was.

“I thought it might be you. And it is.”

A middle-aged man with a bulbous nose and a potbelly. A face I couldn’t forget no matter how hard I tried.

*…Team Leader Kim?*

Kim Sangshik. A founding member and team leader of Sopung Guild, where I’d spent years. He could be summed up in two words.

Former boss.

“Wow, I never expected to run into you here. Good to see you.”

Team Leader Kim thrust out his hand with a hearty laugh. I hesitated for a moment, then shook it.

“Likewise. It’s been a while.”

“What do you mean, a while? It’s only been a few days.”

“Those few days felt pretty long to me.”

I’d said it thinking of Murim, but Team Leader Kim would take it another way. After all, he was the one who’d handed me my dismissal only a few days ago.

“It’s the summer heat. My days have been feeling long lately too.”

“Really?”

Watching him slide past it like a sly old fox, I let out a dry laugh.

He was an entertaining man, every time I saw him.

In more ways than one.

“So what brings you here?”

“I had some business. What about you, Team Leader?”

“I’m here to scout. Heard there was a decent prospect coming in today.”

They’d fired me because restructuring had forced them to cut staff, and now he was here to recruit someone.

“I see.”

That was all I had to say. It was the same tired story everyone knew, and the story was already over. I had no lingering attachment to it.

“What about you? Why are you here? Don’t tell me you’re getting reassessed.”

“Yes.”

Team Leader Kim smiled.

“Well, it’s good to try, but isn’t that a waste of money? A reassessment isn’t cheap. Must be a heavy expense for an F-rank Hunter.”

“I still figured I’d give it a shot. Just in case.”

“You should save while you’re young. What’s going to change if you keep clinging to something that’ll never work?”

“Who knows? I have a feeling this time will be different.”

“It’s not that easy—”

“Team Leader.”

“Huh? What?”

I smiled gently.

“That’s enough.”

A crack appeared in Team Leader Kim’s smile.

“What?”

“I said that’s enough. I’m out of the Guild now, so stay out of my business.”

“What’s that supposed to mean?”

What did he think it meant?

“You know exactly what I mean.”

“…”

*It couldn’t be helped. You’re lucky you survived. Forget the past and make a fresh start.*

“You pretended to console me while working away behind my back.”

“You…”

“Weren’t you the first person to tell the Guild Master to fire me? Did you think I wouldn’t find out?”

Kim Sangshik was a half-baked, petty little man.

Short on humanity and short on ability.

Even inside Gates, he’d always been too busy saving his own skin. His reputation in the Guild had hit rock-bottom.

“Who did you put in my place after you fired me? How much did they pay you to get the job?”

“Hey, Jin Taekyung.”

Team Leader Kim clamped a hand down on my shoulder and growled. He might not have looked it, but he was one of only three D-rank Hunters in Sopung Guild. With that kind of strength, he could toy with an F-rank Hunter like me using one hand.

But—

“Take your hand off.”

I didn’t even blink. The System wasn’t the only thing that had synchronized. My martial arts, my stats, even my steel-like Sinews and Bones had come with it.

“I’ll count to three. Take your hand off.”

“You little bastard. I’ve been letting this slide, but—”

I didn’t hesitate.

“One. Two.”

Three.

The instant I seized Kim Sangshik’s wrist—

Clack.

“Would the next group please come in? Numbers twenty-one through thirty!”

An Association examiner appeared carrying a file, and we sprang apart at the same time. Getting on the Association’s bad side wouldn’t do either of us any good.

“Consider yourself lucky.”

“Who? Me, or you?”

Kim Sangshik’s face, red with rage, looked downright ridiculous.

So did the Level Window I’d read through Qi Sense.

> **System**
>
> Lv. 24 Kim Sangshik

“Seeing you ruined my mood. Let’s never meet again.”

I stood without a shred of regret. My waiting number was thirty.

My steps were no longer stiff as I followed the examiner.

* * *

“Number twenty-one. Please come forward.”

A tense-looking Awakened stepped in front of the measuring device. Made from an A-rank Magic Gem, it scanned his entire body and converted the mana inside him into numerical values.

Bzzzzzt—

The examiner checked the reading.

“Mana distribution: F-rank.”

The Awakened’s face turned ashen. But it was too soon to despair. He still had a second chance.

“Try moving your mana. Concentrate as hard as you can and imagine firing it into the measuring device.”

They were testing his mana control.

Realizing it wasn’t over yet, the Awakened gritted his teeth and gathered his strength.

He strained with everything he had—

Pfffft.

“…”

“…”

Looking ready to vomit, the examiner spoke.

“Control ability: F-rank.”

“Just once! Let me try one more time!”

“No. Next.”

The line moved quickly.

Everyone was E-rank or F-rank. One guy wasn’t even Awakened.

“This is a scam! A scam! That measuring device is made in China, isn’t it? Huh? You bastards!”

“Remove him.”

At the examiner’s command, the security Hunters waiting nearby dragged the fraud away. Even if that guy miraculously Awakened one day, he’d probably end up on the Association’s blacklist.

“Next. Number thirty.”

Here it came.

I took a deep breath and stepped forward. The examiner glanced at the file in his hand.

“You’re here for a reassessment?”

“Yes.”

“Mr. Jin Taekyung, you received an F-rank assessment seven years ago… You know reassessments require a separate fee, correct?”

From his tone, he might as well have been telling me not to waste my money and to go home while I still had the chance.

The usual way people treated an F-rank Hunter.

*Who does he think he’s talking to, some beggar?*

I was used to it, but that didn’t make it any less insulting. When I glared at him, the examiner let out a short laugh.

“I’m only mentioning it in case you weren’t aware, but the fee is two million won.”

“…The price went up?”

“It did a few years ago.”

*Fuck. I didn’t know that.*

How much money did I have in my account right now…?

“Then we’ll begin the assessment.”

With my heart trembling, I closed my eyes.

And the next moment—

Bzzzzzt.

A wave of mana flowed from the measuring device and swept over my entire body.

My fifteen years of internal energy responded, quivering inside me.

*What rank will I get?*

C-rank? No, I’d be happy with D-rank.

But more than ten seconds passed, and the examiner still hadn’t spoken.

“Uh… Why is it doing this?”

“What’s wrong?”

He looked back and forth between the device and me, visibly flustered, then cleared his throat.

“There seems to be some kind of error… We’ll move on to the next step for now.”

I had no idea what was happening, but strangely enough, it didn’t feel ominous.

*I’ve got a good feeling about this.*

My heart pounding, I drew up my internal energy.

Ssshhh.

Answering the call of the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique, fifteen years of internal energy surged through me and shot toward the measuring device.

* * *

At the lobby entrance.

“Well done.”

Kim Sangshik patted a young man on the shoulder. As of today, the promising young man had officially been recognized as a D-rank Awakened.

He would soon join Sopung Guild and be assigned to Kim Sangshik’s team. Imagining that day, Kim Sangshik smiled proudly.

“Father and son on the same team. That’s my boy.”

“Come on, I’m not even an official Hunter yet. I still have to enter the training center.”

“Don’t worry. Your father already made arrangements.”

“Wait, really? Didn’t you say there weren’t any openings in the Guild?”

“There’s always a way.”

He didn’t mention that he’d made room by firing the lowest-rank Hunter, who had long been a thorn in his side.

“Anyway, get plenty of rest this week. Starting next week, we’ll go to work together—”

Kim Sangshik’s expression suddenly twisted.

“What’s wrong?”

“…Nothing. Go wait in the car.”

Once his son had left, Kim Sangshik rolled up his shirtsleeve.

His wrist had already swollen to a dark blue-green. The sight made him grind his teeth.

“Jin Taekyung, that fucking bastard.”

He’d disliked that bastard from the start. He’d hated seeing an F-rank Hunter serve as deputy team leader, and he’d hated the way Taekyung had been like a brother to the former team leader, who was dead now.

*That bastard should’ve fucking died with him back then.*

The tragic accident two years ago had been a stroke of tremendous luck for Kim Sangshik.

After various sex scandals had forced him away from the front lines, he’d made a triumphant return as team leader. A few days ago, he’d even managed to force Jin Taekyung out.

*But did that bastard really reawaken?*

Kim Sangshik stared down at his throbbing wrist.

It had lasted only an instant, but the strength he’d felt had been tremendous. Taekyung might have reawakened as an E-rank, or possibly even a D-rank.

“No. Reawakening isn’t child’s play.”

Maybe he’d grown weaker because he hadn’t exercised lately.

Kim Sangshik was muttering to himself, his thoughts in turmoil, when—

“Breaking news from the assessment room.”

“Someone good?”

“They say a big fish just surfaced. C-rank.”

“C-rank? That’s not bad, but is it really enough to call him a big fish?”

“His mana control is A-rank.”

“What? A-rank? Get your straw in him, now!”

“Yes. This is Choi Minsu from Sangdong Guild. I’m calling because…”

A stir spread among the scouts prowling around the lobby entrance like hyenas.

Most had been dispatched by small and midsized Guilds, but the handful from major Guilds were already moving quickly.

*C-rank alone is impressive, and he’s gifted with mana control on top of that?*

This was a jackpot.

Kim Sangshik’s mind snapped into focus. He shoved every thought of Jin Taekyung aside and pulled out his phone.

—Hey, Team Leader Kim. Did that business go well?

The deep voice on the other end belonged to Sopung Guild’s Guild Master.

Kim Sangshik answered urgently.

“Guild Master, all hell has broken loose here. A C-rank just appeared, and they say his mana control is at the level of a high-ranking Hunter.”

—What? Where did a guy like that come from?

“Exactly. The major Guilds always snatch them away halfway through, but it looks like they were a step too slow this time.”

—Good. So that’s how it is…

Huff. Huff.

Ragged breathing came through the phone, as if the Guild Master was excited. Even the way he addressed Kim Sangshik changed.

—Sangshik. Hold on to this guy no matter what. Tell him we’ll meet any conditions he asks for.

“How high can I go?”

—Don’t worry about the money. Add plenty on top of whatever the others offer. The major Guilds will drop out if they decide he isn’t worth the price. It’s not like they’re desperate.

“Yes, sir.”

—I’m on my way. Keep him there until I arrive. If you pull this off… you know what that means, right?

Kim Sangshik hung up and clenched his fist.

*We’ve got him!*

He was an old hand at this business. Keeping a newly Awakened rookie around would be child’s play.

This was a world where money could put even ghosts to work.

*Twice what everyone else offers. No matter what, I’ll offer double.*

The commotion grew louder. The elevator that had stopped on the third floor, where the assessment room was located, had begun descending toward the lobby.

“He’s coming!”

“Hey, quit shoving!”

Some twenty scouts crowded around the elevator entrance like a swarm of ants. Having muscled his way to the front, Kim Sangshik stood ready with his business card in hand.

Ding.

At last, the elevator doors opened.

Kim Sangshik bowed low and launched into the speech he’d prepared.

“Hello. I’m Team Leader Kim Sangshik of Sopung Guild. We’re a prestigious Guild with a long tradition here in Bucheon—”

“This is the first time I’ve heard anyone call Sopung a prestigious Guild.”

“…What?”

At the familiar voice, Kim Sangshik slowly raised his head.

Their eyes met.

At the same moment, Kim Sangshik’s buttonhole-sized eyes flew wide open.

“You… You…”

Jin Taekyung grinned.

“We meet again, Mr. Kim Sangshik.”

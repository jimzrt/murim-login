# Chapter 790

“Where is The Prophet?”

At that single question, the smile on Team Leader Choi’s lips faded.

Not *What happened to him?* but *Where is he?*

He caught the distinction at once. And I already knew what his brief silence meant.

“You still haven’t found him.”

Team Leader Choi watched me with a grave expression, then nodded.

“No.”

“You don’t even know where he is?”

He didn’t answer. His silence was answer enough.

*Damn it.*

I swallowed the curse and looked up at the night sky.

Being in the same place didn’t mean we were looking at the same thing. Team Leader Choi and I certainly weren’t.

*The Quest still isn’t complete…*

That could only mean one thing.

The being those question marks stood for had never been Michael Silbert.

*Why? Why the hell not?*

I hadn’t seen this coming. I’d been certain that eliminating Michael Silbert was the only way to complete the Quest and stop the coming Cataclysm.

But I’d been wrong. Completely wrong.

*The Prophet.*

That deranged fanatic who called himself God’s messenger and a prophet—the terrorist I’d taken for a lackey following Michael Silbert’s orders—was the real key to this Main Quest.

Or…

He was where the coming Cataclysm would begin.

New holographic windows filled my vision. At the same moment, I understood.

The clock on the bomb called Cataclysm, the one that would soon shake the world, was ticking even now.

* * *

These days, even “global village” sounded outdated.

More happened across the world in a single day than anyone could keep track of. People had grown used to seeing one shocking story after another on TV and the internet, then promptly forgetting them.

But what had happened a week ago had shaken even them.

Across the world, Hunters had launched armed crackdowns and arrests, sudden as land mines going off. Those caught up in them included high-ranking politicians—even presidents—some of the world’s wealthiest people, and Hunters.

Every one of them held influence over global politics, business, or security, far beyond the borders of any one region or country.

People waiting for the new World Hunter Federation’s inaugural ceremony were thrown into confusion. Soon, confusion gave way to shock.

> “The whole truth is in here.”

Choi Minwoo stood before the cameras as interim spokesperson, holding a microchip smaller than a fingernail.

Cameras had been installed throughout the National Assembly to record a moment in history. Only one had survived the tremendous battle, and Choi held what it had recorded.

It was also a Pandora’s box, containing a truth no one could bear to face.

The footage ran for just over three hours. Uploaded to a streaming site for all to see, it plunged the world into silence.

Choi Minwoo had told the truth. Everything was there.

No edits. No lies.

Only facts, plain and undeniable.

Billions of people confronted a truth they could scarcely believe. And inside the Pandora’s box that had suddenly opened, they found something still left at the bottom.

Hope.

But somewhere beyond their sight, something new was already stirring.

Or perhaps a fire was starting—one that would swallow the world.

“Concentrate every force we can mobilize in the Middle East. Right now.”

Choi Minwoo relayed Jin Taekyung’s words straight to the World Hunter Federation’s leadership. No one questioned them.

This wasn’t a request. It was an order.

The first order from the Alliance Leader they had chosen.

And there had never been any doubt about their answer.

“They’re coming.”

A trembling voice echoed through the cave.

The Prophet silently looked down at the man prostrate at their feet.

“What is their estimated strength?”

“Th-that…”

“Answer.”

The man hesitated, then pressed his head to the ground.

“We estimate at least a hundred thousand.”

“…!”

“…!”

A hundred thousand.

The staggering number, far beyond what they had expected, froze the already chilly air in the cave.

Everyone gathered there knew those hundred thousand weren’t ordinary infantry.

*The World Hunter Federation.*

Hunters were rare, whatever their rank. Only one person in a thousand had the chance to awaken, and even then, they had to undergo specific training before they could be recognized as a Hunter.

But their enemy was the World Hunter Federation.

True to its name, every Hunter in the world belonged to that vast organization. From humanity’s billions, millions had beaten the 0.1 percent odds and been chosen by God. Now they were bearing down on the desert.

No—the whole world was.

“The entire desert is under surveillance.”

“We’ve lost contact with our informants in the Pentagon.”

“The residents of nearby villages and small towns are being evacuated.”

“We have reports that the United States has moved an aircraft carrier…”

Their growing fear spilled into their voices. For a long while, they poured out everything they had learned. The situation was already hurtling toward the worst possible outcome.

A week ago, Michael Silbert’s unexpected death had marked the beginning of their downfall.

The newly formed World Hunter Federation had moved boldly, without hesitation. It had begun by bathing its inaugural ceremony in blood, then set about gathering up the seeds Michael Silbert had sown. By revealing the whole truth without hiding or embellishing a thing, it had won support across the world.

Pure goodwill.

Nothing could stop the hammer the World Hunter Federation had brought down. A nation’s president, the head of a corporation powerful enough to sway the world’s stock markets—they had all fallen, bleeding.

Half a century ago, after Osama bin Laden’s terrorist attack, Pakistan and other Middle Eastern countries had refused to let the United States cross their airspace. Even they would not dare oppose this operation.

They could hardly do something as insane as make enemies of the World Hunter Federation and the entire world. If they did, they would be met with artillery fire and a great many Hunters.

But unlike those who could choose to compromise, the people gathered in this cave had no choice at all.

“Prophet!”

“Please, please show us the way!”

Desperate cries rang out from all sides.

Consumed by fanaticism, they knelt and prostrated themselves before one person alone.

They feared the enemy closing in. But they also held fast to their faith in the path they had walked: offering their lives to God, following the prophet He had sent, and witnessing the astounding miracles the Prophet had shown them.

“Guide these lost servants!”

“Defeat the infidels and proclaim God’s will to the whole world!”

*Fwoosh. Fwoosh.*

The torches lighting the cave flickered in the wind. From beneath the Prophet’s deeply drawn hood came a voice whose age and gender no one could place.

“As you wish, I shall carry out God’s will and punish them. Inshallah.”

“O-oh…!”

“God is great!”

“Inshallah. Inshallah!”

The anxiety that had threatened to burst gave way to cheers. As they cried out about God’s greatness and bowed again and again, the Prophet watched them and thought:

The long wait would soon bear fruit.

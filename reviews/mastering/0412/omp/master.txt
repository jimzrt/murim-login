# Chapter 412

*Flap, flap.*

A crow landed in an ancient tree in the middle of the battlefield, its eyes gleaming.

The Arch Lich had perched its Familiar in a spot with an excellent view and was watching the situation unfold with considerable pleasure.

*What will you do, human?*

A lone figure appeared in the crow’s restlessly shifting black eyes.

His mouth hung open. His hands trembled.

The sight of Jin Taekyung made the Arch Lich wonder.

*Was the Death Knight Lord truly defeated by such a cowardly human?*

The Death Knight Lord may have had a few flaws, but it was unquestionably a masterpiece for the ages.

The Arch Lich had been unable to resist coveting Lei Fei, an exceptional specimen with a noble spirit and formidable martial prowess.

That was why it had personally granted him a portion of the mana that formed the very source of its power. Thanks to that gift, Lei Fei had become a being who transcended the limits of an ordinary Death Knight.

There was no way the Death Knight Lord could have fallen to a mere coward.

*What exactly happened?*

That day, the Arch Lich had not watched Jin Taekyung and Lei Fei’s battle to the end. No—it had not thought there was any need to.

By the time the young human arrived, the human army had already been wiped out. The situation on another front had seemed far more important than watching a foregone conclusion play out.

By the time it belatedly sensed the erasure of its precious retainer and sent a Familiar, everything was already over.

All the Arch Lich had seen was Jin Taekyung standing with several thousand humans who had appeared out of nowhere—and the monster army, annihilated to the last.

*That human clearly couldn’t have done it alone. Was he simply lucky?*

But his luck ended here.

Ten Death Knights and Liches possessed the strength of an entire legion, if not more.

*And unlike last time, he has no one capable of helping him.*

There was only one path left for that human.

He would helplessly watch his companions die, then fall himself.

That was the only possible ending—and the punishment the Arch Lich had chosen for him.

*Cawww!*

The crow, sharing its master’s mood, let out a grating cry.

Step. Step.

At that very moment, the human approaching its retainers without fear came to a halt.

Then he raised a trembling hand toward the Death Knights and Liches radiating immense mana.

*Does he intend to surrender?*

The Arch Lich had thought he was merely a coward. It had not expected him to be this stupid as well.

If he believed surrender would persuade the Arch Lich to spare him, he was gravely mistaken—

“One, two, three, four…”

*…?*

The Arch Lich found itself flustered for the first time in ages.

First, because fluent Demon Realm language had flowed from Jin Taekyung’s mouth.

Second, because it had no idea what he was doing.

*What in the world is he up to?*

It did not have to wonder for long.

“…Nine, ten.”

As soon as he finished counting, a drop of saliva fell from Jin Taekyung’s chin.

He wiped his mouth with his sleeve, a satisfied smile spreading across his face.

“The plating is a work of art. Seriously. I hereby award it three Jin-shelin stars.”

*…?*

*…?*

Neither the Arch Lich nor its faithful retainers understood what *plating* or *Jin-shelin* meant.

But they understood what Jin Taekyung’s next action meant.

*Gooooooong.*

The spearhead had already been drawn far back, too fast for the eye to follow. A terrifying torrent of qi gathered around it.

At the sight, the Arch Lich felt as though its cold, hardened heart had plummeted.

A bone-deep chill swept through it, and its thought burst forth.

*Dodge—!*

But the spear shot forward faster than the thought could reach its retainers.

*Woooooong!*

The wind tore apart.

*Kraaaaaaaaaash!*

Space split open.

Within an instant divided into ever smaller slivers of time, a massive vortex wreathed in blue flames swallowed everything.

Countless monsters. Death Knights and Liches.

Even the flock of crows perched in an ancient tree nearby, watching it all unfold.

*Fwoooooosh!*

A blinding flash engulfed everyone’s vision.

* * *

*Whoooooosh.*

A wind from nowhere seeped into my entire body.

It was a wind only I could feel—and the arrival of new power.

*Yes. That’s the stuff.*

I smiled at the empty space beyond the slowly fading light.

That was where the Death Knights, Liches, and countless other monsters had been standing.

Everything had been cleanly erased, leaving the air filled with System notifications.

*Ding. Ding. Ding…*

> **System**
> - You defeated **Lv. 115 Darkened Death Knight**!
> - You defeated **Lv. 120 Darkened Lich**!
> - You defeated **Lv. 122 Darkened Death Knight**!
> - …
> - You have defeated too many monsters!
> - You have gained a massive amount of EXP and Fame!
> - Level Up!
> - Level Up!
> - Level Up!
> - You have accomplished an exceptional feat!
> - As a Reward for your achievement, you have obtained the Title **One Against a Thousand**!
> - Due to the effect of the Title **One Against a Thousand**, all Stats rise by a set amount when facing multiple enemies, and fatigue is greatly reduced! During battle, enemies will be intimidated, while allies’ morale rises dramatically!
> - Because you have accomplished an exceptional feat, a massive amount of EXP and additional Rewards will be granted!
> - Level Up!
> - A new stat, **Intimidation**, has been created!
> - Due to the effect of the Title **One Against a Thousand**, **Intimidation** has increased significantly!
> - Enemies who face you will feel fear and terror!

I closed my eyes and savored the lingering rush the System had given me.

A symphony of System notifications chimed without pause.

So this was what the bells of heaven sounded like.

Four Level Ups had completely washed away the fatigue from One Annihilation. I had gained a Title that would be exceptionally effective on a massive battlefield like this, along with a new stat called Intimidation.

*What a rush. It never gets old. The System is the best.*

If every day were like this, I could die without regrets.

“Is that true? Oh! Ohhh…!”

That fucking bastard.

The Skeleton Warlord’s mood-killing remark made me open my eyes. The first thing I saw was a sea of humans and monsters frozen like statues.

“…Mr. Jin Taekyung.”

「H-Hyung.」

Team Leader Choi and Shao Shen stared at me in a daze.

And it wasn’t just those two.

All two hundred or so members of the suicide squad looked the same.

Their eyes and voices held unmistakable awe as they gazed at me.

「Good heavens.」

「W-What in the world just happened?」

「There were ten Death Knights and Liches. Right there! I saw them with my own eyes!」

Yeah. They had been there.

But now they weren’t.

“Goddamn! Holy shit! What the fuck is this? Fucking! Fucking kimchi! Jesus kimchi!”

“Eeeh, eeeeeh? Nani? Naiii?!”

“…”

Since some of the Hunters had been sent by mercenary companies or the UN, their reactions varied by nationality.

I spoke to Team Leader Choi, who still seemed incapable of forming a sentence.

“Why are you so surprised? It’s not like you’ve never seen it before.”

“Was that what you used when you defeated the Black Drake…?”

“That’s right.”

Team Leader Choi, who had seen One Annihilation once or twice before, swallowed hard.

“…I don’t remember it being this powerful.”

“I was holding back then.”

That was obviously a lie.

The truth was, even I was so stunned by what I had just done that I had no idea what to say.

The first and only time I had used One Annihilation after reaching the Supreme Peak realm was during my battle with the Western Heaven Demon Lord. I had been so exhausted that I lost consciousness immediately afterward.

*I didn’t know it would be this powerful either.*

There had been ten Death Knights and Liches, each nearly a named monster in its own right.

I had thought killing six or seven of them would count as a success.

After all, monsters above Level 100 gave a considerable amount of EXP. Add in all the other small fry, and it should have been enough for a Level Up. I had figured that would keep me from collapsing from Exhaustion and making a spectacle of myself.

*…But this is even more than I expected.*

A double-edged sword.

No—a double-edged spear.

Either way, it was one hell of a finishing move.

It had blown away not only the hundreds of elite monsters packed tightly together, but all ten Death Knights and Liches as well.

That left a massive gap in the monster army surrounding the suicide squad and me, and the monsters themselves were visibly confused.

*I’d be an idiot to waste an opportunity like this.*

I raised my spear without hesitation.

The unexpected turn of events had temporarily brought both sides to a halt. But if the monster army recovered and surrounded the badly outnumbered suicide squad again, there was no telling what would happen.

“What are you waiting for, you bastards?”

“Huh?”

“What?”

“Eeeeeh?”

My shout, charged with internal energy, jolted everyone back to their senses.

There was no time for honorifics or any of that.

Right now, only one thing mattered.

“Wipe out everything in your path!”

“…!”

*Ding.*

> **System**
> - Due to the effect of the Title **One Against a Thousand**, the morale of your allies has risen dramatically! Those who follow your command will form a strong bond and fight even more effectively!
> - Due to the effect of the Title **One Against a Thousand**, the enemy has become greatly intimidated!
> - Due to the Title **One Against a Thousand**, the effect of **Intimidation** has increased!
> - Under the influence of **Intimidation**, the enemy feels fear and backs away!

The System messages described exactly what was happening.

The surviving monsters, having witnessed my overwhelming might, began edging backward. Even the mindless undead flinched and cowered.

As for us?

“Charge!”

*Whoosh! Kra-d-d-d-d-k!*

With a thunderous shout, I leaped into the gap created by One Annihilation and swung my spear in every direction.

The roughly two hundred members of the suicide squad surged forward as one, roaring loudly enough to make my ears ring.

「Waaaaaaaaah!」

「Follow Mr. Jin! Wipe them all out!」

“Fucking! Fucking Jin Genghis Khan! Jesus Kimchiman!”

“Eeeeeeeh?!”

*Splat! Shraaaaaak!*

Green blood sprayed through the air, and undead bones shattered into pieces.

*Slash!*

I neatly split the upper body of a Troll that had turned to flee.

The Skeleton Warlord shouted at me furiously.

“Treacherous human! Over there! Kill that one!”

“What? Who?”

“Kill that strange human who keeps going ‘eeeeeeh’!”

“…”

“Or I’ll kill him myself! If he does it one more time, I swear I’ll kill him!”

“Yeah, okay.”

I’d been hearing it too, and it was starting to piss me off.

* * *

“Ghk!”

The Arch Lich let out a strangled groan.

As if having the Link with its Familiar forcibly severed were not enough, all ten of its guards had been erased in a single blow.

Unlike ordinary monsters, they were retainers imbued with its own power. The Arch Lich could not escape the backlash.

“This is madness. How could a mere human…”

The Arch Lich gathered its disrupted mana, then stopped short.

*A mere human?*

No. That was wrong.

Humans were certainly insignificant beings, but they could never be underestimated.

The Arch Lich had already learned that lesson through bitter experience.

*Could he… Could that bastard be…?*

At the ominous possibility that flashed through its mind, the light in the Arch Lich’s eyes blazed fiercely.

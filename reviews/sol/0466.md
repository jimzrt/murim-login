## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 공력도 사용하지 않고 적당히 힘 조절을 했다지만, 나는 이미 순수한 근력만으로도 인간의 범주를 훌쩍 뛰어넘은 상태다.
- **Current:** Though I said I wasn't using internal energy and was controlling my strength, I had already far surpassed the limits of humanity through pure physical strength alone.
- **Defect:** The draft adds an unspoken claim that Taekyung said this aloud and weakens 적당히 힘 조절을 했다 into a statement that he was merely controlling his strength rather than deliberately holding back.

- **Rationale:** The source directly describes Taekyung fighting without internal energy and moderating his strength; it does not refer to anything he previously said.

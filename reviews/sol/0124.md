# Review

Several actionable fidelity and register issues, including a misassigned subject, altered proficiency, reversed gratitude, lost wordplay, and changed profanity.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “어떤 고마우신 분이 주고 가셨지.”
- **Current:** “Some very grateful person gave it to me before he left.”
- **Defect:** “Very grateful person” makes the giver feel gratitude, whereas the Korean describes him ironically as someone worthy of the narrator’s gratitude.
- **Replacement:** “Some generous soul gave it to me before he left.”
- **Rationale:** 고마우신 분 characterizes the giver as someone the speaker is grateful to, not as someone who is himself grateful.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 효과 : [화염신장]의 습득
- **Current:** **Effect:** Acquire Flame Divine Palm.
- **Defect:** The imperative “Acquire” does not match the source’s nominal description of the item’s effect.
- **Replacement:** **Effect:** Acquisition of Flame Divine Palm.
- **Rationale:** 습득 is presented as the resulting acquisition of the technique, not as a command to the reader.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 초절정 무공을 익힌 조필이 낭인 짓을 하고 있다는 것부터가 수상하긴 하다.
- **Current:** It was suspicious enough that Jopil had been living as a wandering martial artist despite having mastered a Supreme Peak martial art.
- **Defect:** “Mastered” overstates Jopil’s proficiency; the source says only that he learned the martial art.
- **Replacement:** It was suspicious enough that Jopil had been living as a wandering martial artist despite having learned a Supreme Peak martial art.
- **Rationale:** 익힌 establishes acquisition or training, not full mastery, which matters because Jopil’s unexpectedly poor ability is the mystery under discussion.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “……오우야.”
- **Current:** “…Holy shit.”
- **Defect:** The draft adds strong profanity absent from the mild, astonished exclamation in the source.
- **Replacement:** “…Whoa.”
- **Rationale:** 오우야 expresses surprised awe here without the vulgar force of “Holy shit.”

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 삼성이 그 삼성이 아니지만 어쨌든.
- **Current:** It wasn’t those Three Saints, but whatever.
- **Defect:** The translation loses the explicit Korean pun between 삼성, the title just mentioned, and Samsung, the company familiar to the modern narrator.
- **Replacement:** I meant Samsung,[^1] not the Three Saints, but whatever.

[^1]: The Korean name “Samsung” is pronounced *Samseong*, the same as the Korean term rendered here as “Three Saints.”
- **Rationale:** The repeated 삼성이 그 삼성 contrasts two meanings of the same pronunciation; the existing line conveys only a vague distinction and omits the joke.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 화왕은 그것으로 분이 안 풀렸는지 일 년 동안 눈에 보이는 마교도들을 전부 박살 내고 다시 은거했으니까.
- **Current:** Perhaps the Fire King still wasn’t satisfied.
- **Defect:** The draft weakens the specific motive from unappeased anger to generic dissatisfaction.
- **Replacement:** Perhaps that still hadn’t been enough to quell the Fire King’s anger.
- **Rationale:** 분이 안 풀리다 specifically means that one’s anger has not subsided or been vented, explaining his subsequent year-long assault on the Demonic Cult.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 태원진가로 복귀할 수 있을 만한 기력은 충분했다.
- **Current:** he had more than enough strength to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- **Defect:** “More than enough” increases the stated degree of recovery; the source says his energy was sufficient.
- **Replacement:** he had enough strength to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- **Rationale:** 충분했다 establishes sufficiency, not a surplus, while the preceding clause emphasizes that he remained incompletely recovered.

## F09 — Critical

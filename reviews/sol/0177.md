# Review

One critical subject reversal and several meaningful losses of mechanism, characterization, intimacy, and register.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 빙긋 웃는 장천의 모습에 적천강은 말문이 막혔다. 분노도, 배신감도 들지 않았다. 그저 마음 깊숙한 곳에서 뭔가가 치밀어 올라 먹먹해졌다.
- **Current:** At Jangcheon’s faint smile, Jeok Cheongang found himself unable to speak. He felt neither anger nor betrayal. Something merely welled up from deep within his heart and left him numb.
- **Defect:** The draft renders 먹먹해졌다 as emotional numbness, but it describes Jeok Cheongang becoming choked up or overwhelmed as something rises within him.
- **Replacement:** At Jangcheon’s faint smile, Jeok Cheongang found himself unable to speak. He felt neither anger nor betrayal. Something simply welled up from deep within him and left him choked up.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes an emotion rising until he is too choked up to speak, not an absence or deadening of feeling.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 창천검왕이 직접 찾아왔다는 것은 이미 모든 심증과 물증이 갖춰져 있다는 뜻이었지만, 그것으로는 부족했다.
- **Current:** The fact that the Azure Sky Sword King had come in person meant that every suspicion and piece of evidence had already been assembled. But that alone was not enough.
- **Defect:** “Every suspicion and piece of evidence” obscures the explicit evidentiary distinction between 심증 and 물증.
- **Replacement:** The fact that the Azure Sky Sword King had come in person meant that all the circumstantial and physical evidence was already in hand. But that alone was not enough.
- **Rationale:** 심증과 물증 distinguishes the circumstantial case from material or physical evidence rather than referring to suspicions generically.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘방금 드린 절은…… 제 마지막 인사입니다. 저는 이 길로 떠나겠습니다. 멀리 떠나서 다시 돌아오지 않겠습니다.’

- **Rationale:** The source announces Jangcheon’s immediate departure rather than identifying a literal road or route.

# Review

Several action and relationship details are altered, particularly the opening exchange, the training-ground joke, and the promise between Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그 아가리를…….”
- **Current:** “I’ll tear your mouth—”
- **Defect:** The draft supplies the threat before Taekyung gets to finish it for him.
- **Replacement:** “That mouth of yours…”
- **Rationale:** The Demon Lord stops after naming Taekyung’s mouth; the verb comes from Taekyung in the next line.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “찢어 주마.”
- **Current:** “Open it up for you.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung’s completion of the threat loses the explicit act of tearing.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll tear it open.”
- **Rationale:** 찢어 주마 means he will tear it; it completes the Demon Lord’s unfinished line.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나한테 그 말 했다가 죽은 애들이 사열 종대 앉아 번호로 무림맹 연무장 두 바퀴야.
- **Current:** The guys who said that to me are lined up in two rows, counting off, and running laps around the Murim Alliance training ground.
- **Defect:** The men Taekyung killed become living runners, and a column becomes two rows.
- **Replacement:** The guys who said that to me and died could line up in a column, count off, and stretch twice around the Murim Alliance training ground.
- **Rationale:** 죽은 애들이 identifies the dead; 두 바퀴 measures how far their formation would extend, not laps they run.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “참고로 노부는 열 바퀴가 넘는다.”
- **Current:** “For the record, this old man has run more than ten laps.”
- **Defect:** Jeok says his own tally exceeds ten circuits, not that he ran them.
- **Replacement:** “For the record, mine would stretch more than ten times around.”
- **Rationale:** His line extends Taekyung’s joke about lining up the people who died after saying it.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그러니 목숨 걸고 싸우는 멍청한 짓거리 따위는 하지 마라.
- **Current:** “So don’t do anything stupid like fighting as if your life depends on it.
- **Defect:** The idiom makes the fight sound necessary for survival rather than warning Taekyung not to stake his life on it.
- **Replacement:** “So don’t do anything stupid like risking your life in a fight.
- **Rationale:** 목숨 걸고 싸우는 refers to fighting at the risk of one’s life, the behavior Jeok wants him to avoid.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그 약속 지키면, 저한테 뭘 주시렵니까?”
- **Current:** “If you keep that promise, what will you give me?”
- **Defect:** The draft reverses who must keep the promise.
- **Replacement:** “If I keep that promise, what will you give me?”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung is asking what Jeok will give him if he follows Jeok’s request to stay alive.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “오래전, 나와 했던 약속을 기억하느냐?”

- **Defect:** A promise made with the speaker becomes a promise made to him, weakening the deliberate echo between the two conversations.
- **Replacement:** “Do you remember the promise we made long ago?”
- **Rationale:** 나와 했던 describes a promise made with the speaker. The wording recurs when Jeok addresses Taekyung.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “오래전, 노부와 했던 약속을 기억하느냐?”
- **Current:** “Do you remember the promise you made me long ago?”

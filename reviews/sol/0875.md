# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses the assassins’ summons and changes several concrete details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “출(黜)!”

쉬쉬쉬쉭!

그야말로 한순간이었다.

높은 천장에서 뚝 떨어져 내린 십여 개의 날붙이가 내 전신을 에워쌌다.
- **Current:** “Withdraw!”
- **Defect:** The command tells the assassins to leave when the command is followed by their sudden appearance.
- **Replacement:** “Out!”
- **Rationale:** At So Gyo’s cry, the concealed assassins drop from the ceiling and surround Taekyung; she dismisses them only later.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뒤늦게 만독지환의 존재를 눈치채고 강제로 빼앗지 않는 한은.
- **Current:** Unless he noticed the Myriad-Poison Ring too late and forcibly took it.
- **Defect:** “Too late” implies the Emperor has missed his chance, contradicting the possibility that he discovers and seizes the ring.
- **Replacement:** Unless he eventually discovered the Myriad-Poison Ring and took it by force.
- **Rationale:** 뒤늦게 means he notices its existence belatedly, not after seizure has become impossible.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이대로 놈들의 손아귀에 황실이 넘어간다면 끝장이다.
- **Current:** and if we let them take over the imperial court, it was all over.
- **Defect:** The threatened target changes from the imperial family or house to the imperial court.
- **Replacement:** and if the imperial family fell into their hands, it was all over.
- **Rationale:** 황실 denotes the imperial family or house; the concern here is their falling into the Emperor and Dark Heaven’s grasp.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 소교가 허리춤에 찬 연검에 손을 가져가며 대답했다.

“없을 겁니다. 이대로 얌전히 돌아간다면.”
- **Current:** “It won’t. As long as you turn around and leave quietly.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits So Gyo reaching for her weapon as she delivers the threat.
- **Replacement:** So Gyo put a hand on the flexible sword at her waist. “It won’t. As long as you turn around and leave quietly.”
- **Rationale:** The hand moving to her flexible sword makes the warning an immediate physical threat.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 높은 천장에서 뚝 떨어져 내린 십여 개의 날붙이가 내 전신을 에워쌌다.
- **Current:** More than a dozen blades dropped from the high ceiling and surrounded my whole body.
- **Defect:** “More than a dozen” asserts a higher lower bound than 십여 개 warrants.
- **Replacement:** A dozen or so blades dropped from the high ceiling and surrounded me.
- **Rationale:** 십여 개 gives an approximate count of ten-odd blades, not necessarily more than twelve.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 내심 중얼거린 나는 궁인들 사이로 언뜻언뜻 드러나는 어린 왕의 뒷모습을 지켜보았다.
- **Current:** I murmured to myself and watched the young prince’s back, glimpsed now and then between the attendants.
- **Defect:** The narration turns an inward thought into a potentially audible murmur.
- **Replacement:** I thought to myself as I watched the young prince’s back, glimpsed now and then between the attendants.
- **Rationale:** 내심 places the remark in Taekyung’s mind.

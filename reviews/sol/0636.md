# Review

One altered physical action and several meaningful fidelity, hierarchy, causal, and register issues require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내 어이없는 눈빛에 야수묘왕이 입맛을 다셨다.
- **Current:** At my utterly disbelieving stare, the Beast Miao King clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** 입맛을 다셨다 means he smacked his lips, not that he clicked his tongue; the draft changes the physical action.
- **Replacement:** At my utterly disbelieving stare, the Beast Miao King smacked his lips.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the explicitly described action without changing his reaction.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 저게 정말 천년독각사든 뭐든, 짐작대로 이 골짜기가 그리 안전한 곳이 아니라는 것만은 확실해 보였다.
- **Current:** Whether it was really a Thousand-Year Poison Horned Snake or not, one thing was certain: this valley was not as safe as I had hoped.
- **Defect:** The draft changes 짐작대로, “as I had guessed,” into an unexpressed hope and weakens “not a safe place” into a comparative judgment.
- **Replacement:** Whether it really was a Thousand-Year Poison Horned Snake or not, one thing seemed certain: just as I had guessed, this valley was not a safe place.
- **Rationale:** The source confirms Taekyung’s prior suspicion rather than revealing that the valley failed to meet his hopes.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그렇게 만들어진 곳이 독혈지(毒血地)라고 했다.
- **Current:** That place was called the Poisonblood Grounds.[^1]
- **Defect:** The draft omits the causal explanation that the Poisonblood Grounds was created in that manner or for that purpose.
- **Replacement:** The place they created for that purpose was called the Poisonblood Grounds.[^1]
- **Rationale:** 그렇게 만들어진 explicitly connects the Five Poisons Sect’s need for efficient weapons against overwhelming numbers to the creation of this place.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 기록에 따르면 백여 년 전, 남만의 각 부족에서 최고의 정예만을 선발해 독혈지에 진입했다.
- **Current:** According to the records, more than a hundred years ago, the best elites from each Nanman tribe were selected to enter the Poisonblood Grounds.
- **Defect:** 백여 년 means approximately a hundred years, not more than a hundred, and the restrictive 만—only the very finest elites—is omitted.
- **Replacement:** According to the records, around a hundred years ago, only the finest elites from each Nanman tribe were selected to enter the Poisonblood Grounds.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores both the approximate quantity and the exclusivity of the selection.

# Review

The draft contains two altered actions, several lost characterization and register cues, an incorrect role attribution, repeated noncompliance with the established Gaoyuan terminology, and localized punctuation and phrasing issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그 후로는 닥치는 대로 죽이고 사로잡았습니다.”
- **Current:** “After that, we killed and captured them indiscriminately.”
- **Defect:** The draft suggests indiscriminate targeting and uses “and” as though the same people were both killed and captured. The source says they killed or captured everyone they encountered.
- **Replacement:** “After that, we killed or captured every one we came across.”
- **Rationale:** 닥치는 대로 describes dealing with everyone encountered, while 죽이고 사로잡았습니다 presents killing and capture as alternative outcomes.

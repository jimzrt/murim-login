# Review

The draft is broadly faithful but contains one material quantity error, a weakened causal explanation, a lost Korean pun, and several localized action, register, terminology, and Markdown issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 평생을 무교로 살았지만 이제는 아니다.
- **Current:** I had lived my entire life as an atheist, but that was no longer the case.
- **Defect:** 무교 means having no religion, not necessarily affirmatively believing that no god exists.
- **Replacement:** I had lived my entire life without religion, but that was no longer the case.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Taekyung’s lack of religious affiliation without adding an atheist belief.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “10년을 넘게 알았는데 그깟 돈 몇백 때문에 그럴 줄 알았겠냐.”

“하지만 그깟 돈 몇백이 형 전 재산인 거 아냐?”
- **Current:** “I’d known him for more than ten years. How was I supposed to know he’d do that over a few hundred thousand won?”

“But those few hundred thousand won were your entire fortune, weren’t they?”
- **Defect:** The amount is mistranslated by an order of magnitude. In this context 몇백 means several million won, consistent with the explicitly stated five-million-won deposit.
- **Replacement:** “I’d known him for more than ten years. How was I supposed to know he’d do that over a few million won?”

“But those few million won were your entire fortune, weren’t they?”
- **Rationale:** Korean colloquially omits the 만원 unit here; the immediately preceding 보증금 오백 establishes a five-million-won deposit.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘고향이 제주도인가.’

특A급 흑우.

흑우도 이런 흑우가 없다.
- **Current:** *Was he from Jeju Island or something?*

A top-tier sucker.

There was no sucker like him.
- **Defect:** The draft retains the otherwise unexplained Jeju reference but removes the black-cow wordplay that makes it meaningful. 흑우 means both a black cow and, in slang, a gullible sucker, while Jeju is known for black cattle.
- **Replacement:** *Was he from Jeju Island or something?*

A premium-grade black cow.[^2]

There wasn’t a bigger black cow—a bigger sucker—anywhere.

[^2]: “Black cow” is Korean slang for a sucker; Jeju is also known for its native black cattle.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the Jeju setup, the 특A급 grading joke, the repeated 흑우 punchline, and its intended meaning for an English reader.

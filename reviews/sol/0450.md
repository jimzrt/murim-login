# Review

One critical change to location and intent, with additional losses in hierarchy, rank terminology, humor, register, and natural English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 참배객들이 본산 앞마당에서 몰살을 당한 것으로도 모자라 고관대작의 자제들을 건드렸으니 무당파의 분노는 당연했다.
- **Current:** The pilgrims had been slaughtered in front of the main temple, and the killer had even targeted the children of high officials and nobles. Wudang’s fury was only natural.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Wudang’s home ground into a specific main temple and adds that the killer deliberately targeted the officials’ children, whereas the source only says they were among those harmed in the massacre.
- **Replacement:** As if pilgrims being massacred right in Wudang’s own front yard weren’t enough, the victims included children of high officials and nobles. Wudang’s fury was only natural.
- **Rationale:** 본산 앞마당 refers figuratively to the sect’s own home ground, while 건드렸으니 conveys that the children were harmed without establishing that they were specifically targeted.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 일급 낭인이라면 어딜 가도 일류 고수 소리를 듣는다.
- **Current:** A First Rate wandering martial artist was someone who would be recognized as a master wherever he went.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicit First Rate classification of the master he would be considered, weakening the power-scale statement.
- **Replacement:** A first-class wandering martial artist would be considered a First Rate master wherever he went.
- **Rationale:** The source distinguishes 일급 낭인 from the established realm designation 일류 고수 and explicitly states that such a person would be recognized as a First Rate master.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 친우였던 법왕 굉도가 암천에 의해 죽임을 당한 이후, 적천강은 암천의 암자만 나와도 이를 갈긴 했지만 이번 반응은 뭔가 석연찮다.
- **Current:** Ever since his friend Hong Dao had been killed by Dark Heaven, Jeok Cheongang had ground his teeth at the mere mention of its name. But there was something suspicious about his reaction this time.
- **Defect:** The draft omits Hong Dao’s title, Dharma King, and flattens the deliberate joke that even the first syllable of Dark Heaven’s name makes Jeok Cheongang grind his teeth.
- **Replacement:** Ever since his friend, the Dharma King Hong Dao, had been killed by Dark Heaven, Jeok Cheongang had ground his teeth at so much as the first syllable of its name. But there was something suspicious about his reaction this time.
- **Rationale:** 법왕 is the established title Dharma King, and 암자만 나와도 specifically exaggerates Jeok Cheongang’s reaction to hearing even 암, the opening syllable of 암천.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그게 뭔 개소리야?”
- **Current:** “What the fuck does that mean?”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Taekyung’s contemptuous dismissal into a request for clarification, weakening the setup for his immediate reversal once he learns who said it.
- **Replacement:** “What kind of bullshit is that?”
- **Rationale:** 개소리 directly dismisses the statement as bullshit or nonsense; preserving that judgment is necessary for the following comedic backpedal.

## F05 — Minor

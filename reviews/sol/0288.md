# Review

Several subject and action reversals change who performs key actions, while other passages weaken or alter characterization and register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘최대한 시간을 끌게. 놈의 밑천을 탈탈 털어 내.’
- **Current:** *I’ll keep him occupied as long as possible. Find out everything he has.*
- **Defect:** The draft makes Lee Jungryong promise to occupy Taekyung himself, but he is ordering Go Jun to draw out the fight and expose Taekyung’s full capabilities.
- **Replacement:** *Keep him occupied as long as possible. Find out everything he’s got.*
- **Rationale:** The surrounding exchange establishes that Go Jun is asking what he should do, and Lee answers with commands directed at him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “분명 턱을 정확히 가격했는데, 철근보다 단단하더군요. 그런 놈은 처음 봤습니다.”
- **Current:** “He hit me squarely in the jaw, but he was harder than rebar. I’ve never seen anyone like him.”
- **Defect:** The attacker and target are reversed. Go Jun says that he struck Taekyung’s jaw, not that Taekyung struck his.
- **Replacement:** “I hit him squarely in the jaw, but he was harder than rebar. I’ve never seen anyone like him.”
- **Rationale:** 가격했는데 has Go Jun as the contextually established subject, while Taekyung is the person whose jaw proved exceptionally hard.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아차, 하는 순간 팔이 묶였고 발이 땅 깊숙이 박혔다.
- **Current:** In the instant he thought, *Oh, shit,* his arm had been trapped and his foot driven deep into the ground.
- **Defect:** The draft adds profanity absent from the source and renders 아차 as a quoted thought rather than the instant in which Go Jun realizes he has been caught.
- **Replacement:** By the time he realized his mistake, his arm was trapped and his foot had been driven deep into the ground.
- **Rationale:** 아차 conveys a sudden realization that it is already too late; it does not supply the stronger profanity added by the draft.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 늘 기계처럼 무표정하던 그의 얼굴에는 어느새 은은한 두려움이 깔려 있었다.
- **Current:** His face was usually as expressionless as a machine, but now a quiet fear had settled over it.
- **Defect:** The English pronoun most naturally points to Lee Jungryong, the immediately preceding named person, making Lee appear afraid. The source describes Go Jun’s fear of Lee.
- **Replacement:** Go Jun’s usually machine-like expression was now tinged with quiet fear.
- **Rationale:** The sentence follows Go Jun’s inability to meet Lee’s eyes and the statement that Lee is an object of both respect and fear to him.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘내 뒤에서 한 걸음도 나서지 마라.’
- **Current:** *Don’t take a single step in front of me.*
- **Defect:** The command is altered from remaining behind the speaker to merely not stepping in front of him.
- **Replacement:** *Don’t take so much as a single step out from behind me.*
- **Rationale:** 내 뒤에서 … 나서지 마라 explicitly orders Lee not to leave his position behind the speaker, emphasizing the speaker’s protection and authority.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아니, 아직 아니다. 정신을 차리긴 개뿔.
- **Current:** No, we hadn’t. Not even close.
- **Defect:** The draft removes Taekyung’s coarse, self-directed dismissal and changes the singular internal reaction into a collective one.
- **Replacement:** No, not yet. Come to my senses, my ass.
- **Rationale:** 개뿔 is a blunt, dismissive expression that gives the narration its characteristic profane register, while the implied subject remains Taekyung himself.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 박지훈에게 뒤통수를 처맞고 난데없이 이정룡이 튀어나왔을 때, 여기서 더 놀랄 일은 생기지 않을 거라 장담했는데…… 이건 충격과 공포 그 자체다.
- **Current:** When Park Jihoon had hit me in the back of the head and Lee Jungryong had suddenly burst onto the scene, I had confidently declared that nothing could surprise me anymore……
- **Defect:** The idiomatic 뒤통수를 처맞고 is rendered as a literal physical blow rather than Park Jihoon’s betrayal, changing the event Taekyung is recalling.
- **Replacement:** When Park Jihoon stabbed me in the back and Lee Jungryong suddenly burst onto the scene, I’d been certain nothing could surprise me anymore……
- **Rationale:** 뒤통수를 맞다 is the established Korean idiom for being betrayed or blindsided, which fits Taekyung’s recollection of Jihoon’s actions.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 눈을 부릅뜬 채 날 바라보는 최 팀장을 향해 또박또박, 천천히 말을 이었다.
- **Current:** I opened my eyes wide and looked at Team Leader Choi. Then I continued, each word slow and clear.
- **Defect:** The subject of the wide-eyed stare is reversed. Team Leader Choi is staring at Taekyung, not the other way around.
- **Replacement:** To Team Leader Choi, who was staring at me wide-eyed, I continued, each word slow and clear.
- **Rationale:** 눈을 부릅뜬 채 날 바라보는 directly modifies 최 팀장, identifying Choi as the person staring wide-eyed at the narrator.

## F09 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “강하게 만들어 줄게요. 내가.”
- **Current:** “I’ll make us strong. I will.”
- **Defect:** The object changes from Choi and the others to an inclusive “us,” weakening Taekyung’s personal promise that he will make them strong.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll make you strong. I will.”
- **Rationale:** 만들어 줄게요 expresses an offer or promise performed for the addressee; the omitted object is the people Taekyung is addressing, not Taekyung-inclusive “us.”

# Review

The draft contains one subject-and-negation reversal, one distorted causal explanation, one altered reciprocal image, and several localized English issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘욕을 할 수는 있겠지만 적어도 나는 아니야.’
- **Current:** *I could curse him out, but I’m not in a position to.*
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung the person who could condemn Jonghun, whereas the source says that someone else might be entitled to condemn him, but Taekyung is not.
- **Replacement:** *Someone might have the right to curse him out, but not me.*
- **Rationale:** The omitted general subject in 욕을 할 수는 있겠지만 is contrasted with 적어도 나는 아니야, explicitly excluding Taekyung from those entitled to condemn Jonghun.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 나는 후자에 관한 사실은 모르고 있었다. 부산물 처리는 스타 길드의 소관이고, 제아무리 협동 레이드라고 해도 모든 걸 결정하는 지휘관은 원명훈이다.
- **Current:** I hadn’t known about the latter. Processing the monster parts was the Star Guild’s responsibility, and no matter how cooperative the raid was, Won Myunghoon was the commander who made every decision.
- **Defect:** The draft narrows 부산물 to monster parts even though this sentence explains why Taekyung did not know the Magic Gem yield, and it misconstrues 협동 레이드 as a raid with a variable degree of cooperation rather than the established joint-raid arrangement.
- **Replacement:** I hadn’t known about the latter. Handling the byproducts was the Star Guild’s responsibility, and even though this was a joint raid, Won Myunghoon was the commander who made every decision.
- **Rationale:** 부산물 includes the recovered materials at issue, while 협동 레이드 names the type of raid; the point is that the joint operation did not change Won’s sole authority over such decisions.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 놈에게, 놈은 나에게. 서로가 서로에게 씻을 수 없는 기억을 안겨 주었으니까.
- **Current:** I had given it an unforgettable nightmare, and it had given me one in return.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the reciprocal indelible memories into identical nightmares. The source leaves the nature of each memory open: the Wyvern gave Taekyung a nightmare, while Taekyung left it with its wounded eye.
- **Replacement:** Me to it, and it to me. We had each left the other with an indelible memory.
- **Rationale:** 씻을 수 없는 기억 means a memory that cannot be erased, not necessarily a nightmare, and the paired fragments deliberately emphasize the reciprocal but different marks they left on each other.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 간혹 어떨 때는 용기보다 공포를 부추기는 것이 낫다.
- **Current:** Sometimes, it was better to encourage fear than courage.
- **Defect:** “Encourage fear” is an unnatural collocation and weakens the contrast between deliberately stoking fear and inspiring courage.
- **Replacement:** Sometimes, stoking fear worked better than inspiring courage.
- **Rationale:** 부추기다 means to stoke or incite something, while the comparison with 용기 calls for the natural counterpart “inspiring courage.”

## F06 — Minor

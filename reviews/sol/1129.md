# Review

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘하찮은 인간 주제에 이 몸을 그따위 천박한 이름으로 부르다니. 네놈은 이 찬란한 왕관이 보이지 않는 것이냐?’
- **Current:** “Who do you think you are, calling me by such a lowly name? Can you not see this radiant crown?”
- **Defect:** The Skeleton King’s contemptuous address to Jin as an insignificant human is omitted, and 천박한 describes the nickname as vulgar, not lowly.
- **Replacement:** “A mere human like you dares call me by such a vulgar name? Can’t you see this radiant crown?”
- **Rationale:** 하찮은 인간 주제에 establishes the speaker’s disdain; 천박한 이름 condemns the name’s vulgarity.

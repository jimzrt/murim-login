# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses two combat actions and assigns a serious injury to the wrong character. Several smaller changes affect causality, address, and geography.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 오죽하면 외관과는 영 어울리지도 않는, 마조(魔鳥)라는 별호를 스스로 지었겠나.
- **Current:** It was bad enough that he’d given himself a title that didn’t suit his appearance at all: Demon Bird.
- **Defect:** Turns the self-chosen title into another grievance rather than a response to his hated old title.
- **Replacement:** Why else would he have given himself the title Demon Bird, a name that didn’t suit his appearance at all?
- **Rationale:** 오죽하면 presents his choice as evidence of just how much the old title bothered him.

# Review

Several actionable fidelity errors alter subjects, actions, and quantities; the System panel also violates the required formatting.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 빛나는 금안(金眼)으로 자신을 응시하는 그의 모습에, 레비아탄은 감히 동족을 배신한 변절자의 진정한 정체를 깨달았다.
- **Current:** Staring at him with shining golden eyes, Leviathan finally realized the true identity of the traitor who had betrayed its own kind.
- **Defect:** The subject of the gaze is reversed: the Skeleton King is staring at Leviathan with golden eyes, not Leviathan staring at him.
- **Replacement:** When Leviathan saw him staring back with shining golden eyes, it finally realized the true identity of the traitor who had dared to betray his own kind.
- **Rationale:** The possessive 그의 and 자신을 make the crowned Skeleton King the one gazing at Leviathan; the current dangling modifier instead assigns the golden eyes and action to Leviathan.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 자신은 심해에서 처음 눈을 뜬 존재.
- **Current:** Leviathan had been the first being to open its eyes in the deep sea.
- **Defect:** The draft adds an ordinal claim that Leviathan was the first being ever to awaken there.
- **Replacement:** Leviathan was a being that had first opened its eyes in the deep sea.
- **Rationale:** 처음 describes where Leviathan first opened its eyes—its origin or awakening—not precedence over every other being.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 파동에 휩쓸려 사라졌던 뼈들이 다시금 모여든다.

The draft changes Morgoth’s demanded tribute into two deaths, obscures who fills the ruins, loses a typo that drives a joke, and shifts Russia’s conquest from completed to ongoing.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 유령마를 타고 허공을 질주한 데스나이트(Death Knight)들은 각자의 군단을 통솔하여 드넓은 폐허를 메웠다.
- **Current:** Death Knights raced through the air on ghostly horses, each commanding a legion as they filled the vast ruins.
- **Defect:** The English makes it sound as though the Death Knights themselves fill the ruins; the Korean has them lead their legions to do so.
- **Replacement:** Death Knights raced through the air on ghostly horses and led their legions to fill the vast ruins.
- **Rationale:** 각자의 군단을 통솔하여 makes their command of the legions part of how the ruins are filled.

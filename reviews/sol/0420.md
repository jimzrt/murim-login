# Review

Two action-level fidelity errors alter who killed whom and how the mana clouds were struck. Additional findings concern an over-explicit sexual joke, epistemic nuance, characterization, hierarchy, causal clarity, and localized English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 서로를 죽고 죽이는 모습을 보며 실로 오랜만에 커다란 기쁨을 느낄 수 있었다. 인간이 얼마나 하찮은 생물인지 알 수 있게 도와준 네게 감사를 표하마.
- **Current:** Watching them kill each other brought me immense joy for the first time in a very long while. I thank you for helping me understand how insignificant humans are.
- **Defect:** “Them” directly identifies Wu Heixing and Lee Jungryong as having killed each other, but Jin killed both men. The source describes humans killing one another and then explicitly thanks Jin.
- **Replacement:** Watching you humans kill one another brought me immense joy for the first time in a very long while. I thank you for helping me understand how insignificant humans are.
- **Rationale:** The broader human reference preserves the reciprocal wording without falsely assigning Wu and Lee each other’s deaths.

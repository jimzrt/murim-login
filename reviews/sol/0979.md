# Review

Several lines alter physical details, a father’s joke, and the force of his reply to the retainers.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 몇 시진 동안이나 계속된 전투로 혈인(血人)이나 다름없어진 그들은
- **Current:** After fighting for hours, they were little better than walking corpses drenched in blood.
- **Defect:** “Walking corpses” adds a deathlike condition; the source describes fighters covered in blood.
- **Replacement:** After hours of fighting, they were covered in blood from head to toe.
- **Rationale:** 혈인 describes how blood-soaked they have become, not whether they resemble corpses.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 한때 한 자루의 돌격창과 활로 천하를 질타했던 유목민들도
- **Current:** nor the nomads who had once roamed the world with a single lance and bow
- **Defect:** “Roamed” loses the nomads’ former martial dominance.
- **Replacement:** nor the nomads who had once struck fear across the land with lance and bow
- **Rationale:** 천하를 질타했던 evokes formidable power, not merely travel.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 마치 게으른 승려의 머리처럼, 까슬까슬하게 돋아난 머리카락이 타오르는 것처럼 붉다.
- **Current:** His hair was bristly as a lazy monk’s shaved head, and so red it seemed to be on fire.
- **Defect:** A shaved head obscures the image of a monk who has let his hair grow into stubble.
- **Replacement:** His hair had grown into prickly stubble, like a lazy monk’s, and was so red it seemed to be on fire.
- **Rationale:** 돋아난 머리카락 describes hair growing out, which supplies the joke about the lazy monk.

## F04 — Minor

- **Current:** No, wait. Take that back.
- **Defect:** “Take that back” sounds like an order to someone else, though Taekyung retracts his own offer.
- **Replacement:** No, wait. I take that back.
- **Rationale:** 방금 했던 말 refers to what Taekyung himself just said.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 비록 수 갑자에 달하는 공력도, 그들과 어깨를 나란히 할 신위(神威)도 없었으나 알 수 없는 위압감이 있다.
- **Current:** He had neither the internal energy of several jiazi nor the divine presence to stand shoulder to shoulder with them. Yet there was an inexplicable weight to him.
- **Defect:** “Divine presence” turns the martial might he lacks into an aspect of bearing, blurring the contrast with his authority.
- **Replacement:** He had neither several jiazi of internal energy nor the godlike power to stand shoulder to shoulder with them. Yet he commanded an inexplicable authority.
- **Rationale:** 신위 is paired with accumulated internal energy as something the three masters possess; his 위압감 comes from a different source.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 어찌 그러겠습니까. 이공자께서는 타고난 무재가 워낙 뛰어나시니, 속하들로서는 감히 손을 댈 수 없을 지경입니다.
- **Current:** *“How could we possibly do that? The Second Young Master’s natural martial talent is so extraordinary that we wouldn’t dare lay a hand on his training.”*
- **Defect:** The retainer denies being able to guide the child, not merely watching him; “lay a hand on his training” is also misleading.
- **Replacement:** *“We could hardly claim to be teaching him. The Second Young Master is so gifted that there’s nothing we dare try to correct.”*
- **Rationale:** This is a deferential correction of the Family Head’s suggestion that they are overseeing the boy’s training.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래, 당연히 그렇겠지. 누가 낳은 자식인데.
- **Current:** *“Yes, of course. How could he not be? Who do you think gave birth to him?”*
- **Defect:** The father’s boast about whose son the boy is becomes a question about who physically gave birth to him.
- **Replacement:** *“Of course he is. Whose son do you think he is?”*
- **Rationale:** In context, the Family Head puffs out his chest to claim credit for his son’s talent.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99

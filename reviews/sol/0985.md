# Review

The draft largely follows the scene, but it reverses one threat, changes a metaphor’s mechanics, weakens a status title, and leaves square brackets in System notices.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “살아도 산 것 같지 않게 만들어 줘?”
- **Current:** “Want me to make you feel like you’re alive when you’re not?”
- **Defect:** The threat is reversed: Taekyung offers to make Mujin feel as though he is not living, not to make him feel alive.
- **Replacement:** “Want me to make your life a living hell?”
- **Rationale:** 살아도 산 것 같지 않게 describes being alive but unable to feel that way.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 수많은 가지 끝에서 열매를 틔우고, 바다와 같은 호수를 만들었다.
- **Current:** Fruit budded at the ends of countless branches, and together they formed a lake as vast as the sea.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the fruit or branches form the lake, adding a causal mechanism absent from the two images.
- **Replacement:** It bore fruit at the ends of countless branches and created a lake as vast as the sea.
- **Rationale:** The family is the continuing subject; bearing fruit and creating the lake are parallel images.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “그게 무슨. 다른 분들은 몰라도 조장님이 저 자리에 빠지시면 안 될 것 같은데.”
- **Current:** “What do you mean? Maybe no one else needs to be there, but surely Captain, you can’t miss that.”
- **Defect:** Mujin does not say nobody else needs to attend. He says others might be able to miss it, but the Captain should not.

- **Rationale:** 다른 분들은 몰라도 contrasts Taekyung’s obligation with the possibility of others being absent.

# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes who experienced the battlefield deaths, misplaces Jin’s spear, and loses several consequential or character-specific details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 앞서 전장에서 무수한 죽음을 겪은 뒤, 진태경을 경시하던 마음을 이미 일찌감치 내버렸던 도플갱어다.
- **Current:** The Doppelganger had already abandoned its contempt for Jin Taekyung, after witnessing countless deaths on the battlefield.
- **Defect:** The Doppelganger experienced the deaths; it did not merely witness them.
- **Replacement:** After dying countless times on the battlefield, the Doppelganger had long since abandoned its contempt for Jin Taekyung.
- **Rationale:** 무수한 죽음을 겪은 describes deaths the Doppelganger underwent.

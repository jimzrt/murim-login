# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one reversed action, several altered subjects or character beats, an obscured eunuch reference, and localized register and terminology problems.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “으득, 갑시다!”
- **Current:** “Grit your teeth and let’s go!”
- **Defect:** The sound of Gong Ilhyuk grinding his own teeth has been turned into an order for the other men to grit theirs.
- **Replacement:** Gong Ilhyuk ground his teeth. “Let’s go!”
- **Rationale:** 으득 represents Gong Ilhyuk grinding his teeth in suppressed fury; it is not imperative dialogue.

“목소리 줄이라니까. 그리고 난 공 대협한테 실망한 것 없어요. 우리가 친구도 아니고, 서로에게 뭔가를 기대하고 실망할 사이는 아니잖아.”

“I said lower your voice. And I haven’t lost faith in you, Great Hero Gong. We aren’t friends, after all. We’re not close enough to expect anything from each other or feel disappointed.”
- **Defect:** Both instances of disappointment are changed to loss of faith, weakening Hong Jin’s pointed claim that their relationship was never close enough for personal disappointment.
- **Replacement:** “Why are you suddenly acting like this? Are you disappointed in me because of what just happened?”

“I said lower your voice. And I’m not disappointed in you, Great Hero Gong. We aren’t friends, after all. We’re not close enough to expect anything from each other or feel disappointed.”
- **Rationale:** 실망 means disappointment, and Hong Jin deliberately repeats it while denying any personal bond with Gong Ilhyuk.

꼰대? 정확히 무슨 뜻인지는 모르겠지만 그건 중요하지 않다. 뒤에 새끼라는 단어가 붙었으니까.
- **Current:** “Get lost, you old-ass bastards!”

“……!”

“……!”

*Old-ass? I’m not entirely sure what that means, but that isn’t important. He called them bastards.*
- **Defect:** “Old-ass” is not a natural or accurate equivalent for 꼰대, and the unexplained first-person thought assigns the reaction to an unspecified individual rather than the insulted Zhongnan disciples.
- **Replacement:** “Get lost, you boomer bastards!”

“……!”

“……!”

*Boomer?* They didn’t know exactly what it meant, but that wasn’t important. It had been followed by the word *bastards*.
- **Rationale:** 꼰대 is a modern insult for an overbearing, old-fashioned elder; “boomer” preserves the anachronistic term that the Murim characters fail to understand, while the plural subject matches the three insulted men.

- **Source:** “이런 쳐 죽일……!”

# Review

One hierarchy error and several localized subject, pragmatic-cue, humor, terminology, and natural-English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “표두 석도민, 표사 길왕준, 홍석정, 노두삼, 쟁자수 석삼…….”
- **Current:** “Chief Escort Seok D omin, escorts Gil Wangjun, Hong Seokjeong, Noh Dusam, and caravan porter Seok Sam…”
- **Defect:** The draft mistranslates 표두 as the higher rank “Chief Escort,” which is reserved for 총표두, and also introduces an erroneous space into Seok Domin’s name.
- **Replacement:** “Escort Captain Seok Domin, escorts Gil Wangjun, Hong Seokjeong, and Noh Dusam, and caravan porter Seok Sam…”
- **Rationale:** The source identifies Seok Domin as a 표두, not a 총표두, and spells his name continuously as 석도민.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아무리 빈부격차가 심하다지만 은자 천 냥은 평범한 양민이라면 삼 대가 호의호식할 수 있는 거금.
- **Current:** Income inequality might have been severe, but one thousand silver nyang was enough money for an ordinary commoner to live in luxury for three generations.
- **Defect:** The English makes a single commoner live for three generations instead of referring to three generations of a commoner’s family.
- **Replacement:** Income inequality might have been severe, but one thousand silver nyang was enough for an ordinary commoner’s family to live in luxury for three generations.
- **Rationale:** 삼 대가 refers to three successive generations, so the subject must extend beyond one individual.

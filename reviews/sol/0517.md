# Review

Several fidelity defects alter Unnamed’s guilt, turn spoken dialogue into internal thought, lose hierarchy, and change specific imagery or actions; additional localized wording needs correction.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 부끄러울 참. 뉘우칠 회. 참회동은 죄인을 가두는 곳이다.
- **Current:** Repentance. A cave for repentance. Repentance Cave was where criminals were confined.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the source’s deliberate explanation of the two Sino-Korean characters with a tautology, losing the meaning of shame and remorse.
- **Replacement:** The first character meant shame; the second, remorse. Repentance Cave was where criminals were confined.
- **Rationale:** The source separately glosses 참 as shame and 회 as remorse to frame Unnamed’s guilt.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그건 아마도 스승의 열반을 막지 못한 후회가 아니었을까. 무명은 스승이 떠나기 전 맡기고 간 소림의 신물, 녹옥불장(綠玉佛杖)마저 지켜 내지 못한 죄인을 자처하며 그곳에서 석 달이라는 시간을 보낸 것이다.
- **Current:** Perhaps it was regret over failing to prevent his Master’s death. Unnamed had spent three months there, considering himself a sinner who had failed not only to protect his Master, but also the Green Jade Buddha Staff, Shaolin’s sacred treasure entrusted to him before his Master’s death.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Unnamed explicitly consider himself a sinner for failing to protect his Master, whereas the source separately presents that as presumed regret and identifies his self-professed sin as failing to protect even the entrusted staff. It also replaces the Buddhist and euphemistic references to Nirvana and passing away with explicit death.
- **Replacement:** Perhaps he regretted failing to prevent his Master from entering Nirvana. Unnamed had spent three months there, considering himself a sinner because he had failed to protect even the Green Jade Buddha Staff, the sacred treasure of Shaolin that his Master had entrusted to him before passing away.
- **Rationale:** The source distinguishes Taekyung’s inference about Hong Dao’s Nirvana from Unnamed’s guilt over losing the Green Jade Buddha Staff and preserves Buddhist register.

# Review

The draft contains two scene-level subject/action changes, several losses of violent or figurative force, and localized characterization and clarity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “방금 그 말, 믿어도 되냐?”
- **Current:** “You believe what I just said, right?”
- **Defect:** The subject and direction of trust are reversed. Im Changsoo is asking whether he can trust the team member’s promise, not whether the team member believes him.
- **Replacement:** “Can I trust what you just said?”
- **Rationale:** 믿어도 되냐 asks for permission or assurance that the preceding promise can be trusted.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “너 방금 내 욕 했지.”
- **Current:** “You just insulted me.”
- **Defect:** The English implies that Im Changsoo directly insulted Taekyung, but Changsoo only cursed him internally. That distinction sets up Taekyung’s joke about reading his mind.
- **Replacement:** “You were just cursing me in your head, weren’t you?”
- **Rationale:** The surrounding exchange about 관심법 confirms that Taekyung claims to have detected Changsoo’s unspoken abuse.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그 대가로 충성을 받았는데…… 가장 중요한 순간에 외면당하니 뒤통수가 얼얼했다.
- **Current:** He had received their loyalty in exchange, so being abandoned at the most important moment left him feeling numb.
- **Defect:** The generic emotional numbness loses the concrete back-of-the-head image expressing that their betrayal feels like a blow from behind.
- **Replacement:** He had received their loyalty in exchange…but being abandoned at the most important moment felt like a stinging blow to the back of the head.
- **Rationale:** 뒤통수가 얼얼했다 literally evokes the back of his head stinging and figuratively reinforces the sense of being blindsided or betrayed.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘쳐 죽일 놈.’
- **Current:** *That son of a bitch.*
- **Defect:** The translation retains profanity but omits Changsoo’s violent wish to beat the man to death.
- **Replacement:** *That bastard deserves to be beaten to death.*
- **Rationale:** 쳐 죽일 explicitly combines striking or beating with killing, making the thought more violent than a generic insult.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아무리 체면을 구겨도, 이빨이 뽑혀도 호랑이는 호랑이다.
- **Current:** No matter how thoroughly his dignity had been crushed or how many teeth had been knocked out, a tiger was still a tiger.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a figurative, conditional image of a toothless tiger into an assertion that an unspecified number of Changsoo’s teeth were actually knocked out.
- **Replacement:** No matter how badly his dignity had been crushed, even a tiger with its teeth pulled was still a tiger.
- **Rationale:** 이빨이 뽑혀도 means “even if its teeth are pulled,” continuing the tiger metaphor rather than describing an actual injury.

## F07 — Minor

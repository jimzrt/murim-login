# Review

One reversed subject, one potentially misleading physical description, one obscured healing mechanism, and several localized losses of meaning or delivery.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금껏 본 적도, 들어 본 적도 없는 마수의 눈동자에 의문과 죽음이 동시에 내려앉았다.
- **Current:** In the eyes of the monster, which had never seen or heard anything like this before, bewilderment and death settled at the same time.
- **Defect:** The relative clause is attached to the monster as the perceiving subject, making it say that the monster had never seen or heard of the attack. The source instead describes Behemoth as a monster unlike any ever seen or heard of.
- **Replacement:** Bewilderment and death settled at once in the eyes of the monster—a creature unlike any ever seen or heard of.
- **Rationale:** 본 적도, 들어 본 적도 없는 modifies 마수 and expresses the observers’ unprecedented encounter with the creature, not the creature’s experience of the attack.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어두운 얼굴의 금발 외국인과
- **Current:** A blond foreigner with a dark face
- **Defect:** “With a dark face” can describe complexion in English, whereas 어두운 얼굴 indicates a grim or somber expression here.
- **Replacement:** A grim-faced blond foreigner
- **Rationale:** The phrase describes Skeleton King’s troubled expression, consistent with his subsequent guilt and sorrow, rather than his skin color.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 재앙을 막고 불행한 죽음을 막을 수 있는 힘이.
- **Current:** The power to stop disasters and prevent unhappy deaths.
- **Defect:** “Unhappy deaths” is an unnatural collocation and fails to convey the source’s sense of tragic or unfortunate deaths.

- **Rationale:** 불행한 죽음 means deaths marked by tragedy or misfortune, not deaths that are emotionally “unhappy.”

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 조금씩 닫혀 가는 혈도를 붙잡고, 그의 몸 깊숙한 곳에서 꺼져 가는 불꽃을 북돋웠다.
- **Current:** I clung to the acupoints that were slowly closing and fanned the flame dying deep within his body.
- **Defect:** “Clung to the acupoints” obscures the physical mechanism: Taekyung is using his internal energy to keep the closing acupoints open, not merely holding on to them.
- **Replacement:** I held open the acupoints that were slowly closing and fanned the flame dying deep within his body.
- **Rationale:** 붙잡고 in this treatment context means arresting or resisting their closure, which is the mechanism by which he tries to prolong Kim Hwajong’s life.

## F05 — Major

- **Rationale:** 눈짓 섞인 specifies that Taekyung signals with his eyes as he calls to Skeleton King; that silent coordination enables the final deception for Kim Hwajong’s sake.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “내 손…자.”
- **Current:** “My grandson.”

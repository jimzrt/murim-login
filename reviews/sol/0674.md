# Review

One critical causal reversal, four major fidelity or register errors, and three localized presentation issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “어쩌면, 그래. 그랬을 거요. 최소한 그의 뒤를 쫓을 만큼의 시간 정도는 벌 수 있었을지 모르지.”
- **Current:** “Perhaps. Yes, perhaps it was. At the very least, it may have bought him enough time for me to pursue him.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Baeksang’s lingering warmth buy the Beast Miao King time, reversing the subject and beneficiary of the action. Baeksang is admitting that he himself might have bought enough time to pursue him.
- **Replacement:** “Perhaps. Yes, perhaps it was. At the very least, I might have been able to buy enough time to pursue him.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 벌 수 있었을지 is Baeksang, while 그의 뒤를 쫓을 만큼 describes the purpose or extent of the time he might have gained.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 백상은 그녀의 뒷모습을 바라보며 생각했다. 드디어 올 것이 왔다고.
- **Current:** Watching her back, Baeksang thought that the moment he had been waiting for had finally arrived.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an inevitable, dreaded development into something Baeksang had actively been waiting for.
- **Replacement:** Watching her back, Baeksang thought that the inevitable had finally arrived.
- **Rationale:** 올 것이 왔다 means that what was bound to come has arrived; it does not imply anticipation or desire.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “그럴 수밖에 없지 않겠소. 그건 명백한 내 실수였으니.”
- **Current:** “There was no other choice, was there? It was clearly my mistake.”
- **Defect:** The first sentence is Baeksang saying that he cannot help but apologize, not claiming that the underlying action was unavoidable.
- **Replacement:** “How could I not be? It was clearly my mistake.”
- **Rationale:** 그럴 수밖에 없지 않겠소 answers her question about why he is sorry and refers back to his apology.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “우리 신임 궁주님께서 나름대로 애쓰셨다는 건 알았으니까.”
- **Current:** “I knew our new Palace Lord had done his best.”
- **Defect:** “Done his best” sounds like sincere, unqualified praise and loses the patronizing qualification conveyed by 나름대로.
- **Replacement:** “I knew our new Palace Lord had made an effort in his own way.”
- **Rationale:** 나름대로 diminishes Baeksang’s effort while ostensibly acknowledging it, reinforcing the Demon Empress’s superior and condescending register.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “나로서는 대적할 수 없었소. 주위에 대기 중이던 모든 병력을 동원했음에도 포위망을 빠져나가더군.”
- **Current:** “I could not oppose him. Even after mobilizing every soldier waiting nearby, he managed to break through the encirclement.”
- **Defect:** “Could not oppose him” suggests refusal or inability to resist politically, rather than Baeksang being unable to match him in combat.
- **Replacement:** “I was no match for him. Even after I mobilized every soldier waiting nearby, he managed to break through the encirclement.”
- **Rationale:** 대적할 수 없었다 describes an inability to contend with the Beast Miao King as an opponent.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내궁에서도 가장 높은 언덕 위에 우뚝 선 궁주전(宮主殿)의 아래로는 수많은 전각과 가옥이 한눈에 내려다보였다.
- **Current:** From beneath the Palace Lord’s Hall, which stood tall atop the highest hill in the Inner Palace, countless pavilions and houses could be seen at a glance.
- **Defect:** “From beneath” incorrectly places the viewpoint below the Palace Lord’s Hall; the source says the buildings lie below the elevated hall and can be surveyed from it.
- **Replacement:** Below the Palace Lord’s Hall, which stood atop the highest hill in the Inner Palace, countless pavilions and houses spread out in full view.
- **Rationale:** 궁주전의 아래로 describes the location of the visible buildings relative to the hall, not the observer’s vantage point.

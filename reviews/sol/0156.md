# Review

One critical action error plus major fidelity issues involving comparative severity, comic timing, directed instruction, technique terminology, and combat mechanics; several localized register and wording defects also remain.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 순간 숨이 턱 막히는 극통.
- **Current:** A moment later, excruciating pain robbed me of my breath.
- **Defect:** “A moment later” introduces a delay absent from the source, which describes the pain and breathlessness as instantaneous.
- **Replacement:** In an instant, excruciating pain robbed me of my breath.
- **Rationale:** 순간 means “in an instant” or “for an instant,” not a moment afterward.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “……그래, 시발. 쳐라.”
- **Current:** “...Fine, shit. Hit me.”
- **Defect:** The isolated “shit” is unnatural English and softens the force and resigned profanity of 시발.
- **Replacement:** “…Fine, fuck it. Hit me.”
- **Rationale:** 시발 is strong profanity here, while the surrounding resignation is naturally conveyed by “fuck it.”

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 다행히도 우려했던 일은 없었다. 진작 기절해서 쓰러져 있던 혁무진의 엉덩이가 뒤통수를 받쳐 주었기 때문이다.
- **Current:** Fortunately, what I feared didn’t happen. Hyuk Mujin’s butt, which had been supporting his unconscious body for quite some time, caught the back of my head.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Hyuk Mujin’s butt support his own unconscious body. The source instead says Hyuk Mujin had already passed out and fallen, leaving his butt in position to cushion Taekyung’s head.
- **Replacement:** Fortunately, what I feared didn’t happen. Hyuk Mujin had already passed out and fallen over, and his butt cushioned the back of my head.
- **Rationale:** 진작 기절해서 쓰러져 있던 modifies Hyuk Mujin, not his butt, and establishes why his body is already on the ground.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 초면에 그가 빙당호로를 몽땅 줬을 때, 청풍은 눈물이 날 뻔했다.
- **Current:** When Jin Taekyung had given him all the candied hawthorn skewers they had bought when they first met, Cheongpung had nearly cried.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that “they had bought” the skewers, which the source does not state, and omits the required cultural footnote for the established item.
- **Replacement:** When Jin Taekyung had given him every last candied hawthorn skewer[^1] the first time they met, Cheongpung had nearly cried.

[^1]: Traditional fruit skewers coated in hardened sugar.
- **Rationale:** 몽땅 means all or every last one; nothing in the sentence says who purchased them. The ledger specifies that 빙당호로 should be briefly explained at its first meaningful occurrence.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “은인, 소용없어요. 이건 천근추…….”
- **Current:** “Benefactor, that won’t work. This is the Thousand-Kilogram Drop—”
- **Defect:** The technique name incorrectly equates 근 with kilograms, changing the stated traditional weight quantity and inventing an unsupported metric rendering.
- **Replacement:** “Benefactor, that won’t work. This is the Thousand-Catty Drop[^2]—”

[^2]: A catty is a traditional East Asian unit of weight; the technique’s name evokes immense downward force.
- **Rationale:** 천근추 literally invokes one thousand traditional catties, not one thousand kilograms; the unlisted culturally specific technique name also benefits from a brief factual note.

## F12 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 거칠고, 아직 다듬어지지 않은, 그저 그런 무공임에도 묘한 긴장감을 주는 움직임. 자연스럽게 배어 나오는 기세…….
- **Current:** His movements were rough and unrefined. His martial arts were nothing special, yet they created a strange tension. An aura naturally seeped out of him...
- **Defect:** The draft assigns “rough and unrefined” to Taekyung’s movements and makes the martial arts create the tension. The source characterizes the martial arts as rough, unrefined, and ordinary while distinguishing the tension produced by his movements and the aura emanating from him.
- **Replacement:** Though his martial arts were rough, still unrefined, and otherwise unremarkable, his movements created a strange tension. His aura seeped out naturally…
- **Rationale:** The modifiers 거칠고, 아직 다듬어지지 않은, 그저 그런 describe 무공, while 묘한 긴장감을 주는 describes 움직임.

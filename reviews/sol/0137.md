# Review

Two critical subject or identity errors, several altered actions and pragmatic cues, and localized terminology and register issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 별채의 문을 열고 불쑥 들어온 낯익은 얼굴.
- **Current:** A familiar face suddenly appeared as the door to the annex opened.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Mujin’s active, abrupt entrance into a passive appearance, omitting that he opens the door and barges in.
- **Replacement:** A familiar face abruptly opened the annex door and barged in.
- **Rationale:** 문을 열고 불쑥 들어온 explicitly describes the familiar person opening the door and entering uninvited, which motivates Taekyung’s immediate complaint.

## F08 — Major

“아프다니, 그게 무슨?”

“파릇파릇한 무림의 동량들 아닙니까. 강해지려 너무 수련에 몰두한 나머지 몸이 안 좋아져서 픽픽 쓰러진 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “The kids are a little hurt.”

“Hurt? What does that mean?”

“They’re the fresh young pillars of Murim, aren’t they? They focused so intensely on training to become stronger that their bodies gave out, and they keeled over.”

- **Rationale:** 아프다 and 몸이 안 좋아져 preserve Taekyung’s euphemistic cover story; he is pretending their condition is illness from excessive training rather than the result of his beating.

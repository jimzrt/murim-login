# Review

The draft contains several factual shifts, register losses, one established-term mismatch, and an added explanation that makes a euphemistic mercy killing explicit.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 좆 됐다. 아직도 안 되네.
- **Current:** *Fuck. Still not working.*
- **Defect:** The draft reduces a statement that Taekyung is personally fucked to a generic expletive, weakening both the meaning and the strong profanity.
- **Replacement:** *I’m fucked. Still not working.*
- **Rationale:** 좆 됐다 describes Taekyung’s predicament rather than merely expressing frustration.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 가장 먼저 검파를 쥔 것은 나지만, 네 목을 휘두를 때는 혼자가 아닐 것이다. 설령 궁주라 해도 막을 수 없어.
- **Current:** “I may be the first to take hold of the hilt, but I will not be alone when I swing it at your neck. Even the Palace Lord will be unable to stop me.”
- **Defect:** The final clause changes what the Palace Lord cannot stop from the collective execution to Baeksang personally, undermining the point that Baeksang has assembled a coalition.
- **Replacement:** “I may be the first to take hold of the hilt, but when the sword swings for your neck, I will not be alone. Not even the Palace Lord can stop it.”
- **Rationale:** The surrounding lines emphasize that multiple chieftains will act together, and 막을 수 없어 refers to preventing that action rather than merely restraining Baeksang.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99

- **Rationale:** 대회의 is established as “Tribal Grand Council,” and the formal institution matters to the legitimacy of the planned execution.

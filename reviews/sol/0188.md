# Review

The draft contains two critical factual errors involving who speaks with Lee Seowol and the duration of Chulwoo’s duels, along with several major hierarchy, mechanism, action, and characterization errors.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 멸문의 위기와 충성 맹세라는 쉽지 않은 선택의 기로에서 한 단계 성장한 느낌이랄까.
- **Current:** It felt as though she had grown another step while facing the difficult choices between the threat of her sect’s destruction and swearing loyalty to another family.
- **Defect:** The draft presents the threat of the sect’s destruction as one of two choices available to Lee Seowol. The source instead pairs that crisis with her difficult decision to swear loyalty.
- **Replacement:** It felt as though she had grown another step through the crisis that threatened to destroy her sect and the difficult decision to swear loyalty.
- **Rationale:** The threat of annihilation is a circumstance she faced, not an option she could choose.

# Review

Several localized fidelity errors change physical actions, an address, and the final comic beat.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 목말을 타고 있던 불쌍한 노인을 던지듯 내려놓은
- **Current:** tossed down the poor old man riding on his shoulders
- **Defect:** Taishan sets the old man down as if tossing him; the draft says he actually throws him down.
- **Replacement:** set down the poor old man riding on his shoulders as if he were tossing him aside
- **Rationale:** 던지듯 qualifies 내려놓은; it does not replace the action of setting him down.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “오랜만이군, 진 도우(道友).”
- **Current:** “It’s been a while, Daoist Jin.”
- **Defect:** The address makes Jin a Daoist rather than addressing him as a fellow practitioner.
- **Replacement:** “It’s been a while, my friend Jin.”
- **Rationale:** 도우 is an address of fellowship, not a statement that Jin is a Daoist.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “보기보다 좋은 수하를 두었어. 그렇지 않나?”
- **Current:** “You have a better subordinate than I expected. Don’t you think?”
- **Defect:** The comparison is to Taishan’s appearance, not Hyeoncheon’s prior expectations.
- **Replacement:** “He’s a better subordinate than he looks. Don’t you think?”
- **Rationale:** 보기보다 means ‘better than he looks.’

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “빈도(貧道)는 한낱 말코 도사일세. 도가의 경전보다는 검에 심취하여 일평생을 살았고, 피를 흘리며 죽어 가는 제자들을 구할 수도 없었지. 하지만…….”
- **Current:** “I am but a lowly Daoist, a mere ‘horsehair’ of a man. I devoted my life to the sword instead of the Daoist scriptures, and I couldn’t save my Disciples as they bled and died. But…”
- **Defect:** The literal ‘horsehair’ invents an image of the man rather than conveying the dismissive term for a Daoist.
- **Replacement:** “I’m just a wretched old Daoist. I devoted my life to the sword instead of the Daoist scriptures, and I couldn’t save my Disciples as they bled and died. But…”
- **Rationale:** 말코 도사 is a disparaging expression for a Daoist, not a description of horsehair.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99

- **Defect:** The draft excludes junior fellow Disciples.
- **Replacement:** through the sight of their fellow Disciples, drenched in blood from head to toe

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 나는 조용히 눈을 감고 귀를 닫았다.
- **Current:** I quietly closed my eyes and covered my ears.
- **Defect:** Covering his ears adds a physical gesture absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** I quietly closed my eyes and shut out the sound.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “으허허허헝! 어이오고오오!”
- **Current:** “Waaah! Ohhh, you came!”
- **Defect:** The draft turns an indistinct, sobbed cry into a clear statement that Jin has arrived.
- **Replacement:** “Waaah! Uuughhh!”
- **Rationale:** The Korean renders the man’s wailing phonetically; it does not clearly say ‘you came.’ Jin’s ensuing confusion depends on not understanding him.

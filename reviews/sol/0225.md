# Review

The draft contains several factual and relational errors, including altered quantities, an incorrect physical posture, a misassigned metaphor, and softened dialogue.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 이름을 알 수 없는 날짐승들이 무리 지어 날아올랐다.

- 삐이이익!

지진을 피할 수 있었던 것은 거기까지였다.
- **Current:** Flocks of nameless birds took flight.

—Screeeech!

That was as far as anyone could get in avoiding the earthquake.
- **Defect:** The current wording obscures the subject and turns a concrete limitation—the flying creatures were the only ones able to escape—into a vague statement about how far anyone could get.
- **Replacement:** Flocks of nameless birds took flight.

—Screeeech!

They were the only ones able to escape the earthquake.
- **Rationale:** 거기까지였다 refers back to the airborne creatures and establishes that escape was limited to them, which sets up the Star Guild members' lack of wings.

# Review

The draft preserves the main military timeline, but loses Ma Junggeol’s self-correction and changes who is holding his group as leverage. Several smaller dialogue and System-panel choices also need correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저희가 괜히 마적, 아니 마방(馬房)이겠습니까?
- **Current:** Do you think we’re horse caravans for nothing?
- **Defect:** Omits Ma Junggeol’s slip from “mounted bandits” to “horse caravans,” losing a revealing comic beat.
- **Replacement:** Do you think we’re mounted bandits—no, horse caravans—for nothing?
- **Rationale:** He starts to call his group 마적, then corrects himself to 마방.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아직 마중걸을 비롯한 마방들을 향한 의심을 거두지 않은 몇몇 수뇌부들의 강력 주장으로 반쯤 볼모가 된 지금에도, 저리 태평한 모습을 보이고 있으니.
- **Current:** Even now, half the leaders still suspected Ma Junggeol and the other horse-caravan men, and had effectively taken them hostage. Yet he was acting so carefree.
- **Defect:** Changes “some leaders” into “half the leaders” and shifts “half-hostage” from Ma Junggeol’s condition to an apparently complete hostage-taking.
- **Replacement:** Even now, at the strong insistence of a few leaders who still suspected Ma Junggeol and the other horse-caravan men, he was practically being held hostage. Yet he seemed utterly carefree.
- **Rationale:** 몇몇 modifies the leaders; 반쯤 modifies his hostage-like status.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “아이, 씨. 깜짝이야. 갑자기 뭡니까?”
- **Current:** “Shit, you scared me. What’s with you?”
- **Defect:** Turns a clipped, mild curse into an unqualified stronger one and drops the complaint that Ma appeared suddenly.
- **Replacement:** “Ah, damn. You scared me. What’s with you all of a sudden?”

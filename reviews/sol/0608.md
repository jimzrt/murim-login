## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 수조 원의 재산과 그룹을 소유한 재벌이자, 과거 미 대통령이었던 아버지에 이어 미합중국 역사상 역대 세 번째 부자(父子) 대통령이라는 기록을 세운 인물이다.
- **Current:** A tycoon who owned conglomerates and assets worth trillions of won, he had made history as the third father-and-son presidential pair in United States history, following in the footsteps of his father, a former U.S. president.
- **Defect:** The source says he owns a single corporate group and a fortune worth trillions of won; the draft pluralizes the group and grammatically applies the valuation to both the conglomerates and the assets.
- **Replacement:** A tycoon with a business empire and a fortune worth trillions of won, he had followed his father, a former U.S. president, to make history as the third father-and-son presidential pair in United States history.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the source's singular 그룹 and attaches the monetary value specifically to his wealth.

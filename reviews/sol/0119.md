# Review

The draft is broadly faithful but contains one critical factual softening, several altered mechanical or quantitative details, and localized register shifts.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 기존의 공력으로 억눌러도 될까 말까인데, 아예 고삐를 놓은 상태로 풍양과 접전을 벌였더니 점차 악화되어 가고 있었다.
- **Current:** I could barely suppress it with my existing internal energy. After engaging Pung Yang in a fierce battle while letting the reins go completely, my condition was gradually worsening.
- **Defect:** The draft first states that Taekyung was successfully suppressing the qi, then says he had completely released it. The source instead says suppression would have been uncertain even if attempted, while he actually fought with the qi entirely unrestrained.
- **Replacement:** It would have been touch and go even if I’d tried to suppress it with my existing internal energy, but I’d fought Pung Yang at close quarters with the reins completely off, and my condition was steadily worsening.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts a hypothetical attempt at suppression with Taekyung’s actual decision to let the Scorching Yang Qi run completely free.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘지금 상태대로라면 잠력단의 효능은 길어 봤자 한 식경…….’

한 식경, 그 안에 승부를 봐야 한다.

마지막 남은 잠력단은 결코 쓰여선 안 된다. 연이어 세 개를 복용한다면 목숨이 위험할 수도 있다.

“한 식경이라…….”
- **Current:** *At this rate, the Temporary Strength Pill’s effect will last one quarter of an hour at most…*

He had to decide the battle within that time.

He must not use the last Temporary Strength Pill. Taking three in succession could put his life at risk.

“One quarter of an hour…”
- **Defect:** The draft converts the approximate traditional duration 한 식경 into an exact quarter hour and omits that this is the remaining duration from the present moment.
- **Replacement:** *At this rate, the Temporary Strength Pill’s effect will last only one meal’s time longer at most…*

He had to settle the battle within that time.

He must not use the last Temporary Strength Pill. Taking three in succession could put his life at risk.

“One meal’s time…”
- **Rationale:** 한 식경 is an approximate span measured by the time needed to eat a meal, not an exact fifteen-minute quantity; the context also concerns how much longer the effect will persist.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 비록 대장로의 술수에 놀아났다고 해도 서로가 서로를 죽이고, 각자 수많은 피를 흘렸다.
- **Current:** Even if they had been manipulated by the Head Elder’s schemes, they had tried to kill one another and shed countless drops of blood.
- **Defect:** The draft changes actual reciprocal killing into attempted killing, materially softening the established bloodshed between the two factions.
- **Replacement:** Even if they had been manipulated by the Head Elder’s schemes, they had killed one another, and both sides had shed a great deal of blood.
- **Rationale:** 서로가 서로를 죽이고 states that members of the two sides killed one another, not merely that they tried to do so.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 핏물로 흠뻑 젖은 혀를 잡아당기는 거친 손가락이 느껴진다.
- **Current:** I felt his rough fingers grasp my tongue, drenched in blood.
- **Defect:** The draft omits Pung Yang’s physical action of pulling Taekyung’s tongue and also leaves the modifier placement momentarily ambiguous about what is drenched in blood.
- **Replacement:** I felt his rough fingers tugging on my blood-soaked tongue.
- **Rationale:** 잡아당기는 specifically describes the fingers pulling or tugging the tongue, while 핏물로 흠뻑 젖은 modifies the tongue.

## F05 — Minor

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “죽고 싶은 놈들이 널렸군. 아니, 이번에는 년인가?”
- **Current:** “There are plenty of people who want to die. Or is it a woman this time?”
- **Defect:** Rendering 년 as the neutral “woman” sanitizes Pung Yang’s deliberately contemptuous, gendered insult.
- **Replacement:** “There are plenty of bastards who want to die. Or is it a bitch this time?”
- **Rationale:** 놈 and especially 년 are derogatory here; preserving that hostility is necessary to retain Pung Yang’s register.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대신 부탁 하나만 해도 될까요?
- **Current:** But may I ask you for one thing?
- **Defect:** The draft renders 대신 as a generic contrast and loses Lee Seowol’s explicit framing of the request as the price or condition attached to their sacrifice.
- **Replacement:** In return, may I ask you for one thing?
- **Rationale:** 대신 means “in return” or “in exchange” here, establishing the pragmatic link between buying time for Taekyung and asking him to avenge them.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 젠장, 더럽게 아깝네.
- **Current:** *Damn it. What a waste.*
- **Defect:** “What a waste” suggests squandered resources rather than Taekyung’s realization that he missed winning by an extremely narrow margin.
- **Replacement:** *Damn it. I was so damn close.*
- **Rationale:** In context, 아깝네 reacts to Pung Yang’s fading enhancement and his admission that a moment’s delay could have cost him the fight.

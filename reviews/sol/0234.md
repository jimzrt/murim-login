# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter physical detail, pragmatic intent, characterization, and causal meaning.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 내 뒤통수에 돌멩이를 명중시킨 적천강이 느긋한 팔자걸음으로 다가오고 있었다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang was approaching with a leisurely, carefree gait. He had just hit the back of my head with a pebble.
- **Defect:** The translation omits his distinctive splay-footed walk and separates the pebble strike from the modifying description.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang, who had nailed me in the back of the head with a pebble, was approaching at a leisurely walk, his feet turned outward.
- **Rationale:** 팔자걸음 specifically describes walking with the feet turned outward, not merely a carefree gait.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “못 믿겠으면 접든가. 멀리 안 나간다.”
- **Current:** “If you don’t believe me, quit. I won’t go far.”
- **Defect:** The literal rendering misses the dismissive idiom and instead suggests that Jeok Cheongang will remain nearby.
- **Replacement:** “If you don’t believe me, quit. I won’t bother seeing you off.”
- **Rationale:** 멀리 안 나간다 is a curt dismissal meaning that the speaker will not see the departing person out, reinforcing Jeok Cheongang’s feigned indifference.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 싫으면 말고 식으로 굴었지만, 그도 내심 나를 원하고 있다는 것을 진작 알고 있었다.
- **Current:** He had acted as though it didn’t matter whether I refused, but I had known for a while that he secretly wanted me to go through with it.
- **Defect:** The translation changes the object of Jeok Cheongang’s desire from Taekyung himself to Taekyung completing the training.
- **Replacement:** He had acted as though he didn’t care either way, but I had known for a while that deep down, he wanted me.
- **Rationale:** 나를 원하고 있다 expresses that Jeok Cheongang wants Taekyung, preserving the relational implication rather than narrowing it to consent to this exercise.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 공포에 찬 비명이 어둠에 잠긴 구화산을 뒤흔들었다.
- **Current:** My terrified scream shook Mount Jiuhua, swallowed by darkness.
- **Defect:** The dangling modifier can make the scream, rather than Mount Jiuhua, sound as though it is swallowed by darkness.
- **Replacement:** My terrified scream shook Mount Jiuhua, which lay submerged in darkness.
- **Rationale:** 어둠에 잠긴 directly modifies 구화산.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그의 사부는 가차 없었다. 아직 솜털도 가시지 않은 어린아이에게 가혹하다 싶을 정도로.
- **Current:** His Master had been merciless—so much so that it seemed cruel to a child who still had baby fuzz on his face.
- **Defect:** The phrasing ambiguously suggests that the child judged the treatment cruel, rather than that the Master treated the young child with seemingly excessive harshness.
- **Replacement:** His Master had been merciless—harsh enough to seem excessive when inflicted on a child whose baby fuzz had yet to disappear.
- **Rationale:** 어린아이에게 identifies the young Jeok Cheongang as the recipient of the harsh treatment, while 가혹하다 싶을 정도로 characterizes its apparent severity.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 차근차근 하나씩 가르치면 늦는다.

굉도가 예견했던 천기(天氣)가 그러했고 적천강의 현재 상태가 그랬다.
- **Current:** They did not have much time.

That was true of the heavenly patterns Hong Dao had foreseen, and it was true of Jeok Cheongang’s current condition as well.
- **Defect:** The translation makes the heavenly patterns and Jeok Cheongang’s condition themselves subject to the lack of time, rather than evidence explaining why gradual instruction would be too slow.
- **Replacement:** There was no time to teach him everything gradually, one step at a time.

The heavenly patterns Hong Dao had foreseen told him as much, as did Jeok Cheongang’s current condition.
- **Rationale:** The source presents Hong Dao’s heavenly forecast and Jeok Cheongang’s condition as the reasons he knows that a gradual approach would be too late.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 망할 노인네. 무인은 잠자는 시간도 아끼고 어쩌고 하던 인간이 내가 죽을 고생하는 동안 꿀잠을 자?
- **Current:** Damn old man. He was always saying that martial artists should save every minute of sleep and whatnot, yet he slept like a baby while I was going through hell?
- **Defect:** “Save every minute of sleep” implies preserving or maximizing sleep, reversing the source’s meaning that martial artists should begrudge time spent sleeping.
- **Replacement:** Damn old man. He was always going on about how martial artists shouldn’t waste time sleeping, yet he slept like a baby while I was going through hell?
- **Rationale:** 잠자는 시간도 아끼다 means to avoid wasting even one’s sleeping time, not to protect every minute of sleep.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “반 시진 준다. 얼른 주워 입고 다녀오너라.”
- **Current:** “I’ll give you half a shichen. Hurry up, put it on, and go do it.”
- **Defect:** The instruction omits both picking up the iron vest and returning after repeating the course.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll give you half a shichen. Hurry up, pick it up, put it on, and get back here.”
- **Rationale:** 주워 입고 explicitly means to pick it up and put it on, while 다녀오너라 instructs Taekyung to go and return.

# Review

One reversed rule and two changes to the source’s reasoning and magical mechanism.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뭐야, 면회 인원은 한 번에 한 명만 되는 거 아니었어요?
- **Current:** What, only one visitor at a time isn’t allowed?
- **Defect:** Reverses the visitor limit: Jin asks whether only one visitor is allowed at a time, not whether that limit is prohibited.
- **Replacement:** What, wasn’t it one visitor at a time?
- **Rationale:** 한 번에 한 명만 되는 거 아니었어요 asks whether visits are limited to one person at a time.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그는 얼마 전에야 떠올린 반쪽짜리 확신에 대한 답을 들어야 했다.
- **Current:** He needed an answer to the half-formed certainty he’d only just recalled.
- **Defect:** “Recalled” makes his suspicion a recovered memory rather than a conclusion he arrived at recently.
- **Replacement:** He needed an answer to the half-formed conviction he’d reached only recently.
- **Rationale:** 얼마 전에야 떠올린 describes the conviction occurring to him only recently.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.85
- **Source:** 오직 한 사람만이 볼 수 있는 결(結)을 따라, 수백 개의 마법을 잇고 유지하던 거대한 마나의 파도가 갈라졌다.
- **Current:** Following the pattern only one person could see, the massive wave of mana sustaining and binding hundreds of spells was cleaved in two.
- **Defect:** “Pattern” obscures the precise grain or seam along which the mana is cut, making the mechanism sound like a general visible design.
- **Replacement:** Along a seam only one person could see, the massive wave of mana binding and sustaining hundreds of spells split in two.
- **Rationale:** 결 identifies the line of structure the cut follows; the wave binds and sustains the spells.

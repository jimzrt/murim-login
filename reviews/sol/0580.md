# Review

Several localized fidelity and phrasing defects, including an added question, a misattached permission phrase, lost pragmatic force, and an altered action involving the Arch Lich.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘몬스터 웨이브라니.’
- **Current:** *The Monster Wave.*
- **Defect:** The declarative rendering loses the disbelief and dismay conveyed by 라니.
- **Replacement:** *A Monster Wave…*
- **Rationale:** The source presents Go Se-won reacting incredulously to the news, not merely naming the event.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “뒷말은 필요 없으니 집어치우고.”
- **Current:** “Forget the rest. Why are there nine?”
- **Defect:** The draft adds a question that does not occur here and then repeats substantially the same question after the teleportation.
- **Replacement:** “Forget the rest.”
- **Rationale:** The source postpones the question until Go Se-won reaches Area A, where he asks why there are nine rather than ten.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마른침을 삼킨 고세원은 문을 열고 들어갔다.
- **Current:** After swallowing a dry gulp, Go Se-won opened the door and entered.
- **Defect:** “Swallowing a dry gulp” is not natural English for the physical gesture described.
- **Replacement:** Swallowing hard, Go Se-won opened the door and entered.
- **Rationale:** 마른침을 삼키다 describes nervously swallowing despite a dry mouth; “swallowing hard” conveys that naturally.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그런 1팀에서 무단 이탈자가 나온다는 건 몬스터 웨이브보다 희박한 확률이었다.
- **Current:** The odds of someone abandoning their post without permission from Team One were even lower than the odds of a Monster Wave.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly makes Team One the authority whose permission would be required. The source says that a member of the exceptionally loyal Team One going AWOL was nearly inconceivable.
- **Replacement:** The odds of a member of Team One going AWOL were even lower than the odds of a Monster Wave.
- **Rationale:** 그런 1팀에서 modifies where the unauthorized departure would occur; it does not mean permission must come from Team One.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “안 마신다는 뜻이지? 안타깝군. 좋은 술인데.”
- **Current:** “You mean you don’t drink? What a shame. It’s good liquor.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Go Se-won's immediate refusal into a claim that he habitually does not drink.
- **Replacement:** “You mean you won’t drink? What a shame. It’s good liquor.”
- **Rationale:** 안 마신다는 뜻이지 refers to declining the offered drink in this moment, not to abstaining from alcohol generally.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “지금쯤이면 없을 수도 있고.”
- **Current:** “He might not be there anymore by now.”
- **Defect:** The draft reduces Go Jun's ominous euphemism to Kim Ho-jung possibly having changed locations, losing the implication that he may already be dead.
- **Replacement:** “He might be gone by now.”
- **Rationale:** 없을 수도 있고 is deliberately ambiguous between no longer being present and no longer existing; “gone” preserves both readings.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아크 리치도 잡은 놈입니다.
- **Current:** He’s the man who even captured an Arch Lich.
- **Defect:** “Captured” specifically implies taking the Arch Lich alive, while 잡은 only establishes that Jin Taekyung defeated or took it down.
- **Replacement:** He’s the man who even took down an Arch Lich.
- **Rationale:** The neutral combat phrasing preserves the source action without introducing live capture.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 날카로운 구둣발 소리만큼이나 그의 심경도 불편해졌다.
- **Current:** His thoughts grew as unpleasant as the sharp click of his dress shoes.
- **Defect:** “Thoughts grew unpleasant” is an awkward and inaccurate rendering of his increasingly troubled emotional state.
- **Replacement:** His mood soured with each sharp click of his dress shoes.
- **Rationale:** 심경 refers to his state of mind or feelings, while the shoe clicks accompany and underscore his growing unease.

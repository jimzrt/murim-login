- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 중늙은이의 도끼질 한 번에 아름드리나무가 꺾이고, 마른 체구의 여인이 산더미처럼 쌓인 장작을 아무렇지 않게 번쩍 들어 올린다.
- **Current:** With a single swing of an ax, a middle-aged man brought down a massive tree, while a thin woman casually hoisted an enormous pile of firewood onto her shoulder.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that the woman lifts the firewood onto her shoulder, while the source says only that she effortlessly lifts it. It also turns the tree snapping from the force of the blow into the man deliberately bringing it down.

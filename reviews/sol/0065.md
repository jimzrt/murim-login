# Review

One reversed action, several lost register and wordplay cues, an added death threat, and localized terminology errors require correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 너 오늘 잘 걸렸다.
- **Current:** *You picked a fine day to die.*
- **Defect:** The draft adds an explicit threat to kill Taekyung. The source only conveys that Mukyung has caught him at an opportune moment to punish him.
- **Replacement:** *I’ve got you now.*
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Mukyung’s punitive intent without inventing a death threat.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “어떻게 된 거지? 공청석유라도 마셨나?”

“석유를 왜 마셔. 이거 완전히 미친놈이네.”
- **Current:** “What happened to you? Did you drink some kind of stone oil?”

“Why would I drink oil? You’re completely insane.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns 공청석유, a named elixir, into generic “stone oil” and therefore obscures the homophone joke behind Taekyung’s response.
- **Replacement:** “What happened to you? Did you drink *gongcheong seokyu*[^2] or something?”

“Why would I drink oil? You’re completely insane.”

[^2]: *Gongcheong seokyu* is a rare martial-arts elixir; *seokyu* is also the Korean word for petroleum.
- **Rationale:** Transliteration retains the named item, while the footnote makes the source’s petroleum misunderstanding intelligible without inserting an explanation into the dialogue.

## F03 — Major

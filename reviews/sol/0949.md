# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses who faces an enemy in a battle memory and misstates Chinggen’s limitation.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 또 다른 적을 상대하기 위해 눈을 부릅뜬 채 죽어 가는 항산검문의 무인을 지나쳤으며.
- **Current:** He passed a martial artist of the Mount Heng Sword Sect, dying with eyes wide open as he faced yet another enemy.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the dying martial artist face another enemy. Jin Wikyung is the one who passes him to face that enemy.
- **Replacement:** He passed a Mount Heng Sword Sect martial artist who was dying with his eyes wide open, hurrying to face another enemy.
- **Rationale:** 또 다른 적을 상대하기 위해 gives Jin Wikyung’s reason for passing the dying man.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 칭겐은 심계가 깊고 신중하나 지닌바 그릇이 작지.
- **Current:** Chinggen is calculating and cautious, but he isn’t lacking in ambition.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a limitation in Chinggen’s capacity into a claim about his ambition.
- **Replacement:** Chinggen is calculating and cautious, but he lacks the stature for this.
- **Rationale:** 그릇이 작지 describes the limits of what Chinggen is capable of undertaking; the following lines say neither khan has the power to mount this campaign.

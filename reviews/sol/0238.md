# Review

Two subject/action errors materially alter meaning; several additional issues weaken characterization, health-state specificity, terminology, or natural English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아슬아슬하게 이겼다는 뜻이다.
- **Current:** In other words, he had won by the narrowest of margins.
- **Defect:** The pronoun’s nearest antecedent is the Azure Sky Sword King, making it sound as though he defeated Jeok Cheongang. The context indicates that Jeok Cheongang won by half a form.
- **Replacement:** In other words, Jeok Cheongang had won by the narrowest of margins.
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang gives the half-form margin immediately after saying the Sword King entered seclusion following their duel; the narrator interprets this as Jeok Cheongang’s narrow victory.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 만약 잃어버렸다 하더라도 걱정 없다.
- **Current:** And even if they had lost it, there would have been no need to worry.
- **Defect:** The draft says the siblings might lose the iron ball or chain, while the Korean means that even if the siblings themselves became lost, they could deal with the witch using the weapons.
- **Replacement:** Even if they did get lost, there would have been no need to worry.
- **Rationale:** 잃어버렸다 refers contextually to Hansel and Gretel getting lost, continuing the preceding 미아 joke; it does not mean losing the imagined object.

## F04 — Major

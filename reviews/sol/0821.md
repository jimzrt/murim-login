- **Source:** “막……!”

화살을 튕겨 내기 위해 휘둘려졌던 검이, 주인의 팔과 함께 허공으로 솟구쳤다.
- **Current:** “Sto—!”

*Boom!*

His unfinished shout vanished beneath a small explosion.

- **Defect:** The draft identifies the shout and severed arm as the old man’s, although he continues fighting with his scimitar. The Korean leaves the shouter and sword’s wielder unnamed. “막” also calls for blocking, not stopping.
- **Replacement:** “Block—!”

*Boom!*

The unfinished shout vanished beneath a small explosion.

A sword swung to knock the arrows aside shot into the air, along with its wielder’s arm.
- **Rationale:** The source does not identify either person in this passage; assigning both actions to the old man changes the scene.

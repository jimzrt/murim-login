# Review

Two meaningful fidelity issues alter Lei Fei’s appeal to his former comrades and the placement of their flag emblem. The draft also splits one consecutive System panel and contains two localized sound-effect/prose problems.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 「함께 싸우고 싶군. 하지만 그들이 허락해 줄까?」
- **Current:** “I want to fight alongside you. But will they allow it?”
- **Defect:** The draft resolves the unstated companion as Jin and makes the comrades’ permission apply to the act of fighting. In context, Lei Fei is wondering whether his former comrades will accept or permit him personally to fight with them after what he has done.
- **Replacement:** “I’d like to fight together. But will they allow me?”
- **Rationale:** The subject of 허락해 줄까 is 그들이, and the omitted object is naturally Lei Fei himself. Jin’s answer about “the people I know” confirms that Lei Fei is seeking the fallen Hunters’ acceptance.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 부서지고 더럽혀진 갑옷 위, 그들의 상징이자 자부심인 오성홍기가 노을빛을 받아 빛난다.
- **Current:** Above their battered, filthy armor, the Five-Starred Red Flag—their symbol and pride—shone in the glow of the setting sun.
- **Defect:** “Above their armor” relocates the Five-Starred Red Flag as though a separate flag were positioned overhead. The source places the symbol on the damaged armor.
- **Replacement:** On their battered, filthy armor, the Five-Starred Red Flag—their symbol and pride—shone in the glow of the setting sun.
- **Rationale:** 갑옷 위 means on the armor here, describing the national emblem displayed on their uniforms or armor and illuminated by the sunset.

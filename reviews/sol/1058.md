# Review

The draft changes an existing order into an anticipated one, understates two distances, and alters several details of the battle and Hyeoncheon’s recollection.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 십여 장 밖에서 걸음을 멈춘 채 지켜보고 있던 내게도.
- **Current:** Even from a dozen or so yards away, where I’d stopped to watch.
- **Defect:** A dozen or so *jang* is roughly thirty-plus yards, not a dozen yards.
- **Replacement:** Even from some thirty-odd yards away, where I’d stopped to watch.
- **Rationale:** The draft substantially shortens Jin’s stated distance from Sama Pyo.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그보다도 먼 곳에서, 영문도 모른 채 명령을 따라 대기하고 있던 화룡각 대원들에게도.
- **Current:** And even farther off, where the Fire Dragon Pavilion members waited for orders, not knowing what was happening.
- **Defect:** They are waiting in obedience to an order already given, not waiting to receive orders.
- **Replacement:** And even farther off, where the Fire Dragon Pavilion members stood waiting as ordered, with no idea what was happening.
- **Rationale:** 명령을 따라 대기하고 있던 specifies why they remain in place.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** “근래 들어 계속해서 떠오르더군. 정마대전의 한복판에서 도우(道友)의 춘부장을 처음으로 만났을 때가.”
- **Current:** “Lately, I keep thinking of the first time I met your father, in the middle of the Great Faction War.”
- **Defect:** The respectful address to Sama Pyo and honorific reference to his father disappear, obscuring the later shift to “Boy of the Sama family.”
- **Replacement:** “Lately, I keep remembering when I first met your esteemed father, my friend, in the middle of the Great Faction War.”
- **Rationale:** 도우 and 춘부장 are courteous forms; Hyeoncheon abandons that courtesy when he later demands Sama Pyo’s father.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 칠흑과도 같았던 그 거대한 인(人)의 파도는 단숨에 높은 성벽을 무너트리고 모든 것을 휩쓸었다.
- **Current:** That immense human tide, black as night, had swept over the tall ramparts in an instant and swallowed everything.
- **Defect:** The army breaks down the walls; it does not merely sweep over them.
- **Replacement:** That immense human tide, black as night, had brought down the high walls in an instant and swept away everything in its path.
- **Rationale:** 성벽을 무너트리고 describes the physical destruction of the defenses.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 복수를 다짐하며 생사를 오가던 그 순간
- **Current:** At that moment, as he’d risked life and limb, vowing revenge.
- **Defect:** This describes Hyeoncheon hovering between life and death while injured, not taking a risk.
- **Replacement:** Back then, as he hovered between life and death and swore revenge.
- **Rationale:** 생사를 오가던 refers to his condition during recovery from severe injuries.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 은백색의 면사 아래로 흘러나온 그들 한 사람 한 사람의 얼굴과 이름을 떠올릴 때마다, 왜 가슴 깊은 곳에서 불길이 솟구치는 것일까.
- **Current:** Why did a fire surge from deep in his chest every time he pictured their faces and names, one by one, beneath that silver-white veil?
- **Defect:** The faces and names came from the veiled woman’s words; the draft places the men themselves beneath her veil.
- **Replacement:** Why did fire surge from deep in his chest whenever he recalled each face and name the woman had spoken of from behind her silver-white veil?
- **Rationale:** The veiled woman supplied the information that seeded Hyeoncheon’s suspicions.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 사흘간 죽음의 고비를 견딘 끝에 가까스로 부상에서 회복한 현천진인은 생각했더랬다.
- **Current:** After enduring the brink of death for three days, Hyeoncheon had finally recovered enough to move. He’d thought then:
- **Defect:** “Recovered enough to move” substitutes a mobility threshold for recovery from his injuries.
- **Replacement:** After three days at death’s door, Hyeoncheon had barely recovered from his injuries. He’d thought then:
- **Rationale:** 가까스로 부상에서 회복한 says he narrowly recovered from his wounds; it does not mention being able to move.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 화살 같은 속도로 수십여 장의 거리를 단숨에 좁힌 공동파의 제자가
- **Current:** A Kongtong Sect Disciple covered the distance of several dozen yards in an instant, moving like an arrow.
- **Defect:** Several dozen *jang* is substantially farther than several dozen yards.
- **Replacement:** A Kongtong Sect Disciple closed a distance of well over a hundred yards in an instant, moving like an arrow.
- **Rationale:** A jang measures roughly three meters; the shorter draft distance diminishes the speed described.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 누군가의 죽음을 의미하는 흰색 천. 그리고 지금 막 되돌아온 제자의 반응.
- **Current:** The white cloth signaled someone’s death. And the Disciple who had just returned had confirmed it.
- **Defect:** The Disciple’s reaction is a clue, not an explicit confirmation.
- **Replacement:** The white cloth signaled someone’s death. Then there was the reaction of the Disciple who had just returned.
- **Rationale:** The conclusion follows from the cloth and his reaction together; the source does not have him confirm the deaths.

## F10 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “죽어? 죽었단 말이냐? 이렇게, 고작 이런 식으로!”
- **Current:** “Dead? You mean he’s dead? Like this? Is this really how it ends?”
- **Defect:** The singular “he” misidentifies the immediate subject: Hyeoncheon has just learned that both Zhongnan traitors are dead.
- **Replacement:** “Dead? You mean they’re dead? Just like that? Is that all they get?”
- **Rationale:** The preceding passage names Song Il and Hwangbo Eom as the two newly discovered deaths.

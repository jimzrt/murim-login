# Review

One critical identity/subject error, one major hierarchy error, and two localized terminology and phrasing issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그날, 애뇌산에서 그 경이로운 광경을 목격한 것은 백족 사내. 아니, 호위장만이 아니었다.
- **Current:** And on that day, the Bai man who witnessed the wondrous sight on Ailao Mountain was not the only one.

Nor was he merely the Captain of the Guards.
- **Defect:** The draft treats “Captain of the Guards” as a role the Bai man was more than, rather than correcting the man's identification and stating that he was not the only witness.
- **Replacement:** And that day, the Bai man—no, the Captain of the Guards—was not the only one who witnessed the wondrous sight on Ailao Mountain.
- **Rationale:** The corrective 아니 identifies the Bai man more specifically as the Captain of the Guards, while 만이 아니었다 states that others also witnessed the event.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 백천대주 왕호가 묵직한 음성을 토해 냈다.
- **Current:** A middle-aged man whose black hair had turned half gray with the passage of time—the Commander of the Baekcheon Unit, Wang Ho—gave voice to a heavy command.
- **Defect:** Wang Ho speaks in a weighty voice; he does not issue a command. Calling his address a command reverses the hierarchy immediately before he asks the Palace Lord to give the order.
- **Replacement:** A middle-aged man whose black hair had turned half gray with the passage of time—the Commander of the Baekcheon Unit, Wang Ho—spoke in a weighty voice.
- **Rationale:** 묵직한 음성 describes the weight of Wang Ho's voice, whereas the command belongs to the Palace Lord he is addressing.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 옥으로 만들어진 그것은 반으로 갈라진 누군가의 신패(信牌)였고, 백천대를 움직일 수 있는 하나뿐인 증표였다.
- **Current:** Made of jade, it was half of someone’s split token—a信牌—and the one proof capable of moving the Baekcheon Unit.
- **Defect:** The sentence leaves untranslated Chinese characters in the English reading copy and renders 증표 mechanically as “proof capable of moving” rather than a unique token of authority that can mobilize the unit.
- **Replacement:** Made of jade, it was one half of someone’s split identification token—and the sole token of authority capable of mobilizing the Baekcheon Unit.
- **Rationale:** 신패 is an identification or authority token, and 하나뿐인 증표 emphasizes that it is the sole credential by which the Baekcheon Unit can be mobilized.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 흐릿한 불빛이 감돌던 침소 내부, 검수(劍手)의 생명과도 같은 손목을 스스로 끊어 버린 의형제의 얼굴이 눈앞을 스치는 듯했다.
- **Current:** Inside the sleeping quarters, where dim light lingered, the face of his sworn brother flashed before his eyes—the face of the man who had severed his own wrist, the life of a swordsman, with his own hand.
- **Defect:** The apposition “his own wrist, the life of a swordsman” is unnatural and obscures the comparison that a swordsman values his wrist as dearly as life itself.
- **Replacement:** Inside the sleeping quarters, where dim light lingered, the face of his sworn brother seemed to flash before his eyes—the man who had severed with his own hand the wrist a swordsman valued as much as life itself.
- **Rationale:** 생명과도 같은 modifies 손목 figuratively: the wrist is as precious as life to a swordsman.

# Review

The draft largely preserves the scene, but several passages alter a historical reference, the Doppelganger’s motive, or the mechanics of the fight.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 훗날 ‘승리의 날’이라 불리는 대격변의 끝자락에서
- **Current:** At the end of the Great Cataclysm, an event later called Victory Day,
- **Defect:** The draft calls the Great Cataclysm itself Victory Day rather than locating Victory Day at its end.
- **Replacement:** Near the end of the Great Cataclysm, on what would later be called Victory Day,
- **Rationale:** The later name applies to the day of victory, not the entire cataclysm.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 도플갱어가 인류 속으로 스며들었던 것은, 단순한 배신이나 전향(轉向) 따위가 아니었음을.
- **Current:** The Doppelganger had infiltrated humanity for more than simple betrayal or defection.
- **Defect:** “For more than” suggests betrayal or defection was part of its purpose; the source rejects that explanation.
- **Replacement:** The Doppelganger’s infiltration of humanity hadn’t been a simple act of betrayal or defection.
- **Rationale:** Jin realizes it was carrying out Asmodeus’s order, not merely changing sides.

## F03 — Major

- **Source:** 앞서 공격을 시도했던 나와 별다를 것 없이, 반투명한 막에 가로막혀 있던 스켈레톤 킹이 그 광경을 멍하니 바라보았다.
- **Current:** The Skeleton King, like me, still trapped behind a translucent barrier after trying to attack, stared blankly at the sight.
- **Defect:** “Trapped behind” turns a barrier that blocked an attack into one confining the Skeleton King.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.94

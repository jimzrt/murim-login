# Review

The draft reverses the Blood Lord’s final quoted message, assigns two reactions to the wrong person, and misses several register and terminology cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 기다려라. 내가, 혈주(血主)가 돌아 왔…….
- **Current:** “Wait for me. I, the Blood Lord, will be back—”
- **Defect:** Changes an announcement that the Blood Lord has returned into a promise to return later.
- **Replacement:** “Just wait. I, the Blood Lord, have returned—”
- **Rationale:** 돌아 왔 is past tense; the dying disciple is repeating the Blood Lord’s message.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 깊게 가라앉은 눈빛으로 줄곧 그를 주시하던 나도 이제는 묻지 않을 수 없었다.
- **Current:** I’d been watching him the whole time, his gaze sunk deep. Now I couldn’t help asking,
- **Defect:** Assigns the somber gaze to Hak Eui rather than the narrator.
- **Replacement:** I’d been watching him all along with a somber gaze. Now I couldn’t help asking,
- **Rationale:** The narrator is the subject of 주시하던; 깊게 가라앉은 눈빛 describes his gaze.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 얼굴이 딱딱하게 굳은 학의를 향해 나직이 덧붙였다.
- **Current:** I added quietly, my face stiffening as I looked at Hak Eui.
- **Defect:** Transfers Hak Eui’s stiffening expression to the narrator.
- **Replacement:** I added quietly, looking at Hak Eui, whose face had gone rigid.
- **Rationale:** 얼굴이 딱딱하게 굳은 modifies 학의.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “이제야 그 땡중에게 면이 설 수 있겠어.”

무림의 태산북두라 불리는 소림사의 방장이자, 법왕(法王)이라는 별호를 얻을 만큼 신망이 두텁던 굉도였지만 적천강은 그를 늘 땡중이라고 불렀다.
- **Current:** “Now I can face that monk at last.”

The Abbot of Shaolin, called Mount Tai and Northern Dipper of the Murim and so widely respected he’d earned the title of Dharma King—Jeok Cheongang had always called Hong Dao that monk.
- **Defect:** Neutralizes Jeok Cheongang’s affectionate, irreverent 땡중 in both dialogue and narration; “face” also loses the sense of being able to hold his head up before his friend.
- **Replacement:** “Now I can hold my head up in front of that damned monk.”

Hong Dao was the Abbot of Shaolin Temple, hailed as a pillar of the Murim and respected enough to earn the title of Dharma King. Jeok Cheongang had always called him “that damned monk.”
- **Rationale:** 땡중 is a disparagingly familiar way to refer to a monk, while 면이 설 수 conveys recovering one’s standing before someone.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 입술을 질끈 깨무는 녀석과 학의를 물끄러미 응시하던 나는 때맞춰 전음(傳音)을 흘려보냈다.
- **Current:** I watched Gung Gibang bite his lip and Hak Eui’s impassive face. Then I sent my voice directly to him.
- **Defect:** Omits the established term Sound Transmission and leaves its recipient unclear.
- **Replacement:** I watched Gung Gibang bite his lip, then glanced at Hak Eui. At just the right moment, I sent Gung Gibang a Sound Transmission.
- **Rationale:** 전음 is Sound Transmission; Gung Gibang’s ensuing reaction establishes that he receives it. The source does not describe Hak Eui’s face as impassive here.

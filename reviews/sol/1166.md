# Review

One reversal of who would be standing, plus errors in Morgoth’s challenge, combat mechanics, and terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 혹은 진태경이 자신처럼 무한에 가까운 힘을 품은 드래곤 하트(Dragonheart)를 갖고 있었더라면, 지금 서 있는 것은 자신이 아닌 진태경이었을지도 모른다고.
- **Current:** Or if Jin Taekyung had possessed a Dragonheart, brimming with power almost without limit like his own, perhaps it would be Morgoth—not Jin Taekyung—standing there now.
- **Defect:** Reverses who Morgoth imagines would be standing.
- **Replacement:** Or if Jin Taekyung had possessed a Dragonheart, brimming with power almost without limit like his own, perhaps Jin Taekyung would be the one standing there now instead of him.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly says 진태경, not 자신, might be standing there.

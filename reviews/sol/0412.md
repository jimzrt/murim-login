# Review

One critical mechanical mistranslation changes which level threshold yields EXP and adds unsupported multiple level gains. Several localized omissions and wording choices also alter quantity, register, and comic timing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어차피 100레벨 이상부터는 상당한 경험치가 들어오고 다른 잡몹까지 더해진다면 레벨 업에는 충분할 테니, 볼썽사납게 탈진해서 쓰러질 일 따위는 없을 거라고 생각했지, 뭐.
- **Current:** After all, once you reached Level 100, you gained a considerable amount of EXP. With all the other monsters thrown in, that would have been enough to level up several times.

I had not expected to collapse from exhaustion in some embarrassing heap.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the EXP threshold depend on Taekyung reaching Level 100, but the source says monsters at Level 100 or above yield substantial EXP. It also adds several level gains where the source says only that there would be enough EXP to level up, which explains why Taekyung expected the resulting recovery to prevent his collapse.
- **Replacement:** After all, monsters at Level 100 or above gave a considerable amount of EXP. With all the other small fry thrown in, there would be enough to level up, so I’d figured there was no way I’d collapse from exhaustion and make a spectacle of myself.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 100레벨 이상 is the monsters just discussed, while 레벨 업에는 충분 means sufficient for a level-up rather than several. The final clause presents avoiding an exhausted collapse as Taekyung’s prior reasoning.

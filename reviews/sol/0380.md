# Review

Several action, subject, pragmatic, and humor details require correction, including two reversed or misattached actions.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** - 취이이익!

「어, 어어, 어어어…….」
- **Current:** —K-Kiiieee…!
- **Defect:** Two distinct vocalizations are collapsed into one, omitting the separate frightened stammer that follows the monster's cry.
- **Replacement:** —Squeeeeee!

“Uh, uh, uhhh…”

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 공안 무력부의 헌터들은 지원군이라는 세 글자를 떠올렸고
- **Current:** The Hunters of the Public Security Armed Forces Department thought of only three words: *reinforcements have arrived.*
- **Defect:** The source says they thought of the single Korean word meaning “reinforcements”; the draft changes it into a three-word proposition and adds that the reinforcements have already arrived.

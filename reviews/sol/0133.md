# Review

The draft contains two critical semantic reversals, several meaningful shifts in causality, subject, and hierarchy, and two binding terminology mismatches.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 자리에 있는 분들 덕분에 우리 성운표국이 한 계단 올라섰으니 그만한 대접을 해야 하지 않겠소?
- **Current:** “Thanks to everyone here, our Seongun Escort Bureau has climbed another step higher. Don’t you think you deserve to be treated accordingly?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the subject of the obligation: Woo Jintae says that he should treat them accordingly, not that they should declare themselves deserving of such treatment.
- **Replacement:** “Thanks to everyone here, our Seongun Escort Bureau has climbed another step higher, so it’s only right that I treat you accordingly, isn’t it?”
- **Rationale:** The source’s 해야 하지 않겠소 presents the hospitality as Woo Jintae’s own obligation, reinforcing his calculatedly gracious posture.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 예. 그중에서도 최상급이라 그런지 빛깔이 아주 곱더군요.
- **Current:** “Yes. It must be the highest grade, because the color is exceptionally beautiful.
- **Defect:** The causal relationship is reversed. The source identifies the brocade as top-grade and suggests that its grade explains its beautiful color; the draft instead infers its grade from the color.
- **Replacement:** “Yes. It’s the finest grade, and perhaps that’s why the color is so exceptionally beautiful.
- **Rationale:** 최상급이라 그런지 treats the superior grade as the probable cause, not as an uncertain conclusion drawn from its appearance.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 다들 무가의 자제라고 할 만큼 무공을 익힌 몸이라 대화를 듣는 것은 그리 어렵지 않았다.
- **Current:** They had all trained enough in martial arts to be called scions of martial families, so listening to the conversation was not difficult.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly makes martial-family scion status something earned through sufficient training. The source says their martial training was commensurate with their existing status as martial-family scions.
- **Replacement:** They had all trained in martial arts as befitted scions of martial families, so overhearing the conversation was not difficult.
- **Rationale:** 무가의 자제라고 할 만큼 modifies the extent of their martial training; it does not explain how they acquired their family status.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 뻔하지 않습니까. 어쩌다 익힌 삼류 무공 몇 수를 믿고 하염없이 강호를 떠돌다가 죽는 인생.
- **Current:** “Isn’t it obvious? He probably learned a few Third Rate martial arts, trusted in them, and wandered the martial world until he eventually died.”
- **Defect:** The draft puts the stranger’s imagined life in the past and states that he has already died, even though he is alive downstairs. Woo Jintae is describing the kind of doomed life he expects the man to lead.
- **Replacement:** “Isn’t it obvious? He’s the type who picks up a few Third Rate martial arts moves by chance, puts his faith in them, wanders aimlessly through the martial world, and winds up dead.”
- **Rationale:** 죽는 인생 is a contemptuous characterization of his supposed trajectory, not a report that his death has already occurred.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 다들 애지중지 자란 몸이다. 도대체 이게 얼마 만에 들어 보는 반말인가.
- **Current:** They had all been raised precious and pampered. How long had it been since someone had spoken to them so casually?
- **Defect:** Rendering 반말 as merely casual speech loses the status offense that shocks the pampered scions: Taekyung speaks down to them without deference.
- **Replacement:** They had all been raised precious and pampered. How long had it been since anyone had spoken down to them like that?
- **Rationale:** In this context, 반말 marks deliberately non-deferential speech across an expected hierarchy, not relaxed familiarity.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 성룡이, 천우, 명화, 소혜, 마지막으로 넌 진태. 성까지 말해 줘?
- **Current:** “Seongryong, Cheonwoo, Myeonghwa, Sohye, and finally, you—Jintae. Do you want to tell me your family names, too?”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Taekyung offers to reveal their family names himself; he does not ask them to supply those names.
- **Replacement:** “Seongryong, Cheonwoo, Myeonghwa, Sohye, and finally, you—Jintae. Want me to tell you your family names, too?”
- **Rationale:** 말해 줘? continues Taekyung’s demonstration that he already knows their identities and means ‘Want me to say them?’

## F07 — Minor

- **Current:** “The greatest young prodigies of the orthodox Murim!

- **Replacement:** “The greatest young prodigies of the Murim’s orthodox faction!
- **Rationale:** The glossary explicitly establishes 정파 as “orthodox faction,” while 무림 remains “Murim.”

# Review

Several meaningful shifts affect the opening idea, Jin’s absorption, and Johnson’s joke; a few action and quantity details also need correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 직감이라는 것은 어쩌면 예지(叡智)의 다른 이름일지도 모른다.
- **Current:** Perhaps instinct was another name for foresight.
- **Defect:** Changes intuition to instinct and renders 叡智, meaning wisdom, as foresight.
- **Replacement:** Perhaps intuition was just another name for wisdom.
- **Rationale:** The sentence equates 직감 with 예지(叡智), not an instinctive prediction of the future.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “괜찮아. 살짝 지린 것 같긴 하지만.”

“네?”

“오해할 것 같아서 덧붙이자면, 나는 모든 게 큰 사람이지만 이번만큼은 작은 거야.”
- **Current:** “I’m fine. I might’ve pissed myself a little, though.”

“What?”

“Just so you don’t get the wrong idea, I’m a big man in every way—but this time, I mean small.”
- **Defect:** Makes Johnson’s euphemism explicit and leaves the punchline sounding as though he means a part of his body is small.
- **Replacement:** “I’m fine. Though I might’ve had a little accident.”

“What?”

“Just so you don’t get the wrong idea, I’m big in every way. This time, though, it was only a little.”
- **Rationale:** 살짝 지린 것 같긴 하지만 is evasive, and 작은 refers back to how much he leaked; his claim that everything about him is big sets up that contrast.

# Review

The draft contains one reversed explanation relationship, an unsupported total-casualty claim, a changed subject, and an omitted telepathic revelation, alongside several localized fidelity and terminology issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 온 힘을 다해 셀프 귀싸대기를 날린 적천강이 눈을 깜빡이며 나를 바라보더니, 침중한 표정으로 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang had slapped himself across the ear with all his strength. He blinked at me, then opened his mouth with a solemn expression.
- **Defect:** “Slapped himself across the ear” is an unnatural literalization of 귀싸대기 and misstates the target of the slap.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang had slapped himself across the face with all his strength. He blinked at me, then opened his mouth with a solemn expression.
- **Rationale:** 귀싸대기를 날리다 means to deliver a slap across the side of the face, not to slap the ear itself.

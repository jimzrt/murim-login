## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 앞서 마음을 읽힌 것 따위는 아무것도 아니었다.

- **Rationale:** Her earlier mistake exposed her true intentions to Jin; this sentence dismisses that mistake as less important than her failure to understand the title.

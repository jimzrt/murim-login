# Review

The draft conflates the newly introduced Song Ilseom with the established Song Il, mistranslates his rank, and changes a stated interval. Several localized characterization and English-usage issues also require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘송일섬.’

송일섬은 용봉삼표의 다른 두 사람과는 달리 훤칠하고 잘생긴 외모의 소유자였다.
- **Current:** *Song Il.*

Unlike the other two members of the Dragon-Phoenix Three Escorts, Song Il was tall and strikingly handsome.
- **Defect:** 송일섬 is a newly introduced character named Song Ilseom, not the established Zhongnan elder 송일, Song Il. The draft conflates two distinct identities.
- **Replacement:** *Song Ilseom.*

Unlike the other two members of the Dragon-Phoenix Three Escorts, Song Ilseom was tall and strikingly handsome.
- **Rationale:** The source consistently gives this escort captain’s full name as 송일섬, whereas the binding glossary’s Song Il applies only to the exact name 송일.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 용봉표국을 대표하는 뛰어난 세 명의 표두. 이른바 용봉삼표(龍鳳三鏢)라 불리는 이들이었다.
- **Current:** They were the three outstanding escorts who represented the Yongbong Escort Bureau—the men known collectively as the Dragon-Phoenix Three Escorts.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces the three men from 표두, supervisory escort captains, to ordinary escorts and loses their hierarchy within the bureau.
- **Replacement:** They were the three outstanding escort captains who represented the Yongbong Escort Bureau—the men known collectively as the Dragon-Phoenix Three Escorts.
- **Rationale:** 표두 is a ranked leadership position distinct from an ordinary 표사, or escort.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘하지만 설마 송 표두가…….’
- **Current:** *But surely Chief Escort Song wouldn’t…*
- **Defect:** The draft promotes 송 표두 to Chief Escort, but Heo Jun alone is 총표두, the Chief Escort. It also continues the mistaken rendering of 송일섬 as Song Il.
- **Replacement:** *But surely Escort Captain Song Ilseom wouldn’t…*
- **Rationale:** 송 표두 denotes Escort Captain Song; the source reserves 총표두 for Heo Jun.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “아뇨. 한 식경 전에 맞았는데요.”
- **Current:** “No. I was hit a quarter of an hour ago.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the stated interval: 한 식경 is approximately the time required to eat a meal, conventionally about half an hour in this setting, not fifteen minutes.
- **Replacement:** “No. I was hit about half an hour ago.”
- **Rationale:** The joke specifies how recently Mujin was struck, so the source quantity should be preserved.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 용봉표국의 무남독녀지만 금이야 옥이야 자란 것만은 아니다.
- **Current:** She was the only daughter of the Yongbong Escort Bureau, but she had not been raised like a precious flower in a hothouse.
- **Defect:** An Escort Bureau cannot literally have a daughter, and 무남독녀 identifies Hwaran as the bureau chief’s sole child with no brothers. The draft also imports the hothouse image from a later sentence instead of preserving the source’s emphasis on being pampered.
- **Replacement:** She was the bureau chief’s only child, but that did not mean she had been raised pampered and sheltered.
- **Rationale:** The source establishes both Hwaran’s family status and that she was not merely doted on despite being the sole daughter.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래서 더더욱 그런 이들을 의심하고 싶지 않았다. 의심하기 싫었다.
- **Current:** That was why she had not wanted to suspect them even more. She did not want to doubt them.
- **Defect:** “Suspect them even more” incorrectly suggests that her degree of suspicion would increase; 더더욱 instead intensifies her unwillingness to suspect them.
- **Replacement:** That made her all the more unwilling to suspect them. She did not want to doubt them.
- **Rationale:** The source repeats and intensifies Hwaran’s reluctance to distrust the people who supported her.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 주화란은 덤덤한 목소리로 검파를 잡았다.
- **Current:** Ju Hwaran gripped her sword hilt in a calm voice.
- **Defect:** The English attaches “in a calm voice” to the physical act of gripping rather than to Hwaran’s preceding speech.
- **Replacement:** Ju Hwaran said calmly as she gripped her sword hilt.
- **Rationale:** 덤덤한 목소리 describes how she speaks, while 검파를 잡았다 is the simultaneous physical action.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘저게 뭐야.’
- **Current:** *What the hell is that?*
- **Defect:** The draft adds profanity absent from the source and unnecessarily intensifies the observer’s reaction.
- **Replacement:** *What is that?*
- **Rationale:** The Korean is a plain expression of bafflement without a curse.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 주화란이 고개를 바로 하려던 그때, 지금까지 보지 못했던 별 하나가 눈에 띄었다.
- **Current:** Just as Ju Hwaran was about to straighten her head, she noticed a star she had never seen before.
- **Defect:** “Straighten her head” is an unnatural literal rendering of 고개를 바로 하다 in this context.
- **Replacement:** Just as Ju Hwaran was about to face forward again, she noticed a star she had never seen before.
- **Rationale:** Hwaran has been looking up at the night sky; the source says she is about to return her head and gaze to their normal forward position.

- **Source:** ‘20년생이요.’

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99

- **Defect:** The draft turns an apparent trait into an established one, adds an unsupported duration, and renders 동고동락 as “living and dying,” introducing death imagery absent from this sentence.
- **Replacement:** I didn’t know that I would share thick and thin like brothers with that Team Leader who looked so foul-tempered.
- **Rationale:** 성질 더러워 보이는 means he looked foul-tempered, while 동고동락 means sharing hardships and joys; the source does not specify that this lasted for the next few years.

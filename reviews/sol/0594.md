# Review

The draft contains one critical death-state reversal and several meaningful omissions or shifts involving characterization, identity, System mechanics, quantity, and agency.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 놈은 분명히 죽음을 맞이한 상태였다.
- **Current:** And Go Jun had clearly been at death’s door.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Go Jun from unequivocally dead to merely close to death, undermining the central resurrection mystery.
- **Replacement:** And Go Jun had unquestionably been dead.
- **Rationale:** 죽음을 맞이한 상태였다 states that he had already died, not that he was approaching death.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 상대는 나와 같은 인간이 아니라 괴물이니까.
- **Current:** Because my opponent was not a human being like me.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicit conclusion that Go Jun is a monster, weakening Taekyung’s stated justification for feeling no pity.
- **Replacement:** Because my opponent wasn’t human like me. He was a monster.
- **Rationale:** 괴물이니까 explicitly identifies him as a monster and supplies the causal reason for the preceding lack of pity.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 지난 수십 년간 이정룡에게 충성을 바친 제자이자, 세계에서도 손꼽히는 아레스 길드의 실질적인 주인이 내 손에 죽었다.
- **Current:** The Disciple who had served Lee Jungryong faithfully for decades, and the de facto master of one of the most prominent Guilds in the world, had died by my hand.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the established organization name Ares Guild, making Go Jun’s institutional identity less specific.
- **Replacement:** The Disciple who had served Lee Jungryong faithfully for decades—and the de facto master of Ares Guild, one of the foremost Guilds in the world—had died by my hand.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly names 아레스 길드 and describes it as one of the world’s foremost Guilds.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 왜 처치를 알리는 시스템 창은 아직까지도 뜨지 않을까.
- **Current:** Why had the System window announcing his defeat still not appeared?
- **Defect:** “Defeat” weakens the System mechanic from confirmation of a kill or elimination to a generic combat loss, even though Go Jun has already been defeated in battle.
- **Replacement:** Why had the System window announcing his kill still not appeared?
- **Rationale:** 처치 in this context is the System-confirmed elimination that would establish Go Jun’s death, which is why its absence reveals that he is alive.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “네가 가는 곳이 천국이든, 지옥이든. 먼저 가서 기다리고 있어라. 그때는 정말 원 없이 죽여 줄 테니까.”
- **Current:** “Whether you’re going to heaven or hell, go there first and wait for me. When I get there, I’ll kill you to your heart’s content.”
- **Defect:** “To your heart’s content” incorrectly makes Go Jun the one deriving satisfaction from the killing and produces an incoherent threat.
- **Replacement:** “Whether you’re going to heaven or hell, go there first and wait for me. When I get there, I’ll kill you as much as I please.”
- **Rationale:** 원 없이 죽여 줄 테니까 expresses Taekyung’s threat to kill Go Jun without restraint or lingering dissatisfaction, not Go Jun’s desire to be killed.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 두 번의 몬스터 웨이브를 인위적으로 일으켜 수천의 사상자를 발생시키고, 김화종과 이십여 명의 평화 길드원을 희생시킨 대가를 죽음으로 받아 냈다.
- **Current:** He had caused two Monster Waves on purpose, creating thousands of casualties and sacrificing Kim Hwajong and more than twenty members of the Peace Guild.
- **Defect:** “More than twenty” turns the approximate quantity 이십여 명 into a strict lower bound.
- **Replacement:** He had caused two Monster Waves on purpose, creating thousands of casualties and costing Kim Hwajong and some twenty members of the Peace Guild their lives.
- **Rationale:** 이십여 명 means approximately twenty or twenty-odd people, while “more than twenty” specifically excludes twenty and lacks the source’s approximation.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하지만 오늘. 마음 깊은 곳에 간직하고 있던 약속의 장소가 무너졌다.
- **Current:** But today, the place where I had kept that promise deep in my heart had collapsed.
- **Defect:** The English syntax makes the physical place sound like the location inside his heart where he stored the promise, rather than the cherished place associated with that promise.
- **Replacement:** But today, the place from the promise I had kept deep in my heart had collapsed.
- **Rationale:** 마음 깊은 곳에 간직하고 있던 modifies 약속, while 약속의 장소 is the real-world location that collapsed.

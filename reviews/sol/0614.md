# Review

Several substantive fidelity issues alter a subject, hierarchy, and the two-body joke; localized register, action, and terminology errors also remain.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다시 생각해 보니까 두 개는 맞구나. 그걸 번갈아 가며 컨트롤해야 하는 거라 더럽게 피곤한 거지.
- **Current:** Now that I thought about it, two bodies really was the problem. Having to control them by turns was what made it so damn exhausting.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the joke and causal statement: Taekyung realizes that he does, in fact, have two bodies, not that having two bodies is itself the problem.
- **Replacement:** Come to think of it, I really did have two. It was having to control them by turns that made it so damn exhausting.
- **Rationale:** 두 개는 맞구나 confirms that the number two is accurate, while the following sentence identifies alternating control as the exhausting part.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이대로 늘어지게 한숨 자고 싶은 마음이 간절하다.
- **Current:** I desperately wanted to sleep in and laze around just like this.
- **Defect:** “Sleep in” implies remaining in bed until late, whereas the source expresses wanting to stay sprawled out and sleep for a while.
- **Replacement:** I desperately wanted to stay sprawled out like this and sleep for a while.
- **Rationale:** 한숨 자다 means to get some sleep or take a nap, without the time-of-day implication of “sleep in.”

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it reverses one encounter beat, changes who is being addressed near the end, and loses several tactical and character cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러나 까맣게 물든 잿더미의 중심에서 몸을 일으키는 진태경을 기다리고 있던 것은 비단 그뿐만이 아니었다.
- **Current:** But rising from the center of the blackened ash was Jin Taekyung—and he wasn’t alone.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jin's company the point of the sentence. The source says the screams and System alerts were not the only things awaiting him as he rose.
- **Replacement:** But the screams and System alerts weren’t all that awaited Jin Taekyung as he rose from the center of the blackened ash.
- **Rationale:** The Lycanthrope Champion's attack is introduced as another thing awaiting Jin, not as someone rising alongside him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “선지자. 지금 어디 있어?”
- **Current:** “Prophet. Where are you?”
- **Defect:** The draft addresses the Prophet directly, though Jin is asking the Manticore where the Prophet is.
- **Replacement:** “The Prophet. Where is he now?”
- **Rationale:** The Manticore has just referred to the Prophet and answers Jin's question about that person's whereabouts.

## F03 — Major

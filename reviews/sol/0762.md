- **Defect:** The pronoun “it” makes the reporter criticize how long Jin took to suppress the Munich wave. In context, the reporter is using Munich’s much greater size to criticize Michael for taking too long in Cape Town, which is why Huginn immediately notes that the reporter dared to compare them.
- **Replacement:** “According to the German government’s announcement, the Munich Monster Wave was nearly ten times the size of the one in Cape Town! Given that, don’t you think the Cape Town wave took far too long to suppress?”
- **Rationale:** The contrast introduced by 그런데 and the following narration about an audacious comparison establish Cape Town as the implied target of the criticism.

# Review

The draft largely preserves the fight, but obscures the Blood Lord’s reason for envying Jin Taekyung and introduces a few localized ambiguities.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그런 주인에게 애정에 가까운 관심을 한 몸에 받고 있는 진태경을.
- **Current:** Jealous of Jin Taekyung, who received the attention of someone who seemed to care for him almost affectionately.
- **Defect:** “Someone” obscures that the attention comes from the Blood Lord’s own master.
- **Replacement:** Jealous of Jin Taekyung, who had all but monopolized that same master’s almost affectionate attention.
- **Rationale:** 그런 주인 refers directly to the master whose disregard for his servants the Blood Lord resents.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “몰라, 나도.”

“뭐?”

“단지…… 당연하게 알 수 있었다.”
- **Current:** “I don’t know. I just…”

“What?”

“I simply knew. Naturally.”
- **Defect:** The pause is moved into the first answer, and “too” is lost.
- **Replacement:** “I don’t know either.”

“What?”

“I just… knew, as if it were the most natural thing in the world.”
- **Rationale:** 나도 means “me too”; the hesitation belongs to his explanation after the Blood Lord asks again.

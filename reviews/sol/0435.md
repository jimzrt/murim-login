# Review

One critical negation reversal, several meaningful omissions or ambiguities, and localized terminology and prose issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “저놈이 옛날 버릇 못 버린 줄 알았지.”
- **Current:** “I thought that bastard had finally gotten rid of his old habits.”
- **Defect:** The translation reverses the negation: Jeok Cheongang thought Mungyeong had not abandoned his old habits.
- **Replacement:** “I thought that bastard hadn’t kicked his old habits.”
- **Rationale:** 못 버린 means “had not abandoned,” which also directly motivates the following exchange about habits lasting into old age.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “듣는 귀도 없는데 뭐가 어때서. 애초에 여기까지 따라온 마당에 굳이 정체를 감추는 이유가 뭔가?”
- **Current:** “There aren’t any ears listening, so what does it matter? Besides, you followed me all the way here. Why bother hiding your identity?”
- **Defect:** The draft invents Jeok Cheongang as the person Mungyeong followed. The Korean leaves the object unstated and refers to Mungyeong having accompanied the group this far.
- **Replacement:** “There aren’t any ears listening, so what does it matter? Besides, after coming all this way, why bother hiding your identity?”
- **Rationale:** 여기까지 따라온 identifies Mungyeong’s continued accompaniment without saying that he specifically followed Jeok Cheongang.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 장강수로맹주 해상왕의 제자이자, 사천의 강을 호령하는 수룡채의 채주, 선화아(船火兒) 무송은 하늘이 무너지는 듯한 얼굴로 부르짖었다.
- **Current:** Mu Song, Disciple of Seafaring King—the Alliance Leader of the Yangtze River Channel League—and Stronghold Lord of Water Dragon Stronghold, which ruled the rivers of Sichuan, cried out with a face as though the sky had fallen.
- **Defect:** Mu Song’s explicitly stated sobriquet, Ship-Fire Boy, is omitted.
- **Replacement:** Mu Song, known as Ship-Fire Boy, was the Disciple of the Seafaring King—the Alliance Leader of the Yangtze River Channel League—and the Stronghold Lord of Water Dragon Stronghold, which ruled the rivers of Sichuan. He cried out with a face as though the sky had fallen.
- **Rationale:** 선화아 is a named sobriquet and has the established rendering “Ship-Fire Boy”; omitting it loses part of Mu Song’s formal introduction and the fire-related irony of the scene.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그래, 그래. 잘됐군. 기왕 생각난 김에 하는 말인데, 적당히 뺏으라고. 내 알 바는 아니지만 그 사람들도 먹고살아야지.”
- **Current:** “Yes, yes. Good to hear. Since I thought of it, let me say this: take it easy. It’s none of my business, but those people need to make a living too.”
- **Defect:** “Take it easy” makes Jeok Cheongang’s instruction ambiguous and omits the explicit reference to Mu Song robbing people.
- **Replacement:** “Yes, yes. Good to hear. Since it came to mind, let me say this: don’t rob people too much. It’s none of my business, but they need to make a living too.”
- **Rationale:** 적당히 뺏으라고 specifically tells the river bandit to moderate how much he steals, preserving both Jeok Cheongang’s warning and the scene’s humor.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 푸르스름한 안광이 어둠 속에서 빛을 발한다.
- **Current:** Pale-blue eyes gleamed in the darkness.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a bluish gleam in Mungyeong’s eyes into a statement that his eyes themselves are pale blue.
- **Replacement:** A pale-blue gleam shone from his eyes in the darkness.
- **Rationale:** 안광 denotes the light or gleam emitted by the eyes, not their iris color.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “어울리지도 않는 어린놈 흉내는 관두고 목이나 축여.”
- **Current:** “Stop pretending to be a little brat who doesn’t suit you and wet your throat.”
- **Defect:** The syntax incorrectly makes the little brat the thing that does not suit Mungyeong, producing unnatural and confusing English.
- **Replacement:** “Drop the little-brat act—it doesn’t suit you—and wet your throat.”
- **Rationale:** 어울리지도 않는 modifies Mungyeong’s act of pretending to be young: the performance does not suit him.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “늙으면 잠이 없어지거든. 누구처럼.”
- **Current:** “Old people don’t need much sleep. Like someone I know.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes getting less sleep with age into not needing sleep, altering the stated condition.
- **Replacement:** “You get less sleep when you’re old. Like someone I know.”
- **Rationale:** 잠이 없어지다 means that one sleeps less or loses sleep with age, not that the physiological need for sleep disappears.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “그 아이도 대단, 아니 대단히 미친놈 같긴 했지. 확실히 재능으로만 따지면 진태경보다 한 수 위다.”
- **Current:** “That kid was impressive too. No—he was pretty damn crazy. In terms of talent alone, he’s one step above Jin Taekyung.”
- **Defect:** The draft loses the deliberate 대단/대단히 wordplay in Mungyeong’s self-correction from “impressive” to “impressively crazy.”
- **Replacement:** “That kid was impressive too. No—he did seem impressively insane. In terms of talent alone, he’s definitely one step above Jin Taekyung.”
- **Rationale:** The repeated root in 대단, 아니 대단히 creates the joke and must survive the correction while preserving the assessment of Cheongpung’s talent.

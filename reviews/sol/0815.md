Several action and scene details are altered, including the canyon’s width, the undead formation, and how the cracks aid the Doppelganger’s escape. The System panel also needs formatting corrections.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 터무니없이 좁은 협곡은 아니지만, 그렇다고 해서 넓은 것도 아니다.
- **Current:** The canyon was absurdly narrow, though not exactly small.
- **Defect:** Reverses the description of the canyon’s width.
- **Replacement:** The canyon wasn’t absurdly narrow, but it wasn’t wide, either.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly says the canyon is not absurdly narrow.

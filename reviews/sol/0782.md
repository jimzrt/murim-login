# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes who experienced Michael’s past imbalances and omits a key comparison in Jin’s final assessment of his power.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이미 오늘과 같은 불균형은 과거에도 몇 번이나 겪었다.
- **Current:** We’ve had imbalances like this several times before.
- **Defect:** “We” makes the past imbalances a shared experience rather than Michael’s own.
- **Replacement:** I’ve experienced imbalances like this several times before.
- **Rationale:** Michael describes his own recurring imbalance between magical power and mana.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 아스모데우스의 뒤를 이은 새로운 마왕이 되는 것도 나쁘지는 않겠구나.
- **Current:** I thought it wouldn’t be so bad to become a new Demon King, to follow in Asmodeus’s footsteps.
- **Defect:** “Follow in Asmodeus’s footsteps” suggests emulating him rather than succeeding him as Demon King.
- **Replacement:** I thought it wouldn’t be so bad to succeed Asmodeus as the new Demon King.
- **Rationale:** 뒤를 이은 specifies succession.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내가 지닌 삼 갑자의 공력마저 턱없이 부족하게 느껴질 만큼 거대한 저 기운이, 왜 두렵게 느껴지지 않는지.
- **Current:** As I charged toward the wave of Michael Silbert’s energy, surging like a wave in a boundless ocean, I suddenly wondered why the enormous power he wielded felt so insufficient to frighten me.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that Michael’s power makes even Jin’s three jiazi of internal energy feel hopelessly inadequate.
- **Replacement:** As I charged toward his energy, surging like waves across a boundless sea, I suddenly wondered why that immense power—enough to make even my three jiazi of internal energy feel hopelessly inadequate—didn’t frighten me.
- **Rationale:** Jin’s lack of fear is striking precisely because he recognizes how far his own internal energy falls short.

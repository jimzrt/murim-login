# Review

The draft contains two critical changes to chronology and causation, plus several meaningful fidelity issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “화왕이 이곳에서는 부상을 치료하고 있다는 것 정도는.”
- **Current:** “That the Fire King is treating an injury here.”
- **Defect:** The English makes the Fire King sound as though he is administering treatment rather than receiving it.
- **Replacement:** “That the Fire King is here receiving treatment for his injuries.”
- **Rationale:** The context and Korean indicate that the injured Fire King is being treated at this location.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “악행이라…… 노부가 저지른 살생에 관한 죄는 달게 받겠다.
- **Current:** The Heavenly Power Demon’s voice grew heavier.

“I will willingly accept the crimes arising from the lives I took.
- **Defect:** The draft adds an unsupported change in his voice and mistranslates his willingness to accept punishment as accepting the crimes themselves.
- **Replacement:** “I will willingly accept punishment for the lives I have taken.
- **Rationale:** 죄는 달게 받겠다 means that he will accept the punishment or consequences for his killings without complaint.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 만일 너희가 먼저 감숙과 청해를 넘어 본교에 도달했다면, 천하의 대의와 발본색원(拔本塞源)이라는 초라한 변명을 앞세워 교민들을 학살했겠지.
- **Current:** If you had crossed Gansu and Qinghai before reaching the Divine Cult, you would have slaughtered our followers while hiding behind the shabby excuses of the great cause of the world and rooting out evil at its source.
- **Defect:** The translation turns 먼저 into a simple sequence of travel and loses the counterfactual that the orthodox side might have attacked first, which is the causal basis of his argument.
- **Replacement:** If your side had been the first to cross Gansu and Qinghai and reach the Divine Cult, you would have slaughtered our followers under the shabby pretexts of the greater good and rooting us out at the source.
- **Rationale:** 먼저 identifies which side would have initiated the conflict, not merely that Gansu and Qinghai would be crossed before arrival.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 오랜 세월 이어진 반목. 언제 터질지 모르는 화약고 앞에서 화섭자를 쥔 채 서로의 눈치를 살피던 마교와 정파 무림.
- **Current:** The Demonic Cult and the orthodox Murim had opposed each other for centuries, each holding a match while watching the other beside a powder keg that could explode at any moment.
- **Defect:** The precise claim that the hostility lasted centuries is not present in the source.
- **Replacement:** The Demonic Cult and the orthodox Murim had been at odds for ages, each holding a match and watching the other beside a powder keg that could explode at any moment.
- **Rationale:** 오랜 세월 means a long time or many years without establishing a duration of multiple centuries.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “이직인지, 회사 내 부서 이동인지는 아직 모르겠는데 죽기 전까지는 마교가 아니라 암천이라는 곳에서…….”
- **Current:** “I’m not sure whether it was a job change or an internal departmental transfer, but until they die, they’ll be working somewhere called Dark Heaven instead of the Demonic Cult…”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the already-dead Twin Ghosts into living people who will continue working for Dark Heaven until a future death.
- **Replacement:** “I’m not sure whether it was a job change or an internal departmental transfer, but until they died, they were working for someplace called Dark Heaven instead of the Demonic Cult…”
- **Rationale:** 죽기 전까지는 refers retrospectively to the period before their deaths; Taekyung has just stated that both men were killed.

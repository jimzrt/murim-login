## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 피 가래 끓는 소리와 함께 쓰러진 금의위의 시신을 짓밟으며 들이닥친 적들이 닥치는 대로 병장기를 휘두른다.
- **Current:** The enemy rushed in, trampling the fallen guard’s body as he gurgled his last, and swung his weapon wildly at anyone in reach.
- **Defect:** Plural attackers become a single attacker with one weapon.
- **Replacement:** Enemies rushed in, trampling the fallen guard’s body as he gurgled his last, and swung their weapons at anyone in reach.
- **Rationale:** 적들이 and 병장기를 describe multiple enemies wielding weapons.

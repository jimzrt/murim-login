# Review

The draft reverses the opening sequence, narrows a key reflection about human power, and omits Ahomed’s ancestral devotion. System panels also retain square brackets around UI terms.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 하나 다음은 둘. 둘 다음이 셋인 것처럼

- **Defect:** Reverses the first step of the sequence.
- **Replacement:** one is followed by two, and two by three
- **Rationale:** The source says two follows one, then three follows two.

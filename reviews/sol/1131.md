# Review

The draft changes who knows the answer, adds a physical action, and mistakes a remembered instruction for a remembered event.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “대답은 굳이 하지 않아도 되겠군. 이미 잘 알고 있으니.”
- **Current:** “There’s no need to answer. You already know.”
- **Defect:** Changes the speaker’s claim from “I already know” to “you already know.”
- **Replacement:** “You don’t have to answer. I already know.”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung has just realized the old man will not answer his question; he has not learned the old man’s identity.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한데 너는 어찌하여 스스로 행하고도 믿지 못하느냐?
- **Current:** So why don’t you believe what you did with your own hands?
- **Defect:** Adds the hands as the means of an action the source attributes only to Taekyung himself.
- **Replacement:** So why can’t you believe what you yourself did?
- **Rationale:** 스스로 행하고도 means he did it himself; it does not specify a physical mechanism.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 지금의 이 상황을, 비슷한 내용을 언젠가 들어 본 적이 있던 것 같다는 생각이.
- **Current:** Suddenly, I had the sense that I’d heard something like this before.

That there was a similar moment I’d experienced sometime in the past.
- **Defect:** Turns the recollection of having heard similar words into a recollection of experiencing a similar event.
- **Replacement:** Suddenly, I felt as though I’d heard something like this said before, somewhere.
- **Rationale:** 비슷한 내용을 ... 들어 본 적 refers to hearing similar content, not living through a similar moment.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “유형(有形)이었습니다. 그 순간만큼은.”
- **Current:** “It was tangible. For that moment, at least.”
- **Defect:** “Tangible” implies it could be touched rather than that the formless qi took visible form.
- **Replacement:** “It had a form. For that moment, at least.”
- **Rationale:** 유형 directly contrasts with the old man’s 무형; their exchange concerns how Taekyung saw it.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 아니, 정확히는 오랜만에 찾아온 손님이 머물렀던 그 자리를.
- **Current:** Or, more precisely, at the place where his long-awaited guest had stayed.
- **Defect:** “Long-awaited” asserts anticipation rather than a long interval since the guest’s visit.
- **Replacement:** Or, more precisely, at the place where the guest who had visited after so long had stayed.
- **Rationale:** 오랜만에 찾아온 describes the visit as occurring after a long time, without saying the old man expected it.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 혀끝에 맴도는 이름을 흘려 보내며, 노인은 생각했다.
- **Current:** Letting the name linger on the tip of his tongue, the old man wondered:
- **Defect:** “Linger” leaves the name unspoken, while 흘려 보내며 describes letting it out.
- **Replacement:** Letting the name slip from his lips, the old man wondered:
- **Rationale:** The name has just appeared in the old man’s spoken dialogue.

# Review

Two critical mistranslations alter a speaker’s subject and an insult; several additional findings concern lost register shifts, sarcasm, kinship, profanity, and localized English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이상하게 만나는 놈들마다 꼭 저 소리 하더라. 실제로는 화왕이 누구인지 얼굴 한번 본 적 없으면서.”
- **Current:** “For some reason, everyone I meet always says that. Even though I’ve never once seen what the Fire King actually looks like.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the subject of the second sentence from the people Jin meets to Jin himself, falsely stating that Jin has never seen the Fire King.
- **Replacement:** “Funny how everyone I meet says that when none of them have ever even seen the Fire King’s face.”
- **Rationale:** 본 적 없으면서 describes the people who repeatedly compare Jin to the Fire King, not Jin, who is the Fire King’s Disciple.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “노옴! 주둥이 닥치지 못할까!”
- **Current:** “Old man! Can’t you keep your mouth shut?”
- **Defect:** 노옴 is an elongated form of 놈, an insult aimed at Jin, not an address meaning “old man.” The draft therefore changes the addressee’s identity.
- **Replacement:** “You bastard! Shut your mouth!”
- **Rationale:** Black Hand is angrily insulting Jin after Jin taunts both opponents; he is not addressing the slender old man.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “갑자기 웬 하오체에 헛소리냐. 심지어 조금 전에는 차례대로 상대할 테니 한 놈은 빠져 있으라고 했으면서.”

“놈? 놈이요? 제가 그랬습니까?”
- **Current:** “Why are you suddenly talking like some old-fashioned gentleman and spouting nonsense? A moment ago, you told one of us to sit out so you could face us in turn.”

“One of us? Did I say that?”
- **Defect:** The draft removes 놈, the insulting noun Jin immediately pretends to be shocked by, breaking the register-shift joke.
- **Replacement:** “Why are you suddenly talking like some old-fashioned gentleman and spouting nonsense? A moment ago, you said you’d face us one at a time and told one bastard to stay out of it.”

“‘Bastard’? Did I say that?”
- **Rationale:** Jin’s mock-politeness depends on selectively objecting to his own earlier use of 놈 while pretending not to remember it.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “시벌 새끼. 그럼 정성을 봐서라도 속아 주지.”
- **Current:** “Fuck you. At least get fooled out of respect for the effort I put in.”
- **Defect:** “Get fooled” is unnatural and loses the indulgent force of 속아 주지: Jin is asking the old man to play along and pretend to fall for it as a favor.
- **Replacement:** “You fucking bastard. Then at least pretend to fall for it out of respect for the effort.”
- **Rationale:** The benefactive 주다 makes this a demand that the old man humor Jin, which drives the deadpan exchange that follows.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “……혀가 짧아도 너무 짧군. 분명 조금 전까진 노선배였던 것 같은데.”
- **Current:** “……Your tongue is far too short. I’m sure you were calling me Senior just a moment ago.”
- **Defect:** The literal rendering of 혀가 짧다 is opaque in English and fails to convey that Jin’s speech abruptly became much less respectful.
- **Replacement:** “……That tongue of yours got awfully rude, awfully fast. I could’ve sworn I was ‘Senior’ just a moment ago.”
- **Rationale:** The old man is commenting on Jin’s sudden collapse from elaborate deference into insulting casual speech, not the physical length of his tongue.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “우선은 곱게 모셔 두었지. 마후(魔后)께서 돌아오시면 그 연놈들의 질긴 명줄도 끝장이겠지만.”
- **Current:** “For now, they are being kept somewhere safe. But once Her Majesty the Demon Empress returns, the pair of them will meet their end.”
- **Defect:** “Somewhere safe” presents the captives as protected and removes the captor’s sarcastically courteous 곱게 모셔 두었지, while “the pair of them” also softens the contemptuous 연놈들.
- **Replacement:** “For now, we have those two tucked away nice and comfortably. But once Her Majesty the Demon Empress returns, those bastards’ stubborn hold on life will end.”
- **Rationale:** Black Hand mockingly describes imprisoning Heugung and Yohi as treating them like honored guests, then contemptuously threatens their tenacious lives.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “개꿀 정보 고맙다. 일단 남천마후, 그 썅년은 여기에 없다는 소리네. 두 사람도 멀쩡하고.”
- **Current:** “Thanks for the great information. So the Southern Heaven Demon Empress—that bitch—isn’t here. And the two of them are still in one piece.”
- **Defect:** The stiff “great information” loses Jin’s gleefully crude slang, and “that bitch” softens the stronger 썅년.
- **Replacement:** “Thanks for the fucking useful intel. So the Southern Heaven Demon Empress—that fucking bitch—isn’t here. And those two are still in one piece.”
- **Rationale:** Jin abruptly drops into coarse, triumphant speech after tricking the enemy into confirming all three useful facts.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “근데 왜 둘이 손잡고 왔니. 어린놈한테 줘 터질까 봐 쫄려서 형 데려온 거야?”
- **Current:** “Then why did you two come together? Were you scared you’d get your ass kicked by a kid, so you brought your big brother along?”
- **Defect:** The established kinship term 형 is flattened to “big brother,” losing the specifically junior-to-senior male hierarchy Jin is using to mock Black Hand.
- **Replacement:** “Then why did you two come together? Were you scared a kid would kick your ass, so you brought your hyung?”
- **Rationale:** Jin sarcastically casts the colder senior fighter as Black Hand’s hyung; the relationship hierarchy is the point of the taunt.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 노인의 손을 떠난 두 자루의 쌍륜(雙輪)이, 공간을 찢어발기며 날아들었다.
- **Current:** The pair of twin wheels left the old man’s hand and came flying at us, tearing the air to shreds.
- **Defect:** “Pair of twin wheels” is redundant, and “at us” supplies a plural target that the source leaves unstated.
- **Replacement:** The twin wheels flew from the old man’s hand, tearing through the air.
- **Rationale:** 두 자루의 쌍륜 identifies two matching wheel-shaped weapons; the sentence need not add a target beyond their incoming motion.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 지금쯤 어디에서 무엇을 하고 있을지 모르는 자신들의 각주를.
- **Current:** Their Pavilion Master, whose whereabouts and situation they knew nothing about by now.
- **Defect:** “Whose whereabouts and situation they knew nothing about” is awkward and turns the concrete uncertainty about where Jin is and what he is doing into an abstract “situation.”
- **Replacement:** Their Pavilion Master, though they had no idea where he was or what he might be doing now.
- **Rationale:** 어디에서 무엇을 하고 있을지 directly expresses uncertainty about Jin’s present location and actions.

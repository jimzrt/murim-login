# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes an addressee, reverses a physical position, and adds an unsupported movement.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러게 소제(小弟)가 천천히, 사근사근하게 접근하자고 하지 않았습니까.
- **Current:** Didn’t I tell you, Second Brother, that we should approach slowly and gently?
- **Defect:** The speaker calls himself the younger brother; he does not address the giant as Second Brother.
- **Replacement:** Didn’t your little brother tell you we should approach slowly and gently?
- **Rationale:** 소제 is the speaker’s self-reference. Adding Second Brother changes whom he addresses.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그림자를 등진 채 그 광경을 지켜보는 일단의 무리가 있었다.
- **Current:** And on one of the dozens of winding hills, a group watched the scene from the shadows.
- **Defect:** The group has the shadows behind them, not around them.
- **Replacement:** And on one of the dozens of hills, a group watched the scene with their backs to the shadows.
- **Rationale:** 그림자를 등진 채 places the shadows at their backs; “from the shadows” reverses that position.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 수십여 명의 수하들을 뒤로하고 홀로 앞서 나와, 반경 삼십여 장을 빼곡히 뒤덮은 화망(火網)에서 살아남은 거한이 버럭 외쳤다.
- **Current:** The giant had rushed forward alone, leaving dozens of his subordinates behind. He’d survived the net of arrows that had densely covered a thirty-jang radius, and now he bellowed.
- **Defect:** “Rushed” invents a sudden movement; the source says only that he had come forward alone.
- **Replacement:** The giant stood ahead of his dozens of subordinates, alone. Having survived the net of arrows that had densely covered a thirty-jang radius, he bellowed.
- **Rationale:** 홀로 앞서 나와 establishes his position ahead of the others, without specifying a rush.

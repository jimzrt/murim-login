# Review

One altered physical action and two uses of a title that conflicts with the supplied glossary.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 모두가 눈 앞을 가렸던 새하얀 섬광이 사라진 빈자리를
- **Current:** The glaring white flash that had made everyone shield their eyes faded.
- **Defect:** The draft makes everyone shield their eyes; the source says the flash obscured their vision.
- **Replacement:** The blinding white flash that had obscured everyone’s vision faded.
- **Rationale:** The flash covers what they can see; no one is described raising a hand or otherwise shielding their eyes.

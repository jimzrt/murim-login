# Review

Several actionable fidelity errors alter subjects, quantities, actions, and deliberately omitted information; additional localized issues over-specify punishment and time of day.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 종리추와 친분이 있다는 사실 하나만으로도 치도곤을 치를 수도 있다.
- **Current:** Merely being acquainted with Jongni Chu could be enough to get Cheongpung beaten half to death.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the punishment explicitly near-fatal, while the source uses a colorful but less specific expression for undergoing a severe beating or punishment.
- **Replacement:** Merely being acquainted with Jongni Chu could be enough to earn Cheongpung a sound thrashing.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the threat of physical punishment without adding the unsupported degree implied by “half to death.”

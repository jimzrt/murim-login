# Review

Several actionable fidelity defects: a central hair/feet pun is lost, a youth-related insult is mistranslated, the Breath formation mechanism is blurred, and the monster taxonomy reverses the intended contrast between real and bogus experts.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “마, 네가 왜 못 걸어 다니는 줄 알아?”

- 크륵……?

“두발이 없어서.”
- **Current:** “Hey, do you know why you can’t walk?”

—Krrk…?

“Because you don’t have two feet.”
- **Defect:** The answer preserves only the literal walking joke and loses the pun: 두발 can mean both “two feet” and “hair,” making this line part of Taekyung’s sustained baldness taunt.
- **Replacement:** “Hey, know why you can’t walk?”

—Krrk…?

“Because you don’t have two feet. Hell, you don’t even have two hairs.”
- **Rationale:** The expanded English punchline preserves both meanings available simultaneously in 두발 and maintains the transition into the following hair-related abuse.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그런데 저 핏덩이는 아예 부수고, 뜯어 버렸다.
- **Current:** And yet that blood-soaked bastard had crushed them and torn them away.
- **Defect:** 핏덩이 here disparages Taekyung as a mere youngster or young whelp; it does not mean that he is soaked in blood. The draft replaces Jeok Cheongang’s age-based contempt with an incidental visual description.
- **Replacement:** And yet that young whelp had simply smashed them apart and ripped them off.
- **Rationale:** 핏덩이 literally evokes a newborn lump of flesh and is used contemptuously for someone very young or immature.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “자, 우리도 가요. 미미. 벼락치기! 회오리 일으키기!”
- **Current:** “Come on, we should go too. Mimi. Lightning! Make a whirlwind!”
- **Defect:** “Lightning!” turns Cheongpung’s first attempted command into a bare noun. Both phrases are enthusiastic commands telling Mimi to imitate the dragon’s attacks.
- **Replacement:** “Come on, let’s go too. Mimi! Strike with lightning! Whip up a whirlwind!”
- **Rationale:** 벼락치기 and 회오리 일으키기 function here as parallel attack commands, which drives the joke when Mimi cannot perform either one.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 어느덧 쉴새 없이 쏟아져 내리던 비바람도, 벼락도 멈췄다. 숨을 죽였다.
- **Current:** The rain and wind that had been pouring down without pause stopped.

Even the lightning ceased.
- **Defect:** The personified final beat 숨을 죽였다, stating that the storm itself held its breath, is omitted.
- **Replacement:** By then, the wind and rain that had been pouring down without pause had stopped.

So had the lightning.

Everything held its breath.
- **Rationale:** 숨을 죽였다 extends the chapter’s repeated personification of the surroundings and marks the ominous stillness before the Breath attack.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그와 동시에 천천히 벌어지는 거대한 아가리와, 동굴처럼 컴컴한 어둠으로 빨려가듯 휘몰아치는 물의 구(球) 역시도.
- **Current:** At the same time, its enormous maw began to open.

A sphere of water was swept toward it, as though being sucked into a darkness as black as a cavern.
- **Defect:** The draft describes the already-formed sphere as being swept toward an unclear referent. The source depicts the sphere of water churning or swirling as though it were being drawn into the cavern-dark maw.
- **Replacement:** At the same time, its enormous maw slowly opened, revealing cavernous darkness, while a sphere of water churned as though being sucked into its depths.
- **Rationale:** 휘몰아치는 specifies turbulent swirling, and the cavern-dark opening is the destination into which the water appears to be drawn.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이처럼 몬스터들의 특성이 다양하니 실제 전문가들 사이에서도, 인터넷 좆문가들 사이에서도 만났다 하면 갑론을박이 오가기 마련이다.
- **Current:** With monsters possessing such diverse traits, arguments are inevitable whenever the topic comes up—both among actual experts and among internet fucking experts.
- **Defect:** “Internet fucking experts” can still denote genuine experts who happen to be described profanely. 좆문가 is a vulgar portmanteau mocking ignorant online know-it-alls who merely pose as experts, explicitly contrasting them with 실제 전문가들.
- **Replacement:** With monsters possessing such diverse traits, arguments are inevitable whenever the topic comes up—both among actual experts and among every fucking know-it-all on the internet.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the contrast between legitimate specialists and vulgar pseudo-experts while retaining the source’s profanity.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 축복받은 지능과 육체를 타고난 존재들.
- **Current:** Beings blessed with both Intelligence and physical bodies.
- **Defect:** All embodied creatures have physical bodies; the draft loses the claim that dragonkin possess exceptionally gifted physiques.
- **Replacement:** Beings blessed with both Intelligence and extraordinary physiques.
- **Rationale:** 축복받은 modifies both 지능 and 육체, identifying superior intellect and superior physical constitution as innate dragonkin advantages.

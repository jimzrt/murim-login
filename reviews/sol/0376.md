# Review

Two critical subject/agency errors, one altered quantity, and two localized fidelity or natural-English issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 아무도 찾지 않는 언덕 위, 나무 그루터기에 앉아 모든 광경을 지켜보고 있던 소년이 문득 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** And on a hilltop that no one was looking for, a boy sitting on a tree stump and watching the entire scene suddenly spoke.
- **Defect:** “That no one was looking for” treats 찾다 as searching for the hill rather than visiting or coming to it, producing both an altered meaning and unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** And on a deserted hilltop, a boy sitting on a tree stump and watching the entire scene suddenly spoke.
- **Rationale:** 아무도 찾지 않는 describes a place no one visits or frequents; “deserted” preserves that sense naturally.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그는 꼬박 하루를 울었고 가족들을 묻기 위한 구덩이를 팠다.
- **Current:** He cried for an entire day, then dug holes to bury his family.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular 구덩이, one burial pit, into multiple holes.
- **Replacement:** He cried for an entire day, then dug a pit to bury his family.
- **Rationale:** 구덩이를 is singular, and the source describes one pit dug for the family.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그건 지금껏 아무에게도 말하지 않았고, 아무도 인정하지 않으려 했던 사실이었다.
- **Current:** It was a truth he had never told anyone and had never allowed anyone to acknowledge.
- **Defect:** The second clause reverses the subject and agency: the source says no one else had been willing to acknowledge the truth, not that Mungyeong prevented them from doing so.
- **Replacement:** It was a truth he had never told anyone, and one that no one had ever been willing to acknowledge.
- **Rationale:** 아무도 인정하지 않으려 했던 makes “no one” the subject and conveys others’ unwillingness to recognize the truth.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “술시(戌時). 성도의 서쪽 항구에서 출발한다고 했습니다.”
- **Current:** “You said you would depart from the western port of Chengdu at the Hour of the Dog.[^1]”
- **Defect:** The draft invents Mungyeong as the person who stated the departure plan and as the departing subject. The Korean omits the subject in context because Dongbong is relaying Jin Taekyung’s party’s announced time and location.
- **Replacement:** “The Hour of the Dog. They said they would depart from the western port of Chengdu then.[^1]”
- **Rationale:** 한다고 했습니다 reports what the departing party said; it does not mean that Mungyeong previously said he himself would depart.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 번개에 콩 볶듯 호칭을 바꿔 대는 선화아(船火兒) 무송에게 해결책을 제시해 주었다.
- **Current:** I offered a solution to Mu Song, the boatman, who kept changing the way he addressed me as quickly as beans roasting in a lightning fire.
- **Defect:** The literal rendering of 번개에 콩 볶듯 is incomprehensible in English and obscures the joke about Mu Song rapidly cycling through forms of address.
- **Replacement:** I offered a solution to the boatman Mu Song, who was switching forms of address at lightning speed.
- **Rationale:** 번개에 콩 볶듯 is an idiom for doing something extremely quickly; “at lightning speed” preserves its force without an opaque calque.

# Review

Two scene facts are altered, and several localized viewpoint, temporal, and wording issues remain.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 공명정대함으로 이름 높은 정파의 대협은 여인을 간살하는 취미가 있었고, 어느 사파의 고수는 재미 삼아 촌락 하나를 몰살시켰다.
- **Current:** A Great Hero of the orthodox faction, renowned for his fairness and integrity, had a habit of raping women. A master of an unorthodox faction had wiped out an entire village for fun.
- **Defect:** The translation omits the killing in 간살, changing the Great Hero's established crime from rape and murder to rape alone.
- **Replacement:** A Great Hero of the orthodox faction, renowned for his fairness and integrity, had a habit of raping and murdering women. A master of an unorthodox faction had wiped out an entire village for fun.
- **Rationale:** 간살 means to rape and kill; both actions are part of the contrast demonstrating that Mungyeong's victims deserved death.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “술시(戌時). 성도의 서쪽 항구에서 출발한다고 했습니다.”
- **Current:** “The Hour of the Dog.[^2] You said we would depart from the western harbor of Chengdu.”
- **Defect:** The draft inserts “you” as the speaker who supplied the departure information, making Mungyeong responsible for a statement the source leaves unattributed.
- **Replacement:** “The Hour of the Dog.[^2] They said the ship would depart from Chengdu’s western harbor.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject does not support addressing Mungyeong as the person who set or announced the departure; the later exchange identifies Taekyung as the one who mentioned the Hour of the Dog.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 비단 무림인뿐만 아니라 겁 없는 양민들까지 더해지니, 배웅에 나선 인파는 꼬리에 꼬리를 물고 늘어져 셀 수가 없었다.
- **Current:** Not only martial artists, but also fearless ordinary citizens had come to see us off. The crowd stretched endlessly, one person after another, until it was impossible to count them.
- **Defect:** The first-person “us” prematurely shifts the opening's external third-person viewpoint to Taekyung's narration.
- **Replacement:** Not only martial artists, but also fearless ordinary citizens had come to see them off. The crowd stretched endlessly, one person after another, until it was impossible to count them.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the departing pair and the crowd externally; first-person narration does not begin until the later harbor scene.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그리고 아무도 찾지 않는 언덕 위, 나무 그루터기에 앉아 모든 광경을 지켜보고 있던 소년이 문득 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** And on a hill that no one was looking toward, a boy sitting on a tree stump watched the whole scene before suddenly opening his mouth.
- **Defect:** “No one was looking toward” changes an unvisited, secluded hill into a hill merely outside the crowd's line of sight, and “opening his mouth” is an unnatural literalism for speaking.
- **Replacement:** On a deserted hill, a boy sat on a tree stump watching the entire scene. Then he suddenly spoke.
- **Rationale:** 아무도 찾지 않는 describes a place no one visits or seeks out, while 입을 열었다 naturally means that he spoke.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “내가 마교와 싸우지 않았다면, 온 천하가 나를 손가락질했을 것이다. 지금껏 그래 왔던 것처럼.”
- **Current:** “If I had not fought the Demonic Cult, the entire world would have pointed fingers at me. Just as it had until then.”
- **Defect:** “Until then” incorrectly moves the temporal endpoint into the past rather than extending it through the present.
- **Replacement:** “If I had not fought the Demonic Cult, the entire world would have pointed fingers at me. Just as it always had.”
- **Rationale:** 지금껏 means “until now” or “all this time,” not “until then.”

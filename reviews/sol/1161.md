# Review

One contradiction in Morgoth’s understanding of friendship, plus three localized fidelity issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그 사실을 너무나도 잘 알고 있는 모르고스로서는 그저 흥미롭고 놀라울 따름이었다
- **Current:** Morgoth understood that all too well. It could only fascinate and astonish him.
- **Defect:** “Understood that” makes Morgoth seem to understand why people sacrifice themselves for friends, contradicting the preceding sentence.
- **Replacement:** Morgoth knew all too well that they did such things. Why they did so remained a source of fascination and astonishment to him.
- **Rationale:** He knows that people make those choices but cannot understand their reasons.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 아득한 굉음에 파묻혀 지워졌다

- **Defect:** “Distant” makes the roar sound far away rather than overwhelming the screams.
- **Replacement:** swallowed and erased by the deafening roar
- **Rationale:** The screams are lost beneath the immense noise of the collision.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.87

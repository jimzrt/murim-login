# Review

The draft largely preserves the scene, but one line loses the choice between the two khans, and several smaller details need correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 제가 말씀드렸잖습니까. 차라리 머리가 잘 굴러가는 놈을 살리자고.
- **Current:** *I told you, didn’t I? We should keep the one who can think on his feet.*
- **Defect:** The present-tense wording obscures that the speaker had urged Jamukha to spare the smarter khan instead of the one they kept alive.
- **Replacement:** *I told you we should’ve spared the smarter one instead.*
- **Rationale:** 살리자고 refers to the earlier decision about whom to leave alive; 차라리 marks the preferred alternative.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 슬픔과 후회로 가득한 그의 모습에 모두가 고개를 떨군 그 순간이었다.
- **Current:** Everyone lowered their heads at the sight of him, overcome with grief and regret.
- **Defect:** The trailing phrase assigns the grief and regret to everyone rather than to Chinggen’s appearance.
- **Replacement:** At the sight of his grief and regret, everyone lowered their heads.
- **Rationale:** 슬픔과 후회로 가득한 modifies 그의 모습.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 물경 수백에 달하는 불청객들의 정체는 중원의 한족, 그중에서도 무림인이라 불리는 족속들이었다.
- **Current:** The uninvited guests—several hundred of them—were Han Chinese from the Central Plains. Martial artists, as they were called.
- **Defect:** “Martial artists” drops the established Murim designation.
- **Replacement:** The uninvited guests—several hundred of them—were Han Chinese from the Central Plains. They were the sort known as Murim martial artists.
- **Rationale:** 무림인 identifies them specifically as people of Murim, not merely people who practice martial arts.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 두 사람의 주위에서 묵묵히 앞만 보고 내달리던 유목민들의 투구 사이로 살기 어린 눈빛을 번뜩였다.
- **Current:** The nomads racing silently around them, their eyes fixed ahead, flashed murderous looks beneath their helmets.
- **Defect:** “Murderous looks” misses the established term for 살기.
- **Replacement:** The nomads racing silently around them, their eyes fixed ahead, flashed killing intent in their eyes beneath their helmets.
- **Rationale:** 살기 어린 눈빛 describes eyes filled with killing intent.

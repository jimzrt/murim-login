# Review

One critical group-identity error and several meaningful losses involving mechanism, characterization, hierarchy, and humor.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 나한테 사인을 해 달란다. 그것도 왕이.
- **Current:** He wanted my autograph. And he was a king, no less.
- **Defect:** The draft recasts an explicit request as a private desire and makes the follow-up unnecessarily clunky.
- **Replacement:** He was asking me for an autograph. A king, no less.
- **Rationale:** 해 달란다 reports that the king is asking Taekyung to do something, not merely that he wants it.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 사극에서나 나올 법한 고풍스러운 말투 속에는 보이지 않는 간절함이 숨겨져 있다.
- **Current:** Hidden beneath his archaic, historical-drama way of speaking was an unmistakable eagerness.
- **Defect:** “Eagerness” weakens 간절함, which conveys an urgent, almost desperate desire that the child is trying to conceal.
- **Replacement:** Hidden beneath his archaic, historical-drama way of speaking was a desperate eagerness he was trying to conceal.
- **Rationale:** The following fidgeting is offered as evidence of Zhu Bao’s concealed 간절함, making the intensity and attempted concealment meaningful characterization.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 도저히 안 되겠다 싶을 때는 인벤토리를 뒤져 보세요. 반 갑자짜리 영약과 만년한철 무기가 나올 수도 있으니까요.
- **Current:** *When things seem impossible, try rummaging through your Inventory. You might find an elixir that’ll shave half a jiazi off your training time, or a weapon made of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.*
- **Defect:** The draft invents a training-time reduction mechanism. The source describes the elixir itself as being worth half a jiazi.
- **Replacement:** *When things seem impossible, try rummaging through your Inventory. You might find an elixir worth half a jiazi or a weapon made of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.*

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 번씩 싸울 때마다 이게 사람 목숨인지 파리 목숨인지 헷갈릴 정도다.
- **Current:** Every time I fought, I nearly forgot whether human lives were worth more than the lives of flies.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Taekyung’s complaint about his own life feeling as disposable as a fly’s into an abstract comparison of the relative value of human and fly lives.
- **Replacement:** Every fight left me wondering whether I had a human life or a fly’s.
- **Rationale:** 이게 refers to Taekyung’s precarious life in each battle; the joke is that he can no longer tell whether his own life is a human life or a fly’s life.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 리액션이 혜자다 보니 말할 맛이 난다.
- **Current:** His reactions were so generous that talking to him was a pleasure.
- **Defect:** “Reactions were so generous” is an unnatural English collocation and obscures the sense that Zhu Bao’s gratifying responses make Taekyung want to keep talking.
- **Replacement:** He gave such great reactions that it made me want to keep talking.
- **Rationale:** 리액션이 혜자다 colloquially describes reactions that are exceptionally rewarding or satisfying, while 말할 맛이 난다 means they make speaking enjoyable.

## F09 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 무안한 얼굴로 술잔을 쭉 들이켜는데, 한참 떨어진 탁자 끝자리에서 체할 것 같은 얼굴로 앉아 있는 사인방과 시선이 딱 마주쳤다.
- **Current:** I drained my cup of liquor with an embarrassed expression, and my eyes met those of the Four Gates scions sitting at the far end of a table some distance away. They looked as though they were about to suffer indigestion.
- **Defect:** 사인방 means a group of four people, but “the Four Gates scions” turns it into an organizational label and falsely implies that they represent four Gates.
- **Replacement:** I drained my cup of liquor with an embarrassed expression, and my eyes met the four of them sitting at the far end of a table some distance away, all looking as though they were about to get indigestion.
- **Rationale:** The source identifies the people by their quantity, not as scions belonging to an entity called the Four Gates.

# Review

One critical negation reversal and several localized fidelity, register, and wording issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 순수하게 무림에서 보낸 시간만 따져도 어언 1년이 훌쩍 넘었다.
- **Current:** Even counting only the time I had spent in the Murim, more than a year had already passed.
- **Defect:** The translation omits 훌쩍, which emphasizes that the elapsed time was well over a year.
- **Replacement:** Even counting only the time I had spent in the Murim, well over a year had already passed.
- **Rationale:** The source gives a stronger quantity than merely more than one year.

## F02 — Minor

# Review

Several passages alter physical staging or political meaning; others flatten Hong Jin’s register or read unnaturally.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하나같이 경악을 금치 못하는 좌중 속에서 홀로 일어선 사 황자는 담담한 얼굴로 자신을 위해 준비된 무대에 올랐다.
- **Current:** As everyone stood there, stunned, the fourth prince alone rose to his feet and stepped onto the stage prepared for him, his expression calm.
- **Defect:** The draft has everyone standing, although the fourth prince alone rises.
- **Replacement:** As everyone looked on in shock, the fourth prince alone rose to his feet and stepped onto the stage prepared for him, his expression calm.
- **Rationale:** 홀로 일어선 singles out the fourth prince’s action; the others are described as shocked, not standing.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 텅 빈 허공에서 나타난 검은 인영(人影)과 홍진을 번갈아 바라보던 나는 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** I looked from Hong Jin to the black figure that had appeared in midair, then spoke.
- **Defect:** “In midair” places the figure above the ground; the source says it appeared out of empty air.
- **Replacement:** I looked back and forth between Hong Jin and the black figure that had appeared out of thin air, then spoke.
- **Rationale:** 텅 빈 허공에서 나타난 describes its sudden appearance, not a suspended position.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 이미 후계 구도에서 밀려난 네 번째 황자가 어떻게 대국의 황위(皇位)을 손에 넣을 수 있었을까?
- **Current:** How did the fourth prince, already pushed out of the line of succession, manage to seize the throne of a Great Nation?
- **Defect:** “Pushed out of the line of succession” implies he was no longer eligible; the source says he had lost ground in the succession struggle.
- **Replacement:** How did the fourth prince, already sidelined in the struggle for succession, manage to seize the throne of a Great Nation?
- **Rationale:** 후계 구도에서 밀려난 describes his disadvantaged position, not formal removal from succession.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 나는 새도 떨어트리는 금의위 지휘사를 고개 숙이게 만들고
- **Current:** Only thirteen—and already able to make the Commander of the Embroidered Uniform Guard, a man who can bring down flying birds, bow his head.
- **Defect:** The bird image is rendered as a literal ability rather than an expression of the commander’s power.
- **Replacement:** Only thirteen—and already able to make the Commander of the Embroidered Uniform Guard, a man whose influence could knock birds from the sky, bow his head.
- **Rationale:** 나는 새도 떨어트리는 emphasizes formidable influence; the point is that even such a man bows to the prince.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 역모를 알아차리지 못했으니 신하로서 무능(無能)했고, 그 모든 것을 그저 바라만 볼 수밖에 없었으니 무력(無力)했지.
- **Current:** I failed as a servant because I didn’t realize there was a rebellion, and I was powerless because all I could do was watch it happen.
- **Defect:** “Servant” makes Hong Jin’s failure sound domestic rather than a failure of duty to his sovereign.
- **Replacement:** I was incompetent as a subject: I failed to detect the treason. And I was powerless: all I could do was watch it happen.
- **Rationale:** 신하로서 invokes his political duty, while 무능 and 무력 form a deliberate contrast.

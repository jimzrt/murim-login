# Review

Actionable errors in location, geographic quantity, family motivation, the account of growth, and divine terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 반란군의 피와 시체로 뒤덮인 황궁의 대연회장 위, 마침내 정체를 드러낸 궁성(弓星)이 말을 이었다.
- **Current:** Above the grand banquet hall of the imperial palace, strewn with the blood and corpses of the rebel army, the Bow Saint spoke after finally revealing herself.
- **Defect:** “Above” places the Bow Saint over the hall; the source places her in the blood-covered hall and says she revealed her identity.
- **Replacement:** In the imperial palace’s grand banquet hall, covered in the rebels’ blood and corpses, the Bow Saint finally revealed her identity and spoke.
- **Rationale:** 대연회장 위 refers to the scene in the hall, while 정체를 드러낸 specifies the revelation of her identity.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뛰어난 마법사와 기사들의 명성은 바다를 넘어 전 대륙을 뒤흔들었다.
- **Current:** The reputations of their extraordinary mages and knights had shaken the entire continent, crossing the seas.
- **Defect:** Singular “continent” reduces the reach of their reputations from all continents to one.
- **Replacement:** The fame of their extraordinary mages and knights had crossed the seas and shaken every continent.
- **Rationale:** 전 대륙 means all continents; the source has already established that Morgoth’s world has twelve.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 정해진 섭리를 벗어난 존재를 보았고, 신의 선택을 받은 인간 또한 확인했으니.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 불과 이 년 전에는 상상한 적도, 할 수도 없었던 막대한 책임감의 무게도 함께.
- **Current:** Along with it came the weight of an enormous responsibility he couldn’t have imagined, couldn’t even have imagined, just two years ago.
- **Defect:** The repeated English phrase loses the distinction between never having imagined it and being unable to imagine it.
- **Replacement:** And with it came the weight of an enormous responsibility he had never imagined—and could never have imagined—just two years earlier.
- **Rationale:** 상상한 적도, 할 수도 없었던 expresses both that he had not imagined it and that he could not have.

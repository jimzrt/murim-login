# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it misreads one hierarchy cue, loses Wolhwa’s deliberately affected register, alters a stated duration, and contains several localized semantic or action errors.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “거참. 아까부터 말씀이 짧으시네.”
- **Current:** “Well, aren’t you curt? Your answers have been awfully short since earlier.”
- **Defect:** The line mistakes 말씀이 짧다 for physically short answers. Here it criticizes Taekyung for speaking informally and without the expected deference, so the current version loses the hierarchy cue.
- **Replacement:** “Well now. You’ve been awfully informal with me from the start.”
- **Rationale:** The leader is objecting to Taekyung’s consistently casual speech level, not the length of his replies.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 인신매매범과 산적이라. 우열을 가릴 수 없는 조합이다.
- **Current:** Human traffickers and bandits. It was hard to say which combination was worse.
- **Defect:** There is only one combination under discussion. The source says the traffickers and bandits are impossible to rank against each other.
- **Replacement:** Human traffickers and bandits. It was hard to say which was worse.
- **Rationale:** 우열을 가릴 수 없다 compares the two kinds of criminals, not multiple combinations.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “어떡해, 어떡해! 소녀, 너무 무서워요!”
- **Current:** “Oh no, oh no! This poor girl is so scared!”
- **Defect:** “This poor girl” adds self-pity absent from the source and loses Wolhwa’s deliberately affected, demure self-reference 소녀.
- **Replacement:** “Oh no, oh no! This maiden is so scared!”
- **Rationale:** 소녀 marks a conspicuous shift into mock-innocent feminine speech, which is part of Wolhwa’s performance and the scene’s humor.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 불과 십여 초 만에 이어질 모든 불필요한 싸움을 종결지었으니까.
- **Current:** In barely ten seconds, he had brought every unnecessary fight to an end.
- **Defect:** “Barely ten seconds” alters 십여 초, which means a little over or roughly ten seconds, and the current wording drops the prospective sense of the fighting that would otherwise have followed.
- **Replacement:** In just over ten seconds, he had put an end to all the unnecessary fighting that would have followed.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies approximately ten-plus seconds and jokes that Mukyung preemptively ended every further fight.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 눈 한쪽이 시퍼렇게 멍든 혁무진이 내게 속삭였다.
- **Current:** Hyuk Mujin, one eye swollen blue, whispered to me,
- **Defect:** The source says his eye is badly bruised; “swollen” introduces a different physical condition.
- **Replacement:** Hyuk Mujin, one eye bruised deep blue, whispered to me,
- **Rationale:** 멍든 denotes bruising, while 시퍼렇게 intensifies the bruise’s dark blue coloration.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한때 일류에 근접했던 25레벨의 무인을 산송장으로 만들어 버린 범인은 아까부터 계속 이쪽을 힐끗거리고 있다.
- **Current:** The culprit who had turned a Level 25 martial artist who had once approached First Rate into a living corpse had been sneaking glances in our direction for a while.
- **Defect:** “Approached First Rate” is an unnatural rank collocation and can suggest an action rather than proximity to attaining the rank.
- **Replacement:** The culprit who had turned a Level 25 martial artist who had once been close to First Rate into a living corpse had been sneaking glances in our direction for a while.
- **Rationale:** 일류에 근접했던 means the martial artist had been close to reaching First Rate.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “산음(山陰)까지 가려면 쉬지 않고 달려야 한다고. 괜히 늦었다가 목 달아나는 거 아니냐고 걱정까지 하던데요.”
- **Current:** “They said they’d have to ride nonstop to reach Saneum. They were even worried that they might lose their heads if they arrived late.”
- **Defect:** “Ride” adds a specific means of travel not stated here; 달려야 means they must hurry or travel without stopping.
- **Replacement:** “They said they’d have to travel nonstop to reach Saneum. They were even worried that they might lose their heads if they arrived late.”
- **Rationale:** Although they are mounted bandits, this sentence does not explicitly say they will make the journey on horseback.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 날카로운 울음소리와 함께 한 마리의 매가 사당 앞에 내려앉았다.
- **Current:** A hawk descended in front of the shrine with a sharp cry.
- **Defect:** “Descended” omits the completed action of the hawk landing.
- **Replacement:** A hawk landed in front of the shrine with a sharp cry.
- **Rationale:** 내려앉았다 explicitly states that the hawk came down and alighted.

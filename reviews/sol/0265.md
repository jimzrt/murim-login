# Review

Two critical subject changes alter who expends strength and who killed the Sect Leader’s son. The draft also collapses the repeated distinction between connection and destiny, loses a profane register joke, and contains several localized characterization and terminology errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 적천강은 이미 전투 불능이다. 여기서 더 힘을 소모한다면…… 그때는 정말 돌이킬 수 없는 일이 벌어질 것이다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang was already out of the fight. If I expended any more strength here…

Then something truly irreversible would happen.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied subject from Jeok Cheongang to Taekyung, making Taekyung’s further exertion the cause of the irreversible consequence.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang was already out of the fight. If he expended any more strength here… then something truly irreversible would happen.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding sentences explicitly concern Jeok Cheongang’s incapacitation and the danger of him consuming any more strength.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “누가 그런 말을 했었지. 한 번은 우연. 두 번은 인연. 세 번째 만남은 운명이라고.”
- **Current:** “Who was it that said this? Once is coincidence. Twice is fate. A third meeting is destiny.”
- **Defect:** Rendering 인연 as “fate” collapses the deliberate progression from coincidence to a connection or bond and finally to destiny.
- **Replacement:** “Who was it that said this? Once is coincidence. Twice is a connection. A third meeting is destiny.”
- **Rationale:** The ensuing exchange repeatedly contrasts 인연 with 운명, so they must remain distinct in English.

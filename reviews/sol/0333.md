# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains several altered actions and subjects, an implied kinship not present in the source, a misattached physical description, and losses of euphemistic and idiomatic register.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 지금 대협의 앞에 무릎을 꿇고 있는 저자가, 제 부모의 원수입니다.
- **Current:** “The man kneeling before you is the enemy of my parents.”
- **Defect:** “Enemy of my parents” can mean someone who was merely hostile to them, while 부모의 원수 here identifies the person responsible for their deaths.
- **Replacement:** “The man kneeling before you is my parents’ killer.”
- **Rationale:** Mungyeong is identifying Hwang Tae-gu as the object of his filial vengeance, not merely as an adversary his parents had.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이미 모든 것을 체념한 듯 눈을 감고 있던 황태구마저 눈을 번쩍 뜨고 문경을 바라보았다.
- **Current:** Even Hwang Tae-gu, who had seemed to close his eyes and resign himself to everything, suddenly opened them wide and stared at Mungyeong.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the act of closing his eyes uncertain and says he seemed to resign himself. The source says he had actually closed his eyes, apparently resigned.
- **Replacement:** Even Hwang Tae-gu, who had already closed his eyes as though resigned to everything, snapped them open and stared at Mungyeong.
- **Rationale:** The source unambiguously presents closed eyes as the physical action and attaches 듯 to his apparent resignation.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그럴 바에야 이 자리에서 직접 원한을 갚는 것이 낫지 않겠나?
- **Current:** Wouldn’t it be better to personally repay your grudge here?
- **Defect:** “Repay your grudge” is an unnatural calque of 원한을 갚다.
- **Replacement:** Wouldn’t it be better to take your revenge with your own hands, right here?
- **Rationale:** 원한을 갚다 means to avenge oneself or take revenge, while 직접 stresses that Mungyeong could do it himself.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 좋은 의생이 되겠어.
- **Current:** He’ll make a fine medical apprentice.
- **Defect:** The prediction is that Mungyeong will become a good medical practitioner, not that he will become a good apprentice, a status he already occupies.
- **Replacement:** He’ll make a fine physician.
- **Rationale:** 되겠어 looks forward to the accomplished role implied by 좋은 의생 rather than reaffirming his current trainee status.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 꿈틀거리는 그의 칠공(七空)에서 흘러나온 기운이 허공에 흩어지고 있었다.
- **Current:** The qi flowing from his writhing seven apertures was scattering into the air.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Hwang Tae-gu’s seven apertures writhe. In the source, the qi emerging from those apertures is what writhes.
- **Replacement:** The writhing qi flowing from his seven apertures was scattering into the air.
- **Rationale:** 꿈틀거리는 describes 기운, preserving the visible movement of his escaping internal energy rather than assigning impossible motion to the apertures themselves.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘진짜네.’
- **Current:** *He’s right.*
- **Defect:** The draft introduces a male referent for an unidentified speaker. The source simply confirms that the claim is true.
- **Replacement:** *It’s true.*
- **Rationale:** 진짜네 comments on the observed absence of corpses and does not identify the person whose remark prompted the check.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘스물밖에 안 되는 양민들이 어떻게?’
- **Current:** *How did only twenty commoners survive?*
- **Defect:** The English can mean that only twenty people survived out of a larger group, contradicting the preceding statement that nobody died. The source wonders how a mere twenty commoners managed to survive intact.
- **Replacement:** *How had a mere twenty commoners all survived?*
- **Rationale:** 스물밖에 안 되는 emphasizes the group’s small size and vulnerability, while the context establishes that every member survived.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 용무는 무슨. 나이만 보면 내 동생뻘인데 참 대단한 일을 했구나, 싶어서 보고 있던 거지.
- **Current:** “What do you mean, need something? You look about my little brother’s age, and I was just thinking about what an impressive thing you did.”
- **Defect:** “My little brother’s age” implies that Taekyung has a particular younger brother. The source only says Mungyeong is young enough to be like a younger brother to him.
- **Replacement:** “What do you mean, need something? Judging by your age, you’re young enough to be my little brother. I was just thinking you’d done something really impressive.”
- **Rationale:** 내 동생뻘 describes an age-relative category and does not establish an actual sibling.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “물론 약소한 사례는 받겠지만.”
- **Current:** “Of course, we’ll accept a modest fee.”
- **Defect:** “Fee” makes the demand explicitly commercial and loses Mu Song’s euphemistic presentation of the shakedown as a modest expression of gratitude.
- **Replacement:** “Of course, we’ll accept a small token of their appreciation.”
- **Rationale:** 약소한 사례 is deliberately decorous wording for compensation or thanks; that polite register sets up Taekyung’s blunt interpretation that Mu Song is extorting the rescued passengers.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “다름이 아니라, 마침 저도 서안으로 가는 길이었는데 한 번만 태워 주신다면 이 은혜를 결초보은, 각골난망, 이 몸이 죽고 죽어 일천 번 고쳐 죽어 백골이 되어도 잊지 않을…….”
- **Current:** “If you don’t mind giving me a ride just this once, I will repay this kindness with tied grass, engrave it in my bones, and even if this body dies and dies, dies a thousand times over, and becomes white bones, I will never forget—”
- **Defect:** The draft omits Mungyeong’s explanation that he also happens to be traveling to Xi’an. It also renders the stacked idioms as unnatural fragments: 결초보은 means repaying a kindness even after death, while 각골난망 means remembering a kindness as though carved into one’s bones.
- **Replacement:** “The thing is, I happen to be on my way to Xi’an too. If you would give me a ride just this once, I’ll repay this kindness even beyond the grave, remember it to my very bones, and even if this body dies and dies, dies a thousand times over and turns to white bones, I will never forget—”
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the omitted pragmatic reason for his request and preserves the comic escalation through multiple extravagant vows without the opaque English phrase “with tied grass.”

# Review

One scene-action reversal and several meaningful omissions or register problems require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 혁무진은 말에서 떨어질 정도로 놀랐다.
- **Current:** Unlike me, who merely nodded without much interest, Hyuk Mujin was so shocked that he nearly fell off his horse.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an actual fall into a near fall. The later statement that Hyuk Mujin climbs back into the saddle confirms that he fell off.
- **Replacement:** Unlike me, who merely nodded without much interest, Hyuk Mujin was so shocked that he fell off his horse.
- **Rationale:** 말에서 떨어질 정도로, together with the subsequent 다시 안장에 오르며, establishes that Hyuk Mujin falls and then remounts.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “허, 이 중늙은이가 철 소협 같은 소리 하네. 아까는 소대가리라면서?”

“아, 아니 철 대협! 아까는 내가 큰 결례를 저질렀소!”

“저질렀소? 질렀소? 렀소? 소? 지금 나보고 또 소라고 했지?”
- **Current:** “Listen to this old bastard calling me Young Hero Chul. Didn’t you call me a cow’s head earlier?”

“N-No, Great Hero Chul! I committed a grave discourtesy earlier!”

“Committed? You committed? ‘So’? You just called me a cow again, didn’t you?”
- **Defect:** “Old bastard” intensifies 중늙은이, while the central joke is left opaque because the draft neither reproduces the repeated -소 sound nor explains that 소 also means “cow.”
- **Replacement:** “Hah, listen to this old coot calling me Young Hero Chul. Didn’t you call me a cow’s head earlier?”

“N-No, Great Hero Chul! I committed a grave discourtesy, *so*!”

“Committed-*so*? *-so*? Cow? You just called me a cow again, didn’t you?”[^1]

[^1]: The formal Korean sentence ending *-so* (소) is pronounced the same as the Korean word for “cow.”
- **Rationale:** Chulwoo strips 저질렀소 down sound by sound until only 소 remains, then deliberately interprets it as “cow.” A brief footnote is warranted because that bilingual pun cannot be conveyed fully through natural English alone.

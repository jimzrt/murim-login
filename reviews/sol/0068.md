# Review

The draft contains two viewpoint or identity errors, one misassigned action, and several meaningful losses or additions involving register, cultivation mechanics, humor, and implied threats.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “존댓말을 쓰라고 하셨습니다.”
- **Current:** “You said I had to use polite speech.”
- **Defect:** The reply does not visibly switch to deferential speech, so the immediate compliance that completes the joke is lost in English.
- **Replacement:** “You said I was to use polite speech, sir.”
- **Rationale:** The formal ending 하셨습니다 demonstrates that Taekyung has already obeyed Mukyung's demand for 존댓말 despite his defiant narration.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진무경답다고 해야 하나?
- **Current:** Was this what it meant to be Jin Mukyung?
- **Defect:** The English misrenders the idiom and unnaturally asks what it means to be the person rather than whether the room is characteristic of him.
- **Replacement:** Or should I say this was just like Jin Mukyung?
- **Rationale:** 진무경답다 means that the austere room is typical or characteristic of Jin Mukyung.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘나 같아도 이런 놈이 동생이면 두들겨 패고도 남았지.’
- **Current:** *If I had a little brother like this, I would’ve beaten him half to death too.*

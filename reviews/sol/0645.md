# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter combat mechanics, quantities, speaker register, and the subject of the alliance decision.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 파르르 떨리는 백상의 눈동자에, 타들어 가는 그의 새하얀 옷자락과 담담한 내 얼굴이 차례대로 스친다.
- **Current:** Baeksang’s trembling eyes passed over his white robes, burning away beneath the heat, and then over my calm face.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Baeksang deliberately look over the robes and Taekyung’s face, whereas the source describes those images passing across or being reflected in Baeksang’s trembling eyes.
- **Replacement:** In Baeksang’s trembling eyes, his burning white robes and my calm face appeared in turn.
- **Rationale:** The subject of 스친다 is the images of the robes and Taekyung’s face, not Baeksang’s gaze.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 백상의 손목을 옆으로 틀어 다른 한 손을 봉함과 동시에 중심을 흐트린 다음, 남은 한 손으로 옆구리를 향해 일장(一掌).
- **Current:** I twisted Baeksang’s wrist to the side, locking out his other hand while disrupting his balance. At the same time, I drove my remaining palm into his flank.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the palm strike simultaneous with the wrist control, but the source explicitly places it afterward, once Baeksang’s other hand has been sealed and his balance disrupted.
- **Replacement:** I twisted Baeksang’s wrist aside, simultaneously pinning his other hand and throwing him off balance. Then I struck his flank with my free palm.
- **Rationale:** 다음 establishes a sequence: control the wrists and balance first, then deliver the palm strike.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 백번 양보해서 문제의 발단은 나였을지 몰라도, 백상이 펼친 수는 자칫하면 절명에 이를 수 있는 위험한 공격이었다.
- **Current:** Even if I gave myself every benefit of the doubt and admitted that I might have caused the problem in the first place, the attack Baeksang had used had been dangerous enough to kill me.
- **Defect:** “Gave myself every benefit of the doubt” means interpreting the situation in Taekyung’s own favor, the opposite of 백번 양보해서, which means conceding the point as generously as possible to the other side.
- **Replacement:** Even if I conceded that I might have started the whole thing, Baeksang’s attack had still been dangerous enough to kill me.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung is conditionally conceding his own responsibility while stressing that Baeksang escalated with a potentially lethal attack.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 모두가 지켜보는 앞에서 백상의 위상과 명성에는 쉽게 지울 수 없는 흠집이 새겨졌다.
- **Current:** And before everyone’s eyes, an indelible blemish had been carved into Baeksang’s standing and reputation.
- **Defect:** The established rendering of 명성 is “Fame,” but the draft substitutes “reputation.” It also strengthens 쉽게 지울 수 없는, “not easily erased,” into the absolute “indelible.”
- **Replacement:** And before everyone’s eyes, Baeksang’s standing and Fame had suffered a blemish that would not be easily erased.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the binding terminology and the source’s qualified description of how difficult the damage will be to erase.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 오늘 밤. 희생당한 이들을 위한 위령비를 세우고 그들을 위한 연회를 열 것이다.
- **Current:** Tonight, we will erect a memorial stele for those who were sacrificed and hold a banquet in their honor.
- **Defect:** “Those who were sacrificed” suggests that the victims were deliberately offered as sacrifices, which the source does not say.
- **Replacement:** Tonight, we will erect a memorial stele for those who lost their lives and hold a banquet in their honor.
- **Rationale:** 희생당한 이들 refers to people who fell victim or died, not to people ritually sacrificed.

## F07 — Major

- **Defect:** The line is ungrammatical, and “Elder Namho” makes Hyuk Mujin sound respectful even though 남 노인 is his indignant, insulting “old man Nam” address.

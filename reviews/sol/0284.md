# Review

The draft contains one incorrect dialogue addressee, one altered causal inference, several terminology issues involving qi and internal energy, and one misleading injury description.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 겉보기에 박태섭보다 열 살은 어려 보이는 이정룡의 하대 때문에 놀란 것이 아니다.
- **Current:** I wasn’t surprised because Lee Jungryong, who looked at least ten years younger than Park Tae Seop, had spoken down to Park Jihoon.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly identifies Park Jihoon as the person Lee Jungryong spoke down to. The age comparison and preceding exchange refer to Lee Jungryong addressing Park Tae Seop, which materially changes the hierarchy being displayed.
- **Replacement:** I wasn’t surprised by the way Lee Jungryong spoke down to Park Tae Seop despite looking at least ten years younger than him.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly compares Lee Jungryong’s apparent age with Park Tae Seop’s while referring to his condescending speech, so Park Tae Seop is the relevant addressee.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 자식이 그걸 어떻게 알았나 했더니, 지금 보니 스승과 제자 관계였다.
- **Current:** I had wondered how the bastard knew that word. Now that I saw this, they were a master-and-Disciple pair.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a reference to the word “Master.” The narrator is connecting Jihoon’s previously demonstrated knowledge or ability with the newly revealed master-Disciple relationship, not wondering how Jihoon knew an ordinary word.
- **Replacement:** I’d wondered how this bastard knew about it. Now I saw—they were master and Disciple.
- **Rationale:** The source retains the object as the pronoun 그걸 and presents the master-Disciple relationship as the explanation; resolving it as “that word” changes the causal inference.

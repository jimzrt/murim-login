# Review

The draft changes several hierarchy and character details, and omits two revealing descriptions.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그나마 한 발이라도 걸칠 수 있던 이가 명문대파의 장로급이고, 그 밑으로는 진태경에게 아는 척도 못했다.
- **Current:** The highest anyone could get was an Elder of one of the great sects. Those below them couldn’t even get close enough to greet Jin Taekyung.
- **Defect:** Reverses the threshold: Elder rank was the minimum needed to get near him, not the highest rank present.
- **Replacement:** Even an Elder of a great sect could barely get a foot in. Anyone below that rank couldn’t get close enough to greet Jin Taekyung.
- **Rationale:** 그나마 and 그 밑으로 establish Elders as the lowest-ranking people able to approach.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 검성 매종학의 제자이기 이전에 화산파 장문인 직을 맡고 있는 천검진인.
- **Current:** The Heavenly Sword True Person, Sect Leader of Huashan, before he was Sword Saint Mae Jonghak’s Disciple.
- **Defect:** Turns a statement of which role matters foremost into a false chronology.
- **Replacement:** The Heavenly Sword True Person, who was the Sect Leader of Huashan first and foremost, not merely Sword Saint Mae Jonghak’s Disciple.
- **Rationale:** 제자이기 이전에 emphasizes his standing as Sect Leader; it does not say he held that office before becoming Mae Jonghak’s Disciple.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “빌어먹을, 놈들이 사천으로도 올 줄 알았는데…… 그 호랑이 먹이로 던져 줘도 시원치 않을 것들 때문에 발이 묶였었어. 면목이 없군.”
- **Current:** “Damn it. I thought they’d come to Sichuan, too…… Those bastards weren’t fit to be thrown to that tiger for all I cared, but they tied our hands. I have no excuse.”
- **Defect:** Reverses the tiger image: he would gladly have fed those people to the tiger and still not felt satisfied.
- **Replacement:** “Damn it. I thought they’d come to Sichuan, too… But I was held up by those bastards. Even feeding them to that tiger wouldn’t have satisfied me. I have no excuse.”
- **Rationale:** 그 호랑이 먹이로 던져 줘도 시원치 않을 expresses anger that even that punishment would be insufficient.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한마디 말보다 한 방울 독을 선호한다는 만독수라(萬毒修羅) 당사독은 극진한 예를 갖추어 포권을 취했고.

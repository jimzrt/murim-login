# Review

The draft reverses who is lying in the blood pool and misstates the battlefield counterattack. It also assigns Wolhwa the wrong pronoun.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 깊은 피 웅덩이에 잠긴 채, 죽음과 맞닥트린 자신의 제자를 향해 떨리는 손끝을 내뻗던 스승도.
- **Current:** His master, submerged in a deep pool of blood, reaching out a trembling hand toward his disciple as he faced death.
- **Defect:** The draft places the master in the blood pool; the disciple is the one lying there and facing death.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung’s master, reaching a trembling hand toward his disciple, who lay in a deep pool of blood facing death.
- **Rationale:** 잠긴 채 and 죽음과 맞닥트린 describe 제자, the person the master reaches toward.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 압도적인 숫자를 바탕으로 펼쳐진 반격에도 팽팽한 전세를 유지하던 암천의 광신도들은 홀린 듯이 천주를 찬양하는 여덟 글자의 교언(敎言)을 읊었다.
- **Current:** The Dark Heaven fanatics, who had held their ground despite the counterattack made possible by their overwhelming numbers, chanted the eight words of their Lord’s creed as if entranced.
- **Defect:** “Their overwhelming numbers” attributes the numerical advantage behind the counterattack to the fanatics, who are resisting it. The Korean also specifies eight characters, not eight words.
- **Replacement:** The Dark Heaven fanatics, who had kept the battle at a stalemate despite a counterattack backed by overwhelming numbers, chanted the eight-character invocation praising the Lord of Heaven as if entranced.
- **Rationale:** The counterattack is mounted against the fanatics; 여덟 글자 counts the characters of the invocation.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 누가 나한테 그런 서신을 보냈었지. 황하가 맑아질 때를 기다리고 있겠다고. 그때 다시 만나자고.
- **Current:** “Someone once sent me a letter saying he’d wait until the Yellow River ran clear. That we’d meet again then.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes the letter writer male, although the following passage identifies her as Wolhwa.
- **Replacement:** “Someone once sent me a letter saying she’d wait until the Yellow River ran clear. That we’d meet again then.”
- **Rationale:** The source leaves the sender unnamed in this line, then identifies the sender as Wolhwa.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 무신(武神)이라는 하늘이 사라진 지금, 검성 매종학은 가장 높이 떠올라 있는 별이자 당대의 천하제일인(天下第一人)이었으니.
- **Current:** With the Martial God—the heavens—gone, the Sword Saint Mae Jonghak was the brightest star in the sky and the greatest person under heaven in this era.
- **Defect:** “Greatest person under heaven” weakens the established martial distinction and departs from its glossary rendering.
- **Replacement:** With the Martial God—the heavens—gone, the Sword Saint Mae Jonghak was the highest star in the sky and the greatest under heaven in this era.
- **Rationale:** 천하제일인 is the established “greatest under heaven”; 가장 높이 떠올라 describes height, not brightness.

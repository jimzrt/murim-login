# Review

The draft reverses two causal details and misstates a central insult; several smaller changes affect hierarchy, attribution, and the recurring joke.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 제자이자 사형제들로 이루어진 십이밀승은 물론
- **Current:** the Twelve Secret Monks—his Disciples and fellow senior monks
- **Defect:** “Fellow senior monks” turns fellow martial brothers into a rank description.
- **Replacement:** the Twelve Secret Monks—his Disciples and fellow martial brothers
- **Rationale:** 사형제들 denotes martial brothers, not monks senior to him.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정곡을 찌르는 반문에 달뢰라마는 인정 대신 침묵을 택했고, 이미 승려보다는 무림인에 가까운 그의 모습을 말없이 지켜보던 이방인은 한숨을 푹 내쉬었다.
- **Current:** Faced with the question that hit the mark, the Dalai Lama chose silence over admitting the truth. The stranger watched him quietly, looking more like a martial artist than a monk, then let out a long sigh.
- **Defect:** The English makes the stranger, rather than the Dalai Lama, look more like a martial artist than a monk.
- **Replacement:** Faced with the pointed question, the Dalai Lama chose silence over admitting the truth. The stranger watched the Dalai Lama, who already seemed more like a martial artist than a monk, then let out a long sigh.
- **Rationale:** 그의 모습 refers to the Dalai Lama’s appearance as observed by the stranger.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반병신이 아니라, 아예 죄다 병신을 만들어 버렸어야 했는데.
- **Current:** *I should’ve crippled every last one of them instead of stopping at twenty.*
- **Defect:** The punchline changes the degree of injury into the number of victims.
- **Replacement:** *I should’ve crippled them all completely instead of leaving them half-crippled.*
- **Rationale:** 반병신 contrasts with 아예 죄다 병신: he regrets not doing worse to those he already injured, not stopping after twenty people.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그렇게 살계(殺戒)가 열렸다.

양측 모두에서가 아닌, 오직 한쪽에서만 몰아치는 피바람.
- **Current:** And so, the precept against killing was broken.

A storm of blood swept over both sides—but only one of them.
- **Defect:** The second sentence says the bloodstorm swept over both sides before denying it.
- **Replacement:** And so, the killing began.

A storm of blood swept through—not from both sides, but from only one.
- **Rationale:** The source describes a bloodstorm driven by only one side.

## F06 — Critical

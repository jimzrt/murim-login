# Review

One critical identity error assigns Cheon Taemin’s savior achievement to Choi Minwoo. The draft also alters who suffers the consequences of releasing the technique, overstates the terrorists’ losses, obscures a kinship-based request, and has several localized pragmatic and terminology issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “흠. 뭘 또 거창하게 천지창조씩이나. 그래서 보복성 테러는요?”
- **Current:** “Hm. Why make such a big deal out of the Creation? What about retaliatory terrorism?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the complaint from invoking something as grandiose as God’s creation of heaven and earth to making a big deal out of “the Creation,” which sounds like a named work or event.
- **Replacement:** “Hm. Why bring up something as grand as creating heaven and earth? What about retaliatory terrorism?”
- **Rationale:** 천지창조씩이나 criticizes the grandiose comparison to God creating heaven and earth; it does not say that the President made too much of an event called “the Creation.”

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그들은 불과 한 달 전 신설된 태원진가와 하오문의 대동지부장이었다.
- **Current:** They were the Branch Leaders of the Jin Family of Taiyuan and the Lower District Sect’s Datong Branch, both of which had been established only a month ago.
- **Defect:** The syntax makes one man the leader of the Jin Family itself and attaches the Datong Branch only to the Lower District Sect. Both men lead their respective organizations’ newly established Datong Branches.
- **Replacement:** They led the Datong Branches of the Jin Family of Taiyuan and the Lower District Sect, both of which had been established only a month earlier.
- **Rationale:** The shared title 대동지부장 applies to both 태원진가 and 하오문.

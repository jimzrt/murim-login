## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 물론, 최고의 집중 상태인 만큼 오감(五感)이 극대화되는 것은 말할 것도 없고.
- **Current:** And since it required a state of supreme concentration, it went without saying that all five senses were heightened to their limits.
- **Defect:** The draft says circulating qi requires supreme concentration, while the source explains that the practice itself places the practitioner in that concentrated state, which in turn heightens the senses.
- **Replacement:** And since circulating qi brought one into a state of supreme concentration, it went without saying that all five senses were heightened to their limits.
- **Rationale:** 인 만큼 gives the concentrated state as the reason the five senses are maximized; it does not describe concentration as an external prerequisite.

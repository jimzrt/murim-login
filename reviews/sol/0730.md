# Review

Two critical errors alter identity and action, while several passages lose causal cues, kinship specificity, concealment tactics, or precise imagery.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “천태……. 아니, 최 팀장님 외조부께서 아직 건재하다는 뜻이기도 하니까?”
- **Current:** “Which also means that your maternal grandfather, Cheon Tae… I mean, Team Leader Choi, is still going strong?”
- **Defect:** The English syntax makes “Cheon Tae… I mean, Team Leader Choi” an appositive identifying Team Leader Choi as his own maternal grandfather. In the source, Jin starts to say Cheon Taemin’s name, then switches to the respectful description “your maternal grandfather.”
- **Replacement:** “Because it also suggests that Cheon Tae… no, that your maternal grandfather is still going strong, Team Leader Choi. Right?”
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Jin’s aborted reference to Cheon Taemin while keeping Team Leader Choi as the addressee rather than misidentifying him as the grandfather.

# Review

The draft contains several action, hierarchy, viewpoint, identity, and timing errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반사적으로 무기를 뽑으려던 그때
- **Current:** Just as they reflexively reached for their weapons, I warned them in a calm voice.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces an attempted draw already in progress to merely reaching for the weapons, contradicting the subsequently exposed blades.
- **Replacement:** Just as they reflexively began to draw their weapons, I warned them in a calm voice.
- **Rationale:** 뽑으려던 means they were about to draw or had begun trying to draw their weapons, not merely reaching for them.

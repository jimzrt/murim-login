# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues, chiefly the scale of the planned battle and a changed action in Jeok Cheongang’s crude analogy.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 말이 쉬워서 단판 승부지, 이건 단순히 무림인들 사이에서 숱하게 벌어지는 생사결이 아니다.
- **Current:** It was easy to call it a one-on-one showdown, but this wasn’t just another life-and-death duel of the kind that happened all the time between martial artists.
- **Defect:** “One-on-one” makes the proposed decisive battle sound like a duel, contrary to the source’s explicit distinction.
- **Replacement:** It was easy to call it a single decisive battle, but this wasn’t just another life-and-death duel of the kind that happened all the time between martial artists.
- **Rationale:** 단판 승부 means a contest settled in one round, not one fighter against one fighter.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 밑구녕이 찜찜하면 맨손으로라도 닦아야 하는 법. 어디 한번 말해 보거라.
- **Current:** “If something’s itching down there, even a bare hand will do for a good scratch. Go on, tell us.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes feeling unclean and wiping oneself into itching and scratching, losing the crude analogy to Jin’s lingering unease.
- **Replacement:** “If your ass feels dirty, you wipe it—even if you have to use your bare hand. Now tell me.”
- **Rationale:** 찜찜 echoes Jin’s earlier unease; 닦아야 means to wipe, not scratch.

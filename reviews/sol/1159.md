# Review

Three actionable fidelity findings: the scope of the Dragon’s blessing, Morgoth’s compounded insult, and his plural address.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그와는 반대로 온 세상 만물의 축복 속에서 탄생한 존재, 드래곤(Dragon).
- **Current:** And, in contrast, a being born blessed by every living thing in the world: a Dragon.
- **Defect:** “Every living thing” narrows 만물, which includes all things in the world.
- **Replacement:** And, in contrast, a being born with the blessing of all creation: a Dragon.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts the cursed undead with a Dragon blessed by the whole of creation, not only living creatures.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 한낮 언데드, 그것도 스켈레톤에 불과한 자네가 어떻게 그 정도의 힘을 지니게 되었는지 이제야 이해가 되는군.
- **Current:** Now I understand how you, a mere undead—no, a mere Skeleton—came to possess such power.
- **Defect:** “No” makes Morgoth correct himself; the source compounds the insult.
- **Replacement:** Now I understand how you, a mere undead—a Skeleton, at that—came to possess such power.
- **Rationale:** 그것도 adds the more specific, contemptuous description without retracting “undead.”

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는, 너희를 얕보지 않아.
- **Current:** I don’t underestimate you.
- **Defect:** The singular “you” loses Morgoth’s shift to addressing the Skeleton King and others together.
- **Replacement:** I don’t underestimate any of you.
- **Rationale:** 너희 is plural; Morgoth’s conclusion extends beyond the opponent standing before him.

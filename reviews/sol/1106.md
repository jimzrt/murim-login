# Review

The draft reverses the Blood Lord’s guarded demeanor and misstates the cause of the defenders’ fear. It also compresses several battlefield distances.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 여유롭지만, 결코 방심하지 않는 혈주의 태도가.
- **Current:** The Blood Lord’s calm, unguarded confidence.
- **Defect:** “Unguarded” reverses the explicit statement that he never lets his guard down.
- **Replacement:** The Blood Lord’s ease—and the fact that he never let his guard down.
- **Rationale:** 여유롭지만, 결코 방심하지 않는 contrasts ease with vigilance.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 반경 십여 장에 존재하는 모든 핏물을 흡수하고, 그 안에 담겨 있던 생명력과 힘을 집어삼킨 피의 괴물이 희열 어린 눈빛으로 나를 응시했다.
- **Current:** The monster of blood had absorbed all the blood within a dozen yards, devouring the life and strength it contained. He fixed his gaze on me, eyes bright with delight.
- **Defect:** A radius of more than ten jang is over a hundred feet, not a dozen yards.
- **Replacement:** The monster of blood had absorbed all the blood within a radius of more than a hundred feet, devouring the life and strength it contained. He fixed his gaze on me, eyes bright with delight.
- **Rationale:** The shortened distance substantially reduces the reach of his blood absorption.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 믿을 수 없다는 듯이 부릅뜬 채, 각자의 몸뚱어리에 새겨진 커다란 구멍과 수십여 장 밖에 우뚝 서 있는 괴물을 번갈아 바라보다가 이승에서의 마지막 숨결을 내뱉었다.
- **Current:** Eyes wide with disbelief, they looked back and forth between the huge holes torn through their bodies and the monster standing dozens of yards away. Then they breathed their last.
- **Defect:** Several dozen jang is hundreds of feet, not dozens of yards.
- **Replacement:** Eyes wide with disbelief, they looked back and forth between the huge holes torn through their bodies and the monster standing hundreds of feet away. Then they breathed their last.
- **Rationale:** The distance establishes how far the redirected arrows traveled.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 순교를 부르짖으며 달려드는 광신도들의 모습이, 그로 인한 공포의 물결이 일기당천의 효과마저 짓누르고 있었다.
- **Current:** The fanatics rushing at us, shouting about martyrdom, and the wave of terror their deaths inspired were even overpowering the effect of One Against a Thousand.
- **Defect:** The fear comes from the charging fanatics, not from their deaths.
- **Replacement:** The sight of fanatics charging in, shouting about martyrdom, and the wave of fear they stirred up were even overpowering the effect of One Against a Thousand.
- **Rationale:** 그로 인한 refers to their terrifying charge; the source does not make their deaths the cause.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그리고 이 광기에 휩싸인 피바람의 중심에는, 태산(太山)과도 같은 기세로 쏘아지는 괴물이 있었다.
- **Current:** And at the center of this blood-soaked storm of madness stood a monster whose aura shot through the world like Mount Taishan.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the monster stand still while his aura shoots outward; the source has the monster hurtling forward with mountain-like momentum.
- **Replacement:** And at the center of this blood-soaked storm of madness, a monster came hurtling forward with the momentum of Mount Taishan.
- **Rationale:** 쏘아지는 describes the monster’s movement, while 태산과도 같은 기세 describes its force.

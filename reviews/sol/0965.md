# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes an injury count, a distance, and several meaningful cues about resolve and allegiance.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 신월도를 타고 솟구친 도기(刀氣)에 서너 명의 팔다리가 날아간다.
- **Current:** Saber Force surged along a crescent saber, sending three or four arms and legs flying.
- **Defect:** The source counts three or four people whose limbs are severed, not three or four severed limbs.
- **Replacement:** Saber Force surged along a crescent saber, sending limbs flying from three or four men.
- **Rationale:** 서너 명의 specifies the number of people.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 불과 백여 장 밖, 희뿌연 먼지구름을 일으키며 마주 달려오는 수많은 인마의 모습에도 누구 하나 동요하지 않았다.
- **Current:** The sight of countless horsemen charging toward them, raising a hazy cloud of dust from barely a hundred yards away, did not shake a single one of them.
- **Defect:** A hundred-odd jang is roughly three hundred yards, not a hundred yards.
- **Replacement:** The sight of countless horsemen charging toward them through a hazy cloud of dust, just over three hundred yards away, did not shake a single one of them.
- **Rationale:** 장 is a unit of length substantially longer than a yard.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그들 모두는 이미 죽음을 각오했다.
- **Current:** They had all already accepted that they might die.
- **Defect:** Accepting the possibility of death weakens their stated resolve to face it.
- **Replacement:** They had all already steeled themselves to die.
- **Rationale:** 죽음을 각오했다 expresses a firm readiness to die.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 더불어 본가(本家)가 외면하려 했던 그들이다!
- **Current:** And they are the ones our family tried to turn away!
- **Defect:** “Turn away” suggests refusing the Shanxi people entry; the family considered ignoring their plight.
- **Replacement:** And they are the very people our family tried to turn its back on!
- **Rationale:** 외면하려 했던 refers to withholding help, as the following reflection confirms.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 투사의 창칼 앞에 무릎 꿇을, 나약한 패자(敗者)입니다!
- **Current:** “Losers too weak to stand before a fighter’s spear and blade!”
- **Defect:** The answer omits the image of enemies kneeling in defeat.
- **Replacement:** “Weak losers who will kneel before a fighter’s spear and blade!”
- **Rationale:** 무릎 꿇을 explicitly describes what the enemies will do before the fighters’ weapons.

# Review

One meaningful recognition beat is omitted, a key insult loses its Korean wordplay and force, and one description misstates the relationship between the martial art and its qi.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “의외로군. 이 정도면 분명 움직이지 못할 것이라 생각했는데.”

노인이 불쑥 입을 열었다.

아니, 이제는 나 역시 그의 별호를 안다. 사실은 기억해 냈다는 표현이 더 정확할지도 모른다.

“그건 대설귀(大雪鬼), 당신 같은 늙은이나 그런 거고.”
- **Current:** “Unexpected. At this level, I was certain you would be unable to move.”

“That might be true for old men like you, Great Snow Fiend.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits Taekyung’s realization that he now knows—and more precisely has remembered—the old man’s sobriquet. It also renders 이 정도면 as the unnatural and misleading power-level phrase “At this level” rather than referring to the severity of the blow.
- **Replacement:** “Unexpected. I was certain that would leave you unable to move.”

The old man had spoken without warning.

No, I knew his sobriquet now, too. Though perhaps it would be more accurate to say I had remembered it.

“That might be true for old men like you, Great Snow Fiend.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted narration establishes exactly when and how Taekyung identifies the Great Snow Fiend, while 이 정도면 refers to what Taekyung has just endured, not a cultivation level.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “기껏해야 대설귀 대신 빙신(氷神)이었겠지. 이 빙신 새끼야.”
- **Current:** “At most, you would have been the Ice God instead of the Great Snow Fiend. You Ice God bastard.”
- **Defect:** The literal phrase “Ice God bastard” is not an intelligible English insult and loses the deliberate pun between 빙신, “Ice God,” and 병신, a harsh insult. This weakens the provocation that immediately draws the Great Snow Fiend’s first visible reaction.
- **Replacement:** “At best, you’d have been the Ice God instead of the Great Snow Fiend, you fucking idiot.[^3]”

[^3]: “Ice God” (*bingsin*) puns on *byeongsin*, a harsh Korean insult roughly meaning “fucking idiot.”
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly supplies the characters 氷神 to establish the mock title while using its sound as profanity; the replacement delivers the insult naturally and preserves the wordplay without adding plot information.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 상대가 익힌 무공이 음한지기(陰寒之氣)라는 것을 알았더라면.
- **Current:** *What if I had known that the martial art my opponent had mastered was Yin-Cold Qi?*
- **Defect:** The wording equates a martial art with Yin-Cold Qi, creating a category error and an awkward English construction. The source identifies the cold-aligned nature or basis of the opponent’s martial arts.
- **Replacement:** *What if I had known that my opponent’s martial arts were based on Yin-Cold Qi?*
- **Rationale:** This preserves 무공 as martial arts while accurately expressing Yin-Cold Qi as the energy underlying them.

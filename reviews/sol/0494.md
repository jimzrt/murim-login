# Review

One critical subject reversal disrupts the setup for Mungyeong’s ambush. Additional findings correct a spoken-versus-internal line, altered physical detail, an omitted pragmatic cue, binding terminology, and localized unnatural wording.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다행히 사람들 사이에 보이지 않았으니 지금쯤 다른 곳에 있을…….
- **Current:** Fortunately, no one had seen me leave. He should be somewhere else by now…
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the omitted subject. Taekyung is relieved because Mungyeong was not visible among the others, not because nobody saw Taekyung leave. This causal mistake obscures the realization that Mungyeong may already be elsewhere setting an ambush.
- **Replacement:** Fortunately, Mungyeong was nowhere among the others, so he must be somewhere else by now…
- **Rationale:** 보이지 않았으니 refers to Mungyeong being absent from the group, as established by the preceding concern about attracting his notice and the following thought, “다른 곳?”

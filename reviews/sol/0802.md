# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s main sequence, but changes a threat to stop working into a claim of completion, misreads a request for backup, and softens a profane punchline.

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “시발. 나 안 해.”
- **Current:** “Fuck. I’m done.”
- **Defect:** The Skeleton King threatens to stop sharing his vision; the draft says he has finished.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck. I’m not doing this anymore.”
- **Rationale:** 나 안 해 means he refuses to continue, which prompts Jin’s apology. His later admission that he had already finished is the reveal.

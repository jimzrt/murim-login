# Review

The draft preserves the chapter’s main turn, but changes the first visual clue, reverses the enemy in Namho’s metaphor, and introduces several smaller factual and continuity errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 확연하게 눈에 띄는 커다란 코를 휘날리며
- **Current:** he could clearly make out their great, flapping ears.
- **Defect:** The conspicuous moving feature is the elephants’ trunks, not their ears.
- **Replacement:** he could clearly make out their great trunks swinging as they ran.
- **Rationale:** 커다란 코 refers to large trunks, and 휘날리며 describes their motion.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 의형제였던 백상의 죽음에 누구보다 슬퍼했으며
- **Current:** He grieved Baeksang’s death more than anyone.
- **Defect:** Omits that Baeksang was Yayul Cheok’s sworn brother.
- **Replacement:** He grieved the death of his sworn brother Baeksang more than anyone.
- **Rationale:** 의형제였던 explicitly identifies their relationship and gives his grief its context.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 만근거석(萬斤巨石)이나 다름없게 된 적들은 눈 덮인 산비탈을 구르고 굴러 그 아래의 마을을 덮치기 일보 직전이다.
- **Current:** Its enemies, now as heavy as boulders weighing ten thousand pounds, rolled down the snow-covered slope, on the verge of crashing into the villages below.
- **Defect:** Makes the boulders Dark Heaven’s enemies rather than Dark Heaven, the enemy threatening the world.
- **Replacement:** The enemy had become a boulder weighing ten thousand pounds, rolling down the snow-covered slope and on the verge of crushing the villages below.
- **Rationale:** 적들 names the threatening enemy in Namho’s perspective; it does not mean the enemies of the preceding sentence’s Dark Heaven.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 내부 어딘가에서 유령처럼 나타날 적들
- **Current:** the ones who might appear like ghosts from somewhere within their ranks.
- **Defect:** “Within their ranks” suggests infiltrators among Sichuan’s defenders; the concern is enemies appearing somewhere inside the territory.
- **Replacement:** the ones who might appear like ghosts somewhere within Sichuan.
- **Rationale:** 내부 refers to the interior being defended, consistent with the preceding concern about the Moving Formation.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 세인들이 서장(西藏)이라 부르며 중원에서 수만 리나 떨어진 새외(塞外)로 규정지은 땅은, 내가 이해하기로 후자에 가까웠다.

서장.

무림에서 나만이 아는 다른 명칭으로는, 티베트(Tibet).
- **Current:** And the land people called Tibet, thousands of miles from the Central Plains and classified as part of the Outer Lands, was closer to the latter, as far as I understood it.

Tibet.

The name I alone knew from the world of Murim was Tibet.
- **Defect:** Collapses the local name and Taekyung’s modern-world name into “Tibet,” then reverses which world supplies the name only he knows.
- **Replacement:** And the land people here called Xizang, thousands of miles from the Central Plains and classified as part of the Outer Lands, was closer to the latter, as far as I understood it.

Xizang.

In Murim, I alone knew its other name: Tibet.
- **Rationale:** 서장 is the name used locally; 티베트 is the other name Taekyung recognizes from his modern-world knowledge.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 상아는 어디에 한 번 튀기기라도 했는지 거무튀튀했고
- **Current:** their long, beautiful tusks were black and grimy, as if they’d been dipped in something.
- **Defect:** Changes the comic image of tusks looking fried to tusks dipped in something.
- **Replacement:** their long, beautiful tusks were blackened, as if someone had deep-fried them.
- **Rationale:** 튀기기 invokes frying, not dipping.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반지 부수는 난쟁이 영화에서나 보던 거대 코끼리들을 실사화로 마주한 청풍이 반쯤 넋이 나간 목소리로 감상평을 중얼거렸다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung stared, half dazed, at the giant elephants he’d only ever seen in movies about little folk destroying a ring. He murmured his review.
- **Defect:** Attributes knowledge of modern movies to Cheongpung. The film comparison belongs to Taekyung’s narration.
- **Replacement:** Facing real-life versions of the giant elephants from that movie about little folk destroying a ring, Cheongpung murmured his verdict, half dazed.
- **Rationale:** The source compares what Cheongpung faces to movie elephants without saying Cheongpung has seen the movie.

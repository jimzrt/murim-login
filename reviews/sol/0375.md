# Review

One critical reversal of who owes whom, plus several subject, causal, register, quantity, and wording errors.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이제는 하다 하다 의사소통까지 하다니.
- **Current:** *Now she’s even communicating with us.*
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied subject from Cheongpung and expands the exchange to include the narrator. The joke is that Cheongpung can communicate with Mimi.
- **Replacement:** *Now he could even communicate with a snake.*
- **Rationale:** The following Slytherin joke explicitly targets Cheongpung, confirming that he is the one communicating with Mimi.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 십여 명의 사람들이 모습을 드러냈다.
- **Current:** The tightly closed door of the medical room opened, and more than a dozen people emerged.
- **Defect:** “More than a dozen” raises the stated quantity; 십여 명 means approximately ten or a dozen or so.
- **Replacement:** The tightly closed door of the medical room opened, and about a dozen people emerged.
- **Rationale:** The source gives a rough count around ten, not a lower bound exceeding twelve.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 붉게 충혈된 눈으로 하늘을 올려다본 그가 이쪽을 향해 걸어왔다.
- **Current:** With eyes red from crying, he looked up at the sky before walking toward us.
- **Defect:** The draft invents crying as the cause of Tang Horyong’s bloodshot eyes.
- **Replacement:** His eyes bloodshot, he looked up at the sky before walking toward us.
- **Rationale:** The source describes his eyes as red and bloodshot but does not say that he had been crying.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘그전에 빚도 있었고.’
- **Current:** *Besides, he already owed me a debt.*
- **Defect:** The direction of the debt is reversed. Taekyung owes Tang Sadok for helping Jeok Cheongang awaken and lending him the sacred treasure.
- **Replacement:** *Besides, I already owed him one.*
- **Rationale:** The immediately following explanation lists favors Tang Sadok did for Taekyung, establishing that Taekyung is the debtor.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 도대체 앞으로 어떤 개 같은 일들이 벌어지려고 이렇게 퍼 주나 싶어 불안하기까지 할 지경이다.
- **Current:** I was starting to feel uneasy, wondering what kind of awful things were about to happen if the System was handing out rewards this generously.
- **Defect:** “Awful” sanitizes the strong profanity in 개 같은, and the draft unnecessarily makes the System the explicit giver.
- **Replacement:** I was starting to feel uneasy, wondering what kind of shitty things were about to happen for me to be showered with rewards like this.
- **Rationale:** 개 같은 is a profane expression closer to “shitty,” while the source leaves the giver implicit.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 지난 수십 년 동안, 노부가 아무에게도 드러내지 못했던 희로애락(喜怒哀樂)을 나눌 수 있었던 유일한 존재였지.
- **Current:** For the past several decades, he was the only one with whom this old man could share the joys and sorrows I could reveal to no one else.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns Mimi an unsupported male pronoun and reduces the explicit fourfold emotional range—joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure—to only joys and sorrows.
- **Replacement:** For the past several decades, Mimi was the only one with whom I could share all the joy, anger, sorrow, and pleasure I could never show anyone else.
- **Rationale:** The source does not specify Mimi’s sex here and deliberately spells out 희로애락 rather than using a generic reference to joys and sorrows.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 여기서 신기술을 써 버리네.
- **Current:** He was using a new technique here.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the action to Cheongpung, but Mimi is the one who has just performed the new move at his command.
- **Replacement:** And Mimi busts out a new move right here.
- **Rationale:** Cheongpung gives the command, Mimi performs Whirlwind and spins, and the narrator reacts to that performance.

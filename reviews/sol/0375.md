# Review

Two critical subject/action reversals, plus meaningful register, viewpoint, profanity, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “미미가 보고 싶다고 해서요.”
- **Current:** “Because I wanted to see Mimi.”
- **Defect:** The subject and action are reversed. Mimi expressed a desire to see someone; Cheongpung did not say that he wanted to see Mimi.
- **Replacement:** “Because Mimi said she wanted to visit.”
- **Rationale:** 미미가 is the subject of 보고 싶다고 해서요, and the following joke about Cheongpung communicating with Mimi depends on Mimi having conveyed that desire.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 사방에서 진동하는 탕약 냄새를 맡으며 얼마나 걸었을까. 하얀 천으로 코와 입을 가린 의원이 안내해 준 의실로 들어서자, 마침내 낯익은 얼굴들과 마주할 수 있었다.
- **Current:** I don’t know how long we walked while breathing in the smell of medicinal decoctions vibrating through the corridors. When we entered the treatment room where a physician wearing a white cloth over his nose and mouth directed us, we were finally able to meet several familiar faces.
- **Defect:** “Smell … vibrating” is an unnatural literal collocation, and the relative clause incorrectly suggests that the physician directed them while inside the room rather than leading them to it.
- **Replacement:** Who knew how long we walked through the pervasive smell of medicinal decoctions? At last, the physician whose nose and mouth were covered with white cloth led us into a treatment room, where we came face-to-face with several familiar faces.
- **Rationale:** 진동하는 describes the medicinal odor permeating the surroundings, while 의원이 안내해 준 의실 means the physician guided them to the treatment room.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 창백한 얼굴로 고개를 저은 당사독이 나를 똑바로 응시하며 말을 이었다.
- **Current:** Tang Sadok shook his pale face and continued, staring straight at me.
- **Defect:** The physical action is misrendered: Tang Sadok shakes his head, not his face.
- **Replacement:** Pale-faced, Tang Sadok shook his head, stared straight at me, and continued.
- **Rationale:** 고개를 저은 explicitly means that he shook his head; 창백한 얼굴로 separately describes his pallor.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “알고 있네. 아니, 알고 있소. 그렇기에 죄를 청하는 것이오.”
- **Current:** “I know. No—I know that now. That is why I am asking for my crime to be punished.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents a temporal realization with “now” and loses Tang Sadok’s deliberate shift from familiar speech to a more respectful formal register.
- **Replacement:** “I know. No—I understand. That is why I ask to be punished.”
- **Rationale:** 알고 있네 and 알고 있소 have the same temporal meaning; the correction marks a change in speech level toward Jin Wikyung, not newly acquired knowledge.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “……뭘요. 자결?”
- **Current:** “……Do what? Take his life?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes Tang Sadok’s proposed suicide into Taekyung personally killing him.
- **Replacement:** “……About what? His suicide?”
- **Rationale:** 자결 means taking one’s own life. Jin Wikyung has asked whether Tang Sadok would commit suicide if ordered, and Taekyung is repeating that option in disbelief.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 얼굴 몇 번 본 것이 고작인 외부인과 일가의 가주로서 목숨 걸고 지켜야 할 혈육을 저울에 올려 둔다면 나 역시 그와 같은 선택을 했을 것 같았다.
- **Current:** If I placed a blood relative he had to protect with his life on one side of the scales and an outsider whose face he had seen only a few times on the other, I probably would have made the same choice.
- **Defect:** The hypothetical begins in Taekyung’s first-person viewpoint but assigns the relationships and duty to Tang Sadok with “he,” weakening Taekyung’s admission that he would make the same choice if personally placed in that role.
- **Replacement:** If, as the head of a family, I had to weigh blood relatives I was duty-bound to protect with my life against outsiders I’d met only a few times, I probably would’ve made the same choice.
- **Rationale:** 나 역시 establishes Taekyung’s self-comparison: he imagines himself as a Family Head choosing between his own kin and near-strangers.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 도대체 앞으로 어떤 개 같은 일들이 벌어지려고 이렇게 퍼 주나 싶어 불안하기까지 할 지경이다.
- **Current:** I was so uneasy that I began to wonder what kind of hellish events were about to happen if the System was handing things out like this.
- **Defect:** “Hellish events” sanitizes the narrator’s coarse 개 같은 phrasing, while “the System” is an added explicit subject absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** I was getting downright nervous, wondering what kind of shitty things were about to happen for them to be giving so much away like this.
- **Rationale:** 개 같은 is deliberately vulgar and calls for ordinary strong profanity; 퍼 주나 leaves the giver implicit.

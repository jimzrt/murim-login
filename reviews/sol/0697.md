# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters several concrete meanings, including an insult, Honglan’s direct address, Taekyung’s description of being toyed with, and the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s intended attack.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “개 같은 년.”
- **Current:** “You ugly bitch.”
- **Defect:** The source uses a strong dog-based profanity and does not call her physically ugly. Introducing “ugly” here also weakens the later joke specifically targeting her true appearance.
- **Replacement:** “You fucking bitch.”
- **Rationale:** 개 같은 년 is an emphatic vulgar insult equivalent to “fucking bitch,” not a statement that the woman is ugly.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “시원시원하시네요. 내가 이래서 은인을 좋아한다니까.”
- **Current:** That’s why I like Benefactors.
- **Defect:** The plural turns Honglan’s teasing reference to Taekyung into a general preference for benefactors and loses her direct, formally playful address.
- **Replacement:** That’s what I like about you, Benefactor.
- **Rationale:** 은인 refers specifically to Taekyung, and the line says his refreshingly direct behavior is what she likes about him.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 누군가에게는 유희(遊戲)와 다름없었던 농락을 알아차리지 못한 나 자신에 대한 분노.
- **Current:** My anger at myself for failing to realize that the humiliation inflicted on someone had been nothing more than sport to another.
- **Defect:** The draft recasts 농락 as an unspecified person’s humiliation rather than Taekyung’s failure to recognize that he was being deliberately toyed with.
- **Replacement:** My anger at myself for failing to realize I had been toyed with for someone else’s amusement.
- **Rationale:** In context, Honglan deliberately approached and deceived Taekyung; 농락 denotes that manipulation or toying, while 누군가에게는 preserves his oblique reference to her perspective.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 끔찍하리만치 거대한 기파가 태산 같은 압력으로 사방을 짓누르고, 살아 있는 모든 것을 얼어붙게 했다.
- **Current:** A terrifyingly immense aura pressed down on every direction with mountain-like force, freezing everything alive.
- **Defect:** “Pressed down on every direction” and “freezing everything alive” are unnatural and make the latter sound like literal cryogenic preservation rather than every living being freezing under the pressure.
- **Replacement:** A terrifyingly immense wave of qi pressed down in every direction with the weight of a mountain, making every living thing freeze in place.
- **Rationale:** 기파 is a wave of qi exerting pressure in all directions, and 얼어붙게 했다 here describes living beings becoming immobilized under that overwhelming force.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 우뚝 선 채로 자신의 눈 앞에 펼쳐진 믿을 수 없는 아득한 광경을 바라보는 한 청년.
- **Current:** A young man stood motionless, staring at the unbelievable, distant spectacle unfolding before him.
- **Defect:** 우뚝 describes Taekyung standing tall or erect, not motionless, while 아득한 conveys the overwhelming vastness of the spectacle rather than merely its physical distance.
- **Replacement:** A young man stood tall, staring at the unbelievable, overwhelming spectacle unfolding before him.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes his upright stance and the staggering scale of what he sees.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 단숨에 목을 잡아 뜯어 버리고 싶을 만큼.
- **Current:** So pitiful that she wanted to tear his throat out in a single bite.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a biting attack. The source describes seizing and ripping out his throat, with no use of teeth.
- **Replacement:** Enough to make her want to rip his throat out in one go.
- **Rationale:** 목을 잡아 뜯어 버리다 is a grabbing-and-ripping action; 단숨에 means doing it at once or in one go.

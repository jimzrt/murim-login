# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one critical subject error in Michael’s comparison, plus localized losses of kinship specificity and action clarity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 뛰어난 업적과 대중적인 인기를 누려 온 매직 존슨조차 그의 명성과 이름 앞에서는 한 수 아래다.
- **Current:** Even Magic Johnson, who had enjoyed remarkable achievements and widespread popularity, was a level below Cheon Taemin when it came to fame and reputation.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Cheon Taemin the person Magic Johnson cannot rival, but the surrounding internal monologue refers to Michael Silbert’s own fame and name. This changes the basis of Michael’s conclusion that he is the only viable candidate remaining.
- **Replacement:** Even Magic Johnson, despite his remarkable achievements and widespread popularity, could not compare to Michael Silbert in fame or reputation.
- **Rationale:** The possessive 그의 points to Michael within his self-aggrandizing internal monologue, immediately before he concludes that only he remains.

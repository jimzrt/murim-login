# Review

The draft preserves the fight’s sequence, but changes two physical details and adds an unsupported explanation of Taekyung’s internal energy.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 힘주어 뻗은 발끝을 따라 흐르는 화염.
- **Current:** Flames streamed from his forcefully extended toes.
- **Defect:** The flames flow along his extended feet, not out of his toes.
- **Replacement:** Flames streamed along his feet as he thrust them forward.
- **Rationale:** 발끝을 따라 describes the path of the flames, not their point of origin.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96

- **Current:** The old man’s fist, beneath skin so wrinkled it was impossible to guess his age, held overwhelming force—more than Jin Taekyung could hope to resist. Yet he dug in his heels as he was slowly pushed back.
- **Defect:** “More than ... could hope to resist” makes resistance impossible, though Taekyung holds against the force; “dug in his heels” also obscures that his feet are sliding back.

- **Rationale:** 항거하기 힘들 만큼 means difficult to resist, while 서서히 밀려 나가는 발끝 specifies his feet being pushed back.

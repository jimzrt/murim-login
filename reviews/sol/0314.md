## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “첫째. 진 소협의 사문은 삼류가 아니고 그 역시 떨거지가 아니라네. 둘째, 자네는 나보다 한 수 아래야. 우리가 처음 만났던 그 날부터 지금까지 변함없었던 사실이지.”

“지금 뭐라 했소?”

“나보다 한 수 아래라고 했네. 술에 취해 난동을 피우는 자네의 모습을 보니 앞으로도 같을 거라 생각되는군.”
- **Current:** “First, Young Hero Jin’s sect is not Third Rate, and he is not a nobody. Second, you are one move beneath me. That has been an unchanged fact from the day we first met until now.”

“What did you just say?”

“I said you are one move beneath me. Seeing you run amok while drunk, I suspect that will remain true in the future as well.”
- **Defect:** “One move beneath me” misstates 한 수 아래, which describes an overall difference in skill rather than a literal single-move deficit.
- **Replacement:** “First, Young Hero Jin’s sect is not Third Rate, and he is not a nobody. Second, you are a level below me. That has remained true from the day we first met until now.”

“What did you just say?”

“I said you are a level below me. Seeing you run amok while drunk, I suspect that will remain true in the future as well.”
- **Rationale:** The exchange establishes Baek Museong’s continuing superiority over Hyuk Sopyung, not a quantitative difference of one technique or move.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “어, 어떻게 칠 성의 천하삼십육검을 이리 쉽게…….”

“칠 성밖에 안 익혔어? 어쩐지 좀 허접하더라. 최소한 구 성까지는 익히고 다시 덤벼 봐. 나야 이미 겪어 본 적이 있으니 결과는 비슷하겠지만.”

“구, 구 성의 천하삼십육검을 겪어 본 적이 있단 말이냐?”

“있지. 피하기도 했고.”

“말도 안 되는 소리! 구 성까지 익힌 분은 본문에서도 다섯이 채 되지 않는다!”

“그 다섯 중 하나거든. 듣기로는 종남에서 세 손가락 안에 드는 고수라더라고.”
- **Current:** “How could you defeat the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords at the seventh stage so easily…?”

“You only learned seven stages? No wonder it felt so half-assed. Master at least the ninth stage and come at me again. I’ve already experienced it, so the result will probably be the same, but still.”

“You’ve experienced the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords at the ninth stage?”

“I have. I even dodged it.”

“That’s impossible! Fewer than five people in our sect have mastered the ninth stage!”

“I’m talking about one of those five. I heard he’s one of the top three masters in Zhongnan.”
- **Defect:** 칠 성 and 구 성 are seven-tenths and nine-tenths degrees of mastery, not discrete seventh and ninth stages.
- **Replacement:** “H-how could you overcome the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords at seven-tenths mastery so easily…?”

“You’ve only mastered seven-tenths of it? No wonder it felt so half-assed. Get it to at least nine-tenths before you come at me again. I’ve already faced it once, so the result will probably be the same, but still.”

“You’ve faced the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords at n-nine-tenths mastery?”

“I have. I even dodged it.”

“That’s impossible! Fewer than five people in our sect have mastered it to nine-tenths!”

“I’m talking about one of those five. I heard he’s one of the top three masters in Zhongnan.”
- **Rationale:** 성 expresses proportional attainment here. Turning it into numbered stages changes the technique’s mastery system and the quantities being discussed.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters one tactical command, adds unsupported positioning, omits characterization cues, and repeatedly misses deliberate shifts in the Warlord’s speech and Taekyung’s vulgar register.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이렇게 새로운 존재로 거듭난 그에게, 몇 안 되는 인간의 무리 따위는 하찮기 그지없었다.
- **Current:** To this newly reborn existence, the small group of humans surrounding him was utterly insignificant.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that the humans are surrounding him, which the source does not state and which changes the scene's positioning.
- **Replacement:** To him, newly reborn as something else, a mere handful of humans was utterly insignificant.
- **Rationale:** 몇 안 되는 인간의 무리 means only a small group or handful of humans; it does not say they have encircled him.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정신없이 도망치는 오십여 명의 인간들을 흐뭇하게 바라보던 그의 눈에 한 인간이 들어온 것은.
- **Current:** That was when he spotted a human among the fifty or so people fleeing in a panic.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the Warlord is watching the fleeing humans with satisfaction, losing a meaningful characterization cue.
- **Replacement:** That was when, as he watched the fifty or so humans flee in panic with satisfaction, one of them caught his eye.
- **Rationale:** 흐뭇하게 explicitly describes his pleased, satisfied reaction to their flight.

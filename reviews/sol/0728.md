# Review

The draft adds a gender not present in the source, pluralizes a singular deceased person, alters Im Kkeokjeong’s commitment, drops the Arch Lich’s rank, and mistranslates an idiom as a proper name. Several lines also soften the source’s profanity or omit established terminology.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘저놈이 똥이라도 싸 놨으면 골치 아픈데.’
- **Current:** *It’ll be a pain in the ass if that bastard posted anything stupid.*
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the narrator’s deliberately crude image of the Skeleton King leaving shit behind with the sanitized and generic “anything stupid.”
- **Replacement:** *It’ll be a pain in the ass if that bastard left some shit on there.*
- **Rationale:** 똥이라도 싸 놨으면 is explicitly scatological and establishes the profanity and comic register of Taekyung’s concern.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무림맹주도 저렇게는 못한다.
- **Current:** Even the Alliance Leader couldn’t do that.
- **Defect:** The draft omits 무림 and turns the specific Murim Alliance Leader into a generic Alliance Leader.
- **Replacement:** Even the Murim Alliance Leader couldn’t do that.
- **Rationale:** 무림맹주 specifically denotes the leader of the Murim Alliance, and the binding terminology requires “Murim Alliance.”

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 들어 보니까 결국 모두를 위해서 하는 일인데, 싫다고 하면 나만 나쁜 년 되는 거 아냐?
- **Current:** If I say I don’t like it, doesn’t that make me the only bad woman here?
- **Defect:** “Bad woman” is unnatural English and sanitizes the coarse, self-directed 년, weakening Song Song’s blunt register.
- **Replacement:** If I say I don’t want to, doesn’t that make me the only bitch here?
- **Rationale:** 나쁜 년 is a profane characterization, while 싫다고 하면 means refusing or saying she does not want to do it, not merely saying she dislikes it.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 나는 중국에서 아크 리치를 때려잡은 후부터 전 세계에 이름을 알렸고,
- **Current:** Ever since I beat the Lich in China, my name had become known throughout the world.

- **Replacement:** Ever since I took down the Arch Lich in China, my name had become known throughout the world.
- **Rationale:** 아크 리치 explicitly identifies the monster as an Arch Lich; omitting the modifier loses its classification and stature.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그전에 빠질 생각도 없지.
- **Current:** I wasn’t planning to leave before that, either.
- **Defect:** The draft treats 그전에 as an unclear chronological “before that,” obscuring Im Kkeokjeong’s point that he never intended to withdraw regardless of whether it was already too late.
- **Replacement:** I never intended to pull out in the first place.
- **Rationale:** In response to Song Song saying it is already too late to pull out, Im states that he had no intention of doing so even before reaching that point.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 먼저 떠난 사람을 생각해서라도.
- **Current:** At least for the sake of those who left before us.
- **Defect:** The draft pluralizes the singular 사람, implying multiple departed people instead of the one specific person whose absence immediately becomes the focus.
- **Replacement:** At least for the sake of the one who left us first.
- **Rationale:** 사람 is singular here, and the following passage centers on one person’s empty place and grave.

## F07 — Minor

- **Current:** I visited the cemetery today.

- **Rationale:** 산소 refers to a grave or gravesite in this context, reinforced by the immediately following gravestone.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 해서 다음에는 모두 함께 그분을 뵈러 갔으면 합니다.
- **Current:** So next time, I’d like us all to go and visit her together.
- **Defect:** The draft adds the gendered pronoun “her,” although 그분 is honorific and gender-neutral; this introduces identity information absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** So next time, I’d like all of us to go visit that person together.
- **Rationale:** 그분 supplies respect but no gender, and the binding terminology specifically maps it to “that person.”

## F09 — Major

- **Source:** 말하기에 앞서 이미 대강의 논의는 끝내 놓은 상태.
- **Current:** We had already finished the rough discussion before bringing it up.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 젠장. 이건 뭐 똥개 훈련도 아니고…….
- **Current:** “Damn it. This isn’t some Ddong Gae drill……”
- **Defect:** The draft mistakes the common idiom 똥개 훈련 for the proper name Ddong Gae, producing an unintelligible line.
- **Replacement:** “Damn it. They’ve got us running around in circles……”
- **Rationale:** 똥개 훈련 refers to being needlessly made to repeat actions or run back and forth, which fits the department head ordering emergency measures only for the power to return immediately.

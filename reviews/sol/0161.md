# Review

One critical action error, two meaningful attribution/reaction errors, and several localized fidelity or natural-English issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 칭겐은 혀를 내둘렀다.
- **Current:** Chinggen clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** The idiom means he marveled or was astounded, not that he clicked his tongue in disapproval.
- **Replacement:** Chinggen was astounded.
- **Rationale:** 혀를 내두르다 describes an astonished reaction here, whereas “clicked his tongue” conveys a different physical action and attitude.

## F02 — Minor

- **Source:** 어느 정도 상황 파악을 끝낸 칭겐은 침잠한 눈빛으로 흑사를 바라봤다.
- **Current:** He stared at Black Sand with a sunken gaze.
- **Defect:** “Sunken gaze” is an unnatural anatomical calque and does not convey Chinggen’s subdued, serious look.

- **Rationale:** 침잠한 describes a settled, subdued, or contemplative expression rather than physically sunken eyes.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “나와 함께 성(城)을 도모해 보지 않겠소?”
- **Current:** “Would you care to build a stronghold with me?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes Black Sand’s proposal from conspiring to seize or attack a city into constructing a stronghold.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “목소리를 낮춰라. 목젖을 도려내 버리기 전에.”
- **Current:** “Lower your voice. I’ll carve out your throat before you know it.”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the specific threatened target, 목젖, with the broader and anatomically awkward “throat,” and adds “before you know it.”
- **Replacement:** “Lower your voice before I cut out your Adam’s apple.”
- **Rationale:** 목젖 refers here to the protruding throat structure, and 전에 directly makes lowering his voice a condition to avoid the threatened mutilation.

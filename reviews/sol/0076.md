# Review

Several localized fidelity issues alter quantities, hierarchy, speaker attribution, causal explanation, subjects, and register; additional wording weakens two jokes.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 부천에 존재하는 게이트만 백여 개다.
- **Current:** There were more than a hundred Gates in Bucheon.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an approximate quantity of around one hundred into a definite quantity exceeding one hundred.
- **Replacement:** There were around a hundred Gates in Bucheon.
- **Rationale:** 백여 개 means approximately one hundred, not necessarily more than one hundred.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어지간한 중소 길드는 발도 들일 수 없는 동네라는 사실을.
- **Current:** This was a neighborhood where all but the most powerful mid-sized Guilds couldn’t even set foot.
- **Defect:** The draft omits small Guilds and invents a distinction involving unusually powerful mid-sized Guilds, altering the economic hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** This was a neighborhood where your average small or mid-sized Guild couldn’t even set foot.
- **Rationale:** 어지간한 중소 길드 refers to ordinary small and mid-sized Guilds collectively.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “엄청난 쇠고집이셔서 밀집 지역 재개발 당시에 어떤 거액을 제시해도 응하지 않으셨죠. 나중에는 다른 길드들도 이미 자리를 잡은 뒤였고…… 결국 유족분들 통해서 저희가 매입했습니다.”
- **Current:** She had been incredibly stubborn. During the redevelopment of the Gate-dense area, she had refused every enormous offer they made. By the time other Guilds had established themselves, there was no other choice……

“In the end, we purchased the property through her surviving family.”
- **Defect:** Most of Butler Kim’s dialogue is incorrectly converted into narration, and the draft adds the unsupported causal claim that there was no other choice.
- **Replacement:** “She was incredibly stubborn, so during the redevelopment of the Gate-dense area, she refused no matter how much money they offered. By then, the other Guilds had already established themselves… In the end, we purchased the property from her surviving family.”
- **Rationale:** The entire passage is spoken by Butler Kim. The source says the other Guilds had already settled in, but does not explicitly claim that this left no alternative.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “저, 진짜 열심히 해 보겠습니다. 길드 하우스가 순이네 수퍼건 순이네 빌딩이건 상관없어요.”
- **Current:** “I’ll really work hard. I don’t care whether our Guild house is Sooni’s Super or Sooni’s skyscraper.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns a generic building into a skyscraper, exaggerating the contrast beyond the source.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll really work hard. I don’t care whether our Guild house is Sooni’s Super or Sooni’s Building.”
- **Rationale:** 빌딩 means a building here; the joke contrasts the humble store name with a more prestigious building name, not necessarily a skyscraper.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 두 사람이 뭐 이런 새끼가 있나 하는 표정으로 나를 응시하던 그 순간이었다.
- **Current:** Just as they stared at me with expressions that seemed to ask what kind of person I was, a sound rang out.
- **Defect:** The draft sanitizes the explicitly insulting 새끼 into the neutral “person,” losing the contemptuous comic reaction.
- **Replacement:** Just as they stared at me with expressions that seemed to ask, *What kind of asshole is this?* a sound rang out.
- **Rationale:** 뭐 이런 새끼가 있나 is a profane, disparaging reaction, not a neutral question about what kind of person he is.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “젊은 사람이 의리가 있어. 저기 김 씨도 과묵해서 그렇지 사람이 참 괜찮더라고. 송 양이야 말할 것도 없고.”
- **Current:** “Young people these days have loyalty. Kim over there is quiet, but he’s a good man, too. And Miss Song goes without saying.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular subject—the young Team Leader Choi—into a generalization about young people and adds “these days.” It also flattens 김 씨 into the bare surname “Kim.”
- **Replacement:** “That young man knows what loyalty means. Mr. Kim over there doesn’t say much, but he’s a really good man. And Miss Song goes without saying.”
- **Rationale:** 젊은 사람 refers specifically to Team Leader Choi, while 김 씨 is a respectful reference to Mr. Kim.

## F11 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** ‘짝!’이 아니라 퍽?
- **Current:** *Thwack?* Not *smack*?
- **Defect:** “Thwack” and “smack” overlap too closely in English to clearly preserve the joke that Taekyung expected a slap but received a solid punch.

## F12 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** ‘옛날에는 강남에 집 한 채 있으면 금수저 소리 들었지.’
- **Current:** “Back then, if you owned even one house in Gangnam, people called you a silver spoon.”
- **Defect:** “Called you a silver spoon” is not idiomatic English and treats the English idiom as a label for a person.
- **Replacement:** “Back then, if you owned even one house in Gangnam, people said you were born with a silver spoon in your mouth.”
- **Rationale:** 금수저 denotes someone born into wealth; the replacement conveys that class implication in natural English.

# Review

The draft contains two critical scene-fidelity errors, several meaningful omissions and altered actions, and localized register and wording problems.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그는 사천에 위치한 공도문(珙刀門)이라는 문파의 일대 제자로, 이, 삼대 제자들 삼십여 명과 함께 이번 임무에 차출되었다.
- **Current:** He had been called out for this mission along with more than thirty second- and third-generation disciples.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an approximate quantity of around thirty into a definite quantity greater than thirty.
- **Replacement:** He had been called out for this mission along with around thirty second- and third-generation disciples.
- **Rationale:** 삼십여 명 means approximately thirty people, not necessarily more than thirty.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그렇잖습니까. 삼괴인지 뭔지. 진작 도망치고 없는 놈을 엄한 곳에서 찾고 있으니, 원.
- **Current:** You know what I mean. That Three Fiends fellow or whatever. We’re searching every damn place for someone who ran away ages ago.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the claim that they are searching the wrong place with a claim that they are searching everywhere, and it adds stronger profanity.
- **Replacement:** You know what I mean. That Third Fiend or whatever. The bastard ran off ages ago, and we’re searching for him in all the wrong places.
- **Rationale:** 엄한 곳 means an irrelevant or wrong place, while 삼괴 here identifies the third of the Qilian Three Fiends rather than the collective Three Fiends.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 사천당문을 멸문의 위기에서 구한 ‘그들’이 없었다면, 유구한 역사를 지닌 세 개의 명문대파는 닷새 전 무림에서 사라졌을 것이다.
- **Current:** If not for *them*, the three illustrious sects with their long histories would have vanished from the Murim five days ago.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the clause identifying “them” as the people who saved the Sichuan Tang Clan from annihilation, leaving the final member of the preceding three-part list undefined.
- **Replacement:** If not for *them*, who had saved the Sichuan Tang Clan from annihilation, the three illustrious sects with their long histories would have vanished from the Murim five days ago.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly explains what the third group accomplished, completing the parallel references to the rescues of Qingcheng, Emei, and the Sichuan Tang Clan.

- **Current:** Thirty years ago, at the age of one hundred, she had left the mortal world. She had been the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun’s only Senior Aunt, and everyone believed she had already died.
- **Defect:** “Left the mortal world” conventionally means died in English, collapsing the source’s distinction between withdrawing from worldly affairs and subsequently being believed dead.
- **Replacement:** Thirty years ago, at the age of one hundred, she had withdrawn from worldly affairs. She was the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun’s only Senior Aunt, and everyone believed she had since died.

# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it adds an unspoken physical action, weakens a custody detail central to the substitution mystery, reverses the subject of a reaction, leaves one character untranslated, and contains one awkward inheritance construction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그 사실을 깨달은 주화란의 입술에서 피가 흘렀고, 총표두 허준의 얼굴은 시커멓게 물들었다.
- **Current:** When Ju Hwaran realized that, blood seeped from her bitten lips, and Chief Escort Heo Jun’s face darkened.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that Ju Hwaran bit her lips. The source only states that blood flowed from her lips; her explicit lip-biting occurs later.
- **Replacement:** When Ju Hwaran realized that, blood ran from her lips, and Chief Escort Heo Jun’s face darkened.
- **Rationale:** Removing “bitten” preserves the source’s action sequence and avoids introducing an action before it is explicitly narrated.

## F02 — Major

- **Source:** ‘직접 보고, 갖고 있기까지 했는데.’
- **Current:** *I saw it myself. I even carried it.*
- **Defect:** “Carried it” narrows the statement to physical transport, while 갖고 있기까지 했는데 emphasizes that she personally retained possession of the authenticated item. That custody detail is meaningful to the mystery of when it was exchanged.
- **Replacement:** *I saw it myself. I even had it in my possession.*
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both her direct verification and her continued personal custody of the casket.

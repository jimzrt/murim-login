# Review

The draft contains several actionable fidelity issues involving the energy terminology, a changed subject, an overstated quantity, a lost joke setup, and inconsistent place-name terminology.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 말과 동시에 흘려 보낸 기파(氣波)가 부족장을 향해 쏘아지자, 앞으로 나선 부족장의 얼굴이 백지장처럼 새하얗게 물들었다.

“흡.”

이미 백척간두(百尺竿頭)다.

단 한 걸음만 잘못 내디뎌도 추락하는 상황.

흐름에 따라 주도권을 내어 준다면 그것으로 끝장이다.

나는 조용하게, 그러나 강대한 기파를 흘려 보내며 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** At the same time as I spoke, the Force I released shot toward the chieftain. The chieftain who had stepped forward went as pale as a sheet of paper.

“Ghk.”

We were already standing at the edge of a hundred-foot pole.

One wrong step, and we would fall.

If I surrendered the initiative to the flow of events, it would all be over.

I released a quiet but overwhelming wave of Force and spoke.
- **Defect:** The draft renders 기파, an emitted wave of qi, as the capitalized system term “Force,” which is reserved for 강기, and changes Taekyung’s singular predicament into a collective one.
- **Replacement:** As I spoke, the wave of qi I released shot toward the chieftain. The chieftain who had stepped forward went as pale as a sheet.

“Ghk.”

I was already balanced atop a hundred-foot pole.

One wrong step, and I would fall.

If I surrendered the initiative to the flow of events, it would all be over.

I quietly released an overwhelming wave of qi and spoke.
- **Rationale:** Both instances of 기파 denote ordinary Murim qi projected as pressure, not Jin Taekyung’s Force. The following lines describe his own precarious position and the consequences if he yields the initiative.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “나, 백족의 대족장 백상은 죄인을 추궁하기에 앞서 스스로 결백함을 밝히고자 한다. 이는 하늘과 땅, 그리고 나와 함께 있던 열다섯 명의 부족장을 비롯한 일백의 전사들이 입증할 것이다!”
- **Current:** “I, Baeksang, great chieftain of the Bai people, wish to establish my own innocence before interrogating the culprit. Heaven and earth, the fifteen chieftains who were with me, and the one hundred warriors present will all prove it!”
- **Defect:** Calling the warriors “present” makes their current attendance the relevant fact, whereas Baeksang says that the hundred warriors were with him and therefore corroborate his alibi.
- **Replacement:** “I, Baeksang, great chieftain of the Bai people, wish to establish my own innocence before interrogating the culprit. Heaven and earth, along with the fifteen chieftains and the hundred warriors who were with me, will all attest to it!”
- **Rationale:** 나와 함께 있던 modifies both the fifteen chieftains and the hundred warriors; their presence with Baeksang at the relevant time is the mechanism of his proof.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 일백에 달하는 생명이 참혹하게 몰살당했으니까.
- **Current:** And more than a hundred lives had been brutally massacred in the Inner Palace, of all places.
- **Defect:** The draft changes a figure reaching or approaching one hundred into a figure explicitly greater than one hundred.
- **Replacement:** And close to a hundred people had been brutally slaughtered in the Inner Palace, of all places.
- **Rationale:** 일백에 달하는 means reaching or amounting to one hundred, not exceeding it. “People” also avoids the unnatural collocation of massacring “lives.”

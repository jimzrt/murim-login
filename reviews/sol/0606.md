# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one scene-action reversal, repeated narrowing of damage to casualties, an altered monetary quantity, and several localized mechanism, register, and English-usage issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 변이 게이트가 아니라니. 이건 또 무슨 소리야?
- **Current:** “It wasn’t a Mutated Gate? What the hell does that mean?”
- **Defect:** The draft turns internal narration into dialogue spoken aloud, changing the scene action.
- **Replacement:** *It wasn’t a Mutated Gate? What the hell was that supposed to mean?*
- **Rationale:** The unquoted Korean is Jin’s internal reaction, not a spoken line.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 천만다행으로 별다른 피해는 없었어.
- **Current:** Fortunately, there weren’t any significant casualties.
- **Defect:** The draft narrows 피해, which covers harm or damage generally, to casualties alone.
- **Replacement:** By sheer luck, there wasn’t any significant damage.
- **Rationale:** Magic Johnson says there was no significant damage, not merely that no one was killed or injured.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 진정한 문제는 그로 인해 일시적으로 폭증하는 힘.
- **Current:** The real problem was the temporary explosion of power that resulted from it.
- **Defect:** “Explosion of power” is misleading beside the later literal explosion and does not accurately convey 폭증, a rapid surge or spike.
- **Replacement:** The real problem was the temporary surge in power it caused.
- **Rationale:** The source describes power increasing explosively, not an actual explosion at this point.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 한 사람의 성격이나 도량을 논하는 것이 아니라, 얼마나 많은 힘을 품고 흘러넘치지 않게 유지할 수 있냐는 측면에서 그렇다.
- **Current:** Not in terms of a person’s character or capacity, but in terms of how much power they could contain and keep from overflowing.
- **Defect:** Rendering 도량 as “capacity” blurs the source’s explicit contrast between figurative personal caliber and literal capacity to contain power.
- **Replacement:** Not in terms of a person’s character or breadth of mind, but in terms of how much power they could contain without letting it overflow.
- **Rationale:** Here 도량 means breadth of character or mind; the following clause supplies the separate, mechanical sense of capacity.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이번 테러로 피해가 발생하지 않은 것도 다행이지만, 그보다 더 안도할 만한 일은 뒤에 벌어질 만약의 사태를 예방했다는 것이다.
- **Current:** It was fortunate that the terrorist attack had caused no casualties, but an even greater relief was that it had prevented a possible future disaster.
- **Defect:** The draft again narrows 피해 to casualties and turns the broader deterrence of subsequent incidents into one singular future disaster.
- **Replacement:** It was fortunate that the terrorist attack had caused no damage, but the greater relief was that it would deter similar incidents in the future.
- **Rationale:** The source refers broadly to the lack of damage and to preventing possible situations that might otherwise arise afterward.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 훌륭한 재원과 수십억을 우습게 호가하는 마정석을 허무하게 잃어버린 것이다.
- **Current:** They had pointlessly lost both a valuable asset and a Magic Gem worth tens of billions.
- **Defect:** The monetary quantity is inflated: 수십억 means several billion or simply billions, not tens of billions.
- **Replacement:** They had thrown away a valuable asset and a Magic Gem easily worth billions.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the stated magnitude and the sense that the gem readily commands that price.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 사실 요즘 테러 단체의 움직임이 심상치 않아.
- **Current:** Actually, the activities of terrorist organizations have been unusual lately.
- **Defect:** “Unusual” understates 심상치 않아, which signals that their recent movements are alarming or ominous.
- **Replacement:** Actually, terrorist organizations have been making some alarming moves lately.
- **Rationale:** The source conveys danger and cause for concern, supporting the subsequent warning not to lower their guard.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 살아남은 강자들은 자신들만의 규칙으로 세상을 재조립했고 끝난 줄 알았던 분쟁은 지금까지도 이어지고 있었다.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 반사적으로 맞장구친 나는, 매직 존슨의 스태프를 향해 순환되는 마나의 흐름을 느끼고 떨떠름한 표정을 지었다.
- **Current:** I had agreed automatically, but then I felt the flow of mana circulating through Magic Johnson’s staff and made a reluctant face.
- **Defect:** “Made a reluctant face” is not idiomatic English and misstates 떨떠름한, which describes an uneasy or uncomfortable expression rather than the act of reluctance.
- **Replacement:** I had agreed automatically, but then I felt the mana circulating through Magic Johnson’s staff, and my expression turned uneasy.
- **Rationale:** Jin becomes uncomfortable when he realizes Johnson is activating the staff.

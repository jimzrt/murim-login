# Review

Four actionable fidelity issues, chiefly an implication that the Hunters know how the victims died and a loss of the distinction between knowing the Demon Realm exists and knowing what is in it.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 일련의 상황을 몰랐더라면 아마 백이면 백. 자신들이 유적 발굴에 투입되었다고 착각했을 것이다.
- **Current:** If they didn’t know what had happened, every last one of them would have thought they’d been sent to excavate ruins.
- **Defect:** Implies the Hunters know what happened to the victims, though they are wondering what killed them.
- **Replacement:** If they hadn’t known the circumstances, every last one of them would have thought they’d been sent to excavate ruins.
- **Rationale:** 일련의 상황 refers to the surrounding circumstances, not an explanation of the deaths.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 미카엘 실베르트와 같은 배신자의 등장을 이야기하는 것이 아니다.
- **Current:** He wasn’t talking about the rise of another traitor like Michael Silbert.
- **Defect:** Assigns the thought specifically to Choi rather than maintaining the narrator’s framing of the danger.
- **Replacement:** This wasn’t about another traitor like Michael Silbert emerging.
- **Rationale:** The sentence clarifies what kind of internal collapse the narrator means; it does not identify Choi as the subject.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마왕 아스모데우스가 이 세상에 강림한 이래, 차원 저 너머에 존재하는 죽음의 땅은 존재가 알려짐과 동시에 베일에 가려졌다.
- **Current:** Ever since the Demon King Asmodeus descended into this world, the existence of the land of death beyond the dimensions had been shrouded in mystery.
- **Defect:** Makes the realm’s existence mysterious when its existence became known; what remained hidden was the realm itself.
- **Replacement:** Ever since the Demon King Asmodeus descended into this world, humanity had known that a land of death existed beyond the dimensions—but the place itself remained shrouded in mystery.
- **Rationale:** 존재가 알려짐과 동시에 베일에 가려졌다 explicitly contrasts knowledge of its existence with ignorance of what lies there.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 그때까지 내가 알고 있던 건 뼈다귀에 붙은 살점 정도였던 거지.
- **Current:** Until then, all I knew were bits of flesh attached to bones.
- **Defect:** Reads as a claim about monster anatomy rather than the Skeleton King’s joke that his knowledge was meager.
- **Replacement:** Until then, what I knew about monsters amounted to a scrap of flesh on a pile of bones.
- **Rationale:** The flesh-on-bones image expresses how little he knew while retaining the skeletal speaker’s imagery.

# Review

One critical subject reversal changes who advances, while several localized issues lose causal, introspective, or relational nuances.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “스켈레톤 킹은, 그는 몬스터니까요.”
- **Current:** “The Skeleton King… he’s a monster.”
- **Defect:** The translation turns Choi’s causal explanation into a simple assertion, omitting the explicit “because” conveyed by 몬스터니까요.
- **Replacement:** “As for the Skeleton King… it’s because he’s a monster.”
- **Rationale:** The connective ending -니까요 presents the Skeleton King’s being a monster as the reason for Choi’s preceding conclusion.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘몬스터…… 몬스터라.’
- **Current:** *Monster… He’s a monster.*
- **Defect:** The added pronoun turns the second phrase into a declarative statement and loses the Skeleton King’s ruminative reaction.
- **Replacement:** *A monster… A monster, huh.*
- **Rationale:** The sentence-ending 라 marks him dwelling bitterly on the word itself rather than merely restating that he is a monster.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 자신은 심장은커녕, 평범한 감정조차 품지 못할 몬스터인데.
- **Current:** He was a monster incapable of possessing even ordinary emotions, let alone a heart.
- **Defect:** The “let alone” construction reverses the source’s order of emphasis, which begins with his lack of a heart and then stresses that even ordinary emotions should be beyond him.
- **Replacement:** He was a monster who had no heart and could not even harbor ordinary emotions.
- **Rationale:** 심장은커녕 establishes the absence of a heart before 평범한 감정조차 heightens the contradiction created by his emotional relief.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 나도 모르겠다.
- **Current:** I didn’t know either.
- **Defect:** “Either” incorrectly implies agreement with another stated lack of knowledge and sounds unnatural in context.
- **Replacement:** I didn’t even know.
- **Rationale:** Here 나도 모르겠다 conveys Jin’s bewilderment at his own sudden actions, not a response to someone else saying they did not know.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 나는 잠시 뭔가에 사로잡혀 있던 사람처럼 뒷걸음질 쳤다.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 인류에게 있어 몬스터는 건드려서는 안 될 역린(逆鱗)과도 같다.
- **Current:** To humanity, monsters were like a forbidden nerve that must never be touched.
- **Defect:** “Forbidden nerve” is an unnatural literal rendering of 역린 and obscures the intended image of an exceptionally sensitive taboo that provokes a severe reaction when touched.
- **Replacement:** To humanity, monsters were a raw nerve that must never be touched.
- **Rationale:** “Raw nerve” naturally conveys the dangerous sensitivity and untouchable quality of 역린 without changing the source’s comparison.

## F07 — Minor

# Review

The draft loses the mask-and-identity reversal, changes a time interval, and adds or omits several smaller details.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 복면을 밝히고 정체를 벗어라! 감히 본녀(本女)를 기습하다니, 천주 신명이 두렵지 않느냐!
- **Current:** “Show your face and reveal your identity! How dare you ambush me! Do you not fear the Lord of Heaven?”
- **Defect:** Great Sir reverses what one reveals and what one removes; the draft turns his mistake into an ordinary demand and breaks Bow Saint’s correction.
- **Replacement:** “Reveal your mask and take off your identity! How dare you ambush me! Do you not fear the Lord of Heaven?”
- **Rationale:** He says to reveal the mask and remove the identity, prompting Bow Saint to correct the latter phrase.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정체가 아니라 복면을 벗어야죠.
- **Current:** “You should take off your face, not reveal it.”
- **Defect:** Bow Saint corrects which thing he should take off, not whether to reveal his face.
- **Replacement:** “You take off the mask, not your identity.”
- **Rationale:** The correction is explicit and is followed by the observation that he is not wearing a mask.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 식경 전쯤인가, 시킨 대로 근방을 싹 돌아보고 마차로 돌아갔다가 엉덩이도 못 붙여 보고 다시 내쫓겼소.
- **Current:** “About half a shichen ago, I finished scouting the whole area like I was told and went back to the carriage. But before I could even sit down, I got kicked right back out.”
- **Defect:** Half a shichen is about an hour; 한 식경 is about half an hour.
- **Replacement:** “About half an hour ago, I finished scouting the whole area like I was told and went back to the carriage. But before I could even sit down, I got kicked right back out.”
- **Rationale:** The draft doubles the elapsed time by substituting a different unit.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 정확히는, 그보다 먼저 도적처럼 들이닥친 무시무시한 악취가.
- **Current:** More precisely, the stench that had arrived before them, barging in like a thief.

- **Replacement:** More precisely, the horrendous stench that had arrived before him, barging in like a thief.
- **Rationale:** 무시무시한 qualifies the smell, and the guest is subsequently identified as a man.

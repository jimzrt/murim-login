# Review

Several meaningful fidelity issues alter physical positioning, the spear’s motion, hierarchy, and the Skeleton King’s plea; additional localized issues weaken pragmatic cues or produce unnatural English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 믿고 있던 인간 놈들은 그를 앞뒤로 에워쌌고, 만악의 근원이나 다름없는 진태경은 재판관처럼 엄숙하게 선언했다.
- **Current:** The humans he had trusted had surrounded him from both sides, while Jin Taekyung—the root of all evil—solemnly declared, like a judge delivering a verdict:
- **Defect:** “From both sides” changes the humans’ positions from in front of and behind him to his lateral sides.
- **Replacement:** The humans he had trusted had boxed him in from the front and rear, while Jin Taekyung—the root of all evil—solemnly declared, like a judge delivering a verdict:
- **Rationale:** 앞뒤로 specifically places them in front of and behind the Skeleton King.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘세상에, 이토록 숭고한 희생정신이라니. 본 대대장은 진심으로 감동했다.’
- **Current:** “My goodness, such a noble spirit of sacrifice. I am genuinely moved.”
- **Defect:** The translation omits Jin Taekyung’s mock-formal self-reference as the battalion commander, losing the line’s hierarchy and comic register.
- **Replacement:** “My goodness, such a noble spirit of sacrifice. As battalion commander, I am genuinely moved.”
- **Rationale:** 본 대대장 explicitly invokes his position as battalion commander rather than using an unmarked first-person pronoun.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Current:** *You damn humans, please let’s all live together!*
- **Defect:** The literal phrasing sounds like a proposal to share a home or life together, rather than a desperate plea that the humans let him survive too.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 물론 개인적인 사정으로 악취 따위는 맡아 본 적도 없지만, 틀림없이 그랬을 것이다.

- **Defect:** “Anything like that” can imply only that he has not encountered a comparable smell, obscuring the joke that as a skeleton he has never smelled a stench at all; “it had been true” is also unnatural.

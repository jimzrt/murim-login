# Review

The draft contains several semantic and mechanical errors, including a reversed expectation, altered movement, an invented writing system, and softened or overexplicit register.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아니, 워낙 파리 목숨이었던지라 신경 쓸 틈도 없었다고 봐야 맞겠다.
- **Current:** No—in a world where human lives were worth about as much as a fly’s, it would be more accurate to say there hadn’t been time to worry about such things.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the narrator’s own precarious life into a general statement about the value of human life in the world, changing both the subject and the causal explanation.
- **Replacement:** No—my own life had always been so precarious that it would be more accurate to say I’d never had time to worry about such things.
- **Rationale:** The implied subject of 파리 목숨이었던지라 is the narrator; his life was precarious, leaving him no time to care about modern conveniences.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “잘 알고 있군. 내 말 한마디면 거지새끼 수천 명……까지는 아니고 백 명 정도는 데려올 수 있어. 특히 궁기방이라는 놈은 냄새가 무지막지하지. 그놈들이 네놈을 포함한 구족(九族)을 똥지처참할 거다. 그러니까 날 당장 제대로 된 측간으로 안내해!”
- **Current:** “You know it well. With one word from me, I can bring several thousand beggars…”

I paused.

“Okay, maybe not several thousand. Around a hundred. And there’s one fellow named Gung Gibang whose smell is absolutely monstrous. Those bastards will shit-kill your entire nine-clan family, you included. So take me to a proper latrine right now!”
- **Defect:** The draft adds an explicit narrative pause absent from the source, and “shit-kill your entire nine-clan family” is unnatural and obscures the wordplay on execution by dismemberment.
- **Replacement:** “You know it well. With one word from me, I can bring several thousand beggars… Okay, maybe not several thousand. Around a hundred. And there’s one fellow named Gung Gibang whose smell is absolutely monstrous. They’ll shit-dismember you and all nine degrees of your kin. So take me to a proper latrine right now!”
- **Rationale:** The correction occurs within one continuous speech, while 똥지처참 is a scatological parody of an execution by dismemberment and 구족 refers to the condemned person’s extended degrees of kinship.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 빌어먹을. 설마 했는데 이 시궁창이 측간이었다니.
- **Current:** Damn it. I had suspected as much, but this shithole really was the latrine.
- **Defect:** The narrator’s expectation is reversed: 설마 했는데 means he had been hoping or thinking it could not possibly be true, not that he had suspected it was true.
- **Replacement:** Damn it. I’d thought there was no way, but this shithole really was the latrine.
- **Rationale:** 설마 expresses incredulous disbelief here, followed by the unwanted confirmation that the place is indeed the latrine.

## F11 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** [금일(今日). 인시(寅時). 서문(西門).]

그것은 전서였고, 정체를 알 수 없는 누군가가 내게 보내는 초대장이었다.
- **Current:** > Today. Insi. West Gate.

It was written in seal script, and it was an invitation sent to me by someone whose identity I did not know.
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 전서 as “seal script,” inventing a specific writing system. Here it means a written message or missive. It also leaves the traditional time period Insi unexplained.
- **Replacement:** > Today. Insi[^1]. West Gate.

It was a missive—an invitation from someone whose identity I did not know.

[^1]: Insi is the traditional time period from three to five in the morning.
- **Rationale:** The object is identified as a transmitted written message that serves as an invitation, not as text written in seal script; the footnote supplies the materially necessary meaning of Insi without replacing the established term.

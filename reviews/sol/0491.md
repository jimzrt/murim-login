# Review

One subject-object error changes how Jeok Cheongang’s message was expected to be delivered. Four localized issues alter tense, quantity, an interrupted title, or a punchline.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “몰랐나? 어쩐지, 제자를 통해서 말씀하시지 않더라니.”
- **Current:** “You didn’t know? No wonder he didn’t tell you through his Disciple.”
- **Defect:** The translation makes an unspecified Disciple the intermediary who should have delivered the message to Taekyung. Taekyung himself is Jeok Cheongang’s Disciple; Zhuge Feng means he expected Jeok to pass the message to them through Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** “You didn’t know? That explains why he didn’t pass the message along through his Disciple.”
- **Rationale:** 제자를 통해서 describes Jeok communicating through his Disciple, not communicating to Taekyung through another Disciple.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “어, 조장님. 안 주무셨……?”
- **Current:** “Hey, Captain. Are you not asleep—”
- **Defect:** The present-tense question asks an obviously awake person whether he is asleep. Hyuk Mujin is reacting to Taekyung’s appearance and asking whether he failed to sleep.
- **Replacement:** “Hey, Captain. You haven’t slept—?”
- **Rationale:** 안 주무셨 uses the past/perfect sense here: Hyuk Mujin begins asking whether Taekyung did not sleep.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 웬 악독한 살수 새끼가 막사 내부에 십여 개의 암기를 설치했고
- **Current:** Some vicious assassin bastard had installed more than a dozen hidden weapons inside the tent
- **Defect:** “More than a dozen” raises the quantity beyond twelve, while 십여 개 means roughly ten or a dozen or so.
- **Replacement:** Some vicious assassin bastard had installed a dozen or so hidden weapons inside the tent
- **Rationale:** 십여 개 denotes approximately ten, not specifically more than twelve.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘의생 같은 소리하고 있네. 저게 신의냐, 독의지.’
- **Current:** *Medical apprentice, my ass. Is he the Divine Physician or a Poison Physician?*
- **Defect:** The draft turns the second punchline into an either-or question. The source rejects “Divine Physician” and asserts that Mungyeong is a “Poison Physician.”
- **Replacement:** *Medical apprentice, my ass. That’s no Divine Physician. He’s a Poison Physician.*
- **Rationale:** 저게 신의냐 is a rhetorical rejection, and 독의지 supplies the corrective assertion.

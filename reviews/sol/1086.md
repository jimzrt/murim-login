# Review

Four actionable fidelity and Markdown defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 뽕나무들은 현재의 제갈세가의 반석을 다진 그의 후손들이 심은 것이다.
- **Current:** These mulberry trees had been planted by Zhuge Feng’s descendants, who laid the foundation for the Zhuge Clan as it stood today.
- **Defect:** Attributes the planting to Zhuge Feng’s descendants rather than Zhuge Wuhou’s.
- **Replacement:** These mulberry trees had been planted by Zhuge Wuhou’s descendants, who laid the foundation for the Zhuge Clan as it stood today.
- **Rationale:** The antecedent of 그의 is the ancient ancestor Zhuge Wuhou, whose supposed planting of the trees Zhuge Feng has just rejected.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “다시 돌아올 때는, 열매가 맺혀 있길 바라마.”
- **Current:** “When you return, I hope you’ll have fruit.”
- **Defect:** Makes the tree the one that returns, rather than Zhuge Feng.
- **Replacement:** “When I come back, I hope you’ll be bearing fruit.”
- **Rationale:** Zhuge Feng speaks to the tree immediately before leaving; he hopes to find it bearing fruit upon his return.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “이 화원을 중심으로 일백여 장이 전부 가주전(家主殿)입니다. 본가의 직계들도 제 허락이 있어야 들어올 수 있지요.”
- **Current:** “This garden is surrounded on all sides, for over a hundred *jang*, by the Family Head’s Hall. Even direct descendants of our family need my permission to enter.”
- **Defect:** Changes the garden’s place within the restricted area into a garden surrounded by the hall.
- **Replacement:** “This garden and everything within roughly a hundred *jang* of it are part of the Family Head’s Hall. Even our family’s direct descendants need my permission to enter.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean places the garden at the center of an area extending some hundred jang, all of which belongs to the Family Head’s Hall.

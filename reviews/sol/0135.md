## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “똑똑히 기억하오. 저놈, 아니 저분이 계단에서 넘어지면서 내 아래 물건을 쭉 잡아당겼…… 후우, 그때만 생각하면 지금도 아찔하구려.”
- **Current:** “I remember it clearly. That guy—no, that gentleman—fell down the stairs and pulled the thing below my waist straight down… Whew. Just thinking about it still makes me dizzy.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents a downward direction and obscures the source action of grabbing and giving the euphemistically named body part a sustained yank.
- **Replacement:** “I remember it clearly. That guy—no, that gentleman—fell down the stairs, grabbed the thing between my legs, and gave it a long yank… Whew. Just thinking about it still makes me dizzy.”
- **Rationale:** 잡아당겼 means grabbed and pulled or yanked; 쭉 conveys the sustained extent of the pull, not a downward direction.

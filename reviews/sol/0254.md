## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그렇다고 맷집을 키우려고 타구봉법을 맨몸으로 맞는 미친놈이 어디 있겠나.
- **Current:** Besides, what kind of lunatic would take the Dog-Beating Staff Technique with his bare body just to toughen himself up?
- **Defect:** The established System attribute 맷집 is paraphrased instead of rendered as “Toughness.”
- **Replacement:** Besides, what kind of lunatic would take the Dog-Beating Staff Technique head-on just to raise his Toughness?
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly uses 맷집, whose binding established rendering is “Toughness.”

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그리고 길거리에 주저앉아 동냥하던 한 중년 거지는 그 후에도 한동안 사람들이 던져 주는 푼돈에 허리를 굽실거리다 자리에서 일어났다.
- **Current:** Meanwhile, a middle-aged beggar who had been sitting on the street begging for alms continued bowing his back for some time as people tossed him small change. Then he got to his feet.
- **Defect:** “Bowing his back” is an unnatural anatomical rendering that obscures the beggar’s repeated deferential bowing for the coins.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 내가 찜찜한 마음으로 고개를 끄덕인 순간이었다.

“와아아아!”

그리 멀지 않은 곳에서 메아리처럼 들려오는 함성.
- **Current:** The moment I nodded reluctantly, he heard it.

“Waaaaah!”

A roar rose from not far away, echoing through the air.
- **Defect:** The draft invents an action by an unidentified male subject—“he heard it”—and changes 찜찜한 마음으로 from unease or misgiving to reluctance.

A roar echoed from not far away.
- **Rationale:** The source says Taekyung nods despite his uneasy feeling and is immediately interrupted by the nearby roar; it does not state that another man hears anything.

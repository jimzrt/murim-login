# Review

One critical subject reversal changes who is being called a dictator. The draft also softens two profane lines, alters the king’s status, and loses two intentional comic voice beats.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 이 독재자가 어딜 감히!
- **Current:** “How dare you call me a dictator!”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. The Saudi speaker calls the Russian president a dictator; he does not accuse the Russian president of calling him one.
- **Replacement:** “How dare you, you dictator!”
- **Rationale:** 이 독재자 refers directly to the Russian president as “this dictator,” preserving the insult that prompts Furin’s response.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 개 같은…… 당신네 나라 GDP 몇이야?
- **Current:** “Goddammit… What’s your country’s GDP?”
- **Defect:** The targeted, unfinished insult is changed into a general exclamation, softening both its profanity and its attack on the other speaker.
- **Replacement:** “You fucking… What’s your country’s GDP?”
- **Rationale:** 이 개 같은 is an insult directed at the addressee and breaks off before its implied noun, which the replacement preserves without completing or sanitizing it.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 독재자? 왕이라는 작자가 그렇게 말하니 웃기는군.
- **Current:** “A dictator? It’s hilarious hearing that from a man who calls himself a king.
- **Defect:** “Calls himself a king” adds doubt about the Saudi speaker’s status that is absent from the source, which straightforwardly identifies him as a king.
- **Replacement:** “A dictator? That’s rich coming from a king.
- **Rationale:** 왕이라는 작자 means a man who is a king, not a self-proclaimed king.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 똥을 끼얹으려는 고이즈미 총리를 황급히 가로막은 백한성 대통령이 통역기가 부착된 마이크로 상체를 기울였다.
- **Current:** President Baek Hanseong hurriedly cut off Prime Minister Koizumi, who was about to dump something unpleasant into the conversation.
- **Defect:** The explicit, crude image of Koizumi dumping shit over the proceedings is sanitized into the vague phrase “something unpleasant,” weakening Baek’s profane internal voice and the humor.
- **Replacement:** President Baek Hanseong hurriedly cut off Prime Minister Koizumi before he could dump shit all over the discussion.
- **Rationale:** 똥을 끼얹다 literally invokes pouring or dumping shit over something; the source is deliberately cruder and more vivid than the draft.

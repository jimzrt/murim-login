# Review

One meaningful dialogue omission disrupts the exchange about the visitor’s appointment and Hyuk Mujin’s growing nerve. Several localized renderings also lose source wordplay, characterization, or martial hierarchy.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그래? 넌 목이 없어질 것 같은데.”

“음. 소가주님과 이야기하신 것 아닐까요?”

“이 자식은 이제 쫄지도 않네.”

“혁무진 삼 년이면 목숨이 세 개쯤 됩니다.”
- **Current:** “Really? Because it looks like you’re about to lose your head.”

“Three years under Hyuk Mujin means I have about three lives.”
- **Defect:** Two dialogue turns are omitted: Hyuk Mujin suggests that the visitor may have spoken with the Lesser Family Head, and Taekyung observes that Hyuk no longer flinches at his threats. The remaining line also incorrectly makes Hyuk sound as though he trained under himself.
- **Replacement:** “Really? Because it looks like you’re about to lose your head.”

“Hmm. Maybe they spoke with the Lesser Family Head?”

“This bastard doesn’t even flinch anymore.”

“Spend three years as Hyuk Mujin, and you wind up with about three lives.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted lines explain how an appointment might exist and preserve the joke that enduring three years as Hyuk Mujin has effectively given him extra lives.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “슬쩍 얼굴만 보고 왔는데, 귀한 분 같아요.”

“난 천한 놈이냐? 귀하게 돌려보내 드려.”
- **Current:** “I only took a quick look at his face, but he seemed like an important person.”

“Am I not important? Send him back with the utmost courtesy.”
- **Defect:** The rendering replaces the contrast between 귀한, meaning distinguished or highborn, and 천한, meaning lowly, with a generic contrast about importance, weakening Taekyung’s wordplay.
- **Replacement:** “I only caught a glimpse of him, but he looked like someone distinguished.”

“What, am I some lowborn bastard? Send our distinguished guest home with all due courtesy.”
- **Rationale:** The source joke hinges on Taekyung twisting Hyuk’s description of the visitor as 귀한 into an implication that Taekyung himself is 천한.

## F03 — Minor

- **Source:** 무려 구파일방에 속한 공동파의 장문인을 시작으로, 이른바 무림의 명사(名師)라고 불리는 이들의 방문이 줄줄이 이어졌다.

- **Defect:** The sentence is grammatically awkward, and “famous figures” weakens 名師, which identifies these visitors as renowned masters rather than merely notable people.

- **Rationale:** 名師 explicitly carries the sense of distinguished or renowned masters, matching the visitors’ martial standing.

## F04 — Minor

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 갑자기 뇌리를 스치는 생각에, 나는 구석에서 미미쨩과 함께 꼼지락거리고 있는 쌀벌레, 아니 만두 벌레를 바라보았다.
- **Current:** At that thought, I looked toward the rice weevil—or rather, the dumpling weevil—squirming in a corner with Mimi-chan.
- **Defect:** Here 쌀벌레 is an insult for an idle dependent or freeloader, not the established creature name. Rendering it literally as “rice weevil” obscures Taekyung’s insult and produces unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** At that thought, I looked toward the freeloader—or rather, the dumpling grub—fidgeting in a corner with Mimi-chan.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung first calls Cheongpung a food-consuming dependent, then revises the insult around Cheongpung’s obsession with dumplings; the replacement preserves both the characterization and bug imagery.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어떻게 보면 천하 무림을 통틀어 가장 개썅마이웨이를 꼽으라면 청풍이 아닐까.
- **Current:** If I had to pick the most goddamn my-way-or-the-highway person in all Murim, wouldn’t it be Cheongpung?
- **Defect:** “My way or the highway” describes forcing others to accept one’s demands, while 개썅마이웨이 describes someone who utterly disregards convention and does whatever he personally wants. The current wording mischaracterizes Cheongpung’s carefree independence as domineering behavior.
- **Replacement:** If I had to pick the person in all Murim who gave the fewest fucks and did exactly as he pleased, wouldn’t it be Cheongpung?
- **Rationale:** The surrounding exchange emphasizes that Cheongpung casually avoids visitors according to his own whims, not that he imposes an ultimatum on anyone else.

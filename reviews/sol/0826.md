# Review

One critical subject reversal, one unsupported target of mockery, and two smaller fidelity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘텔레포트 마법의 여파를 수습하기도 전에 너무 큰 힘을 소모했다. 약간의 시간이 필요해.’
- **Current:** *It spent too much power before it could recover from the effects of Teleport. It needs a little time.*
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Doppelganger the one who needs time to recover. Jin is assessing his own depleted strength; the next paragraph says the Skeleton King is in the same position.
- **Replacement:** *I used too much power before I could recover from the effects of the Teleport spell. I need a little time.*
- **Rationale:** The following ‘이건 스켈레톤 킹도 마찬가지다’ compares the Skeleton King’s condition with Jin’s, not the Doppelganger’s.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 하지만 한낱 껍데기를 뒤집어쓴 놈의 조롱을 듣고 있자니 기분이 더러운 것도 사실이다.
- **Current:** But it still pissed me off to hear the bastard, wearing nothing but a shell, mock him.
- **Defect:** “Mock him” specifies Bassman as the target of the taunt, which the source does not.
- **Replacement:** But it still pissed me off to listen to the taunts of a bastard wearing someone else’s shell.
- **Rationale:** The source describes Jin’s disgust at the impostor’s mockery without assigning Bassman as its target.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 나무 한 그루, 잡초 하나 자라나지 않는 황무지였고, 이제는 그 누구도 찾지 않는 폐허였다.
- **Current:** A barren wasteland where not a single tree or weed grew, now a ruin no one ever visited.
- **Defect:** “No one ever visited” changes the source’s present abandonment into an absolute claim about the ruin’s history.
- **Replacement:** A barren wasteland where not a single tree or weed grew, now a ruin no one visited anymore.
- **Rationale:** 이제는 marks the ruin as unvisited now.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 너 같은 몬스터 새끼는 예외로 쳐야지.
- **Current:** “A monster like you is the exception.”
- **Defect:** The line drops the insult in 몬스터 새끼, softening Jin’s deliberate hostility.
- **Replacement:** “A monster like you doesn’t count, you bastard.”
- **Rationale:** Jin explicitly pairs ‘monster’ with a personal insult as he rejects the Doppelganger’s claim to Bassman’s standing.

# Review

Two subject reversals alter the reunion, and the house price is overstated by 420 million won. Additional issues affect contact chronology, a character name, an added building scale, action fidelity, and Qi Sense terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “혹시 저 아세요?”
- **Current:** “Do I know you?”
- **Defect:** The subject and object are reversed: the approaching man asks whether Taekyung knows him, not whether he knows Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** “Do you know me?”
- **Rationale:** 저 is the speaker and the omitted object is the addressee, so the question is whether Taekyung recognizes the man.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아무튼 진짜 반갑다. 너 전학 가자마자 연락 끊겨서 엄청 섭섭했던 거 아냐?”
- **Current:** “Anyway, it’s really good to see you. You were pretty upset when I transferred schools and stopped contacting you, weren’t you?”
- **Defect:** The translation reverses both who transferred and who felt hurt. Taekyung was the one who transferred, while Jihoon says that he was upset when they lost contact.
- **Replacement:** “Anyway, it’s really good to see you. You know I was really upset when we lost touch right after you transferred, right?”
- **Rationale:** 너 전학 가자마자 identifies the addressee, Taekyung, as the person who transferred; Jihoon’s following apology and Taekyung’s explanation confirm the exchange.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “미안하긴 무슨. 진작 연락 안 한 내 잘못이지. 근데 너 아직도 여기 사냐?”
- **Current:** “What are you apologizing for? It was my fault for not contacting you first. But do you still live around here?”
- **Defect:** “First” changes the chronology and assigns responsibility for initiating contact; 진작 means sooner or long ago.
- **Replacement:** “What are you apologizing for? It was my fault for not getting in touch sooner. But do you still live around here?”
- **Rationale:** The source has Taekyung blame himself for failing to reconnect earlier, not specifically for failing to make the first attempt.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그렇게 말하니까 할 말이 없다. 입맛을 다시는 내게 지훈이가 명함을 내밀었다.
- **Current:** I had nothing to say to that. As I clicked my tongue, Jihoon held out a business card.
- **Defect:** Clicking one’s tongue is a different physical action and commonly conveys disapproval; the source says Taekyung smacks his lips while at a loss for words.
- **Replacement:** I had nothing to say to that. As I stood there smacking my lips, Jihoon held out a business card.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다시다 describes moving or smacking the lips, not clicking the tongue.

# Review

The draft misidentifies who grows stronger, turns a mountain comparison into a reference to a person, and changes the question that prompts the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s reply.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무슨 수작을 부렸는지는 몰라도 어느 순간마다 놀라울 정도로 강해지고, 빨라지더군. 귀신에 홀린 게 아닌가 싶을 정도로.
- **Current:** “I don’t know what you did, but every so often you get shockingly stronger and faster. It’s almost as if you’re possessed by a ghost.”
- **Defect:** Jeok is describing the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s sudden increases in strength and speed, not Taekyung’s.
- **Replacement:** “I don’t know what trick he’s using, but every so often he gets shockingly stronger and faster. It’s almost as if he’s possessed by a ghost.”
- **Rationale:** The preceding exchange concerns why Jeok cannot handle their opponent alone; 놈 refers to that opponent.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하지만 태산도 베어 낼 듯한 그 검격을, 적천강은 이미 예상하고 있었다는 듯이 옆으로 몸을 날렸다.
- **Current:** But Jeok Cheongang seemed to have anticipated the blow that looked powerful enough to cut through Taishan himself. He threw himself to the side.
- **Defect:** The comparison is to a great mountain, not the character Taishan.
- **Replacement:** But Jeok Cheongang seemed to have anticipated the sword strike, powerful enough to cleave a mountain, and threw himself to the side.
- **Rationale:** 태산도 베어 낼 듯한 describes the scale of the sword strike; no attack on Taishan is depicted.

- **Replacement:** “What exactly… are you after?”
- **Rationale:** 뭘 노리고 있는 거지 asks about their objective, following Taekyung’s speculation about a spell or another scheme.

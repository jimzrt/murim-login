# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes a dynastic fact and misses several consequential nuances.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 비록 이제 고작 삼대(三代)에 접어든 대국의 역사였으나
- **Current:** Though the Great Nation had only entered its third dynasty, the power wielded by its current Emperor equaled—or perhaps surpassed—that of Taizu, who had unified the realm.
- **Defect:** “Third dynasty” changes the state’s history; the source says it has reached only its third generation.
- **Replacement:** Though the Great Nation was only in its third generation, the power wielded by its current Emperor equaled—or perhaps surpassed—that of Taizu, who had unified the realm.
- **Rationale:** 삼대 counts generations, not successive dynasties.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 마치 얼마 남지 않은 자신의 미래처럼.
- **Current:** Just like the future he had so little time left to see.
- **Defect:** The comparison loses the darkness of the Emperor’s own future.
- **Replacement:** Just like his own future, brief and shrouded in darkness.
- **Rationale:** The source compares the dark world beyond the gates to his remaining future, not merely to how little of it he will see.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 처음이자 마지막이 될 친정.
- **Current:** His first campaign—and his last.
- **Defect:** “Campaign” omits the defining point: this will be the first and last campaign the Emperor leads in person.
- **Replacement:** The first campaign he would lead in person—and the last.
- **Rationale:** 친정 specifically means a ruler personally taking the field.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 말고삐를 쥔 채 묵묵하게 나아가던 궁성이 말을 받았다.
- **Current:** Gungseong, who had been riding quietly with the reins in hand, replied.
- **Defect:** Uses a romanization instead of the established title.
- **Replacement:** The Bow Saint, who had been riding quietly with the reins in hand, replied.
- **Rationale:** 궁성 is established as the Bow Saint.

# Review

One critical duration error and four localized fidelity issues involving concealment mechanics, emphasis, figurative meaning, and an unjustified explicit restoration claim.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 백여 년에 가까운 인생에서 가장 길고 힘든 기다림의 시간을 보내던 그는 마침내 참았던 숨을 토해 낼 수 있었다.
- **Current:** After enduring the longest and most difficult wait of his life—one that had lasted nearly a century—he could finally exhale the breath he had been holding.
- **Defect:** The draft says the wait itself lasted nearly a century. The source says the Great Snow Fiend has lived for nearly a century and that this was the longest, hardest wait within that lifetime.
- **Replacement:** After enduring the longest and most difficult wait of his nearly century-long life, he could finally exhale the breath he had been holding.
- **Rationale:** 백여 년에 가까운 modifies 인생, not 기다림의 시간; the current version drastically changes the duration of the immediate ambush.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 사방을 휘감은 짙은 먼지구름과 곳곳에서 쏟아지는 흙과 돌들. 시야과 기척을 가로막은 그것들을 향해 일권(一拳)을 뻗었다.
- **Current:** The thick dust cloud winding around me, the soil and stones pouring down from every direction—those things blocked my sight and presence.

I thrust a fist at them.
- **Defect:** “Blocked my presence” incorrectly makes Jin’s own presence the thing being concealed. 기척 here refers to perceptible signs of movement or another person's presence, which the dust and debris prevent him from detecting.
- **Replacement:** The thick dust cloud winding around me, the soil and stones pouring down from every direction—those things blocked my vision and masked every sign of movement.

I thrust a fist at them.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the obstacles interfering with both vision and the detection of 기척, not concealing Jin from his opponent.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 만약 오늘 이 자리에서 내가 죽는다 해도, 저 영민한 맹수만큼은 살아서 독혈지를 빠져나가길 바랐으니까.
- **Current:** Even if I died here today, I wanted that clever beast to escape the Poisonblood Grounds alive.
- **Defect:** The limiting emphasis 만큼은 is omitted; Jin specifically hoped that at least Muyaho would survive even if he did not.
- **Replacement:** Even if I died here today, I wanted that clever beast, at least, to escape the Poisonblood Grounds alive.
- **Rationale:** 만큼은 marks Muyaho as the minimum person Jin wants spared, reinforcing Jin’s willingness to sacrifice himself.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 분신이나 다름없는 백염을 쏘아 보낸 보람이 있었다.
- **Current:** It had been worth sending White Flame flying, a near-perfect double of myself.
- **Defect:** “A near-perfect double of myself” suggests that the spear physically resembles or duplicates Jin. 분신이나 다름없는 is figurative here, presenting White Flame as virtually an extension of him.
- **Replacement:** It had been worth sending White Flame—practically an extension of myself—flying.
- **Rationale:** The source expresses Jin’s close identification with and control over White Flame, not a literal resemblance between the weapon and its owner.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 목숨이나 다름없는 팔을 잃었다지만 신묘하기 짝이 없는 암천의 술사(術士)들이라면 그리 어렵지 않게 방법을 찾아낼 터.
- **Current:** He had lost an arm as precious as his life, but the uncanny sorcerers of Dark Heaven would surely find a way to restore it without much difficulty.
- **Defect:** The draft explicitly says Dark Heaven will restore the arm, while the source only says its sorcerers will find a way, leaving the nature of the remedy unstated.
- **Replacement:** He had lost an arm as precious as his life, but the uncanny sorcerers of Dark Heaven would surely find some solution without much difficulty.
- **Rationale:** 방법을 찾아낼 does not specify regeneration or restoration; the current wording resolves an intentional ambiguity and adds a concrete capability not stated in the source.

# Review

The draft has two scene-fact errors and several localized fidelity defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 화염인지, 파도인지, 벽력인지 알 수 없는 검푸른 섬광이 공간을 일그러트리며 들이닥쳤다.
- **Current:** A dark-blue flash—neither flame, wave, nor thunderbolt—distorted space as it came crashing down on them.
- **Defect:** The source says no one could tell which of the three it was; the draft asserts it was none of them.
- **Replacement:** A dark-blue flash—impossible to tell whether it was flame, wave, or thunderbolt—distorted space as it came crashing down on them.
- **Rationale:** 알 수 없는 preserves uncertainty about the flash’s nature.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그 손은, 하나뿐이었다.
- **Current:** And that hand was the only one he had.
- **Defect:** The draft implies Jin Taekyung has only one hand. The source says he was gripping the spear with only one hand.
- **Replacement:** And he was holding the spear with only one hand.
- **Rationale:** 하나뿐이었다 refers to the hand engaged in the preceding action, not his total number of hands.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어미의 배 속에서부터 타고난 잔인한 성정은 걸음마를 시작할 무렵부터 익힌 마공(魔功)을 만나 꽃을 피웠고
- **Current:** The cruel nature he’d inherited from his mother’s womb had blossomed when it met the demonic martial arts he’d learned as soon as he could walk.
- **Defect:** “Inherited from his mother” assigns the cruelty a maternal origin absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** The cruelty he’d possessed since before he was born blossomed when it met the demonic martial arts he’d learned around the time he began to walk.
- **Rationale:** 어미의 배 속에서부터 타고난 describes an inborn disposition, not something specifically inherited from his mother.

## F04 — Major

- **Source:** 하지만 세월을 이기지 못해 쓰러지고, 적들이 휘두르는 시퍼런 날붙이에 피를 뿌리며 허물어진 과거의 그들과는 다른 새로운 시대의 영웅임을.

# Review

The draft contains two factual distortions and several meaningful losses of emotional or comedic register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 손잡이만 남은 도검과 굳게 다문 입술.
- **Current:** A sword reduced to its hilt. Tightly pressed lips.
- **Defect:** The source states that both the sword and the saber were reduced to their hilts, but the draft mentions only a sword.
- **Replacement:** A sword and saber reduced to nothing but their hilts. Tightly pressed lips.
- **Rationale:** 도검 collectively identifies the two combatants’ sword and saber; omitting the saber changes the physical result of their clash.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 무림에서 얻은 수하를 처음으로 잃었고, 죽기 직전까지 갔으니까.
- **Current:** I had lost the first subordinate I’d gained in the Murim, and I’d nearly died.
- **Defect:** The draft says the casualty was the first subordinate Taekyung had recruited, whereas the source says this was the first time he had lost one of his Murim subordinates.
- **Replacement:** For the first time, I’d lost one of the subordinates I’d gained in the Murim, and I’d nearly died.
- **Rationale:** 처음으로 modifies 잃었고, describing Taekyung’s first experience of losing a subordinate rather than identifying that subordinate as the first one recruited.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 어떤 굉음도, 진동도 없이 지면이 쩍 갈라지는 광경은 전율 그 자체였다.
- **Current:** The sight of the earth splitting wide open without a single boom or tremor was pure terror.
- **Defect:** “Pure terror” narrows 전율 to fear and adds an emotional judgment not specified by the source, which presents the silent destruction as shudder-inducing.
- **Replacement:** The sight of the earth splitting wide open without a single boom or tremor sent a shiver through him.
- **Rationale:** 전율 conveys an involuntary thrill or shudder and does not necessarily mean terror.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 혹시 미친놈이세요?
- **Current:** “Are you insane?”
- **Defect:** The draft loses the deliberately polite, tentative phrasing that makes Taekyung’s insult deadpan and comic.
- **Replacement:** “Are you, by any chance, a lunatic?”
- **Rationale:** 혹시 and the polite ending -세요 turn the blunt insult 미친놈 into a mock-courteous question; that register contrast is intentional characterization and humor.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 지금까지 만난 무인 중 가장 고강하고 절망적이었던 상대.
- **Current:** He had been the most powerful and hopeless opponent I had ever faced.
- **Defect:** “Hopeless opponent” naturally suggests that the Head Elder himself was ineffectual or beyond saving, rather than that facing him made Taekyung feel hopeless.
- **Replacement:** He had been the most powerful and despair-inducing opponent I had ever faced.
- **Rationale:** 절망적이었던 describes how overwhelmingly hopeless the opponent made the confrontation feel, not a lack of ability on the opponent’s part.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 사람을 보고 덤볐어야지.
- **Current:** “You should have seen who you were attacking before you charged in.”
- **Defect:** The draft is an unnatural literal rendering and obscures the idiomatic warning that Taekyung should have judged his opponent before attacking.
- **Replacement:** “You should’ve picked your opponent more carefully before charging in.”
- **Rationale:** 사람을 보고 덤비다 means to size up or choose one’s opponent before starting a fight, not literally to see who is being attacked.

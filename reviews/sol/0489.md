# Review

Seven actionable findings: one speaker-identity error, one lost martial-artist distinction, and localized register, characterization, humor, and System-formatting defects.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 설마 했는데 화왕, 그 늙은이가 정말로 아무런 언질도 안 해 준 모양이군.
- **Current:** “I suspected as much, but it seems that the Fire King, that old man, really didn’t give you any warning.”
- **Defect:** 언질 means prior notice or a heads-up, not a warning of danger.
- **Replacement:** “I suspected as much, but it seems that the Fire King, that old man, really didn’t give you any heads-up.”
- **Rationale:** The line concerns Jeok Cheongang failing to tell Taekyung about the planned instruction in advance.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “전에 노야께서 그러셨습니다. 살성은 죽일 놈들만 죽인다고. 그래서 제법 괜찮은 살수라고.”
- **Current:** “You said it yourself before, Old Master. The Slaughter Saint only kills people who deserve to die. You said that made him a fairly decent assassin.”
- **Defect:** The translation addresses Mungyeong as Old Master and attributes the prior statement to him. Taekyung is reporting something Jeok Cheongang—the Old Master—previously said about Mungyeong.
- **Replacement:** “The Old Master told me before that the Slaughter Saint only kills people who deserve to die. He said you were a pretty decent assassin.”
- **Rationale:** The deferential subject marker in 노야께서 makes Old Master the subject of 그러셨습니다, not the person being addressed.

## F03 — Minor

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “네놈은 상당한 경지를 이룩한 무인이지만, 무인과 무림인은 다르다. 아직도 반쪽짜리에 불과한 널 진짜 무림인으로 만들어 주지.”
- **Current:** “You have achieved a considerable realm, but a martial artist and a true Murim martial artist are different. I will turn you, who are still only half-formed, into a true martial artist.”
- **Defect:** The draft applies “true” prematurely to the comparison and then drops Murim from the promised transformation, blurring the line’s central distinction between a martial artist and a genuine martial artist of the Murim.
- **Replacement:** “You may be a martial artist of considerable skill, but a martial artist and a Murim martial artist are not the same thing. You’re still only half-formed. I’ll turn you into a true Murim martial artist.”
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts 무인 with 무림인 and reserves 진짜 for the kind of 무림인 Mungyeong promises to make Taekyung.

# Review

Several action and speaker errors need correction, along with an omitted technique name.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진무경은 억눌린 신음을 토해 내는 적의 가슴에서 검을 뽑아냈다.
- **Current:** Jin Mukyung pulled his sword from the enemy’s chest, muffling a groan.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Mukyung the one muffling a groan; the enemy groans.
- **Replacement:** Jin Mukyung pulled his sword from the chest of the enemy who had let out the stifled groan.
- **Rationale:** 억눌린 신음을 토해 내는 modifies 적.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘선명하다.’
- **Current:** *I can see it.*
- **Defect:** The thought asserts sight just before the narration says he can sense things without seeing them.
- **Replacement:** *It’s all so clear.*
- **Rationale:** 선명하다 describes clarity without specifying visual perception.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “더 늦기 전에 떠나라. 지금이라면 충분히 퇴로를 뚫을 수 있을 거다.”
- **Current:** “Leave before it’s too late. We can still break through and open a way out.”
- **Defect:** “We” includes Wipeng in the escape, contradicting his insistence that Mukyung go alone.
- **Replacement:** “Leave before it’s too late. You can still break through and make your escape.”
- **Rationale:** The preceding 너 혼자서라면 establishes Mukyung alone as the person who can escape.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “젠장. 피만 몇 됫박을 쏟았더니 이제는 고작해야 한 줌이구먼.”
- **Current:** “Damn it. I’ve spat out a few *doe* of blood already, and now I can barely manage a handful.”
- **Defect:** The untranslated measure obscures the contrast between a large amount of blood and a handful.
- **Replacement:** “Damn it. I’ve coughed up bucketfuls of blood, and now all I can manage is a handful.”
- **Rationale:** 몇 됫박 conveys an emphatically large quantity; the joke depends on its contrast with 한 줌.

## F07 — Critical

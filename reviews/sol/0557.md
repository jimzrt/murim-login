# Review

Four actionable fidelity issues: one altered attack mechanism, one incorrect numerical threshold, one altered physical action, and one mistranslated utterance.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 엔트의 몸통과, 가지와, 무성한 잎사귀를 장작 삼아 겁화(劫火)로 화한 불꽃이 선에 닿은 모든 것을 집어삼킨 순간.
- **Current:** The trunks, branches, and lush leaves of the Ents became kindling. The flames transformed into hellfire, and in the moment they touched the line, they devoured everything in their path.
- **Defect:** The draft says the flames touched the line and then consumed whatever lay in their path. The source instead says the hellfire consumed everything that the previously drawn line touched.
- **Replacement:** The trunks, branches, and lush leaves of the Ents became kindling. The flames transformed into hellfire and devoured everything the line had touched.
- **Rationale:** The line marks the attack's affected area; it is not something the flames subsequently touch.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** - 일격에 20개체 이상의 [타락한 엔트]를 처치했으므로, 보너스 경험치가 주어집니다!
- **Current:** - Since you defeated more than 20 **Corrupted Ents** with a single strike, bonus EXP has been awarded!
- **Defect:** The draft changes an inclusive threshold of twenty or more into a threshold greater than twenty.
- **Replacement:** - Since you defeated 20 or more **Corrupted Ents** with a single strike, bonus EXP has been awarded!
- **Rationale:** 이상 includes the stated boundary value, so defeating exactly twenty Ents qualifies for the bonus.

## F03 — Minor

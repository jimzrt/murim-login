# Review

Two meaningful fidelity errors alter the mechanism of Jin Taekyung’s hypothetical advancement and the subject of the question that could expose his lie. Several localized wording issues obscure source images, register, or natural meaning.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 밖은 어스름한 새벽이었다. 겨울철 공기는 얼음장처럼 차가웠고 걸을 때마다 연무장 바닥에 낀 서리가 바스러졌다.
- **Current:** It was the dim light of dawn outside. The winter air was cold as ice, and frost crumbled beneath my feet every time I walked across the training hall.
- **Defect:** The opening is unnatural English, and “training hall” suggests an enclosed building despite the explicit outdoor setting and frost-covered training ground.
- **Replacement:** Outside, dawn was dim. The winter air was cold as ice, and frost crumbled underfoot with every step across the training ground.
- **Rationale:** 밖 explicitly establishes an outdoor setting, while 연무장 here is the frost-covered training ground.

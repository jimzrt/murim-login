# Review

The draft preserves the scene overall, but changes a sequence of deaths into repeated deaths of one person and misses several character and dialogue cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 강자로 추앙받던 어디의 누군가가 죽고, 죽고, 또 죽고.
- **Current:** One person or another, once revered as a great warrior, died. Then died again. And again.
- **Defect:** “Then died again” makes it sound as though the same person dies repeatedly.
- **Replacement:** One revered master died, then another, and another.
- **Rationale:** The repeated deaths are of successive people once revered as powerful martial artists.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “저건…….”

도대체 뭐지?

그 뒷말은 차마 소리 내어 내뱉지도 못했다.
- **Current:** “What is that…?”

I couldn’t bring myself to say the rest aloud.
- **Defect:** The draft voices the question that Taekyung cannot bring himself to say, removing the distinction between his speech and thought.
- **Replacement:** “That’s…”

*What the hell is that?*

I couldn’t bring myself to say the rest aloud.
- **Rationale:** He begins aloud, asks what it is internally, then explains why he cannot speak the rest.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “엄대두요? 도대체 그게 뭡니…….”
- **Current:** “Undead? What the hell is tha—”
- **Defect:** Mujin mishears “undead” as “Eom Dae-du”; the draft removes the misunderstanding and adds profanity to his polite question.
- **Replacement:** “Eom Dae-du? What does that mea—”
- **Rationale:** The phonetic mishearing and deferential question are both present in the Korean.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이런 상황에서도 그 어떤 비명이나 동요도, 심지어 당연히 느껴야 할 고통조차 보이지 않은 채.
- **Current:** Even in a situation like this, they showed no fear or agitation—not even the pain they should’ve been feeling.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the absence of screams with an absence of fear.
- **Replacement:** Even in a situation like this, they neither screamed nor showed any agitation—not even a sign of the pain they should’ve been feeling.
- **Rationale:** 비명 means screams; the unnerving observation is that the burned figures make no cry of pain.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 사마외도 중에도 강한 놈들이 얼마나 많은데.
- **Current:** There are plenty of strong people among the demonic, heterodox arts, too.
- **Defect:** “People among … arts” is an unnatural English construction.
- **Replacement:** Plenty of powerful fighters practice demonic and heterodox arts, too.
- **Rationale:** The speaker is pointing out powerful martial artists outside the orthodox faction.

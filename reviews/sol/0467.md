# Review

One speaker attribution error, one reversed emphasis, and two localized phrasing defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “어떻습니까?”

어깨너머로 들려오는 혁무진의 대답에, 내가 고개를 끄덕였다.

“멀쩡해.”
- **Current:** “How is he?”

I nodded at Hyuk Mujin’s answer from over my shoulder.

“He’s fine.”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns Hyuk Mujin’s question to Taekyung, then incorrectly calls it Hyuk Mujin’s answer, making Taekyung appear to ask and answer his own question.
- **Replacement:** “How is he?” Hyuk Mujin asked from over my shoulder.

I nodded. “He’s fine.”
- **Rationale:** 혁무진 is the speaker heard from behind, while Taekyung responds by nodding and declaring that the Dongting Fisherman is fine.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저항은커녕 굼벵이처럼 꿈틀거리지도 못한다.
- **Current:** He could not resist, let alone squirm like a grub.
- **Defect:** The “let alone” construction reverses the source’s scale of inability. The source emphasizes that he cannot even wriggle, much less offer meaningful resistance.
- **Replacement:** He could not even squirm like a grub, much less resist.
- **Rationale:** 저항은커녕 presents resistance as the greater action that is out of the question, followed by the lesser action he cannot perform either.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 육지로 끌어 올려져 있는 나룻배의 표면. 겉으로 보기에도 뜯겨 나간 목재를 드문드문 발견할 수 있었다.
- **Current:** The surface of the ferryboat, which had been hauled up onto the shore, was visible.

Even at a glance, I could see pieces of wood that had been torn away here and there.
- **Defect:** The first sentence is a fragment expanded into the empty and unnatural observation that the boat’s surface “was visible,” while the second makes detached pieces of wood seem visible rather than damaged spots on the boat.
- **Replacement:** Even at a glance, I could see spots where wood had been torn from the surface of the ferryboat hauled up onshore.
- **Rationale:** The source directs attention to visible sections of missing wood on the ferryboat’s surface as evidence that the vessel will not withstand the return trip.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘처음부터 점혈은 풀려 있지 않았어. 이건 그저…… 동정어옹의 몸이 의지와는 별개로 반응한 거야.’
- **Current:** *His Pressure-Point Strikes were never released in the first place. This was simply…… the Dongting Fisherman’s body reacting independently of his will.*
- **Defect:** The possessive “His Pressure-Point Strikes” wrongly associates the technique with the Dongting Fisherman and describes strikes themselves as being released. Taekyung realizes that the acupoint seals he applied never came undone.
- **Replacement:** *His acupoint seals had never come undone in the first place. This was simply…… the Dongting Fisherman’s body reacting independently of his will.*
- **Rationale:** 점혈은 풀려 있지 않았어 means the effects of Taekyung’s Pressure-Point Strikes remained intact, which establishes that the Fisherman’s movement was an involuntary bodily reaction rather than recovery from the seals.

# Review

One line reverses the parties to an alleged assassination; several others lose the Emperor’s emotional or political meaning.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 지금 당장 자네가 짐을 시해하려고 했다는 혼잣말을 하면 어떻게 될 것 같나?
- **Current:** What do you think would happen if you muttered to yourself that I’d tried to assassinate you just now?
- **Defect:** Reverses who supposedly tried to assassinate whom and who would make the accusation.
- **Replacement:** What do you think would happen if I muttered to myself that you’d just tried to assassinate me?
- **Rationale:** The Emperor threatens to say that Taekyung tried to assassinate him.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 각오했던 일이야. 이미 오래전부터. 그러니…….
- **Current:** I knew this day would come. I’ve known for a long time. So…
- **Defect:** Knowing what would happen replaces the Emperor’s claim that he had steeled himself for it.
- **Replacement:** I was prepared for this. Have been for a long time. So…
- **Rationale:** 각오했던 describes resolve or preparedness, not mere foreknowledge.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 눈에 넣어도 아프지 않을 혈육
- **Current:** His flesh and blood, dear enough to hold in his sight without ever tiring of him.
- **Defect:** The literalized image obscures an idiom expressing how precious his younger brother is to him.
- **Replacement:** His flesh and blood, so precious he could never bear to see him hurt.
- **Rationale:** 눈에 넣어도 아프지 않을 conveys extreme affection, not pleasure in looking at someone.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 성군이 아닌 패왕(霸王)이 되어야 한다.
- **Current:** the boy would have to become a conqueror, not a sage king.
- **Defect:** “Conqueror” suggests territorial conquest rather than the forceful, domineering rulership contrasted with a sage king.
- **Replacement:** the boy would have to become a ruthless ruler, not a sage king.
- **Rationale:** 패왕 characterizes the kind of ruler needed to survive the coming war.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 짐은, 아니 나는…….
- **Current:** I… No, I…
- **Defect:** Using the same pronoun twice erases the Emperor’s deliberate shift from imperial self-reference to speaking as a person.
- **Replacement:** We—no, I…
- **Rationale:** 짐은 changes to 나는 immediately before he admits that he wants to live.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 백연과 궁성의 도움이 없었다면
- **Current:** the help of Baek Yeon and Gungseong
- **Defect:** Uses an untranslated form instead of the established title.
- **Replacement:** the help of Baek Yeon and the Bow Saint
- **Rationale:** The packet’s glossary establishes Bow Saint for 궁성.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 궁성과 백연이라는, 말이 필요 없는 훌륭한 스승들의 가르침을 받으며
- **Current:** having been taught by two peerless masters—Gungseong and Baek Yeon—
- **Defect:** The second occurrence also uses an untranslated form of the established title.
- **Replacement:** having been taught by two peerless masters—the Bow Saint and Baek Yeon—
- **Rationale:** The packet’s glossary establishes Bow Saint for 궁성.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 연이어 잔기침을 내뱉던
- **Current:** he let out a series of dry coughs.
- **Defect:** “Dry” specifies a quality absent from the source; the coughs are small or light.
- **Replacement:** he let out a series of small coughs.
- **Rationale:** 잔기침 refers to light, repeated coughing.

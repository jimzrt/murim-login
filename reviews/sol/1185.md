# Review

The draft changes the pill’s timing and Taekyung’s rank, and loses several physical and tonal details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 세 시진이 넘는 시간에 걸쳐 느릿하게 약효가 퍼지기 시작한다면
- **Current:** But once its effects began to spread slowly over the course of more than three shichen, everything changed.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the effects spread over three shichen; the source says they begin spreading after more than three shichen.
- **Replacement:** But after more than three shichen, its effects would slowly begin to spread, and everything changed.
- **Rationale:** The delayed onset explains why the medicine seemed ineffective earlier.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그 어떤 초절정 고수라고 해도 쉽사리 버틸 수 없으리라 확신했다.
- **Current:** The Slaughter Saint had been certain that no Peak master could easily endure all that.
- **Defect:** Peak is a lower realm than the source’s 초절정.
- **Replacement:** The Slaughter Saint had been certain that no Supreme Peak master could easily endure all that.
- **Rationale:** The binding glossary renders 초절정 as Supreme Peak.

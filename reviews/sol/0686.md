# Review

One critical identity error, one substantial omitted metaphor and decision beat, one altered political mechanism, and one overly literal idiom.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 비록 지금은 공력이 금제당했다고는 해도 두 사람 역시 절정의 경지에 오른 무인이다.
- **Current:** Although their internal energy had been sealed for the time being, both men were martial artists who had reached the Peak realm.
- **Defect:** Yohi and Heugung are the two martial artists, but “both men” incorrectly identifies Yohi as male.
- **Replacement:** Although their internal energy had been sealed for the time being, both of them were martial artists who had reached the Peak realm.
- **Rationale:** The source refers neutrally to the two people present, one of whom is Yohi, an established woman.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘이미 너무 늦었을지도 모르지만, 지금이라도.’

남만의 어떤 독물은 특정한 시기마다 허물을 벗는다.

이른바 탈각(脫殼)이라 불리는 행위가 그것이다.

하지만 새로운 몸을 얻는 것이 어찌 쉬울 수 있을까. 탈각에 성공한다면 더욱 아름답고 강해진 몸을 얻을 수 있겠지만, 만약 실패한다면 늙고 병든 몸으로 서서히 죽어 간다.

그리고 요희가 택한 길은 전자였다.

‘더 지체한다면 전부 끝장이야. 어떻게든 이 사실을 사람들에게 알려야 해.’

지금이라도 백상과 관련된 흉계를 밝히고 암천을 저지해야 한다.
- **Current:** *It may already be too late, but even now…*

They had to expose the conspiracy connected to Baeksang and stop Dark Heaven.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the entire molting metaphor, Yohi’s explicit choice to pursue renewal despite the risk of death, and her urgent resolve to inform everyone.
- **Replacement:** *It may already be too late, but I have to act now.*

At certain times, some of Nanman’s venomous creatures shed their skins.

This process was known as molting.

But how could gaining a new body ever be easy? If the molting succeeded, the creature would gain a more beautiful and powerful body. If it failed, it would slowly die in its old, diseased one.

Yohi had chosen the former path.

*If I delay any longer, it’ll all be over. I have to let everyone know about this somehow.*

They had to expose the conspiracy connected to Baeksang and stop Dark Heaven.
- **Rationale:** The omitted passage supplies the central metaphor for Yohi’s attempted transformation and makes her subsequent decision an explicit, dangerous choice rather than an abrupt change of mind.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그를 부족장들과 대회의의 결정이라면 궁주님도 어찌할 수 없었겠죠.
- **Current:** If his takeover was presented as the decision of the tribal chieftains and the Tribal Grand Council, even the Palace Lord wouldn’t be able to do anything.
- **Defect:** “Was presented as” adds the unsupported idea that the council’s decision was merely a deceptive pretense. The source says that a decision by the chieftains and council to put Baeksang in power would bind the Palace Lord.
- **Replacement:** If the tribal chieftains and the Tribal Grand Council decided to put him in power, even the Palace Lord wouldn’t be able to do anything.
- **Rationale:** The political mechanism is the authority of a collective decision, not Baeksang falsely presenting his takeover as one.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그래, 그렇겠지. 깨어나기만 한다면 천군만마가 될 거요.”
- **Current:** “Yes, he should. If he wakes up, he’ll be worth a thousand troops and ten thousand horses.”
- **Defect:** The literal numerical rendering of the idiom is unnatural in English and obscures Heugung’s straightforward claim that Jin alone would provide the strength of an army.
- **Replacement:** “Yes, he should. If he wakes up, he’ll be an army unto himself.”
- **Rationale:** 천군만마 is an idiom for an immense army; “an army unto himself” preserves its force and meaning in natural English without treating the component numbers as literal quantities.

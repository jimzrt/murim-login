# Review

One critical factual substitution changes blood to sweat; additional findings address altered meaning, ambiguity, omitted humor, and unnatural terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 나는 피로 찐득거리는 머리칼을 쓸어올렸다.
- **Current:** I swept back my hair, which was sticky with sweat.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the substance coating Taekyung’s hair from blood to sweat, altering a physical scene fact.
- **Replacement:** I swept back my hair, which was sticky with blood.
- **Rationale:** 피 means blood, not sweat.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 「증조모께서는 어린 나를 무릎에 앉혀 두고 종종 말씀하셨지. 왕족은 신분에 걸맞은 의무를 다해야 한다고. 평민인 자네들은 모르겠지만 말이야.」
- **Current:** “On my great-grandmother’s lap, she would often tell me when I was a child that royalty must fulfill the duties befitting their station. Of course, commoners like you would not know anything about that.”
- **Defect:** The opening is a dangling construction that grammatically places the great-grandmother on her own lap rather than placing the young Felix on it.
- **Replacement:** “When I was a child, my great-grandmother would sit me on her lap and often tell me that royalty must fulfill the duties befitting their station. Of course, commoners like you would not understand.”
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly makes the great-grandmother seat young Felix on her lap before speaking to him.

## F03 — Major

# Review

The draft contains one critical ownership error, several meaningful fidelity and characterization issues, an established-name error, and localized wording problems.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “당연히 그래야지. 인벤토리에서 평생 자중하기 싫으면.”
- **Current:** “That’s what you should do. Unless you want to spend the rest of your life exercising restraint in your Inventory.”
- **Defect:** The possessive assigns the Inventory to the Skeleton King, but Taekyung is threatening to confine him in Taekyung’s own Inventory.
- **Replacement:** “That’s what you should do. Unless you want to spend the rest of your life lying low in my Inventory.”
- **Rationale:** The threat depends on Taekyung controlling the Inventory and being able to imprison the Skeleton King there.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “매직 존슨이 친분이 있는 대마도사에게 특별 제작을 부탁한 거라 성능은 최상급. 부르는 게 값일걸.”
- **Current:** “Magic Johnson commissioned a grand mage he knows to make it specially, so its performance is top-class. You could name your price for it.”
- **Defect:** “You could name your price” means the owner or buyer can choose the price, whereas 부르는 게 값 means it would command whatever price the seller demanded.
- **Replacement:** “Magic Johnson commissioned a grand mage he knows to make it specially, so its performance is top-class. It’s probably worth whatever they ask for it.”
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes the ring’s extreme market value, which directly causes the Skeleton King to reassess its dignity.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 말없이 눈동자를 뒤룩뒤룩 굴리던 스켈레톤 킹이 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** The Skeleton King rolled his eyes around in silence before opening his mouth.
- **Defect:** “Rolled his eyes” conventionally signals contempt or exasperation, while 뒤룩뒤룩 굴리다 here depicts his eyes darting around uneasily after being threatened.
- **Replacement:** The Skeleton King’s eyes darted around as he remained silent, but eventually he spoke.
- **Rationale:** The eye movement shows that the threat has rattled him and sets up his face-saving claim that the world is simply not ready for him.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 모든 것이 계획대로 되었다는 듯, 흐뭇하게 웃고 있는 최 팀장을 포함한 짤막한 소감이었다.

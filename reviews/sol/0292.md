# Review

The draft omits the opening danger and timing of True Qi Guidance, adds an unsupported identity claim, loses a hierarchy marker, alters the bloodline contrast, and assigns the chance of reaching Supreme Peak to the wrong subject.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 젠장. 하필이면 지금?

퀘스트창을 확인하고 싶은 마음이 굴뚝 같다.

하지만 지금은 진기도인 중이다. 나 혼자 하는 운기조식이라면 모르지만 여기서 통제를 풀어 버리면 내상을 입기 십상이다.



- 5초 안에 대답하지 않으면 퀘스트가 자동으로 취소됩니다.

- 오, 사, 삼…….
- **Current:** > - If you do not answer within five seconds, the Quest will be canceled automatically.
>
> - Five, four, three…
- **Defect:** Three paragraphs are omitted, removing Taekyung’s frustration, his desire to inspect the Quest, and the mechanical explanation that abandoning True Qi Guidance could cause an Internal Injury. The omission also incorrectly makes the countdown appear immediately after the initial prompt without the intervening prose.
- **Replacement:** Damn it. Why now?

I was dying to check the Quest window.

But I was in the middle of True Qi Guidance. Circulating my own qi would be one thing, but if I let go of control here, I could easily suffer an Internal Injury.

> **System**
>
> - If you do not answer within five seconds, the Quest will be canceled automatically.
>
> - Five, four, three…
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly states what Taekyung is doing and why releasing control is dangerous before the countdown begins.

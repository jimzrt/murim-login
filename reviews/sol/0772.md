# Review

The draft contains several fidelity errors affecting a proper title, a confirmed historical fact, the mirror’s mechanism, and the number of monsters referenced, plus localized losses of nuance and naturalness.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 선동이자, 연설이며, 선언이 끝났다.
- **Current:** The incitement, the speech, and the declaration were over.
- **Defect:** The draft presents three separate things ending, whereas the source describes one act as simultaneously incitement, a speech, and a declaration.
- **Replacement:** The incitement—at once a speech and a declaration—was over.
- **Rationale:** The constructions 선동이자 and 연설이며 identify multiple aspects of the same act rather than items in a list.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 언제나 강해 보였던 영웅의 뒷모습은 이토록 아름다웠다.

뒷모습은.
- **Current:** The back of a hero who had always seemed so strong was this beautiful.

The back of him.
- **Defect:** The rendering is unnatural English and blunts the repeated 뒷모습 punchline contrasting the hero’s public appearance with what is seen after he turns away.
- **Replacement:** A hero who had always seemed so strong could look this beautiful from behind.

From behind, that is.
- **Rationale:** The repetition of 뒷모습 is deliberate and should land as a short corrective punchline rather than the unidiomatic phrase “the back of him.”

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 갑작스럽게 저 멀리서 전해지던 열기에 전신이 곤두설 것 같았지.
- **Current:** A sudden heat reached me from far away, making me feel as though my entire body were standing on end.
- **Defect:** The literal construction makes Michael’s entire body seem to stand upright rather than conveying the physical sensation of bristling or getting goose bumps.
- **Replacement:** The sudden heat reaching me from so far away made every hair on my body stand on end.
- **Rationale:** 전신이 곤두설 것 같았다 describes a whole-body bristling reaction, naturally expressed in English through hair standing on end.

## F04 — Critical

- **Source:** 감시원들의 보고에 따르면, 스톤 킹이 먼저 호텔을 빠져나가고 얼마 지나지 않아 인근이 뒤흔들렸다고 합니다.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대격변의 영웅 중 한 사람으로 기억되었어도 그를 가로막는 벽은 많았다.

- **Defect:** “Even if” makes Michael’s status as a remembered hero sound hypothetical, although the source treats it as an established fact and contrasts it with the obstacles he still faced.

- **Rationale:** Here -었어도 is concessive—“although” or “even though”—not a conditional casting doubt on his reputation.

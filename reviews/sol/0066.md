# Review

The draft contains three subject or dialogue reversals, one altered mechanical explanation, and several localized register or wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 난리 통에 본 사람이 한둘인가.
- **Current:** There were plenty of people who saw him in that mess.
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. The maid says she saw too many people amid the chaos to single him out, not that many people saw him.
- **Replacement:** With all that chaos, do you think I only saw one or two people?
- **Rationale:** 본 사람이 한둘인가 refers to the many people the maid saw during the commotion, explaining why she failed to recognize Jin Mukyung.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 항산검문이 선전포고를 한 직후 전서응을 날렸으니 그로서는 자세한 내막을 알 방법이 없었다.
- **Current:** Jin Mukyung had sent a carrier hawk immediately after the Mount Heng Sword Sect declared war, so there had been no way for him to learn the details.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jin Mukyung the sender of the carrier hawk, although he was the recipient being informed and the Korean leaves the sender implicit.
- **Replacement:** A carrier hawk had been sent immediately after the Mount Heng Sword Sect declared war, so there had been no way for him to learn the details.
- **Rationale:** The sentence explains why Mukyung's letter lacked details; assigning the sending action to him reverses his role in that communication.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그럼 됐습니다.”

“당시 대장로가…… 뭐라고?”
- **Current:** “Then that’s enough.”

“What?”

“I said, ‘At that time, the Head Elder—’”
- **Defect:** The dialogue order and speakers are altered. Jin Wikyung begins his historical account, then interrupts himself to react to Mukyung's dismissal; the draft instead has Mukyung say “What?” and Wikyung repeat himself.
- **Replacement:** “Then never mind.”

“Back then, the Head Elder… Wait, what did you say?”
- **Rationale:** 뭐라고? is Jin Wikyung's incredulous reaction to 그럼 됐습니다, while 당시 대장로가…… is the beginning of the explanation he was about to give.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “애들한테 헛소리 좀 그만해. 뒤지기 싫으면.”

하지만 소천은 반짝거리는 눈으로 뒷이야기를 기다리는 중이었다.

“전 괜찮습니다.”

“뒤지는 게 모야? 소율이도 뒤질래!”

“……넌 아직 한참 남았어.”
- **Current:** “Stop talking nonsense in front of the kids unless you want to die.”

But Socheon was waiting for the rest of the story with shining eyes.

“I’m fine.”

“What does ‘die’ mean? Soyul wants to die too!”

“……You still have a long way to go.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes talking nonsense to the children into merely speaking in their presence, and neutralizes the coarse, unfamiliar verb 뒤지다 into ordinary “die,” weakening Soyul's repetition and the resulting dark joke.
- **Replacement:** “Stop filling the kids’ heads with nonsense. Unless you want to croak.”

But Socheon was waiting for the rest of the story with shining eyes.

“I’m fine.”

“……You’ve still got a long time before that.”
- **Rationale:** 애들한테 marks the children as the recipients of Hyuk Mujin's lies, while 뒤지다 is a coarse colloquial term whose repetition by Soyul drives the joke. The final line means she has a long life remaining before death.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes the referent of a death, assigns the President’s workload to his guards, and conflates extenuating circumstances with self-defense. It also alters one causal reaction and loses a deliberate comedic self-correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘가족이 죽었으니까.’
- **Current:** *Because his family had died.*
- **Defect:** The English implies that Choi’s family collectively died, while the source refers specifically to Kim Hwajong’s death on the understanding that he was family to Choi.
- **Replacement:** *Because he’d lost someone who was family.*
- **Rationale:** The following lines explicitly identify Kim Hwajong as the family member whose death Choi is grieving.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘충분히 그러고도 남지.’
- **Current:** *And it would have deserved to.*
- **Defect:** The translation introduces a moral judgment that the world deserved to be thrown into turmoil. The source instead says that such turmoil was entirely unsurprising given what happened.
- **Replacement:** *And no wonder.*
- **Rationale:** 그러고도 남지 emphasizes that the preceding reaction was more than understandable or expected, not that it was deserved.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘양해는 무슨. 괜찮습니다. 안 그래도 저 때문에 열 배는 바빠지셨을 텐데.’
- **Current:** “What’s there to understand? It’s fine. I’m sure you’re ten times busier because of me.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes the security officers ten times busier, but the honorific verb in context refers to President Baek Hanseong.
- **Replacement:** “What’s there to understand? It’s fine. I’m sure the President is already ten times busier because of me.”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung is explaining why he does not resent the President’s departure; the subsequent joke escalates how much busier the President has become.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘다른 부분은 정상 참작이 가능한 범위이긴 한데…… 아무래도 현재로서는 상해에 관련된 부분이 가장 큽니다.’
- **Current:** “There are other aspects that may be considered within the bounds of self-defense, but… at present, the most serious matter concerns bodily injury.”
- **Defect:** The draft renders 정상 참작 as self-defense, conflating mitigating or extenuating circumstances with the separate legal defense discussed immediately afterward.
- **Replacement:** “The other matters fall within the scope of extenuating circumstances, but… at present, the most serious issue is the bodily harm you inflicted.”
- **Rationale:** 정상 참작 means that circumstances may mitigate culpability or punishment; 정당방위 in the later sentence is the source’s actual term for self-defense.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘……이런 개 같은 인, 놈을 봤나.’
- **Current:** “……What the hell is wrong with this fucking human?”
- **Defect:** The translation loses the deliberate comic self-correction as the Skeleton King starts to call Taekyung a human and changes the word to an insult.
- **Replacement:** “……You shitty hu—bastard.”
- **Rationale:** The break between 인 and 놈 represents an interrupted 인간 followed by 놈, reinforcing the Skeleton King’s irritation while preserving the joke.

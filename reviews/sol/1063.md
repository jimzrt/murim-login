# Review

Three actionable fidelity defects: the imperial succession title, the scale of Dark Heaven’s forces, and Hyuk Mujin’s ability to dry his clothes with qi.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 일개 사이비(似而非) 집단을 벗어난 어마어마한 머릿수와 그에 따른 전공에 헛숨을 삼킨 것도 잠시
- **Current:** The sheer number of dead and captured—and the feat it represented—was enough to make them gasp. But before long, voices rose from somewhere in the crowd to contradict it.
- **Defect:** Omits that the reported numbers put Dark Heaven beyond the scale of a mere cult.
- **Replacement:** The numbers were staggering—far beyond those of a mere cult—and so was the victory they represented. They had barely caught their breath when voices elsewhere in the crowd rose to contradict the claim.
- **Rationale:** 일개 사이비 집단을 벗어난 explicitly frames the force as having outgrown the scale of a mere cult.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하나밖에 남지 않은 자신의 아우를 황태제(皇太弟)로 세우고
- **Current:** He named his only surviving younger brother Crown Prince,
- **Defect:** Crown Prince does not convey the specific title conferred on an emperor’s younger brother as designated successor.
- **Replacement:** He named his only surviving younger brother Imperial Younger Brother and heir apparent,
- **Rationale:** 황태제 identifies the emperor’s younger brother in the succession, not a crown prince.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “……아. 맞다. 이런 방법도 있었지.”

“그렇지. 너랑은 다르게.”
- **Current:** “…Oh. Right. I could’ve done that.”
- **Defect:** Makes Hyuk Mujin say he could have dried the clothes this way, although Taekyung’s reply establishes that Mujin cannot.
- **Replacement:** “…Oh. Right. You could do that.”
- **Rationale:** 이런 방법도 있었지 acknowledges the method; the immediately following 너랑은 다르게 distinguishes Taekyung’s ability from Mujin’s.

# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter agency, martial hierarchy, wordplay, characterization, and a speech action.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그럼에도 굳이 퀘스트창으로도 알려 주는 걸 보니 적천강이 품는 기대감이 대단한 모양이었다.
- **Current:** Even so, the fact that Jeok Cheongang had gone out of his way to inform me through the Quest window meant that his expectations were probably enormous.
- **Defect:** The draft explicitly makes Jeok Cheongang the agent communicating through the Quest window, whereas the source only treats the Quest window’s notice as evidence of his expectations.
- **Replacement:** Even so, the fact that the Quest window went out of its way to spell it out suggested just how much Jeok Cheongang expected of me.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the Quest window as the source of the information without adding that Jeok Cheongang personally controls or communicates through it.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 규모가 워낙 크다 보니 심사를 통제하는 감독관도 하나같이 상당한 실력자들이다. 최소 초일류에서 절정까지.

내가 속해 있는 조의 감독관은 초일류의 경지였지만, 철수신룡의 손이 어깨를 움켜쥐자 얼굴이 새하얗게 질렸다.
- **Current:** The scale of the Star-Array Grand Banquet was so enormous that every supervisor controlling the examinations was a considerable martial artist, ranging from at least First Rate to Peak.

The supervisor assigned to our group was at the First Rate realm, but the moment the Iron-Water Divine Dragon gripped his shoulder, the supervisor’s face turned deathly pale.
- **Defect:** Both occurrences of 초일류 are reduced to ordinary First Rate, understating the supervisors’ martial level and the Iron-Water Divine Dragon’s dominance over this particular supervisor.
- **Replacement:** The scale of the Star-Array Grand Banquet was so enormous that every supervisor overseeing the examinations was a formidable martial artist, ranging from the upper reaches of First Rate to Peak.

The supervisor assigned to our group was at the upper reaches of First Rate, but the moment the Iron-Water Divine Dragon gripped his shoulder, the supervisor’s face turned deathly pale.
- **Rationale:** The 초 prefix marks a level above an ordinary First Rate martial artist; retaining that distinction is necessary to preserve the power hierarchy.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘애새낀가.’

- **Rationale:** The replacement restores Taekyung’s profanity and the source’s judgment that Cheol Soo is both simplistic and ignorant, without adding a comment about his physique.

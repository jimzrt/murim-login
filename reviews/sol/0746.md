# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains two altered physical/subject actions, several softened register choices, one hierarchy loss, and a few localized English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 뭐 이런 병신…….
- **Current:** “What an idiot…”
- **Defect:** The translation sanitizes a strongly abusive insult, weakening the escalating profanity and comic hostility of the argument.
- **Replacement:** “What a fucking moron…”
- **Rationale:** 병신 is substantially harsher than the neutral English “idiot,” and the source deliberately escalates into explicit profanity.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어허, 이 사람이. 나보다 나이도 한 살 어리던데 욕은 하지 말지.
- **Current:** “Hey, watch your language. He’s a year younger than me.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the subject from the person being directly addressed to an unidentified third party, altering who is younger than the speaker.
- **Replacement:** “Come on, now. You’re a year younger than me, so watch your language.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is the interlocutor being reprimanded; 나보다 나이도 한 살 어리던데 means that person is one year younger than the speaker.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그들이 서로의 멱살을 잡는 그 순간에도, 옆 채널 뉴스에서는 세계 어딘가에서 발생한 변이 게이트나 몬스터 웨이브 속보를 알리고 있었으니까.
- **Current:** Even as they grabbed one another by the collars, the news channel next door was reporting breaking news about mutation Gates or monster waves occurring somewhere in the world.
- **Defect:** “The news channel next door” sounds as though the channel occupies a neighboring physical location rather than being an adjacent television channel.
- **Replacement:** Even as they grabbed one another by the collars, the news on the next channel was reporting breaking developments about mutation Gates or monster waves somewhere in the world.
- **Rationale:** 옆 채널 means another or adjacent television channel; “the news on the next channel” conveys that naturally.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 너희 인간들은…… 참으로 개판이군.
- **Current:** “Your human race is truly a complete mess.”
- **Defect:** The line is stiff and sanitizes the Skeleton King’s blunt, coarse assessment, weakening both his voice and the joke that follows.
- **Replacement:** “You humans… are a real shitshow.”
- **Rationale:** 너희 인간들은 directly addresses “you humans,” while 개판 is a coarse colloquial description of utter disorder rather than the formal-sounding “complete mess.”

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 손수건과 송송이를 번갈아 바라보던 나는 입맛을 다셨다.
- **Current:** I looked back and forth between the handkerchief and Song Song, then clicked my tongue.
- **Defect:** The draft substitutes clicking the tongue for smacking the lips, changing the narrator’s physical action.
- **Replacement:** I looked back and forth between the handkerchief and Song Song, then smacked my lips.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다시다 describes smacking or licking one’s lips, not clicking one’s tongue.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 먼 길 다녀오시느라 고생하셨습니다. 그런데…….
- **Current:** “You must have had a hard time on the long journey. But…”
- **Defect:** The draft turns a deferential expression of appreciation into an observation about presumed hardship, losing Team Leader Choi’s courteous subordinate register.
- **Replacement:** “Thank you for your hard work on such a long trip. But…”
- **Rationale:** 고생하셨습니다 here is a polite acknowledgment of the addressee’s effort, not speculation that the journey was difficult.

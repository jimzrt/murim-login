# Review

The draft omits a pivotal exchange, reverses who is looking at whom in the final confrontation, and changes several source details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 속을 읽을 수 없을 만큼 깊게 가라앉은 눈빛으로, 눈앞에 멈춰 선 원수의 자식을 바라보는 어느 노인의 표정처럼.
- **Current:** He looked at the old man before him—the child of his enemy—with eyes so deeply sunken that his thoughts were impossible to read.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the old man the enemy’s child and Sama Pyo the one looking at him. The old man is looking at Sama Pyo, his enemy’s child.
- **Replacement:** His heart was as calm and still as the expression of the old man before him, who gazed at his enemy’s son with eyes too deep to read.
- **Rationale:** 원수의 자식 is the person standing before the old man, whom the old man 바라보는.

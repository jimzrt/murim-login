# Review

Five actionable findings: two omitted identity/title cues, two localized prose defects, and a lost Dobby punchline.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 얼마 전에는 이 문주까지 찾아와 묻더군요. 주군께 무슨 변고라도 생기신 거 아니냐고.
- **Current:** Not long ago, even the Sect Leader came looking for you and asked whether something had happened to you.
- **Defect:** The surname Lee is omitted from “Sect Leader Lee,” removing the cue that prompts Taekyung to identify Lee Seowol in the next line.
- **Replacement:** Not long ago, even Sect Leader Lee came looking for you and asked whether something had happened to you.
- **Rationale:** 이 문주 means “Sect Leader Lee,” not merely “the Sect Leader.” Preserving the surname maintains the dialogue’s immediate logic.

- **Defect:** Cheol Mubaek’s established sobriquet, Tiger of Mount Heng, is omitted.
- **Replacement:** Great Hero Cheol Mubaek, the Tiger of Mount Heng and a friend of her late father, has also ended his seclusion and is taking an active role in the affairs of the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- **Rationale:** 항산호 is an explicit established title in the source and glossary.

## F03 — Minor

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 태원진가의 전속 도비, 아니 노비로 영입된 장태보의 눈빛은 공허하기 그지없었다.
- **Current:** Jang Taebo’s eyes were utterly vacant as he was recruited as the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s dedicated servant—no, slave.
- **Defect:** Rendering 도비 as the generic “servant” erases the explicit Dobby reference and therefore the correction-based punchline from “Dobby” to “slave.”
- **Replacement:** Jang Taebo’s eyes were utterly vacant as he was recruited as the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s personal Dobby—no, slave.
- **Rationale:** 도비 is Dobby, the enslaved house-elf, and 아니 노비 deliberately pivots from that name to the Korean word for a slave.

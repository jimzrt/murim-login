# Review

One reversed transfer action, one distorted deployment rationale, and several localized fidelity and voice issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그럼 그렇지, 하는 표정으로 혀를 찬 혁무진이 아까 내게 넘겨받은 죽간 두 개를 품에서 꺼냈다.
- **Current:** Hyuk Mujin clicked his tongue with an expression that said *of course*, then pulled the two bamboo slips I had received earlier from inside his robe.
- **Defect:** The direction of the earlier transfer is reversed. Hyuk Mujin had received the slips from the narrator, not the other way around.
- **Replacement:** Hyuk Mujin clicked his tongue with an expression that said *of course*, then pulled the two bamboo slips I had handed him earlier from inside his robe.
- **Rationale:** 내게 넘겨받은 describes Hyuk Mujin receiving the slips from the narrator; the current wording instead says the narrator received them.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 초절정 고수는 불리한 전황을 단번에 뒤집을 수 있는 존재. 사방으로 번지는 전화(戰火)의 불길을 막기 위해서는 당연한 선택이었다.
- **Current:** A Supreme Peak master was someone capable of reversing an unfavorable battlefield in an instant. Choosing one was only natural if we wanted to stop the flames of war spreading in every direction.
- **Defect:** “Choosing one” misstates the strategic mechanism. The context is dispersing or deploying Supreme Peak masters across multiple fronts, not selecting a single master.
- **Replacement:** A Supreme Peak master could reverse a losing battle in an instant. With the flames of war spreading in every direction, deploying them where they were needed was only natural.
- **Rationale:** The following fire-truck analogy and Mae Jonghak’s separation of Taekyung and Cheongpung establish that the choice is to distribute such forces among the spreading conflicts.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 이유라. 곰곰이 생각하던 나는 헛숨을 삼켰다.
- **Current:** After thinking it over, I swallowed a hollow breath.
- **Defect:** “Swallowed a hollow breath” is an unnatural literal rendering and does not clearly convey his startled realization.
- **Replacement:** After thinking it over, I sucked in a startled breath.
- **Rationale:** 헛숨을 삼켰다 describes an involuntary breath or gasp prompted by sudden realization, not literally swallowing a hollow breath.

## F04 — Minor

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 당시에 사경을 헤매고 있던 적천강은 두 사람을 직접 겪어 본 적이 없지만, 깨어난 후 그간 있었던 이야기를 모두 들은 터라 이해하는 데에는 별다른 무리가 없었다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang had been hovering between life and death at the time, so he had never personally experienced the two of them. But after waking up, he had heard everything that happened in the meantime, so he had no trouble understanding.
- **Defect:** “Experienced the two of them” is unnatural and gives 겪다 an inappropriate experiential sense when it means personally encountering or dealing with them.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang had been hovering between life and death at the time, so he had never met either of them in person. But after waking up, he had heard everything that had happened, so he had no trouble following the discussion.
- **Rationale:** 두 사람을 직접 겪어 본 적이 없지만 refers to Jeok Cheongang not having personally encountered the two people, while the latter clause explains why he can still understand the assessment.

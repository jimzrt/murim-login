# Review

Several actionable fidelity errors, chiefly incorrect distance conversions, an added motive of mercy, a singularized paired weapon, and a reversed energy-action mechanism.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 죽음이라는 두 글자가 뇌리를 스쳤지만, 아니다.
- **Current:** The words *death* and *death* flashed through my mind, but no.
- **Defect:** The draft mechanically renders the two written syllables of 죽음 as two repetitions of the English word, producing nonsense.
- **Replacement:** The word *death* flashed through my mind, but no.
- **Rationale:** 두 글자 describes the written form of the single Korean word 죽음; it does not repeat the concept twice.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 작은 크레이터를 만들며 포탄처럼 쏘아진 신형이 단번에 이십여 장에 달하는 공간을 뛰어넘었다.
- **Current:** My body shot forward like a cannonball, creating a small crater as I crossed more than twenty yards in one bound.

# Review

Several subject, relationship, and physical-description errors require correction, including two reversals that materially change who acted and whose engagement shocked Taekyung.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 태산이 말 잘 듣는다. 주군 좋다. 헤헤.
- **Current:** *—Taishan listens well. Lord is good. Hehehe.*
- **Defect:** “Lord is good” misses Taishan’s statement that he likes his lord, as confirmed by Sama Pyo’s reply that he likes Taishan too.
- **Replacement:** *—Taishan listens well. Taishan likes Lord. Hehehe.*
- **Rationale:** The clipped 좋아하다 construction expresses Taishan’s affection for Sama Pyo, not a general judgment that his lord is good.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 좋다. 엄청 강하고. 착하다. 태산이 칭찬해 줬다.
- **Current:** *—Like him. Very strong. Good. Praised Taishan.*
- **Defect:** The translation reverses the subject and object: the source says Taishan praised Jin Taekyung, not that Jin Taekyung praised Taishan.
- **Replacement:** *—Like him. Very strong. Good. Taishan praised him.*
- **Rationale:** 태산이 is the marked subject of 칭찬해 줬다. Jin Taekyung’s earlier praise is discussed separately in the preceding lines.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 섬단 같은 머리카락이 흔들리고 그 사이로 흘러나온 은은한 향기가 공기를 타고 전해졌다.
- **Current:** Her short hair swayed, and the faint fragrance flowing from between the strands drifted through the air.
- **Defect:** The draft invents that Ju Hwaran’s hair is short and omits the source’s silk-like description.
- **Replacement:** Her silken hair swayed, and a faint fragrance drifted from between the strands and through the air.
- **Rationale:** 섬단 같은 describes the hair’s fine, silk-like quality; it does not specify short hair.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아, 그랬죠. 무시하려던 건 아닌데 이게 참.
- **Current:** “Oh, right. I didn’t mean to ignore that, but… well.”

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 절정은 그 부분이었지. 점소이가 더 시킬 음식 있으면 주문표에 추가해 달라고 하니까 노려봤던 거.

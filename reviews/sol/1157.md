# Review

The draft changes the subject of a joke, treats a feared discovery as an accomplished one, and misstates Jin’s remark about dying twice.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 꿈속에서까지 나타날 정도라면, 제가 당신 취향일 수도 있다는 이야기니까.
- **Current:** “If you’re showing up even in my dreams, it might mean you’re my type.”
- **Defect:** Reverses whose dream it is and who might be attracted to whom.
- **Replacement:** “If I’m showing up even in your dreams, maybe I’m your type.”
- **Rationale:** Jin says that appearing in Johnson’s dreams might mean Jin is Johnson’s type.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 두 번이나 죽는 건 사양이라.
- **Current:** “I don’t plan to. I’ve had enough of dying twice.”
- **Defect:** Implies Jin has already died twice rather than rejecting a second death.
- **Replacement:** “I don’t plan to. I’d rather not die a second time.”
- **Rationale:** The source says he has no desire to die twice.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 우리가 모르는 내통자가 발각될 것 같으니 도주를 시도한 건가?
- **Current:** “Yeah. But access to the entire Pentagon has been forbidden for a week now. Did they catch someone on the inside who we didn’t know about, and they’re trying to escape?”
- **Defect:** Changes a suspected impending discovery into someone already having been caught.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. But access to the entire Pentagon has been forbidden for a week now. Is an inside collaborator we don’t know about trying to escape because they think they’re about to be found out?”
- **Rationale:** Johnson speculates that an undiscovered collaborator fears exposure and is attempting to flee.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 스스로가 강해지는 만큼 더욱 거대해진 위협에 홀로 맞서려 들었으니까.
- **Current:** The stronger he became, the greater the threats he faced alone.
- **Defect:** Loses Jin’s choice to take on those threats alone.
- **Replacement:** The stronger he became, the more he tried to face ever greater threats on his own.
- **Rationale:** 맞서려 들었으니까 describes his inclination to confront them alone, not merely the circumstance of facing them.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 단, 추가 명령이 있기 전까지 그 어떤 돌발 행동도 있어서는 안 됩니다. 제가 명령을 내릴 수 없는 상황이라면 다르겠지만요.
- **Current:** “However, no one is to take any action on their own until I give further orders. Unless I’m in a situation where I can’t issue orders.”
- **Defect:** Broadens a prohibition on rash or unexpected moves into a prohibition on all independent action.
- **Replacement:** “However, no one is to do anything rash until I give further orders. Unless I’m in a situation where I can’t issue orders.”
- **Rationale:** 돌발 행동 means an impulsive or unexpected action; the mobilization order does not prohibit every action taken without Jin.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 들리지도, 느껴지지도 않는다. 하늘과 땅을 떨어 울리는 굉음도, 사방을 뒤흔드는 거대한 힘의 여파도.
- **Current:** He couldn’t hear or feel it. No roar that shook heaven and earth. No tremendous force that would send shock waves through everything around him.
- **Defect:** The pronoun “it” appears to refer to the breeze he has just felt, making the observation contradictory.
- **Replacement:** There was no earthshaking roar to hear, no tremendous force rocking everything around him to feel.
- **Rationale:** What he neither hears nor feels is the expected destructive backlash, not the warm breeze.

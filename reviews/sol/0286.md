# Review

Two subject reversals alter the hospital-room interaction, while several passages misstate the source’s social roles or use unnatural English.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 예전에도 그랬다지만, 사람 목숨도 값을 매기는 세상이다.
- **Current:** They might have done the same thing in the past, but this was a world where even human lives were assigned a price.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Peace Guild’s expensive treatment the implied subject of what may have happened in the past. The source instead reflects that human lives were assigned monetary value in the past as well.
- **Replacement:** It may have been the same in the past, but this was a world where even a human life had a price.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 그랬다 refers to the practice of placing a value on human life, not to the Guild previously providing costly medical care.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “굳이 감사 인사를 하셔야겠다면 전주(錢主)에게 하시죠. 얼굴마담인 저는 빠지겠습니다.”

그 말에 임꺽정의 시선이 전주를 향해 스르륵 움직였다.
- **Current:** “If you really must thank someone, thank the paymaster. I’m just the front man, so I’ll step out.”

At those words, Im Kkeokjeong’s gaze slowly shifted toward the paymaster.
- **Defect:** “Paymaster” suggests someone who administers wages, while “front man” can imply a criminal intermediary. The source identifies Team Leader Choi as the financial backer who paid the costs and Butler Kim as merely the Guild’s public figurehead.
- **Replacement:** “If you really must thank someone, thank the one footing the bill. I’m just the figurehead, so leave me out of it.”

At those words, Im Kkeokjeong’s gaze slowly shifted toward the man footing the bill.
- **Rationale:** 전주 means the source of the funds or financial backer here, and 얼굴마담 means a nominal or public-facing figurehead.

# Review

The draft has several action and subject reversals, plus a softened slur and a few localized wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “간악한 인간에게 조센징이라고 했거든.”
- **Current:** “Because the wicked human called him *Chōsenjin*.”
- **Defect:** Reverses who used the slur.
- **Replacement:** “Because the monkey human called the wicked human *Chōsenjin*.”
- **Rationale:** The Skeleton King is explaining why Jin is angry at Yamamoto, not accusing Jin of using the slur.

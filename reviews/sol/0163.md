# Review

Two critical subject and action reversals, one critical quantity and qi-mechanism error, and several localized register, injury, and causality issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 세월이 내리는 저주는 이 갑자의 공력으로도, 초절정 고수의 강기로도 막아 낼 수 없다.
- **Current:** The curse of time could not be held back by sixty years of internal energy, nor by the Scorching Yang Qi of a Supreme Peak master.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces two jiazi of internal energy—one hundred and twenty years—to sixty years, and incorrectly changes a Supreme Peak master's manifested qi into the specifically fire-aligned Scorching Yang Qi.
- **Replacement:** The curse of time could not be held back even by his two jiazi of internal energy, or by the manifested qi of a Supreme Peak master.
- **Rationale:** 이 갑자 means two jiazi, while 강기 is manifested qi and is distinct from 열양지기, which appears later in the chapter.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 듣고 있소, 대답하시오.
- **Current:** — I’m listening. Answer me.
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed: Black Sand is demanding that Chinggen acknowledge that he is listening, not announcing that Black Sand himself is listening.
- **Replacement:** — Are you listening? Answer me.
- **Rationale:** Chinggen has deliberately shown no reaction to the Sound Transmission, so Black Sand presses him to respond.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “달포 후에 다시 오지.”
- **Current:** “Come back in a month.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns the old man's declaration that he will return into an order for Chinggen to return.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll be back in about a month.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted first-person subject is established by the old man immediately departing after assigning the search to Chinggen.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “거기 이마 까진 두 놈도.”
- **Current:** “And you two with the bare foreheads.”
- **Defect:** 까진 means scraped or abraded, not bare; the draft loses the minor injury caused by their earlier prostration.
- **Replacement:** “And you two with the scraped foreheads.”
- **Rationale:** The old man identifies Temur and Chinggen by their scraped foreheads rather than by exposed or hairless foreheads.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그의 한걸음에 풍경이 휙휙 스쳐 지나가고 풀들이 허리를 굽혔다.
- **Current:** With every step, the landscape flashed past, and the grass bent beneath the wind.
- **Defect:** The draft invents wind as the cause of the grass bending, weakening the connection between the phenomenon and the old man's movement.
- **Replacement:** With every step, the landscape flashed past and the grasses bowed low.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the landscape and grass responding as he strides forward and does not mention wind.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “허, 참.”
- **Current:** “Huh. Damn.”
- **Defect:** The draft adds profanity to a mild expression of disbelief and exasperation.
- **Replacement:** “Well, now.”
- **Rationale:** 허, 참 is an exasperated reaction, not a curse, and the translation rules prohibit intensifying ordinary lines into profanity.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 노인은 입을 다물었다.
- **Current:** The old man closed his mouth.
- **Defect:** The wording is an unnatural anatomical calque for becoming silent.
- **Replacement:** The old man fell silent.
- **Rationale:** 입을 다물었다 describes his cessation of speech here, and the replacement preserves that action in natural English.

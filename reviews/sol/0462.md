## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 똑똑히 봤다. 동정호를 책임지고 있는 곽 군관이 쩔쩔매며 존댓말을 사용하는 모습을.
- **Current:** He had seen it clearly: Officer Gwak, who was in charge of Dongting Lake, had been fawning over these people and addressing them with honorifics.
- **Defect:** “Fawning” adds deliberate sycophancy. The source says Officer Gwak was visibly flustered and struggling while speaking deferentially, demonstrating the passengers’ status without assigning him an ingratiating motive.
- **Replacement:** He had seen it clearly: Officer Gwak, who was in charge of Dongting Lake, had been visibly flustered as he addressed these people with honorifics.
- **Rationale:** 쩔쩔매며 describes being flustered or at a loss, not flattering someone for favor.

## F06 — Critical

# Review

The draft contains two altered actions, a guild-and-quantity misreading, a shifted action cue, and several localized terminology or cultural-reference issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 안타깝게도 슈마허는 그 순간의 끝자락에 다다르지 못하고 기습당했지만, 아마 다시 눈을 떴을 때는 한층 강해져 있을 것이다.
- **Current:** Unfortunately, Schumacher had been ambushed right at the end of that moment. But when he opened his eyes again, he would probably be stronger than before.
- **Defect:** The draft says Schumacher reached the end of the transcendent moment before being ambushed, while the source explicitly says he was ambushed before he could reach its end.
- **Replacement:** Unfortunately, Schumacher had been ambushed before he could reach the end of that moment, but when he opened his eyes again, he would probably be stronger than before.
- **Rationale:** 다다르지 못하고 means he failed to reach the end, not that the ambush occurred at the end.

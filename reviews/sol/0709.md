- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가파른 속도로 쏘아지던 진태경의 신형이 미미하게 흔들렸다.
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung’s figure, shooting forward at a steep angle, wavered slightly.
- **Defect:** 가파른 속도 describes breakneck speed, not a steep trajectory; the draft invents an angle of movement.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung’s figure, shooting forward at breakneck speed, wavered slightly.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies how fast he is moving, not the direction or angle of his movement.

# Review

Several actionable fidelity and terminology issues, including a thought misattributed to the dying speaker.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘그리고 그 물건이 무엇인지는, 머지않아 곧 알게 되겠지.’
- **Current:** *“And you’ll find out what that thing is soon enough.”*
- **Defect:** Quotation marks turn Jin’s thought about what he expects to learn into another line of the Demon Lord’s last message.
- **Replacement:** *And I’d find out what that thing was soon enough.*
- **Rationale:** The Demon Lord’s message has already ended; this is Jin’s own thought.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 황태제(皇太弟) 전하께서도 같은 마음이시라는 걸 알아줬으면 해요.
- **Current:** His Highness the Crown Prince couldn’t come with me today, but I hope you know he feels the same way.
- **Defect:** “Crown Prince” obscures the specified relationship: the designated heir is the Emperor’s younger brother.
- **Replacement:** His Highness, the Emperor’s younger brother and heir, couldn’t come with me today, but I hope you know he feels the same way.
- **Rationale:** 황태제 identifies an imperial younger brother designated as heir, not the Emperor’s son.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그럼 홍 동지, 아니 홍 태감이 동창의 새로운 창공(廠公)이 된 겁니까?
- **Current:** “So, Comrade Hong—no, Eunuch Hong—is the new head of the East Depot?”
- **Defect:** The question drops the specific office, Cang Gong, which matters because Hong distinguishes holding that office from running the East Depot.
- **Replacement:** “So, Comrade Hong—no, Eunuch Hong—have you become the East Depot’s new Cang Gong?”
- **Rationale:** The source asks whether Hong has become 창공, not merely whether he is in charge.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 동창을 책임지고 있는 건 맞지만, 창공. 즉 장인태감이라는 지위는 영원히 공석일 가능성이 높아요.
- **Current:** “I’m responsible for the East Depot, yes. But the position of its chief eunuch may remain vacant forever.
- **Defect:** “Chief eunuch” replaces the named Cang Gong office and its specific Seal-Holding Eunuch title.
- **Replacement:** “I’m responsible for the East Depot, yes. But the position of Cang Gong—the Seal-Holding Eunuch—may remain vacant forever.
- **Rationale:** Hong explicitly separates responsibility for the agency from the office of 창공, which he identifies as 장인태감.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 주위 어딘가에 숨겨 둔 암천의 이동진(移動眞)을 이용했을 가능성도 있지.
- **Current:** *Lastly, he could’ve used one of Dark Heaven’s Moving Formations, hidden somewhere nearby.*
- **Defect:** “One of” and the plural add multiple nearby formations where the source posits a hidden formation.
- **Replacement:** *Lastly, he could’ve used a Moving Formation Dark Heaven had hidden somewhere nearby.*
- **Rationale:** The Korean refers to a possible hidden 이동진 without asserting a group of them.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 마삼보 역시 초절정의 경지에 오른 강자인 동시에 엄청난 회복력을 지닌 괴물이고

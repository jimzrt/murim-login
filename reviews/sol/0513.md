# Review

The draft contains one omitted clause, several altered concrete details, and losses in speech-level characterization and weapon specificity.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 장강의 지류와 맞닿은 하남성 서협(西峽)은 오늘도 수많은 이들로 인산인해였다.
- **Current:** Xixia, Henan Province, where a tributary met the Yangtze, was packed with people as usual.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Xixia the confluence where the tributary meets the Yangtze. The source only places Xixia alongside a tributary of the Yangtze.
- **Replacement:** Xixia in Henan Province, situated along a tributary of the Yangtze, was packed with people again today.
- **Rationale:** 맞닿은 describes Xixia as adjoining the tributary; it does not state that the tributary joins the Yangtze there.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 전날 늦게 하남에 당도했다는 소문은 들었는데…… 설마하니 입맹(入盟)을 위해서인가.
- **Current:** “I heard they arrived in Henan late the other day… Could they be here to join the alliance?”
- **Defect:** The concrete arrival time, late the previous day, is weakened to the vague "the other day."
- **Replacement:** “I heard they arrived in Henan late yesterday… Could they be here to join the alliance?”
- **Rationale:** 전날 means the previous day, not an unspecified recent day.

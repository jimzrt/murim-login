# Review

The draft changes a prisoner count, misidentifies who receives the Guard’s loyalty, and alters an official’s rank. It also softens an injury, loses a time cue, and leaves the closing historical allusion unexplained.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하룻밤 사이 수백 명이 넘는 이들이 금의위에 의해 끌려갔다.
- **Current:** In a single night, the Embroidered Uniform Guard dragged away more than a hundred people.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces hundreds of arrests to more than one hundred.
- **Replacement:** In a single night, the Embroidered Uniform Guard dragged away hundreds of people.
- **Rationale:** 수백 명이 넘는 describes a count in the hundreds, not merely over one hundred.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 광활한 천하에서 오직 단 한 사람. 황제에게 절대적인 충성을 바치는.
- **Current:** The one and only person in this vast realm who gave absolute loyalty to the Emperor.
- **Defect:** The draft calls the Guard one person and reverses the point: the Emperor is the sole recipient of the Guard’s loyalty.
- **Replacement:** In this vast realm, they gave their absolute loyalty to one person alone: the Emperor.
- **Rationale:** 금의위 is the implied subject pledging loyalty; 오직 단 한 사람 identifies the Emperor.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 정 천호께서 제아무리 지휘사 영감의 총애를 받는다고 해도
- **Current:** No matter how much the Deputy Military Commissioner favors you
- **Defect:** The draft changes 지휘사 into a deputy commissioner, altering the superior’s rank.
- **Replacement:** No matter how much the Guard Commander favors you
- **Rationale:** 지휘사 denotes the commander here, not a deputy; the sentence invokes that superior’s favor.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 참혹하게 죽이거나, 반병신으로 만들어서라도 상부에 올려보낼 결과물을 만들어야 한다.
- **Current:** They had to produce results for their superiors—even if that meant killing the prisoners horribly or leaving them half-dead.
- **Defect:** “Half-dead” changes the threatened outcome from disabling the prisoners to nearly killing them.
- **Replacement:** They had to produce results for their superiors—even if that meant killing the prisoners horribly or leaving them half-crippled.
- **Rationale:** 반병신으로 만들다 describes leaving someone severely disabled.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마치 십여 년 전처럼.
- **Current:** “As daring as ever. Just like ten years ago.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes an approximate interval of more than ten years exact.
- **Replacement:** “As daring as ever. Just like a little over ten years ago.”
- **Rationale:** 십여 년 전 means a little over ten years ago.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “이번 연회가…… 홍문연(鴻門宴)이 될 수도 있겠네.”
- **Current:** “This banquet… could become a Hongmen Banquet.”
- **Defect:** The untranslated historical allusion leaves the final warning’s implication unclear to an English reader.
- **Replacement:** “This banquet… could become a Hongmen Banquet.[^1]”

[^1]: At the historical Feast at Hong Gate, a banquet became the setting for an attempt on Liu Bang’s life.
- **Rationale:** 홍문연 invokes a banquet used as cover for an attempt to kill a political rival; a brief note preserves the allusion without explaining it inside Hong Jin’s dialogue.

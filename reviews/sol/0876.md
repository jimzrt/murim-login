# Review

Several scene facts change in the draft, including a reversed threat, elapsed time, and physical actions. The ending also adds certainty to Hong Jin’s formal wording.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러니 썩 꺼져. 앞으로도 그 번쩍이는 갑옷을 걸치고 싶다면.
- **Current:** So get the hell out of my way. Unless you want to keep wearing that shiny armor.
- **Defect:** The condition is reversed: Hong Jin threatens their positions if they do not move.
- **Replacement:** So get the hell out of my way, if you want to keep wearing that shiny armor.
- **Rationale:** The source makes continued service in uniform conditional on obeying him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어린 주군이 황제를 알현하기 위해 떠난 지 어언 두 시진.
- **Current:** Two hours had passed since his young lord left to meet the Emperor.
- **Defect:** Two shichen are approximately four hours, not two.
- **Replacement:** Around four hours had passed since his young lord left to meet the Emperor.
- **Rationale:** The draft halves the elapsed time that drives Hong Jin’s anxiety.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99

- **Current:** Just as Hong Jin clenched his teeth without realizing it—
- **Defect:** He bites his lip, not clenches his teeth.
- **Replacement:** Just as Hong Jin bit down hard on his lip without realizing it—
- **Rationale:** The source specifies a different physical action.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 뭐 그래도 하늘을 날아다니지는 않았으니 용이 아니라 이무기였던 건 확실합니다.
- **Current:** Well, it definitely wasn’t a dragon, since it couldn’t fly through the sky.
- **Defect:** The draft claims the creature was unable to fly; Hyuk says only that it did not fly.
- **Replacement:** Well, it didn’t fly through the sky, so it definitely wasn’t a dragon—it was an imugi.
- **Rationale:** Not observing flight does not establish an inability to fly.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97

- **Replacement:** Oh, and he was a Supreme Peak master who looked way older than he was.
- **Rationale:** 겉늙었음 describes premature aging, not an appearance of being a hundred.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “회임(懷妊).”

- **Replacement:** “With child.”
- **Rationale:** This preserves his more formal register against Taekyung’s blunt “Pregnant” without adding a claim about succession.

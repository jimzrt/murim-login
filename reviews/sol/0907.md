# Review

The draft preserves the battle’s main sequence, but changes one character’s title, shifts a speaker’s intended subject, and loses two meaningful cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어째서인지 무릎을 꿇고 있는 어린 왕의 모습도 함께.
- **Current:** and the young prince, kneeling for some reason.
- **Defect:** Calls the kneeling child a prince rather than a king.
- **Replacement:** and the young king, kneeling for some reason.
- **Rationale:** 어린 왕 means “young king,” not “young prince.”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “묻고 싶은 말도, 하고 싶은 말도 많겠지. 하지만 그 일은 모든 것이 끝난 뒤로 미뤄 뒀으면 하오.”
- **Current:** “I’m sure you have questions, and there’s plenty I’d like to ask you, too. But I’d like to leave all that until after this is over.”
- **Defect:** Assigns the things left to ask or say to Jeong, when Jeong is acknowledging what Taekyung wants to ask and say.
- **Replacement:** “I’m sure you have plenty to ask me, and plenty you want to say. But I’d like you to save it until after this is over.”
- **Rationale:** Both 묻고 싶은 말 and 하고 싶은 말 refer to the person Jeong is addressing.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 하지만 그럼에도 불구하고, 지금은 쓰고 달고를 가릴 때가 아니었다.

써도 삼켜야 한다.

스스로 감내해야 한다.
- **Current:** But this wasn’t the time to worry about whether I could handle it.

If I used it, I’d have to swallow the consequences.

I’d have to bear them myself.
- **Defect:** Turns the bitter-or-sweet image into a calculation about whether using the Middle Dantian has manageable consequences.
- **Replacement:** But now wasn’t the time to be picky about what was bitter and what was sweet.

Even if it was bitter, I had to swallow it.

I had to bear it myself.
- **Rationale:** 쓰고 달고 and 써도 삼켜야 한다 sustain the image of swallowing something bitter; they do not say that using the dantian will cause consequences.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “저기서 관음 중인 저 인간, 아군은 맞냐?”

관음이라는 단어에서 움찔한 정호군이 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** “That guy watching from over there—is he on our side?”

Jeong Hogun flinched at the word “watching,” then nodded.
- **Defect:** Flattens the pointed, voyeuristic joke to ordinary watching, leaving Jeong’s flinch unmotivated.
- **Replacement:** “That guy over there playing peeping Tom—is he on our side?”

Jeong Hogun flinched at “peeping Tom,” then nodded.
- **Rationale:** 관음 carries a voyeuristic meaning; Jeong reacts specifically to that choice of word.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 약간의 시간 차를 두고 들이닥친 두 번째 화살비를 향해 수십여 개의 언월도(偃月刀)가 번뜩였다.
- **Current:** A second rain of arrows came moments later, and dozens of crescent blades flashed toward them.
- **Defect:** “Crescent blades” omits that the weapons are polearms.
- **Replacement:** A second rain of arrows came moments later, and dozens of crescent-bladed polearms flashed toward them.
- **Rationale:** 언월도 denotes a crescent-bladed polearm, not merely a blade of that shape.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 구태여 앞에 붙인 그 세 글자의 의미를, 나는 즉각 알아차렸다.
- **Current:** I immediately understood the meaning of those three words he’d taken care to add.
- **Defect:** Counts the English phrase “at least” as three words.
- **Replacement:** I immediately understood why he’d gone out of his way to add that qualifier.
- **Rationale:** 적어도 has three Korean syllables but translates here as two English words.

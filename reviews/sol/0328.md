# Review

The draft is broadly faithful but adds an unsupported kinship hierarchy, alters two causal or temporal details, omits a meaningful form of address, and softens several characterization cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 군자검 주호군의 의형제이자 삼십 년간 용봉표국에 몸담았던 총표두 허준은 그렇게 죽었다.
- **Current:** Heo Jun, the sworn younger brother of Junzi Sword Ju Hogun and the Chief Escort who had spent thirty years with the Yongbong Escort Bureau, died just like that.
- **Defect:** The draft adds an unsupported younger-brother hierarchy. 의형제 establishes sworn brotherhood but does not identify Heo Jun as the younger party.
- **Replacement:** Heo Jun, the sworn brother of Junzi Sword Ju Hogun and the Chief Escort who had spent thirty years with the Yongbong Escort Bureau, died just like that.
- **Rationale:** Removing “younger” preserves the exact kinship information given by the source without inventing seniority.

- **Current:** At the hands of the niece who had once treated him like blood.
- **Defect:** “Treated him like blood” is an unnatural and unclear rendering of 따르던, which describes her once looking up to and following him as though he were family.
- **Replacement:** At the hands of the niece who had once looked up to him as family.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the former affection and direction of the relationship while reading naturally in English.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 길었던 표행의 뒤처리만으로도 하루가 금방 갈 텐데, 다이나믹한 사건들까지 잇따랐으니 몸이 열 개라도 부족할 것이 분명했다.
- **Current:** Even handling the aftermath of such a long escort mission would take an entire day, and then there had been one dynamic incident after another. She probably needed ten bodies just to keep up with everything.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the idiom that the day would fly by into a concrete claim that the work would take exactly one entire day, altering the temporal meaning.
- **Replacement:** The day would fly by just dealing with the aftermath of the long escort mission, and with one crazy incident after another on top of that, even ten bodies wouldn’t be enough.
- **Rationale:** 하루가 금방 가다 means that a day passes quickly while occupied, not that the work has a one-day duration.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96

- **Current:** *Am I an idiot?*

I needed to go to Sichuan. Why was I going to the Yongbong Escort Bureau?
- **Defect:** The self-directed outburst loses the force of 병신인가 and omits 진짜, noticeably sanitizing Taekyung’s frustrated, profane narration.
- **Replacement:** *Am I a fucking idiot? Seriously?*

## F05 — Major

“Then make something to do.”
- **Defect:** Song Ilseom is saying he has no business or reason to go, not that he is unoccupied. The draft changes the basis of his refusal and weakens the ensuing joke.

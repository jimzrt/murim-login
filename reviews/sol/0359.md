# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes one destruction radius, loses a hierarchy cue, misreads several descriptions and metaphors, and weakens the final passage’s characterization and crude wordplay.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러자 지금껏 들어 본 적 없는 굉음과 함께 반경 십여 장의 모든 것이 부서지고 터져 나갔다.
- **Current:** With a roar unlike anything I had ever heard, everything within a radius of several dozen feet shattered and exploded.
- **Defect:** The destruction radius is substantially reduced. 십여 장 means a little over ten jang, or roughly a hundred feet, not merely several dozen feet.
- **Replacement:** With a roar unlike anything I had ever heard, everything within a radius of over a hundred feet shattered and exploded.
- **Rationale:** The source gives a specific radius whose scale demonstrates the force of the collision.

## F02 — Minor

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 경천신니와 그 장로들이 오백여 초를 버텼다면 어떤가?

- **Defect:** “Those Elders” omits 그’s relational force here: they are the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun’s Elders, not an unrelated or merely demonstrative group.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘독기.’

희망은 사람을 일어나게 만드는 동시에 나약하게 만든다.

하지만 독기가 남아 있다면, 마지막까지 싸우고자 하는 의지가 있다면 나는 쓰러지지 않는다.

아니, 쓰러지지 않기로 했다.
- **Current:** *Spite.*

Hope could make people stand back up, but it could also make them weak.

But if spite remained—if I still had the will to fight until the very end—I would not fall.

No.

I had decided not to fall.
- **Defect:** 독기 here means fierce, hard-edged tenacity or bloody-minded resolve, as the following clause explicitly equates it with the will to keep fighting. “Spite” instead implies resentment toward another person and mischaracterizes his motivation.
- **Replacement:** *Sheer grit.*

But as long as that grit remained—as long as I still had the will to fight until the very end—I would not fall.

No.

I had decided not to fall.
- **Rationale:** The source defines 독기 through Taekyung’s refusal to collapse and his will to continue fighting, not through personal malice.

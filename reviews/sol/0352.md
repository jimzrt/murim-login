# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one quantity change, several unsupported or altered details, lost uncertainty and command register, and localized terminology issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** “일원(一元), 삼당(三黨), 오대(五隊)는 적들을 맞이할 준비를 하라!”
- **Current:** “Ilwon, Samdang, and Odae—prepare to meet the enemy!”
- **Defect:** The three organizational designations are left as unexplained romanizations despite having transparent numerical meanings, making the command needlessly opaque in English.
- **Replacement:** “One Origin, Three Divisions, and Five Squads—prepare to meet the enemy!”
- **Rationale:** The source presents named clan formations built around one, three, and five; translating the designations preserves those quantities and makes the command intelligible without inventing ledger terms.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 온통 찢기고 피에 젖은 무복은 틀림없는 전투의 흔적이었다.
- **Current:** The green martial uniform, torn to shreds and soaked in blood, bore unmistakable traces of battle.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that the uniform is green, but the source specifies only a martial uniform; the squad's name does not establish its clothing color.
- **Replacement:** The martial uniform, torn to shreds and soaked in blood, bore unmistakable traces of battle.
- **Rationale:** Removing the unsupported color restores the source's visual information exactly.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “의심을 피하기 위해서인지 여러 무리로 나누어 움직이고 있었으나, 저희가 파악한 바로는 족히 삼백은 되는 듯했습니다.”
- **Current:** “To avoid suspicion, they were moving in several groups. But according to our assessment, there must have been at least three hundred.”
- **Defect:** The report's uncertainty is removed twice: the speaker says they were divided into groups perhaps to avoid suspicion and that there seemed to be at least three hundred, not that either conclusion was certain.
- **Replacement:** “Perhaps to avoid suspicion, they were moving in several groups. But from what we were able to determine, there seemed to be at least three hundred of them.”
- **Rationale:** The constructions 위해서인지 and 듯했습니다 explicitly qualify both the inferred motive and the estimated number.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하나뿐인 조카를 바라보는 그의 눈빛에 담긴 감정은 한심, 그 자체였다.
- **Current:** Tang Sadok looked at his only nephew with pure pity.
- **Defect:** 한심 conveys that Tang Sadok finds his nephew utterly pathetic or contemptible, not that he feels pity for him.
- **Replacement:** Tang Sadok looked at his only nephew with utter contempt.
- **Rationale:** The emotion characterizes Tang Sadok's scorn for his nephew's foolish reasoning; rendering it as pity softens and changes that reaction.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “조, 존명!”

- **Defect:** The response loses 존명, a formal acknowledgment that the command will be obeyed, and reduces it to a generic affirmation and address.

- **Rationale:** 존명 is a deferential acceptance of an order, so the replacement preserves both the guards' hierarchy and their alarm.

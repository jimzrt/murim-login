# Review

One critical speaker-subject error, several meaningful fidelity defects, and localized terminology and prose issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 제가 틀린 말 했습니까. 제가 아무리 제갈 대협을 존중한다지만 이건 아니죠.
- **Current:** “Young Hero Jin is not wrong. I respect you, Sir Zhuge, but this isn’t right.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung’s first-person rhetorical question is changed into a third-person statement about “Young Hero Jin,” altering the speaker-subject relationship.
- **Replacement:** “Am I wrong? I may respect you, Sir Zhuge, but this isn’t right.”
- **Rationale:** 제가 means “I,” and 틀린 말 했습니까 asks whether what Taekyung said was wrong; he does not refer to himself by title in the third person.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “……따라 해 보게. 무량수불.”

“아, 죄송합니다. 무량수불.”
- **Current:** “……Repeat after me. Amitabha Buddha.”

“Ah, my apologies. Amitabha Buddha.”
- **Defect:** The translation conflates 무량수불 with 아미타불, erasing Hyeongong’s correction and the joke created by Taekyung cycling through the wrong religious invocations.
- **Replacement:** “……Repeat after me. Infinite Life Buddha.”

“Ah, my apologies. Infinite Life Buddha.”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung has just said 아미타불, “Amitabha.” Hyeongong deliberately corrects him to the distinct Daoist-style invocation 무량수불, literally “Infinite Life Buddha.”

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 물론 동정어옹을 작살 낸 건 미안하게 생각하지만, 나로서는 어쩔 수 없는 일이었다.
- **Current:** Of course, I felt sorry for what had happened to the Dongting Fisherman, but there had been nothing else I could do.
- **Defect:** The passive “what had happened” removes Taekyung’s explicit acknowledgment that he personally wrecked the Dongting Fisherman.
- **Replacement:** Of course, I felt bad about wrecking the Dongting Fisherman, but there had been nothing else I could do.
- **Rationale:** 동정어옹을 작살 낸 건 directly identifies Taekyung’s violent action against the Dongting Fisherman; it is not a vague misfortune that merely happened to him.

# Review

The draft contains several fidelity errors, including a reversed commission, a misidentified speaker, an altered duration, and an unsupported claim that Song Il is unconscious. It also loses martial kinship, gambling, and two pragmatic cues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “우리 세 사형제는 종종 이곳에 들르고는 했다. 삼십 년도 더 전의 일이지. 그때는 네 사부도 장문인이 아니었고.”
- **Current:** “The three of us used to stop by this place from time to time. That was more than thirty years ago. Your Master wasn’t the Sect Leader back then, either.”
- **Defect:** The translation omits that the three men were martial brothers, losing an explicit relationship that establishes their shared hierarchy and history.
- **Replacement:** “We three martial brothers used to stop by this place from time to time. That was more than thirty years ago. Your Master wasn’t the Sect Leader back then, either.”
- **Rationale:** 세 사형제 explicitly identifies the three as martial brothers, not merely three unspecified companions.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 한 식경이 넘도록 입을 다물고 노인의 다예(茶艺)를 기다린 그의 목소리는 가뭄의 논바닥처럼 쩍쩍 갈라져 있었다.
- **Current:** He had kept his mouth shut for more than an hour while waiting for the old man to finish preparing his tea, and his voice was cracked like a drought-stricken rice paddy.
- **Defect:** The draft more than doubles the stated duration and adds that he was waiting for the old man to finish, which the source does not say.
- **Replacement:** He had kept his mouth shut for more than half an hour while the old man performed his tea ceremony, and his voice was cracked like a drought-stricken rice paddy.
- **Rationale:** 한 식경 is approximately half an hour in this genre, while the source says he waited through the old man’s tea art rather than specifically waiting for its completion.

# Review

Two critical subject/register shifts and several actionable losses of humor, characterization, hierarchy, physical action, and required cultural context.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 기라성 같은 문파들이 즐비한 중원(中原)이라면 모를까, 변방에 속하는 산서성에서는 두 사람 모두 침 좀 뱉고 방귀깨나 뀌는 문파의 후계자들이었다.
- **Current:** In the Central Plains, where countless prestigious sects stood shoulder to shoulder, things might have been different. But in Shanxi Province, a borderland, both men were heirs to sects with enough clout to matter.
- **Defect:** The generic phrase “enough clout to matter” omits the deliberately crude, comic image of sects powerful enough to swagger around spitting and farting.
- **Replacement:** In the Central Plains, where countless prestigious sects stood shoulder to shoulder, things might have been different. But out in borderland Shanxi Province, both men were heirs to sects big enough to swagger around spitting and farting as they pleased.
- **Rationale:** The source uses 침 좀 뱉고 방귀깨나 뀌는 as a humorous vulgar characterization of their sects’ regional importance; flattening it removes both voice and humor.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그 많은 사람 앞에서 외간 남자에게 엉덩이를 흠씬 두들겨 맞았으니 혼삿길 막히는 건 시간문제일 거다.
- **Current:** After having their buttocks thoroughly beaten by a strange man in front of so many people, it was only a matter of time before their marriage prospects were ruined.
- **Defect:** “A strange man” suggests an unfamiliar or odd man, whereas 외간 남자 specifically invokes an unrelated man outside the family, the propriety cue that explains the threat to their marriage prospects.
- **Replacement:** After having their buttocks thoroughly beaten by a man outside their families in front of so many people, it was only a matter of time before their marriage prospects were ruined.
- **Rationale:** The source’s social and sexual-propriety implication is causal, not incidental: public physical exposure to an unrelated man is why their prospects are said to be ruined.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 세상 물정 모르던 금수저들이 줄빠따를 맞고 경각심을 갖게 됐다.
- **Current:** The pampered rich kids had taken a group beating and gained some common sense.
- **Defect:** 경각심 means wariness or vigilance, not common sense; the draft changes what the beating taught them.
- **Replacement:** The pampered rich kids had taken a group beating and finally learned to be cautious.
- **Rationale:** Their decision to investigate the apparent beggar shows newly acquired caution after underestimating Taekyung, not a general gain in practical intelligence.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 가긴 어딜 가. 중간에 웬 잡것들이 끼어드는 바람에 대화도 제대로 못 했는데. 나는 황급히 손을 내저었다.

“에헤이, 밤이 늦었으면 하룻밤 묵고 가셔야지. 여비도 없다면서요?”
- **Current:** “Heading out where? Those idiots interrupted us, and we never even got to talk properly.” I hurriedly waved my hand. “Hey now, if it’s late, you should stay the night. You said you don’t have any travel money, right?”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Taekyung’s internal narration into words spoken aloud to Cheongpung and softens 잡것들 to “idiots.”
- **Replacement:** *Where does he think he’s going? We hadn’t even gotten to talk properly because those random bastards barged in.* I hurriedly waved him off. “Hey now, if it’s late, you should stay the night. You said you don’t have any travel money, right?”
- **Rationale:** The source has no quotation marks until Taekyung asks Cheongpung to stay; therefore the first two sentences are private narration, not dialogue.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그렇긴 하지. 내 빙당호로…….”
- **Current:** “That’s true. With my candied hawthorn skewers—”
- **Defect:** The established culturally specific food term is rendered without the required explanatory footnote.
- **Replacement:** “That’s true. With my candied hawthorn skewers[^1]—”

[^1]: Traditional fruit skewers coated in hardened sugar.
- **Rationale:** The binding glossary explicitly identifies 빙당호로 as candied hawthorn skewers and requires a brief footnote explaining the food.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 아까부터 내 신분을 알고 있었음에도 별 동요가 없다. 아, 그렇구나. 딱 이 정도 반응이다.
- **Current:** He had known my identity from the beginning, yet he hadn’t shown the slightest agitation.

*Ah, I see. That’s exactly as much of a reaction as it deserves.*
- **Defect:** The italics assign “Ah, I see” to Taekyung as a direct thought, but the source uses it to characterize Cheongpung’s minimal reaction. “From the beginning” also overstates 아까부터, which means since earlier.
- **Replacement:** He had known who I was for a while, yet he hadn’t reacted at all. His response amounted to, *Oh, I see,* and nothing more.
- **Rationale:** 딱 이 정도 반응이다 identifies the preceding phrase as the extent of Cheongpung’s response; it is not Taekyung judging how much reaction his identity deserves.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그가 시원한 웃음과 함께 돌아서자, 대화가 마무리됐다고 생각한 총관이 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** He turned away with a refreshing laugh. Thinking the conversation had come to an end, the chief steward spoke up.
- **Defect:** “A refreshing laugh” is an unnatural literal collocation for 시원한 웃음 in this context.
- **Replacement:** He turned away with a hearty laugh. Thinking the conversation had come to an end, the chief steward spoke up.
- **Rationale:** 시원한 describes Cheongpung’s open, uninhibited manner here; “hearty” conveys that naturally without changing the action.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어느덧 팔 성에 이른 칠매검(七梅劍)이 서늘한 새벽 공기를 가르고 있을 때, 전령(傳令) 하나가 대문을 열고 들어왔다.
- **Current:** The Seven Plum Sword, which he had mastered to eight-tenths, cut through the cold dawn air when a messenger entered through the main gate.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the messenger’s explicit action of opening the main gate.
- **Replacement:** The Seven Plum Sword, in which he had reached eight-tenths mastery, cut through the cold dawn air when a messenger opened the main gate and entered.
- **Rationale:** 대문을 열고 들어왔다 states that the messenger opened the gate before entering; merely entering through it does not preserve that physical action.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그러나 성주이자 고귀한 핏줄을 타고난 상산왕의 명령이다. 사내는 별수 없이 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** But the order had come from Prince Shangshan, the City Lord and a man born of noble blood. The man had no choice but to nod.
- **Defect:** “Noble blood” weakens the prince’s royal hierarchy to ordinary aristocratic ancestry.
- **Replacement:** But the order had come from Prince Shangshan, the City Lord and a man of royal blood. The man had no choice but to nod.
- **Rationale:** 상산왕 is a prince of the imperial family, already explicitly identified as 황족 earlier in the chapter; his bloodline is royal, not merely noble.

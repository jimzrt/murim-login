## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내일, 마지막 대회의의 안건은 한족 진태경의 처형입니다.
- **Current:** Tomorrow, the final agenda of the Tribal Grand Council will be the execution of the Han Chinese Jin Taekyung.
- **Defect:** “Final” modifies the Tribal Grand Council itself, not the execution’s position as the council’s final agenda item.
- **Replacement:** Tomorrow, the agenda of the final Tribal Grand Council will be the execution of the Han Chinese Jin Taekyung.
- **Rationale:** 마지막 대회의 means “the final Tribal Grand Council,” whereas “the final agenda” changes the meeting sequence.

# Review

The draft changes how Qi Sense is described at a key moment and substitutes one of Great Sir’s claimed names. It also obscures the joke in two other names.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그를 둘러싸고 있는 비밀의 장막을 걷어 줄 푸른 원이, 마침내 목표에 닿는 것을 똑똑히 목격할 수 있었다.
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung watched the blue circle, the veil of mystery around the man, finally reach its target.
- **Defect:** The apposition makes the blue circle the veil rather than the means of lifting it.
- **Replacement:** He clearly saw the blue circle that would lift the veil of mystery around the man finally reach its target.
- **Rationale:** The Korean says the blue circle will remove the veil surrounding the man.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 어느덧 9성에 도달한 [기감]의 푸른 원이 빠르게 뻗어 나갔다.
- **Current:** the blue circle of [Qi Sense], which had reached Level 9, shot outward.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a nine-star Skill progression into a Level.
- **Replacement:** the blue circle of [Qi Sense], which had reached nine stars, shot outward.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies 9성, not Level 9.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** - 스킬, [기감]이 반쯤 성공했습니다!
- **Current:** > **System**

- **Replacement:** > **System**

## F04 — Major

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “갈 때마다 이름이 바뀝디다. 개똥이, 소똥이였던 적도 있소.”
- **Current:** “His name changes every time I visit. There were times it was Gaettong or Sso-Ttong.”
- **Defect:** Sso-Ttong misrenders 소똥이, and leaving both names unexplained hides their shared poop-name joke.
- **Replacement:** “His name changes every time I visit. He’s even gone by Gaettong—Dog Poop—and Sottong—Cow Poop.”
- **Rationale:** 개똥 and 소똥 mean dog poop and cow poop; the paired meanings account for the absurdity of the names.

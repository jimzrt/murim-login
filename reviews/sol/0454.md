# Review

The draft contains one reversed subject, several altered kinship and characterization details, a misplaced modifier that changes who arrived late, and meaningful losses of profanity and pragmatic force.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무슨 소리인지는 몰라도 사람이라면 누구나 위험을 감지하는 법.
- **Current:** I did not know what I was saying, but any human being knew how to sense danger.
- **Defect:** The omitted subject is Ju Wongong, who does not understand Taekyung's words. The draft instead makes Taekyung unaware of what he himself is saying.
- **Replacement:** Though he had no idea what I meant, anyone could sense danger.
- **Rationale:** The sentence explains why Ju Wongong instinctively begins to obey despite not understanding Taekyung's joke.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “우선 존귀한 이 몸을 알아보지 못한 네 놈의 그 오만한 눈깔을 파 버리고, 오체분시를 한 다음……!”
- **Current:** “First, I shall gouge out those arrogant eyes of yours for failing to recognize this noble self, then I will dismember you piece by piece, and afterward—”
- **Defect:** The draft softens the vulgar 눈깔 and turns the specific threat of tearing the victim's body apart into the less precise “dismember you piece by piece.”
- **Replacement:** “First, I shall gouge out those arrogant eyeballs of yours for failing to recognize this noble self, then tear you limb from limb, and afterward—”
- **Rationale:** Ju Wongong abruptly mixes pompous self-reference with crude, graphic language; both the register and the form of the threat characterize him.

- **Defect:** The draft invents that Zhu Bao was targeted and spared. The source calls him the ruthless Emperor's 아픈 손가락—his emotional soft spot or cherished concern.

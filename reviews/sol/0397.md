# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters several quantities and tactical details, omits a military rank and a characterization cue, and contains a few localized register and viewpoint problems.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가파르게 두방망이질 치는 심장과 달리, 그의 머릿속은 차갑게 식어 있었다.
- **Current:** Unlike his heart, which was pounding as if it had been struck by two clubs, his mind was ice-cold.
- **Defect:** The draft literalizes the idiom as two clubs striking his heart, producing an inaccurate and unnatural physical image. The source simply describes his heart pounding violently.
- **Replacement:** Unlike his violently pounding heart, his mind was ice-cold.
- **Rationale:** 두방망이질 치다 is an idiom for pounding or hammering rapidly, not for being struck by two literal clubs.

# Review

Several subject, action, kinship, injury, and characterization errors require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 정룡이 잘 지내냐? 슬슬 실버타운에 집 알아봐야 하는 거 아냐?
- **Current:** How’s Jungryong doing? Isn’t it about time you found him a place in a retirement community?
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied subject of the second question from Jungryong to Go Jun, making Go Jun responsible for finding his Master a retirement home.
- **Replacement:** How’s Jungryong doing? Isn’t it about time he started looking for a place in a retirement community?
- **Rationale:** Jungryong remains the topic across both sentences; the joke is that Jungryong himself is old enough to start looking into retirement housing.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 새우처럼 허리를 굽힌 채 속을 게워 낸 석고준은 눈을 부릅떴다.
- **Current:** Bending at the waist like a shrimp, Go Jun vomited up the contents of his stomach and opened his eyes wide.
- **Defect:** The draft specifies ordinary stomach contents, weakening and contradicting the immediately revealed fact that pieces of his internal organs have come out.
- **Replacement:** Bent over like a shrimp, Go Jun vomited up his insides and stared with bulging eyes.
- **Rationale:** 속을 게워 내다 is left visceral here, and the following lines explicitly identify the pale objects in the blood as pieces of his internal organs.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 바로 그 두려움이 석고준의 머리채를 움켜잡고 천천히 들어 올린다.
- **Current:** That very fear seized Go Jun by the hair and slowly lifted him up.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Go Jun’s entire body rise, but the action raises his head by the hair so that his gaze travels upward over Taekyung’s body.
- **Replacement:** That very fear seizes Go Jun by the hair and slowly raises his head.
- **Rationale:** The next paragraph traces his sight from the foot through the leg and torso to the eyes, confirming that it is his head and gaze being lifted; the source also deliberately shifts into the present tense.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘우리 정룡 아저씨. 골머리 좀 썩겠는데.’
- **Current:** Our Uncle Jungryong is going to have one hell of a headache.
- **Defect:** Capitalized “Uncle” falsely gives Jungryong a familial relationship to Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** Looks like Mr. Jungryong is in for one hell of a headache.
- **Rationale:** 아저씨 is mock-familiar address for an older man here, not a kinship term; Taekyung and Lee Jungryong are adversaries with no family relationship.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “걱정은 아니고, 기대는 되네요. 이정룡 씨에게 꼭 보고하세요.”
- **Current:** “Worried? No. I’m curious. Be sure to report it to Lee Jungryong.”
- **Defect:** “I’m curious” removes Taekyung’s eager anticipation of Lee Jungryong’s reaction.
- **Replacement:** “Worried? No. But I am looking forward to it. Be sure to report it to Lee Jungryong.”
- **Rationale:** 기대는 되네요 means that he looks forward to the outcome, characterizing him as openly defiant rather than merely inquisitive.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이 전쟁에는 종전(終戰)만 있을 뿐, 휴전은 존재하지 않는다는 것을.
- **Current:** This war would end eventually, but there would be no truce.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the contrast between a conclusive end and a temporary ceasefire into a generic prediction that the war will eventually finish.
- **Replacement:** This war could only end for good; there would be no truce.
- **Rationale:** 종전만 있을 뿐 means only a definitive end to the war is possible, not merely that an ending will occur at some unspecified future time.

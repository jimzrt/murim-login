# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains two subject/action ambiguities and several localized meaning or characterization shifts.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 애처로운 모습으로 창에 몸을 지탱하고 있으려니 아직 적천강이 가르쳐 주지 않은 화신귀무(火神鬼舞)가 아쉽게 느껴졌다.
- **Current:** As I leaned against the spear in a pitiful state, I found myself regretting the Dance of the Fire God and Demon, which Jeok Cheongang still hadn’t taught me.
- **Defect:** “Regretting the Dance” suggests that Taekyung regrets the technique itself, whereas he wishes Jeok Cheongang had taught it to him.
- **Replacement:** As I leaned pitifully against the spear for support, I couldn’t help wishing Jeok Cheongang had taught me the Dance of the Fire God and Demon.
- **Rationale:** The source expresses disappointment that the technique remains unavailable to him, not regret about the technique.

숨만 붙은 채 경련하는 천년지주를 쿠션 삼아 지면에 내려앉은 야수묘왕이 붉게 상기된 얼굴로 내게 두 팔을 벌렸다.

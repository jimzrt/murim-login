# Review

Seven actionable findings: one reversed quantity, three characterization or subject errors, one singular/plural error, and two localized wording defects.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** - 그대로군.
- **Current:** —Just as you were.
- **Defect:** The English is an unnatural fragment and frames the resemblance as belonging only to the past, while the dragon is recognizing that Taekyung remains exactly as seen.
- **Replacement:** —You’re exactly the same.
- **Rationale:** 그대로군 expresses recognition that someone or something is unchanged or exactly as previously seen.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한때 누구보다 단단하고 강대한 힘을 품었던 동체가 허물어지고, 이내 동정호의 강물 위로 쓰러진다.
- **Current:** The body that had once held strength harder and more immense than any other slowly crumbled, then fell onto the waters of Dongting Lake.
- **Defect:** “Strength harder” is not a valid English collocation, and “slowly crumbled” suggests disintegration rather than the body giving way and collapsing.
- **Replacement:** The body that had once held unparalleled resilience and power gave way, then fell onto the waters of Dongting Lake.
- **Rationale:** 단단하고 강대한 conveys formidable toughness and power, while 허물어지고 describes the body collapsing or giving way.

# Review

The draft contains one critical subject reversal, a distorted rhetorical insult, one glossary mismatch, one emotional misreading, and softened profanity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 일 초. 아니 그 반의반만 늦었더라도 신의는 이미 이 세상 사람이 아니었을 것이다.
- **Current:** If the Divine Physician had been even one second late—or even a quarter of a second—he would already have been dead.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Divine Physician the one who was nearly late, but Taekyung's intervention is what moved him out of the attack's path.
- **Replacement:** Had I been even one second later—no, even a quarter of a second—the Divine Physician would already have been dead.
- **Rationale:** The immediately preceding action is Taekyung using his internal energy to push the Divine Physician away before the Finger Qi crosses the spot where the physician's head had been.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “누가 들으면 시벌, 만독지환이 성감대라도 되는 줄 알겠네. 이제는 늙어서 서지도 않는 새끼가.”
- **Current:** “Who’d hear you and think the Myriad-Poison Ring was an erogenous zone? You’re an old bastard who can’t even get it up anymore.”
- **Defect:** The opening rhetorical construction is reversed into asking who would draw that conclusion, rather than saying anyone overhearing him would; it also omits the explicit opening profanity.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck, anyone listening to you would think the Myriad-Poison Ring was an erogenous zone. You’re so old you can’t even get it up anymore, asshole.”
- **Rationale:** 누가 들으면 means that someone hearing him would get that impression, while 시벌 supplies an explicit profane interjection and 새끼 sustains the insult.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 서천마군 같은 초절정 고수가 지금처럼 삼류 흑도나 쓸 법한 방법을, 한 치의 망설임도 없이 실행에 옮길 줄은 몰랐기 때문이다.
- **Current:** I had never imagined that a Supreme Peak master like the Western Heaven Demon Lord would carry out a method fit for third-rate dark-path figures without a moment’s hesitation.
- **Defect:** The established rendering of 삼류 is not used, and “carry out a method” is an unnatural collocation.
- **Replacement:** I had never imagined that a Supreme Peak master like the Western Heaven Demon Lord would resort without hesitation to tactics fit for some Third Rate dark-path thug.
- **Rationale:** 삼류 requires the established term “Third Rate,” and the sentence describes resorting to tactics characteristic of a low-level dark-path figure.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한심과 경멸이 어린 한 쌍의 눈동자가 나를 꿰뚫는다.
- **Current:** A pair of eyes filled with contempt and pity bored into me.
- **Defect:** 한심 conveys disdain or the judgment that someone is pathetic, not pity.
- **Replacement:** His eyes bored into me, filled with scorn and contempt.
- **Rationale:** The Demon Lord is looking down on Taekyung for failing to understand the sacred treasure, with no sympathetic or pitying emotion in the source.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “좆 까. 이…… 병신아.”
- **Current:** “Go fuck yourself. You… dumbass.”
- **Defect:** “Dumbass” substantially softens the force of 병신 in an explicitly profane insult.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck off, you… fucking moron.”
- **Rationale:** 좆 까 and 병신 form a strongly vulgar, contemptuous dismissal; the replacement preserves that register without adding meaning.

# Review

The draft contains several actionable fidelity issues involving disrespectful register, agency, causal logic, temporal meaning, and Mae Jonghak’s age comparison, plus two localized prose problems.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “산서잠룡 진태경이잖나. 어제 아는 사람한테 들었는데, 산서잠룡이 매종학 대협한테 이놈 저놈 하면서 목을 잡고 막, 어?”
- **Current:** “That’s the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Jin Taekyung. I heard from someone yesterday that the Sleeping Dragon grabbed Great Hero Mae Jonghak by the neck while calling him this and that, and then—huh?”
- **Defect:** “Calling him this and that” does not convey that Taekyung used offensively familiar forms such as 이놈 and 저놈 toward a revered elder, while the final “huh?” misreads the speaker’s trailing, suggestive 어.
- **Replacement:** “That’s the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Jin Taekyung. I heard from someone yesterday that he grabbed Great Hero Mae Jonghak by the neck and talked to him like he was some punk, and—well, you know?”
- **Rationale:** The gossip specifically emphasizes Taekyung’s shockingly disrespectful manner of address and physical handling of Mae Jonghak, which motivates the ensuing Mad Dragon joke and Taekyung’s embarrassment.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 금방 훌훌 털고 일어날 것 같았던 적천강이다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang was supposed to shake everything off and get back on his feet in no time.
- **Defect:** “Was supposed to” suggests an expectation or obligation imposed on Jeok Cheongang, whereas the source describes how readily Taekyung had imagined him recovering.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang had seemed like he would shake everything off and get back on his feet in no time.
- **Rationale:** The line expresses Taekyung’s prior impression of Jeok Cheongang’s resilience, not something Jeok Cheongang was required or scheduled to do.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘아니야. 별일 없겠지.’
- **Current:** *No. Nothing serious happened. He’ll be fine.*
- **Defect:** The first sentence changes a worried prediction about what will happen into a past-tense claim that nothing has happened, despite Jeok Cheongang already being gravely injured and unconscious.
- **Replacement:** *No. He’ll be all right.*
- **Rationale:** 별일 없겠지 is Taekyung reassuring himself that no further serious problem will arise and that Jeok Cheongang will recover.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하지만 희생된 것은 법왕 굉도와 소림사뿐이다.
- **Current:** “But all they sacrificed were Dharma King Hong Dao and Shaolin Temple. They chose clear targets and acted from the very beginning.”
- **Defect:** “They sacrificed” assigns the sacrifice to Dark Heaven as though Hong Dao and Shaolin were resources belonging to them. The source instead identifies Hong Dao and Shaolin as the only victims or losses caused by the attack.
- **Replacement:** “Yet only Dharma King Hong Dao and Shaolin Temple had paid the price. They chose clear targets and acted from the very beginning.”
- **Rationale:** The passive 희생된 것은 describes who suffered, not something Dark Heaven voluntarily gave up.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이런 식으로 모습을 드러냈다면 어느 정도 싸울 준비를 끝냈다는 이야긴데…… 대놓고 정체를 드러내면서 은자 대신 철전을 집어 간 느낌이에요.”
- **Current:** “If they were prepared enough to reveal themselves like that, then it feels like they openly announced who they were and picked up iron coins instead of silver nyang.”
- **Defect:** The conditional logic is reversed. Taekyung says that openly revealing themselves implies they had already made preparations to fight; the draft instead makes being prepared the condition that enabled the revelation.
- **Replacement:** “Coming out into the open like this means they must have made at least some preparations for a fight… Yet it feels like they openly revealed themselves only to pick up iron coins instead of silver nyang.”
- **Rationale:** The revelation is Taekyung’s evidence for inferred battle preparations, which supports his confusion over why Dark Heaven then settled for a comparatively limited target.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 매종학은 정마대전을 온몸으로 겪은 산증인이다.
- **Current:** Mae Jonghak was a living witness who had experienced the Great Faction War with his entire body.
- **Defect:** “Experienced … with his entire body” is an unnatural Korean-syntax calque in English.
- **Replacement:** Mae Jonghak was a living witness to the Great Faction War, having lived through it himself.
- **Rationale:** 온몸으로 겪은 emphasizes direct, personal experience; the replacement preserves that emphasis without the awkward bodily phrasing.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “풍이 그 아이가 열여덟 때 절정의 벽을 넘었지. 자네도 그 정도는 했을 것 같은데?”
- **Current:** “Pung broke through the wall to the Peak realm when he was eighteen. I imagine you’ve accomplished at least that much?”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Mae Jonghak’s comparison of what Taekyung had achieved by age eighteen into a question about his present accomplishments, weakening the setup for Taekyung’s answer about what he was doing “back then.” It also adds “at least,” which is absent.
- **Replacement:** “Pung broke through the wall to the Peak realm when he was eighteen. I’d have thought you had done about the same by that age.”
- **Rationale:** 자네도 그 정도는 했을 것 같은데 refers back to Cheongpung’s achievement at eighteen, as confirmed by Taekyung’s immediate reply about his own life at that age.

# Review

The draft contains several factual errors involving agency, direction, quantity, and organizational role, along with an omitted System-window transition and localized terminology/register issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 며칠 안에 초가삼간 다 태워 먹고, 집 잃은 동 노인께서 개방 입문 한다에 무진이 왼쪽 손목 걸겠습니다.
- **Current:** “Within a few days, he’ll burn down all three thatched cottages, and Old Man Dong will lose his home and join the Beggars’ Sect. Mujin will bet his left wrist on it.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents three separate cottages and changes the person making the wager: Taekyung bets Mujin’s wrist, not Mujin himself.
- **Replacement:** “Within a few days, he’ll burn this whole place down and leave Old Man Dong homeless—and I’ll bet Mujin’s left wrist the old man ends up joining the Beggars’ Sect.”
- **Rationale:** 초가삼간 refers to the whole modest thatched dwelling rather than three cottages, while 걸겠습니다 has Taekyung as the understood subject and 무진이 왼쪽 손목 as the stake.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뭔 거지발싸개 같은 소리 하고 있어. 일 생기면 발바닥에 불이 나도록 뛰어와야지.
- **Current:** “What kind of beggar’s-ass nonsense is that? If something happens, you need to run here so fast your feet catch fire.”
- **Defect:** Because Gung Gibang is being left at the clinic, telling him to run “here” reverses the intended direction. He is supposed to run to Taekyung’s party at the Tang Clan.

# Review

The draft contains two critical factual errors involving the party’s composition and a father-son relationship, plus several meaningful losses of mechanism, quantity, profanity, euphemism, and humor.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 지금까지 반강제로 진행되었던 몇몇 퀘스트와는 달리 이번에는 버젓이 선택지가 있었지만, 내게 있어 이건 거부할 수 없는 제안이나 마찬가지였다.
- **Current:** Unlike the several Quests that had forced me to proceed up until now, this one had an actual choice. But to me, it was no different from an offer I couldn’t refuse.
- **Defect:** The draft turns 반강제로, meaning that the earlier Quests were semi-forced or effectively compulsory, into unqualified force.
- **Replacement:** Unlike the several Quests I’d been more or less forced to undertake until now, this one offered me an actual choice. But to me, it was no different from an offer I couldn’t refuse.
- **Rationale:** 반강제로 explicitly qualifies the coercion rather than describing the earlier Quests as wholly forced.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters two quantities or subjects, misstates a martial-art attainment level, weakens several character beats, intensifies a nonlethal duel description, and uses an unsupported nickname romanization.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 잠시 후, 울음을 그친 아이의 양손에는 당과와 어른 손바닥만 한 목각 인형이 들려 있었다.
- **Current:** A short while later, the child held a piece of candy in each hand, along with a wooden doll about the size of an adult’s palm.
- **Defect:** The draft invents two pieces of candy and leaves the child holding three objects. The source only says that candy and a wooden doll were in his hands.
- **Replacement:** A short while later, the child held some candy and a wooden doll about the size of an adult’s palm in his hands.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the two named items without adding an unsupported quantity.

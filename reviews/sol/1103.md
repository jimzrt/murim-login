# Review

Three action and positioning errors, plus characterization and terminology defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 온 힘을 다해 손에 쥔 병장기를 흩뿌리면서.
- **Current:** then roared and flung their weapons with all their strength.
- **Defect:** “Flung their weapons” says the attackers let go of them; the source describes unleashing attacks with the weapons in their hands.
- **Replacement:** then roared and lashed out with the weapons in their hands, putting all their strength behind each blow.
- **Rationale:** 손에 쥔 keeps the weapons in their grasp during the attack.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 어느 때보다 강렬한 무아지경의 감각이 전신을 지배하고 있었다.
- **Current:** Now the sensation of No-self was more intense than ever, taking over my entire body.
- **Defect:** Uses the established term for 무아 rather than the distinct glossary term for 무아지경.
- **Replacement:** Now the sensation of Trance was more intense than ever, taking over my entire body.
- **Rationale:** The binding glossary distinguishes 무아지경 (“Trance”) from 무아 (“No-self”).

## F03 — Critical

- **Defect:** Changes getting to his feet into sitting up.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung struggled to his feet.

## F05 — Critical

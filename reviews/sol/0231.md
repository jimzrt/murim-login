# Review

Several subject, causal, register, quantity, and terminology errors require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아미타불, 자, 잠시 지나가도 되겠습니까?”
- **Current:** “Amitabha. E-excuse me, but might we pass through for a moment?”
- **Defect:** The monk asks permission for himself to pass, but the draft changes the singular subject to “we,” implying that he represents a group.
- **Replacement:** “Amitabha. Excuse me, but may I pass through for a moment?”
- **Rationale:** The scene identifies a single arriving monk, and the omitted Korean subject is resolved by his subsequent solitary movement through the swordsmen.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “자주 들었지.”
- **Current:** “I’ve been here often enough.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes hearing about the place into physically visiting it, altering Jeok Cheongang’s stated experience.
- **Replacement:** “I’ve heard plenty about it.”
- **Rationale:** 들었지 is from 듣다, “to hear”; “to stop by” would take a form such as 들렀지.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 반나절 동안 함께 이동하며 들은 바로는, 타고난 성격이 내성적이라 한 번 눈이 돌아 버리면 주체를 할 수 없다고 했다.
- **Current:** From what I had heard while traveling with him for half a day, he was naturally introverted. Once he lost control, however, he couldn’t rein himself in.
- **Defect:** Dividing the statement into two independent traits omits the source’s causal link between his innately introverted nature and his inability to control himself once he snaps.
- **Replacement:** From what I had heard during the half day we’d traveled together, he was so naturally introverted that once he snapped, he couldn’t control himself.
- **Rationale:** The connective -이라 explicitly presents his introversion as the reason he becomes uncontrollable once pushed past his limit.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “……허, 신통한 땡중 같으니. 앉아서 천 리를 내다보는 건 여전하군.”
- **Current:** “…Hah. That shameless old bastard hasn’t changed. He can still see a thousand li while sitting on his ass.”
- **Defect:** “Shameless” omits 신통한, which characterizes Hong Dao’s insight as uncanny or extraordinary, while “sitting on his ass” adds vulgarity not attached to 앉아서 in the source.
- **Replacement:** “…Hah. That damned monk is as uncanny as ever. He can still see a thousand li without leaving his seat.”
- **Rationale:** 신통한 conveys extraordinary, almost supernatural perceptiveness; 땡중 supplies the disparaging register without making the neutral act of sitting vulgar.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it reverses one characterization cue, omits a meaningful duty-related detail, prematurely completes a forgotten name, and mishandles the sole footnote.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 악불군은 겉모습처럼 그리 뛰어난 달변가는 아니었다.
- **Current:** Ak Bulgun was not as eloquent as his appearance suggested.
- **Defect:** This reverses the relationship between Ak Bulgun’s appearance and his speaking ability. The source says his unremarkable eloquence matches his outward appearance, not that his appearance suggested he would be eloquent.
- **Replacement:** As his appearance suggested, Ak Bulgun was not much of an orator.
- **Rationale:** 겉모습처럼 links his dry, unimpressive delivery to the impression given by his appearance.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그러니까 우리를 초청한다는 말이죠. 그 성, 성라…….”
- **Current:** “So you’re inviting us to the… Star-Array Grand Banquet.[^1]”
- **Defect:** Taekyung incorrectly finishes the event’s full name before immediately admitting that he cannot remember it. The source has him trail off after recalling only the beginning, allowing Jeok Cheongang to supply the name.
- **Replacement:** “So you’re inviting us to that… Star… Star-Array…”
- **Rationale:** The incomplete 성, 성라…… is the setup for Taekyung’s lapse of memory and Jeok Cheongang’s interjection.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 여기 놈들은 왜 다 하나같이 이 모양이냐? 소가주라는 놈은 가문의 중대사도 내팽개치고 네 녀석을 보러 들락거리지를 않나, 의원이라는 것들은 죄다 돌팔이라 죽네 사네 입방아만 찧어 대고.
- **Current:** “Why are all the people here like this? The kid who calls himself the Lesser Family Head keeps dropping everything to come see you, and those physicians are all quacks who do nothing but gossip about whether you’re going to live or die.”
- **Defect:** “Dropping everything” omits that Jin Wikyung is specifically neglecting major affairs of the family, weakening both his governing responsibility and the extent of his concern for Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** “Why is everyone here like this? That so-called Lesser Family Head keeps neglecting major family business to come by and see you, and those physicians are all quacks who do nothing but gossip about whether you’re going to live or die.”
- **Rationale:** 가문의 중대사 explicitly means important family affairs, a hierarchy- and characterization-bearing detail.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대기엔 충만한 기운이 느껴졌고 누군가 내 눈에 야시경을 심은 것처럼 사방이 환했다.
- **Current:** The air was filled with energy, and everything around me was so bright that it was as if someone had implanted night-vision goggles in my eyes.
- **Defect:** Murim 기운 is rendered generically as “energy,” contrary to the required qi terminology, and the experiential cue that Taekyung can sense it is flattened.
- **Replacement:** I could feel qi saturating the air, and everything around me was so bright that it was as if someone had implanted night-vision goggles in my eyes.
- **Rationale:** The scene demonstrates Taekyung’s altered perception after opening the vessels; 느껴졌고 indicates that he senses the ambient qi.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 지은 지 얼마 안 된 방 안에는 목재 특유의 냄새가 은은하게 감돌았다.
- **Current:** The faint scent unique to new wood lingered in the air.
- **Defect:** The draft transfers “new” to the wood and omits the explicit fact that the room itself was built recently.
- **Replacement:** The faint scent of wood lingered in the recently built room.
- **Rationale:** 지은 지 얼마 안 된 modifies 방, while 목재 특유 describes the characteristic scent of wood.

## F08 — Minor

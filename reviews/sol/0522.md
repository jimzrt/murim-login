# Review

One reversed time quantity, two meaningful subject or disclosure errors, and two localized fidelity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 달도 전에 홀로 뱃일을 하러 나갔다가 자취를 감추었다더군.
- **Current:** They say he went out alone to fish less than a month ago and vanished without a trace.
- **Defect:** The elapsed time is reversed. 한 달도 전에 means more than a month ago, not less than a month ago.
- **Replacement:** They say he went out alone to fish more than a month ago and vanished without a trace.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly places Jang Sam’s disappearance over a month earlier.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 유일하게 색이 들어간 붉은 눈은 사마외도(邪魔外道)라는 네 글자로 설명할 수 있는 종류가 아니었다.
- **Current:** Its eyes were the only part drawn in color, and their red hue could not be explained simply as *demonic, heterodox arts*.
- **Defect:** The draft says the color itself cannot be explained as martial arts, creating a category error. The source says the red eyes represented something beyond what the label 사마외도 could explain.
- **Replacement:** Its red eyes were the only part drawn in color, and they were beyond anything the four words *demonic, heterodox arts* could explain.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts the creature’s visibly unnatural eyes with an ordinary Murim explanation, not the color red with a type of martial art.

## F03 — Minor

- **Source:** 그리고 사실 그런 사정보다 더욱 주목해야 하는 것은, 흔하디흔한 장삼이사(張三李四) 중 하나였던 초로의 어부가 어쩌다 흉측한 괴물이 되어 살귀라 불렸냐는 거다.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “며칠 후면 알게 될 이야기요. 단, 그때까지는 새어 나가지 않게 조심해 주시오.”

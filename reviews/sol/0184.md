# Review

Two critical subject/negation errors, two meaningful omissions, one hierarchy error, and one altered incomplete question.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “철 소협, 모르시오?”
- **Current:** “Senior Chulwoo, you don’t know?”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the respectful martial address “Young Hero Chul” with “Senior Chulwoo,” incorrectly implying that the speaker recognizes Chulwoo as a hierarchical senior.
- **Replacement:** “Young Hero Chul, you don’t know?”
- **Rationale:** 소협 is the established address “Young Hero,” not “Senior”; preserving it matters because this exchange revolves around unrecognized sect hierarchy.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 차라리 가 봤으면 억울하지나 않지.

기루는 시발, 헌팅 술집도 못 가 봤는데.
- **Current:** It would have been unfair if I had actually gone to pleasure houses.

Hell, I couldn’t even go to a pickup bar.
- **Defect:** The first sentence reverses the source: Taekyung says he would not feel wronged if he had actually visited a pleasure house, not that visiting one would make the accusation unfair. The second sentence also turns a lack of past experience into a vague inability and softens the profanity.
- **Replacement:** If I’d actually been to one, at least I wouldn’t feel so wronged.

Fuck, I’d never even been to a pickup bar, let alone a pleasure house.
- **Rationale:** 가 봤으면 억울하지나 않지 negates his grievance under the hypothetical, while 못 가 봤는데 means he has never even experienced going to the less explicit venue.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 검성이 은거한 지 이미 수십 년이다.

살았는지, 죽었는지도 잘 알려지지 않은 그의 제자가 갑자기 무림에 나오다니?
- **Current:** The Sword Saint had been living in seclusion for several decades already.

And now his Disciple, whose life or death was barely known, had suddenly appeared in Murim?
- **Defect:** The draft attaches the unknown life-or-death status to the Disciple. The source says no one knows whether the long-secluded Sword Saint is alive or dead, making his Disciple’s sudden appearance startling.
- **Replacement:** The Sword Saint had already been living in seclusion for decades.

No one even knew whether he was alive or dead, and now his Disciple had suddenly appeared in Murim?
- **Rationale:** The contextual subject of 살았는지, 죽었는지도 잘 알려지지 않은 is the Sword Saint, whose decades-long seclusion is stated immediately beforehand.

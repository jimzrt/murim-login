# Review

One subject reversal, one misleading departure implication, and several localized losses of humor, register, and characterization.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그래서 말했을 텐데. 네놈 허락은 필요 없다고.”
- **Current:** “That is why I said you didn’t need permission.”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Mungyeong says that he does not need Taekyung’s permission, not that Taekyung does not need permission.
- **Replacement:** “Like I said, I don’t need your permission.”
- **Rationale:** 네놈 허락은 필요 없다 explicitly means that Mungyeong has no need for Taekyung’s permission.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 뭐여, 시벌.
- **Current:** *What the hell?*
- **Defect:** The draft sanitizes Taekyung’s profane reaction.
- **Replacement:** *What the fuck?*
- **Rationale:** 시벌 is a deliberately distorted but unmistakable form of strong profanity; “hell” weakens its register.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “무려 여섯이죠.”
- **Current:** “Six people is a lot.”
- **Defect:** The draft explains the joke rather than preserving Taekyung’s mock-grandiose phrasing and deadpan timing.
- **Replacement:** “A whole six.”
- **Rationale:** 무려 ironically emphasizes the supposedly impressive number of six; the concise phrasing carries the humor.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “아니, 왜 킹갓검성을 놔두고 굳이 성격도 더러운…… 죄송합니다. 말이 헛나왔네요.”
- **Current:** “Why would he leave the Sword Saint and deliberately learn from someone with such a foul temper—sorry. That came out wrong.”
- **Defect:** The plain title “Sword Saint” omits the comic, exaggerated praise in 킹갓검성.
- **Replacement:** “Why would he pass up the almighty Sword Saint and go out of his way to learn from someone with such a foul temper—sorry. That came out wrong.”
- **Rationale:** 킹갓 is humorous hyperbolic praise, contrasting the Sword Saint with Mungyeong immediately before Taekyung insults Mungyeong’s temperament.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “오래 살고 볼 일이군.”
- **Current:** “I have lived a long time.”
- **Defect:** The draft renders the idiom literally and loses Mungyeong’s surprise that he has witnessed something so unexpected.
- **Replacement:** “Well, now I’ve seen everything.”
- **Rationale:** 오래 살고 볼 일이다 is an idiomatic reaction to an astonishing or unprecedented development, not a factual statement about age.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그래도 마지막인데 얼굴 정도는 보러 와 주지.
- **Current:** Even so, it was the last time. Couldn’t they at least come to see my face?
- **Defect:** “The last time” implies a permanent final meeting, while the source means the last opportunity before Taekyung departs. The plural pronoun also obscures that he is waiting for Jeok Cheongang.
- **Replacement:** Still, this was the last chance before I left. Couldn’t he at least come see me?
- **Rationale:** 마지막인데 refers contextually to Taekyung’s imminent departure, and the preceding reflection identifies Jeok Cheongang as the person whose absence disappoints him.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** - 그러니 헛되이 죽지 말거라. 네놈이 남만에서 죽어 버리면, 내가 지금까지 참은 것이 아까워지니까.
- **Current:** —So don’t die a pointless death. If you die in Nanman, all the restraint I’ve shown until now will have been for nothing.
- **Defect:** “The restraint I’ve shown” suggests Mungyeong has refrained from acting, whereas 참은 것 means everything he has endured or put up with during Taekyung’s instruction.
- **Replacement:** —So don’t die a pointless death. If you die in Nanman, all that I’ve put up with until now will have been for nothing.
- **Rationale:** The line expresses Mungyeong’s gruff concern through the sunk cost of what he has endured, not through self-restraint.

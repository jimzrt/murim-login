# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s main action, but changes a kinship claim and several details of Jeok Cheongang’s memories and realization.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이곳에서 하나뿐인 피붙이가 죽었다고.
- **Current:** His only family in the world had died here.
- **Defect:** “Family” loses Jeok Cheongang’s specific claim that the dead man was his only blood relative.
- **Replacement:** His only blood relative had died here.
- **Rationale:** 피붙이 specifically means a blood relative.

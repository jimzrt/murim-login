# Review

Several lines change the degree of violence or lose cues in Jeok Cheongang’s threats and reunion with Jin.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그러면 어떨 것 같으냐?
- **Current:** So, what do you think would happen if a certain kind of story got around Murim right now?
- **Defect:** Jeok asks how he would feel, not what would happen. Tang Sadok’s answer about Jeok being displeased responds to the former.
- **Replacement:** So, how do you think I’d feel if a certain kind of story got around Murim right now?
- **Rationale:** The following answer, “불쾌하실 것 같습니다,” identifies Jeok’s feelings as the question’s subject.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 석 달 뒤에 갑자기 제 선친을 두들겨 패셨다고…….
- **Current:** then suddenly beat my father half to death three months later…
- **Defect:** “Half to death” adds an unreported degree of injury.
- **Replacement:** then suddenly beat my father up three months later…
- **Rationale:** 두들겨 패셨다고 says Jeok beat his father up; it does not say the beating nearly killed him.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그러나 그와는 반대로 애정과 반가움이 묻어나오는 그의 눈빛을.
- **Current:** His eyes, however, were filled with affection and relief.
- **Defect:** “Relief” replaces the pleasure of seeing Jin again.
- **Replacement:** His eyes, however, held affection and joy at seeing me again.
- **Rationale:** 반가움 conveys gladness at a reunion, not relief that a danger has passed.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 엄살떨지 말고 냉큼 일어나지 못하겠느냐.
- **Current:** Stop acting like a coward and get up already.
- **Defect:** Jeok accuses Jin of making a fuss, not cowardice.
- **Replacement:** Stop making such a fuss and get up already.
- **Rationale:** 엄살떨다 means exaggerating one’s pain or distress; “coward” changes the character of the rebuke.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그들은 서로가 가진 것을 필요로 했기에 맺어졌고, 그렇기에 구배지례는 아무런 의미도 없는 허례허식에 불과했다.
- **Current:** They’d come together because each needed what the other had. And so the nine-bow ritual was nothing but an empty formality, meaningless to either of them.
- **Defect:** “To either of them” adds a claim about both people’s feelings that the narration does not make.
- **Replacement:** They’d come together because each needed what the other had. And so the nine-bow ritual was nothing but meaningless ceremony.
- **Rationale:** The source calls the ritual meaningless without assigning that judgment separately to both characters; Jin immediately admits that Jeok’s dismissal stings.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 언제 맺혔는지 모를 식은땀 한 방울이 당사독의 목덜미를 타고 굴러떨어졌다.
- **Current:** A bead of cold sweat rolled down Tang Sadok’s neck.
- **Defect:** The draft drops the cue that the sweat appeared without Tang Sadok noticing when.
- **Replacement:** A bead of cold sweat he hadn’t even noticed forming rolled down Tang Sadok’s neck.
- **Rationale:** 언제 맺혔는지 모를 makes his involuntary fear more immediate.

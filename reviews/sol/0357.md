# Review

The draft contains several material fidelity errors, including an inserted negation, two incorrect distance conversions, a reversal of who was ahead in Cheongpung’s recollection, and a technique’s power level being mistaken for its stage.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그는 홀로 이질적인 존재였고, 사천당문을 가득 메운 죽음의 기운이 범접할 수 있는 유일한 사람이었다.
- **Current:** He alone seemed out of place—the only person the deathly energy filling the Sichuan Tang Clan could not touch.
- **Defect:** The draft inserts a negation absent from the source, reversing whether the aura of death can touch him.
- **Replacement:** He alone stood apart—the only person whom the aura of death filling the Sichuan Tang Clan could touch.
- **Rationale:** The source says 범접할 수 있는, “could approach/touch,” not 범접할 수 없는, “could not approach/touch.”

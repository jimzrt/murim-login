# Review

Three subject and action reversals materially invert the confrontation; several localized phrases also misstate emotional or physical cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “허어, 당장 물러서지 않으면 실격패를 당할 터인데?”
- **Current:** “Good grief. If I don’t withdraw immediately, I’ll be disqualified.”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Jongni Chu is warning Taekyung that Taekyung will be disqualified, not saying that he himself will be disqualified.
- **Replacement:** “Good grief. If you don’t withdraw immediately, you’ll be disqualified.”
- **Rationale:** The following exchange confirms that Taekyung’s disqualification would hand Jongni Chu an easy victory.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그럼 손속에 사정을 둬야겠구려.”

“제자라는 놈을 너무 물로 보는 것 아닌가?”

“기대는 되지만 아마 내 상대는 안 될 거요.”
- **Current:** “Then I suppose you’ll have to show some mercy.”

“Are you underestimating that so-called Disciple of yours?”

“I’m looking forward to it, but I probably won’t be able to match him.”
- **Defect:** The subjects and matchup are repeatedly reversed. Jongni Chu says that he will go easy on Jeok Cheongang’s Disciple; Jeok asks whether Jongni Chu is underestimating his Disciple; Jongni Chu then says the Disciple will probably not be a match for him.
- **Replacement:** “Then I suppose I’ll have to go easy on him.”

“Are you underestimating my Disciple?”

“I’m looking forward to it, but he probably won’t be a match for me.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean establishes Jongni Chu as the prospective killer and Taekyung as the opponent he confidently expects to defeat. The draft instead makes Jeok responsible for showing mercy and has Jongni Chu predict his own inferiority.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그것은 지금으로부터 일 년 전, 처음으로 기감을 감지해 냈던 한 사람에 대한 기억이었다.
- **Current:** A memory from a year ago, when I had first sensed Qi in another person.
- **Defect:** The sensing action and its subject are reversed. The memory concerns the first person who detected Taekyung’s Qi Sense, not Taekyung first sensing another person’s qi.
- **Replacement:** It was a memory from a year ago, of the first person who had ever detected my Qi Sense.
- **Rationale:** The next paragraph identifies Jeok Cheongang as that person and explains that the encounter first showed Taekyung the System was not omnipotent.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그는 예기치 못한 돌발 사태에 당황한 기색이 역력했다.
- **Current:** His embarrassment at the unexpected situation was obvious.
- **Defect:** 당황 describes being visibly flustered or disconcerted, not embarrassed.
- **Replacement:** He was visibly flustered by the unexpected turn of events.
- **Rationale:** The judge is reacting to a sudden disruption of the duel rather than experiencing social embarrassment.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “내 안목이 정확했어. 자네는 역시 재미있는 친구일세.”
- **Current:** “My eye was accurate. You really are an interesting friend.”
- **Defect:** “My eye was accurate” is an unnatural literal rendering of a remark about having judged someone correctly.
- **Replacement:** “I was right about you. You really are an interesting friend.”
- **Rationale:** 안목이 정확했다 means that Jongni Chu’s assessment of Taekyung proved correct, not that his physical eyesight was accurate.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 적천강이 가라앉은 눈빛으로 종리추를 노려봤다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang glared at Jongni Chu with sunken eyes.
- **Defect:** “Sunken eyes” describes hollow physical eye sockets, while 가라앉은 눈빛 describes a grave, subdued, or heavy gaze.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang fixed Jongni Chu with a grave, steady glare.
- **Rationale:** The source marks the controlled seriousness of Jeok Cheongang’s expression rather than changing his physical appearance.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 기감의 경지가 팔 성에 이르자 한 사람에게만 집중할 수 있게 되었다.
- **Current:** Once Qi Sense reached the eighth stage, I was able to focus on only one person.
- **Defect:** The placement of “only” makes the upgrade sound like a restriction limiting Qi Sense to one person.
- **Replacement:** Once Qi Sense reached the eighth stage, I could focus it entirely on a single person.
- **Rationale:** The Korean describes gaining the ability to concentrate Qi Sense selectively on one target, not losing the ability to sense more than one person.

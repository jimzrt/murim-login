# Review

The draft contains one reversed mechanical explanation, several altered quantities or relationships, and localized omissions/additions affecting characterization and action clarity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 진즉 잿가루가 되고도 남았을 새하얀 손에는 강대한 기운이 집약되어 있었다.
- **Current:** The powerful energy concentrated within her snow-white hands should have been enough to turn them to ash long ago.
- **Defect:** The sentence reverses the mechanism: the source says her hands should already have been reduced to ash, yet tremendous energy was concentrated within them. The draft instead makes that energy the apparent cause that should have burned them.
- **Replacement:** Her snow-white hands, which should have been reduced to ash long ago, held a tremendous concentration of energy.
- **Rationale:** The relative clause modifies the hands, not the concentrated energy.

# Review

Six actionable fidelity defects: two reversed subjects/actions, one mistranslated insult, one lost wordplay, and one altered emotional fact.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 검게 그을린 채 분노하는 신화 속 마수를, 반백의 노집사는 담담한 눈빛으로 바라보았다.
- **Current:** The old butler, his hair singed half-black, calmly gazed at the enraged mythical monster.
- **Defect:** The draft attaches the blackened state to Kim Hwajong’s hair, but the monster is the one charred black; 반백 describes the old butler’s graying hair.
- **Replacement:** The mythical monster raged, its body charred black, while the gray-haired old butler calmly gazed at it.
- **Rationale:** The source assigns 검게 그을린 채 to the mythical monster and 반백 to the old butler, so the draft reverses the physical descriptions of the two figures.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 노. 옴!
- **Current:** —Old. Man!
- **Defect:** 놈 is a contemptuous insult such as “wretch” or “bastard,” not a reference to the target’s age.
- **Replacement:** —You. Wretch!
- **Rationale:** The segmented utterance stretches 놈 for emphasis; translating it as “Old Man” changes Behemoth’s insult and register.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 굉음과 함께 단단한 암석에 틀어박힌 등이 부서지고, 막을 수 없는 무언가가 내부 깊숙한 곳에서부터 솟구쳐 올랐다.
- **Current:** His back slammed into solid rock with a thunderous crash and broke through it. Something unstoppable surged up from deep inside him.
- **Defect:** The draft says Kim Hwajong’s back broke through the rock, but the source says his back itself broke after being driven into the rock.
- **Replacement:** With a thunderous crash, his back smashed into solid rock and broke. Something unstoppable surged up from deep inside him.
- **Rationale:** 등이 is the subject of 부서지고; the injury is to his back, not the rock.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘다행히 안 꺼져도 되겠군. 자네와 여기 있는 사람들을 도와줄 거거든.’
- **Current:** *“Fortunately, I don’t have to leave. I’m here to help you and the people here.”*
- **Defect:** The draft loses the joke linking Kim Hwajong’s command 꺼지쇼 (“get lost”) with 꺼지다 meaning for a flame to “go out.”
- **Replacement:** *“Fortunately, I won’t have to ‘go out,’ then. I’m going to help you and everyone here.”*[^1]

[^1]: The same verb means both “get lost” and for a flame to “go out.”
- **Rationale:** Cheon Taemin deliberately echoes the preceding verb to make a fire-related pun while announcing that he will help.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘그럼 길드에 들어와라. 네가 필요해. 정룡이와 천우도 받아들였다.’
- **Current:** *“Then join the Guild. We need you. Jungryong and Cheonwoo have accepted too.”*
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jungryong and Cheonwoo the people who accepted an offer. In the source, Cheon Taemin says that he accepted or brought them in as well.
- **Replacement:** *“Then join the Guild. I need you. I’ve brought Jungryong and Cheonwoo in too.”*
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 받아들였다 continues to be Cheon Taemin; 정룡이와 천우 are the people he accepted, not the actors who accepted something.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘분명 오늘은…… 내 인생 최고의 날이었는데.’
- **Current:** *Today was supposed to be the best day of my life…*
- **Defect:** “Was supposed to be” makes the thought counterfactual, whereas Kim Hwajong affirms that today truly was the best day of his life despite its tragic end.
- **Replacement:** *Today really was… the best day of my life.*
- **Rationale:** 분명 and 이었는데 establish the day as genuinely his best, with the trailing contrast arising from his present death rather than from the day failing to meet an expectation.

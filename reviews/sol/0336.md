# Review

The draft contains two subject/action errors, an omitted exchange, a mistranslated insult, and repeated use of a name that conflicts with the binding glossary.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “맞습니다. 숙부님. 검성 매종학 대협께서 보내신…….”
- **Current:** “Yes, Uncle. It was sent by Great Hero Mae Jonghak, the—”
- **Defect:** The draft adds an unfinished appositive after Mae Jonghak’s name, while the source trails off after saying that he sent the letter.
- **Replacement:** “Yes, Uncle. It was sent by Great Hero Mae Jonghak…”
- **Rationale:** 보내신 is the interrupted verb phrase “sent by,” not the beginning of another title or description.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 자신의 조카를 말 몇 마디로 매몰차게 내쫓은 당사독이 가죽 의자에 비스듬히 몸을 기댔다.
- **Current:** Tang Sadok had driven away his nephew with only a few words. Then he leaned sideways against a leather chair.
- **Defect:** The draft omits 매몰차게, losing the deliberate coldness with which Tang Sadok dismisses his own nephew, and “leaned sideways against” is an awkward rendering of reclining in the chair.
- **Replacement:** After coldly driving his own nephew out with a few words, Tang Sadok reclined in a leather chair.
- **Rationale:** 매몰차게 explicitly characterizes the dismissal as cold and heartless, while 몸을 기댔다 describes resting or reclining against the chair.

- **Defect:** 재수 없는 놈 means an obnoxious or irritating bastard here, not someone who was unlucky. The draft changes Tang Sadok’s contempt into a comment about Jin Baekyang’s fortune.

# Review

The draft contains one reversed subject, two altered action/participant details, and several localized terminology and prose issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 며칠 전, 내가 장강 찍먹 형벌을 내린 뒤 곧바로 로그아웃한 덕분에 장장 한 시진 동안 장강 아쿠아리움을 구경한 혁무진이다.
- **Current:** A few days earlier, after I had sentenced him to the Yangtze-dipping punishment, Hyuk Mujin had immediately logged out. As a result, he had spent an entire shichen sightseeing at the Yangtze Aquarium.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the subject of the Logout action. Taekyung logged out after imposing the punishment; Hyuk Mujin remained behind in the Yangtze for a full shichen.
- **Replacement:** A few days earlier, after sentencing him to the Yangtze-dipping punishment, I had immediately logged out, leaving Hyuk Mujin to spend an entire shichen sightseeing at the Yangtze Aquarium.
- **Rationale:** The explicit subject of 로그아웃한 is 내가, and 덕분에 causally connects Taekyung’s departure to Hyuk Mujin’s prolonged punishment.

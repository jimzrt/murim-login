# Review

The draft has several actionable errors in object identity, subject attribution, rank, and the final assessment of the qi exchange.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내 이름으로 된 호패는 구경도 못 해 봤고 앞으로도 만들 생각조차 없지만
- **Current:** I’d never even seen a household register with my name on it, and had no intention of getting one.
- **Defect:** A 호패 is a personal identity tag, not a household register.
- **Replacement:** I’d never even seen an identity tag bearing my name, and had no intention of getting one.
- **Rationale:** The narrator is referring to an individual identification object that he has neither obtained nor plans to obtain.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 무수한 숙청 속에서 간신히 살아남아, 유일하게 곁에 남은 어느 환관과 산서성으로 떠나야 했던 어린아이의 모습만을 기억하고 있던 무소불위의 권력자도.
- **Current:** And so had that all-powerful man who’d barely survived countless purges, remembering only the child who’d had to leave for Shanxi Province with the one eunuch who’d remained at his side.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the all-powerful man the survivor of the purges; the child is the one who survived and left with the eunuch.
- **Replacement:** Perhaps even the all-powerful man who remembered him only as a child who had barely survived countless purges and been forced to leave for Shanxi Province with the sole eunuch left at his side had underestimated him.
- **Rationale:** The Korean modifiers describing survival and departure belong to 어린아이, the child remembered by the powerful man.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 가진 힘의 절반.

아니, 그 절반의 반도 되지 않는 기세로 벌인 신경전.
- **Current:** The aura he’d used to provoke me contained half his power.

No—less than half of that.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the measured power specifically to Baek Yeon and states that his provoking aura contained half his power. The source leaves the possessor unstated and corrects the estimate to less than a quarter.

No—not even a quarter of it had gone into that contest of auras.
- **Rationale:** The Korean describes the limited force used in their contest without identifying whose total strength is being measured.

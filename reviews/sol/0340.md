# Review

One critical reversal, two meaningful omissions, and several localized fidelity and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 나도 안다. 궁기방과 혁무진이 아니라, 내가 직접 갔어도 묘령사태를 막을 수 없었을 거라는 것을.
- **Current:** I knew. If Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin had gone in my place, they would not have been able to stop Venerable Myoryeong either.
- **Defect:** The substitution is reversed. Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin actually went; Taekyung reflects that even if he himself had gone instead of them, he could not have stopped her.
- **Replacement:** I knew. Even if I had gone myself instead of Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin, I wouldn’t have been able to stop Venerable Myoryeong.
- **Rationale:** 내가 직접 갔어도 makes Taekyung the hypothetical person going in their place, not the reverse.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “가주이신 만독수라 당사독 대협은 복수를 천명하셨고, 지금쯤 가솔들과 함께 놈을 추격 중일 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “The Family Head, Great Hero Tang Sadok, declared that he would have his revenge. By now, he should be pursuing the culprit with his family.”
- **Defect:** The established epithet Myriad-Poison Asura is omitted, and 가솔들 refers to members of his household rather than simply his blood family.
- **Replacement:** “The Family Head, Great Hero Tang Sadok—the Myriad-Poison Asura—declared that he would have his revenge. By now, he should be pursuing the culprit with the members of his household.”
- **Rationale:** 만독수라 is an explicit established title, while 가솔 denotes the people belonging to and serving within a household.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 현재 문파의 상당수가 빠져나간 사천당문과 청성파는 무주공산(無主空山)과 같다. 만일을 대비해 경고해 줘야 한다.
- **Current:** With most of their sect members away, the Sichuan Tang Clan and the Qingcheng Sect were like ownerless mountains. We had to warn them in case something happened.
- **Defect:** 상당수 is strengthened from a substantial portion to most, while the literal rendering “ownerless mountains” obscures the idiom’s point that both sects have been left virtually undefended.
- **Replacement:** With a substantial portion of their members away, the Sichuan Tang Clan and the Qingcheng Sect had both been left virtually undefended. We had to warn them in case something happened.
- **Rationale:** The source gives a limited quantity and uses 무주공산 idiomatically for an open, undefended place.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 중년의 여승, 묘령사태(卯嶺師太)는 파리한 안색으로 힘겹게 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** The middle-aged nun, Venerable Myoryeong, struggled to open her pale lips.
- **Defect:** The source describes Myoryeong’s complexion as pallid; the draft transfers that detail to her lips.
- **Replacement:** The middle-aged nun, Venerable Myoryeong, struggled to speak, her face pallid.
- **Rationale:** 파리한 안색 modifies her complexion, while 입을 열었다 means she began to speak.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘성격이 아주 화통했다. 적들 앞에서는 물불 가리지 않았고 무공도 뛰어났어. 굉도, 그 게으름뱅이 땡중보다도 나을 때가 있었지.’
- **Current:** She had a very forthright personality. She never hesitated before an enemy, and her martial arts were outstanding. There were even times when she was better than Hong Dao, that lazy bald monk.
- **Defect:** “Never hesitated” softens 물불 가리지 않았다, which characterizes her as stopping at nothing against her enemies.
- **Replacement:** She had a very forthright personality. She stopped at nothing against her enemies, and her martial arts were outstanding. There were even times when she was better than Hong Dao, that lazy bald monk.
- **Rationale:** 물불 가리지 않다 conveys reckless or uncompromising action, not merely an absence of hesitation.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 품에 끌어안은 여승의 무게는 놀랄 만큼 가벼웠고, 맞닿은 몸을 통해 느껴지는 기운은 위태로웠다.
- **Current:** The nun in my arms was astonishingly light, while the energy I felt through our contact was dangerously unstable.
- **Defect:** The draft describes her energy as unstable, suggesting erratic behavior, whereas the source says the qi he senses is in a precarious state.
- **Replacement:** The nun in my arms was astonishingly light, and the qi I felt through the contact between our bodies was perilously faint.
- **Rationale:** 위태로웠다 describes her life or qi as endangered and precarious; it does not indicate fluctuating or unstable energy.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 벌어진 경천동지(驚天動地)할 전투. 봉우리가 무너지고 아미산이 몸을 떨었다고 했다.
- **Current:** Then a battle that shook the heavens and earth had erupted. Peaks collapsed, and Mount Emei itself trembled.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular 봉우리, one mountain peak, into multiple peaks.
- **Replacement:** Then a battle that shook heaven and earth had erupted. A mountain peak collapsed, and Mount Emei itself trembled.
- **Rationale:** 봉우리가 is singular in this description; multiplying the collapsed peaks alters the scale of the battle.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 정확히 어떤 치료를 했는지는 모르지만, 수적들의 말에 의하면 제법 실력도 뛰어난 수준이다.
- **Current:** I did not know exactly what kind of treatment he had provided, but according to the bandits, he had been quite skilled.
- **Defect:** The generic “bandits” drops the source’s identification of them as river bandits.
- **Replacement:** I did not know exactly what kind of treatment he had provided, but according to the river bandits, he was quite skilled.
- **Rationale:** 수적 specifically means river bandits, preserving the connection to the Swift Tide and its crew.

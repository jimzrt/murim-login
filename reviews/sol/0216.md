# Review

Several fidelity and register issues, including a reversed Wyvern reference and Hunter energy rendered as Murim qi.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 녀석은 온통 새카만 흑색으로 뒤덮여 있었다. 반면 지금 상공에서 배회하는 저 와이번의 몸통은 환한 녹색이다.
- **Current:** This one was covered entirely in pitch-black scales. By contrast, the Wyvern circling overhead now had a bright green body.
- **Defect:** “This one” incorrectly identifies the current Green Wyvern as black; the source refers to the Wyvern from three years ago.
- **Replacement:** That one had been pitch-black all over. By contrast, the Wyvern circling overhead now had a bright green body.
- **Rationale:** The contrast between 녀석 and 지금 상공에서 배회하는 저 와이번 distinguishes the past black Wyvern from the current green one.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 촘촘한 근육 위로 핏줄이 불끈 솟고, 막대한 기운이 그의 창을 향해 물밀 듯이 흘러 들어갔다.
- **Current:** Veins bulged over his dense muscles, and an immense force flowed toward his spear like a surging tide.
- **Defect:** “Force” obscures that Won is channeling Hunter-system energy into the spear.
- **Replacement:** Veins bulged over his dense muscles, and an immense amount of mana surged into his spear like a rising tide.
- **Rationale:** In this modern Hunter context, 기운 is mana, and the flow into the spear is the mechanism producing Aura.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그것은 창기(槍氣). 현대에서는 오러(Aura)라 불리는 강력한 기의 집약체였다.
- **Current:** It was Spear Energy—a powerful concentration of qi known as Aura in the modern world.
- **Defect:** “Qi” incorrectly applies Murim terminology to modern Hunter energy.
- **Replacement:** It was Spear Energy—a powerful concentration of mana known as Aura in the modern world.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly frames this as the modern-world phenomenon called Aura; the terminology rules reserve qi for Murim energy and mana for the Hunter system.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이러다가 오촌 당숙 이름까지 나오겠다.
- **Current:** At this rate, he was going to start calling out the name of some distant uncle.
- **Defect:** The generic “distant uncle” omits the deliberately specific and increasingly remote kinship relation used for the joke.
- **Replacement:** At this rate, he would start calling out his father’s cousin next.
- **Rationale:** 오촌 당숙 specifically denotes one’s father’s male cousin, and that precise remoteness drives the escalation.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “랭커는 무슨. 다 옛날 얘기지.”
- **Current:** “Ranker, my ass. That was a long time ago.”
- **Defect:** The translation adds profanity to a lightly self-deprecating dismissal, intensifying Won’s public register.
- **Replacement:** “What ranker? That was a long time ago.”
- **Rationale:** 랭커는 무슨 dismisses the compliment but contains no profanity.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “저 자식이 원래 그래. 가볍고 덤벙거리는 성격이라 계약서도 잘못 보내고…….”
- **Current:** “That guy’s always like that. He’s lighthearted and clumsy, so he even sends contracts to the wrong place…”
- **Defect:** “To the wrong place” invents a destination error; the source only says he sends the wrong contract or sends a contract incorrectly, preserving the precise mistake as unstated.
- **Replacement:** “That guy’s always like that. He’s flighty and careless enough to even send the wrong contract…”
- **Rationale:** 계약서도 잘못 보내고 leaves the nature of the sending mistake implicit; the added destination narrows the mechanism without support.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 7성 호텔 코스 요리를 예약했는데 명태 순살 조림과 육고기 비빔 소스가 나온 기분이랄까.
- **Current:** It was like reserving a seven-star hotel course meal and being served braised boneless pollock and a meat-mixing sauce.
- **Defect:** “Meat-mixing sauce” is an unnatural literal calque that fails to identify the Korean dish-style sauce.
- **Replacement:** It was like reserving a seven-star hotel course meal and being served braised boneless pollock and beef bibim sauce.
- **Rationale:** 비빔 소스 is a bibim-style sauce, not generically a sauce used for mixing meat; retaining “bibim” preserves the food reference naturally.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘내 물건은 소중하니까.’
- **Current:** *My possessions are precious.*
- **Defect:** The formal plural “possessions” erases the singular euphemistic double meaning that sets up the sexual joke.
- **Replacement:** *My stuff is precious.*
- **Rationale:** 물건 is deliberately euphemistic here, referring both to the necklace and his anatomy without becoming explicit.

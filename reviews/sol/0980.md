The draft reverses who previously met Jeok Cheongang and who has yet to speak, and loses several cues about identity, hierarchy, and forced cheerfulness.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 적천강이야 내가 태원진가에 머무를 때 본 적이 있으니 그렇다 치더라도, 궁성의 존재감은 아직 심신이 미약한 진무경에게 큰 충격을 선사한 것이 틀림없었다.
- **Current:** I’d met Jeok Cheongang back when I was staying with the Jin Family of Taiyuan, so that was one thing. But the Bow Saint’s presence must have come as a major shock to Jin Mukyung, whose mind and body were still weak.
- **Defect:** Makes Taekyung the person who had seen Jeok, rather than Mukyung. That reverses why Jeok’s presence is less surprising to Mukyung.
- **Replacement:** Jin Mukyung had seen Jeok Cheongang while I was staying with the Jin Family of Taiyuan, so that was one thing. But the Bow Saint’s presence must have come as a major shock to him, weak as he still was.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 본 적이 있으니 is Jin Mukyung, whose reaction the sentence explains.

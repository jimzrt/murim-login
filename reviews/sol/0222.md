# Review

Several scene facts and combat mechanics are altered, and some jokes and register shifts are obscured by literal or unnatural phrasing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 집채만 한 괴수의 머리.
- **Current:** The head of a monster as large as a house.
- **Defect:** The English makes the monster itself house-sized, while the source specifically says its head is as large as a house.
- **Replacement:** The monster’s house-sized head.
- **Rationale:** 집채만 한 directly modifies 괴수의 머리, establishing the size of the head rather than the overall creature.

## F02 — Critical

- **Current:** That was the entirety of my preparation. This spear was my magic, and there was no way it could stop me.
- **Defect:** The object of Carus’s inability to stop is changed from the spear to Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** That was the entirety of my preparation. This spear was my magic, and the creature would never be able to stop it.
- **Rationale:** 이 창 is the antecedent of what 놈 cannot stop; the source describes the spear’s unstoppable force, not merely Taekyung’s advance.

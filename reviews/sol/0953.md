# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes one named subject and several meaningful cues in the villagers’ dialogue and narration.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진태경을 곁눈질하던 늙은 목수가 허허 웃었다.
- **Current:** The old carpenter, who had been glancing sideways at Jin Wikyung, chuckled.
- **Defect:** Changes the named person the carpenter glances at from Jin Taekyung to Jin Wikyung.
- **Replacement:** The old carpenter, who had been glancing sideways at Jin Taekyung, chuckled.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly names 진태경, even though the surrounding scene focuses on Jin Wikyung.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘이미 우리 마을 사람들도 동의했습니다. 오랑캐 놈들 주둥이로 쌀 한 톨이라도 들어갔다간 내가 찢겨 죽을 판입니다.’
- **Current:** “Everyone in our village has already agreed. If even one grain of rice gets into those barbarian bastards’ mouths, I’ll die by the time they’ve finished tearing me apart.”
- **Defect:** The English makes the barbarians appear to be the people who would tear the speaker apart; the Korean leaves the threatened attackers unstated.
- **Replacement:** “Everyone in our village has already agreed. If even one grain of rice gets into those barbarian bastards’ mouths, I’ll be torn to pieces.”
- **Rationale:** 내가 찢겨 죽을 판입니다 expresses what would happen to the speaker without assigning the action to the barbarians.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘차라리 소나 돼지한테 쌀밥을 처먹이고 말지, 내 눈에 흙이 들어가도 그 꼴은 못 보겠소!’
- **Current:** “I’d sooner feed rice to pigs and cows. Even if they buried my eyes in dirt, I wouldn’t stand for that!”
- **Defect:** Treats an idiom meaning death as an action someone performs on the speaker.
- **Replacement:** “I’d sooner feed rice to pigs and cows. I wouldn’t stand for that even after I’m dead!”
- **Rationale:** 내 눈에 흙이 들어가도 invokes the speaker being in the grave; it does not introduce people burying dirt in his eyes.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 착한 오랑캐는 죽은 오랑캐뿐이라는, 누군가가 들었다면 깜짝 놀랐을 이야기를 하면서.
- **Current:** They said things they’d have been shocked to hear from anyone else: the only good barbarian was a dead one.
- **Defect:** Changes who would be shocked: the draft says the villagers would be shocked, while the source points to an unspecified listener.
- **Replacement:** All while saying that the only good barbarian was a dead one—words that would have shocked someone if they’d heard them.
- **Rationale:** 누군가가 들었다면 refers to someone hearing the villagers’ words, not the villagers hearing the same words from others.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 진천검이라는 별호에서 비롯된 그의 수하들 역시도.
- **Current:** So did the men under him, named for his title, the Heaven Shaking Sword.
- **Defect:** Suggests the men themselves bear his title rather than serving in a squad named after his epithet.
- **Replacement:** So did his men, whose squad took its name from his epithet, the Heaven Shaking Sword.
- **Rationale:** The name derives from Jin Mukyung’s 별호; it is not each subordinate’s title.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘내가 한 번 먹어 봤어. 한 됫박 마셨다가 뒈지는 줄 알았소.’
- **Current:** “I tried it once. Drank a whole doe of the stuff. Thought I was going to die.”
- **Defect:** Leaves a Korean volume measure unexplained, obscuring how much foul water he drank.
- **Replacement:** “I tried it once. Drank nearly half a gallon of the stuff. Thought I was going to die.”
- **Rationale:** A 됫박 is roughly 1.8 liters, close to half a US gallon; the amount is part of the punchline.

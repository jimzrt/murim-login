# Review

Several consequential errors alter the injury, the prince’s pledge, and who authorizes the execution. Other findings concern omitted relationships and pragmatic cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 쩍 갈라진 목젖 사이로 피가래가 끓는다.
- **Current:** Blood and phlegm bubbled up through his neatly severed windpipe.
- **Defect:** The draft changes a split throat into a neatly severed windpipe.
- **Replacement:** Blood and phlegm bubbled through the gaping wound in his throat.
- **Rationale:** The source describes blood and phlegm bubbling through his split throat, not a severed windpipe.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** “또한, 그 어떤 불행의 씨앗도 뿌리지 않겠소. 설령 그것이 어떠한 결과로 되돌아온다 해도, 오롯이 감내하고 거둬들이겠소.”
- **Current:** “And I will sow no seeds of misfortune. Whatever consequences may return to me, I will bear them and reap what I have sown.”
- **Defect:** “Reap what I have sown” implies accepting the results of harmful seeds he planted, rather than accepting whatever consequences follow from his refusal to plant them.
- **Replacement:** “And I will sow no seeds of misfortune. Whatever consequences that choice brings, I will bear them and take responsibility for them.”
- **Rationale:** His pledge is to refrain from causing misfortune even if that choice has consequences for him.

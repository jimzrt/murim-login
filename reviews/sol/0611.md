# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter a subject, a causal condition, and Chuck Hagel’s inclusive stance; the draft also omits the protagonist’s prior decision, drops the Arch Lich rank, and softens a deliberately vulgar joke.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 몬스터긴 한데, 소격변 때 아크 리치에게서 제 목숨을 구해 줬습니다.
- **Current:** He is a monster, but he saved my life from a Lich during the Small Cataclysm.
- **Defect:** The draft drops “Arch” from “Arch Lich,” removing the monster’s stated rank.
- **Replacement:** He is a monster, but he saved my life from an Arch Lich during the Small Cataclysm.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly says 아크 리치, not merely 리치.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 인종 차별을 넘어 종족 차별도 인정하는 척 헤이글의 한마디에 가슴이 웅장해졌다.
- **Current:** My chest swelled at Chuck Hagel’s declaration that went beyond racial discrimination and embraced species discrimination as well.
- **Defect:** “Embraced species discrimination” says Chuck supports discriminatory treatment, reversing the point of his declaration that even bone color does not matter.
- **Replacement:** My chest swelled at Chuck Hagel’s declaration of equality that transcended race and extended even across species.
- **Rationale:** Chuck’s preceding line rejects distinctions based on either skin or bone color; the narration celebrates that unusually broad acceptance.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 맞는 말이다. 설령 테러가 성공했다면 모를까, 알 카에다가 굳이 아까운 헌터와 마정석을 버려 가며 병신 인증 자살 쇼를 보여 줄 이유는 없었다.
- **Current:** He was right.

Even if the terrorist attack had succeeded, there was no reason for Al-Qaeda to throw away valuable Hunters and Magic Gems just to put on a suicidal demonstration of their own stupidity.
- **Defect:** “Even if” reverses the conditional logic. The source says success might have made the sacrifice explicable, whereas the actual failed attacks gave Al-Qaeda no reason to waste those resources.
- **Replacement:** He was right.

It might have been a different story if the terrorist attacks had succeeded, but Al-Qaeda had no reason to waste valuable Hunters and Magic Gems on a suicide show that only proved how stupid they were.
- **Rationale:** 성공했다면 모를까 means that success would be the possible exception to the conclusion that the attacks made no sense for Al-Qaeda.

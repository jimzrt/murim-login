# Review

The draft contains four factual or semantic shifts, two meaningful register losses, and several localized wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 레이페이가 실종된 지 어느덧 보름.
- **Current:** It had already been two weeks since Lei Fei had gone missing.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the stated duration from fifteen days to fourteen days.
- **Replacement:** It had already been fifteen days since Lei Fei had gone missing.
- **Rationale:** 보름 specifically means fifteen days, not two weeks.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “사랑한다고 전해 달라더군요.”
- **Current:** “He told me to say that he loved you.”
- **Defect:** The draft adds Wei Fenghu as the explicit object of Lei Fei’s love, although the source leaves both the recipient and object unstated.
- **Replacement:** “He asked me to pass along his love.”
- **Rationale:** 전해 달라 asks Jin to pass the message along without specifying that Lei Fei meant Wei Fenghu alone.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 사지가 잘려 나가는 고통 속에서도 손에 쥔 무기를 놓지 않을 만큼.
- **Current:** They had held on to their weapons even while enduring the pain of having their limbs torn off.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the injury mechanism from limbs being cut or severed to limbs being torn off.
- **Replacement:** They had held on to their weapons even while enduring the pain of having their limbs severed.
- **Rationale:** 잘려 나가다 denotes being cut or severed, not torn away.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 올랐다는 능력치도 정말 쥐꼬리만큼이라 느껴지지도 않았다.
- **Current:** The increase in my stats was so tiny that I could barely feel it.
- **Defect:** The draft weakens an explicit negation into a barely perceptible sensation.
- **Replacement:** The increase in my stats was so tiny that I could not feel it at all.
- **Rationale:** 느껴지지도 않았다 states that he could not even feel the increase, which is reinforced by the following lake analogy.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아니면 삭초제근(削草除根)한다는 마음가짐으로 쓱싹…….
- **Current:** If not, I would cut them down root and branch.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Jin’s euphemistic weed-cutting image and trailing snipping punchline into a direct declaration that he will kill them.
- **Replacement:** If not, I’d cut the weeds out by the roots and—snip…
- **Rationale:** 삭초제근 sustains a weed-eradication metaphor, while 쓱싹 is a light cutting sound that supplies the joke’s deliberately indirect finish.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어이없네. 그냥 해 본 생각이었는데.
- **Current:** What the hell? I was only thinking about trying it.
- **Defect:** The draft adds profanity and makes “it” sound like the sword test, rather than Jin protesting that his malicious idea was only a passing thought.
- **Replacement:** Come on. It was just a passing thought.
- **Rationale:** 그냥 해 본 생각 refers to the preceding thought about eliminating people rejected by the sword; 어이없네 is an incredulous complaint rather than strong profanity.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 「진 선생님. 국무부장 동지께서 찾으십니다.」
- **Current:** “Mr. Jin. The Minister of State would like to see you.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicitly political and hierarchical address “Comrade.”
- **Replacement:** “Mr. Jin. Comrade Minister of State would like to see you.”
- **Rationale:** 동지 is part of the official’s title and preserves the speaker’s formal Chinese political register.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Rationale:** 명검 denotes an excellent or exceptional sword; the source does not state that it is famous.

## F12 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 평소에는 나사 빠진 놈이지만 명색이 네임드 몬스터이자 망자 군단의 사령관이다.
- **Current:** He was usually a screw loose, but he was still a named monster and commander of the army of the dead.
- **Defect:** “Was a screw loose” is not grammatical English for describing someone as scatterbrained or eccentric.
- **Replacement:** He usually had a screw loose, but he was still a named monster and commander of the army of the dead.
- **Rationale:** The natural English idiom corresponding to 나사 빠진 놈 is “had a screw loose.”

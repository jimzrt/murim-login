# Review

Two action/subject errors and several localized terminology, register, and natural-English issues require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “그냥 뭐, 파리 한두 마리 정도였지. 잡아도 잡아도 계속 들어온다길래 놔뒀더니 잠잠해지던데? 별로 날아다니지도 않고.”
- **Current:** “Well, there were a couple of flies. She said they kept coming in no matter how many she killed, so she left them alone. They quieted down after that. They didn’t fly around much, either.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Im Kkeokjeong’s wife the subject of leaving the flies alone, but the Korean shifts from what she said to Im’s own action in the main clause.
- **Replacement:** “Nothing much, just a fly or two. She said they kept coming in no matter how many she caught, so I left them alone, and then they quieted down. They hardly flew around, either.”
- **Rationale:** The construction 들어온다길래 놔뒀더니 presents her report as the reason the speaker left the flies alone; assigning both actions to her alters the subject and action.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다음 순간, 그녀가 가리키는 방향을 따라 자연스럽게 고개를 돌린 길드원들이 입을 딱 벌렸다.
- **Current:** The guild members naturally turned their heads in the direction she indicated. Then all three of them stood there with their mouths hanging open.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that the three guild members stood up, although they are still seated at the table and only turn their heads and gape.
- **Replacement:** The next moment, the guild members naturally turned in the direction she indicated, and their mouths fell open.
- **Rationale:** The Korean describes only turning their heads and opening their mouths; the actual moment when everyone springs to their feet occurs later.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “생채기 하나 안 났고, 처음부터 끝까지 말하자면 길어. 엄마는? 내 소식 아셔?”
- **Current:** “I didn’t even get a scratch. The full story is long. What about Mom? Does she know about me?”
- **Defect:** “Does she know about me?” asks whether their mother knows who Taekyung is rather than whether she has heard the news about him.
- **Replacement:** “I didn’t even get a scratch, and it’d take too long to tell you the whole story. What about Mom? Has she heard the news about me?”
- **Rationale:** 내 소식 아셔 asks whether their mother has heard about the incident or his recent news.

## F04 — Minor

- **Current:** Time had passed while they were laughing and chatting. It had been long past the time to report for work.

## F05 — Minor

- **Source:** 오죽 당황했으면 시종일관 침착하던 김 집사마저 말을 더듬을 정도다.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** - 저기, 호적 메이트 씨. 내가 지금 믿을 수 없는 걸 봤는데 말이지.

“호적 메이트? 죽고 싶냐, 너?”
- **Current:** “Hey, fellow family-register buddy. I just saw something I can’t believe.”

“Family-register buddy? Are you trying to die?”
- **Defect:** “Fellow family-register buddy” is an unnatural calque, and “fellow” does not convey Hayeon’s joking implication that they are siblings only on paper.
- **Replacement:** “Hey, sibling-on-paper. I just saw something I can’t believe.”

“Sibling-on-paper? Are you trying to die?”
- **Rationale:** 호적 메이트 is a teasing euphemism for a sibling framed as merely someone listed on the same family register; the replacement preserves both the joke and its repetition.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “들어갑니다. 스탠바이…… 큐!”
- **Current:** “We’re going in. Stand by… cue!”
- **Defect:** “We’re going in” is not the natural production cue for beginning a live broadcast.

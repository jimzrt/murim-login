# Review

The draft preserves the battle’s main sequence, but it weakens Jeok Cheongang’s threat and loses key cues in Prince Shangshan’s plea.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 태워 죽여도 시원치 않을 방화범들이었지.
- **Current:** They were arsonists who deserved to burn.
- **Defect:** Softens Jeok Cheongang’s anger: burning them to death would not have satisfied him, rather than being a fitting punishment.
- **Replacement:** They were arsonists I wouldn’t have been satisfied with even if I’d burned them alive.
- **Rationale:** 태워 죽여도 시원치 않을 says that even burning them to death would not have been enough.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “도대체!”

당장이라도 자신을 붙잡은 궁인들의 손을 뿌리치고 전장으로 달려 나갈 듯이 몸부림치며.

“도대체 무슨 생각이신 겁니까!”
- **Current:** “Why?”

He struggled as if he might shake off the palace attendants’ hands at any moment and rush onto the battlefield.

“Why would you do this?”
- **Defect:** Changes the prince’s repeated, incredulous demand about what the Emperor is thinking into questions about a deed.
- **Replacement:** “What on earth—”

He struggled as if he might shake off the palace attendants’ hands at any moment and rush onto the battlefield.

“What on earth are you thinking?”
- **Rationale:** 무슨 생각이신 겁니까 challenges the Emperor’s thinking amid his refusal to intervene; the preceding 도대체 begins the same outburst.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “원하시는 바가 있다면 무엇이든 하겠습니다. 소제(小弟)더러 대국을 떠나라 하시면, 그 역시 명을 따르겠습니다. 다만 이 아우가 처음이자 마지막으로 드리는 부탁만큼은 거절하지 마시옵소서.”
- **Current:** “If there is anything you wish of me, I will do it. If you order me to leave the Great Nation, I will obey. But please, do not refuse this one request—my first and last.”
- **Defect:** Omits both self-references as the Emperor’s younger brother, flattening the familial appeal that immediately precedes the Emperor’s unspoken wish to be called older brother.
- **Replacement:** “If there is anything you wish of me, I will do it. If you order your younger brother to leave the Great Nation, I will obey. But please, do not refuse this request from your brother—the first and last I will ever make of you.”
- **Rationale:** 소제 and 이 아우 both explicitly identify the speaker as the Emperor’s younger brother.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “부디, 부디 저자를 살려 주십시오.”
- **Current:** “Please, please spare him.”
- **Defect:** “Spare him” suggests withholding punishment or an intended killing; the prince is asking the Emperor to save a friend caught in the fighting.
- **Replacement:** “Please, please save him.”
- **Rationale:** 살려 주십시오 asks for his life to be saved, and the prince has just seen Jin Taekyung struggling on the battlefield.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘과인도 그대처럼 될 수 있을까?’
- **Current:** *Could I become like you?*
- **Defect:** Formats a remembered spoken question as a direct thought.
- **Replacement:** “Could I become like you?”
- **Rationale:** The narration calls it a question the prince put to Jin Taekyung, who answered aloud.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마침내 모습을 드러내는 심연(深淵) 앞에서, 김화종이 그 어느 때보다 어둡게 가라앉은 눈빛으로 젊은 주인의 앞을 가로막았다.
- **Current:** Before the abyss finally revealed itself, Kim Hwajong stepped in front of his young master. His eyes had sunk darker than ever before.
- **Defect:** The English reads “before” temporally, making Hwajong move prior to the abyss revealing itself. The Korean uses 앞에서 spatially: he faces the now-revealed abyss and shields Choi.
- **Replacement:** Facing the abyss as it finally revealed itself, Kim Hwajong stepped in front of his young master, his gaze darker than ever.
- **Rationale:** 심연 앞에서 means “in front of/facing the abyss,” while 모습을 드러내는 presents the abyss as revealing itself at that moment.

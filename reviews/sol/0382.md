# Review

One critical identity error: the draft turns the singular Arch Lich into the three captives. Additional localized findings affect physical detail, characterization, register, hierarchy, and natural English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 예. 예. 아크 리치가 맞습니다.
- **Current:** - Yes. Yes. We are indeed Arch Liches.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular Arch Lich being discussed into the three speakers themselves and pluralizes the identity.
- **Replacement:** - Yes. Yes. It is indeed the Arch Lich.
- **Rationale:** The Korean retains 아크 리치 as singular and does not supply a first-person plural subject. The subsequent reference to the Arch Lich’s first appearance also treats it as a distinct singular entity.

# Review

The draft contains several actionable subject reversals, including who owes an apology, who visited whom, who could have struck whom, and who defied the Sect Leader. It also loses an important seniority distinction and misstates the nature and extent of Cheongpung’s martial training.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “사문의 어른을 몰라뵙고 결례를 저질렀으니 백번 사죄해야 마땅합니다. 그렇지 않느냐?”
- **Current:** “I failed to recognize an elder of my sect and committed a grave discourtesy. I should apologize a hundred times. Is that not so?”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Baek Museong confess and owe the apology, but he is admonishing Chulwoo for Chulwoo’s failure to recognize Cheongpung.
- **Replacement:** “You failed to recognize an elder of our sect and committed a grave discourtesy. It is only right that you apologize a hundred times. Is that not so?”
- **Rationale:** Baek Museong immediately prompts Chulwoo, who then bows and asks Cheongpung’s forgiveness.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “제 스승께서는 천검진인이라는 별호를 쓰십니다. 항렬로 따지자면 사숙의 대사형이 되시지요.”

“대사형이요?”
- **Current:** “My Master goes by the title of the Heavenly Sword True Person. In terms of seniority, he is your Senior Brother.”

“My Senior Brother?”
- **Defect:** Both lines omit 대, losing the fact that the Heavenly Sword True Person is Cheongpung’s eldest Senior Brother rather than merely one unspecified Senior Brother.
- **Replacement:** “My Master goes by the title of the Heavenly Sword True Person. In terms of seniority, he is your eldest Senior Brother.”

“My eldest Senior Brother?”
- **Rationale:** The hierarchy is explicitly 대사형 in both the explanation and Cheongpung’s surprised repetition.

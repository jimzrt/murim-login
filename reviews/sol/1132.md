# Review

The closing line reverses who kept the promise; an earlier passage also obscures why the witnesses dismissed the moving finger.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “약속, 지켰네요.”
- **Current:** “You kept your promise.”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the fulfilled promise to Jeok Cheongang rather than to the Disciple who has returned.
- **Replacement:** “I kept my promise.”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung speaks after enduring the transformation to return. The Korean leaves the subject implicit; the scene supports Taekyung reporting that he kept his promise.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그저 제자의 시신을 끌어안고 오열하는 스승의 비통한 손길이나 아직도 멎지 않은 전장의 울림. 혹은 단순한 착각이라고 생각했을 뿐이었다.
- **Current:** They thought only of the Master’s grief-stricken hands clutching his Disciple’s body as he sobbed, or the battle’s echoes that still hadn’t faded. Or perhaps they thought it was simply their imagination.
- **Defect:** The witnesses are attributing the finger’s movement to other causes, not merely thinking about the Master’s hands or the battlefield.
- **Replacement:** They put the movement down to the grieving Master’s hands as he held his Disciple’s body and sobbed, the lingering vibrations of the battlefield—or simply their imagination.
- **Rationale:** The Korean lists the explanations the witnesses gave for what they saw, establishing why they missed the sign of life.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 소멸이라는 두 글자에 가려진, 정화(淨化)의 성질은 유심히 들여다보지 못한 채.
- **Current:** They failed to look closely at the quality hidden behind those two words: purification.
- **Defect:** “Those two words” carries over a reference to the Korean written term that does not work for the single English word “destruction.”
- **Replacement:** They failed to look past destruction and see fire’s power to purify.
- **Rationale:** The contrast is between fire’s destructive and purifying qualities; the Korean character count need not survive in English.

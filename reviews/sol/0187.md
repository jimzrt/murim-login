# Review

The draft contains one critical action error, two meaningful hierarchy or participation errors, and several localized fidelity and prose issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 바로 그가 내게 원단 초대장을 들려 보낸 장본인이니까.
- **Current:** He was the one who had sent me the New Year’s Day invitation.
- **Defect:** This changes the action from Jin Wikyung sending Taekyung out carrying the invitation to merely sending the invitation to Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** He was the one who had sent me to deliver the New Year’s Day invitation.
- **Rationale:** The construction 내게 … 들려 보낸 means that Jin Wikyung sent the narrator with the invitation in hand, which also explains why his prior contact with Lee Seowol did not surprise him.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 태원진가의 무인들을 중심으로 거대한 함성이 터져 나왔다.
- **Current:** A tremendous roar erupted from the martial artists of the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- **Defect:** The draft limits the roar to the Jin martial artists, while the source says the roar erupted with them at its center, indicating participation spreading beyond them.
- **Replacement:** A tremendous roar erupted from the crowd, centered on the martial artists of the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- **Rationale:** 중심으로 identifies the Jin martial artists as the center of the response, not its sole participants.

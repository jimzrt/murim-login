# Review

One critical conditional-action mistranslation, plus several subject, pragmatic-cue, characterization, imagery, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘그랬다면 후회하게 만들어 줬을 텐데.’
- **Current:** *You should be glad.*
- **Defect:** The conditional consequence has been replaced with a generic warning that also duplicates the later thought about Won being lucky. Taekyung specifically thinks that if Won had attacked, he would have made Won regret it.
- **Replacement:** *If he had, I would’ve made him regret it.*
- **Rationale:** 그랬다면 refers to Won attacking despite his hesitation, while 후회하게 만들어 줬을 텐데 states the retaliation Taekyung would have inflicted.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 붉게 물든 놈의 다른 눈동자가 정확히 나를 응시하고 있었다.
- **Current:** The red eye that remained fixed on me.
- **Defect:** The sentence is a fragment and omits that this is the Wyvern’s other eye, contrasted with its cloudy damaged eye.
- **Replacement:** Its other eye, stained red, stared directly at me.
- **Rationale:** 다른 눈동자 explicitly identifies the other eye, and 정확히 나를 응시하고 있었다 is a complete statement that it was staring directly at Taekyung.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “세상에, 그걸 모를 수가 있어요?”
- **Current:** “Good grief. How could you not know?”
- **Defect:** The English assigns the knowing to Taekyung, making Song Song ask how he could fail to know his own intended confession. Her response instead means that his feelings were so obvious that she could not have failed to notice.
- **Replacement:** “Good grief. How could I not know?”
- **Rationale:** This directly answers Taekyung’s question about how she knew and preserves the joke that his attraction was conspicuous.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “솔직히 태경 씨 정도면 월척이긴 한데, 제 성격상 좋아하는 사람 생기면 한 번에 낚아 올리고 말지. 어장 같은 거 못 만들어요.”
- **Current:** “To be honest, you’d be quite a catch, Mr. Taekyung. But if I start liking someone, I’ll just reel them in all at once. I can’t keep a whole fishing pond.”
- **Defect:** The literal “keep a whole fishing pond” is not intelligible natural English and fails to convey the dating metaphor of keeping multiple romantic prospects on the hook.
- **Replacement:** “To be honest, you’d be quite a catch, Mr. Taekyung. But when I like someone, I’m the type to reel him in right away. I can’t string a bunch of men along.”
- **Rationale:** 월척 and 낚아 올리다 sustain the fishing joke, while 어장 만들다 means maintaining a pool of romantic prospects rather than literally owning a pond.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘어린놈이 고생하는 게 자꾸 눈에 밟혀서. 너만 한 막냇동생이 있거든.’
- **Current:** *I keep noticing a young kid like you having a hard time. I’ve got a youngest sibling about your age.*
- **Defect:** “Keep noticing” strips 눈에 밟히다 of its emotional force and makes Cheonsu’s concern sound like detached observation rather than something that continually troubled him.
- **Replacement:** *It kept eating at me, seeing a kid like you struggle. My youngest sibling is about your age.*
- **Rationale:** 눈에 밟히다 expresses being unable to put a troubling or affecting sight out of one’s mind, which is central to the apparent reason Cheonsu gives for caring for Taekyung.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 작은 태양과 같은 그것은 어느덧 일 갑자를 넘어선 열양지기인 동시에 쌓이고 쌓여 덩어리가 되어 버린 분노였다.
- **Current:** It was like a small sun—a mass of Scorching Yang Qi that had surpassed one jiazi, and rage that had accumulated until it became something solid.
- **Defect:** The culturally specific unit “jiazi” is left unexplained even though its sixty-year magnitude is necessary to understand the scale of Taekyung’s accumulated qi.
- **Replacement:** It was like a small sun—both Scorching Yang Qi that had surpassed one jiazi[^1] and rage that had accumulated until it solidified.

[^1]: A *jiazi* is a traditional sixty-year cycle.
- **Rationale:** 일 갑자 denotes one traditional sixty-year cycle; the concise footnote supplies materially necessary context without adding plot information.

# Review

Several localized fidelity errors change quantities, a promised reward, and the threatened action.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 고작 일각 전에 네놈 주둥이로 들어간 육포만 따져도 송아지 한 마린데.
- **Current:** The jerky you stuffed into your mouth barely fifteen minutes ago would’ve been enough for a whole calf.
- **Defect:** Changes the amount of jerky Taishan ate into an amount sufficient to feed a calf.
- **Replacement:** The jerky you stuffed into your mouth barely fifteen minutes ago amounted to a whole calf.
- **Rationale:** Namho says the jerky Taishan ate alone amounted to an entire calf.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 빌어먹을, 돌아가는 대로 하나 더 주마.
- **Current:** “Damn it. I’ll give you another once we’re on our way.”
- **Defect:** Moves the promised second sweet from their return to their departure.
- **Replacement:** “Damn it. I’ll give you another as soon as we get back.”
- **Rationale:** 돌아가는 대로 means upon returning, not once they set off.

## F03 — Critical

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 전부 뼈도 못 추렸어.
- **Current:** Not one of them had enough left to bury.

- **Replacement:** None of them stood a chance.
- **Rationale:** 뼈도 못 추리다 conveys being thoroughly beaten or destroyed; it does not say their remains were too scant to bury.

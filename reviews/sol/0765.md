# Review

One major characterization error and several localized action, nuance, and prose issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 어느덧 미카엘 실베르트와 나를 중심으로 흘러나온 기운이 보이지 않는 선을 경계 삼아 서로를 향하고 있었다.
- **Current:** By then, the energy flowing from Michael Silbert and me had taken an invisible line as its boundary and was surging toward one another.
- **Defect:** The singular subject cannot naturally surge toward “one another,” and “surging” changes the opposed auras from facing each other across a boundary to actively rushing forward.
- **Replacement:** By then, the auras flowing from Michael Silbert and me were facing each other across an invisible boundary.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the two characters’ respective auras confronting each other with an invisible line between them.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그와 동시에 이곳을 향해 빛살과도 같은 속도로 쏘아지는 수십의 인기척이 느껴졌다.
- **Current:** At the same time, I sensed dozens of presences shooting toward us at the speed of light.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a comparison to rays of light into the literal physical speed of light.
- **Replacement:** At the same time, I sensed dozens of presences shooting toward us as swiftly as rays of light.
- **Rationale:** The construction 빛살과도 같은 속도로 is explicitly comparative rather than a precise claim about their velocity.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “이제 좀 한결 낫군.”
- **Current:** “You seem to be feeling better now.”
- **Defect:** Michael is commenting that the situation or atmosphere has improved after Jin retracts his aura, not expressing concern for Jin’s well-being.
- **Replacement:** “That is much better.”
- **Rationale:** The line immediately follows Jin reining in his aura, and 한결 낫군 marks approval of the resulting de-escalation.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 어떤 국가와 제약도 무시하는 강력한 힘을 가진 무력 단체가, 영웅의 탈을 뒤집어쓴 괴물의 손아귀에 들어가는 것이다.
- **Current:** A powerful armed organization that disregarded every country and restriction would fall into the hands of a monster wearing a human face.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces Michael’s public disguise as a hero with the unrelated image of a monster wearing a human face, losing the contrast between his heroic reputation and true nature.
- **Replacement:** A powerful armed organization that disregarded every country and restriction would fall into the hands of a monster wearing a hero’s mask.
- **Rationale:** 영웅의 탈 specifically means a hero’s mask or guise; the distinct “human skin” image appears later in the chapter as 인두겁.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금 이 순간만큼은 분노에 모든 것을 잊고, 분노에 모든 것을 맡겼다.
- **Current:** At this moment, I forgot everything to my anger and entrusted everything to it.
- **Defect:** “Forgot everything to my anger” is unidiomatic English and obscures the deliberate repetition of anger as both the cause of his oblivion and the force to which he surrenders.
- **Replacement:** At that moment, I forgot everything in my rage and surrendered everything to it.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both clauses and the repeated causal role of 분노 while reading naturally in English.

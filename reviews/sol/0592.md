# Review

The draft contains one critical viewpoint error, several altered or omitted details, and losses of repetition, hesitation, and a deliberate register shift.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 내뱉는 숨을 따라 붉은 안개가 피어오른다.

그것은 숨을 쉬지 않는다고 착각할 만큼 길고 느린 호흡이었다. 아주 오래전, 스승으로부터 배운 비기(祕器)이기도 했다.
- **Current:** A red mist rose with the breath I exhaled.

It was a long, slow breath—so long that one might mistake it for the breath of someone who had stopped breathing altogether. It was also a secret technique I had learned from my Master long ago.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the third-person subject to the first-person narrator, making Jin Taekyung appear to perform Go Jun’s breathing technique. It also misconstrues the slow breathing as the breath of someone who has stopped breathing.
- **Replacement:** A red mist rose with his exhaled breath.

It was a long, slow breath—so long and slow that one might mistake him for not breathing at all. It was also a secret technique he had learned from his Master long ago.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding scene identifies Go Jun as the person breathing and recalling what his Master taught him; the Korean says the breathing was slow enough to make him seem not to breathe.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 눈가가 뜨겁게 달아오른다. 120층에 달하는 마천루(摩天樓)와 천여 명의 적들을 돌파하며 단 한 번도 흔들리지 않았던 마음이 격동한다.
- **Current:** My eyes grew hot. My heart, which had not wavered even once while breaking through a 120-story skyscraper and more than a thousand enemies, began to churn.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an approximate quantity of around one thousand enemies into more than one thousand.
- **Replacement:** My eyes grew hot. My heart, which had not wavered even once while breaking through a 120-story skyscraper and around a thousand enemies, began to churn.
- **Rationale:** 천여 명 means approximately or a little over one thousand, not definitively more than one thousand.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 끊임없이 공력을 소모한 단전은 반쯤 비어 있었으나 그것만으로도 충분했다.
- **Current:** My dantian, which had been continuously consuming internal energy, was half empty. But that was enough.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the dantian itself the agent consuming internal energy rather than describing it as depleted because Taekyung has continuously expended that energy.
- **Replacement:** My dantian was half empty from the internal energy I had continuously expended. But that was enough.
- **Rationale:** The source explains the mechanical cause of the dantian’s depleted state: ongoing expenditure of internal energy.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 몸을 일으키자 건물의 잔해와 대리석 조각이 떨어진다.
- **Current:** When I stood up, rubble and pieces of marble fell from the building.
- **Defect:** The draft adds an incorrect point of origin, saying the debris fell from the building rather than simply falling as Taekyung rose from the crater.
- **Replacement:** As I rose, rubble and pieces of marble tumbled down.
- **Rationale:** The Korean does not say the debris falls from the building; it falls in direct response to Taekyung getting up.

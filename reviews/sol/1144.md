# Review

Four actionable fidelity and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 누가 흔들어 깨어도 모를 만큼 깊고 선명한 잠에.
- **Current:** So deeply and soundly that even if someone shook him, he wouldn’t wake.
- **Defect:** The draft says shaking him would fail to wake him; the source says he would not know even if someone shook him awake.
- **Replacement:** Into a sleep so deep and vivid that he wouldn’t know it even if someone shook him awake.
- **Rationale:** 깨어도 explicitly allows for his being awakened; 모를 describes his lack of awareness.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 우리는 무수한 피를 흘려야만 했소.
- **Current:** we’ve been forced to shed countless lives.
- **Defect:** Changes the blood they have shed into lives they have taken or lost.
- **Replacement:** we’ve had to shed so much blood.
- **Rationale:** 피를 흘리다 refers to bleeding or bloodshed, not shedding lives.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그때부터 하루가 멀다하고 끈질기게 달라붙었었지.
- **Current:** and from then on, he’d pestered me for it every other day.
- **Defect:** “Every other day” understates how often he pestered Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** and from then on, he’d pestered me for it practically every day.
- **Rationale:** 하루가 멀다하고 means nearly every day.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그것의 유일한 시침은 본래의 위치에서 한 칸 벗어나 있었다.
- **Current:** Its sole hand was one mark away from its original position.
- **Defect:** “Mark” introduces a visible dial marking that the source does not establish.
- **Replacement:** Its sole hand had moved one position from where it had originally been.
- **Rationale:** 한 칸 describes a step in position; the passage says the watch has no numbers and does not identify marked divisions.

# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s main events, but misidentifies whose head and arms Sama Pyo proposes to sacrifice and blurs the threat his father says he will inherit.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 가솔과 문파, 광대한 토지와 수많은 재화…… 그리고 가장 중요한, 반드시 해결해야 할 원한까지도.
- **Current:** Your family and sect, vast lands and untold wealth… and, most importantly, the vendetta you must settle.
- **Defect:** “Family” replaces the household retainers, while “the vendetta you must settle” suggests a grievance Sama Pyo intends to avenge rather than the hostile grudges he must resolve.
- **Replacement:** Your household and sect, vast lands and untold wealth… and, most importantly, the grudges you’ll have to deal with.
- **Rationale:** 가솔 refers to the people of his household; the following warning concerns enemies who will come for the Black Dragon Demon Gate.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 설령 저들이 수급으로 만족하지 못한다면, 팔다리를 하나씩 떼어 내는 정도까지 각오해야겠지요.
- **Current:** Even if that isn’t enough to satisfy them, we should be prepared to lose a limb at a time.
- **Defect:** “That” omits the heads offered first, obscuring the escalation from executing leaders to sacrificing further members.
- **Replacement:** If the heads alone don’t satisfy them, I’ll have to be prepared to cut off the limbs one by one.
- **Rationale:** 수급 means severed heads; 팔다리를 하나씩 떼어 내다 continues the body metaphor with limbs removed one by one.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 객이라. 적이 아니라 손님을 대접하는 것이니, 곧 주인이 될 네가 직접 준비해야겠구나.
- **Current:** “Guests, is it? Since you’re about to become the host, you’ll have to prepare the welcome yourself.”
- **Defect:** The reply drops Sima Gong’s pointed contrast between treating the visitors as guests and treating them as enemies.
- **Replacement:** “Guests, is it? If you’re welcoming guests rather than facing enemies, then you, as their soon-to-be host, will have to make the arrangements yourself.”
- **Rationale:** 적이 아니라 손님을 대접하는 것이니 makes the enemies-versus-guests distinction explicit.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 스스로 머리와 팔을 잘라 바치고, 합리적인 보상안을 제시한다면 복수의 명분은 힘을 잃을 겁니다.
- **Current:** “If I cut off and offer my own head and arms, then present reasonable compensation, their justification for revenge will lose its force.”
- **Defect:** Sama Pyo is not offering his own head. He proposes personally sacrificing the Gate’s head—his father—and its arms—the senior members who followed him.
- **Replacement:** “If I cut off the Gate’s head and arms myself and offer them up, then propose reasonable compensation, their grounds for revenge will crumble.”
- **Rationale:** Sima Gong has just identified himself and his senior members as those Sama Pyo intends to cut down; 머리와 팔 carries that organizational metaphor forward.

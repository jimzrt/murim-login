# Review

One critical subject reversal, several hierarchy and quantity errors, and localized action, voice, and formatting issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 부릅떠진 장 웨이의 눈동자에, 사방에서 쇄도해 오는 열 기의 데스나이트가 비쳤다.
- **Current:** The black knight’s red eyes reflected in Zhang Wei’s wide-open gaze as the ten Death Knights charged in from every direction.
- **Defect:** The reflected subject is reversed. Zhang Wei’s eyes reflect the ten charging Death Knights, not the black knight’s red eyes.
- **Replacement:** The ten Death Knights charging from every direction were reflected in Zhang Wei’s wide-open eyes.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly makes 열 기의 데스나이트 the figures reflected in 장 웨이의 눈동자.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘군대와 함께 먼저 가서 도시를 점령하라니? 샤오 연대장이 내린 명령과는 다른데.’
- **Current:** *“Advance ahead with the army and seize the city? That’s different from the order Colonel Shao gave me.”*
- **Defect:** The draft replaces Shao’s functional title, regimental commander, with a rank and subsequently creates a rank inconsistency.
- **Replacement:** *Advance ahead with the army and seize the city? That’s different from the order Regimental Commander Shao gave me.*
- **Rationale:** 연대장 means regimental commander, while the later dialogue separately establishes Shao’s military rank.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 「샤오 쉔 연대장은 왕 상교님과 같은 계급입니다. 그것도 곧 소장 진급을 앞두고 계신.」
- **Current:** “Colonel Shao Shen holds the same rank as you, Senior Colonel Wang. And he’s about to be promoted to major general.”
- **Defect:** Calling Shao a Colonel while calling Wang a Senior Colonel contradicts the sentence’s explicit statement that they hold the same rank.
- **Replacement:** “Regimental Commander Shao Shen holds the same rank as you, Senior Colonel Wang. And he’s about to be promoted to major general.”
- **Rationale:** The source identifies Shao by his post as 연대장 and then states that his rank equals Wang’s.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 이 단어를 어디서 들어 봤던가.

검은 기사는 문득 드는 의문을 뒤로하고 손을 뻗었다. 눈에 보이는 인간은 말살시켜야 한다. 그것이 그가 받은 명령이었다.

하지만.

- ……?

어째서인지 손이 나아가지 않았다.
- **Current:** Where had it heard that word before?

The black knight pushed the question aside and extended its hand. The humans within sight had to be annihilated. That was the order it had received.

But—

—……?

For some reason, its hand would not move forward.
- **Defect:** The draft repeatedly calls the black knight “it,” although the source uses the personal subject 그 and the draft itself later switches to “he.”
- **Replacement:** Where had he heard that word before?

The black knight pushed the question aside and extended his hand. The humans within sight had to be annihilated. That was the order he had received.

But—

—……?

For some reason, his hand would not move forward.
- **Rationale:** 그가 받은 명령 explicitly uses the personal subject for the sentient black knight; consistent personal pronouns also avoid an unsupported mid-scene identity shift.

## F12 — Minor

- **Source:** 무릎을 꿇어 예를 표한 열 기의 데스나이트는 우두머리의 뒤를 따라 말을 달렸다.

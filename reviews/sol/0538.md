# Review

One mistranslated censored username, altered profanity, and several localized fidelity or natural-English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당사* : 아, 미미야…….
- **Current:** Party Involved*: Ah, Mimi…
- **Defect:** The censored username abbreviates Tang Sadok’s name; it does not mean “Party Involved.” The current rendering obscures the speaker’s identity and the joke.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “그래, 알았다. 이 시뻘놈아.”

“뭐라? 이런 개호로 잡놈을 봤나…….”
- **Current:** “Fine, I understand, you red-faced bastard.”

“What did you say? Why, you filthy bastard…”
- **Defect:** “Red-faced” invents a physical description absent from the insult, while “filthy bastard” substantially softens the answering 개호로 잡놈. This weakens the profane escalation that makes the exchange absurd.
- **Replacement:** “Fine. Got it, you fucker.”

“What was that? You goddamn son of a bitch…”
- **Rationale:** 시뻘놈 is a vulgar deformation of a strong insult, not a reference to facial color, and 개호로 잡놈 is markedly stronger than “filthy bastard.”

## F04 — Minor

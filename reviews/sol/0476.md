# Review

Two meaningful mistranslations affect the raid dialogue and Cheongpung’s response; several localized omissions and wording choices weaken quantities, hierarchy, humor, or physical actions.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 똥개도 제 앞마당에서는 반은 먹고 들어간다는데, 놈이 도망치고자 마음먹는다면 나로서도 막을 수 없었다.
- **Current:** They said that even a mutt had a home-field advantage in its own yard. If the bastard decided to run, there was no way I could stop him.
- **Defect:** The translation generalizes the explicit claim that a mutt starts with half the battle already won into an unspecified advantage.
- **Replacement:** They said even a mutt had half the battle won in its own yard. If the bastard decided to run, there was no way I could stop him.
- **Rationale:** 반은 먹고 들어간다 explicitly quantifies the home-ground advantage as half the contest.

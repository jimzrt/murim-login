## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “모처럼 인정하는 모습이 보기 좋네. 어때, 기왕 온 김에 한 손 거들어 줘?”

“돕겠다고?”
- **Current:** “It’s nice to see you admit I’m right for once. How about lending a hand while I’m here?”

“Lend a hand?”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jin ask Jeong for help; Jin is offering to help Jeong.
- **Replacement:** “It’s nice to see you admit I’m right for once. How about I lend you a hand while I’m here?”

“You want to help?”
- **Rationale:** Jeong’s reply, 돕겠다고?, questions Jin’s offer to help.

## F02 — Critical

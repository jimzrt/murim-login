# Review

Ten actionable findings, including altered kinship, age, hierarchy, dialogue attribution, omitted humor, and an unreferenced footnote.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그쪽이 김씨 아줌마 아들이야?”
- **Current:** “So you’re Kim ajumma’s son?”
- **Defect:** The first use of “ajumma” does not reference the existing explanatory footnote, leaving footnote 2 orphaned.
- **Replacement:** “So you’re the son of Kim ajumma[^2]?”
- **Rationale:** The retained Korean form is culturally specific and already has a useful, spoiler-free definition, but the Markdown reference is missing.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그런 의미에서 저희는 이만 가 보겠습니다. 엄마, 옷 갈아입고 나가자.”
- **Current:** “Anyway, we’ll be going now. Mom, go change so we can leave.”
- **Defect:** “Anyway” omits the explicit logical connection that turns the owner's complaint into Taekyung's justification for leaving with his mother.
- **Replacement:** “In that case, we’ll be going now. Mom, go change so we can leave.”
- **Rationale:** 그런 의미에서 means “in that sense/in that case”; the causal pivot is essential to Taekyung's rhetorical trap and the resulting joke.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “우리 엄마, 이제 그쪽 직원 아니거든요.”
- **Current:** “Our mother isn’t your employee anymore.”
- **Defect:** The plural possessive falsely makes the restaurant owner share Taekyung's mother.
- **Replacement:** “My mother isn’t your employee anymore.”
- **Rationale:** 우리 엄마 is the ordinary Korean expression for the speaker's own mother here; only Taekyung is asserting that relationship.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 50대에 접어든 나이와 고도 비만에 이른 몸뚱어리에는 한계가 분명했으니까.

## F06 — Minor

- **Current:** I was impressed that she had managed to come up with a line like that.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 순간 말문이 막힌 내 모습을 오해했는지 사장이 피식피식 비웃음을 흘린다.
- **Current:** Perhaps she mistook my momentary silence for shock. The owner let out a quiet laugh.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 중년 아저씨의 한마디에 대여섯 명의 부하 직원들이 뒤따라 일어났다.
- **Current:** At the middle-aged man’s words, five or six employees stood up and followed him.
- **Defect:** Calling them merely “employees” removes their subordinate relationship to the middle-aged manager and can make them sound like restaurant staff.
- **Replacement:** At the middle-aged man’s words, five or six of his subordinates stood and followed him.
- **Rationale:** 부하 직원들 specifically identifies them as employees under the man's authority, reinforcing his preceding statement that he is a section manager.

## F10 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** - ……약속한 40억은 보내 드렸는데요.
- **Current:** “…”

“I sent you the promised four billion won.”
- **Defect:** Splitting the ellipsis and sentence into separate dialogue paragraphs creates a phantom turn and, under normal dialogue alternation, can assign “I sent you” to Taekyung instead of Im Changsoo. It also omits the defensive nuance of 그런데요.
- **Replacement:** “…I sent you the promised four billion won, though.”
- **Rationale:** The source is one continuous reply from Im Changsoo: after hesitating, he points out that he already sent Taekyung the promised payment.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but several passages change who acted, how an action worked, or what Taekyung means.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 레벨 업을 이용한 회복력이 있었음에도 이 지경까지 왔는데 시스템이 없는 지금은 어떻겠는가.
- **Current:** I’d made it this far despite having the recovery that came with Level Ups. What would happen now, without the System?
- **Defect:** “Made it this far” turns the point into endurance; Taekyung is emphasizing that his body reached its present dire state even with Level-Up recovery.
- **Replacement:** Even with the recovery I got from Level Ups, my body had ended up like this. What would happen now, without the System?
- **Rationale:** 이 지경까지 왔는데 refers to his depleted condition, not how far he has progressed.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 잠깐의 반항은 곧이어 솟구친 청백색의 화염과 함께 사라졌고, 나는 전력을 다해 신형을 쏘았다.
- **Current:** Its brief resistance vanished along with the blue-white flames that flared up, and I launched myself forward with all my strength.
- **Defect:** “Vanished along with” makes the flames disappear. The blade’s resistance disappears when the flames rise.
- **Replacement:** Its brief resistance vanished as blue-white flames flared up, and I launched myself forward with all my strength.
- **Rationale:** 사라졌고 applies to the resistance; 솟구친 describes the flames rising.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그곳에 무슨 결과가 기다리고 있는지도.
- **Current:** “Choose. Decide which path to take—and what awaits you at the end of it.”
- **Defect:** The draft suggests Taekyung can choose the outcome, rather than warning him to consider the consequences of his path.
- **Replacement:** “Choose which path to take. And think about what awaits you at the end of it.”
- **Rationale:** The result is something waiting on the chosen path, not another thing he gets to decide.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그것도 약점까지 잡은 지금, 암천의 끄나풀들이 나를 이대로 순순히 놔주겠다고?
- **Current:** And they’d let me go just like that? Even after using my weakness against me?
- **Defect:** The draft drops Taekyung’s identification of them as Dark Heaven’s lackeys and says they have already used his weakness against him. His point is that they now have leverage.
- **Replacement:** The lackeys of Dark Heaven would just let me go, now that they had leverage over me?
- **Rationale:** 암천의 끄나풀들 names the people in his thought, while 약점까지 잡은 지금 describes their current hold over him.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 사천당가의 지하 뇌옥. 분명 숨이 끊겼던 서천마군의 몸을 빌려 잠시나마 마주했던 그 짙은 어둠의 속삭임이.
- **Current:** The underground prison beneath the Sichuan Tang Clan. That thick darkness whose whispers I’d heard for a brief moment, while using the body of the Western Heaven Demon Lord after his breath had stopped.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung the one using the dead Demon Lord’s body. The darkness borrowed that body to encounter him.
- **Replacement:** The underground prison beneath the Sichuan Tang Clan. The whispers of that deep darkness I’d encountered briefly when it borrowed the body of the Western Heaven Demon Lord, who had unmistakably died.
- **Rationale:** 몸을 빌려 describes how the darkness manifested, not an action Taekyung performed.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그래, 기왕 하는 거 당연히 그러길 바란다. 하지만 그것 때문만은 아니야.
- **Current:** “Yeah. If he’s going to do it, of course I want him to. But that’s not the only reason.”
- **Defect:** “If he’s going to do it” makes the child’s own attempt to become Emperor the condition. Taekyung is referring to the undertaking already in motion.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. Since we’re going this far, of course I want him to become Emperor. But that’s not the only reason.”
- **Rationale:** 기왕 하는 거 means that, given what is already being undertaken, Taekyung naturally wants that outcome.

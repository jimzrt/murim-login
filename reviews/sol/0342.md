# Review

Two hierarchy and capability statements are weakened, the quest title loses its numeric hyperbole, and several localized fidelity or wording issues remain.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 신법을 절정으로 익힌 무인들은 이와 같은 기예를 사용할 수 있었는데, 눈밭을 달리면서도 흔적이 남지 않는 답설무흔(踏雪無痕)과 물 위를 평지처럼 걸어갈 수 있다는 등평도수(登萍渡水)도 비슷한 맥락이다.
- **Current:** Martial artists who had mastered their movement techniques to the highest level could use arts like this.
- **Defect:** The established Peak realm is replaced with the generic and potentially absolute “highest level,” obscuring the hierarchy that the following comparison with Supreme Peak masters establishes.
- **Replacement:** Martial artists who had mastered their movement techniques to the Peak realm could perform feats like this.
- **Rationale:** 절정 is the established Peak realm, and the passage distinguishes techniques available at that level from Stepping on Empty Air, which requires a Supreme Peak master.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘물론 초상비 정도는 나도 마음만 먹으면 못 할 건 없기는 한데.’
- **Current:** *Of course, if I really put my mind to it, I could probably use Flying Over Grass too.*
- **Defect:** “Probably” turns Taekyung’s qualified but definite statement of capability into uncertainty.
- **Replacement:** *Of course, Flying Over Grass isn’t beyond me either if I put my mind to it.*
- **Rationale:** 못 할 건 없다 states that he can do it if he chooses; it does not express doubt about whether he is capable.

## F03 — Minor

- **Replacement:** > - A Chain Quest, **Thirty Thousand Li in Search of an Elixir**, has been generated.
- **Rationale:** 삼만리 specifically means thirty thousand li; reducing it to “a long journey” removes the title’s concrete exaggeration.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 멀뚱멀뚱 나를 바라보던 두 사제(師弟)는 동시에 손을 내밀어 내가 내민 물건을 받아든다.
- **Current:** The two Master and Disciple stared blankly at me before reaching out at the same time and accepting the object I offered them.
- **Defect:** “The two Master and Disciple” is ungrammatical and treats the relationship labels as a malformed collective noun.
- **Replacement:** Master and Disciple alike stared blankly at me, then reached out at the same time to take the object I held out.
- **Rationale:** The parenthetical characters 師弟 identify the pair as master and disciple; the replacement preserves both that relationship and their simultaneous action.

## F06 — Minor

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 혹시 내가 부담스러워 거절할까 봐 천년설삼을 백년설삼으로 속이면서까지 선물해 준 그녀를 생각하자 실소가 새어 나왔다.
- **Current:** Thinking of how she had given it to me as a gift while lying that it was Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng in case I found the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng burdensome and refused it, I let out a quiet laugh.
- **Defect:** The sentence awkwardly makes the ginseng itself sound “burdensome” and flattens 실소 into a generic quiet laugh.
- **Replacement:** I let out a wry chuckle as I thought of how she had given it to me while passing the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng off as Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng, worried I might feel burdened and refuse it otherwise.
- **Rationale:** 내가 부담스러워 describes Taekyung feeling burdened by such a valuable gift, while 실소 is a wry or incredulous chuckle rather than merely a quiet one.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 바로 옆, 자그마한 신형이 빠르게 나아간다.
- **Current:** A small figure moved swiftly beside us.
- **Defect:** The plural “us” changes the narrator-relative position, and “beside” drops the emphasis of 바로.
- **Replacement:** A small figure sped along right beside me.
- **Rationale:** 바로 옆 is “right beside” the first-person narrator in this context, not generically beside the whole group.

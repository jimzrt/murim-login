- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하긴, 중간도 되지 않는 지점에서 고블린을 상대로 버벅거리는 걸 보니 하급 헌터인 것은 둘째치고, 그리 경험 많은 자들이 아니다.
- **Current:** No wonder they were low-level Hunters. They were struggling against goblins from barely halfway across the cave, so they clearly weren’t very experienced either.
- **Defect:** The draft turns their position before the halfway point of the Gate into the goblins attacking from halfway across the cave, and changes the relationship between their low level and lack of experience.
- **Replacement:** Judging by how they were floundering against goblins before they’d even made it halfway through the Gate, they weren’t just low-level Hunters—they weren’t particularly experienced, either.
- **Rationale:** 중간도 되지 않는 지점 describes how little progress the party has made through the Gate, while 하급 헌터인 것은 둘째치고 distinguishes their low rank from their evident inexperience.

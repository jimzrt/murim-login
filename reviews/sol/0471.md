# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it omits Jeok Cheongang’s childhood destitution, blurs a hierarchy comparison, changes the subject of battle damage, weakens and mistimes a curse, and renders the final piercing impact as a blunt thud.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 유리걸식하던 소년은 어느 날 스승을 만나 열화문(烈火門)의 십팔 대 계승자가 되었고
- **Current:** The boy who had wandered from place to place met a master one day and became the eighteenth successor of the Fire Gate Clan.
- **Defect:** “Wandered from place to place” omits that the boy survived by begging for food, erasing the destitution central to Jeok Cheongang’s early life.
- **Replacement:** The boy who had wandered from place to place, begging for food, met a master one day and became the eighteenth successor of the Fire Gate Clan.
- **Rationale:** 유리걸식 specifically describes roaming and begging for food, not merely traveling without a settled home.

## F05 — Minor

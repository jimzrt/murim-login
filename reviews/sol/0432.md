# Review

The draft contains one reversed physical action, several altered causal or pragmatic details, and localized register and terminology issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한 나라의 얼굴이나 다름없는 S급 헌터가 둘이나 죽은 대사건, 심지어 그중 한 사람은 한때 매직 존슨과 같은 전장에서 싸웠던 전우이기도 했다.
- **Current:** Two S-rank Hunters—men who were practically the faces of their country—had died in a major incident. One of them had even been a comrade who had once fought alongside Magic Johnson on the same battlefield.
- **Defect:** The singular “their country” implies that both Hunters represented the same country, although they were prominent figures of their respective nations.
- **Replacement:** It was a major incident in which two S-rank Hunters—each practically the face of his own country—had died. One of them had even been a comrade who had once fought alongside Magic Johnson on the same battlefield.
- **Rationale:** 한 나라의 얼굴이나 다름없는 characterizes each Hunter as effectively the face of a nation, not both as faces of one shared country.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it loses several localized jokes, one hierarchy cue, and two exact physical/cultural details.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “아, 닥치라고! 예?”
- **Current:** “Shut up already! What?”
- **Defect:** The brusque “What?” makes Taekyung sound as though he is snapping at Choi. In the source, he shouts at the Skeleton Warlord and then switches back to a polite acknowledgment for Choi.
- **Replacement:** “Ah, shut up already! Sorry, what?”
- **Rationale:** The polite 예? marks the abrupt change in addressee and preserves Taekyung’s hierarchical register with Choi.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대신 엉덩이를 걷어차 줄 수는 있지.
- **Current:** Still, I could kick him.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the specified target of the kick, weakening the physical action and colloquial aggression.
- **Replacement:** Still, I could kick him in the ass.
- **Rationale:** 엉덩이를 걷어차다 explicitly means to kick him in the butt or ass, matching the scene’s crude comic voice.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “일어나, 이 도쿄핫 같은 새끼야.”
- **Current:** “Wake up, you Tokyo Hot bastard.”
- **Defect:** The culturally specific adult-video reference is transliterated without explanation, leaving the insult’s connection to Mujin’s wet dream opaque to many English readers.
- **Replacement:** “Wake up, you Tokyo Hot[^1] bastard.”

[^1]: Tokyo Hot is a Japanese adult-video studio.
- **Rationale:** A brief factual footnote preserves the source reference and explains why Taekyung uses it here without replacing the joke.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아, 그게. 사정이 있어서.”

“…….”

사정이 있었던 거야, 할 뻔한 거야.
- **Current:** “Ah, well. There were circumstances.”

“…”

*Were there circumstances, or had he merely been about to create some?*
- **Defect:** The replacement “create some” is unnatural and fails to convey the pun on 사정, which means both circumstances and ejaculation. The sexual punchline is therefore lost.
- **Replacement:** “Ah, well. Something came up.”

“…”

*Did something come up, or were you about to come?*
- **Rationale:** The replacement recreates the double meaning naturally while retaining the source’s euphemistic setup and sexual payoff.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 내가 아주 그냥. 저 자식을 보면 울화통이 터지고 가슴이 답답해서 못 참겠다.

화병을 높이 들어 올리자 혁무진이 주춤주춤 뒷걸음질 쳤다.
- **Current:** I couldn’t take it anymore. Whenever I looked at this bastard, my blood boiled and my chest tightened.

I raised the vase high, and Hyuk Mujin cautiously backed away.
- **Defect:** The draft preserves the anger and the vase but loses the wordplay connecting symptoms of hwabyeong, or anger illness, with 화병, the flower vase Taekyung raises.
- **Replacement:** I’d had it. Just looking at that bastard made my pent-up rage flare and my chest feel so tight I couldn’t stand it—*hwabyeong*[^2] in the making.

I raised the flower vase high, and Hyuk Mujin cautiously backed away.

[^2]: *Hwabyeong* literally means “anger illness”; *byeong* can also mean “bottle” or “vase,” creating a pun with the flower vase.
- **Rationale:** The source deliberately places anger-related chest symptoms immediately before 화병. The footnote preserves that culturally specific pun without distorting the action.

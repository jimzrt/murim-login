# Review

The draft contains two critical semantic errors involving the direction of the messenger eagle and the identity of the Slaughter Saint, plus several localized terminology, hierarchy, and humor issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아무리 늦어도 칠 주야 안에는 소식이 갈 거다.”
- **Current:** “We’ll hear back within seven days and nights at the latest.”
- **Defect:** The translation reverses the direction of the communication. Gung Gibang says their message will reach Cheongpung’s grandfather within seven days, not that the party will receive a reply by then.
- **Replacement:** “The message will reach him within seven days at the latest.”
- **Rationale:** 소식이 갈 거다 means that the news or message will go to its recipient; it does not promise a response.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 검성(劍星)과 궁성(弓星), 마지막으로 살성(殺星)이라 불리는 그의 존재를.
- **Current:** The man known as the Sword Saint, the Bow Saint, and finally, the Slaughter Saint.
- **Defect:** This makes one man the bearer of all three titles. The source enumerates the Sword Saint, the Bow Saint, and lastly the separate man called the Slaughter Saint.
- **Replacement:** The Sword Saint. The Bow Saint. And finally, the man known as the Slaughter Saint.
- **Rationale:** 검성과 궁성 are separate members of the Three Saints, while 그 refers specifically to the final figure, the Slaughter Saint.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아까 보니 약방문에 푹 빠져서 누가 들어온 줄도 모르더라.
- **Current:** When I saw him earlier, he was so absorbed in his medical books that he didn’t even notice someone had entered.
- **Defect:** 약방문 is a medicinal prescription or formula, not a collection of medical books. The change obscures what Mungyeong is specifically studying and seeking from the Tang Clan.
- **Replacement:** When I saw him earlier, he was so absorbed in a medicinal prescription that he didn’t even notice someone had entered.
- **Rationale:** The source names a prescription directly, consistent with Mungyeong’s established purpose in visiting the Tang Clan.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “입은 삐뚤어져도 말은 바로 하랬다고, 오해 마십시오. 저는 궁 소협만 무시한 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “They say that even if your mouth is crooked, your words should be straight, so don’t misunderstand. I only insulted you, Gung.”
- **Defect:** The line calques the proverb awkwardly, changes 무시하다 from looking down on someone to insulting them, and drops the pointedly polite address Young Hero Gung that carries the joke’s register contrast.
- **Replacement:** “They say you should call a spade a spade, so don’t misunderstand. I only look down on you, Young Hero Gung.”
- **Rationale:** Mujin claims he is merely stating the truth and disrespecting Gung personally rather than the Beggars’ Sect; his formal address sharpens that deliberate distinction.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “와! 은인! 혁 소협이 궁 소협 무시했대요!”
- **Current:** “Wow! Benefactor! Young Hero Hyuk insulted Young Hero Gung!”
- **Defect:** Cheongpung repeats that Hyuk looked down on Gung, not merely that he insulted him. The current wording weakens the direct comic echo of Hyuk’s preceding line.
- **Replacement:** “Wow! Benefactor! Young Hero Hyuk said he looks down on Young Hero Gung!”
- **Rationale:** 무시했대요 explicitly reports Hyuk’s claim of disregarding or looking down on Gung.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 들었던 바에 의하면 독왕의 거처는 아무도 찾지 않는 심산유곡에 마련되어 있었고, 당사독이 보낸 식솔이 비밀리에 한 번씩 들릴 뿐이었으니까.

- **Defect:** The translation turns a singular member of Tang Sadok’s household into multiple blood relatives. 식솔 here denotes someone from his household and does not establish familial kinship.
- **Replacement:** Only a member of Tang Sadok’s household would secretly stop by from time to time.
- **Rationale:** 당사독이 보낸 식솔 is a household member or dependent sent by Tang Sadok, singular in this construction.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 넓고 차가운 전각 안. 창틈 사이를 비집고 들어온 외풍(外風)에 창가에 놓인 호롱불이 흔들린다.
- **Current:** Inside the broad, chilly hall, a draft forced its way through the gaps around the windows, making the oil lamp beside one of them flicker.
- **Defect:** The established rendering for an unnamed 전각 is pavilion, not hall.
- **Replacement:** Inside the broad, chilly pavilion, a draft forced its way through the gaps around the windows, making the oil lamp beside one of them flicker.
- **Rationale:** The binding terminology requires “pavilion” for 전각 unless a specific building has already been established as a hall.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91

- **Current:** Slowly, an old man’s face seemed to emerge over the wavering flame.

# Review

One critical subject reversal, one altered action, one anatomical mistranslation, and two localized wording defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 너 그거 지우면 내가 직접 문화재청에 신고 넣을 거야. 최소 가석방 없는 징역 25년 본다.
- **Current:** If you erase that thing, I’ll report you directly to the Cultural Heritage Administration. I’m looking at a minimum of twenty-five years in prison with no chance of parole.
- **Defect:** The prison sentence is assigned to Jin-ho rather than Taekyung. The Korean means Taekyung would face at least twenty-five years without parole if Jin-ho reported him.
- **Replacement:** If you erase that thing, I’ll report you directly to the Cultural Heritage Administration. You’re looking at a minimum of twenty-five years in prison with no chance of parole.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 본다 is the person Jin-ho is threatening—Taekyung—not Jin-ho himself.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘인벤토리 오픈. 소환.’

스윽, 탁.

명령어와 동시에 예리한 소검의 검자루가 손아귀에 잡혔다.
- **Current:** At the same time as I spoke the command, the hilt of a sharp short sword appeared in my hand.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that Taekyung spoke the command aloud. The source presents it as an italicized internal command and does not say he vocalized it.
- **Replacement:** The instant I gave the command, the hilt of a sharp short sword appeared in my hand.
- **Rationale:** 명령어와 동시에 means the weapon appears simultaneously with the command; it does not specify speech, while the source’s thought formatting indicates an internal invocation.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 창날에 실린 열양지기에 의해 놈의 한쪽 눈동자가 녹아내렸다.
- **Current:** The Scorching Yang Qi carried by the spearhead had melted one of its pupils.
- **Defect:** 눈동자 here refers to the creature’s eye, not merely its pupil. A pupil cannot literally melt, and the following text establishes that the dragon has only one eye remaining.
- **Replacement:** The Scorching Yang Qi carried by the spearhead had melted one of its eyes.
- **Rationale:** The injury destroys one eye, leaving the other as its 하나밖에 남지 않은 눈동자; 동공 is used later when the source specifically means the pupil.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 당시에는 한 대 칠 뻔했는데, 이제 와서 생각해 보니 대한민국 건국 이래 최고의 명언이 따로 없다.
- **Current:** But looking back now, those words were without question the greatest saying in the history of Korea.
- **Defect:** “In the history of Korea” broadens 대한민국 건국 이래, which specifically means since the founding of Korea.
- **Replacement:** But looking back now, those words were without question the greatest saying since the founding of Korea.
- **Rationale:** The source gives a defined starting point—the country’s founding—rather than all Korean history.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 위기감 때문이었을까. 내가 멈칫한 사이, 동아줄처럼 꼬여 창날과도 같은 형태를 취한 빛의 창이 얼굴을 향해 날아들었다.
- **Current:** While I hesitated, a spear of light twisted like a rope and taking the shape of a spearhead flew toward my face.
- **Defect:** The mixed finite and participial construction—“twisted” and “taking”—is ungrammatical and obscures that the strands are twisted together into a spearhead shape.
- **Replacement:** While I hesitated, a spear of light, twisted together like a rope into the shape of a spearhead, flew toward my face.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both linked physical details in 동아줄처럼 꼬여 and 창날과도 같은 형태를 취한 while restoring natural English syntax.

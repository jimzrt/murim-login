# Review

One meaningful System-mechanic mistranslation and several localized fidelity or natural-English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “아직 처치 알림이 안 떴어. 놈이 살아 있…….”

- 처치 알림인지 뭔지 간에, 치료부터 하라고!
- **Current:** “I haven’t gotten a defeat notification yet. That means it’s still ali—”

—Who cares about a defeat notification or whatever! Treat yourself first!
- **Defect:** “Defeat notification” weakens the System mechanic: Jin specifically concludes that the Arch Lich is still alive because he has received no notification confirming its death.
- **Replacement:** “I haven’t gotten a kill notification yet. That means it’s still ali—”

—Who cares about a kill notification or whatever! Treat yourself first!
- **Rationale:** 처치 here means killing or eliminating the monster, as confirmed by Jin’s immediate inference that the Arch Lich remains alive.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 느려진 세상 속에서 튕겨 나간 최상급 포션이 희뿌연 안개 너머로 사라졌다.
- **Current:** In the slowed world, the top-grade potion bounced away and disappeared beyond the pale haze.
- **Defect:** “Bounced away” implies an additional rebound or impact not stated in the source; the potion is knocked from his hand and sent flying.
- **Replacement:** In the slowed world, the top-grade potion went flying and disappeared beyond the pale haze.
- **Rationale:** 튕겨 나간 describes the potion being knocked or flung away after the vine strikes Jin’s wrist.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아크 리치의 입에서 그 어느 때보다 다급한 음성이 터져 나왔다.
- **Current:** A voice more desperate than any I had heard before burst from the Arch Lich’s mouth.
- **Defect:** The comparison is broadened to every voice Jin has ever heard, whereas the source says the Arch Lich sounds more urgent than it ever has before.
- **Replacement:** The Arch Lich’s voice burst out, more urgent than ever.
- **Rationale:** 그 어느 때보다 compares this moment with the Arch Lich’s previous manner, and 다급한 means urgent or pressed rather than globally unmatched desperation.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 더 없이 눈에 익은 누군가의 마법.
- **Current:** A spell belonging to someone far too familiar.
- **Defect:** The English makes the unidentified person familiar, while the Korean directly characterizes the magic as visually unmistakable and familiar.
- **Replacement:** Someone’s all-too-familiar magic.
- **Rationale:** 눈에 익은 modifies the magic Jin recognizes, not his degree of personal familiarity with its caster.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 머리부터 발끝까지, 온통 핏물로 흠뻑 젖은 전신에서 핏방울이 흘러내리고 자의와는 상관없이 팔과 다리가 사시나무처럼 떨렸다.
- **Current:** Blood dripped from my entire body, soaked from head to toe, while my arms and legs trembled like slender branches entirely beyond my control.
- **Defect:** “Slender branches” invents a physical quality and removes the source’s aspen image.
- **Replacement:** Blood dripped from my entire body, which was soaked from head to toe, while my arms and legs trembled like aspen leaves beyond my control.
- **Rationale:** 사시나무처럼 invokes the familiar image of an aspen trembling; “aspen leaves” conveys that image naturally in English.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그랬다가는 겨우 간격을 좁힌 아크 리치를 놓치고 말 것이다.
- **Current:** But if I did that, I would lose the Arch Lich, whose distance I had only just managed to close.
- **Defect:** The possessive relative clause is unnatural English and incorrectly frames the gap as “the Arch Lich’s distance.”
- **Replacement:** But if I did that, I would lose the Arch Lich after only just managing to close the distance.
- **Rationale:** 간격 is the distance between Jin and the Arch Lich, which Jin has only just succeeded in closing.

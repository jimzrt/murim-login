# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes a named cultivation stage, softens an assessment of Taekyung’s survival, gives an admiring thought a disapproving connotation, inconsistently renders Murim qi as force, and contains a duplicated footnote marker.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 최민우는 알지 못했지만, 그는 지금 막 진가심법의 사 성(成)에 도달한 상태였다.
- **Current:** Choi Minwoo did not know it, but he had just reached the fourth level of the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique.
- **Defect:** The draft converts the specifically named cultivation stage 사 성, Four Stars, into the generic fourth level.
- **Replacement:** Choi Minwoo did not know it, but he had just reached Four Stars in the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique.
- **Rationale:** The source identifies a named stage in the technique's progression, not merely its ordinal level.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “어렵지. 벌써 한 시간째 감감무소식이잖아.”
- **Current:** “It's hard to say. We've heard nothing for an hour now.”
- **Defect:** The source gives a pessimistic assessment that Taekyung is unlikely to have survived, while the draft changes it into uncertainty.
- **Replacement:** “It doesn't look good. We haven't heard a thing for an hour now.”
- **Rationale:** In response to whether Taekyung is dead, 어렵지 means his prospects are poor, as reinforced by the reporter's subsequent claim that anyone else would already be presumed dead.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘그런 엄청난 비전을 아무렇지 않게 내놓는 배포, 네임드 몬스터를 홀로 처치하는 실력, 이정룡과 아레스 길드의 이름에도 물러서지 않는 배짱…… 이런 사람이 또 있을까?’
- **Current:** *The nerve to casually offer such an incredible secret art, the skill to defeat a Named Monster alone, the courage not to back down even before Lee Jungryong and the Ares Guild… Is there anyone else like him?*
- **Defect:** The idiom “the nerve to” normally conveys indignation or disapproval, contradicting Minwoo's admiration for Taekyung's bold generosity.
- **Replacement:** *The sheer boldness to casually hand over such an incredible secret art, the skill to defeat a Named Monster alone, the courage not to back down even before Lee Jungryong and the Ares Guild… Is there anyone else like him?*
- **Rationale:** 배포 praises Taekyung's extraordinary boldness and largeness of spirit in freely sharing the technique.

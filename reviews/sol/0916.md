# Review

Two action and causality errors, plus one loss of specificity in Jeok Cheongang’s realization.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 선홍빛 핏물을 뿜어내며 천천히 튕겨 나가는 익숙한 뒷모습
- **Current:** a familiar back slowly spun away, spraying scarlet blood
- **Defect:** “Spun away” changes the motion: the disciple is flung away, not described as rotating.
- **Replacement:** a familiar figure was slowly flung away, his back to Jeok Cheongang as scarlet blood sprayed from him
- **Rationale:** 튕겨 나가는 describes being propelled away; 뒷모습 specifies the view of his back.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그리고 그가 늘그막에 거두어들인 두 번째 제자가, 적천강 자신에게 있어 어떤 의미였는지를.
- **Current:** And that his second Disciple, the one he had taken in in his twilight years, meant something to Jeok Cheongang himself.
- **Defect:** “Meant something” weakens the realization of what the disciple meant to him.
- **Replacement:** And what the second Disciple he had taken in late in life meant to him.
- **Rationale:** 어떤 의미였는지를 frames this as Jeok Cheongang coming to understand the disciple’s significance to him.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 빌어먹게도 충직하게 자신의 명령을 따르느라 멈추지 못했던 수하를, 그래서 화왕이라는 용의 역린(逆鱗)을 건드려 버린 스스로를 탓하며.
- **Current:** He blamed himself for his subordinate, who had obediently followed his command to the end and couldn’t stop—and for having touched the dragon’s reverse scale: the Fire King.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Fire King the reverse scale rather than the dragon whose reverse scale was touched, and loses the separate blame directed at the subordinate.
- **Replacement:** He blamed the subordinate who had been so damned faithful to his orders that he couldn’t stop, and blamed himself for bringing about the touch upon the reverse scale of the dragon called the Fire King.
- **Rationale:** The Fire King is the dragon in the metaphor; his disciple is the vulnerable point the subordinate struck. 수하를 and 스스로를 are both objects of 탓하며.

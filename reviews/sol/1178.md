# Review

The draft changes two speakers’ meaning and weakens the Bow Saint’s reason for obeying the letter. Several smaller wording choices obscure source details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 혁무진이 당당하게 폈던 어깨를 다시 움츠리며 중얼거렸다.

“저돈데요.”
- **Current:** Hyuk Mujin drew in his shoulders again and muttered, “That’s me.”
- **Defect:** Mujin says he, too, told only one person. The draft instead identifies him as the person who was told.
- **Replacement:** Hyuk Mujin drew in his shoulders again and muttered, “Me too.”
- **Rationale:** 저도 means “me too”; his shoulders sink after Cheongpung reveals how widely the secret spread.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “사실 사람이 아니라 신선(神仙)이라든지, 뭐 그런 거요.”
- **Current:** “Actually, you’re not human. You’re an immortal or something.”
- **Defect:** Ju Hwaran is asking whether he might be an immortal, not asserting that he is one.
- **Replacement:** “I mean, you’re not actually an immortal or something instead of a human, are you?”
- **Rationale:** Her tentative question continues “아니죠?” and prompts Taekyung’s reassurance.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 이 서신을 남긴 이가 무신이었기에 그저 믿고 따랐을 뿐, 그 안에 담긴 내용을 쉬이 납득할 수 없었던 것은 처음부터 지금까지 변한 적이 없었다.
- **Current:** She had trusted the person who’d left the letter because he was the Martial God. But she’d never been able to accept what it contained easily—not then, and not now.
- **Defect:** The draft makes her trust in the Martial God the action; the source says she believed and followed the letter because he left it, despite never understanding its contents.
- **Replacement:** She had believed and followed what the letter said only because the Martial God had left it. From the beginning until now, she had never been able to make sense of its contents.
- **Rationale:** 믿고 따랐을 뿐 describes her response to the letter, while 납득할 수 없었던 preserves her continuing doubt.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “선계, 선계라.”
- **Current:** “The realm of immortals. So that’s what it was.”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces her reflective repetition with a conclusion she states separately in the next line.
- **Replacement:** “The realm of immortals… The realm of immortals.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean repeats 선계 without yet declaring what it explains.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그림자의 주인은 이미 오래전 한서불침(寒暑不侵)의 경지를 넘어선 고수이니 당연한 일이었지만, 단지 그렇기 때문만은 아니었다.
- **Current:** It was only natural. The master who cast the shadow had long since surpassed the realm of being unaffected by cold or heat.

But that wasn’t the only reason.
- **Defect:** The draft paraphrases an established named condition instead of using its binding rendering.
- **Replacement:** It was only natural. The master who cast the shadow had long since surpassed the realm of Unaffected by Cold and Heat.

But that wasn’t the only reason.
- **Rationale:** 한서불침 is listed as “Unaffected by Cold and Heat” in the supplied glossary.

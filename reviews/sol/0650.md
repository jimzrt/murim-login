# Review

The draft contains several actionable fidelity errors, including unsupported gendering, an unidentified sender assigned a male pronoun, a reversed reply, and altered implications in key dialogue.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내 처조카가 내궁 경비 소속이잖나. 한 시진 전에 잠깐 비상이 걸린 게 저 친구 때문이었다는군.
- **Current:** “My niece by marriage is part of the Inner Palace guard, you know. Apparently, the brief alert one shichen ago was because of that fellow.”
- **Defect:** The gender-neutral 처조카 is identified specifically as a niece, adding an unsupported identity detail.
- **Replacement:** “My wife’s sibling’s kid is with the Inner Palace guard, you know. Apparently, the brief alert one shichen ago was because of that fellow.”
- **Rationale:** 처조카 specifies the speaker’s wife’s niece or nephew but does not reveal the relative’s gender.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 처조카 말로는 무기를 들고 있는 줄 알고 공격할 뻔했다더군. 횃불로 비춰 보기 전까지는 그게 창인 줄 알았다던데.
- **Current:** “According to my niece, she nearly attacked him because she thought he was holding a weapon. Until she shone a torch on him, she thought it was a spear.”
- **Defect:** The draft repeatedly assigns female identity and pronouns to the gender-neutral relative.
- **Replacement:** “According to the kid, they nearly attacked him because they thought he was holding a weapon. Until they shone a torch on him, they thought it was a spear.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean continues to omit the relative’s gender, so the English must preserve that ambiguity.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 자네라면 피할 거라고 생각했네. 백상 대족장도 자네에게 낭패를 면하지 못했는데, 화살 하나 피하지 못하겠나.
- **Current:** —I thought you’d dodge it. Even Baeksang, a great chieftain in his own right, couldn’t avoid being humiliated by you. Did you really think you couldn’t dodge a single arrow?
- **Defect:** The draft overstates Baeksang’s setback as humiliation and changes Heugung’s rhetorical assessment of Taekyung’s ability into a question about what Taekyung himself believed.
- **Replacement:** —I thought you’d dodge it. Even Great Chieftain Baeksang suffered a setback at your hands. How could you fail to dodge a single arrow?
- **Rationale:** 낭패 denotes a setback or serious difficulty, while 피하지 못하겠나 means Heugung considered it inconceivable that Taekyung could not evade one arrow.

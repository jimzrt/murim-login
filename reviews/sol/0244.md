# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues, including reversed recovery semantics, altered physical mechanics, and a misassigned family resemblance.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 모든 피로와 체력이 회복되었습니다!
- **Current:** - All fatigue and stamina have been restored!
- **Defect:** The English says fatigue itself was restored, which reverses the intended recovery effect.
- **Replacement:** - All fatigue has been cleared, and all stamina has been restored!
- **Rationale:** The notice means that the character is no longer fatigued and that his stamina has been replenished.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한심하다는 표정으로 나를 바라보던 적천강은 한숨을 푹 내쉬었다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang had stared at me with an utterly pathetic expression before letting out a deep sigh.
- **Defect:** The current wording makes Jeok Cheongang’s expression look pathetic rather than conveying that he finds the narrator pathetic.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang looked at me as though I were pathetic, then let out a deep sigh.
- **Rationale:** 한심하다는 표정 describes a look of contempt or exasperation directed at the narrator.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘노야는 어느 정도 걸릴 거라 예상하시는데요?’
- **Current:** How long did you expect it to take?
- **Defect:** The past-tense question changes a prediction being requested within the remembered conversation into a retrospective question.
- **Replacement:** How long do you think it’ll take me?
- **Rationale:** 걸릴 거라 예상하시는데요 asks Jeok Cheongang for his current estimate of how long the narrator will need.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘기생 오래비같이 생겼네.’
- **Current:** You look like a male gisaeng’s older brother.
- **Defect:** The modifier “male” incorrectly describes the gisaeng, and 오래비 here means a gisaeng’s brother rather than necessarily an older brother.
- **Replacement:** You look like a gisaeng’s brother.
- **Rationale:** 기생 오래비 is a culturally specific expression for a delicately handsome man; the existing footnote already supplies that implication.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 대답과 동시에 창을 짧게 잡고 그었다.
- **Current:** At the same time as I answered, I gripped the spear closer to the base and slashed.
- **Defect:** “Closer to the base” reverses the weapon handling: 잡고 짧게 means choking up on the spear to shorten its effective reach, not gripping it nearer the butt.
- **Replacement:** As I answered, I choked up on the spear and slashed.
- **Rationale:** The shortened grip enables the close-range sweep that immediately cuts away the four restraints.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 호랑이가 없으니 여우가 왕 노릇을 하고 있었군.
- **Current:** With no tiger around, the foxes have been acting like kings.
- **Defect:** The singular fox and king have been pluralized, weakening the pointed insult that casts Jeok Cheongang as the fox ruling in the tiger’s absence.
- **Replacement:** With the tiger away, the fox has been playing king.
- **Rationale:** The source uses the singular proverbial hierarchy 호랑이–여우–왕 to belittle Jeok Cheongang specifically.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 얼마 전에 고손자를 봤지.
- **Current:** I saw my great-great-grandson recently.
- **Defect:** The literal “saw” reads as an ordinary visit rather than the idiomatic statement that a great-great-grandson was recently born into his family.
- **Replacement:** I welcomed a great-great-grandson not long ago.
- **Rationale:** In this kinship context, 고손자를 봤지 means that he recently became a great-great-grandfather.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 증손주 며느리가 하북제일미인데, 제 어미를 쏙 빼닮아서 아주 귀엽지.
- **Current:** My great-grandson’s wife is Hebei’s greatest beauty. She takes after her mother to a tee, and she’s absolutely adorable.
- **Defect:** The current translation assigns the resemblance and cuteness to the great-grandson’s wife, but the implied subject is the newly mentioned great-great-grandson, who resembles his mother.
- **Replacement:** My great-grandson’s wife is Hebei’s greatest beauty, and the boy takes after his mother to a tee. He’s absolutely adorable.
- **Rationale:** 제 어미 refers to the child’s own mother—the great-grandson’s wife—so it is the great-great-grandson who resembles her and is cute.

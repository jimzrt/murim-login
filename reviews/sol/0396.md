# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters one preparatory command into an immediate action, weakens two causal or hierarchical details, and contains several localized quantity, idiom, and wording errors.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어둠에서 태어난 존재는 어둠이라는 의미를 모른다.
- **Current:** A being born from darkness had no reason to understand what darkness was.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an assertion that such a being does not understand darkness into a claim that it merely has no reason to understand it.
- **Replacement:** A being born from darkness does not know what darkness means.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the inherent ignorance of a being born in darkness, not its lack of motivation or reason to understand darkness.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 휘하에 놓인 열 기의 데스나이트를 다섯 군단의 사령관으로 삼고, 스스로 한 군단의 선봉장이 되어 이곳에 왔다.
- **Current:** He had made the ten Death Knights under his command the commanders of five legions, then come here as the vanguard of one of them.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the black knight himself into the vanguard rather than preserving his role as the vanguard commander.
- **Replacement:** He had appointed the ten Death Knights under his command to lead five legions, then personally taken command of one legion’s vanguard and come here.
- **Rationale:** 선봉장 is the commander or leader of a vanguard, so the source assigns him a command role rather than identifying him with the vanguard unit itself.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이어지는 지시에 이백여 기의 듀라한(Dullahan)이 정렬했다.
- **Current:** At the next command, more than two hundred Dullahans formed ranks.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the quantity strictly greater than two hundred, while 이백여 indicates an approximate count.
- **Replacement:** At the next command, roughly two hundred Dullahans formed ranks.
- **Rationale:** The source gives an estimated quantity of around two hundred rather than a firm lower bound above two hundred.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 돌격.

- 준비.
- **Current:** —Charge.

—Prepare.
- **Defect:** The two fragments jointly order the troops to prepare for a charge, but the draft first commands them to charge immediately and only afterward says to prepare.
- **Replacement:** —Prepare.

—for the charge.
- **Rationale:** 돌격 준비 means preparation for a charge. This occurs before the spearheads are lowered and before the legion actually begins advancing, so converting it into an immediate charge changes the action sequence.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 드넓은 곡창(穀倉)을 뒤덮은 언데드 군단이 자신들의 사령관을 따라 천천히 진격한다.
- **Current:** The undead legion covering the vast granary advanced slowly behind its commander.
- **Defect:** “Granary” denotes a storage building and does not fit an army spread over sprouting farmland; 곡창 here means a broad grain-producing region or breadbasket.
- **Replacement:** The undead legion blanketing the vast breadbasket advanced slowly behind its commander.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding description places the army across expansive cultivated land, not on or inside a grain-storage structure.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 「지금은 곤란해.」
- **Current:** —It’s not possible right now.
- **Defect:** The draft makes teleportation categorically impossible, although the subsequent discussion establishes that it remains possible but carries an extreme risk.
- **Replacement:** —It’d be too risky right now.
- **Rationale:** 곤란해 expresses that the attempt is difficult or problematic in the present circumstances; Johnson immediately explains that interference could kill them rather than saying teleportation cannot be performed.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 「그래. 하지만 텔레포트를 하는 과정에서 네가 개죽음을 당한다면, 더 많은 사상자가 나오겠지.」
- **Current:** —Yes. But if you die a dog’s death during the teleportation process, even more people will become casualties.
- **Defect:** “Die a dog’s death” is an opaque literal calque of 개죽음을 당하다, whose meaning here is to die pointlessly or for nothing.
- **Replacement:** —Yes. But if you get yourself killed for nothing during the teleportation, there’ll be even more casualties.
- **Rationale:** Johnson is warning that Jin’s needless death would worsen the broader casualty count; the Korean idiom does not refer literally to the manner of a dog’s death.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 쉴 새 없이 욕설을 중얼거리는 매직 존슨을 보며 나는 피식 웃었다.
- **Current:** I snorted quietly as I watched Magic Johnson mutter one curse after another.
- **Defect:** “Snorted” can suggest disdain or irritation and omits the source’s explicit brief laugh.
- **Replacement:** I let out a quiet laugh as I watched Magic Johnson mutter one curse after another.
- **Rationale:** 피식 웃었다 explicitly describes a short, subdued laugh prompted by Jin already understanding Johnson’s reaction.

# Review

One critical subject reversal changes who received the settlement. Several additional passages alter agency, an inclusive teaching arrangement, an idiomatic warning about poor raid performance, and an age-based insult.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 원명훈이 길드장으로 있던 스타 길드에서 천문학적인 금액의 합의금을 받아 냈다는 말이 사실인 모양이다.
- **Current:** Apparently, it was true that Won Myunghoon had extracted an astronomical settlement from the Star Guild.
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed: the settlement was extracted from the Star Guild that Won Myunghoon had led, not extracted by Won Myunghoon from his own Guild.
- **Replacement:** Apparently, it was true that the Peace Guild had extracted an astronomical settlement from the Star Guild, where Won Myunghoon had been Guild Master.
- **Rationale:** The modifier 원명훈이 길드장으로 있던 identifies the Star Guild as the Guild where Won served as Guild Master; it does not make Won the recipient of the settlement.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 혀를 내두르며 펼쳐져 있는 신문 중 하나를 곁눈질했다.
- **Current:** I clicked my tongue and glanced at one of the open newspapers.
- **Defect:** 혀를 내두르다 expresses amazement or astonishment here, whereas “clicked my tongue” suggests annoyance or disapproval.

## F04 — Major

- **Source:** 안 그래도 오늘 아침에 김 집사님과 송이 씨에게 말해 뒀습니다. 돌아가면서 가르치자고요.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내가 안 괜찮아. 그거 알아요? 도움받는 거 버릇되면 레이드 때 똥 싸는 것도 버릇 든다?
- **Current:** I’m not fine with it. You know what happens when you get used to being helped? You get used to shitting yourself during raids, too.
- **Defect:** The draft renders the slang 똥 싸다 literally as losing control from fear. In raid slang, it means performing badly or screwing up, which is the consequence Taekyung links to habitual reliance on help.
- **Replacement:** I’m not fine with it. You know what happens if you make a habit of getting help? You make a habit of shitting the bed during raids, too.
- **Rationale:** “Shitting the bed” preserves both the vulgar register and the intended meaning of repeatedly performing poorly during raids.

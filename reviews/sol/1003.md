# Review

Four actionable fidelity issues: an invented quantity, a changed subject, a lost punchline, and an altered causal image.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 녕하성의 마적단들은 이미 해산된 지 오래입니다. 가장 악명을 떨쳤던 두목들은 모두 죽임을 당했고, 나머지는 저항할 엄두조차 못 냈죠.
- **Current:** “The mounted bandits in Ningxia Province were disbanded a long time ago. Their two most notorious leaders were killed, and the rest didn’t dare resist.
- **Defect:** “Two” invents a number of leaders; the source gives none. It also says the bandit gangs disbanded, not that the bandits themselves did.
- **Replacement:** “The mounted-bandit gangs in Ningxia Province disbanded long ago. Their most notorious leaders were all killed, and the rest didn’t dare resist.
- **Rationale:** 두목들 means leaders, without a count; 마적단들 refers to the gangs.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 만약 그가 암천이 천하 곳곳에 심어둔 또 하나의 복검(覆劍)일 가능성이 조금이라도 있다면, 지금 당장 말머리를 돌리는 것부터 고민해 볼 일이었다.
- **Current:** If there was even the slightest chance he was another hidden weapon Dark Heaven had planted across the world, then we needed to consider turning back right now.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the person considering the decision from the narrator to the group and drops the concrete act of turning his horse.
- **Replacement:** If there was even the slightest chance he was another hidden weapon Dark Heaven had planted across the world, then I’d have to consider turning my horse around right now.
- **Rationale:** The narrator frames turning his own horse around as something he must consider.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그래, 애초에 이곳이 고향인 놈도 있다.

정확히는 ‘놈들’이라고 해야 맞겠지만.
- **Current:** Right. Some of them were natives.

More accurately, though, I should’ve said “some of them.”
- **Defect:** Repeating “some of them” erases the correction from one person to more than one—and the beat that points from Taishan to Sama Pyo.
- **Replacement:** Right. One of them was from here to begin with.

Actually, make that two.
- **Rationale:** 놈 is singular; 놈들 is plural. The narrator deliberately corrects himself.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 화약의 불씨를 당기는 것은 한 사람의 손으로도 충분하지만, 그 폭발의 여파를 잠재우는 데에는 수많은 이들의 도움이 필요한 법이니까.
- **Current:** One person was enough to light the fuse, but it took many people to contain the explosion.
- **Defect:** The draft says many people contain the explosion itself; the source says they must quell its aftermath.
- **Replacement:** One person was enough to light the fuse, but it took many people to deal with the aftermath of the explosion.
- **Rationale:** 폭발의 여파 is the explosion’s aftermath or repercussions, not the explosion.

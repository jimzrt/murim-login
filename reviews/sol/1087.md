# Review

The draft misattributes the Gansu defeat, implies the Blood Lord’s arm regrew, and loses several physical and character cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 감숙에서의 실패를 지적당한 대술사가 싸늘하게 미소 지었다.
- **Current:** The Grand Mage smiled coldly at the reminder of his failure in Gansu.
- **Defect:** The possessive assigns the defeat to the Blood Lord rather than the Grand Mage.
- **Replacement:** The Grand Mage smiled coldly at the reminder of her failure in Gansu.
- **Rationale:** The Grand Mage is the one being reminded of the failure.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 다시 떠올리는 것만으로도 마음 한구석이 서늘해지는, 그때의 자줏빛 검강(劍罡)이 베어 냈던 부위가 새로운 팔이 자리 잡은 지금까지도 욱신거릴 만큼.
- **Current:** Even now, just remembering it made the place where that violet Sword Force had cut him ache deep in his heart—and continued to throb even after his new arm had grown in.
- **Defect:** “Grown in” invents regrowth; the translation also merges his inward chill with the physical throbbing of the severed site.
- **Replacement:** Even remembering that violet Sword Force sent a chill through him. The place it had severed still throbbed, even now that a new arm was in place.
- **Rationale:** The source says a new arm now occupies the place, not how it got there, and distinguishes the chilling memory from the lingering pain.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 혈주의 물음에 더욱 자세를 낮추어 부복한 전령이 대답했다.
- **Current:** At the Blood Lord’s question, the messenger bowed lower before answering.
- **Defect:** Bowing lower loses the messenger’s prostration.
- **Replacement:** At the Blood Lord’s question, the messenger lowered himself further, prostrating himself before he answered.
- **Rationale:** 부복한 specifies that he lies prostrate, reinforcing his fear and subordinate position.

## F04 — Major

- **Source:** “주둥이 닥쳐라. 그 자리에 있지도 않았던 네년 따위가 왈가왈부할 만한 일이 아니니까.”
- **Current:** “Shut your mouth. You weren’t even there. This is no business of yours.”

- **Rationale:** 네년 따위 is a contemptuous address, and 왈가왈부 dismisses her right to comment on what happened.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “넌 결정을 내리기에 앞서 내게 상의해야 했어. 단 두 번밖에 쓸 수 없는 귀한 마법진(魔法陳)을 고작 그런 것에 쓰다니.”
- **Current:** “You should have consulted me before making your decision. To waste a precious magic formation, one that can only be used twice, on something like this…”

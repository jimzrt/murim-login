# Review

Six actionable findings: two altered scene facts, one lost wordplay, one characterization error, one mistranslated cultural metaphor, and one added frequency.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 굳이 내가 아니더라도 함께 하는 누군가가 크나큰 위험에 처했을 때, 한 번쯤은 목숨을 건질 수 있을 테니까.
- **Current:** Even if it was not me, someone traveling with him might find their life in grave danger one day. At least then, they might save their life once.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the endangered companion save their own life. The source means Mungyeong could save the life of someone accompanying them, even if that person were not Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** Even if it wasn’t me, Mungyeong might one day save the life of someone traveling with us when they found themselves in grave danger.
- **Rationale:** 목숨을 건질 수 있을 테니까 describes the endangered companion surviving because Mungyeong is present; it does not make that companion the agent of their own rescue.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 한 시진 전쯤이었나.
- **Current:** It had been about an hour earlier.
- **Defect:** The draft halves the stated time. One shichen is approximately two hours, not one hour.
- **Replacement:** It had been about one shichen earlier.
- **Rationale:** 시진 is the established term and denotes a traditional interval of roughly two hours.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 껌뻑 죽기는 니미. 껌뻑 죽이는 거겠지.
- **Current:** Do anything, my ass. He would kill anyone he looked at.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Mungyeong’s prospective victim from Hyuk Mujin to an indiscriminate “anyone” and loses the wordplay between being utterly devoted and killing someone in a blink.
- **Replacement:** Do anything I say, my ass. He’d kill you in the blink of an eye.
- **Rationale:** The second 껌뻑 죽이는 turns Mujin’s boast back on him: rather than Mungyeong metaphorically dying for Mujin, Mungyeong would kill Mujin almost instantly.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금껏 누구와도 견줄 수 없던 천재가, 처음으로 호승심을 불태우고 있었다.
- **Current:** A genius who had never been compared to anyone else was burning with competitive pride for the first time.
- **Defect:** The draft says Cheongpung had simply never been compared with anyone. The source says no one had previously been comparable to or able to rival him.
- **Replacement:** A genius no one had ever been able to rival was burning with competitive pride for the first time.
- **Rationale:** 누구와도 견줄 수 없던 expresses Cheongpung’s unmatched ability, which explains why Taekyung is the first person to awaken his competitive pride.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 그리고 지금의 하남은 수많은 용과 호랑이가 득실거리는 잠저(潛邸)다.
- **Current:** And Henan right now was a hidden court teeming with countless dragons and tigers.
- **Defect:** “Hidden court” does not convey 잠저, the residence and nascent court of a future ruler before accession. The metaphor presents Henan as a place from which Taekyung can recruit the talented retainers of his future organization.
- **Replacement:** And Henan right now was a future king’s court, teeming with countless dragons and tigers.
- **Rationale:** 잠저 refers to a ruler’s residence before ascending the throne, not a court concealed from view.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 물론 함께 있다 보면 심심찮게 암살 시도를 당할 일이 있겠지만, 혹여나 서천마군 같은 괴물을 만나 죽는 것보다야 피똥 몇 번 싸는 것이 훨씬 낫다는 것이 내 지론이었다.
- **Current:** Of course, if I stayed with him, I would probably have to deal with assassination attempts three times a day. But in my view, shitting blood a few times was far preferable to dying after running into a monster like the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- **Defect:** The draft adds “three times a day” to the actual assassination attempts. That frequency appears only in the following paragraph, where Taekyung says Mungyeong wants to kill him about three times a day.
- **Replacement:** Of course, if I stayed with him, I would probably find myself on the receiving end of assassination attempts from time to time. But in my view, shitting blood a few times was far preferable to dying after running into a monster like the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- **Rationale:** 심심찮게 means fairly often or from time to time; it does not specify three daily attempts or equate Mungyeong’s murderous urges with actual attempts.

# Review

The draft contains one invented magical mechanism, omits the explicit one-man-army characterization, alters the mutual-slaughter description, and weakens several important action and thematic beats.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 홀로 능히 일천, 일만의 몫을 해내는 일인군단(一人軍團)의 헌터가.
- **Current:** A Hunter who could single-handedly do the work of a thousand—or even ten thousand.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicit description of the needed Hunter as a “one-man army,” weakening the source’s characterization and emphasis.
- **Replacement:** A Hunter who was a one-man army, capable of doing the work of a thousand—or even ten thousand—single-handedly.
- **Rationale:** 일인군단 explicitly identifies such a Hunter as a one-man army rather than merely describing his capabilities.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 회색빛을 넘어 검기까지 한 안개가 흘러나와 곳곳으로 스며든다.
- **Current:** Mist darker than gray—even black with Sword Energy—flowed out and seeped into every corner.
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 검기까지 한, meaning that the mist was dark enough to be black, as the technical term Sword Energy and invents a magical property absent from the scene.
- **Replacement:** A mist darker than gray—black, in fact—flowed out and seeped into every corner.
- **Rationale:** Here 검기 is the nominalized adjectival sense of 검다, “to be black”; the construction says the mist was even black, not infused with Sword Energy.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 오직 서로를 죽고 죽여야만 끝나는, 끔찍한 혈투의 시작이었다.
- **Current:** It was the beginning of a horrific bloodbath that could end only when one side killed the other.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the reciprocal image of both sides killing and being killed into a conventional victory condition in which one side defeats the other.
- **Replacement:** It was the beginning of a horrific bloodbath that could end only after they had killed and been killed by one another.
- **Rationale:** 서로를 죽고 죽여야 describes mutual killing and death, emphasizing the battle’s horrific attrition rather than merely the elimination of one side.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 위버맨쉬는 비극 속에서도 일어나는 초인이며, 가능성을 극한까지 실현하고자 하는 위대한 극복자라고.
- **Current:** The Übermensch was a superhuman who rose even in tragedy, a great overcomer who sought to realize the limits of possibility.
- **Defect:** “Realize the limits of possibility” suggests discovering where possibility ends, rather than realizing one’s potential to the utmost.
- **Replacement:** The Übermensch was a superhuman who rose even amid tragedy, a great overcomer who sought to realize his potential to the utmost.
- **Rationale:** 가능성을 극한까지 실현하다 means to actualize potential as fully as possible, which is the thematic basis for Schumacher’s apparent breakthrough.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘베였다.’

벤 것이 아니라, 베였다.
- **Current:** *I’ve been cut.*

Not cut something.

Been cut.
- **Defect:** The central contrast between having cut the enemy and having been cut is rendered in ungrammatical fragments that obscure the realization.
- **Replacement:** *I’d been cut.*

Not *I cut it.*

*I’d been cut.*
- **Rationale:** The source pointedly contrasts the active 벤 것 with the passive 베였다; the replacement preserves that grammatical and dramatic reversal.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마지막 순간. 모든 감각이 우두머리를 향해 집중된 틈을 타, 그의 등을 녹슨 검으로 벤 미노타우로스의 목을 쳐 날린 동료들이 그를 부축했다.
- **Current:** At the final moment, while all his senses were focused on the chieftain, his comrades had struck down the Minotaur that had slashed his back with a rusted sword and rushed to support him.
- **Defect:** “Struck down” omits the specific action that Schumacher’s comrades hacked the attacking Minotaur’s head off.
- **Replacement:** At the last moment, while all his senses were fixed on the chieftain, a Minotaur had slashed his back with a rusty sword. His comrades hacked its head off and caught him as he staggered.
- **Rationale:** 목을 쳐 날린 explicitly states that the comrades severed and sent the Minotaur’s head flying before supporting Schumacher.

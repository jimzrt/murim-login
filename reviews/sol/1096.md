# Review

The draft changes a sect’s identity, a distance, and the scope of the proposed self-injury. It also omits a tactical action and implies a blood relationship the source does not establish.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 공동과 곤륜이라는 저 유서 깊은 두 도맥(道脈)을 이끄는 현천진인과 청허자.
- **Current:** Perfected Being Hyeoncheon and Cheongheoja, who led the venerable Daoist lineages of Wudang and Kunlun.
- **Defect:** Wudang replaces Kongtong, changing Hyeoncheon’s sect in this passage.
- **Replacement:** Perfected Being Hyeoncheon and Cheongheoja, who led the venerable Daoist lineages of Kongtong and Kunlun.
- **Rationale:** The source names 공동 and 곤륜: Kongtong and Kunlun.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 서서히 속도를 줄인 포달랍궁의 군세가 이십여 장 밖에서 멈춰섰다.
- **Current:** The Potala Palace’s army slowed and came to a halt some twenty yards away.
- **Defect:** Twenty jang is about sixty meters, not twenty yards.
- **Replacement:** The Potala Palace’s army slowed and came to a halt some sixty meters away.
- **Rationale:** 이십여 장 specifies roughly twenty jang; the draft substantially shortens the distance.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진태경이 스스로 사지근맥을 끊는 멍청한 짓거리를 하지 않기를 바랐다.
- **Current:** But deep down, he hoped Jin Taekyung wouldn’t do something foolish and sever his own Sinews and Meridians.
- **Defect:** The draft drops 사지, which specifies the sinews and meridians of all four limbs, and turns the phrase into a capitalized attribute.
- **Replacement:** But deep down, he hoped Jin Taekyung wouldn’t do something foolish and sever the sinews and meridians in his own arms and legs.
- **Rationale:** 사지 means the four limbs; the source describes a specific physical act.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 조금 전 혈주의 손짓과 함께, 좌우로 갈라진 포위망을 벗어나 성문으로 향하는 적들의 뒷모습을 바라보던 대술사가 입술을 뗐다.
- **Current:** The Grand Mage, who had watched the enemy retreat toward the gates after the Blood Lord’s signal moments earlier, spoke up.
- **Defect:** The encirclement splitting to either side so the enemy could pass is omitted.
- **Replacement:** The Grand Mage watched the enemy’s backs as they passed through the encirclement, which had parted to either side at the Blood Lord’s signal, and headed for the gates. Then she spoke.
- **Rationale:** 좌우로 갈라진 포위망을 벗어나 describes how the Blood Lord let them leave.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 궁주께서 그토록 찾아 헤매던 열화문(烈火門)의 종자들도 저들 중에 있었지요.
- **Current:** The descendants of the Fire Gate Clan you’ve been searching for so desperately were among them.
- **Defect:** “Descendants” implies a blood relationship not established by 종자들 here.
- **Replacement:** The Fire Gate Clan’s scions you’ve been searching for so desperately were among them.
- **Rationale:** 종자들 can refer to those who carry on the clan; the named pair are Jeok Cheongang and his Disciple Jin Taekyung.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 화왕 그 늙은이의 신병은 물론이고 충분한 보상을 드리지요.
- **Current:** I’ll hand over the Fire King’s person, and give you a suitable reward.
- **Defect:** “The Fire King’s person” obscures the promised transfer of custody over Jeok Cheongang.
- **Replacement:** I’ll hand that old man, the Fire King, over to you—and give you a generous reward besides.
- **Rationale:** 신병 means custody of the person; 충분한 보상 is ample compensation.

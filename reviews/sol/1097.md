# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it loses a shift in the balance of power and mischaracterizes Taekyung’s earlier fight. It also conflicts with the packet’s established regional rendering.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 광활한 서장의 지배자인 자신조차도
- **Current:** even the ruler of Tibet’s vast lands
- **Defect:** Uses “Tibet” where the packet’s continuity decision calls for “Xizang” in Murim narration.
- **Replacement:** even the ruler of Xizang’s vast lands
- **Rationale:** The viewpoint is the Dalai Lama’s, not Taekyung identifying the region from his modern-world perspective.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 서장의 지배하는 교국(敎國)의 왕
- **Current:** the king of the theocratic state that ruled Tibet
- **Defect:** Repeats the inconsistent regional rendering.
- **Replacement:** the king of the theocratic state that ruled Xizang
- **Rationale:** The packet specifies “Xizang” for the Murim region outside Taekyung’s modern-world identification.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 손톱만큼이라도 수평에 가까워졌던 힘의 무게추가, 포달랍궁의 가세로 인하여 전보다도 더욱 기울어져 버린 것이다.
- **Current:** The balance of power had tipped even further against us now that the Potala Palace had joined the fight.
- **Defect:** Omits that their victory had briefly brought the balance a tiny bit closer to even before the Potala Palace’s arrival reversed that gain.
- **Replacement:** For a moment, the balance of power had edged the tiniest bit closer to even. With the Potala Palace’s arrival, it had tipped further against us than before.
- **Rationale:** 손톱만큼이라도 수평에 가까워졌던 explicitly marks the small, temporary improvement.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 감히 자신과 맞섰던 하룻강아지의 그 처절하던 모습을.
- **Current:** the pitiful sight of that young pup who’d dared to face him.
- **Defect:** “Pitiful” recasts Taekyung’s desperate struggle as a pathetic appearance.
- **Replacement:** how desperately that young pup had fought when he dared to face him.
- **Rationale:** 처절하던 describes the harrowing intensity of his struggle; it does not say he looked pitiful.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 성공 시에 얻게 될 것을 생각한다면, 이 목숨 하나로는 충분히 남는 장사지.
- **Current:** Considering what we’d gain if we succeeded, my life would be more than enough to pay for it.
- **Defect:** Frames his life as more than the necessary price rather than the risk being worthwhile for the potential gain.
- **Replacement:** Considering what we’d gain if we succeeded, staking my one life would be a bargain.
- **Rationale:** 남는 장사 describes a profitable trade from the speaker’s perspective.

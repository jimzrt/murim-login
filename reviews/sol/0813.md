# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes whether the first strike landed and loses the item’s single-use limit. Several lines also soften or obscure the source.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가장 처음의 일격은 심장 어림을 정확히 파고들었다.
- **Current:** My very first strike had gone straight for the area around his heart.
- **Defect:** “Gone straight for” describes the strike’s aim, not its successful penetration.
- **Replacement:** My very first strike had pierced the area around his heart.
- **Rationale:** 파고들었다 says the blow drove into the area around his heart.

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 종류 : 일회용 아이템

- **Defect:** “Consumable” does not convey that the item can be used only once.
- **Replacement:** > **Type:** Single-Use Item
- **Rationale:** 일회용 explicitly specifies one use.

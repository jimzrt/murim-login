# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters two physical or factual details, loses several pragmatic nuances, and contains a few localized terminology and English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 설마 했는데.
- **Current:** I had suspected as much.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the narrator’s disbelieving hope that his fear was unfounded into confident prior suspicion.
- **Replacement:** I’d thought there was no way.
- **Rationale:** 설마 conveys incredulity or the hope that an alarming possibility cannot be true; the following sentence then confirms that his unease was correct.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 씁쓸한 입맛을 다시며 그가 내민 손을 굳게 맞잡았다.
- **Current:** I gave a bitter smile and firmly grasped the hand he held out.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the narrator’s bitter lip-smacking gesture with a smile.
- **Replacement:** I smacked my lips bitterly and firmly grasped the hand he held out.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다시며 describes smacking or working one’s lips, not smiling; this changes the stated physical action.

## F03 — Minor

- **Rationale:** 전혀요 directly means “Not at all,” which preserves the terse, dry response to whether they have been well.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** “결계야. 어지간한 S급 헌터조차 눈치채지 못할 만큼 비밀스럽고, 그만큼 완벽하지.”
- **Current:** “It’s a barrier. It’s so discreet that even most S-rank Hunters wouldn’t notice it—and that’s precisely what makes it so perfect.”
- **Defect:** The draft introduces a causal claim that the barrier’s secrecy is what makes it perfect, rather than describing secrecy and perfection as corresponding qualities.
- **Replacement:** “It’s a barrier. It’s hidden well enough to escape even most S-rank Hunters’ notice, and it’s every bit as flawless.”
- **Rationale:** 그만큼 correlates the barrier’s degree of perfection with how well concealed it is; it does not explicitly state that concealment causes its perfection.

## F08 — Minor

- **Rationale:** 더욱 가파르게 증가 describes an increasingly steep rise, not the distribution itself becoming geometrically steeper.

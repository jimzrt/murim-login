# Review

The draft contains two critical action and identity errors, two major causal or evidentiary distortions, and two localized issues involving humor and natural terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그는 장 씨의 집을 나선 순간부터 그들의 대화를 듣고 있었다.
- **Current:** He had been listening to their conversation ever since he left Old Man Jang’s house.
- **Defect:** The draft identifies the house as Jang Taebo’s, which incorrectly implies that Jeok Cheongang had just left the destination he was approaching. The source refers to the Jang household he departed from—Jang-pal’s house.
- **Replacement:** He had been listening to their conversation ever since he left Jang-pal’s house.
- **Rationale:** This preserves Jeok Cheongang’s route and distinguishes Jang-pal’s home from Jang Taebo’s destination.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 창의 이미지를 떠올리며 마음속으로 외치기만 하면 된다.
- **Current:** All I had to do was picture the Inventory window and shout the words in my mind.
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 창 as “window.” Here it means the spear Taekyung intends to summon, changing the physical mechanism of Inventory summoning.
- **Replacement:** All I had to do was picture the spear and shout the words in my mind.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding passage explicitly concerns summoning a weapon, and Taekyung must visualize the spear rather than an Inventory interface.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘저 성취에 만년한철까지 가져올 정도면 구파일방? 뭐, 어쨌든 명문 정파 소속인 건 확실해 보이는군.’
- **Current:** *With that level of skill and enough Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron to bring here, could they be from the Nine Sects and One Gang? Well, either way, they clearly belong to a prestigious orthodox faction.*
- **Defect:** “Enough … to bring here” invents a quantity-based inference. Jeok Cheongang is judging their affiliation from their accomplishments and their possession of the exceptional material.
- **Replacement:** *Given their accomplishments and the fact that they’d even brought Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, could they be from the Nine Sects and One Gang? Well, either way, they clearly belong to a prestigious orthodox faction.*
- **Rationale:** The source treats bringing Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron as additional evidence of prestigious backing, not as a statement about having a sufficient quantity.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 이만하면 얻어먹은 밥값으로 충분하니 이 길로 떠날 생각이었다.
- **Current:** The meal he had received was more than enough payment. He intended to leave immediately.
- **Defect:** The causal relationship is reversed: the meal was not payment to Jeok Cheongang. He considers what he has done sufficient repayment for the meal he was given.
- **Replacement:** He had done enough to repay the meal he’d been given, so he intended to leave at once.
- **Rationale:** 밥값 refers to repaying the hospitality he received, which explains why he feels free to depart.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 짬에서 나오는 바이브인가.
- **Current:** *Is that the vibe you get after spending years in the same place?*
- **Defect:** The draft interprets 짬 as time spent in one location rather than accumulated experience or seniority, weakening the joke.
- **Replacement:** *Is that the vibe that comes with experience?*
- **Rationale:** The line jokes that the old man’s intimidating authority comes from his immense accumulated experience, not from remaining somewhere for years.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96

- **Current:** “The Jin Family of Taiyuan? Are you perhaps from the demonic, heterodox arts?”

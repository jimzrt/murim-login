# Review

Two critical errors reverse the political hierarchy and inflate the final bill from 105 to 405 nyang. Several additional passages alter subjects, actions, or meaningful pragmatic cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그건 태원진가가 산서성의 맹주(盟主)가 아닌 패자(霸者)일 때나 가능한 이야기예요.
- **Current:** That would only be possible if the Jin Family of Taiyuan were the Alliance Leader of Shanxi rather than its hegemon.
- **Defect:** The political distinction is reversed: Wolhwa says seizing the reward would be possible if the family were Shanxi’s hegemon rather than its Alliance Leader.
- **Replacement:** That would only be possible if the Jin Family of Taiyuan were Shanxi’s hegemon rather than its Alliance Leader.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly contrasts 패자 with 맹주 in that order, and the distinction drives Wolhwa’s explanation of why the family cannot simply seize territory.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 은자 백오 냥 하고도 철전 이십삼 냥입니다.
- **Current:** “Four hundred and five nyang, along with twenty-three iron coins.”
- **Defect:** The quantity is mistranslated: 백오 is 105, not 405.
- **Replacement:** “One hundred and five nyang, along with twenty-three iron coins.”
- **Rationale:** The inflated bill is specifically 105 nyang and twenty-three iron coins; changing it to 405 materially alters the scene’s financial fact.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 나흘 전에 확인하신 것이 전부입니다. 추가 정보를 수집하고는 있습니다만…….
- **Current:** “Four days ago was everything we were able to confirm. We are still gathering additional information, but…”
- **Defect:** The first sentence is grammatically malformed and changes the honorific subject: the source says that what Wolhwa confirmed four days ago remains the entirety of their information.
- **Replacement:** “What you confirmed four days ago is all we have. We’re still gathering additional information, but…”
- **Rationale:** 확인하신 addresses Wolhwa honorifically as the person who confirmed the information; it does not mean that the subordinate’s group confirmed everything four days ago.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이곳에 들어온 후부터 내부 장기까지 훤히 읽히고 있는 거나 마찬가지겠지.
- **Current:** Ever since we had entered the place, she had probably seen straight through every detail of its inner workings.
- **Defect:** The possessive subject is changed. The source hyperbolically says Wolhwa can see through the visitors down to their internal organs, not that she understands the inn’s inner workings.
- **Replacement:** Ever since we entered this place, she had probably seen right through us, down to our innards.
- **Rationale:** 내부 장기 refers to their internal organs and emphasizes how completely the information merchant has them under observation.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 검을 펼치다 보니 흥에 취했다.
- **Current:** “I got carried away while drawing my sword.”
- **Defect:** The physical action is altered. Jin Mukyung was performing or practicing his sword technique, not merely drawing the weapon.
- **Replacement:** “I got carried away while practicing my swordsmanship.”
- **Rationale:** 검을 펼치다 means to unfold or display one’s sword technique in this context; reducing it to drawing the sword changes the mechanism by which he cut down five trees.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 되로 주고 말로 받았군.
- **Current:** She’d thrown my own words right back at me.
- **Defect:** The rendering loses the idiom’s disproportionate-payback joke, which says he gave a small measure and received a much larger one in return.
- **Replacement:** Talk about getting paid back tenfold.
- **Rationale:** A 말 is ten times a 되; the following sentence already explains that Wolhwa returned his own words, so this line should preserve the idiom’s comic escalation.

# Review

The draft contains several altered subjects, quantities, actions, and implied identities, along with a euphemism made misleadingly explicit.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “……귀가 밝구먼. 알았으니까 와서 앉기나 하게.”
- **Current:** “……He’s got good ears. Fine, just come sit down.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied subject from Cheongpung, whom the porter is addressing, to a third-person remark about him.
- **Replacement:** “……You’ve got sharp ears. Fine, just come sit down.”
- **Rationale:** 귀가 밝구먼 directly comments on Cheongpung’s sharp hearing before telling him to come sit down.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “어떻게 이런 놈을 옆에 끼고 달포씩이나 버티셨소?”
- **Current:** “How have you lasted nearly a month with someone like him beside you?”
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the duration: 달포 is a little over a month, not less than one month.
- **Replacement:** “How have you lasted over a month with someone like him beside you?”
- **Rationale:** 달포 denotes a period slightly longer than a month, while “nearly a month” denotes less than a month.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “저거 뭐 하는 놈입니까?”
- **Current:** “What the hell is he doing?”
- **Defect:** The porter is asking what kind of person Cheongpung is, not what activity he is currently performing.
- **Replacement:** “What kind of guy is he?”
- **Rationale:** 뭐 하는 놈 in this context questions Cheongpung’s character or background after his bizarre reply.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “전 재밌었는데요?”
- **Current:** “I thought it was fun.”
- **Defect:** The declarative English loses the contrasting, mildly puzzled response conveyed by -는데요.
- **Replacement:** “But I had fun.”
- **Rationale:** Cheongpung is casually contradicting their assumption that his life must have been difficult, and that pragmatic contrast is part of the humor.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “네. 어쩌다 보니 헛걸음을 하게 됐는데…… 지금도 나쁘지 않아요. 표행이란 것도 상당히 재밌어요.”
- **Current:** “Yes. I ended up making a wasted trip somehow, but I don’t mind it even now. This escort work is pretty interesting, too.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes 지금도 나쁘지 않아요 mean that Cheongpung still does not mind the wasted trip; the source says his present circumstances are not bad despite that wasted trip.
- **Replacement:** “Yes. It turned out to be a wasted trip, but… things aren’t bad now, either. This escort work is pretty interesting, too.”
- **Rationale:** 지금도 refers to his current situation, which he immediately illustrates by saying that escort work is interesting.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 더군다나 평생 산에서 살아온 화전민 출신이니 그럴 법도 하다.
- **Current:** Besides, he had lived in the mountains his entire life as the son of a slash-and-burn farmer. It made sense that he would feel this way.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a parent-child identity. The source says Seokchil believes Cheongpung himself comes from a slash-and-burn farming background, not specifically that his parent was such a farmer.
- **Replacement:** Besides, he had lived in the mountains his entire life and came from a slash-and-burn farming community. It made sense that he would feel this way.
- **Rationale:** 화전민 출신 identifies Cheongpung’s presumed background; it does not identify his father or mother.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 청풍이 품에서 누리끼리한 죽간 하나를 꺼내어 보여 줬다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung pulled a yellowish bamboo slip from inside his clothes and handed it over.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the physical action from showing the contract to handing it over.
- **Replacement:** Cheongpung pulled a yellowish bamboo slip from inside his clothes and showed it to them.
- **Rationale:** 보여 줬다 means he displayed or showed the slip; the source reserves an explicit handing-over action for the silver ingots later.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “혹시 몸으로 때우라는?”
- **Current:** “Does it mean I have to pay with my body?”
- **Defect:** The literal wording introduces a strong sexual implication absent from the Korean euphemism.
- **Replacement:** “Does it mean I’d have to work it off?”
- **Rationale:** 몸으로 때우다 here means discharging the debt through labor or bodily service rather than paying money; it is not a sexual proposition.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 석칠의 눈이 뒤집힌 그때였다.
- **Current:** It was then that Seokchil’s eyes nearly rolled back in his head.
- **Defect:** The draft renders the Korean anger idiom as a literal physical symptom suggestive of fainting or seizure.
- **Replacement:** It was just then, as Seokchil was about to snap.
- **Rationale:** 눈이 뒤집히다 in this context means becoming enraged or losing control, not literally rolling one’s eyes backward.

## F10 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “집 나오면서 노잣돈을 좀 받았거든요.”
- **Current:** “When I left home, my family gave me some traveling money.”
- **Defect:** The draft supplies Cheongpung’s family as the giver even though the source deliberately omits who gave him the money.
- **Replacement:** “I was given some traveling money when I left home.”
- **Rationale:** 받았거든요 states only that Cheongpung received the money. The following narration speculates about his household, so the dialogue should not prematurely resolve the giver.

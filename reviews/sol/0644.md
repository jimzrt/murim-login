# Review

One altered physical action, one misgrouped flood report, and several losses of wordplay, cultural context, and idiomatic force.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 화륵. 훅.

위태롭게 흔들리던 횃불이 사라진다.
- **Current:** *Fwoosh. Whoomph.*

The wavering torch vanished.
- **Defect:** The draft says the physical torch vanished, changing the action. The source describes the wavering flame or torchlight going out and plunging the hall into darkness.
- **Replacement:** *Fwoosh. Whoomph.*

The wavering torchlight went out.
- **Rationale:** The ensuing darkness is caused by the torchlight being extinguished, not by the torch itself physically disappearing.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 갑작스럽게 내린 폭우로 홍수가 발생했습니다. 목초지를 비롯한 인근 가호(家戶) 오십여 채가 물에 잠겼고…….
- **Current:** “Sudden torrential rain caused a flood. More than fifty nearby households, including the pastures, were submerged……”
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly makes the pastures part of the fifty households. The source lists the pasture and approximately fifty nearby homes as separate things that were flooded.
- **Replacement:** “Sudden torrential rain caused a flood. The pasture and around fifty nearby homes were submerged……”
- **Rationale:** 목초지를 비롯한 introduces the pasture alongside the nearby households; a pasture cannot be included within a count of households.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “존명.”

이제는 환청까지 왔는지, 존명이 좆망으로 들린다.

‘남만 좆망.’
- **Current:** “Yes, my lord.”

Now I was hallucinating voices too, because *Yes, my lord* sounded like *Nanman is fucked*.

*What kind of tribal grand council is this supposed to be?*
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the source’s explicit 존명/좆망 sound-based joke with two English phrases that do not sound alike, and it omits the separate punchline “Nanman is fucked.” It instead moves the following thought into that position.
- **Replacement:** “Jonmyeong.”[^2]

Now I was hearing things too, because *jonmyeong* sounded like *jonmang*—“fucked.”

*Nanman is fucked.*

[^2]: *Jonmyeong* is a formal acknowledgment meaning “As you command”; it differs by one vowel from *jonmang*, crude slang for being utterly ruined.
- **Rationale:** Retaining the closely matched Korean sounds with a concise footnote preserves both the mechanism and the uncensored punchline of the joke.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** K-무림식 해석으로는 단군 할아버지 절친이 세운 문파, 뭐 그쯤 되겠지.
- **Current:** By a K-Murim-style interpretation, it was more or less a sect founded by Grandfather Dangun’s best friend.
- **Defect:** The culturally specific “Grandfather Dangun” ancestry joke is retained without enough context for an English reader to understand why it signifies an extremely remote historical connection.
- **Replacement:** By K-Murim logic, it was basically a sect founded by Grandfather Dangun’s best friend.[^1]

[^1]: Dangun is the legendary founder of Korea’s first kingdom; “Grandfather Dangun” is a familiar, joking way to invoke remote shared ancestry.
- **Rationale:** The footnote supplies the factual cultural premise needed for the joke without adding plot information.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “내가 헷갈렸군. 자네가 자식을 한둘 낳았어야지. 아무리 기운이 좋아도 열다섯 명이나 낳는 게 말이 되나?”
- **Current:** “I got confused. You should have had one or two children. No matter how vigorous you are, how can you have fifteen?”
- **Defect:** “You should have had one or two children” reads as a literal recommendation to have children rather than the sarcastic complaint that he had far too many for anyone to keep straight.
- **Replacement:** “I got confused. If only you’d stopped at one or two. No matter how vigorous you are, how can you have fifteen?”
- **Rationale:** The replacement expresses the idiomatic force of 자식을 한둘 낳았어야지 while preserving the speaker’s teasing complaint.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이어지려는 목소리를 힘주어 끊어 낸 나는, 가라앉은 눈빛으로 백상을 응시하며 말을 이었다.
- **Current:** I forcefully cut off the words that were about to continue, fixed Baeksang with a sunken gaze, and went on.
- **Defect:** “A sunken gaze” suggests hollow or recessed eyes and is not natural English for 가라앉은 눈빛, which describes a grave, subdued, or cold steadiness. “Cut off the words that were about to continue” is also an awkward impersonal rendering of interrupting Baeksang.
- **Replacement:** I forcefully cut him off before he could continue, fixing Baeksang with a grave, steady gaze as I went on.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the interruption and Taekyung’s controlled, serious expression without introducing a physical description of his eyes.

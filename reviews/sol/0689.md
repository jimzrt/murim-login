# Review

Several fidelity issues alter duration, emotional contrast, rhetorical register, and animal staging; localized details also lose anatomical, quantitative, and visual precision.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그리고 멍하니 이마 위로 떨어지는 물방울을 바라보던 그녀의 뇌리에, 잠시 잊고 있던 기억들이 하나둘씩 떠올랐다.
- **Current:** As she stared blankly at the drops of water falling onto her forehead, long-forgotten memories began returning to her one by one.
- **Defect:** “Long-forgotten” changes the duration: Yohi had forgotten these memories only briefly, not for a long period.
- **Replacement:** As she stared blankly at the drops of water falling onto her forehead, memories she had briefly forgotten began returning one by one.
- **Rationale:** 잠시 means “briefly” or “for a moment,” so the draft introduces a substantially longer lapse than the source.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 온갖 영약을 섭취하며 축적한 공력이 하단전에 충만한 것을 느낄 수 있었으니까.
- **Current:** She could feel the internal energy she had accumulated by consuming all kinds of elixirs filling her dantian.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the internal energy fills her lower dantian specifically.
- **Replacement:** She could feel the internal energy she had accumulated by consuming all kinds of elixirs filling her lower dantian.
- **Rationale:** 하단전 identifies the lower dantian rather than the dantian generically.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이제는 암천에 의해 사로잡혔다는 절망감이 아닌, 이해할 수 없는 공간에 들어왔다는 막막함이 들 정도로.
- **Current:** She no longer felt only despair at having been captured by Dark Heaven. Now, she was overwhelmed by the hopelessness of having entered an incomprehensible space.
- **Defect:** “No longer felt only despair” makes the two emotions coexist, while the source explicitly replaces her despair over capture with helpless bewilderment about the space.
- **Replacement:** She no longer felt the despair of having been captured by Dark Heaven, but rather the helpless bewilderment of finding herself in an incomprehensible place.
- **Rationale:** 아닌 establishes a direct contrast between the former explanation and her new emotional response.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 남만에 닥친 위기? 요족의 대족장으로서 지켜야 할 체면?
- **Current:** A crisis had befallen Nanman. She was the Great Chieftain of the Yao people, and there was a dignity she was supposed to uphold.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Yohi’s dismissive rhetorical questions into neutral declarative exposition, weakening the abrupt register shift that drives the comic breakdown.
- **Replacement:** The crisis facing Nanman? The dignity she was supposed to uphold as Great Chieftain of the Yao people?
- **Rationale:** Both source fragments are pointed rhetorical questions that Yohi immediately dismisses because she believes she is dead.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 이러한 요희의 생각을 읽은 것처럼, 그녀의 주위를 맴돌던 무야호가 움직이기 시작했다.
- **Current:** As though it had read Yohi’s thoughts, Muyaho began moving around her.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the action sequence: Muyaho had already been circling Yohi before it made its next move.
- **Replacement:** As though it had read Yohi’s thoughts, Muyaho, which had been circling her, made its move.
- **Rationale:** 그녀의 주위를 맴돌던 is a prior ongoing action modifying Muyaho, not the movement it begins afterward.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 우거진 풀숲 속에서 함께 햇빛을 즐기고 있던 노루와 멧돼지.

그리고 이 어울리지 않는 한 쌍이 기댈 수 있도록 자신의 몸을 빌려주고 있는 곰까지.
- **Current:** Deer and wild boars enjoyed the sunlight together in the thick grass.

And even a bear was lending its body so that this mismatched pair could lean against it.
- **Defect:** The plural animals leave “this mismatched pair” without a clear antecedent and can make it appear to refer to Yohi and Muyaho. The source depicts one roe deer and one wild boar leaning against a bear.
- **Replacement:** A roe deer and a wild boar basked together in the sunlight amid the thick grass.

There was even a bear lending the mismatched pair its body to lean against.
- **Rationale:** 한 쌍 explicitly identifies the previously mentioned roe deer and wild boar as the unlikely pair supported by the bear.

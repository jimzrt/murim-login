# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s main events but changes a question into a statement, loses a deliberate shift in address, and misattributes the limit-breaking magic.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 역시, 처음부터 그럴 생각이었나.
- **Current:** “So you were planning this from the very beginning.”
- **Defect:** Morgoth’s question becomes a statement.
- **Replacement:** “Just as I thought. Was that your plan from the start?”
- **Rationale:** The Korean question ending asks whether the Skeleton King intended this all along.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 너희를…… 아니 자네를 과소평가하고 있던 모양이야.
- **Current:** It seems I underestimated you—or rather, you.
- **Defect:** The shift from addressing a group to addressing the Skeleton King alone is unintelligible in English.
- **Replacement:** It seems I underestimated all of you… No, I underestimated you.
- **Rationale:** 너희 is plural; 자네 singles out the Skeleton King.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 무영창 주문과 함께, 블링크 마법의 한계를 초월하여 나타난 모르고스의 일격이 스켈레톤 킹의 등줄기를 후려쳤다.
- **Current:** With an incantationless spell that came as naturally as breathing, Morgoth’s strike transcended the limits of Blink magic and slammed into the Skeleton King’s back.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the strike, rather than Morgoth’s use of Blink, transcend the spell’s limits.
- **Replacement:** Morgoth cast a wordless spell as naturally as breathing, pushed Blink magic past its limits, and appeared to strike the Skeleton King in the back.
- **Rationale:** The source describes Morgoth appearing through an extraordinary use of Blink before his blow lands.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 분명 두 글자를 떠올렸을 것이다.

종말(終末).
- **Current:** they would surely have thought of two words:

The end.
- **Defect:** 종말 is one word of two Korean characters, not two words; “The end” also weakens its apocalyptic sense.
- **Replacement:** they would surely have thought of one word:

Apocalypse.
- **Rationale:** The source names an end-of-the-world event, followed by images of the world’s and all living things’ final moments.

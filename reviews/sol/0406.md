# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one reversed action, two meaningful losses of context or hierarchy, an added profanity, and several localized translation or English issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 美 대통령 도람프 공식 석상서 파격 발언
- **Current:** President Doramp makes an outrageous statement in public
- **Defect:** “Outrageous” implies offensiveness or impropriety, while 파격 means startling or unconventional, and 공식 석상 specifically denotes an official public event.
- **Replacement:** President Doramp makes a startling statement at an official event
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both the unusual nature of the remark and the official setting without adding a negative judgment.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “도대체 코리안들이 틈만 나면 죽여 대는 주모(Jumo)가 누구야?”
- **Current:** “Who the hell is Jumo, the Lady of the House, whom Koreans keep killing whenever they get the chance?”
- **Defect:** The inserted appositive identifies Jumo inside a quotation whose joke depends on the foreign speaker not knowing what or who Jumo is; the relative-clause construction also obscures the punchline.
- **Replacement:** “Who the hell is this ‘Lady of the House’ Koreans keep killing every chance they get?”
- **Rationale:** This uses the binding rendering of 주모 while preserving the speaker’s confusion and the deliberate absurdity of the mistranslation.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘하긴, 요즘 들어서 희소식이 많이 들려오긴 하지.’
- **Current:** *He does have a point. We’ve been hearing a lot of good news lately.*
- **Defect:** “He does have a point” invents an unidentified male antecedent and turns the man’s reflective concession into agreement with another person.

# Review

The draft contains three scene-fact errors involving bodily position, action ownership, and scale, plus several localized fidelity and English issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 간신히 자세를 바로잡기도 전에 파공성이 귓가를 파고들었다.
- **Current:** Before I could barely regain my balance, the sound of splitting air pierced my ears.
- **Defect:** “Before I could barely” is logically and grammatically contradictory; the source means he was attacked before he could even manage to recover his balance.
- **Replacement:** Before I could even regain my balance, the sound of splitting air pierced my ears.
- **Rationale:** 간신히 and -기도 전에 indicate that even the difficult act of recovering his balance could not be completed before the next attack.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 동생이라면 껌뻑 죽는 진위경부터 동생이라면 껌뻑 죽이고 싶어 하는 진무경.

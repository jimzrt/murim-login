# Review

The draft has two scene-fact errors and several losses of voice and characterization.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 강렬한 바람이 나와 궁성을 휘감았다.

아니, 맞부딪쳤다.

그리고 첨예하게 맞닿은 서로의 기파(氣波)가 크게 부풀어 오르려던 그때
- **Current:** A powerful gust of wind swept around us.

No—it collided with us.

Our opposing waves of energy surged, about to swell into something sharp and overwhelming.
- **Defect:** The draft has the wind collide with the two people. The correction in the source is that their opposing waves of energy collide with each other.
- **Replacement:** A powerful gust of wind swept around the Bow Saint and me.

No—our winds collided.

Our opposing waves of qi met head-on, poised to swell.
- **Rationale:** 서로의 기파 identifies the clashing forces as theirs, while 첨예하게 맞닿은 describes their point of contact.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당연하게도 암천, 보다 정확히는 그자의 애첩이 벌인 소행이었지.
- **Current:** Naturally, it was Dark Heaven—or, more precisely, his concubine—who had done it.
- **Defect:** The pronoun makes Aehyang sound like Dark Heaven’s concubine rather than the City Lord’s.
- **Replacement:** Naturally, it was Dark Heaven’s doing—or, more precisely, the doing of the City Lord’s favorite concubine.
- **Rationale:** 그자의 애첩 refers to the City Lord’s concubine, whom Taekyung immediately identifies as Aehyang.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 궁성은 흐릿하게 웃었다. 나를 바라보는 그녀의 눈빛에는 약간의 기특함이 깃들어 있었다.
- **Current:** The Bow Saint smiled faintly. A trace of fondness showed in her eyes as she looked at me.
- **Defect:** Fondness loses her approving, elder-to-junior response to how he handled the test.
- **Replacement:** The Bow Saint smiled faintly. As she looked at me, there was a hint of approval in her eyes.
- **Rationale:** 기특함 conveys that she finds his conduct commendable, not merely that she feels affection.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 아직도 크고 작은 실권을 쥔 지방관들과 일군(一軍)을 지휘하는 장수들도 상당수가 암천과 협력하고 있겠지.
- **Current:** Many local officials who hold significant power, both large and small, and a great many generals who command entire armies are likely still working with Dark Heaven.
- **Defect:** “Significant power, both large and small” contradicts itself and obscures that officials with varying degrees of real authority are implicated.
- **Replacement:** A considerable number of local officials wielding various degrees of real power, along with generals commanding armies, are likely still working with Dark Heaven.
- **Rationale:** 크고 작은 modifies the extent of the officials’ 실권; the warning reaches across levels of authority.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “내가, 씨발, 내가 도대체 어떻게 해야 하는 거냐고!”
- **Current:** “What the hell am I supposed to do? What the hell am I supposed to do?!”
- **Defect:** The draft softens the profanity and replaces his broken, repeated “I” with a repeated complete question.
- **Replacement:** “What am I—fuck—what am I supposed to do?!”
- **Rationale:** 씨발 is strong profanity, and the interrupted repetition of 내가 shows him struggling to get the demand out.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “그래도 명색이 제자라고 부르는 놈인데, 어느 고약한 할망구와 단둘이 있으면 불안하지 않겠나.”
- **Current:** “He may be a brat, but I do call him my Disciple. Wouldn’t you be worried if he were alone with some nasty old hag?”
- **Defect:** “He may be a brat” adds a contrast absent from the source, while the question shifts Jeok Cheongang’s own concern onto his listener.
- **Replacement:** “I do call him my Disciple, after all. How could I not worry when he’s alone with some nasty old hag?”
- **Rationale:** 명색이 제자라고 부르는 놈인데 grounds Jeok’s worry in his relationship with Taekyung.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “흠, 머리에 피도 안 마른 녀석이 어른 앞에서 못하는 말이 없구나.”

“진짜 어른이었으면 머리에 피도 안 마른 놈을 이런 식으로 놀리진 않습니다.”
- **Current:** “Hm. You haven’t even dried the blood on your head, and you’re already speaking to your elders like that.”

“If you were really my elder, you wouldn’t tease someone whose head hasn’t even dried yet.”
- **Defect:** The literal idiom sounds unintentionally anatomical in English and blunts the quick exchange.
- **Replacement:** “Hm. Still wet behind the ears, and you talk to your elders like that?”

“If you were really an elder, you wouldn’t toy with someone who’s still wet behind the ears.”
- **Rationale:** 머리에 피도 안 마른 is an idiom for youth and inexperience; repeating the natural English idiom preserves Taekyung’s retort.

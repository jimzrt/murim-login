# Review

One critical quantity error, with additional losses in the Gate mechanism, character relationships, humor, euphemism, terminology, and natural description.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “게이트화는 대격변 때도 드문 현상이었지. 그러기 위해서는 매우 강력한 몬스터가 주축이 되어 마력을 뿌리내려야 하니 말일세.”
- **Current:** “Gate transformations were rare even during the Great Cataclysm. For one to occur, an extremely powerful monster had to take center stage and sink its mana into the area.”
- **Defect:** “Take center stage” loses the mechanical explanation that the monster must serve as the phenomenon’s core, while “sink its mana” does not clearly preserve the image of mana taking root.
- **Replacement:** “Gate transformations were rare even during the Great Cataclysm. For one to occur, an extremely powerful monster had to serve as its core and anchor its mana in the area.”
- **Rationale:** 주축이 되다 means to become the central axis or core, and 마력을 뿌리내리다 describes establishing or rooting mana in the location.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 분명 최후의 승자는 인간이 되겠지만, 수많은 도시가 파괴되고 수십, 아니 어쩌면 수백만 이상의 사람들이 죽을 것이다.
- **Current:** Humanity would undoubtedly be the final victor, but countless cities would be destroyed, and tens—or perhaps hundreds—of millions of people would die.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly applies “millions” to both numerical terms, inflating the projected deaths from hundreds of thousands or millions to tens or hundreds of millions.
- **Replacement:** Humanity would undoubtedly emerge victorious in the end, but countless cities would be destroyed, and hundreds of thousands—no, perhaps millions—of people would die.
- **Rationale:** 수십 in this escalating construction elides 만 before the correction to 수백만, yielding hundreds of thousands followed by perhaps millions, not tens or hundreds of millions.

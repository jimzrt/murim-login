# Review

The draft changes a beggar count and the viewpoint of the lake description, and blurs two character references.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 십만 거지 떼를 이끌 차세대의 왕초 거지, 궁기방이었다.
- **Current:** Gung Gibang—the next beggar king, destined to lead the Ten Thousand Beggars.
- **Defect:** 십만 is one hundred thousand, not ten thousand.
- **Replacement:** Gung Gibang—the next beggar king, destined to lead a hundred thousand beggars.
- **Rationale:** The draft reduces the stated number of beggars by a factor of ten.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 난생처음 보는 거대한 호수와 그 주위에 옹기종기 모여 모닥불을 피우고 있는 한 무리의 거지들이었다.
- **Current:** What awaited us was an enormous lake none of us had ever seen before, and a group of beggars gathered around it, huddled together as they lit campfires.
- **Defect:** The narrator says he had never seen the lake; the draft extends that claim to every ally.
- **Replacement:** What awaited us was an enormous lake I’d never seen before, and a group of beggars huddled around it, lighting campfires.
- **Rationale:** 난생처음 보는 is narrated from Taekyung’s viewpoint and does not establish the others’ experience.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 물론, 그러는 저 녀석도 만만치 않은 천둥벌거숭이지만.
- **Current:** “Though that one’s just as much of a reckless brat.”
- **Defect:** “That one” can point back to Cheongpung, but the Slaughter Saint is looking at Taekyung and calls him reckless too.
- **Replacement:** “Though the one laughing is just as much of a reckless brat.”
- **Rationale:** 저 녀석 refers to the narrator, whose laughter the Slaughter Saint has just explained.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 나는 잠시 당혹감과 반가움이 반반 섞인 채로 멍하니 눈앞의 첫 경험 빌런을 바라보다가, 이내 간신히 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** I stared at the first-encounter menace before me, half bewildered and half glad to see him. After a moment, I finally managed to speak.
- **Defect:** “First-encounter” makes 첫 경험 sound like their first meeting rather than a first-time experience.
- **Replacement:** I stared at the first-time-experience menace before me, half bewildered and half glad to see him. After a moment, I finally managed to speak.
- **Rationale:** 첫 경험 means a first experience; “encounter” changes the joke’s referent.

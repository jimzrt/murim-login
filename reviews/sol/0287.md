# Review

The draft contains one critical kinship/subject error, several meaningful semantic and register losses, and a missed Korean profanity pun.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 이정룡. 이 정신 나간 늙은이를 봤나. 자식한테 죽은 어머니가 생전에 바람을 피웠냐고 물어보다니.
- **Current:** Lee Jungryong had asked his own son whether his dead mother had cheated on him while she was alive.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a father-son relationship between Lee Jungryong and Team Leader Choi and also makes the son the person his mother supposedly cheated on. The source only says Lee asked a woman’s child whether his late mother had been unfaithful.
- **Replacement:** Lee Jungryong had asked Team Leader Choi whether his late mother had cheated while she was alive—her own son, no less.
- **Rationale:** 자식한테 identifies the person being asked as the deceased woman’s child, while 바람을 피웠냐고 has no stated object and does not mean she cheated on her son.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “형님과는 무슨 관계냐?”
- **Current:** “What is your relationship with Hyung?”
- **Defect:** Capitalizing “Hyung” makes the kinship term look like an unexplained personal name and omits that Lee is referring to his own respected older brother.
- **Replacement:** “What is your relationship with my older brother?”
- **Rationale:** 형님 is Lee’s respectful reference to an older brother or brother-like senior, not a proper name.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “가끔은 그런 생각을 합니다. 어머니가 천태민의 딸이 아닌 다른 누군가로 태어났으면 어땠을까, 하는 생각 말입니다.”
- **Current:** “Sometimes I wonder what it would have been like if my mother had been born someone else’s daughter, rather than Cheon Taemin’s.”
- **Defect:** The draft narrows the counterfactual to her being another person’s daughter. The source more broadly wonders what her life would have been like had she been born as someone other than Cheon Taemin’s daughter.
- **Replacement:** “Sometimes I wonder what it would have been like if my mother had been born as someone other than Cheon Taemin’s daughter.”
- **Rationale:** 천태민의 딸이 아닌 다른 누군가로 contrasts her actual identity as Cheon Taemin’s daughter with being someone else, not specifically with having a different father.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 도무지 끝이 보이지 않는 재력(才力), 대격변의 숨은 영웅인 A급 마법사가 도련님이라 부르는 20대 남성.
- **Current:** A man in his twenties with seemingly limitless ability and resources, whom an A-rank mage known as a hidden hero of the Great Cataclysm called Young Master.
- **Defect:** The draft adds material or financial “resources” not present in the source.
- **Replacement:** A man in his twenties with seemingly limitless ability, whom an A-rank mage known as a hidden hero of the Great Cataclysm called Young Master.
- **Rationale:** The supplied hanja 才力 means ability or talent; it does not denote financial means or other resources.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “정확히는 18년입니다. 십팔 년.”
- **Current:** “Eighteen years, to be precise. Eighteen years.”
- **Defect:** The rendering loses the deliberate near-profanity in the repeated Korean pronunciation, leaving the following remarks about stressing a particular word and Butler Kim’s hostility without their joke.
- **Replacement:** “Eighteen years, to be precise. *Ship-pal* years.”[^1]

[^1]: *Ship-pal*, meaning “eighteen” in Korean, sounds similar to *ssibal*, a strong profanity.
- **Rationale:** The separated repetition 십팔 년 foregrounds the sound of 십팔, which resembles the curse 씨발 and drives the narrator’s subsequent realization.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “착각하셨군요. 우리 중 부길드장님을 초대할 사람은 아무도 없습니다.”
- **Current:** “You’re mistaken. Not one of us invited the Vice Guild Master.”
- **Defect:** The past-tense statement merely denies that an invitation occurred, losing Team Leader Choi’s sharper assertion that nobody present would be willing to invite Lee.
- **Replacement:** “You’re mistaken. Not one of us would invite you, Vice Guild Master.”
- **Rationale:** 초대할 사람은 아무도 없습니다 expresses that there is no one among them who would invite him, reinforcing the hostile register of the exchange.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “성질머리 고약한 것으로 따지자면 제가 선배님께 몇 수는 접어야지요. 안 그렇습니까?”
- **Current:** “If we’re comparing bad tempers, I’d have to give you several points’ advantage, Senior. Wouldn’t you agree?”
- **Defect:** The handicap metaphor is reversed and unnatural: Butler Kim says that he would need to receive a handicap because Lee is several levels worse, not that he would grant Lee an advantage.
- **Replacement:** “When it comes to foul tempers, I’m still several levels below you, Senior. Wouldn’t you agree?”
- **Rationale:** 몇 수는 접어야지요 means conceding that the other person is several moves or levels superior—in this case, superior at having a foul temper.

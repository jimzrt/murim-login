- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 강제적인 재능기부로 호북성까지 오게 된 무송은 감격으로 몸을 부르르 떨었다.
- **Current:** Mu Song, who had been dragged all the way to Hubei Province under the guise of compulsory volunteer work, trembled with emotion.
- **Defect:** “Under the guise” adds an element of deception absent from the source and weakens the oxymoronic joke that his contribution of talent was compulsory.
- **Replacement:** Mu Song, who had been forced into volunteering his talents all the way to Hubei Province, trembled with emotion.
- **Rationale:** 강제적인 재능기부 directly describes a coerced donation of talent, not coercion concealed behind a pretext.

# Review

Three critical subject/action reversals, one meaningful register loss, and three localized terminology or wording issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 상상보다 더 거지 같은 현실이 나를 기다리고 있기 때문이었다.
- **Current:** Reality worse than my imagination was waiting for me.
- **Defect:** The translation omits the repeated crude register of 거지 같은, weakening the deliberate echo of the preceding line and its darkly comic contrast.
- **Replacement:** A reality even shittier than anything I could imagine was waiting for me.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly describes reality as even more 거지 같은 after imagining something that would make him feel 거지 같아질, so the repeated vulgar phrasing should remain audible.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 조금 전까지만 하더라도 신병이기 부럽지 않게 날카롭던 감각은 버려진 오두막의 도끼처럼 무뎌졌고, 언제나 팔랑개비처럼 휘둘렀던 백염은 세상에서 가장 무거운 것이 되어 버렸다.
- **Current:** My senses, which had been sharp enough to rival a divine artifact only moments ago, had grown dull as an ax abandoned in a ruined cabin.
- **Defect:** 신병이기 refers specifically to extraordinary or divine weapons, not the broader category of divine artifacts.
- **Replacement:** My senses, which had been sharp enough to rival a divine weapon only moments ago, had grown dull as an ax abandoned in a ruined cabin.
- **Rationale:** The weapon comparison supports the repeated image of sharpness and should not be conflated with the established general term 신물, rendered as “divine artifact.”

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 어림잡아 일백에 달하는 그들을 기다리고 있던 것은 일섬이 불러온 강대한 와류였다.
- **Current:** Around a hundred of them had been waiting for the immense vortex created by One Annihilation.
- **Defect:** The action is reversed: the mutants were not waiting for the vortex; the vortex was what awaited them.
- **Replacement:** What awaited roughly one hundred of them was the immense vortex created by One Annihilation.
- **Rationale:** The Korean makes 그들을 the object of 기다리고 있던 and the vortex its subject, describing the lethal force they charged into rather than an intentional wait.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 제 몸을 가누는 것조차 힘든 진태경과, 그 앞을 가로막은 어느 노린내 나는 짐승을 갈기갈기 찢어 버릴 수 있게 해 주는 축복.
- **Current:** and the rank-smelling beast blocking his path.

# Review

One subject reversal changes Ju Hwaran’s argument, while several passages alter causal nuance, direct-question force, characterization, or register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 처음부터 그럴 생각이었다면 지원서도 없이 왔겠죠?
- **Current:** “If you had intended to do that from the beginning, would I have come without an application?”
- **Defect:** The conditional subject is reversed from Ju Hwaran to Taekyung, making her argument logically incoherent.
- **Replacement:** “If I’d planned to rely on our connection from the start, I would’ve come without an application, wouldn’t I?”
- **Rationale:** Ju Hwaran says that if she had intended to rely on their existing relationship, she would have come without submitting an application.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 위험하다. 알면서도 왜 지원하느냐.
- **Current:** “That it would be dangerous. That they shouldn’t apply if they knew that.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Taekyung’s question about why someone would apply despite the danger into a statement that they should not apply.
- **Replacement:** “‘It’s dangerous. Why apply when you know that?’”
- **Rationale:** Ju Hwaran directly paraphrases the two things Taekyung said to her: that the work is dangerous and a question about why she would apply despite knowing that.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 아무런 문책도 없을 게다. 길이 달라졌을 뿐, 이것 역시 아버지가 원하는 곳을 향하고 있으니까.
- **Current:** “He will not punish me. Our paths have merely diverged. This path, too, is headed toward the place my father wants to reach.”
- **Defect:** “Our paths have diverged” implies a split between father and son, whereas Sama Pyo says only the route has changed and that his present course still advances his father’s objective.
- **Replacement:** “There will be no punishment. The route has merely changed; this still leads toward the destination my father wants.”
- **Rationale:** The source explains why disobeying the specific order will not bring punishment: Sama Pyo is taking a different route toward the same desired endpoint.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 소싯적의 자신을 뛰어넘는 무재(武才)와 깊은 심계(審計)를 지닌 사마표를 대체할 후계자는 없다는 것 역시 같은 맥락이었다.
- **Current:** It also meant that he understood there was no successor who could replace Sama Pyo, whose martial talent surpassed his father’s in his youth and whose calculating mind ran deep.
- **Defect:** The possessive construction makes it ambiguous whose youth is meant and weakens the explicit comparison between Sama Pyo and the younger Sima Gong.
- **Replacement:** By the same logic, there was no other successor who could replace Sama Pyo, whose martial talent surpassed even Sima Gong’s in his younger days and whose calculating mind ran deep.
- **Rationale:** 소싯적의 자신 refers to Sima Gong himself in his younger days; Sama Pyo’s martial talent exceeds what his father possessed then.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 삼처사첩(三妻四妾)으로도 모자라 열 명이 넘는 첩을 두었으니 위로도, 밑으로도 피가 이어진 혈육이 수두룩했다.
- **Current:** Three wives and four concubines had not been enough for him. He had taken more than ten concubines, and blood relatives had multiplied in every direction, above and below him.
- **Defect:** “Multiplied in every direction, above and below him” is unnatural and obscures that Sama Pyo has numerous blood relatives both older and younger than himself.
- **Replacement:** Three wives and four concubines had not been enough for him. He had taken more than ten concubines, leaving Sama Pyo with countless blood relatives both older and younger than himself.
- **Rationale:** 위로도, 밑으로도 describes relatives on both sides of Sama Pyo in age or birth order.

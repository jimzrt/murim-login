# Review

Several localized changes alter a joke, a physical action, and Jin Taekyung’s reaction; other lines add or soften meaning.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그 믿을 수 없는 무용담(武勇談)을 들을 때마다 과인의 가슴이 벅찰 지경이었지.
- **Current:** Every time I hear one of those unbelievable tales of your exploits, my heart swells with pride.
- **Defect:** “With pride” adds an emotion the prince does not specify.
- **Replacement:** Every time I hear one of those unbelievable tales of your exploits, I can hardly contain my excitement.
- **Rationale:** 가슴이 벅차다 conveys being overwhelmed or stirred, not necessarily pride.

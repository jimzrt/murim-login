# Review

One critical subject reversal changes who is grinning; several localized issues alter a pragmatic cue, anatomical location, or natural phrasing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 있는 힘껏 의기양양하게 웃고 있는 진무경의 정강이를 발로 깠다.
- **Current:** I grinned as arrogantly as I could and kicked Jin Mukyung in the shin.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the triumphant grin to Taekyung, but Jin Mukyung is the one grinning while Taekyung kicks him.
- **Replacement:** With all my strength, I kicked Jin Mukyung in the shin as he grinned triumphantly.
- **Rationale:** The participial phrase 의기양양하게 웃고 있는 directly modifies 진무경, while 있는 힘껏 describes the force of Taekyung’s kick.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러나 이미 늦었다. 슬쩍 올라간 진무경의 입꼬리가 눈앞에 있었다.
- **Current:** I turned and swung my spear at the same time, but I was already too late. Jin Mukyung’s raised mouth was right in front of me.
- **Defect:** “Raised mouth” is an unnatural and anatomically inaccurate rendering of his slightly upturned mouth corner.
- **Replacement:** I turned and swung my spear at the same time, but I was already too late. The faintly upturned corner of Jin Mukyung’s mouth was right in front of me.
- **Rationale:** 입꼬리 specifically means the corner of the mouth, and 슬쩍 indicates that it is raised only slightly.

# Review

Several lines alter who takes a blow, the threatened penalty, or the brothers’ reactions. A few smaller choices lose kinship and comic nuance.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대가리에 멸염신권을 맞아도 그런 놈은 수하로 안 부린다.
- **Current:** I wouldn’t put a man like that under me even if he took a Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist to the head.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the prospective subordinate take the blow; Jeok says he himself would still refuse even if struck in the head.
- **Replacement:** Even if I took a Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist to the head, I wouldn’t take a man like that as a subordinate.
- **Rationale:** The hypothetical blow is to the speaker’s head, not the other man’s.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 지금부터 뒤져서 나오면 한 음절에 열 대다. 이래도 없느냐?
- **Current:** “Then I’ll search you from head to toe. Ten hits for every syllable I find. Still nothing?”
- **Defect:** Jeok threatens punishment for any story that turns up later, not for syllables found by physically searching Ma.
- **Replacement:** “If I dig around and anything comes out later, it’s ten hits per syllable. Still nothing?”
- **Rationale:** The threat concerns withheld information, continuing Jeok’s demand that Ma tell him more.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 설령 남은 이야기가 있더라도 죽을 각오로 버티지.
- **Current:** Even if he had more to tell, he’d just grit his teeth and take it.
- **Defect:** The draft says Ma would take the beating; Jin says he would hold out even at the risk of death.
- **Replacement:** Even if he had more to tell, he’d hold out even if it killed him.
- **Rationale:** 죽을 각오로 버티지 describes resisting with a willingness to die, not submitting to punishment.

## F04 — Major

# Review

Several draft lines change whose secrets were shared, why people were not invited, and the Lord of Heaven’s reply.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 가장 깊은 비밀들을 털어놓았지만, 그 짧은 시간 만에 모든 것을 수용하고 받아들인 것은 아니다.
- **Current:** He’d revealed their deepest secrets, but that didn’t mean they could accept everything in such a short time.
- **Defect:** “Their deepest secrets” makes the listeners the owners of the secrets Jin disclosed.
- **Replacement:** He’d shared the deepest secrets with them, but that didn’t mean they could accept everything so quickly.
- **Rationale:** Jin has confided secrets to the group; the Korean does not call them the listeners’ secrets.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 덩치가 산만 한 남자들 사이에서 홀로 한 자리를 차지하고 있던 송송이가 눈짓으로 옆자리를 가리켰다.
- **Current:** Song Song, the only one occupying a seat among all the men built like mountains, glanced at the space beside her.
- **Defect:** “The only one occupying a seat” implies the men are not seated.
- **Replacement:** Song Song, sitting alone among the mountain-sized men, glanced at the spot beside her.
- **Rationale:** The Korean singles her out among the large men, not as the only person seated.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 혹은 이런 상황을 받아들이지 못할 가족과 고시원 시절부터 절친했던 성진호처럼.
- **Current:** Or his family, and Seong Jinho, who’d been his closest friend since his days at the goshiwon—people who wouldn’t be able to accept a situation like this.
- **Defect:** The draft extends Jin’s judgment that they could not accept this situation to Seong Jinho.
- **Replacement:** Or his family, who wouldn’t be able to accept a situation like this, and Seong Jinho, his closest friend since his days at the goshiwon.
- **Rationale:** The Korean attaches that judgment to his family; it describes Seong Jinho by their long friendship.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 고장난 회중시계.

아마도 현대와 무림, 두 세상을 시간이라는 절대적인 힘으로 잇고 있을 그것이 움직이고 있었다.
- **Current:** A broken pocket watch.

It was probably moving—the thing that connected the modern world and Murim through the absolute force of time.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the watch’s movement uncertain instead of its role in connecting the worlds.
- **Replacement:** The broken pocket watch.

It was moving—the thing that probably linked the modern world and Murim through the absolute force of time.
- **Rationale:** The Korean states that the watch was moving and qualifies the proposed connection with “probably.”

## F06 — Minor

- **Source:** 음성이라 부를 수 없을 것 같은 그것은 얼음장처럼 차가웠으나, 대술사는 기쁨과 감격으로 몸을 떨었다.

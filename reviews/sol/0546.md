# Review

One critical reversal of the Jin Family’s recovery, one hierarchy error, and several localized terminology and wording defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 불과 일 년 하고도 수개월. 가주 대행이라는 이름으로 태원진가의 사령탑에 앉아 서서히 몰락해 가던 가문을 무섭게 성장시킨 그는, 어느덧 이들 중 한 사람이 되어 있었다.
- **Current:** In just over a year, he had gone from sitting at the helm of the Jin Family of Taiyuan under the title of Acting Family Head—while the family slowly declined—to becoming one of the people gathered here.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses Wikyung’s achievement: it says the family continued declining under him, whereas he caused the previously declining family to grow at a tremendous rate.
- **Replacement:** In the space of just a year and several months, he had taken the helm of the Jin Family of Taiyuan as Acting Family Head and brought astonishing growth to a family that had been slowly declining. Before anyone knew it, he had become one of their number.
- **Rationale:** 서서히 몰락해 가던 가문 is the object of 무섭게 성장시킨: Wikyung rapidly grew a family that had been declining.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이 자리에 모인 한 사람, 한 사람이 무림맹이라는 거대한 단체를 움직이는 중요한 기관이자 톱니바퀴니까.
- **Current:** Every person gathered here was an important institution and cog that helped move the enormous organization known as the Murim Alliance.
- **Defect:** Calling each individual an “institution” is an unnatural and misleading rendering of 기관 in this mechanical metaphor.
- **Replacement:** Each person gathered here was a vital part of the vast Murim Alliance, one of the cogs that kept it moving.
- **Rationale:** 기관 here means a functional organ or component, paired metaphorically with 톱니바퀴, not a social institution.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 외당(外堂)에 속한 단주며, 대주들까지 더한다면 이 넓은 대회의실로도 자리가 부족해서 연무장을 써야 할 것이다.
- **Current:** If the leaders of the divisions belonging to the Outer Hall and the Squad Commanders were added, even this vast conference hall would not have enough room. They would have to use the training ground.
- **Defect:** The draft renders 단주 as “leaders of the divisions,” conflating their units with the established Divisions denoted by 당 and distorting the Outer Hall’s hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** If the unit heads and squad commanders of the Outer Hall were included, even this vast conference hall would not have enough room. They would have to use the training ground.
- **Rationale:** 단주 and 대주 are two leadership levels within the Outer Hall; neither occurrence identifies 단 as one of the established Divisions.

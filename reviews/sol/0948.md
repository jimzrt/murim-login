# Review

The draft has two substantive errors in its account of the northern regions and trade-route infiltration, plus several localized losses of meaning.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 진정한 변방이라 할 수 있는 요녕성(遼寧省)의 패자이자 수백 여년 전 일국의 왕을 자처하던 선비족의 후예들은 오대 세가의 일원으로 당당히 자리매김한 지 오래였다.
- **Current:** At the edge of this vast realm, in Liaoning Province—the true frontier—the descendants of the Xianbei, once a people who had proclaimed themselves rulers of their own kingdom centuries ago, had long since secured their place among the Five Great Families.
- **Defect:** Omits the Murong Family’s dominance of Liaoning and changes the ancestors’ claim to be kings of a nation into a claim about ruling their own kingdom.
- **Replacement:** The descendants of the Xianbei, whose ancestors had proclaimed themselves kings of a nation centuries ago, ruled Liaoning Province—the true frontier—and had long since secured their place among the Five Great Families.
- **Rationale:** 요녕성의 패자 identifies them as Liaoning’s dominant power; 일국의 왕을 자처하던 describes their ancestors’ claim to kingship.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 진정한 의미의 북부. 온갖 암수와 창칼이 도사린 무림보다 더한 무법자들의 땅.
- **Current:** The real north. A lawless land full of people more ruthless than the Murim, with every kind of hidden scheme and blade lurking in its depths.
- **Defect:** Moves the lurking schemes and blades from the Murim to the grasslands, altering the comparison.
- **Replacement:** The real north. A land of outlaws even more lawless than the Murim, where hidden schemes and blades lurked at every turn.
- **Rationale:** 온갖 암수와 창칼이 도사린 modifies 무림; the northern land is being compared against that dangerous Murim.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 개방은 곧 유입을 뜻하는 법.
- **Current:** The Beggars’ Sect was a sign of people coming in.
- **Defect:** Mistakes the ordinary noun 개방, meaning opening, for the identically written organization name and invents the Beggars’ Sect’s involvement.
- **Replacement:** Opening the trade routes meant opening the way for people, too.
- **Rationale:** The preceding line describes newly opened trade routes; the following lines explain how people, including Peng informants, entered through them.

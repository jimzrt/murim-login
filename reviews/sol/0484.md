# Review

One critical kinship reversal, two major losses of characterization or narration, and five localized fidelity defects.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이미 한차례 오가며 물길을 익힌 수부들은 능숙하게 선박을 몰아 나아갔다.
- **Current:** The boatmen had already traveled this route once, and with the waterways memorized, they guided the vessel forward with practiced ease.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces a completed trip there and back to traveling the route once.
- **Replacement:** The boatmen had already made one round trip and learned the waterways, so they guided the vessel forward with practiced ease.
- **Rationale:** 오가며 indicates that the boatmen had traveled both ways, which explains their familiarity with the waterways.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하지만 종이접기 아저씨, 아니 무당파가 자랑하는 초절정 고수인 현공진인과 청풍이라면 못할 것도 없다.
- **Current:** But if the Wudang Sect’s famed Supreme Peak master, Perfected Being Hyeongong—also known as the Paper-Folding Grandpa—and Cheongpung had done it, it wasn’t impossible.
- **Defect:** The narrator's comic self-correction is turned into an invented alias, and 아저씨 is changed to “Grandpa.”
- **Replacement:** But with Origami Man—no, Perfected Being Hyeongong, the Supreme Peak master Wudang was so proud of—and Cheongpung, it was entirely possible.
- **Rationale:** 종이접기 아저씨, 아니 is Taekyung jokingly calling Hyeongong “Origami Man” before correcting himself, not supplying an established sobriquet.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “나까지 한 묶음으로 엮지 마라. 기분 나쁘니까.”
- **Current:** “Don’t lump me in with them. It’s insulting.”
- **Defect:** The referent is reversed. Mungyeong objects to Jeok Cheongang grouping him with Jeok's generation, not to being grouped with the young monsters.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t lump me in with you. It’s insulting.”
- **Rationale:** Jeok has just generalized about “when we were young.” Mungyeong rejects that grouping, prompting Jeok to challenge him about how young he was when he reached the Supreme Peak realm.

## F04 — Minor

- **Source:** “당연하지. 뿔 달린 물뱀이랑 다니는 인간은 당신밖에 없으니까.”

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 평상시에는 천자님, 천자님 하면서 떠받드는 순박한 백성들도 홍수나 지진이 일어나면 나라에 망조가 들었다며 혁무진 급 태세 전환을 보여 준다.
- **Current:** Under ordinary circumstances, simple folk who worshiped the Emperor as the Son of Heaven would instantly change their tune when a flood or earthquake struck, declaring that the country had fallen under an evil omen.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicit Hyuk Mujin callback that characterizes the speed and extremity of the public's about-face.
- **Replacement:** Under ordinary circumstances, the simple folk who fawned over the Son of Heaven would pull a Hyuk Mujin-level about-face the moment a flood or earthquake struck, declaring it an omen of the nation’s ruin.
- **Rationale:** 혁무진 급 is a direct comic comparison to Hyuk Mujin, not merely an intensifier meaning “instantly.”

## F06 — Minor

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그리고 발걸음이 왜 이리 느려 터졌느냐? 네놈보다 노부의 할머니가 더 빠르겠다.”

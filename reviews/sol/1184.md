# Review

Two action details and one established term need correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 바로 그 ‘아주 미세한 차이’로 독에 가까운 약을 만들어낸 장본인은 작게 한숨을 내쉬며 덧붙였다.
- **Current:** The man who’d made a medicine close to poison by crossing that “very fine line” gave a quiet sigh and continued,
- **Defect:** Crossing the line implies he made poison. He used the fine distinction to make something that remained medicine.
- **Replacement:** The man who’d used that “very fine line” to make a medicine almost indistinguishable from poison sighed softly and added,
- **Rationale:** 독에 가까운 약 remains medicine, explaining why the poison warning and ring did not activate.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 쌍장(雙掌)과 쌍도(雙刀)가 부딪혔다.
- **Current:** Their palms and twin sabers collided.
- **Defect:** The draft leaves out that Taekyung strikes with both palms.
- **Replacement:** Both his palms slammed into her twin sabers.
- **Rationale:** 쌍장 and 쌍도 specify two palms against two sabers.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 아니 어쩌면, 하늘이 선택한 천주(天主)의 유일한 대적자.
- **Current:** No. Perhaps the only adversary of the Lord of Heaven, chosen by Heaven itself.
- **Defect:** The established rendering of 대적자 is “the Adversary.”
- **Replacement:** No. Perhaps the Lord of Heaven’s only Adversary, chosen by Heaven itself.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the source’s uncertainty and uses the packet’s binding term.

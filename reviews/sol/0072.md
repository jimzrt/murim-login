# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it loses one kinship distinction, softens an explicit reference to sexual preference, changes an emotional beat, adds dialogue, and contains several localized wording issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “내 아우들 근처에 얼씬거리지 못하게 해. 알았나!”
- **Current:** “Don’t let him go anywhere near my brothers. Understood?”
- **Defect:** The translation omits that Jin Mukyung and Jin Taekyung are Wikyung’s younger brothers, losing the stated kinship hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t let him go anywhere near my younger brothers. Understood?”
- **Rationale:** 아우들 specifically means younger brothers, not brothers generally.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 개인적인 성적 취향이야 그렇다 쳐도, 그 대상이 되는 건 사양이었다.
- **Current:** Personal preferences were one thing, but he had no desire to become the object of someone else’s.
- **Defect:** The translation removes the explicit sexual qualifier from 성적 취향 and leaves the second clause with an awkward, incomplete possessive.
- **Replacement:** Personal sexual preferences were one thing, but he had no desire to be the object of them.
- **Rationale:** 성적 취향 explicitly means sexual preference, while 그 대상 refers to becoming the target or object of that preference.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 그는 난생처음 느껴 보는 분노와 서러움에 눈물이 날 것 같았다.
- **Current:** He felt as though he might cry from the anger and humiliation he was experiencing for the first time in his life.
- **Defect:** Humiliation replaces 서러움, which conveys sorrow and wounded resentment rather than embarrassment or loss of dignity.
- **Replacement:** For the first time in his life, anger and wounded sorrow brought him close to tears.
- **Rationale:** 서러움 is the aggrieved sadness of feeling hurt or unfairly treated; preserving it maintains the comic emotional beat.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “안 돼. 하지 마! 바치지 마!”
- **Current:** “No. Don’t! Stop! Don’t offer it!”
- **Defect:** The draft turns one command into two and adds an unsupported “Stop!”
- **Replacement:** “No. Don’t do it! Don’t offer it!”
- **Rationale:** 하지 마 is one command meaning “don’t do it,” followed separately by 바치지 마, “don’t offer it.”

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 진무경이 가라앉은 눈으로 나를 응시했다.
- **Current:** Jin Mukyung stared at me with lowered eyes.
- **Defect:** Lowered eyes suggests that Mukyung is looking downward, whereas 가라앉은 describes his gaze as subdued, grave, or somber.
- **Replacement:** Jin Mukyung fixed me with a somber gaze.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the emotional quality of his gaze, not a downward eye movement.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 서늘한 목소리가 이어졌다.
- **Current:** A chill voice followed.
- **Defect:** “A chill voice followed” is an unnatural English collocation and obscures that the same speaker continues.
- **Replacement:** The cold voice continued.
- **Rationale:** 이어졌다 indicates continuation, while 서늘한 characterizes the voice as cold or chilling.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천 번, 만 번도 넘게 펼친 무공이 낯설다.
- **Current:** The martial arts I’ve performed more than a thousand, ten thousand times feel unfamiliar.
- **Defect:** The quantity construction is ungrammatical and fails to convey the escalating sense of thousands and even tens of thousands of repetitions.
- **Replacement:** The martial arts I’ve performed thousands—even tens of thousands—of times feel unfamiliar.
- **Rationale:** 천 번, 만 번도 넘게 is an escalating expression emphasizing that he has performed them thousands, even tens of thousands, of times.

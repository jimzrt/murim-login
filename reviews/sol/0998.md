# Review

Several action and subject errors need correction, along with a lost distinction between the two senior Daoists and softened profanity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내가 아닌 다른 누군가에게 뒤통수를 맞았다면 눈에 쌍심지를 켰겠지만, 그 누군가가 바로 화왕 적천강이라면 이야기가 달라진다.
- **Current:** If anyone else had hit me on the back of the head, I’d have seen red. But when that someone was the Fire King, Jeok Cheongang, it was a different story.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the narrator the person struck and angered. The source says Hyuk Mujin would have reacted angrily if someone other than the narrator had struck him.
- **Replacement:** If anyone but me had smacked Hyuk Mujin on the back of the head, he’d have been glaring daggers. But when that someone was the Fire King, Jeok Cheongang, it was a different story.
- **Rationale:** The conditional distinguishes the narrator from other people who might hit Hyuk Mujin; Jeok’s status explains why Hyuk holds back.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 숨을 헐떡이는 초원마의 말안장 위, 일필휘지로 수놓아진 글자를 읽은 선두의 청년이 작게 중얼거렸다.
- **Current:** On the saddle of the lead rider, a young man gasped for breath as he read the characters embroidered in a single flowing stroke. He muttered under his breath,
- **Defect:** The draft makes the young man gasp for breath; the panting belongs to the grassland horse beneath him.
- **Replacement:** From atop his panting grassland horse, the young man riding in front read the characters embroidered in a single flowing stroke and muttered under his breath,
- **Rationale:** 숨을 헐떡이는 modifies 초원마, not 청년.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “난데없이 휴식이라니. 대관절 그게 무슨 말씀이십니까. 대사형.”
- **Current:** “Why are we stopping for a rest out of nowhere? What on earth do you mean, Senior Brother?”
- **Defect:** Calling the first elder merely Senior Brother loses his distinction as the eldest senior brother, which matters when another senior brother enters the exchange.
- **Replacement:** “Why are we stopping for a rest out of nowhere? What on earth do you mean, Eldest Senior Brother?”
- **Rationale:** 대사형 distinguishes this elder from the other 사형 in the scene.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “이 사형까지 왜 그러시는 겁니까. 본산(本山)을 떠난 지 벌써 이틀이 지났는데, 아직도 감숙에 닿지 못했다는 걸 아시는 분들께서…….”

“그만.”

이 사형이라 불린 그, 풍성한 백염(白髥)으로 신선처럼 보이는 노도사가 손을 들어 말을 끊었다.
- **Current:** “Why are you doing this too, Senior Brother? It’s been two days since we left the main sect, and we still haven’t reached Gansu. You know that…”

“Enough.”

The white-bearded old Daoist, who looked like an immortal, lifted a hand and cut him off.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the second elder’s identifying address, Senior Brother Lee, both in speech and in the narration that identifies who was addressed.
- **Replacement:** “Why are you doing this too, Senior Brother Lee? It’s been two days since we left the main sect, and we still haven’t reached Gansu. You know that…”

The man he’d called Senior Brother Lee, an old Daoist with a luxuriant white beard who looked like an immortal, lifted a hand and cut him off.
- **Rationale:** 이 사형 is repeated as an address and then as the narrator’s identification of the man who interrupts.

## F08 — Major

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “뛰어야지. 존나게.”

“그건 당연히 알죠. 그런데 제가 여쭙고 싶은 건…….”

“존나게 뛰는 건 좋은데 언제까지 뛰냐, 그거겠지.”
- **Current:** “We run. Like hell.”

“I know that much, of course. But what I wanted to ask was…”

“You mean, running like hell is fine, but how long do we keep running?”
- **Defect:** Both uses of the emphatic profanity 존나게 are softened, flattening the narrator’s deliberately crude repetition.
- **Replacement:** “We run. Fucking hard.”

“I know that much, of course. But what I wanted to ask was…”

“You mean, running fucking hard is fine, but how long do we keep it up?”
- **Rationale:** The source repeats the same strong vulgar intensifier in the answer and in the narrator’s teasing restatement.

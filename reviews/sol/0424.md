# Review

Two critical errors alter the Adversary’s possible identity and falsely state that White Flame pierced Jin’s chest; several additional lines lose action, resolve, clipped speech, or the Skeleton Warlord’s register shift.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마지막까지 기다렸지. 인간에게 당하는 수모를 겪으면서도 참고 인내했다. 그리고 마침내…… 이 몸의 승리다.
- **Current:** —I waited until the very end. I endured the humiliation of being toyed with by a human and bore it patiently. And finally… victory belongs to me.
- **Defect:** “Being toyed with” invents a specific action. The Arch Lich says it endured the humiliation it suffered at a human’s hands, not that Jin played with it.
- **Replacement:** —I waited until the very end. I held back and endured even the humiliation of suffering at a human’s hands. And finally… victory belongs to me.
- **Rationale:** 인간에게 당하는 수모 means humiliation suffered at a human’s hands; it does not specify being toyed with.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는…… 반드시 살아남을 것이다.
- **Current:** I would… survive.
- **Defect:** The translation omits 반드시, weakening Jin’s absolute determination at the moment that directly precedes the increase in Endurance.
- **Replacement:** I would… survive, no matter what.
- **Rationale:** 반드시 expresses certainty and resolve—“definitely” or “no matter what”—rather than a neutral prediction.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 한없이 느린 세상 속에서 우뚝 서 있는 놈을 향해 손에 들린 창을 뻗었다.
- **Current:** In a world that had slowed to a crawl, I extended the spear in my hand toward the Arch Lich standing motionless before me.
- **Defect:** “Motionless” is unsupported and changes the Arch Lich’s physical action; 우뚝 서 있는 describes it standing tall or upright.
- **Replacement:** In a world that had slowed to a crawl, I extended the spear in my hand toward the Arch Lich standing tall before me.
- **Rationale:** 우뚝 denotes an imposing upright stance, not an absence of movement.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그걸 말이라고.
- **Current:** What kind of thing was that supposed to mean?
- **Defect:** The translation turns a dismissive, deadpan response to obvious advice into genuine confusion about its meaning.
- **Replacement:** Like that needed saying.
- **Rationale:** 그걸 말이라고 is a sarcastic dismissal equivalent to saying that the statement is too obvious to be worth making.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** - ……해라, 인간! 어서!
- **Current:** —Do it, human! Quickly!
- **Defect:** The leading ellipsis indicating that Jin hears only the clipped end of the Skeleton Warlord’s command has been omitted.
- **Replacement:** —…do it, human! Hurry!
- **Rationale:** The obscured beginning is mechanically important because Jin’s burst eardrums prevent him from understanding what he is being told to do.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 잘 가거라. 새로운 대적자여, 혹은 대적자였을지도 모르는 한낱 어리석은 인간이여.
- **Current:** —Farewell, new Adversary—or perhaps merely a foolish human who might once have been the Adversary.
- **Defect:** “Once have been” implies that Jin may formerly have been the Adversary but is no longer one. The source questions whether he was the Adversary at all.
- **Replacement:** —Farewell, new Adversary—or perhaps merely a foolish human who might have been the Adversary.
- **Rationale:** 대적자였을지도 모르는 expresses uncertain identity, while “once” adds an unsupported temporal loss of that identity.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 백염의 창날이 뼈를 부수며 가슴 한가운데를 관통했고, 나는 눈을 부릅뜬 채 굳어 버렸다.
- **Current:** The spearhead of White Flame shattered bone and pierced straight through the center of my chest. I froze with my eyes wide open.
- **Defect:** The added possessive “my” falsely states that White Flame pierced Jin’s chest. The following reveal establishes that it pierced the Skeleton Warlord, stopping a handspan short of Jin.
- **Replacement:** White Flame’s spearhead shattered bone as it punched through a chest, and I froze with my eyes wide open.
- **Rationale:** The Korean deliberately omits the possessor of 가슴 to preserve the reveal; identifying it as Jin’s chest contradicts the scene’s physical mechanics.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** - ……그것도 모르겠다. 빌어먹을. 이제는 아무것도 모르겠어. 어쩌면 이 검에 홀린 것일지도 모르지.
- **Current:** —…I do not know that, either. Damn it. Now I do not know anything anymore. Perhaps this sword possessed me.
- **Defect:** The consistently formal contractions-free wording misses the Skeleton Warlord’s shift into a more vulnerable, colloquial register, while “possessed” overstates 홀린, which means bewitched or entranced.
- **Replacement:** —…I don’t know that either. Damn it. I don’t know anything anymore. Maybe this sword bewitched me.
- **Rationale:** 모르겠어 marks a clear register drop from the Warlord’s usual formal speech, and 홀린 describes enchantment or fascination rather than literal possession.

# Review

Two scene facts are altered, and the Beast Miao King’s muffled speech and its accompanying jokes are partly erased. Several localized terminology and wording issues remain.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그러나 날카로운 이빨도, 철갑처럼 두꺼운 가죽도 수도에 서린 기운을 막기에는 역부족이다.
- **Current:** But neither its sharp teeth nor its iron-thick hide was enough to block the energy gathered around my hand.
- **Defect:** The rendering uses the awkward phrase “iron-thick,” reduces 철갑처럼 두꺼운 to thickness rather than armor-like toughness, and translates Murim 기운 generically instead of as qi.
- **Replacement:** But neither its sharp teeth nor its armor-thick hide could stop the qi imbued in my knife-hand.
- **Rationale:** The source compares the hide to iron armor and describes Murim qi gathered in Taekyung’s hand blade.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 늪지대 사이에 숨어 있던 뱀이 목을 향해 화살처럼 튀어 오른다.
- **Current:** A snake hiding between the swamps shot toward my neck like an arrow.
- **Defect:** “Between the swamps” unnaturally treats the swamp as multiple separate locations; the snake is concealed within the marshland.
- **Replacement:** A snake hidden in the marsh shot toward my neck like an arrow.
- **Rationale:** 늪지대 refers to the surrounding swamp or marshland, not several swamps with the snake positioned between them.

- **Current:** “Don’t let your guard down.”

“Don’t let my what?”

“Be careful.”

“……Ah. Right.”

His pronunciation had become more awkward than an eight-year-old elementary school student’s because he was holding the poison-warding pearl in his mouth.
- **Defect:** The dialogue normalizes the Beast Miao King’s mouth-full pronunciation, making Taekyung’s request for clarification incoherent. It also omits Taekyung’s rough translation and the characterization that the speaker is Nanman’s greatest warrior and over eighty years old.

“Don’t let my what?”

“Be gareful.”

Nanman’s greatest warrior, now over eighty years old, was speaking less clearly than an eight-year-old because his mouth was stuffed with the poison-warding pearl.
- **Rationale:** The malformed Korean pronunciation, Taekyung’s interpretation, and the contrast between the elderly master’s stature and childish articulation are all explicit parts of the joke.

## F05 — Critical

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 야수묘왕은 떨떠름한 눈빛으로 나를 노려봤지만, 이내 별말 없이 커다란 피독주를 입에 물었다.
- **Current:** The Beast Miao King glared at me with an awkward expression, but soon said nothing and put the large poison-warding pearl in his mouth.
- **Defect:** “Awkward expression” does not convey his displeased, reluctant reaction, and “soon said nothing” is unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** The Beast Miao King shot me a displeased look, but then put the large poison-warding pearl in his mouth without another word.
- **Rationale:** 떨떠름한 conveys displeasure or reluctance, while 별말 없이 means he complied without further comment.

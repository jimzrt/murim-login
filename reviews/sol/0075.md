# Review

Three substantive fidelity issues alter a negation, the ramen misunderstanding, and Taekyung’s relationship with Jinho. Additional findings concern lost contempt and profanity, food terminology, and localized wording.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “네 돈이 내 돈. 내 돈이 내 돈 아니냐.”
- **Current:** “Your money is my money. My money isn’t my money, is it?”
- **Defect:** The second sentence reverses the source negation and changes Jinho’s joke from claiming ownership of both men’s money to denying ownership of his own.
- **Replacement:** “Your money is my money. And my money is my money, isn’t it?”
- **Rationale:** 내 돈이 내 돈 아니냐 is a rhetorical affirmation meaning that his own money is, naturally, also his.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “무슨 얘기야 그게. 갑자기 뭘 끓여.”
- **Current:** “What are you talking about? Why did you suddenly cook ramen?”
- **Defect:** The draft prematurely identifies ramen and turns Taekyung’s question into a request for a reason, making Jinho’s ensuing answer, “Obviously, ramen,” logically incoherent and weakening the misunderstanding that drives the joke.
- **Replacement:** “What are you talking about? What’s this about cooking something all of a sudden?”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung is asking what Jinho suddenly means by talking about having cooked something; Jinho identifies that something as ramen in the next line.

## F03 — Major

# Review

The draft contains two reversed subjects/actions, one broken causal question, several lost pragmatic or register cues, and inconsistent use of the binding name “Huashan.”

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “전 정말 괜찮습니다. 기다리는 일행도 있고요.”
- **Current:** “I’m really fine. I have companions waiting for me, too.”
- **Defect:** The subject of the waiting is reversed. The young man is waiting for his companions; they are not waiting for him.
- **Replacement:** “I’m really fine. I’m waiting for my companions.”
- **Rationale:** His subsequent statement that he has no choice but to wait confirms that he is the one waiting.

- **Current:** Still, the scholar couldn’t force himself into someone else’s party when the young man said he had companions.
- **Defect:** The proposed action is reversed. The scholar had invited the young man to join his table; he was not trying to insert himself into the young man’s party.
- **Replacement:** Still, with the young man saying he had companions, the scholar could hardly insist that he join them.
- **Rationale:** 합석하자고 continues the scholar’s offer for the young man to sit with the two scholars.

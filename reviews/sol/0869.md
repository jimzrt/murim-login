# Review

The draft changes the brothers’ reunion joke, weakens the formal audience with the Emperor, and makes one sexual euphemism explicit. Several smaller details also need correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그래. 그걸 네 글자로 줄이면 형제 상봉이잖아.”

“강호의 무뢰배라 그런지 도무지 이해를 못 하는군. 이건 단순한 형제간의 상봉이 아니라…….”

“아, 이해했다.”

진지한 얼굴로 고개를 끄덕인 내가 말을 이었다.

“다소 복잡한 형제 상봉. 맞지?”

“…….”

“이것도 좀 아닌 것 같아? 너무 길면 우당탕탕 형제 상봉으로 바꿀까? 한 글자가 줄긴 했어도 의미는 비슷해.”
- **Current:** “Right. And if you boil that down to four words, it’s a family reunion.”

“You’re a lawless thug from the martial world, so it seems you can’t understand. This is not merely a reunion between brothers. It’s…”

“Oh, I get it.”

I nodded with a solemn expression.

“A complicated family reunion. Right?”

“……”

“Not quite? Too long? Should we call it a chaotic family reunion instead? One word shorter, but pretty much the same thing.”
- **Defect:** “Family reunion” loses the specific brotherhood, while “four words” and “one word shorter” contradict the English phrases’ word counts. The second proposed phrase is no shorter than the first in the draft.
- **Replacement:** “Right. Four characters: brothers reunited.”

“You’re a lawless thug from the martial world, so it seems you can’t understand. This is not merely a reunion between brothers. It’s…”

“Oh, I get it.”

I nodded with a solemn expression.

“A somewhat complicated reunion of brothers. Right?”

“……”

“Not quite? Too long? How about a chaotic reunion of brothers? One word shorter, but pretty much the same thing.”
- **Rationale:** 형제 specifies brothers. The joke first compresses the encounter into four characters, then offers a second, shorter variation; the proposed English variations differ by one word.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “상산왕 전하께서 황제 폐하를 알현(謁見)하는 것이다.”
- **Current:** “His Highness Prince Shangshan is to meet with His Majesty the Emperor.”
- **Defect:** “Meet with” flattens a formal, hierarchical imperial audience into an ordinary meeting.
- **Replacement:** “His Highness Prince Shangshan is to have an audience with His Majesty the Emperor.”
- **Rationale:** 알현 denotes a formal audience with a sovereign, which is precisely the distinction Jeong tries to make against Taekyung’s description of a brothers’ reunion.

- **Current:** “You need a comfortable bed to sleep well. Could you get a good night’s sleep with a bunch of men in black surrounding you on every side?”

- **Rationale:** 잠자리 refers here to the circumstances in which he sleeps; 시커먼 사내놈들 describes the men disparagingly without establishing black clothing.

# Review

The draft reverses the subject of Taekyung’s charge and repeatedly inflates four billion won to forty billion. It also obscures a combat mechanism, hierarchy cues, and two character jokes.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** E급 헌터인 임꺽정은 닿을 수도, 제대로 볼 수도 없는 속도로 질주를 시작했다.
- **Current:** Im Kkeokjeong, an E-rank Hunter, began sprinting at a speed beyond his ability to reach—or even properly see.
- **Defect:** The sentence makes Im Kkeokjeong the person who begins sprinting, but the omitted Korean subject is Jin Taekyung; Im Kkeokjeong is the E-rank observer who cannot catch or properly see him.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung began sprinting at a speed that Im Kkeokjeong, an E-rank Hunter, could neither match nor properly see.
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence states that Jin Taekyung vanished, and the following lines track his charge across the cavern.

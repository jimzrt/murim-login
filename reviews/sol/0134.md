# Review

The draft contains several altered actions, subjects, and quantities, plus a mistranslated sequence marker and localized terminology and register issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 찰진 소리와 함께 우진태의 고개가 옆으로 돌아갔다. 그는 멍하니 자신의 뺨을 쓰다듬었다.
- **Current:** Woo Jintae’s head snapped to the side with a satisfyingly solid sound. He stared blankly at the floor, one hand pressed to his cheek.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces his action of blankly stroking his cheek with staring at the floor and merely pressing a hand to it.
- **Replacement:** Woo Jintae’s head snapped to the side with a satisfyingly solid sound. He blankly stroked his cheek.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly describes him rubbing or stroking his cheek and says nothing about looking at the floor.

## F02 — Minor

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it loses the dragon-species distinction, reverses who has leverage over Taekyung, omits a profane characterization beat, and prematurely resolves an intentional pronoun ambiguity.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “드래곤 말입니까?”
- **Current:** “A dragon?”
- **Defect:** The draft collapses the source’s distinction between 용 and the English loanword 드래곤, obscuring why Taekyung says the creature was slightly different.
- **Replacement:** “A Western dragon?”
- **Rationale:** The loanword 드래곤 conventionally evokes a Western dragon here, in contrast to the Water God Dragon that Taekyung actually fought.

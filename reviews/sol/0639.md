# Review

One critical action error, two major mechanical or intent errors, and three localized fidelity issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 철사만큼이나 단단하고 탄력을 지닌 그것이 가파른 속도를 만나 예리함을 지니게 된 것이다.
- **Current:** It was as tough and elastic as steel wire, and its steep velocity had given it a razor-sharp edge.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the velocity to the web itself. The source explains that the tough web became dangerous when encountered at high speed.
- **Replacement:** It was as tough and elastic as steel wire, and striking it at such high speed had turned it into a razor-sharp edge.
- **Rationale:** The causal mechanism is the high-speed collision with the web, not the web traveling at high velocity.

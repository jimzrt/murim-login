# Review

Actionable errors in the reported direction, a reversed comparison, the Collapse’s progression, and two jokes.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 동서쪽 3.2km 밖에서 몬스터 웨이브 발생!
- **Current:** —Monster Wave detected 3.2 kilometers to the east!
- **Defect:** Reports the wave only to the east; the source says east and west.
- **Replacement:** —Monster Wave detected 3.2 kilometers away to the east and west!
- **Rationale:** 동서쪽 names both directions, not east alone.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 균열의 단계를 넘어 붕괴가 시작되었다.
- **Current:** The rift had passed its threshold. The Collapse had begun.
- **Defect:** Changes progression beyond the rift stage into a rift crossing its own threshold.
- **Replacement:** The rift stage was over. The Collapse had begun.
- **Rationale:** 균열의 단계를 넘어 describes moving beyond the rift stage.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다행히도 앞서 느낀 그 웃음은 흉악범의 일반인 코스프레가 아니었다.

- **Rationale:** 흉악범의 일반인 코스프레 means a vicious criminal masquerading as an ordinary person.

## F04 — Major

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 염병, 그놈의 라떼 또 나왔네. 허구헌날 모카 골드만 처먹는 양반이.
- **Current:** “Jesus, there he goes with the ‘back in my day’ again. The man lives on Mocha Gold.”
- **Defect:** Loses the latte wordplay that prompts the exchange about Mocha Gold and White Gold.
- **Replacement:** “Jesus, here comes his ‘back in my day’ latte again.[^1] And all he ever drinks is Mocha Gold.”

[^1]: The Korean phrase for “back in my day” sounds like “latte,” setting up the instant-coffee joke.
- **Rationale:** 그놈의 라떼 plays on the sound of the stock ‘back in my day’ phrase; the next lines continue the coffee joke.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래, 거의 반년 내내 그랬지. 단 하루도 안 빼놓고, 꼭두새벽부터 저녁까지.
- **Current:** “Yeah. Almost every day for half a year. From the crack of dawn until evening, without missing a single day.”
- **Defect:** Makes attendance ‘almost every day,’ contradicting the explicit statement that he missed no days. The approximation applies to the duration.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. For nearly half a year. From the crack of dawn until evening, without missing a single day.”
- **Rationale:** 거의 modifies 반년, while 단 하루도 안 빼놓고 rules out missed days.

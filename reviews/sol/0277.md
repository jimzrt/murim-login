# Review

Several action mechanics and subjects are altered, most notably who grits their teeth, the number and trajectory of the iron arrows, and the identity of the final fist technique.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그 살인멸구를 하는 게 우리 역할이다. 그리고 어차피 네놈은 여기서 죽어.”
- **Current:** “That is our role. And either way, you’re going to die here.”
- **Defect:** The vague pronoun obscures Im Yeongjun’s explicit statement that his organization is the one that murders people to silence them.
- **Replacement:** “We’re the ones who kill people to silence them. And either way, you’re going to die here.”
- **Rationale:** 그 살인멸구를 하는 게 우리 역할이다 explicitly identifies the Black Hunters as the perpetrators of such killings, sharpening both the threat and their organizational role.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 나라는 존재에 대한 긴장. 한편으로는 호시탐탐 기회를 엿보는 이리의 눈이 그곳에 있었다.
- **Current:** There was tension toward my existence in them. At the same time, I saw the eyes of a wolf watching for an opportunity.
- **Defect:** “Tension toward my existence” is an unnatural literal collocation and blurs the intended wariness directed at Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** There was wariness of me in them. At the same time, they were the eyes of a wolf constantly watching for an opening.
- **Rationale:** 나라는 존재에 대한 긴장 describes Im Yeongjun’s apprehension or wariness toward Taekyung, while 호시탐탐 emphasizes persistently waiting to seize an opportunity.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 나는 놈의 고함을 들으며 땅을 박찼다. 이를 악문 채 달려드는 블랙 헌터들을 향해 창을 내리그었다.
- **Current:** I heard his shout as I kicked off the ground.

I ground my teeth and swung my spear at the Black Hunters charging toward me.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the teeth-gritting action to Taekyung, but the source assigns it to the charging Black Hunters.
- **Replacement:** I heard his shout as I kicked off the ground.

The Black Hunters charged toward me with gritted teeth, and I brought my spear down at them.
- **Rationale:** 이를 악문 채 modifies 달려드는 블랙 헌터들, not the narrator; the altered subject changes the combatants’ emotional presentation.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그래도 대장이랍시고, 역시 이놈이 제일 낫다.
- **Current:** Even if he was their leader, he was still the best of them.
- **Defect:** “Even if” incorrectly makes his leadership concessive, as though being the leader worked against the conclusion that he was the strongest.
- **Replacement:** He was their leader for a reason. As expected, he was the best of them.
- **Rationale:** 대장이랍시고 and 역시 frame his superior ability as fitting or expected for the group’s leader, not surprising despite that role.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘사격(四擊).’

전력을 다한 멸천신권이 방어막을 후려쳤다.

숭산의 어느 이름 모를 절벽처럼, 멸천신권은 앞을 막아서는 모든 것을 깨트리고 부쉈다.
- **Current:** *Four Strike.*

The Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, unleashed with all my strength, hammered into the barriers.

Like some nameless cliff on Mount Song, the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist shattered and crushed everything in its path.
- **Defect:** The draft substitutes the earlier Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist (멸염신권) for the explicitly different 멸천신권 and also creates an illogical comparison in which the fist destroys things like a cliff does.
- **Replacement:** *Four Strike.*

The Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist, unleashed with all my strength, hammered into the barriers.

Just as it had shattered some nameless cliff on Mount Song, the Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist broke and crushed everything blocking its path.
- **Rationale:** The source changes from 멸염신권 in Three Strike to 멸천신권 in Four Strike, so retaining the earlier technique changes the named attack. The Mount Song line invokes what the technique did to a cliff rather than comparing the technique itself to a cliff.

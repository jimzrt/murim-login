# Review

The draft contains one critical identity/mechanism error and several meaningful losses involving perception, rank terminology, quantity, battle characterization, euphemistic phrasing, and dialogue logic.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 평범한 민간인이 이 모습을 지켜봤다면 무슨 엉뚱한 소리인가 싶겠지만, 적어도 나는 아니다.
- **Current:** But I wasn’t an ordinary civilian.
- **Defect:** The source contrasts an ordinary civilian’s confusion with Jin’s understanding; the draft instead makes a statement about Jin’s civilian status.
- **Replacement:** But I knew exactly what he meant.
- **Rationale:** 적어도 나는 아니다 refers back to wondering what Michael meant, not to being an ordinary civilian.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 내 어깨 너머로 굳게 닫힌 문을 물끄러미 바라보던 미카엘 실베르트가 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** Michael Silbert stared absently at the firmly closed door over my shoulder, then nodded.
- **Defect:** “Absently” makes Michael seem distracted, whereas 물끄러미 describes a sustained, deliberate gaze immediately before he confirms the concealed target’s presence.
- **Replacement:** Michael Silbert gazed steadily at the firmly closed door over my shoulder, then nodded.
- **Rationale:** The deliberate gaze supports the following detection mechanism and Michael’s controlled demeanor.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 원하는 목표를 발견하자마자 순식간에 주인의 몸 안으로 갈무리되는 놈의 기파(氣波)를.
- **Current:** The moment he discovered the target he wanted, his qi wave was instantly gathered back into his master’s body.
- **Defect:** “His master’s body” invents a separate superior or owner for Michael, changing identity and hierarchy. The qi wave is being withdrawn into the body of its owner—Michael himself.
- **Replacement:** The instant the desired target was found, his qi wave was drawn back into his own body.
- **Rationale:** 주인의 몸 refers to the body belonging to the qi wave’s owner, not the body of Michael’s master.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그것은 아주 자연스러우면서도 숙련된 솜씨였고, 헌터보다는 무림의 초절정 고수에 가까운 모습이었다.
- **Current:** It was completely natural, carried out with practiced skill. He looked less like a Hunter and more like a supreme martial arts master of the Murim.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the formal realm 초절정 into a generic description and therefore loses Michael’s stated power hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** It was completely natural, carried out with practiced skill. He looked less like a Hunter and more like a Supreme Peak master of the Murim.
- **Rationale:** 초절정 is the established realm term “Supreme Peak,” not merely an adjective meaning highly skilled.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 파리에 주둔하고 있던 천 명의 헌터들은 수십 배에 달하는 몬스터들을 상대로 시가전을 펼쳤더랬다.
- **Current:** A thousand Hunters stationed in the city fought a street battle against monsters numbering dozens of times their own strength.
- **Defect:** The source compares the monsters’ number with the Hunters’ number, while “their own strength” shifts the comparison toward combat power and produces an incoherent quantity.
- **Replacement:** A thousand Hunters stationed in the city waged an urban battle against monsters that outnumbered them dozens of times over.
- **Rationale:** 수십 배에 달하는 describes a force numbering dozens of times as many monsters as the thousand Hunters.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 어떤 지원도 없이. 말 그대로 처절하게.
- **Current:** Without any support.

It had been nothing short of slaughter.
- **Defect:** “Slaughter” recasts the desperate, grueling nature of the unsupported fighting as a massacre, changing the battle’s characterization.
- **Replacement:** Without any support.

A desperate struggle in every sense of the word.
- **Rationale:** 처절하게 emphasizes desperate and harrowing struggle, not necessarily one-sided slaughter.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 일곱 번의 밤낮이 지나고 2020년의 마지막 해마저 완전히 기울었을 때.
- **Current:** when even the last year of 2020 had completely drawn to a close
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 해 as “year,” yielding the impossible phrase “the last year of 2020” and losing the image of the year’s final sun setting.
- **Replacement:** when even the final sun of 2020 had fully set
- **Rationale:** Here 해 is the sun, reinforced by 기울었을 때, which describes it sinking or setting.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 내가 수업을 끝마치고 돌아오면 항상 낡아빠진 소파 위에 누워 흐느적거리고 있었지. 처음 보는 낯선 남자들과 함께.
- **Current:** Whenever I came home after class, she was always lying on that worn-out sofa, twitching and moaning. With strange men I had never seen before.
- **Defect:** “Twitching and moaning” adds explicit physical and sexual behavior absent from the source, which leaves her condition and activity euphemistic.
- **Replacement:** Whenever I came home after class, she was always sprawled limply across that worn-out sofa. With strange men I had never seen before.
- **Rationale:** 흐느적거리고 conveys limp or languid movement; it does not state that she moaned.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “마치 괴물을 마주한 것 같은 눈빛이군. 이제 자네도 내가 두려워졌나?”

“자네도?”
- **Current:** “You’re looking at me as though I were a monster. Are you afraid of me now?”

“Now?”
- **Defect:** The draft treats 이제 as the point Jin challenges and drops the additive 도. Jin is questioning Michael’s claim that he, too, has joined the others who fear him.
- **Replacement:** “You’re looking at me as though I were a monster. Now you’re afraid of me too?”

“Me too?”
- **Rationale:** 자네도 means “you too,” which directly prompts Michael’s explanation that everyone who knows him reacts with fear and respect.

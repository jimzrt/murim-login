# Review

Two meaningful subject/dialogue errors and four localized fidelity or wording issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘이제라도 알게 된 것이 다행인가?’
- **Current:** *Perhaps it’s fortunate that he found out even now.*
- **Defect:** The direct thought changes the implied subject from Jeok Cheongang himself to an ambiguous third person, most naturally Jin Wikyung, who has not yet learned the relevant fact.
- **Replacement:** *Perhaps it was fortunate that I found out now.*
- **Rationale:** This is Jeok Cheongang’s internal thought about his own belated discovery of Jin Taekyung’s extraordinary constitution.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “기루나 들락거리던 삼류 한량이 절정 고수가 되기까지 걸린 시간이기도 하지요.”
- **Current:** “It’s also how long it took a Third Rate pleasure-house loafer to become a Peak master.”
- **Defect:** The compound “pleasure-house loafer” is unnatural and obscures the explicit action that Taekyung habitually frequented pleasure houses.
- **Replacement:** “It’s also how long it took a Third Rate wastrel who frequented pleasure houses to become a Peak master.”
- **Rationale:** 들락거리던 means he regularly went in and out of pleasure houses; it does not describe a type of loafer associated with such a place.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “아뇨. 술 맛이 너무 좋아서 그만.”
- **Current:** “No, nothing. The liquor tastes so good that I lost track of what I was saying.”
- **Defect:** The translation invents an explanation about losing track of what he was saying; the Korean gives only a hurried, trailing excuse about the liquor tasting good.
- **Replacement:** “No. The liquor just tastes so good, that’s all.”
- **Rationale:** 그만 leaves the excuse understated and does not say that Taekyung forgot what he was saying.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 뜨거운 열기와 함께 순간 눈앞이 아찔해질 정도의 주독(酒毒)이 뿜어져 나온다.

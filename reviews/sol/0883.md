# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it assigns an unspecified sibling a gender, omits who commands the Inner Palace guard, and alters several smaller details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아버지와 어머니, 내 동생…….
- **Current:** “T-Twelve years ago. Grrk. My father and mother, my little brother… Cough.”
- **Defect:** The draft identifies the younger sibling as a brother, though the source gives no gender.
- **Replacement:** “T-Twelve years ago. Grrk. My father and mother, my younger sibling… Cough.”
- **Rationale:** 동생 specifies a younger sibling, not a brother.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 외궁을 지키는 금위군도 충분히 정예병이지만, 내궁 경비는 황제 직속 친위대인 금의위가 맡고 있죠.
- **Current:** The Embroidered Uniform Guard is responsible for the Inner Palace, though the Imperial Guard protecting the Outer Palace is elite enough in its own right.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the Embroidered Uniform Guard is the Emperor’s personal guard.
- **Replacement:** The Imperial Guard protecting the Outer Palace is elite enough, but the Inner Palace is guarded by the Embroidered Uniform Guard—the Emperor’s personal guard.
- **Rationale:** 황제 직속 친위대 establishes the Guard’s direct relationship to the Emperor and the hierarchy between the two palace defenses.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 자랑스러운 열화문의 선조들께서 이 광경을 보았다면
- **Current:** If the proud ancestors of Fire Gate had seen this, they would have wept underground… or maybe not.
- **Defect:** The established organization name is shortened.
- **Replacement:** If the proud ancestors of the Fire Gate Clan had seen this, they would have wept underground… or maybe not.
- **Rationale:** 열화문 has the binding rendering “Fire Gate Clan.”

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 툭하면 요절(夭折)하는 게 우리 열화문의 전통 아닙니까.
- **Current:** Isn’t dying young practically a Fire Gate tradition?
- **Defect:** The established organization name is shortened again.
- **Replacement:** Isn’t dying young practically a tradition in our Fire Gate Clan?
- **Rationale:** The source names 열화문 and says 우리, “our.”

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 태산. 아니, 거산 저 친구의 덩치가 워낙 거대하니
- **Current:** Taishan—or Geosan, as you called him—is enormous.
- **Defect:** “As you called him” assigns the mistaken name to the listener rather than preserving the speaker’s own correction.
- **Replacement:** Taishan—no, Geosan—that fellow is enormous.
- **Rationale:** The Divine Physician says the first name, corrects himself, and then continues; he does not attribute the name to Jeok Cheongang.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 청초한 미모를 자랑하는 기녀는 깜짝 놀라 눈을 깜빡이더니 이내 몸을 내던지듯 바닥에 엎드렸다.
- **Current:** Her eyes widened in surprise, then she threw herself face-first onto the ground.
- **Defect:** The draft changes her surprised blink into widened eyes.
- **Replacement:** She blinked in surprise, then threw herself face-first onto the ground.
- **Rationale:** 눈을 깜빡이더니 describes blinking, not widening her eyes.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 멀뚱멀뚱 눈을 굴리는 태산의 모습에, 적천강은 고개를 숙이며 생각했다.
- **Current:** As Taishan blinked vacantly, Jeok Cheongang lowered his head and thought.
- **Defect:** Taishan looks around blankly; he does not blink.
- **Replacement:** As Taishan looked around blankly, Jeok Cheongang lowered his head and thought.
- **Rationale:** 눈을 굴리는 describes his eyes moving, while 멀뚱멀뚱 conveys his blank expression.

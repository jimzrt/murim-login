## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이십 대 후반의 모태솔로인 나로서는 거구의 흑인과 깍지 손을 낀다는 게 불편할 수밖에 없었다.
- **Current:** As a lifelong bachelor in his late twenties, I couldn’t help feeling uncomfortable about interlocking fingers with a huge Black man.
- **Defect:** “Lifelong bachelor” only establishes that Jin has never married, while 모태솔로 means he has never been in a romantic relationship.
- **Replacement:** As a guy in his late twenties who had been single his entire life, I couldn’t help feeling uncomfortable about interlocking fingers with a huge Black man.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly invokes Jin’s complete lack of romantic experience as the reason interlocking fingers feels uncomfortable.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 「그럼 화력 지원은 기대하기 어렵게 됐네. 큰 거 한 방 잘못 쐈다가는 대참사가 벌어질 테니까.」
- **Current:** “Then it looks like we can’t expect fire support. If someone fires a big one in the wrong place, it’ll cause a catastrophe.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes fire support categorically unavailable and narrows the danger to firing at the wrong location. The source says support has become difficult because any major shot that goes wrong could cause a catastrophe.
- **Replacement:** “Then it looks like fire support will be difficult. If one of the big shots goes wrong, it could cause a catastrophe.”
- **Rationale:** 기대하기 어렵게 됐네 expresses difficulty rather than impossibility, while 잘못 쐈다가는 refers broadly to a shot going wrong, not specifically being fired in the wrong place.

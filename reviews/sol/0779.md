# Review

Several meaningful shifts in the draft affect the scene’s logic, attribution, and final reveal.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 인류가 신으로부터 선물 받은 힘. 오라(Aura)의 광휘가 온 사방을 물들였고, 이내 한 존재를 향해 겨누어졌다.
- **Current:** The radiance of Aura, the power gifted to humanity by the gods, spread in every direction, then turned toward one being.
- **Defect:** Changes the singular 신 to multiple gods.
- **Replacement:** The radiance of Aura, the power God had gifted to humanity, spread in every direction, then turned toward one being.
- **Rationale:** The source names one divine giver, not several.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 자리의 모두는 오직 몬스터와 대적하기 위해 신에게 선택받고, 인류로부터 사냥꾼(Hunter)이라는 이름을 부여받은 이들.
- **Current:** Everyone here had been chosen by the gods to face monsters, and given the name Hunter by humanity.
- **Defect:** Again pluralizes 신 and drops the exclusivity of the purpose for which the Hunters were chosen.
- **Replacement:** Everyone here had been chosen by God for the sole purpose of fighting monsters, and given the name Hunter by humanity.
- **Rationale:** 신 is singular, and 오직 makes fighting monsters their sole appointed purpose.

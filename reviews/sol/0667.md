# Review

The draft is broadly faithful but contains two altered quantities, an expanded subject, a misplaced temporal modifier, and several localized hierarchy, causality, and English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제각각 다른 가면을 쓴 흑의인 이십여 명이 사방을 경계하며 점혈 당한 이들을 풀숲 사이에 숨기는 중이었다.
- **Current:** More than twenty men in black, each wearing a different mask, were keeping watch in every direction while hiding the Pressure-Point Struck warriors among the grass.
- **Defect:** 이십여 명 means approximately twenty, not more than twenty. The rendering of 점혈 당한 이들 is also an unnatural, incorrectly capitalized construction.
- **Replacement:** Around twenty black-clad figures, each wearing a different mask, kept watch in every direction while hiding those whose pressure points had been sealed among the grass.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the approximate quantity and naturally describes the incapacitated warriors without turning the technique into an adjectival label.

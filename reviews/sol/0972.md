# Review

Several passages alter the attack’s scale, the effect of the signal, or the dialogue’s meaning.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 전력을 다한 것까지는 아니더라도, 칠 할 이상의 힘을 쏟아부은 일격을 피했다는 것은 충분한 여력이 있었다는 증거.
- **Current:** Even if they hadn’t dodged an attack unleashed with his full power, evading one in which he’d put more than seventy percent of his strength was proof they’d had plenty of energy to spare.
- **Defect:** The opening clause says they had not dodged a full-power attack, rather than saying the attack they dodged was not at full power.
- **Replacement:** The attack hadn’t been at full power, but they’d dodged a strike backed by more than seventy percent of his strength. That proved they still had plenty in reserve.
- **Rationale:** The qualification concerns how much power he put into the attack; the two did evade it.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 크게 부풀어 오르며 폭발한 충격파가 반경 십여 장을 휩쓸었다.
- **Current:** The impact swelled and exploded, sweeping across an area more than ten *zhang* wide.
- **Defect:** A radius of over ten zhang becomes an area over ten zhang wide, reducing the stated reach.
- **Replacement:** The shock wave swelled and exploded, sweeping everything within a radius of more than ten *zhang*.
- **Rationale:** 반경 specifies the radius, not the total width.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 궁성이 신속하게 쏘아 보낸 빛의 화살이 그중 대부분을 휩쓸었으나, 허공에서 뿔뿔이 흩어진 일부마저 어찌할 수는 없었다.
- **Current:** The Bow Saint quickly fired arrows of light that swept away most of it, but she couldn’t stop the few scattered flares that had already spread through the air.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 천하를 도모했던 천마(天魔)가, 그랬던 그를 쓰러트리고 천하를 안정시켰던 무신(武神)이 바라보았던 세상을 조금이나마 알 것 같다고.
- **Current:** He felt he could almost understand the world as the Heavenly Demon had seen it, ruling over the Ten Thousand Demonic Paths with martial prowess that reached the heavens, and as the Martial God had seen it when he defeated the Heavenly Demon and brought peace to the land.
- **Defect:** The Heavenly Demon’s ambition to seize the world is omitted.

## F05 — Major

- **Source:** 제 똘똘이가 얼마나 똘똘하게 잘 생겼는데.
- **Current:** My little guy is so handsome and well-proportioned.
- **Defect:** “Well-proportioned” replaces the joke that 똘똘이, a euphemism for his penis, is 똘똘하게—clever-looking.

## F07 — Minor

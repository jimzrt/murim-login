# Review

Several localized errors change quantities, characterization, and a recurring metaphor.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한여름의 어느 날에는 장맛비 대신 우박이 쏟아지기도 했고
- **Current:** On some midsummer days, hail poured down in place of the monsoon rains.
- **Defect:** Changes a single midsummer day into multiple days.
- **Replacement:** One midsummer day, hail poured down in place of the monsoon rains.
- **Rationale:** 어느 날 refers to one day.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한 가마니의 쌀은 몇 사람의 손을 거쳐 한 홉으로 줄어들었다.
- **Current:** by the time a sack of it had passed through a few hands, it had dwindled to a single handful.
- **Defect:** Renders the measured quantity 한 홉 as a handful.
- **Replacement:** by the time a sack of it had passed through a few hands, it had dwindled to a single cup.
- **Rationale:** A hop is a volume measure of roughly 180 milliliters, not a handful.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 자신이 어째서 이렇게 된 걸까, 하는 의문을.
- **Current:** *How had things come to this?*
- **Defect:** Turns each doomed bandit’s question about their own fate into a general question about events.
- **Replacement:** *How had I ended up like this?*
- **Rationale:** 자신이 makes the question personal to the person facing death.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 상대는 혼자만의 힘으로 반경 백여 장 안에 벌어지는 모든 일을 파악할 수 있으니.
- **Current:** His counterpart, however, could learn everything happening within a hundred-odd yards by his own power alone.
- **Defect:** Treats a jang as a yard, shrinking the radius of Mae Jonghak’s perception by roughly a factor of three.
- **Replacement:** His counterpart, however, could sense everything happening within a radius of more than three hundred yards by his own power alone.
- **Rationale:** 백여 장 is over one hundred jang; one jang is roughly 3.3 meters.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 계속해서 흘러나오는 자연스러운 하대.
- **Current:** The man continued to speak down to the old man without hesitation.
- **Defect:** Implies condescension where the source describes Mae Jonghak’s natural use of familiar speech.
- **Replacement:** The man continued to address the old man with the easy familiarity of a senior.
- **Rationale:** 하대 marks speech hierarchy here; the following sentence establishes his standing to use it, not contempt for Song Ho.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 산서성에 배치된 관군들은 상당한 정예들이지만
- **Current:** Shanxi Province had a considerable number of elite government troops.
- **Defect:** Adds a claim about how many elite troops Shanxi had.
- **Replacement:** The government troops stationed in Shanxi Province were highly capable.
- **Rationale:** 상당한 정예 describes the troops’ caliber, not the number of elite troops.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 숲 전체를 살피기에 바빠, 그 안의 작은 나뭇가지를 보지 못했다는 것.
- **Current:** “You were so busy taking in the whole forest that you failed to see the small Go within it.”
- **Defect:** Replaces a small branch in the forest metaphor with the unrelated game Go.
- **Replacement:** “You were so busy looking at the whole forest that you failed to see the small branch within it.”
- **Rationale:** 나뭇가지 is a tree branch; Mae Jonghak’s metaphor leads directly into Song Ho’s later reference to that branch.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 매종학의 손짓과 함께 뻗어 나온 기운이 굳게 닫혀 있던 문을 열어젖힌 것도.
- **Current:** With a motion of Mae Jonghak’s hand, a surge of energy reached out and pushed open the firmly shut door.
- **Defect:** Uses generic energy for Murim 기운 and weakens 열어젖힌, which indicates the door was flung open.
- **Replacement:** With a motion of Mae Jonghak’s hand, a surge of qi reached out and flung open the firmly shut door.
- **Rationale:** The source identifies Murim energy and describes a forceful opening.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 제가 미처 보지 못한 그 가지의 이름을.
- **Current:** “I understand now. I know the name of the Go I failed to see.”
- **Defect:** Repeats the mistaken Go rendering and breaks Song Ho’s reference back to Mae Jonghak’s branch metaphor.
- **Replacement:** “I understand now. I know the name of the branch I failed to see.”

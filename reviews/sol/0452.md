# Review

Several meaningful fidelity issues remain, chiefly an altered martial hierarchy, a lost beggar-related exchange, and an omitted K-pop wordplay beat.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대부분은 일류에도 못 미치는 이, 삼류 칼잡이들이고
- **Current:** Most of them were Third Rate or barely below First Rate swordsmen.
- **Defect:** The draft collapses the explicitly stated Second and Third Rate ranks into an unclear range and suggests that some are nearly First Rate.
- **Replacement:** Most were Second or Third Rate swordsmen who fell short of First Rate.
- **Rationale:** 이, 삼류 explicitly identifies them as Second or Third Rate, while 일류에도 못 미치는 states that they do not reach First Rate.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제갈세가는 무당파와 함께 호북성을 양분하는 명문 대파.
- **Current:** The Zhuge Clan was one of the great Murim families that divided control of Hubei Province with Wudang.
- **Defect:** The draft calls both parties “great Murim families,” incorrectly categorizing Wudang as a family and replacing the source's broader description of the Zhuge Clan as a prestigious major faction.
- **Replacement:** The Zhuge Clan was a prestigious major faction that divided control of Hubei Province with Wudang.
- **Rationale:** 명문 대파 means a prestigious major faction or power, not specifically a great family, and Wudang is a sect.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 은자 하나를 갖기 위해 아귀다툼을 벌이는 양민들의 모습은 씁쓸하면서도 짠했다.
- **Current:** The commoners fighting tooth and nail for a single silver nyang were bitter and pitiful.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the commoners themselves “bitter,” implying their emotional state, whereas the source describes Taekyung's reaction to seeing their desperate struggle as bitter and sympathetic.
- **Replacement:** The sight of the commoners fighting tooth and nail for a single silver nyang left a bitter taste in my mouth and made me feel sorry for them.
- **Rationale:** 씁쓸하면서도 짠했다 expresses the narrator's mixed bitterness and sympathy toward the sight, not bitterness felt by the commoners.

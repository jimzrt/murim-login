# Review

One major humor/register mistranslation and three localized terminology or wording issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 똥이 아니라 대변이라고 하는 거랑 뭐가 다른 거야.
- **Current:** What’s the difference between saying “tie someone up” and “secure someone”?
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the source’s crude euphemism joke with a literal restatement of the preceding exchange, losing both the shit-versus-feces comparison and Taekyung’s irreverent register.
- **Replacement:** How was that any different from calling shit “feces”?
- **Rationale:** Taekyung specifically compares Jeok Cheongang’s substitution of “secure” for “tie up” to using the more polite word 대변 instead of 똥.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 젊은 놈이라 그런지 기력도 금방 회복되는군.
- **Current:** “Your energy recovers quickly because you’re young. We can move on to the next step right away.”
- **Defect:** “Energy” is ambiguous in this Murim context and can be mistaken for internal energy, while 기력 here means physical strength or vitality.
- **Replacement:** “I suppose being young means you recover your strength quickly. We can move on to the next step right away.”
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang is commenting on Taekyung’s general physical recovery after collapsing, not specifically the restoration of his internal energy.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 참을 수 없는 고통에 핏발 선 진태경의 눈동자와는 달리 적천강의 그것은 열기로 번뜩이고 있었다.
- **Current:** Unlike Jin Taekyung’s bloodshot eyes, sharpened by unbearable pain, Jeok Cheongang’s eyes gleamed with heat.
- **Defect:** “Sharpened by unbearable pain” incorrectly modifies the eyes and introduces an image absent from the source; the pain has made Taekyung’s eyes bloodshot.
- **Replacement:** Unlike Jin Taekyung’s eyes, bloodshot from unbearable pain, Jeok Cheongang’s gleamed with heat.
- **Rationale:** The source directly attributes the bloodshot condition of Taekyung’s eyes to the unbearable pain and contrasts them with the heat gleaming in Jeok Cheongang’s eyes.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 길든 투우(鬪牛)는 돌격하는 방법을 종종 까먹는 법.
- **Current:** A trained fighting bull sometimes forgot how to charge.
- **Defect:** “Trained” reverses the metaphor’s relevant quality: 길든 means tamed or domesticated, not trained for combat.
- **Replacement:** A tamed fighting bull sometimes forgot how to charge.
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang’s point is that restraining and taming the newly introduced energy too soon could rob it of its force, just as a tamed bull may forget how to charge.

# Review

Several passages change who or what is acted upon, including a severed head, spoken dialogue, and a blood-soaked weapon.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 며칠 전, 승전보 대신 되돌아온 수하의 목에 남은 상흔(傷痕)을 보았기에 이런 판단을 내릴 수밖에 없었다.
- **Current:** He had seen the scars on the neck of the subordinate who had returned a few days ago instead of bringing back news of victory. That was why he had no choice but to make this decision.
- **Defect:** The subordinate did not return alive; only his severed head came back. The source also describes a wound, not multiple scars.
- **Replacement:** A few days ago, his subordinate’s severed head had returned instead of news of victory. Jamukha had seen the wound on its neck, leaving him no choice but to make this decision.
- **Rationale:** The preceding passage establishes that only the subordinate’s head returned, and Jamukha bases his judgment on the mark left on it.

아니, 정확히는 그의 손에 들려 있는 병장기 때문이었다.
- **Current:** Jamukha recognized him as one of the Keshik who had headed into the gorge with the three commanders moments earlier. His gaze darkened.

No—the weapons in the man’s hands did.
- **Defect:** The correction identifies why Jamukha’s gaze darkens; it does not say the weapons themselves darken.

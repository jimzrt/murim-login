# Review

The draft misidentifies the War Mage, narrows casualties to deaths, and makes Johnson’s last contact sound as though it was with Jin. Both System panels also retain brackets prohibited by the binding format.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마치 저격이라도 하는 것처럼 완벽에 가까운 명중률.
- **Current:** Their accuracy was almost perfect, as if she were sniping her targets.
- **Defect:** The narration calls the War Mage “she,” but the War Mage in this scene is Magic Johnson.
- **Replacement:** His accuracy was almost perfect, as if he were sniping his targets.
- **Rationale:** The source does not introduce a female caster; it identifies the arriving Grand Mage as Magic Johnson.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다행히 우리 쪽 사상자는 없……
- **Current:** “Thank goodness none of our people were killed… What is that insolent look for, human?”
- **Defect:** “Killed” excludes injuries from the Skeleton King’s claim.
- **Replacement:** “Thankfully, we didn’t suffer any casualties… What’s that insolent look for, human?”
- **Rationale:** 사상자 covers both the dead and the injured.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마지막으로 연락이 닿았을 때만 해도 네가 깨어났다는 소식은 듣지 못했으니까.
- **Current:** “Ah, I see. The last time I was in contact with you, I hadn’t heard that you’d woken up.”
- **Defect:** “With you” makes Jin the person Johnson contacted, although Johnson says he had not heard Jin was awake.

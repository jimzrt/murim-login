# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains two meaningful semantic distortions, one altered emotional cue, and several localized register or natural-English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하지만 뭐랄까, 순간 눈앞의 청년에게서 느꼈던 기운은 딱 그 정도였다.
- **Current:** But, what could I say? The qi I sensed from the young man in front of me felt exactly that impressive.
- **Defect:** The current wording reinforces the preceding description of his achievement as remarkable, whereas 딱 그 정도였다 limits Taekyung's assessment: the young man's qi amounted to no more than an ordinary Peak master's.
- **Replacement:** But how should I put it? At that moment, the qi I sensed from the young man before me seemed to amount to no more than that.
- **Rationale:** 딱 그 정도였다 establishes the ceiling that leads directly into Taekyung's punchline, “An ordinary Peak master?”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 살아남은 산적들이 철구와 사슬을 든 괴인의 짓이라고 했다던데?
- **Current:** They said the surviving bandits claimed it was the work of a monster carrying iron balls and chains.
- **Defect:** 괴인 means a strange, bizarre, or mysterious person here, not a literal monster. The draft changes what the witnesses identified Taekyung as.
- **Replacement:** They said the surviving bandits claimed it was the work of a strange man carrying iron balls and chains.
- **Rationale:** The description concerns an unidentified human martial artist distinguished by his unusual appearance and weapons.

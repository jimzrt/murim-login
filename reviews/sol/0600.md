# Review

One critical speech-attribution error, two major losses of causal or honorific nuance, and several localized terminology and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 국가장이 진행되는 내내 곁에 있던 백한성 대통령을 떠올리며 물었다.

세상 누구보다 속마음을 숨기는 것에 능숙한 직업군이 있다면, 그건 바로 정치인일 것이다.

“정말 그럴까요?”
- **Current:** I thought of President Baek Hanseong, who had been beside me throughout the national funeral, and asked:

“If there’s anyone in the world best at hiding what they really think, it would be politicians.”

“Are you sure?”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Jin’s internal observation about politicians into dialogue, changing what he actually says to Team Leader Choi.
- **Replacement:** I thought of President Baek Hanseong, who had been beside me throughout the national funeral, and asked. If there was one profession better than any other at hiding what they truly thought, it was politicians.

“Are you sure?”
- **Rationale:** Only “정말 그럴까요?” is spoken dialogue in the source; the preceding sentence is narration explaining Jin’s suspicion.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “이정룡이나 송천우가 손을 쓴 것은 아닌 것 같습니다.”
- **Current:** “I don’t think Lee Jungryong or Song Cheonwoo had anything to do with it.”
- **Defect:** “Had anything to do with it” broadly denies their involvement, even though they concealed Cheon Taemin’s condition. The source specifically denies that they caused or inflicted his condition.
- **Replacement:** “I don’t think Lee Jungryong or Song Cheonwoo were the ones who did that to him.”
- **Rationale:** 손을 쓴 here refers to foul play against Cheon Taemin, not to every form of involvement in the matter.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** “……의식을 잃어요? 천태민이. 아니, 그분께서?”
- **Current:** “…Lost consciousness? Cheon Taemin? No, he did?”
- **Defect:** The awkward final question loses Jin’s immediate correction from casually naming Cheon Taemin to referring to him with marked respect.
- **Replacement:** “…He lost consciousness? Cheon Taemin? I mean… *the* Cheon Taemin?”
- **Rationale:** 그분께서 is an honorific self-correction prompted by Cheon Taemin’s immense stature; emphasizing “the” conveys that respectful elevation naturally in English.

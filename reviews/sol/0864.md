# Review

Several passages change the authority at issue, a character’s action, the viewpoint, or the timing of an encounter.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천자(天子). 하늘을 대신하여 이 천하를 다스리시는 황제 폐하를 제외한다면, 그 누가 감히 금의위를 사사로이 벌할 수 있겠나.
- **Current:** “The Son of Heaven. Who would dare punish the Embroidered Uniform Guard for its own purposes, if not His Majesty the Emperor, who rules this world on Heaven’s behalf?”
- **Defect:** The wording suggests that the Emperor might punish the Guard for his own purposes. Baek Yeon’s point is that no one but the Emperor has authority to punish it.
- **Replacement:** “The Son of Heaven. Aside from His Majesty the Emperor, who rules this world on Heaven’s behalf, who would dare punish the Embroidered Uniform Guard on a private whim?”
- **Rationale:** 황제 폐하를 제외한다면 excludes the Emperor from the challenge to everyone else’s authority; 사사로이 describes an unauthorized, private punishment.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 호위인지 포위인지 알 수 없는 그 움직임 속에서 백연은 나직한 전음(傳音)을 흘려보냈다.
- **Current:** As they moved in a way that made it impossible to tell whether they were escorting or surrounding us, Baek Yeon sent a quiet Sound Transmission.
- **Defect:** “Us” abruptly makes the narration first-person, though this passage has no first-person narrator.
- **Replacement:** As the guards moved in a way that made it impossible to tell whether they were escorting the party or surrounding them, Baek Yeon sent a quiet Sound Transmission.
- **Rationale:** The source leaves the escort-versus-encirclement ambiguous without introducing a first-person participant.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 장장 십여 년 만에 마주한 그 어린아이는 훌쩍 자라나 있었다. 몸도, 마음도.
- **Current:** The young child he’d met again after more than ten years had grown considerably. In body and in mind.
- **Defect:** The draft places the growth after a reunion with a young child. Baek Yeon is seeing the child again now, after more than a decade, and finds that he has grown.
- **Replacement:** The child he was seeing again after more than a decade had grown considerably—in body and in mind.
- **Rationale:** 십여 년 만에 마주한 describes the present encounter after a long separation; 자라나 있었다 describes the change already evident at that encounter.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아, 정 천호는 잠시 남고.”
- **Current:** “Oh, and Captain Jeong, stay behind a moment.”
- **Defect:** “Captain” drops Jeong Hogun’s specified Thousand Captain rank.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, and Thousand Captain Jeong, stay behind a moment.”
- **Rationale:** 천호 is his rank, and the distinction matters in the Guard’s command hierarchy.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 금의위가 주시해야 할 이는 용의 핏줄뿐만 아니라, 감히 신룡(神龍)이라 칭해진 강호의 무뢰배 또한 있었으니.
- **Current:** The Embroidered Uniform Guard had to keep its eyes on more than just the bloodline of the dragon. There was also a martial-world ruffian who dared call himself the Divine Dragon.
- **Defect:** The draft says Jin bestowed the title on himself; the source says he was called the Divine Dragon.
- **Replacement:** The Embroidered Uniform Guard had to keep its eyes on more than just the bloodline of the dragon. There was also the martial-world ruffian who had dared to bear the title of Divine Dragon.
- **Rationale:** 칭해진 is passive and does not attribute the naming to Jin.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 백연은 착 가라앉은 눈동자로 주위를 훑었다.
- **Current:** Baek Yeon swept his lowered gaze over those around him.
- **Defect:** “Lowered gaze” makes Baek Yeon look downward; the source describes the quality of his eyes, not the direction of his gaze.
- **Replacement:** Baek Yeon swept a cold, steady gaze over those around him.
- **Rationale:** 착 가라앉은 눈동자 conveys a settled, subdued intensity as he surveys the group.

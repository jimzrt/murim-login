# Review

Four actionable findings: an invented blood oath, a changed artifact history, a mistranslated reaction, and a weakened political motive.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그가 전날 밤 언급했던 연판장(連判狀)에 서명한 이들은 충분한 준비가 되었을까.
- **Current:** Were the people who had signed the blood-signed pact he’d mentioned the night before fully prepared?
- **Defect:** “Blood-signed” invents a blood oath; the source only says they signed a collective pledge.
- **Replacement:** Were the people who had signed the collective pledge he’d mentioned the night before fully prepared?
- **Rationale:** 연판장 identifies a document signed jointly, not a pledge signed in blood.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 사천당가의 신물이자 암천의 표적이 되었던 신병이기 중 하나.
- **Current:** One of the divine weapons that had once been a sacred treasure of the Sichuan Tang Clan and a target of Dark Heaven.
- **Defect:** “Had once been” implies the ring is no longer the clan’s artifact, and “sacred treasure” misses the binding rendering of 신물.
- **Replacement:** A divine artifact of the Sichuan Tang Clan, and one of the divine weapons Dark Heaven had targeted.
- **Rationale:** The source states the ring’s status as the clan’s 신물 and places the past tense on Dark Heaven’s targeting.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그런 나를 바라보며 입맛을 다신 황제가 문득 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** The Emperor watched me and clicked his tongue, then suddenly spoke.
- **Defect:** Clicking his tongue changes the Emperor’s lip-smacking reaction into a gesture of disapproval.
- **Replacement:** The Emperor watched me and smacked his lips, then suddenly spoke.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다시다 describes smacking or licking one’s lips, not clicking one’s tongue.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 상산왕을 황도까지 불러 손아귀에 넣을 만큼 명분을 중요시하는 황제가, 자신의 하나뿐인 동생을 죽이고자 한다면 가장 큰 가능성은 바로 독살(毒殺)이었다.
- **Current:** The Emperor cared enough about appearances to summon Prince Shangshan to the imperial capital and bring him under his control.
- **Defect:** “Appearances” weakens the need for political justification that underpins the narrator’s prediction of poisoning.
- **Replacement:** The Emperor cared enough about having a legitimate pretext to summon Prince Shangshan to the imperial capital before bringing him under his control.
- **Rationale:** 명분 is a defensible justification for an action, not merely how it looks.

# Review

Several action and mechanism errors, plus localized hierarchy, register, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “각 제대 별로 대열 갖춰! 집합! 집하압-!”
- **Current:** “Form ranks by platoon! Assemble! Assemble—!”
- **Defect:** The draft narrows 제대, a general military unit or echelon, to the specific hierarchy level “platoon” without source support.
- **Replacement:** “Form ranks by unit! Assemble! Assembllllle—!”
- **Rationale:** The source orders each unit to form ranks and elongates the final 집합 for force; it does not specify platoons.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 비록 몬스터들의 마력과 상극이라 할 수 있는 마나(Mana)의 힘에 비할 바는 아니지만, 일거에 화력을 집중시키니 비행 몬스터들도 주춤할 수밖에 없었다.
- **Current:** The power of mana, which was practically the antithesis of the monsters’ magic, was beyond comparison. But with all their firepower concentrated in one place, even the flying monsters had no choice but to falter.
- **Defect:** The draft leaves the comparison without its subject and changes simultaneous concentrated fire into fire concentrated spatially “in one place,” obscuring the combat mechanism.
- **Replacement:** Though modern weapons were no match for mana—the natural counter to monsters’ magic—concentrating all that firepower at once still forced even the flying monsters to falter.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts modern weaponry with mana, then explains that concentrating the weapons' fire all at once nevertheless checks the flying monsters.

## F05 — Major

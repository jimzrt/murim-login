# Review

The draft contains one critical alteration to the climactic action, along with several meaningful errors in monster quantity, combat mechanics, and characterization.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그들이 발사한 화기는 고작해야 하급 몬스터들에게만 통하는 수준이었다.
- **Current:** The weapons they fired were barely effective against low-level monsters.
- **Defect:** The draft says the weapons have only limited effectiveness against low-level monsters, while the source says low-level monsters are the only ones they affect at all.
- **Replacement:** The weapons they fired were effective only against low-level monsters at best.
- **Rationale:** The restrictive cue 만 preserves the mechanical distinction between weapons working on low-level monsters and having no effect on stronger ones.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 창공의 공포라 불리는 와이번을 선두로 수십 마리의 그리폰(Griffon), 가고일(Gargoyle)이 뒤따른다.

수 미터의 날개를 비스듬히 꺾으며 하강한 A급 몬스터들이 아직 미처 이륙하지 못한 전투기들을 덮쳤다.
- **Current:** Leading the way were Wyverns, known as the terrors of the sky, followed by dozens of Griffons and Gargoyles.

The A-rank monsters folded their wings at an angle and descended, swooping down upon the fighter jets that had not yet managed to take off.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the single leading Wyvern into multiple Wyverns and omits that the descending monsters' wings span several meters.
- **Replacement:** A Wyvern, known as the terror of the skies, led the way, followed by dozens of Griffons and Gargoyles.

The A-rank monsters angled their several-meter-long wings and dove upon the fighter jets that had not yet managed to take off.
- **Rationale:** The singular 와이번 is the specific creature leading the formation, while 수 미터 explicitly describes the size of the attackers' wings.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 가지각색의 마법과 마나를 한껏 머금은 화살이 그리폰을 꿰뚫었다.
- **Current:** Arrows saturated with all kinds of magic and mana pierced the Griffon.
- **Defect:** The draft collapses two distinct attacks—various spells and mana-laden arrows—into arrows containing different kinds of magic.
- **Replacement:** All manner of spells and arrows brimming with mana pierced the Griffon.
- **Rationale:** The coordination joins 가지각색의 마법 with 마나를 한껏 머금은 화살, so both spells and arrows strike the Griffon.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 비록 몬스터들의 마력과 상극이라 할 수 있는 마나(Mana)의 힘에 비할 바는 아니지만, 일거에 화력을 집중시키니 비행 몬스터들도 주춤할 수밖에 없었다.
- **Current:** It could not compare to the power of mana, which was the natural opposite of the monsters’ own mana, but the concentrated firepower forced even the flying monsters to hesitate.
- **Defect:** By rendering both 마나 and the monsters' 마력 as “mana,” the draft nonsensically states that mana is the natural opposite of itself and obscures the stated counter relationship.
- **Replacement:** Though modern weapons could not compare to mana—the natural counter to the monsters’ magical power—concentrating their firepower all at once forced even the flying monsters to hesitate.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly distinguishes Hunter mana from the monsters' magical power and explains that the former counters the latter.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그린 와이번이 쏘아 보낸 포이즌 브레스(Poison Breath)가 반경 백여 미터를 뒤덮었고, 사단장을 포함한 참모 지휘부는 강력한 산성 독을 뒤집어쓴 채 녹아내렸다.
- **Current:** Poison Breath fired by a Green Wyvern covered a radius of more than a hundred meters, and the divisional commander and the staff command were melted beneath the powerful acidic venom.
- **Defect:** “The staff command” is not natural English, and “melted beneath” blurs the explicit sequence in which the command staff are drenched in acid and then melt.
- **Replacement:** The Poison Breath fired by a Green Wyvern blanketed an area over a hundred meters in radius, and the divisional commander and his command staff were drenched in the powerful acidic poison and melted away.
- **Rationale:** 참모 지휘부 means the command staff, while 뒤집어쓴 채 explicitly depicts them covered in the acidic poison as they melt.

## F06 — Minor

# Review

Five actionable fidelity findings: a wrong unit, a misleading sword action, a reversed timing cue, a misapplied System term, and a lost reference to the Demon Lord’s former self.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘전방으로부터 삼 장. 좌측으로 세 치.’
- **Current:** *Three *jang* straight ahead. Three *chun* to the left.*
- **Defect:** The source says three chi, not three chun; the nested italics also break the thought’s Markdown.
- **Replacement:** *Three jang straight ahead. Three chi to the left.*
- **Rationale:** 치 is the unit used for the leftward offset. 촌 would be chun.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 혈검마군이 온 힘을 다해 검을 흩뿌렸다.
- **Current:** He flung his sword with all his strength.
- **Defect:** “Flung his sword” suggests he threw it, though he retains it for the horizontal slash that follows.
- **Replacement:** He whipped his sword through the air with all his strength.
- **Rationale:** The source describes wielding the sword, not releasing it.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아니, 정확히는 내리그으려 했다.

어쩌면 조금 전의 혈검마군만큼, 혹은 그 이상이라 할 수 있는 필사적인 의지를 품은 누군가가 움직이기 전까지는.
- **Current:** He didn’t get the chance, not until someone moved with a desperate resolve equal to, or perhaps even greater than, the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s moments earlier.
- **Defect:** “Not until” implies he got the chance after the other person moved. That movement instead stops his attempted slash.
- **Replacement:** But before he could, someone moved with a desperate resolve equal to—or perhaps greater than—his own moments earlier.
- **Rationale:** He only tries to bring the sword down; the other person acts first.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마치 창날에 실려 있던 필사적인 염원을 비웃기라도 하듯, 놀라울 정도로 손쉽게.
- **Current:** It did so with astonishing ease, as if mocking the desperate Will behind the blow.
- **Defect:** Capitalized “Will” invokes the established System attribute, but 염원 here means a desperate hope or wish.
- **Replacement:** It did so with astonishing ease, as if mocking the desperate hope behind the blow.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the hope carried in Taekyung’s spear strike, not the System attribute 의지.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 본래의 그였다면. 아니, 최소한 피륙으로 이루어진 인간이라면 이미 몇 번이나 죽어도 이상하지 않을 정도의 부상.
- **Current:** Under ordinary circumstances—or if he were a human made of flesh and blood—his injuries would have killed him several times over.
- **Defect:** “Under ordinary circumstances” loses the contrast with the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s former self, before his body was strengthened.
- **Replacement:** If he were his former self—or even just an ordinary flesh-and-blood human—those injuries would have killed him several times over.
- **Rationale:** 본래의 그 refers to what he himself used to be; the following sentence credits his extremely strengthened body with keeping him alive.

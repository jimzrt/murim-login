# Review

The draft is broadly faithful but contains one vessel mistranslation, several meaningful register and characterization shifts, localized terminology issues, awkward English, and an unreferenced footnote.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어차피 돌아가 봤자 쾌조선 한복판에서 멍청하게 장강 구경이나 해야 한다.
- **Current:** Even if I went back, all I could do was stupidly stare at the Yangtze from the middle of the Happy Voyage.
- **Defect:** The common vessel term 쾌조선 is incorrectly presented as the invented proper name “Happy Voyage,” obscuring that Jin would be stuck aboard a fast transport ship.
- **Replacement:** Even if I went back, I’d just be stuck aboard the express ship, staring stupidly at the Yangtze.
- **Rationale:** 쾌조선 denotes the vessel Jin is traveling on, not a ship named “Happy Voyage.”

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “아, 뇌파 감지? 이거 기계 아니에요.”
- **Current:** “Oh, the brain-wave detector? This isn’t just a machine.”
- **Defect:** Adding “just” removes the deliberate contradiction with Jin’s next line, weakening the deadpan reveal that it technically is a machine but runs on a Magic Gem.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, the brain-wave detector? This isn’t a machine.”
- **Rationale:** The source flatly says 기계 아니에요 before immediately qualifying that statement with 기계는 기계인데.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 차마 가족들에게까지 숨길 수가 없어서 비밀리에 최 팀장을 통해 내가 털끝 하나 다치지 않았고 이미 깨어났음을 알려 주긴 했는데, 잠시 후 있을 첫 면회가 걱정이다.
- **Current:** I hadn’t been able to hide everything from my family, so I had secretly had Team Leader Choi tell them that I hadn’t been hurt in the slightest and had already awakened. Still, I was worried about the first visit that would take place soon.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Jin’s emotional inability to bring himself to conceal the truth from his family into a vague inability to hide everything, losing the pragmatic cue and implying that concealment simply failed.
- **Replacement:** I couldn’t bring myself to hide it even from my family, so I’d secretly had Team Leader Choi tell them that I hadn’t been hurt in the slightest and was already awake. Still, I was worried about their first visit, which was coming up soon.
- **Rationale:** 차마 … 숨길 수가 없어서 expresses that Jin could not bring himself to do it, not that he proved unable to keep all the facts hidden.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그리고…… 무엇보다 대격변의 영웅이자 누구보다 존경했던 대선배님. 이정룡 헌터님께서 돌아가신 것에 대해 깊은 슬픔을 느낍니다. 정말 좋은 분이셨는데.”
- **Current:** “And… more than anyone, Hunter Lee Jungryong was a hero of the Great Cataclysm and the Senior I respected most. I feel profound sorrow over his death. He was truly a good man.”
- **Defect:** The opening modifier is attached awkwardly to Lee being a hero, and the rendering weakens 대선배님, Jin’s ostentatiously respectful description of Lee as an eminent Senior.
- **Replacement:** “And… above all, I feel profound sorrow over the death of Hunter Lee Jungryong, a hero of the Great Cataclysm and the great Senior I respected more than anyone. He was truly a good man.”
- **Rationale:** 누구보다 modifies how deeply Jin supposedly respected Lee, while 대선배님 heightens Lee’s seniority and Jin’s performative deference.

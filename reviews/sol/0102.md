# Review

The draft contains several factual and register errors, including an altered causal explanation, an overstated relationship, a changed quantity, and dialogue that incorrectly places the capsule upstairs.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 게이트에서의 포메이션과 위기 상황이 닥쳤을 때 취해야 할 행동, 몬스터들의 약점 등을 달달 꿰고 있는 내가 법 관련 사항에서는 문외한인 것도 같은 맥락이었다.
- **Current:** Being clueless about legal matters was simply the same principle applied in reverse.
- **Defect:** The source says his legal ignorance follows from the same principle that everyone has a different specialty; it does not describe that principle as operating in reverse.
- **Replacement:** My being clueless about legal matters was simply another example of everyone having their own specialty.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the stated causal connection between specialized expertise and ignorance outside one's field.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “법무사님도요.”

- **Replacement:** “You too.”
- **Rationale:** The honorific addressee is already clear from context, while the source's function is simply to return the courtesy.

# Review

The draft has two scene-fact errors and several localized losses of meaning or register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 황제를 배반하고 창공이라는 거죽을 뒤집어쓴 채 살아왔던 동천마군(東天魔君)의 그늘로 숨어든 역적들.
- **Current:** They were also traitors who’d betrayed the Emperor and hidden in the shadow of the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, living under the borrowed name of Cang Gong.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Imperial Guards the ones living under Cang Gong’s name. The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord wore that identity.
- **Replacement:** They were also traitors who’d betrayed the Emperor and taken refuge in the shadow of the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, who had lived behind the guise of Cang Gong.
- **Rationale:** 창공이라는 거죽을 뒤집어쓴 채 살아왔던 modifies 동천마군, not 역적들.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 터져 나가며 핏방울을 흩뿌린다.

내 것이 아닌, 또 다른 누군가의 핏물과 함께 뒤섞이며.
- **Current:** It burst open, scattering drops of blood.

Mixed with someone else’s blood—not mine.
- **Defect:** “Not mine” appears to identify the blood from the narrator’s split skin as someone else’s. The source says his blood mixes with blood that is not his.
- **Replacement:** It burst open, scattering drops of my blood.

They mingled with someone else’s.
- **Rationale:** The preceding skin is the narrator’s; 내 것이 아닌 describes the other blood with which his blood mixes.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “지금이라도 황실의 편에 선다면, 역적이 되는 것만은 어떻게 해서는 막아 주지.”
- **Current:** “If you side with the imperial court now, I’ll do everything I can to keep you from being branded traitors.”
- **Defect:** “Do everything I can” weakens his promise, while “branded” shifts it from preventing their becoming traitors to managing their reputation.
- **Replacement:** “If you side with the imperial court now, I’ll make sure you don’t end up as traitors, one way or another.”
- **Rationale:** 어떻게 해서든 막아 주지 is a firm promise to prevent 역적이 되는 것.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘포섭. 강압. 혹은 거부할 수 없는 명령.’
- **Current:** *Bribery. Coercion. Or orders they couldn’t refuse.*
- **Defect:** Bribery specifies payment that the source does not establish; 포섭 means winning people over or recruiting them.
- **Replacement:** *Winning them over. Coercion. Or orders they couldn’t refuse.*
- **Rationale:** The narrator lists possible ways Dark Heaven drew the guards into its control without identifying money as one of them.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “내 생각에는 이 정도면 충분한 것 같은데, 혹시 부연 설명이 더 필요하면 직접 씨부려 봐.”
- **Current:** “That seems like enough to me. But if you need me to spell it out, go ahead and ask.”
- **Defect:** “Go ahead and ask” loses the contemptuous force of 씨부려 봐.
- **Replacement:** “That seems like enough to me. If you need more explanation, spit it out.”
- **Rationale:** The narrator brusquely tells the man to speak up; he does not offer a courteous invitation to ask.

# Review

Several passages alter who would have noticed an occupation, the scale of bloodshed, and the setup of a character exchange.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그런데 작은 마을도 아닌 도시가 점령당한 마당에 그 사실을 모를 수 있었을까?
- **Current:** Could we really have failed to notice a city—not just a small village—being occupied?
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung’s party the ones who might have missed the occupation. He is questioning whether the Murim Alliance’s surveillance network could have missed it.
- **Replacement:** Could the Murim Alliance really have failed to notice a city—not just a small village—being occupied?
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence establishes the Murim Alliance’s surveillance network as the basis for his inference.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하물며 한 도시를 점령하는 과정에 흘렀을 무수한 피를 생각한다면 더더욱.
- **Current:** Even less so when I considered the countless lives that must have been lost in the process of occupying an entire city.
- **Defect:** The source refers to a great deal of blood spilled, not a confirmed number of lives lost.
- **Replacement:** Even less so when I considered how much blood must have been spilled in taking an entire city.
- **Rationale:** The amount of blood matters to Taekyung’s argument that its smell could not have vanished so quickly.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “애가 원래 좀 아픕니다. 병이 있어요.”
- **Current:** “He’s not quite right in the head. He has a condition.”
- **Defect:** The draft specifies a mental impairment that Taekyung does not claim.
- **Replacement:** “He’s always been a bit sick. He has a condition.”
- **Rationale:** 좀 아픕니다 and 병이 있어요 leave the supposed illness unspecified.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “많이도 데려왔군. 개떼처럼 드글거리는 꼴이 아주 볼만해.”
- **Current:** “You brought quite a crowd. They’re packed in here like a dog pack. Quite a sight.”
- **Defect:** “Like a dog pack” is an unnatural English rendering of Jeok’s contemptuous image.
- **Replacement:** “You brought quite a crowd. Swarming around like a pack of dogs. Quite a sight.”
- **Rationale:** 개떼처럼 드글거리는 conveys both the dog comparison and the sense of a crowd milling everywhere.

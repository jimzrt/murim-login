# Review

Six actionable fidelity defects, including altered subjects, attack targets, combat mechanics, hierarchy, and causality.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무명의 말에 어이가 없어진 건 나뿐만이 아니었다.
- **Current:** Unnamed wasn’t the only one left speechless by the question.
- **Defect:** The reaction’s subject is reversed. The source says Taekyung was not the only person dumbfounded by Unnamed’s words; it does not say Unnamed was also left speechless.
- **Replacement:** I wasn’t the only one dumbfounded by Unnamed’s words.
- **Rationale:** The construction “나뿐만이 아니었다” explicitly centers Taekyung and indicates that Jeok Cheongang shared his incredulous reaction.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “……일단 맞자.”
- **Current:** “…”

*Let’s just get hit.*
- **Defect:** Jeok Cheongang’s spoken threat is converted into an italicized thought and reverses who is about to receive the beating.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이 자리에 모인 무림인들의 입장에서는 본 적도 없고, 볼 수도 없었던 일권(一拳).
- **Current:** From the perspective of the Murim practitioners gathered there, it was a single punch they had never seen before—and would never see again.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an imperceptibly fast punch into one that the spectators merely will not see again in the future.
- **Replacement:** The punch was something none of the martial artists gathered there had seen—or had even been capable of seeing.
- **Rationale:** “볼 수도 없었던” means they could not see the punch, describing its speed and their inability to perceive it, not its future uniqueness.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 절친한 벗이었던 법왕 굉도가 남긴 유일한 제자는 그에게 있어 아픈 손가락이었다.
- **Current:** Hong Dao, his close friend, had left behind only one Disciple. To the Thunderbolt Saber King, Unnamed was a particularly painful spot.
- **Defect:** The established title “Dharma King” is omitted, and the literal rendering “a particularly painful spot” obscures the idiom’s meaning that Unnamed inspires special concern and protectiveness.
- **Replacement:** Unnamed, the only Disciple left behind by his close friend the Dharma King Hong Dao, was someone the Thunderbolt Saber King couldn’t help but feel protective of.
- **Rationale:** “아픈 손가락” refers to someone whose circumstances make them an object of particular concern or sympathy, while “법왕” explicitly identifies Hong Dao as the Dharma King.

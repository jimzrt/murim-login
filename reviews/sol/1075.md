# Review

Several lines change who knew, spoke, or made a decision; two also alter how the secrecy warning works.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그렇게 말씀하실 줄 아시고, 상대가 설령 은인이라 할지라도 함구하랬어요.
- **Current:** “I knew you’d say that, so he told me not to say anything, even if it was you, Benefactor.”
- **Defect:** Cheongpung says the Slaughter Saint anticipated Taekyung’s response, not that Cheongpung anticipated it.
- **Replacement:** “He knew you’d say that, so he told me not to say anything, even to you, Benefactor.”
- **Rationale:** 아시고 refers to the elder who gave the instruction.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 우와, 신기하다. 혹시 은인께서 전음으로 말하면 괜찮다고 꼬드겨도 함구하랬어요.
- **Current:** “Wow, that’s amazing. I wonder if he also told me to keep quiet if you tried to talk me into it with Sound Transmission.”
- **Defect:** Cheongpung is reporting an instruction, not wondering whether one exists. Taekyung proposes using Sound Transmission; the warning anticipates that proposal rather than persuasion conducted through it.
- **Replacement:** “Wow, that’s amazing. He even told me to keep quiet if you tried to convince me it would be fine to tell you by Sound Transmission.”
- **Rationale:** 함구하랬어요 states what the Slaughter Saint told him; 전음으로 말하면 괜찮다고 describes Taekyung’s proposed exception.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뭐든 말하면 죽여 버린대요.
- **Current:** He said he’d kill anyone who found out.
- **Defect:** The threat targets Cheongpung if he talks, not people who learn the secret.
- **Replacement:** He said he’d kill me if I said anything.
- **Rationale:** 말하면 makes speaking the condition; Cheongpung is the person being warned.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ……그냥 안 들을게.
- **Current:** “……I’ll just pretend I didn’t hear anything.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung declines to hear the secret; he does not pretend to forget something already disclosed.
- **Replacement:** “……Then I just won’t ask.”
- **Rationale:** 안 들을게 refers to not hearing the account Cheongpung has withheld.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그래서 더욱 자세히 파고들기 위해 곤륜파 내부로 잠입하려고 하셨죠.
- **Current:** So he wanted to look into things more closely by infiltrating the Kunlun Sect.
- **Defect:** “Wanted to” reduces an intended infiltration to a desire, weakening the attempted-versus-completed distinction made in the next line.
- **Replacement:** So he was going to infiltrate the Kunlun Sect to investigate further.
- **Rationale:** 잠입하려고 하셨죠 expresses what he was about to do; the following sentence contrasts that with having done it.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 작은 할아버지께서도 그걸 느끼셨는지, 때마침 하산하는 괴물들의 틈에 섞여 되돌아오기로 하셨어요.
- **Current:** Little Grandpa must have felt it, too, because we decided to return with the monsters who were coming down the mountain.
- **Defect:** The Slaughter Saint makes the decision, not the pair jointly; they blend into the descending monsters rather than merely travel with them.
- **Replacement:** Little Grandpa must have felt it too, because he decided we should slip in among the monsters that happened to be coming down the mountain and head back.
- **Rationale:** 되돌아오기로 하셨어요 assigns the decision to the elder, while 틈에 섞여 describes hiding among the monsters.

# Review

Several localized fidelity issues alter actions, certainty, hierarchy, characterization, and a central joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “사실 나설 필요도 없더구먼요. 풍양이 죽고 적풍단이 아작 났다는 걸 알았는지 근처에 얼씬도 안 합디다.”
- **Current:** “As a matter of fact, we didn’t even need to go out. Once they learned that Pung Yang was dead and the Red Wind Band had been wrecked, they didn’t come anywhere near the area.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes not needing to intervene into not needing to leave, and turns the speaker’s inference about what the bandits may have heard into a confirmed sequence of events.
- **Replacement:** “Truth is, we didn’t even need to step in. They must’ve heard Pung Yang was dead and the Red Wind Band had been wrecked, because they didn’t come anywhere near the area.”
- **Rationale:** 나설 필요도 없다 means there was no need to step in or take action, while 알았는지 marks conjecture rather than confirmed knowledge.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “사실과는 좀 다르긴 한데…… 적당히 양념 쳐. 남의 집 싸움에 우리가 피 흘릴 수는 없잖아?”
- **Current:** “It’s not exactly true, but… Add enough seasoning. We can’t bleed for somebody else’s family feud, can we?”
- **Defect:** “Family feud” invents an intrafamilial conflict absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** “It’s not exactly true, but… Add enough seasoning. We can’t bleed for someone else’s fight, can we?”
- **Rationale:** 남의 집 싸움 means someone else’s fight or conflict; it does not characterize that conflict as a feud among relatives.

## F03 — Major

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “그리고 무슨 제의를 하더라도 큰형님이 있는 한 어림없다. 널 정략혼으로 엮으실 분은 아니니까.”
- **Current:** “And no matter what she offers, it’s impossible as long as our eldest brother is around. He isn’t the sort of person to force you into a political marriage.”

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “급식, 고딩, 철컹, 철컹…….”
- **Current:** [^1]: “School lunch” can also be slang for a school-age kid, especially a teenager; the deliberately odd pairing with “high schooler” is part of the joke.
- **Defect:** The note misidentifies the joke as the pairing of two age terms and omits the decisive implication of “clank, clank”: handcuffs or prison bars prompted by sexual interest in a minor.
- **Replacement:** [^1]: “School lunch” is Korean slang for a school-age kid, while “clank, clank” evokes handcuffs or prison bars—the joke is that sexual interest in a high schooler could land someone in jail.
- **Rationale:** The sequence 급식, 고딩, 철컹철컹 invokes the Korean joke that pursuing a school-age minor leads to arrest or imprisonment; without that connection, the punchline remains unexplained.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 중상을 입었다면 산더미처럼 쌓인 동료들의 곁으로 돌아갈 것이고, 경상을 입었다면 생명을 조금 더 연장시킬 수 있을 것이다.
- **Current:** Those with Severe Injuries would be sent back to the mountain-high pile of their comrades, while those with minor injuries might have their lives prolonged a little longer.
- **Defect:** Capitalizing “Severe Injuries” incorrectly presents an ordinary description of the captives’ wounds as the named System condition, and “would be sent back” dulls the source’s euphemistic statement that they will rejoin their dead comrades.
- **Replacement:** Those with serious injuries would rejoin the mountain-high pile of their comrades, while those with minor injuries might live a little longer.
- **Rationale:** 중상 and 경상 are parallel ordinary injury descriptions in this passage, not a System Status display; 돌아가다 sustains the grim euphemism for killing the severely wounded.

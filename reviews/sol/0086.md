# Review

The draft contains a critical subject reversal in the bank manager’s explanation and three critical tenfold currency errors, along with several localized register, characterization, and fidelity issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아이고, 고생하셨습니다.”
- **Current:** “Good work.”
- **Defect:** The greeting is rendered as praise from a superior rather than courteous thanks from the official to the returning Hunters.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, thank you for your hard work.”
- **Rationale:** 고생하셨습니다 expresses respectful appreciation for someone’s effort or hardship; it does not mean the speaker is evaluating their work.

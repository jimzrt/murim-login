# Review

Two critical subject and marital-status errors, plus several localized terminology, humor, and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 송 양도 알지?
- **Current:** You know Miss Song too, right?
- **Defect:** This reverses the subject and object. Im Kkeokjeong is asking Miss Song whether she has heard about Taekyung, not asking Taekyung whether he knows Miss Song.
- **Replacement:** You’ve heard about him too, right, Miss Song?
- **Rationale:** 송 양 is the person being addressed, while the contextually omitted object is Jin Taekyung.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 임 헌터님, 미혼 맞죠?
- **Current:** “Hunter Im is married, right?”
- **Defect:** The question reverses Im Kkeokjeong’s marital status: 미혼 means unmarried, which sets up the surprised response that he is actually married with two children.
- **Replacement:** “Hunter Im is unmarried, right?”
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly asks whether he is unmarried; the following 기혼입니다 provides the correction.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “송이 씨요?”
- **Current:** “Miss Song?”
- **Defect:** The translation makes Taekyung appear merely to repeat the surname-based address already used throughout the scene, obscuring that he has just realized her given name is Song and prompting her full-name introduction.
- **Replacement:** “Song is your first name?”
- **Rationale:** 송이 씨 addresses her by her given name, Songi; because the established English name renders both parts as Song, the distinction must be made explicit for the reveal and reaction to remain intelligible.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 자칭 연애 고수라는 진호 형은 마음에 드는 여성에게는 중저음을 사용하라고 누누이 강조해 왔다.
- **Current:** Jinho hyung, who called himself a dating expert, had always stressed that I should use a deep, resonant voice with a woman I liked.
- **Defect:** “Dating expert” does not follow the binding rendering of 고수 as “master.”
- **Replacement:** Jinho hyung, a self-proclaimed master of romance, had always stressed that I should use a deep, resonant voice with a woman I liked.
- **Rationale:** The replacement uses the required terminology while preserving the comic boast and natural English.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 고기를 씹으면 육즙이 아주 그냥 입 안에서 주르륵.
- **Current:** And when you bite into it, the juices run all over your mouth.
- **Defect:** “All over your mouth” can suggest juices running over the outside of the face, whereas 입 안에서 explicitly locates them inside the mouth.
- **Replacement:** And when you bite into it, the juices flood your mouth.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the exaggerated gush of juices without altering its physical location.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘아, 송이 씨. 당신은 도덕책.’
- **Current:** *Oh, Miss Song. You're a paragon of virtue.*
- **Defect:** The paraphrase removes the deliberate “ethics textbook” wordplay and turns the absurd punchline into ordinary praise.
- **Replacement:** *Oh, Miss Song. You’re an ethics textbook.*[^2]

[^2]: In Korean, “ethics textbook” is a pun on a phrase meaning “what on earth are you?”
- **Rationale:** 도덕책 literally means “ethics textbook” and is used here as a pun on 도대체; a brief footnote preserves the joke without inventing an unrelated English one.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어쩜 좋아. 생활력 강한 것도 딱 내 스타일이야.
- **Current:** *This is perfect. Someone with strong survival skills is exactly my type.*
- **Defect:** “Survival skills” suggests wilderness or emergency competence rather than the practical resourcefulness and ability to support oneself conveyed by 생활력.
- **Replacement:** *What am I going to do? Even her resourcefulness is exactly my type.*
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores Taekyung’s giddy rhetorical reaction and the ordinary-life competence described by 생활력.

## F08 — Minor

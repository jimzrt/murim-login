# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it conflates two imperial forces, lowers the vanguard’s stated martial level, compresses key distances, and obscures how So Gyo’s bow forms its shot.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 금의위와 화룡각 대원들도
- **Current:** The Imperial Guards and Fire Dragon Pavilion members fighting the dead who kept rising no matter how many times they were killed.
- **Defect:** Identifies the defenders as the Imperial Guards, a different force in this chapter.
- **Replacement:** The Embroidered Uniform Guard and Fire Dragon Pavilion members fighting the dead who kept rising no matter how many times they were killed.
- **Rationale:** 금의위 is the Embroidered Uniform Guard; 금위군 denotes the Imperial Guards.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 오늘 이 자리에서 재가 되어 스러진 금의위들 역시 죽음을 각오했었으니.
- **Current:** The Imperial Guards who had fallen to ash here today had also been prepared to die.
- **Defect:** Attributes the sacrifice to the wrong imperial force.
- **Replacement:** The Embroidered Uniform Guard who had fallen to ash here today had also been prepared to die.
- **Rationale:** The source again names 금의위, not 금위군.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그들 한 사람 한 사람이 초일류에서 절정의 경지를 오가던 실력자들.
- **Current:** ranging from First Rate to Peak
- **Defect:** Lowers the bottom of the fighters’ stated range by one established tier.
- **Replacement:** ranging from Supreme First Rate to Peak
- **Rationale:** 초일류 is Supreme First Rate, distinct from 일류, First Rate.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “북을, 전고(戰鼓)를 울리게.”
- **Current:** “Have the drums sounded. The war drums.”
- **Defect:** Makes the Emperor’s request plural despite the single drum Baek Yeon immediately strikes.
- **Replacement:** “Sound the drum. The war drum.”
- **Rationale:** 북 and 전고 refer to the drum identified in the following action as the great drum that opened the banquet.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 두 사람 사이에 놓인 백여 장의 거리는 지금 이 순간 아무런 의미도 없었다.
- **Current:** The hundred or so yards between them meant nothing now.
- **Defect:** Compresses the distance between the two characters to roughly a third of its stated length.
- **Replacement:** The more than three hundred yards between them meant nothing now.
- **Rationale:** A 장 is approximately 3.3 meters; 백여 장 is more than three hundred yards, not about one hundred.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 종횡으로 수백여 장에 이르는 대연회장은 핏물로 잠겨 있었다.
- **Current:** The grand banquet hall, stretching hundreds of yards in every direction, was submerged in blood.
- **Defect:** Again treats 장 as yards, understating the battlefield’s dimensions.
- **Replacement:** The grand banquet hall, stretching nearly a thousand yards in both length and breadth, was submerged in blood.
- **Rationale:** The source measures both dimensions in hundreds of 장, each substantially longer than a yard.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 허공에서 그려지듯 나타난, 섬광에 걸린 휘황한 빛줄기.
- **Current:** A brilliant beam of light, caught in the flash, appeared as though drawn in the air.
- **Defect:** Obscures that the new shaft of light is set against the luminous bowstring So Gyo has just drawn.
- **Replacement:** A brilliant shaft of light appeared as though traced in midair, set against the shining bowstring.
- **Rationale:** The 빛줄기 is 걸린 on the 섬광 joining the bow’s ends; this is part of the bow’s firing mechanism, not simply a beam caught inside a flash.

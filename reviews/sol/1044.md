# Review

Two scene facts change in the draft: the Fire Gate pair’s relationship and what the Zhongnan elders intended by their revenge. The draft also adds a refusal that the source does not state.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 지금 이 순간에도 그들은, 열화문의 두 사제(師弟)에게 품었던 원한을 완전히 버리지 못하고 있었으니까.
- **Current:** Even now, they hadn’t been able to let go of the grudge they held against the two Senior Brothers of the Fire Gate Clan.
- **Defect:** Identifies Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung as two Senior Brothers rather than Master and Disciple.
- **Replacement:** Even now, they hadn’t been able to let go of their grudge against the Fire Gate Clan’s Master and Disciple.
- **Rationale:** The parenthetical 師弟 specifies a master–disciple pair.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 설령 그 방식이 죽음이 아니더라도, 그들이 겪은 것과 같은 수모를 열화문에 안겨 주어야 했다.
- **Current:** Even if they didn’t kill the Fire Gate Clan, they had to inflict the same humiliation on them that they’d suffered.
- **Defect:** Turns a revenge method short of death into a decision not to kill the Fire Gate Clan.
- **Replacement:** Even if their revenge stopped short of killing, they had to make the Fire Gate Clan suffer the same humiliation they had.
- **Rationale:** 그 방식이 죽음이 아니더라도 qualifies the form their revenge might take; it does not make the clan as a whole the object of a proposed killing.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 만남을 거부하는 뜻을 밝혔음에도 끝끝내 대면한 불청객은, 도무지 믿을 수 없을 정도로 후한 호의를 의심하는 두 사형제에게 거부하지 못할 한마디를 던졌다.
- **Current:** Though they’d made clear they refused to meet, the uninvited guest had insisted on facing them. The two Senior Brothers, unable to trust such unbelievable generosity, had refused—until the visitor offered them a single, irresistible line.
- **Defect:** Adds a refusal of the offer before the visitor speaks; the only stated refusal was to meet him.
- **Replacement:** Though they’d made clear they did not want to meet, the uninvited guest had secured an audience anyway. Seeing the two martial brothers distrust his almost unbelievable generosity, he said something they could not refuse.
- **Rationale:** The source says they doubted the generosity, then heard the visitor’s persuasive words. It does not say they rejected his offer at that point.

## F04 — Major

# Review

The draft changes who the western enemies are, narrows an unconfirmed killing to witnesses, and loses several physical and social details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 황명에 따라 무림맹과 손잡고 서쪽의 흉적(凶賊)들과 맞서는.
- **Current:** By imperial decree, they’ve joined hands with the Murim Alliance to fight the vicious bandits in the west.
- **Defect:** Identifies the western enemies as bandits, confusing them with the Green Forest Alliance.
- **Replacement:** By imperial decree, they’ve joined forces with the Murim Alliance against the vicious enemies in the west.
- **Rationale:** 흉적 does not identify the western enemies as bandits.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 모종의 이유로 살인멸구(殺人滅口)를 하고 산사태를 빙자해 은폐하려 한 거야.
- **Current:** They killed the witnesses for some reason, then covered it up by pretending there’d been a landslide.
- **Defect:** Specifies that the people killed were witnesses, though the speaker does not establish who was killed.
- **Replacement:** For some reason, they killed to silence whoever knew, then tried to cover it up by passing it off as a landslide.
- **Rationale:** 살인멸구 conveys killing to prevent disclosure; the victims’ identity remains unconfirmed.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 모용세가(慕容世家)도 흉적과 손을 잡았는데, 도적놈들이라고 오죽할까.
- **Current:** joined hands with the vicious bandits. What do you expect from a bunch of thieves?
- **Defect:** Calls the Murong Family’s allies bandits, erasing the contrast between a respected orthodox family and the thieves being discussed.
- **Replacement:** joined forces with those vicious enemies. What do you expect from a bunch of thieves?
- **Rationale:** 흉적 refers to the enemy force; 도적놈들 refers to the bandits whose possible betrayal the villagers are debating.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 토사에 휩쓸려 온 나무들의 잘린 단면을 슥 보아하니, 이게 확 꺾이고 부러진 게 아니라 어째 누가 도끼질이라도 깔짝거린 것 같다 이 말일세.
- **Current:** From what they saw when they took a look at the snapped ends of the trees swept down in the earth and sand, it wasn’t like they’d been violently bent and broken. It looked more like somebody had taken a few halfhearted swings with an axe.
- **Defect:** Describes the tree ends as snapped before explaining that they were not broken; this obscures the physical evidence for deliberate cutting.
- **Replacement:** The herb gatherers took one look at the cut ends of the trees swept down with the earth and sand. They hadn’t been bent and snapped clean off—it looked as though someone had given them a few halfhearted chops with an axe.
- **Rationale:** 잘린 단면 are cut surfaces, contrasted explicitly with trees bent and broken by a landslide.

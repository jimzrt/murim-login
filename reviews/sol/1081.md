# Review

The draft reverses a recognition cue, omits Jin’s established title, misidentifies who the trio is watching, and renders the execution method incorrectly.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “곤륜파 일대제자 학의, 열화신룡 진태경 대협을 뵙습니다.”
- **Current:** “I am Hak Eui, a First-Generation Disciple of the Kunlun Sect. It’s an honor to meet you, Great Hero Jin Taekyung.”
- **Defect:** Hak Eui omits Jin’s sobriquet from his formal introduction.
- **Replacement:** “I am Hak Eui, a First-Generation Disciple of the Kunlun Sect. It’s an honor to meet you, Great Hero Jin Taekyung, the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.”
- **Rationale:** 열화신룡 is part of Hak Eui’s address to Jin.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “처음이군요. 이렇게 뵙는 건.”

상대의 신분을 모른다면 할 수 없는 대답이었지만
- **Current:** “This is our first time meeting, isn’t it?”

If I hadn’t known who he was, that would have been a reasonable answer.
- **Defect:** The narration reverses the condition: Jin could only say this because he knew Hak Eui’s identity. The dialogue also drops “like this,” making the meeting sound unqualifiedly their first.
- **Replacement:** “This is the first time we’ve met like this.”

I couldn’t have said that without knowing who he was.
- **Rationale:** 모른다면 할 수 없는 대답 explicitly says the reply would be impossible without recognizing him.

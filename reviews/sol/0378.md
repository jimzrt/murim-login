# Review

One critical subject reversal changes Taekyung’s request for permission into a request that the captain open the door. The draft also implies the Lich physically arrived, genericizes a specifically repeated anti-Korean slur, and loses two smaller characterization and register details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “잠깐만 문 열어도 돼요?”

“예?”

“비행기 문 좀 열어도 되냐고요.”
- **Current:** “Can you open the door for a moment?”

“What?”

“I’m asking if you can open the plane door.”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Taekyung asks whether he may open the door, not whether the captain can open it.
- **Replacement:** “Can I open the door for a moment?”

“What?”

“I’m asking if it’s okay for me to open the plane door.”
- **Rationale:** This request establishes that Taekyung already intends to open the door himself, directly setting up his immediate decision to cut it open.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 현대 시간으로 일주일 전, 유례없는 몬스터 웨이브와 함께 나타난 최상위 언데드 몬스터가 어느새 여기까지 마수를 뻗친 것이 틀림없었다.
- **Current:** A week ago, by modern-world time, the highest-tier undead monster that had appeared alongside an unprecedented monster wave had clearly reached this place with its claws.
- **Defect:** The draft says the Lich itself reached this location, whereas 마수를 뻗치다 means that it extended its sinister reach or influence here and does not establish its physical presence.
- **Replacement:** A week ago, by modern-world time, the highest-tier undead monster had appeared alongside an unprecedented monster wave. It had undoubtedly extended its sinister reach all the way here.
- **Rationale:** The source attributes the attack to the Lich’s expanding reach without revealing that the Lich is physically present.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “하지 말라고! 이 빵즈(棒子) 놈들아! 우릴 다 죽일 셈이냐!”

“……뭐, 빵즈?”
- **Current:** “Don’t! You Korean bastards! Are you trying to kill us all?!”

“…What did you just call us?”
- **Defect:** The specific Chinese anti-Korean slur is replaced with a generic insult, and Taekyung’s pointed repetition of that exact slur is omitted.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t! You *bangzi* bastards! Are you trying to kill us all?!”

“…What, *bangzi*?”[^1]

[^1]: *Bangzi* is a derogatory Chinese term for Koreans.
- **Rationale:** The captain’s culturally specific slur and Taekyung’s immediate repetition of it drive the ensuing exchange; a brief footnote preserves that force for English readers.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** - 잘했다! 조금 덜 간악한 인간이여!
- **Current:** —Well done! You are slightly less wicked now, human!
- **Defect:** Adding “now” implies that slapping Taekyung has just made Team Leader Choi less wicked. The source simply distinguishes Choi as the human who is comparatively less wicked.
- **Replacement:** —Well done, you slightly less wicked human!
- **Rationale:** 조금 덜 간악한 describes Choi relative to the “wicked human” Taekyung rather than a change in Choi’s moral state.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “그, 그러고 보니 저 헌터님에 관해서 들은 적이 있어. 샤오 양 주석 동지께서도 특별히 요청할 만큼 대단한 실력자라던데.”
- **Current:** “N-Now that you mention it, I’ve heard about that Hunter. Chairman Xiao Yang himself made a special request for him, so he must be incredibly skilled.”
- **Defect:** The political form of address 동지, “Comrade,” is omitted, flattening the speaker’s distinctly Chinese official register.
- **Replacement:** “N-Now that you mention it, I’ve heard about that Hunter. They say he’s so capable that even Comrade Chairman Xiao Yang made a special request for him.”
- **Rationale:** 주석 동지 explicitly combines the title “Chairman” with the political address “Comrade,” and 도 marks the chairman’s involvement as emphatic.

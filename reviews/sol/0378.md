# Review

The draft changes who proposes opening the door, overstates all three approximate wyvern counts, omits political and dragon-lineage register, and contains several terminology errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “잠깐만 문 열어도 돼요?”

“예?”

“비행기 문 좀 열어도 되냐고요.”
- **Current:** “Can we open the door for a second?”

“What?”

“I said, can we open the plane door?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes Taekyung’s request to open the door himself into a collective action.
- **Replacement:** “Can I open the door for a second?”

“What?”

“I said, can I open the plane door?”
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is resolved by the surrounding exchange and Taekyung’s later statement that he is opening it.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그것도 무려 십여 마리나!
- **Current:** And there were more than a dozen of them!
- **Defect:** The draft changes an approximate count of around ten into a definite count exceeding twelve.
- **Replacement:** And there were around a dozen of them!
- **Rationale:** 십여 마리 means roughly ten or a dozen, not more than twelve.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “그, 그러고 보니 저 헌터님에 관해서 들은 적이 있어. 샤오 양 주석 동지께서도 특별히 요청할 만큼 대단한 실력자라던데.”
- **Current:** “Come to think of it, I’ve heard about that Hunter. They say he’s such an incredible fighter that Chairman Xiao Yang personally requested him.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits 동지, erasing the speaker’s conspicuously Communist political register and form of address.
- **Replacement:** “Come to think of it, I’ve heard about that Hunter. They say he’s so formidable that even Comrade Chairman Xiao Yang made a special request for him.”
- **Rationale:** 주석 동지 explicitly combines the title Chairman with Comrade; even also preserves the force of 도.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어떻게 인간 따위가 감히 위대한 드래곤의 후예를…….
- **Current:** How could a mere human dare to—
- **Defect:** The draft omits the wyverns’ self-identification as descendants of the great dragons, losing the basis of their pride and disbelief.
- **Replacement:** How could a mere human dare lay a hand on a descendant of the great dragons…
- **Rationale:** 위대한 드래곤의 후예 is an explicit characterization and hierarchy cue, not merely an unfinished generic objection.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 감히 자랑스러운 대한의 김치맨 앞에서 한국인을 비하하는 말을 하다니.
- **Current:** The nerve of him, insulting Koreans right in front of a proud son of Daehan—this kimchi man!
- **Defect:** Daehan is left as an unestablished romanization, while “son of” is added and the sentence is awkwardly fragmented.
- **Replacement:** The nerve of him, insulting Koreans right in front of a proud Korean kimchi man like me!
- **Rationale:** 대한 refers to Korea here; the replacement preserves Taekyung’s mock-patriotic self-description without inventing a filial metaphor.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 서천마군이 내뿜던 기파에 비교하면, 이 정도는 살랑거리는 봄바람이나 다름없었으니까.
- **Current:** Compared to the aura emitted by the Western Heaven Demon Lord, this was no more than a gentle spring breeze.

- **Replacement:** Compared to the waves of qi the Western Heaven Demon Lord had emitted, this was no more than a gentle spring breeze.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the physical qi waves he gave off, which are the direct mechanical comparison for the wind pressure.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 창날에 서린 화염의 강기가 공기를 태우고 바람을 가른다.
- **Current:** The flame-infused sword qi surrounding the spearhead burned through the air and cleaved the wind.
- **Defect:** The draft calls the energy on a spearhead “sword qi,” although this occurrence is simply 강기 and does not specify a sword.
- **Replacement:** Fiery concentrated qi sheathed the spearhead, burning the air and cleaving the wind.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the concentrated Murim energy and its coating action without introducing the wrong weapon category.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “하지 말라고! 이 빵즈(棒子) 놈들아! 우릴 다 죽일 셈이냐!”
- **Current:** “Don’t! You bingzi bastards![^1] Are you trying to kill us all?”
- **Defect:** The Chinese slur 棒子 is incorrectly romanized as bingzi.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t! You *bangzi* bastards![^1] Are you trying to kill us all?”
- **Rationale:** The standard Mandarin romanization of 棒子 is bangzi; Korean 빵즈 reflects that pronunciation.

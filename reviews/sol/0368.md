# Review

One critical subject reversal and several localized fidelity and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “다행이구나. 늦지 않아서.”
- **Current:** “I’m glad. You weren’t too late.”
- **Defect:** The omitted subject of 늦지 않아서 is the speaker, Mungyeong, not his addressee. The draft reverses who arrived in time.
- **Replacement:** “What a relief. I wasn’t too late.”
- **Rationale:** Mungyeong is saying that he himself is relieved to have arrived before it was too late; Dong Feng’s ensuing reply that he is already too late depends on that meaning.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 눈앞에서 펼쳐진 아득한 무학의 경지에 전율이 흐르는 가운데, 늙은 의원은 작게 고개를 가로저었다.
- **Current:** A shiver ran through everyone at the distant martial realm unfolding before their eyes. The old physician quietly shook his head.
- **Defect:** “Distant martial realm” is an unnatural literal rendering of 아득한 무학의 경지 and suggests physical distance rather than an unfathomably advanced level of martial arts.
- **Replacement:** Everyone shuddered at the unfathomable heights of martial arts displayed before their eyes. The old physician quietly shook his head.
- **Rationale:** 아득한 describes the displayed martial achievement as far beyond ordinary comprehension.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “단주!”
- **Current:** “First Captain!”
- **Defect:** The draft adds “First,” although the speaker only calls out the rank 단주.
- **Replacement:** “Captain!”
- **Rationale:** The source does not use 일 단주 or otherwise qualify the title in this exclamation.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 한 됫박은 될 법한 핏물을 쏟아낸 당사독은 숨을 헐떡였다.
- **Current:** Tang Sadok vomited nearly a basinful of blood and gasped for breath.
- **Defect:** A basin has no fixed capacity and can imply substantially more than the specific traditional measure 됫박.
- **Replacement:** Tang Sadok vomited nearly half a gallon of blood and gasped for breath.
- **Rationale:** A 됫박 is approximately 1.8 liters, just under half a US gallon, so the replacement preserves the stated quantity naturally.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 사천당문의 선조들과 못난 가주를 만나 애꿎은 목숨을 잃은 식솔들, 그리고 지금쯤 서천마군에 의해 처참한 죽음을 맞이했을 진태경 일행에게 보내는 사죄였다.
- **Current:** He was apologizing to the ancestors of the Sichuan Tang Clan, to the family members who had lost their lives because of an incompetent Family Head, and to Jin Taekyung and the others, who were likely even now suffering a horrible death at the hands of the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- **Defect:** The draft omits 애꿎은, which emphasizes that the household members were blameless victims, and renders their presumed completed deaths as ongoing suffering.
- **Replacement:** He was apologizing to the ancestors of the Sichuan Tang Clan, to the blameless members of his household who had lost their lives under an unworthy Family Head, and to Jin Taekyung and the others, who had likely already met gruesome deaths at the hands of the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- **Rationale:** 애꿎은 marks the dead as innocent, while 맞이했을 presents Jin Taekyung’s party as people Tang Sadok believes have probably already met their deaths.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 새하얀 몸통을 지닌 한 마리의 뱀이 당사독의 품을 파고들었다.
- **Current:** A snake with a pure-white body burrowed into Tang Sadok’s robes.
- **Defect:** The draft changes 품, his bosom or embrace, into his clothing and thereby alters the snake’s physical action.
- **Replacement:** A snake with a pure-white body burrowed into Tang Sadok’s embrace.
- **Rationale:** 품 denotes the space against his chest or within his embrace, not specifically the inside of his robes.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 혹여나 난전(亂戰) 중에 상하지는 않을까 걱정되어 풀어 두었던 녀석이다.
- **Current:** He had set Mimi free because he was worried she might be injured in the melee.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns Mimi a feminine gender that the source does not establish.
- **Replacement:** He had let Mimi loose because he was worried the snake might be injured in the melee.
- **Rationale:** 녀석 is gender-neutral in this context, so the translation should preserve that ambiguity.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 몇 방울의 눈물을 남긴 천년독각사, 미미는 새하얀 몸통을 이끌고 미끄러지듯 사라졌다.

기척이 멀어지자 당사독의 입가에 언뜻 씁쓸한 미소가 맺혔다.
- **Current:** After leaving behind several tears, Mimi led her white body away, slipping across the ground and disappearing.

As her presence faded into the distance, a bitter smile briefly formed at the corner of Tang Sadok’s mouth.
- **Defect:** The passage again adds an unestablished feminine gender, and “led her white body away” is unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** Leaving behind a few tears, Mimi slithered away and disappeared, the snake’s white body gliding across the ground.

As Mimi’s presence faded into the distance, a bitter smile briefly formed at the corner of Tang Sadok’s mouth.
- **Rationale:** The source remains gender-neutral and describes Mimi moving away with the white body sliding smoothly over the ground.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 몸 안에 흐르는 피도, 심지어 종(種)도 달랐지만 당미미는 가주가 인정한 사천당문의 일원이었다.
- **Current:** Her blood was different. Even her species was different. But Tang Mimi was a member of the Sichuan Tang Clan, recognized as such by its Family Head.
- **Defect:** The draft once more assigns Mimi a feminine gender absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** The blood flowing through Mimi was different. Even the snake’s species was different. But Tang Mimi was a member of the Sichuan Tang Clan, recognized as such by its Family Head.
- **Rationale:** The Korean sentence identifies differences of blood and species without revealing Mimi’s sex.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “본가, 본가는 어찌 되었나?”
- **Current:** “What happened to the family?”
- **Defect:** The draft drops the possessive force and repeated hesitation of 본가, weakening Tang Sadok’s identification with his own clan.
- **Replacement:** “Our family—what happened to our family?”
- **Rationale:** 본가 here means Tang Sadok’s own family or clan, and its repetition conveys his alarm.

## F11 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 청풍의 손이 당사독의 명문혈을 짚었다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung’s hand touched his Mingmen acupoint.
- **Defect:** “Touched” is too weak and imprecise for 짚었다 in an acupoint-treatment action.
- **Replacement:** Cheongpung pressed Tang Sadok’s Mingmen acupoint.
- **Rationale:** 짚었다 describes deliberately placing pressure on the acupoint to begin True Qi Guidance, not merely making contact.

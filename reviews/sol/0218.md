## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러나 사실은 과거 용족(龍族)의 노예이자 식용 가축임을 증명하는 족쇄이다. 이 물건을 소유하고 있는 자는 종족에 상관없이 용족 몬스터의 표적이 된다.
- **Current:** In truth, however, it is a shackle proving that the wearer was once a slave and livestock raised for food by the dragonkin. Anyone who possesses this item becomes a target of dragonkin monsters, regardless of their species.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the necklace prove that its current wearer was formerly dragonkin livestock, which would falsely apply that history to Taekyung. The final pronoun also leaves unclear whose species is irrelevant.
- **Replacement:** In truth, however, it is a shackle marking a yeti as a former slave and food animal of the dragonkin. Anyone who possesses this item becomes a target of dragonkin monsters, regardless of the possessor’s species.
- **Rationale:** The historical enslavement pertains to the yeti associated with the shackle, while the targeting effect applies separately to any current possessor regardless of species.

# Review

The draft contains two critical subject/negation reversals, one altered physical action, and several meaningful losses of humor, register, and agency.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “정파가 어쩌고, 대종남파가 어쩌고 하더니 꼭지 한 번 도니까 마두가 따로 없네. 차라리 이참에 종간나 파로 이름 바꾸는 게 어떻습니까.”
- **Current:** “You were going on about the orthodox faction and the Great Zhongnan Sect, but the moment you lost your temper, you turned into a demon king. Why not just rename the sect the Zhong-gangnan Sect while you’re at it?”
- **Defect:** The opaque invented form “Zhong-gangnan” fails to convey the insulting wordplay on Zhongnan’s name, while “demon king” overstates the generic 마두 as a specific exalted title.
- **Replacement:** “You went on about the orthodox faction and the Great Zhongnan Sect, but the second you lost your temper, you became no better than a demonic fiend. Why not rename yourselves the Zhong-Bastard Sect while you’re at it?”
- **Rationale:** 종간나 파 is a profane distortion of the sect’s name, and 마두 means a demonic fiend rather than a demon king.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “이건, 지금 사숙께서 보이는 모습은…… 대종남파의 제자가 할 짓이 아니지 않습니까.”
- **Current:** “The way you’re acting right now, Senior Martial Uncle… Isn’t this something a disciple of the Great Zhongnan Sect should do.”
- **Defect:** The English reverses the negation and says that Hwangbo Eom’s conduct is appropriate for a Great Zhongnan disciple.
- **Replacement:** “The way you’re acting right now, Senior Martial Uncle… Isn’t this beneath a disciple of the Great Zhongnan Sect?”
- **Rationale:** Hyuk Sopyung is condemning Hwangbo Eom’s conduct as something a Great Zhongnan disciple should not do.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “네 사부가 노부에게 맡기고 간 본문의 장문령부이니라. 이래도 거부할 생각이더냐?”
- **Current:** “This is the Sect Leader’s Command Token, entrusted to this old man by your Master. Do you still intend to refuse?”
- **Defect:** The draft omits 본문의, which explicitly identifies the token as belonging to their sect.
- **Replacement:** “This is our sect’s Sect Leader’s Command Token, entrusted to this old man by your Master. Do you still intend to refuse?”
- **Rationale:** 본문 means “our sect” or “this sect” here and reinforces the institutional authority Hwangbo Eom is invoking.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이내 쓴웃음을 지은 그가 동패를 향해 큰절과 함께 머리를 찧었다.
- **Current:** Then he gave a bitter smile and slammed his head against the copper tablet in a deep prostration.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the physical action by stating that Hyuk Sopyung strikes the tablet itself; the source says he prostrates toward the tablet and bangs his head down.
- **Replacement:** Then he gave a bitter smile, bowed deeply toward the copper tablet, and slammed his forehead against the floor.
- **Rationale:** 동패를 향해 modifies the direction of his prostration, while 머리를 찧었다 describes the forceful kowtow that splits his forehead.

## F05 — Major

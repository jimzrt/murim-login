# Review

One critical subject reversal changes who reacts before the battle resumes. Additional issues weaken a physical outcome, an approximate quantity, two recurring jokes, a sound effect, and Taekyung’s profanity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 갑작스러운 동족의 변화에 어리둥절하던 것도 잠시, 통제가 되지 않는 몬스터들이 재차 날뛰기 시작하자 다시금 전투가 시작되었다.
- **Current:** The Hunters had barely recovered from their confusion at the sudden change among their enemies when the uncontrolled monsters began rampaging again, and the battle resumed.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the momentary confusion to the Hunters, but the source says the uncontrolled monsters are briefly bewildered by the change in their own kind. This reverses the subject and invents the Hunters’ recovery.
- **Replacement:** The uncontrolled monsters were only briefly bewildered by the sudden change in their own kind before they began rampaging again, and the battle resumed.
- **Rationale:** 동족 refers to the monsters’ own kind, and 통제가 되지 않는 몬스터들이 is the subject that transitions from bewilderment to renewed rampaging.

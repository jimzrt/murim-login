# Review

Five actionable findings: two altered quantities, one ambiguous description of ethnicity, one reversed physical action, one incorrect firing mode, and one reversed characterization beat.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 여러 인종이 뒤섞인 백여 명의 헌터들이 욕설 섞인 고함을 내질렀다.
- **Current:** More than a hundred Hunters of various races shouted and cursed.
- **Defect:** “Various races” is ambiguous in a fantasy setting and can imply different species rather than the source’s mixture of human ethnicities; “백여 명” is also approximately one hundred, not definitively more than one hundred.
- **Replacement:** Around a hundred Hunters of different ethnicities shouted and cursed.
- **Rationale:** The source describes roughly one hundred Hunters from a mixture of racial or ethnic backgrounds.

## F02 — Major

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 모래 깊숙이 파고든 놈들의 꼬리가 지면 위로 솟구치고, 비명과 핏물이 사방에서 터져 나온다.
- **Current:** Their tails rose above the surface after burrowing deep into the sand, and screams and spurts of blood erupted in every direction.
- **Defect:** The draft grammatically makes the tails burrow into the sand and then merely rise. In the source, the scorpions are buried deep in the sand and their tails shoot up through the ground, causing the surrounding screams and bloodshed.
- **Replacement:** The creatures had burrowed deep into the sand; their tails shot up through the surface, and screams and spurts of blood erupted in every direction.
- **Rationale:** 파고든 describes the creatures’ position, while 솟구치고 conveys the tails surging upward in a sudden attack.

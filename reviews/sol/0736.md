# Review

One critical subject error and several localized quantity, terminology, register, and wording issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한때 50여 층에 달하는 건물의 일부였던 크고 작은 파편들이 불길에 휩싸인 채 쏟아져 내렸다.
- **Current:** Large and small fragments that had once been part of a building more than fifty stories tall came pouring down, engulfed in flames.
- **Defect:** “More than fifty” turns the source’s approximate quantity into a definite lower bound.
- **Replacement:** Large and small fragments that had once been part of a building roughly fifty stories tall came pouring down, engulfed in flames.
- **Rationale:** The suffix 여 makes the building approximately fifty stories tall, not necessarily taller than fifty stories.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어느덧 일백을 헤아리는 괴물들이 약속이라도 한 것처럼 고개를 들었다.
- **Current:** By then, nearly a hundred monsters had raised their heads as though they had made a silent pact.
- **Defect:** “Nearly a hundred” changes the stated quantity from approximately or fully one hundred to fewer than one hundred.
- **Replacement:** By then, the hundred or so monsters had raised their heads as though they had made a silent pact.
- **Rationale:** 일백을 헤아리는 describes the monsters as numbering around one hundred without imposing the draft’s below-one-hundred limit.

# Review

Several factual and structural errors alter quantities, lodging type, governmental jurisdiction, institutional hierarchy, and kinship; the System panels also violate the required Markdown format.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 녀석은 오십 기의 기마, 호위대의 위압감에 한 번 놀라고 나와 혁무진을 보고 두 번 놀랐다.
- **Current:** He was startled once by the fifty mounted riders and the intimidating presence of the escort, then twice more when he recognized Hyuk Mujin and me.
- **Defect:** “Twice more” makes him react three times in total. The Korean counts the first shock and then a second shock.
- **Replacement:** He was startled once by the fifty mounted riders and the intimidating presence of the escort, then a second time when he recognized Hyuk Mujin and me.
- **Rationale:** 한 번 놀라고 … 두 번 놀랐다 means he was startled once and then for the second time, not two additional times.

## F02 — Critical

- **Source:** “별채가 하룻밤에 은자 오십 냥. 맞지?”
- **Current:** “The private room is fifty nyang of silver for one night. That’s right, isn’t it?”
- **Defect:** 별채 is a separate annex or detached lodging, not merely a private room.
- **Replacement:** “The private annex is fifty nyang of silver for one night. That’s right, isn’t it?”
- **Rationale:** The current wording changes the type and scale of accommodation being rented.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 바로 그 황제의 명을 받들어 각 성을 다스리는 자가 성주이니 상당한 힘과 권한이 있지.
- **Current:** The City Lord is the one who governs each city under the Emperor’s command, so he possesses considerable power and authority.
- **Defect:** The translation changes 각 성 from the provinces to cities, substantially understating the City Lord’s jurisdiction.
- **Replacement:** The City Lords govern their respective provinces under the Emperor’s command, so they possess considerable power and authority.
- **Rationale:** Here 성 refers to a province, as confirmed by Shanxi Province and the explanation of the City Lord’s extensive authority.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “이공자, 본 가는 아직 구파일방도, 오대세가도 되지 못합니다. 자칫 문제가 생길 말은 삼가십시오.”

- **Rationale:** The current version changes the identities and quantities of two major institutional groupings invoked to explain the Jin Family’s limited standing.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 하지만 마적 놈들은 그런 거 없이 닥치는 대로 죽이고 불태우는 놈들 아닌가?
- **Current:** But the mounted bandits killed and burned people indiscriminately, didn’t they?
- **Defect:** The draft makes people the explicit object of both killing and burning, whereas the Korean leaves the object of 불태우다 unstated and broadly describes indiscriminate killing and arson.
- **Replacement:** But the mounted bandits killed indiscriminately and burned everything in their path, didn’t they?
- **Rationale:** 닥치는 대로 modifies the bandits’ indiscriminate destruction without specifically stating that they burn people alive.

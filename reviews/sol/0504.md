# Review

One critical quantity error, several altered physical or character details, a lost wordplay mechanism, and two established-terminology errors.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 괜한 오지랖은 아니었던 모양이군.
- **Current:** “Looks like it wasn’t meddling for nothing.”
- **Defect:** The phrasing is an unnatural literalism that obscures Mungyeong’s conclusion that his intervention was not needless.
- **Replacement:** “Seems I wasn’t meddling for nothing after all.”
- **Rationale:** 괜한 qualifies the meddling as needless or unwarranted, while 모양이군 marks Mungyeong’s understated realization.

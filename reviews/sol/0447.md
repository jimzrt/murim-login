# Review

Actionable fidelity issues include altered scope and spatial relations, lost hierarchy and register, an omitted characterization joke, and changed causal and continuity details.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 빠르게 물살을 가르며 나아가는 쾌조선에는 침묵만이 감돌았다.
- **Current:** Silence hung over the fast ships as they cut rapidly through the current.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the immediate setting from the single fast ship carrying the narrator to all four ships.
- **Replacement:** Silence hung over the fast ship as it cut rapidly through the current.
- **Rationale:** The source uses the singular 쾌조선에는; the fleet of four is introduced separately afterward.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 호북성을 양분하는 두 명문 대파의 제자들이 네 척의 쾌조선을 가득 채웠다.
- **Current:** Disciples from the two prestigious sects dividing Hubei Province filled the four fast ships.
- **Defect:** Calling both powers sects misclassifies the Zhuge Clan.
- **Replacement:** Disciples from the two prestigious great factions that divided Hubei Province filled the four fast ships.
- **Rationale:** The two powers are the Zhuge Clan and Wudang; 대파 functions broadly here and does not make the Zhuge Clan a sect.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “이렇게 된 김에 여쭤보는 건데, 춘추가 정확히 얼마나 되시는 겁니까?”

“춘추?”

평소에도 늙었다는 소리를 죽여 달라는 것과 동의어로 받아들이는 적천강이다. 나는 떨떠름하게 단어를 고쳤다.

“……그럼, 연세?”

“연세?”
- **Current:** “Since we’re on the subject, how old are you, exactly?”

“Old?”

Jeok Cheongang usually took any mention of his age as synonymous with asking someone to kill him. I awkwardly changed the word.

“…Then, how many years have you lived?”

“How many years?”
- **Defect:** The first question directly calls him old, while the source uses one highly respectful term for age and then switches to another; the narration also broadens his reaction from being called old to any mention of age.
- **Replacement:** “Since we’re on the subject, may I ask your venerable age?”

“My venerable age?”

Even under ordinary circumstances, Jeok Cheongang treated being called old as synonymous with asking him to kill you. I awkwardly tried another term.

“…Then, how many years have you lived, sir?”

“How many years?”
- **Rationale:** 춘추 and 연세 are both deferential age terms. Preserving Taekyung’s escalating politeness is necessary for the punchline when he finally addresses Jeok Cheongang like a child.

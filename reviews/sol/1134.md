# Review

Several errors alter the arrival, the trap, and Ma Sanbao’s reactions.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 살아 있을 적보다도 거대해진 몸뚱어리를 굽혀 자신의 주인을 맞이했다.
- **Current:** The mountain lord[^1] who had ruled Mount Kunlun only a month ago lowered his now-larger-than-life body to greet his master.
- **Defect:** “Larger-than-life” is figurative; the mountain lord’s body has physically grown larger than it was when he was alive.
- **Replacement:** The mountain lord[^1] who had ruled Mount Kunlun only a month ago bent his body, now larger than it had been in life, to greet his master.
- **Rationale:** 살아 있을 적보다도 거대해진 explicitly compares his present size with his size while alive.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어느덧 곤륜파의 상징과 같은 태청전(太淸殿)을 빈틈없이 둘러싸고 있던, 수천의 인마(人魔)와 함께.
- **Current:** Along with thousands of human demons who had, before then, surrounded Taiqing Hall—the very symbol of the Kunlun Sect—without leaving a single gap.
- **Defect:** “Before then” makes the encirclement sound finished; the thousands are surrounding the hall at this moment.
- **Replacement:** Along with thousands of human demons who now surrounded Taiqing Hall—the very symbol of the Kunlun Sect—without leaving a single gap.
- **Rationale:** 둘러싸고 있던 describes their ongoing position around the hall.

## F03 — Major

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금 당장은 새하얗게 물든 뇌리를 가득 채운 두 글자를 이해하는 것만으로도 벅찼으니까.

‘함정……!’

그래, 이건 함정이었다.

그것도 아주 치밀하게 준비한.
- **Current:** For now, even understanding the two words filling his blank mind was more than he could manage.

*An ambush……!*

Yes, it was an ambush.

One laid with extraordinary care.
- **Defect:** He can barely process the realization, rather than failing to understand it. 함정 is a trap, specifically the one laid in the formation, not an ambush.
- **Replacement:** For now, he could barely process the word filling his blank mind.

*A trap…!*

Yes, it was a trap.

One laid with extraordinary care.
- **Rationale:** 이해하는 것만으로도 벅찼으니까 conveys the effort of grasping the realization; the repeated 함정 identifies the mechanism revealed immediately afterward.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그래. 기억하고 있었지. 오늘 이후로는 머릿속에서 잊히겠지만.”
- **Current:** “I do. I’ll remember you—until after today.”
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the timing of Ma Sanbao’s threat: he has remembered Zhuge Feng until now and expects to forget him after today.
- **Replacement:** “I do. I remembered you. After today, though, I’ll forget you.”
- **Rationale:** 기억하고 있었지 is retrospective; 오늘 이후로는 … 잊히겠지만 states that Zhuge Feng will leave his mind after today.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 천고의 죄인을.
- **Current:** No—at the age-old criminal who had dared to overthrow the imperial household.
- **Defect:** “Age-old criminal” suggests the criminal is old; 천고 describes the magnitude of his treason.
- **Replacement:** No—at the criminal for the ages who had dared to overthrow the imperial household.
- **Rationale:** 천고의 죄인 condemns him as a criminal whose offense will be remembered for ages.

# Review

The draft preserves the battle’s broad sequence, but several passages change what happened, why people acted, or how long an event lasted.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 항아리 모양의 협곡에서 두 번에 걸쳐 산서성의 명운을 건 대전투가 벌어진 것은 그만한 이유가 있기 때문이다.
- **Current:** But there was a reason Shanxi Province’s fate had been decided in two great battles fought in this jar-shaped gorge.
- **Defect:** Says the province’s fate was decided there; the source says two battles with its fate at stake took place there.
- **Replacement:** But there was a reason two great battles with Shanxi Province’s fate at stake had been fought in this jar-shaped gorge.
- **Rationale:** 명운을 건 describes what was at stake, not an established outcome.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘그건 버리고, 오늘부터는 이놈을 쓰시게. 일찍부터 이공자를 위해 벼려 둔 검이니.’
- **Current:** *“Put that one away. Starting today, use this. I forged it for you, Second Young Master, a long time ago.”*
- **Defect:** Changes an instruction to discard the old sword into one to store it, and renders advance preparation as a remote date.
- **Replacement:** *“Get rid of that one. Use this from today on. I forged it for you well in advance, Second Young Master.”*
- **Rationale:** 버리고 means to throw it away; 일찍부터 … 벼려 둔 emphasizes that the sword had been forged ahead of time for him.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 모든 것을 내팽개치고 태원진가로 달려온 장인들은 결코 의리 따위로 움직이지 않는다는 것을.
- **Current:** The artisans who had left everything behind and rushed to the Jin Family of Taiyuan hadn’t come out of mere loyalty.
- **Defect:** “Mere loyalty” implies loyalty may have contributed, whereas the source rules it out as their motive.
- **Replacement:** The artisans who had left everything behind and rushed to the Jin Family of Taiyuan had not been moved by loyalty at all.
- **Rationale:** 결코 의리 따위로 움직이지 않는다는 것을 emphatically rejects loyalty as the reason.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 아마 과거의 인연 때문이겠지요.
- **Current:** It was probably because of the connection they’d made in the past.
- **Defect:** The plural pronoun can point to Jang Taebo and the Third Young Master, making their connection the reason the guild artisans came.
- **Replacement:** It was probably because of his old ties to them.
- **Rationale:** In this sentence the retainer is explaining how Jang brought the former Ironcraft Guild artisans with him; his prior ties to those artisans are the relevant connection.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어둠 속, 지축을 울리며 암반을 타고 쏟아져 내리는 검은색의 구체들.
- **Current:** Black spheres hurtled out of the darkness, rumbling over the bedrock and shaking the earth beneath them.
- **Defect:** Omits that the boulders are pouring down the rock face onto the people in the gorge.
- **Replacement:** Black spheres poured down the rock face out of the darkness, shaking the earth as they came.
- **Rationale:** 암반을 타고 쏟아져 내리는 specifies their downward route, which explains the attack from the cliffs.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 말똥 냄새 풀풀 풍기는 게르에서 보낸 세월만 몇 년이었나.
- **Current:** How many years had it been since he’d spent his days in a ger that reeked of horse manure?
- **Defect:** Changes the number of years spent in the ger into the number of years since that stay.
- **Replacement:** How many years had he spent in a ger reeking of horse manure?

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘보면 모르나? 이 늙은이가 말년에 개고생해 가며 만든 최고의 역작 중 하나지. 만년한철(萬年寒鐵)과도 비견될 만한 것으로 벼려 냈으니, 부디 잘 다뤄 주시게.’
- **Current:** *“Can’t you tell? This old man’s one of his finest masterpieces, made after a whole lot of backbreaking work in his later years. I forged it from something that can rival Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron. I hope you’ll take good care of it.”*
- **Defect:** The possessive construction calls the old man a masterpiece rather than the sword.
- **Replacement:** *“Can’t you tell? It’s one of the finest things this old man has ever made, and I worked myself to the bone making it in my old age. I forged it from something that can rival Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, so take good care of it.”*
- **Rationale:** 만든 최고의 역작 중 하나 refers to the sword Jang made; the current English assigns that description to Jang.

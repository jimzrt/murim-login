# Review

The draft preserves the chapter’s main sequence, but changes the hero’s identity, the disguise’s limits, and Morgoth’s relationship to Asmodeus.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 영웅은 다재다능했어.
- **Current:** The hero was a man of many talents.
- **Defect:** Calls the hero a man, although the source later explicitly says he was not human.
- **Replacement:** The hero had many talents.
- **Rationale:** The Korean describes his talents without identifying him as human.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 몇 번의 워프 게이트를 갈아타며 수천 킬로미터를 이동한 끝에 이곳까지 오게 되었다.
- **Current:** After changing trains at several Warp Gates and traveling thousands of kilometers, he had made it here.
- **Defect:** Introduces trains where the source says he transferred between Warp Gates.
- **Replacement:** After traveling thousands of kilometers by way of several Warp Gates, he had made it here.
- **Rationale:** 갈아타며 describes switching gates, not boarding trains.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 꾸며 낼 수 있는 건, 결국 겉모습뿐이니까.
- **Current:** *In the end, all you can change is the appearance.*
- **Defect:** Turns a limit on what can be faked into a limit on what can be changed, weakening the contrast with his real power in the land ahead.
- **Replacement:** *In the end, only appearances can be faked.*
- **Rationale:** 꾸며 낼 conveys fabrication; 겉모습 is outward appearance.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 스켈레톤 킹은 힘주어 발걸음을 옮겼다.
- **Current:** the Skeleton King stepped forward, setting his jaw.
- **Defect:** Adds a jaw-setting gesture and loses the emphasis on the force of his step.
- **Replacement:** the Skeleton King took a firm step forward.
- **Rationale:** The source describes only his deliberate, forceful step.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 최고의 수준을 충족하지 않는 한, 그의 호기심은 멈출 줄을 몰랐다.
- **Current:** Unless his curiosity was fully satisfied, it never stopped.
- **Defect:** Loses Morgoth’s exacting standard: only the highest level would satisfy his curiosity.
- **Replacement:** Nothing short of the very best could satisfy his curiosity.
- **Rationale:** 최고의 수준 specifies a standard of excellence, not merely complete satisfaction.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 자신은 다른 이들과 달리 마왕 아스모데우스에게 충성을 다 하지도, 그를 위해 목숨을 바칠 생각도 없었으니까.
- **Current:** Unlike the others, he had never pledged his loyalty to Demon King Asmodeus, nor had he intended to give his life for him.
- **Defect:** Changes lack of loyal service into lack of a formal pledge.
- **Replacement:** Unlike the others, he was not devoted to serving Demon King Asmodeus, nor did he intend to give his life for him.
- **Rationale:** 충성을 다 하지도 concerns loyalty in conduct; the source says nothing about whether he made a pledge.

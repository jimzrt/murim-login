# Review

Several subject reversals and scope changes affect scene facts, alongside omitted honorifics, a causal error, softened profanity, and an altered oath.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고…… 유일한 희망은 신의 경지에 다다른 의술을 지닌 한 사람뿐이다.
- **Current:** And… there was only one person who possessed medical skills that had reached the realm of the gods.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the claim from this person being Jeok Cheongang’s only hope to being the only person who possesses such medical skills.
- **Replacement:** And… our only hope was one person whose medical skills had reached the realm of the gods.
- **Rationale:** 유일한 희망 describes the person’s role as the sole hope, not their exclusive possession of godlike medical skill.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 다만 처음 이름을 알린 시점이 사십여 년 전이니, 적어도 환갑이 넘은 연배이지 싶다.
- **Current:** The first reports of the Divine Physician had appeared more than forty years ago, though, so they had to be at least sixty years old.
- **Defect:** The draft weakens 넘은 from older than sixty to at least sixty and recasts becoming renowned as reports appearing.
- **Replacement:** The Divine Physician first became known more than forty years ago, though, so I figured they had to be over sixty.
- **Rationale:** 처음 이름을 알린 means first became known, and 환갑이 넘은 specifies an age over sixty.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘제가 반드시 찾아내서…… 깨워 드릴게요.’
- **Current:** *I’ll find you no matter what… and wake you up.*
- **Defect:** The English makes Jeok Cheongang the apparent object of both “find” and “wake,” even though Taekyung means he will find the Divine Physician and wake Jeok Cheongang.
- **Replacement:** *I’ll find the Divine Physician no matter what… and wake you up.*
- **Rationale:** The surrounding passage identifies the Divine Physician as the person Taekyung must find, while these thoughts are addressed to the unconscious Jeok Cheongang.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “무, 무송 대협이 말을 전하라고 하셔서요.”
- **Current:** “M-Mu Song asked me to deliver a message.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits Mungyeong’s deferential 대협 address for Mu Song.

- **Rationale:** The honorific marks Mungyeong’s respectful relationship to the senior martial artist and is binding terminology.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “거, 거의 다 도착했으니까 준비하라고 하셨습니다.”
- **Current:** “He said we’re almost there and that we should prepare.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied addressee into an inclusive “we,” making Mungyeong part of the group being told to prepare.
- **Replacement:** “H-He said you’re almost there, so you should get ready.”
- **Rationale:** Mungyeong is relaying Mu Song’s instruction to Taekyung’s party; the following order for Mungyeong to gather his own things confirms he was not included in that instruction.

## F06 — Critical

- **Current:** “That’s because I have a head like a rock.”

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘진 공자님이 화왕 적천강 대협의 제자라고요? 와아!’

‘검성 매종학 대협의 위명은 저도 익히 들었습니다. 청 공자님도 엄청난 분이셨군요.’
- **Current:** *“Young Master Jin, you’re Fire King Jeok Cheongang’s Disciple? Wow!”*

*“I’ve heard plenty about the Sword Saint Mae Jonghak’s reputation. Young Master Cheong must be incredible, too.”*
- **Defect:** Both recollected lines omit Mungyeong’s 대협 honorifics, flattening his excited but deferential register toward Jeok Cheongang and Mae Jonghak.
- **Replacement:** *“Young Master Jin, you’re the Disciple of Great Hero Jeok Cheongang, the Fire King? Wow!”*

*“I’ve heard plenty about Great Hero Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint. Young Master Cheong must be incredible, too.”*
- **Rationale:** 대협 is explicitly used with both names and should be preserved as Great Hero under the binding terminology.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 나는 까치발을 들고 내 어깨 너머를 힐끗거리는 문경의 어깨를 붙잡았다.
- **Current:** I rose onto my tiptoes and grabbed Mungyeong by the shoulder as he craned his neck to peek over mine.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung stand on tiptoe, but Mungyeong is the one standing on tiptoe to peer over Taekyung’s shoulder.
- **Replacement:** I grabbed Mungyeong by the shoulder as he stood on tiptoe and peeked over mine.
- **Rationale:** 까치발을 들고 내 어깨 너머를 힐끗거리는 modifies 문경, while 나는 is the subject of 붙잡았다.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “우리는 활동 반경이 조금 넓어졌고, 관에서는 수적들에게 죽었다는 탄원이 줄었으니 서로 이득 아니겠나.”
- **Current:** “Our range of activity has widened a little, while the authorities have received fewer complaints from people who were killed by river bandits. Isn’t that a benefit to both sides?”
- **Defect:** The draft illogically makes dead victims submit the complaints, altering the mechanism of Mu Song’s compromise.
- **Replacement:** “Our range of activity has widened a little, while the authorities have received fewer petitions over people being killed by river bandits. Isn’t that a benefit to both sides?”
- **Rationale:** 수적들에게 죽었다는 탄원 means petitions reporting that people had been killed by river bandits, not complaints submitted by the dead.

## F10 — Minor

- **Source:** ……염병. 나라 꼴 잘 돌아간다.
- **Current:** *Damn. The country is doing just fine.*
- **Defect:** “Damn” softens the stronger irritated profanity 염병 and weakens the deadpan disgust.

## F11 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “다름이 아니라, 마침 저도 사천당가로 가는 길이었는데 동행할 수 있다면 이 은혜, 이 몸이 죽고 죽어 일만 번 고쳐 죽어 넋이라도 있고 없을 때까지 잊지 않을 것…….”
- **Current:** “The thing is, I happen to be on my way to the Sichuan Tang Clan, too. If I could travel with you, I would never forget this favor—not even after dying, dying, and dying ten thousand times over, until my soul had departed or ceased to exist, and even if I were—”
- **Defect:** The draft turns the deliberately paradoxical “whether my soul exists or not” into two synonymous forms of disappearance and adds an unfinished “even if I were” clause absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** “The thing is, I happen to be on my way to the Sichuan Tang Clan, too. If I could travel with you, I would never forget this favor even if this body died and died, then died ten thousand times more, until whether my soul existed or not—”
- **Rationale:** 넋이라도 있고 없을 때까지 preserves the exaggerated oath’s paired existence/nonexistence wording, and the source is interrupted without introducing another conditional clause.

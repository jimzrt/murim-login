# Review

One altered physical action, one loss of coercive agency, and several characterization, humor, and terminology issues require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무송이 턱짓하자 그의 수하들이 다가와 수적들을 질질 끌고 갔다.
- **Current:** At Mu Song’s nod, his subordinates approached and dragged the river bandits away.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Mu Song’s chin gesture into a nod, altering the specified physical action.
- **Replacement:** At a jerk of Mu Song’s chin, his subordinates approached and dragged the river bandits away.
- **Rationale:** 턱짓 is a gesture made with the chin, not a nod.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 다른 수적들과는 달리 쇠사슬에 꽁꽁 묶인 채 무송의 앞에 무릎 꿇려진 오십 대의 사내.
- **Current:** Unlike the other river bandits, a man in his fifties knelt before Mu Song with his body tightly bound in chains.
- **Defect:** The draft loses the causative force of 무릎 꿇려진 and makes the prisoner appear to kneel rather than having been forced to his knees.
- **Replacement:** Unlike the other river bandits, a man in his fifties had been forced to his knees before Mu Song, tightly bound in chains.
- **Rationale:** 무릎 꿇려진 explicitly describes a posture imposed on Hwang Tae-gu by others.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 예. 무모, 아니 용맹하게 선박을 그대로 관통해 버리시던 모습이 지금도 눈에 훤합니다.
- **Current:** Yes. I can still vividly see the way you boldly—no, courageously—pierced straight through the ship.
- **Defect:** “Boldly” does not preserve Mujin’s slip into calling the act reckless before hastily correcting himself.
- **Replacement:** Yes. I can still vividly see the way you recklessly—no, courageously—pierced straight through the ship.
- **Rationale:** 무모 means reckless; the self-correction to 용맹하게 is the joke and reflects Mujin’s fear of Taekyung’s reaction.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** ‘이렇게라도 성장했다면…… 차라리 다행이지.’
- **Current:** *If this is how he grew, then… it’s better than nothing.*
- **Defect:** “Better than nothing” dismisses Cheongpung’s growth as inadequate, whereas Taekyung concludes that growth achieved even through these painful circumstances may be fortunate or for the best.
- **Replacement:** *If he managed to grow even this way… maybe it was for the best.*
- **Rationale:** 차라리 다행이지 expresses qualified relief about the outcome, not dissatisfaction with the amount or quality of Cheongpung’s growth.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 바로 그 끝에 맺힌 강대한 기운.
- **Current:** Powerful energy gathered at the tips of both weapons.
- **Defect:** The generic “energy” fails to render Murim 기운 as qi under the binding terminology distinction.
- **Replacement:** Powerful qi gathered at the tips of both weapons.
- **Rationale:** This is martial energy gathered on a harpoon and spear immediately before the release of Spear Energy, so 기운 denotes Murim qi.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 족히 이백여 장은 떨어진 거리. 그러나 인간 독수리라고 불러도 될 만큼 향상된 시력은 모든 광경을 일목요연하게 담아냈다.
- **Current:** The distance was easily over two hundred zhang. But my vision had improved enough to be called eagle-eyed, allowing me to take in the entire scene at a glance.
- **Defect:** “Eagle-eyed” normalizes away the narrator’s deliberately exaggerated image of himself as a literal “human eagle.”
- **Replacement:** The distance was easily over two hundred zhang. But my vision had improved enough that you could call me a human eagle, allowing me to take in the entire scene at a glance.
- **Rationale:** 인간 독수리 is “human eagle,” and preserving that odd image retains Taekyung’s comic narrative voice.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 오늘 줄초상 나겠군. 쯧쯧.
- **Current:** There’ll be a mass funeral today. Tsk, tsk.
- **Defect:** A “mass funeral” is one ceremony for multiple dead people, while 줄초상 predicts a succession of deaths and funerals.
- **Replacement:** There’ll be funerals one after another today. Tsk, tsk.
- **Rationale:** 줄초상 refers to multiple deaths or funerals occurring in succession, matching Gung Gibang’s prediction that the bandits will be killed one after another.

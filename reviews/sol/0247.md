# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues remain, including a reversed subject in the Sound Transmission and inaccurate explanations of Kwak Cheolyung’s financial ruin.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 너무 괘념치 말거라. 이미 수뇌부 쪽에서 실컷 자랑하고 오신 모양이더라.
- **Current:** —Don’t take it too seriously. It seems you already bragged to the higher-ups to your heart’s content.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung the person who bragged, but the honorific verb identifies Jeok Cheongang as the one who went to the leadership and bragged about Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** —Don’t let it bother you too much. It seems he already went and bragged about you to the higher-ups to his heart’s content.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 오신 is Jeok Cheongang, as confirmed by the surrounding joke about his concealed pride and immediate throat-clearing.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 지금까지 번 돈에, 은퇴 후 지낼 장원과 땅까지 담보로 잡아 고리대금을 끌어 썼다는데…… 쯧쯧.
- **Current:** I heard he mortgaged all the money he’d earned, along with the estate and land he planned to live on after retirement, and took out high-interest loans… Tsk, tsk.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly says he mortgaged money. He sank his accumulated earnings into the wagers, then mortgaged his retirement estate and land to borrow at usurious rates.
- **Replacement:** I heard he sank all the money he’d earned into it, then even mortgaged the estate and land where he’d planned to retire to borrow from loan sharks… Tsk, tsk.
- **Rationale:** Only the estate and land are collateral in 담보로 잡아; 지금까지 번 돈 is an additional part of the wealth he risked, while 고리대금 specifically denotes usurious lending.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 비틀거리는 걸음으로 도박장을 빠져나가는 곽철융이 손에 쥔 것은 개평으로 받은 철전 몇 푼이 전부였다.
- **Current:** Kwak Cheolyung staggered out of the gambling den. The only thing in his hand was a few iron coins he had received as a share of the house cut.
- **Defect:** 개평 is a small consolation handout from someone’s winnings, not a share of the gambling house’s cut.
- **Replacement:** Kwak Cheolyung staggered out of the gambling den. The only thing in his hand was a few iron coins he had been given as a consolation handout.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes that the formerly wealthy gambler leaves with nothing but a few coins given to him out of another gambler’s winnings.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 그런 자의 제자가 도곤과 노름을 할 일은 없다.
- **Current:** Someone like that would never let his Disciple gamble with a Dogon.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the Disciple’s improbable action into the Fire King withholding permission, introducing control and agency absent from the source.

# Review

One altered physical action and several localized fidelity, register, and emphasis issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 입술을 질끈 깨문 나는 속도를 더욱 높였다.
- **Current:** I clenched my teeth and increased my speed.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Taekyung biting his lip into clenching his teeth.
- **Replacement:** I bit down hard on my lip and increased my speed.
- **Rationale:** 입술을 질끈 깨문 explicitly describes biting down on his lip, not clenching his teeth.

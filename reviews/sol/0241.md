# Review

Several factual and mechanical details need correction, along with two binding terminology issues and localized cultural or idiomatic wording.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그 술, 여아홍 맞소?”
- **Current:** “Is that Yeoahong?”
- **Defect:** The unestablished transliteration gives English readers no indication of what Yeoahong is.
- **Replacement:** “Is that Yeoahong[^1]?”

[^1]: Yeoahong, literally “Daughter’s Red,” is a traditional Chinese rice wine.
- **Rationale:** 여아홍 is a culturally specific wine name whose significance is not conveyed by bare romanization; a concise first-use footnote preserves the name without leaving it unexplained.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 벌떡 일어서려는 상인을 동료가 만류했다.
- **Current:** One of the merchants shot to his feet, but his companion stopped him.
- **Defect:** The draft completes the merchant’s action, while the source says his companion stops him as he is about to stand.
- **Replacement:** One of the merchants was about to spring to his feet, but his companion stopped him.
- **Rationale:** 일어서려는 marks an intended, not completed, action; preserving the interruption matters to the physical sequence.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “강호의 동량이셨구려. 실례지만 어느 문파의 제자인지 물어볼 수 있겠소?”
- **Current:** “You must be a promising young man of the martial world. If you don’t mind, may I ask which sect you belong to?”
- **Defect:** The draft omits both the merchants’ exaggerated description of him as a future pillar of the martial world and the binding term Disciple.
- **Replacement:** “You must be a future pillar of the martial world. If you don’t mind, may I ask which sect you are a Disciple of?”
- **Rationale:** 강호의 동량 is flattering hyperbole meaning a future pillar or leading figure, while 제자 specifically identifies him as a sect’s Disciple rather than merely an affiliate.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “뭐라더라, 졸개 놈들이 말하기로는 웬 쇳덩이들을 주렁주렁 달고 있었다던데. 워낙 깊은 밤이라 얼굴은 제대로 못 봤고, 번쩍하더니 적살부가 쓰러졌다고 하더이다.”
- **Current:** “What was it? The underlings said the man had several lumps of iron hanging all over him. It was so late at night that they couldn’t see his face properly. They just saw a flash, and then the Red-Killing Axe fell.”
- **Defect:** The draft identifies the unknown attacker as male even though the source leaves the person’s identity and gender unstated.
- **Replacement:** “What was it? The underlings said whoever it was had chunks of iron dangling all over them. It was so late at night that they couldn’t see their face properly. They just saw a flash, and then the Red-Killing Axe fell.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean omits a gendered subject and explicitly emphasizes that the witnesses could not see the attacker’s face.

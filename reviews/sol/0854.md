# Review

Several scene facts and physical details are altered, chiefly who survived, how the merchant indicated the bodies, the attackers’ injuries, and the battle’s duration.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이렇게 살아서 다시 보니 반갑군. 장 무관.
- **Current:** “It’s good to see you alive, Officer Jang.”
- **Defect:** The line makes Jang Il the person who survived, though the speaker is the one returning alive.
- **Replacement:** “Glad I survived to see you again, Officer Jang.”
- **Rationale:** The merchant master has survived the attack and is greeting Jang Il.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 상단주의 손끝을 따라 고개를 돌린 장일은
- **Current:** Jang Il followed the trading company master’s gaze and felt the world go dark.
- **Defect:** The merchant master points toward the bodies; the draft changes that action to a look.
- **Replacement:** Jang Il looked where the trading company master pointed and felt the world go dark.
- **Rationale:** 손끝 identifies the direction indicated by his fingertip, not his gaze.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 다리가 베이고, 팔이 날아가도 달려들었지.
- **Current:** “Even when their legs were cut off, even when their arms were gone, they kept coming.
- **Defect:** The draft severs the attackers’ legs, while the source says their legs were cut and distinguishes that from arms being torn off.
- **Replacement:** “Even with their legs slashed and their arms torn off, they kept coming.
- **Rationale:** 다리가 베이고 describes cut legs; 팔이 날아가도 supplies the severed-limb image.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 전투는 불과 반 시진 만에 일방적으로 끝났고
- **Current:** “The battle was over in less than half a shichen, and our side broke.
- **Defect:** “Less than” changes the stated duration, and the draft drops that the battle ended one-sidedly.
- **Replacement:** “The battle ended decisively in just half a shichen—roughly an hour—and our side broke.
- **Rationale:** 불과 반 시진 만에 specifies just half a shichen; 일방적으로 describes the one-sided outcome.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Replacement:** Someone’s death set off a fierce battle.
- **Rationale:** 누군가의 죽음 does not identify the dead person’s side.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94

- **Replacement:** The fighters trained by the trading companies and the hired wandering martial artists fought back.

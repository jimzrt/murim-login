# Review

The draft contains critical subject, identity, magical-mechanism, and quantity errors, along with several register shifts and localized natural-English issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 보안팀장이 개인적으로 연락을 취했습니다.
- **Current:** The Security Team Leader contacted him personally.
- **Defect:** The added object “him” implies that the Security Team Leader contacted Jin Taekyung, contradicting the reporting chain and the following explanation.
- **Replacement:** The Security Team Leader contacted me directly.
- **Rationale:** Team Leader 1 is recounting the Security Team Leader’s direct report to him; the source does not say that the target was contacted.

Several scene facts and character cues need correction, especially Mukyung’s kinship, his final wish, and the target of Murong Baek’s gaze.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 이후에는 태원진가의 긍지이자 자랑스러운 아우가 되고 싶었다.
- **Current:** After that, he’d wanted to become a source of pride to the Jin Family of Taiyuan—and a brother his younger sibling could be proud of.
- **Defect:** Makes Mukyung the older brother in an ambition that concerns being a younger brother.
- **Replacement:** After that, he’d wanted to become the pride of the Jin Family of Taiyuan—and a younger brother his elder brother could be proud of.
- **Rationale:** 아우 means younger brother. His separate wish to be an older brother worthy of Taekyung follows in the next paragraph.

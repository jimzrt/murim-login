# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter a dialogue subject, Go Jun’s deference toward his Master, Lee Jungryong’s family status, and the Arch Lich terminology. Additional localized issues narrow conduct to speech, mistranslate a funerary object, and add an unsupported gender.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고…… 눈이 있었다.
- **Current:** And… he had eyes.
- **Defect:** The literal phrasing sounds comically self-evident and fails to convey the source’s dramatic shift of focus to the man’s eyes.
- **Replacement:** And then… there were his eyes.
- **Rationale:** The source isolates his eyes as the next striking feature rather than merely stating that he possessed them.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 비록 상대가 국내에서도 손꼽히는 실력을 지닌 헌터라지만 자신 역시 환갑이 되도록 정치라는 진흙탕에서 구른 몸.
- **Current:** His opponent was one of the most skilled Hunters in Korea, but the Prime Minister himself had spent his entire life rolling through the political mud pit, all the way to his sixties.
- **Defect:** “His entire life” adds the unsupported claim that his political career began at birth, while “his sixties” makes the specific age of sixty indefinite.
- **Replacement:** His opponent was one of the most skilled Hunters in Korea, but the Prime Minister himself had spent decades rolling through the political mud and was now sixty.
- **Rationale:** 환갑 specifically denotes reaching sixty; the source presents his age and long political experience without claiming that politics occupied his entire life.

# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses who would fail to notice a takeover and blurs the distinction between the two consciousnesses.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어느 날 갑자기 그놈이 네 몸뚱어리를 차지해도 눈치채지 못할 것 아니냐.
- **Current:** If he suddenly took over your body one day, you might not even notice.
- **Defect:** Changes the person who might fail to notice from Jeok Cheongang to Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** If he suddenly took over your body one day, I might not even notice.
- **Rationale:** Jeok is explaining why Taekyung’s behavior worries him: Jeok might not be able to tell the two apart.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 만약 처음부터 네 녀석과 진태경…… 제기랄. 이걸 뭐라고 불러야 할지 모르겠군. 그래, 그 망나니 놈과 의식이 뒤섞여 있었다면 말이 되지 않겠느냐?
- **Current:** “If you and Jin Taekyung had been mixed together from the beginning… Damn it. I don’t even know what to call it. How about that good-for-nothing’s consciousness and yours? Wouldn’t that make sense?”
- **Defect:** Turns Jeok’s correction of what to call the original Jin Taekyung into a proposed way of phrasing the question, obscuring his hypothesis.
- **Replacement:** “If your consciousness and Jin Taekyung’s had been mixed together from the beginning… Damn it. I don’t know what to call him. Fine, if your consciousness and that good-for-nothing’s had been mixed together, wouldn’t that make sense?”
- **Rationale:** Jeok falters over the shared name, calls the original owner ‘that good-for-nothing,’ and asks whether their consciousnesses were mixed from the start.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한낱 망나니에 지나지 않았던 이 세상의 진태경과 내 의식은 완전한 별개다.
- **Current:** The Jin Taekyung of this world, who’d been nothing but a good-for-nothing, and I were completely separate.
- **Defect:** Says the two people were separate rather than specifically their consciousnesses.
- **Replacement:** My consciousness and that of this world’s Jin Taekyung, who’d been nothing but a good-for-nothing, were completely separate.
- **Rationale:** The distinction between consciousnesses is the point of Taekyung’s conclusion and the question Jeok has just raised.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 막 첫걸음을 내디딘 나에게 주어진 것은 현대와 같은 이름 석 자.
- **Current:** All I’d been given as I took my first steps here were a name and three characters, just like in the modern world.
- **Defect:** Splits one three-character name into two things: a name and three characters.
- **Replacement:** All I’d been given when I first set foot here was the same three-syllable name I’d had in the modern world.
- **Rationale:** 이름 석 자 refers to his three-syllable name, which matched the one he had in the modern world.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그런 내 모습을 말없이 바라보던 적천강이 입을 열기 전까지.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang watched me in silence. Then, before he could say anything—
- **Defect:** Introduces the ensuing dialogue as occurring before Jeok can speak, though Jeok is the one who speaks.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang watched me in silence until he finally spoke.
- **Rationale:** The Korean ends Taekyung’s speechlessness at the moment Jeok opens his mouth.

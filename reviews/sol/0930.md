# Review

One reversed promise, one lost double entendre, and one localized joke omission.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반드시 살아남아, 머지않은 미래에 다시 돌려주겠노라고 약속했던 소년의 모습이 눈앞에 어른거린다.
- **Current:** The boy’s face floated before my eyes. I’d promised I’d survive, then return it to him before long.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses who promised to survive and return the ring.
- **Replacement:** The boy who’d promised to survive and return it to me before long appeared before my eyes.
- **Rationale:** The boy is the subject of 약속했던; he made the promise to Taekyung.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “하지만, 나름대로의 사정이 있었습니다.”

“태산이 안다! 사정 안다! 그거 뭔지 안다!”
- **Current:** “But I had my reasons.”

“Taishan knows! Taishan knows the reason! Taishan knows what it is!”
- **Defect:** Rendering both uses of 사정 as “reason” loses Taishan’s accidental sexual double entendre.
- **Replacement:** “But I had…something to take care of.”

“Taishan knows! Taishan knows about taking care of that! Taishan knows what it is!”
- **Rationale:** 사정 means circumstances in Taekyung’s defense but can also mean ejaculation; the exchange relies on Taishan hearing the sexual meaning without Taekyung stating it outright.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 청경채 마켓에 가져다 팔아도 은자 수백 냥은 너끈히 나갈 법한 씨알 굵은 야명주를 반짝이 스티커처럼 덕지덕지 처바르는 건 황제나 할 수 있는 일이니까.
- **Current:** Only an emperor could plaster the place with pearls so large each one could fetch hundreds of silver nyang at a bok choy market.
- **Defect:** The draft drops the glitter-sticker comparison and treats the marketplace name as a generic type of market.
- **Replacement:** Only an emperor could plaster the place with night-shining pearls big enough to fetch hundreds of silver nyang each on Bok Choy Market, as if they were glitter stickers.
- **Rationale:** The source names 청경채 마켓 and compares the extravagant scattering of valuable pearls to slapping on glitter stickers.

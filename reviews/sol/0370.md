# Review

The draft contains repeated critical mistranslations of the singular Third Fiend as the collective Three Fiends, along with meaningful losses of ambiguity, hierarchy, characterization, and Murim terminology.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 어지간히 이름난 명사조차 쉽게 드나들 수 없고
- **Current:** Even renowned masters could not easily enter or leave its grounds.
- **Defect:** The source says prominent or renowned people, not martial masters; “masters” adds martial status.
- **Replacement:** Even prominent figures could not easily come and go.
- **Rationale:** 명사 means a distinguished or prominent person here, whereas “master” has a specific martial meaning in this setting.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “원아진생무별염 아미타불독상수 심심상계옥호광…….”
- **Current:** “May we be reborn in the Pure Land with no thought but Amitabha, our hearts forever bound to the light of the jeweled urn…”
- **Defect:** The translation changes a prayer about having no other thought at life’s end into a wish for rebirth, and 옥호광 refers to the radiance of the Buddha’s white urna rather than a “jeweled urn.”
- **Replacement:** “May I have no other thought at the end of this life, with Amitabha alone beside me, my heart forever bound to the light from the white curl between his brows…”
- **Rationale:** The source invokes Amitabha’s sole presence at the end of life and the radiance of his 옥호, the auspicious white curl between the brows.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “갑자기 환자가 피를 토했다니. 안정된 것 아니었나?”
- **Current:** “I heard the patient suddenly started vomiting blood. Wasn’t he stable?”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the unidentified patient a male gender that the source does not supply.
- **Replacement:** “The patient suddenly started vomiting blood? Weren’t they stable?”
- **Rationale:** Korean omits the patient’s gender, so the genuine ambiguity should be retained.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그걸 알면 내가 지금 여기 있겠소? 심각한 내상을 입은 건 분명한데 도무지 무슨 증상인지…….”
- **Current:** “If I knew that, would I be standing here? His internal injuries are unquestionably severe, but I can’t figure out what’s causing these symptoms…”
- **Defect:** The draft again assigns the unidentified patient a male gender and weakens the source’s statement that the patient clearly suffered serious internal injuries.
- **Replacement:** “If I knew that, would I be here right now? The patient clearly suffered serious internal injuries, but I can’t make sense of these symptoms…”
- **Rationale:** No gender is stated, and 심각한 내상을 입은 건 분명한데 describes the confirmed severity of the patient’s internal injuries.

## F10 — Critical

- **Current:** Even the Three Fiends, who had vanished without a trace, had been captured by an unknown fiend.

## F11 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 말이 나왔으니 말인데, 문경이가 나이에 비해서 실력이 좋긴 하지만 조장을 맡기기에는 좀 그렇지 않습니까?

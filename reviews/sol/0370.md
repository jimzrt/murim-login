# Review

Several factual and attribution errors alter quantities, responsibility, subjects, and speaker identity; additional issues weaken a revealing register shift and established terminology.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 출가외인(出嫁外人)이 가문의 무공과 기밀을 누출할까 염려한 탓에 데릴사위를 들여 당씨 성을 잇게 했다.
- **Current:** Fearing that women who married out might leak the family’s martial arts and secrets, the clan took in male in-laws and had them carry on the Tang surname.
- **Defect:** “Male in-laws” omits the specific live-in son-in-law arrangement by which the clan retained its daughters and continued the Tang surname.
- **Replacement:** Fearing that daughters who married out might leak the clan’s martial arts and secrets, the clan instead took in live-in sons-in-law and had them carry on the Tang surname.
- **Rationale:** 데릴사위 specifically means a son-in-law brought into the wife’s household, not merely any male relative by marriage.

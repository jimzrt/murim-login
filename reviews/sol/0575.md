# Review

Several fidelity defects alter an actor, omit a named speaker, narrow unresolved culpability, and lose explicit kinship and quantity details; one System line also violates the required formatting.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어떻게든 살려서 배후의 진짜 정체를 알아냈어야 했다.
- **Current:** Somehow, I should have saved it and learned the true identity of the people behind it.
- **Defect:** The source leaves the number and identity of whoever was behind the Kraken unresolved, while “the people” explicitly establishes multiple perpetrators.
- **Replacement:** Somehow, I should have kept it alive and learned the true identity of whoever was behind it.
- **Rationale:** 배후 is number-neutral here, and the responsible party remains an unresolved mystery.

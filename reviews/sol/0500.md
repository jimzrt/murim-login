# Review

One reversed historical reaction, repeated flattening of an official title, one rank inflation, and several localized fidelity defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제갈풍이 탄생하던 날, 제갈세가가 기쁨에 들썩였겠지만 내가 태어나던 날에는 전 인류가 들썩였다.
- **Current:** The Zhuge Clan might have shaken with joy on the day Zhuge Feng was born, but on the day I was born, all of humanity had been thrown into turmoil.
- **Defect:** “Thrown into turmoil” gives humanity’s reaction a negative, chaotic valence. The source parallels the Zhuge Clan’s joyful excitement with all humanity being stirred by the Demon King’s defeat and the end of the Great Cataclysm.
- **Replacement:** The Zhuge Clan might have been abuzz with joy on the day Zhuge Feng was born, but on the day I was born, all of humanity was abuzz.
- **Rationale:** 들썩였다 describes widespread excitement or commotion here, not humanity being plunged into turmoil.

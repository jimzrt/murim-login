# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it narrows the narrator’s debt to one person and blurs a qi mechanism and two deliberate terms.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 기의 수발이 매우 자유로워집니다.
- **Current:** You can now control qi with great freedom.
- **Defect:** General control replaces the specific ability to draw in and release qi.
- **Replacement:** You can now draw in and release qi with great freedom.
- **Rationale:** 수발 names the receiving and sending of qi, not merely control over it.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘빚을 졌군. 그것도 엄청나게 많은 빚을.’
- **Current:** *I owe him. I owe him a hell of a lot.*
- **Defect:** “Him” makes Peng the sole creditor, though the source leaves the debt open and the narration immediately connects the rewards to all the dead.
- **Replacement:** *I’m in debt. Deep in debt.*
- **Rationale:** The Korean does not specify whom he owes.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 위대한 업적, [전신성형]을 달성했습니다!
- **Current:** You have earned the great achievement **Full-Body Makeover**!
- **Defect:** “Makeover” loses the achievement name’s plastic-surgery joke.
- **Replacement:** You have earned the great achievement **Full-Body Plastic Surgery**!
- **Rationale:** 전신성형 literally invokes full-body plastic surgery, a deliberately comic label for his physical transformation.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.85
- **Source:** 이내 무아(無我)의 세계로 빠져들던 내 귓가로 아스라이 울려 퍼지던 누군가의 한 마디를.
- **Current:** And then, as I slipped into a state of nothingness, a voice faintly echoed in my ear.
- **Defect:** “Nothingness” obscures the loss-of-self state described by 무아.
- **Replacement:** And then, as I slipped into a trance where I lost all sense of self, someone’s words echoed faintly in my ear.
- **Rationale:** 무아 refers here to being without awareness of oneself, not to an absence of everything.

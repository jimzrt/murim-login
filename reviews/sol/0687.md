# Review

The draft contains one plot-consequence error and several meaningful fidelity and register issues, including softened complicity, altered hierarchy, and weakened mockery.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 비록 백상의 뜻에 동조했다고는 하나, 어디까지나 묵인을 앞세운 간접적인 동조자에 불과했으니까.
- **Current:** Though she had sympathized with Baeksang’s intentions, she had been nothing more than an indirect accomplice who had chosen tacit consent.
- **Defect:** “Sympathized with” softens Yohi’s deliberate alignment with Baeksang, while “chosen tacit consent” is an unnatural rendering of her indirect participation through acquiescence.
- **Replacement:** Although she had sided with Baeksang, she had only been an indirect accomplice whose involvement amounted to tacit acquiescence.
- **Rationale:** 동조 means siding with or supporting Baeksang, not merely sympathizing with his intentions; 묵인을 앞세운 describes tacit acquiescence as the form her complicity took.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “멍청한 놈들. 마후께서 그토록 주의하라 하셨거늘.”
- **Current:** “Fools. The Demon Empress warned them to be careful so many times.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents repeated warnings. 그토록 intensifies the degree of caution urged, not the number of times the warning was given.
- **Replacement:** “Fools. The Demon Empress told them to be so careful.”
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes how strongly they were cautioned, without specifying repeated warnings.

# Review

Two critical subject/object errors, plus actionable losses in humor, hierarchy, characterization, action timing, and pragmatic detail.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아니면 살살 아려 오는 배를 무시하고 호법을 서다 깜빡 졸았던 것?
- **Current:** Or when he had ignored his mildly aching stomach, stood guard, and dozed off?
- **Defect:** The coordination makes standing guard and dozing off separate sequential actions, while the source says he accidentally fell asleep while on guard duty.
- **Replacement:** Or when he had ignored his mildly aching stomach and accidentally dozed off while standing guard?
- **Rationale:** The construction 호법을 서다 깜빡 졸았던 것 directly links the lapse to his ongoing guard duty.

# Review

Three actionable fidelity issues: a reversed force-allocation subject, a missed reaction to the promised news, and an omitted restraint.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 놈들에게 이동진(移動陳)이 있는 이상, 청해성 한 곳에 완전한 총력을 기울이는 건 불가능에 가깝다.
- **Current:** *As long as they have the Moving Formation, it’s almost impossible to commit all their forces to Qinghai.*
- **Defect:** “Their forces” points to Dark Heaven, but Taekyung is explaining why his own side cannot concentrate its entire strength in Qinghai.
- **Replacement:** *As long as they have the Moving Formation, it’s almost impossible for us to commit all our forces to Qinghai.*
- **Rationale:** Dark Heaven’s ability to move elsewhere is the reason Taekyung’s allies cannot leave other places undefended.

# Review

Several scene facts and mechanisms are altered, including the price paid for two deaths, the Emei elder’s gender, the direction and outcome of Cheongpung’s techniques, Taekyung entering the cells, and an added identity confirmation at the chapter’s end. There are also localized quantity, characterization, humor, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 깊게 가라앉은 눈과 철탑처럼 선 그들에게서 흘러나온 숨 막히는 기세가 동공을 잠식하고 있었다.
- **Current:** The suffocating aura flowing from their sunken eyes and rigid bodies, standing straight as iron towers, was swallowing up the cavern.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the aura flow from the men’s eyes and bodies, while the source describes the men as having deep-set eyes and standing like iron towers, with the aura emanating from them as people.
- **Replacement:** The suffocating aura emanating from the men—their eyes deep-set, their bodies upright as iron towers—was swallowing the cavern.
- **Rationale:** The modifiers 깊게 가라앉은 눈 and 철탑처럼 선 describe the black-robed men; 그들에게서 identifies the men themselves as the source of the aura.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “두 사람의 목숨값으로 치렀다.”
- **Current:** “I paid for it with two lives.”
- **Defect:** The causal exchange is reversed. The draft says two lives were the payment for his arm, whereas the source says his arm was the price he paid to take two lives.
- **Replacement:** “It was the price I paid to take two lives.”
- **Rationale:** The empty sleeve is what he 치렀다 as 두 사람의 목숨값: he lost the arm in exchange for killing the two people.

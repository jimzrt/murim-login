# Review

One line reverses the intended agreement about avoiding Michael, while several other passages omit urgency, alter Jin’s denial, misread a clipped form of address, or need localized terminology correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이 자리에 그러고 싶은 사람 아무도 없어.”
- **Current:** “No one here wants to do that.”
- **Defect:** The pronoun appears to refer to avoiding Michael, which reverses Jin’s meaning: he is agreeing that nobody present wants to run into Michael.
- **Replacement:** “No one here wants to run into him.”
- **Rationale:** The Skeleton King says he wants to avoid encountering Michael, and Jin confirms that nobody present wants to encounter him; the following “그거야 그렇지만” also treats Jin’s statement as true.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘지금이라도 멈춰야 한다. 어떻게든.’
- **Current:** *I have to stop him. Somehow.*
- **Defect:** The translation omits 지금이라도, losing the thought’s explicit urgency and sense that Michael must be stopped now, while there is still time.
- **Replacement:** *I have to stop him now, while there’s still time. Somehow.*
- **Rationale:** 지금이라도 means acting now or at least now before the opportunity is lost, not merely stopping him at some unspecified point.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아프도록 눈을 파고드는 저 다섯 글자.
- **Current:** Those five letters that stabbed painfully into my eyes.
- **Defect:** 글자 refers to written characters, not alphabetic letters; “Main Quest” does not contain five English letters.
- **Replacement:** Those five characters that stabbed painfully into my eyes.
- **Rationale:** 메인 퀘스트 consists of five Korean written characters, so “characters” preserves the stated quantity without mistranslating 글자 as English letters.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아니, 내심 짐작하면서도 애써 그것만은 아닐 것이라 부정하고 있다.
- **Current:** No. I had a vague idea deep down, but I was desperately denying that it could mean only that.
- **Defect:** “Only that” introduces an exclusivity claim absent from the source. Jin is denying that the feared possibility he suspects is the answer, not arguing that there may be additional meanings.
- **Replacement:** No. Deep down, I had an inkling, but I was desperately insisting it couldn’t be that.
- **Rationale:** 그것만은 아닐 것 expresses an emotionally charged refusal to accept that particular dreaded possibility.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “……간. 인간?”
- **Current:** “...Jin. Human?”
- **Defect:** The draft invents Jin’s name. 간 is the clipped ending of 인간 as the distant voice comes into focus, not 진.
- **Replacement:** “…man. Human?”
- **Rationale:** Rendering the first fragment as the tail end of “human” preserves both the interrupted perception and the Skeleton King’s established form of address.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “출발하죠. 더 늦기 전에.”
- **Current:** “Let’s go. Before we’re any later.”
- **Defect:** “Before we’re any later” is not natural English and obscures the urgency of departing before more time is lost.
- **Replacement:** “Let’s go. Before we lose any more time.”
- **Rationale:** 더 늦기 전에 naturally means leaving before further delay, and the replacement preserves the brisk spoken register.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대한민국에서는 흔히들 남아공이라 부르는 이 나라는
- **Current:** Commonly called Namgong in Korea, this country
- **Defect:** “Namgong” drops the middle syllable of 남아공 and presents the Korean abbreviation as though it were an unexplained proper name.
- **Replacement:** Commonly known in Korea by the abbreviation Nam-agong, this country
- **Rationale:** 남아공 is the Korean abbreviation for South Africa and is syllabically rendered Nam-agong.

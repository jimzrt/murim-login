# Review

Two meaningful subject/register defects, several localized fidelity issues, and terminology/natural-English corrections.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 목적지가 가까워질수록 몇 배로 힘들어지는 것은 인간의 본능이고,
- **Current:** The closer one gets to one's destination, the more difficult things become. It was human instinct.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicit several-fold increase expressed by 몇 배로 and turns the single causal observation into an unnatural fragment.
- **Replacement:** It was human nature for things to feel several times harder the closer one got to one's destination.
- **Rationale:** 몇 배로 means several times or many times harder, not merely more difficult.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 퉁퉁 불어 있던 몸뚱어리에 달라붙어 있던 수초(水草)가 강물과 함께 쏟아져 내린다.
- **Current:** The river poured out along with the water plants clinging to my bloated body.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the river itself pour out of him; the source describes river water and the plants cascading off his swollen body.
- **Replacement:** River water and the plants clinging to my bloated body came pouring off me.
- **Rationale:** 강물과 함께 modifies the plants falling away with the river water; it does not mean the river itself pours out.

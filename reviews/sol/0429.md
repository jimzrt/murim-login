# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one critical location distinction error, several meaningful register and humor losses, and a few altered mechanical or quantitative details.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 「진 상! 진 상!」

네가 진상이다, 인마.
- **Current:** “Mr. Jin! Mr. Jin!”

*You’re the nuisance here, asshole.*
- **Defect:** The translation removes the wordplay between the Japanese address “Jin-san” and the Korean word jinsang, meaning an obnoxious nuisance.
- **Replacement:** “Jin-san! Jin-san!”[^1]

*You’re the real jinsang here, asshole.*

[^1]: The Japanese address “Jin-san” sounds like the Korean word *jinsang*, meaning an obnoxious nuisance.
- **Rationale:** The narration directly answers the reporter’s repeated address with a bilingual pun, so translating both expressions generically erases the joke.

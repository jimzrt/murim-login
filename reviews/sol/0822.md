# Review

Several action and meaning reversals, plus a few losses of hierarchy and imagery.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이, 이건 말도 안…….”

“돼.”
- **Current:** “T-This makes no—”

“—sense.”
- **Defect:** The Skeleton King agrees that it makes no sense instead of contradicting the man.
- **Replacement:** “Th-That’s impossible—”

“—It’s possible.”
- **Rationale:** 돼 completes 말도 안 돼 by rejecting its negation: the Skeleton King says the impossible is possible.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 위대하신 그분께서 친히 선지자를 보내어 당신의 전사들을 약속의 땅으로 인도하셨단 말이다!
- **Current:** His greatness sent the Prophet himself to lead His warriors to the promised land!
- **Defect:** The draft makes “His greatness” the sender and places “himself” on the Prophet rather than on God.
- **Replacement:** The great God Himself sent the Prophet to lead His warriors to the promised land!
- **Rationale:** 그분께서 친히 makes God the one who personally sent the Prophet.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 세상은 자신과 같은 몬스터를 괴물이라 부르지만, 진정한 괴물은 따로 있었다.
- **Current:** The world called monsters like him beasts, but the real monster was someone else.
- **Defect:** “Beasts” loses the deliberate repetition of “monster.”
- **Replacement:** The world called monsters like him monsters, but the real monster was someone else.
- **Rationale:** 괴물 occurs on both sides of the contrast.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 가족이 있고, 친구가 있다. 사랑하는 연인이, 지켜야 할 가치가 있었다.
- **Current:** They had families and friends. Beloved partners. Things they had to protect.
- **Defect:** “Things” drops the explicit reference to values.
- **Replacement:** They had families and friends. People they loved. Values worth protecting.
- **Rationale:** 지켜야 할 가치 refers to values, not unspecified things.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 선택받은 자에게만 허락된 휘황한 빛을 뿜어 내는 [영웅의 검]을 든 채, 자신을 응시하는 악마를 향해 이렇게 묻고야 말았다.
- **Current:** Holding the [Hero’s Sword], which shone with a brilliant light permitted only to the chosen, the man looked at the demon before him and found himself asking:
- **Defect:** The draft makes the man hold the sword; the Skeleton King holds it and stares at him.
- **Replacement:** The Skeleton King held the [Hero’s Sword], shining with a brilliant light reserved for the chosen, and stared at him. The man found himself asking the demon:
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence identifies the Skeleton King as the sword’s holder, and 자신을 응시하는 describes the demon staring at the man.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그저 믿고 싶은 대로 보고, 보고 싶은 대로 믿었겠지.
- **Current:** You saw what you wanted to see, and believed what you wanted to believe.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the crossed relationship between belief and sight with two ordinary repetitions.
- **Replacement:** You saw what you wanted to believe, and believed what you wanted to see.
- **Rationale:** The Korean first links seeing to what they wanted to believe, then belief to what they wanted to see.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 공간을 가르며 쏘아지던 화살과 단검이 왔던 곳으로 되돌아가 주인의 목숨을 앗았다.
- **Current:** Arrows and daggers that had shot through the air turned around and returned from where they had come, taking their owners’ lives.
- **Defect:** “Returned from where they had come” reverses the projectiles’ destination.
- **Replacement:** Arrows and daggers that had shot through the air turned around and flew back to where they had come from, taking their owners’ lives.
- **Rationale:** 왔던 곳으로 되돌아가 says they went back to their point of origin.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 푹. 털썩.

한 줄기 열풍(熱風).
- **Current:** *Thump. Thud.*

A single blast of hot wind.
- **Defect:** The first sound becomes another dull impact, losing the piercing strike before the man falls.
- **Replacement:** *Thwip. Thud.*

A single blast of hot wind.
- **Rationale:** 푹 marks the penetrating hit; 털썩 is the subsequent fall.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 아직 날 서 있던 수많은 병장기가 무릎이 피에 젖은 모래알 위를 덮었다.
- **Current:** Countless weapons, still sharp, covered the blood-soaked grains of sand.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the knees coming down onto the sand.
- **Replacement:** Countless still-sharp weapons and knees covered the blood-soaked sand.
- **Rationale:** The source names both 병장기 and 무릎 in the image that follows Jin’s victory.

# Review

One action is reversed, Muninn’s succession is blurred, and several smaller details need correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그날 밤, 고물 캡슐은 겁도 없이 자신의 안으로 기어들어 와 잠을 청한 멍청이를 새로운 세상으로 내던졌고
- **Current:** That night, the beat-up capsule had fearlessly crawled inside itself, then tossed the fool who’d settled down to sleep into a new world.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the capsule crawl inside itself. Jin is the one who climbed into it to sleep.
- **Replacement:** That night, the beat-up capsule had tossed the fool who’d been reckless enough to crawl inside it for a nap into a new world.
- **Rationale:** The Korean describes the fool entering the capsule; the capsule throws him into a new world.

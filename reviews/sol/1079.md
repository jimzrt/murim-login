# Review

The draft preserves the main events, but omits a lethal-acupoint warning and mistranslates a count in the interrogation scene.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그럴 리가. 여기는 사혈(死血)인데?”
- **Current:** “Huh? Wasn’t it two vertebrae above?”

“How confusing. Shouldn’t you go ask him again, Captain?”
- **Defect:** Taekyung’s reply is omitted. It identifies the proposed spot as a lethal acupoint, making their apparent uncertainty more threatening.
- **Replacement:** “Huh? Wasn’t it two vertebrae above?”

“No way. That’s a lethal acupoint.”

“I’m confused. Shouldn’t you go ask him again, Captain?”
- **Rationale:** 여기는 사혈인데 explicitly objects that the spot is a lethal acupoint.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “세, 세 마디.”
- **Current:** “Th-three things.”
- **Defect:** The captive is supplying the number of vertebrae, not introducing three things.
- **Replacement:** “Th-three vertebrae.”
- **Rationale:** 세 마디 answers their dispute over how many vertebrae below the cervical vertebrae to stab.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “풀렸으면 다시 조여야지.”
- **Current:** “If they’re glazed over, we’ll just have to sharpen them up again.”
- **Defect:** “Sharpen” loses the loose-versus-tight wordplay that sets up the slap.
- **Replacement:** “If they’ve gone slack, we’ll just have to tighten them up again.”
- **Rationale:** 풀렸으면 and 조여야지 contrast something coming loose with tightening it again.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “천재까지는 아니고. 수재 정도.”
- **Current:** “Not quite. Maybe a prodigy.”
- **Defect:** “Prodigy” does not clearly step down from “genius,” weakening Taekyung’s deliberately modest distinction.
- **Replacement:** “Not a genius. Just exceptionally gifted.”
- **Rationale:** 수재 is presented here as a lesser claim than 천재.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 오직 공포로 가득 차 있는 그 눈빛과 음성을 본 순간
- **Current:** One look at his voice and those eyes, filled with nothing but terror, told me he had been reborn as a far more honest man.
- **Defect:** A voice cannot be looked at; the source describes both his gaze and voice as full of fear.
- **Replacement:** The terror in his eyes and voice told me he had been reborn as a far more honest man.
- **Rationale:** 눈빛과 음성 are separate perceived signs of his fear.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 알아봤자 청 소협은 달라지지 않을 거야.
- **Current:** Even if I explained it, Young Master Cheongpung wouldn’t change.
- **Defect:** The draft renders 소협 as “Young Master” rather than the specified martial address “Young Hero.”
- **Replacement:** Even if I explained it, Young Hero Cheong wouldn’t change.
- **Rationale:** The packet’s exact glossary maps 소협 to Young Hero; 청 is the surname used with that address.

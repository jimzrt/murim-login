# Review

The draft changes who moves in the final attack and makes a severing cut sound superficial. It also obscures Mujin’s promise to Taekyung.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 물론 그중에서도 일부만을 사용했으나, 만년한철이 무려 넉 냥이나 들어갔으니 그 강도와 예리함은 과히 명검이라 부르기에 손색이 없을 정도.
- **Current:** They’d used only a portion of it, of course, but it had contained a full four nyang of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron. Its strength and sharpness were more than worthy of being called a famous sword.
- **Defect:** “It” makes the four nyang a property of the original chain rather than the sword made from it.
- **Replacement:** Only part of the chain had gone into the sword, of course, but the blade contained a full four nyang of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron. Its strength and sharpness more than earned it the name of a famous sword.
- **Rationale:** The source says that despite using only part of the chain, the resulting sword contains four nyang of the material.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 당연히 이번에도 우리가 이기겠지만, 예. 반드시 그러겠습니다.
- **Current:** —Of course we’ll win this time, too. Yes. I’ll make sure of it.
- **Defect:** “I’ll make sure of it” can mean Mujin promises to ensure victory; he is agreeing to Taekyung’s request to die where Taekyung can see him if things go wrong.
- **Replacement:** —Of course we’ll win this time, too. But yes. If it comes to that, I promise I will.
- **Rationale:** The following narration explicitly treats this answer as a promise to Taekyung about Mujin’s death.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “어……서.”
- **Current:** “Go… on.”
- **Defect:** Mujin urges Taishan to act quickly, rather than telling him to proceed.
- **Replacement:** “Hur…ry.”
- **Rationale:** 어서 is an urgent prompt here, immediately before Taishan throws Mujin toward the sorcerer.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그와 동시에, 어느덧 허공을 가로질러 코앞까지 성큼 다가온 흑의인의 부릅뜬 눈동자를 보았다.
- **Current:** And at the same time, he saw the wide eyes of the black-robed man, now closing in through the air, right in front of him.
- **Defect:** The draft has the black-robed man closing in through the air; Mujin is the one flying toward him.
- **Replacement:** And as he flew through the air, he saw the black-robed man’s wide eyes suddenly right in front of him.
- **Rationale:** Taishan has just hurled Mujin toward the sorcerer standing by the gate.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마지막 힘이 실린 섬광이, 강시술사의 목을 스치듯 가로질렀다.
- **Current:** A flash, carrying his last remaining strength, grazed the jiangshi sorcerer’s neck.
- **Defect:** “Grazed” suggests a superficial wound, but the strike cuts through the sorcerer’s neck and severs his head.
- **Replacement:** A flash carrying the last of his strength swept across the jiangshi sorcerer’s neck.
- **Rationale:** 스치듯 describes the fleeting motion of the cut; the next scene confirms that the head falls.

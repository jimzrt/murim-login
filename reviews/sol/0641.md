# Review

The draft alters a killing idiom into a specific method, softens deliberate profanity, turns an epitaph into an instruction, and mislabels a pointed statement as a question.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “이런 쳐 죽일 놈들을 봤나!”

나는 분노로 눈가가 붉게 달아오른 야수묘왕을 진정시켰다.

“그, 말씀 중에 죄송한데, 이미 쳐 죽이셨습니다.”
- **Current:** “Would you look at these bastards! They ought to be beaten to death!”

I calmed the Beast Miao King, whose eyes had reddened with fury.

“Uh, sorry to interrupt, but you already beat them to death.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns the intensive idiom 쳐 죽이다 into the specific physical action of beating them to death, asserting a method absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** “Would you look at these goddamn bastards! They deserve to die!”

I calmed the Beast Miao King, whose eyes had reddened with fury.

“Uh, sorry to interrupt, but you already killed them.”
- **Rationale:** The source repeats 죽이다 to set up the deadpan reminder that the spiders are already dead; 쳐 intensifies the killing rather than establishing that they were beaten.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘이곳에 잠들다.’

‘이곳에 잠들. 예?’
- **Current:** *Rest here.*

“Rest here. Huh?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes an epitaph meaning “lies/sleeps here” into an imperative telling Jang Sam to rest.
- **Replacement:** *Lies here.*

“Lies here. Huh?”
- **Rationale:** Together with the previously dictated name and the wooden tablet, 이곳에 잠들다 forms a mock epitaph, which drives Jang Sam’s delayed alarm.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “거미 새끼들. 오랫동안 많이도 해 처먹었네.”
- **Current:** “Those damn spiders. They’ve been eating their fill for a long, long time.”
- **Defect:** The draft substantially softens both 새끼들 and the deliberately vulgar 처먹다.
- **Replacement:** “Spider bastards. They’ve sure been stuffing their faces for a long time.”
- **Rationale:** The source uses two distinct vulgar expressions to convey Jin Taekyung’s disgust; the replacement preserves that coarse register without intensifying it.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다분히 의도가 묻어나는 내 말에 잠시 침묵하던 야수묘왕이 불쑥 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** The Beast Miao King fell silent for a moment at my clearly loaded question, then suddenly spoke.
- **Defect:** The narration calls Jin Taekyung’s preceding declarative statement a question.
- **Replacement:** The Beast Miao King fell silent for a moment at my clearly pointed words, then abruptly spoke.
- **Rationale:** The source says 내 말, “my words,” and characterizes their deliberate implication; it does not identify them as a question.

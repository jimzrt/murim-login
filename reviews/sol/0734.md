# Review

The draft contains one unsupported subject/identity shift, one altered physical action, and several meaningful losses of institutional specificity and profane register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 우리가 공성(攻城)으로 아레스 길드를 차지했듯이, 저들도 마찬가지다. 이제는 주위에 바글대는 경쟁자들로부터 성을 지켜 내고, 최대한 영토를 넓혀야 하는 것이 성주의 의무였다.
- **Current:** Just as we had taken Ares Guild through a siege, they would do the same. Now that I was the City Lord, my duty was to defend my castle from the competitors swarming around it and expand my territory as much as possible.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the comparison into a future action and explicitly identifies Jin as the City Lord, although the source makes a general statement about the duty of whoever holds the castle.
- **Replacement:** Just as we had taken Ares Guild by siege, the same was true of them. Now it was a lord’s duty to defend his castle from the competitors swarming around it and expand his territory as much as possible.
- **Rationale:** 저들도 마찬가지다 means the same already applies to them, while 성주의 의무 describes the holder’s general duty and does not establish that Jin personally bears the City Lord title.

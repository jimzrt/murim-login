# Review

Several subject and action errors affect the dialogue, alongside losses in the name joke, medical wording, and final cannon count.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 단지 너무 놀라셨을 뿐이에요. 잘 주무시다가 불현듯 잠에서 깨어나셨는데, 눈을 뜨자마자 제 얼굴을 보셨거든요.
- **Current:** You just startled me. You were sleeping soundly, then suddenly woke up. The first thing you saw when you opened your eyes was my face.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jin the sleeper who wakes and sees Sudal. Sudal is explaining why his father punched him.
- **Replacement:** My father was just startled. He’d been sleeping soundly, then suddenly woke up and saw my face the moment he opened his eyes.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of the honorific verbs is Sudal’s father, mentioned immediately before this explanation.

## F02 — Critical

- **Source:** 아, 꼭 이 정도로 시끄럽게 소리 질러야 하나? 가뜩이나 조장님 힘드신데. 주화입마라도 걸리면 어쩌려고 저러는 거지?

- **Defect:** Hwaran is objecting to Sudal’s shouting, not asking whether she herself must shout.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “잘들 노네요.”

생각을 끝마친 내가 피식 웃으며 내뱉은 한 마디에, 남호의 눈이 크게 뜨였다. 그리고 이내 그 역시 쓴웃음을 머금었다.
- **Current:** When I finished thinking and let out a quiet laugh, Namho’s eyes widened. Then he, too, gave a bitter smile.
- **Defect:** The narration omits that Jin speaks the preceding line, leaving Namho’s reaction attributed only to a laugh.
- **Replacement:** I finished thinking, gave a quiet laugh, and said it aloud. Namho’s eyes widened at my words. Then he, too, gave a bitter smile.
- **Rationale:** 내뱉은 한 마디 explicitly identifies Jin’s spoken remark as what prompts Namho’s reaction.

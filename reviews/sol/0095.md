# Review

The draft contains several substantive subject and ownership errors, prematurely assigns the kitten a sex the characters have not established, adds content to the System label, and makes multiple euphemisms more sexually explicit than the source.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “응. 여름에 태어났으니까 한여름. 이름 예쁘지?”

“한여름은 무슨. 덩치 보니까 3개월은 되어 보이는데 늦봄에 태어났으니까 늦봄이라고 하든가.”

“……그게 말이야, 방구야? 아무튼 얘 이름은 오늘부터 여름이야. 그치, 여름아?”
- **Current:** “Yeah. She was born in summer, so I named her Midsummer. Pretty, right?”

“What do you mean, Midsummer? Judging by her size, she looks about three months old. She was born in late spring, so call her Late Spring or something.”

“…Is that supposed to be a joke or what? Anyway, her name is Yeoreum from today onward. Right, Yeoreum?”
- **Defect:** The translation repeatedly identifies the kitten as female before either sibling has checked its sex, undermining the uncertainty that drives the following scene.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. Born in summer, so Midsummer. Pretty, right?”

“What do you mean, Midsummer? Judging by the size, the kitten looks about three months old. That would mean it was born in late spring, so call it Late Spring or something.”

“…Is that supposed to be a joke or what? Anyway, this kitten’s name is Yeoreum from today onward. Right, Yeoreum?”
- **Rationale:** Korean permits the passage to remain gender-neutral, and the characters immediately establish that they do not yet know the kitten’s sex.

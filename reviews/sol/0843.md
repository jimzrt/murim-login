# Review

The draft has a premature viewpoint shift and several localized changes to the source’s imagery, causal reasoning, and final clue.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진태경이 고개를 끄덕였다.

처음에는 주위를 둘러싼 모든 것이 마냥 낯설고 다르게 느껴졌지만, 이제는 아니다.
- **Current:** At first, everything around me had felt unfamiliar and different. But not anymore.
- **Defect:** Switches to first-person narration before the source’s scene break and viewpoint shift.
- **Replacement:** At first, everything around him had felt unfamiliar and different. But not anymore.
- **Rationale:** This passage remains in third person; Jin’s first-person narration begins after the scene break.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters several subjects and quantities, weakens two recurring jokes, changes a literal downward gaze into protective watching, and introduces unsupported gendered pronouns for the Lord of Heaven.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “결국, 다시 한번 중원으로 향하게 되었군요.”
- **Current:** “Ultimately, you’re heading to the Central Plains once more.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes only the Beast Miao King the subject, while the unmarked Korean refers to their collective decision to head to the Central Plains.
- **Replacement:** “In the end, we’re heading to the Central Plains once again.”
- **Rationale:** The lack of an honorific subject marker and the surrounding discussion of the Nanman Beast Palace’s collective deployment support an inclusive “we,” not “you.”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “술맛 한번 좋군. 그렇지 않으냐?”
- **Current:** “What excellent wine. Don’t you agree?”
- **Defect:** The draft omits the claim that the wine tastes good, weakening the immediate joke that follows when everyone is puzzled because he has not tasted a drop.
- **Replacement:** “This wine tastes excellent. Don’t you agree?”
- **Rationale:** 술맛 explicitly means the taste of the wine, and that detail supplies the causal setup for the onlookers’ reaction.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그들을 굽어보고 있던 새하얀 백호가 포효를 내질렀다.
- **Current:** a snow-white White Tiger watching over them from somewhere atop a high hill let out a roar.
- **Defect:** “Watching over” adds a protective or guardian implication absent from the physical action of looking down from above.
- **Replacement:** a snow-white White Tiger gazing down at them from somewhere atop a high hill let out a roar.
- **Rationale:** 굽어보다 describes looking down from an elevated position; it does not itself mean guarding or protecting.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 수천, 수만에 달하는 짐승과 인간이 힘껏 발을 구르고 함성을 내질렀다.
- **Current:** Thousands upon thousands of beasts and humans stamped their feet and raised their voices.
- **Defect:** The draft compresses the explicit scale of “thousands, tens of thousands” into the less precise “thousands upon thousands.”
- **Replacement:** Thousands—tens of thousands—of beasts and humans stamped their feet and roared.
- **Rationale:** 수천, 수만에 달하는 explicitly escalates the quantity from thousands to tens of thousands and should be retained.

## F05 — Critical

- **Rationale:** 사람이, 사람을 is deliberately aphoristic and singular on both sides; Hyuk Mujin is repeating that formulation for comic effect.

## F07 — Major

## F08 — Critical

## F09 — Critical

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “이야. 이름부터가 범상치 않은 처자일세. 딱 봐도 양갓집 규수 같은…….”
- **Current:** “Wow. That young lady has quite an extraordinary name. At first glance, she looks like the daughter of a respectable family…”
- **Defect:** “At first glance” and “looks like” falsely imply that Hyuk Mujin can see the woman; he is jokingly inferring her background solely from the name.
- **Replacement:** “Wow. That’s quite an extraordinary name for a young lady. She sounds like the daughter of a respectable family…”
- **Rationale:** 이름부터 makes the name the basis of his mistaken inference, while 딱 봐도 is figurative here rather than a literal visual observation.

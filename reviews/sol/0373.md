# Review

Two subject reversals change who apologizes and who took Mungyeong out, while several lines weaken register, characterization, or physical detail.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다음부터는 송구스러워할 일을 만들지 말게. 알았나?
- **Current:** “Don’t give us anything to apologize for next time. Understood?”
- **Defect:** The apology subject is reversed. Jeok Cheongang tells Cheongpung the Ancient Sword not to do anything that will leave Cheongpung feeling apologetic; he does not say that Cheongpung should avoid making Jeok Cheongang and the Slaughter Saint apologize.
- **Replacement:** “Next time, don’t do anything you’ll need to apologize for. Understood?”
- **Rationale:** 송구스러워할 describes the addressee feeling apologetic, whereas “give us anything to apologize for” assigns that feeling to the two Seniors.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 애가 울적해 보이니까 바람 좀 쐬게 해 준 거 아냐. 넌 왜 그렇게 멍청하냐?
- **Current:** “The kid looked down, so I took him out for some fresh air. Why are you so stupid?”
- **Defect:** The draft makes the beggar claim that he took Mungyeong out, contradicting Hyuk Mujin’s immediately preceding question to the Captain. The beggar is explaining that Taekyung took Mungyeong out because the boy looked gloomy.
- **Replacement:** “Isn’t it obvious he took the kid out for some fresh air because he looked down? Why are you so stupid?”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject is established by the exchange: Hyuk Mujin asks Taekyung where he went with Mungyeong, and the beggar answers that question before insulting Mujin.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “그래, 알았다. 문경아.”

“……!”

그 순간, 앞서나가던 한 사람의 신형이 비틀거렸다.
- **Current:** “All right, Mungyeong.”

“…”

At that moment, the figure of one person walking ahead of us stumbled.
- **Defect:** The draft weakens Taekyung’s deliberately overfamiliar banmal response and removes the exclamation from Mungyeong’s shocked silence, blunting the joke that causes him to stumble. The final narration is also unnaturally phrased.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah, got it, Mungyeong.”

“…!”

At that moment, the figure walking ahead of us stumbled.
- **Rationale:** 그래, 알았다 is bluntly casual, 문경아 is an intimate downward form of address, and ……! explicitly marks Mungyeong’s startled reaction.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 정파는 물론이고 사마외도(邪魔外道)에서도 그들은 환영받지 못한다.
- **Current:** They weren’t welcome among the orthodox factions, nor among the demonic and heterodox arts.
- **Defect:** The sentence treats martial arts themselves as social groups capable of welcoming assassins, producing both a category error and unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** They weren’t welcome among the orthodox factions, or even among practitioners of the demonic, heterodox arts.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts the orthodox side with the people or forces belonging to 사마외도; practitioners can reject assassins, while abstract arts cannot.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 떨떠름한 표정의 청풍고검을 향해 살성이 말을 이었다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung the Ancient Sword still looked somewhat sour when the Slaughter Saint continued.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 일결 제자 때 구걸한 돈으로 그거 빌려 보다가 왕초한테 먼지 나게 얻어맞았는데.
- **Current:** When I was a one-knot disciple, I borrowed one of those with money I’d begged for and got beaten half to death by the gang boss.
- **Defect:** “Beaten half to death” introduces a near-fatal degree of injury absent from the idiom 먼지 나게 얻어맞다, which means being thoroughly beaten. “Borrowed one of those with money” is also an awkward rendering of paying to rent a book.
- **Replacement:** When I was a one-knot Disciple, I used the money I’d begged for to rent one of those and got beaten black and blue by the gang boss.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the severe beating without adding a claim that it nearly killed him and clarifies how the begged money was used.

# Review

One critical subject reversal, two meaningful omissions, and several localized voice and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “애가 울적해 보이니까 바람 좀 쐬게 해 준 거 아냐. 넌 왜 그렇게 멍청하냐?”
- **Current:** “The kid looked depressed, so I took him out for some fresh air. Why are you such an idiot?”
- **Defect:** The draft makes the beggar claim that he took Mungyeong out, reversing the omitted but contextually clear subject: Jin Taekyung was the one who went with Mungyeong.
- **Replacement:** “The kid looked depressed, so Captain must’ve taken him out for some fresh air. Why are you such an idiot?”
- **Rationale:** The speaker is answering Hyuk Mujin’s question about where the Captain went with Mungyeong, not describing his own actions.

# Review

Several semantic and register issues require correction, including an ambiguous action, misidentified locals, an altered quantity, and unwarranted uncertainty about the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그건 곤란하지.”
- **Current:** “That would be difficult.”
- **Defect:** The pronoun is ambiguous in English and can appear to reject going inside, whereas the following explanation establishes that Taekyung is rejecting the alternative of fighting the locals.
- **Replacement:** “Fighting them is out of the question.”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung immediately explains that they cannot make all the natives of Nanman their enemies, fixing the referent of 그건 as fighting them.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 살벌한 기세를 뿜어내는 이민족 무리를 힐끗 바라본 뒤 독화루의 문을 열었다.
- **Current:** I glanced at the group of foreigners radiating a murderous aura, then opened the door to the Poison Flower Pavilion.
- **Defect:** Calling the group “foreigners” reverses their local status and blurs the ethnic distinction: they are the non-Han locals, while Taekyung's party are the outsiders.
- **Replacement:** I glanced at the group of non-Han locals radiating a murderous aura, then opened the door to the Poison Flower Pavilion.
- **Rationale:** 이민족 identifies the local ethnic peoples from the Han narrator's perspective, not foreigners within Yeongin.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나중에 확인해 보니 그날 밤에만 자그마치 이백여 명이 죽었더군.
- **Current:** But when we checked later, we found that more than two hundred people had died that very night.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an approximate quantity into a definite lower bound.
- **Replacement:** But when we checked later, we found that some two hundred people had died that very night.
- **Rationale:** 이백여 명 means approximately or around two hundred people, not necessarily more than two hundred.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘남천마후라면 대단한 수준의 역용술(逆用術)을 익혔어도 전혀 이상하지 않지.’
- **Current:** *If she really was the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, it wouldn’t be strange for her to have mastered a disguise technique of an extraordinary level.*
- **Defect:** “If she really was” introduces doubt about Honglan's established identity as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, which the source does not express here.
- **Replacement:** *It wouldn’t be strange at all for the Southern Heaven Demon Empress to have mastered an extraordinary disguise technique.*
- **Rationale:** 남천마후라면 characterizes what someone of her identity and ability could plausibly do; it does not reopen the question of whether she is the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘천면호리가 알려 준 대로라면 여기가 맞긴 할 텐데.’
- **Current:** *If the Thousand-Faced Fox told me the truth, this should be the place.*
- **Defect:** The draft adds suspicion that the Thousand-Faced Fox may have lied, rather than uncertainty about whether this dilapidated building matches the information he provided.
- **Replacement:** *If the Thousand-Faced Fox’s directions were right, this should be the place.*
- **Rationale:** 알려 준 대로라면 means “if it is as he told me,” without specifically questioning his truthfulness.

## F06 — Minor

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “무식할 만큼 기운찬 놈이 하나 있었군. 그럼 닭고기를 준비해 주지.”
- **Current:** “There was one fellow here with enough energy to be stupid. Then I’ll prepare chicken.”
- **Defect:** “Enough energy to be stupid” is an unnatural causal reading and weakens the joke about Taishan being absurdly energetic despite everyone else's exhaustion.

- **Rationale:** 무식할 만큼 modifies the degree of his vigor—so energetic that it is ridiculous—not stupidity caused by having energy.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 한 오십 년 전쯤 용산에서 태어났다면 건물주가 되었을 양반이다.
- **Current:** If he’d been born in Yongsan some fifty years ago, he would have become a landlord.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 노인은 불그스름하게 달아오른 눈빛으로 그날의 기억을 천천히 회상했다.
- **Current:** The old man’s reddish eyes slowly recalled what had happened that day.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “이미 늙을 대로 늙은 은영각 요원도 마찬가지지.”
- **Current:** “The Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent, who is already old beyond old age.”
- **Defect:** “Old beyond old age” is unnatural English and blunts the old man's dry self-deprecating reveal.

# Review

Five actionable fidelity findings, including an incorrect royal identity and a lost realm distinction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “황제 폐하 만세! 황태제 전하 천세!”
- **Current:** “Long live His Majesty the Emperor! May His Highness the Crown Prince live ten thousand years!”
- **Defect:** 황태제 is the emperor’s younger brother and designated heir, not his son; 천세 is a thousand years, not ten thousand.
- **Replacement:** “Long live His Majesty the Emperor! May His Highness the Imperial Crown Brother live a thousand years!”
- **Rationale:** The two titles identify different heirs, and the acclamation deliberately gives them different numbers of years.

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 나는 광인이 아니오. 이제 와서 두 번 배신하는 미친 짓을 벌이기에는 너무 이성적이지. 그렇기에 당신들과 손을 잡은 것이고.
- **Current:** “I’m no madman. I’m far too rational to do something insane like betray you twice. That’s why I joined hands with you.”
- **Defect:** “Betray you twice” says Sima Gong has already betrayed Dark Heaven once. He means that turning on Dark Heaven now would make him a double traitor.
- **Replacement:** “I’m no madman. I’m too rational to betray one side only to turn on the other. That’s why I joined hands with you.”
- **Rationale:** 두 번 배신 counts betrayals of successive sides, not two betrayals of the same addressee.

Several action and tactical details are altered, including a mutual bond between comrades and how the fanatics use their numbers.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 단 네 글자로 설명할 수 있었다.

일진일퇴(一進一退).
- **Current:** could be described in just four words:

A back-and-forth struggle.
- **Defect:** The source says four characters, not four words.
- **Replacement:** could be summed up in four characters:

Advance and retreat.
- **Rationale:** 일진일퇴 is a four-character expression for the battle’s back-and-forth course.

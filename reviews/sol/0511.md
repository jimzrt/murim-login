# Review

Two altered actions require correction, along with one glossary mismatch and several localized fidelity, register, and idiom issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그런 것치고는 신분도 확인 안 하고 휙 가 버리던디.
- **Current:** They didn’t even check anyone’s identity before turning around and taking off.
- **Defect:** The draft adds a turn that does not occur. The ships continue onward and swiftly disappear rather than reversing direction.
- **Replacement:** They didn’t even check anyone’s identity before speeding off.
- **Rationale:** 휙 가 버리다 means to leave or speed away abruptly; it does not indicate turning around.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 고작 반 시진 뒤, 어디선가 정체 모를 괴성이 들려오기 전까지는 그랬다.
- **Current:** That was how things remained until an hour later, when an unidentified, monstrous cry rang out from somewhere.
- **Defect:** The draft converts 반 시진 into an hour despite the binding glossary requiring the established term “shichen,” and it omits 고작, which emphasizes how little time passed.
- **Replacement:** That was how things remained until, just half a shichen later, an unidentified, monstrous cry rang out from somewhere.
- **Rationale:** 반 시진 is “half a shichen,” while 고작 adds the sense of “only” or “just.”

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 시벌 안 되겄네. 내가 얼른 가서 밑창에 구멍 뚫고 올라니까, 아무도 말리지 마소.
- **Current:** Damn it, that’s enough. I’m going over there, drilling a hole in their hull, and climbing aboard, so nobody try to stop me.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the fisherman’s intended action by making him climb aboard the pleasure boat. In context, the dialectal 올라니까 means he will go punch a hole in the hull and come back.
- **Replacement:** Fuck this. I’ll hurry over, punch a hole in their hull, and come right back, so nobody try to stop me.
- **Rationale:** The fisherman is threatening a quick act of sabotage followed by his return, not boarding the other vessel.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 모두가 깨달았다. 저 미친 소리가, 자신들이 보고 있는 현실이라는 것을.
- **Current:** And everyone realized that the insane sight before them was reality.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the object of realization from the bizarre noise they heard to the sight itself, losing the link between the earlier cries and the spectacle now producing them.
- **Replacement:** And everyone realized that the bizarre noise they had heard was part of the reality unfolding before their eyes.
- **Rationale:** 저 미친 소리 explicitly refers back to the strange cries, which the witnesses now connect to what they are seeing.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 후미에 딱 봐도 성격 개같이 생긴 어린애 하나 서 있고.
- **Current:** There was an obviously foul-tempered-looking kid standing at the stern.
- **Defect:** The draft sanitizes the strongly vulgar 개같이 and turns the deliberately insulting description into restrained narration.
- **Replacement:** There was a kid standing at the stern who looked like a nasty little shit.
- **Rationale:** 성격 개같이 생긴 crudely describes the child as visibly having an awful personality; Taekyung’s profanity and contempt should remain strong.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 반 시진이요? 거의 다 따라잡았네. 후우, 시벌 거. 피똥 쌌다.
- **Current:** Half a shichen? I’ve almost caught up. Whew, fuck. I’ve been shitting blood.
- **Defect:** The literal rendering of 피똥 쌌다 can be read as an actual medical condition in English rather than the source idiom for having endured extreme exertion.
- **Replacement:** Half a shichen? I’ve almost caught up. Whew, fuck me. I busted my ass getting here.
- **Rationale:** 피똥 싸다 is a vulgar hyperbolic idiom for going through severe hardship or exertion, not a statement that he literally passed blood.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 나는 한 놈을 붙잡아 정신 교육한 뒤 동족을 불러왔고, 상어 수상스키단을 창단했다.
- **Current:** I grabbed one of them, gave it a little mental education, summoned its companions, and founded the Shark Water-Ski Team.
- **Defect:** “Gave it a little mental education” is an unnatural dictionary calque that obscures the euphemistic joke about beating the shark into submission.
- **Replacement:** I grabbed one of them, taught it a lesson, called over more of its kind, and founded the Shark Water-Ski Team.
- **Rationale:** 정신 교육하다 is used humorously here to mean disciplining or forcibly teaching it a lesson; the replacement preserves that euphemism in natural English.

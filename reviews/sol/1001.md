# Review

Several draft choices change the party’s size, physical actions, and the force of a spoken line. Two also lose a deliberate correction and the nature of Jeok Cheongang’s threat.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반올림 좀 더해서 천여 명으로 불어난 일행은 감숙성을 향해 이동을 시작했고
- **Current:** Our party had swelled to well over a thousand, if you rounded up a bit, and we set off toward Gansu.
- **Defect:** “Well over a thousand” makes the party substantially larger than the roughly thousand people reached by generous rounding.
- **Replacement:** Our party had swelled to around a thousand, rounding up a bit, and we set off toward Gansu.
- **Rationale:** 반올림 좀 더해서 천여 명 qualifies the estimate; it does not say the party was well over a thousand.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 입맛을 다시며 중얼거리는 적천강의 모습에, 나는 작게 혀를 찼다.
- **Current:** At Jeok Cheongang’s muttered words, accompanied by a click of his tongue, I clicked mine too.
- **Defect:** The draft gives Jeok Cheongang the narrator’s tongue-clicking action.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang smacked his lips as he muttered, and I clicked my tongue softly.
- **Rationale:** Jeok smacks his lips; the narrator is the one who clicks his tongue.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 목에 힘을 주어 내지른 외침도, 별다른 공력도 실려 있지 않은 나직한 음성.
- **Current:** It wasn’t a shout, and there was no particular internal energy behind the low voice, though it came from a man speaking forcefully.
- **Defect:** “Speaking forcefully” contradicts the description of a low voice without a forceful shout or internal energy behind it.
- **Replacement:** It was a low voice, neither shouted with force nor backed by any particular internal energy.
- **Rationale:** The contrast is that the quiet, unamplified command stops the two Daoists.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 힘차게 지면을 내달리는 초원마의 말발굽.
- **Current:** The hooves of our grassland-bred horses thundered across the ground.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular grassland horse into multiple horses.
- **Replacement:** My grassland horse’s hooves thundered across the ground.
- **Rationale:** 초원마의 말발굽 describes the hooves of one horse as the narrator urges his mount forward.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “노, 아니 스승님.”
- **Current:** “M-Master.”
- **Defect:** A deliberate correction from an initial form of address becomes a nervous stammer.
- **Replacement:** “Old—no, Master.”
- **Rationale:** 노, 아니 marks the narrator starting one address and correcting himself to 스승님.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘지금 이 순간부터 감숙성에 도착하기 전까지, 조금이라도 뒤처지거나 뺀질대는 놈들은 노부가 친히 개인 면담을 해 주마.’
- **Current:** “From this moment until we reach Gansu, if any of you fall behind or try to slack off, this old man will personally give you a one-on-one lesson.”
- **Defect:** “Lesson” turns Jeok’s euphemistic threat of a private meeting into an explicit training session.
- **Replacement:** “From this moment until we reach Gansu, if any of you fall behind or try to slack off, this old man will personally have a private talk with you.”
- **Rationale:** 개인 면담 is a private meeting or talk; the threat lies in who would conduct it.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “노 선배. 저는 그저 확인 차…….”
- **Current:** “Senior Jeok, I was only trying to make sure…”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the spoken address from 노 선배 to “Senior Jeok.”
- **Replacement:** “Senior, I was only trying to make sure…”
- **Rationale:** The speaker does not address him as 적 선배; “Senior” preserves the deference without substituting a different name.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “누구긴 누굽니까요. 그 흉악하기 짝이 없는 마적단 놈들이지.”
- **Current:** “Who else? Those vicious mounted bandits.”
- **Defect:** The English loses the fact that the speaker identifies a bandit gang, rather than merely describing mounted bandits.
- **Replacement:** “Who else? That vicious gang of mounted bandits.”
- **Rationale:** 마적단 names a group or gang of mounted bandits.

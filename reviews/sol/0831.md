# Review

Several localized fidelity issues, including an omitted visual detail and a reversed time relationship in Jin’s taunt. The System panels also retain brackets prohibited by the binding Markdown rules.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 짙은 어둠에 파묻혀 보이지 않는 놈의 모습을, 그 안에 웅크린 힘을 잠시나마 느끼고 싶었다.
- **Current:** I wanted, if only for a moment, to feel the power coiled within him, hidden beneath the deep darkness.
- **Defect:** Omits Jin’s wish to perceive Asmodeus’s appearance, which the darkness conceals.
- **Replacement:** I wanted to catch even a glimpse of him buried in the deep darkness, to feel the power coiled within him, if only for a moment.
- **Rationale:** The source names both his obscured appearance and the power within the darkness.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 마지막 발악, 잘 봤다.
- **Current:** I saw your last-ditch effort. Nice work.
- **Defect:** Adds an approving remark where Jin only says he saw the attempt.
- **Replacement:** I got a good look at your last-ditch effort.
- **Rationale:** 잘 봤다 describes Jin’s observation, not praise for the Doppelganger’s work.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 끝끝내 확인하지 못한 채로 떠나보내야 했던 마왕 아스모데우스의 마지막 모습을
- **Current:** I thought of Demon King Asmodeus’s final form, which I’d had to leave without ever seeing clearly.
- **Defect:** “Final form” suggests a transformed state rather than the last sight Jin had of Asmodeus.
- **Replacement:** I thought of that last glimpse of Demon King Asmodeus, whose appearance I’d never managed to make out clearly.
- **Rationale:** 마지막 모습 refers to Asmodeus’s appearance at the end of the vision; no transformation is stated.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 허상은 그저 허상일 뿐이야. 미래가 어떻게 바뀔지는 두고 봐야 알겠지만…… 당장 뒈질 것 같은 어떤 새끼는 이미 세상에 없을 테니 알 바 아니고.
- **Current:** “An illusion is just an illusion. We’ll have to wait and see how the future changes… but I don’t give a shit about some bastard who’s already out of this world and about to die.”
- **Defect:** Says the Doppelganger is already dead while still about to die, and changes the point of the taunt: it will not be around to learn how the future changes.

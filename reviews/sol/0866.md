# Review

Several localized fidelity and terminology errors, including a missing agent in the sound-suppression scene and an incorrect martial rank.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 미처 큰 소리로 토해 내지 못한 그 외침이, 사방을 둘러싼 내 공력에 가로막혀 덧없이 흩어진다.
- **Current:** His cry, unable to escape in a louder voice, was blocked by the internal energy surrounding us and scattered into nothing.
- **Defect:** Omits that the surrounding internal energy is the narrator’s, obscuring who contains the cry.
- **Replacement:** The cry he couldn’t let out at full volume struck the barrier of my internal energy surrounding us and faded away.
- **Rationale:** 내 공력 explicitly identifies Jin Taekyung’s internal energy as what blocks the sound.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지난 십여 년간 숨겨 왔던 감정을 드러낸 마삼보는 입술을 파르르 떨었다.
- **Current:** Ma Sanbao, revealing emotions he’d kept hidden for over a decade, trembled as he pressed his lips together.
- **Defect:** Changes quivering lips into the action of pressing them together.
- **Replacement:** Ma Sanbao’s lips quivered as he revealed emotions he’d kept hidden for over a decade.
- **Rationale:** 입술을 파르르 떨었다 describes his lips trembling, not being pressed together.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 금의위 중 일부가 정체를 감추고 북상하고 있다는 정보
- **Current:** some members of the Embroidered Uniform Guard were heading north in disguise
- **Defect:** Specifies a disguise where the source says only that they concealed their identities.
- **Replacement:** some members of the Embroidered Uniform Guard were heading north while concealing their identities
- **Rationale:** 정체를 감추고 does not establish that they changed their appearance.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 최소가 초일류, 과반수가 절정 고수였으니까.
- **Current:** At least First Rate, and more than half were Peak masters.
- **Defect:** Lowers the stated minimum martial rank from Supreme First Rate to First Rate.
- **Replacement:** Even the weakest were Supreme First Rate, and more than half were Peak masters.
- **Rationale:** 초일류 is the established Supreme First Rate rank, distinct from 일류.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 이름은 어디 옆집 개똥이 수준이다.
- **Current:** But the name was on the level of some neighbor’s dog, Fido.
- **Defect:** Turns a crude, ordinary name for a person into the name of an actual dog.
- **Replacement:** But his name sounded like something you’d call the kid next door: Dogshit.
- **Rationale:** 옆집 개똥이 refers to a neighboring person with the lowly nickname Gaeddong, not a neighbor’s pet.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** 불쑥 들려온 마삼보의 목소리에, 나는 내심 입맛을 다시며 대답했다.
- **Current:** At Ma Sanbao’s sudden remark, I answered, privately wishing I could smack my lips.
- **Defect:** Renders an inward reaction of chagrin as a wish to perform a physical action.
- **Replacement:** At Ma Sanbao’s sudden remark, I answered, inwardly clicking my tongue.
- **Rationale:** 내심 입맛을 다시며 conveys a private, rueful reaction to being noticed.

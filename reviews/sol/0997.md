# Review

Several factual reversals and lost relationship cues need correction; the remaining issues are localized.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아직 귀환하지 못한 궁성과 금의위 역시 크게 다르지 않았다.
- **Current:** It was much the same for Gungsung and the Embroidered Uniform Guard, who still hadn’t returned after rooting out the Murong Family’s threat.
- **Defect:** Uses an unmapped transliteration instead of the established title.
- **Replacement:** It was much the same for the Bow Saint and the Embroidered Uniform Guard, who still hadn’t returned after rooting out the Murong Family’s threat.
- **Rationale:** 궁성 is the Bow Saint, not a personal name.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그리고 결코 짧지 않은 그 시간차가
- **Current:** And that gap in timing, however brief, might become a butterfly’s wings flapping somewhere far away and change a great many things.
- **Defect:** Reverses the duration: the source says the gap is by no means short.
- **Replacement:** And that considerable gap in timing might become the flap of a butterfly’s wings somewhere far away and change a great many things.
- **Rationale:** 결코 짧지 않은 explicitly rules out a brief gap.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 우리 집 둘째 아냐?
- **Current:** “Oh, would you look at that. Is that my little brother?”
- **Defect:** Calls Mukyung Taekyung’s little brother rather than the family’s second son, reversing their seniority and obscuring the ensuing joke about omitting “hyung.”
- **Replacement:** “Oh, would you look at that. Isn’t that our family’s second son?”
- **Rationale:** 둘째 identifies the second-born sibling; Taekyung is the younger brother.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 둘째 맞잖아.
- **Current:** “What? Did I say anything wrong? You are my little brother.”
- **Defect:** Again reverses the brothers’ seniority and loses Taekyung’s deliberately title-free way of calling Mukyung the second son.
- **Replacement:** “What? Did I say anything wrong? You are the second son.”
- **Rationale:** Mukyung objects to the missing 형님, not to being called a younger brother.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 절대 다른 놈한테 쓰러지지 말 것.
- **Current:** “Don’t you dare let anyone else beat you before I catch up.”
- **Defect:** Turns a plea that Taekyung not fall to someone else into a warning against losing a contest.
- **Replacement:** “Until I catch up, don’t let anyone else take you down. Ever.”
- **Rationale:** 쓰러지지 말 것 carries the danger of being struck down, which gives the farewell its emotional force.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가장 든든한 후견인이자 또 한 사람의 아버지나 다름없던 의숙, 항산호 철무백의 죽음을 뒤로한 채 애써 미소 짓고 있는 이소월.
- **Current:** Lee Seowol, forcing a smile as she put the death of her uncle and steadfast guardian, Cheol Mubaek—the Tiger of Mount Heng—behind her.
- **Defect:** Omits that Cheol Mubaek was like another father to Seowol.
- **Replacement:** Lee Seowol, forcing a smile despite the death of her uncle, Cheol Mubaek—the Tiger of Mount Heng—who had been her staunchest guardian and almost another father to her.
- **Rationale:** 또 한 사람의 아버지나 다름없던 states a distinct, emotionally important relationship.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 당연하다는 듯 흘러나오는 하대.
- **Current:** The words came out in a casual, familiar tone.
- **Defect:** Loses the marked hierarchy: the younger Mae speaks down to the much older Song Ho as though it were natural.
- **Replacement:** He spoke to the older man as an elder would to a junior, as though it were the most natural thing in the world.
- **Rationale:** 하대 denotes downward, informal address; the preceding age contrast makes that register shift salient.

# Review

Several actionable fidelity and register issues, including altered physical actions, reversed gratitude, overstated certainty, and two flattened euphemisms.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 입술을 질끈 깨문 나는 스켈레톤 킹을 향해 재차 물었다.
- **Current:** I clenched my teeth and asked the Skeleton King again.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Jin’s physical action from biting down on his lip to clenching his teeth.
- **Replacement:** I bit down hard on my lip and asked the Skeleton King again.
- **Rationale:** 입술을 깨물다 specifically means to bite one’s lip, not clench one’s teeth.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저 고마운 인간
- **Current:** that grateful human
- **Defect:** The direction of gratitude is reversed: the Skeleton King is grateful to Johnson, rather than describing Johnson as grateful.
- **Replacement:** that human I’m grateful to
- **Rationale:** 고마운 인간 denotes someone for whom the speaker feels gratitude, consistent with Johnson having created the Skeleton King’s human appearance.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마법진의 용도는 이 몸이 언데드라 가능했던 일이었고.
- **Current:** I was able to determine the magic circle’s purpose because this body is undead.
- **Defect:** “Determine” overstates the Skeleton King’s certainty immediately after he explicitly calls his conclusion a personal inference that he cannot guarantee.
- **Replacement:** I could guess what the magic circle was for only because I’m undead.
- **Rationale:** The source explains why he was able to infer the circle’s purpose, not why he could establish it conclusively.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 비교적 어두운 폐허 너머, 익숙한 얼굴이 무너진 건물을 헤치며 걸어오고 있었다.
- **Current:** Beyond the relatively dark wreckage of the collapsed buildings, a familiar face was walking toward me.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the action of the approaching person making his way through a collapsed building and instead folds the collapsed buildings into the static setting.
- **Replacement:** Beyond the relatively dark ruins, a familiar figure was making his way through a collapsed building toward me.
- **Rationale:** 무너진 건물을 헤치며 describes Go Jun actively navigating through the collapsed structure as he approaches.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 비록 인간의 거죽에 가려져 있지만, 그 안에 숨겨진 골격의 곡선이 끝내줘.
- **Current:** Though concealed beneath a human exterior, the curves of the skeleton hidden inside are exquisite.

- **Rationale:** 거죽 refers concretely to an outer skin or hide, contrasting it with the skeleton beneath.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 생각해 보니까 작업은 천천히 걸어도 될 것 같다.
- **Current:** Now that I think about it, I suppose the task can be done at a leisurely pace.

- **Replacement:** Come to think of it, I suppose I can take my time making a move on her.
- **Rationale:** 작업을 걸다 is a colloquial expression for attempting to pick someone up; that meaning drives the surrounding joke.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 며칠 전에 존슨한테 들었는데 근처에 물 좋은 클럽이 있다더군.
- **Current:** Johnson told me a few days ago that there is a club nearby with an excellent selection of attractive people.
- **Defect:** The draft explicitly explains the euphemism and turns the club’s patrons into an impersonal “selection,” flattening the setup for the gay-bar joke.
- **Replacement:** Johnson told me a few days ago that there’s a club nearby with a great crowd.
- **Rationale:** 물 좋은 is an intentionally indirect colloquial description of a venue with attractive patrons; the translation should preserve that euphemistic ambiguity.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 어느 때보다 딱딱하고 냉기가 흐르는 목소리에, 나는 천천히 돌아섰다.
- **Current:** The voice was colder and more rigid than ever before.

I slowly turned around.
- **Defect:** “Rigid” is not a natural description of a voice, and splitting the sentence loses the immediate connection between hearing it and turning around.
- **Replacement:** At the sound of the voice—harder and colder than ever before—I slowly turned around.
- **Rationale:** 딱딱한 describes the voice as hard or stern, while the connective 에 ties Jin’s reaction directly to hearing it.

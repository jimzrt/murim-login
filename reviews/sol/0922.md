# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes a completed assault into an attempted one, shifts Dark Heaven’s reach from people to places, and loses a martial-arts joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 유일하게 남은 자식을 지키기 위해 저항하다 병사들에게 유린당한 어머니의 찢어지는 비명은
- **Current:** His mother’s piercing scream as she resisted the soldiers trying to violate her to protect her only surviving child, his Master’s final words—those memories were so vivid.
- **Defect:** “Trying to violate her” makes the soldiers’ completed abuse an attempt and makes her resistance sound like it succeeded.
- **Replacement:** His mother’s piercing scream as the soldiers brutalized her while she fought to protect her only surviving child, and his Master’s final words—those memories were so vivid.
- **Rationale:** 유린당한 describes what the soldiers did to her, not merely what they tried to do. “Brutalized” preserves the source’s restraint without changing the event.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그의 넓은 그늘에 잠시 몸을 의탁하지 않은 자가 없고, 암천의 손이 닿지 않은 자가 없다.
- **Current:** There wasn’t a single person who hadn’t taken shelter beneath his vast shadow, and there wasn’t a place Dark Heaven’s hand hadn’t reached.
- **Defect:** The second clause changes Dark Heaven’s reach from people to places, obscuring the network of people established by both clauses.
- **Replacement:** There wasn’t a single person who hadn’t taken shelter beneath his vast shadow, and not one of them was beyond Dark Heaven’s reach.
- **Rationale:** Both clauses end in 자, referring to people; the following account describes officials spreading throughout the land.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** “엄마신장은 영혼을 때리는 거야. 몸이 아니라.”
- **Current:** “Mom’s smack hits your soul. Not your body.”
- **Defect:** “Smack” drops the mock martial-arts technique name that delivers the joke.
- **Replacement:** “Mom’s Divine Palm hits your soul. Not your body.”
- **Rationale:** 엄마신장 casts his mother’s blow as a named palm technique; the contrast with a bodily blow is the punchline.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 공연의 막이 내려도 커튼콜(Curtain call)이 남아 있듯이.
- **Current:** Just as the curtain falling on a performance didn’t mean the curtain call was over.
- **Defect:** The draft implies a curtain call already underway rather than one still to come.
- **Replacement:** Just as a curtain call still follows when the curtain falls on a performance.
- **Rationale:** 남아 있듯이 says the curtain call remains after the curtain falls, paralleling the roles that remain after the battle.

## F05 — Minor

- **Defect:** The draft turns looking at the flags into physically passing them.
- **Replacement:** Looking toward the dozens of flags rising high into the air.
- **Rationale:** 바라보며 specifies an act of looking, not a route past the flags.

## F06 — Minor

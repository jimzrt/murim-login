## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “저 친구가 속가 중에서는 제법 잘나갔거든. 본문과의 친선 비무에서 나한테 패배하기 전까지는 말이야, 안 그런가?”

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “사실이야. 다만 그분은 여전히 화산에 남아 계셨네. 워낙 깊이 은거하시는 바람에 찾지 못했을 뿐.”
- **Current:** “It’s true. However, he was still living at Huashan. He had simply secluded himself so deeply that no one could find him.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied subject from Huashan’s people having failed to locate Mae Jonghak to the absolute claim that no one could find him, which conflicts with Li Feng’s immediate account of their successfully reaching him.
- **Replacement:** “It’s true. However, he was still living at Huashan. He had simply gone into such deep seclusion that we hadn’t been able to find him.”
- **Rationale:** 찾지 못했을 뿐 refers contextually to Huashan’s prior inability to locate him, not to an intrinsic impossibility of anyone finding him.

Several action, mechanism, and voice details need correction, most notably the curtain movement, the final rebel’s injury, and the loss of the child’s terrified stammer and the caster’s aged voice.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한발 빠르게 허공섭물(虛空攝物)의 수법으로 폐쇄형 커튼을 친 나는 어두운 사무실 내부를 가득 채우기 시작한 홀로그램 영상에 집중했다.
- **Current:** I quickly used Seizing an Object Through Empty Space to throw up a sealed curtain, then focused on the holographic footage beginning to fill the dark office.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung appear to erect or create a sealed curtain. He uses the technique to draw the existing blackout curtains, which darkens the office.
- **Replacement:** I quickly used Seizing an Object Through Empty Space to draw the blackout curtains, then focused on the holographic footage beginning to fill the dark office.
- **Rationale:** 커튼을 친 describes drawing or closing the curtains, not throwing up a new barrier; the resulting darkness confirms their blackout function.

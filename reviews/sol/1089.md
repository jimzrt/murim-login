# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s main sequence, but changes the patrol’s delay and the number of figures crossing the gravel, and adds an unsupported pronoun for the Blood Lord’s superior.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그, 사실 평소보다 반 각 정도 늦긴 했습니다만 코앞까진 왔을 테니
- **Current:** they’re about half a shichen later than usual, but they must be close by now.
- **Defect:** Changes the patrol’s delay from half a gak to half a shichen.
- **Replacement:** they’re about half a gak later than usual, but they must be close by now.
- **Rationale:** 각 and 시진 are different time units; the draft substantially lengthens the delay.

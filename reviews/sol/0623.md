# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains two altered subjects, an unsupported spatial placement, and several localized losses of action, reaction, and register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 청년은 눈처럼 새하얀 백호(白虎)의 갈기를 부드럽게 쓰다듬었다.
- **Current:** The young man gently stroked the snow-white mane of the white tiger beside him.
- **Defect:** “Beside him” places the tiger next to the young man, although the preceding growl and vibration come from below him and the later action establishes that he is riding it.
- **Replacement:** The young man gently stroked the snow-white mane of the white tiger beneath him.
- **Rationale:** The draft changes the characters’ physical arrangement instead of preserving the source-supported mounting position.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “오메.”
- **Current:** “Oh, come on.”
- **Defect:** The translation turns a startled exclamation into a complaint or protest.
- **Replacement:** “Good grief.”
- **Rationale:** 오메 here is an exclamation of dismay at the unexpectedly enlarged fire, not an objection directed at someone.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “이런 미친! 도대체 무슨 짓을 한 건가!”
- **Current:** “You crazy bastard! What on earth did you do?”
- **Defect:** The draft converts Namho’s exclamation that the situation is insane into a direct personal insult aimed at Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** “This is insane! What the hell did you do?”
- **Rationale:** 이런 미친 is an exclamation here; the source does not call Taekyung a bastard.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 가까운 곳에 남아 있는 마지막 불씨를 발로 비벼끈 내가 어깨를 으쓱해 보였다.

- **Defect:** “Crushed” omits the specific action of rubbing or grinding the ember out with his foot.
- **Replacement:** I walked over to the last ember nearby, ground it out beneath my foot, and shrugged.
- **Rationale:** 비벼끈 describes extinguishing the ember by grinding it underfoot, not merely stepping on or crushing it.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Defect:** “Can you really say” makes Namho sound like the person who threatened him, although Yayul Mok said it. “Hovering between life and death” also replaces the age joke that Namho might drop dead any day.

- **Rationale:** The source leaves the threat’s speaker implicit but contextually refers to Yayul Mok, while 오늘내일하다 jokes that the elderly Namho is already liable to die at any moment.

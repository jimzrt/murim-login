# Review

Several lines alter a spoken name, battlefield responsibilities, hierarchy, or a martial-arts comparison.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “안돼! 홀로구!”
- **Current:** “No! The Khan’s—!”
- **Defect:** Replaces a cry to Holrogu with an unfinished reference to the Khan.
- **Replacement:** “No! Holrogu!”
- **Rationale:** The speaker calls out 홀로구 by name; the line contains neither a title nor an unfinished phrase.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “협곡을 정면 돌파하려는 적들은 우군(右軍)이 견제할 테니, 우리 좌군(左軍)은 절벽을 오르는 자들을 처리해 달라 청하셨습니다.”
- **Current:** “He asked our left wing to deal with the men climbing the cliffs, since the right wing will keep the enemy from breaking through the gorge head-on.”
- **Defect:** Turns the right wing’s task of checking or harrying a frontal assault into a promise to prevent a breakthrough.
- **Replacement:** “He asked our left wing to deal with the men climbing the cliffs, while the right wing keeps the enemy attempting a frontal assault through the gorge in check.”
- **Rationale:** 견제 describes holding the attackers in check, not necessarily stopping their breakthrough outright.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “청은 무슨. 나이로 따졌을 때는 노부가 좌장(座長)이지만, 지금 같은 전장에서는 장군의 명령대로 따라야지. 안 그런가?”
- **Current:** “Asked? I may be the presiding chair by age, but on a battlefield like this, I follow the general’s orders. Don’t I?”
- **Defect:** Makes an awkward claim to a formal-sounding chairmanship where the old man is contrasting seniority by age with the general’s battlefield authority.
- **Replacement:** “Asked? I may be the senior one by age, but on a battlefield like this, I follow the general’s orders. Don’t I?”
- **Rationale:** 나이로 따졌을 때 explicitly limits his standing here to age; his point is that the general’s instruction is an order, not a request.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “도지휘첨사(都指揮僉事)께서 청하시길…….”
- **Current:** “Assistant Military Commissioner—”
- **Defect:** Drops the officer’s cautious attempt to restate that the commissioner made a request.
- **Replacement:** “The Assistant Military Commissioner did ask…”
- **Rationale:** 청하시길 repeats the deferential ‘asked’ that the old man has just challenged, explaining why he interrupts the officer.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 경지에 오른 벽호공(壁虎功)의 고수조차 따라 할 수 없을 만큼 쾌속했고
- **Current:** He was faster than even a master who had reached great skill in the Wall Lizard Technique could follow,

- **Replacement:** He moved so fast that even a master of the Wall Lizard Technique could not have replicated the feat,

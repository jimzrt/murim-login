# Review

The draft contains one outcome-changing mistranslation, repeated invention of a personal name from an epithet, and several localized fidelity and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “이번 예선을 수석으로 통과한다는 것에 걸겠네.”
- **Current:** “I’m betting that he’ll be the first to pass the preliminaries.”
- **Defect:** 수석 means achieving the highest result or taking first place, not being the earliest participant to pass. This changes the condition of the wager.
- **Replacement:** “I’m betting that he’ll take first place in the preliminaries.”
- **Rationale:** The later bettor uses the same 수석 통과 construction, which the draft correctly renders as taking first place.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “장강수로맹의 철수신룡(鐵水神龍)에게 은자 천 냥.”
- **Current:** “A thousand silver nyang on Cheol Soo, the Iron-Water Divine Dragon of the Yangtze River Channel League.”
- **Defect:** The draft splits 철수신룡 into an invented personal name, Cheol Soo, plus an epithet. The supplied Hanja identifies the entire expression as the epithet Iron-Water Divine Dragon.
- **Replacement:** “A thousand silver nyang on the Iron-Water Divine Dragon of the Yangtze River Channel League.”
- **Rationale:** 鐵水神龍 corresponds in full to Iron-Water Divine Dragon; the source does not separately name this person Cheol Soo.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그러니까 가서 철수신룡이나 지켜봐. 생각해 보니까 신참 새끼가 건방지게.”
- **Current:** “So go keep an eye on Cheol Soo. Now that I think about it, that rookie’s gotten pretty damn cocky.”
- **Defect:** The repeated epithet 철수신룡 is again reduced to the invented personal name Cheol Soo.
- **Replacement:** “So go keep an eye on the Iron-Water Divine Dragon. Now that I think about it, that rookie’s gotten pretty damn cocky.”
- **Rationale:** The speaker tells the swordsman to watch the contestant identified throughout the exchange by the epithet Iron-Water Divine Dragon.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그의 모습은 일 년 전보다 확연히 늙어 있었다. 나도 모르게 가슴이 울렁거리던 그때였다.
- **Current:** He was visibly older than he had been a year ago. My chest began to feel strangely tight.
- **Defect:** 가슴이 울렁거리다 describes an involuntary churning or queasy sensation, not tightness. The wording also sets up the following joke that Jeok Cheongang’s threat acts as motion-sickness medicine.
- **Replacement:** He was visibly older than he had been a year ago. My chest churned before I knew it.
- **Rationale:** Preserving the churning sensation retains both Taekyung’s emotional reaction and the immediate motion-sickness punchline.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 알은척할까 하다가 귀찮아질 것 같아 접었다.
- **Current:** I considered pretending to recognize him, but decided against it. It seemed like it would only become a hassle.
- **Defect:** 알은척하다 means acknowledging or greeting someone one knows, not pretending to recognize him.
- **Replacement:** I considered saying hello, but decided against it. It seemed like it would only become a hassle.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung genuinely recognizes Jongni Chu and merely decides not to acknowledge him.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 가급적이면 날 만나지 않게 해 달라고 기도하고.
- **Current:** And pray that we don’t run into each other.
- **Defect:** The replacement shifts from past-tense narration into an imperative and changes the source’s one-sided wish that Jongni Chu not encounter Taekyung into a mutual encounter.
- **Replacement:** And prayed that, if possible, he wouldn’t run into me.
- **Rationale:** The Korean continues Taekyung’s narrated action and specifically frames Jongni Chu as the person who should be spared from meeting him.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그나마 이번 무인은 일류 정도의 실력이다. 그래도 공력을 실어 때릴 줄은 알았다.
- **Current:** This one had at least First Rate skill. He even knew how to infuse his strike with internal energy.
- **Defect:** 일류 정도 means approximately First Rate, whereas at least First Rate raises the lower bound. 그래도 is also concessive—at least he knew how—not additive even.
- **Replacement:** This martial artist was around the First Rate level. At least he knew how to put internal energy behind his strike.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the approximate rank and the narrator’s qualified assessment of the contestant.

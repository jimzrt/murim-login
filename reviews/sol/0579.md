# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it pluralizes a singular hero, reverses who was observing whom, mistrenders a solemn gaze as a physical feature, introduces an official post absent from the causal explanation, and adds an unsupported direction to the final movement.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 혼란하고 위태롭던 대격변의 한 줄을 장식했던 옛 영웅조차 막을 수 없었던 벼락.
- **Current:** The kind of lightning even the old heroes who had adorned one chapter of the chaotic, perilous Great Cataclysm had been unable to stop.
- **Defect:** The singular 옛 영웅 is changed into multiple heroes, obscuring the passage’s reference to one specific hero and his connection to Choi Minwoo.
- **Replacement:** The kind of lightning even a hero of old—one who had left his mark on the chaotic, perilous Great Cataclysm—had been unable to stop.
- **Rationale:** The source describes one 옛 영웅, not a group, and 한 줄을 장식했던 means that he left his mark on that history rather than literally adorning a chapter.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그저 옆에서 지켜보는 것만으로도 존경과 두려움을 느낄 수밖에 없던 존재.
- **Current:** A being who had inspired respect and fear simply by standing nearby and watching.
- **Defect:** The observer is reversed. The source says that merely watching this person from nearby made others feel respect and fear; the draft instead makes the person stand nearby and watch.
- **Replacement:** A being who inspired respect and fear in anyone who merely watched him from nearby.
- **Rationale:** 지켜보는 is the act performed by those witnessing the person, while the person is the object whose presence produces their respect and fear.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 깊게 가라앉은 눈빛이 왜 변심했느냐 묻는 듯했다.
- **Current:** His deeply sunken eyes seemed to ask why Song Cheonwoo had changed sides.
- **Defect:** “Sunken eyes” describes a physical facial feature, whereas 가라앉은 눈빛 describes a grave, subdued, or deeply composed gaze.
- **Replacement:** The deep, solemn look in his eyes seemed to ask why Song Cheonwoo had changed sides.
- **Rationale:** The source characterizes the emotional quality of Choi Minwoo’s gaze rather than the shape or condition of his eyes.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 자리를 비운 것이 화근이었다.
- **Current:** Leaving my post was the cause of this.
- **Defect:** “My post” adds an official position or duty absent from the source. Song is blaming his physical absence for creating the opening that led to the crisis.
- **Replacement:** My absence was the root of all this.
- **Rationale:** 자리를 비우다 means to be away or leave one’s place; 화근 identifies that absence as the root cause.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 눈앞의 노인에게 남은 것이 무엇인지.
- **Current:** What the old man had left.
- **Defect:** The English can mean something the old man abandoned or bequeathed, rather than what he still possessed.
- **Replacement:** What the old man still had left.
- **Rationale:** 남은 것이 refers to what remained to Song Cheonwoo—his family—which Choi then identifies as the leverage behind the offer.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 마음 같아서는 당신을 죽이고 싶습니다. 백 번, 천 번도 더.
- **Current:** If I had my way, I would kill you. A hundred times. A thousand.
- **Defect:** The draft omits 더 and leaves the final quantity unnaturally incomplete, weakening the emphatic “a thousand times over—or more.”
- **Replacement:** If I had my way, I would kill you a hundred times, a thousand times over.
- **Rationale:** 백 번, 천 번도 더 intensifies the repeated killing beyond a hundred or even a thousand times.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 사방으로 튀는 눈더미 사이, 초인적인 힘으로 몸을 날린 송천우의 신형이 거대한 균열의 틈으로 미끄러졌다.
- **Current:** Amid the snow exploding in every direction, Song Cheonwoo hurled himself forward with superhuman force and slipped into the gap of the enormous crevasse.
- **Defect:** “Forward” adds a direction absent from the source and conflicts with the crevasse having previously been behind Song Cheonwoo.
- **Replacement:** Amid the snow exploding in every direction, Song Cheonwoo launched himself with superhuman force and slipped into the enormous crevasse.
- **Rationale:** 몸을 날린 states that he threw or launched himself but deliberately does not specify a direction; the result is only that he enters the crevasse.

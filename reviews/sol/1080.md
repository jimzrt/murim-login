# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s main sequence but loses two threats’ physical meaning and misstates who triggers the guards’ action.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 성내의 수뇌부는 그런 이들을 위해 술과 고기를 내주었다.
- **Current:** the city’s leaders provided them with food and drink.
- **Defect:** The specific provisions, liquor and meat, become generic food and drink.
- **Replacement:** the city’s leaders provided them with liquor and meat.
- **Rationale:** 술과 고기 names what the leaders gave the crowd.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “혀가 기시네. 좀 짧게 해 드릴까.”
- **Current:** “You’re getting long-winded. Want me to make it easier?”
- **Defect:** The implied threat to shorten the City Lord’s tongue disappears.
- **Replacement:** “That’s a long tongue you’ve got. Want me to shorten it?”
- **Rationale:** 혀가 기시네 and 좀 짧게 해 드릴까 turn his evasive answer into a threat against his tongue.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “해 뜨면 보내라. 혼자 가면 외로우니까 길동무 붙여 주는 것도 잊지 말고.”
- **Current:** “Take him away at sunrise. Don’t forget to send him with a companion so he won’t be lonely.”
- **Defect:** “Take him away” presents the sunrise order as removal rather than the euphemistic order to send him to his death.
- **Replacement:** “Send him off at sunrise. And don’t forget to give him some company on the way. He might get lonely.”
- **Rationale:** 보내라 leaves the destination euphemistic; the subsequent choice between beheading as a corrupt official and destruction as a traitor supplies its lethal meaning.

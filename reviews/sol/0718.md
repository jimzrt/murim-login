# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but contains one critical subject reversal involving Baekhwi’s captivity, plus several meaningful losses of register, humor, and natural dialogue.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 죽은 줄로만 알았던 자식을 인질로 붙잡힌 채, 암천의 사냥개 노릇을 해 왔던 그에게 면죄부를 내려 주고 싶었을 것이다.
- **Current:** He must have wanted to absolve the man who had acted as Dark Heaven’s hunting dog while holding hostage the son he had believed dead.
- **Defect:** The English makes Baeksang the person holding his son hostage. The source says Baeksang served Dark Heaven while his child—whom he had believed dead—was being held hostage.
- **Replacement:** He must have wanted to absolve the man who had served as Dark Heaven’s hunting dog while his child—the child he had believed dead—was held hostage.
- **Rationale:** The passive construction 붙잡힌 채 applies to Baeksang’s child and explains the coercion behind Baeksang’s service to Dark Heaven.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그게 당연한 거 아닙니까. 저 같은 새끼가 뭐 대단한 놈이라고.”
- **Current:** “Of course. I’m only human. I’m not anything special.”
- **Defect:** The draft softens Taekyung’s profane self-deprecation and replaces his rhetorical challenge with an explicit statement that belongs conceptually to the following line.
- **Replacement:** “Isn’t that only natural? What makes a bastard like me so special?”
- **Rationale:** 저 같은 새끼 preserves Taekyung’s coarse, self-mocking register, while 뭐 대단한 놈이라고 is a rhetorical question rather than a neutral declaration.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금까지의 일을 생각한다면 내가 백상의 위패를 반으로 쪼갠 뒤 침을 뱉어도 정당방위다.
- **Current:** Given everything that had happened, I would have been justified in splitting Baeksang’s tablet in half and spitting on it.
- **Defect:** Rendering 정당방위 merely as “justified” removes the deliberately absurd claim that desecrating a memorial tablet would count as self-defense, flattening the dark joke.
- **Replacement:** Given everything that had happened, even if I split Baeksang’s tablet in half and spat on it, it would count as self-defense.
- **Rationale:** 정당방위 specifically means self-defense; its incongruous use here supplies the humor and should be retained.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “허어, 괜찮다. 몸도 불편한 놈이 뭘 이렇게까지.”
- **Current:** “Oh, it’s fine. What can a fellow who’s still injured do about all this?”
- **Defect:** The second sentence changes the meaning into a question about what the injured Beast Miao King is capable of doing. Jeok is instead feigning consideration and saying that an injured man need not go so far for him.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, it’s fine. You’re still injured—there’s no need to go this far.”
- **Rationale:** 뭘 이렇게까지 means there is no need to make such an effort; the line humorously follows Jeok’s threat that forced him to surrender the chief seat.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “됐으니 그만 앉아라. 대가리에 피도 안 마른 놈이 오라 가라 한 건 마음에 안 들지만, 우선은 맘대로 지껄여 봐.”
- **Current:** “Enough. Sit down already. I don’t like a punk whose brain hasn’t even finished drying ordering me around, but for now, go ahead and spout whatever you want.”
- **Defect:** “Whose brain hasn’t even finished drying” is an unintelligible literal calque of an idiom for someone young and inexperienced.
- **Replacement:** “Enough. Sit down already. I don’t like some wet-behind-the-ears punk ordering me around, but for now, go ahead and spout whatever you want.”
- **Rationale:** 대가리에 피도 안 마른 놈 contemptuously calls someone too young and inexperienced; “wet behind the ears” preserves that meaning naturally.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “이렇게 적 노까지 모신 것은 한 가지 중대 사안에 관하여 여쭙기 위해섭니다.”
- **Current:** “I summoned even Old Master Jeok because I wished to ask about one grave matter.”
- **Defect:** “Summoned” reverses the hierarchy implied by the deferential 모신, making the Beast Miao King sound as though he commanded Jeok Cheongang to attend.
- **Replacement:** “I asked you to join us, Old Master Jeok, because I wished to consult you about a grave matter.”
- **Rationale:** 모신 is deferential, and 여쭙기 likewise frames Jeok as the respected senior whose counsel is being sought.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “지금까지 뒷수습도 원만히 되어 가는 것으로 아는데. 혹 암천의 꼬리라도 밟았느냐?”
- **Current:** “From what I’ve heard, the aftermath has been handled smoothly so far. Did you perhaps get on the trail of Dark Heaven’s tail?”
- **Defect:** “Get on the trail of Dark Heaven’s tail” mixes two metaphors and is unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** “From what I’ve heard, the aftermath has been handled smoothly so far. Did you perhaps manage to pick up Dark Heaven’s trail?”
- **Rationale:** 꼬리를 밟다 is an idiom for finding or picking up someone’s trail; the replacement conveys that meaning without the malformed double metaphor.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “그냥. 그냥 이러고 싶었습니다. 단지 그뿐이에요.”
- **Current:** “I just wanted to. That’s all.”
- **Defect:** Because the immediately preceding dialogue asks whether Taekyung wanted to knock over the tablets, “I just wanted to” can incorrectly take that destructive action as its object. The source’s 이러고 refers to what he actually did—bowing.
- **Replacement:** “I just… felt like doing this. That’s all.”
- **Rationale:** Making “this” explicit preserves the source’s reference to his act of paying respects and avoids implying that he wanted to overturn the tablets.

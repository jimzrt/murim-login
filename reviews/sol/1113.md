# Review

The draft preserves the chapter’s main events, but changes a character’s identity in the Blood Lord’s thought and misassigns several actions and relationships.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘대술사, 네년만큼은 끼어들지 말거라. 아무리 그래도 내 손으로 그분이 아끼시는 종년을 죽이고 싶진 않으니.’
- **Current:** *Grand Mage, you’d better not get involved. Even so, I’d rather not kill that woman who serves him and whom he cherishes.*
- **Defect:** “That woman” makes the favored servant sound like someone other than the Grand Mage and drops the Blood Lord’s insults.
- **Replacement:** *Grand Mage, you bitch, stay out of this. Even I don’t want to kill a servant girl the Lord of Heaven favors with my own hands.*
- **Rationale:** He addresses the Grand Mage directly as 네년 and calls that same woman 그분이 아끼시는 종년.

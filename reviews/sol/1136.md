# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes who made the Emperor’s life different, makes bows rather than arrows rain down, and blurs several dialogue and battle details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아니, 달라지게 만들었다.

바로 그가, 진태경이 모든 것을 뒤바꾸고 오래전 잃어버렸던 웃음을 되찾아 주었다.
- **Current:** No—he had made it different.

Jin Taekyung had changed everything and returned the laughter he had lost so long ago.
- **Defect:** The first “he” appears to make the Son of Heaven the agent of the change; the source credits Jin Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** No—Jin Taekyung had made it different. He had changed everything and given the Son of Heaven back the laughter he’d lost long ago.
- **Rationale:** The next source sentence explicitly identifies Jin Taekyung as the one who changed everything and restored the Emperor’s laughter.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그들이 일시에 내공을 실어 쏘아 보내는 강궁(强弓)이 실로 강맹한 속도와 위력으로 하늘을 뒤덮으며 쏟아져 내리고 있었다.

수십여 장 밖, 야트막한 분지에서 죽을 힘을 다해 몸부림치는 적들을 향해.
- **Current:** Their great bows, reinforced with internal energy, filled the sky and rained down with astonishing speed and power.

They were aimed at the enemies struggling with all their might in the shallow basin dozens of yards away.
- **Defect:** The bows themselves appear to rain down, and internal energy appears to reinforce the equipment rather than the shots. “Dozens of yards” also understates dozens of jang.
- **Replacement:** With internal energy behind each shot, arrows from their powerful bows filled the sky and rained down with astonishing speed and force.

They fell upon enemies struggling with all their might in the shallow basin hundreds of feet away.
- **Rationale:** The guards shoot with internal energy; the ensuing rain is of arrows. A jang is several yards long.

## F03 — Major

- **Source:** “그럴 리가 있겠습니까. 큰일은 저들이 다했고, 황실이야 마지막에 한 손 거든 것뿐이지요.”

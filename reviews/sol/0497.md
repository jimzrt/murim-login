# Review

One critical dialogue reversal changes Taekyung’s initial concession from defeat to death, undermining Mungyeong’s correction. Additional findings concern added culpability, lost emphasis on hierarchy, and two localized fidelity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “졌습니다. 그만하시죠.”
- **Current:** “I’m dead. Let’s stop now.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung says he lost, not that he died. The mistranslation eliminates the distinction that motivates Mungyeong to reject his answer and demand that he admit he would have died.
- **Replacement:** “I lost. Let’s stop now.”
- **Rationale:** 졌습니다 means “I lost.” Taekyung only says 죽었습니다, “I’m dead,” after Mungyeong corrects him.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “잘못된 길을 택했고, 그에 대한 대가를 치렀지. 해 줄 말은 그뿐이다.”
- **Current:** “We chose the wrong path and paid the price. That is all I have to say.”
- **Defect:** The added first-person plural explicitly includes Mungyeong among those who chose the wrong path. The Korean omits the subject and does not confirm his personal culpability, particularly after he declines to answer whether he was the Sect Leader.
- **Replacement:** “The wrong path was chosen, and the price was paid. That is all I have to say.”
- **Rationale:** The subjectless Korean deliberately leaves unstated who made the choice. The replacement preserves that ambiguity rather than adding “we.”

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 기밀을 위해 믿을 만한 정예들만을 선별했다고는 하나, 그들의 면면과 숫자는 누구도 무시할 수 없을 정도였다.
- **Current:** Although they had selected only trustworthy elites for the sake of secrecy, the identities and number of those present were impossible to ignore.
- **Defect:** 면면 refers to the assembled members’ caliber or impressive composition, not merely their identities. “The identities and number” is also unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** Although they had selected only trustworthy elites for the sake of secrecy, the caliber and sheer number of those present were impossible to ignore.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes both the quality and quantity of the assembled martial artists.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “알겠다. 다른 사람도 아니고 진 대협의 수련을 방해할 수는 없지. 다음 근무자들에게도 전달하도록 하마.”
- **Current:** “Understood. We can’t interrupt Great Hero Jin’s training, especially not for someone else. I’ll pass that along to the next guards.”
- **Defect:** “Especially not for someone else” misreads 다른 사람도 아니고 and obscures the guard’s emphasis that Jin Taekyung is no ordinary person whose training they might interrupt.
- **Replacement:** “Understood. This is Great Hero Jin we’re talking about—we can’t interrupt his training. I’ll pass that along to the next guards.”
- **Rationale:** 다른 사람도 아니고 진 대협 conveys “of all people, it is Great Hero Jin,” reinforcing his status and the guard’s deference.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 뒤에 덧붙인 무인의 질문은 미처 한 사람의 귓가에 닿지 못했다.
- **Current:** The rest of the guard’s question never reached Mungyeong’s ears.
- **Defect:** The Korean refers to the follow-up question the guard added, not an unheard remainder of a single question. The draft implies that Mungyeong heard only part of “Was Great Hero Jin badly injured?”
- **Replacement:** The guard’s follow-up question never reached Mungyeong’s ears.
- **Rationale:** 뒤에 덧붙인 질문 identifies the subsequently added question as a whole.

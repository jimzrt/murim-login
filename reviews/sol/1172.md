# Review

The draft reverses two scene details and weakens several consequential details of the spreading catastrophe.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 다만 서릿발 같은 마력에 얼어붙은 오감과 정신이 미처 제때 반응하지 못했을 뿐이다.

지금으로부터 불과 수십 년 전, 한 마디의 예고도 없이 찾아온 ‘그날’의 기억을.
- **Current:** Their senses and minds had simply frozen in the frost-like magical power, too slow to respond.

They remembered *that day*, which had come without a word of warning just a few decades ago.
- **Defect:** The draft says they remembered that day; the source says their frozen senses and minds had not yet reacted to its memory.
- **Replacement:** Their senses and minds, frozen by the icy magical power, simply hadn’t reacted in time to the memory of *that day*—the day that had arrived without a word of warning just a few decades ago.
- **Rationale:** 기억을 is the object of 미처 제때 반응하지 못했을 뿐이다, not a separate assertion that they remembered.

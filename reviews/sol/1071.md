# Review

The draft reverses who controls the monsters in the closing scene and changes several smaller details of the battle and dialogue.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 그런 위협을 줄 수 있는 이들은, 수백여 장이나 떨어진 곳에서 무수한 괴물들과 맞서 싸우는 와중이었다.

정확히는, 그들의 조종에 따라 움직이는 수하들이었지만.
- **Current:** And the people capable of posing such a threat were hundreds of yards away, fighting countless monsters.

More precisely, their subordinates were fighting under their command.
- **Defect:** The second sentence makes the distant fighters’ subordinates the combatants. The source identifies the monsters they are fighting as the concealed figures’ controlled subordinates.
- **Replacement:** The people capable of seeing through their concealment were hundreds of yards away, fighting countless monsters.

Those monsters were the concealed figures’ subordinates, moving under their control.
- **Rationale:** 그들의 조종에 따라 움직이는 수하들 describes the minions under the concealed figures’ control, not subordinates fighting on the distant people’s side.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “씨바 거, 인생 뭐 있습니까? 조장님 뒤꽁무니만 쫓다 보니까 이제는 천마 할애비가 나타나도 안 쫄립니다.”
- **Current:** “Come on, what else is there to life? I’ve spent all this time following your ass around, Captain. Now I wouldn’t even flinch if the fucking Heavenly Demon himself showed up.”
- **Defect:** The punchline changes the Heavenly Demon’s grandfather into the Heavenly Demon himself.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck it, what’s life anyway? I’ve spent all this time following your ass around, Captain. Now I wouldn’t even flinch if the Heavenly Demon’s granddad showed up.”
- **Rationale:** 천마 할애비 explicitly means the Heavenly Demon’s grandfather; the exaggerated kinship is part of Mujin’s joke.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 하늘이 갈라지는 듯한 굉음과 함께, 무수한 그림자들이 온 사방을 뒤덮으며 쏟아졌다.
- **Current:** With a deafening roar that sounded as if the sky had split apart, countless shadows poured down, blanketing every direction.
- **Defect:** “Poured down” suggests an attack from above, though the force has encircled them and the source does not give the shadows a downward trajectory.
- **Replacement:** With a deafening roar that sounded as if the sky had split apart, countless shadows came pouring in from every direction.
- **Rationale:** 하늘이 갈라지는 듯한 modifies the roar; 온 사방 describes where the shadows arrive from.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 생존에 있어 비겁함이란 없다.
- **Current:** There was no shame in being a coward if it meant surviving.
- **Defect:** The draft calls a person who retreats a coward, then excuses it. The source rejects that label in a matter of survival.
- **Replacement:** There was no such thing as cowardice when it came to survival.
- **Rationale:** 비겁함이란 없다 denies cowardice rather than treating it as shame-free.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그렇게라도 살아서 돌아가고자 하는 이가 있다면, 나는 그를 기꺼이 보호할 것이다.
- **Current:** If someone wanted to live and return home, even if they had to do whatever it took, I’d gladly protect them.
- **Defect:** “Do whatever it took” loses the specific choice just offered: leaving the battle line.
- **Replacement:** If anyone wanted to fall back so they could make it home alive, I’d gladly protect them.
- **Rationale:** 그렇게라도 points back to 물러나라, Jin’s invitation to withdraw from the formation.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 자신들이 이곳에 있어야 하는 이유를 떠올리며, 놈들에 의해 죽어 간 그리운 얼굴들을 떠올리며.
- **Current:** Remembering why they had to be here. Remembering the familiar faces those monsters had killed.
- **Defect:** “Familiar” identifies the dead but drops the grief in 그리운.
- **Replacement:** Remembering why they had to be here. Remembering the faces of those they missed, killed by the monsters.
- **Rationale:** 그리운 conveys longing for the dead, not merely recognition.

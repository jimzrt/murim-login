# Review

The draft has one changed subject and several losses of meaning or tone.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 앞서 오신 분들께 미리 언질을 들었습니다. 우선 이리로.
- **Current:** I was informed in advance that you were coming. This way, please.
- **Defect:** Omits that the people who arrived earlier gave the Captain the advance notice.
- **Replacement:** Those who arrived before you told me to expect you. This way, please.
- **Rationale:** 앞서 오신 분들 identifies the source of the information.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 간지러움에 꺄르르 웃는 태산과, 그런 녀석에게 한바탕 쌍욕을 퍼부으려던 남호가 자신을 응시하는 호위장을 향해 허허 웃어 보였다.
- **Current:** Taishan giggled at the tickling while Namho prepared to unleash a torrent of curses at him. Then they both looked at the Captain of the Guards and gave him an awkward laugh.
- **Defect:** Makes both men laugh at the Captain; only Namho does. Taishan is laughing because he is ticklish.
- **Replacement:** Taishan giggled at the tickling. Namho was about to unleash a torrent of curses at him, but instead gave the Captain of the Guards, who was watching him, a hearty laugh.
- **Rationale:** 남호가 is the subject of the laugh directed at the Captain.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 저 값비싼 야명주를 쓴 미친놈이 이번에 죽은 성주라면, 그럭저럭 잘 뒈졌군.
- **Current:** If the bastard who used those expensive night-shining pearls was the City Lord who just died, then at least he died well.
- **Defect:** “Died well” suggests a good manner of death rather than Namho’s contemptuous approval that the man is dead.
- **Replacement:** If the bastard who put up those expensive night-shining pearls was the City Lord who just died, then good riddance to him.
- **Rationale:** 잘 뒈졌군 is a hostile judgment about the death, not a description of how he died.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 간혹 남호가 슬쩍 내비치는 이런 모습들을 보면 확실히 짬밥이라는 게 있구나 싶다.
- **Current:** When I saw Namho let his guard down and show this side of himself, I could tell he really did have years of experience behind him.
- **Defect:** Adds that Namho lets his guard down; the source says his experience occasionally shows through.
- **Replacement:** Whenever Namho let this side of himself show, even for a moment, I could tell he had years of experience behind him.
- **Rationale:** 슬쩍 내비치는 describes a subtle glimpse, not a lapse in vigilance.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 똥인지 춘장인지 꼭 찍어 먹어 봐야 압니까?
- **Current:** Do you have to taste-test shit to know it isn’t soybean paste?
- **Defect:** Soybean paste is not 춘장, weakening the comparison behind the joke.
- **Replacement:** Do you have to taste shit to tell it from black bean sauce?
- **Rationale:** 춘장 is the dark sauce used for jjajangmyeon; its appearance makes the comparison work.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 우리가 사천성주의 거처인 동시에 관청인 성주부(星州府)에 도착하기까지 걸린 시간이었다.
- **Current:** That was how long it took us to reach the City Lord’s residence—the City Lord’s residence and government offices, known as the City Lord’s Hall.
- **Defect:** Repeats “the City Lord’s residence” and obscures the building’s dual purpose.

- **Rationale:** 거처인 동시에 관청 states those two functions once.

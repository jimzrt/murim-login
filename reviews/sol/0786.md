# Review

Several action and subject errors need correction, along with a legal-status change and localized terminology and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이미 앞서 처참히 꺾이고 부서졌음에도, 서서히 회복해 나가던 누군가의 손아귀가 뿔과 함께 붙잡힌다.
- **Current:** Someone’s hand, horribly bent and broken moments ago but slowly healing, clamped down on the horn along with him.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the injured hand grasp the horn; the Skeleton King catches that hand along with the horn.
- **Replacement:** He caught the hand gripping the horn as well—a hand that had been horribly bent and broken earlier but was slowly healing.
- **Rationale:** 손아귀가 뿔과 함께 붙잡힌다 makes the hand something seized, not the actor seizing the horn.

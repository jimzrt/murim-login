# Review

One critical subject error misidentifies Jongni Chu as the Beggars’ Sect Leader; several additional passages alter an idiom, academic ranking, timing, hierarchy, or register.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 눈이 뒤집혔다.
- **Current:** My eyes bulged.
- **Defect:** The Korean idiom means that Taekyung flew into a rage or saw red, not that his eyes physically bulged.
- **Replacement:** I saw red.
- **Rationale:** 눈이 뒤집히다 describes losing one's temper here, as confirmed by the furious pursuit and shouted profanity that immediately follow.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 내신 7등급이어도 만유인력의 법칙은 알고 있다.
- **Current:** Even with my seventh-grade school record, I knew the law of universal gravitation.
- **Defect:** “Seventh-grade” implies a school year in English, whereas 7등급 is the seventh tier of Korea's academic ranking system.
- **Replacement:** Even with my seventh-tier grades, I knew the law of universal gravitation.
- **Rationale:** The joke is that Taekyung performed poorly in school but still understands basic gravity; the replacement preserves the numbered tier without turning it into a grade level.

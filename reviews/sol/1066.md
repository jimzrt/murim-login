# Review

The draft changes the meaning of the revenge passage, omits what the System certified about the Great Sir, and obscures how the approaching enemies used darkness.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 시스템이 미친놈이라고 인증마크까지 박아 준 이상 의심의 여지도 없다.
- **Current:** He’d been out of his mind for ages, and now the System had stamped him with an official seal of approval. There was no room left for doubt.
- **Defect:** The System certified him as a madman, not as generally approved or trustworthy.
- **Replacement:** He’d been out of his mind for ages, and now the System had officially certified him a Madman. There was no room left for doubt.
- **Rationale:** The certification explains why Taekyung stops trying to make sense of the Great Sir’s knowledge; it does not endorse him.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 본산에 속하지 않은 속가제자(俗家弟子)들까지 끌어모아야 가능한 숫자다.
- **Current:** would have to gather their Disciples from outside the main sect to reach those numbers.
- **Defect:** The draft loses the distinction that these are lay Disciples.
- **Replacement:** would have to call in even their lay Disciples outside the main sect to reach those numbers.
- **Rationale:** 속가제자 specifies lay Disciples, not every Disciple based away from the main sect.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 그건 빚을 갚기 위함인 동시에 이 땅에 어둠을 가져올 복수심이었고.

그 자체로 어둠을 밝힐 빛이었다.
- **Current:** They were here to repay their debt, yes—but also driven by the desire for revenge against the darkness descending on this land.

And that desire, in itself, was a light that could drive the darkness back.
- **Defect:** The draft makes darkness the target of their revenge. The source says their desire for revenge would bring darkness to this land, while also calling it a light that would illuminate darkness.
- **Replacement:** They had come to repay a debt, but they were also driven by a desire for revenge that would bring darkness to this land.

And that desire was, in itself, a light that would illuminate the darkness.
- **Rationale:** The source preserves a deliberate contrast between the darkness their revenge may bring and the light it represents.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 저 멀리에서부터 뒤늦게 전해진 미세한 진동과 함께, 알 수 없는 무언가의 괴성이 스산한 바람을 타고 귓가로 흘러들어왔다.
- **Current:** A faint tremor reached us from far away, belatedly followed by the eerie roar of something unknown, carried on the cold wind.
- **Defect:** The draft puts the roar after the tremor. In the source, the tremor arrives belatedly and the cry comes with it.
- **Replacement:** A faint tremor finally reached us from far away. With it came the eerie cry of something unknown, carried to my ears on the desolate wind.
- **Rationale:** 뒤늦게 modifies the arrival of the tremor; 함께 links the tremor and cry.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그리고 때아닌 불청객들을 맞이하기 위해 이른 마중을 나온 어둠 너머의 적들을 응시하며, 청해성에서의 첫 명령을 내렸다.
- **Current:** Then, watching the enemies beyond the darkness who’d come out to meet us early, prepared to greet the uninvited guests who’d arrived at such an ungodly hour. I gave my first command in Qinghai.
- **Defect:** The first sentence is a fragment, and its wording makes it unclear that the enemies have come out early to greet Taekyung’s group as unexpected visitors.
- **Replacement:** I stared into the darkness at the enemies who had come out early to greet their unexpected visitors, then gave my first command in Qinghai.
- **Rationale:** The enemies are the early welcoming party; Taekyung’s group are the unexpected guests.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 혈검마군(血劍魔君)도 피에 굶주린 살귀였지만, 그보다도 마음에 들지 않는 것이 바로 혈주였다.
- **Current:** The Blood-Sword Demon Lord had been a bloodthirsty Killing Ghost, but the Blood Lord was even more unbearable.
- **Defect:** Capitalizing “Killing Ghost” makes a descriptive insult look like the established sobriquet.
- **Replacement:** The Blood-Sword Demon Lord had been a bloodthirsty killer, but the Blood Lord was even more unbearable.
- **Rationale:** Here 살귀 describes the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s nature; the passage does not assign him a title.

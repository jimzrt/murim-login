# Review

Three semantic errors alter an action, the mission’s completion status, and the subject of a recalled fight; two localized passages require grammatical or idiomatic correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 단지 그것만으로도 시원한 향기가 코끝을 맴돌다가 이내 사라졌다.
- **Current:** Even that brief opening allowed a refreshing fragrance to linger at the tip of her nose before vanishing.
- **Defect:** The draft invents an opening of the tightly sealed casket. The fragrance appears merely from taking out and checking the casket.
- **Replacement:** Even that alone allowed a refreshing fragrance to linger at the tip of her nose before vanishing.
- **Rationale:** 단지 그것만으로도 means “even that alone” and introduces no act of opening; the preceding sentence explicitly describes the wooden casket as tightly sealed.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “하지만 받아들였지. 훌륭하게 해냈고.”
- **Current:** “But we accepted them. And we completed the mission admirably.”
- **Defect:** The draft falsely states that the escort mission has been completed, contradicting the chapter’s explicit statement that completion requires delivering the ginseng.
- **Replacement:** “But we accepted them. And we’ve handled them admirably.”
- **Rationale:** 해냈고 refers to successfully handling the difficult conditions thus far, not completing the still-unfinished escort mission.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 도무지 이해가 되지 않는다는 듯한 허준의 눈빛에 주화란이 무겁게 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** Ju Hwaran opened her mouth heavily beneath Heo Jun’s baffled gaze.
- **Defect:** “Opened her mouth heavily” is an unnatural Korean-syntax calque that obscures the intended grave manner of speaking.
- **Replacement:** Under Heo Jun’s utterly baffled gaze, Ju Hwaran spoke gravely.
- **Rationale:** 무겁게 입을 열었다 idiomatically means that she began speaking in a grave or serious manner.

## F04 — Minor

- **Source:** 종남파 장문인의 두 사형 중 한 사람.

- **Rationale:** The source identifies the Taeeul Merciless Sword as one of the Zhongnan Sect Leader’s two Senior Brothers.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 다음에 만날 때는 이번처럼 누군가와 박 터지게 싸우고 있지만 않기를 바랄 뿐이다.

- **Rationale:** The thought concerns what Taekyung hopes Baek Museong will not be doing when they next meet; 싸우고 있다 describes Baek being engaged in a fierce fight, without the added reversal.

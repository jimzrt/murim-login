## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 난 내일모레면 환갑이란다.
- **Current:** “I’ll be turning sixty the day after tomorrow.”
- **Defect:** The idiom 내일모레면 means that she is approaching sixty, not that her sixtieth birthday is literally in two days.

- **Rationale:** The current version invents an exact birthday date and changes the character’s age-related statement.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “감사합니다, 어머니.”

“그런데 왜 몬스터도 아니고, 여덟 살이나 차이 나는 여동생이랑 그렇게 싸워 대니?”

“어머니. 전 사람과는 싸우지 않습니다. 고로 저것은 제 여동생이 아니라 몬스터입니다.”
- **Current:** “Thank you, Mom.”

“Then why are you fighting so fiercely with your little sister, who’s eight years younger than you, instead of a monster?”

“Mom. I don’t fight people. Therefore, that thing isn’t my little sister. It’s a monster.”
- **Defect:** Both instances of 어머니 are flattened to the ordinary familiar “Mom,” losing Taekyung’s conspicuously formal, mock-deferential register while arguing for mercy.
- **Replacement:** “Thank you, Mother.”

“Then why do you keep fighting with your little sister, who’s eight years younger than you, instead of a monster?”

“Mother. I don’t fight people. Therefore, that thing isn’t my little sister. It’s a monster.”
- **Rationale:** The shift to 어머니 is part of the comic hierarchy and characterization; 싸워 대니 also means repeatedly fighting rather than necessarily fighting fiercely.

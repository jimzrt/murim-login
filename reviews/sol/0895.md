# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it assigns a male identity to the black-clad master, reverses a comparison of So Gyo’s strength, obscures whose Disciple Jin is, omits a rain transition, and replaces Cang Gong’s established name throughout.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 백연과 흑의인.
- **Current:** Baek Yeon and the man in black.
- **Defect:** “Man” assigns a male identity to the black-clad master, whom this account identifies as So Gyo.
- **Replacement:** Baek Yeon and the figure in black.
- **Rationale:** 흑의인 identifies the person by clothing, not gender.

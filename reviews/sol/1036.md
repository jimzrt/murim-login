# Review

The draft omits Jeok Cheongang’s rescue action and turns a hundred summoned weapons into a hundred mountains of weapons.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반경 수십여 장에 달하는 공간을 단숨에 집어삼킨 강대한 격돌의 중심에는, 석상처럼 굳어 있던 나를 밀어 내고 혈검마군을 막아선 적천강이 있었다.
- **Current:** At the center of that tremendous clash, which had swallowed up an area dozens of *jang* across, Jeok Cheongang stood between the Blood-Sword Demon Lord and me, who’d been frozen like a statue.
- **Defect:** Omits Jeok Cheongang pushing the frozen narrator away before stopping the Blood-Sword Demon Lord.
- **Replacement:** At the center of that tremendous clash, which had swallowed an area dozens of *jang* across, Jeok Cheongang had shoved me aside when I froze like a statue and stepped in to stop the Blood-Sword Demon Lord.
- **Rationale:** 밀어 내고 and 막아선 specify both actions and their order.

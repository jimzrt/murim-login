# Review

Actionable subject, currency-material, register, characterization, and wording errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “땀 냄새 납니다.”
- **Current:** “I smell like sweat.”
- **Defect:** The omitted subject is resolved incorrectly as the speaker; Wipeng is telling Jin Mukyung that Mukyung smells of sweat.
- **Replacement:** “You smell like sweat.”
- **Rationale:** The surrounding exchange addresses Jin Mukyung directly: he came from training, smells of sweat, and is therefore ordered to wash.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 은자 천 냥이면 철전으로는 십만 냥이다.
- **Current:** A thousand silver nyang was one hundred thousand nyang in copper coins.
- **Defect:** The coin material is changed from iron to copper.
- **Replacement:** A thousand silver nyang was worth one hundred thousand nyang in iron coins.
- **Rationale:** 철전 explicitly means iron coins, and the source gives the conversion in those terms.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “은자 천 냥. 철전으로는 십만 냥.”
- **Current:** “A thousand silver nyang. One hundred thousand nyang in copper coins.”
- **Defect:** The repeated conversion again changes iron coins to copper coins.
- **Replacement:** “A thousand silver nyang. That’s one hundred thousand nyang in iron coins.”
- **Rationale:** 철전 denotes iron coins; the material must remain consistent in Hong Jin’s explanation.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “괜찮은 유생은 무슨. 자넨 눈도 없나?”
- **Current:** “Decent scholars, my ass. Do you have no eyes?”
- **Defect:** The translation adds explicit profanity to a dismissive but nonprofane retort, unnecessarily intensifying Jin Wikyung’s register.
- **Replacement:** “Decent scholars? Please. Do you have no eye for people?”
- **Rationale:** 무슨 dismisses Wipeng’s description without the obscenity introduced by “my ass,” while 눈도 없나 questions his judgment of people.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 진위경 역시 들어 본 기억이 있다. 군부의 꼭대기에 앉아 혼자서 어린 왕을 쥐락펴락한다는 내관에 관한 소문을.
- **Current:** Jin Wikyung had heard the rumors about the palace attendant who sat at the top of the military hierarchy and controlled the young prince with a single hand.
- **Defect:** “With a single hand” literalizes 혼자서 and fails to convey that Hong Jin allegedly dominates and manipulates the prince single-handedly.
- **Replacement:** Jin Wikyung had heard the rumors about the palace attendant who sat at the top of the military hierarchy and single-handedly kept the young prince under his thumb.
- **Rationale:** 혼자서 means by himself, while 쥐락펴락하다 means to control or manipulate someone at will.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그를 뇌물에 넘어간 속물처럼 표현하는 홍진이 곱게 보이지 않는 것이다.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “본가에 이 정도로 사람이 없나? 아니지, 산서성에 기재가 이 정도로 없어?”
- **Current:** “Are there really this few people in the main family? No, wait. Are there really this few capable people in all of Shanxi Province?”
- **Defect:** “Main family” does not follow the binding rendering of 본가, and both instances of “this few” are awkward in this question.
- **Replacement:** “Does our family really have so few people? No—are there really so few capable people in all of Shanxi Province?”
- **Rationale:** 본가 refers to the speaker’s own family here, and “so few” is the natural English degree construction.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 정도면 탔다, 탔어.
- **Current:** At this point, he was cooked—cooked through.
- **Defect:** The punchline changes 탔다, meaning that Wikyung has burned, into being merely cooked through, weakening the escalation from 구워삶다.
- **Replacement:** At this point, he was burned—burned to a crisp.
- **Rationale:** The joke escalates from figuratively cooking someone into submission to having thoroughly burned him.

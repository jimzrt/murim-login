# Review

One reversed negation, two meaningful fidelity errors, and several localized terminology, phrasing, and punctuation issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “허, 참. 웃기지도 않은 놈이로고. 노부가 네놈 주려고 가져온 줄 아느냐?”
- **Current:** “Hah. What an amusing fellow. Do you think I brought it for you?”
- **Defect:** The translation reverses the explicit negation: Jeok calls Taekyung unfunny, not amusing.
- **Replacement:** “Hah. You’re not even funny. Did you think I brought it for you?”
- **Rationale:** 웃기지도 않은 means “not even funny,” continuing Jeok’s irritation rather than expressing amusement.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 형편에도 맞지 않는 값비싼 안전 구역(Safety Sector)에 가족이 살 집을 마련하고, 어머니의 병원비를 대기 위해 쉴 틈 없이 레이드에 매달렸던 F급 헌터였다면 상어를 이용한 꼼수 따위는 부리지 않았을 거다.
- **Current:** If I had still been the F-rank Hunter who had rented a home for my family in an expensive Safety Sector far beyond our means and thrown myself into raids without rest to pay my mother’s hospital bills, I would never have used some cheap trick involving sharks.
- **Defect:** The draft adds the unsupported detail that the family home was rented.
- **Replacement:** If I had still been the F-rank Hunter who had secured a home for my family in an expensive Safety Sector far beyond our means and thrown myself into raids without rest to pay my mother’s hospital bills, I would never have used some cheap trick involving sharks.
- **Rationale:** 집을 마련하고 says he obtained or secured a home; it does not specify whether he rented or purchased it.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “아무래도 찝찝해서. 그럴 생각입니다.”
- **Current:** “I have a bad feeling about this. That’s what I intend to do.”
- **Defect:** “A bad feeling” suggests a premonition of danger, while Taekyung means that his earlier shortcut still leaves him morally uneasy.

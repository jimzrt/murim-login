# Review

Several actionable fidelity errors alter lineage, a referent, a location, and Ju Hwaran’s central emotional question; additional issues affect causality, quantity, terminology, and a named martial-arts joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 말했듯이 광동진가의 명맥은 이미 끊겼다.
- **Current:** As I said, the Guangdong Chen Family’s bloodline has already died out.
- **Defect:** “Bloodline” incorrectly claims that the family’s biological descendants are extinct even though Song Ilseom has just revealed that he is one of them. 명맥 here refers to the family’s continuing institutional line or existence.
- **Replacement:** As I said, the Guangdong Chen Family ceased to exist long ago.
- **Rationale:** The source says the family’s 명맥 was severed, not that no biological descendants remain.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 탐욕은 정파와 사마외도를 가리지 않는다.
- **Current:** Greed makes no distinction between the orthodox factions and demonic, heterodox arts.
- **Defect:** The current sentence incorrectly places organizations and martial arts in parallel as things distinguished by greed. The source means greedy people exist on both sides.
- **Replacement:** Greed does not discriminate between members of orthodox factions and practitioners of demonic, heterodox arts.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the established terminology while restoring the source’s contrast between people belonging to the orthodox and heterodox sides.

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 주 소저도 사람이잖아요. 못해도 되고, 힘내지 않아도 괜찮아요.
- **Current:** Young Lady Ju, you’re a person too. It’s okay if you can’t do it. And it’s okay if you don’t have the strength to keep going.
- **Defect:** The translation changes voluntary permission not to force herself to stay strong into an inability to continue, and “can’t do it” fails to answer her concern about not doing well.
- **Replacement:** Young Lady Ju, you’re only human too. It’s okay if you don’t do well, and it’s okay not to force yourself to stay strong.

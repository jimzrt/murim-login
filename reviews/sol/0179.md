# Review

The draft contains two consequential subject changes, several losses of mouth-full speech and profanity, and localized action and pragmatic inaccuracies.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “으이! 마이어으! 드스 브스으!”

“……너나 많이 드세요.”

“즈 므그스느드!”
- **Current:** “Here! It’s delicious! Try some!”

“…You eat plenty.”

“I’m eating!”
- **Defect:** Cheongpung's words are clearly distorted because his mouth is full, but the draft renders them as ordinary speech. The final line also changes his response from the mouth-full equivalent of “Thanks, I will” to the ongoing action “I’m eating.”
- **Replacement:** “Hewe! It’s delishush! Try shome!”

“…You eat plenty.”

“Thanksh, I will!”
- **Rationale:** The phonetic distortions are the physical mechanism of the joke, and 먹겠습니다 expresses an eager acceptance rather than a statement that he is already eating.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 내 비록 무림인은 아니지만 보고 들은 경륜은 제법 되니 기대해 보게.
- **Current:** I may not be a martial artist, but I’ve seen and heard enough in my time, so you should look forward to it.
- **Defect:** “Look forward to it” implies positive anticipation. Jang Taebo is instead invoking his worldly experience to tell Taekyung to trust his prediction that a large, disorderly crowd will appear.
- **Replacement:** I may not be a martial artist, but I have enough worldly experience to know. Just wait and see.
- **Rationale:** 기대해 보게 functions here as confidence in Jang Taebo's forecast, not an invitation to anticipate the event with pleasure.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “은자 만 냥을? 태원 진가 기둥뿌리를 죄다 뽑아야겠구먼.”
- **Current:** “Ten thousand silver nyang? We’ll have to pull up every last pillar of the Jin Family of Taiyuan.”
- **Defect:** The inclusive “we” makes Jang Taebo share responsibility for paying his own fee. He is telling Taekyung that such a payment would strip the Jin Family of Taiyuan bare.
- **Replacement:** “Ten thousand silver nyang? You’d have to pull up every last pillar of the Jin Family of Taiyuan.”
- **Rationale:** The implied subject is Taekyung and his family, whose money Taekyung has just offered to spend; Jang Taebo is the proposed recipient.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저 새끼는 자서전 써도 될 것 같다. 제목은 [맞아 뒤지는 방법 100선].
- **Current:** That man could probably write an autobiography. He could call it *A Hundred Ways to Get Yourself Beaten to Death*.
- **Defect:** “That man” sanitizes the strongly contemptuous 저 새끼 and weakens Taekyung's profane narrative voice.
- **Replacement:** That bastard could probably write an autobiography. He could call it *A Hundred Ways to Get Yourself Beaten to Death*.
- **Rationale:** 새끼 is a direct insult here, prompted by Hyuk Mujin's shameless request, and should not be neutralized.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘시발, 화염신장.’
- **Current:** *Damn it. Flame Divine Palm.*
- **Defect:** “Damn it” softens the explicitly strong profanity 시발 to the same level as the chapter's separate uses of 젠장.
- **Replacement:** *Fuck. Flame Divine Palm.*
- **Rationale:** The source deliberately escalates to stronger profanity when Taekyung realizes Jeok Cheongang is heating the space beneath the table with the Flame Divine Palm.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “네놈이 처먹었지.”
- **Current:** “You ate it.”
- **Defect:** The neutral verb “ate” removes the contemptuous vulgarity of 처먹었지 and flattens Jeok Cheongang's insulting delivery.
- **Replacement:** “You swallowed the damn thing.”
- **Rationale:** 처먹다 is a deliberately coarse form of “eat”; the replacement preserves that register while remaining natural for a pill.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “제가 익힐 수도 있습니다!”
- **Current:** “I could learn them!”
- **Defect:** The plural pronoun treats all the possessions as learnable, even though Taekyung is referring to learning the martial art recorded in the manual.
- **Replacement:** “What if I learn the martial art?”
- **Rationale:** 익히다 in this context means mastering or practicing the Flame Divine Palm, not learning the physical items collectively.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘아니, 시발.’
- **Current:** *No, damn it.*
- **Defect:** The draft again softens 시발 and produces an unnatural English reaction.
- **Replacement:** *What the fuck?*
- **Rationale:** The source is Taekyung's profane disbelief at being forced to retain property he is trying to return.

## F09 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “하하버히, 가히게호?”
- **Current:** “G-Grandpa, are we g-going?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the subject from Jeok Cheongang to a collective “we” and represents mouth-obstructed speech as nervous stuttering.
- **Replacement:** “Gwandpa, awe you leaving?”
- **Rationale:** 가시게요 asks whether the addressed elder is leaving, while the distorted syllables show that Cheongpung is speaking with his mouth full.

## F10 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 장태보와도 눈인사를 주고받은 그는 의미심장한 한마디를 던지고 돌아섰다.
- **Current:** After exchanging a nod with Jang Taebo, Jeok Cheongang turned away and tossed out one meaningful remark.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an exchanged greeting made with the eyes into a nod, introducing a different physical action.
- **Replacement:** After exchanging a silent glance of farewell with Jang Taebo, Jeok Cheongang turned away and tossed out one meaningful remark.
- **Rationale:** 눈인사 specifically denotes acknowledging or greeting someone with the eyes; the source does not describe either man nodding.

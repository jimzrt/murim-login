# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but several passages change who bears the risk, misstate an East Depot title, or lose a joke’s referent.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 황실과 관련된 이상, 이건 개인의 일탈로 취급될 수 있는 일이 아니다.
- **Current:** With the imperial family involved, this couldn’t be treated as some personal act of rebellion.
- **Defect:** “Personal act of rebellion” narrows the point. Taekyung fears the affair cannot be dismissed as one person’s misconduct, leaving his family exposed.
- **Replacement:** With the imperial family involved, this couldn’t be dismissed as one person’s wrongdoing.
- **Rationale:** 개인의 일탈 means an individual transgression, not specifically an individual rebellion.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 동창의 수장인 장인태감(掌印太監). 즉 창공은 뜻을 함께하는 동지들에게 바로 그런 잡초가 되어 살아남기를 종용했다.
- **Current:** The Director—the East Depot’s Chief Eunuch, the Chief Eunuch of the Seal—had urged his like-minded comrades to survive by becoming weeds like that.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the specific Seal-Holding Eunuch title into two generic Chief Eunuch titles.
- **Replacement:** The Director, the East Depot’s head and its Seal-Holding Eunuch, had urged his like-minded comrades to survive by becoming weeds like that.
- **Rationale:** 장인태감 identifies the eunuch who holds the seal; 동창의 수장 separately identifies him as head of the East Depot.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 호법이라는 새끼가 그 정도로 깊게 잠들면 털끝 하나 빼고 다 손댔겠다.
- **Current:** If you fall that deeply asleep while you’re supposed to be standing guard, they could do anything to you except pluck out a hair.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Mujin the person intruders could touch. Taekyung means Mujin’s sleep would leave Taekyung himself unprotected, prompting Mujin’s mistaken sexual interpretation.
- **Replacement:** If my bodyguard sleeps that soundly, they could lay hands on every part of me but a single hair.
- **Rationale:** Mujin immediately asks who has already laid a hand on the Captain, confirming that Taekyung is the implied target.

## F04 — Major

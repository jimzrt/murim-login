# Review

Two critical fidelity errors alter the door-opening subject and the number of Beggars’ Sect disciples. Additional findings address omitted humor, added profanity, an unsupported group count, hierarchy terminology, and localized English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 문을 열고 들어오는 낯익은 얼굴을 확인한 나는 망설이지 않고 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** The moment I opened the door and saw the familiar face walking in, I spoke without hesitation.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the physical action by making Taekyung open the door. The familiar visitor is the one opening it and entering.
- **Replacement:** The moment I saw the familiar face open the door and walk in, I spoke without hesitation.
- **Rationale:** 문을 열고 들어오는 modifies 낯익은 얼굴, so the familiar person opens the door and comes inside.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 언젠가 십만 개방도에게 둘러싸여 매타작당하는 날이 올 거다.
- **Current:** One day, you’ll find yourself surrounded by ten thousand Beggars’ Sect disciples and beaten half to death.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces the explicitly stated number from one hundred thousand to ten thousand.
- **Replacement:** One day, you’ll find yourself surrounded by a hundred thousand Beggars’ Sect disciples and beaten half to death.
- **Rationale:** 십만 means one hundred thousand, not ten thousand.

- **Source:** 오늘 하루만 살 것처럼 구는 낭인들은 물론, 소위 명문대파라 불리는 대문파나 세가의 문하 제자들도 그렇다.
- **Current:** That included wandering martial artists who acted as if they would only live for the day, as well as disciples of the great sects and families known as prestigious major factions.
- **Defect:** The draft flattens the established Murim category 세가 to generic “families” and leaves the relationship between the sects, great families, and prestigious factions awkwardly expressed.
- **Replacement:** That included not only wandering martial artists who acted as though they lived only for today, but also disciples of the great sects and great families collectively known as prestigious major factions.
- **Rationale:** 세가 is the established category “great family,” while 대문파 and 세가 are both included among the so-called 명문대파.

## F08 — Minor

- **Current:** “Young Lady Ju, why don’t you accept a drink?”

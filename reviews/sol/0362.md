# Review

The draft contains one subject reversal that breaks a punchline, omits a named transfer technique and a hostile reaction, and weakens a tactical causal explanation. Several localized phrases also need grammatical or idiomatic correction.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “인정하지. 자네는 괴력난신, 그 자체야.”
- **Current:** “I admit it. You are supernatural powers incarnate.”
- **Defect:** The established plural term “supernatural powers” cannot naturally function as the complement of “incarnate” in this construction.
- **Replacement:** “I admit it. You are the very embodiment of supernatural powers.”
- **Rationale:** The source calls Jin Taekyung 괴력난신 itself; “the very embodiment of supernatural powers” preserves that identification while producing grammatical English.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이것이야말로 괴력난신이며, 이적(異蹟)이 아닌가.
- **Current:** Was this not supernatural powers? Was this not a miracle?
- **Defect:** “Was this not supernatural powers?” is ungrammatical because the plural abstract term cannot serve as this singular predicate.
- **Replacement:** Was this not the very manifestation of supernatural powers—a miracle?
- **Rationale:** The source identifies the recovery as both 괴력난신 and a miracle; the replacement preserves both descriptions in natural English.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그러나 천력마가 격체전공(隔體傳功)을 통해 넘겨준 막대한 공력이 더해지니 이야기가 달라졌다.
- **Current:** But once the enormous internal energy the Heavenly Power Demon had transferred to me was added, everything changed.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the explicitly named technique through which the internal energy was transferred, losing part of the mechanical explanation and an established term.
- **Replacement:** But the enormous internal energy the Heavenly Power Demon had given me through Transmitting Internal Energy Across the Body changed everything.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly states that the transfer occurred through 격체전공, whose binding rendering is “Transmitting Internal Energy Across the Body.”

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “넌 내가 꼭…….”
- **Current:** “You’re going to make me…”
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the subject of the unfinished threat: Taekyung is saying that he will do something to his opponent, not that his opponent will make him do something.
- **Replacement:** “I swear, I’m going to…”
- **Rationale:** 넌 내가 꼭 begins a first-person threat directed at “you” and leads into “I’m going to circumcise that mouth of yours.” The current line reverses that relationship and leaves the punchline grammatically disconnected.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마음이 흔들렸으니 검 또한 흔들리기 마련.
- **Current:** His heart had been shaken, so naturally his sword had been shaken as well.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Taekyung’s causal tactical prediction into an awkward statement that both the opponent’s literal heart and sword have already been shaken.
- **Replacement:** With his composure shaken, his sword was bound to waver as well.

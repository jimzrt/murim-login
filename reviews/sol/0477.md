# Review

Several localized fidelity defects remain, including a metaphor turned into a literal event, omitted cultural and characterization cues, an omitted description of the Zaha Divine Technique, and altered agency in the dragon’s final effort.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 꼭 공부가 성공의 왕도라고 생각하지 않으셨고, 고등학교 시절 전 과목 7등급으로 슬롯머신 잭팟을 찍었던 성적표를 보시고도 짤막한 한 마디로 훈계를 마무리 지으셨다.
- **Current:** She never believed studying was the only path to success. Even when she saw my report card from high school, where I had scored a seven in every subject and hit the jackpot on a slot machine, she ended her lecture with a single short sentence.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung literally hit a slot-machine jackpot in addition to receiving sevens. The source compares the row of sevens on his report card to a slot-machine jackpot.
- **Replacement:** She never believed studying was the only path to success. Even when she saw my high school report card—sevens in every subject, like a slot-machine jackpot—she ended her lecture with one short remark.
- **Rationale:** The jackpot is a visual joke about the repeated sevens, not a separate event in Taekyung’s past.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 밧줄 대신 청테이프로 꽁꽁 묶인 어린 딸을 본 어머니는 비명을 질렀고, 효자손으로 불효자가 된 아들의 종아리를 때렸지만
- **Current:** When my mother saw her young daughter bound from head to toe with blue packing tape, she screamed. Then she beat the calves of her now-unfilial son with a back scratcher.
- **Defect:** Rendering 효자손 only as “back scratcher” loses the explicit wordplay between the implement’s name, literally “filial son’s hand,” and the son who has become unfilial.
- **Replacement:** When my mother saw her young daughter bound from head to toe with blue packing tape, she screamed. Then she beat the calves of her now-unfilial son with a *hyojason*.[^1]

[^1]: A Korean back scratcher whose name literally means “filial son’s hand.”
- **Rationale:** The source directly contrasts 효자손 with 불효자가 된 아들; the cultural term and concise footnote preserve that joke.

## F03 — Minor

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 나는 실현할 수 없으리라 생각했던 동심을 떠올리며 입을 열었다.

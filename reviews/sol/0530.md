- **Source:** 기특하긴 시벌.

큰일 날 것 같다고 한 게 진짜 큰일 말하는 거였네.

When he said something serious might happen, he had meant something genuinely serious.

- **Replacement:** Endearing, my ass.

When he said something serious might happen, he’d meant a real emergency.
- **Rationale:** 기특하긴 시벌 explicitly repudiates Taekyung’s thought that Hyuk Mujin was being endearing, with strong profanity, before continuing the 큰일 wordplay.

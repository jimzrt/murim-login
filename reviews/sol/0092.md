# Review

The draft contains two reversed or misassigned actions, one altered characterization, and one incorrect fly-species term.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 하지만 임창수 팀장, 아니 임창수 헌터와 해당 레이드에 참여했던 인원들의 증언에 의하면 진태경은 B급 몬스터인 미노타우로스 무리를 단신으로 해치웠습니다.
- **Current:** “But according to the testimony of Hunter Im Changsoo—or Team Leader Im Changsoo at the time—and the others who participated in that raid, Jin Taekyung single-handedly defeated a group of B-rank monsters, the Minotaurs.”
- **Defect:** The self-correction is reversed. The speaker starts with Im Changsoo’s former title, Team Leader, then corrects himself to Hunter; the draft instead starts with Hunter and identifies Team Leader as his title at the time.
- **Replacement:** “But according to the testimony of Team Leader Im Changsoo—no, Hunter Im Changsoo—and the others who participated in that raid, Jin Taekyung single-handedly defeated a group of B-rank monsters, the Minotaurs.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean explicitly orders the titles as ‘Team Leader Im Changsoo—no, Hunter Im Changsoo,’ reflecting that Changsoo is no longer a Team Leader.

- **Defect:** The draft softens the contemptuous description of the Team Leader while intensifying the Guild Master’s characterization into stupidity. The source calls the former insolent and the latter clueless or guileless, with a plain chuckle rather than foolish laughter.

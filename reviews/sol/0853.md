# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes a retainer’s identity, a physical restraint, a negated remark, and an alternative route.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제 말이라면 절대적으로 충성하는 가솔입니다.
- **Current:** He’s a member of the family who’s absolutely loyal to me.
- **Defect:** Calls a household retainer a family member, changing his relationship to Tang Sadok.
- **Replacement:** He’s a household retainer who’s absolutely loyal to me.
- **Rationale:** 가솔 refers to a household retainer here, not a relative.

## F02 — Critical

# Review

The draft changes the number of pursuits, reverses an explanation of the undead, and changes who would be left behind. It also loses a register distinction and a Buddhist profanity joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 산맥을 완전히 내려오기도 전에 있었던 첫 습격을 시작으로, 이미 세 번이나 적들의 추격을 허용한 상황.
- **Current:** We’d been ambushed once before we’d even come down from the mountain range. Since then, we’d let the enemy catch up three times.
- **Defect:** “Since then” makes the first attack separate from the three pursuits; the source counts it as the first of three.
- **Replacement:** The first attack had come before we’d even made it all the way down from the mountain range. By now, the enemy had caught up with us three times.
- **Rationale:** 첫 습격을 시작으로 identifies the early attack as the start of the three pursuits.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 죽지 않았으나 이미 죽은 것이나 다름없는 존재이기에 감각도 잊었으며, 그와 같은 이유로 피로조차 느끼지 못한다.
- **Current:** They were no longer alive, but they weren’t dead, either—and for the same reason, they’d forgotten how to feel. They couldn’t even get tired.
- **Defect:** The draft says they were not dead; the source says they had not died but were already as good as dead.
- **Replacement:** They hadn’t died, yet they were already as good as dead. They’d forgotten sensation, and for the same reason, they couldn’t even feel fatigue.
- **Rationale:** 죽지 않았으나 이미 죽은 것이나 다름없는 preserves the distinction between not having died and being as good as dead.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나중에 뒤처졌을 때 버리고 가면 그만이니까.
- **Current:** When you fall behind, we can leave it behind.
- **Defect:** The threat is to abandon Jeong Hogun, not the armor.
- **Replacement:** When you fall behind, we can just leave you behind.
- **Rationale:** 뒤처졌을 때 refers to Jeong falling behind; the implicit object of 버리고 가면 is him.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 설령 내가 아니라 석가모니라 할지라도 아미시벌 관세음뒤짐을 외치며 목탁으로 대인의 뚝배기를 깼을 것이다.
- **Current:** Even if I’d been Shakyamuni himself, I’d have cursed the heavens and cracked Great Sir over the head with a wooden fish.
- **Defect:** “Cursed the heavens” removes the profane parody of Buddhist invocations.
- **Replacement:** Even Shakyamuni himself would have shouted, “Amitabha, fuck this! Guanyin, drop dead!” and cracked Great Sir’s skull with a wooden fish.
- **Rationale:** 아미시벌 관세음뒤짐 turns Buddhist names into an expletive and a death wish; the blasphemous joke is the point of the line.

## F06 — Minor

- **Source:** 우리의 목적지, 곤륜파를 비롯한 청해성의 아군들이 머무르고 있을 저 머나먼 동쪽으로.

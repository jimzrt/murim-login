# Review

One subject reversal obscures the Blood Lord’s taunt; several smaller issues affect timing, address, and phrasing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무사하다 못해 훌륭하지. 날 봐, 이 힘이 느껴지지 않나?
- **Current:** “He’s more than safe. He’s doing great. Look at me. Can’t you feel this power?”
- **Defect:** The Blood Lord answers a question about Jin Taekyung by talking about himself, not by saying Jin is doing great.
- **Replacement:** “Safe? I’m better than fine. Look at me. Can’t you feel this power?”
- **Rationale:** His invitation to look at him and feel his power makes the shift of subject the point of the reply.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 본능적으로 온 힘을 다해 내게 잡힌 손목을 빼내려 하지만, 그보다 한발 앞서 뻗어 나온 뜨거운 열기가 놈을 휘감았다.
- **Current:** He instinctively used all his strength to wrench his wrist out of my grasp, but the scorching heat that shot out ahead of him wrapped around him first.
- **Defect:** “Ahead of him” makes the heat’s lead sound spatial rather than a matter of timing.
- **Replacement:** He instinctively tried to wrench his wrist out of my grasp with all his strength, but before he could, a surge of scorching heat reached out and engulfed him.
- **Rationale:** 그보다 한발 앞서 means the heat reaches him before he can free his wrist.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “노……야.”
- **Current:** “Gra…ndpa.”
- **Defect:** “Grandpa” gives Taekyung the familial address Cheongpung uses for Jeok Cheongang, rather than Taekyung’s respectful address.
- **Replacement:** “El…der.”
- **Rationale:** 노야 addresses an elder respectfully; it does not establish a grandfather relationship.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “길을. 길을 열어 주십시오.”
- **Current:** “Clear…a path for me.”
- **Defect:** The command loses the repeated plea and polite request.
- **Replacement:** “A path. Please open a path for me.”
- **Rationale:** The source repeats 길을 and ends with the polite request 주십시오.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 눈먼 칼이 어깨를 스친다.
- **Current:** A blind swing of a sword grazed my shoulder.
- **Defect:** “Blind swing” describes how someone swung; the source describes a stray blade amid the fighting.
- **Replacement:** A stray blade grazed my shoulder.
- **Rationale:** 눈먼 칼 identifies an indiscriminate or stray strike, without establishing the swordsman’s manner of swinging.

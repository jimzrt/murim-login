# Review

One critical subject reversal, one characterization distortion, and several localized natural-English issues require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “왜, 못 할 것 같나?”
- **Current:** “What? You think you can’t do that?”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Jin is challenging Huginn to doubt Jin’s ability to carry out the threat, not asking whether Huginn is incapable of obeying him.
- **Replacement:** “What? You think I can’t do it?”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 못 할 것 같나 is Jin, referring back to his threat to tear Huginn’s mouth open.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 미카엘 실베르트는 호랑이라고 해도 부족함이 없는 위인이지만, 적어도 지금의 나는 열화신룡(烈火神龍)이었다.
- **Current:** Michael Silbert was a great man for whom even the word *tiger* seemed insufficient. But at least right now, I was the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.
- **Defect:** “Great man” introduces an admiring or morally positive characterization absent from context, while “the word tiger seemed insufficient” is an unnatural rendering of the comparison.
- **Replacement:** Michael Silbert was formidable enough that even calling him a tiger would undersell him, but at least right now, I was the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.
- **Rationale:** 위인 refers contextually to a formidable individual, and 호랑이라고 해도 부족함이 없는 means that even likening him to a tiger would not fully capture how dangerous he is.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “당했습니다. 전부 이걸 위한 거였어요.”
- **Current:** “We were had. This was all for this.”
- **Defect:** The second sentence is an unnatural English calque, and the first is needlessly dated for the speaker’s urgent register.
- **Replacement:** “We’ve been played. This was what it was all for.”
- **Rationale:** The source says they were deceived and that everything preceding this moment had been in service of Michael’s present move.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마치 도망치듯 카메라를 피해 돌아선 내 시야에 빠르게 가까워지는 두 인영이 들어왔다.
- **Current:** As I turned away from the cameras as though fleeing, two figures quickly approached through my field of vision.
- **Defect:** “Approached through my field of vision” is not idiomatic English and obscures the simple action of Jin seeing the two figures approaching him.
- **Replacement:** As I turned away from the cameras as though fleeing, I saw two figures rapidly approaching.
- **Rationale:** The source states that the two approaching figures entered Jin’s view as he turned away from the cameras.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 그 수많은 고민 끝에, 문득 한 사람의 얼굴이 떠올랐다.
- **Current:** After all those questions tangled through my mind, one person’s face suddenly came to me.
- **Defect:** A face does not naturally “come to” someone in this sense; the intended English expression is “came to mind.”
- **Replacement:** After wrestling with all those questions, one person’s face suddenly came to mind.
- **Rationale:** 떠올랐다 means that the person’s face arose in Jin’s thoughts after his internal deliberation.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 커핏잔의 김을 억누르며 피어오르는 수증기 속에서, 나는 나직한 음성으로 속삭였다.
- **Current:** As vapor rose and suppressed the steam from the coffee cup, I whispered in a low voice.
- **Defect:** “Vapor suppressed the steam” is an awkward mechanical calque that makes two near-synonyms interact unnaturally.
- **Replacement:** As vapor billowed up, overpowering the steam from the coffee cup, I whispered in a low voice.
- **Rationale:** The heat from Jin’s hand produces vapor that overwhelms the gentler steam rising from the coffee.

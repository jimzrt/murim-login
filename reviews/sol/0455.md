# Review

One critical reversal of whether the needed intelligence was obtained, plus three meaningful action/mechanism errors and several localized terminology or English issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그러나 그중 대부분은 쓸모없거나 지나간 일에 불과했고, 가장 필요한 정보는 마지막까지 나오지 않았다.
- **Current:** Yet most of it was useless or concerned events from the distant past, and the information I needed most had not emerged until the very end.
- **Defect:** “Had not emerged until the very end” implies that the needed information finally emerged at the end. The source says it never emerged, even by the end of the interrogation.
- **Replacement:** Yet most of it was useless or concerned events from the past, and the information I needed most had never come out.
- **Rationale:** 마지막까지 나오지 않았다 explicitly means the information remained undisclosed through the end, which is central to Taekyung’s decision to continue the interrogation.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “궁금하면 당장 금제를 풀어라. 노부가 친히 알려 줄 테니.”
- **Current:** “If you’re curious, release this old man’s restraints right now. I’ll tell you personally.”

## F03 — Major

- **Source:** 처음 몇 시진은 악으로 버티더니, 고문이 막바지에 다다르자 죽간 다섯 개를 글자로 빼곡하게 채울 만큼 말이 많아졌으니까.
- **Current:** He had held out through sheer stubbornness for the first few shichen,[^2] but once the torture reached its final stages, he had become so talkative that he filled five bamboo slips with writing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 작은 중얼거림과 함께 혼절한 삼괴의 완맥을 틀어쥐었다.
- **Current:** With a quiet mutter, I seized the Three Fiends’ wrist and felt for his pulse.
- **Defect:** The draft adds a diagnostic pulse check that Taekyung does not perform here. He grips the wrist pulse point in preparation for channeling internal energy; the torturer checks the prisoner’s pulse later.

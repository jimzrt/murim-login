# Review

Several passages alter who is being described, resolve deliberate ambiguity, or soften the force of the source.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 감히 폭군이나 다름없는 상관의 싸움에 관여하지 못한 채 전투를 이어 가던 유목민들도
- **Current:** The nomads, still fighting without daring to interfere in their superior’s battle with someone who was practically a tyrant.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the superior’s opponent sound like the tyrant; the source calls the superior himself practically a tyrant.
- **Replacement:** The nomads kept fighting, not daring to interfere in the battle of their superior, who was practically a tyrant.
- **Rationale:** 폭군이나 다름없는 modifies 상관, not the person he is fighting.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 조금 전, 유난히도 선명하게 울려 퍼진 한 줄기의 절삭음은 아직도 그들의 귓가에 맴돌고 있었다.
- **Current:** The single, unusually clear sound of a blade slicing through flesh still rang in their ears.
- **Defect:** The draft specifies flesh before the onlookers understand what was cut.
- **Replacement:** The single, unusually clear slicing sound from moments ago still rang in their ears.
- **Rationale:** 절삭음 identifies a sound of cutting, but does not identify what was cut.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그리고 이내, 누군가의 새로운 피가 그 빈자리를 메웠다.
- **Current:** And soon, someone else’s fresh blood filled the empty space.
- **Defect:** “Someone else’s” asserts that the new blood belongs to a different person, though its owner has not yet been identified.
- **Replacement:** And soon, fresh blood from someone filled the space it had left.
- **Rationale:** 누군가 leaves the person unidentified; it does not establish a contrast with the owner of the blood already in the pool.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 자신조차 잊은 무아(無我)의 상황에서 새로운 경지로 발돋움한 이들은 늘 같은 대답을 하곤 했으니까.
- **Current:** Those who stepped into a new realm in a state of selflessness they couldn’t even remember always gave the same answer.
- **Defect:** The draft says they cannot remember the state; the source says they lose awareness of themselves while in it.
- **Replacement:** Those who stepped into a new realm in a state of selflessness, forgetting even themselves, always gave the same answer.
- **Rationale:** 자신조차 잊은 describes losing oneself in the experience, not an inability to recall it afterward.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아니, 애써 모르는 척하며.
- **Current:** Or perhaps he was simply pretending not to notice.
- **Defect:** “Perhaps” turns the narrator’s correction into speculation.
- **Replacement:** No—he was making an effort to pretend he hadn’t noticed.
- **Rationale:** 아니 corrects the preceding claim that he had no idea; 애써 makes his pretense deliberate.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “감히 이 땅을 침범한 오랑캐들을 모조리 쓸어 버려라!”
- **Current:** “Drive every barbarian who dares invade this land out of here!”
- **Defect:** “Drive ... out” turns an order to wipe out the invaders into an order merely to expel them.
- **Replacement:** “Wipe out every barbarian who dared invade this land!”
- **Rationale:** 모조리 쓸어 버려라 calls for their complete destruction in this battle.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그리고 그 중심에, 비틀거리는 신형으로 무수한 피를 집어삼키는 한 귀신이 있었다.
- **Current:** And at the center of it all was a demon, staggering as he swallowed up countless lives.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces blood with lives and changes the physical image.
- **Replacement:** And at the center of it all was a ghost, staggering as he drank in blood without end.
- **Rationale:** 무수한 피 is blood, and 귀신 evokes a ghostlike figure rather than naming him a demon.

# Review

Several actionable fidelity errors alter quantities, physical actions, characterization, and the final comedic beat.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 불과 몇 초 전, 내가 서 있던 자리를 중심으로 반경 수백여 미터가 초토화되어 있었다.
- **Current:** An area several hundred meters across, centered on the spot where I had been standing only a few seconds earlier, had been reduced to ruins.
- **Defect:** The draft changes a radius of several hundred meters into a total width of several hundred meters, substantially reducing the stated blast area.
- **Replacement:** The area within a radius of several hundred meters, centered on the spot where I had been standing only a few seconds earlier, had been devastated.
- **Rationale:** 반경 explicitly means “radius,” not “across.”

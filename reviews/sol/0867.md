# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes one location detail and loses or adds several meaningful cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 설마 궁 안까지 발을 디딘 것은 아니겠지.
- **Current:** “Don’t tell me he made it into the inner palace.”
- **Defect:** “Inner palace” specifies a distinct part of the palace that the source does not name.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t tell me he got inside the palace.”
- **Rationale:** Baek Yeon asks whether the intruder entered the palace, not whether he reached an inner-palace precinct.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 얇은 비단 사이로 눈부신 백의(白衣)를 걸친 누군가의 뒷모습이 비쳤다.
- **Current:** the silhouette of someone in dazzling white showed through the thin curtains.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the person is seen from behind.
- **Replacement:** the back of a figure dressed in dazzling white showed through the thin curtains.
- **Rationale:** 뒷모습 specifically identifies a view of the person’s back.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** “부디 잊지 마시오. 그날의 약조를.”
- **Current:** “Please don’t forget the promise you made that day.”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the promise solely to the Emperor, though the source leaves who made it unstated.
- **Replacement:** “Please don’t forget the promise from that day.”
- **Rationale:** 그날의 약조 identifies the promise by its occasion, without specifying its maker.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.84
- **Source:** 천자가 실소하듯 중얼거렸다.
- **Current:** The Son of Heaven muttered with a scornful laugh.
- **Defect:** “Scornful” adds contempt to the Emperor’s reaction.
- **Replacement:** The Son of Heaven muttered with a short, incredulous laugh.
- **Rationale:** 실소 suggests an involuntary or incredulous laugh here, not necessarily scorn.

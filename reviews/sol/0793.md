# Review

The draft preserves the main events, but obscures two jokes, leaves a consequential pronoun ambiguous, and retains prohibited brackets around Quest titles.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어, 어떻게 진 선생님처럼 누추하신 분이 이 귀한 곳까지!
- **Current:** “H-how could someone as humble as Mr. Jin come all the way to a place this precious?”
- **Defect:** “Humble” turns Xiao Shen’s accidental insult into a compliment, weakening Jin’s correction.
- **Replacement:** “H-how could someone as shabby as Mr. Jin come all the way to a place this precious?”

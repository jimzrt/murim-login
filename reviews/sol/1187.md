# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes the mist’s lure, anticipates the disappearance, and blurs two character beats.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 지금 이대로 안개에 몸을 맡기면 모든 것이 편해질 것만 같은 기분.
- **Current:** if he gave himself over to the mist now, everything would be at peace.
- **Defect:** The mist promises relief from his troubles, not that everything will be peaceful.
- **Replacement:** if he gave himself over to the mist now, everything would be easier.
- **Rationale:** 모든 것이 편해질 describes things becoming easier for him; the peaceful paradise is introduced in the following sentence.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 보이지 않는다.
- **Current:** They were gone.
- **Defect:** The draft declares the companions gone before Jeok reaches that conclusion.
- **Replacement:** He couldn't see them.
- **Rationale:** Jeok first notices that he cannot see them, then corrects himself with 사라졌다—'They disappeared.'

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그냥 참아라. 털끝 하나 정도는 상해도 안 죽는다.
- **Current:** Just put up with it. Losing a hair or two won’t kill you.
- **Defect:** The punchline changes one hair being harmed into losing multiple hairs.
- **Replacement:** Just bear with it. One hair getting hurt won’t kill you.
- **Rationale:** 털끝 하나 repeats the single-hair promise Jeok has just begun to make, then abruptly lowers his standard.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 무엇 하느냐. 당장 쳐 기어 나오지 않고.
- **Current:** What are you waiting for? Get your asses out here!
- **Defect:** The command drops Jeok’s contemptuous demand that the hidden beings crawl out.
- **Replacement:** What are you waiting for? Crawl the hell out here!
- **Rationale:** 쳐 기어 나오지 않고 is a rough, belittling demand to crawl out, not merely to come out.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그와 동시에 바람을 만난 등불이 꺼지듯, 힘없이 사그라지는 안광에 누군가의 모습이 비쳤다.
- **Current:** At the same time, the gleaming eyes went out as helplessly as a lamp in the wind, and someone’s face appeared in them.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the eyes themselves go out and specifies a face before the source does.
- **Replacement:** At the same time, the fierce light in his eyes faded like a lamp caught by the wind, and someone’s figure was reflected in them.
- **Rationale:** 안광 is the light in Jeok’s eyes; 누군가의 모습 is someone’s appearance. The next sentence identifies it as a face.

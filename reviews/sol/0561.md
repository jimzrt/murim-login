# Review

One critical subject reversal and several localized fidelity, terminology, and natural-English defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진작 이렇게 나왔어야지. 뒤질라고.
- **Current:** *You should’ve come around to this from the start. You trying to get yourself killed?*
- **Defect:** The first sentence reverses the omitted subject. Taekyung is thinking that he himself should have threatened the Skeleton King’s smartphone from the start, not that the Skeleton King should have changed his attitude.
- **Replacement:** *I should’ve done this from the start. You trying to get yourself killed?*
- **Rationale:** 이렇게 나왔어야지 refers to Taekyung’s newly effective approach of threatening the phone; the following 뒤질라고 remains directed at the Skeleton King.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 정말 괜찮으니까, 어서 가 봐. 아레스 길드에도 분명히 좋은 사람들이 많을 텐데. 고작 이런 사적인 이유로 돕지 않는다면 두고두고 후회할 거다. 내가 아는 너는 그런 사람이야.

“……!”
- **Current:** “I’m really fine, so go on. There must be plenty of good people in Ares Guild, too. If you refuse to help over a merely personal reason, you’ll regret it for the rest of your life. The you I know is that kind of person.”

“……”
- **Defect:** The final sentence is ambiguously attached to refusing help, rather than identifying Taekyung as someone who would deeply regret such a refusal. The draft also changes his startled realization into neutral silence.
- **Replacement:** “I’m really fine, so hurry up and go. There must be plenty of good people in Ares Guild, too. If you let a petty personal reason stop you from helping, you’ll regret it for a long time. At least, the Taekyung I know would.”

“…!”
- **Rationale:** 그런 사람이야 refers to Taekyung being the kind of person who would continue regretting a failure to help, while ……! explicitly marks his shocked realization.

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이정룡은 각 등급의 실력자들만을 선별해서 높고 거대한 우리 안에 가두었다.
- **Current:** Lee Jungryong had selected only the strongest people from each Grade and locked them inside a tall, enormous cage.
- **Defect:** Capitalized “Grade” is reserved for System classifications, while this passage refers to Hunter ranks. “Strongest” also overstates 실력자, which means skilled or capable people.

- **Rationale:** 각 등급 refers to the established Hunter ranking structure, and 실력자 denotes capable elites without necessarily identifying the single strongest individuals.

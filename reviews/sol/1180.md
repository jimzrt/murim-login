# Review

The draft largely follows the scene, but changes one character judgment, misses a deliberate shift in formality, and uses the wrong established name for Great Sir.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 사막과 어둠만이 들었던 대인의 마지막 뇌까림은 반은 맞고, 반은 틀렸다.
- **Current:** The Big Man’s final mutterings, heard only by the desert and the darkness, had been half right and half wrong.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the established name 대인 with “The Big Man.”
- **Replacement:** Great Sir’s final mutterings, heard only by the desert and the darkness, had been half right and half wrong.
- **Rationale:** The binding glossary renders 대인 as Great Sir; the draft uses “The Big Man” for him throughout.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “인원을 선별하여 인근을 수색하거라. 아니, 하게. 나는 여기 계신 송 대협과 긴히 나눌 이야기가 있으니.”
- **Current:** “Pick some men and search the surrounding area. No—go ahead. I have something important to discuss with Great Hero Song here.”
- **Defect:** “No—go ahead” loses Wikyung’s deliberate correction from a familiar command to a more formal one after Mukyung insists on being addressed as Commander.
- **Replacement:** “Pick some men and search the surrounding area. No—Commander, please see to it. I need to speak privately with Great Hero Song.”
- **Rationale:** 하거라 becomes 하게; the change in address continues the brothers’ public-formality joke.

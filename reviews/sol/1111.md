# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes how long the Bow Saint is asked to hold the gate and misreads the Slaughter Saint’s uncertainty about her motives.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 내성으로 가야겠소. 그때까지 이곳을 지켜 주시오.
- **Current:** —I have to go to the Inner City. Hold this place until I return.
- **Defect:** “Until I return” adds a promise to come back and changes the requested duration of the defense.
- **Replacement:** —I have to go to the Inner City. Hold this place until I get there.
- **Rationale:** 그때까지 refers to the point he has just mentioned—going to the Inner City—not his return.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 단지 그가 반드시 살아나리라는 믿음이 아닌, 지금으로서는 도무지 짐작조차 할 수 없는 저 뜻 모를 눈빛과 목소리가 무엇을 의미하는 것인지도.
- **Current:** He couldn’t tell whether she believed he would definitely survive, or what the incomprehensible look in her eyes and the meaning in her voice were meant to convey.
- **Defect:** The draft makes her belief in his survival an open yes-or-no question. The source instead contrasts that simple explanation with an inscrutable meaning in her look and voice.
- **Replacement:** Her strange look and voice suggested something beyond simple faith that he would survive—something he couldn’t begin to fathom right now.
- **Rationale:** 단지 … 믿음이 아닌 rules out mere confidence in his survival as a sufficient explanation; it does not say he cannot tell whether she has that confidence.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 남문을 중심으로, 반경 일백여 장에 달하는 지면이 그 무겁고도 거대한 육신을 일으켰다.
- **Current:** Centered on the South Gate, the ground across a hundred jang rose up with the weight of its massive body.
- **Defect:** “Across a hundred jang” loses the stated radius and makes the area affected by the earthquake smaller or indeterminate.
- **Replacement:** Centered on the South Gate, the ground within a radius of over a hundred jang heaved its massive, heavy body upward.
- **Rationale:** 반경 specifies a radius of roughly a hundred jang, not a total distance across the upheaval.

# Review

The draft preserves the battle’s main actions and sequence. Three localized wording defects are actionable.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 빗방울보다도 투명한 목소리와 함께, 그와는 어울리지 않는 투박한 손가락이 시위를 당긴다.
- **Current:** With a voice as clear as raindrops, he drew a bowstring with a rough, work-worn finger that seemed entirely at odds with it.
- **Defect:** “Work-worn” invents a history for the finger; the source describes it only as rough.
- **Replacement:** With a voice clearer than raindrops, he drew the bowstring with a rough finger that seemed entirely at odds with it.
- **Rationale:** 투박한 describes the finger’s appearance, not how it became that way.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 인간의 힘을 벗어난 존재는, 마법이라 불리는 저 끔찍한 사술(詐術)만이 아니라는 것을.
- **Current:** Magic, that terrifying sorcery, wasn’t the only thing in the world beyond the power of ordinary people.
- **Defect:** “Sorcery” misses the binding rendering of 사술, while “ordinary people” weakens the source’s contrast with human power itself.
- **Replacement:** Those terrifying dark arts called magic weren’t the only thing beyond human power.
- **Rationale:** The realization is that the masters, too, possess power beyond human limits; the supplied glossary renders 사술 as “dark arts.”

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 번뜩이는 벼락이, 끔찍한 냉기를 간직한 얼음이, 정수리를 적셔야 할 빗줄기가 화살이 되어 날아들어도.
- **Current:** Even as flashes of lightning, dreadful cold, and ice struck toward them—and even the rain that should have soaked their heads came flying like arrows.
- **Defect:** The draft makes dreadful cold a separate incoming threat instead of a property of the ice.
- **Replacement:** Even as flashing lightning, ice bearing a dreadful chill, and rain that should have soaked their heads came flying at them—the rain transformed into arrows.
- **Rationale:** 끔찍한 냉기를 간직한 modifies 얼음; the rain, specifically, becomes arrows.

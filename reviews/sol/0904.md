# Review

The draft has a few scene-fact and terminology errors, plus a mistranslated joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아직 끝나지 않은 또 하나의 연회가 남아 있었다.
- **Current:** another banquet remained—one that had yet to begin.
- **Defect:** Changes a banquet that has not ended into one that has not begun.
- **Replacement:** another banquet remained—one that had not yet ended.
- **Rationale:** 끝나지 않은 means “not yet ended,” not “not yet begun.”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 서로를 향해 웃음 대신 병장기를, 술 대신 피가 흩뿌려질 홍문연(鴻門宴)이.
- **Current:** A Hongmen Banquet,[^1] where weapons would be exchanged instead of smiles, and blood splattered in place of wine.
- **Defect:** “Weapons would be exchanged” suggests trading weapons rather than turning them on one another.
- **Replacement:** A Hongmen Banquet,[^1] where they would turn weapons on one another instead of smiling, and spill blood instead of wine.
- **Rationale:** 서로를 향해 makes the weapons directed at each other.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하나같이 초일류에서 절정의 무위를 지닌 대국 제일의 정예군이
- **Current:** Every one of them was among the Great Nation’s finest troops, martial artists ranging from First Rate to Peak.
- **Defect:** Lowers the stated minimum martial-arts level from Supreme First Rate to First Rate.
- **Replacement:** Every one of them was among the Great Nation’s finest troops, martial artists ranging from Supreme First Rate to Peak.
- **Rationale:** The source says 초일류에서 절정, and the packet’s glossary distinguishes 초일류 from 일류.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 남들보다 조금 더 긴 고통을 느껴야 했던, 불행한 누군가의 목숨을 제물 삼아.
- **Current:** And in sacrifice, the lives of the unfortunate who had to endure a little more pain than everyone else.
- **Defect:** Turns one dying person’s life into multiple lives and changes the duration of his suffering into its intensity.
- **Replacement:** And the life of one unfortunate man, made to suffer a little longer than the others, as its sacrifice.
- **Rationale:** 누군가의 목숨 is singular, and 조금 더 긴 고통 refers to suffering for a little longer.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 옆집 똥개도 알아들을 개소리를 지껄이는 마삼보를 향해, 나는 대답 대신 창날을 내리그었다.
- **Current:** Instead of answering Ma Sanbao’s idiocy—the kind even the neighbor’s Ddong Gae could understand—I brought my spearhead down.
- **Defect:** Capitalizes the ordinary mutt in this joke as the established character-name rendering “Ddong Gae.”
- **Replacement:** Instead of answering Ma Sanbao’s bullshit—the kind even the mutt next door could understand—I brought my spearhead down.
- **Rationale:** 옆집 똥개 means the mutt next door here; it is not an address to or reference to the named character.

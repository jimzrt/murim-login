# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it introduces one factual contradiction about the child’s sex, overstates the training program’s safety, alters the rationale for deploying the Second Regiment, and loses several smaller action and register details.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘중화 육성 훈련은 심혈을 기울여 제작한 프로젝트입니다. 안전도 철저하고, 효과도 확실합니다. 같은 나이인 우헤이싱도 잘 해내고 있고요.’
- **Current:** “Zhonghua Development Training is a project we created with our whole hearts. It is completely safe, and its results are certain. Wu Heixing, who is the same age, is doing very well too.”
- **Defect:** “Completely safe” replaces the narrower claim that the program has thorough safety measures, while “created with our whole hearts” is an unnatural rendering of the painstaking effort invested in its development.
- **Replacement:** “Zhonghua Development Training is a project we developed with painstaking care. Its safety measures are thorough, and its effectiveness is proven. Wu Heixing, who is the same age, is doing well too.”
- **Rationale:** The source asserts thorough precautions and dependable effectiveness, not absolute freedom from danger.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** ‘어어? 아닌데. 난 그냥 네가 좋다면 뭐든…… 앗!’
- **Current:** “Wh-What? No. I just thought that if you like it, then anything… Ah!”
- **Defect:** The English is grammatically incomplete in a way that obscures Lei Fei’s accommodating response: he is saying that anything is fine if it is what Wu Heixing wants.
- **Replacement:** “Huh? No. I just… whatever you want is fine with me—ah!”
- **Rationale:** This preserves the interruption while making the intended conciliatory meaning intelligible.

## F07 — Major

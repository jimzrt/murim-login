# Review

The draft contains one reversed subject, one false implication that an attack connected, a resurrection-like mistrendering, and several meaningful losses of negation, repetition, force, and profanity.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 모르지 않았다. 단지 기다렸을 뿐이다.
- **Current:** I knew. I had only been waiting.
- **Defect:** The direct statement loses the emphatic double negation contrasting ignorance with deliberate waiting.
- **Replacement:** It wasn’t that I didn’t know. I had only been waiting.
- **Rationale:** 모르지 않았다 explicitly rejects the idea that he did not know; that pragmatic correction sets up his claim that he was knowingly waiting for the betrayal.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 놈들이 송곳니를 들이미는 그 순간을.
- **Current:** Waiting for the moment they bared their fangs.
- **Defect:** The draft changes thrusting or sinking their fangs into merely displaying them, weakening the anticipated act from an attack to a revelation of hostility.
- **Replacement:** Waiting for the moment they sank their fangs into me.
- **Rationale:** 송곳니를 들이밀다 describes putting the fangs into the target, matching the physical betrayal that immediately follows.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 등 뒤에서 파공성이 일었다. 전신 요혈을 파고드는 세 줄기의 바람.
- **Current:** A sound rang out behind me—the sound of three streaks of wind cutting into the vital points throughout my body.
- **Defect:** The draft says the three attacks cut into his vital points, falsely implying that they struck him before the next sentence establishes that all three missed.
- **Replacement:** The sound of displaced air rang out behind me. Three streaks of wind bored toward vital points across my body.
- **Rationale:** 파고드는 describes the streaks bearing down on the targeted vital points, not successfully penetrating them.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00

- **Current:** “You aren’t someone you can handle. Wait for a moment.”

- **Rationale:** 자네가 is the subject of 상대할 수 있는, while 자 refers to Jin Taekyung.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그럴 리가. 다만 애석할 뿐이다.”
- **Current:** “There’s no need for that. I only regret what happened.”
- **Defect:** The first sentence no longer denies Jin’s accusation that Lee might blame his subordinates, and the second adds an unsupported object for Lee’s regret.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “착짱죽짱. 개씹정룡. 내가 방금 정한 너희 묘비명인데, 마음에 드냐?”

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 죽음을 딛고 일어나 장렬히 돌격하던 오백 명의 공안무력부 헌터들.
- **Current:** The five hundred Public Security Armed Forces Department Hunters who rose from death and charged gloriously.
- **Defect:** “Rose from death” implies that the five hundred Hunters were resurrected, changing a scene fact in a fantasy setting where resurrection is materially distinct.
- **Replacement:** The five hundred Public Security Armed Forces Department Hunters who rose in defiance of death and charged valiantly.
- **Rationale:** 죽음을 딛고 일어나 means rising despite or in defiance of death, while 장렬히 describes their heroic, death-defying charge.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이번 전쟁 어디에서도 보인 적 없던, 이정룡이 진심을 담아 휘두른 일격이다.
- **Current:** It was a strike Lee Jungryong had unleashed with complete sincerity, one he had not shown anywhere else in this war.
- **Defect:** “Complete sincerity” is an unnatural literalism that obscures the mechanical point that Lee is attacking in earnest with his full power.
- **Replacement:** It was a full-power strike from Lee Jungryong, the likes of which he had not unleashed anywhere else in this war.
- **Rationale:** 진심을 담아 in this combat context means Lee has become serious and is putting his full strength into the blow, not that the strike is emotionally sincere.

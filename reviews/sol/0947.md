# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it misassigns the marquis title, mistakes the imperial family for a tribe, and blurs several subjects and commands.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정식으로 열후(列侯)의 반열에 오른 나보다는 몇 계단 아래라는 것.
- **Current:** He was a few rungs below me, a man formally elevated to the ranks of the nobles.
- **Defect:** The apposition makes the City Lord, rather than the narrator, the newly elevated noble.
- **Replacement:** He was a few rungs below me, now that I’d formally been made a marquis.
- **Rationale:** 나 is the person elevated to the rank of 열후.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 황족이 아닌 이상, 아니 설령 황족이라 해도 처벌할 수 있는 권한이 충분하다.
- **Current:** Unless he was of the Huang tribe—even if he were, in fact—I had more than enough authority to punish him.
- **Defect:** 황족 here means a member of the imperial family, not the Huang tribe.
- **Replacement:** Unless he belonged to the imperial family—no, even if he did—I had more than enough authority to punish him.
- **Rationale:** The narrator is assessing the authority granted by his imperial titles; the concession concerns royal status.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 언제부터 시작되었는지 모를 그 열기가, 깊숙한 내부에서부터 오장육부를 불태우고 있다는 사실을.
- **Current:** That heat, whose source I couldn’t pinpoint, had been burning my insides from deep within, consuming my five viscera and six bowels.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an unknown starting time into an unknown source and makes the collective danger a physical affliction of the narrator.
- **Replacement:** That heat, which had begun who knows when, was burning through us from deep within, consuming our very organs.
- **Rationale:** 언제부터 concerns when it began; the surrounding passage describes a threat spreading through Murim, not the narrator’s own illness.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 특히나 서로를 소닭 보듯이 하던 무림인에게 전달받은, 사실상의 명령이나 다름 없으니까.
- **Current:** Especially not when it came from martial artists who’d treated each other like strangers—and amounted to an order in all but name.
- **Defect:** The draft says martial artists treated one another like strangers; the mutual estrangement is between the government and Murim.
- **Replacement:** Especially when it came from martial artists whom officials had long regarded with indifference—and who felt the same way about them—and amounted to an order in all but name.
- **Rationale:** 서로 refers back to 관과 무림, the two sides whose noninterference has just been explained.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 성주 곁에 찰싹 붙어서 지켜보다가, 영 수상하다 싶으면 걔들한테 말해요. 그놈 모가지 따도 된다고.
- **Current:** “Stick close to the City Lord and keep an eye on him. If anything seems really suspicious, tell them they can take his head.”
- **Defect:** “Anything” broadens the condition; it is the City Lord whose suspicious behavior would warrant the warning.
- **Replacement:** “Stick close to the City Lord and keep an eye on him. If he seems really suspicious, tell them they can take his head.”
- **Rationale:** The order is to watch 성주 and, if he appears suspect, tell the arriving forces they may kill him.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 지금은 엄연한 전시상황인 데다가, 나는 열후라는 지고한 직위에 더해 황실 직속 기관인 금의위의 천호이기도 하다.
- **Current:** We were in a state of war, and I wasn’t just a noble of the highest rank. I was also a Thousand Captain in the Embroidered Uniform Guard, an imperial agency.
- **Defect:** “Of the highest rank” overstates the narrator’s position, and “an imperial agency” loses that the Guard answers directly to the imperial household.
- **Replacement:** We were unmistakably at war, and besides holding the exalted rank of marquis, I was a Thousand Captain in the Embroidered Uniform Guard, which answered directly to the imperial household.
- **Rationale:** 지고한 describes an exalted position, while 황실 직속 specifies direct imperial subordination.

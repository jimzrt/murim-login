## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 내 무슨 소리인지는 모르겠지만, 한국에서는 오늘을 주모 기일이라 부른다던데…….
- **Current:** I don’t know what they mean, but apparently people in Korea are calling today the tavern lady’s death anniversary…
- **Defect:** Rendering 주모 only as “the tavern lady” leaves the culturally specific celebration joke opaque and omits a materially useful footnote.

- **Rationale:** The line invokes the Korean internet convention of calling for a jumo during exuberant national celebration; retaining the term and briefly defining it preserves the joke without adding plot information.

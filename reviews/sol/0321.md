# Review

Four actionable findings: one altered qi mechanism, one mistranslated idiom, one changed quantity, and one altered elixir effect.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 노호성과 함께 황보엄을 중심으로 막대한 기파가 뻗어 나왔다.
- **Current:** With his roar, an immense aura burst outward from Hwangbo Eom.
- **Defect:** The draft renders 기파, a physically propagating wave of qi, as a generic aura, obscuring the mechanism that produces the violent shaking immediately afterward.
- **Replacement:** With his roar, an immense wave of qi burst outward from Hwangbo Eom.
- **Rationale:** 기파 means a wave of qi or energy radiating from him, not merely his presence or aura.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 복용만으로도 단숨에 일 갑자의 공력을 얻게 된다는, 그야말로 천고의 영약.
- **Current:** It was a peerless elixir said to grant a full jiazi of internal energy in a single gulp.
- **Defect:** “In a single gulp” changes 단숨에 from the immediacy of the elixir’s effect to a specific manner of drinking it.
- **Replacement:** It was a peerless elixir said to grant a full jiazi of internal energy instantly upon consumption.
- **Rationale:** 복용만으로도 단숨에 means that merely consuming it grants the internal energy immediately; it does not specify swallowing it in one gulp.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 산서 촌놈이 화왕의 제자가 되었다고 보이는 것이 없느냐?
- **Current:** Just because a country bumpkin from Shanxi became the Fire King’s Disciple, do you think you can see everything now?
- **Defect:** The idiom 보이는 것이 없다 is mistranslated as omniscience. Hwangbo Eom is accusing Taekyung of becoming so arrogant that he recognizes no one as being above or worthy of fear.
- **Replacement:** Just because a country bumpkin from Shanxi became the Fire King’s Disciple, do you think no one is above you now?
- **Rationale:** In context, 보이는 것이 없느냐 describes reckless arrogance or acting as though nobody else matters, which also sets up Taekyung’s retort about Hwangbo Eom’s aging eyesight.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 순간이었다. 다루의 문이 열리고 십여 명의 사람들이 우르르 밀려온 것은.
- **Current:** That was the moment the pavilion’s doors opened and more than a dozen people came streaming inside.
- **Defect:** The draft changes 십여 명, approximately ten or a dozen people, into more than twelve people.
- **Replacement:** That was the moment the pavilion’s doors opened and about a dozen people came streaming inside.
- **Rationale:** 십여 명 denotes roughly ten people rather than a lower bound exceeding twelve; the following groups are being concretely counted.

# Review

Multiple actionable fidelity issues, including misassigned actions and subjects, an altered counterfactual, and a tenfold area-conversion error.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 감히 그 누구도 범접할 수 없었던 천태민의 위명에 한 걸음씩 가까워지는 그가 최민우의 옆에 있는 한, 그 무엇도 두 사람의 앞을 막아설 수 없을 것이다.
- **Current:** As long as he stood beside Choi Minwoo—a man who was steadily drawing closer to the unrivaled reputation of Cheon Taemin—nothing could stand in the way of the two of them.
- **Defect:** The appositive grammatically identifies Choi Minwoo, rather than Jin Taekyung, as the person approaching Cheon Taemin’s reputation.
- **Replacement:** As long as Jin Taekyung—who was steadily drawing closer to Cheon Taemin’s unrivaled reputation—stood beside Choi Minwoo, nothing could stand in their way.
- **Rationale:** The source subject drawing closer to Cheon Taemin’s fame is Jin Taekyung, who is then described as standing beside Choi Minwoo.

# Review

One subject reversal and several losses of hierarchy, register, or specific meaning.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 산서성 도지휘동지 대감을 아시는 것 같습니다만.
- **Current:** “He seems to know the Deputy Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province.”
- **Defect:** Changes who knows the commissioner. The eunuch is addressing Jeok Cheongang, not describing the person he serves.
- **Replacement:** “You seem to know the Deputy Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province.”
- **Rationale:** 아시는 addresses Jeok; the eunuch explains his master's connection to the commissioner only in the following sentence.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 대가리에 피도 안 마른 어린 노무 새끼가 혀가 반 토막이 났나. 뭐? 그대?
- **Current:** “You little shit! You’re too young for the blood to have dried on your head, but your tongue’s already been cut in half? ‘Could your title be’?”
- **Defect:** Jeok objects to being addressed as an equal with 그대, not to the wording of the question about his title.
- **Replacement:** “You little shit! You’re still wet behind the ears, and half your tongue seems to be missing. What was that—‘you’?”
- **Rationale:** The pointed repetition of 그대 makes the man's form of address the cause of Jeok's anger.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘아무래도 조진 것 같습니다.’라는 명문(名文)으로 시작되는 진태경의 서신
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung’s missive began with the eloquent line, *“Looks like I fucked up.”*
- **Defect:** Turns a tentative assessment that the situation has gone badly into Taekyung's admission of personal fault, and drops his polite register toward Jeok.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung’s missive began with the eloquent line, *“I’m afraid we may be screwed.”*
- **Rationale:** 아무래도 and 것 같습니다 qualify the assessment; 조진 describes the predicament without assigning blame to Taekyung.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 진즉 말했다면 굳이 때리진 않았을 텐데. 노부의 무례를 용서하게. 고자 양반.

환관은 고자에게 고자라고 하는 것만큼 무례한 언사가 없다고 말하고 싶었지만, 꾹 눌러 참았다.
- **Current:** “If you’d said that sooner, I wouldn’t have had to hit you. Forgive this old man’s rudeness, eunuch.”

The eunuch wanted to say that calling an eunuch an eunuch was about the rudest thing you could do, but he held his tongue.
- **Defect:** Using the neutral office term “eunuch” for Jeok's insult makes the following complaint about its rudeness puzzling.
- **Replacement:** “If you’d said that sooner, I wouldn’t have had to hit you. Forgive this old man’s rudeness, my good castrate.”

The eunuch wanted to say that calling a eunuch a castrate was about the rudest thing you could do, but he held his tongue.
- **Rationale:** Jeok says 고자 양반 rather than 환관, and the narration explicitly identifies calling him 고자 as the insult.

## F05 — Minor

# Review

The draft changes Morgoth’s reasoning, loses a named weapon and nickname, and misstates several details in Taekyung’s memories and the soldiers’ arrival.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하지만 앞서 모르고스가 그랬듯, 나 역시 놈을 마주한 그 순간 명확하게 깨달았다.
- **Current:** But just as Morgoth had said earlier, the moment I faced him, I understood with absolute clarity.
- **Defect:** Changes Morgoth’s earlier realization into something he said.
- **Replacement:** But just as Morgoth had, I understood with absolute clarity the moment I faced him.
- **Rationale:** 그랬듯 refers to Morgoth also realizing something upon their encounter, not to his speech.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘야. 워로드 몬.’
- **Current:** > “Hey, Warlord.”
- **Defect:** Drops Mon from Taekyung’s mocking form of address.
- **Replacement:** > “Hey, Warlord Mon.”
- **Rationale:** The source says 워로드 몬, not simply 워로드.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 움직일 수 없을 만큼 지치고 다친 내게 아크 리치가 쏘아 보낸 백염의 창날이.
- **Current:** The spearhead of white flame the Arch Lich had fired at me as I lay too exhausted and injured to move.
- **Defect:** Renders the named spear White Flame as a material made of flame.
- **Replacement:** The spearhead of White Flame, which the Arch Lich had sent flying at me when I was too exhausted and injured to move.
- **Rationale:** 백염 is the established name White Flame; 창날 is its spearhead.

## F04 — Major

- **Source:** 마지막인 줄만 알았던 그 목소리가.
- **Current:** The voice I’d thought was the last I’d ever hear.
- **Defect:** Implies Taekyung expected this to be the last voice he would hear before dying.
- **Replacement:** The voice I’d thought I would never hear again.
- **Rationale:** He believed he had heard the Skeleton King’s voice for the last time.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 인간들의 땅 위에서, 인간을 지키기 위해 희생한 새로운 존재가 빛나는 왕관과 함께 다시 일어났다.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 어둠만이 일렁이는 그 틈새 너머로 지금껏 드러나지 않았던 존재들이 모습을 나타냈다.
- **Current:** The warped space opened its jaws, and beyond the gap, where only darkness rippled, beings never seen before emerged.
- **Defect:** Says the beings had never been seen, although the source only says they had not appeared until now; Taekyung immediately recalls seeing their kind before.

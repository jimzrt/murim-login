# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s broad sequence, but it loses the name joke, changes one physical action, inflates Ju Wongong’s title, and alters several concrete details.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그럼요. 평소에도 주인공 공자님에 관련된 칭찬을 귀에 못이 박이도록 하지 않으셨습니까.”
- **Current:** “Of course. You’ve praised Young Master Wongong so often I’ve got calluses on my ears.”
- **Defect:** Mujin says 주인공, meaning “protagonist,” not Wongong. This is the mistake that sets up Ju Wongong’s correction.
- **Replacement:** “Of course. You’ve praised Young Master Protagonist so often I’ve got calluses on my ears.”
- **Rationale:** Mujin has mistaken Ju Wongong’s name for the ordinary Korean word 주인공.

# Review

The draft preserves the main exchange, but changes the fireballs’ target, obscures who is fighting the Guardians, and adds an unintended proper-name reference.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 일순간 붉게 물든 하늘 너머, 먹구름 사이로 모습을 드러낸 십여 개의 화염구가 흑룡의 머리 위를 향해 겨누어졌다.
- **Current:** Beyond the sky, which turned red in an instant, more than a dozen fireballs emerged from between the storm clouds and took aim above the Black Dragon’s head.
- **Defect:** The fireballs are aimed at the dragon’s head, not at a point above it. 십여 개 is roughly ten or a dozen, not necessarily more than twelve.
- **Replacement:** Against the suddenly reddened sky, a dozen or so fireballs emerged from the storm clouds and took aim at the Black Dragon’s head.
- **Rationale:** 머리 위를 향해 identifies the dragon’s head as the target; 십여 개 gives an approximate count.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 저 멀리 가디언들과의 일전 도중 위험을 감수하고 나선 매직 존슨이 피를 토하며 무릎을 꿇었다.
- **Current:** Far away, Magic Johnson—who had risked himself to step in while the Guardians were fighting—coughed up blood and dropped to his knees.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Guardians’ fight sound separate from Johnson. He intervenes at risk to himself during his fight with them.
- **Replacement:** Far away, Magic Johnson—who had risked intervening in the middle of his fight with the Guardians—coughed up blood and dropped to his knees.
- **Rationale:** 가디언들과의 일전 describes a battle against the Guardians, not merely a time when they were fighting.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 태산처럼 드리워진 용의 거대한 그림자가 가까워졌을 때
- **Current:** as the Dragon’s enormous shadow loomed over him like Mount Taishan
- **Defect:** Capitalizing the mountain image as Mount Taishan introduces an apparent reference to the named character Taishan.
- **Replacement:** as the Dragon’s enormous shadow loomed over him like a mountain
- **Rationale:** 태산처럼 functions here as an image of immense size, not a reference to the character.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92

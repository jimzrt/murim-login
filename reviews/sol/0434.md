## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 깜빡 잊고 있던 사실이었지만, 아크 리치의 목에 걸린 현상금은 무려 50조였다.

한 달가량 이어진 전쟁으로 얻게 될 보수는 거스름돈 취급해도 될 만큼 천문학적인 금액.
- **Current:** The compensation for the month-long war was such an astronomical sum that anything I earned from it could be treated as pocket change.
- **Defect:** The comparison is reversed. The fifty-trillion bounty is the astronomical sum, while his separate compensation for the month-long war is what would look like pocket change beside it.
- **Replacement:** The bounty was so astronomical that the compensation I would receive for the month-long war would look like pocket change by comparison.
- **Rationale:** The subject of 천문학적인 금액 is the bounty established in the preceding sentence, and 전쟁으로 얻게 될 보수 is explicitly the lesser amount.

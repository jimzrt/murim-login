# Review

The draft contains four critical factual or referential changes, several meaningful losses of subject clarity and register, and a few localized action and quantity errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘돌잡이 때 처음 보고, 장례식 때가 마지막이었나.’
- **Current:** *I first saw him at his doljanchi, and his funeral was the last time……?*
- **Defect:** The draft turns an unspecified funeral into Choi Minwoo’s own funeral, contradicting the fact that he is alive.
- **Replacement:** *The first time I saw him was at his first-birthday ceremony, and the last was at the funeral…wasn’t it?*
- **Rationale:** The source says only 장례식, “the funeral,” without identifying it as Choi Minwoo’s. It also refers specifically to the first-birthday ceremony at which doljabi takes place.

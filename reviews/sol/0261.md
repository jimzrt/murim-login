# Review

Two critical meaning changes affect kinship and the final threat; one injury is rendered unclearly, with two additional localized English issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 수십 년간 우정을 쌓은 사제는 그 흔적조차 찾을 수 없다.
- **Current:** The Senior and Junior Brothers with whom he had spent decades building a friendship had been reduced to remains he could not even find.
- **Defect:** The draft changes one Junior Brother into multiple Senior and Junior Brothers, altering both the kinship relationship and quantity.
- **Replacement:** The Junior Brother with whom he had built a friendship over decades had vanished without a trace.
- **Rationale:** 사제 is singular and specifically means Hongcheon's Junior Brother here; the source neither mentions Senior Brothers nor pluralizes the relationship.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 어깻죽지 아래가 텅 비어 버린 굉천이 비명을 내질렀다.
- **Current:** Hongcheon screamed as everything below his shoulder was hollowed out.
- **Defect:** “Everything below his shoulder was hollowed out” is anatomically unclear and suggests a cavity in his body rather than that the blow took off his arm below the shoulder.
- **Replacement:** Hongcheon screamed, his arm gone from the shoulder down.
- **Rationale:** 어깻죽지 아래가 텅 비어 버린 depicts the space below the shoulder left empty after the arm is destroyed.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “고추 바사삭 만들어 버리죠.”
- **Current:** “Let’s crisp him up like a chili pepper.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes a euphemistic threat to crisp the man's genitals into a threat to crisp his entire body, removing the crude punchline and altering the threatened action.
- **Replacement:** “Let’s crisp up his chili pepper.”
- **Rationale:** 고추 is the deliberately euphemistic object of 만들어 버리죠, referring to Flame Tiger's penis; it is not a comparison describing how to burn him.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “그 어떤 깨달음도 죽음 앞에서는 이어질 수 없다네.”
- **Current:** “No enlightenment can continue in the face of death.”
- **Defect:** “Enlightenment can continue” is an unnatural English collocation that weakens the intended aphoristic delivery.
- **Replacement:** “No enlightenment can endure in the face of death.”
- **Rationale:** 이어질 수 없다 means that enlightenment cannot persist or endure before death; “endure” preserves that meaning in natural English.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “내가 이래서 중놈들을 싫어하는 거다. 곧 뒈질 것들이 갖은 척이란 척은 다 하거든.”
- **Current:** “This is why I hate monk bastards. They’re about to die, yet they put on every kind of act imaginable.”
- **Defect:** “Put on every kind of act imaginable” is a literal, opaque rendering of the insult and does not naturally convey Flame Tiger's complaint that doomed monks insist on acting tough.
- **Replacement:** “This is why I hate monk bastards. They’re about to die, but they still insist on acting tough.”
- **Rationale:** 갖은 척이란 척은 다 하다 means to engage in every sort of posturing or pretense; “acting tough” fits the immediate defiance and Flame Tiger's rough voice.

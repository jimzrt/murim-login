# Review

The draft preserves the main sequence, but loses two jokes built on misheard words, makes one euphemism explicit, and omits a comic self-correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그나저나 이걸 뭐라고 부르는지 까먹었군. 회장? 화장 시계였나?
- **Current:** “Come to think of it, I’ve forgotten what it’s called. A pocket watch? A pocket clock?”
- **Defect:** Jeok correctly guesses “pocket watch” before Taekyung supplies the name. In the source, both guesses are mangled words.
- **Replacement:** “Come to think of it, I’ve forgotten what it’s called. Chairman? Was it a makeup watch?”
- **Rationale:** 회장 means “chairman” and 화장 can mean “makeup”; Jeok is fumbling for 회중시계, not naming it correctly.

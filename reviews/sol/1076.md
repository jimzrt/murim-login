# Review

Several scene facts and relationships are altered, chiefly the sect’s lost headquarters, the old man’s movement through the air, and Hak Woo’s association with Gung Gibang.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이미 본산(本山)을 빼앗겼는데도?
- **Current:** “Even though we’ve already lost our home?”
- **Defect:** “Home” obscures that the sect has lost its headquarters.
- **Replacement:** “Even though we’ve already lost our sect’s headquarters?”
- **Rationale:** 본산 refers to the sect’s principal mountain or headquarters, not an ordinary home.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 스스로를 실속 없는 늙은이라 칭한 노인이
- **Current:** The old man, who had called himself an empty-handed old man,
- **Defect:** “Empty-handed” changes his self-deprecating joke from lacking substance to lacking possessions.
- **Replacement:** The old man, who had just called himself all talk and no substance,
- **Rationale:** 실속 없는 echoes his claim that the quoted speaker sounds impressive but has nothing of substance to offer.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 한 마리의 비조(飛鳥)처럼 높이 솟구친 노인의 신형이 자그마치 수십여 장의 허공을 가로질러 떨어져 내리는 광경에, 곳곳에서 탄성이 터져 나왔다.
- **Current:** The old man soared upward like a bird, then came sailing down from dozens of yards in the air. Exclamations rose from all around us.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the distance he crosses through the air into his height above the ground and reduces dozens of jang to dozens of yards.
- **Replacement:** The old man soared like a bird, crossed dozens of jang through the air, and descended. Exclamations rose from all around us.
- **Rationale:** 수십여 장의 허공을 가로질러 describes the span traversed; a jang is substantially longer than a yard.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 머나먼 서쪽 너머의 땅, 그곳의 하늘과 맞닿은 거대한 산자락으로.
- **Current:** To a land far to the west, to the foot of a great mountain where the sky met the earth.
- **Defect:** “The foot” replaces the great mountain slopes, and “where the sky met the earth” weakens the image of those slopes touching the sky.
- **Replacement:** To a land far to the west, to the vast slopes of a mountain that touched the sky.
- **Rationale:** 거대한 산자락 is the vast mountain slopes; 그곳의 하늘과 맞닿은 describes them reaching the sky.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 제각각 중년, 장년, 소년의 모습을 하고 있지만
- **Current:** They might each look middle-aged, elderly, and young, respectively,
- **Defect:** The Bow Saint’s apparent age is raised from a mature adult to elderly.
- **Replacement:** They might look middle-aged, mature, and boyish, respectively,
- **Rationale:** 장년 denotes mature adulthood, while 소년 specifically evokes a boy, contrasting their appearances with their accumulated years in Murim.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 성라대연이 한창 진행되던 당시, 한동안 궁기방 등과 함께 어울려 다니던 곤륜운룡(崑崙雲龍) 학우가 바로 청허자의 제자이기도 했으니.
- **Current:** During the Star-Array Grand Banquet, I’d spent some time with Gung Gibang and the others. One of them had been Hak Woo, the Kunlun Cloud Dragon—and Hak Woo was Cheongheoja’s Disciple.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung the person who spent time with Gung Gibang’s group; the source describes Hak Woo associating with them.
- **Replacement:** During the Star-Array Grand Banquet, Hak Woo, the Kunlun Cloud Dragon, had spent some time hanging around with Gung Gibang and the others. He was Cheongheoja’s Disciple.
- **Rationale:** 한동안 궁기방 등과 함께 어울려 다니던 modifies 학우, not the narrator.

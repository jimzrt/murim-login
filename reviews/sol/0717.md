# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but several localized action, register, and phrasing errors remain, including one passage that obscures the Beast Miao King’s relationship to his sworn brother.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 뭐 어쩌겠느냐. 어디서 굴러먹다 왔는지도 모를 개뼈다귀도 아니고. 천하의 화왕(火王)이 죄다 집어치우라는데.
- **Current:** “What could they do? He isn’t some stray dog bone that rolled in from who knows where. When the Fire King of the world tells them to drop everything, they have to drop everything.”
- **Defect:** The literal phrases “stray dog bone” and “Fire King of the world” are unnatural in English and weaken the contrast between an irrelevant nobody and the renowned Fire King.
- **Replacement:** “What choice did they have? This wasn’t some nobody who’d crawled in off the street. The great Fire King himself told them to drop everything.”
- **Rationale:** The source dismisses the possibility that the order came from an unknown nobody and emphasizes the Fire King’s overwhelming stature.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 말없이 나를 바라보던 남호가 입맛을 다셨다.
- **Current:** Namho stared at me silently, then clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** The physical action is altered: 입맛을 다셨다 means he smacked his lips, not that he clicked his tongue.
- **Replacement:** Namho stared at me silently, then smacked his lips.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies a lip-smacking gesture, and preserving the small action avoids changing Namho’s reaction.

## F03 — Minor

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “지키지 못했기 때문이다. 나를 따르는 수많은 부족민을, 그리고 가장 가까운 곳에서 고통받던 내 하나뿐인 의형제를.”

- **Rationale:** 가장 가까운 곳에서 conveys that his sworn brother was suffering at his very side, which is central to the Beast Miao King’s guilt.

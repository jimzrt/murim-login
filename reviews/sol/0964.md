# Review

One scene-fact reversal and several localized fidelity and clarity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 못 박힌 듯 서 있던 그를 향한 가솔의 외침은 적들의 함성에 파묻혀 개울가의 징검다리처럼 뚝뚝 끊겨 있었다.
- **Current:** The cries of his retainers, who stood rooted to the spot, reached him only in broken snatches, like stepping-stones scattered along a stream.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the retainers stand rooted to the spot; the person standing motionless is Jin Wikyung. It also drops the enemy shouts that drown out the retainer’s voice.
- **Replacement:** His retainer’s cries to him as he stood rooted to the spot were drowned out by the enemy’s shouts, reaching him only in broken snatches, like stepping-stones across a stream.
- **Rationale:** 그 refers to Jin Wikyung, and the retainer’s cries are broken up because they are buried beneath the enemy’s shouts.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “……서, 어서!”
- **Current:** “L-Lesser Family Head, hurry!”
- **Defect:** The draft invents a title in speech that Jin Wikyung cannot properly hear.
- **Replacement:** “…urry, hurry!”
- **Rationale:** The source gives a clipped fragment of 어서 followed by 어서, not an address.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 돌격창과 화살이 스쳐 지나가며 만들어 낸 상처에서 비롯된 고통에, 전신의 피가 뜨겁게 달아올랐다.
- **Current:** Pain from the wounds left by thrusting spears and arrows coursing past him made the blood in his body burn hot.
- **Defect:** “Thrusting spears and arrows coursing past him” obscures that the weapons grazed him and caused the wounds.
- **Replacement:** Pain flared from the wounds where lances and arrows had grazed him, setting his blood ablaze.
- **Rationale:** 스쳐 지나가며 만들어 낸 상처 identifies grazing blows as the cause of the wounds.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 서늘하게 흩뿌려지는 예기(銳氣).
- **Current:** A chill of killing intent scattered through the air.
- **Defect:** “Killing intent” substitutes intent for the sharp edge or aura emanating from the sword.
- **Replacement:** A cold, sharp edge filled the air.
- **Rationale:** 예기 denotes sharpness here; the source does not state killing intent.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 아주 잠시.

잠시 동안만 의(義)를 배신하고, 협(俠)을 외면하면 모두가 살아남을 수 있다는 희망.
- **Current:** For a brief moment.

The hope that if he betrayed *ui* and turned away from *hyeop* just this once, everyone could survive.
- **Defect:** The draft moves “brief” onto the moment of feeling hope rather than the proposed duration of his betrayal. The unexplained romanizations also hide what he considers betraying.
- **Replacement:** Just for a little while.

The hope that if he betrayed righteousness and turned his back on chivalry for only a short time, everyone could survive.
- **Rationale:** 잠시 동안만 qualifies how long he would betray 의 and turn away from 협; that temporary compromise is the temptation.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 조금 전보다 흐릿해진 미소를 띤 자무카를 향해, 진무경은 발걸음을 뗐다.
- **Current:** Jamukha’s smile grew faintly less certain as Jin Mukyung stepped toward him.
- **Defect:** “Less certain” assigns an emotion to Jamukha’s fading smile that the source does not specify.
- **Replacement:** Jamukha’s smile had faded a little as Jin Mukyung stepped toward him.

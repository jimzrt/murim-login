# Review

Several consequential errors affect who survived, a martial artist’s level, the pill’s duration, and the proposed mechanism.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 만약 그날의 전투에서 살아남은 것이 내가 아니라 풍양이었다면
- **Current:** If I, not Pung Yang, had survived that battle
- **Defect:** Reverses who survived in the hypothetical.
- **Replacement:** If Pung Yang, not I, had survived that battle
- **Rationale:** The narrator wonders what would have happened if Pung Yang had survived instead of him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 절정의 끝자락에 도달해 있던 대장로가 선천지기(先天眞氣)까지 끌어올린 후에야 그런 힘을 보일 수 있었다는 것을 생각한다면 더더욱.
- **Current:** the Head Elder, who’d reached the very threshold of the Peak realm, could only show power like that after drawing on his innate qi
- **Defect:** Places the Head Elder at the threshold of Peak rather than near its upper limit.
- **Replacement:** the Head Elder, who’d reached the very limits of the Peak realm, could only show power like that after drawing on his innate qi
- **Rationale:** 절정의 끝자락 denotes the far end of the Peak realm, which matters to the comparison of martial power.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 물론 엄청난 효력만큼이나 부작용이 끔찍한 데다 유지 시간도 길지 않지만, 그야말로 순리(順理)를 뒤트는 힘.
- **Current:** Its effects were tremendous, but so were its terrible side effects, and they didn’t last long. It was a power that twisted the natural order itself.
- **Defect:** Makes the side effects, rather than the pill’s power, sound short-lived.
- **Replacement:** Its effects were tremendous, but the side effects were terrible, and the power didn’t last long. It twisted the natural order itself.
- **Rationale:** 유지 시간 refers to how long the pill’s enhanced power lasts.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그러고 보니, 언제부턴가 웬 비단 주머니를 항상 갖고 다녔습니다.
- **Current:** Come to think of it, he always carried around a silk pouch for a while.
- **Defect:** Changes an ongoing habit that began at some point into one that lasted only a while.
- **Replacement:** Come to think of it, at some point he started carrying a silk pouch everywhere.
- **Rationale:** 언제부턴가 marks when the habit began; 항상 says he carried it consistently thereafter.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “효력을 줄인 만큼, 그에 뒤따르는 부작용도 감소시켰거나.”
- **Current:** “They reduced its effects, and with them, the side effects that follow.”
- **Defect:** States the second possibility as fact rather than leaving it hypothetical.
- **Replacement:** “Or they reduced its effects—and the side effects along with them.”
- **Rationale:** The line completes the preceding either-or speculation; Taekyung explicitly says afterward that they cannot yet be sure.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “놈들이 그것을 만든 이유는, 단지 자신들의 전력을 높이기 위해서만이 아니다.”
- **Current:** “They didn’t make it just to increase their own forces.”
- **Defect:** Turns an increase in fighting strength into an increase in the number of forces.
- **Replacement:** “They didn’t make it just to bolster their own fighting strength.”
- **Rationale:** 전력 here means combat capability; the point is that the pill has a purpose beyond strengthening Dark Heaven’s fighters.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 산적 나부랭이 따위가 어찌 그것을 손에 넣었겠느냐?
- **Current:** and a bunch of ordinary bandits have gotten their hands on it?
- **Defect:** Changes the single bandit under discussion into a group.
- **Replacement:** and some lowly bandit have gotten his hands on it?
- **Rationale:** 산적 나부랭이 따위 refers disparagingly to the individual bandit whose use of the pill prompted this conversation.

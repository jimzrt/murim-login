# Review

Several passages alter a physical detail, a joke, or the Emperor’s meaning; the succession scene also softens an explicit reference to war.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 내 말의 의미를 파악하지 못해 잠시 고개를 갸웃거린 황제가 입맛을 다셨다.
- **Current:** The Emperor tilted his head, not quite grasping what I meant, then clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** Clicking his tongue replaces the Emperor’s lip-smacking gesture.
- **Replacement:** The Emperor tilted his head, not quite grasping what I meant, then smacked his lips.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다셨다 describes smacking one’s lips, not clicking one’s tongue.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 단단히 매어져 있던 끈이 풀어진다.
- **Current:** The tightly fastened clasp came undone.
- **Defect:** The cloak is held by a tied cord, not a clasp.
- **Replacement:** The tightly tied cord came loose.
- **Rationale:** 끈 is a cord, and 매어져 있던 specifies that it was tied.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그럼 짐의 손이 떨어지기 전에, 더더욱 이 귀중한 선물을 받고 싶어질 테니.
- **Current:** Then you’ll be all the more eager to accept this precious gift before I let go.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the Emperor’s joke about his hand falling off from holding out the cloak into a suggestion that he will release it.
- **Replacement:** Then you’ll be all the more eager to accept this precious gift before my hand falls off.
- **Rationale:** 짐의 손이 떨어지기 전에 refers to his hand falling off, not to his letting go.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 거리낌 없이 자신의 목숨을 판 돈으로 내던졌고, 그 선택을 후회하진 않는다.
- **Current:** He’d thrown away his life without hesitation, and he didn’t regret that choice.
- **Defect:** “Thrown away his life” implies that he lost it; the source says he staked it.
- **Replacement:** He’d wagered his life without hesitation, and he didn’t regret that choice.
- **Rationale:** 목숨을 판 돈으로 내던졌고 uses a gambling stake as its image. The Emperor risked his life rather than having already lost it.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 성공하면 군왕이요(成卽君王). 실패하면 역적이라(敗卽逆賊).
- **Current:** “Success makes you a prince; failure makes you a traitor.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes king or ruler to prince.
- **Replacement:** “Success makes you a king; failure makes you a traitor.”
- **Rationale:** 군왕 means king or sovereign, not prince; the contrast concerns who gets to rule and who is condemned as a rebel.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 대국의 후계자로서 전란과 맞서야 하는 주표는, 결코 울어서는 안 되는 사람이 되었다.
- **Current:** Zhu Bao, who had to face the turmoil as heir to the Great Nation, had become someone who must never cry.
- **Defect:** “Turmoil” obscures the war the heir must face.
- **Replacement:** As heir to the Great Nation, Zhu Bao would have to face the coming war. He had become someone who must never cry.
- **Rationale:** 전란 denotes war and its ravages, not merely general unrest.

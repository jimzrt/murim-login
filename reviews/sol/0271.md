# Review

The draft contains one unsupported subject assignment, one consequential mistranslation of Taekyung’s former status, and several localized issues involving action mechanics, training agency, natural English, and comedic sequencing.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 쟤는 교육 좀 받아야겠다. 팀원이 위험에 처한 것 같으면 근처에 굴러다니는 짱돌이라도 주워서 견제해 줘야지. 저게 뭐 하는 짓이야.
- **Current:** That guy needed some training. If a teammate looked like they were in danger, he should have picked up a rock lying nearby and at least tried to distract the enemy. What was he doing?
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the unidentified rookie a male gender even though the Korean subject remains ungendered.
- **Replacement:** That rookie needed some training. If a teammate looked like they were in danger, they should have picked up a rock lying nearby and at least tried to distract the enemy. What were they doing?
- **Rationale:** The repeated ungendered pronouns 쟤 and 저게 identify only a nearby rookie, not the rookie’s gender.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes a time quantity and the agent of an action, misdirects a line of dialogue, and softens two profane reactions.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 자그마치 삼백여 명에 달하던 산적과 수적들은 불과 반 시진 만에 전멸당했다.
- **Current:** A full three hundred bandits and river pirates had been wiped out in less than half an hour.
- **Defect:** Half a shichen is one hour, not half an hour.
- **Replacement:** A full three hundred bandits and river pirates had been wiped out in less than an hour.
- **Rationale:** A shichen is approximately two hours; the draft halves the elapsed time again.

# Review

One critical role reversal in the Code Red announcement, plus losses in humor, threat meaning, idiomatic meaning, and response details.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 두 시간 간격으로 발생한 두 번의 몬스터 웨이브는 큰 피해를 낳았지만, 완벽한 초동조치로 빠르게 혼란을 잠재울 수 있었습니다.
- **Current:** Two Monster Waves occurred two hours apart and caused tremendous damage, but swift initial action managed to quell the chaos quickly.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the initial response from flawless to merely swift, even though speed is separately conveyed by 빠르게.
- **Replacement:** Two Monster Waves occurred two hours apart and caused tremendous damage, but a flawless initial response brought the chaos under control quickly.
- **Rationale:** 완벽한 means flawless or perfect, while 빠르게 supplies the separate idea of speed.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “방금 깨진 게 뭐였지? 미술 교과서에서 봤던 건데. 로댕의 생각 난 사람이었나?”
- **Current:** “What was that statue that just broke? I saw it in an art textbook. Was it Rodin’s Thinker?”
- **Defect:** The draft silently corrects Taekyung’s mangled recollection of the statue’s title and removes the intentional joke.

- **Rationale:** 생각 난 사람 is Taekyung’s comic misnaming of 생각하는 사람, the Korean title of Rodin’s *The Thinker*.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “장담하는데, 지금 당장 무기 내려놓고 도망가지 않으면 늦어도 오 분 안에 전부 사람 구실 못 하게 될 줄 알아라.”
- **Current:** “And I guarantee that if you don’t put down your weapons and run right now, all of you will be in no shape to act like human beings within five minutes at the latest.”
- **Defect:** “Act like human beings” sounds like a judgment about their behavior. The Korean threatens to leave them physically unable to function normally.
- **Replacement:** “And I guarantee that if you don’t put down your weapons and run right now, within five minutes, every last one of you will be physically incapable of functioning normally.”
- **Rationale:** 사람 구실 못 하게 되다 here describes severe physical incapacitation, reinforced by the following threat to cripple them.

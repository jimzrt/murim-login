## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 박태섭이 불만을 토로한 것은 하루 이틀 일이 아니지만, 이렇게 노골적으로 나오는 건 처음이다.
- **Current:** Park Tae Seop had been voicing his dissatisfaction for days, but this was the first time he had been so blatant about it.

- **Rationale:** The source characterizes the dissatisfaction as an established pattern without specifying a short duration.

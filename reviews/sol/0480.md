# Review

One significant causal/characterization error, one omitted name reveal, and three localized fidelity or prose issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 워낙 대중없이 자란 탓에 정확한 나이는 모르지만 아마 고희(古稀)는 넘기지 않았을까 싶은뎁쇼.
- **Current:** I was raised without much discipline, so I don’t know my exact age, but I suppose I must be past seventy.
- **Defect:** “Without much discipline” falsely characterizes Gwak as poorly disciplined and does not explain why he does not know his age. The source means that no precise account or standard was kept while he was growing up.
- **Replacement:** No one kept proper track when I was growing up, so I don’t know my exact age, but I reckon I must be past seventy.
- **Rationale:** The clause 대중없이 자란 탓에 provides the cause of his uncertain age, not a judgment about his conduct or upbringing.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 더불어 젊다 못해 어려 보이는 청년의 정체 역시도.
- **Current:** He also realized who the young man was—if anything, he looked younger than his years.
- **Defect:** “Younger than his years” implies knowledge of the young man’s actual age and weakens the source’s point that he looks not merely young but almost boyish.
- **Replacement:** He also realized the identity of the young man, who looked barely more than a boy.
- **Rationale:** 젊다 못해 어려 보이는 intensifies his youthful appearance to the point of looking boyish; it does not compare his appearance with a known age.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 언제는 옆 마을 과부 하나가 깊은 밤에 찾아와서 백추를 구경시켜 달라더니, 갑자기 옷고름을 확 풀어 버리는데……!
- **Current:** Once, a widow from the neighboring village came to see me late at night and asked me to show her the Baekchu. Then she suddenly untied her dress ribbon, but…
- **Defect:** The added “but” makes the boatman sound as though he is introducing a contrast or stopping himself. In the source he excitedly reaches the suggestive climax and is then interrupted by the medical apprentice.
- **Replacement:** Once, a widow from the neighboring village came to see me late at night and asked me to show her the Baekchu. Then she suddenly yanked her dress ties open…!
- **Rationale:** The emphatic 확 and terminal exclamation preserve his excitement and the comic timing of the following interruption.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 따스한 온기가 흘러나와 환자의 내부를 가득 채웠다.

짐작할 수 없는 현상에 의해 비로소 안정을 되찾은 뱃사공이 숨을 헐떡였다.
- **Current:** Warmth flowed out and filled the inside of his body.

The boatman finally regained his composure through an incomprehensible phenomenon and began gasping for breath.
- **Defect:** “Filled the inside of his body” and “regained his composure through an incomprehensible phenomenon” are conspicuously unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** Warmth flowed from the hand and filled his body.

The inexplicable phenomenon finally calmed the boatman, and he gasped for breath.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the warmth entering and filling him, the unexplained nature of the effect, and his resulting calm without literal Korean collocations.

# Review

Two critical factual changes and several meaningful losses of register, hierarchy, agency, and delivery mechanics require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 중심에 자리한 원탁(圓卓)에는, 홀로그램 특유의 초록색 빛무리와 함께 나타난 십여 명의 인물들이 그를 기다리고 있었다.
- **Current:** Around the round table at its center, more than a dozen figures waited for him, appearing amid the green glow unique to holograms.
- **Defect:** 십여 명 means approximately or slightly more than ten people, not more than twelve; the draft changes the stated quantity.
- **Replacement:** At the round table in its center, over ten figures waited for him, appearing amid the green glow unique to holograms.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the source quantity without inflating the group beyond a dozen.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** - 빌어먹을. 30년 전이라면 대격변 때잖아. 개고생하던 기억은 떠올리기도 싫으니까 그만둬.
- **Current:** —Damn it. Thirty years ago was the Great Cataclysm. I don’t even want to remember how hard we had it back then, so let’s stop.
- **Defect:** “How hard we had it” sanitizes the strongly colloquial 개고생 and “let’s stop” weakens the speaker’s blunt command.
- **Replacement:** —Damn it. Thirty years ago was the Great Cataclysm. I don’t even want to remember the hell we went through, so drop it.
- **Rationale:** 개고생 conveys severe, crudely expressed hardship, while 그만둬 is a direct command to stop the topic.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당장 이 회의에 참석한 인원 중 거물(巨物)이라 불리지 않는 사람은 누구도 없었지만, 중년인에게는 감히 범접할 수 없었다.
- **Current:** There was not a single person attending the meeting who could not be called a major figure. Yet none of them dared approach the middle-aged man.
- **Defect:** “Dared approach” reads as physical proximity, whereas 범접할 수 없었다 means that none of these powerful people could rival, challenge, or stand on equal footing with him.
- **Replacement:** Every person attending the meeting was a major figure, yet none of them could hope to rival the middle-aged man.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts the attendees’ status with Michael’s overwhelming superiority rather than describing their willingness to move near him.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “후긴을 보냈는데도 말귀를 알아듣지 못하더군. 믿을 수 없이 무모하지만…… 그만큼 자신이 있다는 뜻이겠지.”

- **Defect:** 말귀를 알아듣지 못하다 here means that they refused to listen or take the warning, not that they literally failed to understand it.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** - 맞습니다. 결과적으로는 언론도 감히 우리를 비난하지 못했고, 무엇보다 오딘 길드장님께서 발 빠르게 나서 주신 덕분이지요.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “틀림없이 전달했습니다.”
- **Current:** “They were delivered without fail.”
- **Defect:** “Delivered” implies that the gifts have already reached their targets, contradicting the subsequent statement that the first gift will be delivered in an hour. 전달 here is the handoff to those who will carry out the attacks, distinct from the later 배달.
- **Replacement:** “I handed them over without fail.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the two-stage mechanism: Huginn has passed the gifts to the collaborators, and the first will reach its target an hour later.

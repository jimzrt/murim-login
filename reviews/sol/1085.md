# Review

The draft preserves the chapter’s main events, but changes the timing of the reinforcements, adds a geographic direction, and misrenders several cues in the final exchange.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 거기에 더하여, 저 머나먼 장성(長城) 밖에서도 먹구름이 몰려들고 있음을 사람들은 뒤늦게 알아차렸다.
- **Current:** And on top of that, people belatedly realized that storm clouds were gathering beyond the Great Wall, far to the west.
- **Defect:** Adds a westward location that the source does not give.
- **Replacement:** And on top of that, people belatedly realized that storm clouds were gathering beyond the distant Great Wall.
- **Rationale:** 머나먼 describes the distant Great Wall; the passage does not say the threat is west of it.

## F02 — Major

- **Current:** Unlike the North Sea Ice Palace, which—as always—stuck to the middle path, Nanman Beast Palace had overcome the threat of civil war thanks to the astonishing exploits of a young man and resolved to ally itself with the Murim Alliance.
- **Defect:** “Stuck to the middle path” suggests a doctrine rather than the North Sea Ice Palace’s neutral position between sides.
- **Replacement:** Unlike the North Sea Ice Palace, which—as always—remained neutral, Nanman Beast Palace had overcome the threat of civil war thanks to the astonishing exploits of a young man and resolved to ally itself with the Murim Alliance.
- **Rationale:** 중도를 지키고 있는 contrasts the palace’s neutrality with Nanman Beast Palace’s decision to ally with the Murim Alliance.

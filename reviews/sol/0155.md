# Review

One critical action reversal, two meaningful changes to recruitment and personnel decisions, one unsupported biographical addition, and one organization-name error.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 정말 아쉬웠다면 손발을 보낼 게 아니라 머리가 직접 왔어야지.
- **Current:** If they were truly sorry, they should have sent their heads instead of their hands and feet.
- **Defect:** The action is reversed: Jin Wikyung says the leaders—the metaphorical heads—should have come personally rather than sending subordinates, not that severed heads should have been sent.
- **Replacement:** If they were truly sorry, the heads themselves should have come in person instead of sending their hands and feet.
- **Rationale:** 머리가 직접 왔어야지 explicitly requires the head to come personally, while 손발을 보낼 게 아니라 rejects sending the hands and feet.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 수백을 충원해도 수백이 죽어 나가겠지.
- **Current:** Even if we send several hundred, several hundred will die.
- **Defect:** 충원 means recruiting or adding personnel, not dispatching them. The source explains that inexperienced newcomers would die as quickly as the family replenished its ranks.
- **Replacement:** Even if we recruit several hundred, several hundred will die.
- **Rationale:** The distinction is part of Jin Wikyung's reason for refusing to mobilize the family's newly joined martial artists.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 옥석을 가려내고 떠날 자는 떠나보내야지.
- **Current:** We need to separate the jade from the stone, and let those who intend to leave go.
- **Defect:** The draft makes departure voluntary by adding an intention to leave. In context, Jin Wikyung is evaluating the recruits and will dismiss those judged unsuitable.
- **Replacement:** We need to separate the jade from the stones and send away those who need to go.
- **Rationale:** 옥석을 가려내고 describes screening the recruits, and the causative 떠나보내다 makes Jin Wikyung the one who sends the rejected candidates away.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 방구석에서 서책이나 들여다보던 백면서생의 표본이다.
- **Current:** He was the very picture of a pale-faced scholar who had spent his life staring at books in a corner of a room.
- **Defect:** The draft adds the unsupported claim that he had spent his entire life this way.
- **Replacement:** He was the very picture of a pale-faced scholar who did nothing but pore over books in his room.
- **Rationale:** The source derides him as a sheltered bookworm but does not specify a lifelong history.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 황하방과 소공문 사이에 분쟁이 일어났습니다.
- **Current:** There’s been a dispute between the Yellow River Sect and the Lesser Empty Sect.
- **Defect:** 방 is an organization suffix corresponding to a gang, not a sect, while “Lesser Empty” assigns an unsupported literal meaning to the unglossed proper name 소공.
- **Replacement:** There’s been a dispute between the Yellow River Gang and the Sogong Sect.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the distinct organization types and avoids inventing a semantic expansion for a proper name whose underlying characters are not supplied.

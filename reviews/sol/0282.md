# Review

The draft contains one reversed hierarchy, several meaningful shifts in consequence, culpability, and characterization, and localized terminology and action issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 1팀장이랑 통화하는 내용을 들어 보니 이건 뭐, 숫제 상관이었고.
- **Current:** From the conversation I overheard between you and the Team 1 Leader, he was practically your superior.
- **Defect:** The hierarchy is reversed. The source says Park Jihoon sounded like the Team 1 Leader’s superior, not that the Team 1 Leader was Jihoon’s superior.
- **Replacement:** From the conversation I overheard between you and the Team 1 Leader, you were practically his superior.
- **Rationale:** This observation is central to Taekyung’s deduction that Jihoon is not an ordinary team member.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 끝까지 가면 양패구상(兩敗毆傷)이야. 너도 그걸 모르지는 않겠지.
- **Current:** “If we take this all the way, we’ll both lose everything. You know that, too.”
- **Defect:** “Lose everything” overstates the consequence. 양패구상 means that both sides will suffer serious harm, not necessarily total loss.
- **Replacement:** “If we take this all the way, we’ll both come out wounded. You know that, too.”
- **Rationale:** The source frames continued conflict as mutually damaging rather than guaranteeing the complete destruction of both sides.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 하지만 내가 생각한 또 다른 피의자는 박태섭이 아니었다.
- **Current:** But Park Tae Seop was not the other culprit I had in mind.
- **Defect:** 피의자 means a suspect, while “culprit” treats guilt as established and prematurely increases Taekyung’s certainty.
- **Replacement:** But Park Tae Seop was not the other suspect I had in mind.
- **Rationale:** At this point Taekyung is still testing a suspicion about another person’s involvement.

# Review

The draft contains one quantity error and several actionable fidelity, register, terminology, and causal-description issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 기적적으로 살아남은 생존자들은 그 진실을 알고 있었고, 그렇기에 자신들의 맞은편에서 날아오는 수십여 대의 전투기들을 발견하고 피를 토하듯 부르짖을 수밖에 없었다.
- **Current:** The survivors who had miraculously lived through it knew the truth. That was why, upon spotting dozens of fighter jets flying toward them from the opposite direction, they had no choice but to cry out until their voices seemed to tear their throats apart.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the source’s specific image of crying out as though coughing up blood with an invented image of voices tearing throats apart.
- **Replacement:** The survivors who had miraculously lived through it knew the truth. That was why, upon spotting dozens of fighter jets flying toward them from the opposite direction, they could only scream as though coughing up blood.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the source’s 피를 토하듯 image and its desperate register without adding a different physical effect.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하지만 작전 통제실을 죽음 같은 침묵으로 몰아넣은 것은, 이미 열 개에 달하는 시(市)를 집어삼키고 수도마저 위협하는 저 쓰나미의 존재 때문만은 아니었다.
- **Current:** But the thing that had plunged the operations control room into deathly silence was not merely the tsunami that had already swallowed nearly ten cities and now threatened the capital.
- **Defect:** “Nearly ten” means fewer than ten, whereas 열 개에 달하는 means the number has reached or amounts to as many as ten.
- **Replacement:** But the thing that had plunged the operations control room into deathly silence was not merely the tsunami that had already swallowed as many as ten cities and now threatened the capital.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the stated scale of the destruction instead of reducing the number of cities affected.

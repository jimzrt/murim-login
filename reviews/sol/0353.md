# Review

Several factual and register errors require correction, including a substantially shortened distance, altered physical actions, lost assignment roles, softened contempt, and Murim-energy terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 백여 장 앞, 철옹성처럼 사천당문을 둘러싼 성벽 위에서 자신을 기다리는 하룻강아지들을 보니 기분이 썩 유쾌해진다.
- **Current:** Seeing the wet-behind-the-ears pups waiting for him atop the walls surrounding the Sichuan Tang Clan like an impregnable fortress, a little over a hundred yards ahead, put him in an excellent mood.
- **Defect:** 백여 장 is roughly three hundred yards, not a little over one hundred yards, so the draft substantially changes the distance.
- **Replacement:** Seeing the wet-behind-the-ears pups waiting for him atop the walls surrounding the Sichuan Tang Clan like an impregnable fortress, roughly three hundred yards ahead, put him in an excellent mood.
- **Rationale:** A jang is approximately three meters; about one hundred jang is therefore approximately three hundred meters, or somewhat over three hundred yards.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 태어나길 난산(難産)이라, 추한 생김새를 타고났다.
- **Current:** He had been born ugly, a difficult birth from the start.
- **Defect:** The draft conflates his ugly appearance with the difficult childbirth and produces an unclear causal statement.

- **Rationale:** 난산 explicitly denotes a difficult childbirth, while 추한 생김새를 타고났다 separately states that he was born ugly.

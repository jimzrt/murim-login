# Review

The draft contains one altered age quantity, a misrepresented hierarchy, an unsupported translated group name, one over-explicit euphemism, and two localized terminology/register losses.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “오, 올해 약관을 넘겼습니다.”
- **Current:** “I-I turned twenty this year.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the young man’s age. He says he passed the age of twenty this year, not that he turned twenty.
- **Replacement:** “I-I passed twenty this year.”
- **Rationale:** 약관 denotes the age of twenty, while 넘겼습니다 means he has passed it; the following response also treats him as already beyond that threshold.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대항산검문의 대주. 당주, 혹은 각주.
- **Current:** The Iron Sword Squad Leader was one of the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s commanders—a Hall Leader, or perhaps a Pavilion Leader.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly treats Hall Leader and Pavilion Leader as uncertain alternative titles for the Iron Sword Squad Leader. The source is enumerating the senior offices held by the assembled people.
- **Replacement:** They were squad leaders, hall leaders, and pavilion leaders of the great Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- **Rationale:** 대주, 당주, and 각주 are separate leadership positions represented among the gathered senior figures, not competing descriptions of one man’s position.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마차 바퀴보다 큰 사내는 아이라도 가차 없이 죽이고, 여인은 취하거나 노예로 팔아 버린다.
- **Current:** Any male taller than a cartwheel—even a child—was killed without mercy, while the women were taken for pleasure or sold as slaves.
- **Defect:** “For pleasure” makes the source’s euphemistic 취하다 more sexually explicit.
- **Replacement:** Any male taller than a cartwheel—even a child—was killed without mercy, while the women were taken or sold as slaves.
- **Rationale:** 취하다 literally leaves what is done to the women implicit; “were taken” preserves that deliberate euphemism without censoring its implication.

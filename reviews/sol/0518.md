# Review

Two scene facts are reversed or altered, and several lines lose explicit killing intent, speaker targeting, dialect, or natural terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 지금으로부터 이십여 년 전, 그는 한 사람의 목숨을 적천강에게 일임하는 대가로 비무를 약속받았다.
- **Current:** More than twenty years ago, he had promised Jeok Cheongang a duel in exchange for entrusting him with one person’s life.
- **Defect:** The direction of the promise is reversed. The Azure Sky Sword King entrusted a life to Jeok Cheongang and received Jeok’s promise of a duel; he did not promise Jeok a duel.
- **Replacement:** More than twenty years ago, in exchange for entrusting one person’s life to Jeok Cheongang, he had been promised a duel.
- **Rationale:** 약속받았다 means that he was promised the duel, while 적천강에게 일임 indicates that the life was entrusted to Jeok Cheongang.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “서 있는 사람이 마시는 것으로 하십시다.”
- **Current:** “Then let’s drink standing up.”
- **Defect:** The proposed condition is changed into a physical drinking posture. The source says that whoever remains standing after the duel may drink the liquor.
- **Replacement:** “Let whoever is left standing drink it.”
- **Rationale:** 서 있는 사람 is the person left standing, making the liquor a wager or reward for the duel.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “만전에 주의를 가했건만, 결국 또 다시 이런 일이 터지는군요. 이렇게 된 이상, 소승이 역시 놈들을 상대로 온 힘을 다하여 살계(殺戒)를 펼쳐야겠습니다.”
- **Current:** “Though we took every precaution, this has happened again. In this situation, this humble monk will have no choice but to set aside the precept against killing and fight them with all his strength.”
- **Defect:** The translation softens the monk’s explicit intention to kill into merely fighting after setting the precept aside, weakening both the violent meaning and the joke continued in Taekyung’s response.
- **Replacement:** “Though we took every precaution, this has happened again. Given that, this humble monk will have no choice but to break the precept against killing and go all out against them.”
- **Rationale:** 살계를 펼치다 explicitly invokes the precept against killing while indicating that he will violate it against Dark Heaven; Taekyung immediately echoes that phrasing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “야, 시벌. 다 조져!”
- **Current:** “Hey, fuckers! Wreck them all!”
- **Defect:** The added vocative “fuckers” appears to insult the allied Murim practitioners Taekyung is rallying. In the source, 시벌 is a free-standing expletive, while the implied targets of 다 조져 are the enemy.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck! Wreck ’em all!”
- **Rationale:** The source contains profanity but does not address Taekyung’s allies as fuckers.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 자신이 누구인가. 이미 오래전 검기상인(劒氣傷人)의 경지에 오른 특급 낭인이다.
- **Current:** He was a top-tier wandering martial artist who had reached the realm of Sword Energy Injures People long ago.
- **Defect:** “Sword Energy Injures People” is an ungrammatical literal gloss presented as a formal realm name. The phrase describes his ability to wound people with Sword Energy.
- **Replacement:** He was a top-tier wandering martial artist who had long since reached the level of injuring others with Sword Energy.
- **Rationale:** 검기상인 literally describes causing injury with Sword Energy; the source does not require an unnatural English proper name.

## F08 — Minor

- **Source:** 그리고 객잔 안의 누군가가 그 사실을 알아차리기도 전에, 두 절대자는 서로를 향해 출수(出手)했다.
- **Current:** And before anyone in the inn could notice, the two supreme masters launched themselves at each other.

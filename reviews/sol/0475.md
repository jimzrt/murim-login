# Review

Two meaningful fidelity defects and four localized wording issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘인벤토리 수납. 오픈. 소환.’
- **Current:** *Inventory Open. Summon.*
- **Defect:** The Inventory storage command is omitted, obscuring the first half of the store-and-resummon maneuver.
- **Replacement:** *Store in Inventory. Open. Summon.*
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly has Taekyung store White Flame in his Inventory before opening it and summoning the weapon back into his hand.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 자기를 아구아맨이라고 불러 달라고 했어요.
- **Current:** He asked me to call him Aquaman.
- **Defect:** The translation silently corrects the deliberately malformed or misremembered name 아구아맨 to Aquaman, removing the joke in Cheongpung’s recollection.
- **Replacement:** He asked me to call him Aguaman.
- **Rationale:** The source says 아구아맨 rather than the standard Korean rendering 아쿠아맨, and the surrounding hesitation foregrounds Cheongpung’s imperfect recollection.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 갑작스럽게 동체가 흔들리자 빠르게 완성되어 가던 물의 구가 주춤했지만, 소기의 목적을 달성한 내 얼굴은 딱딱하게 굳었다.
- **Current:** The sudden jolt to its body made the sphere of water, which had been nearing completion, falter. But the satisfaction on my face hardened at once.
- **Defect:** The source says Taekyung’s expression hardened despite accomplishing his immediate objective; it does not describe satisfaction hardening on his face.
- **Replacement:** The sudden jolt to its body made the sphere of water, which had been nearing completion, falter. But despite accomplishing what I’d intended, my expression hardened.
- **Rationale:** 소기의 목적을 달성한 means that he achieved his intended purpose, while 얼굴은 딱딱하게 굳었다 describes his expression turning rigid.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “몇 가지 묻고 싶은 게 있는데. 혹시 못 본 사이에 수공(水功)이라도 익혔나?”
- **Current:** “There’s something I want to ask. Did you happen to learn water arts while I wasn’t looking?”
- **Defect:** 몇 가지 is plural, and 못 본 사이 means during the time they had not seen each other, not while Mungyeong literally was not looking.
- **Replacement:** “There are a few things I want to ask. Did you happen to learn water arts since we last saw each other?”
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both the plural setup and the interval between their meetings.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “정말이지 제멋대로군. 결국 수공을 익히지 않았다는 이야기로 들리는데.”
- **Current:** “You really are arbitrary. It sounds like you’re saying you never learned water arts after all.”
- **Defect:** “You really are arbitrary” is an unnatural dictionary gloss and does not convey Mungyeong’s criticism that Jeok Cheongang simply does whatever he pleases.
- **Replacement:** “You really do whatever you please. It sounds like you never learned water arts after all.”
- **Rationale:** 제멋대로 describes self-willed behavior, and the replacement preserves Mungyeong’s dry, blunt register.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “공격 대형, 갖춰!”
- **Current:** “Attack formation, form up!”
- **Defect:** The command is redundant and unnatural in English.
- **Replacement:** “Form an attack formation!”
- **Rationale:** The source gives a direct command to assume an attack formation; the replacement conveys it without repeating “formation” and “form.”

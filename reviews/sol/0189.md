# Review

Several subject, kinship, causal-mechanism, and register errors require correction, alongside localized wording and dialogue-formatting issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그는 당신을 쓰러트림으로써 사랑을 쟁취하고자 합니다.
- **Current:** He seeks to win your love by knocking you down.
- **Defect:** The translation makes Jin Taekyung the source of the love Chulwoo wants, rather than the person Chulwoo intends to defeat in order to win the woman's love.
- **Replacement:** He seeks to win her love by knocking you down.
- **Rationale:** 당신 is the person being knocked down, while 사랑 refers contextually to the woman's love described in the preceding sentence.

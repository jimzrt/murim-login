# Review

The draft contains one reversed action, several meaningful shifts in action or intent, a downgraded item grade, and localized register and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 악취 나는 뇌수를 뒤집어쓰며 달려오는 듀라한은 단 일격에 몸뚱이가 양단되었고
- **Current:** A Dullahan charged toward me while showering me in foul-smelling brains, only for its body to be cleaved in two by a single blow.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses who is covered in the brain matter: the Dullahan is covered in it, not showering Jin with it.
- **Replacement:** A Dullahan came charging at me, covered in foul-smelling brain matter, only to be cleaved in two with a single blow.
- **Rationale:** 뒤집어쓰며 modifies the charging Dullahan and describes it as being covered or drenched in the brain matter.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 수천의 몬스터는…… 어느 순간 압도당하기 시작했다.
- **Current:** At some point, they began to feel overwhelmed.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the monsters' objective defeat into a subjective feeling.

- **Rationale:** 압도당하기 시작했다 states that they began to be overpowered, not merely that they felt intimidated.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 묵빛 검신이 채 뽑히기도 전에 검집으로 돌아갔을 때는, 내 일권(一拳)이 놈의 가슴을 관통한 후였으니까.
- **Current:** By the time the dark-gray blade had barely emerged before sliding back into its scabbard, my One Strike had already pierced through the creature’s chest.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly treats 일권, meaning a single fist or punch, as the named term “One Strike,” obscuring the physical action.
- **Replacement:** The dark-gray blade had barely begun to emerge before it slid back into its scabbard—but by then, my fist had already punched through the creature’s chest.
- **Rationale:** 일권(一拳) explicitly identifies Jin's fist or punch; it is not 일격, the glossary term rendered “One Strike.”

## F05 — Major

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그는, 그들은 어떤 기분이었을까.

죽음을 향해 돌격하며 어떤 생각을 품었을까. 감히 짐작할 수 없다.
- **Current:** What had he felt?

What had they thought as they charged toward death? I could not even begin to guess.
- **Defect:** The draft loses the deliberate mid-sentence expansion from one man to the entire group.
- **Replacement:** How had he—how had they—felt?

What had they been thinking as they charged toward death? I could not even begin to guess.
- **Rationale:** 그는, 그들은 explicitly shifts from singular to plural within the first question, broadening Jin's grief from Team Leader Choi to all of the dead.

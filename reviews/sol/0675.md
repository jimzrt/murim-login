# Review

The draft contains two critical perspective/action distortions, one incorrect time quantity, and several localized subject or pragmatic-cue issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 누구도 막을 수 없는 존재. 지금껏 머릿속에서만 그렸을 뿐, 단 한 번도 적이 되어 맞서 싸우리라 생각지 못했던 강자.
- **Current:** An existence no one could stop. A powerful man I had only ever imagined in my head, never thinking that I would one day face him as an enemy.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung the person imagining and facing the powerful man, even though Taekyung himself is the powerhouse whom the surrounding enemies never imagined they would face.
- **Replacement:** An existence no one could stop. The kind of powerhouse they had only ever imagined, never once thinking they would have to face one as an enemy.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subjects are established by the surrounding enemy reactions: the passage describes how Taekyung appears to them, not how another powerful man appears to Taekyung.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “어. 비키지 못하겠는데.”
- **Current:** “Uh. I can’t move.”
- **Defect:** The English suggests that Taekyung is physically unable to move. He is deliberately refusing to get out of the fleeing chieftain’s way.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. I’m afraid I can’t get out of your way.”
- **Rationale:** 비키다 means to move aside or clear the way; the line is a deadpan refusal as Taekyung blocks the escape route.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘여기서 한 시라도 지체한다면…….’
- **Current:** *If I delay here for even an hour…*
- **Defect:** The draft turns an idiomatic warning against even the slightest delay into a specific one-hour interval, weakening and altering the urgency.
- **Replacement:** *If I delay here for even a moment…*
- **Rationale:** 한 시라도 in this construction means even for a moment or instant, not literally one hour.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 무야호는 죽어라 뛰게 시키면서 나는 등에 앉아 편하게 경치 구경만 했겠나. 이에 관한 생각들은 오는 길에 이미 끝냈다.
- **Current:** Did I really make Muyaho run himself to death while I sat on his back comfortably sightseeing? I had already finished thinking things through on the way here.
- **Defect:** The first question omits the limiting cue 만 and can deny that Taekyung rode while Muyaho ran at all. The point is that he was not merely sightseeing during the ride; he was using the journey to think through the plan. It also assigns Muyaho a male pronoun not supplied here.
- **Replacement:** Did you think I’d made Muyaho run like hell while I sat comfortably on the tiger’s back doing nothing but sightseeing? I had already finished thinking things through on the way here.
- **Rationale:** 구경만 했겠나 is a rhetorical rejection of ‘doing nothing but sightseeing,’ directly setting up the statement that he completed his planning en route.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “오, 저 동물학대범 새끼.”
- **Current:** “Oh, you animal-abusing bastards.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular target of Taekyung’s insult into a plural address to the group.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, that animal-abusing bastard.”
- **Rationale:** 저 동물학대범 새끼 is singular and refers to the person who just ordered the beasts forward.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 방금 쓰러트린 놈이 동료였나?
- **Current:** Was the man I had just knocked down his companion?
- **Defect:** The possessive pronoun has no clear antecedent in English, obscuring that Taekyung is inferring a relationship between the new attacker and the man he just felled.
- **Replacement:** Was the guy I’d just taken down a comrade of his?
- **Rationale:** 동료였나 questions whether the fallen man was the attacker’s comrade; the replacement preserves that inference while making the relationship clear.

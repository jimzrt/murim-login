# Review

The draft has several actionable fidelity issues, chiefly a reversed attribution of temperament and an ambiguous account of who traveled together.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 비록 적천강은 몰랐지만, 굉도가 법왕이라는 별호를 얻은 것에는 일신의 무공을 떠나 그의 지랄 맞은 성격을 제어할 수 있다는 점도 크게 한몫했다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang didn’t know this, but Hong Dao’s foul temper was one of the reasons he’d earned the title Dharma King: he was able to keep Jeok Cheongang’s in check, setting aside his personal martial arts.
- **Defect:** Attributes the foul temper to Hong Dao rather than Jeok Cheongang.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang didn’t know it, but apart from his martial arts, Hong Dao’s ability to keep Jeok Cheongang’s foul temper in check had played a large part in earning him the title Dharma King.
- **Rationale:** Hong Dao earned the title partly because he could control Jeok Cheongang’s temper.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 운명인 듯 우연처럼 만난 두 노소(老少)가 산서를 떠나 하남에 다다를 때까지.
- **Current:** It kept shining as the two of them—an old man and a young one who had met as if by fate, though it might have been chance—left Shanxi and made their way to Henan.
- **Defect:** “The two of them” can refer to Jeok Cheongang and the recently named Hong Dao, rather than Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** It kept shining as Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung—an old man and a young man who had met by chance as though by fate—left Shanxi and reached Henan.
- **Rationale:** The old and young travelers are Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung; the following account of their year together depends on that identification.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 정마대전과는 비교도 되지 않는 환란이라니.
- **Current:** A calamity that couldn’t compare to the Great Faction War?
- **Defect:** Leaves the direction of the comparison unclear, obscuring how much greater the predicted calamity would be.
- **Replacement:** A calamity that would dwarf the Great Faction War?
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang is struggling to believe the coming calamity will exceed even that war.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 언젠가 온 천하가 어둠에 잠긴다 해도, 그 신성만큼은 빛을 잃지 않을 것이라고도 했었지.
- **Current:** “He also said that even when the whole world was plunged into darkness one day, that Morning Star would never lose its light.”
- **Defect:** Changes an “even if” condition into a definite “when” prediction.
- **Replacement:** “He also said that even if the whole world were plunged into darkness one day, that Morning Star would never lose its light.”
- **Rationale:** 잠긴다 해도 presents the darkness as a condition under which the star would still shine.

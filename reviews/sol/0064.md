# Review

One critical subject reversal, one added lethal implication, and several losses of hierarchy, profanity, characterization, and comic register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 설마, 하는 눈빛에 위팽이 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** At Wipeng's look of *Surely you don’t mean…*, Jin Wikyung nodded.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses both the look and the nod: Jin Wikyung gives the questioning look, and Wipeng confirms it by nodding.
- **Replacement:** At the disbelief in Jin Wikyung’s eyes, Wipeng nodded.
- **Rationale:** The explicit subject of 고개를 끄덕였다 is Wipeng, while the questioning look belongs contextually to Jin Wikyung.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 정확하게 타격했다면 지금의 한 방이 마지막 공격이 됐을 거다.
- **Current:** If it had landed cleanly, that blow would have been the last attack I ever faced.
- **Defect:** “Ever faced” implies that the punch would have killed Taekyung, while the source only says it would have ended the exchange.
- **Replacement:** If it had landed cleanly, that would have been the last blow.
- **Rationale:** 마지막 공격 describes the final attack in context and does not establish Taekyung’s death.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 일각이라도 버텨 봐라. 그럼 네가 내 형이다.
- **Current:** Hold out for a quarter of an hour. Then you can be my hyung.
- **Defect:** “Can be” weakens the categorical hierarchy-reversal taunt into permission, and retaining “hyung” is less natural than expressing the relationship directly.
- **Replacement:** Hold out for a quarter of an hour. Then you’re my big brother.
- **Rationale:** 그럼 네가 내 형이다 literally makes Taekyung the older brother if he lasts that long; the certainty is the punchline.

## F04 — Major

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00

- **Current:** *Jesus Christ, what kind of name is that for a fist technique?*
- **Defect:** “Jesus Christ” substitutes a milder religious exclamation for the source’s strong profanity.

## F06 — Major

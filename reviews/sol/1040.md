# Review

The draft obscures why Jin attacks the mages’ targets, renders a mountain invocation like the name of a person, and misformats both System panels.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 무시무시한 불의 고리가 그리는 궤적에는, 마법의 범위에 포함되어 있던 십여 명의 적들이 있었다.
- **Current:** A terrifying ring of fire traced its arc, and more than ten enemies caught within its range were swept up in it.
- **Defect:** “Its range” reads as the fire’s range, rather than the spell’s. This obscures Jin’s reason for killing those enemies before the body-strengthening magic takes effect.
- **Replacement:** The terrifying ring of fire swept through a dozen or so enemies within the spell’s range.
- **Rationale:** 마법의 범위 specifies the magic’s range; the preceding sentence establishes that its targets must be alive for it to work.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “태산(太山)이여.”
- **Current:** “Taishan.”
- **Defect:** The invocation reads as an address to the character Taishan rather than a call upon a mountain.
- **Replacement:** “Great mountain.”
- **Rationale:** The spell brings down immense pressure, and Jin subsequently cuts through the invoked 태산.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 태산을 베었다.
- **Current:** It cut through Taishan.
- **Defect:** This makes the spearhead appear to cut through the character Taishan.
- **Replacement:** It cut through the great mountain.
- **Rationale:** 태산 here refers back to the mountain invoked by the spell, not the named character.

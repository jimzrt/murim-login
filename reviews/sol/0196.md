# Review

Two critical factual errors alter the elapsed time and conflate Seok Family Manor with Prince Shangshan. The draft also omits the merchant’s dismissive response about his son and contains several localized register and wording issues.

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 일각 후, 태원진가와 항산검문의 전쟁부터 지금까지의 전후사정을 모두 들은 상인이 넋 나간 얼굴로 중얼거렸다.
- **Current:** An hour later, the merchant had heard the entire story, from the war between the Jin Family of Taiyuan and the Mount Heng Sword Sect to the present day. He muttered with a dazed expression:
- **Defect:** The draft changes the elapsed time from one gak, approximately fifteen minutes, to one hour.
- **Replacement:** Fifteen minutes later, the merchant had heard the entire story, from the war between the Jin Family of Taiyuan and the Mount Heng Sword Sect to the present day. He muttered with a dazed expression:
- **Rationale:** 일각 denotes approximately fifteen minutes, not an hour.

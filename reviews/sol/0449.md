# Review

One critical factual contradiction plus several mechanism, register, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 상반신의 절반이 사라져 있고 얼굴이 부풀어 오른 시신을 확인한 무송이 눈을 부릅떴다.
- **Current:** Half of its upper body was gone, and its face had swollen beyond recognition. Mu Song’s eyes widened when he saw it.
- **Defect:** “Beyond recognition” contradicts the source and the immediately following identification; the face is swollen but still recognizable.
- **Replacement:** Half of its upper body was gone, and its face was bloated. Mu Song’s eyes widened when he saw it.
- **Rationale:** The source says only that the face was swollen, and Mu Song subsequently confirms that it is not damaged beyond recognition.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 성큼 다가온 진태경의 물음에, 무송이 망연한 표정으로 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** At Jin Taekyung’s question, the young man having strode over to him, Mu Song nodded with a dazed expression.
- **Defect:** The modifier is grammatically malformed and obscures that Jin Taekyung is the person who strode over.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung strode over to him, and Mu Song nodded dazedly at the question.
- **Rationale:** The source clearly assigns the approach to Jin Taekyung and the nod to Mu Song.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 화염신장(火焰神掌)에 실린 열양지기가 수분을 증발시키고 쾌조선을 앞으로 밀어 낸다.
- **Current:** The Scorching Yang Qi carried by the Flame Divine Palm evaporated the moisture in the air and pushed the fast ship forward.
- **Defect:** The draft adds “in the air,” changing the propulsion mechanism: the qi evaporates the surrounding water to drive the ship forward.
- **Replacement:** The Scorching Yang Qi carried by the Flame Divine Palm evaporated the river water and propelled the fast ship forward.
- **Rationale:** 수분 refers to the water being evaporated in this river setting; restricting it to airborne moisture is unsupported and mechanically incoherent with pushing the ship.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “가주께서 급히 모셔오라 하셨습니다.”
- **Current:** “The Family Head ordered us to bring you at once.”
- **Defect:** The wording makes the respectful summons sound coercive and changes the single messenger’s deferential escort language into an order to seize or convey the group.
- **Replacement:** “The Family Head ordered me to escort you to him at once.”
- **Rationale:** 모셔오라 is honorific and directs the messenger to respectfully escort the addressees to the Family Head.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무림과 관이 상호 불가침의 관계라는 것은 삼척동자도 아는 사실.
- **Current:** Even a three-year-old knows that Murim and the authorities maintain a relationship of mutual noninterference.
- **Defect:** 삼척동자 is an idiom meaning even a mere child, not specifically a three-year-old.
- **Replacement:** Even a child knows that Murim and the authorities maintain a relationship of mutual noninterference.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the idiom’s intended sense without introducing a false exact age.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 워프, 아니 이동진이라고 불리는 그 진법이 이곳 어딘가에 숨겨져 있다면 그걸로 모든 것이 해결되니까요.
- **Current:** If the Moving Formation they call a warp gate was hidden somewhere around here, that would solve everything.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses Taekyung’s self-correction and incorrectly says that the Moving Formation is called a warp gate, losing both the established term and the modern-versus-Murim register joke.
- **Replacement:** If a warp—no, if the formation they call a Moving Formation were hidden somewhere around here, that would explain everything.
- **Rationale:** Taekyung first uses the modern term “warp,” then corrects himself to the established Murim term “Moving Formation.”

# Review

Three actionable fidelity findings: one reversed subject and two altered physical actions.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이제 더는 손가락 하나 까딱하지 못하는 상황에서도, 죽어 가는 자신의 모습을 똑바로 직시하고 있는 저 타오르는 눈동자의 주인을.
- **Current:** Even now, when he could no longer move a finger, the owner of those blazing eyes was staring straight at him as he died.
- **Defect:** The English makes the Blood Lord the one unable to move a finger. The source describes the owner of the blazing eyes, Jin Taekyung, as unable to move.
- **Replacement:** Even though the owner of those blazing eyes could no longer lift a finger, he was watching the Blood Lord die.
- **Rationale:** The Blood Lord moves his fingers in the next passage; the immobile person watching him is Jin Taekyung.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 손가락의 뼈 마디마디를 잘게 부수고, 살과 피를 불태웠다.
- **Current:** The bones in every finger cracked. Flesh and blood burned.
- **Defect:** The passive wording obscures that the blocking hand crushes the monster’s fingers and burns his flesh and blood.
- **Replacement:** It crushed the bones in the monster’s fingers and burned his flesh and blood.
- **Rationale:** The actions continue from the hand that intercepts the monster’s strike; they are injuries inflicted on the attacker.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하염없이 입가를 타고 흘러넘치는 핏물을 삼켜 내며, 온 힘을 다해 목소리를 쥐어 짜냈다.
- **Current:** Blood kept spilling from the corners of his mouth. Summoning all his strength, he forced out his voice.
- **Defect:** The draft omits his attempt to swallow the blood spilling from his mouth.
- **Replacement:** He swallowed the blood that kept spilling over his lips and summoned all his strength to force out the words.
- **Rationale:** 삼켜 내며 explicitly describes swallowing the overflowing blood while struggling to speak.

# Review

One sentence reverses the logic of the chest’s value; the draft also misidentifies a royal title and departs from the required System-panel format.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아무리 돈이 많아도 정상인이라면 무기를 만드는 데 투자하는 물건.
- **Current:** And no sane person, no matter how rich, would invest it in a weapon.
- **Defect:** Reverses where a sane person would use the rare metal.
- **Replacement:** And no matter how rich you were, any sane person would use it to forge a weapon.
- **Rationale:** The source says a sensible person would put the metal toward making a weapon, rather than a mere storage chest.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아, 황태제 전하께서 이 말을 전해 달래.
- **Current:** Oh, His Highness the Crown Prince asked me to tell you this.
- **Defect:** Crown Prince implies the emperor’s son; 황태제 identifies his younger brother as heir.
- **Replacement:** Oh, His Highness the Emperor’s Brother and Heir asked me to tell you this.
- **Rationale:** The title preserves both the familial relationship and the succession status expressed by 황태제.

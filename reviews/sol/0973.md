# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses the Keshiks’ defensive maneuver and alters two quantities. Several smaller choices change the narrator’s meaning or tone.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 곧장 아군의 중심을 파고들어 잇따른 추가 공격을 대비하고 있었으니.
- **Current:** Now they were plunging straight into the heart of her allies’ formation, ready for another wave of attacks.
- **Defect:** “Ready for another wave of attacks” suggests the Keshiks are preparing to attack again. They move among her allies to protect themselves against the Bow Saint’s follow-up attacks.
- **Replacement:** They had plunged straight into the heart of her allies’ formation to guard against her follow-up attacks.
- **Rationale:** The maneuver follows their losses to her attacks and prevents her from firing again without hitting allies.

## F02 — Critical

- **Source:** 이미 몇 시진 째 이어지는 전투에서

- **Defect:** A *shichen* is approximately two hours; “hours” loses the stated unit and understates the duration.
- **Replacement:** The battle had already dragged on for several *shichen*.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies several 시진, and the packet’s glossary establishes *shichen*.

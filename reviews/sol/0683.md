# Review

The draft contains several altered or omitted combat mechanics, including the shattered boulder, Force-wreathed weapons, the numbered spear form, an unspecified parry turned into a kick, and clouded judgment changed to impaired vision.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 퍼엉!

가슴을 통해 전해지는 열기는 뜨거웠고.
- **Current:** The heat coming through his chest was scorching.
- **Defect:** The opening impact sound is omitted.
- **Replacement:** BOOM!

The heat coming through his chest was scorching.
- **Rationale:** The source opens with a distinct explosive impact before describing the heat transmitted through his chest.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하지만 거대한 만근거석(萬斤巨石)을 부수며 처박힌 대설귀를 더욱 괴롭게 하는 것은 고통도, 내상도 아니었다.
- **Current:** But what tormented the Great Snow Fiend even more than the pain or his internal injuries as he lay embedded in the massive boulder was neither of those things.
- **Defect:** The draft omits both the boulder's specified ten-thousand-geun weight and the fact that the Great Snow Fiend smashed through it as he was hurled away.
- **Replacement:** But what tormented the Great Snow Fiend, who had been hurled through a massive ten-thousand-geun boulder, was neither the pain nor his internal injuries.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly describes him breaking the enormous weighted boulder as he crashes into it, rather than merely lying embedded in an intact boulder.

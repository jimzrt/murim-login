# Review

The draft reverses who is excluded from the audience and makes a deliberately vague threat explicit. The juvenile-immunity joke also loses its legal premise.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그런데 용의 핏줄로 태어나 반역으로 천하를 움켜쥔 황제가, 홍진도 허락받지 않은 자리에 나를 부르다니.
- **Current:** And yet the Emperor, born of the dragon’s blood and having seized the realm through rebellion, had summoned me to an audience Prince Shangshan wasn’t even allowed to attend.
- **Defect:** Replaces the excluded Hong Jin with Prince Shangshan, who has been summoned.
- **Replacement:** And yet the Emperor, born of the dragon’s blood and having seized the realm through rebellion, had summoned me to an audience even Hong Jin wasn’t allowed to attend.
- **Rationale:** 홍진도 허락받지 않은 identifies Hong Jin as the person not permitted to attend.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 여봐라. 지금 즉시 형부(刑部)에 연통을 넣어라. 가장 넓은 곳으로 준비하라고.
- **Current:** “Guards. Contact the Ministry of Punishments at once. Tell them to prepare their largest cell.”
- **Defect:** Adds “cell” before Jeong reveals what he means by sending Jin to the Ministry of Punishments.
- **Replacement:** “Guards. Contact the Ministry of Punishments at once. Tell them to prepare the most spacious accommodations.”
- **Rationale:** 가장 넓은 곳 means the most spacious place; the source leaves its purpose unstated for the ensuing exchange.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “뭐 좀 물어볼 게 있는데. 혹시 촉법소년이라고 아나?”

“촉법, 뭐?”

“촉법소년.”

“그게 뭐지? 처음 들어보는데.”

“……아냐. 됐다.”
- **Current:** “Hey, can I ask you something? Do you know what the age of criminal responsibility is?”

“The age of what?”

“The age of criminal responsibility.”

“What’s that? I’ve never heard of it.”

“……Never mind.”
- **Defect:** Changes a category of child who cannot be criminally prosecuted into the legal age threshold itself, weakening the setup for Jin’s fantasy.
- **Replacement:** “Hey, can I ask you something? Ever heard of *chokbeop sonyeon*[^1]?”

“Chokbeop what?”

“*Chokbeop sonyeon*.”

“What’s that? Never heard of it.”

“…Never mind.”

[^1]: A Korean legal term for a child too young to be criminally prosecuted.
- **Rationale:** 촉법소년 names the child, not the age; the distinction is what Jin hopes Prince Shangshan could exploit.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 응. 나 아직 촉법소년~
- **Current:** —Yeah. I’m still underage!
- **Defect:** Being underage alone does not convey Jin’s imagined exemption from prosecution.
- **Replacement:** —Yep. I’m still too young to be prosecuted!

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 촉법소년이라서 황제가 되다니.
- **Current:** He’d become Emperor because he was underage.
- **Defect:** Repeats the broader underage claim rather than the imagined immunity that makes the succession joke work.
- **Replacement:** Becoming Emperor because he was too young to be prosecuted.
- **Rationale:** Jin is imagining the prince escaping punishment for killing the Emperor, not merely succeeding him while young.

## F06 — Minor

# Review

The draft changes the extent of Jeong Hogun’s moral self-assessment and blurs what survives the guards’ final stand.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 비록 지금껏 행한 모든 일이 완벽한 정의(正義)는 아니었을지라도, 진정한 충의(忠義)를 지키며 살아온 일평생이었으니.
- **Current:** Though nothing he’d done in his life had been perfect justice, he had lived by true loyalty.
- **Defect:** “Nothing he’d done” says none of his actions were perfectly just; the source concedes only that not every action was.
- **Replacement:** Though not everything he’d done had been perfectly just, he had lived his life true to his loyalty.
- **Rationale:** 모든 일이 … 아니었을지라도 qualifies the whole of his record, not each individual deed.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 비록 그 육신은 필멸(必滅)이나, 끝내는 불멸(不滅)로 남을 최후를 위해.
- **Current:** Toward a final stand in which their bodies would perish, but they themselves would remain immortal.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the guards themselves immortal. The source says their final stand will endure.
- **Replacement:** Toward a final stand that would endure even after their mortal bodies perished.
- **Rationale:** 불멸로 남을 modifies 최후, their end or final stand.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 불현듯 터져 나와 사방을 물들이는, 거대한 핏빛 섬광에 파묻혀 스러지는 그 마지막 순간까지도.
- **Current:** Right up to the final moment, when a massive blood-red flash erupted without warning and swallowed everything around them.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the guards fall within the flash and substitutes an unsupported claim that it swallows everything around them.
- **Replacement:** Right up to their final moment, when they fell, engulfed by a massive blood-red flash that erupted without warning and stained everything around them.
- **Rationale:** 파묻혀 스러지는 describes their deaths; 사방을 물들이는 describes the flash coloring the surroundings.

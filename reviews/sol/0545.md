# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues remain, including a garbled food-offer joke mistranslated as a question about fear, an incorrect martial rank, and altered characterization or degree.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** “들어가라. 뒈지기 싫으면.”
- **Current:** “Go ahead. If you don’t want to die.”
- **Defect:** “Go ahead” sounds as though Jeok Cheongang is granting Hyuk Mujin permission to join the Five Kings Hall. The Korean is a dismissive command backed by a death threat.
- **Replacement:** “Go. Unless you want to die.”
- **Rationale:** The imperative 들어가라 dismisses Hyuk Mujin rather than approving his request, while 뒈지기 싫으면 supplies the threat.

## F03 — Minor

# Review

The draft contains several subject and action errors in the cemetery sequence, misidentifies what the Small Cataclysm names, and loses meaningful distinctions in terminology, emphasis, quantity, and emotional phrasing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래, 아마 소격변(小激變)이라 명명된 아크 리치와의 전투 이후였을 것이다.
- **Current:** Yes. It had probably been after the battle with the Arch Lich known as the Small Cataclysm.
- **Defect:** The modifier makes the Arch Lich itself sound as though it was known as the Small Cataclysm, whereas the source says the battle was given that name.
- **Replacement:** Yes. It had probably been after the battle with the Arch Lich—the incident dubbed the Small Cataclysm.
- **Rationale:** 소격변이라 명명된 modifies the battle or incident, not the Arch Lich.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마력 수치는 비정상적으로 상승했고, 그에 따라 몬스터는 강력해졌으며, 대격변 이후 몰라볼 만큼 줄어들었던 인명 피해가 곳곳에서 속출했다.
- **Current:** Magic levels were rising abnormally. Monsters had grown stronger along with them, and human casualties—which had decreased so drastically after the Great Cataclysm that they had seemed almost nonexistent—were now appearing everywhere.
- **Defect:** The draft uses “Magic” instead of the modern Hunter-system term “mana,” weakens the causal link expressed by 그에 따라, and adds the unsupported claim that casualties had seemed almost nonexistent.
- **Replacement:** Mana levels had risen abnormally, monsters had grown stronger accordingly, and human casualties—which had fallen dramatically after the Great Cataclysm—were once again mounting everywhere.
- **Rationale:** 마력 is mana in this setting, 그에 따라 explicitly links the monsters’ increasing strength to rising mana, and 몰라볼 만큼 줄어들었던 means casualties had fallen dramatically, not that they had nearly disappeared.

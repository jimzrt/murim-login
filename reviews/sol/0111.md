# Review

The draft contains several subject and quantity reversals, a blurred family-death timeline, and localized losses in hierarchy, mechanism, terminology, and System formatting.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 멀찍이 선두에서 앞서 달리던 나를 발견한 마적들이 누런 이를 드러내며 웃는다.
- **Current:** The mounted bandits who had been riding ahead of us at some distance spotted me at the front. They grinned, yellow teeth exposed.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the mounted bandits the ones riding ahead, reversing the action; Taekyung was riding well ahead of his companions when the stationary bandits spotted him.
- **Replacement:** The mounted bandits spotted me riding well out in front and grinned, baring their yellow teeth.
- **Rationale:** 앞서 달리던 modifies 나, not 마적들.

# Review

The draft changes two consequential facts: where the cannonballs land and whether Mu Song has decided to refuse his master’s order. It also loses the recoil behind his landing and the reason he briefly considers himself a hero.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한참이나 선체를 빗나가 수면 위를 후려치는 포탄들을 본 무송이 눈매를 좁혔다.
- **Current:** Even so, Mu Song narrowed his eyes as the cannonballs fell far short of the hulls and slammed into the water.
- **Defect:** “Fell far short” specifies a direction the source does not give; the cannonballs miss the hulls by a wide margin.
- **Replacement:** Even so, Mu Song narrowed his eyes as cannonballs missed the hulls by a wide margin and slammed into the water.
- **Rationale:** 한참이나 선체를 빗나가 describes the extent of the miss, not whether the shots landed short.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘제아무리 스승님의 명령이라지만…… 도무지 내키지 않는군.’
- **Current:** *Even if it’s my Master’s order… I can’t bring myself to go along with it.*
- **Defect:** Mu Song’s reluctance becomes an inability or decision to comply, though he continues the attack.
- **Replacement:** *Even if it’s my Master’s order… this doesn’t sit right with me at all.*
- **Rationale:** 도무지 내키지 않는군 expresses strong unwillingness, not refusal.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 포탄을 튕겨낸 반동으로 뱃머리에 착지한 무송은 저릿해진 손아귀를 느끼며 외쳤다.
- **Current:** Amid the thunderous blast, Mu Song landed on the bow, deflecting the cannonball. He felt the tingling in his grip and shouted:
- **Defect:** The draft omits the recoil from deflecting the cannonball as the cause of his landing.
- **Replacement:** Amid the thunderous blast, the recoil from deflecting the cannonball sent Mu Song back onto the bow. His grip tingled, and he shouted:
- **Rationale:** 포탄을 튕겨낸 반동으로 explicitly links the deflection’s recoil to his landing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘그러고 보면, 그들을 도왔던 나 역시 잠시 동안은 협객이었던 셈인가.’
- **Current:** *Come to think of it, that made me a chivalrous hero too, for a little while.*
- **Defect:** The thought omits that Mu Song helped those heroes—the specific basis for his brief claim to chivalry.
- **Replacement:** *Come to think of it, since I helped them, was I a chivalrous hero for a little while too?*
- **Rationale:** 그들을 도왔던 나 makes his own aid to them essential to the thought.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “……단숨에 끝낸다. 다들 준비해라.”
- **Current:** “…We’ll finish this in one strike. Everyone, get ready.”
- **Defect:** “One strike” introduces a single-blow tactic absent from the order.
- **Replacement:** “…We’ll finish this quickly. Everyone, get ready.”
- **Rationale:** 단숨에 means in one go or without delay, not with one literal strike.

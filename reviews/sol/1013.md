# Review

The draft preserves the scene overall, but changes a few consequential details of timing, speech, and address.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “시발, 진짜 좆 됐다…….”
- **Current:** *Fuck. I’m so screwed…*
- **Defect:** Formats Ma Junggeol’s barely audible speech as an unspoken thought.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck. I’m so fucking screwed…”
- **Rationale:** His lips move around words he cannot bring himself to say loudly; the source presents them as speech.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 당연히 그동안은 엄두도 내지 못했지만.
- **Current:** Of course, until then, we hadn’t even dared consider it.
- **Defect:** Says they had not considered the route before searching for it, contradicting their years of planning.
- **Replacement:** Of course, we hadn’t dared attempt it all that time.
- **Rationale:** They had considered and investigated the route but had not dared undertake it.

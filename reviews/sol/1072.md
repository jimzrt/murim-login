# Review

Action reversals and several meaningful terminology and characterization errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그 거대한 체격과는 어울리지 않는 속도로 삽시간에 주위를 에워싸는 한편, 철벽처럼 앞을 가로막은 괴물들 너머로 흑의인은 씹어뱉듯 말을 이었다.
- **Current:** The monsters swept around them with speed that belied their enormous frames. Beyond them, the black-robed man spoke through clenched teeth to the intruder, blocked from view by a wall of monsters.
- **Defect:** The draft places the wall of monsters in front of the intruder rather than in front of the black-robed man.
- **Replacement:** The monsters swept around them with speed that belied their enormous frames, forming an impenetrable wall in front of the black-robed man. From behind it, he spoke through clenched teeth.
- **Rationale:** 철벽처럼 앞을 가로막은 괴물들 너머로 흑의인은 places the speaker behind the monsters blocking the way.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 주절주절 떠들던 불청객이 헙, 하고 입을 다물었다.
- **Current:** The intruder rambled on, then clapped a hand over his mouth.
- **Defect:** The draft adds a hand gesture; he abruptly closes his mouth.
- **Replacement:** The intruder stopped rambling and snapped his mouth shut with a little gasp.
- **Rationale:** 입을 다물었다 describes closing his mouth, with no hand action.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 귀를 쫑긋 세운 채, 두 손으로 나팔 모양까지 만들어 주위의 소리를 듣고 있던 불청객을 향해.
- **Current:** The intruder, surrounded by horrible monsters, had perked up his ears and cupped his hands around his mouth like a trumpet to listen.
- **Defect:** Cupping his hands around his mouth would project his voice, not help him hear; the action is directed at listening.
- **Replacement:** The intruder, surrounded by horrible monsters, had perked up his ears and even cupped his hands around them like a trumpet to listen.
- **Rationale:** 두 손으로 나팔 모양까지 만들어 주위의 소리를 듣고 있던 ties the hand gesture to hearing the sounds around him.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 갑자 단위의 공력도, 신공(神功)이라 불릴 정도로 뛰어난 무공도 없지만
- **Current:** He had neither the internal energy of several jiazi nor martial arts exceptional enough to be called divine.
- **Defect:** “Several” invents a quantity absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** He had neither internal energy measured in jiazi nor martial arts exceptional enough to be called divine.
- **Rationale:** 갑자 단위 means measured in units of jiazi; it does not specify several.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “네놈…… 정파의 끄나풀이로군.”
- **Current:** “You… You’re a spy for the orthodox faction.”
- **Defect:** “Spy” assigns a specific role where the accusation is that he is a lackey or agent.
- **Replacement:** “You… You’re an orthodox faction lackey.”
- **Rationale:** 끄나풀 marks a subordinate doing another group’s bidding, not necessarily an intelligence operative.

## F06 — Major

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 보다 정확히는, 대답할 정신조차 남아 있지 않았다.
- **Current:** More precisely, he had no awareness left with which to answer.

# Review

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그럼 볼 것 없이 쓸어 버리면 될 것을. 무슨 헛짓거리들을 하고 있는 거지?
- **Current:** “Then they should just sweep them away without bothering to look. What kind of pointless nonsense are they doing?”
- **Defect:** “Without bothering to look” literalizes the idiom 볼 것 없이 and introduces an absent physical action.
- **Replacement:** “Then they should just wipe them out without a second thought. What kind of pointless nonsense are they doing?”
- **Rationale:** 볼 것 없이 means that no consideration or hesitation is necessary, not that they should avoid looking.

- **Defect:** The draft conflates the organizational post 일 단주 with the man’s existing sobriquet, First Fiend, making the explanation tautological and losing the command hierarchy.

- **Rationale:** 일 단주 names his assigned captaincy, while 일괴 is his separate identity as First Fiend.

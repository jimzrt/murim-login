# Review

The draft preserves the scene, but several cues are mistranslated or lost.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** ‘……아.’
- **Current:** *…Ah.*
- **Defect:** “Ah” reads as an exclamation, not the audible ending of the name the father has spoken.
- **Replacement:** *…a.*
- **Rationale:** The Korean leaves only the intimate vocative ending visible while withholding the name until the later reveal.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “엄청 뜨겁네요.”
- **Current:** “That looks really hot.”
- **Defect:** Changes Taekyung’s perception of heat into a visual judgment, weakening his next line about being able to feel it.
- **Replacement:** “Wow, that’s hot.”
- **Rationale:** He uses the heat he can feel as evidence that he has come to his senses.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 칭얼거리는 것이 조금도 어색하게 느껴지지 않을 정도의 어린아이.
- **Current:** A young child, who could whine like that without finding it the least bit strange.
- **Defect:** Makes the child, rather than the observing narrator, the one who finds the whining unsurprising.
- **Replacement:** A child so young that hearing myself whine didn’t feel strange at all.
- **Rationale:** The line describes how natural the child’s whining feels to Taekyung as he experiences the memory.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 아들을 향한 아버지의 음성을. 그 이름을.
- **Current:** A father’s voice speaking to his son. His name.
- **Defect:** “His name” can refer to the father, obscuring that Taekyung heard the son’s name.
- **Replacement:** A father’s voice speaking to his son. His son’s name.
- **Rationale:** The name revealed later is the name the father spoke to his son.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.89
- **Source:** “……장님, 조장님!”
- **Current:** “…Captain!”
- **Defect:** Drops the speaker’s clipped first attempt at the address.
- **Replacement:** “…tain, Captain!”
- **Rationale:** 장님 is the end of 조장님; the repeated address conveys the speaker’s alarm.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 극양(極陽)의 기운
- **Current:** the Extreme Yang energy that flowed through me.
- **Defect:** Uses generic “energy” for Murim qi.
- **Replacement:** the Extreme Yang qi flowing through me.
- **Rationale:** The source identifies the flowing 기운 as Extreme Yang; the binding terminology specifies qi for Murim 기운.

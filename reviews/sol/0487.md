# Review

Two major fidelity defects affect Hyuk Mujin’s description of Cheongpung and the Gate’s operating state. Additional localized issues alter an exact duration, a place name, a wordplay-based injury, profanity, and the containment mechanism.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그 과정에서 청풍과 혁무진은 제갈풍을 따라갔으니 꼭 이틀 만에 보는 얼굴이다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin had followed Zhuge Feng, so this was the first time I had seen them in almost two full days.
- **Defect:** The source says exactly two days, not almost two days.
- **Replacement:** Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin had followed Zhuge Feng, so this was the first time I had seen them in exactly two days.
- **Rationale:** 꼭 이틀 means precisely or exactly two days; “almost” changes the stated duration.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 급부상했다면서 때린 다음 급 부상당했다. 뭐 그러실 것 같아서요.
- **Current:** I thought you might say I was shooting up, then hit me and make me get shot up.
- **Defect:** The second half of the Korean means suddenly suffering an injury, while “get shot up” suggests gunfire or being propelled upward.
- **Replacement:** I thought you might say I was on the rise, then hit me and leave me laid up.
- **Rationale:** The joke splits 급부상, “rapid rise,” into 급 부상, “sudden injury.” The replacement preserves the rise/injury contrast without introducing shooting.

## F03 — Minor

- **Source:** 태원진가의 북부 고원 평정을 통해 쾌풍검(快風劍)이라는 그럴듯한 별호까지 얻은 무인이다.
- **Current:** Hyuk Mujin might be treated like a nobody here, but he was a martial artist who had earned the impressive sobriquet Swift Wind Sword after helping the Jin Family of Taiyuan pacify the northern plateau.
- **Defect:** The established place name Gaoyuan is translated generically as “the northern plateau.”
- **Replacement:** Hyuk Mujin might be treated like a nobody here, but he was a martial artist who had earned the impressive sobriquet Swift Wind Sword after helping the Jin Family of Taiyuan pacify northern Gaoyuan.
- **Rationale:** 고원 is the established location Gaoyuan in the binding glossary.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 화산파의 신성이요, 검성의 진전을 이은 초절정 고수가 펼치는 금나수(擒拿手)를 버틸 리가 있나.
- **Current:** How could Hyuk Mujin possibly resist a grappling technique performed by a Supreme Peak master of the Huashan Sect who had inherited the Sword Saint’s legacy?
- **Defect:** The draft omits Cheongpung’s status as Huashan’s Morning Star.
- **Replacement:** How could Hyuk Mujin possibly resist the grappling technique of Huashan’s Morning Star, a Supreme Peak master who had inherited the Sword Saint’s legacy?
- **Rationale:** 화산파의 신성 explicitly identifies him as Huashan’s Morning Star; this is meaningful reputation and hierarchy, not redundant ornament.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 불과 한 두시진 전 직접 확인한 바에 따르면, 게이트는 제 기능을 상실했음에도 미약한 마력을 흘리고 있었다.
- **Current:** And according to what I had confirmed personally just one or two shichen earlier, the Gate was still leaking a faint amount of mana despite having lost most of its functions.
- **Defect:** The draft weakens a complete loss of function into the loss of only most functions.
- **Replacement:** And according to what I had confirmed personally just one or two shichen earlier, the Gate was still leaking a faint amount of mana despite having ceased to function.
- **Rationale:** 제 기능을 상실했다 means the Gate had ceased performing its function. “Most of its functions” adds a degree of continued operation absent from the source and alters the Gate’s mechanics.

# Review

One critical quantity error and several meaningful fidelity and register issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 「놈들을 사로잡거나 죽이는 사람에게는 즉시 삼천만 달러다!」
- **Current:** “Three million dollars immediately to whoever captures or kills them!”
- **Defect:** The bounty is reduced by a factor of ten, also breaking the subsequent joke that it rose from ten million dollars.
- **Replacement:** “Thirty million dollars immediately to whoever captures or kills them!”
- **Rationale:** 삼천만 달러 means thirty million dollars, not three million.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 너와 대립하고 있는 또 다른 무장 테러 단체에 관해서도.
- **Current:** We also know about the other armed terrorist organizations at odds with you.
- **Defect:** The draft changes a single opposing terrorist organization into multiple organizations.
- **Replacement:** We also know about the other armed terrorist organization at odds with you.
- **Rationale:** 또 다른 무장 테러 단체 is singular: another armed terrorist organization.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그는 언제나 죽음을 내리는 사람이었지, 받아들이는 사람이 아니었으니까.
- **Current:** He had always been the one who dealt death, never the one who accepted it.
- **Defect:** “Accepted it” implies psychological acceptance of mortality rather than being the person who receives death, weakening the source's executioner-versus-victim contrast.
- **Replacement:** He had always been the one who dealt death, never the one on the receiving end.
- **Rationale:** 받아들이는 사람이 contrasts with 죽음을 내리는 사람이 and means the recipient of death in this context.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 「그걸 우리한테 물으면 안 되지. 네가 그렇게 팔아먹었던 신에게 물어봐라. 어차피 잠시 후면 만나게 될 테니.」
- **Current:** “Don’t ask us. Ask the god you’ve been selling out like that. You’ll meet him soon enough anyway.”
- **Defect:** “Selling out” suggests betraying God, while the source accuses Muhammad of exploiting and peddling God's name for his own purposes.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t ask us. Ask the god whose name you’ve been peddling. You’ll meet him soon enough anyway.”
- **Rationale:** 신을 팔아먹다 here means to exploit or trade on God's name, matching the later accusation that he used God's name to commit atrocities.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “어. 네가 실컷 빨아 재끼는 그 알라.”
- **Current:** “Yeah. The Allah you keep sucking dry.”
- **Defect:** “Sucking dry” implies draining or exploiting Allah and misses the source's deliberately obscene accusation of excessive devotion or sycophancy.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. That Allah you’re always sucking off.”
- **Rationale:** 빨아 재끼는 is a coarse, sexualized insult meaning to suck up to or suck off someone, and the source does not soften it.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 효자면 효자고, 손이면 손이지 효자손은 또 뭐란 말인가.
- **Current:** A son was a son, and a hand was a hand. So what the hell was a back scratcher supposed to be?
- **Defect:** The English no longer contains the compound that Muhammad is mentally splitting into “filial son” and “hand,” leaving the joke logically incoherent.
- **Replacement:** A filial son was one thing, and a hand was another. So what the hell was a “filial son’s hand”[^2] supposed to be?

[^2]: The Korean word *hyojason* literally means “a filial son’s hand” and refers to a back scratcher.
- **Rationale:** 효자손 is the ordinary Korean word for a back scratcher but literally consists of 효자, “filial son,” and 손, “hand”; Muhammad's confusion depends on that wordplay.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 요약하자면, 뭐 빠지게 돌아다녀야 했다는 소리다.
- **Current:** In short, we had to run around like crazy.
- **Defect:** The draft sanitizes the narrator's crude euphemism and loses the abrupt register shift.
- **Replacement:** In short, we had to run our asses off.
- **Rationale:** 뭐 빠지게 is a deliberately vulgar euphemistic intensifier; “like crazy” removes that profanity and voice.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 회심의 다이아 공격!
- **Current:** *A desperate diamond attack!*
- **Defect:** “Desperate” changes the narration from presenting the diamonds as Muhammad's confident trump card to merely describing his desperation.
- **Replacement:** *His ultimate diamond attack!*
- **Rationale:** 회심의 describes a move confidently intended to succeed—a decisive or trump-card attack—not a desperate one.

# Review

Five actionable findings: two subject/action ambiguities, one altered weapon quantity, one omitted name element, and one inconsistent place-name romanization.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다들 몬스터를 상대한 직후라 마나 피로도가 극심한 데다가
- **Current:** You’ve all just fought monsters, so your mana fatigue is severe.
- **Defect:** The pronouns incorrectly assign the severe mana fatigue to the people being addressed rather than to the mages explaining why they cannot cast Teleport.
- **Replacement:** We’ve all just fought monsters, so our mana fatigue is severe.
- **Rationale:** The mage is describing the condition of the group of mages denoted by 다들; this condition is part of their explanation for being unable to cast Teleport.

# Review

Three scene-fact errors alter objects or subjects, including who held the secret meeting and who sent the unanswered letters. Several localized issues weaken physical detail, hierarchy, register, or natural English.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “찻잔이 비었군.”
- **Current:** “Your teacup is empty.”
- **Defect:** The possessive changes whose cup is empty. Hwangbo Gun’s cup is later explicitly described as not even half empty, so this creates a physical contradiction and makes Mae Jonghak’s indirect dismissal too explicit.
- **Replacement:** “The teacup is empty.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean does not specify “your,” and the later description confirms that the empty cup cannot be Hwangbo Gun’s.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99

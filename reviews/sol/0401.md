# Review

Several substantive fidelity issues alter kinship, actions, characterization, and the stated degree of martial mastery.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 웨이펑후 국방부장의 하나뿐인 외조카이자, 중국이 비밀리에 육성한 S급 헌터.
- **Current:** Wei Fenghu’s only nephew on his mother’s side, and an S-rank Hunter secretly raised by China.
- **Defect:** “On his mother’s side” incorrectly describes a relative connected through Wei Fenghu’s mother. 외조카 here means his sister’s son, consistent with Wei Fenghu being Lei Fei’s maternal uncle.
- **Replacement:** Wei Fenghu’s only nephew through his sister, and an S-rank Hunter secretly raised by China.
- **Rationale:** The current wording changes the specified kinship relationship.

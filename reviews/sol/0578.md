# Review

One critical subject error changes who survived because of his strength; several major findings alter age, former brotherhood, and the necklace’s identification mechanism.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 현역에서 물러나 어느덧 칠순에 접어든 노인이 되었지만 송천우는 누구도 부정할 수 없는 강자였다.
- **Current:** Song Cheonwoo had retired from active duty and had already entered his seventies, but he remained a powerhouse no one could deny.
- **Defect:** 칠순에 접어든 means that Song Cheonwoo is approaching seventy, not that he has already entered his seventies.
- **Replacement:** Song Cheonwoo had retired from active duty and was now an old man nearing seventy, but he remained a powerhouse no one could deny.
- **Rationale:** The draft changes the stated age range from nearly seventy to anywhere from seventy through seventy-nine.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한때 서로를 형과 동생으로 불렀던 그를.
- **Current:** The man who had once called him elder brother and younger brother.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Kim Hwajong call Song Cheonwoo both elder and younger brother, rather than conveying their reciprocal relationship as older and younger brothers.
- **Replacement:** The man with whom he had once shared the bond of older and younger brothers.
- **Rationale:** 서로 indicates reciprocity: the two men once treated each other as brothers, with one senior and the other junior.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “예티의 목걸이다. 네가 이곳으로 오라고 했을 때, 반드시 쓰임새가 있을 거라 생각했지.”
- **Current:** “It’s a Yeti’s Necklace. When you told me to come here, I thought it would have a use someday.”
- **Defect:** The draft weakens 반드시, Song Cheonwoo’s certainty that the necklace would prove useful, and adds the unsupported timing “someday.”
- **Replacement:** “It’s a Yeti’s Necklace. When you told me to come here, I knew it was bound to come in handy.”
- **Rationale:** 반드시 expresses confidence or inevitability, not merely a vague possibility at some future time.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 불과 몇 달 전, 블랙 와이번의 둥지에서 원명훈이 진태경을 함정에 빠트리기 위해 준비했던 마법 아이템이었으니까.
- **Current:** Only a few months earlier, Won Myunghoon had prepared it as a magic item to use against Jin Taekyung in a wyvern’s nest.
- **Defect:** The specific Black Wyvern is reduced to a generic wyvern.
- **Replacement:** Only a few months earlier, Won Myunghoon had prepared it as a magic item to trap Jin Taekyung in the Black Wyvern’s nest.
- **Rationale:** 블랙 와이번 explicitly identifies the monster as the Black Wyvern, and 함정에 빠트리기 means to trap rather than merely to use the item against someone.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이 목걸이는, 용족의 노예였음을 알리는 표식인 동시에 예티에게는 동족이라는 증표다.
- **Current:** It was a mark declaring that its wearer had once been a slave of the dragonkin—and, to yetis, proof that the wearer was one of their own kind.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the necklace’s active identification of the wearer as a dragonkin slave into a claim about the wearer’s personal past.
- **Replacement:** It marked its wearer as a slave of the dragonkin—and, to yetis, as one of their own kind.
- **Rationale:** The necklace functions as a deceptive identifying mark: it causes yetis to recognize the wearer as one of their kind. “Had once been” incorrectly suggests a true historical status that has since ended.

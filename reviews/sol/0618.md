# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one reversed subject, a characterization-changing causal addition, a mistranslated environmental hazard, omitted Bai wordplay, and several terminology or formatting issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “음. 킹치만 그럼 너무 오래 기다려야 하는걸?”
- **Current:** “Hmm. But then you’d have to wait too long, wouldn’t you?”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Taekyung is complaining that he himself would have to wait too long if he called for the ships only after finishing his business, not that the Deputy Stronghold Lord would have to wait.
- **Replacement:** “Hmm. But then I’d have to wait way too long.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is resolved by Taekyung’s concern about securing transportation for his return, and the coercive joke depends on his treating his own waiting time as decisive.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하긴 수하들 앞에서 심심하면 대가리도 박고, 엎드려 뻗친 적도 있으니 오늘의 헤어짐이 행복하긴 할 거다.
- **Current:** Then again, he’d been forced to bang his head against the ground and do push-ups whenever I got bored in front of his men. He probably really was happy to say goodbye today.
- **Defect:** The draft turns 심심하면, meaning something done habitually or at the drop of a hat, into the invented claim that Taekyung punished him specifically whenever Taekyung became bored. It also converts the disciplinary position 엎드려 뻗쳐 into completed push-ups.
- **Replacement:** Then again, he’d had to bang his head against the ground and hold a push-up position in front of his men at the drop of a hat. He probably really was happy to say goodbye today.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes the Deputy Stronghold Lord’s repeated humiliation without assigning Taekyung boredom as its cause, and it refers to assuming or holding the punishment position rather than necessarily performing push-ups.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 남만은 날씨가 어지럽고, 갖가지 맹수와 독물들로 가득한 험지(險地). 외부인은 늘 이곳의 풍토병과 독을 조심해야 합니다.
- **Current:** > Nanman is a perilous land with erratic weather, countless venomous beasts, and poisonous creatures. Outsiders must always beware of endemic diseases and poison.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the wild beasts themselves venomous. The source separately lists 맹수, wild or ferocious beasts, and 독물, venomous creatures.
- **Replacement:** > Nanman is a perilous land with erratic weather, filled with all manner of wild beasts and venomous creatures. Outsiders must always beware of endemic diseases and poison.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the source’s two distinct categories of danger and renders 갖가지 as “all manner of” rather than changing it to a quantity.

## F04 — Major

- **Current:** As if the Bai people hadn’t been given that name for nothing, an ethnic tribesman dressed in white glared at us with a menacing look before disappearing from sight.
- **Defect:** The English retains the structure of the joke but omits the information that Bai means “White,” leaving readers unable to understand why the man’s white clothing validates the name.

- **Rationale:** The source explicitly links the ethnonym 白族 to the man’s white clothes; supplying the name’s meaning preserves that wordplay without adding plot information.

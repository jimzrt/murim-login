# Review

The draft contains one altered duration, several meaningful losses of characterization or hierarchy, misplaced narrative viewpoint, and localized register and terminology issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 운기조식을 할 거라더니 죽은 듯이 자고 있는 또 다른 청년, 진태경도 마찬가지다.
- **Current:** The other young man, Jin Taekyung, had also claimed he would circulate his qi before promptly falling into a dead sleep.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung similar in having claimed he would circulate his qi, but the source says the same combination of exceptional looks and talent also applies to him.
- **Replacement:** The same went for the other young man, Jin Taekyung, who had said he would circulate his qi only to fall into a deathlike sleep.
- **Rationale:** The topic marked by 도 마찬가지다 refers back to the qualities Hyuk Mujin envies, while the intervening clause identifies Taekyung by his current behavior.

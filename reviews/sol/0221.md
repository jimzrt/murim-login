# Review

Several subject, quantity, action, and mechanical errors require correction, including assigning past injuries to Taekyung, repeatedly giving the one-eyed Wyvern multiple eyes, and literalizing an idiom as a physical eye movement.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 임독양맥을 타통하며 일 갑자를 넘어선 열양지기가 창날을 향해 썰물처럼 밀려든다.
- **Current:** Scorching Yang Qi that had surpassed one jiazi surged toward the spearhead like the ebbing tide as it passed through the Conception and Governor Vessels.
- **Defect:** The draft says the qi is currently passing through the vessels, but the source identifies its having surpassed one jiazi as a result of opening those vessels.
- **Replacement:** The Scorching Yang Qi that had surpassed one jiazi when I opened my Conception and Governor Vessels surged toward the spearhead like an ebb tide.
- **Rationale:** 타통하며 refers to Taekyung opening the Conception and Governor Vessels, not to the qi merely traveling through them during the present attack.

# Review

Actionable issues include two departures from established organization terminology, altered military equipment and tactical mechanics, loss of the acupoint-strike action, one misread narration clause, and several localized fidelity or wording problems.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 엄연한 타국 사람이자 생면부지인 나도 이럴진대, 뒤따라 도착한 공안 무력부 헌터들이 느낀 슬픔과 분노는 말로 표현할 수 없었다.
- **Current:** If even I, an outsider from another country who had never met these people, felt that way, then the grief and rage felt by the Public Security Armed Forces Hunters who arrived behind me could not be expressed in words.
- **Defect:** The established organization name omits “Department.”
- **Replacement:** If even I, an outsider from another country who had never met these people, felt that way, then the grief and rage of the Hunters from the Public Security Armed Forces Department who arrived behind me could not be expressed in words.
- **Rationale:** 공안 무력부 is established as “Public Security Armed Forces Department,” and the natural construction is “Hunters from” that department.

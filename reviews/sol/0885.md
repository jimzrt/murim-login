# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes a threat of punishment into a death threat, reverses a joke’s image, and softens one profane response.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 만약 하나라도 상한 물건이 있다면 내 경을 칠 것이다!
- **Current:** If even one item is damaged, I’ll have your head!
- **Defect:** The official threatens punishment, not execution.
- **Replacement:** If even one item is damaged, you’ll pay for it!
- **Rationale:** 경을 치다 means to be punished or get into serious trouble; it does not specify beheading.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 네 녀석의 대가리는 속이 꽉 찬 돌과 진배없으니 간단히 예를 들어 주마.
- **Current:** Your head’s as empty as a rock, so I’ll give you a simple example.
- **Defect:** “Empty” reverses the image of a solid stone head and weakens the later joke about breaking stone.
- **Replacement:** Your head’s as solid as a rock, so I’ll give you a simple example.
- **Rationale:** 속이 꽉 찬 돌 describes a rock solid all the way through, setting up his threat to break a stone head.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘좆 같죠. 밥 먹는데.’
- **Current:** *“That’d suck. I’m eating.”*
- **Defect:** The response softens explicit profanity and loses some of Taekyung’s bluntness.
- **Replacement:** *“That’d be fucking awful. I’m trying to eat.”*
- **Rationale:** 좆 같죠 is strongly profane; the abrupt complaint about his interrupted meal is the joke.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.86
- **Source:** 언제 그랬냐는 듯, 형형한 안광을 빛내는 초로인을 향해 나는 짤막하게 답했다.
- **Current:** The old man’s eyes gleamed sharply, as though he’d never said a thing like that. I gave him a brief answer.
- **Defect:** “Never said a thing like that” assigns the contrast to his words rather than his sudden change from a deferential laborer.
- **Replacement:** The old man’s eyes gleamed fiercely, as if he’d never played the humble laborer. I gave him a brief answer.
- **Rationale:** 언제 그랬냐는 듯 marks how abruptly his bearing changes; the source does not retract anything he said.

# Review

One incorrect subject attribution, one omitted identity detail, and one unsupported temporal addition.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당연하지. 다른 것도 아닌 만년한철을 넉 냥이나 섞어 검날을 벼렸으니까.
- **Current:** “Of course it is. I forged the blade with four nyang of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron mixed into it.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Hyeongong the person who forged the sword, although the Korean does not assign the forging to him. This also conflicts with the immediately established fact that the sword was a gift from his late master.
- **Replacement:** “Of course it is. The blade was forged with four nyang of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron mixed into it.”
- **Rationale:** The source explains why the edge remains sharp without identifying Hyeongong as the smith. A passive construction preserves that omission and avoids changing the sword’s history.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 젊은이의 정체는 검성의 제자인 화산신룡 청풍과 함께 무림을 뒤흔들고 있는 돌풍의 주역이었다.
- **Current:** The young man was one of the driving forces behind the whirlwind shaking Murim alongside Cheongpung, the Huashan Divine Dragon.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that Cheongpung is the Sword Saint’s Disciple.
- **Replacement:** The young man was one of the driving forces behind the whirlwind shaking Murim alongside the Sword Saint’s Disciple, Cheongpung—the Huashan Divine Dragon.
- **Rationale:** 검성의 제자인 explicitly identifies Cheongpung as the Sword Saint’s Disciple, a meaningful status and lineage detail.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 저는 이무기의 뼈로 만든 병장기가 갖고 싶더라고요.
- **Current:** “I’ve always wanted a weapon made from an imugi’s bones.”
- **Defect:** “Always” adds an unsupported longstanding desire. The source presents Taekyung as finding that he wants such a weapon now.
- **Replacement:** “I’ve found myself wanting a weapon made from an imugi’s bones.”
- **Rationale:** 갖고 싶더라고요 conveys a realized or discovered desire, not something he has wanted indefinitely.

# Review

Several lines change the meaning of the eunuch joke, the account of which sects survived near the capital, and the martial-arts-school joke.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아, 미안합니다. 그것도 없으셨죠.”
- **Current:** “Ah, sorry. You only have one missing.”
- **Defect:** Jin says Hong lacks those too; he does not realize Hong is missing only one until Hong corrects him.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, sorry. You don’t have those either.”
- **Rationale:** The mistaken assumption sets up Hong’s correction that eunuchs are not necessarily missing both.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 흔히들 말하는 사마외도는 물론이고, 정파의 거목이자 오대세가의 일원인 바로 그 남궁세가도 고관대작(高官大爵)들과의 연줄을 총동원해야 했죠.
- **Current:** Even the demonic, heterodox arts, and the Nangong Family itself, a towering tree of the orthodox faction and one of the Five Great Families, had to pull every string they had with high-ranking officials.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the arts themselves seek official connections and assigns the Nangong Family’s tactic to them.
- **Replacement:** Practitioners of the so-called demonic, heterodox arts couldn’t run rampant, of course. Even the Nangong Family—a towering tree of the orthodox faction and one of the Five Great Families—had to call on every connection it had among high-ranking officials.
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence concerns restrictions on martial artists; this sentence singles out even the orthodox Nangong Family as needing official connections.

## F03 — Major

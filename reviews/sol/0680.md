# Review

One critical reversal in Jin’s stated choices, plus losses in violent register, humor, euphemistic force, and combat positioning.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 쌍륜에 목이 잘려 듀라한(Dullahan) 후보생이 되는 대신 흑수권마를 끝장내거나, 놈을 놓아주고 나도 살거나.
- **Current:** Let the twin wheels cut off my head and become a Dullahan candidate, or finish off the Black Hand and let him live while I survived as well.
- **Defect:** The two alternatives are reversed and conflated. Finishing off the Black Hand Fist Demon belongs with sacrificing Jin’s life, while letting the enemy go belongs with Jin surviving; the draft instead says Jin could finish him off and let him live.
- **Replacement:** Either finish off the Black Hand Fist Demon at the cost of letting the twin wheels take my head and turn me into a Dullahan candidate, or let him go and save my own life.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly contrasts killing the Black Hand Fist Demon despite being decapitated with abandoning that attack so Jin can survive.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이 찢어 죽여도 시원치 않을……!”
- **Current:** “You wretched—!”
- **Defect:** The draft reduces an explicit threat that even tearing Jin to death would not satisfy the speaker to a generic insult, substantially weakening Black Hand’s rage and violent register.
- **Replacement:** “You—tearing you limb from limb wouldn’t be enough…!”
- **Rationale:** 찢어 죽여도 시원치 않을 expresses that even ripping the target apart and killing him would not be satisfying enough.

## F03 — Minor

- **Current:** A full-bodied, heartfelt insult involving his parents.
- **Defect:** “Full-bodied, heartfelt” misreads the baseball metaphor and makes 회심 sound sincere. The joke describes a devastating parent insult delivered like a perfectly placed inside pitch.

- **Rationale:** 몸 쪽 꽉 찬 evokes a pitch placed tight on the batter’s inside side, while 회심 marks it as a confidently landed blow rather than a heartfelt remark.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 자식이랍시고 뭐 빠지게 키워 놨더니 찾아오지도 않고.
- **Current:** They raised their child with everything they had, and now he will not even come to visit.
- **Defect:** The draft flattens the coarse euphemistic complaint 뭐 빠지게 into the neutral phrase “with everything they had,” losing Jin’s deliberately vulgar, needling register.
- **Replacement:** They worked themselves half to death raising you, and now you won’t even visit.
- **Rationale:** 뭐 빠지게 is a coarse euphemism for doing something with punishing effort; “worked themselves half to death” preserves its force without making the euphemism more anatomically explicit.

## F05 — Major

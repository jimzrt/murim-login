# Review

One action is reversed, and several smaller details alter the scene’s mechanics or imagery.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 저벅.
- **Current:** Clop.
- **Defect:** A footstep sounds like a horse’s hoofbeat in an indoor scene.
- **Replacement:** Thud.
- **Rationale:** The sound marks the Emperor’s footstep as he stops.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천하에서 가장 뛰어난 의원에게, 그 어느 때보다도 환하게 빛나는 희망을 건네어 돌려보냈다.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 황실의 어의(御醫)들은 황제의 건강에 조금이라도 이상이 생긴다면 죽음까지 각오해야 할 정도였으니까.
- **Current:** If the imperial physicians found even the slightest problem with his health, they might have to prepare for death themselves.
- **Defect:** Finding a health problem is substituted for the Emperor developing one, changing why the physician fears the consequences.
- **Replacement:** If anything at all went wrong with the Emperor’s health, the imperial physicians might have to prepare for death themselves.
- **Rationale:** The source makes an adverse change in the Emperor’s health—not its detection—the danger to his physicians.

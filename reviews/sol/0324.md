# Review

The draft contains several altered physical actions, two major quantity errors, an added reaction, and localized losses in humor, terminology, and faction identity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 수십 개로 분열되어 어른거리던 태을분광검이 만들어 낸 잔상을 지우고, 마침내 하나로 합쳐진 검신에 닿았다.
- **Current:** It erased the afterimages created by the Taeeul Light-Dividing Sword, which had split into dozens of wavering images, before finally merging into a single blade.
- **Defect:** The dangling construction makes the hellfire merge into a blade. In the source, the sword has already recombined into one blade, and the hellfire reaches it after erasing its afterimages.
- **Replacement:** It erased the wavering afterimages created by the Taeeul Light-Dividing Sword when it split into dozens, then reached the blade as it finally recombined into one.
- **Rationale:** The subject of 하나로 합쳐진 is the sword blade, while the hellfire’s action is 닿았다, reaching that blade.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 바람을 휘감은 푸른 화염이 다루의 천장을 뚫고 용오름 치듯 솟구쳤다.
- **Current:** Blue flames wrapped in wind tore through the tea house’s ceiling and shot upward like a waterspout.
- **Defect:** The wrapping relationship is reversed: the flames wrap around the wind, not the wind around the flames.
- **Replacement:** Blue flames coiled around the wind, tore through the tea house’s ceiling, and surged upward like a waterspout.
- **Rationale:** 바람을 휘감은 modifies 푸른 화염 and identifies the wind as what the flames encircle.

# Review

One critical speaker-identity error, two major losses of hierarchy and wordplay, and two minor fidelity or wording issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “적 대협과 전 아무런 관계가……. 예?”
- **Current:** “I have no relationship with Great Hero Jeok at all. We’re just—pardon?”
- **Defect:** The draft adds an interrupted second explanation that is absent from the spoken line, delaying the reaction to Jeok Cheongang’s declaration.
- **Replacement:** “Great Hero Jeok and I have no relationship at all… Pardon?”
- **Rationale:** The source moves directly from Taekyung’s denial to his startled response.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “이 악 모가 큰 결례를 저질렀습니다.
- **Current:** “This old man has committed a grave discourtesy.
- **Defect:** The translation gives Ak Bulgun Jeok Cheongang’s characteristic self-reference and obscures the explicit subject: 악 모 means Ak Bulgun is referring to himself by surname.
- **Replacement:** “I have committed a grave discourtesy.
- **Rationale:** Ak Bulgun, not Jeok Cheongang or an unspecified old man, is apologizing for his own discourtesy.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “큰절부터 올려도 모자랄 판에 목소리를 높여? 이 대가리에 피도 안 마른 놈을 확 그냥.”
- **Current:** “You should be prostrating yourself before me, and you dare raise your voice? You green-blooded brat!”
- **Defect:** The draft loses that even a full prostration would be insufficient, renders the youth idiom unnaturally as “green-blooded,” and drops Jeok Cheongang’s trailing threat.
- **Replacement:** “Even a full prostration wouldn’t be enough, and you dare raise your voice? You wet-behind-the-ears little bastard, I ought to…”
- **Rationale:** 모자랄 판 preserves Jeok Cheongang’s overwhelming hierarchy, 대가리에 피도 안 마른 describes an inexperienced youth, and 확 그냥 trails off as a physical threat.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “자, 잠시만요! 고정하세요! 고정!”

“고정? 백골이 진토 될 때까지 무덤에 고정당하고 싶으냐?”
- **Current:** “W-Wait a moment! Please calm down! Calm down!”

“Calm down? You want to be fixed in a grave until your bones turn to dust?”
- **Defect:** The first line corrects Taekyung’s panicked malapropism to “calm down,” so Jeok Cheongang’s literal repetition of “fixed” no longer follows from what Taekyung said and the exchange’s wordplay is lost.
- **Replacement:** “W-Wait! F-Fix yourself! Fix yourself!”

“Fix myself? Do you want to be fixed in a grave until your bones turn to dust?”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung mistakenly says 고정, “fix,” instead of the expected 진정, “calm down”; Jeok Cheongang seizes on that exact word to threaten to fix him permanently in a grave.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 양로원 큰형님 같은 눈앞의 노인네가 무림에서 악명 높은 일진이라는 사실을.

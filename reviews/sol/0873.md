# Review

The draft preserves the confrontation, but changes the proposed noble rank and misassigns a key comparison about maturity. Several smaller wording choices obscure whom the Emperor is targeting.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 아니면 대국의 법도를 무시하고 천하를 종횡하는 강호의 무뢰배?
- **Current:** Or those lawless martial-world ruffians who disregard the Great Nation’s laws and roam the land?
- **Defect:** The plural obscures that the Emperor is singling out Taekyung; 법도 refers to propriety or established order, not just laws.
- **Replacement:** Or that martial-world ruffian who flouts the Great Nation’s rules of propriety and roams the land?
- **Rationale:** The source uses a singular ruffian, whom the narrator immediately identifies as himself.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 아무런 대가 없이 주어지는 시간 속에서 나이만 먹은 성인들조차 할 수 없는 일을, 열셋의 상산왕은 이미 홀로 입증해 보였다.
- **Current:** In the time he’d been given without earning it, the thirteen-year-old Prince Shangshan had already proved by himself what even grown men, who’d done nothing but get older, couldn’t.
- **Defect:** The draft attaches the unearned passage of time to Prince Shangshan rather than to adults who have merely aged.
- **Replacement:** The thirteen-year-old Prince Shangshan had already proved on his own that he could do what even adults who’d done nothing but age with the time handed to them could not.
- **Rationale:** The source contrasts the prince’s independent action with adults who have received time for free and only grown older.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 짐이 이 자리에서 당장 황명으로 상산왕에게 주어진 산서성 임지를 철회하고, 관내후(關內侯)에 봉하여 내 곁에 둔다면 어찌하겠느냐?
- **Current:** What if I were to revoke the Shanxi Province post granted to Prince Shangshan by imperial command, appoint him a Baron of Guannei, and keep him by my side?
- **Defect:** 관내후 is a marquis title, not a barony; 임지 is his assigned territory, not an administrative post.
- **Replacement:** What if I issued an imperial command right here and now to revoke Prince Shangshan’s assigned territory in Shanxi Province, grant him the title Marquis Within the Passes, and keep him by my side?
- **Rationale:** The Emperor threatens to take away the prince’s Shanxi territory and confer a different noble title.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 만약 그렇게 된다면, 상산왕 전하의 거취는 어찌 되시는 겁니까.
- **Current:** If that happens, what will become of His Highness?
- **Defect:** The question specifically asks where the prince will go or reside, rather than broadly what will become of him.
- **Replacement:** If you do that, where will His Highness stay?
- **Rationale:** 거취 concerns the prince’s whereabouts, which the Emperor answers by describing life in the palace.

## F05 — Minor

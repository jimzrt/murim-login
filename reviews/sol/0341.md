# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one identity-changing tense error, several meaningful shifts in characterization and humor, and a mistranslated Quest title.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “신의. 네가 바로 신의의 제자였어.”
- **Current:** “Divine Physician. You were the Divine Physician’s Disciple.”
- **Defect:** The past tense implies that Mungyeong is no longer the Divine Physician’s Disciple, changing the identity being revealed.
- **Replacement:** “The Divine Physician. You’re the Divine Physician’s Disciple.”
- **Rationale:** 네가 바로 신의의 제자였어 is Taekyung’s realization of Mungyeong’s present identity; 였어 reflects the realization, not a terminated relationship.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 수적들에 의해 장강 밑바닥으로 가라앉을 뻔한 녀석이 넉살 좋게 쾌조선에 얻어 타지를 않나, 무림에서도 한 성깔 하기로 유명한 사천당문에 고작 약방문을 얻으러 갔다.
- **Current:** The boy who had nearly sunk to the bottom of the Yangtze because of the river bandits had somehow talked his way onto the Swift Tide. Then he had gone to the Sichuan Tang Clan, famous for being one of the fiercest forces in the Murim, just to obtain a prescription.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the Tang Clan’s notoriously fierce temper into institutional or military strength and weakens Mungyeong’s brazen, easygoing manner into an unspecified act of persuasion.
- **Replacement:** The boy who had nearly sunk to the bottom of the Yangtze because of the river bandits had brazenly hitched a ride aboard the Swift Tide, then gone to the Sichuan Tang Clan—infamous throughout the Murim for their vicious tempers—just to get a prescription.
- **Rationale:** 넉살 좋게 describes Mungyeong’s unabashed manner, while 한 성깔 하기로 유명한 refers to the Tang Clan’s fearsome temper rather than its standing as a powerful force.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 기쁨과 허탈함이 뒤섞인 내 말을 들은 다른 녀석들이 눈을 크게 뜨고 문경을 바라보았다.
- **Current:** The others stared wide-eyed at Mungyeong after hearing my words, which were a mixture of joy and disbelief.
- **Defect:** Disbelief replaces Taekyung’s deflation at discovering that the answer had been beside him, changing the emotional beat.
- **Replacement:** The others stared wide-eyed at Mungyeong after hearing the joy and deflation mingled in my words.
- **Rationale:** 허탈함 is a sense of emptiness, futility, or deflation, not disbelief.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 날고 긴다는 무림인과 수많은 관군을 풀어놓으면 뭐 하나.
- **Current:** What good was deploying every elite martial artist and countless government troops in the land?
- **Defect:** The draft adds the absolute quantity “every” and the unsupported scope “in the land.”
- **Replacement:** What good was it to deploy top-tier Murim artists and countless government troops?
- **Rationale:** 날고 긴다는 means exceptionally capable or top-tier; it does not mean every such martial artist, and the source contains no equivalent of “in the land.”

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이집트 행정 보급관이 애들 굴려서 지었겠지 뭐.

파라오가 근엄한 얼굴로 본 파라오는 너희에게 실망했다, 몇 번 날려 주면 채찍 맞으면서 돌 나르게 되어 있다.
- **Current:** The Egyptian logistics officer probably just worked a bunch of kids to death to build them.

A few stern-faced speeches from the pharaoh about how disappointed he was in everyone, and they would be hauling stones under the whip.
- **Defect:** The draft explicitly adds child deaths absent from the source and removes the mock-imperial first-person quotation that carries the joke.
- **Replacement:** Some Egyptian logistics officer probably just ran his workers ragged building them.

All the pharaoh had to do was put on a solemn face and say, “This pharaoh is disappointed in you,” a few times, and they would be hauling stones while getting whipped.
- **Rationale:** 애들 굴려서 colloquially means working people hard or running them ragged, not killing children. 본 파라오는 너희에게 실망했다 is a deliberately pompous direct quotation whose comic voice should be preserved.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** - 퀘스트, [사천에서 신 서방 찾기]를 성공적으로 완료했습니다!
- **Current:** > - Quest, **Find the Divine Physician in Sichuan**, successfully completed!
- **Defect:** The Quest title is normalized into the Divine Physician’s formal title, erasing the source’s deliberately coy mock-name 신 서방.
- **Replacement:** > - Quest, **Find Mr. Shin in Sichuan**, successfully completed!
- **Rationale:** The source does not call the Quest 신의 찾기; it uses 신 서방, treating Shin as a surname followed by a familiar form of address.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 며칠 전, 문경과 나누었던 대화가 뇌리를 스쳤다.
- **Current:** A conversation I had shared with Mungyeong several days ago flashed through my mind.
- **Defect:** “A conversation I had shared” is an awkward English collocation.
- **Replacement:** A conversation I’d had with Mungyeong several days ago flashed through my mind.
- **Rationale:** 나누었던 대화 naturally means a conversation Taekyung had with Mungyeong.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 눈으로 온통 새하얗던 한겨울의 풍경이 아닌 완연한 봄으로.
- **Current:** The midwinter landscape, white in every direction, had become full spring.
- **Defect:** “Become full spring” is not natural contemporary English.
- **Replacement:** The snow-white midwinter landscape had given way to spring in full bloom.
- **Rationale:** 완연한 봄 describes an unmistakable, fully realized spring landscape.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 주름진 눈매와 투명한 눈동자.
- **Current:** His eyes were wrinkled at the corners, and his pupils were clear.
- **Defect:** “His pupils were clear” is an unnatural anatomical rendering of a description of his gaze.
- **Replacement:** Deep wrinkles framed his clear eyes.
- **Rationale:** 투명한 눈동자 describes the limpid clarity of the old man’s eyes, not the physical transparency of his pupils.

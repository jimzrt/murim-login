# Review

One critical subject reversal and several localized fidelity, mechanism, and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “호신강기로도 온전히 막아 내지 못했다니.”
- **Current:** “So he couldn’t block it completely even with Body-Protecting Qi.”
- **Defect:** The omitted subject is the Western Heaven Demon Lord himself, but the draft changes it to a third-person subject.
- **Replacement:** “To think I couldn’t completely block it even with Body-Protecting Qi.”
- **Rationale:** He is reflecting on his own failure to block Taekyung’s attack, as confirmed by the immediately following description of Taekyung piercing his defense and wounding him.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 진태경은 한낱 피륙으로 호신강기를 뚫고 자신에게 상처를 입혔다.
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung had pierced through his Body-Protecting Qi with nothing more than flesh and fabric and wounded him.
- **Defect:** The draft adds “flesh”; 피륙 means cloth or fabric. This changes the stated mechanism of the extraordinary attack.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung had pierced his Body-Protecting Qi with a mere scrap of cloth and wounded him.
- **Rationale:** The source specifically emphasizes that mere fabric penetrated the defensive qi.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 온통 찢어지고 피로 물든 장삼이 아니었다면 그는 어디서나 볼 수 있는 흔한 중년인이었다.
- **Current:** With an ordinary face and build, he would have looked like any middle-aged man one might encounter anywhere if not for his Jang Sam, which was torn and stained entirely with blood.
- **Defect:** 장삼 is an ordinary long robe in this context, not the personal name Jang Sam. The capitalization and unexplained romanization misidentify the garment.
- **Replacement:** With his ordinary face and build, he would have looked like any unremarkable middle-aged man if not for his long robe, torn to shreds and soaked in blood.
- **Rationale:** The sentence contrasts his commonplace appearance with his blood-soaked clothing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가공할 공력의 소유자만이 보일 수 있는 허공섭물(虛空攝物)의 기예.
- **Current:** The art of Seizing an Object Through Empty Space—a technique visible only to someone who possessed terrifying internal energy.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the technique perceptible only to a powerful observer, whereas the source says only a person with formidable internal energy can perform or display it.
- **Replacement:** The art of Seizing an Object Through Empty Space—a feat only someone with terrifying internal energy could perform.
- **Rationale:** 보일 수 있는 describes the practitioner’s ability to demonstrate the feat, not an observer’s ability to see it.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 무언가에 퉁겨지듯 날아가, 서천마군의 손아귀에 목이 잡힌 신의의 안색이 창백하게 질렸다.
- **Current:** The Divine Physician flew as though he had been thrown against something. When his neck was caught in the Western Heaven Demon Lord’s hand, his face turned deathly pale.
- **Defect:** The draft says he was thrown against an object, introducing a collision absent from the source. He is propelled through the air as though struck or repelled, then caught by the throat.
- **Replacement:** Flung through the air as though something had struck him, the Divine Physician turned deathly pale as the Western Heaven Demon Lord caught him by the throat.
- **Rationale:** 퉁겨지듯 describes the manner in which he is sent flying, not his being thrown into another object.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “거리낌 없이 살수(殺手)를 펼친 의원이 할 말은 아닌 듯싶은데. 가만있자, 그럼 자네 역시 악귀가 아닌가?”
- **Current:** “That does not seem like something a physician who just used an assassin’s technique should say. Come to think of it, does that make you a fiend as well?”
- **Defect:** “An assassin’s technique” treats 살수 as a professional style rather than the lethal move the physician has just unleashed.
- **Replacement:** “That hardly seems like something a physician who unleashed a killing move without hesitation should say. Wait, wouldn’t that make you a fiend too?”
- **Rationale:** In context, the accusation concerns the physician’s readiness to use lethal force, creating the Western Heaven Demon Lord’s hypocritical joke.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 쉬익, 턱.
- **Current:** Whoosh. Crack.
- **Defect:** “Crack” suggests something breaking, but 턱 marks the palm strike being abruptly caught or blocked; the hand is broken only in the following beat.
- **Replacement:** Whoosh. Thunk.
- **Rationale:** The source separates the blocked strike from the subsequent 우두둑 of the physician’s hand being broken.

## F08 — Minor

- **Source:** “당 가주가 한 말이 맞았군. 그대가 갖고 있었어.”
- **Current:** “The Family Head was right. You had it.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits 당, losing the explicit identification of the Tang Family Head.
- **Replacement:** “The Tang Family Head was right. You had it.”
- **Rationale:** The source names which Family Head supplied the information.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그저 저 너머에 있을 한 사람을 향해 계속해서 나아갈 뿐이다.
- **Current:** I simply continued toward the one person who was waiting somewhere beyond it.
- **Defect:** “Was waiting” adds an intentional action absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** I simply continued toward the one person somewhere beyond it.
- **Rationale:** The Korean says only that the person would be beyond the darkness, not that the person was consciously waiting.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “공짜는 아니야. 한 가지 부탁을 받았지.”
- **Current:** “It wasn’t free. I was given a favor to fulfill.”
- **Defect:** “Given a favor” reverses the ordinary semantic roles and is unnatural English; Taekyung received a request.
- **Replacement:** “It wasn’t free. I was asked to do one thing.”
- **Rationale:** 부탁을 받다 means to be asked a favor or receive a request, clarified immediately afterward as the request to kill the Western Heaven Demon Lord.

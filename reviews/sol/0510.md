# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues: one reversed negation, one altered death/agency detail, a misattributed moral debt, a substantially reduced distance, an overstatement of Mungyeong’s revealed identity, and an omitted line of dialogue. Minor issues include omitted gambling and malformed System terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘그거 맞다. 단체로 귀가 잘못된 게 틀림없어.’
- **Current:** *That’s right. There’s no way all of us misheard him at once.*
- **Defect:** The negation reverses the joke. The bandits conclude that all of them must have collectively heard wrong, not that collective mishearing is impossible.
- **Replacement:** *That’s right. All our ears must have malfunctioned at once.*
- **Rationale:** 단체로 귀가 잘못된 게 틀림없어 explicitly means that their ears must all have been wrong together.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 얼마 전에는 친혈육 같았던 장강일도 황충을 떠나보내야 했다.
- **Current:** Not long ago, he had also been forced to send Hwang Chung, who was like a blood relative to him, to his death.
- **Defect:** The draft gives Mu Song agency in sending Hwang Chung to his death and makes the death explicit. The source euphemistically says Mu Song had to let him go or bid him farewell.
- **Replacement:** Not long ago, he had also had to say goodbye to Hwang Chung, the Yangtze One Saber, who had been like family to him.
- **Rationale:** 떠나보내야 했다 denotes having to let someone go or see them off; it does not say Mu Song dispatched Hwang Chung to his death. The draft also omits the established sobriquet 장강일도.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그에 대한 은원(恩怨)은 갚아야 한다는 생각으로 진태경 일행을 하남까지 데려다주기로 했는데
- **Current:** He had decided to take Jin Taekyung’s group as far as Henan because their gratitude and grudges had to be repaid.
- **Defect:** “Their” misattributes the moral debt to Jin Taekyung’s group. 그 refers back to Hwang Chung: Mu Song intends to settle what he owes Hwang Chung by escorting the group.
- **Replacement:** Believing he had to settle what he owed Hwang Chung, he had agreed to take Jin Taekyung’s group as far as Henan.
- **Rationale:** 그에 대한 points to the previously mentioned Hwang Chung, while 은원 refers to Mu Song’s debt of gratitude and grievance toward him.

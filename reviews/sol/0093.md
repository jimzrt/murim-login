- **Source:** 어느 놈인지는 몰라도 나름 조심한다고 한 마리씩만 슬금슬금 들어오긴 하는데, 오히려 진태경의 경계심만 돋우는 꼴이다.

- **Defect:** The draft makes the flies the agents exercising caution, while the source refers to the unidentified person controlling and sending them in one at a time.

- **Rationale:** 어느 놈 refers to the unknown controller, whose attempt at caution paradoxically heightens Taekyung’s vigilance.

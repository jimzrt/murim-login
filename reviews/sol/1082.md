# Review

The draft loses Great Sir’s wrong-name joke, changes a person into a mountain range, and substantially shortens the distance between the two kings.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 강풍 자네는 나이에 비해 너무 어리숙한 면이 있어.
- **Current:** As I suspected. You are an idiot. Cheongpung, you’re far too naïve for your age.
- **Defect:** Great Sir calls Cheongpung by the wrong name, Kang Pung; the draft removes the setup for Cheongpung’s correction.
- **Replacement:** As I suspected. You are an idiot. Kang Pung, you’re far too naïve for your age.
- **Rationale:** 강풍 is Kang Pung, not 청풍.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 내 말이 바로 그 말일세. 강풍.
- **Current:** “That’s exactly what I said. Cheongpung.”
- **Defect:** Great Sir repeats the wrong name while insisting he was right; the draft makes him say the correct name.
- **Replacement:** “That’s exactly what I said. Kang Pung.”
- **Rationale:** The repeated 강풍 completes the wrong-name joke.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 처음 불렀고, 솔직히 말씀하세요. 뭐 때문에 왔는지 기억 안 나시죠?
- **Current:** “You only just started talking to me, and be honest. You don’t remember why you came, do you?”
- **Defect:** Cheongpung says this was the first time he called Great Sir, rebutting the accusation that he keeps calling him. The draft instead comments on when Great Sir started talking.

- **Rationale:** 처음 불렀고 answers 왜 자꾸 부르나 directly.

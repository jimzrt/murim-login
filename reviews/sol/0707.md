# Review

Several actionable fidelity errors alter attack subjects, injury mechanics, quantity, and pragmatic modality; the draft also loses a deliberate singular-to-plural reveal.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 진즉 피를 토하며 무릎을 꿇고도 남았을 남천마후의 공격은 전처럼 무겁지 않았다.
- **Current:** And the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s attacks, which should have left her vomiting blood and collapsing to her knees long ago, were no longer as heavy as before.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the implied target of the attacks, saying the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s own attacks should have left her vomiting blood and kneeling.
- **Replacement:** And the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s attacks—which should long ago have left the Beast Miao King vomiting blood and collapsing to his knees—were no longer as heavy as before.
- **Rationale:** 남천마후의 공격 identifies her as the attacker; the one who should have been made to vomit blood and kneel is her opponent.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가슴을 후려치고 심장을 터트려야 했을 작은 주먹에서 뼈가 어긋나는 소리가 새어 나온다.
- **Current:** A small fist that should have struck her chest and burst her heart had made the bones in her hand shift out of place.
- **Defect:** The draft again reverses the target, making her fist supposed to strike her own chest and burst her own heart.
- **Replacement:** The bones shifted out of place in the small fist that should have smashed into his chest and burst his heart.
- **Rationale:** The fist belongs to the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and was intended to crush the Beast Miao King’s chest and heart.

## F03 — Critical

악문 잇새 사이로 핏물이 흘러나온다.
- **Current:** The Southern Heaven Demon Empress retched.

Blood leaked through her clenched teeth.
- **Defect:** 울컥 describes blood surging or welling up here, not the Southern Heaven Demon Empress retching.

- **Source:** 마치 처음부터 약속이라도 한 것처럼, 그들은 한 줄기의 바람이 되어 쇄도했다.

더 이상 찰나의 틈도, 유언을 남길 시간도 주지 않겠다는 듯이. 남천마후라는 악귀(惡鬼)의 숨통을 끊어 놓겠다는 일념 하나만으로.
- **Current:** As though they had agreed on it from the beginning, they surged forward as one streak of wind.

They had only one resolve: to cut off the breath of the Fiend known as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the narrator’s inferred impression into a categorical statement of the attackers’ intent, dropping the 듯이 pragmatic qualification.
- **Replacement:** As though they had agreed from the start, they became a single streak of wind and surged forward—as if to deny her even the briefest opening, let alone time for final words.

They had only one resolve: to cut off the breath of the Fiend known as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- **Rationale:** 주지 않겠다는 듯이 means they moved as if determined to give her no opening or time for final words; the source presents that intent as an impression conveyed by their charge.

# Review

One contact reversal and several localized fidelity and action-mechanics defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 전신의 요혈을 아슬아슬하게 스쳐 지나간 빛줄기가 단단한 동굴 바닥을 꿰뚫고 부수었다.
- **Current:** The streak of light barely grazed the vital acupoints throughout my body before piercing and smashing through the solid cave floor.
- **Defect:** “Barely grazed” indicates physical contact, but the attack narrowly passed the vital points without touching him, as the following paragraph explicitly confirms.
- **Replacement:** The streak of light swept within a hair’s breadth of the vital acupoints throughout my body before piercing and smashing through the solid cave floor.
- **Rationale:** 아슬아슬하게 스쳐 지나간 means it passed dangerously close, not that it grazed him.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 누군가가 물어뜯은 선명한 이빨 자국을 몸에 남긴 채.
- **Current:** It had been left with clear teeth marks where something had bitten into it.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the implied agent from “someone” to “something,” weakening the clue that a person bit the fish.
- **Replacement:** Clear teeth marks from where someone had bitten into it remained on its body.
- **Rationale:** 누군가 explicitly means “someone.”

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 주름진 입가에 묻은 붉은 핏물과 반짝이는 비늘.
- **Current:** Red blood stained the wrinkles around his mouth. Shining scales clung to his face.
- **Defect:** The scales are specifically stuck around his wrinkled mouth, not somewhere unspecified on his face.
- **Replacement:** Red blood and glinting scales clung to the wrinkles around his mouth.
- **Rationale:** 입가 locates both the blood and scales at the corners or surrounding area of his mouth, directly supporting the realization that he ate the fish.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97

## F05 — Minor

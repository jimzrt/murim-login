# Review

Several meaningful subject, idiom, register, and humor errors are present, along with localized terminology and Markdown issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘어느 육시랄 놈들이.’
- **Current:** *What kind of six-shit bastards are making all that noise?*
- **Defect:** “Six-shit” is a nonsensical literalization of the Korean curse and does not convey the old man’s natural, irritated profanity.
- **Replacement:** *What goddamn bastards are making all that noise?*
- **Rationale:** 육시랄 is a strong conventional curse here, not a compositional reference to the number six.

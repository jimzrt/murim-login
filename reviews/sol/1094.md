# Review

The draft changes several actions and subjects, obscures the balance of the encirclement, and omits Cheongpung’s technique.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그래서 짓밟았다.

더욱 철저하게.
- **Current:** So the Blood Lord had crushed him.

More thoroughly than before.
- **Defect:** “Than before” invents an earlier beating; at their first meeting, the Blood Lord only watched from afar.
- **Replacement:** So the Blood Lord had crushed him.

Thoroughly.
- **Rationale:** 더욱 철저하게 intensifies the second encounter without establishing a prior attack.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 어느 때보다 예리한 감각을 끌어올려 내부의 기척을 살핀 나는 확신이 들고 나서야 다시 이동을 시작했다.

- **Defect:** The draft broadens 기척 into all signs of life, contradicting the fish and aquatic plants inside the passage. Taekyung is checking for a human presence or potential enemy.

- **Rationale:** In context, 기척 refers to the presence Taekyung is guarding against, not every living organism in the passage.

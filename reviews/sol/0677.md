# Review

The draft contains two critical subject/action distortions, along with meaningful losses in causal precision, insulting register, and attack mechanics.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그건 여인의 몸으로 대족장의 자리에 오른 직후, 처음으로 대면한 자리에서 백상이 했던 말이었다.
- **Current:** That was what Baeksang had said when they first met, shortly after he had risen to the position of Great Chieftain.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the rise to Great Chieftain to Baeksang, but the source says this meeting occurred shortly after Yohi became a Great Chieftain.
- **Replacement:** That was what Baeksang had said when they first met, shortly after Yohi had risen to the position of Great Chieftain.
- **Rationale:** 여인의 몸으로 대족장의 자리에 오른 refers to Yohi attaining the position as a woman, not to Baeksang attaining it.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “그때 백상, 그놈의 표정이 참으로 볼만했지. 어떻게든 그 늙은이를 설득해 보겠다며 끝까지 시간을 끌더니…… 그래도 명줄 하나는 질긴 놈이라 그런지 달려들진 않더군. 아쉬운 일이야.”
- **Current:** “Baeksang’s expression back then was truly something to see. He kept stalling until the end, saying he would somehow persuade the old man… Still, perhaps because the bastard had a tenacious life, he didn’t attack him directly. What a shame.”
- **Defect:** The added object “him” makes Baeksang refrain from attacking the old Yao chieftain he was trying to persuade. The source instead says Baeksang did not come charging in, with no such object.
- **Replacement:** “Baeksang’s expression back then was truly something to see. He dragged things out to the bitter end, insisting he would somehow persuade the old man… Still, perhaps the bastard had a strong survival instinct, because he never came charging in. What a shame.”
- **Rationale:** 달려들진 않더군 means that Baeksang did not rush or charge in; it does not say that he declined to attack the old chieftain directly.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “천둥벌거숭이처럼 구는 것은 여기까지다. 설마 마후(魔后)께서 내리신 명을 잊은 것은 아니겠지.”

흑수의 얼굴이 일그러졌다.

“천둥벌거숭이라. 이 나이에 그런 말을 들을 줄은 몰랐구려.”
- **Current:** “Your reckless behavior ends here. You haven’t forgotten the order given by Her Majesty the Demon Empress, have you?”

Black Hand’s face twisted.

“Reckless behavior, is it? I never expected to hear something like that at my age.”
- **Defect:** The draft reduces 천둥벌거숭이, a belittling label for a reckless and immature person, to the abstract phrase “reckless behavior.” This removes the insult that makes Black Hand object specifically on account of his age.
- **Replacement:** “Stop acting like a reckless brat. You haven’t forgotten the order given by Her Majesty the Demon Empress, have you?”

Black Hand’s face twisted.

“A reckless brat? I never thought I’d be called that at my age.”
- **Rationale:** The repeated 천둥벌거숭이 directly characterizes Black Hand as an immature, reckless person, and his response hinges on being called such a thing at his age.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 암천의 주구 노릇을 한 것으로도 모자라 죽을 위기에 처한 지금의 상황이 도무지 믿어지지 않았다.
- **Current:** She could not believe that she had served as a pawn of Dark Heaven—and that she was now facing death because of it.
- **Defect:** The draft adds a direct causal claim that Yohi is facing death because she served Dark Heaven. The source juxtaposes the two horrifying facts without explicitly making one the cause of the other.
- **Replacement:** She could not believe the situation she was in: not only had she served as a pawn of Dark Heaven, she was now in danger of dying.
- **Rationale:** 한 것으로도 모자라 coordinates an additional calamity—being in mortal danger—rather than explicitly stating that her service caused that danger.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “왜 저놈의 말을 듣지 않았느냐. 노부의 얼굴을 보지 않았다면 일각이라도 더 연명할 수 있었을 터인데.”
- **Current:** “Why didn’t you listen to that fellow? If you hadn’t seen this old man’s face, you might have lived for another quarter of an hour.”
- **Defect:** The rendering does not use the binding established translation for 일각.
- **Replacement:** “Why didn’t you listen to that fellow? If you hadn’t seen this old man’s face, you might have lived for another fifteen minutes.”
- **Rationale:** The glossary explicitly establishes 일각 as “fifteen minutes.”

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “거, 이제는 눈치 보여서 장난도 못 치겠군.”
- **Current:** “Come now. I can’t even play a little anymore without worrying about someone watching.”
- **Defect:** “Someone watching” incorrectly suggests surveillance. 눈치 보여서 means Black Hand feels constrained by another person’s disapproval or authority.
- **Replacement:** “Come on. Now I can’t even have a little fun without having to watch myself.”
- **Rationale:** The complaint is that the unseen superior makes him mind his behavior, not that he fears being observed.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “마음 같아서는 일장에 너희를 쳐 죽이고 싶지만…… 내 이번만큼은 참아 주마. 그보다 먼저 해야 할 일이 있거든.”
- **Current:** “I’d like nothing more than to kill you both with a single strike, but… I’ll restrain myself this time. There’s something I have to do first.”
- **Defect:** The generic “single strike” omits that Black Hand specifically says he could kill them with one palm strike.
- **Replacement:** “I’d like nothing more than to kill you both with a single palm strike, but… I’ll restrain myself this time. There’s something I have to do first.”
- **Rationale:** 일장 denotes one palm strike, a concrete martial action rather than an unspecified blow.

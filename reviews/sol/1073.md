# Review

Three actionable findings: one reversed action, one altered moral distinction, and one glossary mismatch.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 제가 맞으면 너무 아플 것 같아서.
- **Current:** If I hit you, it seems like it’d hurt too much.
- **Defect:** Reverses who would be hit.
- **Replacement:** If you hit me, it’d hurt too much.
- **Rationale:** Jin asks to hit Jeok because Jin expects being hit himself would hurt.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그렇게 된다면 모든 것이 당연해져 버릴 테니까.

그리고 누군가의 희생과 죽음은, 결코 당연해져서는 안 되는 종류의 것이니까.
- **Current:** Because then, everything would start to feel inevitable.

And another person’s sacrifice and death must never be treated as inevitable.
- **Defect:** Changes the danger of growing accustomed to deaths into a claim about whether deaths are inevitable.
- **Replacement:** Because then, I’d start taking everything for granted.

And another person’s sacrifice and death must never become something I take for granted.
- **Rationale:** 당연해져 버릴 and 당연해져서는 안 되는 refer to treating sacrifice and death as ordinary or expected, continuing the thought about never getting used to them.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 개똥이가 왔으니
- **Current:** Gaettongi, a divine general descended upon this land in answer to Heaven’s will, has arrived!
- **Defect:** Uses Gaettongi instead of the established name Gaettong.
- **Replacement:** Gaettong, a divine general descended upon this land in answer to Heaven’s will, has arrived!
- **Rationale:** The glossary maps 개똥이 to Gaettong; the final 이 here is not part of the established English name.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it omits one quantity, alters several pragmatic and temporal cues, softens vulgar register, and changes the repeated ox-head insult that triggers the final confrontation.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래도 저희 쪽에서 귀한 정보도 하나 드렸잖아요?
- **Current:** “Still, our side did give you some valuable information, didn’t we?”
- **Defect:** The draft omits 하나, changing one specific valuable piece of information into an unspecified amount.
- **Replacement:** “Still, our side did give you one valuable piece of information, didn’t we?”
- **Rationale:** 하나 explicitly specifies one item of information.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 시치미 뚝 뗀 대답에 월화가 눈을 가늘게 떴다.
- **Current:** Wolhwa narrowed her eyes at his shamelessly evasive answer.
- **Defect:** The draft generalizes Jin Wikyung’s deliberate pretense of knowing nothing into mere evasiveness, losing the specific characterization cue.
- **Replacement:** Wolhwa narrowed her eyes at his answer, delivered as though he knew nothing.
- **Rationale:** 시치미를 떼다 specifically means to feign ignorance or pretend not to know.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 자네가 웬일인가? 이런 말을 할 때마다 체통이니 뭐니 따분한 잔소리만 하던 위인이.
- **Current:** “What’s gotten into you? Every time someone says something like that, you’re the one who lectures them about dignity and propriety.”
- **Defect:** The draft introduces an indefinite speaker and object, making Wipeng someone who lectures anyone who speaks this way rather than someone who habitually lectures Jin Wikyung.
- **Replacement:** “What’s gotten into you? Whenever I say something like that, you’re always lecturing me about dignity and propriety.”
- **Rationale:** The immediate exchange and Jin Wikyung’s familiar 자네 establish his own prior remarks and Wipeng’s lectures to him as the subject of the complaint.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 자네 오늘 작정했군. 뭔가? 어서 말해 보게!
- **Current:** “You’re really making an occasion of this today. What is it? Tell me already!”
- **Defect:** “Making an occasion of this” is unnatural and obscures Jin Wikyung’s amused observation that Wipeng has deliberately committed to flattering or indulging him today.
- **Replacement:** “You’re really going all out today. What is it? Tell me already!”
- **Rationale:** 작정했군 means that Wipeng has evidently made up his mind to pursue this behavior in earnest.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이런 시기에 나 몰라라 나간 것으로도 모자라서 소식이 뚝 끊겼어요.
- **Current:** “The Third Young Master absolutely cannot be let off the hook. It wasn’t enough for him to leave at a time like this as if none of it concerned him—the moment he left, all word from him stopped.”
- **Defect:** The draft adds the unsupported claim that contact stopped at the exact moment he left.
- **Replacement:** “The Third Young Master absolutely cannot be let off the hook. It wasn’t enough for him to leave at a time like this as if none of it concerned him—on top of that, all word from him has completely stopped.”
- **Rationale:** 소식이 뚝 끊겼어요 says that all news has abruptly or completely ceased, but does not identify the precise moment when that happened.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그들 중에서도 못난 아들 덕분에 한창 똥줄이 타고 있는 성운표국의 국주, 우황태는 그 정도가 더욱 심했다.
- **Current:** Of them, Woo Hwangtae, the chief of the Seongun Escort Bureau, was in the worst state by far. Thanks to his worthless son, he was frantic.
- **Defect:** The neutral word “frantic” strips out the deliberately crude, comic register of 똥줄이 타다.
- **Replacement:** Of them, Woo Hwangtae, the chief of the Seongun Escort Bureau, was in the worst state by far, shitting himself with worry thanks to his worthless son.
- **Rationale:** 똥줄이 타다 is a vulgar colloquial expression for suffering extreme anxiety or impatience; the source intentionally contrasts that register with Woo Hwangtae’s status.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어허, 이 사람아. 그게 무슨 소리인가? 나 성운표국 국주라니까?
- **Current:** “Come now, man. What do you mean? I’m the chief of the Seongun Escort Bureau, remember?”
- **Defect:** “Remember?” implies that the guard was previously told and has forgotten, while the Korean emphatically insists on Woo Hwangtae’s identity without that implication.
- **Replacement:** “Come now, man. What do you mean? I’m telling you, I’m the chief of the Seongun Escort Bureau!”
- **Rationale:** 라니까 marks emphatic insistence—“I’m telling you”—rather than a request to remember.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 우진태와 마찬가지로 가문의 후광을 톡톡히 누리며 살았던 그다.
- **Current:** Like Woo Jintae, he had lived comfortably beneath the halo of his family.
- **Defect:** “Lived comfortably beneath the halo” is an unnatural calque and weakens the point that he actively enjoyed the substantial advantages of his family’s prestige.
- **Replacement:** Like Woo Jintae, he had always enjoyed every advantage of his family’s prestige.
- **Rationale:** 후광을 톡톡히 누리다 means to benefit substantially from reflected status or influence.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 쳐 죽일 놈들. 제아무리 위세가 높다지만 사람을 이렇게 푸대접해?
- **Current:** *Those bastards. No matter how powerful they are, how can they treat someone like this?*
- **Defect:** The draft sanitizes the violent profanity in 쳐 죽일 놈들.
- **Replacement:** *Those fucking bastards. No matter how powerful they are, how can they treat someone like this?*
- **Rationale:** 쳐 죽일 놈들 is substantially stronger than the unmodified “bastards” and reflects Woo Hwangtae’s furious internal voice.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 장사치 놈을 그냥, 확.
- **Current:** *I ought to just take this merchant down.*
- **Defect:** The draft invents a completed intention to “take down” Woo Hwangtae and removes the source’s deliberately unfinished, exasperated threat.
- **Replacement:** *This damned merchant, I ought to just…*
- **Rationale:** The Korean trails off after 확, preserving an unspoken impulse rather than specifying a concrete action.

## F11 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이런 소대가리 같은 놈을 봤나! 너 거기 안 서!”

그때, 거한이 몸을 움찔 떨었다.

“지금, 뭐라고?”

“소대가리 같은 놈이라고 했다. 이 소만도 못한 새끼야!”
- **Current:** “Look at this cow-brained bastard! You there, stop!”

The giant’s body trembled.

“What did you just say?”

“I called you cow-brained. You son of a bitch, you’re lower than a cow!”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the repeated physical comparison “ox-headed” into an insult about intelligence and renders the giant’s sudden flinch as sustained trembling, weakening the specific trigger and reaction behind the confrontation.
- **Replacement:** “You ox-headed bastard! Stop right there!”

The giant flinched.

“What did you just say?”

“I called you ox-headed. You bastard, you’re worse than an ox!”
- **Rationale:** 소대가리 같은 literally compares his head to an ox’s, and the phrase is deliberately repeated after 움찔 describes a brief involuntary flinch.

## F12 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 폭발하기 직전의 사제를 만류한 화산일학(華山一鶴) 백무성은 우황태를 향해 빙긋 웃어 보였다.
- **Current:** Baek Museong, Huashan’s Lone Crane, had restrained his junior brother just before he exploded. He smiled at Woo Hwangtae.
- **Defect:** “Restrained” can imply physical restraint, but Baek Museong stops his Junior Brother with the spoken prohibition immediately preceding this sentence.
- **Replacement:** Baek Museong, Huashan’s Lone Crane, had stopped his Junior Brother just before he exploded. He smiled at Woo Hwangtae.
- **Rationale:** 만류하다 means to dissuade or stop someone from acting; no physical restraint is described.

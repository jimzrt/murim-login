# Review

The main defects are an impossible subject in the chest-stab sentence, a mistranslated creed, and wording that obscures the Sword Lord’s shock and his assessment of Jin.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “천상천하(天上天下).”
- **Current:** “Heaven above, heaven below.”
- **Defect:** The creed says heaven and earth, not heaven both above and below.
- **Replacement:** “In heaven and on earth.”
- **Rationale:** 천상천하 refers to heaven above and the world below.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가슴에 검을 꽂고 나서야 우뚝 끊기는 교언.
- **Current:** Only after driving his sword into the man’s chest did the follower’s words finally stop.
- **Defect:** The sentence makes the follower appear to drive the sword into his own chest.
- **Replacement:** The follower’s words stopped only when the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord drove his sword into the man’s chest.
- **Rationale:** The Sword Lord stabs the follower to silence him.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 순간, 풍운검군은 그제야 이 기시감의 정체를 알아차렸다.
- **Current:** Then, at last, the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord recognized the source of his unease.
- **Defect:** Unease replaces the specific sense of déjà vu.
- **Replacement:** Then, at last, the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord understood why this felt familiar.
- **Rationale:** 기시감 is a feeling of having encountered something before.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 손목을 찌르는 듯한 통증을 느꼈다.

그와는 비교도 할 수 없을 만큼, 엄청난 충격도 함께.
- **Current:** He barely steadied himself, pain like a stab piercing his wrist.

The impact itself was beyond comparison.
- **Defect:** The second sentence reads as another physical impact rather than the far greater shock he feels at his opponent’s strength.
- **Replacement:** He barely steadied himself, pain stabbing through his wrist.

Far worse than the pain was the shock.
- **Rationale:** The shock is compared with his wrist pain and leads directly into his stunned recognition.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천하의 그 누구보다 강한 것은 아니지만, 지금껏 언제나 그 누구도 예상치 못한 변화의 바람을 불어왔던 누군가를.
- **Current:** Someone not stronger than anyone else in the world, but who had always brought winds of change no one could have predicted.
- **Defect:** Not stronger than anyone else can suggest he is weaker than everybody, rather than simply not the strongest.
- **Replacement:** Someone who wasn’t the strongest person in the world, but who had always brought winds of change no one could have predicted.
- **Rationale:** The Korean denies that he surpasses everyone; it does not place him below everyone.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 섬광의 종착지는 정확히 적의 미간을 겨누고 있었다.
- **Current:** The flash of its blade flew straight for the enemy’s forehead.
- **Defect:** Forehead loses the precise target.
- **Replacement:** The flash of its blade flew straight for the spot between the enemy’s brows.
- **Rationale:** 미간 specifies the space between the eyebrows.

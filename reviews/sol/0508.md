# Review

Several actionable fidelity defects remain, including a reversed speaker intention, altered physical actions, a broken wordplay mechanism, and a relationship-defining idiom mistranslated as moral perfection.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그렇군. 노부는 감 잡았다! 죽이는 대신 단전을 폐하거나 사지 한 군데 정도 자를 생각이야!”
- **Current:** “I see now! This old man has figured it out! Instead of killing him, I’ll cripple his dantian or cut off one of his limbs!”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jeok Cheongang announce that he himself will cripple Mu Song. In the source, Jeok is triumphantly claiming to have guessed Mungyeong’s plan.
- **Replacement:** “I see now! This old man has figured it out! Instead of killing him, you’re planning to cripple his dantian or cut off one of his limbs!”
- **Rationale:** The implied subject of 생각이야 is Mungyeong, whose intentions Jeok claims to have discerned; changing it to “I’ll” reverses the intended actor and causes Mungyeong’s ensuing murderous irritation to rest on the wrong exchange.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아쉬움 가득한 얼굴로 입맛을 다신 적천강이 내 옆구리를 쿡 찔렀다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang clicked his tongue, his face full of disappointment, and poked me in the side.
- **Defect:** 입맛을 다시다 here is smacking or licking one’s lips, not clicking one’s tongue. The draft changes Jeok Cheongang’s comic physical reaction.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang smacked his lips, his face full of disappointment, and poked me in the side.
- **Rationale:** The source depicts him savoring the prospect of trouble despite his disappointment, whereas tongue-clicking signals disapproval.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “인의(人意)가 있는데 어찌 그러겠냐는 뜻이었습니다.”
- **Current:** “I meant, ‘How could I do such a thing when I have a conscience?’”
- **Defect:** The excuse does not continue the audible fragment “admi,” so the source’s joke—Taekyung inventing an alternative completion for the syllable Mungyeong heard—no longer works in English.
- **Replacement:** “I meant, ‘Admittedly, I have a conscience. How could I do such a thing?’”
- **Rationale:** Beginning the fabricated excuse with “Admittedly” gives “admi” a second plausible completion, preserving the source’s evasive wordplay without changing Taekyung’s claimed meaning.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그런가? 노부는 허물이 없다고 생각했는데. 우리는 서로 생각이 다른 모양이야. 아니면 누군가가 애써 그런 생각을 무시하고 있거나.”
- **Current:** “Did I? I thought he had no faults. It seems we have different opinions. Or perhaps someone is deliberately ignoring those thoughts.”
- **Defect:** 허물이 없다 means being close enough to dispense with reserve or ceremony here, not believing that Taekyung has no faults. The draft loses Jeok Cheongang’s characterization of their intimate master-disciple relationship and obscures his pointed implication toward Mungyeong.
- **Replacement:** “Is that so? I thought we were close enough not to stand on ceremony. It seems we see things differently. Or perhaps someone is deliberately ignoring that thought.”
- **Rationale:** Jeok is reframing Taekyung’s lack of formality as evidence of closeness, then suggesting that someone else may be suppressing the same view. Moral faultlessness is not asserted.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 팔짱을 낀 채 강 건너 불구경하고 있던 적천강이 피식 웃었다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang had been leaning against the wooden railing of the swift ship and watching the fire from across the river. He snorted quietly.
- **Defect:** The draft literalizes the idiom 강 건너 불구경 as an actual fire, adds leaning against the railing at this point, and omits his folded arms. 피식 웃었다 is also a brief laugh rather than necessarily a snort.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang, who had been watching with his arms folded as though none of this concerned him, let out a quiet laugh.
- **Rationale:** 강 건너 불구경 describes detachedly watching someone else’s trouble. No fire is present in the scene, while 팔짱을 낀 explicitly specifies his posture.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 적천강이 경을 치든 종을 치든 뭔 상관이냐.
- **Current:** What did it matter whether Jeok Cheongang got me in trouble or rang a bell?
- **Defect:** The draft changes the explicit subject: the source says Jeok Cheongang might suffer or be punished, not that he might get Taekyung in trouble. This also breaks the 경을 치다／종을 치다 verbal joke.
- **Replacement:** What did it matter whether Jeok Cheongang caught hell or rang a bell?
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang is the subject of both alternatives. “Caught hell” preserves the idiomatic sense of 경을 치다 while retaining the contrast with literally ringing a bell.

# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter subjects, implied actions, military register, quantities, and physical details; the most serious make an internal thought spoken dialogue, reverse the direction of a favor request, misidentify who is being questioned, and change a kick from the shin to the ribs.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “늦지 않아서 다행입니다. 춘수, 자네도.”
- **Current:** “I’m glad I’m not late. Chunsoo, you’re here too.”
- **Defect:** The draft resolves the deliberately incomplete “자네도” by adding that Chunsoo is present.
- **Replacement:** “I’m glad I’m not too late. You too, Chunsoo.”
- **Rationale:** The source leaves Kim Hwajong’s second thought elliptical and does not say “you’re here.”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 30년 만에 외치는 관등성명에 산이 들썩였다.
- **Current:** The mountain shook with the name he had shouted for the first time in thirty years.
- **Defect:** The draft reduces 관등성명, a formal military identification consisting of rank or position and name, to merely “the name,” weakening the military-conditioning joke and its later callback.
- **Replacement:** The mountain shook as he bellowed out his military identification for the first time in thirty years.
- **Rationale:** Im Chunsoo shouts both “Trainee Number One” and his name, and the source explicitly calls the formula 관등성명.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Current:** For some people, the walk back down the mountain was a hellish experience.
- **Defect:** The plural “some people” changes the source’s singular 누군가, which points to one particular person.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 미리 말해 두는데, 괜한 청탁 같은 건 하지 않길 바랍니다.
- **Current:** Let me tell you in advance: don’t bother making any pointless requests on my behalf.

- **Rationale:** Team Leader 1 is warning the Security Team Leader not to solicit his intervention or support.

## F06 — Critical

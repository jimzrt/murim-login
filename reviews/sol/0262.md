# Review

The draft contains three critical subject or direction errors, plus localized quantity, lineage, and dialogue-clarity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “흐흐, 늙은이가 드디어 죽을 자리를 찾았구나.”
- **Current:** “Heh heh. This old man has finally found the place where he’ll die.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Flame Tiger say that he himself has found a place to die, but he is taunting Jeok Cheongang by saying that Jeok has found his place of death.
- **Replacement:** “Heh heh. Looks like you’ve finally found a place to die, old man.”
- **Rationale:** 늙은이 refers to the old man Flame Tiger is addressing, as confirmed by Jeok Cheongang’s reply that this is a place where he will kill rather than die.

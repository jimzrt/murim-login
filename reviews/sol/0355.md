# Review

Two critical subject/scope errors, several meaningful lineage and combat-result distortions, terminology issues, a misattributed footnote, and repeated noncompliant ASCII ellipses.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “본가와는 아무런 연관도 없는…… 뭐?”
- **Current:** “You have nothing to do with our family... What?”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. Tang Sadok is saying that Cheongpung has no connection to the Tang Clan, not that the Western Heaven Demon Lord has no connection to it.
- **Replacement:** “He has nothing to do with our family… What?”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject refers to 저 아이 in Tang Sadok’s preceding line. The current version changes who lacks a relationship with the Tang Clan.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “지금 당문에서 치료 중인 화왕과 진태경이라는 청년 역시 말일세.”
- **Current:** “I mean the Fire King and the young man named Jin Taekyung, who are also being treated at the Tang Clan.”
- **Defect:** The relative clause incorrectly says that both the Fire King and Jin Taekyung are receiving treatment. The source modifies the Fire King alone. “I mean” also incorrectly recasts the previous reference to Cheongpung rather than adding two more people.
- **Replacement:** “The same goes for the Fire King being treated at the Tang Clan and the young man named Jin Taekyung.”
- **Rationale:** 역시 adds the Fire King and Jin Taekyung to Cheongpung, while 치료 중인 directly describes 화왕. Jin Taekyung is not identified here as a patient.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 우열이 갈리는 데까지는 그리 오랜 시간이 필요하지 않았다.
- **Current:** It did not take long for the victor to be decided.
- **Defect:** The draft overstates the result as a decided victory, although the fight continues. The source says only that the difference in superiority quickly became clear.
- **Replacement:** It did not take long for it to become clear who held the advantage.
- **Rationale:** 우열이 갈리다 establishes which side has the upper hand; it does not mean that the final victor has already been decided.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99

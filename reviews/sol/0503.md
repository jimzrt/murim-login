# Review

One critical subject reversal misstates Jeok Cheongang’s motivation, and two major errors distort the torture explanation and the mother’s rescue. Several localized fidelity and formatting issues remain.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 비록 문경의 이야기 속에 등장하는 여인처럼 버려지지는 않으나, 소중한 누군가의 짐이 될까 두려워 떠나고자 하는 마음은 같았다.
- **Current:** She had not been abandoned like the woman in Mungyeong’s story, but her fear of becoming a burden to someone precious was the same as his.
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. The source says Jeok Cheongang is not being abandoned like the woman but shares the impulse to leave because he fears burdening someone precious. The draft instead says the woman was not abandoned and assigns the fear of becoming a burden to her.
- **Replacement:** Though he was not being abandoned like the woman in Mungyeong’s story, the impulse to leave for fear of becoming a burden to someone precious was much the same.
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is Jeok Cheongang, established by the preceding sentence about his decision to leave. The woman was explicitly abandoned, so the draft also contradicts the story’s central fact.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 생포된 표적을 기다리는 말로는 둘 중 하나다.

약간의 고통을 겪고 난 뒤 입을 열거나, 끔찍한 고통을 겪고 난 뒤 입을 열거나.
- **Current:** There were only two ways a captured target could respond.

He could speak after suffering a little pain, or he could speak after suffering unbearable pain.
- **Defect:** The source describes the two possible fates awaiting a captured target, not two different ways the target could respond. In both cases the response is the same—he talks—and only the amount of torture differs.
- **Replacement:** Only one of two fates awaited a captured target.

He would either talk after a little pain, or talk after excruciating pain.
- **Rationale:** 말로 means the target’s eventual fate or end here. Preserving that setup is necessary for the following explanation that death awaits either way.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 후 인자의 어미는 천수를 모두 누리고 양지바른 곳에 묻혔다. 마지막 말을 듣고 뒤늦게 정신을 차린 불효자가 다시 어미를 모셔 갔기 때문이지.
- **Current:** After that, the ninja’s mother lived out her natural lifespan and was buried in a sunny place. The unfilial son came to his senses too late after hearing her final words and took her back.
- **Defect:** “Too late” implies that the son acted after rescue was no longer possible, contradicting the explicit fact that he brought his mother home and she lived out her natural lifespan. 뒤늦게 means belatedly, not too late.
- **Replacement:** After that, the ninja’s mother lived out her natural lifespan and was buried in a sunny place. After hearing her last words, the unfilial son belatedly came to his senses and brought his mother home again.
- **Rationale:** The son’s belated change of heart causes the mother’s rescue and long life; it does not occur after her death or after the opportunity has passed.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “물론 들어는 봤지. 왜구들이 노략질하러 온 것이 하루 이틀인가?”
- **Current:** “Of course I’ve heard of it. Is it only yesterday or today that Japanese pirates began coming to plunder our shores?”
- **Defect:** The Korean rhetorical idiom means the raids have been happening for a long time. The literal “yesterday or today” construction is unnatural English, and “our shores” is not present in the source.
- **Replacement:** “Of course I’ve heard of it. It’s not as if Japanese pirates only started coming to raid us yesterday.”
- **Rationale:** 하루 이틀인가 rhetorically denies that something is recent or uncommon; it is not asking which of two days it began.

## F05 — Minor

- **Replacement:** They abandon them without caring whether they starve to death or are mauled to death by wild animals.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 호통을 쳐 놓고도 돌아서서 피식 웃고, 철구를 추가로 매달면서도 이놈이 다치는 게 아닌가 싶어 몰래 숨어서 지켜보았던 순간들을.
- **Current:** The moments when he had turned away after shouting at the boy and quietly laughed to himself, when he had added more iron balls but secretly watched from hiding to make sure the boy had not been hurt.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jeok watch to verify whether Taekyung had already been hurt. The source says he watched because he was worried Taekyung might get hurt.
- **Replacement:** He remembered turning away after barking at the boy and letting out a quiet laugh, and secretly watching from hiding whenever he added more weight, worried that the boy might get hurt.
- **Rationale:** 다치는 게 아닌가 싶어 expresses prospective concern about possible injury, not a retrospective check for an injury that has already occurred.

# Review

The draft contains two factual alterations involving subject and time quantity, one omitted outburst, an added threat consequence, and several losses of hierarchy, characterization, or source-specific wording.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 너 같은 놈한테 친구도 있어?
- **Current:** “You have friends?”
- **Defect:** The taunt aimed specifically at someone like Taekyung is omitted, weakening Jeok Cheongang’s mockery and their established register.
- **Replacement:** “Even someone like you has friends?”
- **Rationale:** 너 같은 놈한테 explicitly means “someone like you,” not merely “you.”

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 뭐라 할 새도 없이 적천강을 향해 인사를 건넸다.
- **Current:** Before he could say anything else, he turned toward Jeok Cheongang and bowed.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the interrupted opportunity to Zhuge Gyun rather than the narrator and adds a bow not stated in the source.
- **Replacement:** Before I could say anything, he turned toward Jeok Cheongang and offered his greetings.
- **Rationale:** Zhuge Gyun has just cut off Taekyung’s words; the implied subject of 뭐라 할 새도 없이 is Taekyung, while 인사를 건넸다 means that Zhuge Gyun greeted Jeok Cheongang.

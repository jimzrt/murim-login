# Review

One altered family-history fact, one overstated threat, and one incomplete proper name.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마흔이 넘어 처음으로 본 아들도, 몇 달 후 태어날 둘째는 벌써부터 눈에 넣어도 아프지 않았다.
- **Current:** His first son, whom he had seen for the first time after turning forty, and the second child who would be born in a few months were already so precious that he could not bear even the thought of harm coming to them.
- **Defect:** The draft misreads the idiomatic 아들을 보다 as literally seeing his son, implying that Go Se-won did not meet him until after turning forty. The source says he had his first son after turning forty.
- **Replacement:** His first son, born after he turned forty, and his second child, due in a few months, were already unimaginably precious to him.
- **Rationale:** In this context, 마흔이 넘어 처음으로 본 아들 means the first son he had after the age of forty, not a son he first encountered at that age.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 득은 없고, 실만 있을 겁니다.
- **Current:** You’d gain nothing and lose everything.
- **Defect:** “Lose everything” adds total ruin. The source says there would be no benefit and only loss, without claiming that Song Cheonwoo would lose everything.
- **Replacement:** You’d gain nothing and only stand to lose.
- **Rationale:** 실만 있을 겁니다 describes exclusively negative consequences, not the loss of everything Song possesses.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 나는 남극 세종 기지 건물 위에 쌓인 눈보다 차가운 시선으로 놈을 바라보았다.
- **Current:** I looked at him with a gaze colder than the snow piled atop the Sejong Station building in Antarctica.
- **Defect:** The official proper name of the Antarctic research facility is incomplete.
- **Replacement:** I looked at him with a gaze colder than the snow piled atop the King Sejong Station building in Antarctica.
- **Rationale:** 남극 세종 기지 refers to King Sejong Station, the established English name of South Korea’s Antarctic research station.

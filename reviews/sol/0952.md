# Review

The draft preserves the scene’s broad progression, but several lines change hierarchy, the survivor count, or the force of the dialogue.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘형님이 말씀하신 그까짓 천무학관에 가려면, 명문대파의 제자들도 몇 년씩 줄을 서야 합니다.’
- **Current:** *“The disciples of the great sects have to wait in line for years to get into that Heaven’s Gate Temple you called so special.”*
- **Defect:** “So special” reverses the older brother’s dismissive “그까짓,” blunting Mukyung’s retort.
- **Replacement:** *“Even disciples of the great sects wait years to get into that Heaven’s Gate Temple you call so unimportant.”*
- **Rationale:** Mukyung throws his brother’s belittling description of the institution back at him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 지금의 태원진가는, 그리고 행방이 묘연한 가주를 대신해 그들 모두를 이끄는 진위경은 산서 무림의 맹주(盟主).
- **Current:** The Jin Family of Taiyuan—and Jin Wikyung, who led them all in place of the missing Family Head—were now the Alliance Leaders of Shanxi Murim.
- **Defect:** The plural “Alliance Leaders” assigns the individual office to both the family and Wikyung.
- **Replacement:** The Jin Family of Taiyuan now stood at the head of Shanxi Murim, and Jin Wikyung, who led them all in place of the missing Family Head, was its Alliance Leader.
- **Rationale:** Wikyung is the Alliance Leader; the family is not a second holder of his title.

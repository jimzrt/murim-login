# Review

The draft contains an omitted escalation in Cheongpung’s interference, altered narration and dialogue attribution, softened life-or-death language, one reversed mental action, and repeated use of “Squad Leader” instead of the binding “Captain.”

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “이보게, 장 아우. 혹시 벽호공 익혀 본 적 있나?”
- **Current:** “Hey, Jang, little brother. Have you ever learned the Wall Lizard Technique?”
- **Defect:** “Jang, little brother” is an unnatural literal rendering of the older speaker’s familiar address.
- **Replacement:** “Say, Brother Jang. Have you ever learned the Wall Lizard Technique?”
- **Rationale:** 장 아우 is a familiar senior-to-junior form of address, not a literal statement that Jang is the speaker’s younger sibling.

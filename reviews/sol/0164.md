# Review

The draft contains one contradictory level quantity, one altered crafting subject, a lost slang-based misunderstanding, softened profanity, one terminology mismatch, and several System-panel formatting violations.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘레벨도 벌써 71이 넘었고.’
- **Current:** *I’m already past Level 71, too.*
- **Defect:** “Past Level 71” means Level 72 or higher, contradicting the Status Window, which shows Level 71.
- **Replacement:** *I’m already Level 71, too.*
- **Rationale:** The displayed level fixes Taekyung’s current level at 71; the Korean phrasing expresses that he has already reached that milestone, not that he has exceeded the displayed value.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 만년한철. 천하제일의 광물을 얻었으니 나만의 애병(愛兵)을 만들 수 있다.
- **Current:** I had obtained the finest mineral in the world. I could make myself a weapon of my own.
- **Defect:** The current wording makes Taekyung sound like the person who will craft the weapon, despite the following Quest establishing that a master artisan must make it. It also loses the sense of a personally cherished weapon in 애병.
- **Replacement:** I had obtained the finest mineral in the world. I could have a beloved weapon of my own made.
- **Rationale:** In context, Taekyung possesses the material and will commission an artisan; he is not claiming the ability to forge it himself.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그런 거 하지 마세요. 쪽팔리니까.”

“쪽팔리다? 그게 무슨 뜻이냐?”

“창피하다고요.”

진위경이 충격받은 얼굴로 물었다.

“형이, 이 형이 창피한 게냐?”
- **Current:** “Don’t do anything like that. It’s embarrassing.”

“Embarrassing? What does that mean?”

“I mean it’s humiliating.”

Jin Wikyung asked with a shocked expression,

“Am I embarrassing you? Me—your brother?”
- **Defect:** The translation replaces the unfamiliar modern slang 쪽팔리다 with ordinary English, making Jin Wikyung’s request for a definition incoherent. It then mistranslates 창피하다 as the stronger “humiliating,” obscuring the register shift that drives his misunderstanding.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t do anything like that. It’s cringe.”

“Cringe? What does that mean?”

“It means embarrassing.”

Jin Wikyung asked with a shocked expression,

“Are you saying you’re embarrassed by me? By your own brother?”
- **Rationale:** The source joke depends on Taekyung using slang Jin Wikyung does not recognize, then defining it with an ordinary word that Jin Wikyung immediately takes personally.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 당연히 아니지, 병신아.
- **Current:** Of course it wasn’t, you idiot.
- **Defect:** “Idiot” softens the strongly insulting 병신아 and weakens Taekyung’s profane internal voice.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 절정 고수, 저 네 글자에 이렇게 행복질 수 있다니.
- **Current:** Who knew those four letters—Peak Master—could make me this happy?
- **Defect:** “Peak Master” does not contain four letters, so the literal carryover creates a false statement in English.
- **Replacement:** Who knew the four characters meaning “Peak Master” could make me this happy?
- **Rationale:** 네 글자 refers to the four written Korean characters in 절정 고수; “characters meaning” preserves that quantity without claiming the English rendering has four letters.

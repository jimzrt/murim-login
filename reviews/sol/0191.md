Eight actionable findings: four scene-level subject, action, and hierarchy errors, plus four register and wording issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 또렷하게 귓가를 파고든 늙수그레한 목소리.
- **Current:** An old, slightly hoarse voice had pierced my ears with perfect clarity.
- **Defect:** The draft invents hoarseness; the source describes the voice only as elderly or aged.
- **Replacement:** An elderly voice pierced my ears with perfect clarity.
- **Rationale:** 늙수그레한 means elderly-looking or elderly-sounding, while 또렷하게 supplies the voice’s clarity. Nothing indicates a hoarse quality.

# Review

The draft contains two altered combat details, loses the one-shot wordplay, changes the Dongting Lake location, and misstates the Title’s twenty-percent penalty.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 깨달음과 함께 느려진 세상 속에서 레비아탄은 똑똑히 보고 느낄 수 있었다.
- **Current:** As the world slowed around it, Leviathan could see and feel everything clearly.
- **Defect:** The translation omits the realization that accompanies Leviathan’s slowed perception.
- **Replacement:** As realization dawned and the world slowed around it, Leviathan could see and feel everything clearly.
- **Rationale:** 깨달음과 함께 explicitly links the altered perception to a realization.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 화염과 이빨이 닿은 그 순간, 머리를 뒤흔들 만큼 강렬한 충격이 찾아오기 전까지는.
- **Current:** That was what it believed until the instant its flames and teeth collided, bringing a violent impact that shook its head.
- **Defect:** The possessive incorrectly makes the attacking flames Leviathan’s own.
- **Replacement:** That was what it believed until the instant the flames and its teeth collided, bringing a violent impact that shook its head.
- **Rationale:** The flames belong to the incoming spear attack; only the teeth belong to Leviathan.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 주위에서 흔히들 말한다. 원래 인생은 한 방이라고.

동의한다. 한 방을 노리다가, 바로 그 한 방에 인생을 날려 먹을 수 있다는 점에서 특히.
- **Current:** People often say that life is all about one big chance.

I agree. Especially because you can gamble everything on that one chance and lose your entire life on it.
- **Defect:** Rendering 한 방 as “chance” erases the repeated one-shot/one-blow wordplay that connects the saying to the attack and its recoil.
- **Replacement:** People often say that life comes down to one big shot.

I agree—especially because when you go for that one shot, it can be the very shot that costs you your life.
- **Rationale:** The source repeats 한 방 three times for a dark joke about staking everything on a single attack and being destroyed by that same attack.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 과거 한 입으로 항공모함을 토막 냈다던 아가리가 한쪽 눈과 함께 절반이나 뜯겨 나갔고
- **Current:** The maw that had once cut an aircraft carrier in half with a single bite had been torn away by half, along with one of its eyes.
- **Defect:** “Cut ... in half” invents a two-piece result; 토막 냈다 means the carrier was torn into pieces. The injury clause is also unnaturally phrased.
- **Replacement:** The maw said to have once torn an aircraft carrier to pieces with a single bite had been ripped halfway off, along with one eye.
- **Rationale:** The source describes Leviathan’s former feat as reducing a carrier to pieces, while half of its maw and one eye are now gone.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 몇 달 전, 동정호에서의 퀘스트를 완료하고 얻은 [수상 구조대원]의 칭호 효과.
- **Current:** This was the effect of the **Water Rescue Worker** Title I had obtained after completing the Quest beneath Dongting Lake a few months ago.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the Quest’s location from Dongting Lake to beneath the lake.
- **Replacement:** This was the effect of the **Water Rescue Worker** Title I had obtained after completing the Quest at Dongting Lake a few months ago.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 한번 발동 시 24시간 동안만 유지 가능한데다 내가 익힌 [열양지기]의 상성으로 인해 모든 위력이 20퍼센트나 감소 되는 막대한 패널티가 있지만, 그 모든 걸 감수할 만큼 효과는 확실했다.
- **Current:** Once activated, it lasted for only twenty-four hours. And because of its poor compatibility with the **Scorching Yang Qi** I had learned, it came with the massive penalty of reducing all its power by twenty percent.

Even so, its effects were undeniable.
- **Defect:** “Its power” makes the penalty sound limited to the Title’s effectiveness, whereas the source says all of Taekyung’s power is reduced by twenty percent.
- **Replacement:** Once activated, it lasted for only twenty-four hours. Worse, because it clashed with the **Scorching Yang Qi** I had learned, all my power was reduced by a full twenty percent.

Even so, the effect was worth every drawback.
- **Rationale:** 모든 위력이 20퍼센트나 감소 describes a broad reduction to all power, not merely a reduction in the aquatic Title’s own effect; the final clause says the effect is valuable enough to accept all those drawbacks.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 정확히 반으로 쪼개진 파동이 양옆으로 흩어진 그 순간. 나는 볼 수 있었다.

경악으로 부릅뜬 레비아탄의 눈동자를.
- **Current:** In that instant, I saw Leviathan’s eyes widened in shock.
- **Defect:** The verb form after “saw” is ungrammatical.
- **Replacement:** In that instant, I saw Leviathan’s eyes widen in shock.
- **Rationale:** After the perception verb “saw,” the source action requires the bare infinitive “widen.”

# Review

Several actionable fidelity defects, including altered geography, reversed travel timing, wrong route subject, and invented physical action.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “잘 알아보고 쓰시오. 이제는 무형지독(無形至毒)도 견딜 자신이 생겼으니까.”
- **Current:** “Be careful what you use. I’m confident I can withstand even Formless Ultimate Poison now.”
- **Defect:** Jeok tells Mae to research the poison thoroughly before using it, not merely to be careful about his choice.
- **Replacement:** “Do your research before you use it. I’m confident I can withstand even Formless Ultimate Poison now.”
- **Rationale:** 잘 알아보고 쓰시오 explicitly means to investigate or learn about it properly before using it, which also motivates Mae’s reply that he will investigate.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “……그걸 지금 말이라고 하는 거요?”

“일단 말은 맞소. 지금 막 내 입으로 한 말이니.”
- **Current:** “...You call that an answer?”

“It is technically correct. I just said it with my own mouth.”
- **Defect:** Mae does not claim that his prediction is technically correct. He facetiously argues that it qualifies as something said because it came from his mouth.
- **Replacement:** “…And you call that something to say?”

“Well, it was something I said. It just came out of my mouth.”
- **Rationale:** The exchange hinges on 말 meaning something spoken, not on the factual correctness of Mae’s noncommittal answer.

## F04 — Critical

- **Source:** 소림사와 사천문을 비롯한 몇몇 문파가 큰 피해를 입긴 했으나, 아직 정파의 영역이라 할 수 있는 구주(九州)에 속한 어느 성도 무너지지 않았으니까.
- **Current:** Although Shaolin Temple, the Sichuan Sect, and several other sects had suffered heavy losses, not a single city in the Nine Provinces—territory that could still be called the orthodox faction’s domain—had fallen.
- **Defect:** 성 means a province here, not a city, substantially reducing the geographic scale of what has remained unconquered.
- **Replacement:** Although Shaolin Temple, the Sichuan Sect, and several other sects had suffered heavy losses, none of the provinces within the Nine Provinces—the territory still held by the orthodox faction—had fallen.
- **Rationale:** The source states that no province belonging to the orthodox faction’s Nine Provinces has fallen.

- **Current:** *Two shichen at most to Mount Daebyeol. I have plenty of time.*

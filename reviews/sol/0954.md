# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it misattributes a character’s emotion, contradicts Temur and Chinggen’s blood relationship in a footnote, and softens or changes two pointed expressions.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 자무카는 새카만 어둠 속을 응시했다. 달빛조차 먹구름에 가려져 흐릿한 그곳에서, 떨리는 마음을 애써 억누르며 창칼을 곧추세운 적들이 보이는 듯했다.
- **Current:** Jamukha gazed into the pitch-black darkness. Beneath the clouds that obscured even the moonlight, he seemed to see the enemy, their hearts trembling as they held their spears and swords ready. He forced down his own restless excitement.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the effort to suppress a trembling heart to Jamukha; in the source, it describes the enemies he imagines seeing.
- **Replacement:** Jamukha gazed into the pitch-black darkness. There, where even the moonlight was dimmed by dark clouds, he could almost see the enemy holding their spears and swords upright, struggling to master their trembling hearts.
- **Rationale:** 떨리는 마음을 애써 억누르며 modifies the imagined enemies, not Jamukha.

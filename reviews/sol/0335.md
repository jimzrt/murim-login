# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one malformed physical action, omits an established epithet, weakens two jokes through unnatural wording, and assigns reluctance to the wrong character.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 개썅마이웨이, 노빠꾸.
- **Current:** *Total fuck-you-my-way, no-brakes energy.*
- **Defect:** The invented compound “fuck-you-my-way” is unnatural and obscures the blunt slang characterization that the Tang Clan does whatever it wants and never backs down.
- **Replacement:** *They did whatever the fuck they wanted. No backing down.*
- **Rationale:** The source uses two terse, profane slang fragments to characterize the clan as fiercely independent and unwilling to retreat; the replacement preserves that force in natural contemporary English.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 녀석의 목덜미를 낚아채기 위해 발을 뻗었지만, 사천당문의 답장은 그보다 빨랐다.
- **Current:** I stretched out a foot to grab Cheongpung by the scruff of his neck, but the Sichuan Tang Clan’s answer came faster.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung attempt to grab Cheongpung with his foot. The source describes him moving or lunging forward in order to seize Cheongpung by the neck.
- **Replacement:** I lunged forward to grab Cheongpung by the scruff of his neck, but the Sichuan Tang Clan’s answer came faster.
- **Rationale:** 발을 뻗었지만 marks Taekyung launching into motion here; 낚아채기 위해 identifies the intended hand action. Rendering the foot as the grabbing instrument changes the physical action into an implausible one.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 석벽 위에서 맹렬하게 쏘아진 것은 성인 남성의 키만 한 거대한 창이었다. 정확히 청풍의 발 앞에 박힌 창대가 부르르 몸을 떨었다.
- **Current:** A massive spear, as tall as an adult man, shot fiercely from the top of the stone wall. Its shaft struck the ground exactly in front of Cheongpung’s foot and trembled violently.
- **Defect:** The draft says the spear merely struck the ground, omitting that it embedded itself there.
- **Replacement:** A massive spear as tall as a grown man hurtled down from atop the stone wall. It buried itself directly in front of Cheongpung’s foot, its shaft quivering.
- **Rationale:** 박힌 explicitly establishes that the spear is lodged in the ground, while the trembling shaft follows from that impact. This mechanical detail is part of the warning shot’s force.

## F04 — Major

## F06 — Minor

- **Source:** “암기가 아니라 물건이에요.”

“Pardon?”

- **Defect:** “It’s an object” is not an idiomatic euphemism in English and blunts the sexual misunderstanding underlying the exchange.

“It’s mine. You know… that.”
- **Rationale:** 물건 and 그거 remain deliberately euphemistic rather than anatomically explicit. “My thing” conveys the same embarrassed implication naturally without intensifying it.

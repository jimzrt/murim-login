# Review

The draft preserves the scene overall, but changes a distance and a cannonball count, blurs one physical action, shifts an order to the group into a shared intention, and omits Ju Wongong’s stated kinship.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이백여 장의 거리를 격하고 쇄도한 쇳덩이들이
- **Current:** The iron balls came hurtling across a distance of more than two hundred yards
- **Defect:** Two hundred jang is roughly six hundred meters, not two hundred yards.
- **Replacement:** The iron balls came hurtling across more than six hundred yards
- **Rationale:** The conversion substantially shortens the stated firing distance.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 여파에 휘말려 재수 없게 갑판까지 날아온 생선을 허공에서 잡아챈 태산이 울상을 지었다.
- **Current:** Taishan caught a fish that had been swept all the way onto the deck in the spray. He looked dejected.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the fish sound as though it had reached the deck; Taishan snatches it out of the air while it is flying toward the deck.
- **Replacement:** Taishan snatched a fish out of the air as the blast sent it flying toward the deck. He looked dejected.
- **Rationale:** 허공에서 잡아챈 specifies where and how he catches the fish.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “밥값 좀 하자.”
- **Current:** “Let’s earn our keep.”
- **Defect:** The inclusive “let’s” makes Jin part of the effort, though he has just said the others can stop the cannonballs without him.
- **Replacement:** “Earn your keep.”
- **Rationale:** The line prompts the companions serving as his ‘human shields’ to act.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금까지 모든 군선에서 퍼부은 대포알만 족히 수백 발은 된다.
- **Current:** Every military vessel had poured out cannonballs by the hundreds.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns hundreds of shots to each vessel rather than hundreds across all the vessels combined.
- **Replacement:** Together, the military vessels had fired hundreds of cannonballs by now.
- **Rationale:** 모든 군선에서 퍼부은 대포알만 족히 수백 발 counts the collective barrage.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “공격 중지! 중지잇! 이건 황명, 아니 황상 폐하의 팔촌 되는 몸으로서 내리는 황족명이다!”
- **Current:** “Cease your attack! Cease it! I’m ordering you, as a relative of His Majesty the Emperor!”
- **Defect:** Ju Wongong’s self-correction from an imperial command to an invented imperial-family command is lost, as is his boast that he is related to the Emperor in the eighth degree.
- **Replacement:** “Cease fire! Cease! This is an imperial command—no, an imperial-family command, issued by me as His Majesty the Emperor’s eighth-degree relative!”
- **Rationale:** 황명, 아니 황족명 is the distinction Jin questions in the next line; 팔촌 states the precise, distant kinship Ju invokes.

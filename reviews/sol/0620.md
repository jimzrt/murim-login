# Review

The draft contains a critical injury escalation, a substantial omitted line, an altered kinship reference, and several localized fidelity and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정확히 뒤통수를 강타한 술병에 혁무진의 뚝배기가 깨져 나가고, 싸구려 술병이 산산 조각나며 사방으로 튀었다.
- **Current:** A liquor bottle struck Hyuk Mujin squarely in the back of the head. His skull cracked open, and the cheap bottle shattered into fragments that flew in every direction.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the colloquial description of Hyuk Mujin getting cracked over the head into a literal open-skull injury, contradicting his immediately continuing to argue.
- **Replacement:** A liquor bottle struck Hyuk Mujin squarely in the back of the head, cracking him over the skull. The cheap bottle shattered, sending fragments flying in every direction.
- **Rationale:** 뚝배기가 깨져 나가고 is comic slang for taking a hard blow to the head here, not a statement that his skull was physically split open.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘반 시진 뒤. 십 리 밖에 있는 호숫가에서 보세.’
- **Current:** *Half a shichen from now. Meet me by the lakeside ten li away.*
- **Defect:** The traditional time and distance units are retained without the brief factual explanation needed for an English reader to understand the rendezvous.
- **Replacement:** *Half a shichen[^1] from now. Meet me by the lakeside ten li away.*

[^1]: A shichen is approximately two hours, while ten li is approximately five kilometers.
- **Rationale:** The rendezvous is in roughly one hour at a lake roughly five kilometers away; a concise footnote preserves the source units while making their practical meaning clear.

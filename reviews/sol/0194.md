## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “화왕이 당황하면 우화왕 좌화왕!”
- **Current:** “If the Fire King gets flustered, Fire King on the right, Fire King on the left!”
- **Defect:** The literal wording leaves the joke unintelligible in English because it does not explain the pun on 좌왕우왕, a Korean expression for being flustered or running around in confusion.
- **Replacement:** “When the Fire King gets flustered, he goes Right Fire King, Left Fire King![^2]”

[^2]: This puns on *jwa-wang-u-wang*, a Korean expression for being flustered or running around in confusion; *jwa* and *u* mean “left” and “right,” while *wang* means “king.”
- **Rationale:** The source deliberately constructs a groan-worthy pun from 화왕 and 좌왕우왕; a concise footnote preserves both the literal joke and its intended comic function.

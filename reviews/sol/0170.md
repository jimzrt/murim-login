# Review

Several actionable fidelity and register issues, including a changed subject, a distorted fatherless-child warning, and a reversed explanation of the metal’s rarity.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “가 봐야겠소. 어르신께서 무슨 봉변이라도 당했을지 몰라.”
- **Current:** “We need to go. The elder might have suffered some kind of misfortune.”
- **Defect:** The plural subject incorrectly says the family should go together, while Jang-pal states that he alone must go.
- **Replacement:** “I need to go. The elder might have suffered some kind of misfortune.”
- **Rationale:** The first-person singular 가 봐야겠소 and Jang-pal’s immediately following instruction that his wife remain with Hanga establish that only he intends to leave.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “당신은 여기서 항아랑 있으시오. 살짝 보고만 올 테니까 걱정 말고.”
- **Current:** “You stay here with Hanga. I’ll just take a quick look. Don’t worry.”
- **Defect:** The translation renders 살짝 as speed and omits the cue that Jang-pal intends to check the situation discreetly.
- **Replacement:** “You stay here with Hanga. I’ll just sneak over and take a look, so don’t worry.”
- **Rationale:** 살짝 보고만 올 describes a cautious, unobtrusive look rather than merely a quick one, which matters because Jang-pal fears the martial artists.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “만약 놈들이 조금, 아주 조금이라도 질이 나쁜 놈이라면 자네는 돌아올 수 없을 걸세. 아이를 아비 없이 키울 생각인가?”
- **Current:** “If they’re even a little—just a little—bad, you won’t be able to return. Are you planning to raise your child without a father?”
- **Defect:** The final question illogically makes Jang-pal the person who would raise his own child without a father, rather than warning that his death would leave Hanga fatherless.
- **Replacement:** “If they’re even a little—just a little—bad, you won’t be able to return. Do you mean to leave your child to grow up without a father?”
- **Rationale:** 아이를 아비 없이 키우다 means causing or allowing the child to be raised fatherless; the warning is about the consequence of Jang-pal failing to return.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 시바, 이럴 줄 알았으면 진작 보여 줄걸.
- **Current:** *Fuck. If I’d known this would happen, I would’ve shown him the iron sooner.*
- **Defect:** The draft turns the deliberately softened minced oath 시바 into fully explicit profanity and unnecessarily supplies “the iron,” which the source leaves implicit.
- **Replacement:** *Shit. If I’d known this would happen, I would’ve shown him sooner.*
- **Rationale:** 시바 is an evasive deformation of stronger profanity, so “Fuck” intensifies the register; the omitted object in 보여 줄걸 should remain naturally implicit in English.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천하제일의 광물이라더니, 조금씩 떼서 날에 붙일 정도라면 엄청나게 귀하긴 한 모양이다.
- **Current:** They called it the greatest mineral under heaven, so if there was enough of it to peel off pieces and attach them to a blade, it must truly be incredibly rare.
- **Defect:** The translation reverses the mechanical implication by saying there is enough metal to remove pieces, whereas Taekyung infers its rarity from smiths using only tiny pieces on a blade.
- **Replacement:** They called it the greatest mineral under heaven, and judging by how people only shaved off tiny pieces to attach to a blade, it really must be incredibly rare.
- **Rationale:** 조금씩 떼서 날에 붙일 정도 indicates sparing use in small attached portions, not an abundance sufficient for pieces to be removed.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “여기가 옆옆옆 집에 사는 장씨 할부지…… 니미럴. 그새 옮았네. 아무튼 장 노인 댁이오?”
- **Current:** “Is this the house of Grandpa Jang, who lives three houses over…? Damn it. I’ve already caught it. Anyway, is this Old Man Jang’s place?”
- **Defect:** “I’ve already caught it” has no clear English antecedent and obscures the joke that Hanga’s repetitive way of identifying the house has rubbed off on the speaker.
- **Replacement:** “Is this the house of Grandpa Jang, who lives three houses over…? Damn it. It’s rubbed off on me already. Anyway, is this Old Man Jang’s place?”
- **Rationale:** 그새 옮았네 figuratively says the child’s speech habit has already spread to or rubbed off on him.

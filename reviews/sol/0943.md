# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes a time interval and a sensory detail, loses a name-based joke and Namho’s role in the reinforcements, and adds profanity absent from the source.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 시진 전쯤?
- **Current:** An hour or so ago, I think.
- **Defect:** A shichen is approximately two hours, not one.
- **Replacement:** About two hours ago, I think.
- **Rationale:** The source places the hawks’ passage roughly one shichen earlier.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 되도록 지원군을 이끌고 후발대로 향하지.
- **Current:** I’ll go with the reinforcements as part of the second wave.
- **Defect:** Namho says he will lead the reinforcements, not merely accompany them.
- **Replacement:** I’ll try to lead the reinforcements and follow behind you.
- **Rationale:** 이끌고 assigns Namho an active leadership role; 되도록 also qualifies his intention.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “일섬. 마표.”
- **Current:** “Ilseom. Sama Pyo.”
- **Defect:** Using Sama Pyo’s full name removes the deliberate misnaming that prompts his correction.
- **Replacement:** “Ilseom. Ma Pyo.”
- **Rationale:** Taekyung calls him 마표 rather than 사마표, then confirms he did it on purpose.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그리고 수십이나 되는 인기척이.
- **Current:** And dozens of people.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the people visually present; the bandit detects their presence.
- **Replacement:** And he could sense dozens of people.
- **Rationale:** 인기척 refers to signs or the sensed presence of people, distinct from the visible flags.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아주 쥐 죽은 듯이 조용한 게
- **Current:** It’s so quiet you can hear a mouse piss.
- **Defect:** The draft introduces a crude image and profanity absent from the ordinary expression for dead silence.
- **Replacement:** It’s dead quiet.
- **Rationale:** 쥐 죽은 듯이 조용한 describes an eerie absence of sound, not a sound made by a mouse.

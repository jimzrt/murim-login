# Review

Several passages change who or what is being described, narrow civilian harm to deaths, or lose the reason Ningxia’s factions were constrained.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 중원에 비하면 변방 촌구석 취급받는 산서성이라 해도, 일개 마적단에게 주어진 한계는 명백했다.
- **Current:** Even Shanxi Province—treated like some backwater in comparison to the Central Plains—had clear limits on how much it would tolerate from a band of mounted bandits.
- **Defect:** Makes Shanxi’s tolerance the limiting factor rather than the limits of what a mere bandit group could accomplish there.
- **Replacement:** Even in Shanxi Province, dismissed as a backwater compared with the Central Plains, there were clear limits to what a mere band of mounted bandits could do.
- **Rationale:** 일개 마적단에게 주어진 한계 assigns the limits to the mounted-bandit group.

## F05 — Major

- **Source:** “바로 그겁니다. 녕하성은 제법 가까우니 사막 너머의 암천보다는 더욱 위협적이죠. 더군다나 저처럼 무공을 익히지 않은 몸이라면 오죽하겠습니까.”

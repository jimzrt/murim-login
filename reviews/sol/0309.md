# Review

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 속도로는 개방 제일의 경신법이라는 만리추풍신법(萬里追風身法)을 익히면 뭐하나. 그래 봤자 오성의 경지인데.
- **Current:** What good was mastering the Beggars’ Sect’s finest movement technique, the Myriad-Li Chasing Wind Movement Technique? It only reached the fifth stage.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the technique itself reach only a fifth stage. The source instead says Gung Gibang has attained only five-tenths mastery of it.
- **Replacement:** What good was learning the Myriad-Li Chasing Wind Movement Technique, said to be the Beggars’ Sect’s finest movement technique for speed? He had only mastered it to five-tenths.
- **Rationale:** 오성의 경지 describes the practitioner’s degree of attainment, not a limitation inherent to the technique.

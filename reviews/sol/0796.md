# Review

Several localized errors alter the Prophet’s position, Yamamoto’s record of absences, and an internet joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 높은 모래 언덕 위, 자욱한 먼지구름을 일으키며 움직이는 후송 차량들을 확인한 선지자는 눈을 빛냈다.
- **Current:** On top of a high sand dune, The Prophet spotted evacuation vehicles moving through a thick cloud of dust. His eyes lit up.
- **Defect:** The draft places the Prophet on the dune. The source places the moving vehicles there and says they are kicking up the dust.
- **Replacement:** The Prophet’s eyes lit up as he spotted transport vehicles moving along a high sand dune, kicking up a thick cloud of dust.
- **Rationale:** 높은 모래 언덕 위 describes where the vehicles are moving; 일으키며 makes them the cause of the dust cloud.

# Review

Four critical subject, mechanism, and location errors, plus one minor profanity/wordplay issue.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아닙니다. 웬 중년 사내와 동석했습니다.”
- **Current:** “No. I was sitting with some middle-aged man.”
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed: Utu-ri says Jin shared a table with a middle-aged man, not that Utu-ri himself sat with one.
- **Replacement:** “No. He was sharing a table with some middle-aged man.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 동석했습니다 is the previously discussed suspect, Jin, and the ensuing questions concern Jin’s companion.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 당장 늙은 소면 가게 주인장의 말이 거짓말이라 우길 수도 있겠지만…… 글쎄. 백상이 그 말에 순순히 속아 줄 만큼 멍청했다면 여기까지 오지도 않았을 거다.
- **Current:** I could insist that the old noodle-shop owner’s statement was a lie, but… who knew? If Baeksang were stupid enough to be taken in so easily, we would never have made it this far.
- **Defect:** The final clause changes the subject from Baeksang to Jin’s group and also renders 글쎄 as uncertainty rather than a dismissive pause.
- **Replacement:** I could insist that the old noodle-shop owner’s statement was a lie, but… please. If Baeksang were stupid enough to fall for that so easily, he never would have made it this far.
- **Rationale:** 여기까지 오지도 않았을 거다 refers to Baeksang’s success in engineering the current situation; Jin is arguing that Baeksang could not have gotten this far if he were that easy to fool.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘게다가 백상 정도의 고수라면 우리를 습격한 살수들의 상흔(傷痕)만 봐도 알아차리겠지. 대부분이 내 솜씨가 아니라는 걸.’
- **Current:** *Besides, a master like Baeksang would realize just by looking at the wounds left by the assassins who attacked us that most of them weren’t my handiwork.*
- **Defect:** The draft makes the wounds something inflicted by the assassins, whereas the source refers to wounds on the assassins and whether Jin inflicted them.
- **Replacement:** *Besides, a master like Baeksang would realize just by looking at the wounds on the assassins who attacked us that most of them weren’t my handiwork.*
- **Rationale:** 살수들의 상흔 means the assassins’ wounds. Those wounds reveal that most of the attackers were killed or injured by someone other than Jin.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 피에 젖은 모래 알갱이가 가죽신에 짓밟히고, 동시에 펄럭인 수십여 개의 옷자락이 화원의 꽃들을 스쳤다.
- **Current:** Blood-soaked grains of sand were crushed beneath leather shoes, while dozens of fluttering hems brushed against the flowers in the Fire Courtyard.
- **Defect:** 화원 is the common noun “garden” here, not the established proper name Fire Courtyard; the draft relocates the action to an unrelated named location.
- **Replacement:** Blood-soaked grains of sand were crushed beneath leather shoes, while dozens of fluttering hems brushed against the flowers in the garden.
- **Rationale:** The possessive phrase 화원의 꽃들 describes flowers in the surrounding garden. Nothing in this scene identifies the setting as the proper location called the Fire Courtyard.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “퍽이나 그러겠다, 시시발발 새끼야.”
- **Current:** “Yeah, right, you right-and-wrong son of a bitch.”
- **Defect:** The English is an unnatural noun phrase and weakens the profanity-based distortion of 시시비비 into 시시발발.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah, fucking right, you right-and-wrong bastard.”
- **Rationale:** The source sarcastically rejects Baeksang’s promise and crudely twists his formal 시시비비 into a phrase containing 시발; the replacement preserves both the riff and the strong profanity in spoken English.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 소와 말까지 두루 섭렵하며 기어코 똥 시리즈 삼부작을 완성.
- **Current:** he’d gone on to master the names of cows and horses, completing the trilogy of poop names.
- **Defect:** “Names of cows and horses” makes the joke sound like animal naming rather than successive Cow Poop and Horse Poop identities.
- **Replacement:** he’d tried Cow Poop and Horse Poop too, completing his trilogy of poop names.
- **Rationale:** The cow and horse extend the sequence of names built on 똥, meaning “poop.”

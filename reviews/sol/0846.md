# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but several details change the physical action, a speaker’s meaning, or the characters’ register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 석벽 위에서 터져 나온 고함과 함께, 섬광처럼 쏘아진 십여 발의 화살이 기마병들의 투구 깃을 관통한 것은.
- **Current:** At the shout that rang out from atop the stone wall, more than a dozen arrows shot forth like flashes of light, piercing the horsemen’s helmet plumes.
- **Defect:** The arrows pierce parts of the helmets, not decorative plumes.
- **Replacement:** At the shout from atop the stone wall, a dozen or so arrows flashed through the air and pierced the edges of the horsemen’s helmets.
- **Rationale:** 투구 깃 refers to an edge or flap of a helmet; 십여 발 means approximately a dozen arrows, not necessarily more than twelve.

Several semantic and register issues remain, including a premature declaration of victory, loss of Wudang’s martial hierarchy, two omitted speech-level corrections, and an unsupported increase in Gung Gibang’s injury.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 딱 반걸음 차이.
- **Current:** The difference was exactly half a step.
- **Defect:** The wording leaves both the action and the two things being compared unclear; the source means Cheongpung avoided the strike by a margin of half a step.
- **Replacement:** Cheongpung had dodged it by exactly half a step.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding action establishes that the half-step is the narrow margin by which Cheongpung evades the descending sword.

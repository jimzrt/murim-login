# Review

Several actionable fidelity and terminology issues, including a reversed reaction, an invented System-like stat, lost colloquial register, and combat moves mislabeled as forms.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘와아, 그래서요?’
- **Current:** *Wow, so what?*
- **Defect:** The curious prompt “and then?” is changed into the dismissive “so what?”, reversing Cheongpung’s attitude. It is also spoken dialogue, not an internal thought.
- **Replacement:** “Wow, and then?”
- **Rationale:** 그래서요 asks what happened next in an eager, polite register; it does not express indifference or contempt.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한 주먹에 호랑이를 쓰러트린다? 확실히 그럴 만한 파괴력을 지닌 초식이다. 비무를 겪을 때마다 쭉쭉 상승하는 맷집이 아니었다면 어제처럼 무릎을 꿇었을 것이다.

‘그래도 어제보단 많이 나아진 걸 위안 삼아야 하나?’

올라간 것은 맷집뿐만이 아니다. 청풍의 무공을 직접 몸으로 겪으면서 점점 익숙해지고 있었다.
- **Current:** A single fist that knocked down a tiger? It certainly possessed enough destructive power to justify the name. If my Toughness hadn’t been increasing rapidly every time I sparred, I would have dropped to my knees just like yesterday.

*Should I take comfort in the fact that I’m doing much better than yesterday?*

Toughness wasn’t the only thing that had improved. By experiencing Cheongpung’s martial arts with my own body, I was gradually getting used to them.
- **Defect:** Capitalizing “Toughness” invents a named System stat where the source refers generically to Taekyung’s improving ability to withstand blows.
- **Replacement:** A single fist that knocked down a tiger? It certainly possessed enough destructive power to justify the name. If I hadn’t been getting so much better at taking hits with every duel, I would have dropped to my knees just like yesterday.

*Should I take comfort in the fact that I’m doing much better than yesterday?*

My ability to take a hit wasn’t the only thing that had improved. By experiencing Cheongpung’s martial arts with my own body, I was gradually getting used to them.
- **Rationale:** 맷집 is ordinary physical durability or an ability to take hits, not a capitalized game attribute.

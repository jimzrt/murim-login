# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but two passages assign actions or possessions to the wrong person, and several others alter concrete details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘혼자만의 능력이 아니었어.’
- **Current:** *He wasn’t acting alone.*
- **Defect:** The draft makes this a claim about the Prophet acting with others. The thought instead qualifies Michael Silbert’s rise: it was not achieved through his abilities alone.
- **Replacement:** *Michael Silbert hadn’t done it on his own.*
- **Rationale:** The following sentence attributes Michael’s position to the Muninns who served him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘하지만 이제는 그 모든 걸 갖게 됐지.’
- **Current:** *But now I have all of it.*
- **Defect:** The draft gives Michael’s hidden resources to the narrator instead of the Prophet.
- **Replacement:** *But now the Prophet had all of it.*
- **Rationale:** The next lines describe Muninn forming a new power base and inheriting what his dead master concealed.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 최소 뇌진탕이긴 한데, 뭐 포션도 뒤집어썼으니까 괜찮겠지.
- **Current:** Probably a concussion at the very least, but I’d dumped a potion all over him, too. He’d be fine.
- **Defect:** The draft adds a deliberate action by the narrator. Huginn was drenched when the potion bottle broke on his head.
- **Replacement:** Probably a concussion at the very least, but he’d been drenched in potion, too. He’d be fine.
- **Rationale:** The source states the resulting condition, not that Jin separately poured potion over Huginn.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “정확히는 첫 번째 무닌이었지. 처음이자 마지막으로 봤던.”
- **Current:** “More precisely, the first Muninn. The first and last one I ever saw.”
- **Defect:** This says Huginn never saw any later Muninn, contradicting his account that he met three as an adult. He saw the first Muninn only once.
- **Replacement:** “More precisely, the first Muninn. That was the first and last time I saw him.”
- **Rationale:** 처음이자 마지막으로 봤던 refers to seeing that first Muninn, not to seeing Muninns altogether.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 짧게는 일 년. 보통은 수년에 한 번씩 비밀리에 미카엘 실베르트를 찾아오는 숨겨진 검.
- **Current:** A hidden blade that had secretly visited Michael Silbert every year or so—sometimes after a few years.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the usual interval: visits were ordinarily years apart, with one year the shorter interval.
- **Replacement:** A hidden blade that secretly visited Michael Silbert once every few years—sometimes after just one.
- **Rationale:** 보통은 수년에 한 번씩 establishes the norm; 짧게는 일 년 gives the minimum.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 퀘스트, [알 수 없는 죽음]을 성공적으로 완료했습니다!
- **Current:** Quest [An Unknown Death] successfully completed!
- **Defect:** Square brackets around a System-panel quest title violate the specified panel format.
- **Replacement:** Quest *An Unknown Death* successfully completed!
- **Rationale:** The blockquote and System heading already identify the notice; UI terms should not retain square brackets.

## F07 — Minor

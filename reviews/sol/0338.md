# Review

One major humor/voice omission and five localized fidelity or register issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 고결한 황족인 친왕(親王)이 바로 내 광팬이다.
- **Current:** He was the one and only younger brother of the emperor of the Great Nation that ruled the world, a noble imperial Prince—and my biggest fan.
- **Defect:** “Biggest fan” introduces an unsupported superlative; 광팬 means an extremely enthusiastic fan, not necessarily the most enthusiastic one.
- **Replacement:** He was the one and only younger brother of the emperor of the Great Nation that ruled the world, a noble imperial Prince—and a huge fan of mine.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the intensity of 광팬 without adding a comparative claim.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 청풍은 어디로 튈지 모르는 놈이니 내가 직접 데리고 다니고, 궁기방과 혁무진은 지금쯤 아미파로 열심히 달려가고 있을 것이다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung was impossible to predict, so I would take him with me personally. By now, Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin were probably running as hard as they could toward the Emei Sect.
- **Defect:** “Impossible to predict” generalizes the specific concern that Cheongpung might suddenly run off somewhere, weakening the reason Taekyung must personally keep him close.
- **Replacement:** There was no telling where Cheongpung might run off to, so I would keep him with me. By now, Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin were probably running as hard as they could toward the Emei Sect.
- **Rationale:** 어디로 튈지 모르는 놈 specifically characterizes Cheongpung as liable to dart or wander off unpredictably.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 안 봐도 가상현실이다. 학구열에 불타서 무리한 탓에 늦잠을 잔 것이 뻔하지.
- **Current:** I didn’t need to see it to know what had happened. He had clearly stayed up too late, burning with enthusiasm for learning, and then overslept.
- **Defect:** The translation removes the deliberate “virtual reality” punchline, normalizing the sentence into plain exposition and losing Taekyung’s modern, self-aware humor.
- **Replacement:** I didn’t even have to look. I could see the whole thing in virtual reality: he had clearly pushed himself too hard in his burning enthusiasm for learning, then overslept.
- **Rationale:** 안 봐도 가상현실이다 humorously updates the familiar idea of seeing something without looking by invoking virtual reality; that explicit modern reference must remain.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 청성파의 장로들은 별 미친놈 다 보겠다는 듯한 눈빛으로 우리를 바라봤지만, 장문인인 청풍고검은 생각했던 것 이상으로 심플한 인물이었다.
- **Current:** The Elders of the Qingcheng Sect looked at us as if they had just encountered every kind of lunatic in existence, but their Sect Leader, Cheongpung the Ancient Sword, was even more straightforward than I had expected.
- **Defect:** “Every kind of lunatic in existence” misreads the idiomatic 별 미친놈 다 보겠다 and produces unnatural English; the Elders are reacting as though an extraordinary new breed of lunatic has appeared before them.
- **Replacement:** The Elders of the Qingcheng Sect looked at us as if wondering what kind of lunatics had just walked in, but their Sect Leader, Cheongpung the Ancient Sword, was even more straightforward than I had expected.
- **Rationale:** The Korean conveys incredulous contempt toward the people before them, not a claim that the Elders have encountered all possible varieties of lunatic.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 사제나 나나, 뒷방 늙은이 신세가 된 지 오래였는데 이런 일이라면 기쁘게 나서야지. 허허.
- **Current:** Junior Brother and I have been old men relegated to the back room for a long time now. If it’s for something like this, we should gladly step forward. Ho ho.
- **Defect:** “Old men relegated to the back room” is a stiff literal calque of 뒷방 늙은이 신세 and does not naturally convey that the Elders have long been put out to pasture.
- **Replacement:** Junior Brother and I have been put out to pasture for years, but for something like this, we’ll gladly step forward. Ho ho.
- **Rationale:** 뒷방 늙은이 describes elderly people sidelined from active affairs; the idiomatic replacement preserves the self-deprecating register.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이걸 어떻게 찾누…….
- **Current:** “How the hell are we supposed to find him…?”
- **Defect:** The translation adds profanity absent from this speaker’s line, flattening the contrast with the other Daoist’s preceding curse and unnecessarily intensifying the register.
- **Replacement:** “How are we supposed to find him…?”
- **Rationale:** The rustic ending 찾누 conveys a weary, colloquial lament, but contains no profanity; the strong language belongs only to the preceding 니미럴.

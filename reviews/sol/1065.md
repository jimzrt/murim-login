# Review

Several scene facts and quantities need correction; one line reverses Hyeoncheon’s reason for continuing.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 이는 선택할 수도, 거부할 수도 없다.
- **Current:** They couldn’t choose otherwise. They couldn’t refuse.
- **Defect:** The draft implies survivors can choose between capture and flight, though the source says they cannot choose or reject the fate awaiting them.
- **Replacement:** They had no say in which fate awaited them, and no way to refuse it.
- **Rationale:** 선택할 수도, 거부할 수도 없다 denies both choice and refusal.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그렇기에 지금으로부터 사흘 전, 대설산(大雪山)을 둘러싼 혈전이 아군의 대승으로 막을 내린 직후 우리에게는 새로운 선택지가 주어졌다.
- **Current:** So, just after the bloody battle around the Great Snow Mountain ended in a crushing victory for our side, we were given a new choice.
- **Defect:** Omits that the battle ended three days before the present narration.
- **Replacement:** So, three days ago, just after the bloody battle around the Great Snow Mountain ended in a crushing victory for our side, we were given a new choice.
- **Rationale:** 지금으로부터 사흘 전 explicitly dates the victory.

## F03 — Major

- **Defect:** Changes advancing enemy forces into forces gathering in place.
- **Replacement:** Or would we move out to stop the Dark Heaven forces advancing somewhere else?

## F04 — Critical

- **Source:** 끝에 다다르기 전까지는 결코 멈추지 않겠네. 지금쯤 저 구름 위에서 원시천존(號出天等)의 보살핌을 받고 있을 넋들을 위해서라도.

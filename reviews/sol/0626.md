# Review

Several register and hierarchy cues are weakened, one line assigns the question to the wrong grammatical subject, and one relationship description is rendered as an unnatural literal calque.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그 흰둥이들 말하는 거죠?
- **Current:** You mean those white guys, right?
- **Defect:** The neutral phrase “white guys” loses the deliberately rude, jarring force of 흰둥이들, which prompts Namho to repeat the word in discomfort.
- **Replacement:** You mean those whiteys, right?
- **Rationale:** 흰둥이들 is a disparaging label rather than a neutral description, and the following response depends on its abrasive register.

## F02 — Major

- **Defect:** The vague phrase “lay down a nice fire” sanitizes the aggressively vulgar threat conveyed by 싸질러.

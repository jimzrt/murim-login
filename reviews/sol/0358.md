# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters two physical/action details, misplaces a line around a character beat, weakens some profanity and modality, and contains several localized wording errors.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 밑창이 사라져 버린 가죽 신발을 벗어 던지며, 저 멀리 보이는 놈을 노려보았다.
- **Current:** I kicked off the leather shoes whose soles had been completely torn away and glared at the bastard in the distance.
- **Defect:** The draft changes taking off and throwing away the shoes into kicking them off, altering the narrated physical action.
- **Replacement:** I pulled off the leather shoes whose soles had completely disappeared, tossed them aside, and glared at the bastard in the distance.
- **Rationale:** 벗어 던지며 explicitly describes removing the shoes and throwing them aside, while 밑창이 사라져 버린 says the soles were gone rather than merely torn.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “시벌놈. 좀 한다?”
- **Current:** “You bastard. Not bad.”
- **Defect:** The translation sanitizes the strong profanity and changes a taunting question into a flat statement.
- **Replacement:** “Fucking bastard. You’re pretty good, huh?”
- **Rationale:** 시벌놈 is strongly profane, and 좀 한다? is an impressed but confrontational question.

“Why he wanted to kill you so badly.”

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 서천마군이 나직한 목소리와 함께 피투성이가 된 손바닥을 쥐었다가 폈다.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “젊어서 그런지 혈기가 지나쳐. 혹시 처음 같은 행운을 기대한다면, 고이 접어 두게. 그 일격이 자네의 전력이었다는 것쯤은 알고 있으니 말일세.”
- **Current:** “You are young, so your blood runs hot. If you are hoping for luck like last time, put that thought away. I know that strike was your full strength.”
- **Defect:** “Last time” incorrectly points to an earlier occasion, while 처음 refers to the luck Taekyung enjoyed at the beginning of this fight.
- **Replacement:** “You are young, so your blood runs hot. If you are hoping for the same luck you had at the start, forget it. I know that strike was your full strength.”
- **Rationale:** 처음 means the beginning or initial exchange here, not a separate previous encounter.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 절벽을 무너트린 일격이, 서천마군의 옆구리에 틀어박혔다.
- **Current:** The strike that could have brought down a cliff crashed into the Western Heaven Demon Lord’s side.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an accomplished fact into a hypothetical capability.
- **Replacement:** The strike that had brought down a cliff slammed into the Western Heaven Demon Lord’s side.
- **Rationale:** 무너트린 is attributive past and identifies this as the strike that brought down a cliff, not merely one capable of doing so.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “그래. 너 때문에 생목숨 몇 개 날아갔다. 어차피 죽어야 할 놈들이었지만.”
- **Current:** “Yeah. A few perfectly good lives were wasted because of you. They were going to die anyway, though.”
- **Defect:** The second sentence changes Taekyung’s judgment that the prisoners deserved or needed to die into a prediction that their deaths were already inevitable, while also dropping the contempt in 놈들.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. A few perfectly good lives were wasted because of you. They were bastards who deserved to die anyway, though.”
- **Rationale:** 죽어야 할 놈들이었지만 is deontic and contemptuous: they were bastards who ought to die, not simply people who happened to be destined for death.

# Review

Several passages alter motive, voting mechanics, or the elders’ reaction; others need localized corrections.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 자네가 무슨 마음으로 이곳에 왔는지는 모르겠지만, 지금부터는 한 가지만 기억하게.
- **Current:** *I don’t know what brought you here, but from now on, remember one thing.*
- **Defect:** “What brought you here” asks about the cause of Jin’s arrival rather than what he intends to do.
- **Replacement:** *I don’t know what you came here intending to do, but from now on, remember one thing.*
- **Rationale:** 무슨 마음으로 concerns Jin’s intentions, which Michael is warning him to reconsider.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.89
- **Source:** “병세가 깊다니. 자네의 외조부께서 어떤 분이신지 몰라도 유분수지!”
- **Current:** “He’s seriously ill? Whoever your grandfather is, there are limits!”
- **Defect:** “Whoever your grandfather is” makes the elder sound unaware of Cheon Taemin’s identity and drops the maternal relationship.
- **Replacement:** “Gravely ill? Even for someone like your maternal grandfather, that’s too much to believe!”
- **Rationale:** The elder knows whose grandson Choi is; his protest is that such an illness seems impossible for Cheon Taemin.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 사람들이 느낀 충격은 이내 허탈감이 되었고, 몇몇 원로들은 분노마저 느꼈다.
- **Current:** The shock people felt soon turned into helplessness, and some of the elders even began to feel anger.
- **Defect:** “Helplessness” substitutes an inability to act for the hollow, deflated feeling in 허탈감.
- **Replacement:** The shock soon gave way to a hollow sense of loss, and some of the elders even felt anger.
- **Rationale:** 허탈감 describes emotional emptiness or deflation, not helplessness.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 어느 순간부터 서서히 증가하기 시작한 마력 수치와 이변(異變)들.
- **Current:** The gradual rise in magical power readings, and the strange events that had begun to follow.
- **Defect:** “Begun to follow” imposes a sequence between the readings and the strange events that the source does not state.
- **Replacement:** The magical power readings that had begun to rise, and the strange events that had begun to occur.
- **Rationale:** The source recalls both developments without saying the strange events followed the rise.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 여기에 가장 많이 이름이 쓰인 사람은 추방되는 것이 아니라 세계 헌터 연맹의 지도자가 된다는 것이다.
- **Current:** And whoever had their name written on it the most wouldn’t be banished—they’d become the leader of the World Hunter Federation.
- **Defect:** “On it the most” suggests repeated names on the single fragment in Jin’s hand rather than names counted across voting fragments.
- **Replacement:** And the person whose name appeared on the most fragments wouldn’t be banished—they’d become the leader of the World Hunter Federation.
- **Rationale:** The passage describes choosing a leader by which name receives the most votes.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 미친 새끼. 웬일인가 했더니, 아직도 그 소리네.
- **Current:** *You crazy bastard. I wondered what was different, but you’re still on about that.*
- **Defect:** Jin’s 웬일인가 refers to his surprise that the Skeleton King answered this time, not to something being different.
- **Replacement:** *You crazy bastard. I wondered why you’d answered me, but you’re still on about that.*
- **Rationale:** Jin has just remarked on receiving an unexpected reply; this line reacts to the reason the Skeleton King gave.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 나는 대답했다. 어쩌면 처음 녀석을 만난 그날부터 정해져 있던 그 사실 그대로.
- **Current:** I gave him the same answer as the truth that might have been decided from the very day we first met.
- **Defect:** The sentence is unnatural and “the same answer” implies a previous answer that the source does not mention.
- **Replacement:** I answered with a truth that might have been settled the day I first met him.
- **Rationale:** Jin states a truth about their friendship that may have been settled since their first meeting.

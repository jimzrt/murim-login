# Review

Several actionable fidelity and terminology issues, chiefly altered subjects, a lost self-correction joke, and an incorrect rendering of 南蠻.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “남만에서 들여온 흑단목(黑檀木)일세. 쇠보다 단단한 목재지.”
- **Current:** “They’re made of ebony imported from the Southern Man.[^1] Wood harder than steel.”
- **Defect:** The plural pronoun changes the subject from the single broken chair to multiple chairs, and the second sentence is an unnatural fragment.
- **Replacement:** “It’s made of ebony imported from Nanman.[^1] The wood is harder than steel.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean uses a singular implied subject referring to the chair and identifies ebony as a wood harder than steel.

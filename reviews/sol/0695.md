# Review

Several action, subject, temporal, role, and spatial details are altered, including two reversed physical actions near the climax.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “순찰 다녀오는 동안 그늘에 앉아 계신 분이 할 말입니까?”
- **Current:** “Is that something you can say while sitting in the shade after coming back from patrol?”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the return from patrol to the older warrior, but the junior is the one who went on patrol while the older warrior remained in the shade.
- **Replacement:** “Is that something you can say after sitting in the shade while I was out on patrol?”
- **Rationale:** 순찰 다녀오는 동안 describes the period when the junior was away on patrol, during which the older warrior was sitting in the shade.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 정도 숫자의 전사들이 모인 것은 오십여 년 전, 정마대전 이후 처음 있는 일이다.
- **Current:** The last time so many warriors had gathered in one place had been more than fifty years ago, after the Great Faction War.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the previous gathering an event that occurred after the Great Faction War. The source says this is the first such gathering since that war more than fifty years ago.
- **Replacement:** Not since the Great Faction War more than fifty years earlier had so many warriors gathered in one place.
- **Rationale:** 정마대전 이후 처음 means “the first time since the Great Faction War,” establishing that war as the temporal benchmark.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 윗분들한테 걸리면 결국 수문장인 나만 욕 처먹기 딱 좋다.
- **Current:** If the higher-ups catch us, I’ll be the only one who gets cursed out as the Captain of the Guards.
- **Defect:** “Captain of the Guards” broadens and elevates his role beyond 수문장, which specifically identifies him as the officer responsible for the gate.
- **Replacement:** If the higher-ups catch us, I’m the gate captain, so I’ll be the only one who gets my ass chewed out.
- **Rationale:** 수문장 is a gatekeeper or gate captain, and 욕 처먹다 is blunt and vulgar enough to warrant stronger colloquial English than “gets cursed out.”

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 이미 중천에 오른 태양은 숨 막히는 열기를 뿜어냈고, 북문 밖으로 펼쳐진 너른 목초지와 외궁이 훤히 내려다보이는 높은 언덕은 아지랑이에 의해 일렁였…….
- **Current:** The sun already high in the sky radiated suffocating heat, and the wide pasture beyond the North Gate and the high hill overlooking the Outer Palace shimmered in the heat haze—
- **Defect:** The draft treats the pasture and hill as two things shimmering. In the source, the high hill overlooks both the pasture beyond the North Gate and the Outer Palace, and it is the hill that shimmers in the heat haze.
- **Replacement:** The sun, already high overhead, radiated suffocating heat, and the high hill overlooking both the broad pasture beyond the North Gate and the Outer Palace shimmered in the heat haze—
- **Rationale:** 너른 목초지와 외궁이 훤히 내려다보이는 modifies 높은 언덕, describing what can be seen from the hill.

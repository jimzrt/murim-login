# Review

Several scene facts and speaker assignments are altered, including living ogres being described as already defeated, Captain Yoo’s line being reassigned, the newcomers’ clothing being conflated, and the C-rank/C-cup comparison being reversed. There are also localized register, terminology, and joke-rendering issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 새끼들이 어디서 다 잡은 몹을 스틸하려고.
- **Current:** These bastards were trying to steal monsters I had already taken down.
- **Defect:** The draft says the monsters had already been taken down, but all four ogres are still alive. 다 잡은 means Taekyung already had them effectively beaten or claimed, not that he had killed them.
- **Replacement:** These bastards were trying to steal mobs I already had dead to rights.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Taekyung’s claim that the ogres were effectively his kills without contradicting their continued presence in the scene.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “정 팀장이 물어봤는데, 우선 도축 끝내고 얘기하자더군요.”
- **Current:** “I asked him, and he said we could talk after he finished butchering.”
- **Defect:** The explicit subject 정 팀장 shows that someone else—Captain Yoo—is reporting what Team Leader Jeong asked. Changing it to “I asked him” assigns the line to Jeong and alters the speaker.
- **Replacement:** “Team Leader Jeong asked him, but he said they could talk once he finished butchering.”
- **Rationale:** This retains the stated subject and correctly distinguishes Captain Yoo’s contribution from Team Leader Jeong’s preceding answer.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it omits one material disguise action, narrows Mungyeong’s identity-management instruction, softens profanity and sexual conduct, adds an unsupported physical action, and loses several comedic pragmatic cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어디서 구했는지 감히 관군의 복식까지 훔쳐 입고 대국의 질서를 어지럽히다니.
- **Current:** “Don’t remind me. They dared to steal government uniforms from somewhere and disrupt the order of the Great Nation.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the martial artists wore the stolen government uniforms, removing the disguise action central to the City Lord’s complaint.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t remind me. They dared to steal government uniforms from who knows where, put them on, and disrupt the order of the Great Nation.”
- **Rationale:** 훔쳐 입고 explicitly means that they stole the uniforms and wore them.

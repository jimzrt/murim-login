# Review

Several factual and characterization issues remain, including a wrong time unit, an altered institutional quantity, an omitted line, and a subject/title substitution.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 한 식경 전에 그들이 탄 장강수로맹의 쾌조선이 출항했습니다.
- **Current:** one shichen ago.[^1]
- **Defect:** The draft changes 식경 to shichen. A sikgyeong is roughly thirty minutes, while a shichen is roughly two hours.
- **Replacement:** one sikgyeong ago.[^1]
- **Rationale:** The source specifies 식경, so the current rendering substantially changes the elapsed time.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 삼공(三公)을 배출한 세도가의 핏줄로 태어나 탄탄대로를 걸어온 사천성주다.
- **Current:** The Sichuan Governor had been born into a powerful family that had produced three Grand Councilors and had walked a smooth road throughout his life.
- **Defect:** The draft reads 삼공 as a quantity of three officials. Here 삼공 is the collective title for the highest ministerial offices, so the source does not state that the family produced exactly three Grand Councilors.
- **Replacement:** The Sichuan Governor had been born into a powerful family that had produced Grand Councilors, and his path through life had always been smooth.
- **Rationale:** Removing the invented numerical claim preserves both the family’s political stature and the institutional reference.

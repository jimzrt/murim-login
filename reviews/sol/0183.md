# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter physical actions, a threat’s target, elapsed time, and who can hear the final exchange; there are also localized losses in humor and natural phrasing.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 미노타우로스를 연상시키는 덩치가 퉁방울만 한 눈을 부라렸다.
- **Current:** A hulking man reminiscent of a Minotaur glared with eyes as big as brass bowls.
- **Defect:** “Brass bowls” invents a material image absent from 퉁방울만 한, which describes extremely large, bulging eyes.
- **Replacement:** A hulking man reminiscent of a Minotaur glared at me with eyes as big as saucers.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes the man’s huge, glaring eyes without comparing them specifically to brass objects.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 만약 저런 놈이 건달이라면 민중의 지팡이가 아니라 민중의 오함마가 와도 안 된다.
- **Current:** If someone like that were a thug, even the people’s sledgehammer wouldn’t be enough to deal with him.
- **Defect:** The translation omits the contrast with 민중의 지팡이, the Korean epithet for the police, leaving the “people’s sledgehammer” punchline without its setup.
- **Replacement:** If a guy like that were a thug, the police—the people’s cane—wouldn’t stand a chance. Even the people’s sledgehammer wouldn’t be enough.
- **Rationale:** The joke escalates from the police as the “people’s cane” to an imagined “people’s sledgehammer”; both halves are required.

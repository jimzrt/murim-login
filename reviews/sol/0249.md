# Review

The draft contains one reversed farewell, one misassigned dialogue exchange, and several hierarchy, quantity, and terminology errors.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하나같이 천하 어디를 가도 인정받는 초일류, 혹은 절정 고수들이다.
- **Current:** Every single one was either a First Rate martial artist recognized anywhere under heaven or a Peak master.
- **Defect:** The translation omits 초, flattening the finalists from top-tier First Rate martial artists to ordinary First Rate martial artists and weakening the stated hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** Every single one was either a top-tier First Rate martial artist recognized anywhere under heaven or a Peak master.
- **Rationale:** 초일류 explicitly denotes the highest tier within the First Rate realm, which matters to the competitors’ demonstrated caliber.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그럼 뒷방 늙은이는 이만 물러가도록 하지. 모두 즐거웠네.”
- **Current:** “Then this old man from the back room will take his leave. Enjoy yourselves.”
- **Defect:** The second sentence changes Song Ho’s retrospective statement that he enjoyed meeting everyone into an instruction for the others to enjoy themselves.
- **Replacement:** “Then this old relic will take his leave. It was a pleasure, everyone.”
- **Rationale:** 모두 즐거웠네 expresses Song Ho’s own assessment of the encounter, not a parting wish or command directed at the finalists.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “후, 후발선제(後發先制)?”
- **Current:** “L-Later strike, first control?”
- **Defect:** The martial maxim is rendered as an unnatural string of noun phrases rather than Baek Woo’s recognition that Taekyung moved second but seized control first.
- **Replacement:** “M-Moving second, yet taking control first?”
- **Rationale:** 後發先制 describes acting after the opponent yet gaining control before him, matching the immediately explained exchange.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “허나 안타깝게도 나는 항룡십팔장을 오 성까지밖에 익히지 못했다.”
- **Current:** “Unfortunately, I have only learned the Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms up to the fifth stage.”
- **Defect:** 오 성 means fifty percent mastery here, in direct contrast with the preceding 팔 성, not the fifth stage of the technique.
- **Replacement:** “Unfortunately, I have only mastered fifty percent of the Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms.”
- **Rationale:** The paired quantities 팔 성 and 오 성 indicate eight-tenths and five-tenths of mastery; converting the latter into an ordinal stage changes the technique’s progression mechanism.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “기, 기방이? 존댓말?”

“강자지존. 모르냐?”

“그래도 그건 좀…….”
- **Current:** “M-Might Makes Right. Haven’t you heard of it?”

“Even so, that’s a bit much…”
- **Defect:** The draft omits Gung Gibang’s shocked objection to being called casually by name and ordered to use polite speech, then assigns Taekyung’s “Might Makes Right” retort to Gung Gibang.
- **Replacement:** “G-Gibang? Polite speech?”

“Might Makes Right. Haven’t you heard of it?”

“Even so, that’s a bit much…”
- **Rationale:** The source is a three-turn exchange: Gung Gibang protests, Taekyung invokes 강자지존, and Gung Gibang objects again. Preserving the speakers is necessary for the hierarchy joke and characterization.

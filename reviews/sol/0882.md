# Review

The draft largely follows the scene, but it loses a key hierarchy cue and obscures the food-shout joke.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 닥치시오! 감히 어느 안전이라고…… 황궁 앞에서 이 무슨 망발이오!
- **Current:** “Shut your mouth! How dare you speak that way right outside the imperial palace… What kind of disgraceful nonsense is this?”
- **Defect:** Omits the accusation that the scholar is speaking this way in the Emperor’s presence, reducing the force of the rebuke to a complaint about location.
- **Replacement:** “Shut your mouth! How dare you say that in His Majesty’s presence… Right outside the imperial palace, of all places!”
- **Rationale:** 어느 안전이라고 invokes the ruler’s presence; 황궁 앞에서 separately names the location.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “오오햐아아앙자아앙유우우욱!”
- **Current:** “Ooohhyyaaangjaaanngyuuk!”
- **Defect:** Romanizing the drawn-out food name prevents an English reader from recognizing what the giant shouts, obscuring the joke that the crowd and guards hear him call for a dish.
- **Replacement:** “Fiiive-spiiice poooork!”
- **Rationale:** The shout is an elongated 오향장육, rendered elsewhere in this draft as “five-spice pork.”

## F03 — Minor

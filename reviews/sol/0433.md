# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes a survivor from singular to plural, weakens Chairman Shao Yang’s apology, introduces unsupported duration and severity into China’s projected difficulties, and creates ambiguity around the healer’s expertise. Several localized action, register, and wording issues also need correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당신들이 밟고 있는 그 아래에 생존자 있으니까 좀 비키라고.
- **Current:** “Wow, you’re really slow on the uptake. There are survivors beneath the ground you’re standing on, so I’m telling you to move.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular survivor into multiple survivors, altering the rescue fact immediately before one old woman is found.
- **Replacement:** “Wow, you’re really slow on the uptake. There’s a survivor under where you’re standing, so I’m telling you to move.”
- **Rationale:** 생존자 is singular in context, and the subsequent rescue reveals that specific survivor.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 샤오 양 주석은 이에 대해 깊이 사죄하며, 지난달 있었던 회의 녹취록을 공개했습니다.
- **Current:** Chairman Shao Yang has expressed his deepest regret over this and released the transcripts of last month’s meeting.
- **Defect:** Expressing regret is weaker than 사죄, which means making an apology and accepting blame. The draft also pluralizes a transcript that the source treats collectively as singular.
- **Replacement:** Chairman Shao Yang has offered a profound apology for this and released the transcript of last month’s meeting.
- **Rationale:** 깊이 사죄하며 explicitly describes a deep apology, an important political and pragmatic distinction in the report.

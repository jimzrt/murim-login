# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it reverses Namho’s movement, loses a key hierarchy cue, and makes a recurring euphemism more explicit than the source.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 감사를 표하며 막 돌아선 남호가 무심코 말꼬리를 흐리자
- **Current:** Namho had just turned away after thanking him, but his voice faltered without his meaning to.
- **Defect:** Namho turns to thank Sama Pyo; he does not turn away after completing his thanks.
- **Replacement:** Namho had just turned to thank him when his voice faltered despite himself.
- **Rationale:** 막 돌아선 and the unfinished thanks place his turn before the attempted expression of gratitude.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그러나 현재의 상황에서 중요한 것은, 수많은 저 검은 고양이들의 목에 방울을 단 이가 누구이며 그 진정한 의도가 무엇이냐는 것이었다.
- **Current:** But what mattered now was who had put bells around the necks of all those black cats—and what their true intentions were.
- **Defect:** The plural possessive makes the intentions sound like the cats’ rather than the bell-ringer’s.
- **Replacement:** But what mattered now was who had put bells around the necks of all those black cats—and what that person truly intended.
- **Rationale:** 그 진정한 의도 refers to the intentions of the person controlling them.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96

- **Defect:** The troops’ prostration is omitted, and ‘behind Hong Pyo’ turns following his example into a position.

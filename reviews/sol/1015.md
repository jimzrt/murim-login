# Review

One reversed speaker, two meaningful omissions, and two smaller fidelity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “저 보고 있었잖아요.”
- **Current:** “You were looking at it.”
- **Defect:** Reverses who was reading the missive.
- **Replacement:** “I was reading it.”
- **Rationale:** 저 is Ju Hwaran referring to herself; she wants him to reopen the missive because she was reading along.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 내가 태을무정검을 족치며 역으로 뜯어낸 막대한 액수의 금자는
- **Current:** the huge sum of gold I’d squeezed out of the Taeeul Merciless Sword
- **Defect:** Omits that he beat the Taeeul Merciless Sword and extracted the money in return.
- **Replacement:** the huge sum of gold I’d beaten out of the Taeeul Merciless Sword in return
- **Rationale:** 족치며 supplies the beating, while 역으로 marks the extraction as a reversal.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96

- **Defect:** Drops the narrator’s pointed correction from the Murong great family to the diminished Murong household.
- **Replacement:** filling the void left by the Murong great family—or rather, what remained of the Murong household
- **Rationale:** 아니 explicitly corrects 모용세가 to 모용가, marking the loss of its former standing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97

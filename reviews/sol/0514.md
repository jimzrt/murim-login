# Review

Two scene facts are altered: the group contains twenty monks, not fewer than twenty, and Sama Pyo does not claim that he personally killed Blood Cudgel. The draft also drops a meaningful hesitation and mistranslates two descriptions of eyes.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 스무 명에 달하는 소림사의 승려들.
- **Current:** There were nearly twenty Shaolin monks.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the group’s stated number from twenty to fewer than twenty, contradicting the later reference to twenty approaching figures.
- **Replacement:** There were twenty Shaolin monks.
- **Rationale:** 스무 명에 달하는 identifies a group numbering twenty, which is confirmed by the later 스무 개의 신형.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 상대가 저를 죽이기 전에 죽여야 했습니다.
- **Current:** I had to kill him before he killed me.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Sama Pyo claim that he personally killed Blood Cudgel, although the Korean leaves the killer unstated here and Sama Pyo immediately identifies his subordinate as the culprit.
- **Replacement:** He had to be killed before he killed me.
- **Rationale:** 죽여야 했습니다 has no explicit first-person subject. A passive construction preserves that omission and remains consistent with the subsequent disclosure that the giant dealt the killing blow.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “허락해 주셔서 감사합니다. 사……숙.”
- **Current:** “Thank you for granting permission, Martial Uncle.”
- **Defect:** The draft removes the speaker’s conspicuous hesitation over an address that the next sentence explicitly says is still unfamiliar to him.
- **Replacement:** “Thank you for granting permission, Martial… Uncle.”
- **Rationale:** The pause within 사……숙 is a meaningful emotional and hierarchical cue, reflecting the monk’s discomfort with the newly adopted relationship.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 딱딱한 누군가의 목소리에, 거한이 송아지 같은 눈을 깜빡였다.
- **Current:** At someone’s curt voice, the giant blinked his calf-sized eyes.
- **Defect:** The draft treats the calf comparison as a measurement, giving the giant impossibly enormous eyes rather than comparing their expression or appearance to a calf’s.
- **Replacement:** At someone’s curt voice, the giant blinked his large, calf-like eyes.
- **Rationale:** 송아지 같은 means “like a calf,” not “the size of a calf.” The image characterizes the giant’s incongruously innocent appearance.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 가라앉은 눈빛으로 젊은 무인을 응시하던 정호가 불쑥 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** Jung Ho stared at the young martial artist with sunken eyes before speaking abruptly.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a subdued or grave gaze into the physical condition of having recessed eyes.
- **Replacement:** Jung Ho regarded the young martial artist with a grave gaze, then spoke abruptly.
- **Rationale:** 가라앉은 modifies 눈빛, the quality of his gaze, rather than describing the anatomical position of his eyes.

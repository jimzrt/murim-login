# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it names opium before Taekyung’s realization, changes a classroom joke, and softens a pointed slur.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “희한하네. 내가 아는 무인들은 술은 곧잘 마셔도 앵속(罌粟)은 잘 안 하던데.”

“앵속이라면…….”
- **Current:** “How curious. The martial artists I know will drink without a second thought, but they don’t usually smoke opium.”

“Opium?”
- **Defect:** Hong Jin names poppy, not opium. Naming opium in both lines gives away the realization Taekyung has only after hearing the explanation.
- **Replacement:** “How curious. The martial artists I know drink readily enough, but they rarely touch poppy.”

“Poppy…?”
- **Rationale:** 앵속 means poppy; Taekyung identifies its dried sap as opium in the following beat.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘아편. 아니 앵속의 그 독특한 향…… 이미 맡아 본 적이 있어. 홍진의 것보다도 더욱 진하게.’
- **Current:** *Opium. No, that distinctive scent of the opium Hong Jin calls yingsu… I’ve smelled it before. And much stronger than this.*
- **Defect:** The thought equates Hong Jin’s word for poppy with opium and adds an unnecessary romanization.
- **Replacement:** *Opium. No, that distinctive scent of poppy… I’ve smelled it before. Stronger than Hong Jin’s.*
- **Rationale:** Taekyung corrects his own thought from 아편 to 앵속 and recalls a stronger instance of the scent.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘아청 전쟁이요?’

‘아청…… 야, 반장아. 김민정이.’

‘네?’

‘지금 바로 경찰 불러라. 그냥 내 오늘 점마 조지고 폭력 교사로 잡혀 들어갈란다.’
- **Current:** *“What? The Blue Opium War?”*

*“The Blue Opium… Hey, class president! Kim Minjeong!”*

*“Yes?”*

*“Call the cops right now. I’m going to beat this punk and get arrested for assaulting a student.”*
- **Defect:** “Blue Opium” invents a different mistake and loses the Korean joke’s reference to child-and-youth sex-crime protections.
- **Replacement:** *“What? The Youth Protection War?[^1]”*

*“Youth Protection… Hey, class president! Kim Minjeong!”*

*“Yes?”*

*“Call the cops right now. I’m going to beat this punk and get arrested for assaulting a student.”*

[^1]: Taekyung mishears *apyeon* (opium) as *acheong*, shorthand associated with Korea’s law protecting children and youth from sexual crimes.
- **Rationale:** 아청 is the source’s mistaken word, not a color or a kind of opium. The brief note preserves the reference without making the spoken line more explicit than the Korean.

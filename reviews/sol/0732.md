# Review

Several localized fidelity and natural-English issues, including an omitted location qualifier, altered physical actions, and a lost literary allusion.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그게 초저녁부터 개소리를 들어서 그래요.
- **Current:** That’s because you’ve been listening to dogshit since early evening.
- **Defect:** “Dogshit” is an unnatural literalization of 개소리 in this auditory context; the Korean means profane nonsense or bullshit.
- **Replacement:** That’s because you’ve been listening to bullshit since early evening.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the source’s profanity and meaning while allowing the following barking metaphor to carry the canine wordplay naturally.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 주인이 누구인지는 모르겠지만 말세다, 말세.
- **Current:** I don’t know who the owner is, but this is the end of the world.
- **Defect:** The literal phrase “this is the end of the world” does not naturally convey the idiomatic lament 말세다 and loses its exasperated register.
- **Replacement:** I don’t know who the owner is, but the world’s really gone to hell.
- **Rationale:** 말세다 is a complaint that society has gone badly wrong, not a literal prediction of the world ending.

## F03 — Minor

# Review

The draft changes the subject of an attack, makes a galloping horse appear airborne, omits Hak Su’s reveal, and loses a character joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘왜지? 일부러 맹공을 퍼붓지도 않았는데 청허자가 어째서.’
- **Current:** *Why? Cheongheoja hasn’t even launched a full assault. Why would he…?*
- **Defect:** The draft makes Cheongheoja the one who has not launched an assault. The thought concerns the attackers’ decision not to press him.
- **Replacement:** *Why? We haven’t even pressed the attack. Why would Cheongheoja…?*
- **Rationale:** The observer is trying to explain why Cheongheoja would unleash Force when his own side has not deliberately mounted a fierce assault.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 새하얀 뼈를 드러낸 유령마가 바람처럼 공간을 가로지르고
- **Current:** A ghost horse, its white bones exposed, raced through the air like the wind, with several thousand fanatics charging behind it.
- **Defect:** “Through the air” makes the horse appear to fly; the source describes its speed, not flight.
- **Replacement:** A ghost horse, its white bones exposed, raced forward like the wind, with several thousand fanatics charging behind it.
- **Rationale:** 공간을 가로지르고 describes crossing the space ahead; nothing here says the horse leaves the ground.

## F03 — Critical

# Review

One critical time-unit error and two meaningful dialogue errors require correction; the associated footnote also defines the wrong unit.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “너무 뭐라 하진 마. 나도 이렇게 허접한 짓거리를 계획한 건 아니었으니까.
- **Current:** “Don’t be too hard on him. I didn’t plan something this shoddy, either.
- **Defect:** The added pronoun “him” makes the Blood Lord tell Jeok Cheongang not to criticize Hong Dao. In context, he is responding to Jeok Cheongang calling him a bastard and telling Jeok not to blame or berate him too much.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t give me too much grief. I hadn’t planned anything this shoddy, either.
- **Rationale:** The omitted object of 뭐라 하다 is contextually the Blood Lord himself, not Hong Dao; the following sentence is his justification for his own plan.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “청이라. 뭐든 말해 보세요. 고생했으니 내 성심성의껏 들어줘야지.”
- **Current:** “A request. Go ahead and tell me. You completed your mission admirably, so I should hear you out with all due sincerity.”
- **Defect:** “Hear you out” only promises to listen, while 들어주다 here promises to grant Han Su’s request. This weakens the Blood Lord’s explicit offer of a reward and makes Han Su’s delighted response less coherent.
- **Replacement:** “A request? Go ahead and tell me. You worked hard, so I’ll do my utmost to grant it.”
- **Rationale:** 성심성의껏 들어주다 means to grant or fulfill the request sincerely, not merely listen to it.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “선배님은 산서잠룡. 나는 화왕. 늦어도 일다경이면 마무리될 것 같은데?”
- **Current:** “You take the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi. I’ll take the Fire King. We should be able to finish within a shichen at the latest.”[^2]
- **Defect:** The draft replaces 일다경, the brief time needed to drink a cup of tea, with a shichen of approximately two hours. This drastically changes the Blood Lord’s claimed speed in killing Jeok Cheongang.
- **Replacement:** “You take the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi. I’ll take the Fire King. We should be able to finish within an ildagyeong at the latest.”[^2]
- **Rationale:** The following narration explicitly glosses 일다경 as 차 한 잔 마실 짧은 시간, so it cannot be rendered as the much longer 시진.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 일다경. 차 한 잔 마실 짧은 시간에 화왕 적천강을 죽일 수 있다고 단언했다.
- **Current:** [^2]: A *shichen* is a traditional East Asian time unit of approximately two hours.
- **Defect:** The footnote defines a different time unit from the source and reinforces the draft’s erroneous two-hour duration.
- **Replacement:** [^2]: An *ildagyeong* is the short time it takes to drink a cup of tea.
- **Rationale:** The source itself defines 일다경 as the brief time required to drink one cup of tea.

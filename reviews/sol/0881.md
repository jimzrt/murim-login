# Review

Several passages alter a quantity, the returned child’s age, or who is expected to fight. Other findings concern a surveillance image and a lost shift in register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 폭풍은 이미 코앞까지 다가왔고, 서로가 가진 모든 힘을 꺼내어 부딪쳐야 할 겁니다.
- **Current:** The storm is almost upon us, and we’ll have to bring all our strength to bear against one another.
- **Defect:** “Against one another” makes the assembled allies sound as though they will fight each other, rather than the opposing side.
- **Replacement:** The storm is almost upon us, and both sides will have to throw everything they have into the clash.
- **Rationale:** 서로 refers to the sides about to clash; it does not turn the conspirators against one another.

## F02 — Major

- **Source:** 강보에 싸여 변방으로 떠났던 어린아이는 어엿한 소년이 되어 돌아왔으며

- **Defect:** “Young man” ages the returning child beyond the source’s 소년.
- **Replacement:** The child who had left for the frontier, bundled in swaddling clothes, had returned as a boy.
- **Rationale:** The contrast is between an infant in swaddling clothes and a boy, not an adult.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “내가, 아니 제가 실언을 했습니다.”

## F05 — Minor

# Review

The draft has several actionable fidelity errors, including a reversed subject in the final confrontation.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저주받은 핏줄을 이어받은 황가가 몰살당하는 광경을 이 두 눈으로 똑똑히 지켜보고, 비명에 간 스승과 사형제들을 위해 제를 올리기 전까지는 죽을 수 없었다.
- **Current:** He could not die until he had watched with his own two eyes as the imperial family, cursed with that bloodline, was wiped out—and offered a memorial rite for his Master and fellow disciples, who had died screaming.
- **Defect:** “Died screaming” invents how his Master and fellow disciples died.
- **Replacement:** He could not die until he had watched with his own two eyes as the imperial family, heirs to that cursed bloodline, was wiped out—and offered a memorial rite for his Master and fellow disciples, who had met untimely deaths.
- **Rationale:** 비명에 간 means they met untimely deaths; it does not say they screamed.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “이름 부르지 마. 정들어.”
- **Current:** “Don’t call my name. You’ll get attached.”
- **Defect:** The punchline assigns the attachment to the Demon Lord instead of Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t say my name. I might get attached.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 정들어 is naturally the speaker in this retort.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그는 여느 때와 다름없이 돌아온 제자의 모습을 말없이 바라보다가, 이내 크게 소리 내어 웃었다.
- **Current:** He silently looked at his Disciple, back as if nothing had changed. Then he burst out laughing.
- **Defect:** “As if nothing had changed” replaces the recurring action of the Disciple coming back as he always did.
- **Replacement:** He silently gazed at his Disciple, who had come back as he always did. Then he burst out laughing.
- **Rationale:** 여느 때와 다름없이 modifies the Disciple’s return, not whether the ordeal changed anything.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내장 조각이 섞인 핏물을 토해 내며 삶의 끝자락에서 꿈틀거리던 죽음의 그림자를.
- **Current:** The shadow of death stirring at the edge of his life as he coughed up bloody phlegm mixed with pieces of his organs.
- **Defect:** “Phlegm” adds a substance absent from the injury description.
- **Replacement:** The shadow of death stirring at the edge of his life as he coughed up blood mixed with pieces of his organs.
- **Rationale:** 핏물 is blood, not phlegm.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 아직 은영술에 휩싸인 자신을 정확히 응시하는 진태경의 모습을 보며. 전신을 꿰뚫는 듯한 그 시선을 느끼며.
- **Current:** He saw Jin Taekyung, still wrapped in concealment techniques, staring straight at him. He felt that gaze pierce his entire body.
- **Defect:** The draft puts Jin Taekyung in concealment instead of Heaven’s Slaughter, reversing who has detected whom.
- **Replacement:** Though he himself was still cloaked by his concealment technique, he saw Jin Taekyung staring straight at him. He felt that gaze pierce his entire body.
- **Rationale:** 자신 refers to Heaven’s Slaughter: Jin is looking directly at him despite his concealment.

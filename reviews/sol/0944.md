# Review

The draft omits a key stretch of the bandit’s interrogation and changes several quantities, subjects, and relationships.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 모두의 앞에 우뚝 서 있는 대머리와 웬 여인에게 뭔가를 열심히 설명하고 있던 부두령이, 돌연 십여 장 밖에서 멍하니 이 광경을 지켜보고 있던 산적을 발견하고 눈을 크게 떴다.
- **Current:** The deputy chief, who’d been standing in front of everyone and earnestly explaining something to a bald man and a woman, suddenly spotted the bandit staring blankly from a dozen yards away. His eyes widened.
- **Defect:** The draft places the deputy chief in front of everyone; the bald man and woman are the ones standing there. It also substantially shortens the stated distance.
- **Replacement:** The deputy chief was earnestly explaining something to a bald man and a woman standing before everyone when he spotted the bandit watching blankly from some forty yards away. His eyes widened.
- **Rationale:** 모두의 앞에 우뚝 서 있는 modifies the bald man and woman, while 십여 장 is considerably farther than a dozen yards.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그래. 장일. 나무꾼이란 말이지.”

작게 고개를 끄덕인 그놈이 허리춤에 걸린 도끼를 힐끗 바라보았다.

“도끼도 갖고 있네. 나무꾼이라서 그런 거겠지?”

“예, 예!”

“그런데 지게는?”

“……예?”

“지게. 힘들게 장작 패서 손으로 옮길 거야?”

“그러니까, 그것이.”

잠시 머뭇거리던 산적은 다급하게 말을 이었다.

“밑에! 밑에 두고 왔습니다.”
- **Current:** “N-No. I meant to say I’m a woodcutter, but…”

After a brief hesitation, the bandit hurriedly continued,

“It’s down below! I left it down below.”
- **Defect:** The draft skips the nod, axe, missing carrier frame, and questions that prompt the bandit’s answer. As written, “it” has no antecedent and the ensuing check of his story loses its cause.
- **Replacement:** “N-No. I meant to say I’m a woodcutter, but…”

“Right, Jang Il. A woodcutter.”

The young man gave a small nod and glanced at the axe hanging from the bandit’s waist.

“You’ve got an axe, too. Because you’re a woodcutter, I suppose?”

“Y-Yes!”

“Then where’s your carrier frame?”

“…What?”

“Your carrier frame. Are you planning to haul all that firewood by hand after chopping it?”

“Well, I…”

The bandit hesitated, then hurriedly added,

“Down below! I left it down below.”
- **Rationale:** The source tests his woodcutter claim in stages; the carrier-frame question is what forces his lie.

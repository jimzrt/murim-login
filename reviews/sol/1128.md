# Review

Two scene details and the origin of the spies are mistranslated; the countdown windows also need System-panel formatting.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 번뜩이는 강철의 숲이 성내를 뒤덮고, 붉은 꽃을 피워 올리며 전진한다.
- **Current:** A forest of glittering steel covered the city walls and advanced, raising red flowers as it went.
- **Defect:** 성내 means inside the city, not the city walls.
- **Replacement:** A forest of glittering steel covered the city and advanced, raising red flowers as it went.
- **Rationale:** The fighters are advancing through the city; the source does not place them on its walls.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천마(天魔)를 중심으로 한 십만마도와도 맞서 싸웠던, 흑도의 두 거인마저 마음이 흔들릴 만큼.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그들은 분명 암천의 힘을 두려워하고 있었고, 이미 오래전부터 내부에 심어져 있던 간자들은 굴복을 통한 생존을 제의했었지.
- **Current:** They were afraid of Dark Heaven’s power, and the spies they’d planted inside long ago had proposed surrendering to survive.
- **Defect:** “They’d planted” makes the two leaders the agents who installed the spies. The source identifies the spies as having been planted within their ranks, without assigning the planting to those leaders.
- **Replacement:** They were afraid of Dark Heaven’s power, and spies planted in their ranks long ago had urged them to submit in order to survive.
- **Rationale:** 내부에 심어져 있던 is passive; the spies' proposal was directed at the fearful leaders.

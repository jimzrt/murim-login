# Review

The draft contains two critical physical-action reversals, along with altered timeframe, currency, age comparison, and several localized fidelity issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 언젠가부터 끊임없이 투쟁하고 있었다.
- **Current:** I had been fighting constantly for as long as I could remember.
- **Defect:** The draft changes an unspecified point in the past into Jin’s entire remembered life.
- **Replacement:** At some point, I had begun fighting without end.
- **Rationale:** 언젠가부터 means “from some point onward,” not “for as long as I could remember.”

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 나는 한 치의 물러섬도 없이 검신을 향해 맞닿아 있던 주먹을 천천히 거두어들였다.
- **Current:** Without retreating even an inch, I slowly pulled back the fist pressed against the Sword God’s blade.
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 검신 as the title “Sword God” and invents that the blade belongs to that person.
- **Replacement:** Without retreating even an inch, I slowly drew back the fist that had been locked against the sword blade.
- **Rationale:** Here 검신 denotes the body or blade of Michael’s sword, as confirmed by the ongoing fist-versus-sword clash.

## F03 — Minor

- **Defect:** The draft specifies blades as the source of the air-splitting sounds, although the source does not identify what produced them.
- **Replacement:** And at the moment powerful killing intent and sharp, air-splitting sounds hurtled toward me—
- **Rationale:** 파공성 describes sounds produced by something tearing through the air; “blades” is an unsupported addition.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 마치 깊은 늪으로 끌려 들어가는 그 소름 끼치는 감각에 나는 반사적으로 검신을 뿌리쳤다.
- **Current:** That horrifying sensation of being dragged into a deep swamp made me reflexively lash out with my sword.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the physical action by making Jin attack with a sword instead of knocking Michael’s sword blade away.
- **Replacement:** That horrifying sensation of being dragged into a deep swamp made me reflexively knock the sword blade away.
- **Rationale:** 검신을 뿌리쳤다 means Jin shook or knocked away the sword blade with which his fist was locked; it does not say he wielded a sword.

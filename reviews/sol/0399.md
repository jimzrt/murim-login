# Review

One critical action-modifier error changes when the spear moves at extreme speed. The draft also obscures the spear-line gag, repeatedly renders modern Hunter-system mana as generic magical power, and contains several localized terminology and phrasing defects.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 치지직. 라이칸스로프의 발톱이 지면을 긁으며 창 안쪽으로 넘어왔다.
- **Current:** Scrape, scrape. The Lycanthrope’s claws scratched against the ground as it slid across the spear toward me.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the monster slide across the physical spear rather than showing it being dragged over to the narrator’s side of the spear, obscuring the setup for the claim that it has now crossed the line.
- **Replacement:** Scrape, scrape. The Lycanthrope’s claws raked the ground as I dragged it over to my side of the spear.
- **Rationale:** 창 안쪽으로 넘어왔다 means the impaled monster crosses over to the inner, narrator-facing side of the spear after the shaft is pulled.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 여기까지다. 빛살 같은 속도로 박혀 있던 창을 뽑아낸 나는, 전면을 향해 횡으로 휘둘렀다.
- **Current:** That was far enough. I pulled out the spear that had been lodged at the speed of a streak of light, then swung it horizontally toward the front.
- **Defect:** The draft attaches the lightlike speed to the spear becoming lodged, whereas the source applies it to Jin pulling the embedded spear free.
- **Replacement:** That was far enough. I yanked the embedded spear free at lightning speed, then swept it horizontally in front of me.
- **Rationale:** 빛살 같은 속도로 modifies 뽑아낸, making the extraction—not the earlier impact—the high-speed action.

# Review

Several localized fidelity defects alter the force of attacks, Jin Taekyung’s threat, the Hunters’ motivation, and the mage’s permanent injury.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 공격 마법과 디버프가 머리 위를 덮고, 온 힘을 실어 쏘아 보낸 창과 화살 따위가 소나기처럼 퍼부어졌다.
- **Current:** Attack Magic and debuffs covered the space overhead, while spears, arrows, and other weapons fired with all their strength rained down like a sudden shower.
- **Defect:** The weapons are made the agents exerting their own strength; the source says they were launched with full force.
- **Replacement:** Attack Magic and debuffs covered the space overhead, while spears, arrows, and other weapons launched with full force rained down like a sudden shower.
- **Rationale:** 온 힘을 실어 쏘아 보낸 describes the force with which the weapons were fired, not strength possessed or exerted by the weapons themselves.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 걸음과 동시에 비스듬히 내리그은 백염(白炎)의 창날로부터 초고온의 열기가 터져 나와 사방을 불태웠다.
- **Current:** As I moved, scorching heat erupted from the blade of White Flame’s spear, slashing diagonally downward and setting everything around me ablaze.
- **Defect:** White Flame is itself the spear, but the possessive construction makes the blade belong to a separate spear owned by White Flame.
- **Replacement:** As I moved, scorching heat erupted from White Flame’s spearhead as I slashed diagonally downward, setting everything around me ablaze.
- **Rationale:** 백염의 창날 means the spearhead of the weapon named White Flame.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 살살 쐈어야지. 그럼 덜 아프게 때려 줄 텐데.
- **Current:** “You should’ve shot more gently. Then I would’ve hit you less.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes reduced pain into fewer blows.
- **Replacement:** “You should’ve shot more gently. Then I would’ve made it hurt less.”
- **Rationale:** 덜 아프게 means less painfully, not fewer times.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 하지만 갈등 끝에 적으로 돌변한 로비의 헌터들은 쉬지 않고 달려들었다.
- **Current:** Yet the Hunters in the lobby, who had become enemies after struggling with their consciences, continued rushing at me without rest.
- **Defect:** The draft specifies a moral struggle with conscience, while the source only says they turned against him after wavering or internal conflict.
- **Replacement:** Yet the Hunters in the lobby, who had turned against me after wavering, continued rushing at me without rest.
- **Rationale:** 갈등 does not establish that their hesitation was specifically a matter of conscience; that added motive changes their characterization.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 탁, 쉬쉬쉭!

목을 노리고 날아드는 화살을 잡아채어 왔던 곳으로 흩뿌렸다.
- **Current:** Tap—swish-swish-swish!

I caught an arrow flying toward my throat and scattered it back toward the place it had come from.
- **Defect:** A single arrow cannot naturally be “scattered”; the source says he snatched it and sent it back toward its origin.
- **Replacement:** Tap—swish-swish-swish!

I snatched an arrow flying toward my throat and flung it back the way it had come.
- **Rationale:** 흩뿌렸다 conveys forcefully casting or spraying it back; with one arrow, “flung” preserves the action without the draft’s number mismatch.

## F06 — Minor

- **Source:** 아마도 마법사들을 이끄는 팀장이었던가. 과거 TV에서 보았던 A급 마법사가 살기 어린 눈빛으로 입을 열었다.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그런 의미에서, 지금 눈을 까뒤집고 쓰러진 이놈은 살아남는다 해도 마법사 행세하기에는 영 글렀다고 볼 수 있었다.
- **Current:** In that sense, the man now lying unconscious with his eyes rolled back had suffered an injury that left him utterly unfit to continue pretending to be a mage, even if he survived.
- **Defect:** “Pretending to be a mage” implies that he was an impostor. The source says his injury has ended his ability to function as a mage.
- **Replacement:** In that sense, even if the man now lying unconscious with his eyes rolled back survived, his days as a mage were over.
- **Rationale:** 마법사 행세하기에는 영 글렀다 idiomatically means he can no longer operate or live as a mage, not that his prior status was fraudulent.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 다시는 막아서지 마. 두 번은 없다.
- **Current:** “Don’t block my way again. There won’t be a second warning.”
- **Defect:** The draft adds an unmentioned warning and recasts the threat as warning escalation. Jin is saying there will be no second instance of the mercy he has just shown.
- **Replacement:** “Don’t ever stand in my way again. There won’t be a second time.”
- **Rationale:** 두 번은 없다 literally and contextually means there will be no second time; “warning” is an unsupported addition that weakens the lethal implication.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 삼 갑자의 공력을 실어, 지면을 박차고 솟구쳤다.
- **Current:** Then I poured three jiazi of internal energy into my body, kicked off the ground, and shot upward.
- **Defect:** The draft adds his body as the destination of the internal energy, whereas the source describes powering the leap.
- **Replacement:** Then, channeling three jiazi of internal energy, I kicked off the ground and shot upward.
- **Rationale:** 공력을 실어 modifies the powered action of kicking off and rising; it does not state that he pours the energy into his body.

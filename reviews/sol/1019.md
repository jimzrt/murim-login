# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it loses a hair-related joke, omits whose troops assembled, and alters several images and implications.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “아니, 연장자 대우부터 해 주시고 그런 말씀을 하십시오. 양심에 털 나셨습니까?”
- **Current:** “Hey, show me some respect as your senior before you say that. Do you have no conscience?”
- **Defect:** The idiom’s hair imagery disappears, breaking the setup for Taekyung’s immediate threat to pluck Mujin’s hair.
- **Replacement:** “Hey, show me some respect as your senior before you say that. What, has your conscience sprouted hair?”
- **Rationale:** 양심에 털 나다 conveys shamelessness through an image of hair growing on one’s conscience; the next line plays on that image.

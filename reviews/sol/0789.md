# Review

The draft has a reversed opening timeline and two consequential shifts in the nightmare and its aftermath.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 시간과 장소를 불문하고 눈만 감으면 기절하듯 잠들었던 내가 악몽에 시달리기 시작한 것은.
- **Current:** The nightmares began when I started falling asleep the moment I closed my eyes, no matter the time or place.
- **Defect:** The draft makes effortless sleep begin alongside the nightmares. The source says he had already been able to fall asleep anywhere, and then began suffering nightmares.
- **Replacement:** That was when I, who used to fall asleep as if I’d passed out whenever I closed my eyes, no matter where or when, began having nightmares.
- **Rationale:** 잠들었던 describes his prior habit; 시달리기 시작한 것은 is what began a few years ago.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 무수한 피와 시신을 장작 삼아 일어난 거대한 화마(火魔)가, 이 지옥도를 완성시킨다.
- **Current:** A monstrous blaze, born from countless bodies and rivers of blood, completed the hellscape.
- **Defect:** “Born from” loses the image of the fire feeding on blood and corpses as fuel.
- **Replacement:** A monstrous blaze fed on the blood and countless corpses like firewood, completing the hellscape.
- **Rationale:** 장작 삼아 specifies what fuels the blaze, following the flames’ spread across the pooled blood.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그날 국회의사당에서 벌어진 전투는 그들의 목숨과 수만, 수십 만의 희생을 맞바꾼 피의 거래였다.
- **Current:** The battle in the National Assembly had been a bloody deal: their lives in exchange for tens or hundreds of thousands of others.
- **Defect:** The draft leaves the others’ fate unstated, making it sound as though their lives were exchanged for those people rather than for averting their deaths.
- **Replacement:** The battle at the National Assembly had been a bloody bargain: their lives to prevent tens, perhaps hundreds, of thousands of others from dying.
- **Rationale:** The preceding lines say letting those people live would have caused a larger civil war; their deaths prevented its mass casualties.

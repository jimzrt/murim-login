# Review

Several lines change who acts or speaks, and one misstates Jeok Cheongang’s relationship with the Thunderbolt Saber King.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 친구는 니미럴. 한참 어린놈이 웃어른한테 못하는 말이 없네.
- **Current:** “Friend, my ass. That brat’s got no filter when he talks to his elders.”
- **Defect:** Jeok is addressing the Thunderbolt Saber King, who has just called him a friend; the draft instead sounds as though Jeok is criticizing his Disciple.
- **Replacement:** “Friend, my ass. You’ve got a lot of nerve talking to someone so much older than you like that.”
- **Rationale:** 한참 어린놈 refers to the younger man Jeok is arguing with, and 웃어른 refers to Jeok himself.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그것이 곧장 너를 찾은 이유다.
- **Current:** “That’s why I came to you right away.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Thunderbolt Saber King go to Taekyung, though he was waiting inside and had Taekyung brought to him.
- **Replacement:** “That’s why I asked for you right away.
- **Rationale:** 찾은 describes seeking Taekyung out, not physically coming to him.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 설령 그 제안을 한 이가, 수십여 년간 미운 정을 쌓아 온 끝에 죽음을 목전에 둔 벽력도왕이라 할지라도.
- **Current:** Not even if it came from the Thunderbolt Saber King, a man he’d spent decades growing to hate—and who was now on the verge of death.
- **Defect:** “Growing to hate” erases the reluctant affection in 미운 정.
- **Replacement:** Not even if it came from the Thunderbolt Saber King, a man he’d spent decades trading barbs with and grown fond of despite himself—and who was now on the verge of death.
- **Rationale:** 미운 정 describes affection formed through a long, contentious relationship, not mounting hatred.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 위급했던 상황도. 진태경이 한 사람의 무인으로서 이룩해 낸 무위도.
- **Current:** The desperate situation. The martial prowess Jin Taekyung had built up as a martial artist.
- **Defect:** The fragments omit the stated comparison between the earlier transfer and the present one.
- **Replacement:** The circumstances were different. So was the martial prowess Jin Taekyung had achieved since then.
- **Rationale:** These are the two respects in which 그 당시와 지금의 차이—the difference between then and now—is clear.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 거궐혈(巨闕穴)을 통해 맞닿은 두 사람을 바라보았다.
- **Current:** then watched the two men, joined at the Great Pouch Acupoint, with a gaze sunk deep in thought.
- **Defect:** “Great Pouch” mistranslates the named acupoint.
- **Replacement:** then watched the two men, joined at the Great Palace acupoint, with a deeply troubled gaze.
- **Rationale:** 거궐혈 names the Juque, or Great Palace, acupoint; the draft’s anatomical name is inaccurate.

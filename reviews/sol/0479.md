# Review

One System-state mistranslation and three localized wording defects.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** [기억의 파편]이 해제되었습니다!
- **Current:** **Memory Fragment** has been released!
- **Defect:** “Released” can mean newly unlocked or made available, but the Memory Fragment experience has just ended and 해제 indicates that its active state has been lifted.
- **Replacement:** **Memory Fragment** has ended!
- **Rationale:** The surrounding action returns Taekyung from the memory to reality; the notification announces that the Memory Fragment state is over, not that new content has been unlocked.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ‘고생했습니다.’
- **Current:** *You worked hard.*
- **Defect:** The literal wording sounds like praise for completing a task rather than a respectful acknowledgment of the suffering the Water God Dragon endured.
- **Replacement:** *You’ve been through a lot.*
- **Rationale:** 고생했습니다 here compassionately recognizes the dragon’s long hardship as Taekyung closes its eyes.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘대갓집 규수도 아니고, 기껏해야 일개 가기(歌妓)인데 뭘.’
- **Current:** *She isn’t the daughter of some great household. At worst, she’s only a singing courtesan.*
- **Defect:** “At worst” reverses the scalar force of 기껏해야 and makes Officer Song sound as though being a courtesan is merely the least favorable possibility rather than the lowly status he confidently assigns her.
- **Replacement:** *She isn’t the daughter of some great household. She’s just a mere singing courtesan.*
- **Rationale:** 기껏해야 일개 dismissively reduces Honglan to no more than a common singing courtesan, reflecting Officer Song’s class prejudice.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 수국(水菊)이 만개한 듯한 웃음.
- **Current:** Her smile bloomed like chrysanthemums in full flower.
- **Defect:** 수국 means hydrangea, not chrysanthemum.
- **Replacement:** Her smile was like a hydrangea in full bloom.
- **Rationale:** The draft substitutes a different flower and therefore changes the source image.

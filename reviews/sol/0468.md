# Review

One critical action error, two major characterization and hierarchy losses, and six localized humor, imagery, terminology, voice, and formatting defects.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “계속 저어! 궁둥짝에 힘 빡 주고 저으란 말이다!”
- **Current:** “Keep rowing! Put your backs into it and row!”
- **Defect:** The standard expression “put your backs into it” removes the deliberately crude reference to their butts and weakens the comic command.
- **Replacement:** “Keep rowing! Put your asses into it and row!”
- **Rationale:** 궁둥짝 explicitly means the buttocks and supplies the line’s coarse humor.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘아쉽구나. 네놈의 독심과 무재(武才)가 배 다루는 솜씨의 절반만 되었더라면 네 사형을 대신할 수 있었을 터인데.’
- **Current:** “It’s a shame. If your discernment and martial talent had been even half as good as your skill with ships, you might have taken your Senior Brother’s place.”
- **Defect:** “Discernment” mistranslates 독심, which refers to Mu Song’s fierce tenacity or grit, not his judgment.
- **Replacement:** “It’s a shame. If your grit and martial talent had been even half as good as your skill with ships, you might have taken your Senior Brother’s place.”
- **Rationale:** The Seafaring King is evaluating Mu Song’s temperament and martial aptitude; 독심 denotes hard, tenacious resolve in this context.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 자그마한 상선(商船) 한 척을 빌려 진태경 일행이 향했다는 곳으로 나아갔다.

아니, 나아가려 했다.
- **Current:** They had borrowed a small merchant vessel instead and set out for the place where Jin Taekyung’s group was said to have gone.

No, they had tried to set out.
- **Defect:** The correction changes the action from trying and failing to advance toward the destination into trying and failing to set out at all, contradicting their ongoing voyage across the lake.
- **Replacement:** They had borrowed a small merchant vessel instead and headed for the place where Jin Taekyung’s group was said to have gone.

Or rather, they had tried to.
- **Rationale:** 나아가려 했다 corrects 나아갔다: they successfully launched but could not continue advancing toward the destination.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 까마득한 높이의 그것이 좁은 폭을 틀어막은 채 상선을 향해 다가오자
- **Current:** It was impossibly tall, and as it approached the merchant vessel with its narrow column blocking the way
- **Defect:** The draft makes the waterspout’s column narrow, while the source says the towering waterspout blocks the narrow waterway.
- **Replacement:** It towered to a dizzying height, blocking the narrow waterway as it advanced toward the merchant vessel
- **Rationale:** 좁은 폭 is the confined passage the waterspout obstructs, not a description of the waterspout’s column.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 소림의 달마대사도 강을 건너기 위해서 갈대잎을 사용했지.
- **Current:** Even Bodhidharma of Shaolin used a reed leaf to cross a river.
- **Defect:** The established monastic title 대사 is omitted.
- **Replacement:** Even Master Bodhidharma of Shaolin used a reed leaf to cross a river.
- **Rationale:** 대사 is an honorific for a senior Buddhist monk and is established here as “Master.”

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 문경은 짐짓 눈살을 찌푸렸다.
- **Current:** Mungyeong deliberately furrowed his brow.
- **Defect:** “Deliberately” does not clearly preserve 짐짓, which indicates that Mungyeong is putting on or feigning the expression.

- **Rationale:** 짐짓 marks the frown as affected rather than merely intentional, a small but explicit characterization cue.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무공을 우선시했다면 차라리 무당파의 현공(玄空), 그 아이를 데려왔겠지.
- **Current:** If I had prioritized martial arts, I would have brought Hyeongong of Wudang instead.
- **Defect:** The draft omits Mungyeong’s pointed description of the venerable Hyeongong as a youngster, even though that display of extreme seniority directly causes the onlookers’ shock in the following paragraph.
- **Replacement:** If I had prioritized martial arts, I would have brought that youngster Hyeongong of Wudang instead.
- **Rationale:** 그 아이 literally treats Hyeongong as a child; preserving that hierarchy is necessary to support the immediate reaction.

## F08 — Minor

- **Current:** A movement technique both stealthy and peerless.
- **Defect:** “Peerless” means unrivaled and does not convey 표횰, which describes the technique as elusive and difficult to track.

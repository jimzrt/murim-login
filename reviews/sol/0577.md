# Review

One critical negation reversal, several relationship and epistemic distortions, and localized attribution and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 맹렬하게 몰아치는 눈보라에 송천우의 목소리가 파묻히지 않았다면, 나는 듯이 달려와 배신자를 죽이려 들었을 충복이었다.
- **Current:** If Song Cheonwoo’s voice had been swallowed by the howling blizzard, that loyal servant would have rushed over and tried to kill the traitor.
- **Defect:** The condition is reversed. The source says Butler Kim would have attacked if the blizzard had not drowned out Song Cheonwoo’s voice.
- **Replacement:** Had Song Cheonwoo’s voice not been swallowed by the howling blizzard, that loyal servant would have flown over and tried to kill the traitor.
- **Rationale:** The explicit negation in 파묻히지 않았다면 is essential to the mechanics of why the distant Butler Kim has not intervened.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 그 마음은 종전(終戰) 이후 태어난 한 생명에게 고스란히 이어졌지.
- **Current:** And that loyalty was passed down in its entirety to a life born after the war ended.
- **Defect:** “Passed down” suggests that the child inherited Hwa-jong’s loyalty. The source says Hwa-jong extended the same undiminished devotion to the child.
- **Replacement:** And he gave that same loyalty, whole and undiminished, to a life born after the war ended.
- **Rationale:** 그 마음 refers to Hwa-jong’s loyal heart, while 이어졌지 describes that devotion continuing toward Choi Minwoo rather than being inherited by him.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 모든 것이 달라진 그 날에도, 그분의 가장 충실한 심복은 네 곁에 있었다.
- **Current:** “Even on the day when everything changed, his most loyal aide was at your side.”
- **Defect:** The pronoun “his” has the young child as its nearest antecedent, obscuring the source’s explicit statement that Hwa-jong was Cheon Taemin’s most loyal aide.
- **Replacement:** “Even on the day everything changed, your grandfather’s most loyal aide was at your side.”
- **Rationale:** 그분 refers reverentially to Cheon Taemin; the relationship matters because Song is explaining how Cheon Taemin’s loyal retainer came to protect Choi Minwoo.

## F04 — Major

- **Source:** “그 와중에 지사장님은 용케 살아남으셨군요. 참으로 질긴 목숨입니다.”

최민우의 신랄한 어조에 송천우가 무력한 목소리로 대답했다.
- **Current:** “You managed to survive through all that, Director Song. What a tenacious life.”

Song Cheonwoo answered in a powerless voice, his tone cuttingly sarcastic.
- **Defect:** The narration wrongly attributes the cutting sarcasm to Song Cheonwoo rather than Choi Minwoo. It also flattens Choi’s pointed use of Song’s regional-director title.

Song Cheonwoo answered Choi Minwoo’s cutting sarcasm in a feeble voice.
- **Rationale:** 최민우의 신랄한 어조 identifies Choi’s preceding line as sarcastic, while Song answers 무력한 목소리로, in a weak or helpless voice.

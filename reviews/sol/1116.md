# Review

The draft changes the staged meeting, the decoy’s physical evidence, and several details of the reveal.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 잘려 나간 사제의 목과 피가 흐르는 검을 들고 있던 스승의 모습을 볼 수 있었다.
- **Current:** He’d seen his master holding a bloodied sword beside his Junior Brother’s severed head.
- **Defect:** The draft places the severed head beside Cheongheoja; the source says he was holding both the head and the bloody sword.
- **Replacement:** He’d seen his master holding his Junior Brother’s severed head and a sword dripping with blood.
- **Rationale:** The master’s possession of the head is part of the apparent proof of Hak Eui’s death.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “누구에게 말이냐?”

- **Defect:** Cheongheoja asks who is running out of time, not whom Hak Su is addressing.
- **Replacement:** “Who is out of time?”
- **Rationale:** The question turns Hak Su’s warning back on him; Cheongheoja repeats it explicitly in his next line.

## F04 — Major

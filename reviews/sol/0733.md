# Review

The draft contains several altered actions and facts, including a kinship error, an inaccurate casualty quantity and timing, overstated certainty about Odin Guild, and losses of humor and register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 상쾌한 목소리와 함께 자리에 앉은 나를, 커다란 식탁 맞은편의 최 팀장이 떨떠름하게 바라보았다.
- **Current:** As I sat up with a refreshed voice, Team Leader Choi, seated across the enormous dining table, looked at me with displeasure.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Taekyung taking his seat at the table into sitting up, and “with a refreshed voice” is an unnatural rendering of how he sounds.
- **Replacement:** Sounding refreshed, I took my seat, and Team Leader Choi looked at me sourly from across the enormous dining table.
- **Rationale:** 자리에 앉은 describes taking a seat, while 상쾌한 목소리 describes the quality of Taekyung’s voice.

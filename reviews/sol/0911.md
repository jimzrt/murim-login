# Review

Two passages misstate the action or its consequence; several others lose martial-technique precision.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 동천마군의 시야가 수없이 뒤집히던 그때. 적천강은 충격을 이기지 못하고 포탄처럼 쏘아지는 그를 향해 걸음을 떼었다.
- **Current:** As the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s view spun around and around amid a fierce rush of air, Jeok Cheongang stepped toward him, hurtling like a cannonball, unable to withstand the impact.
- **Defect:** The English makes Jeok Cheongang the one hurtling away from the impact.
- **Replacement:** As the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s view spun around and around amid a fierce rush of air, Jeok Cheongang stepped toward him. It was the Demon Lord who had been hurled away like a cannonball by the impact.
- **Rationale:** The source says Jeok steps toward the Demon Lord as the latter is sent flying.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 십성(十成)의 경지에 다다른, 아니 이제는 어쩌면 그것마저 넘어선 멸염신권이 공간을 새하얗게 물들였다.
- **Current:** The Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, which had reached the tenth stage—or perhaps now gone beyond even that—washed the space in white.
- **Defect:** “Tenth stage” turns ten-tenths mastery of the technique into a numbered stage.
- **Replacement:** The Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, brought to full mastery—or perhaps now beyond even that—washed the space in white.
- **Rationale:** 십성 describes the degree to which the technique has been perfected, not its tenth stage.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 공간을 격하고 쏘아진 화염신장은 다가오던 적들을 맹렬하게 휩쓸었다.
- **Current:** while Flame Divine Palms shot across the space and swept fiercely over the approaching enemies.
- **Defect:** The established technique name is changed to a plural.
- **Replacement:** while the Flame Divine Palm shot across the space and swept fiercely over the approaching enemies.
- **Rationale:** 화염신장 names the Flame Divine Palm; the source does not specify multiple palm strikes here.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 용암과도 같은 열양지기가 수십여 명의 적들을 집어삼킨다.
- **Current:** Yang Qi, hot as lava, swallowed dozens of enemies.
- **Defect:** The established term is shortened, losing its specified scorching quality.
- **Replacement:** Scorching Yang Qi, hot as lava, swallowed dozens of enemies.
- **Rationale:** 열양지기 has the binding rendering “Scorching Yang Qi.”

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 그건 적천강과 동천마군 사이에 놓인 거리였고, 그들 중 누군가의 생사(生死)를 가르기에는 너무나도 짧은 거리였다.
- **Current:** That was the distance between Jeok Cheongang and the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord. Far too short to decide which of them would live or die.
- **Defect:** The English suggests the distance prevents a decisive outcome, rather than emphasizing how close they are to a fatal one.
- **Replacement:** That was the distance between Jeok Cheongang and the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord. At such close range, one of them could be dead in an instant.
- **Rationale:** The source treats five steps as a dangerously short distance with either combatant’s life at stake.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 전장을 떨어 울리는 창룡후(蒼龍吼)와 함께, 외침에 실린 공력이 압축된 공기를 터트렸다.
- **Current:** With the azure dragon’s roar shaking the battlefield, the energy carried by his shout burst the compressed air.
- **Defect:** Generic “energy” loses the mechanism: internal energy carried in the shout bursts the compressed air.
- **Replacement:** As the azure dragon’s roar shook the battlefield, the internal energy carried in his shout burst the compressed air.
- **Rationale:** 공력 is internal energy, specifically identified as the force carried by the cry.

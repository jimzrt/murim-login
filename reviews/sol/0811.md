# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes who runs out of magical power, loses a deadpan exchange, and alters a terrain detail.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** 물론 금세 바닥을 드러낸 마력과 함께 언데드들의 추적도 아무런 소득 없이 멈췄지만
- **Current:** Of course, the undead’s pursuit had soon ground to a halt when their magical power ran out, yielding nothing.
- **Defect:** “Their” attributes the depleted magical power to the undead rather than leaving it with the Skeleton King who controls them.
- **Replacement:** Of course, the Skeleton King had soon run low on magical power, and the undead’s pursuit had ended without yielding anything.
- **Rationale:** The passage links depleted magical power to the end of the Skeleton King’s undead pursuit; it does not say the undead exhausted their own power.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “아니, 진 사마! 그런 게 어디 있습니까!”

“여기 있어.”
- **Current:** “Come on, Jin-sama! You can’t do this!”

“I can. Here I am.”
- **Defect:** Yamamoto’s rhetorical “where” and Jin’s literal “here” no longer form the source’s deadpan exchange.
- **Replacement:** “Jin-sama! Where does that even happen?”

“Right here.”
- **Rationale:** Jin answers the implied question of where such a thing happens, rather than asserting that he can do it.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 주어진 공간도 좁고, 도주로도 좁다.
- **Current:** Little room to move. Few ways to escape.
- **Defect:** The draft changes a narrow escape route into a small number of escape routes.
- **Replacement:** Little room to move. A narrow escape route.
- **Rationale:** Both the available space and the escape route are described as narrow, which matters to Jin’s assessment of the canyon.

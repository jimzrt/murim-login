# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but several lines change who is leaving, what Yamamoto reported, and how Choi is characterized.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “곧 출발할 거야. 준비해.”
- **Current:** “I’ll be leaving soon. Get ready.”
- **Defect:** The English says Jin is leaving alone, though he is telling Yamamoto to prepare to leave with him.
- **Replacement:** “We’re leaving soon. Get ready.”
- **Rationale:** The subsequent exchange establishes that the departure includes Yamamoto.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그때 놈이 했다는 그 말. 확실해?”
- **Current:** “What he said back then. Are you sure?”
- **Defect:** It omits that Jin is confirming Yamamoto’s account of what The Prophet said, rather than something Jin heard directly.
- **Replacement:** “What you said he told you back then. Are you sure?”
- **Rationale:** 했다는 marks the words as reported to Jin.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “당연히 그럴 가능성은 충분하죠. 선지자, 그 새끼가 어떤 새낀데.”
- **Current:** “Of course that’s a real possibility. The Prophet, that bastard, could be anything.”
- **Defect:** The rhetorical comment about The Prophet’s character becomes uncertainty about what he might be.
- **Replacement:** “Of course that’s a real possibility. You know what that bastard Prophet is like.”
- **Rationale:** 어떤 새낀데 explains why Jin expects a trap; it does not speculate about The Prophet’s identity.

- **Replacement:** Add the cowardly Yamamoto Genji and Team Leader Choi, and I was sure we could smash our way through whatever came.

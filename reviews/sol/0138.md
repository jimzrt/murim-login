# Review

Several fidelity issues alter actions, hierarchy, subject attribution, and character register; localized wording also weakens two physical and vocal cues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘볼래요! 보겠습니다! 보게 해 주세요!’
- **Current:** *“I have? I want to! I’ll see one! Please let me see one!”*
- **Defect:** The draft invents “I have?”, making Cheongpung appear to question whether he has already seen an imperial family member. The source contains only three eager declarations.
- **Replacement:** *“I want to! I’ll see one! Please let me see one!”*
- **Rationale:** 볼래요, 보겠습니다, and 보게 해 주세요 respectively express desire, determination, and a plea to be allowed to see one; none means “I have?”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 약속이라도 한 듯이 동시에 고개를 돌린 우리의 시선에, 뭔가를 움켜쥐고 있는 청풍이 보였다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung was standing there with something clutched in his hands.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that Cheongpung is standing, a physical action absent from the source and particularly consequential inside a speeding carriage.
- **Replacement:** There was Cheongpung, clutching something in his hands.
- **Rationale:** The source only says that their simultaneously turning gazes found Cheongpung holding something; it does not specify his posture.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “산서성 도지휘첨사(都指揮僉事), 이풍(李灃) 영감 듭시오!”
- **Current:** “Li Feng, Assistant Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province, requests entry!”
- **Defect:** The draft changes the guard’s announcement that Li Feng is entering into a request attributed to Li Feng, altering both the action and the official’s posture.

## F04 — Minor

## F09 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “그나저나 출세했네. 자네 주제에 도지휘첨사라…… 화산에서 은자깨나 뿌렸겠어. 응?”
- **Current:** “Anyway, you’ve risen in the world. An Assistant Military Commissioner, despite being who you are… You must have scattered quite a bit of silver around Huashan, hmm?”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the spending to Li Feng and places it at Huashan, obscuring Gong Ilhyuk’s accusation that Huashan bought Li Feng’s official advancement. It also renders 자네 주제에 unnaturally.
- **Replacement:** “Anyway, you’ve done well for yourself. Assistant Military Commissioner, someone like you… Huashan must have spread around quite a bit of silver for you. Hmm?”
- **Rationale:** 화산에서 functions institutionally here: Gong Ilhyuk insinuates that Huashan spent silver to secure Li Feng’s post, which is why Li Feng immediately accuses him of insulting Huashan.

# Review

The draft changes a key either-or account of the Black Ghosts’ past, turns Qi Sense’s degree of mastery into a rank, and mistakes ordinary intent for the System attribute Will. System panels also retain forbidden brackets.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정마대전에서 제 분수도 모른 채 천둥벌거숭이처럼 날뛰다가 죽거나, 진정한 하늘을 제대로 알아보지 못하고 되레 칼을 들이댔으니.
- **Current:** During the Great Faction War, they ran wild like reckless fools, never knowing their place. They died without even recognizing the true Heaven—and went so far as to raise their swords against it.
- **Defect:** The source gives two alternatives: they died after running wild, or they raised their swords against the true Heaven. The draft makes both happen to the same men.
- **Replacement:** During the Great Faction War, they either ran wild like reckless fools who didn’t know their place and got themselves killed, or failed to recognize the true Heaven and raised their swords against it.
- **Rationale:** The connective 죽거나 marks the first fate as an alternative to the second.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이건 설령 오랜 세월 동안 경륜을 쌓은 누군가가 곁에 있다 해도 알 수 없는 부분이야.
- **Current:** That’s not something even someone with many years of experience could know.
- **Defect:** Omits the pointed claim that having such an experienced person beside Taekyung would not help.
- **Replacement:** Even with someone who’s spent many years gaining experience at your side, you couldn’t have learned those names.
- **Rationale:** 곁에 있다 해도 means ‘even if [that person] were at your side,’ following the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s glance between Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang.

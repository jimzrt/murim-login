# Review

Several lines alter who acts or inherits, and the draft understates the guards’ martial rank.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 네깟놈이 상산왕 전하를 어찌 알아보느냐.
- **Current:** How would a nobody like me recognize His Highness, Prince Shangshan?
- **Defect:** The speaker recalls challenging the gambler’s claim, not doubting his own ability to recognize the prince.
- **Replacement:** How could the likes of you recognize His Highness, Prince Shangshan?
- **Rationale:** 네깟놈이 addresses the person who claimed to have seen the prince.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천륜까지 어겨가며 그 자리에 오른 핏줄을 고스란히 물려받…….
- **Current:** His bloodline inherited the throne after defying the bonds of kinship—
- **Defect:** This makes the bloodline inherit the throne; the speaker objects to the heir inheriting the Emperor’s blood.
- **Replacement:** What blessing? The child will inherit the blood of a man who broke the bonds of kinship to take the throne—
- **Rationale:** 핏줄을 물려받 refers to receiving the bloodline, while the Emperor is the one who took the throne.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그들 한 사람, 한 사람이 초일류에서 절정의 무공을 익힌 고수들.
- **Current:** Every one of them was a master of martial arts, from First Rate to Peak.
- **Defect:** First Rate lowers the stated minimum level of the guards.
- **Replacement:** Every one of them was a martial arts master at Supreme First Rate or Peak.
- **Rationale:** 초일류 is the established Supreme First Rate tier, not First Rate.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “충(忠)!”
- **Current:** “Understood!”
- **Defect:** A shouted declaration of loyalty becomes a routine acknowledgment.
- **Replacement:** “Loyalty!”
- **Rationale:** 충 is the word for loyalty, delivered here with a formal military salute.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 대업을 망치기 전에 적당한 선에서 제지하는 게 좋겠군.
- **Current:** We should stop him before he ruins the Great Nation’s great undertaking.
- **Defect:** “Stop him” drops the Emperor’s qualification that Jin should be restrained only to an appropriate degree.
- **Replacement:** We’d better rein him in before he jeopardizes the great undertaking.
- **Rationale:** 적당한 선에서 제지 specifies measured restraint, not an unqualified stop.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 죽립을 눌러쓴 열 명의 사내.
- **Current:** ten men with their heads bowed beneath bamboo hats
- **Defect:** The men have pulled their hats low; they have not bowed their heads.
- **Replacement:** ten men with bamboo hats pulled low
- **Rationale:** 눌러쓴 describes how the hats are worn.

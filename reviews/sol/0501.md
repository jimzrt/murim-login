# Review

One identity omission, one altered relationship beat, and several localized fidelity and register issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘아, 그건 그렇고 이 가에 홍천이라는 함자를 쓰시는 분을 알고 계시오?’
- **Current:** *“By the way, do you happen to know someone here named Hongcheon?”*
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 이 가에 as “here” and omits Hongcheon’s surname, Yi, changing the person’s stated identity.
- **Replacement:** *“By the way, do you happen to know a man surnamed Yi whose given name is Hongcheon?”*
- **Rationale:** 이 가 identifies the Yi family or surname, while 홍천 is explicitly presented as the person’s given name.

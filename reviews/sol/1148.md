# Review

The draft loses a recognition cue and a food-based exchange, and changes a contrast in the Skeleton King’s dialogue.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 네놈처럼 괴물 같은 인간도 있는데, 저런 놈들도 있을 수 있잖나.
- **Current:** “If monsters like you exist, why can’t there be monsters like them?”
- **Defect:** The Skeleton King calls Jin a monstrous human, not a monster. The draft erases the human-versus-monster contrast.
- **Replacement:** “If a human as monstrous as you can exist, why can’t monsters like them?”
- **Rationale:** 괴물 같은 인간 explicitly identifies Jin as human while comparing him to the monsters.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “밥맛 없는 소리를 너무 당연하게 하는 거 아닌가?”

“밥맛을 논하다니, 아주 한국인 다 됐구만. 심지어 원래 컨셉은 외국인 아니었냐?”
- **Current:** “Do you have to say something so obnoxious like it’s the most natural thing in the world?”

“Talking about obnoxious? You’ve really become Korean. Weren’t you supposed to be playing a foreigner?”
- **Defect:** Rendering 밥맛 없는 as “obnoxious” removes the reference to appetite that Jin immediately picks up for his joke.
- **Replacement:** “Do you have to say things that put me off my food like it’s nothing?”

“Food? You’ve really become Korean. Weren’t you supposed to be playing a foreigner?”
- **Rationale:** Jin’s response turns on the Skeleton King using 밥맛, literally ‘taste for food’ or appetite.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 그 누구보다 먼저 기시감을 느꼈고, 동시에 높은 허공 위에서 불현듯 드리워지는 거대한 기운의 존재를 깨달았다.
- **Current:** I sensed it before anyone else, and at the same time became aware of a tremendous presence that suddenly appeared high overhead.
- **Defect:** “Sensed it” omits Jin’s feeling of déjà vu; the awareness of the presence is a separate, simultaneous realization.
- **Replacement:** I felt a sense of déjà vu before anyone else, and at the same time became aware of a tremendous presence suddenly looming high overhead.
- **Rationale:** 기시감 is the feeling that something has happened before, while 깨달았다 separately describes his recognition of the presence.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 미국 조지아주 애틀란타 출신의 스톤 킹 씨
- **Current:** Mr. Stone King, born in Atlanta, Georgia,

# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues alter the medical mechanism, assign agency or intent absent from the source, weaken Dark Heaven’s stated role, and change Yayul Mok’s pragmatic meaning.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내장기관은 상하지 않은 곳이 없었고, 절정 고수답게 넓고 튼튼해야 할 혈도는 낡은 폐가의 담벼락처럼 허물어져 있었다.
- **Current:** Not a single internal organ remained undamaged, and the acupoints that should have been broad and sturdy in a Peak master had collapsed like the walls of a dilapidated house.
- **Defect:** Acupoints cannot be broad or collapse like walls; here 혈도 refers collectively to the body's internal qi pathways, not individual points.
- **Replacement:** Not a single internal organ remained undamaged, and the qi pathways that should have been broad and sturdy in a Peak master had collapsed like the walls of a dilapidated house.
- **Rationale:** The descriptions 넓고 튼튼해야 할 and 허물어져 있었다 characterize extended pathways capable of being broad, sturdy, and structurally ruined.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아니, 하고 싶어도 하지 못했던 거다.
- **Current:** They had wanted to speak. They simply had not been able to.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a conditional statement into a factual assertion that the men wanted to speak.
- **Replacement:** No. Even if they had wanted to speak, they couldn't.
- **Rationale:** 하고 싶어도 means “even if they wanted to,” preserving uncertainty about their desire while stressing their physical inability.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “음, 다른 놈들도 마찬가지다. 혀의 단면이 아물어 있는 것을 보아하니, 이미 오래전에 조치를 취해 놓은 모양이야.”
- **Current:** “Hmm. The others are the same. The cut surfaces on their tongues have healed, so it seems they took care of this a long time ago.”
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the mutilation to the masked men themselves, while the Korean leaves whoever took the measure unspecified.
- **Replacement:** “Hmm. The others are the same. Judging by how the severed ends of their tongues have healed, it looks like this was done a long time ago.”
- **Rationale:** 조치를 취해 놓은 모양이야 does not identify an agent; retaining the passive avoids adding responsibility that the scene has not established.

## F04 — Major

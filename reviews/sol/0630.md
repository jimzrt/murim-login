# Review

The draft contains two critical subject errors and one critical reversal of Yohi’s political ambition, along with several losses of quantity, action, romantic register, and profanity.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 지금 나와 눈이 마주친, 호랑이 가면을 쓴 칠척장신의 사내. 이쯤하고 앞으로 나오지?
- **Current:** “The tall man wearing a tiger mask who just made eye contact with me. That’s enough. Come forward.”
- **Defect:** The specific seven-cheok height used to single Taekyung out is reduced to the generic “tall.”
- **Replacement:** “The seven-foot-tall man wearing a tiger mask who just made eye contact with me. That’s enough. Come forward.”
- **Rationale:** 칠척장신 explicitly describes a man seven cheok tall, not merely someone tall.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 옆에서 쉴 새 없이 과일을 집어 먹던 이족의 대족장이 헤실헤실 웃으며 그녀의 말에 맞장구쳤다.
- **Current:** Beside her, the great chieftain of the Yi people, who had been constantly picking fruit off a nearby plate, grinned broadly and agreed with her.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that Heugung was continuously eating the fruit and adds a nearby plate absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** Beside her, the great chieftain of the Yi people, who had been popping fruit into his mouth nonstop, grinned broadly and chimed in.
- **Rationale:** 집어 먹던 describes repeatedly picking up and eating fruit; no plate is specified.

## F04 — Minor

- **Source:** 나? 나는 아무래도 상관없어. 남만야수궁이 어떤 피해를 입어도, 우리 요족이 사대부족을 아우르기를 바라지.

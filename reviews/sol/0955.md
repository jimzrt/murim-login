# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes how Taekyung traveled, blurs the nightmare’s aftermath, and misses two relationship cues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가장 신속한 길을 찾아 밤낮을 가리지 않고 쉼 없이 달려왔으니까.
- **Current:** I’d searched for the fastest route and ridden without stopping, day or night.
- **Defect:** After abandoning his horse, Taekyung is described as riding rather than running.
- **Replacement:** I’d sought the fastest route and run without rest, day or night.
- **Rationale:** 달려왔으니까 describes his continuing on foot in the context of discarding the horse.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 처참하게 죽음을 맞이한 그들의 모습이 눈앞을 어른거리는 듯했다.
- **Current:** it seemed as if the sight of them dying horribly still hovered before my eyes.
- **Defect:** The draft makes him see them dying, although his nightmare showed that they were already dead when he arrived.
- **Replacement:** the sight of them, all horribly dead, still seemed to hover before my eyes.
- **Rationale:** 죽음을 맞이한 describes people who have met their deaths; the preceding account says he found everyone dead.

## F03 — Major

- **Defect:** The draft turns a failure to achieve the hoped-for result into a claim that the outcome never changed.
- **Replacement:** I gave every moment my best, but the results still fell short.
- **Rationale:** The contrast is between doing one’s best and the results, not between effort and an unchanging outcome.

# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses one speaker’s meaning, changes a recurring torture method, and loses two pointed shifts in the exchange.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그럼 아무런 대답도 듣지 못하겠군. 난 어떤 것도 필요 없…….
- **Current:** “Then I won’t hear anything at all. I don’t need anything…”
- **Defect:** Huginn says Jin will get no answer, not that Huginn will hear nothing.
- **Replacement:** “Then you won’t get an answer. I don’t need anything…”
- **Rationale:** 듣지 못하겠군 refers to the interrogator receiving an answer; Huginn is refusing the proposed bargain.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 칼질 끝날 때마다 포션으로 샤워시켜 줄 거 아냐.
- **Current:** They’ll probably shower you with potions after every round of beatings.
- **Defect:** Beatings replace the source’s cutting with blades.
- **Replacement:** They’ll probably shower you with potions after every round of cutting.
- **Rationale:** 칼질 specifically describes cutting; the potion restores Huginn after that injury.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 혹시 지금 나한테 소리 지른 거야? 막, 막 억울해서 미치겠고 그래?
- **Current:** “Are you yelling at me right now? Are you—are you so outraged you can’t stand it?”
- **Defect:** Outrage loses Jin’s pointed suggestion that Huginn feels unfairly treated.
- **Replacement:** “Are you yelling at me right now? Do you—do you feel so wronged you can’t stand it?”
- **Rationale:** 억울해서 is about feeling wronged, which sharpens the irony of Jin scolding the prisoner he is torturing.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.83
- **Source:** 그런데 지금 당신 나이가 어떻게 되더라. 내가 알기로는 오십 언저리니까…… 아직 젊네.
- **Current:** “By the way, how old are you now? Around fifty, if I remember right… Still young.
- **Defect:** The sudden, pointed formality of 당신 disappears.
- **Replacement:** “By the way, how old are you now, sir? Around fifty, if I remember right… Still young.
- **Rationale:** After addressing Huginn bluntly, Jin switches to 당신 as he begins his mock-courteous discussion of Huginn’s long future.

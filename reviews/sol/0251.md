## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “완전히 농락당했습니다. 털끝 하나 못 건드렸어요.”
- **Current:** “I was completely toyed with. I couldn’t even touch the hem of his clothes.”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the source image of failing to touch even a single hair on his opponent with an invented reference to the hem of his clothes.
- **Replacement:** “I was completely toyed with. I couldn’t touch so much as a hair on his head.”
- **Rationale:** 털끝 하나 못 건드리다 emphasizes that Zhuge Gyun could not touch even one hair on his opponent; no clothing is mentioned.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 충분히 예상은 했지만, 역시나 무공 수련은 쥐뿔도 안 하고 실컷 돌아다니며 놀고먹은 이야기만 가득했다.
- **Current:** I had expected as much, but he really had not trained in martial arts at all. His story was filled entirely with tales of wandering around, playing, and eating to his heart’s content.
- **Defect:** The draft softens the narrator’s coarse 쥐뿔도 and uses an adversative “but” where the source confirms Taekyung’s expectation.

- **Rationale:** 역시나 means “sure enough,” while 쥐뿔도 is a deliberately crude way of saying “not a damn bit,” matching Taekyung’s irreverent narration.

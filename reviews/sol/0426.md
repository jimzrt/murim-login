# Review

The draft contains three scene-fact changes involving size, imminent death, and an invented character gender, along with several localized fidelity and voice issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 창날로부터 뛰쳐나온 거대한 화룡을 마주한 그 순간, 아크 리치는 깨달았다.
- **Current:** The instant the Arch Lich faced the enormous fire dragon that burst from the spearhead, it realized.
- **Defect:** “Realized” is left without an object or complement, producing unnatural and incomplete English.
- **Replacement:** The instant the Arch Lich faced the enormous fire dragon that burst from the spearhead, it realized the truth.
- **Rationale:** The Korean uses 깨달았다 to introduce the realization expressed by the following thought; English requires a natural complement here.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 고통도 잊은 채 굳어 버린 아크 리치를 향해, 겁화로 타오르는 화룡이 아가리를 벌렸다.
- **Current:** The fire dragon, blazing with hellfire, opened its jaws toward the Arch Lich, which had gone rigid without even feeling the pain.
- **Defect:** The draft says the Arch Lich did not feel the pain, while the source says it had forgotten or become oblivious to the pain as it froze.
- **Replacement:** The fire dragon, blazing with hellfire, opened its jaws at the Arch Lich, which had frozen in place, oblivious even to the pain.
- **Rationale:** 고통도 잊은 채 describes pain being forgotten amid the shock, not an absence of sensation.

- **Current:** The Arch Lich opened its red eye-lights wide and reached out, but no voice came from it, and the fire dragon swallowed the Gate—almost as large as itself—whole.
- **Defect:** “Almost as large” changes the stated relative size of the Gate.
- **Replacement:** The Arch Lich opened its red eye-lights wide and reached out, but no voice came from it, and the fire dragon swallowed the Gate—as large as itself—whole.

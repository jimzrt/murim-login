# Review

Seven actionable findings: one mistranslated description, one lost vulgar register, two altered intentions or plans, one incorrect mastery quantity, one omitted weapon owner, and one misleading impact sound.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “너만 약 처먹으니까 좋았냐?”

“뭐?”

“혼자 약 처먹으니까 좋았냐고.”
- **Current:** “Did you enjoy being the only one taking pills?”

“What?”

“I asked if you enjoyed taking pills all by yourself.”
- **Defect:** The neutral verb “taking” sanitizes the deliberately vulgar 처먹다 in both iterations, weakening Taekyung’s insulting register and the provocation created by the repetition.
- **Replacement:** “Was it fun being the only one shoving pills down your throat?”

“What?”

“I asked if it was fun shoving pills down your throat all by yourself.”
- **Rationale:** 처먹다 is a contemptuous, vulgar form of eating or taking something; the English should preserve that aggression without adding a separate insult.

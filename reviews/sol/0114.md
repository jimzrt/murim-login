# Review

The draft contains several scene-fact and subject reversals, including an unsupported explosive breach, a mistranslated accusation about Lee Seogwang, an added discovery of bodies, an altered bandit count, and a reversed speaker responsibility in the final exchange. It also loses some hierarchy, characterization, and register.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뻥 뚫린 입구는 마차 두 대가 지나가고도 남을 만큼 넓었지만 오십여 명의 적풍단은 아무도 발을 내딛지 못했다.
- **Current:** The entrance had been blown wide open, broad enough for two carriages to pass through side by side, yet not one of the fifty-odd mounted bandits of the Red Wind Band dared set foot inside.
- **Defect:** “Blown wide open” adds an explosive mechanism absent from the source, which only describes the entrance as gaping open.
- **Replacement:** The entrance gaped wide enough for two carriages to pass through side by side, yet not one of the fifty-odd mounted bandits of the Red Wind Band dared set foot inside.
- **Rationale:** 뻥 뚫린 describes a wide, unobstructed opening; it does not establish that an explosion created it.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 머리가 터져 죽고, 복부를 뚫려 죽고, 사지가 꺾여서 죽었다.
- **Current:** Some had died with their heads blown apart. Others had been pierced through the abdomen or had their limbs twisted out of shape.
- **Defect:** The draft changes broken limbs into limbs merely twisted out of shape, altering the stated fatal injury.

- **Rationale:** 사지가 꺾여서 죽었다 explicitly says they died after their limbs were broken.

## F05 — Critical

- **Current:** “And killing Seogwang wasn’t enough?”

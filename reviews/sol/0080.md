# Review

The draft contains one altered action and subject, several meaningful losses of mechanism, hierarchy, and register, plus a System-panel formatting error.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “네, 그럼 저희는 장비로 갈아입고 오겠습니다.”

“게이트 앞에서 기다리죠.”
- **Current:** “Yes, well, we’ll go change into our equipment.”

“I’ll see you in front of the Gate.”
- **Defect:** The first line omits that they will return after changing, while the second changes both the subject and action: Im Changsoo’s group will wait at the Gate, not merely meet them there.
- **Replacement:** “All right, then. We’ll go change into our gear and be right back.”

“We’ll wait for you in front of the Gate.”
- **Rationale:** 오겠습니다 explicitly includes returning, and 기다리죠 means “we’ll wait,” preserving the implied group subject and physical action.

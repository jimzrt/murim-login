# Review

The draft is broadly faithful but alters the chapter’s central path metaphor, changes one physical gesture, softens the deaths of innocent victims, repeatedly removes Baeksang’s startled reactions, reverses a dialogue-action sequence, and changes self-imposed prohibition into inability.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그날, 너희가 알려 주었다. 내가 어떤 길을 가야 하는지.”
- **Current:** “That day, you people showed me what I had to do.”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces Baeksang’s statement about being shown which path to take with the more compulsory and generic idea of being shown what he had to do, losing the path metaphor that structures the chapter.
- **Replacement:** “That day, you people showed me which path I should take.”
- **Rationale:** 어떤 길을 가야 하는지 explicitly refers to which path he should follow, anticipating the repeated imagery of paths, roads, and crossing a river.

# Review

Several scene facts are altered, including who reacts first, whom the Emperor addresses, and whose martial ability is being described.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 황제보다 진태경이, 그리고 진태경보다 백연의 반응이 조금 더 빨랐다.
- **Current:** The Emperor reacted first, then Jin Taekyung—and Baek Yeon was faster than either of them.
- **Defect:** Reverses the Emperor’s and Jin Taekyung’s reaction order.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung reacted a little faster than the Emperor, and Baek Yeon faster still.
- **Rationale:** The source places Jin ahead of the Emperor and Baek Yeon ahead of Jin.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “창공은 신경 쓸 것 없다. 두 사람 또한 이미 이곳으로 오고 있으니.”
- **Current:** “Don’t concern yourself with Cang Gong. The two of them are already on their way here.”
- **Defect:** Makes Cang Gong the object of concern instead of the person being told not to worry.
- **Replacement:** “Cang Gong, you needn’t concern yourself. The two of them are already on their way here.”
- **Rationale:** The Emperor is answering Cang Gong’s question about the two absent members of the imperial family.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 비록 황제라는 후광에 가려져 있으나, 그 역시 초인의 영역에 접어든 한 사람의 초절정 고수.
- **Current:** Though obscured by the Emperor’s aura, he too was a master who had stepped into the realm of the superhuman, a Supreme Peak master.
- **Defect:** Suggests an unnamed man is obscured by the Emperor’s aura; the source says the Emperor’s status overshadows his own martial ability.
- **Replacement:** His status as Emperor overshadowed it, but he too had entered the realm of the superhuman. He was a Supreme Peak master.
- **Rationale:** The Emperor is the Supreme Peak master described here, and 황제라는 후광 refers to the prestige of his title.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이제는 죽고 없는 선황의 충신들.
- **Current:** The late Emperor’s loyal subjects, now gone.
- **Defect:** Says the loyal subjects are dead, rather than the former Emperor.
- **Replacement:** The late Emperor’s loyal subjects.
- **Rationale:** 이제는 죽고 없는 modifies 선황. His surviving loyalists are among the prospective targets.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “안타깝군. 짐이 보낸 어의(御醫)의 조언을 따라 탕약을 제조했다면 더 빨리 병마를 이겨 낼 수 있었을 터인데.”
- **Current:** “That’s unfortunate. If you’d followed the advice of the imperial physician I sent and taken the prescribed decoction, you might have overcome your illness sooner.”
- **Defect:** Changes having the decoction prepared according to the physician’s advice into taking a prescribed decoction.
- **Replacement:** “That’s unfortunate. If you’d had the decoction made as the imperial physician I sent advised, you might have overcome your illness sooner.”
- **Rationale:** 탕약을 제조했다면 specifies preparing the medicine; it does not say Wei Zhong took it.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그렇다면 참으로 다행입니다. 이렇듯 관용을 베푸시는 성군이 되셨으니 만세(萬世), 만만세(萬萬歲)까지 천하를 다스리셔야 하지 않겠습니까.”
- **Current:** “Then I’m greatly relieved. Now that you’ve become a sage king who shows such mercy, you must rule over the land for ten thousand years—ten million times ten thousand years.”
- **Defect:** Inflates 만만세 by rendering it as ten million times ten thousand.
- **Replacement:** “Then I’m greatly relieved. Now that you’ve become a sage king who shows such mercy, you must rule over the land for ten thousand years—ten thousand times ten thousand years.”
- **Rationale:** 만만세 is ten thousand times ten thousand, not ten million times ten thousand.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it drops several pragmatic and comedic cues, introduces one false implication about Team Leader Choi, intensifies profanity, and contains a few unnatural or malformed renderings.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘분명히 헌터가 맞는데.’
- **Current:** *He’s definitely a Hunter.*
- **Defect:** The translation drops the trailing contrast conveyed by 는데, making the thought sound conclusive instead of hesitant in light of the man’s incongruous appearance.
- **Replacement:** *He’s definitely a Hunter, but…*
- **Rationale:** The following narration explains why Taekyung finds himself questioning the otherwise obvious conclusion that the man is a Hunter.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 체대 입시생이던 그는 스무 살 생일에 A급 헌터로 각성, 뛰어난 실력과 외모로 인생의 승리자라는 타이틀을 거머쥐었다.
- **Current:** A student preparing to enter a physical education college, he had awakened as an A-rank Hunter on his twentieth birthday. With his exceptional ability and good looks, he had earned the title of a winner at life.
- **Defect:** “A winner at life” is an unnatural literal calque that does not read as contemporary English.
- **Replacement:** While preparing for college entrance exams in physical education, he awakened as an A-rank Hunter on his twentieth birthday. With his exceptional ability and good looks, he earned a reputation as a man who had it all.
- **Rationale:** 인생의 승리자 describes someone enviably successful or fortunate in every respect; “a man who had it all” preserves that sense naturally.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 내가 시범 삼아 몇 소절을 흥얼거리자 최 팀장도 아는 척을 했다.
- **Current:** I hummed a few lines as a demonstration, and Team Leader Choi pretended to recognize the song.
- **Defect:** “Pretended” asserts that Team Leader Choi was faking recognition, whereas the context shows that the song genuinely sounded familiar to him.

- **Rationale:** Here 아는 척을 했다 means that Choi indicated recognition, as confirmed by his immediate statement that he thinks he has heard the song.

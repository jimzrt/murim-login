# Review

The draft preserves the confrontation, but changes a factional distinction, Jeok Cheongang’s reaction, two distances, and the effect of the Blood Lord’s shout.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 못한 것이 아니라, 그럴 수 없었다.
- **Current:** It wasn’t that they couldn’t. They simply couldn’t bring themselves to.
- **Defect:** The draft turns an emphatic impossibility into reluctance and contradicts itself.
- **Replacement:** It wasn’t merely that they failed to see its beauty. They couldn’t.
- **Rationale:** 그럴 수 없었다 stresses that seeing the scene as beautiful was impossible, not that the onlookers were unwilling.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정사마(正邪魔) 어디에도 몸담지 않고 오직 자신만의 길을 걸어온.
- **Current:** he belonged to neither the orthodox faction, the Demonic Path, nor Dark Heaven. He had walked only his own path.
- **Defect:** Dark Heaven replaces the unorthodox faction in a three-way distinction.
- **Replacement:** he belonged to none of the orthodox, unorthodox, or demonic factions. He had walked only his own path.
- **Rationale:** 정사마 distinguishes orthodox, unorthodox, and demonic affiliations; it does not name Dark Heaven.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그렇지 않아도 혈주를 뚫어질 듯이 응시하던 적천강의 눈동자가 불그스름하게 달아올랐다.
- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang, who had already been staring hard at the Blood Lord, watched his eyes turn reddish.
- **Defect:** The wording makes Jeok appear to watch someone’s eyes change rather than showing his own eyes reddening.
- **Replacement:** Jeok Cheongang, who had already been staring hard at the Blood Lord, had a reddish gleam in his eyes.
- **Rationale:** 적천강의 눈동자 identifies Jeok’s eyes as the ones changing.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 마치 섬광처럼 십여 장 앞까지 다가온 세 사람이 동시에 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** Then the three men—now a dozen or so yards away, moving like flashes of light—spoke at once.
- **Defect:** 십여 장 is over ten jang, not a dozen yards; the draft places the men much closer.
- **Replacement:** Then the three men—now over thirty yards away, moving like flashes of light—spoke at once.
- **Rationale:** One jang is roughly three meters, making 십여 장 more than thirty yards.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “아마도. 어차피 초면이라 할 말도 딱히 없다.”
- **Current:** “Probably. But we’ve only just met, so I don’t have much to say.”
- **Defect:** After being asked whether he has anything else to say, “Probably” suggests that he does; his explanation and Taekyung’s next line indicate that he is done.
- **Replacement:** “Probably not. We’ve only just met, so I don’t have much to say.”
- **Rationale:** 초면이라 할 말도 딱히 없다 explains why the Slaughter Saint has nothing further to add.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 일백여 장이나 떨어져 있는 성벽 위의 사람들도 숨을 삼켰다.
- **Current:** and the people on the wall a hundred yards away, all hold their breath.
- **Defect:** The wall’s distance is reduced by roughly a factor of three.
- **Replacement:** and the people on the wall over three hundred yards away, all hold their breath.
- **Rationale:** 일백여 장 means over a hundred jang, or over three hundred yards.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러나 정작, 가장 가까운 곳에서 그 기파를 온전히 느낀 세 사람만큼은 예외였다.
- **Current:** But the three men closest to him were the only ones untouched.
- **Defect:** The draft says they were untouched by the energy wave, although they felt its full force.
- **Replacement:** But the three men closest to him, who felt the full force of that wave, were the exception.
- **Rationale:** They are exceptions to the preceding reaction of holding one’s breath, not exceptions to feeling the wave.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92

- **Current:** “This time will be different. Everything else about me.”

- **Replacement:** “This time will be different. Everything else will be.”
- **Rationale:** 그 외의 모든 것이 means everything else, without limiting the contrast to Taekyung himself.

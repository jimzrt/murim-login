# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it conflates the Ten-Thousand-Li and Thousand-Li Tracking Scents, omits the western location qualifier from three references, and alters several consequential cues involving agency, register, and Taishan’s reaction.

- **Defect:** The draft calls 만리추종향 “Thousand-Li Tracking Scent,” collapsing it into the distinct 천리추종향 and making the later reference to ten thousand li internally contradictory. It also leaves Gung Gibang’s dismissive jab incomplete.

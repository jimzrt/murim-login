# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it omits one joke, mistranslates part of the Nine Yin Severed Meridians mechanism, softens a profane register beat, and contains several localized dialogue and idiom errors.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아니, 이게 뭔 소리야. 얼핏 들으면 로맨스 드라마 남주 저리 가라다.
- **Current:** What the hell was he talking about? At first glance, it sounded like something straight out of a romance drama.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the comparison that Jeok Cheongang's line could put a romance-drama male lead to shame, weakening the joke that prompts Taekyung to protect his vital areas.
- **Replacement:** What the hell was he talking about? Taken out of context, that line could put a romance-drama male lead to shame.
- **Rationale:** 남주 저리 가라 means the remark rivals or surpasses something a male lead would say, not merely that it resembles dialogue from a romance drama.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “……핵심은 맞다. 선천적으로 타고난 강한 음기가 혈맥을 틀어막고 이내 죽음에 이르게 되는 절맥증의 일종이지.”
- **Current:** “You have the gist of it. It is a type of severed-meridian illness in which an innate abundance of yin energy blocks the blood vessels and eventually leads to death.”
- **Defect:** The draft renders the Murim-specific 혈맥 as ordinary blood vessels and weakens 이내, which indicates that death follows before long rather than merely eventually.
- **Replacement:** “You have the gist of it. It is a type of severed-meridian condition in which powerful innate yin energy blocks the meridians and leads to death before long.”
- **Rationale:** The explanation concerns a meridian disorder and its lethal progression; preserving both the affected channels and the relatively prompt consequence is necessary for the mechanism.

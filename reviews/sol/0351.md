# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one reversed action, several altered quantities or mechanisms, and a few losses of register and pragmatic detail.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아마 내가 21세기 대한민국에 존재하는 수많은 20대 중 한 사람이었다면 지금쯤 반쯤 폐인이 되었을지도 모르겠다.
- **Current:** If I had been one of the countless twenty-somethings living in twenty-first-century Korea, I might have become a complete wreck by now.
- **Defect:** “Complete wreck” intensifies 반쯤 폐인 and drops the explicit degree of only being halfway to that state.
- **Replacement:** If I had been one of the countless twenty-somethings living in twenty-first-century Korea, I might have become half a wreck by now.
- **Rationale:** 반쯤 means “halfway” or “half,” not “complete.”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 운기조식을 하는 동안 곁에서 호법, 은 개뿔 쿨쿨 자고 있던 청풍이 부스스 눈을 떴다.
- **Current:** While I was circulating my qi, Cheongpung—who had been snoring away instead of standing guard beside me—blearily opened his eyes.
- **Defect:** The draft conveys the failed guard duty but omits the narrator’s profane, sarcastic interruption 은 개뿔, weakening the joke and Taekyung’s register.
- **Replacement:** While I was circulating my qi, Cheongpung—standing guard, my ass; he’d been snoring away beside me—blearily opened his eyes.
- **Rationale:** 호법, 은 개뿔 abruptly mocks the idea that Cheongpung was protecting him; the dismissive profanity is an intentional part of the narration.

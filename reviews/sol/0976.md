# Review

Several action and quantity details change in the draft; one established term is also missing.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 찰나의 순간 비좁은 협곡을 집어삼킨 그 거대한 먼지구름 사이로, 두 줄기의 바람이 불었다.
- **Current:** For one brief instant, it swallowed the narrow gorge whole. Then, from within the enormous cloud of dust, two gusts of wind blew.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the shock wave, rather than the dust cloud, engulf the gorge.
- **Replacement:** An enormous cloud of dust swallowed the narrow gorge in an instant. From within it, two gusts of wind blew.
- **Rationale:** The subject of 집어삼킨 is 그 거대한 먼지구름.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 불과 한 줌도 되지 않는 그 미량의 핏물만이 북천마군이 취할 수 있었던 모든 것이었고, 두 스승과 제자는 기회를 놓치지 않았다.
- **Current:** That tiny trickle—barely more than a handful—was all the North Heaven Demon Lord had managed to draw. The Master and Disciple didn’t let the opportunity slip.
- **Defect:** “Barely more than a handful” reverses the stated amount.
- **Replacement:** That tiny trickle—not even a handful—was all the North Heaven Demon Lord had managed to draw. The Master and Disciple didn’t let the opportunity slip.
- **Rationale:** 한 줌도 되지 않는 means less than a handful.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 새벽의 찬 공기가, 그 안에 스며든 습기가 단숨에 증발한다.
- **Current:** The cold air of dawn—and the moisture in it—vaporized in an instant.
- **Defect:** The draft says the air itself vaporizes; the source specifies the moisture in it.
- **Replacement:** The moisture in the cold dawn air evaporated in an instant.
- **Rationale:** 그 안에 스며든 습기가 identifies the moisture as what evaporates.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 더 이상 선택의 여지도, 새로운 선택지를 찾을 시간조차 없다.
- **Current:** There was no time to choose another option, much less find one.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a lack of available choices into a lack of time to choose.
- **Replacement:** There were no choices left, not even time to look for another.
- **Rationale:** The first clause rules out any remaining choice; the second rules out time to find a new one.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그리고 찰나의 고통을 감내하며 다급하게 뒷걸음질 치는 북천마군을 향해, 그를 떠나 새로운 주인을 찾은 창날이 번뜩였다.
- **Current:** And as the North Heaven Demon Lord endured that instant of pain and stumbled backward in a desperate retreat, the spearhead he had abandoned flashed toward him in search of a new master.
- **Defect:** The draft leaves the spear searching for a master, whereas the source says it has found one.
- **Replacement:** And as the North Heaven Demon Lord endured that instant of pain and stumbled backward in a desperate retreat, the spearhead that had left him for a new master flashed toward him.
- **Rationale:** 새로운 주인을 찾은 describes a completed change of wielder.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 휘몰아치는 먼지구름을 뚫고, 암벽과 허공을 밟으며 쏘아졌다.
- **Current:** He burst through the churning dust cloud, launching himself off the cliff face and into open air.

## F07 — Minor

# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it reverses one question, conflates two offices, and makes several uncertain claims more definite than the source.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당신 나 알아? 그리고 오해는 무슨. 당장 이 역적 새끼 끌고 가서 형옥에 처박아요.
- **Current:** “Do I know you? And what do you mean, a misunderstanding? Drag this traitorous bastard to the jail and throw him in.”
- **Defect:** Hong Jin asks whether the man knows him, not whether Hong Jin knows the man.
- **Replacement:** “Do you know me? And what do you mean, a misunderstanding? Drag this traitorous bastard to the jail and throw him in.”
- **Rationale:** 당신 나 알아? makes the other man the subject of ‘know,’ preserving Hong Jin’s pointed response to his familiar address.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “이미 아시잖소. 도독첨사(都督僉事) 영감.”
- **Current:** “You already know, don’t you, old Deputy Military Commissioner?”
- **Defect:** The general’s 도독첨사 is rendered as Deputy Military Commissioner, the established English title for Hong Jin’s different office, 도지휘동지.
- **Replacement:** “You already know, don’t you, old Assistant Military Commissioner?”
- **Rationale:** The two Korean office titles are distinct; using Hong Jin’s title here falsely gives the general the same office.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.82
- **Source:** “자, 잠깐! 홍 첩형(貼刑)!”

“나 첩형 아니에요. 산서성 도지휘동지로 임명된 지가 언젠데. 괜히 시끄럽게 사람 부르지 말고 먼저 가 있어요. 아, 불알 잘 닦아 놓고.”
- **Current:** “W-wait! Deputy Hong!”

“I’m not a deputy anymore. It’s been ages since I was appointed Deputy Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province. Don’t make a scene calling me over. Go on ahead. Oh, and make sure you wash your balls.”
- **Defect:** Calling Hong ‘Deputy’ makes his correction contradictory: the appointment he cites is itself rendered Deputy Military Commissioner. The man addresses him by an older penal-service title.
- **Replacement:** “W-wait! Torturer Hong!”

“I’m not a torturer anymore. It’s been ages since I was appointed Deputy Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province. Don’t make a scene calling me over. Go on ahead. Oh, and make sure you wash your balls.”
- **Rationale:** Hong rejects 첩형 before naming his current office; the English must keep those positions distinct for the exchange to work.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** ― 그래, 너였구나.

아직 해결하지 못한 의문이, 그 목소리가 새벽에 불어온 바람처럼 아스라이 귓가를 맴돌았다.

― 무신(武神)께서 말씀하셨던, 선택받은 자가.
- **Current:** The question I still hadn’t resolved drifted faintly through my mind, like a breeze blowing in the early morning.
- **Defect:** The draft drops the remembered voice and moves what lingers at his ears into his own thoughts.
- **Replacement:** The question remained unresolved, and that voice lingered faintly in my ears like a breeze at dawn.
- **Rationale:** 그 목소리가 … 귓가를 맴돌았다 describes a voice lingering at his ears, alongside the unresolved question.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘바로 그 무신이 이렇게 갑작스럽게 등장하다니.’
- **Current:** *For that very Martial God to show up so suddenly.*
- **Defect:** ‘Show up’ suggests the Martial God has appeared in person, though at this point he has only entered the conversation.
- **Replacement:** *For the Martial God himself to come up so suddenly.*
- **Rationale:** The narrator immediately says he has never seen even the Martial God’s little finger and reacts to the mention of his title.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 가장 가까이에서 무신이라는 존재를 보고 접했던 이들에게도, 그리고 나에게도.

- **Defect:** ‘Been closest’ implies a personal relationship rather than firsthand proximity and contact.
- **Replacement:** Even to those who had seen and encountered the Martial God up close—and to me.
- **Rationale:** 가장 가까이에서 … 보고 접했던 describes people who saw and encountered him at close range, without establishing intimacy.

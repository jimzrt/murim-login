# Review

The draft contains two scene-fact errors involving subject identity and action order, plus several localized fidelity and register issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 정도면 조카뻘이나 다름없다.
- **Current:** At this point, she was practically old enough to be my niece.
- **Defect:** “Old enough to be my niece” misstates the age comparison; the narrator means that their ten-year gap makes her effectively niece-aged relative to him.
- **Replacement:** With an age gap like that, she might as well have been my niece.
- **Rationale:** 조카뻘 describes someone belonging to the age or relational bracket of a niece, not someone meeting a minimum age to become one.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “은인이지, 나한테는.”
- **Current:** “She’s a Benefactor to me.”
- **Defect:** The pronoun assigns Benefactor status to Lee Seowol, but Cheol Mubaek is contradicting Taekyung’s dismissal of Hyuk Mujin and referring to Hyuk Mujin.
- **Replacement:** “He’s a Benefactor to me.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is established by the immediately preceding exchange about whether Hyuk Mujin counts as an 은인.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그가 잔잔한 눈빛으로 나와 진무경, 혁무진을 차례대로 응시했다.
- **Current:** He gazed calmly at Jin Mukyung, Hyuk Mujin, and me in turn.
- **Defect:** The sequence of Cheol Mubaek’s gaze is changed.
- **Replacement:** He gazed calmly at me, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin in turn.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly orders the three as 나, 진무경, 혁무진: Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, then Hyuk Mujin.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “안녕하십니까.”
- **Current:** “Hello.”
- **Defect:** The casual greeting loses the deliberately respectful register Taekyung adopts toward Lee Seowol as a Sect Leader.
- **Replacement:** “Greetings.”
- **Rationale:** 안녕하십니까 is a formal-polite greeting, and the following narration explicitly characterizes both greetings as 정중한.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 오늘의 이소월은 특히 아름다웠다. 눈처럼 새하얀 흰색 궁장에, 옥색 비녀로 틀어 올린 머리카락은 윤기가 흘렀다.
- **Current:** Lee Seowol was especially beautiful today. Her snow-white formal robe suited her perfectly, and her hair, twisted up with a jade hairpin, gleamed with a healthy sheen.
- **Defect:** The draft adds that the robe suited her perfectly and interprets her hair’s luster as specifically healthy, neither of which appears in the source.
- **Replacement:** Lee Seowol was especially beautiful today. She wore a snow-white formal robe, and her hair, twisted up with a jade hairpin, gleamed.
- **Rationale:** The source describes her clothing and lustrous hair without evaluating how well the robe suits her or attributing the sheen to health.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “지난번에 듣지 못한 대답, 기대할게요.”
- **Current:** “I’ll look forward to the answer you didn’t give me last time.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Seowol’s statement about an answer she did not get to hear into a direct accusation that Taekyung refused to give it.
- **Replacement:** “I’m looking forward to hearing the answer I didn’t get last time.”
- **Rationale:** 듣지 못한 keeps the line centered on what Seowol did not hear; it does not explicitly characterize Taekyung’s conduct as withholding an answer.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 내 말을 알아들었는지 이소월과 철무백이 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** Whether they understood my meaning or not, Lee Seowol and Cheol Mubaek both nodded.
- **Defect:** The draft incorrectly presents their understanding as unresolved despite the narrator inferring it from their nods.
- **Replacement:** Apparently understanding what I meant, Lee Seowol and Cheol Mubaek both nodded.
- **Rationale:** Here, 알아들었는지 introduces the narrator’s inference—roughly “perhaps because they understood”—rather than an either-way concession.

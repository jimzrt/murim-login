# Review

The draft reverses who is approaching the vanguard, changes a key description of Jin Taekyung, and misrenders two physical and hierarchical details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무림 연합군의 선봉, 바로 이곳을 향해서.
- **Current:** The vanguard of the Murim allied forces was heading straight for them.
- **Defect:** Reverses the direction of the attack. The monsters are rushing toward the vanguard, not the vanguard toward the monsters.
- **Replacement:** Straight toward the Murim allied forces’ vanguard. Straight toward where they stood.
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence identifies the approaching answers as the monstrous threat; this fragment names its target.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 공포를 느낀 말들이 거세게 투레질했다.
- **Current:** The frightened horses whinnied and tossed their heads.
- **Defect:** Changes the horses’ action from forceful snorting to whinnying and head-tossing.
- **Replacement:** The frightened horses snorted fiercely.
- **Rationale:** 투레질 refers to snorting or blowing air through the nose.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하루아침에 모든 것이 달라진, 그리고 이내 주위의 모든 것을 뒤바꾼 자신의 아우를.
- **Current:** His younger brother, who’d changed everything overnight—and then gone on to change everything around him.
- **Defect:** Makes Taekyung the agent of the first overnight change. The source says everything changed for him before he changed his surroundings.
- **Replacement:** His younger brother, whose whole life had changed overnight—and who had then changed everything around him.
- **Rationale:** 모든 것이 달라진 describes the change Taekyung experienced; 뒤바꾼 describes the change he subsequently caused.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 네 명의 십왕(十王)을 필두로 한 구파일방과 오대세가의 장문인들이
- **Current:** the Sect Leaders of the Nine Sects and One Gang and the Five Great Families plunged down like meteors, led by four of the Ten Kings.
- **Defect:** Calls the leaders of the Five Great Families Sect Leaders, incorrectly assigning them a sect hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** the leaders of the Nine Sects and One Gang and the heads of the Five Great Families plunged down like meteors, led by four of the Ten Kings.
- **Rationale:** The Five Great Families are families, not sects; the English must not give their heads sect titles.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.89
- **Source:** “조금 전에는 형님이라고 부른 것 같은데?”
- **Current:** “I thought you just called me ‘hyung.’”
- **Defect:** Drops the extra deference in 형님, which gives Wikyung a specific form of address to tease Mukyung about.
- **Replacement:** “I thought you called me ‘hyungnim’ a moment ago.”
- **Rationale:** 형님 is more deferential than 형; the distinction matters because the address itself is the subject of their exchange.

# Review

Four actionable findings: one altered time quantity, one distorted age comparison, one weakened causal emphasis, and one omitted reaction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 참혹했던 삼문혈사의 그날로부터 어언 칠 주야.
- **Current:** Nearly seven days and nights had passed since the horrific day of the Three-Sect Bloodbath.
- **Defect:** “Nearly seven” changes the stated elapsed time to less than seven days; the source says seven days and nights had already passed.
- **Replacement:** Seven days and nights had already passed since the horrific day of the Three-Sect Bloodbath.
- **Rationale:** 어언 marks the realization that the full period has already elapsed, while 칠 주야 specifies exactly seven days and nights.

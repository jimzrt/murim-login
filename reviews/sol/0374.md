# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but several passages alter characterization, casualty composition, or the timing and ambiguity of Jin Wikyung’s proposal. There are also localized terminology and humor issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 정마대전이라는 전란 속에서 숱한 전공을 쌓고, 무너졌던 가문을 더욱 단단하게 일으켜 세운 철혈의 가주이기도 했다.
- **Current:** He was a cold-blooded Family Head who had accomplished countless military feats during the Great Faction War and rebuilt his fallen family into something even stronger than before.
- **Defect:** “Cold-blooded” characterizes Tang Sadok as cruel or unfeeling, whereas 철혈 describes his iron-willed, formidable leadership.
- **Replacement:** He was also an iron-willed Family Head who had accomplished countless military feats during the Great Faction War and rebuilt his fallen family stronger than ever.
- **Rationale:** 철혈 is an approving description of stern, resolute leadership here, not an accusation of emotional coldness.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 구 할에 달하는 식솔들이 유명을 달리했고, 사천당문의 경내 대부분이 파괴되었으니까.
- **Current:** Nearly ninety percent of their family members had died, and most of the Tang Clan’s grounds had been destroyed.
- **Defect:** “Family members” narrows 식솔 to blood relatives, although the term encompasses the clan’s household members and dependents.
- **Replacement:** Nearly ninety percent of the clan’s people had died, and most of the Tang Clan’s grounds had been destroyed.
- **Rationale:** The casualty figure applies broadly to the people belonging to the clan household, not specifically to relatives.

## F03 — Major

- **Source:** 그런 연유로 드리는 말씀인데, 하남으로 가시는 것이 어떻겠습니까.
- **Current:** That is why I came to ask you this. Would you consider moving to Henan?

- **Rationale:** The source links the question to the impending war without redefining Jin Wikyung’s original purpose or revealing the proposal’s full meaning early.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그대도 알다시피 가주 대행으로서 본가의 식솔들을…….
- **Current:** As you know, as the Acting Family Head, I must remain here with the members of our family…
- **Defect:** The draft completes Tang Horyong’s unfinished thought by explicitly stating that he must remain there. The source deliberately trails off after mentioning his family’s people, allowing Jin Wikyung to finish the implication.
- **Replacement:** As you know, as the Acting Family Head, the members of our family…
- **Rationale:** Preserving the interruption is necessary for Jin Wikyung’s following response and the delayed realization of what he is proposing.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 오직 당문(唐門)이었기 때문입니다. 앞의 두 글자를 떼어 낸다 한들 그 사실은 변하지 않습니다.
- **Current:** Because it was the Tang Clan. Even if you strip away the first two characters, that fact does not change.
- **Defect:** “The first two characters” mechanically reproduces Korean orthography and is opaque in English; the intended contrast is between “Sichuan Tang Clan” and “Tang Clan.”
- **Replacement:** Because it was the Tang Clan. Even if you strip away the name “Sichuan,” that fact does not change.
- **Rationale:** 사천당문 becomes 당문 when the first two Korean characters, 사천, are removed. Naming “Sichuan” conveys the same argument naturally in English.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 구파일방과 오대세가에도 본가를 불편하게 생각하는 이들이 있지.

- **Rationale:** The source refers to strained relations created by the Tang Clan’s many enemies, without limiting the problem to suspicion.

## F07 — Minor

- **Current:** What a shame. I could have walked off with the Myriad-Poison Ring for free.

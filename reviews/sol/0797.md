# Review

Several passages alter how Siegfried could have been approached, what the Hunters actually struck, or the timing and force of the scene.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 본대 지휘부는 불과 며칠 만에 편성된 것치고는 짜임새 있는 체계와 엄청난 전력을 구축할 수 있었다.
- **Current:** For a command center assembled in only a few days, the main headquarters had managed to build an impressive structure and amass tremendous forces.
- **Defect:** Turns the main force’s command organization into a command center and makes its organizational system sound like a physical structure.
- **Replacement:** Though the main force’s command had been assembled in only a few days, it had established a well-organized system and amassed tremendous fighting strength.
- **Rationale:** 본대 지휘부 refers to the main force’s command; 짜임새 있는 체계 is its coherent organization.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 나야 친하진 않더라도 면식이 있는 상태니까 손쉽게 처치할 수 있었겠지. 하지만 지크프리트의 폐쇄적인 성향을 생각한다면, 글쎄. 거의 불가능에 가까워.
- **Current:** “I knew Siegfried, even if we weren’t close, so I could’ve taken him out easily. But given how reclusive Siegfried was… who knows? It would’ve been close to impossible.”
- **Defect:** Leaves Hagel’s easy access and the near-impossibility sounding contradictory, obscuring that the obstacle applies to someone Siegfried did not know.
- **Replacement:** “I’d met Siegfried, even if we weren’t close, so I could’ve taken him out easily. But for someone he didn’t know, given how reclusive he was? That’d be close to impossible.”
- **Rationale:** Hagel contrasts his existing acquaintance with the difficulty of approaching the reclusive Siegfried without one.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아니면…… 놈의 정체가 지크프리트와 일면식이 있는 누군가거나.
- **Current:** “The Prophet was someone Siegfried knew personally.”
- **Defect:** Implies a closer relationship than the mere prior acquaintance needed for Hagel’s explanation.
- **Replacement:** “The Prophet was someone Siegfried had met before.”
- **Rationale:** 일면식 indicates that they had met, not necessarily that they knew each other personally.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 최 팀장의 신중한 질문에 척 헤이글이 입맛을 다셨다.
- **Current:** At Team Leader Choi’s cautious question, Chuck Hagel clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** Changes a lip-smacking gesture into a tongue click, suggesting a different reaction.
- **Replacement:** At Team Leader Choi’s cautious question, Chuck Hagel smacked his lips.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다셨다 describes smacking or wetting one’s lips.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 풍압에 휩쓸려 솟아오른 모래를 베고, 소중한 동료를 죽인 적을 난도질했다.

아니, 그럴 수 있을 것이라 생각했다.
- **Current:** Caught in the pressure, the sand rose into the air and was cut apart as they hacked at the enemy who had killed their precious comrade.

Or so they thought.
- **Defect:** Says the Hunters hacked at the enemy; the source says they cut the rising sand and only thought they could cut the enemy down.
- **Replacement:** They sliced through sand thrown up by the force of their swings, certain they could hack apart the enemy who had killed their precious comrade.

Or so they thought.
- **Rationale:** The sand is what their blades actually cut; striking the enemy remains an expectation overturned by the next attack.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 수백이 일백으로, 일백이 수십으로 줄어들기까지 걸린 시간은 불과 십여 분 남짓.
- **Current:** Hundreds dwindled to a hundred, and a hundred to dozens. It took barely ten minutes.
- **Defect:** Barely ten minutes suggests ten or fewer, rather than a little over ten.
- **Replacement:** Hundreds dwindled to a hundred, and a hundred to dozens. It took only a little over ten minutes.
- **Rationale:** 십여 분 남짓 places the duration just over ten minutes.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 겁에 질려 도망칠 수조차 없을 만큼. 뽑아 든 검을 휘두를 수조차 없을 만큼.
- **Current:** A gulf so great that he couldn’t even flee in fear. So great that he couldn’t swing the sword he’d drawn.
- **Defect:** “Flee in fear” suggests flight driven by fear, whereas fear has immobilized him.
- **Replacement:** He was too terrified to run. Too terrified even to swing the sword he’d drawn.
- **Rationale:** 겁에 질려 makes terror the reason he cannot flee or use his sword.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 깊게 눌러쓴 로브 아래로 드러난 입꼬리가 부드럽게 솟구친다.
- **Current:** Beneath the hood pulled low over his face, the figure’s lips curled gently upward.
- **Defect:** Assigns the intentionally indistinct Prophet a masculine pronoun absent from this description.
- **Replacement:** Beneath the low-drawn hood, the figure’s lips curled gently upward.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the robe and visible mouth without specifying the Prophet’s gender.

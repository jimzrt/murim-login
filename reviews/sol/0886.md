# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it obscures So Gyo’s demonstrated skill, loses the consequence Taekyung fears if he tells the truth, and weakens several exchanges.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 신법도 일류 수준은 절대 아닌 것 같고……
- **Current:** “Your movement technique doesn’t seem First Rate, either… And are you good at lying, taunting people, and running off?”
- **Defect:** “Doesn’t seem First Rate” can suggest a technique below First Rate, when Taekyung has just seen her evade an attack that should have caught a First Rate martial artist.
- **Replacement:** “Your movement technique is definitely beyond First Rate… Are you good at lying, getting under people’s skin, and running off, too?”
- **Rationale:** Her effortless evasion makes him reassess her skill upward.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 곧이곧대로 진실을 밝혔다간 빼도 박도 못하는 사마외도(邪魔外道), 그중에서도 엄청난 마공을 익힌 무림공적으로 낙인찍혀도 이상하지 않을 테니까.
- **Current:** If I told the truth outright, I could be branded a demonic, heterodox martial artist without any way to defend myself—and not just any one, but a wanted criminal who’d mastered terrifying demonic martial arts.
- **Defect:** “Wanted criminal” loses the specific consequence of being branded an enemy of all Murim.
- **Replacement:** If I told the truth outright, I could be branded an irredeemable practitioner of demonic, heterodox arts—or worse, an enemy of all Murim who’d mastered terrifying demonic martial arts.
- **Rationale:** 무림공적 identifies someone treated as a public enemy by Murim, not merely a criminal wanted by unspecified authorities.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 최소한 ‘마삼보랑 어디까지 붙어먹었냐’와 같은 말이 나올 것을 대비해 표정 관리를 하고 있던 나는 맥이 탁 풀림과 동시에 의문을 느꼈다.
- **Current:** I’d been managing my expression in case she asked something like, *How far have you and Ma Sanbao gone behind my back?* Instead, all the tension drained out of me—and I was left wondering.
- **Defect:** “How far have you ... gone” sounds like a question about a sexual relationship rather than the extent of Taekyung’s covert dealings with Ma Sanbao.
- **Replacement:** I’d been keeping my expression in check, expecting a question like, *How deep are you in with Ma Sanbao?* Instead, all the tension drained out of me—and I was left wondering.
- **Rationale:** 붙어먹었냐 is a crude way to ask how closely they have joined forces in this context; the imagined question is about their association.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “보기보다, 를 잘못 말씀하신 것 같은데.”
- **Current:** “I think you meant ‘rash as you seem.’”
- **Defect:** The comeback substitutes “seem” for the contrast between his appearance and his behavior.
- **Replacement:** “I think you meant ‘more rash than I look.’”
- **Rationale:** 보기보다 means “more than I look,” setting up the repeated joke about what his appearance suggests.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “이번에는 보이는 것처럼, 을 잘못 말한 것 같은데.”
- **Current:** “This time, I think you meant ‘perceptive as you seem.’”
- **Defect:** The line loses Taekyung’s boast that he looks perceptive, and with it the reversal of their earlier exchange.
- **Replacement:** “This time, I think you meant ‘as perceptive as I look.’”
- **Rationale:** 보이는 것처럼 refers to how he looks, explicitly reversing his earlier correction.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it changes the scale of the Doppelganger’s remaining lives, obscures whose stolen reserves are draining away, and completes a thought the source leaves unfinished.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 일천을 헤아리던 수많은 생명은 어디에도 없다.
- **Current:** The countless lives it had once been able to count in the thousands were nowhere to be found.
- **Defect:** Changes roughly a thousand lives to thousands of lives.
- **Replacement:** The lives that had once numbered around a thousand were nowhere to be found.
- **Rationale:** 일천을 헤아리던 indicates a count around one thousand, not multiple thousands.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 한때 영웅으로 칭송받던 대마도사의 마나가, 기억이, 아끼고 아껴 왔던 귀중한 생명력이 썰물처럼 빠져나가는 것을.
- **Current:** The Grand Mage who had once been hailed as a hero was losing his mana, his memories, and the precious vitality he had so carefully saved, like water receding with the tide.
- **Defect:** Makes the Grand Mage the person actively losing his reserves, rather than presenting his stolen mana, memories, and vitality as draining from the Doppelganger.
- **Replacement:** The mana, memories, and carefully hoarded vitality of the Grand Mage once hailed as a hero were draining away like the tide.
- **Rationale:** The Doppelganger feels those resources leave as the soul binding breaks and its borrowed life ends.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘겨우 이런 놈한테.’
- **Current:** *And I struggled against this thing…*
- **Defect:** Supplies a specific thought about Jin’s struggle that the source deliberately leaves unsaid.
- **Replacement:** *To think, a thing like this…*
- **Rationale:** 겨우 이런 놈한테 trails off before stating what was done by or suffered at the hands of this being; Jin swallows the rest of the thought.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 반면 몸 안을 파고든 창날에서 터져 나온 불길은 어찌나 뜨겁던지.
- **Current:** And how hot the flames that burst from the spearhead and entered its body.
- **Defect:** Makes the flames enter the body; in the source, the spearhead has driven into the body and flames burst from it.
- **Replacement:** And how hot the flames bursting from the spearhead buried in its body.
- **Rationale:** 몸 안을 파고든 modifies 창날, not the flames.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 자리를 빠져나갈 수 있는 마지막 비행기의 티켓이, 지금 막 매진됐다는 것을.
- **Current:** Its last ticket out of this place had just sold out.
- **Defect:** Drops the incongruous last-flight image.
- **Replacement:** The ticket for the last flight out of here had just sold out.
- **Rationale:** 마지막 비행기의 티켓 explicitly makes this an airline-ticket metaphor.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마치 옛 동화 속에 등장하는 콩 나무처럼 순식간에 재생된 신체.
- **Current:** Its body regenerated in an instant, like the magic beansprout from an old fairy tale.
- **Defect:** Renders a towering fairy-tale beanstalk as a beansprout.
- **Replacement:** Its body regenerated in an instant, like a beanstalk shooting up in an old fairy tale.
- **Rationale:** 콩 나무 refers to the beanstalk image, not a sprout.

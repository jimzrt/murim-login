# Review

One critical factual error implies Michael died while saving people. Additional findings concern the terrorists’ role as his transactional counterpart, a weakened threat disguised as courtesy, altered reasoning, physical-action fidelity, and several localized wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 사람들은 목숨 바쳐 자신들을 구해 준 미카엘 실베르트를 사랑했고, 나 역시 그를 동경했다.
- **Current:** People loved Michael Silbert for saving them at the cost of his own life.
- **Defect:** The draft says Michael gave up his life and therefore died. The Korean means he risked or devoted his life to saving people.
- **Replacement:** People loved Michael Silbert, who had risked his life to save them.
- **Rationale:** 목숨 바쳐 describes risking or dedicating one’s life, not necessarily dying, and Michael is alive in the scene.

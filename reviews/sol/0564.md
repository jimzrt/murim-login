# Review

Several localized fidelity and register issues, including altered dialogue, softened contempt, and modern magical energy mislabeled as qi.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 다들 각성자 출신이라 그런지 작업 속도가 장난 아니다.
- **Current:** Maybe it was because they had all once been Awakened, but their work speed was no joke.
- **Defect:** “Once been Awakened” implies that they are no longer Awakened, and “but” incorrectly opposes the stated causal relationship.
- **Replacement:** Maybe it was because they were all Awakened, but they worked at an incredible pace.
- **Rationale:** The source attributes their exceptional speed to their Awakened backgrounds without suggesting that their Awakened status ended.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “왜긴, 일 시키려고 그러지. 알아들었으면 이쪽으로 F급 다섯만 붙어. E급도 한두 명 끼워서.”
- **Current:** “Why do you think? To put you to work. If you understand, send five F-ranks over here. Throw in one or two E-ranks, too.”
- **Defect:** The foreman orders five F-ranks to join the work here; he does not tell the respondents to send other people.
- **Replacement:** “Why do you think? To put you to work. If you understand, five F-ranks get over here. Add one or two E-ranks, too.”
- **Rationale:** 붙어 is a direct assignment to assemble or join the group, not an instruction to dispatch someone else.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “썩코춘이 아니라 석고준이요. 그리고 뭐, 그 자식은 어쨌든 이정룡 경호팀장이었잖아요.”

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 쓸데없이 단호한 거 뭔데.

이 방면으로는 귀신이네, 진짜.
- **Current:** *Why is he so damn adamant about this?*

He really was a demon in this department.
- **Defect:** “Demon in this department” is an unnatural literal rendering of an idiom meaning uncannily skilled, while “damn” adds profanity absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** *Why was he so needlessly adamant about that?*

When it came to this sort of thing, his instincts were downright uncanny.
- **Rationale:** 귀신이다 describes extraordinary or uncanny aptitude here, not literal demonic qualities.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “누구보다 수련이 간절한 사람이요.”
- **Current:** “Someone who wants to train more than anyone.”
- **Defect:** “Wants” weakens the person’s desperate need and emotional urgency.
- **Replacement:** “Someone more desperate to train than anyone else.”
- **Rationale:** 간절한 conveys an earnest, desperate longing, central to the injured Hunter’s characterization in this scene.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 누구보다 괜찮은 척하지만, 무참히 꺾여버린 마음으로 다시 일어서려는 그의 의지가.
- **Current:** A man pretending more than anyone else that he was fine, his will trying to rise again after his heart had been brutally broken.
- **Defect:** “Heart had been brutally broken” strongly suggests romantic heartbreak and obscures that his spirit was crushed by his defeat and injuries; the English construction also makes his will itself rise rather than expressing his determination to stand again.
- **Replacement:** A man who tried harder than anyone to pretend he was fine, yet willed himself to stand again after his spirit had been brutally crushed.
- **Rationale:** 마음 is his crushed spirit or morale in context, and 의지 is his determination to get back on his feet.

## F11 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 미세한 기의 흐름과 함께 품 안을 파고드는 자그마한 물체.
- **Current:** A small object slipped into my clothes amid a faint flow of qi.
- **Defect:** The translation identifies Johnson’s energy as Murim qi even though he is a modern mage and the source uses generic 기 rather than specifying Murim energy.
- **Replacement:** A small object slipped into my clothes on a faint current of energy.
- **Rationale:** The source leaves the energy generic; labeling it qi introduces an unsupported mechanism and conflicts with the required distinction between Murim qi and modern mana.

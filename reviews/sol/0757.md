# Review

One subject error, one omitted reaction, and one untranslated sound effect are actionable.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 셋째. 진태경이 일본을 구했고, 극진한 효자인 대니얼 다이스케의 어머니는 도쿄 출신 일본인이다.
- **Current:** Third. Jin Taekyung had saved Japan, and Daniel Daisuke’s mother—a son so devoted to his mother that he was almost excessively filial—was Japanese and from Tokyo.
- **Defect:** The appositive grammatically identifies Daniel Daisuke’s mother as “a son,” reversing the subject of the filial characterization.
- **Replacement:** Third. Jin Taekyung had saved Japan, and the exceedingly devoted Daniel Daisuke’s mother was a Japanese woman from Tokyo.
- **Rationale:** 극진한 효자인 modifies Daniel Daisuke, while his mother is identified as a Japanese woman from Tokyo.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 해상 자위대의 선원들은 자신들의 눈앞에서 모든 것이 사라졌음을 깨닫고 망연자실했다.
- **Current:** The sailors of the Maritime Self-Defense Force had stared blankly as everything disappeared right in front of their eyes.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the sailors’ explicit realization and devastated reaction with merely staring blankly during the disappearance.
- **Replacement:** The sailors of the Maritime Self-Defense Force realized that everything had vanished before their eyes and were left utterly devastated.
- **Rationale:** 깨닫고 specifies that they realized what had happened, and 망연자실했다 describes the resulting devastation and helpless shock.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 파파파팡!
- **Current:** *Papapapang!*
- **Defect:** The Korean camera-flash sound effect is transliterated rather than rendered as an English sound effect.
- **Replacement:** *Pop-pop-pop-pop!*
- **Rationale:** The source represents a rapid barrage of camera flashes; an English onomatopoeia preserves the effect without introducing an unexplained romanization.

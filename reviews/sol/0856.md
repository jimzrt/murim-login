# Review

The draft changes the elapsed time and the founder’s family relationship, and misdirects one exchange about the pace they need to maintain.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 사천당가를 떠난 직후, 칠 주야라는 시간이 눈 깜짝할 사이에 흘러갔다.
- **Current:** Eleven days passed in the blink of an eye after we left the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- **Defect:** Changes the elapsed time from seven days and nights to eleven days. The eleventh dawn occurs later in the chapter.
- **Replacement:** Seven days and nights passed in the blink of an eye after we left the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- **Rationale:** 칠 주야 means seven days and nights; 열한 번째 서광 later identifies the eleventh dawn.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.89
- **Source:** “네. 생각했던 것 이상이에요.”
- **Current:** “Yes. It’s taking longer than I expected.”
- **Defect:** Makes the reply about elapsed travel time rather than how much faster they must go.
- **Replacement:** “Yes. Faster than I thought we’d have to.”
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang has just asked whether they must increase their speed even beyond their current pace.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그러나 그로부터 긴 세월이 흐르고, 그의 피를 이은 자식 중 하나가 황위를 이어받은 후에도 바뀌는 것은 아무것도 없었다.
- **Current:** But even after a long time had passed and one of his descendants inherited the throne, nothing changed.
- **Defect:** Changes a direct child of the founding ruler into an unspecified descendant.
- **Replacement:** But even after many years had passed and one of his own children inherited the throne, nothing changed.
- **Rationale:** 그의 피를 이은 자식 중 하나 specifies one of his children, not merely a later descendant.

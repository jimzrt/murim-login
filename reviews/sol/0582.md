# Review

Several action, subject, quantity, and dialogue errors require correction.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 비명과도 같은 울음소리를 내지르며 도망치는 이백여 마리의 예티들.
- **Current:** More than two hundred yetis fled while screaming like they were being tortured.
- **Defect:** The draft adds torture imagery absent from the source, which only compares the yetis’ cries to screams.
- **Replacement:** More than two hundred yetis fled, their cries sounding like screams.
- **Rationale:** 비명과도 같은 means “like screams”; it does not imply that the yetis sounded as though they were being tortured.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이십여 쌍의 눈동자.
- **Current:** Twenty pairs of eyes.
- **Defect:** The approximate quantity is made exact, despite the group comprising Choi Minwoo, Kim Hwajong, and twenty other Hunters.
- **Replacement:** Twenty-odd pairs of eyes.
- **Rationale:** 이십여 means approximately or somewhat more than twenty, not exactly twenty.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “여기 계셨군요.”
- **Current:** “You’re here.”
- **Defect:** The draft loses the realization conveyed by 군요 and changes the retrospective recognition into a plain statement about the present.
- **Replacement:** “So you were here.”
- **Rationale:** 계셨군요 expresses Kim Hwajong’s deferential realization that the person from his memories was present in Choi Minwoo.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “으아, 으아아!”
- **Current:** “W-We’re all going to die!”
- **Defect:** A wordless cry of terror is replaced with an explicit prediction that everyone will die.
- **Replacement:** “A-Aaaah!”
- **Rationale:** The Korean contains only frightened vocalizations and supplies no statement about dying.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 만약 그들이 천운으로 눈앞의 괴수를 피해 도망친다면, 베히모스의 다음 표적은 저들이 될 것이다.
- **Current:** If they were lucky enough to escape the monster before them, they would become Behemoth’s next targets.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the fleeing group and Behemoth’s subsequent targets the same people, obscuring the causal warning that if the Hunters flee, the civilians below will be targeted.
- **Replacement:** If the Hunters escaped the monster before them through sheer luck, those people would become Behemoth’s next targets.
- **Rationale:** 그들이 refers to the Hunters considering whether they can confront or escape Behemoth, while 저들이 points back to the more than one thousand civilians scattering below.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 불쾌한 빛을 뿜어내며 자신을 향해 다가오는 인간들을 짓밟고, 씹어 삼킬 준비가 되어 있었다.
- **Current:** It was ready to trample and chew up the humans approaching it while emitting an unpleasant light.
- **Defect:** The English attaches the emitted light to Behemoth rather than to the approaching humans and also omits that Behemoth intends to swallow them.
- **Replacement:** It was ready to trample, chew, and swallow the humans approaching it while radiating that irritating light.
- **Rationale:** 불쾌한 빛을 뿜어내며 modifies the humans approaching Behemoth; 짓밟고, 씹어 삼킬 explicitly means to trample, chew, and swallow them.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** - 오. 너. 라. 인. 간. 들. 이. 여.
- **Current:** —Oh. You. Hu. Mans.
- **Defect:** The draft mistranslates the monster’s command to come as a fragmented address beginning with “Oh, you,” removing the summons entirely.
- **Replacement:** —Come. Hu. Mans.
- **Rationale:** 오너라 is an imperative meaning “come,” followed by 인간들이여, a vocative address to the humans.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “여긴 끝났습니다.”
- **Current:** “It’s over here.”
- **Defect:** The English can only naturally mean that something is located nearby, rather than that the fighting in this area is finished.
- **Replacement:** “We’re done here.”
- **Rationale:** 여긴 끝났습니다 states that this location has been cleared and the work here is over.

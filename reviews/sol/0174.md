# Review

Several fidelity issues alter kinship, quantity, physical destruction, speaker address, and register; the draft also inconsistently formats direct thoughts.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “천륜(天倫)을 거스르고 화양검을 죽이다니. 네놈이 진정 사람이더냐?”
- **Current:** “You defied the bonds of heaven and killed the Blade of Flowers. Are you truly human?”
- **Defect:** “Bonds of heaven” obscures that 천륜 specifically invokes the sacred bonds and duties of blood kin, which is the basis of Jeok Cheongang’s outrage.
- **Replacement:** “You violated the sacred bonds of kinship and killed the Blade of Flowers. Are you truly human?”
- **Rationale:** Jin Baekyang was Taekyung’s great-uncle, and 천륜 frames the killing as an offense against their familial relationship rather than an abstract violation of heaven.

## F02 — Major

# Review

The draft contains two scene-fact errors involving personnel count and the cat’s actions, plus several meaningful shifts in operational implication, register, and terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 귀중한 패밀리어 마법사를 경호하기 위해 남아 있던 한 사람이다.
- **Current:** He was one of the people who had stayed behind to protect their valuable Familiar mage.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the single colleague who remained behind into one member of an unspecified group, altering the surveillance team’s deployment.
- **Replacement:** He was the one person who had stayed behind to protect their valuable Familiar mage.
- **Rationale:** 한 사람 identifies one person who remained with Junsu, not one of multiple people who stayed behind.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 유일한 사건이라면 기다림에 지쳐 야옹거리며 울다가 경비 아저씨한테 쫓겨날 뻔했던 것뿐이다.
- **Current:** The only incident had been him getting exhausted from waiting, meowing and crying until a security guard almost chased him away.
- **Defect:** The pronoun makes the target the one who became exhausted, meowed, and was nearly chased away; those actions belong to the cat.
- **Replacement:** The only incident was when the cat, exhausted from waiting, started meowing and was nearly chased away by a security guard.
- **Rationale:** The surrounding passage establishes that the newly acquired cat was stationed at the apartment entrance and was the one that cried from boredom.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 팀장 반응 봐서는 그쪽에서 뭐 하나 건진 거 같긴 한데 잘은 모르겠고.
- **Current:** Judging by the Team Leader’s reaction, it seems like they got something out of them, but I’m not sure what.
- **Defect:** “Got something out of them” implies that the team extracted information or cooperation from the two targets, while the source only says the operation on that side turned up something.

- **Rationale:** 그쪽에서 means “over there/on that end,” and 뭐 하나 건진 indicates obtaining some useful result without specifying that it came directly from the people under surveillance.

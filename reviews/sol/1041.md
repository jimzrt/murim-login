# Review

Two localized fidelity issues: the draft narrows the distance between Jin and the Grand Mage and omits the pressure of the binding roots.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 아니, 정확히는 어느덧 코앞까지 다가온 나와 단둘이.
- **Current:** No—more precisely, she and I, now close enough to touch.
- **Defect:** “Close enough to touch” makes the distance shorter than the source establishes.
- **Replacement:** No—more precisely, it was just her and me, now almost face-to-face.
- **Rationale:** 코앞까지 다가온 conveys that Jin has come right up to her, but the passage later specifies that nearly one jang still separates them.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 사지 는 물론 허리 전체와 목을 칭칭 휘감은 그것으로부터 전해지는 압박감에, 나는 손을 뻗어 그대로 잡아 뜯었다.
- **Current:** With them wrapped tight around my limbs, waist, and neck, I reached out and tore them apart.

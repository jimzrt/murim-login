# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes who could have sought treatment, multiplies a distinguishing scar, and alters several meaningful lines of dialogue and imagery.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그들의 의술로 치료할 수 있는 병이었다면 진즉 손을 썼을 거야.
- **Current:** If their medicine could’ve treated this, they would’ve done something long ago.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the physicians responsible for failing to act, although the Emperor says he would have taken action; he has kept the illness from them.
- **Replacement:** If their medicine could’ve treated this, I would’ve sought their help long ago.
- **Rationale:** The following sentence explicitly says the Emperor hid his condition even from the imperial physicians.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 새로 생긴 흉터로 드디어 얼굴을 구분할 수 있게 된 쌍둥이 무장이 나를 기다리고 있었다.
- **Current:** Thanks to the new scars that had finally made their faces distinguishable, I could tell them apart now.
- **Defect:** Plural “scars” changes the single new scar used to distinguish the twins.
- **Replacement:** A new scar finally let me tell their faces apart.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies 흉터 in the singular as the distinguishing mark.

## F03 — Major

- **Replacement:** “Well. I don’t see why you need to know.”
- **Rationale:** 글쎄 is an evasive preface here; the Emperor subsequently gives the timing.

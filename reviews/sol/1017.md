# Review

Several lines alter the nature of the dispute, the timing of the meeting, or a speaker’s register; others lose pointed characterization.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 남의 구역에서 감 내놔라, 배 내놔라 하지 마라.
- **Current:** Don’t go telling people how to run their own territory.
- **Defect:** Changes a complaint about making demands in someone else’s territory into one about directing its governance.
- **Replacement:** Don’t go demanding this and that in someone else’s territory.
- **Rationale:** 감 내놔라, 배 내놔라 evokes demanding one thing after another.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 물론, 또 눈썹 하나 까딱하지 않은 채 이 모든 광경을 지켜보던 누군가는 아니었지만.
- **Current:** Of course, there was one person who kept watching the whole scene without so much as twitching an eyebrow.
- **Defect:** Omits the contrast with the preceding sentence: unlike the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord, this person did not back down.
- **Replacement:** But not the one who had watched it all without so much as twitching an eyebrow.
- **Rationale:** 아니었지만 makes this watcher the exception to having to retreat.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 만년설(萬年雪)이 끼어 있는 마당에 모기는 무슨 얼어 죽을.
- **Current:** In a courtyard surrounded by perpetual snow, what mosquito was going to freeze to death?
- **Defect:** Treats 마당에 as a physical courtyard and 얼어 죽을 as a claim that a mosquito would freeze, losing the narrator’s dismissive punchline.
- **Replacement:** With perennial snow on the ground, what damn mosquito?
- **Rationale:** 마당에 means ‘given the circumstances’ here; 얼어 죽을 emphatically dismisses the idea of a mosquito.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94

- **Current:** “With you here beside us, what could an army of a thousand matter to us?”

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Critical

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Current:** “Of course. I suddenly needed to take a piss, so I didn’t hear him out.”

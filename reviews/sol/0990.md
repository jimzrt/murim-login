# Review

Several lines change the meaning of the death, the boy’s correction, or Cheongpung’s reason for leaving; the draft also misses a speech-level joke and leaves a footnote unreferenced.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “아무리 그래도 그렇지. 설마하니 도왕(刀王)께서 이처럼 덧없이 떠나실 줄이야.”

“덧없이? 그런 헛소리는 내 앞에서 두 번 다시 꺼내지 말게. 팽 대협께서는 마지막까지 의기(義氣)를 잃지 않으셨어.”
- **Current:** “Even so, who could’ve imagined the Saber King would leave us so suddenly?”

“Suddenly? Don’t ever say something so foolish in front of me again. Great Hero Peng never lost his sense of justice, right up to the end.”
- **Defect:** “Suddenly” makes the objection about timing rather than the suggestion that Peng’s death was futile.
- **Replacement:** “Even so, who could’ve imagined the Saber King would meet such a pointless end?”

“Pointless? Don’t ever say that in front of me again. Great Hero Peng held fast to his chivalrous spirit until the very end.”
- **Rationale:** The second speaker objects to 덧없이 because Peng retained 의기 to the end.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “안 속아……요.”
- **Current:** “I’m not falling for—”
- **Defect:** The line drops the belated polite ending, losing the joke of the boy catching himself speaking casually.
- **Replacement:** “I’m not falling for… that, sir.”
- **Rationale:** The boy starts with informal 안 속아, then adds 요; the interruption reflects his attempted return to polite speech.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “그분이야.”

“뭐?”

“그분. 그 사람이 아니라, 그분이라고.”
- **Current:** “He’s…”

“What?”

“He. Not ‘that person’—he.”
- **Defect:** English “he” does not convey the honorific distinction the boy insists on.
- **Replacement:** “Show him some respect.”

“What?”

“Don’t call him ‘that person.’ Speak of him with respect.”
- **Rationale:** The boy corrects 그 사람 to the respectful 그분, revealing his reverence for his idol.

- **Source:** - 빙당호로가 떨어졌어요.
- **Current:** —The candied hawthorn skewers fell.
- **Defect:** “Fell” turns running out of food into a physical accident and changes Cheongpung’s reason for leaving. The existing footnote also has no reference.
- **Replacement:** —I ran out of candied hawthorn skewers[^1].

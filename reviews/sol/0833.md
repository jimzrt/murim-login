# Review

Several localized fidelity errors affect viewpoint, the ritual’s mechanics, and Hyuk Mujin’s boast.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 몽롱하다.
- **Current:** I felt dazed.
- **Defect:** Introduces first-person narration before the source identifies Jin Taekyung in third person.
- **Replacement:** Dazed.
- **Rationale:** The opening is a subjectless impression; the explicit first-person viewpoint begins after the nightmare.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 끝없이. 모든 기운을 쏟아붓고 잿가루가 되어 사라질 때까지.
- **Current:** On and on. Until every last bit of energy had been poured out and turned to ash.
- **Defect:** Makes the energy, rather than the Magic Gems, appear to turn to ash.
- **Replacement:** On and on. Until the Magic Gems had poured out all their energy and crumbled to ash.
- **Rationale:** The next lines show the spent Magic Gems crumbling and scattering.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 그리고 조금 전 보였던 안광이 무색할 만큼, 어느덧 새카맣게 물든 눈동자로 새로운 세상을 눈에 담았다.
- **Current:** Then, making the red glow he’d shown just moments ago seem dull by comparison, his eyes turned pitch-black. He took in the new world with his gaze, looking around and sensing everything.
- **Defect:** “Dull by comparison” suggests the black eyes outshine the red glow, rather than replacing its striking appearance.
- **Replacement:** Then, with the red glint from moments ago all but forgotten, he took in the new world through eyes that had turned pitch-black. He looked around and sensed everything.
- **Rationale:** The contrast is between his earlier red gaze and his now-black eyes.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 외마디 신음과 함께 눈을 깜빡였다.
- **Current:** I blinked at the single groan.
- **Defect:** Makes the groan sound like something he reacts to rather than something he utters.
- **Replacement:** I let out a short groan and blinked.
- **Rationale:** The groan and blink are his simultaneous actions.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금 중원 무림은 누구 중심으로 돌아가는 것이냐, 우리 조장님. 대태원진가의 열화신룡을 중심으로 돌아가게 되어 있다고.
- **Current:** Now, who does the Central Plains martial world revolve around, Captain? It revolves around our Captain, the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon of the great Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- **Defect:** Treats “Captain” as a vocative addressed to the listener, when Hyuk Mujin is naming his absent captain as the answer.
- **Replacement:** Now, who does the Central Plains martial world revolve around? Our Captain. The Blazing Flame Divine Dragon of the great Jin Family of Taiyuan—that’s who.
- **Rationale:** 우리 조장님 answers Mujin’s rhetorical question; it does not address the person hearing his boast.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 나는 조장님의 보좌를 딱 잡고 살기 때문에! 가장 신뢰하는 수하이기 때문에 딱 잡고 있어요.
- **Current:** Because I’ve got a firm grip on the Captain’s support! Because I’m his most trusted subordinate!
- **Defect:** Reverses the support relationship: Mujin claims a secure place assisting the Captain, not that he controls the Captain’s support for him.
- **Replacement:** Because I’ve got my place at the Captain’s side locked down! I’m his most trusted subordinate—that’s why my place is secure.
- **Rationale:** 조장님의 보좌 refers to Mujin’s position as the Captain’s aide; his repeated 딱 잡고 sets up the listener’s question.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 조장님. 꼼짝 마! 열화신룡 까불면 나한테 죽어!
- **Current:** Captain! Don’t move! If the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon gets fresh with me, I’ll kill him!
- **Defect:** Changes Mujin’s mock direct threat to his captain into a conditional statement about a third party; “gets fresh with me” also introduces a sexual implication absent from 까불면.
- **Replacement:** Captain, don’t move! Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, mess with me and you’re dead!
- **Rationale:** Mujin is acting out how boldly he supposedly speaks to Taekyung, using both addresses for the same person.

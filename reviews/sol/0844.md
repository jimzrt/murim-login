# Review

Two scene facts are changed; two passages need localized correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 청풍과는 달리 태생부터 버러지였던 놈이 지렁이가 되더니
- **Current:** Unlike Cheongpung, who had been born a bug, he became an earthworm
- **Defect:** The draft makes Cheongpung the one born a bug. The source says Jin Taekyung was born a bug, unlike Cheongpung.
- **Replacement:** Unlike Cheongpung, Jin Taekyung had been born a bug. Then he became an earthworm
- **Rationale:** 태생부터 버러지였던 놈 refers to Jin Taekyung.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 불과 한 달 전에는 한 성(城)을 뒤흔들었던 적도 있었다.

아니, 정확히는 뒤흔들 ‘뻔’했었다.
- **Current:** Just a month ago, one had even shaken an entire province.

No—more precisely, it had *almost* shaken one.
- **Defect:** 성(城) is a city here, not a province; the draft changes the scale of the near-disaster.
- **Replacement:** Just a month ago, one had even shaken an entire city.

No—more precisely, it had *almost* shaken one.
- **Rationale:** The 城 character specifies a city, and the following sentence retracts the claim that it was actually shaken.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 사마외도(邪魔外道)의 무공도 예외는 아니었다.
- **Current:** Even the martial arts of demonic, heterodox arts, which were mastered far faster than orthodox techniques, were no exception.
- **Defect:** “Martial arts of demonic, heterodox arts” is redundant and ungrammatical.
- **Replacement:** Even demonic, heterodox martial arts, which could be mastered far faster than orthodox techniques, were no exception.
- **Rationale:** 사마외도의 무공 refers to heterodox martial arts themselves.

## F04 — Minor

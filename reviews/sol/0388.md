# Review

Several subject reversals and meaning changes require correction, along with lost wordplay, an internal-arts distinction, and System-panel formatting.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대 자로 뻗어 있던 우헤이싱이 끓어오르는 음성을 내뱉은 것은 그로부터 십여 분이 흐른 뒤였다.
- **Current:** It was more than ten minutes later when Wu Heixing, who had been sprawled out flat, finally forced out a boiling voice.
- **Defect:** “Sprawled out flat” omits the spread-eagled posture conveyed by 대 자로, while “forced out a boiling voice” is an unnatural English collocation.
- **Replacement:** It was a little over ten minutes later that Wu Heixing, still sprawled spread-eagled, finally growled in a seething voice.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly describes his limbs forming the character 大 and his voice seething with anger.

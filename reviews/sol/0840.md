# Review

Several action and quantity errors, plus losses of characterization and comic meaning.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 지목당한 늙은 청년, 남호가 똥 씹은 얼굴로 대꾸했다.
- **Current:** Namho, the old man in question, replied with a sour look.
- **Defect:** Drops the narrator’s deliberately contradictory description of Namho as an old young man.
- **Replacement:** Namho, the old young man singled out, answered with a face like he’d bitten into something foul.
- **Rationale:** 늙은 청년 preserves the joke that he is counted among the Pavilion’s young members despite his age.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아니, 이럴 거면 처음부터 말이라도 하지 말든가.
- **Current:** I mean, why not say that from the start?
- **Defect:** Reverses the complaint: Jin wishes Taishan had not spoken up at all.
- **Replacement:** I mean, if he was going to do that, why say anything in the first place?
- **Rationale:** 처음부터 말이라도 하지 말든가 means he should have kept quiet from the outset, not said it sooner.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 염치가 없어 몇 번이나 돌아가도 좋다고 했지만…… 그럼에도 끝까지 본가에 남아 있네.
- **Current:** I’ve told them more than once that they could go home, though I had no right to ask. But they’ve stayed with the family to the very end.
- **Defect:** “I had no right to ask” makes his offer of departure sound like an improper request, obscuring his embarrassment at continuing to rely on them.
- **Replacement:** I felt ashamed to keep relying on them, so I told them more than once that they were free to go… Yet they’ve stayed with our family all this time.
- **Rationale:** 염치가 없어 expresses Tang Sadok’s sense that imposing on their generosity is shameless; he repeatedly gives them leave to return.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그때만 해도 진짜…… 한 성격 하셨었는데.
- **Current:** Back then, you were really…quite a character.
- **Defect:** Softens Jin’s pointed reference to Tang Sadok’s difficult temper into a vague description.
- **Replacement:** Back then, you really…had quite a temper.
- **Rationale:** 한 성격 하다 refers to having a strong or difficult temper, which is the contrast driving Jin’s surprise at Tang Sadok’s change.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “노, 아니 스승님께서 후원에 머무르고 계십니까?”
- **Current:** “Is the…no, is my Master staying in the Rear Court?”
- **Defect:** Omits Jin’s aborted familiar address before he corrects himself to “Master.”
- **Replacement:** “Is the old—no, is my Master staying in the Rear Court?”
- **Rationale:** 노, 아니 스승님 preserves the audible self-correction and its shift in address.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 백여 장도 넘게 떨어져 있음에도, 천둥처럼 울려 퍼지는 목소리가 귓가를 파고든 것은.
- **Current:** And despite being over a hundred yards away, a voice boomed like thunder and reached my ears.
- **Defect:** Changes the distance by treating 장 as a yard.
- **Replacement:** And though I was more than three hundred yards away, a voice boomed like thunder and pierced my ears.
- **Rationale:** A 장 is roughly 3.3 meters; 백여 장 is more than three hundred yards, not merely a hundred.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** ‘추왕(醜王) 적천강……!’
- **Current:** *The Ugly King, Jeok Cheongang…!*
- **Defect:** “Ugly King” suggests physical appearance, while the coined title mocks Jeok Cheongang’s disgraceful display.
- **Replacement:** *The Disgrace King, Jeok Cheongang…!*
- **Rationale:** The preceding 추하다 describes the embarrassing spectacle of the argument; the new epithet extends that judgment.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 나를 위해 여기까지 와서 신의를 땅콩처럼 달달 볶고 있는 모습에 감동해야 하는데 그게 참 쉽지가 않다.
- **Current:** I should’ve been moved that he’d come all this way for me, only to nag the Divine Physician like he was a peanut he could endlessly crack open.
- **Defect:** Invents cracking open a peanut, replacing the image of relentlessly roasting or badgering someone.
- **Replacement:** I should’ve been moved that he’d come all this way for me and was roasting the Divine Physician like a peanut over a fire. But it wasn’t easy.
- **Rationale:** 땅콩처럼 달달 볶다 evokes persistent roasting and harrying, not cracking a peanut open.

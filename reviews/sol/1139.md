# Review

Four actionable fidelity and joke-logic defects.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 무공을 익힐수록 강시화가 진행된다는 점에서 독이 든 성배나 다름없었지만, 천자는 그것을 기꺼이 받아마신 모양이다.

성배는 성배니까.
- **Current:** The more one practiced it, the more one became a jiangshi. It was no better than a poisoned chalice, but the Son of Heaven seemed to have drunk from it willingly.

A chalice was still a chalice, after all.
- **Defect:** “Chalice” drops the Holy Grail image that makes the punchline work: the offered salvation is poisoned, but it is still salvation.
- **Replacement:** The more one practiced it, the more one became a jiangshi. It was no better than a poisoned Holy Grail, but the Son of Heaven seemed to have drunk from it willingly.

A Holy Grail was still a Holy Grail, after all.
- **Rationale:** 성배 retains its sacred, coveted sense in both sentences.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 떠난 이들을 위해서라도.
- **Current:** Not for the sake of those who are gone.
- **Defect:** “Not for” reverses Taekyung’s reason for asking Mae not to apologize.
- **Replacement:** If only for the sake of those who are gone.
- **Rationale:** 위해서라도 makes the dead an additional reason not to apologize, not an exception to that reason.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 전투에 도움이 되면 뭐하느냐. 저놈이 입을 열 때마다 주화입마가 올 것 같은데.
- **Current:** “What good is he in battle? Every time that man opens his mouth, I feel like I’m about to suffer qi deviation.”
- **Defect:** Jeok concedes that the man helps in battle; the draft instead questions whether he does.
- **Replacement:** “So what if he’s useful in battle? Every time that man opens his mouth, I feel like I’m about to suffer qi deviation.”
- **Rationale:** 전투에 도움이 되면 뭐하느냐 dismisses the value of an acknowledged benefit.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 전투 때는 홍길동이라도 되는지 동에 번쩍 서에 번쩍하면서 상당히 많은 아군을 살렸다는데, 전투 이후에는 그냥 길똥이가 되어 버렸다.
- **Current:** They said he’d been darting all over the battlefield like Hong Gil-dong, saving a whole lot of our people. But after the battle, he’d become just Gil-dong.
- **Defect:** “Gil-dong” misses the 길똥 wordplay that turns the hero’s name into “dung” and sets up the next sentence’s pile-of-shit image.
- **Replacement:** They said he’d been darting all over the battlefield like Hong Gil-dong, saving a whole lot of our people. But after the battle, he’d become plain old Gil-dung.
- **Rationale:** 길똥 substitutes 똥 (“dung”) into 길동; the draft leaves the substitution untranslated.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제가 뭘요. 스승님이 예전부터 그렇게 가르치셨잖아요. 염라대왕 앞으로 끌려가도 할 말은 다 하고 살라고.
- **Current:** “What did I do? You’re the one who taught me to always speak my mind—even if I’m dragged before Yama.”
- **Defect:** Omitting Taekyung’s direct “Master” removes the address that prompts Jeok’s pleased reaction and his ensuing comment on the title.
- **Replacement:** “What did I do? You’ve always taught me, Master, to speak my mind—even if I’m dragged before Yama.”
- **Rationale:** 스승님 is spoken here; Jeok immediately says it sounds nice and then considers whether the title is too stiff.

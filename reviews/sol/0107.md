# Review

Two time quantities are halved, Wolhwa’s hierarchy is initially omitted, and several localized choices alter physical detail, social category, register, or martial terminology.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하오문 산서지부장인 월화가 데려온 사람이다.
- **Current:** He was someone Wolhwa from the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi Branch had brought with her.
- **Defect:** Wolhwa’s position as Shanxi Branch Leader is omitted, flattening the hierarchy that explains why the coachman she supplied cannot be ordinary.
- **Replacement:** He was someone Wolhwa, the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi Branch Leader, had brought with her.
- **Rationale:** 산서지부장 explicitly identifies Wolhwa as the Shanxi Branch Leader, not merely someone affiliated with that branch.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어지간한 방 하나 크기라 좌석도 넓었다.
- **Current:** The inside was about the size of an ordinary room, with plenty of space between the seats.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the physical detail from the seats themselves being spacious to there being ample space between them.
- **Replacement:** The inside was about the size of an ordinary room, and the seats were spacious too.
- **Rationale:** 좌석도 넓었다 describes the seats as roomy; it does not specifically describe the gaps between them.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 마차가 멈춘 것은 그로부터 두 시진이 지난 후, 자정 무렵이었다.
- **Current:** The carriage stopped two hours after that, around midnight.
- **Defect:** The elapsed time is halved: two 시진 equal four modern hours, not two.
- **Replacement:** The carriage stopped four hours later, around midnight.
- **Rationale:** A 시진 is a traditional double hour, so 두 시진 denotes approximately four hours.

## F04 — Critical

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Major

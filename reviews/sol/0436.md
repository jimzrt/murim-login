# Review

Two scene actions are mistranslated, including the speaker in the ship dispute; several pragmatic, causal, and register details are also lost or softened.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “회의? 어떤 정신 나간 놈들이 이따위 일정을 짰어? 호북까지 가는 데에만 아무리 빨라도 칠 주야는 걸리겠다!”
- **Current:** “A meeting? What kind of lunatics came up with this schedule? It’ll take at least seven days just to reach Hubei!”
- **Defect:** The translation drops 주야, which explicitly describes seven complete days and nights.
- **Replacement:** “A meeting? What kind of lunatics came up with this schedule? Even at the fastest pace, it’ll take seven days and nights just to reach Hubei!”
- **Rationale:** 아무리 빨라도 칠 주야 specifies the minimum travel time as seven day-and-night cycles.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 한 줄 요약하자면, 배 째라는 소리다.
- **Current:** In short, he was telling them to screw themselves.
- **Defect:** 배 째라 is a defiant refusal meaning “do your worst,” not an instruction for the planners to screw themselves; the current version redirects the hostility and alters the pragmatic force.
- **Replacement:** In short, he was telling them to do their worst.
- **Rationale:** The idiom expresses Jeok Cheongang’s willingness to ignore any consequences rather than a direct insult aimed at a particular group.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “대가리에 피도 안 마른 놈이.
- **Current:** “You haven’t even dried the blood on your head yet.
- **Defect:** The Korean idiom is rendered as an unnatural literal image instead of its established English equivalent, and 놈’s insulting address is weakened.
- **Replacement:** “You little punk, you’re still wet behind the ears.
- **Rationale:** 대가리에 피도 안 마르다 describes someone as extremely young and inexperienced, equivalent to “wet behind the ears.”

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그러기 위해서는 무림과 현대를 오가며 처리해야 할 일들이 많았다.
- **Current:** To do that, I had plenty of things to deal with in both Murim and the modern world.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that Taekyung must go back and forth between the two worlds, a mechanical detail supporting why overland travel would leave him less time.
- **Replacement:** To do that, I had a lot to take care of while going back and forth between Murim and the modern world.
- **Rationale:** 오가며 explicitly means traveling back and forth, not merely having business in both places.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “근래에 얻은 깨달음이 있는데, 육로로 이동하게 되면 가르침을 받을 시간이 충분하지 않습니다.”

“가르침이라…….”

“저로서는 도저히, 머리를 쥐어 싸매고 고민을 해 봐도, 죽었다 깨어나도 알 수 없는 부분이 있어서 그렇습니다.”
- **Current:** “I’ve gained some insight recently, but even after racking my brain and thinking about it until I nearly died, there are parts I simply can’t understand. If we travel overland, there won’t be enough time for you to teach me.”

“Instruction…”

“I’m saying there’s something I can never figure out on my own, no matter how much I think about it.”
- **Defect:** The draft moves Taekyung’s emphatic plea ahead of Jeok Cheongang’s reaction, mistranslates 죽었다 깨어나도 as merely thinking until he nearly died, and then replaces the source’s escalation with a repetitive paraphrase.
- **Replacement:** “I’ve gained some insight recently. If we travel overland, there won’t be enough time for you to teach me.”

“Instruction…”

“There are parts I could never understand on my own, no matter how hard I rack my brain over them—even if I died and came back to life.”
- **Rationale:** The source first introduces the need for instruction, pauses for Jeok Cheongang’s interested response, and only then intensifies the claim with the idiom 죽었다 깨어나도, “even if I died and came back to life.”

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “아주 취랄을 하는구나.”
- **Current:** “You’re really talking drunken nonsense now.”
- **Defect:** The current line sanitizes the deliberately vulgar portmanteau 취랄 and sounds as though Taekyung is actually drunk.
- **Replacement:** “What a load of drunken bullshit.”
- **Rationale:** 취랄 blends 취하다 with the profanity 지랄, mockingly turning Taekyung’s claim of drunkenness into a vulgar accusation that he is talking nonsense.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 잠시 고민하던 그가 입맛을 다셨다.
- **Current:** He considered it for a moment, then clicked his tongue.
- **Defect:** The physical action is changed: 입맛을 다시다 means smacking one’s lips, whereas clicking one’s tongue is a different gesture with a different emotional implication.
- **Replacement:** He considered it for a moment, then smacked his lips.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly describes Jeok Cheongang smacking his lips after deliberating; the later sentence 끌끌 혀를 찬 is the actual tongue-clicking action.

## F09 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 배 주인, 무송의 말에 진위경이 대답하려던 그때. 적천강이 두 사람의 사이를 가로막았다.
- **Current:** Just as Mu Song, the owner of the ship, was about to answer, Jeok Cheongang stepped between them.
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed: Jin Wikyung is about to answer Mu Song, not Mu Song about to answer someone else.
- **Replacement:** Just as Jin Wikyung was about to answer Mu Song, the owner of the ship, Jeok Cheongang stepped between them.
- **Rationale:** 진위경이 is the explicit subject of 대답하려던, while 무송의 말에 identifies the statement he was answering.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 실로 오랜만에 사람의 온기를 느낀 무송의 눈시울이 붉어졌다.
- **Current:** Mu Song’s eyes grew misty.

It had been a long time since he had felt the warmth of human kindness.
- **Defect:** Splitting and reversing the clauses weakens the explicit causal relationship: feeling human warmth after a long absence is what makes Mu Song’s eyes redden.
- **Replacement:** Feeling the warmth of human kindness for the first time in ages, Mu Song’s eyes grew misty.
- **Rationale:** The modifier 사람의 온기를 느낀 directly explains Mu Song’s emotional reaction in the main clause.

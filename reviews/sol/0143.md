## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 잠깐 지켜본 바로는 우직하고, 과묵하다.
- **Current:** From what I had observed, he was stubborn and taciturn.
- **Defect:** “Stubborn” gives Li Feng a negatively inflexible temperament, whereas 우직하다 describes him as steadfast, honest, and dependable.

# Review

Several localized fidelity issues affect troop numbers, who is observing the meeting, and the source of the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord’s realization. The explanatory footnote also lacks a reference.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 삼만 하고도 수천에 달하는 아군 병력이 있다고 해도
- **Current:** Even if we have more than thirty thousand allied troops
- **Defect:** “More than thirty thousand” omits the additional several thousand specified in the source.
- **Replacement:** Even if we have thirty thousand allied troops and several thousand more
- **Rationale:** 삼만 하고도 수천 gives a total in the thirty-thousands, not merely a number above thirty thousand.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 눈 앞에 펼쳐진 광경을 말없이 응시하던 내 시야에, 그들 중 유일하게 허리를 꼿꼿이 펴고 있던 누군가의 모습이 들어온 것은.
- **Current:** And that was when I noticed someone in the middle of the scene before me, silently watching it all: the only one standing straight-backed.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the silent watching to Sama Pyo instead of the narrator and implies Pyo is standing.
- **Replacement:** And that was when, as I silently watched the scene before me, I noticed the only one among them sitting with his back straight.
- **Rationale:** 내 시야에 identifies the narrator as the observer; 허리를 꼿꼿이 펴고 describes posture, not standing.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 마치 우두머리를 따르는 졸개들처럼.
- **Current:** Like followers obeying their leader.
- **Defect:** “Followers” loses the narrator’s disparaging comparison to lackeys.
- **Replacement:** Like lackeys following their boss.
- **Rationale:** 졸개들 carries a contemptuous sense absent from the neutral “followers.”

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 짧게는 수십 년에서 길게는 백 년이 넘도록 감숙성의 터줏대감이었던 그들이
- **Current:** The ones who had ruled Gansu for decades, some of them for over a hundred years?
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the longer tenure to particular individuals and changes longstanding local fixtures into rulers.
- **Replacement:** Men whose families had been fixtures in Gansu for decades, even more than a century?
- **Rationale:** 터줏대감 conveys being long-established in the region; the passage does not say that individual men ruled it for over a century.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.87
- **Source:** 앞서 흘린 음성의 여운이 사라지기도 전에
- **Current:** Before the echo of his own voice had faded
- **Defect:** “His own voice” incorrectly ties the lingering words to the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord rather than Sima Gong’s preceding answer.
- **Replacement:** Before Sima Gong’s words had even faded
- **Rationale:** The realization follows Sima Gong’s answer about choosing the best odds of victory.

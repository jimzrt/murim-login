# Review

One action is reversed, and two combat-mechanics details are misstated. Both System panels also retain brackets prohibited by the binding format.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 모르고스는 그들의 행동을 좀처럼 이해하지 못했다.

고작 도시 몇 개를 불태우고, 왕국 하나를 없앴다고 자신의 레어까지 찾아와 행패를 부리다니.
- **Current:** Morgoth had never understood their behavior.

They would burn down a few cities and destroy an entire kingdom, then come all the way to his lair to make a scene over it.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the heroes burn the cities and destroy the kingdom. Morgoth did those things; the heroes came to his lair in response.
- **Replacement:** Morgoth could never quite understand their behavior.

All he had done was burn a few cities and wipe out a kingdom, and they came all the way to his lair to make a scene.
- **Rationale:** The complaint is Morgoth’s: he considers his own destruction a trivial reason for the heroes to confront him.

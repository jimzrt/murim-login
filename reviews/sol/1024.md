# Review

The draft changes two time intervals, assigns an unidentified reaction to Taekyung, and blurs several consequential actions and references.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 시진 전, 인근에서 맞닥트린 대설산의 척후조 일백이 싸늘한 주검이 되었다는 것은 이제 그들만이 아는 비밀이 아니었다.
- **Current:** By now, it was no secret to them alone that an entire hundred-man scouting party from the Great Snow Mountain had been found nearby an hour earlier, their bodies cold.
- **Defect:** The scouts were encountered, not found dead, and one shichen is approximately two hours, not one.
- **Replacement:** By now, they weren’t the only ones who knew that the hundred scouts from the Great Snow Mountain they had encountered nearby two hours earlier were dead.
- **Rationale:** 맞닥트린 describes the encounter; 한 시진 전 places it approximately two hours earlier.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 두 시진 전, 적들의 동태를 파악하기 위해 떠났다는 그들은 한 몸이 되어 돌아왔다.
- **Current:** They had left two hours earlier to check on the enemy’s movements. Now they’d returned as one.
- **Defect:** Two shichen becomes two hours instead of approximately four.
- **Replacement:** They had left four hours earlier to check on the enemy’s movements. Now they’d returned as one.
- **Rationale:** 두 시진 is approximately four hours.

## F03 — Critical

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** ‘한심한 것들 같으니. 고작 이런 놈들로 천하를 도모하려 했단 말인가.’
- **Current:** *Pathetic fools. Was I really supposed to take over the world with the likes of them?*
- **Defect:** “I” assigns the attempted conquest to the Blood-Sword Demon Lord; the source does not specify that subject, and the following lines extend his scorn to the Cult Leader.
- **Replacement:** *Pathetic fools. Had they really tried to conquer the world with the likes of these men?*
- **Rationale:** The Korean leaves the actor implicit rather than making the Demon Lord’s own plan the object of ridicule.

## F05 — Major

## F06 — Major

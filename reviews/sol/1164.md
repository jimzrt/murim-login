# Review

Action, mechanism, and distance errors; one line implies the Skeleton King died.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 순간, 진태경이 손끝을 따라 본능적으로 고개를 든 모르고스는
- **Current:** Following Jin Taekyung’s gaze, Morgoth instinctively raised his head.
- **Defect:** Morgoth follows Jin’s pointing finger, not his gaze.
- **Replacement:** Following the direction Jin Taekyung was pointing, Morgoth instinctively raised his head.
- **Rationale:** 손끝 specifies the direction indicated by Jin’s fingertip.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 스켈레톤 킹이 목숨을 내던져 일으킨 그 거대한 힘의 폭발이, 지금의 이 상황을 만들어 낸 것이다.
- **Current:** The Skeleton King had thrown his life away and unleashed a tremendous explosion of power. That was what had created the situation before them.
- **Defect:** “Thrown his life away” presents the Skeleton King as dead, though the passage establishes his willingness to face Erasure rather than confirming his death.
- **Replacement:** The Skeleton King had risked his life to unleash a tremendous explosion of power. That was what had created the situation before them.
- **Rationale:** 목숨을 내던져 conveys the mortal risk he took; the source does not establish that he died.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 정도 규모의 워프(Warp)와 대범위 마법이라
- **Current:** A Warp on this scale, followed by long-range Magic.
- **Defect:** 대범위 describes the spell’s area of effect, not its range; “followed by” also adds a sequence to Morgoth’s assessment.
- **Replacement:** A Warp on this scale, and wide-area Magic.
- **Rationale:** Morgoth appraises the scale of the Warp and the breadth of the Magic.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 쓸데없는 말과 행동으로 시간을 낭비하고 빈틈을 보인 걸까.
- **Current:** So why waste time running their mouths and making mistakes?
- **Defect:** The draft drops the wasteful actions and replaces exposed openings with generic mistakes.
- **Replacement:** So why waste time on pointless words and actions, leaving themselves open?
- **Rationale:** 말과 행동 and 빈틈을 보인 specify both their conduct and the opening it created.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 수천, 수만 킬로미터의 거리를 몇 번이고 뛰어넘어 부름에 답한 그들의 마음으로.
- **Current:** In the hearts of those who had crossed tens of thousands, then hundreds of thousands of kilometers over and over to answer his call.
- **Defect:** Both distance bands are inflated tenfold.
- **Replacement:** In the hearts of those who had crossed thousands, then tens of thousands of kilometers, time and again, to answer his call.
- **Rationale:** 수천, 수만 means thousands and tens of thousands, not tens and hundreds of thousands.

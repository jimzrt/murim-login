# Review

The draft changes one consequence of Jin’s possible death, overstates a Hunter’s standing and a battle count, and misformats System and monster speech.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 내가 죽는다면 그들 역시 그러할 테니.
- **Current:** If I died, they’d remember me, too.
- **Defect:** Changes the consequence of Jin’s death from the Hunters dying as well to the Hunters remembering him.
- **Replacement:** If I died, they would, too.
- **Rationale:** 그들 역시 그러할 refers to dying, not remembering Jin.

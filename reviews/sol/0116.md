# Review

The draft contains altered subjects, a repeated mistranslation of martial exchanges as seconds, an added payment mechanism, omitted tactical and register details, and several terminology or wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘이 싸움, 이겼어.’
- **Current:** I won.
- **Defect:** The draft adds Jin Taekyung as the personal subject, while the source declares the fight won without claiming an individual victory.
- **Replacement:** This fight is won.
- **Rationale:** The source refers to the outcome of 이 싸움, not specifically to Taekyung defeating someone.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘진무경이 강한 건지, 아니면 풍양이 생각했던 것보다 약했던 건지.’
- **Current:** Is Jin Mukyung really that strong, or was Pung Yang weaker than he thought?
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the comparison to Pung Yang, changing the implied subject of 생각했던 from the first-person narrator to Pung Yang.
- **Replacement:** Is Jin Mukyung really that strong, or was Pung Yang weaker than I thought?
- **Rationale:** This is Taekyung’s internal assessment of whether Pung Yang was weaker than Taekyung had expected.

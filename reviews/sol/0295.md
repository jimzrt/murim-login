# Review

Several meaningful fidelity issues remain, including an omitted tactical explanation, altered command hierarchy, an overexplicit threat, and a reversed punchline.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 몬스터가 그 몬스터였나? 이거 진화도 있는 거였어?
- **Current:** Wasn’t this supposed to be a monster? Did monsters have evolution, too?
- **Defect:** The first question becomes a generic objection instead of preserving the joke that Digimon are themselves a kind of “monster”; the direct thought is also left unitalicized.
- **Replacement:** *So monsters were that kind of monster? They could evolve, too?*
- **Rationale:** The Korean plays on the preceding Digimon reference and marks both questions as direct thought.

# Review

The draft contains one incorrect subject assignment, an omitted subordinate relationship, softened profanity and wordplay, several localized meaning shifts, and a misnamed location.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뇌옥을 지키던 네 수하들을 살려 두라 말했을 때부터 이미 각오하고 있었다.
- **Current:** I was already prepared for this when I told you to spare the men guarding the underground prison.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that the prison guards were Baeksang’s subordinates, weakening the hierarchy and the Beast Miao King’s reason for anticipating Baeksang’s response.
- **Replacement:** I was already prepared for this when I told you to spare your subordinates guarding the underground prison.
- **Rationale:** 네 수하들 explicitly means “your subordinates,” not merely unidentified men.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 입으로는 투덜거려도 눈은 웃고 있다.
- **Current:** They grumbled with their mouths, but their eyes were smiling.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the grumbling and smiling to the gathered warriors, but the subject in context is Wonhu.
- **Replacement:** Though Wonhu grumbled, his eyes were smiling.
- **Rationale:** The sentence is framed by Wonhu’s dialogue and the following clause in which Wonhu passes around the gourd; the singular implicit subject remains Wonhu.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 허어, 개새끼들만 모아 놨나. 왜 이리들 코가 좋아?
- **Current:** Good grief. Did they gather nothing but dogs here? Why do you all have such sharp noses?
- **Defect:** Rendering 개새끼들 as neutral “dogs” sanitizes the insult and weakens the crude dog-and-sharp-noses joke.
- **Replacement:** Hell, did they round up nothing but a pack of fucking mutts? Why are all your noses so sharp?
- **Rationale:** 개새끼들 is strongly insulting while also supplying the literal canine image needed for the nose joke.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 이거 알고 보니, 개새끼들이 아니라 순 허풍쟁이들이었군.
- **Current:** So this is what I find out? You aren’t dogs. You’re all just a bunch of braggarts.
- **Defect:** The payoff again neutralizes 개새끼들 and reads as though Wonhu had literally mistaken the men for dogs.
- **Replacement:** Turns out you aren’t fucking mutts after all—just a bunch of blowhards.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both the vulgar register and the callback to Wonhu’s earlier canine insult.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 다 아는 처지에 뭘. 호랑이 좆이나 까 잡수쇼.
- **Current:** We both know what’s going on, so why bother? Go gnaw on a tiger’s dick.
- **Defect:** “Gnaw on” changes the deliberately absurd action in the insult, which tells the listener to peel and eat it.
- **Replacement:** We both know what’s going on, so why bother? Go peel and eat a tiger’s dick.
- **Rationale:** 까 잡수쇼 retains both the peeling action and the sarcastically polite command to eat.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 창백하다는 표현이 어울릴 만큼 새하얀 피부.
- **Current:** His skin was so pale that the word *white* seemed entirely appropriate.
- **Defect:** The description reverses which adjective is being judged appropriate: the source says his starkly white skin merits the description “pallid,” not that pale skin merits the word “white.”
- **Replacement:** His skin was so starkly white that *pallid* was the only word for it.
- **Rationale:** 창백하다는 표현이 어울릴 만큼 makes “pallid” the resulting characterization of his exceptionally white skin.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 지금 이 순간에도 빠르게 스쳐 지나가는 풍경 속에는 사람의 그림자도, 여러 가지 독특한 형태를 띤 가옥(家屋)도 없었고 오직 풀숲만이 가득했다.
- **Current:** Even as the landscape rushed past us, there was not a single human shadow or a house of any unusual shape in sight. Nothing but grassland stretched in every direction.
- **Defect:** The draft awkwardly makes the absence apply only to houses that might have unusual shapes and changes dense grassy undergrowth into open grassland.
- **Replacement:** Even as the landscape rushed past us, there was not a single person or one of the oddly shaped houses in sight—only thickets everywhere.
- **Rationale:** The source separately excludes people and the distinctively shaped houses, then says the surroundings are filled only with 풀숲, grassy thickets or undergrowth.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 아니, 시도하지 못했다고 하는 것이 옳은 표현일 것이다.
- **Current:** No. It would be more accurate to say I had not been able to try.
- **Defect:** The English is unnatural and does not clearly convey Taekyung’s emotional inability to bring himself to wake Yayul Mok.
- **Replacement:** No. It would be more accurate to say I couldn’t bring myself to try.
- **Rationale:** In context, 못했다 marks that Taekyung could not make himself attempt it because he knew Yayul Mok would try to return.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 요서부에서 발견했던 요희의 향낭(香囊)을.
- **Current:** The scent pouch belonging to Yohi that I had discovered in the Western Region of Yeo.
- **Defect:** The draft misnames the location as “Yeo,” introducing an unestablished place and severing its connection to Yohi’s Yao people.
- **Replacement:** The scent pouch belonging to Yohi that I had discovered in the western Yao territory.
- **Rationale:** 요서부 refers to the western part of the Yao territory; the established ethnic name is Yao, not Yeo.

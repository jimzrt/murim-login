# Review

The draft contains two scene-fact errors, one lost euphemistic joke, one obscured chronological quantity, and two localized wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저 멀리, 아스라이 들려오는 굉음과 함께 지면이 미세하게 떨렸다.
- **Current:** The ground trembled faintly, accompanied by the distant rumble of an explosion that reached us from far away.
- **Defect:** The draft identifies the noise as an explosion and adds a first-person reference, although the source only describes a distant roar whose martial origin is established afterward.
- **Replacement:** The ground trembled faintly as a distant roar rolled in from far away.
- **Rationale:** 굉음 denotes a tremendous roar or booming noise here, not an identified explosion, and the Korean contains no equivalent of “us.”

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 정마대전으로부터 강산이 다섯 번 바뀔 만큼의 세월이 흘렀다.
- **Current:** Enough time had passed since the Great Faction War for the mountains and rivers to change five times.
- **Defect:** The literal rendering leaves the chronological quantity opaque in unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** Half a century had passed since the Great Faction War.
- **Rationale:** The traditional expression treats one change of the mountains and rivers as roughly ten years, so five changes conveys approximately fifty years.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 우리를 중심으로 솟아오른 용오름이 다른 이들의 시야를 가렸다.
- **Current:** A waterspout of dust rose around us, blocking everyone else’s view.
- **Defect:** A waterspout is specifically a vortex over water and is incompatible with the dust-filled arena described here.
- **Replacement:** A whirlwind rose around us, blocking everyone else’s view.
- **Rationale:** 용오름 describes the rising vortex itself; the following sentence separately establishes that dust is swirling through it.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “써 보지도 않은 허리를!”
- **Current:** “You hit my back before I even got to use it!”
- **Defect:** The draft invents the explicit action “You hit my back” and loses Taekyung’s euphemistic joke about not yet having had occasion to use his hips.
- **Replacement:** “I haven’t even gotten to use these hips yet!”
- **Rationale:** The Korean is a deliberately elliptical complaint centered on his unused 허리, carrying a suggestive comic undertone without explicitly describing the preceding blow.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it reverses an injury, treats an unfinished struggle as accomplished, and alters several combat and dialogue details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 미카엘 실베르트가 죽을힘을 다해 휘둘렀을 일권은 살을 터트리고 뼈를 부수지 못했다.
- **Current:** Michael Silbert’s punch, thrown with all his might, had burst my skin but failed to break my bones.
- **Defect:** The draft says the punch burst Jin’s skin; the source says it did not burst his skin or break his bones.
- **Replacement:** Michael Silbert’s punch, thrown with all his might, had neither split my skin nor broken my bones.
- **Rationale:** The negation applies to both injuries.

# Review

Several localized fidelity and register issues remain, including a mistranslated idiom, sanitized profanity, and a muddled qi-circulation mechanism.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 씨벌, 못 해 먹겠네.
- **Current:** This is impossible.
- **Defect:** The translation removes the strong profanity and changes an exasperated refusal into a neutral statement about impossibility.
- **Replacement:** Fuck, I can't work like this.
- **Rationale:** 씨벌 is strong profanity, while 못 해 먹겠네 expresses frustrated inability or unwillingness to continue under the present conditions.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이미 볼 장 다 봤다고 생각했지만, 마음 한구석이 아려 오는 건 어쩔 수 없다.
- **Current:** I thought we had already been through everything together, but I couldn't help feeling a dull ache somewhere inside.
- **Defect:** The idiom 볼 장 다 봤다 means that their relationship was already finished or had nothing left to lose, not that they had shared every kind of experience together.
- **Replacement:** I thought things between us were already over, but I couldn't help the dull ache in a corner of my heart.
- **Rationale:** The line refers to Taekyung believing his romantic prospects with Song Song were already exhausted, while admitting that her reaction still hurts him.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이 부분을 보시면 운기(運氣)를 통해 기를 순환시키고 축적시킨다고 적혀 있지 않습니까?
- **Current:** If you look here, it says that qi is circulated and accumulated through circulating internal energy, doesn’t it?
- **Defect:** The wording incorrectly makes internal energy the separate means by which qi is circulated, muddling the source's explanation that 운기, the act of channeling energy, circulates and accumulates qi.
- **Replacement:** If you look here, it says that channeling circulates and accumulates qi, doesn’t it?
- **Rationale:** The source describes the mechanism of 운기 acting upon 기; it does not distinguish a second substance called internal energy in this sentence.

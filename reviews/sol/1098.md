- **Defect:** “What I just did” presents the Slaughter Saint’s gamble as an action already taken rather than a course he proposed.
- **Replacement:** If the gamble I proposed earlier rests on confidence in my own skill, you’re trying to make a deal with someone you can’t trust in the first place.
- **Rationale:** The Korean contrasts his contemplated gamble with Taekyung’s contemplated deal; neither is described here as completed.

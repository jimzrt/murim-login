# Review

Several source details are altered, including the footage’s duration, the Federation’s membership, and whether the followers have died for their faith.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 불과 세 시간 남짓한 영상.
- **Current:** The footage was barely three hours long.
- **Defect:** “Barely three hours” suggests less than three hours; 남짓 means a little more.
- **Replacement:** The footage ran a little over three hours.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies a duration just over three hours.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 명칭처럼 전 세계의 모든 헌터가 소속된 거대 단체.
- **Current:** As its name implied, it was a vast organization made up of Hunters from all over the world.
- **Defect:** “Hunters from all over the world” omits that every Hunter belongs to the Federation.
- **Replacement:** As its name implied, it was a vast organization to which every Hunter in the world belonged.
- **Rationale:** 모든 헌터 makes membership universal, not merely international.

- **Rationale:** Justice would be 正義; the supplied characters explicitly distinguish the intended meaning.

# Review

Several action, negation, register, terminology, and formatting errors require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 혀를 내두른 하연이가 수저를 내려놨다.
- **Current:** Hayeon stuck out her tongue and put down her spoon.
- **Defect:** The idiom expresses speechless disbelief or exasperation; it does not describe Hayeon playfully sticking out her tongue.
- **Replacement:** Hayeon could only shake her head in disbelief as she put down her spoon.
- **Rationale:** 혀를 내두르다 conveys being astonished or appalled enough to shake one’s head, so the current version changes her physical reaction and characterization.

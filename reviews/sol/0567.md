# Review

Several subject, quantity, mechanism, kinship, and characterization errors require correction.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “이 늙은이가 노망이 났나…….”
- **Current:** “This old bastard has gone senile……”
- **Defect:** The rhetorical question is turned into a declaration, and “bastard” intensifies the insult beyond 늙은이.
- **Replacement:** “Has this old man gone senile…?”
- **Rationale:** 났나 questions whether Go Jun has become senile; it does not state it as a fact.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “입국 직후입니다. 당시에 보고드렸지만 부길드장님께서 워낙 바쁘신 것 같아, 우선 제 선에서 조치해 뒀습니다.”
- **Current:** “Immediately after they entered the country. I reported it at the time, but you seemed extremely busy, so I took care of it on my own authority first.”
- **Defect:** The plural “they” falsely makes Song Cheonwoo’s London-based family the people who entered the country. The context refers to measures taken after Song Cheonwoo’s arrival.
- **Replacement:** “Immediately after Song Cheonwoo entered the country. I reported it at the time, but you seemed extremely busy, so I took care of it on my own authority first.”
- **Rationale:** 입국 직후 has an omitted singular subject recoverable from the surrounding discussion of Song Cheonwoo’s return and his family remaining near London.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그 양반이 예전부터 눈치는 귀신이었지.”
- **Current:** “That man has always had the instincts of a ghost.”
- **Defect:** The Korean idiom means that Director Hong is exceptionally perceptive, not that he possesses ghostlike instincts.
- **Replacement:** “That man has always been uncannily perceptive.”
- **Rationale:** 눈치는 귀신이다 describes extraordinary social or situational awareness.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘송천우. 그 양반 막내 손자가 딱 우리 상호 나이였지, 아마.’
- **Current:** *Song Cheonwoo. His youngest grandchild was about the same age as our Sangho, wasn’t he?*
- **Defect:** The draft changes the explicitly male kinship 손자, “grandson,” into the gender-neutral “grandchild.”
- **Replacement:** *Song Cheonwoo. His youngest grandson was about the same age as our Sangho, wasn’t he?*
- **Rationale:** 막내 손자 specifically identifies Song Cheonwoo’s youngest grandson.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 경호팀장답게 압도적인 실력을 지닌 고세원도 이들 중 다섯 이상이 뭉치면 쉽게 승리를 장담할 수 없을 정도이니, 상대가 아무리 철저히 방비해 뒀다 해도 막을 수 없을 것이다.
- **Current:** Go Se-won possessed overwhelming skill befitting the Head of Security, but even he could not easily guarantee victory if more than five of these men joined forces. No matter how thoroughly the target had prepared, there was no way to stop them.
- **Defect:** “More than five” excludes exactly five, changing the stated combat threshold.
- **Replacement:** Go Se-won possessed overwhelming skill befitting the Head of Security, but even he could not easily guarantee victory if five or more of these men joined forces. No matter how thoroughly the target had prepared, there was no way to stop them.
- **Rationale:** 다섯 이상 means five or more, not more than five.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “팀원들의 희생이 컸습니다. 최대한 이점을 살려 기습했습니다만, 예상외로 저택의 경비가 삼엄했던 탓에 사망자가 둘이나…….”
- **Current:** “The team members paid a heavy price. We took advantage of the surprise as much as possible, but the mansion’s security was heavier than expected, and two men were killed……”
- **Defect:** The draft turns the surprise attack itself into an advantage they exploited, omitting the action that they deliberately launched an ambush while exploiting their advantages.
- **Replacement:** “The team members paid a heavy price. We exploited every advantage we had and launched a surprise attack, but the mansion was guarded more heavily than expected, and two men were killed…”
- **Rationale:** 이점을 살려 modifies how they carried out 기습; 기습 is the surprise attack they launched, not an existing circumstance they merely took advantage of.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “예. 통신망도 장악했고, 현혹 마법으로 모든 암어와 보고 체계를 알아냈으니 저쪽에서 연락이 오더라도 크게 의심하지 못할 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “Yes. We’ve taken control of their communications network and learned all their code words and reporting procedures through illusion magic. Even if they contact the outside, no one will become overly suspicious.”
- **Defect:** The draft mistranslates 현혹 마법 as illusion magic and reverses the communication direction: the concern is the other side contacting the infiltrators, not the captives contacting outsiders.
- **Replacement:** “Yes. We’ve seized their communications network, and we used enchantment magic to learn all their passwords and reporting procedures. Even if their people contact us, they’re unlikely to suspect anything.”
- **Rationale:** 현혹 denotes magical beguilement or enchantment, while 저쪽에서 연락이 오더라도 means even if contact comes from the other side.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 뜻을 알 수 없는 묘한 눈빛으로 상관을 응시하던 고세원이 재빨리 안색을 고쳤다.
- **Current:** Go Se-won, who had been staring at his superior with a strange look he could not quite understand, quickly composed his expression.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Go Se-won unable to understand his own expression. The source says his gaze is inscrutable to others.
- **Replacement:** Go Se-won, who had been staring at his superior with an inscrutable look, quickly composed his expression.
- **Rationale:** 뜻을 알 수 없는 modifies the quality of Go Se-won’s gaze; it does not describe his own failure to interpret it.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 먼지 하나 없이 반질반질한 대리석 표면에 비친 어떤 사내를 응시하는 그의 눈빛은, 어느덧 깊게 가라앉아 있었다.
- **Current:** His gaze, reflected in the spotless, gleaming marble surface, had by then sunk deep and become unreadable.
- **Defect:** The draft says his gaze is reflected and omits the action of Go Se-won staring at the man—implicitly himself—reflected in the marble.
- **Replacement:** By then, his gaze had grown deep and unreadable as he stared at the man reflected in the spotless, gleaming marble.
- **Rationale:** The object reflected in the marble is 어떤 사내, “a man,” whom Go Se-won is watching; the reflective self-confrontation is part of the characterization.

## F10 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 백여 명, 혹은 백여 마리에 달하는 적들의 시체는 내가 그려 낸 상상 속의 허상일 뿐이니까.
- **Current:** The bodies of more than a hundred enemies—or perhaps more than a hundred monsters—were nothing more than phantoms from my imagination.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the quantity from approximately one hundred to more than one hundred and obscures the source’s distinction between human and nonhuman enemy counts.
- **Replacement:** The corpses of a hundred or so human enemies—or perhaps a hundred or so monsters—were nothing more than phantoms I had conjured in my imagination.
- **Rationale:** 백여 means approximately a hundred, while the counters 명 and 마리 distinguish people from creatures.

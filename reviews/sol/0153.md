# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues remain, including a reversed size comparison, an obscured death joke, altered physical actions, and losses of quantity and dismissive register.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 한 3분 전까지만 하더라도 태초 마을에서 온 강시 취급 하더니, 지금은 삼대독자 아들 대하듯 조심스럽다.
- **Current:** Only three minutes ago, he had treated me like a jiangshi from Taecho Village. Now he was being as careful with me as though I were his only son.
- **Defect:** The translation omits 삼대독자, losing the three-generation sole-heir image that explains the man's exaggerated care.
- **Replacement:** Only three minutes ago, he had treated me like a jiangshi from Taecho Village. Now he was handling me as carefully as though I were his family’s precious only son, three generations in the making.
- **Rationale:** 삼대독자 refers to an exceptionally precious only son in a family line that has produced a sole male heir for three generations.

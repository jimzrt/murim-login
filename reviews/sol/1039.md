# Review

Five actionable fidelity findings: a misread movement, an added physical detail, a lost terminology distinction, and two changes to who perceives or gains strength.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 혈검마군의 측면으로 방향을 틀어 재차 쏘아지는 진태경의 신형.
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung had changed direction alongside the Blood-Sword Demon Lord, then shot forward again.
- **Defect:** “Alongside” suggests they changed direction together; Taekyung veers toward the Demon Lord’s flank.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung veered toward the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s flank, then shot forward again.
- **Rationale:** 혈검마군의 측면으로 identifies the direction of Taekyung’s turn, not a shared movement.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아주 천둥벌거숭이지만 대단한 핏덩이 하나를 제자로 내려 줄 터이니, 녀석이 어른 구실 할 때까지 다른 놈들 관짝이나 짜 주라고.
- **Current:** “He said he’d send me a hell-raising little brat of a Disciple, a fine little blood-soaked whelp, and that I should build coffins for all the other bastards until the boy learned to act like an adult.”
- **Defect:** “Blood-soaked” adds an image of bloodshed to a term for a young person.
- **Replacement:** “He said he’d send me one hell-raising but remarkable young brat as my Disciple, and that I should build coffins for the other bastards until the boy learned to act like an adult.”
- **Rationale:** 대단한 핏덩이 praises the youngster despite his immaturity; it does not describe him as covered in blood.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 마법사, 아니 혈검마군이 술사라 부르는 그들에게서 부여받은 가공할 힘이 검신에 실리자
- **Current:** The fearsome power bestowed on the Blood-Sword Demon Lord by those he called mages poured into his sword.
- **Defect:** The draft attributes “mages” to the Demon Lord and loses the narrator’s correction to his different term, 술사.
- **Replacement:** The fearsome power bestowed on him by the mages—or sorcerers, as the Blood-Sword Demon Lord called them—poured into his sword.
- **Rationale:** The narration first calls them 마법사, then distinguishes the Demon Lord’s name for them.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** ‘강해졌다. 조금 전보다.’
- **Current:** *He got stronger. Faster than a moment ago.*
- **Defect:** The thought concerns the attackers growing stronger, not one man becoming faster.
- **Replacement:** *They’d grown stronger. Stronger than they were a moment ago.*
- **Rationale:** The preceding observation is that 놈들이—the attackers—were faster; this thought separately says they had grown stronger.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금 이 순간, 위기를 느끼는 것은 나뿐만이 아니었다.
- **Current:** At this moment, I wasn’t the only one in danger.
- **Defect:** Being in danger replaces sensing danger, losing the lead-in to the mages’ fear for their lives.
- **Replacement:** At this moment, I wasn’t the only one who sensed danger.
- **Rationale:** 위기를 느끼는 explicitly describes perceiving danger; the following lines identify the mages as also feeling threatened.

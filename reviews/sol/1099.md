# Review

Five actionable fidelity and wording findings.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 적어도 무신께서 건재하셨다면 그들 또한 마음을 달리 먹었을 것이라 확신할 수 있지.
- **Current:** I’m certain that if the Martial God had remained in his prime, they would have thought twice.
- **Defect:** “In his prime” implies youth rather than continued strength, while “thought twice” stops short of the different decision Cheongheoja asserts.
- **Replacement:** I’m certain that if the Martial God had still been around and strong, they would have made a different choice.
- **Rationale:** 건재 describes his continued presence and strength; 마음을 달리 먹었을 states that they would have changed their minds.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it mistranslates one cardinal-direction joke, changes the Western Heaven Demon Lord’s defeat into a capture, weakens gendered profanity central to the feminist joke, and contains several localized register or wording defects.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래서 동쪽은 동정모쏠이고 북쪽은 북괴김씨냐?
- **Current:** So is the eastern one the Dongting Forever Alone, and the northern one the North Korean Kim?
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 동정 as Dongting. Here 동정 means “virgin” and combines with 모쏠, someone who has never dated, to form an insulting east-themed nickname.
- **Replacement:** So is the eastern one the East Virgin Forever-Alone, and the northern one North Korea’s Kim?
- **Rationale:** The joke invents mocking titles beginning with the relevant cardinal-direction syllables. Referring to Dongting Lake removes the sexual insult and changes the joke’s meaning.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 서천마군도 죽을 고비를 몇 번이나 넘긴 끝에 겨우 잡은 괴물인데, 그런 연놈들이 최소 셋이나 더 있다니.
- **Current:** The Western Heaven Demon Lord was already a monster I had barely managed to capture after narrowly surviving death several times. And there were at least three more like him?
- **Defect:** “Capture” states that the Western Heaven Demon Lord was taken alive or detained. In context, 잡은 means that Taekyung barely managed to defeat or take him down.
- **Replacement:** The Western Heaven Demon Lord was already a monster I had barely managed to take down after several brushes with death. And there were at least three more like him?
- **Rationale:** The current wording changes the outcome of the earlier confrontation and therefore a scene fact; “take down” preserves the contextual sense without adding certainty about his ultimate fate.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 산 채로 태워 죽여도 시원찮을 개호로 새끼겠지. 감히 뉘 앞에서 함부로 요설(妖舌)을 놀리느냐.
- **Current:** “You’re talking about a fucking bastard who wouldn’t be worth the satisfaction of burning alive. How dare you spout that demonic drivel in front of whom?”
- **Defect:** “In front of whom?” is ungrammatical and fails to convey Jeok Cheongang’s imperious rhetorical question about whom she is speaking before.
- **Replacement:** “You’re talking about a fucking bastard who wouldn’t be worth the satisfaction of burning alive. How dare you wag that wicked tongue before this old man?”
- **Rationale:** 뉘 앞에서 challenges her for speaking that way in Jeok Cheongang’s presence, while 요설을 놀리다 is an accusation of recklessly wagging a deceptive or wicked tongue.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 하면 지금껏 노부가 써 내려간 살생부에 놈이 아니라 년을 포함시킬까 하는데, 어찌 생각하느냐?
- **Current:** “Then should this old man add a woman, rather than a man, to the kill list? What do you think?”
- **Defect:** The neutral “woman” and “man” sanitize the deliberately insulting contrast between 년 and 놈, weakening Jeok Cheongang’s voice and the setup for Taekyung calling him a feminist.
- **Replacement:** “Then how about this old man adds a bitch instead of a bastard to his kill list? What do you think?”
- **Rationale:** The source uses paired gendered insults, not neutral nouns. Their equal hostility is the basis of the ensuing feminist joke.

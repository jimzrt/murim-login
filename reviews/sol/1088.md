# Review

The draft preserves the main decision and jade metaphor, but changes the alliance’s response to its leaders’ removal, misrenders a remembered image, and misses the established title and a teasing cue.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 그래서 더 문제인 거죠. 이제는 파륜과 태군악이 어떻게 된다 하더라도 저희에게 달려들 테니까.
- **Current:** “That’s what makes it worse. Even if something happens to Pa Ryun and Tae Gunak now, the rest will still come after us.”
- **Defect:** “The rest” suggests only the survivors or Dark Heaven’s troops will attack. The point is that removing the two bandit leaders will no longer stop their forces from attacking.
- **Replacement:** “That’s what makes it worse. Even if Pa Ryun and Tae Gunak are taken out now, their forces will still come after us.”
- **Rationale:** Dark Heaven’s presence keeps the allied forces in line even if their two leaders fall.

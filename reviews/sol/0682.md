# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters one-handed weapon choreography, misstates the direction of an incoming attack, weakens an impact mechanism and a hierarchical insult, and omits the opening charge sound.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 가파르게 회전하던 쌍륜(雙輪)이 주름진 손을 떠난 것은.
- **Current:** The rapidly spinning twin wheels left his wrinkled hands.
- **Defect:** The source specifies one wrinkled hand, while the draft changes the launch to both hands. This distinction matters because he explicitly uses both hands later to direct the wheels.
- **Replacement:** The rapidly spinning twin wheels left his wrinkled hand.
- **Rationale:** 손 is singular here, whereas the later 양손 explicitly means both hands.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 파도처럼 덮쳐 오는 흑색 강기.
- **Current:** Black Force surged over him like a wave.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Force sound as though it has already swept over Jin, but the source describes it surging toward him before his spear intercepts it.
- **Replacement:** A wave of black Force surged toward him.
- **Rationale:** 덮쳐 오는 marks an incoming attack, and the following action has Jin pierce its center before it reaches him.

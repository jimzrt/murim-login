# Review

The draft contains two consequential quantity errors, several altered subjects or scene conditions, and one culturally specific punchline that needs clarification.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** S급 헌터들에 일반 헌터만 10만 명 참전했다는데 뭐가 그리 걱정임.
- **Current:** They say S-rank Hunters and ordinary Hunters alone have more than 100,000 people participating.
- **Defect:** The draft both groups the S-rank Hunters into the figure and changes exactly 100,000 ordinary Hunters to more than 100,000 total participants.
- **Replacement:** They say S-rank Hunters are fighting alongside 100,000 ordinary Hunters.
- **Rationale:** The particle 만 applies to 일반 헌터: the ordinary Hunters alone number 100,000, in addition to the S-rank Hunters.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 작년에 전역했는데 왜 수통에서 아직도 노르망디 물맛이 나냐.
- **Current:** I was discharged last year, so why does my canteen still taste like Normandy water?

- **Rationale:** The line jokes that the military canteen and its water seemed impossibly ancient; 전역했는데 is contrastive here.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 입 다물고 셔터 내려라. 오늘 주모 제삿날이다…….
- **Current:** Shut your mouth and pull down the shutters. Today is the Lady of the House’s memorial day…….
- **Defect:** The literal rendering preserves the words but loses the Korean internet joke connecting intense national pride with calling a tavern proprietress for celebratory drinks.

## F06 — Major

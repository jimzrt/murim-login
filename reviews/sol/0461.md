# Review

One meaningful progression error and several localized fidelity, terminology, and prose issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 운기조식으로 기운을 보충한 청풍, 궁기방, 혁무진의 대답에 내가 빠르게 지시를 내렸다.
- **Current:** After Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, and Hyuk Mujin replenished their energy through qi circulation, I rapidly gave them their orders.
- **Defect:** Murim 기운 is rendered as generic “energy,” and “through qi circulation” is stiff rather than using the established verbal terminology.
- **Replacement:** Once Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, and Hyuk Mujin had replenished their qi by circulating it, I quickly gave them their orders.
- **Rationale:** The source describes them replenishing their Murim qi through 운기조식, for which the binding terminology is “circulate one’s qi.”

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 일반적인 선박보다 날렵한 선체를 지닌 쾌조선에도 미치지 못할 만큼, 훨씬 작고 가벼운 나룻배가 허공을 날았다.
- **Current:** The ferryboat was much smaller and lighter than even the sleek-hulled swift ships, yet it shot into the air.
- **Defect:** “Yet” adds a false contrast: the boat’s smaller, lighter construction facilitates its launch rather than making it surprising.
- **Replacement:** Far smaller and lighter even than the sleek-hulled swift ships, the ferryboat shot into the air.
- **Rationale:** The source simply identifies the ferryboat as much smaller and lighter before describing it flying; it does not establish a concessive relationship.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어느덧 팔 성에 다다른 화염신장(火焰神掌)의 열기에 주위의 공기가 후끈 달아오르고, 내려가는 듯싶던 나룻배의 선체가 날치처럼 튀어 오른다.
- **Current:** The heat of the Flame Divine Palm, which had reached the eighth stage, made the air around us blaze. The ferryboat, which seemed to be descending, sprang upward like a flying fish.
- **Defect:** 팔 성 denotes eight-tenths, or eighty-percent, mastery; “the eighth stage” invents a discrete advancement stage and changes the stated proficiency measure.
- **Replacement:** With my mastery of the Flame Divine Palm now at eighty percent, its heat made the surrounding air scorching hot. The ferryboat, which had seemed about to descend, sprang upward like a flying fish.
- **Rationale:** The source gives a fractional degree of Taekyung’s mastery, not the ordinal number of a formal stage.

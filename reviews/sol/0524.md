# Review

Two meaningful fidelity defects: Mujin taking a message is changed into assuming Taekyung’s duties, and a line essential to the marketplace misunderstanding is omitted. Several localized wording issues also alter physical detail or produce unnatural English.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 조장님은 한창 운기조식 중이셔서 제가 대신 들었습니다.
- **Current:** “About one or two shichen ago. Since you were deep in qi circulation, I took over for you.”
- **Defect:** Mujin says he received Gung Gibang’s message on Taekyung’s behalf, not that he took over Taekyung’s duties.
- **Replacement:** “About one or two shichen ago. You were busy circulating your qi, so I took the message for you.”
- **Rationale:** 제가 대신 들었습니다 means that Mujin heard or received what Gung Gibang said in Taekyung’s stead. “I took over for you” changes the action and leaves the object unstated.

- **Defect:** “Chopping” misrenders 다지고 in the medicinal-preparation context, and “in quite a rush” changes 정신없다 from being overwhelmingly busy to moving hurriedly.

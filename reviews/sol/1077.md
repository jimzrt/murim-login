# Review

Actionable errors in distance, an omitted description, the monsters’ movements, and who would force the captive to betray them.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반경 십여 리에 걸쳐 펼쳐진 광활한 갈대숲
- **Current:** the vast reed beds stretching across a dozen or so miles
- **Defect:** Changes a radius of a little over ten li into a span of a dozen miles.
- **Replacement:** the vast reed beds extending for a little over ten li in every direction
- **Rationale:** 반경 specifies a radius, and 리 is not a mile.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천 리에 달하는 거리를 쉼 없이 날갯짓해야 하는 고난이 뒤따르지만
- **Current:** They had to beat their wings without rest across a thousand miles.
- **Defect:** Converts a thousand li into a thousand miles, substantially increasing the journey.
- **Replacement:** They had to beat their wings without rest across a thousand li.
- **Rationale:** The source gives the distance in li.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘고작 귀신의 조화 따위가 아니다. 그분의 권능은 천신(天神)과 맞닿아 있어.’

하늘 그 자체이자, 전지전능한 신.
- **Current:** *This is no mere supernatural mischief. His power reaches the gods themselves.*
- **Defect:** Makes the heavenly god plural and omits the following description of the Lord of Heaven as heaven itself and an omnipotent, all-knowing god.
- **Replacement:** *This is no mere supernatural mischief. His power borders on that of a heavenly god.*

Heaven itself. An all-knowing, all-powerful god.
- **Rationale:** 천신 is singular; the next sentence supplies Ma Sanbao’s conception of his master.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 어찌하여 그 믿을 수 없는 권능의 일부를, 당신의 수족이었던 스승조차 버리고 도망쳐 온 자신에게 나누어 주었는지.
- **Current:** And why had he shared a portion of that unbelievable power with Ma Sanbao, who had been his servant, yet abandoned even his own master and fled?
- **Defect:** Identifies Ma Sanbao, rather than his master, as the Lord of Heaven’s former servant.
- **Replacement:** And why had he shared a portion of that unbelievable power with Ma Sanbao, who had abandoned and fled from his own master—a man who had served the Lord of Heaven himself?
- **Rationale:** 당신의 수족이었던 modifies 스승: the master Ma Sanbao abandoned had been the Lord of Heaven’s servant.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 굽혀졌던 무릎이, 뻣뻣하게 굳어 있던 허리가, 마지막으로 굳게 감겨 있던 눈이 반개(半開)한다.
- **Current:** Knees that had been bent, backs stiff as boards, and, finally, eyes that had been tightly shut all opened halfway.
- **Defect:** Has knees and backs open halfway, rather than straighten as the monsters rise; only the eyes half-open.
- **Replacement:** Bent knees straightened, rigid backs unbent, and, finally, tightly shut eyes opened halfway.
- **Rationale:** 반개 applies to the eyes; the preceding body parts move as the monsters get up.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 끈질긴 조사와 추격으로 무영의 손에 고혼이 된 시체 한 구를 손에 넣은 바 있었던 마삼보는 그 사실을 익히 알고 있었다.
- **Current:** After a relentless investigation and pursuit, he had once obtained a corpse—a solitary ghost who had fallen to No Shadow’s hand.
- **Defect:** Calls the corpse itself a ghost, obscuring that Ma Sanbao obtained the body of someone No Shadow had killed.
- **Replacement:** After a relentless investigation and pursuit, he had once obtained the corpse of one of No Shadow’s victims.
- **Rationale:** 고혼이 된 describes the dead victim, while 시체 한 구 is the body Ma Sanbao obtained.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그토록 조심하라 일렀거늘, 죽은 것도 아니고 생포라니.
- **Current:** After all the warnings to be careful, he hadn’t merely died—he’d been captured.
- **Defect:** Implies the subordinate died as well as being captured; the source contrasts capture with death.
- **Replacement:** After all his warnings to be careful, the man hadn’t even died—he’d been captured.
- **Rationale:** 죽은 것도 아니고 생포라니 expresses Ma Sanbao’s dismay that the man was taken alive instead of killed.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “놈은 배반할 것이다. 아니, 반드시 그렇게 만들겠지.”
- **Current:** “He will betray us. No—he’ll make sure it happens.”

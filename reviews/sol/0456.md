## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 과거 내가 사천에서 그랬듯, 지게를 짊어진 청풍이 고개를 끄덕였다. 지게 위에는 모든 혈도를 제압당한 채 쇠사슬에 묶인 삼괴가 있었다.
- **Current:** Just as I had once done in Sichuan, Cheongpung nodded while carrying a wooden frame on his back. The Three Fiends was lying on the frame, his acupoints sealed and his body bound in chains.
- **Defect:** The introductory comparison grammatically modifies Cheongpung’s nod, implying that Taekyung had similarly nodded in Sichuan. In the source, the comparison concerns Cheongpung carrying the wooden pack frame as Taekyung once did.
- **Replacement:** Cheongpung nodded, carrying a wooden pack frame on his back just as I had in Sichuan. The Three Fiends lay on it, all his acupoints sealed and his body bound in chains.
- **Rationale:** 과거 내가 사천에서 그랬듯 modifies 지게를 짊어진: Cheongpung is shouldering a pack frame in the same manner Taekyung previously did in Sichuan.

# Review

The draft changes one failed attempt into a missed shot and loses two important contrasts in the confrontation.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘틀렸어.’
- **Current:** *I missed.*
- **Defect:** Implies the spear has missed its target, though it has not reached the target yet. Jin realizes his attempt cannot stop the spell.
- **Replacement:** *It’s no use.*
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence says the spearhead has not reached its target; the following lines explain that its power and speed are insufficient.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금의 진태경은, 공격은커녕 스스로의 힘으로 일어서는 것조차 버거운 상황이라는 것을 알고 있었으니까.
- **Current:** She knew Jin Taekyung was in no condition to attack, let alone stand up under his own power.
- **Defect:** Reverses the escalation: he can barely get up, much less attack.
- **Replacement:** She knew Jin Taekyung could barely get to his feet under his own power, let alone attack.
- **Rationale:** 공격은커녕 … 일어서는 것조차 makes standing up the lesser feat he can barely manage.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “네년이, 너희가 절대 날 죽일 수 없다는 것도.”
- **Current:** “You know that you—and the rest of you—can’t kill me.”
- **Defect:** Changes who knows: Jin is continuing his own answer, not telling the Grand Mage what she knows. It also drops 절대, “never.”
- **Replacement:** “I also know you—and the rest of your lot—can never kill me.”
- **Rationale:** This continues Jin’s “그래, 알지” and retains his absolute claim about the enemy.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 한 사람의 강적보다 한 명의 아군을, 수만 명이나 되는 목숨들을 지키기 위해서였으니까.
- **Current:** He’d done it to protect one ally, to protect tens of thousands of lives.
- **Defect:** Omits the explicit choice to protect an ally rather than defeat a formidable enemy.
- **Replacement:** He’d fought to protect one ally rather than bring down one formidable enemy—to protect tens of thousands of lives.
- **Rationale:** 한 사람의 강적보다 한 명의 아군을 contrasts the enemy he could kill with the ally he chooses to save.

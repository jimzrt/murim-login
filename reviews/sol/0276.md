# Review

One identity error and several actionable fidelity, cultural-reference, mechanism, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 세 사람의 당황한 시선이 우수수 날아와 꽂혔다.
- **Current:** The three men stared at me in utter confusion.
- **Defect:** The draft identifies all three people as men, but the group includes Song Song.
- **Replacement:** All three of them shot me bewildered looks.
- **Rationale:** The source says 세 사람, “three people,” and does not alter their identities or genders.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아닌데요. 뚱인데요.”
- **Current:** “Nope. I’m Fatty.”
- **Defect:** 뚱이 is the Korean name for Patrick Star, not a generic claim that the speaker is fat. The current rendering loses the cultural-reference joke.
- **Replacement:** “Nope. This is Patrick.”
- **Rationale:** The exchange echoes Patrick Star’s well-known denial gag, making Patrick the appropriate English localized name.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 서늘한 창대가 잡힘과 동시에 임영준이 비명처럼 외쳤다.
- **Current:** The moment the cool spear shaft filled my grasp, Im Yeongjun shouted like he was screaming for his life.
- **Defect:** “For his life” adds a specific implication that Im Yeongjun is terrified for his own survival. The source only describes his shout as sounding like a scream.
- **Replacement:** The moment the cool spear shaft filled my grasp, Im Yeongjun shouted, his voice rising almost to a scream.
- **Rationale:** 비명처럼 describes the sound and intensity of the shout without supplying a motive for it.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “네. 일루전(Illusion) 마법이 걸린 아티팩트를 썼는지 약간의 차이는 있었지만 확실합니다.”
- **Current:** “Yes. There were slight discrepancies, possibly because he used an artifact affected by Illusion magic, but there is no doubt.”
- **Defect:** “Affected by” is vague and misstates the construction: the artifact had Illusion magic placed on it.
- **Replacement:** “Yes. There were slight discrepancies, possibly because he used an artifact enchanted with Illusion magic, but there is no doubt.”
- **Rationale:** 마법이 걸린 describes an artifact bearing an enchantment or spell, not merely one incidentally affected by magic.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 손에 맺힌 수강(手罡)으로 철창을 가르며 정원으로 발을 디딘 그 순간.

부드러운 점막을 통과한 듯한 기분과 함께 요란한 사이렌 소리가 울려 퍼졌다.
- **Current:** The moment I cut through the iron bars with the hand force gathered in my palm and stepped into the yard, a blaring siren rang out with the sensation of passing through a soft membrane.
- **Defect:** “Hand force” is unnatural and does not clearly convey condensed Murim energy formed around his hand; “rang out with the sensation” is also an awkward attachment.
- **Replacement:** The moment I sliced through the iron bars with qi condensed around my hand and stepped into the yard, a blaring siren rang out as I felt as though I’d passed through a soft membrane.
- **Rationale:** 수강 is a condensed martial force formed at the hand, while the second clause presents the membrane-like sensation as accompanying the alarm.

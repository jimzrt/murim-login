# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it contains one altered action, one significant power-mechanism error, and several localized subject or terminology issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 경악과 의문에 사로잡힌 그녀의 옆에 비스듬히 꽂혀 있는 한 자루의 선장(禪杖)을.
- **Current:** And beside her, caught in shock and confusion, a single Zen staff planted at an angle.
- **Defect:** The dangling modifier grammatically makes the Zen staff appear caught in shock and confusion, whereas those emotions belong to the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- **Replacement:** And a single Zen staff planted at an angle beside her as she stood gripped by shock and confusion.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly assigns the shock and confusion to 그녀, not to the staff.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 아니, 눈을 감으려 했다.

모든 것이 새하얗게 물들던 그때, 손 하나 까딱할 힘조차 남아 있지 않은 몸뚱어리를 덮치는 누군가의 신형이 아니었다면 그러했을 것이다.

와락!
- **Current:** No—she tried to close them.

If not for the figure of someone crashing down over her body, striking her even though she no longer had the strength to move a finger, everything would have gone white.

Crack!
- **Defect:** The conditional consequence is reversed: the source says she would have closed her eyes if someone had not thrown themselves over her, not that the intervention prevented everything from turning white. The draft also adds that the person struck her and renders the pouncing/grabbing sound as a crack.
- **Replacement:** No—she tried to close them.

And she would have, if someone had not suddenly thrown themselves over her body—too drained to move so much as a finger—just as everything turned white.

Whump!
- **Rationale:** 그러했을 것이다 refers back to closing her eyes, while 덮치는 describes someone abruptly throwing themselves over her body. 와락 marks that sudden bodily action rather than a fracture.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그가 남천마후를 향해 쏘아 보냈던 것은 자그마치 십이성(十二成)의 경지에 이른 화염신장이다.
- **Current:** What he had fired at the Southern Heaven Demon Empress was the Flame Divine Palm at the twelfth stage.
- **Defect:** 십이성 means twelve-tenths—power driven beyond its normal limit—not the twelfth stage of a ranked technique. The draft changes both the quantity and the attack's power mechanism.
- **Replacement:** What he had unleashed at the Southern Heaven Demon Empress was the Flame Divine Palm, driven to a staggering twelve-tenths of its normal limit.
- **Rationale:** Here 성 expresses a proportion of exerted power, with 십이성 indicating 120 percent, rather than a realm or technique stage.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 귓가를 파고드는 맑은 종소리와 함께, 따스한 온기가 나를 감싸 안았다.
- **Current:** Along with the clear ringing of a bell in my ear, warm energy wrapped around me.
- **Defect:** The draft turns ordinary warmth into an unspecified energy, adding a potentially supernatural mechanism absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** Along with the clear ringing of a bell in my ear, a gentle warmth enveloped me.
- **Rationale:** 온기 means warmth; the source does not identify it as qi, internal energy, or another form of power.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그 염병할 노환이 재발했거나, 그게 아니면…….
- **Current:** Either that damned infirmity of old age had returned, or else…
- **Defect:** The established rendering of 노환 is not used.
- **Replacement:** Either those damned infirmities of old age had returned, or else…
- **Rationale:** The binding glossary establishes 노환 as “infirmities of old age,” and the plural also reads more naturally for recurring age-related ailments.

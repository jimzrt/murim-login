# Review

The draft alters three combat distances, the size of the strength gap, and a taunt about revenge.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그 격차가 설령 일초 반식이라 할지라도
- **Current:** even if it amounted to no more than a move and a half
- **Defect:** Changes a gap of one move or even half a move into one and a half moves.
- **Replacement:** even if the gap was only one move—or half a move
- **Rationale:** 일초 반식 names a move and a half-move as progressively smaller margins, not their sum.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 십여 장 밖에 우뚝 서 있던 진태경의 신형
- **Current:** Jin Taekyung’s figure, standing tall a dozen yards away
- **Defect:** Reduces a distance of more than ten jang to roughly one-third its length.
- **Replacement:** Jin Taekyung’s figure, standing tall over thirty meters away
- **Rationale:** A jang is approximately three meters.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 하긴, 나는 몰라도 검성에게 복수하려면 뭐라도 떼서 붙여야 했겠지. 안 그래?
- **Current:** Well, even if you didn’t, you’d have had to tear something off and stick it on to get revenge on the Sword Saint. Right?
- **Defect:** Turns “forget about me” into “even if you didn’t,” contradicting the preceding question about wanting strength.
- **Replacement:** Well, forget about me. To get revenge on the Sword Saint, you’d have had to tear something off and stick it on. Right?
- **Rationale:** 나는 몰라도 sets Taekyung aside as an opponent before naming the Sword Saint as the reason the Blood Lord needed the graft.

# Review

The draft omits the Blood Lord’s urge to refute Taekyung, reverses one speaker’s subject, and changes several scene details.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이 세상의 진정한 지배자이자 자신들의 신에게 바치는, 더불어 그런 그가 친히 은총을 내린 단 여섯 명의 사도(使徒)에게만 허락된 경의.
- **Current:** Reverence offered to the true ruler of this world—their god—and to the mere six Apostles to whom he had personally granted his favor.
- **Defect:** The Apostles receive the reverence in the draft; the source says they are the only people besides their god permitted to receive it.
- **Replacement:** Reverence offered to the true ruler of this world—their god—and permitted only to the six Apostles he had personally favored.
- **Rationale:** 에게만 허락된 경의 identifies who is allowed that reverence.

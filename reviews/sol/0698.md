# Review

The draft contains three critical fidelity errors involving reversed agency, an altered quantity of encounters, and a misassigned subject, plus several meaningful terminology and wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** ‘……막혔다고? 그것도 균열에서 불러온 마기(魔氣)가?’
- **Current:** *It was stopped? By the demonic qi summoned from the rift?*
- **Defect:** The draft makes the demonic qi the force that stopped the darkness, reversing the source, where the Southern Heaven Demon Empress is shocked that even the rift’s demonic qi has been stopped.
- **Replacement:** *It was blocked? Even the demonic qi summoned from the rift?*
- **Rationale:** 그것도 marks the demonic qi as the especially surprising thing that was blocked, not the agent responsible for blocking it.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 하지만 강산이 변할 만큼의 세월이 흘렀음에도 수왕석의 실체에 관한 작은 단서조차 얻지 못했고, 남천마후는 케케묵은 전설을 뒤로하고 대계(大計)를 시작해야 했다.
- **Current:** Yet even after enough time had passed for mountains and rivers to change, she had not obtained the slightest clue about the Beast King Stone’s true nature.
- **Defect:** “True nature” suggests that she found the stone but could not understand its properties. The source says she failed to uncover even the slightest trace or concrete clue concerning the stone itself.
- **Replacement:** Yet even after enough time had passed for mountains and rivers to change, she had failed to find so much as the slightest trace of the Beast King Stone itself.
- **Rationale:** 실체에 관한 작은 단서 refers to evidence or concrete information about the artifact she had spent years searching for, not merely knowledge of its nature.

## F03 — Major

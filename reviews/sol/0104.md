# Review

The draft contains one incorrect time conversion, several meaningful fidelity losses involving kinship, finances, quantity, omitted taunts, and a deliberate mispronunciation joke, plus two localized English issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 내가 저놈 형이었어야 했는데.
- **Current:** *I should have been that guy’s brother.*
- **Defect:** The translation omits that Taekyung wishes he were Mukyung’s older brother, the specific family position whose authority Mukyung obeys.
- **Replacement:** *I should’ve been that bastard’s older brother.*
- **Rationale:** The preceding and following lines establish that Mukyung is powerless specifically before his one older brother; 형 therefore carries meaningful kinship hierarchy here.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 반 시진 전만 하더라도 혁무진의 기분은 최고조에 다다라 있었다.
- **Current:** Just half an hour ago, Hyuk Mujin’s mood had reached its peak.
- **Defect:** The elapsed time is halved: half a traditional shichen is approximately one modern hour, not half an hour.
- **Replacement:** Just an hour ago, Hyuk Mujin’s mood had reached its peak.
- **Rationale:** 반 시진 denotes half of the traditional two-hour unit, so the source specifies roughly one hour.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘경비로 받은 건 딱 오십 냥이 전부인데.’
- **Current:** *The fifty nyang I received for expenses is everything I have.*

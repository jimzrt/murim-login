## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그때, 등 뒤로 인기척이 가까워지더니 나직한 목소리가 귓가를 울렸다.
- **Current:** Just then, footsteps approached from behind me, followed by a quiet voice ringing beside my ear.
- **Defect:** The source says Taekyung sensed someone approaching, not that he heard footsteps; the draft adds a specific physical action and sound.
- **Replacement:** Just then, I sensed someone approaching from behind, and a quiet voice sounded in my ear.
- **Rationale:** 인기척 denotes signs of another person's presence and does not specifically establish footsteps.

# Review

Several localized fidelity defects narrow implications, weaken a comic register contrast, or alter a physical action.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 매종학은 내가 고개를 꾸벅 숙여 보이자 아쉽다는 듯 입맛을 다신 뒤, 이번에는 적천강을 향해 말을 건넸다.
- **Current:** When I gave Mae Jonghak a small bow, he clicked his tongue as though disappointed, then spoke to Jeok Cheongang.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Mae Jonghak's physical action from smacking his lips to clicking his tongue.
- **Replacement:** When I dipped my head in greeting, Mae Jonghak smacked his lips in disappointment, then turned to Jeok Cheongang.
- **Rationale:** 입맛을 다시다 denotes smacking one's lips, while clicking one's tongue is a different gesture with different connotations.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 당 가주의 병세가 좋지 않다고 들었는데, 대대적인 원조를 떠나 우선 이렇게라도 도움을 주어야지.

- **Rationale:** 대대적인 원조를 떠나 means apart from or setting aside large-scale aid, not rather than providing it.

## F03 — Major

- **Source:** 함께 밖으로 나가 거닐지 않겠소? 이런 날도 오늘로 마지막일지도 모르는데.
- **Current:** Would you like to take a walk outside with me? This may be the last day we get to enjoy weather like this.

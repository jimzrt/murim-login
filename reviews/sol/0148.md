# Review

Several subject and referent changes alter scene facts, alongside localized register, setting, and mechanical inaccuracies.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 으허허, 우리 막내…… 아니. 제 아우가 마음에 쏙 드신 모양이군요.
- **Current:** “Uhehehe. It seems our youngest—no. It seems my younger brother made quite an impression on you.”
- **Defect:** The translation makes Hong Jin the person impressed by Taekyung, but Jin Wikyung is saying that His Highness appears to have taken a liking to his younger brother.
- **Replacement:** “Uhehehe. It seems our youngest—no. It seems His Highness has taken quite a liking to my younger brother.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 드신 is His Highness from the preceding exchange, not Hong Jin.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 무게 잡는 것도 잊고 신이 나서 떠들어 대는 진위경의 모습에 홍진이 얼굴을 굳혔다.
- **Current:** Hong Jin’s expression hardened at Jin Wikyung’s excited chatter, which had completely forgotten to maintain its dignity.
- **Defect:** The relative clause grammatically makes the chatter forget to maintain its dignity, whereas Jin Wikyung is the one who gets carried away and drops his dignified demeanor.
- **Replacement:** Hong Jin’s expression hardened as Jin Wikyung got carried away, chattering excitedly and forgetting all about maintaining his dignity.
- **Rationale:** The subject of 무게 잡는 것도 잊고 is Jin Wikyung.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 없어서 못 먹어요.
- **Current:** “I can’t drink if there’s nothing to drink.”
- **Defect:** The wording does not clearly deliver the idiomatic reversal: Hong Jin is revealing that he loves alcohol and only fails to drink when none is available.
- **Replacement:** “The only time I can’t drink is when there’s none to be had.”
- **Rationale:** 없어서 못 먹다 is a joking idiom meaning that scarcity, not reluctance or low tolerance, is the only obstacle.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 말문이 막혀 입맛만 다시던 그때, 또랑또랑하고 맑은 목소리가 울려 퍼졌다.
- **Current:** As I stood there at a loss, merely smacking my lips, a clear, ringing voice echoed through the room.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a room even though the source leaves the location unspecified and the ensuing scene describes exposed ground melting and scorching.

- **Rationale:** 울려 퍼졌다 describes the voice ringing out without identifying an enclosed setting.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 화염신장의 비급을 봤을 때보다 몇 배는 놀란 표정이다.
- **Current:** They looked several times more shocked than when they had seen the Flame Divine Palm manual.

## F07 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 놀라움을 금치 못하는 두 사람을 번갈아 보던 청풍이 해맑게 웃었다.
- **Current:** As the two men took turns staring at Cheongpung in disbelief, he smiled brightly.

- **Replacement:** Cheongpung looked back and forth between the two astonished men, then smiled brightly.
- **Rationale:** The modifier 두 사람을 번갈아 보던 directly describes Cheongpung looking alternately at the two men.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 난 그분을 만나 본 적도 없는데…… 과찬을 하셨군.
- **Current:** “I’ve never even met him… He praised you too much.”
- **Defect:** The object of the praise is reversed. Jin Mukyung means that Mae Jonghak praised him too highly, not that he praised Cheongpung.
- **Replacement:** “I’ve never even met him… He praised me too highly.”
- **Rationale:** Cheongpung has just quoted Mae Jonghak comparing him unfavorably with the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes, so Jin Mukyung interprets that comparison as praise directed at himself.

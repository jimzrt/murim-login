## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 나는 차량 내부를 볼 수 없게끔 특수처리된 창문을 바라보며 생각에 잠겼다.
- **Current:** I sank into thought as I stared through the specially treated window that kept me from seeing inside the vehicle.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the window’s function by saying it prevents Jin, who is already inside the vehicle, from seeing the interior. The treatment prevents people outside from seeing in.

- **Rationale:** The source describes a privacy-treated window through which the vehicle’s interior cannot be seen, not a window that blocks Jin’s view of the interior.

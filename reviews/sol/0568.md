# Review

One major causal error, one omitted monster classification, and two localized humor defects.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “미룰 만하니까 미루지. 내가 없어도 너 있잖아.”
- **Current:** “I put it off because it could wait. You were there even if I wasn’t.”
- **Defect:** The draft says the emergency response itself could wait, but Taekyung says he delegated the job because the Skeleton King could handle it in his place.
- **Replacement:** “I delegated it because I could. Even if I wasn’t there, they still had you.”
- **Rationale:** The following explanation confirms that Taekyung assessed the Gate and sent the Skeleton King instead of going himself; he did not postpone the emergency response.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아크 리치와의 전투를 통해 더욱 강력한 존재로 거듭났다.
- **Current:** He had possessed powerful abilities even back when he was called a Warlord, but after his battle with the Lich, he had evolved into an even more powerful being.
- **Defect:** The draft drops the source’s explicit “Arch” classification from “Arch Lich.”
- **Replacement:** He had possessed powerful abilities even back when he was called a Warlord, but after his battle with the Arch Lich, he had evolved into an even more powerful being.
- **Rationale:** 아크 리치 specifies an Arch Lich, not merely a Lich.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 존귀한 부비부비 같은 소리 하고 자빠졌네.
- **Current:** What the hell was a noble grinding session?
- **Defect:** The draft turns Taekyung’s profane, contemptuous dismissal into a genuine-sounding question.
- **Replacement:** A noble grinding session, my ass.
- **Rationale:** 같은 소리 하고 자빠졌네 rejects and mocks the preceding phrase rather than asking what it means.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 무엇 때문인지는 안 봐도 홀로그램이다.
- **Current:** I didn’t need to look to know what it was.
- **Defect:** The meaning is retained, but the draft omits the deliberate “hologram” variation on the familiar expression 안 봐도 비디오, losing the source’s joke.
- **Replacement:** I didn’t need to look to know why. The answer might as well have been projected as a hologram.
- **Rationale:** 홀로그램 is an intentional technological update to the idiom and supplies the sentence’s humor.

# Review

One critical timing error and five localized terminology, idiom, and voice issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “오랜만이네. 마지막으로 본 게 한 식경 정도 됐나?”
- **Current:** “It’s been a while. Was the last time I saw you about a couple of hours ago?”
- **Defect:** The draft changes one sikyeong—roughly half an hour—into a couple of hours, altering the elapsed time.
- **Replacement:** “It’s been a while. Has it been about half an hour since I last saw you?”
- **Rationale:** 한 식경 denotes approximately the time needed to eat a meal, conventionally around thirty minutes, not multiple hours.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 하지만 놈들은 모습을 드러내지 않은 누군가가 죄수들을 점혈해서 따로 옮겨 두었다는 것까지 예측하지 못했다.
- **Current:** What they had failed to anticipate was that someone who had not revealed himself had pressure-point struck the prisoners and moved them elsewhere.
- **Defect:** “Had pressure-point struck” is unnatural English and fails to use the established technique name.
- **Replacement:** What they had failed to anticipate was that someone unseen had used Pressure-Point Strike on the prisoners and moved them elsewhere.
- **Rationale:** 점혈 is the established Pressure-Point Strike technique, and 모습을 드러내지 않은 describes the person as unseen rather than specifically concealing his identity.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “거 참. 사람이 융통성이라는 게 있어야지, 계속 그렇게 고지식하게 살다가는 제명에 못 죽어.”
- **Current:** “You really need to show some flexibility. If you keep living so rigidly, you won’t die in your allotted time.”
- **Defect:** The literal rendering of 제명에 못 죽어 is ambiguous and misses the idiom’s clear warning that he will die prematurely.
- **Replacement:** “You really need to show some flexibility. If you keep living so rigidly, you’ll die before your time.”
- **Rationale:** 제명에 못 죽다 means to fail to live out one’s natural lifespan, which sets up the ensuing wager over who will live longer.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “조, 좆 까.”
- **Current:** “Fuck off.”
- **Defect:** The draft omits the speaker’s deliberate stammer, weakening the comic delivery that immediately precedes the killing.
- **Replacement:** “F-Fuck off.”
- **Rationale:** 조, marks a false start before the profanity and should be preserved as part of Taekyung’s delivery.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 다가가서 혈을 풀어 주자 그제야 성난 목소리가 터져 나온다.
- **Current:** I approached and released the seals. Only then did an angry voice burst forth.
- **Defect:** “The seals” is unintroduced and obscures the physical action of releasing the blocked acupoints.
- **Replacement:** I walked over and unsealed his acupoints. Only then did his angry voice burst out.
- **Rationale:** 혈을 풀어 주다 specifically means releasing the acupoints that Taekyung had just said he struck.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “후욱, 훅.”
- **Current:** “Huff. Hff.”
- **Defect:** “Hff” reads as a typographical error rather than a natural breath or snort.
- **Replacement:** “Huff. Huff.”
- **Rationale:** Both Korean sound effects represent forceful exhalations as the Heavenly Power Demon suppresses his anger.

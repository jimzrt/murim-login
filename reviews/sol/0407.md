# Review

One quantity error changes the reported enemy strength; additional issues weaken characterization, preserve unsupported ambiguity, mishandle wordplay, and use misleading or unnatural English terminology.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 「첩보대를 맡은 다니엘 이노우에는 예정된 작전 시간을 훌쩍 넘긴 여덟 시간 만에 쑤이닝시에서 탈출했고, 3만여 마리에 달하는 몬스터 군단을 발견했다고 전했소.」
- **Current:** “Daniel Inoue, who was in charge of the intelligence team, escaped from Suining City after eight hours—far beyond the scheduled operation time—and reported discovering a monster army numbering more than thirty thousand.”
- **Defect:** The source reports approximately thirty thousand monsters, not more than thirty thousand. The distinction matters because the next exchange separately establishes that the actual force may be twice that estimate.
- **Replacement:** “Daniel Inoue, who was in charge of the intelligence team, escaped from Suining City after eight hours—far beyond the scheduled operation time—and reported discovering a monster army numbering around thirty thousand.”
- **Rationale:** The approximate expression 3만여 마리 means roughly or around thirty thousand; it does not establish a lower bound above thirty thousand.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘긴장이 너무 과하면 오히려 실수를 연발하기 마련이지.’
- **Current:** *When the tension gets too high, people tend to make mistake after mistake.*
- **Defect:** The phrase is grammatically incorrect because the count noun “mistake” requires an article in this construction.
- **Replacement:** *When the tension gets too high, people tend to make one mistake after another.*
- **Rationale:** This is natural English for 실수를 연발하다 while preserving Inoue’s observation that excessive tension causes repeated mistakes.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 말없이 술잔을 기울이던 파이 첸의 한 마디에 필릭스 왕자가 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** At Faye Chen’s remark as he silently tilted his liquor glass, Prince Felix nodded.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “……!”

“자영업자신가. 왜 이렇게 자꾸 이익거려.”
- **Current:** “Profit…!”

“What are you, a small-business owner? Why do you keep yelling ‘profit’?”
- **Defect:** Wu is making an angry grunt, not literally shouting the noun “profit.” The source joke depends on the grunt 이익 sounding identical to the Korean word for profit.
- **Replacement:** “Iik…!”[^1]

“What are you, self-employed? Why do you keep going *iik*?”

[^1]: *Iik* is an angry Korean grunt that sounds identical to the Korean word for “profit.”
- **Rationale:** Retaining the grunt and briefly footnoting the homophone preserves both Wu’s anger and Jin’s self-employment joke without making Wu say something nonsensical.

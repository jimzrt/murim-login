# Review

The draft changes the reason Sima Gong acted, misattributes Taishan’s Caesar-like line, and obscures who helped whom in Taishan’s memory.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘아버지, 흑룡마문 도왔다. 그런데 죽었다. 슬프다.’
- **Current:** “Father, they helped Black Dragon Demon Gate. But they died. Sad.”
- **Defect:** Taishan is saying his father helped the Gate and then died; the draft changes both references to an unspecified plural group.
- **Replacement:** “Father helped Black Dragon Demon Gate. But he died. Taishan sad.”
- **Rationale:** The following lines establish that Taishan is looking for his father’s body so he can bury him.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 자신의 모든 것을 물려줄 후계자를 구하기 위해서?
- **Current:** To find an heir to inherit everything he had?
- **Defect:** Sima Gong considers whether he acted to save his heir, not to find one.
- **Replacement:** To save the heir who would inherit everything he had?
- **Rationale:** 구하기 위해서 means “to save”; Sama Pyo is already his chosen heir.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 카이사르 시저도 울고 갈 명언을 내뱉은 태산의 어깨에 걸터앉은 남호를 시작으로, 낯익은 얼굴들이 빛살처럼 튀어나왔다.
- **Current:** Namho, perched on Taishan’s shoulder, had just delivered a quote to rival even Julius Caesar. Then familiar faces came bursting out like streaks of light.
- **Defect:** The draft attributes the line to Namho. Taishan says it; Namho is perched on his shoulder.
- **Replacement:** Starting with Namho, perched on the shoulder of Taishan—who had just delivered a line to rival even Julius Caesar—familiar faces burst out like streaks of light.
- **Rationale:** 명언을 내뱉은 modifies 태산, not 남호.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그런 혁무진을 구하며, 단숨에 남아 있는 적들을 베어내는 송일섬과…… 잠깐, 저 새카만 털북숭이 아저씨가 누구였더라.
- **Current:** Song Ilseom rescued Hyuk Mujin and cut down the remaining enemies in a flash. And—wait, who was that black, shaggy old guy?
- **Defect:** The draft separates the shaggy man from the action, though the Korean presents him alongside Song Ilseom as they save Hyuk Mujin and cut down the enemies.
- **Replacement:** Song Ilseom and… wait, who was that black, shaggy old guy? Together, they saved Hyuk Mujin and cut down the remaining enemies in a flash.
- **Rationale:** 송일섬과 introduces the second person as Song Ilseom’s partner in the preceding action.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “흑마칠종(黑馬七宗)?”
- **Current:** “The Seven Masters of Heukma?”
- **Defect:** Romanizing 흑마 hides the Black Horse/White Horse mistake that prompts Ma Junggeol’s correction.
- **Replacement:** “The Seven Masters of the Black Horse?”
- **Rationale:** 흑마 means “black horse”; the reply explicitly corrects it to 백마, “white horse.”

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “흑마가 아니고 백마! 백마칠종의 맏형 마중걸이오! 내가 누구 때문에 이 개고생을 했는데 그걸 까먹……!”
- **Current:** “Not Heukma, Baekma! I’m Ma Junggeol, eldest of the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang! Who do you think I went through all this shit for? And you forgot me…!”
- **Defect:** The romanized correction does not let an English reader hear the horse-color contrast.
- **Replacement:** “Not Black Horse—White Horse! I’m Ma Junggeol, eldest of the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang! Who do you think I went through all this shit for? And you forgot me…!”
- **Rationale:** The humor depends on correcting 흑마 to 백마, not merely on swapping unfamiliar names.

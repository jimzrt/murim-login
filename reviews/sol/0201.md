# Review

One critical subject error, one critical relationship-reference error, and several hierarchy, register, action, and terminology issues require correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금의 태원 진가는 분명 황금 알을 품은 거위지만 종남파를 포함, 화왕을 통해 불편한 관계를 맺고 있는 거대 문파나 세가도 존재한다.
- **Current:** The Jin Family of Taiyuan was clearly a goose laying golden eggs, but it also had uncomfortable ties with powerful sects and great families—including the Zhongnan Sect—that had formed relationships with the Fire King.
- **Defect:** The relative clause incorrectly says the powerful factions had formed relationships with the Fire King. The source says the Jin Family has uneasy relations with those factions because of or through the Fire King.
- **Replacement:** The Jin Family of Taiyuan was clearly a goose laying golden eggs, but there were also powerful sects and great families—including the Zhongnan Sect—with which it had an uneasy relationship because of the Fire King.
- **Rationale:** 화왕을 통해 modifies how the Jin Family came to have 불편한 관계 with the powerful sects and families; it does not describe those factions forming relationships with Jeok Cheongang.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘많이 남겨 먹지는 못할 거요. 본가가 당신네들 손에 놀아날 일은 오지 않을 테니까.’
- **Current:** *You won’t get to keep very much of it. Our family will never be played in your hands.*
- **Defect:** The first sentence obscures that Jin Wikyung means they will not make much profit from his family, while “played in your hands” is an unnatural and unclear rendering of being manipulated.
- **Replacement:** *You won’t make that much off us. Our family will never dance to your tune.*
- **Rationale:** 남겨 먹다 refers to making a profit, and 손에 놀아나다 means being controlled or manipulated by someone.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “예. 거의 반병신이 돼서 돌아왔습니다. 해서 염치 불고하고 소가주님께 부탁을 드리는 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “No. I came back nearly crippled. That’s why I’m shamelessly asking you for a favor, Lesser Family Head.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Chief Ha the person who returned nearly crippled. The omitted subject is the subordinate he had just sent to request the meeting.
- **Replacement:** “No. My subordinate came back nearly crippled. That’s why I’m shamelessly asking you for a favor, Lesser Family Head.”
- **Rationale:** The immediately preceding 수하를 시켜 establishes the subordinate as the person sent to Jeok Cheongang and therefore the person who 돌아왔습니다 in this sentence.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “제 둘째 아우를 지도하고 계실 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “He must be instructing my second younger brother.”
- **Defect:** The literal kinship phrase is unnatural and ambiguously suggests a brother occupying the second-child position. Jin Wikyung means the second of his younger brothers, Jin Taekyung, who is his youngest brother.
- **Replacement:** “He must be instructing my youngest brother.”
- **Rationale:** 둘째 아우 denotes Jin Wikyung’s second younger brother; established family hierarchy identifies that person as his youngest brother, Jin Taekyung.

## F05 — Minor

- **Source:** “뭐, 뭡니까?”

- **Replacement:** “W-What are you doing?”
- **Rationale:** 뭐, 뭡니까 conveys a startled, stammering question in polite speech, not a profane outburst.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “……스승님이요?”
- **Current:** “……Your Master?”
- **Defect:** The possessive changes the referent, making Taekyung appear to ask about Jeok Cheongang’s master rather than incredulously echoing Jeok’s claim that Taekyung should treat him as Master.
- **Replacement:** “……Master?”
- **Rationale:** 스승님 repeats the title Jeok Cheongang has just applied to himself in relation to Taekyung; no second master is introduced.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “여하간 이리 농땡이 피우는 꼴은 못 본다. 썩 따라 나오거라.”
- **Current:** “Regardless, I won’t stand for you loafing around like this. Get out here.”

- **Rationale:** 따라 나오거라 is a forceful command to follow the speaker out.

# Review

Several localized fidelity errors affect the Slaughter Saint’s answer, the description of Tianshan, and a misheard-word joke.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 방법도, 도리도 없네. 당장 밤낮도 알 수 없는 상황에서 뭘 할 수 있단 말인가?
- **Current:** “There’s no method, and there’s no reason to it. We don’t even know day from night. What can we possibly do?”
- **Defect:** “No reason to it” describes the phenomenon as irrational; he says they have no way or recourse to deal with it.
- **Replacement:** “There’s no way forward, nothing we can do. We can’t even tell day from night. What can we possibly do?”
- **Rationale:** Both 방법 and 도리 refer here to a means of acting in their predicament, as the following question makes clear.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천산산맥은 넓다.

아니, 단순히 넓다는 표현을 넘어서 광대(廣大)하다.
- **Current:** The Tianshan Mountains were vast.

No—“vast” wasn’t enough to describe them.
- **Defect:** The draft starts with the stronger word and then rejects it, losing the source’s escalation from wide to vast.
- **Replacement:** The Tianshan Mountains were wide.

No, “wide” didn’t do them justice. They were vast.
- **Rationale:** The narrator explicitly replaces 넓다, “wide,” with the stronger 광대하다, “vast.”

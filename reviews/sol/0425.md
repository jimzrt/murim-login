# Review

Several fidelity and register issues remain, including a changed theological quantity, lost military humor, altered willingness around Erasure, an omitted physical action, and localized terminology and prose problems.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 몬스터와 인간은 신이 정한 숙적의 관계다.
- **Current:** Monsters and humans were sworn enemies, as ordained by the gods.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the singular 신 into plural gods, altering a stated world fact.
- **Replacement:** Monsters and humans were sworn enemies, as ordained by God.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly uses the singular subject 신이.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “내가 웃겠다는데 왜 지랄이야. 네가 내 맞선임이냐?”
- **Current:** “I’m saying I’ll laugh, so why the fuck do you care? Are you my senior or something?”
- **Defect:** The generic “senior” loses 맞선임’s specifically military sense of the service member directly senior to the speaker, weakening the joke and Jin’s register.
- **Replacement:** “Why the fuck do you care if I laugh? What are you, my immediate senior in the army?”
- **Rationale:** 맞선임 is a military-hierarchy term, not a generic senior person.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 아크 리치의 안광이 파르르 떨렸다. 그것은 나 역시 크게 다르지 않았다.
- **Current:** The Arch Lich’s eye-lights trembled.

I was no different.
- **Defect:** The second sentence grammatically suggests that Jin’s nonexistent eye-lights also trembled; the source means he was similarly shaken by the unexpected event.
- **Replacement:** The Arch Lich’s eye-lights trembled.

I was just as shaken.
- **Rationale:** 그것은 나 역시 크게 다르지 않았다 compares their startled reactions, not their physical eye-lights.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 어째서 소멸을 각오하면서까지 스스로 방패를 자처했느냐고.
- **Current:** Why had it chosen to become my shield even though it knew it would be erased?
- **Defect:** The draft turns the Skeleton Warlord’s resolve to face possible Erasure into certain foreknowledge that it would be erased, and it does not use the established term Erasure.
- **Replacement:** Why had it chosen to become my shield even though it had been prepared to face Erasure?
- **Rationale:** 소멸을 각오하다 means being resolved or prepared to face Erasure, not knowing with certainty that it will happen.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “많이 컸네. 우리 골골이.”
- **Current:** “You’ve gotten big, our Golgoli.”
- **Defect:** The unestablished romanization “Golgoli” leaves the affectionate bone-based nickname opaque, while “our” is unnatural English address.
- **Replacement:** “Look how much you’ve grown, Bones.”
- **Rationale:** 골골이 is an affectionate nickname for the skeleton; “Bones” conveys that function naturally without introducing an unexplained proper name.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 모든 부상과 능력치가 회복됩니다!
- **Current:** - All injuries and attributes have been restored!
- **Defect:** Injuries cannot naturally be “restored”; they are healed, while attributes are restored.
- **Replacement:** - All injuries have been healed, and all attributes restored!
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves both recoveries while giving each object its natural English verb.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 단전에서 날개를 펴고 날아오른 화룡이 불덩이가 되어 사지 백해로 솟구친다.
- **Current:** The fire dragon spread its wings and soared from my dantian, transforming into a ball of flame as it surged through my four limbs and hundred bones.
- **Defect:** “Four limbs and hundred bones” is a stiff literal calque of the idiom 사지 백해 rather than natural contemporary English.
- **Replacement:** The fire dragon spread its wings and soared from my dantian, transforming into a ball of flame as it surged through every limb and bone in my body.
- **Rationale:** 사지 백해 denotes the whole body, especially every limb and bone, rather than a literal anatomical count.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아크 리치가 발광하듯 손발을 흩뿌렸지만, 놈이 쏘아 보낸 마법은 내게 닿기도 전에 방어막처럼 주위를 둘러싼 열기와 부딪쳐 사라졌다.
- **Current:** The Arch Lich flung its hands about in a frenzy, but the magic it fired collided with the heat surrounding me like a barrier and vanished before it could even reach me.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the Arch Lich flailing its feet as well as its hands, changing the described full-body action.
- **Replacement:** The Arch Lich flailed its arms and legs in a frenzy, but the magic it fired collided with the heat surrounding me like a barrier and vanished before it could even reach me.
- **Rationale:** 손발 explicitly includes both upper and lower limbs; the Arch Lich is thrashing with its whole body.

# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes one emphatic endorsement, obscures who leads the Hebei force, and loses several characterizing cues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “약왕당주인지 뭔지 하는 저 돌팔이 말이 백번 옳다. 네 녀석은 몸이나 추스르고 있거라. 남은 일은 다른 놈들이 알아서 처리할 것인즉.”
- **Current:** “That quack calling himself the Medicine King Hall Master is right for once. You just focus on recovering. The rest will be taken care of by the others.”
- **Defect:** “Right for once” implies rare agreement; 백번 옳다 emphatically says the physician is right.
- **Replacement:** “That quack they call the Medicine King Hall Master is absolutely right. You focus on recovering. The others will take care of the rest.”
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang fully endorses the demand that Jin Wikyung rest, despite insulting the physician.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그러나 그런 그들의 선두에는, 하북팽가의 가주인 팽철영조차 감히 앞에 설 수 없는 거인이 있었다.
- **Current:** But at their head stood a giant whom even Peng Cheolyeong, Family Head of the Hebei Peng Family, could not dare to stand before.
- **Defect:** “Stand before” can suggest facing the giant rather than taking a position ahead of her.
- **Replacement:** But leading them was a giant whom even Peng Cheolyeong, Family Head of the Hebei Peng Family, would not dare step ahead of.
- **Rationale:** 앞에 설 수 없는 establishes the Bow Saint’s precedence at the head of the returning force.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그것이 수천 리 길을 마다하고 도우러 온 은인에게 할 말인가?”
- **Current:** “Is that what you say to someone who came all this way to help you?”
- **Defect:** The line drops both the journey of thousands of li and Jeong Hogun’s teasing claim to be Taekyung’s Benefactor.
- **Replacement:** “Is that what you say to a Benefactor who traveled thousands of li to help you?”
- **Rationale:** Both 수천 리 and 은인 are explicit, and the latter sets up Taekyung’s retort that he helped Jeong first.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “아니……. 그걸 왜 이제 말해, 이 새끼야.”
- **Current:** “Wait… Why the hell didn’t you tell me sooner?”
- **Defect:** The profanity moves into an adverb and drops Taekyung’s direct insult to Mujin.
- **Replacement:** “Wait… Why are you only telling me now, you bastard?”
- **Rationale:** 이 새끼야 is a direct, irritated address that punctures Taekyung’s solemn thoughts.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그게 좀, 갑자기 눈에 힘주고 멋있는 표정을 짓고 계시길래.”
- **Current:** “You suddenly got this intense look in your eyes and struck a cool pose.”
- **Defect:** A facial expression becomes a bodily pose.
- **Replacement:** “Well, you suddenly got this intense look in your eyes and started making a cool face.”
- **Rationale:** 멋있는 표정을 짓고 refers to the expression on Taekyung’s face.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 한때 광활한 초원을 아우르는 대칸을 꿈꾸었던 젊은 부족장은 힘없이 복종했다.
- **Current:** The young chieftain who had once dreamed of uniting the vast steppe as its Great Khan submitted without a word of protest.
- **Defect:** “Without a word of protest” replaces Temur’s defeated, strengthless manner with mere compliance.
- **Replacement:** The young chieftain who had once dreamed of uniting the vast steppe as its Great Khan submitted helplessly.
- **Rationale:** 힘없이 characterizes how he submits, not whether he voices an objection.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but one sentence critically misidentifies Jin Taekyung as the Supreme Peak master, and several passages alter subjects, characterization, or causal meaning.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아끼는 제자에 비하면 늙은 목숨 정도는 맡길 수 있을 것 같은데. 내가 착각한 건가?”
- **Current:** “I thought I could entrust an old life to me, compared to your cherished Disciple. Was I mistaken?”
- **Defect:** The English makes Mungyeong the person entrusting the life to himself. The source says Jeok Cheongang should be able to entrust his own old life to Mungyeong if he can entrust his cherished Disciple to him.
- **Replacement:** “I thought you could trust me with an old man’s life, if not your precious Disciple’s. Was I mistaken?”
- **Rationale:** 맡길 수 있을 것 같은데 addresses Jeok Cheongang’s ability to entrust his life to Mungyeong; the current subject and object are reversed.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “염병할. 재수 없는 살수 놈이로고.”
- **Current:** “Damn it. What an unlucky assassin bastard.”
- **Defect:** 재수 없는 is an insult meaning obnoxious or aggravating here, not a statement that the assassin has bad luck.
- **Replacement:** “Damn it. What an obnoxious bastard of an assassin.”
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang is cursing Mungyeong for being irritatingly competent, not describing his fortune.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “다짜고짜 욕을 하다니. 참으로 성질머리가 고약한 주인이군.”
- **Current:** “Cursing someone without warning. You truly are an ill-tempered master.”
- **Defect:** 주인 refers to the host or owner of the cave in the ongoing uninvited-guest exchange. “Master” introduces an unintended martial hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** “Cursing me out of nowhere. You’re one foul-tempered host.”
- **Rationale:** The line answers Jeok Cheongang’s insistence that Mungyeong is an uninvited guest, so 주인 is the corresponding host.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 제아무리 초절정 고수라고는 하나 몸에 무리가 가는 일인 것은 명백하다.
- **Current:** No matter how great a Supreme Peak master was, putting that much strain on the body was undeniably dangerous.
- **Defect:** The source says the fasting clearly burdens or strains his body; “dangerous” strengthens that observation into an explicit danger assessment.
- **Replacement:** Even for a Supreme Peak master, it was clearly taking a toll on his body.
- **Rationale:** 몸에 무리가 가다 means to overtax or place strain on the body, without necessarily asserting immediate danger.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 문경이 모르는 척 지나쳐 주길 바랐던 한 사람의 이름은 억지로 가라앉혔던 마음에 파문을 일으키기에 충분했다.
- **Current:** The name of the one person he had hoped Mungyeong would pretend not to notice was enough to send ripples through the feelings he had forcibly suppressed.
- **Defect:** The source says Jeok Cheongang hoped Mungyeong would knowingly let the subject pass, not pretend not to notice the person.
- **Replacement:** The name he had hoped Mungyeong would tactfully leave unmentioned was enough to send ripples through the emotions he had forcibly suppressed.
- **Rationale:** 모르는 척 지나쳐 주다 describes deliberately overlooking and passing over the subject in conversation.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 하늘이 내린 근골에 뛰어난 무재. 자신만의 무공을 정립한 초절정 고수라면 누구인들 제자로 탐내지 않을 수 없을 것이다.
- **Current:** With a physique granted by Heaven and extraordinary martial talent, he was a Supreme Peak master who had established his own martial arts. Who could possibly not want him as a Disciple?
- **Defect:** The draft makes Jin Taekyung a Supreme Peak master who has established his own martial arts. In the source, the Supreme Peak masters are the people who would covet the exceptionally gifted Jin Taekyung as their Disciple.
- **Replacement:** With his Heaven-given physique and extraordinary martial talent, he was someone any Supreme Peak master who had established their own martial arts would covet as a Disciple.
- **Rationale:** 자신만의 무공을 정립한 초절정 고수 is the subject of 탐내다, while Jin Taekyung is the desired Disciple.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “처음에는 그저 놀라웠고, 그다음은 안타까웠다. 노부에게 허락된 시간은 그리 많지 않았으니까.”
- **Current:** “At first, I was simply amazed. After that, I felt sorry for him. I didn’t have much time left.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns Jeok Cheongang’s sorrow or regret over his own limited remaining time into pity directed at Jin Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** “At first, I was simply amazed. Then I felt a pang of regret. I didn’t have much time left.”
- **Rationale:** The following sentence explicitly grounds 안타까웠다 in the limited time available to Jeok Cheongang, not in Jin Taekyung’s circumstances.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하지만 진태경을 만난 뒤 깨달았다. 자신이 늦은 것은 그뿐만이 아니었다는 것을.
- **Current:** But after meeting Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang realized that this was not the only thing he had been late to understand.
- **Defect:** The draft adds “to understand,” changing the point from Jeok Cheongang having left more than one matter too late to his having understood those matters belatedly.
- **Replacement:** But after meeting Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang realized he had been too late in more ways than one.
- **Rationale:** 자신이 늦은 것은 concerns Jeok Cheongang being too late to act—especially regarding a successor and his remaining time—not delayed comprehension.

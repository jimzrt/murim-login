## F01 — Major

- **Source:** 김화종은 이미 죽음을 맞이했고, 피로가 누적되어 한계에 다다른 최 팀장의 육체는 온전치 않다.

깊이를 짐작할 수 없는 슬픔과 분노도 함께.
- **Current:** Kim Hwajong had already died, and Team Leader Choi’s body was far from whole after reaching its limit beneath the accumulated fatigue.

Along with grief and rage whose depths could not be measured.
- **Defect:** “Far from whole” implies that Choi’s body is physically incomplete or mutilated, while the source says accumulated fatigue has left him in poor condition. The following sentence also obscures the causal mechanism: forcibly waking and treating him would bring a severalfold backlash.

Even if I forced him back to consciousness and gave him a potion, the backlash would hit him several times harder—along with immeasurable grief and rage.
- **Rationale:** 온전치 않다 describes Choi’s compromised physical condition, not missing bodily integrity, and 그 대가는 몇 배로 돌아와 explicitly presents the consequence as a multiplied cost or backlash.

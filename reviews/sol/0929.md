# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it omits an identifying surname and changes the sense of one collective image.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “노부는 과거 태사(太師)로서 선황을 섬겼던 이 모라고 하네. 그대의 이름은 무엇인가?”
- **Current:** “This old man once served the late Emperor as Grand Preceptor. What is your name?”
- **Defect:** The old man identifies himself as someone surnamed Lee; the draft omits his surname.
- **Replacement:** “I am Lee, once Grand Preceptor to the late Emperor. What is your name?”
- **Rationale:** 이 모 supplies his surname while leaving his given name unstated.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 어느덧 하나의 군세(軍勢)로 거듭난 그들의 손에는 흙 묻은 낫이, 붉게 녹슨 창칼과 도끼가 들려 있었다.
- **Current:** Before long, they had become an army. In their hands were dirt-stained sickles and spears and axes red with rust.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the swords among the rusted weapons.
- **Replacement:** Before long, they had become an army. In their hands were dirt-stained sickles, and spears, swords, and axes red with rust.
- **Rationale:** 창칼 includes both spears and swords.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 깊게 가라앉은 수천 쌍의 눈동자가, 어둠 속에서 빛난다.
- **Current:** Thousands of eyes, sunk deep in their faces, gleamed in the darkness.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a subdued, grave expression into a physical description of deep-set eyes and loses the pairs.
- **Replacement:** Thousands of pairs of solemn eyes gleamed in the darkness.
- **Rationale:** 깊게 가라앉은 describes the cast of their eyes here; 수천 쌍 specifies thousands of pairs.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그대는 황태제(皇太弟) 주표의 깃발을 들고 그대를 따르는 이들과 함께 역적들을 척결하라.
- **Current:** Raise the banner of Crown Prince Zhu Bao, Huangtaidi, and join with those who follow you to eliminate the traitors.
- **Defect:** “Huangtaidi” redundantly leaves the title untranslated after “Crown Prince” and makes the command awkward to read.
- **Replacement:** Raise the banner of Crown Prince Zhu Bao, and together with those who follow you, eliminate the traitors.
- **Rationale:** The packet’s established rendering of 황태제 in this succession context is “Crown Prince.”

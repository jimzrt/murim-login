# Review

The draft contains two scene-fact errors involving the battle site and the number of stretchers, plus several mistranslated jokes, omitted action details, and localized register issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 아니 그러니까 뭘 구조하냐고. 시체 구조하면 그게 구조냐, 수습이지.
- **Current:** That’s what I’m saying—what are they rescuing? If they’re rescuing a corpse, that isn’t a rescue. It’s cleanup.
- **Defect:** “Cleanup” is broader and more dismissive than 수습, which specifically contrasts rescuing a living person with recovering a corpse.
- **Replacement:** That’s what I’m saying—what are they rescuing? You don’t rescue a corpse. You recover it.
- **Rationale:** The source hinges on the distinction between 구조, rescue, and 수습, recovery of the dead.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 누군가의 일침처럼 분위기는 생각보다 심각했다.
- **Current:** Like someone’s sharp rebuke, the situation was more serious than expected.
- **Defect:** The comparison is attached to the situation itself, producing an illogical image instead of attributing the observation to the preceding commenter.
- **Replacement:** As one commenter had bluntly pointed out, the situation was more serious than expected.
- **Rationale:** 누군가의 일침처럼 refers back to the pointed chat remark that exposed the gravity of the situation.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 근데 왜 아무도 안 와. 전투지도 게이트에서 엎어지면 코 닿을 거리라며.
- **Current:** Then why hasn’t anyone come out? They said the Battle Map Gate is close enough to touch with your nose if you fell flat on your face.
- **Defect:** The draft misparses 전투지도 as a proper name, “Battle Map Gate.” The source says the battle site is extremely close to the Gate.
- **Replacement:** Then why hasn’t anyone come out? They said the battle site was just a stone’s throw from the Gate.
- **Rationale:** 전투지 is the battle site, while 도 is the topic particle; the sentence describes the distance between that site and the Gate.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 그들의 손에는 커다란 천에 덮인 들것이 들려 있었다. 걸음에 들것이 흔들리자 피에 젖은 손 하나가 천 밖으로 툭 밀려 나왔다.

# Review

Several passages change who knew what or imply conclusions the source has not established; one reverses the Bow Saint’s initial reaction to Taekyung.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 극소수의 인물만이 알고 있는 내 특별함이 어찌 ‘저들’에게까지 알려졌는지에 대한 것이었다.
- **Current:** how had the handful of people who knew about my unusual power managed to let *them* find out?
- **Defect:** Assigns responsibility for the disclosure to the handful of people close to Taekyung. The source only asks how the information reached the Bow Saint and Martial God.
- **Replacement:** how had *they* learned about something only a handful of people knew about me?
- **Rationale:** 어찌 ‘저들’에게까지 알려졌는지 leaves the route of disclosure unknown.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 처음에는 낯설면서도 관심이 가지 않았다.
- **Current:** At first, I found the news strange, but interesting.
- **Defect:** Reverses her initial lack of interest into interest.
- **Replacement:** At first, the names were unfamiliar, and they didn’t interest me.
- **Rationale:** 관심이 가지 않았다 explicitly says she was not interested.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 그리고 그 선택받은 자는 비단 어느 한 사람만을 지칭하는 것이 아니었지.
- **Current:** And the chosen one didn’t refer to just one person.
- **Defect:** Suggests that multiple people are the chosen one, rather than that the description did not identify one particular person.
- **Replacement:** And ‘the chosen one’ didn’t point to any one specific person.
- **Rationale:** Her subsequent search weighs possible candidates; she has not established that there are multiple chosen ones.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그분은, 무신이니까.
- **Current:** Because the Martial God had said so.
- **Defect:** Turns her faith in who he is into reliance on something he said.
- **Replacement:** Because he was the Martial God.
- **Rationale:** The source makes his identity itself the reason she could not dismiss the letter.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “그 사람. 아니, 그분이 어떻게…….”
- **Current:** “How does that person—no, that *His*—”
- **Defect:** The interrupted question is broken English and loses Taekyung’s audible correction to a more respectful way of referring to the Martial God.
- **Replacement:** “How could that man—no, how could the Martial God…”
- **Rationale:** 그 사람. 아니, 그분 marks a deliberate shift in deference before the Bow Saint finishes his question.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** 그분께서 준비하신 안배(按排). 아니, 어쩌면 일종의 예언(豫言).
- **Current:** “A contingency he had prepared. No—perhaps a kind of prophecy.”
- **Defect:** “Contingency” implies a backup measure rather than an arrangement or plan.
- **Replacement:** “An arrangement he had made. No—perhaps a kind of prophecy.”
- **Rationale:** 안배 describes something deliberately arranged; the source does not say it was a fallback.

# Review

The draft contains one critical subject error, several unsupported causal or situational inferences, and localized omissions or wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 화왕의 독문무공을 훔쳐봤다가는 뜨거운 맛을 보게 될 거다. 온몸이 불탈 정도의 뜨거운 맛을.
- **Current:** If I took a peek at the Fire King’s personal martial art, I would be made to feel the heat. Heat hot enough to burn my entire body.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the subject of the warning to Taekyung, even though the context explains what would happen to anyone, particularly Wipeng, who secretly inspected the Fire King’s exclusive martial art.
- **Replacement:** Anyone who stole a look at the Fire King’s signature martial art would get a taste of the heat—enough heat to burn their whole body.
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is generic in context, while 훔쳐봤다가는 describes the consequence of secretly looking at the martial art rather than something Taekyung himself intends to do.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 듣기로는 여간 괴팍한 성정이 아니던데, 본가에 화가 미치지는 않을까 걱정입니다.
- **Current:** I’ve heard he has an extremely eccentric temperament. I’m worried that his anger might reach our family.
- **Defect:** The draft narrows 화가 미치다, meaning that calamity or harm might befall the family, into the Fire King’s personal anger.
- **Replacement:** I’ve heard he has an exceptionally eccentric temperament. I’m worried he might bring calamity down on our family.
- **Rationale:** 화 here denotes trouble or calamity reaching the Jin Family; the source does not specifically identify anger as the danger.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 저라도 신경 써 드려야 하지 않겠습니까. 주모(主母)도 안 계시는 마당에.
- **Current:** Shouldn’t I at least worry about you, with the Lady of the House away?
- **Defect:** The draft asserts that the Lady of the House is temporarily away, although the source only says she is not present and leaves the reason unspecified.
- **Replacement:** Shouldn’t I at least look after you, with the Lady of the House not here?
- **Rationale:** 안 계시는 does not establish a temporary trip or absence, and 신경 써 드리다 is more naturally rendered as looking after someone in this exchange.

## F04 — Major

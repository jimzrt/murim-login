# Review

The draft contains one critical action/subject error, several meaningful losses in combat mechanics and viewpoint, and localized register and prose issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 미사일도 우습게 막아 내는 비늘을 단번에 가르고 들어간 창날은 단순히 스친 것만으로도 수십 킬로그램의 살점을 베어 냈다.
- **Current:** The spearhead, attached to scales that could easily stop a missile, sliced straight through them. Merely grazing Leviathan was enough to cut away dozens of kilograms of flesh.
- **Defect:** The draft says the spearhead is attached to Leviathan’s scales, reversing the grammatical relationship and changing the physical action. The spearhead cuts through the scales.
- **Replacement:** The spearhead sliced clean through scales that could shrug off missiles. A mere graze sheared dozens of kilograms of flesh from Leviathan.
- **Rationale:** The source makes the missile-resistant scales the object cut through by the spearhead, not something attached to it.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 비록 상처는 아물지 않았으나, 마력의 질과 양은 하루 전에 비할 바가 아니다.
- **Current:** Its wounds had not yet healed, but the quality and quantity of its magical power were incomparable to the day before.
- **Defect:** The comparison is grammatically malformed and loses the source’s directional meaning that Leviathan’s magical power is now far greater than it was a day ago.
- **Replacement:** Its wounds had not yet healed, but both the quality and quantity of its magical power far surpassed what they had been a day earlier.
- **Rationale:** 비할 바가 아니다 here means the current power is beyond comparison with, and specifically superior to, its prior state.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 처음부터 존재하지 않았다면 모를까, 지능을 제외한 모든 능력치가 대폭 하락하면서 커다란 공백이 생긴 신체가 머리에서 전달된 명령을 제대로 수행하지 못하고 있었다.
- **Current:** If the condition had never existed in the first place, that would have been one thing. But with every attribute except Intelligence drastically reduced, his body had been left with a massive gap and was failing to properly carry out the commands sent by his mind.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the nonexistent thing the debuff itself, which obscures the mechanism. The point is that Jin’s body had once possessed the now-lost capabilities, so their sudden reduction left a gap between his commands and what his body could execute.
- **Replacement:** It might have been different if he had never possessed those capabilities in the first place. But with every attribute except Intelligence drastically reduced, the resulting void left his body unable to properly carry out the commands from his mind.
- **Rationale:** The contrast introduced by 처음부터 존재하지 않았다면 모를까 explains why losing established physical capabilities is more disruptive than never having possessed them.

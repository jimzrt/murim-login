# Review

Several errors change age, sequence, address, and the subject of a physical transformation; there are also localized losses of quantity and setting.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 고작 이 년 전에야 약관을 넘긴 저 핏덩이에게 있어
- **Current:** For that callow brat, who’d only turned twenty-two two years ago,
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung twenty-two two years earlier. The source says he passed the age of twenty only two years earlier.
- **Replacement:** For that callow brat, who’d only passed twenty two years ago,
- **Rationale:** 약관 is the age of twenty; 이 년 전에야 places that milestone two years ago.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 파육음과 함께 전신 곳곳으로 번져가는 잔 떨림.

자신도 겪지 못한 환골탈태(換骨奪胎)의 과정에 들어선 진태경의 모습이
- **Current:** With a soft tearing sound, tremors spread through his body.
- **Defect:** After two sentences about Peng’s posture and gaze, “his body” points to Peng. The trembling accompanies Jin Taekyung’s Bone Transformation.
- **Replacement:** With the sound of flesh tearing, fine tremors spread throughout Jin Taekyung’s body.
- **Rationale:** The following sentence identifies Jin as the one undergoing the transformation Peng is watching.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “적 형만큼은…… 변치 마시오.”
- **Current:** “More than anyone… don’t change, Jeok hyung.”
- **Defect:** “More than anyone” turns a plea that Jeok, at least, remain unchanged into a comparison with other people.
- **Replacement:** “You, at least… don’t change, Jeok hyung.”
- **Rationale:** 만큼은 marks Jeok as the person Peng particularly asks not to change, without ranking him against others.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 앞서 어둠을 집어삼킨 겁화(劫火)와 벽력(霹靂)의 싸움은 치열했고, 또한 처절했다.
- **Current:** The hellfire that had swallowed the darkness and the thunderbolt that followed fought fiercely—and bitterly.
- **Defect:** “That followed” adds a sequence for the lightning that the source does not give; the two forces are already intertwined when they clash.
- **Replacement:** The fight between the hellfire that had swallowed the darkness and the lightning was fierce—and desperate.
- **Rationale:** 앞서 describes when the hellfire swallowed the darkness, not when the lightning appeared.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 일대를 호령하는 하북팽가의 핏줄로 태어나, 남부러울 것 없이 흘러간 유년 시절.
- **Current:** Born into the bloodline of the Hebei Peng Family, which ruled over its generation, he’d spent a childhood with nothing to envy.
- **Defect:** “Its generation” makes 일대 temporal here; it refers to the family’s dominance over an area.
- **Replacement:** Born into the Hebei Peng Family, which commanded the region, he’d spent a childhood wanting for nothing.
- **Rationale:** 일대를 호령하는 describes regional standing, while 남부러울 것 없이 describes his privileged childhood.

- **Current:** Why did those two simple syllables make something stir in Jeok Cheongang’s chest?

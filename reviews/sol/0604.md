# Review

Several physical details, quantities, pragmatic cues, and culturally specific jokes are altered or weakened.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 삼 갑자의 열양지기가 불꽃으로 화한다. 청백색의 화염이 깃든 두 손을 말없이 내려다보던 나는, 지그시 눈을 감은 채 손을 뻗었다.
- **Current:** Three jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi transformed into flame. I silently looked down at my hands, wrapped in bluish-white fire, then closed my eyes and reached out.
- **Defect:** The draft says the fire has already wrapped around his hands, prematurely completing the encircling action that occurs only after he concentrates and channels his internal energy.
- **Replacement:** Three jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi transformed into flame. I silently looked down at my hands, bluish-white flames flickering over them, then closed my eyes and reached out.
- **Rationale:** 깃든 indicates that the flames have manifested in or upon his hands, while the subsequent 완전히 휘감는다 explicitly marks the later point when they completely envelop his hand.

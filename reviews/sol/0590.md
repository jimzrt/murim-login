# Review

Several localized fidelity and register issues, including one altered quantity, an invented movement, softened profanity, and a garbled description of the statue.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 겹겹이 설치된 강력한 방어 마법도, 단단하기 그지없는 마법 재료로 만들어진 천장도 초고온의 열기에 무너지고 녹아내렸다.
- **Current:** The powerful defensive Magic layered throughout the area collapsed beneath the ultra-high temperature. Even the ceiling, made from exceptionally durable magical materials, melted away.
- **Defect:** The source says both the layered defensive Magic and the ceiling collapsed and melted; the draft assigns only one destructive effect to each.
- **Replacement:** Layer after layer of powerful defensive Magic—and even the ceiling, made from exceptionally durable magical materials—collapsed and melted beneath the extreme heat.
- **Rationale:** The shared predicates 무너지고 녹아내렸다 apply to both subjects.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 복도의 끝에서 십여 명의 힐러가 입을 딱 벌린 채 자신들의 눈 앞에 펼쳐진 광경을 바라보고 있었다.
- **Current:** At the end of the corridor, more than a dozen healers stood with their mouths hanging open, staring at the scene before them.
- **Defect:** 십여 명 means roughly ten or a dozen, not more than twelve; the draft increases the stated number.
- **Replacement:** At the end of the corridor, a dozen or so healers stood with their mouths hanging open, staring at the scene before them.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the source's approximate quantity without imposing a minimum of thirteen.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 코드 레드. 그 네 글자에 나 죽이겠다고 우르르 몰려온 인간들이 할 소린 아닌 것 같은데.
- **Current:** “I don’t think people who came swarming over to kill me because of those four letters—Code Red—have the right to say that.”
- **Defect:** Code Red does not consist of four English letters; mechanically retaining the Korean character count produces a false and distracting statement.
- **Replacement:** “I don’t think people who came swarming over to kill me over those words—Code Red—have any right to say that.”
- **Rationale:** 그 네 글자 refers to the four Korean written syllable blocks in 코드 레드; “those words” preserves the rhetorical reference naturally in English.

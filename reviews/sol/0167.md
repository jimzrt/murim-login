# Review

Actionable fidelity errors include two reversed subjects or actions, a lost time quantity, misleading terminology, an untranslated gambling reference, and prohibited System-panel brackets.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이거, 지금 분위기로 봐서는 죽었다 깨어나는 한이 있어도 내 의뢰는 안 받아 줄 기세다.
- **Current:** Judging by the atmosphere, he looked ready to refuse my commission even if he had to die and come back to life.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the dying and returning to life to Jang Taebo, but the implied subject is the narrator: Jang Taebo would refuse even if the narrator died and came back.
- **Replacement:** Judging by the way things were going, he wouldn’t accept my commission even if I died and came back to life.
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject belongs to the narrator in the idiomatic construction 죽었다 깨어나는 한이 있어도; assigning it to Jang Taebo reverses who would undergo the hypothetical ordeal.

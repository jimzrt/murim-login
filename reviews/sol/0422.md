# Review

Several actionable fidelity issues remain, including altered System mechanics, a reversed spell tradeoff, omitted dialogue and sound cues, and a missing named technique.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** - 레벨 업의 효과로 모든 상태 이상과 피로, 일부 부상이 회복됩니다!
- **Current:** - As an effect of leveling up, all status effects and fatigue, as well as some injuries, have been recovered!
- **Defect:** The translation says all status effects are recovered, which can include beneficial effects, while 상태 이상 specifically means abnormal or adverse status conditions. It also unnaturally treats fatigue and status ailments as things being recovered.
- **Replacement:** - As an effect of leveling up, all status ailments and fatigue have been cleared, and some injuries have healed!
- **Rationale:** The System notice states that adverse conditions and fatigue are removed and some injuries are healed.

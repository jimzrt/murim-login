# Review

Two casualty figures are rendered as deaths, and Furin’s shift to respectful address is lost.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 현재까지의 추정 사상자만 무려 5천만.
- **Current:** The estimated death toll alone had already reached fifty million.
- **Defect:** 사상자 counts the dead and injured, not deaths alone.
- **Replacement:** Estimates already put the number of dead and injured at fifty million.
- **Rationale:** The draft changes the scale of confirmed deaths.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 놈과의 전투가 있던 날, 백만에 달하는 인명 피해가 있었지. 무려 네 명의 S급이 지휘하던 천 명의 헌터들도 함께 죽었고.
- **Current:** “A million people died the day we fought it. A thousand Hunters, led by no fewer than four S-ranks, died as well.”
- **Defect:** 인명 피해 means casualties, not deaths; “we fought it” also places Furin among the combatants without source support.
- **Replacement:** “The day of the battle against it, there were a million casualties. A thousand Hunters led by no fewer than four S-ranks died as well.”
- **Rationale:** The source gives a casualty figure for the battle and does not identify Furin as a participant.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “그렇다면 너, 아니 당신은 지금 말한 그 이계에서 온 건가?”
- **Current:** “Then you—you came from the other world you mentioned?”
- **Defect:** Furin corrects his address from familiar 너 to more respectful 당신; repeating “you” loses the deliberate adjustment.
- **Replacement:** “Then you—no, sir. Did you come from the other world you mentioned?”
- **Rationale:** The correction marks Furin’s attempt to address Morgoth more respectfully as he sees a chance to negotiate.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 약 일 년 전, 아크 리치의 출현을 시작으로 여러 대사건이 있었지만, 지난 열흘간 벌어진 일들에 비하면 새 발의 피에 불과했다.
- **Current:** There had been several major incidents over the past year, beginning with the appearance of the Lich. But compared with what had happened over the last ten days, they were a drop in the bucket.
- **Defect:** 아크 리치 loses its Arch designation.
- **Replacement:** There had been several major incidents since the Arch Lich appeared about a year ago. But compared with what had happened over the last ten days, they were a drop in the bucket.
- **Rationale:** The source names the Arch Lich and dates its appearance to approximately a year earlier.

# Review

One reversed action and several material errors in the battle mechanics and physical details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 사형들을 비난하는 이들을 막아 세우고 변호했다.
- **Current:** He’d stopped and defended those who criticized his Senior Brothers.
- **Defect:** The draft makes him defend the critics rather than his Senior Brothers.
- **Replacement:** He’d stood up to those who criticized his Senior Brothers and defended them.
- **Rationale:** He stops the critics and speaks in defense of his Senior Brothers.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그 어느 때보다 거대한, 그렇기에 아직 절반도 채 응집되지 못한 일섬
- **Current:** a One Annihilation larger than ever before—yet not even half-compressed—
- **Defect:** “Compressed” changes the mechanism, and “yet” loses the causal link between its size and its incomplete formation.
- **Replacement:** a One Annihilation larger than ever before, and therefore not even half-formed,
- **Rationale:** The strike has not yet gathered even halfway because it is so immense.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이제는 검자루밖에 남지 않은 애검을 으스러져라 움켜쥐었다.
- **Current:** gripped what was left of his beloved sword so tightly it seemed ready to break.
- **Defect:** The draft omits that only the hilt remains.
- **Replacement:** gripped the hilt—all that remained of his beloved sword—so tightly it seemed ready to break.
- **Rationale:** 검자루밖에 남지 않은 specifies the sword’s physical state before his final attack.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 이미 허공을 밟으며 쏘아지던 풍운검군의 일격은 섬광을 비집고 나아갔다는 것.
- **Current:** while the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord’s strike, already flying through the air, slipped through the flash.
- **Defect:** “The flash” points back to the distant blast, obscuring that his strike passes through the two opponents’ attacks.
- **Replacement:** while the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord’s strike, already hurtling through the air, slipped between their two streaks of light.
- **Rationale:** The preceding streaks are the Black Ghosts’ ax and sword attacks; the shock wave throws them off balance and creates an opening.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 정의(情義)라는 두 글자를 잊은 적은 없었다.
- **Current:** he’d never forgotten those two words: loyalty and honor.
- **Defect:** The rendering replaces 情義—the bonds of affection and duty toward others—with abstract personal honor.
- **Replacement:** he’d never forgotten the bonds of affection and loyalty.
- **Rationale:** 情義 describes human ties and the obligations they carry, central to his refusal to abandon the allies still fighting.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 단내가 풀풀 풍기는 숨결이 느릿하게 흩어진다.
- **Current:** His breath, thick with the smell of blood, slowly dispersed.
- **Defect:** The draft substitutes the smell of blood for the sweetish smell specified in the source.
- **Replacement:** His sweetish-smelling breath slowly dispersed.
- **Rationale:** 단내 is a sweetish smell, not the smell of blood.

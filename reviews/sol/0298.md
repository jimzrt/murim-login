# Review

One meaningful historical-metaphor mistranslation, two localized voice/idiom issues, one altered wordplay exchange, and three System-panel formatting violations.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** - 마마 잃은 중천공? 별 희한한 말도 있군.

“마마를 왜 잃어. 패드립 치네 시벌놈이. 확 그냥 소멸시켜 버릴까 보다.”
- **Current:** > - A sky that lost its mama? What a strange saying.

“Why would the sky lose its mama? Are you insulting my mother, you bastard? Maybe I should just destroy you right now.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents the sky as the one that lost its mother and changes Taekyung’s accusation of a crude mom joke into a direct insult against his own mother. It also softens the profanity.
- **Replacement:** > - A man who lost his mama? What a strange saying.

“Why would he lose his mama? Making mom jokes now, you fucking bastard? Maybe I should just destroy you.”
- **Rationale:** The Warlord mangles the proverb into a phrase about someone losing his mother, and Taekyung reacts by accusing him of 패드립, a crude joke or insult involving someone’s parents.

# Review

One hierarchy error, one altered quantity, two lost pragmatic cues, and two footnote-related issues are actionable.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “그럼 꺽정 아저씨부터 깨워 줄래요?”
- **Current:** “Then could you wake up Kkeokjeong Hyung-nim first?”
- **Defect:** Song Song addresses Kkeokjeong as 아저씨, but the draft gives her Taekyung’s male junior-to-senior address, changing their relationship and her voice.
- **Replacement:** “Then could you wake Uncle Kkeokjeong first?”
- **Rationale:** The established alias “Uncle Kkeokjeong” preserves her distinct 아저씨 address without assigning her Taekyung’s Hyung-nim hierarchy.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 동시에 마나를 머금은 이십여 발의 화살이 선두에 선 미노타우로스의 머리에 꽂혔다.
- **Current:** At the same time, more than twenty arrows infused with mana struck the head of the lead Minotaur.
- **Defect:** “More than twenty” makes the quantity strictly greater than twenty, while 이십여 발 means approximately twenty.
- **Replacement:** At the same time, around twenty arrows infused with mana struck the head of the lead Minotaur.
- **Rationale:** “Around twenty” preserves the source’s approximate quantity.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 젠장. 좋다 말았네. 내 인생이 이렇지 뭐.
- **Current:** Damn it. I’d been happy for nothing. That was just how my life went.
- **Defect:** 좋다 말았네 means his excitement was abruptly cut short, not that his happiness had been baseless.
- **Replacement:** Damn it. And just when I was getting excited. That was my life for you.
- **Rationale:** The replacement restores the comic beat of anticipation being immediately spoiled and retains Taekyung’s self-deprecating register.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그나저나 송이 씨가 해장국이라니.
- **Current:** Anyway, Miss Song had ordered hangover soup.
- **Defect:** The plain statement omits the surprised, incongruous reaction conveyed by 라니.
- **Replacement:** Still, I never would’ve expected Miss Song to order hangover soup.
- **Rationale:** The source marks her choice as surprising before contrasting it with her pampered appearance.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 테이블에 올라온 것은 뜨끈한 해장국이었다.
- **Current:** But what arrived on the table was a steaming bowl of haejangguk, a Korean soup traditionally eaten to ease a hangover.[^1]
- **Defect:** The inline explanation duplicates footnote 1 and adds an expository gloss directly to the narration.
- **Replacement:** But what arrived on the table was a steaming bowl of haejangguk.[^1]
- **Rationale:** The existing footnote already supplies the useful cultural explanation, so the reading copy should retain only the source’s reveal.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “어디긴, 어제에 이어서 또 달려야지. 오늘은 막걸리 어때? 내가 잘하는 집 알아.”
- **Current:** “Where else? We’ve got to keep the party going from yesterday. How about makgeolli today? I know a place that makes it well.”
- **Defect:** The makgeolli footnote is left unreferenced, and “a place that makes it well” awkwardly suggests production rather than a good establishment.
- **Replacement:** “Where else? We’ve got to keep the party going from yesterday. How about makgeolli today?[^2] I know a good place.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement activates the existing cultural footnote and naturally renders 잘하는 집 as a place known for serving it well.

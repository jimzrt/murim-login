# Review

The draft changes the archers’ firing angle and the result of a saber strike, and misstates what fills Taekyung’s hand.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 촘촘한 화망(火網)을 갖춘 채 직사에 가깝게 떨어져 내리는 화살 비로 암천의 교도들을 저지하는 한편
- **Current:** We’d formed a dense wall of arrows, firing them at a near-vertical angle to stop the Dark Heaven followers, while bringing out the countermeasures we’d prepared for the monsters.
- **Defect:** “Near-vertical” reverses 직사, which describes nearly direct fire.
- **Replacement:** We held back the Dark Heaven followers with a dense rain of arrows fired on nearly direct trajectories, while bringing out the countermeasures we’d prepared for the monsters.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies 직사에 가깝게, not steeply arcing or vertical fire.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 시스템 알림과 함께 텅 비어 있던 손아귀를 채우는 단단한 촉감.
- **Current:** A solid touch filled my empty hand with a System notification.
- **Defect:** The notification accompanies the solid object appearing in his hand; it does not fill his hand.
- **Replacement:** Along with the System notification came the solid feel of something filling my empty hand.
- **Rationale:** 와 함께 links the notification to the sensation; 텅 비어 있던 손아귀를 채우는 describes what he feels in his hand.

# Review

Several action details and one central comparison change the source meaning.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 만약 곧바로 맞받아치는 것으로 대응했다면, 그것만으로도 분명 상당한 힘이 소모되었을 테니까.
- **Current:** If I’d met the attack head-on, it would have cost me a considerable amount of strength just to stop it.
- **Defect:** Changes an immediate counterattack into merely stopping the attack.
- **Replacement:** If I’d counterattacked at once, even that would have cost me a considerable amount of strength.
- **Rationale:** 맞받아치다 specifies striking back, not just blocking or stopping.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 휘황한 섬광이 반경 십여 장을 감싸 안고
- **Current:** A brilliant flash enveloped an area more than ten *jang* across.
- **Defect:** Turns a radius of more than ten jang into a total width of more than ten jang.
- **Replacement:** A brilliant flash enveloped everything within a radius of more than ten *jang*.
- **Rationale:** 반경 explicitly means radius.

## F03 — Major

- **Current:** I didn’t miss the opening I hadn’t seen before—the one they’d exposed themselves.

- **Rationale:** 보이지 않던 describes the opening; 스스로 드러낸 identifies the Black Ghosts’ action.

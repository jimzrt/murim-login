# Review

The draft contains one reversed subject, several losses of source-specific meaning and humor, and two System-panel formatting violations.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 통화 상대가 누군지는 모르지만 물건 잘 갖고 있다면서, 본인도 슬쩍 꺼내서 확인했다고 합니다. 준수가 직접 봤다니까 확실합니다.
- **Current:** Yes. We don’t know who he was talking to, but the other person said he was keeping the item safe and had even taken it out to check it himself. Junsu saw it directly, so it’s certain.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the subject of both actions, attributing possession and inspection of the USB to the person on the other end of the call rather than to Jin Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** Yes. We don’t know who he was talking to, but he said he was keeping the item safe, and he even slipped it out to check it himself. Junsu saw it directly, so it’s certain.
- **Rationale:** 본인도 identifies the target himself as the person who took out and checked the USB; Junsu directly witnessed that action.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그런 거면 진짜 특급 정보죠.
- **Current:** Then it’s truly top-secret information.
- **Defect:** “Top-secret” introduces an official secrecy classification absent from 특급, which describes the information as exceptionally valuable or first-rate.
- **Replacement:** Then that’s seriously high-value intel.
- **Rationale:** The speakers are excited about the information’s value as leverage for bonuses and promotions, not assigning it a formal security classification.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 옛말에 이르기를 쇠뿔도 단김에 빼라고 했다. 그에게 있어 C급 헌터는 한 손으로도 뽑을 수 있을 만큼 물렁한 뿔이다.
- **Current:** There was an old saying: strike while the iron is hot. To him, a C-rank Hunter was a horn soft enough to pull out with one hand.
- **Defect:** Replacing the Korean ox-horn proverb with an English iron proverb severs the deliberate horn callback in the following sentence.
- **Replacement:** As the old saying went, you had to pull the ox’s horn while it was hot. To him, a C-rank Hunter was a soft horn he could yank out one-handed.
- **Rationale:** Both sentences use 뿔 so that the Team Leader’s confidence becomes a literal extension of the proverb; retaining the horn is necessary to preserve the joke.

# Review

The draft contains several action, subject, dialogue, terminology, and register errors, particularly around Hong Jin’s castration disclosure.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “다녀오겠습니다, 전하.”
- **Current:** “We’ll be going now, Your Highness.”
- **Defect:** The translation changes Hong Jin’s first-person promise to return into a plural departure announcement.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll return, Your Highness.”
- **Rationale:** 다녀오겠습니다 indicates that the speaker is leaving temporarily and will come back; it does not mean merely that a group is departing.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “귀빈이시다. 태원진가까지 모셔다드려라.”
- **Current:** “They are honored guests. Escort them to the Jin Family of Taiyuan.”
- **Defect:** The singular honored guest and singular escort order are changed into plural references, extending Li Feng’s formal designation to the entire party.
- **Replacement:** “He is an honored guest. Escort him to the Jin Family of Taiyuan.”
- **Rationale:** 귀빈이시다 and 모셔다드려라 both refer honorifically to one person, Hong Jin.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “왜, 뭐.”

“저, 저희도 가는 길인데요.”
- **Current:** “We’re going the same way.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung’s intervening response is omitted, and the heirs’ stammer is lost, flattening both the speaker exchange and their nervousness.
- **Replacement:** “Why? What is it?”

“W-we’re going the same way.”
- **Rationale:** The source contains two separate turns: Taekyung asks what is wrong, and one of the heirs hesitantly explains that they are headed the same way.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** ‘아예 지금 확 바꿔 버려?’
- **Current:** *Should I make a clean switch right now?*
- **Defect:** The wording obscures that Taekyung is considering redeeming the token immediately, not generally switching weapons or plans.
- **Replacement:** *Should I just trade it in right now?*
- **Rationale:** 바꿔 버려 refers directly to exchanging the token for the weapon he has just considered.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 확 그냥 무릎을 부숴 버릴까 보다.
- **Current:** *I might just smash that lap.*
- **Defect:** The threatened body part is changed from Hong Jin’s knee to his lap.
- **Replacement:** *Maybe I should just smash his knee.*
- **Rationale:** 무릎 means “knee”; “lap” changes the physical action and produces an unintended image.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘내관 출신이라고 했지.’

내관이면 내시 아닌가?
- **Current:** *He said he used to be a palace eunuch.*

A eunuch meant a castrated man, didn’t it?
- **Defect:** The draft collapses palace attendant, eunuch, and castrated man into one term, eliminating the narrator’s reasoning and creating a contradiction with his next observation that not every eunuch was necessarily castrated.
- **Replacement:** *He said he used to be a palace attendant.*

Didn’t that make him a eunuch?
- **Rationale:** The source first identifies Hong Jin as a former 내관, then has Taekyung infer that this would make him a 내시; his physical status remains uncertain until Hong Jin discloses it.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “고자라고.”
- **Current:** “I’m a eunuch.”
- **Defect:** The occupational designation “eunuch” replaces Hong Jin’s explicit confirmation that he has been castrated, undermining the distinction established in the preceding narration.
- **Replacement:** “I’ve been castrated.”
- **Rationale:** 고자 identifies his physical condition, while 내시 is the separate institutional term discussed immediately beforehand.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “고자가 뭐예요?”
- **Current:** “What’s a eunuch?”
- **Defect:** Cheongpung asks about the meaning of Hong Jin’s statement that he is castrated, not about the already introduced occupation of eunuch.
- **Replacement:** “What does ‘castrated’ mean?”
- **Rationale:** The repeated word 고자 links Cheongpung’s question directly to Hong Jin’s disclosure.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 고추가 없다잖아, 고추가!
- **Current:** *He said he doesn’t have a dick, a dick!*
- **Defect:** The childish euphemism 고추 is intensified into the overtly vulgar “dick.”
- **Replacement:** *He said he doesn’t have his thing—his thing!*
- **Rationale:** The joke uses a euphemistic, child-accessible word because Cheongpung does not understand castration; stronger profanity changes the register.

## F10 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 알려 줘 봤자 ‘와, 저 고추 없는 사람 처음 봐요!’ 이딴 소리 지껄일 확률이 99.99%니까.
- **Current:** Even if I explained it, there was a 99.99 percent chance he would say something like, *Wow, I’ve never seen someone without a dick before!*
- **Defect:** The imagined childish outburst is made more graphically vulgar than the source.
- **Replacement:** Even if I explained it, there was a 99.99 percent chance he would say something like, *Wow, I’ve never met anyone without one before!*
- **Rationale:** 고추 remains a euphemism here, and the humor comes from Cheongpung’s tactless innocence rather than explicit profanity.

## F11 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “고자는 고추가 없어요.”

“와, 저 고추 없는 사람 처음…….”

“아, 닥치라고!”
- **Current:** “A eunuch is someone without a dick.”

“Wow, I’ve never seen someone without a—”

“Oh, shut the fuck up!”
- **Defect:** The exchange again substitutes the occupation “eunuch” for castration, makes the euphemism graphically vulgar, and intensifies Taekyung’s sharp command with an unsupported expletive.
- **Replacement:** “It means a man doesn’t have his thing.”

“Wow, I’ve never met anyone who didn’t have—”

“Oh, shut up already!”
- **Rationale:** Hong Jin is defining 고자 through the euphemistic 고추, while 닥치라고 is forceful but contains no equivalent of “fuck.”

# Review

The draft contains two scene-fact reversals, a mistranslated historical reference with a false footnote, and several localized terminology and natural-English issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 굉음과 함께 창날을 흘려 낸 검 끝이 곧장 목젖을 향해 찔러 들어왔다.
- **Current:** The tip of my sword deflected the spearhead, then stabbed straight toward Lee Jungryong's throat.
- **Defect:** The weapons, attacker, and target are reversed. Lee Jungryong's sword deflects Jin Taekyung's spearhead and then thrusts at Jin's throat.
- **Replacement:** With a thunderous boom, Lee Jungryong's sword swept my spearhead aside and thrust straight toward my throat.
- **Rationale:** The subject is 검 끝, the sword tip, while 창날 is the spearhead it deflects; the first-person viewpoint makes 목젖 Jin Taekyung's throat.

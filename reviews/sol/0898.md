# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but one spoken line becomes an unspoken thought, and several choices alter characterization or obscure the decision to send the pavilion away.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아, 이번만큼은 내가 조장님을 구해 줄 수도 있겠구나, 하는 생각이요.”
- **Current:** *Oh. This time, maybe I’ll be able to save the Captain.*
- **Defect:** Mujin says this to the narrator; italics turn it into an unspoken thought and obscure who is speaking.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, I thought, maybe this time I can be the one to save you, Captain.”
- **Rationale:** The line follows Mujin looking directly at him and speaking, and ends by identifying it as the thought Mujin had.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그럼 이번만큼은 저 같은 하수도 도움이 되지 않겠어요?
- **Current:** Then this time, wouldn’t even a lowlife like me be useful?
- **Defect:** “Lowlife” suggests bad character rather than inferior martial skill.
- **Replacement:** Then this time, couldn’t even a low-level fighter like me be useful?
- **Rationale:** 하수 describes someone less skilled than the masters he has just mentioned.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94

- **Current:** Keep this up and I might get fed up and return to the family estate on my own.

- **Replacement:** Keep this up and I might get fed up and head back to the Jin Family on my own.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 하지만 생각보다 오래 걸리는데요. 지금 같은 상황에서 자리를 비우는 건 좀…… 조장님 몸 상태도 평소 같지 않으시잖아요.
- **Current:** But that could take a while. In a situation like this, it doesn’t feel right to leave you behind. You’re not in your usual condition, either.
- **Defect:** The draft drops “longer than expected” and changes Mujin’s concern about being away from his post into a statement about leaving the Captain behind.
- **Replacement:** But that’s longer than I expected. Being away at a time like this is a bit… And you’re not in your usual condition, either.
- **Rationale:** 생각보다 marks a comparison with his expectation; 자리를 비우는 건 concerns his absence during the crisis.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이미 이틀 전 준비해 두었던, 마지막까지 꺼낼까 말까 망설였던 물건이었다.
- **Current:** It was something I’d prepared two days ago, but had hesitated to take out until the very end.
- **Defect:** “Hesitated to take out until the very end” implies a delay; he was undecided whether to take out the note at all.
- **Replacement:** I’d prepared it two days ago, but until the last moment, I’d wavered over whether to take it out at all.
- **Rationale:** 꺼낼까 말까 expresses a choice between taking it out and not taking it out.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “하긴, 뱁새가 천응(天鷹)을 걱정했네요.”

“보통은 천응이 아니라 황새일 텐데.”
- **Current:** “Right, I was a shrike worrying about a Heavenly Eagle.”

“Usually, it’d be a crane you were worried about, not a Heavenly Eagle.”
- **Defect:** Both birds in the altered idiom are misidentified: 뱁새 is a crow-tit, and 황새 is a stork.
- **Replacement:** “Right, I was a crow-tit worrying about a Heavenly Eagle.”

“Usually, it’d be a stork you were worried about, not a Heavenly Eagle.”
- **Rationale:** The Captain’s correction depends on the familiar crow-tit-and-stork pairing; Mujin has substituted the grander Heavenly Eagle.

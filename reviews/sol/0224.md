# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it mistranslates the ambush formation, loses a meaningful honorific withdrawal, makes an implied consequence explicitly fatal, and has several localized register and prose issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “여기서 포위망 펼친다.”

“예?”

“포위망이라니. 이 상황에서 말입니까?”
- **Current:** “We’ll set up a perimeter here.”

“What?”

“You mean a perimeter? In this situation?”
- **Defect:** “Perimeter” suggests a defensive boundary rather than the enclosing cordon Won intends to use to intercept survivors.
- **Replacement:** “We’ll form a cordon here.”

“What?”

“You mean a cordon? In this situation?”
- **Rationale:** 포위망 is an encircling formation around a target or route, not merely the perimeter of an area.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “더 이상 길게 말 안 한다. 숨어서 포위망 펼쳐. 내 신호가 떨어지면 누구든 죽여 버릴 수 있게.”

원명훈의 살기 어린 목소리는 대법원의 판결문이나 다름없었다.

항소 따위는 통하지 않는다는 것을 깨달은 사람들이 뿔뿔이 흩어져 포위망을 형성하기 시작했다.
- **Current:** “I won’t waste any more time talking. Hide and form a perimeter. When I give the signal, be ready to kill anyone.”

Won Myunghoon’s voice, thick with killing intent, was no different from a written verdict handed down by the Supreme Court.

Realizing that there would be no appeal, the Guild members scattered and began forming a perimeter.
- **Defect:** The repeated “perimeter” continues to frame the formation as defensive rather than as a concealed cordon intended to surround and kill anyone approaching.
- **Replacement:** “I won’t waste any more time talking. Hide and form a cordon. When I give the signal, be ready to kill anyone.”

Won Myunghoon’s voice, thick with killing intent, was no different from a written verdict handed down by the Supreme Court.

Realizing that there would be no appeal, the Guild members scattered and began forming the cordon.
- **Rationale:** Both occurrences of 포위망 refer to the same ambush formation and require consistent tactical terminology.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “1팀장님, 아니 1팀장은 어떻게 할까요?”
- **Current:** “Team Leader 1—I mean, what should we do about Team Leader 1?”
- **Defect:** The draft repeats the same English title and therefore loses the speaker’s deliberate correction from the respectful 팀장님 to the stripped, non-honorific 팀장.
- **Replacement:** “Team Leader One, sir—I mean, what should we do about Team Leader One?”
- **Rationale:** The mid-sentence withdrawal of respect shows the Guild member adapting to Won Myunghoon’s contempt for Jonghun.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘입을 열었다가는 저놈도 무사하지 못했을 테니까.’
- **Current:** *If he opened his mouth, he wouldn’t have survived either.*
- **Defect:** The draft turns the source’s euphemistic threat of serious consequences into an explicit assertion that Jonghun would have died.
- **Replacement:** *If he opened his mouth, he wouldn’t have gotten off unscathed either.*
- **Rationale:** 무사하지 못하다 means not emerging unharmed or unscathed; it does not specifically state death.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “저 싸가지 없는 새끼. 어른한테 못 하는 말이 없네. 그렇죠?”
- **Current:** “What a rude bastard. He really doesn’t hold back with his elders, does he? Right?”
- **Defect:** “Doesn’t hold back” weakens the complaint that there is apparently nothing Won will not say to an elder, and the plural “elders” blurs the immediate hierarchy.
- **Replacement:** “What a rude bastard. There’s nothing he won’t say to an elder, is there? Right?”
- **Rationale:** 못 하는 말이 없다 specifically means that no remark is beyond him, while 어른 invokes the respect owed to an older person.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “여러모로 나쁘지 않아. 진태경이 죽었으니 우리 쪽도 A급 하나 정도는 끼워 줘야 그림이 살지.”
- **Current:** “It’s not a bad idea in several ways. Since Jin Taekyung is dead, we need at least one A-Rank Hunter on our side to make the picture work.”
- **Defect:** “Make the picture work” is an unnatural calque and obscures Won’s point that Jonghun’s death will make their fabricated account look convincing.
- **Replacement:** “It works out well in more ways than one. Since Jin Taekyung is dead, we need to throw in an A-Rank Hunter from our side to make the story convincing.”
- **Rationale:** 그림이 살다 refers figuratively to making the overall scenario or appearance credible, not to a literal picture.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 일순간 후려친 주먹에 아름드리나무가 박살 났다.
- **Current:** A single punch smashed apart a thick tree.
- **Defect:** “Thick tree” omits the specific scale conveyed by 아름드리나무.
- **Replacement:** A single punch smashed apart a tree thick enough to wrap both arms around.
- **Rationale:** 아름드리 describes a trunk roughly as broad as a person can encircle with both arms.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “다시는 이빨 드러내지 말라고.”
- **Current:** “I told you never to bare your teeth at me again.”
- **Defect:** The draft unnecessarily repeats “I told you” immediately after the preceding line, “Didn’t I tell you?”, dulling the source’s two-beat threat.
- **Replacement:** “Never bare your teeth at me again.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean completes the preceding question directly without restating 말했다.

## F09 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 동시에 불끈 쥔 주먹을, 놈의 아가리에 박아넣었다.
- **Current:** At the same time, I drove my tightly clenched fist into his mouth.
- **Defect:** The neutral “mouth” sanitizes the deliberately coarse body-part term 아가리.
- **Replacement:** At the same time, I drove my tightly clenched fist into his maw.
- **Rationale:** 아가리 is a contemptuous, dehumanizing term for the mouth; “maw” preserves that force without adding a new action.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 전해지기로는 이 절벽에서 수련하시던 도중 문득 깨달음을 얻어 무공을 펼쳤는데, 일격에 절벽 밑이 뻥 뚫렸다는군.
- **Current:** According to the story, he suddenly attained enlightenment while training on this cliff and unleashed a martial art. The force of that One Strike blasted a hole through the bottom of the cliff.
- **Defect:** The draft turns the cave opening at the base of the cliff into a hole blasted through the cliff’s bottom, altering the described geometry and mechanism.

- **Rationale:** 절벽 밑이 뻥 뚫렸다 means the base or area beneath the cliff was blown open, creating the training cave entrance; it does not describe a hole passing through the cliff’s bottom.

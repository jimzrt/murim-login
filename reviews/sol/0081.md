# Review

The draft contains several subject reversals and shifts in characterization, along with softened profanity, altered pragmatic cues, awkward calques, and two System-format violations.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “불안하면 집에 가라. 분위기 좆같이 만들지 말고.”
- **Current:** “If you’re nervous, go home. Don’t ruin the mood.”
- **Defect:** The second sentence sanitizes the strong profanity in 좆같이 and weakens Changsoo’s openly abusive register.
- **Replacement:** “If you’re nervous, go home. Don’t make the mood fucking miserable.”
- **Rationale:** The source uses explicit profanity to show Changsoo viciously blaming the woman for spoiling the atmosphere.

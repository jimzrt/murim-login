# Review

Several action and subject errors alter the scene; smaller errors affect the injury joke and sound description.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아, 그리고 고추도 밟으려고 했는데 사람들이 말렸다. 태산이도 말렸다. 그건 사람 할 짓 아니다. 아, 고추 듬뿍 넣은 사천식 오향장육 먹고 싶다.
- **Current:** “Oh, Taishan tried to stomp on his pepper, too, but people stopped Taishan. Taishan stopped Taishan, too. That’s not something a person should do. Ah, Taishan wants Sichuan-style five-spice pork with lots of pepper.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taishan both the attacker and his own restrainer. He is describing the Pavilion Master’s attempt and saying he helped stop it.
- **Replacement:** “Oh, the Pavilion Master tried to stomp on his pepper, too, but people stopped him. Taishan helped stop him. No one should do that. Ah, Taishan wants Sichuan-style five-spice pork with lots of pepper.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 밟으려고 했는데 continues from 각주 in Taishan’s account; 태산이도 말렸다 explicitly says Taishan also stopped him.

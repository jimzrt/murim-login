# Review

One critical subject reversal and several major fidelity and terminology errors require correction.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 내가 하는 말을 못 알아들을 정도로 멍청한 놈이 아니다.
- **Current:** I wasn’t so stupid that I failed to understand what he was saying.
- **Defect:** The subject and direction of understanding are reversed. The source says Mukyung is not too stupid to understand the narrator, not that the narrator understands Mukyung.
- **Replacement:** He wasn’t so stupid that he couldn’t understand what I was saying.
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject is Jin Mukyung, while 내가 하는 말 means “what I was saying.”

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그랬지. 하지만 처음에는 목숨을 걸고 싸웠단다.
- **Current:** “That’s true. But at first, we fought for our lives.”
- **Defect:** “Fought for our lives” implies fighting for survival, while the source says they deliberately fought with their lives at stake.
- **Replacement:** “That’s true. But at first, we fought with our lives on the line.”
- **Rationale:** 목숨을 걸고 describes staking or risking one’s life, which matters because this was a voluntarily initiated contest between martial artists.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 철 숙부께서 지금까지 재야에 머물러 계시는 것만 봐도 알 수 있는 사실인데.
- **Current:** I can tell just by the fact that Uncle Cheol has remained outside the martial world all this time.
- **Defect:** 재야 means remaining unaffiliated or outside official organizations, not being outside the martial world altogether. Cheol Mubaek is plainly an active and renowned martial artist.
- **Replacement:** I can tell just from the fact that you’ve remained unaffiliated all this time, Uncle Cheol.
- **Rationale:** The line infers the duel’s outcome from Cheol Mubaek never joining Lee Cheonbaek’s proposed sect, so organizational independence is the relevant fact.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 수라멸권은 일인전승(一人傳承), 비인부전(非人不傳)의 원칙을 따른다.

- **Rationale:** 비인부전 prohibits transmission to an unsuitable or unworthy person, rather than merely to someone who has not been selected.

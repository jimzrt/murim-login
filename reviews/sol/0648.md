# Review

One critical subject/action reversal near the ending, plus hierarchy, kinship, register, and localized wording errors.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 순식간에 찾아온 정적 속에서, 지그시 나를 응시하던 백상의 냉담한 눈빛을 가로막은 것은 야수묘왕의 한 마디였다.
- **Current:** And in the silence that descended in an instant, it was the Beast Miao King’s single word that blocked Baeksang’s cold gaze as he stared fixedly at me.
- **Defect:** 한 마디 means a remark or utterance here, not literally one word; the Beast Miao King’s ensuing line contains several words.
- **Replacement:** And in the silence that descended in an instant, it was a single remark from the Beast Miao King that blocked Baeksang’s cold gaze as he stared fixedly at me.
- **Rationale:** Rendering 한 마디 as “remark” preserves the unit of speech without falsely implying a one-word command.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 아무리 남만야수궁이 일종의 분할 통치제로 유지되는 부족 사회라 해도 궁주의 권위는 강력하다.
- **Current:** Even though the Nanman Beast Palace was a tribal society maintained through a kind of divided rule, the Palace Lord’s authority was absolute.
- **Defect:** The draft strengthens 강력하다, meaning powerful or formidable, into “absolute,” materially overstating the Palace Lord’s political authority within the divided tribal structure.
- **Replacement:** Even though the Nanman Beast Palace was a tribal society maintained through a kind of divided rule, the Palace Lord still wielded enormous authority.
- **Rationale:** The source emphasizes strong authority despite divided rule, not unlimited or absolute sovereignty.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “거 더럽게 따지네. 그냥 마시면 되지.”
- **Current:** “You’re being ridiculously picky. Just drink it.”
- **Defect:** “Ridiculously” sanitizes the coarse colloquial force of 더럽게 and sounds more analytical than Jin’s blunt speech.
- **Replacement:** “You’re so damn picky. Just drink it.”
- **Rationale:** “Damn” naturally preserves the source’s mildly profane intensifier without making the line more vulgar than the Korean.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘반드시 네놈을 찢어 죽여, 사형제들과 벗들의 원수를 갚겠다.’
- **Current:** “I’ll tear you apart and kill you to avenge my Senior Brothers and friends!”
- **Defect:** 사형제들 collectively means martial brothers or fellow disciples, not only senior brothers; the draft imposes an elder-only hierarchy absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** “I’ll tear you apart and kill you to avenge my martial brothers and friends!”
- **Rationale:** “Martial brothers” retains the sect relationship without incorrectly restricting the dead to senior disciples.

## F07 — Minor

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 귀주(貴州)의 어느 이름 모를 협곡에 다시 한번 자신의 족적을 새긴 괴인은 잠시 바위에 기대 두었던 자신의 무기를 집어 들었다.
- **Current:** The strange man, who had once again left his mark in an unnamed gorge in Guizhou, briefly leaned against a rock and picked up his weapon.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the subject of the leaning action: the source says the weapon had been left propped against the rock, not that the strange man leaned against it.
- **Replacement:** The strange man, who had once again left his mark in an unnamed gorge in Guizhou, picked up the weapon he had left leaning against a rock.
- **Rationale:** 잠시 바위에 기대 두었던 modifies 자신의 무기 and describes where he had placed the weapon.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it reverses two descriptions of Michael’s power and loses several cues in the confrontation.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 기운의 크기만큼은 지금껏 싸워 왔던 누구와도 비교할 수 없었다.
- **Current:** its sheer scale couldn’t compare to anyone I’d fought so far.
- **Defect:** “Couldn’t compare” suggests Michael’s power falls short; the source says its magnitude is beyond comparison with past opponents.
- **Replacement:** its sheer scale was beyond anything I’d faced before.
- **Rationale:** 누구와도 비교할 수 없었다 describes an unmatched magnitude, not an inferior one.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 어느 절대자의 눈을 피해 장장 수십여 년간 힘을 축적해 온 괴물이 바로 눈앞에 있었다.
- **Current:** A monster that had spent decades building up its power right under the nose of an absolute being stood before me.
- **Defect:** “Right under the nose” replaces Michael’s deliberate evasion of that being’s notice with mere proximity.
- **Replacement:** A monster that had spent decades building up its power while evading the notice of an absolute being stood before me.
- **Rationale:** 눈을 피해 means avoiding someone’s watch or notice.

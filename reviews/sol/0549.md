## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 운남이 현대에서는 베트남과 미얀마던가. 그쯤 어딘가에 있을 텐데 세계지리 시간에 잠만 처자느라 기억이 가물가물하다.
- **Current:** Yunnan was somewhere around Vietnam and Myanmar in the modern world, wasn’t it? I could not remember exactly. I had spent geography class sleeping my ass off.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Taekyung’s mistaken speculation that modern Yunnan might be Vietnam or Myanmar into the geographically sensible claim that it is merely near them, weakening the joke about his ignorance.
- **Replacement:** In the modern world, was Yunnan Vietnam or Myanmar? It had to be somewhere around there, but my memory was hazy because I’d spent world geography class sleeping my ass off.
- **Rationale:** The source uses 베트남과 미얀마던가 to make Taekyung uncertain whether Yunnan corresponds to Vietnam or Myanmar, then attributes that confusion to sleeping through class.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “안 된다! 태산이! 오늘 저녁! 고기 먹기로 했다!”
- **Current:** “No! Taishan! Eat meat tonight! We promised!”
- **Defect:** The draft invents a promise involving an unspecified second party. Taishan only says that he had planned or was supposed to eat meat that evening.
- **Replacement:** “No! Taishan! Was supposed to eat meat tonight!”

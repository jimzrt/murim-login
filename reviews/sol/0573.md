# Review

Several concrete fidelity defects remain, including an invented Jintae correction, altered quantities and penetration mechanics, an omitted mask detail, and incorrect Hunter-rank terminology.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 따개비가 달라붙은 삼지창은 인간의 몸을 꿰뚫고 그대로 날아가, 신호 대기 중이던 차량마저 관통했다.
- **Current:** A barnacle-covered trident pierced through a human body and continued flying, impaling a vehicle waiting at the traffic light.
- **Defect:** The trident passes completely through the vehicle in the source; “impaling” suggests that it lodges in the vehicle.
- **Replacement:** A barnacle-covered trident pierced through a human body and continued flying, punching straight through even a vehicle waiting at the light.
- **Rationale:** 관통했다 explicitly describes complete penetration through the vehicle.

# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it alters several quantities and combat details, understates the attackers’ minimum level, and omits Sama Pyo’s explicit fearlessness.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 대답 대신 돌아온 것은 십여 자루의 비도였다.
- **Current:** The answer that came instead was a volley of more than a dozen throwing blades.
- **Defect:** 십여 means ten or more, or roughly a dozen; “more than a dozen” incorrectly establishes a minimum of thirteen.
- **Replacement:** The answer that came instead was a volley of a dozen or so throwing blades.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the source’s approximate quantity without raising its lower bound.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘중원으로 치자면 하나하나가 초일류에서 절정. 도대체 남만 어디에서 이런 놈들이 나타난 거지?’
- **Current:** *By Central Plains standards, every one of them is between First Rate and Peak. Where in Nanman did these people come from?*
- **Defect:** 초일류 specifies the upper echelon of First Rate, not the entire First Rate level. The draft understates the weakest attackers.
- **Replacement:** *By Central Plains standards, every one of them is between upper First Rate and Peak. Where in Nanman did these people come from?*
- **Rationale:** The narrower lower bound is important to Sama Pyo’s assessment of the attackers’ unusually high collective strength.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 씹어뱉듯 중얼거린 사마표는 쓰러지려는 두 시신을 끌어당겨 앞뒤로 세웠다.
- **Current:** Sama Pyo muttered the word through clenched teeth, then pulled the two collapsing bodies toward him and stood them one behind the other.
- **Defect:** 앞뒤로 means Sama Pyo positioned the corpses in front of and behind himself. The draft instead places both bodies in a single line, changing his defensive maneuver and coverage.
- **Replacement:** Sama Pyo spat the word out, then pulled the two collapsing bodies toward him and propped one in front of himself and the other behind him.
- **Rationale:** The source describes Sama Pyo shielding himself from attacks coming from both directions.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 관절이 삐걱거리는 몸을 일으키며 남호는 굳게 다짐했다.
- **Current:** As Namho rose from his creaking body, he made a firm resolve.
- **Defect:** The English incorrectly makes Namho rise from his own body; the source says he raises himself while his joints creak.
- **Replacement:** As Namho pushed himself upright, his joints creaking, he made a firm resolve.
- **Rationale:** This restores the physical action and produces natural English.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 하지만 더욱 무서운 것은 죽음을 두려워하지 않는 그의 태도였다.

- **Defect:** The draft generalizes the explicit characterization that Sama Pyo does not fear death.

- **Rationale:** The source directly identifies fearlessness, rather than an unspecified attitude, as what intimidates the attackers.

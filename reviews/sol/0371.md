# Review

The draft contains two critical factual errors involving the subject of Jeok Cheongang’s praise and the number of captured Fiends, along with several meaningful register, attribution, and terminology issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그사이 스킬이 늘었네.
- **Current:** Looks like Mimi had learned another Skill while I wasn’t looking.
- **Defect:** The draft turns colloquial 스킬 into a discrete, capitalized System Skill and replaces the temporal cue “in the meantime” with an added statement about the narrator not looking.
- **Replacement:** Looks like Mimi had picked up some new tricks in the meantime.
- **Rationale:** Here 스킬 humorously refers to Mimi’s expanding repertoire, not a newly acquired game-system ability.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 천둥벌거숭이 같으니. 아주 한바탕 난리를 치더구나.
- **Current:** “You little thunderstruck fool. You certainly made quite a ruckus.”
- **Defect:** “Thunderstruck” means astonished or shocked and does not convey 천둥벌거숭이, an idiom for a reckless, unruly youngster.
- **Replacement:** “You reckless little brat. You certainly made quite a ruckus.”
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Jeok Cheongang’s gruff characterization and the idiom’s intended criticism.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “아, 잘했다고!”
- **Current:** “I said I did a good job!”
- **Defect:** The draft reverses the subject of the praise: Jeok Cheongang is praising Jin Taekyung, not claiming that he himself did well.
- **Replacement:** “I said you did well!”
- **Rationale:** 잘했다고 has Jin Taekyung’s conduct as its contextually omitted subject, which is why the praise satisfies him in the following narration.

## F04 — Minor

- **Current:** “The junior has committed a discourtesy. However, given the circumstances…”

- **Replacement:** “This junior was discourteous. However, given the circumstances…”
- **Rationale:** 후배 is a formal, self-effacing reference to the speaker in relation to the senior Jeok Cheongang.

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 춘추가 어떻게 되시길래.
- **Current:** *How old is she, anyway?*
- **Defect:** The draft makes the question blunt and casual, losing the deliberately respectful euphemism 춘추 and Taekyung’s newly cautious manner.
- **Replacement:** *Just how advanced in years is she?*
- **Rationale:** 춘추 is an honorific, indirect way of asking an elder’s age, particularly apt immediately after the warning to be careful about it.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “반갑네, 진 시주.”
- **Current:** “Good to meet you, Young Master Jin.”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the Buddhist form of address 시주 with the unrelated secular rank “Young Master.”
- **Replacement:** “Good to meet you, Benefactor Jin.”
- **Rationale:** As a Buddhist nun, the Extinction Divine Nun addresses Jin with 시주, preserving her religious voice rather than calling him 공자.

## F08 — Minor

## F10 — Minor

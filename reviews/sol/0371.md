# Review

Two critical subject and quantity errors, two meaningful characterization losses, and two localized wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “……했다.”

“예?”

“아, 잘했다고!”
- **Current:** “…I did.”

“Pardon?”

“Ah, I said I did well!”
- **Defect:** Jeok Cheongang is reluctantly telling Jin Taekyung that Jin did well, not praising his own actions.
- **Replacement:** “…You did well.”

“Pardon?”

“Ah, I said you did well!”
- **Rationale:** The omitted subject of 잘했다고 is the addressee, Jin Taekyung. Making Jeok Cheongang say “I did well” reverses the subject and removes the intended praise.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘인간 트롤마냥 재생하지를 않나, 팔에 양면테이프라도 붙였는지 뗐다 붙이고. 마지막에는 빙의까지 했지.’
- **Current:** *One of them regenerated like a human troll, another detached and reattached his arm as if it had double-sided tape on it, and the last one even possessed someone.*
- **Defect:** The draft invents three separate actors and distributes the abilities among them, although the source merely lists bizarre abilities without assigning them to “one,” “another,” and “the last.”
- **Replacement:** *Regenerating like a human troll, detaching and reattaching an arm as if it had double-sided tape on it—and to top it all off, even possession.*
- **Rationale:** The source contains no three-part subject structure and does not specify that three different people performed these actions. The replacement preserves the deliberately compressed, subject-omitting list.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 눈치라고는 죽었다가 깨어나도 없는 청풍이나 혁무진과는 달리, 뼛속까지 성골 거지인 궁기방은 즉각 움직였다.
- **Current:** Unlike Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin, who had no sense even after dying and coming back to life, Gung Gibang was a beggar through and through. He moved at once.
- **Defect:** “Had no sense” loses the specific inability to read the room, while “beggar through and through” omits the ironic 성골 image of elite or royal pedigree.
- **Replacement:** Unlike Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin, who wouldn’t know how to read the room even if they died and came back to life, Gung Gibang—a blue-blooded beggar to the bone—moved at once.
- **Rationale:** 눈치 refers specifically to social awareness here, and 성골 humorously casts Gung Gibang as belonging to the highest pedigree of beggars. Both cues explain why he immediately understands Jeok Cheongang’s intent.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 나는 보쌈집 회장님 같아 보이는 왕 할머니에게 넙죽 허리를 숙였다.
- **Current:** I bent deeply at the waist to Grandma Wang, who looked like the chairwoman of a bossam restaurant.[^1]
- **Defect:** The draft misreads 왕 as the surname Wang and invents a named “Grandma Wang.” It is an intensifier describing an extremely old grandmotherly woman.
- **Replacement:** I bowed deeply to the ancient granny who looked like the chairwoman of a bossam restaurant.[^1]
- **Rationale:** Nothing in the source names her Wang. The phrase 왕 할머니 humorously emphasizes her extreme age and grandmotherly appearance.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “천둥벌거숭이 같으니. 아주 한바탕 난리를 치더구나.”
- **Current:** “You little thunderstruck fool. You really caused quite a scene.”
- **Defect:** “Thunderstruck fool” is a literal but incorrect rendering of 천둥벌거숭이, which means a reckless, unruly brat rather than someone struck by thunder.
- **Replacement:** “You reckless brat. You really caused quite a scene.”
- **Rationale:** The idiom characterizes Jin Taekyung as wild and heedless; the current wording is unnatural and conveys the wrong image.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “소문대로군. 아니, 그 이상이야.”
- **Current:** “So the rumors were true. No—more than true.”
- **Defect:** “More than true” is unnatural and obscures that Jin Taekyung exceeds what the rumors said about him.
- **Replacement:** “Just as the rumors say. No—you’re even more impressive.”
- **Rationale:** 그 이상이야 evaluates Jin Taekyung as surpassing his reputation, not the rumors themselves as possessing a greater degree of truth.

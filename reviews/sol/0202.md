# Review

One critical quantity error, three major fidelity or humor losses, and three localized wording issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 물론이다. 이 갑자 이상의 공력과 본문의 무공을 극성으로 익힌다면.
- **Current:** “Of course. If you possess more than a jiazi’s worth of internal energy[^1] and master our sect’s martial arts.”
- **Defect:** The threshold is changed from at least two jiazi to more than one jiazi, and the requirement to cultivate the sect’s martial arts to their utmost limit is omitted.
- **Replacement:** “Of course. If you possess at least two jiazi’s worth of internal energy[^1] and master our sect’s martial arts to the utmost.”
- **Rationale:** 이 갑자 means two jiazi, 이상의 means at least that amount, and 극성으로 explicitly requires mastery to the highest degree.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 우드득.

꽉 움켜잡은 창대가 힘을 견디지 못하고 우그러진다.
- **Current:** Crack.

The spear shaft I gripped tightly bent under the force.
- **Defect:** The draft weakens the physical action: the shaft is crushed or buckles because it cannot withstand Taekyung’s grip, rather than merely bending under unspecified force.
- **Replacement:** Crack.

The spear shaft buckled under the force of my crushing grip.
- **Rationale:** 꽉 움켜잡은 and 우그러진다 describe deformation caused specifically by his powerful grip.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “아니, 그걸 말이라고 하십니까?”

“말이 아니면 망아지냐?”
- **Current:** “Are you seriously saying that as though it explains anything?”

“If it isn’t an explanation, is it a colt?”
- **Defect:** The first line replaces 말 with “explanation,” leaving the reply about a colt nonsensical and erasing the deliberate Korean word/horse pun.
- **Replacement:** “Do you seriously call that *mal*?”

“If it isn’t *mal*—words—then is it a foal?”[^2]

[^2]: *Mal* means “words,” but can also mean “horse,” setting up Jeok’s reply about a foal.
- **Rationale:** 말 means “words” here but is also a homonym for “horse”; 망아지, “foal,” is Jeok Cheongang’s intentional pun and needs preservation with a brief linguistic footnote.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** 단언컨대 석년의 검성도 이 정도는 아니었으리라.
- **Current:** He could state with certainty that even the Sword Saint of old had never produced anything like this.
- **Defect:** The draft turns a comparison of degree into an absolute claim that the Sword Saint never produced a similar result at any point.
- **Replacement:** He was certain that even the Sword Saint of years past had not been capable of this much.
- **Rationale:** 이 정도는 아니었으리라 means the Sword Saint likely was not at this level, not that he categorically never created anything similar.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 나는 허탈하면서도 기쁜 표정의 적천강을 향해 달려들었다.
- **Current:** I charged at Jeok Cheongang, whose expression was both exasperated and pleased.
- **Defect:** “Exasperated” adds irritation absent from 허탈, which conveys stunned disbelief or speechlessness.
- **Replacement:** I charged at Jeok Cheongang, who looked both dumbfounded and pleased.
- **Rationale:** 허탈하면서도 기쁜 describes an incongruous mixture of stunned disbelief and pleasure.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 필시 적천강이 나를 위해 불어넣어 준 공력일 것이다.
- **Current:** It was surely the internal energy Jeok Cheongang was breathing into me.
- **Defect:** “Breathing into me” is an overly literal and mechanically misleading rendering of energy being infused or channeled into Taekyung.
- **Replacement:** It had to be internal energy Jeok Cheongang was channeling into me.
- **Rationale:** 불어넣어 준 is idiomatic here for transferring or infusing internal energy, which the preceding sentence says is entering through Taekyung’s back.

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 대답 대신 들려온 코골이에 적천강은 박장대소를 터트렸다.
- **Current:** In response to the snoring, Jeok Cheongang threw back his head and burst into thunderous laughter.
- **Defect:** The draft omits the comic cue that the snoring comes instead of an answer and adds an unsupported head movement.
- **Replacement:** At the snore that came instead of an answer, Jeok Cheongang burst into hearty laughter.
- **Rationale:** 대답 대신 is the punchline’s explicit contrast, while the source says only that Jeok Cheongang breaks into hearty laughter.

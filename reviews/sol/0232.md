# Review

The draft contains one critical subject reversal, several meaningful subject, mechanism, and register errors, plus an unused footnote marker.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 단신인 적천강과 달리, 법왕(法王) 굉도는 훌쩍 큰 키의 노인이었다.
- **Current:** Unlike the short and stocky Jeok Cheongang, Dharma King Hong Dao was a remarkably tall old man.
- **Defect:** The source describes Jeok Cheongang only as short; “stocky” adds an unsupported physical characteristic.
- **Replacement:** Unlike the short Jeok Cheongang, Dharma King Hong Dao was a remarkably tall old man.
- **Rationale:** 단신 means short in stature and does not establish that Jeok Cheongang is stocky.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 두 분이 친해지신 계기 말입니다. 정마대전 당시 스승님께서 몰래 술과 고기를 먹고 계셨는데, 적 시주께 들킨 후로 술친구가 되셨다고 합니다.
- **Current:** “What brought the two of them together. During the Great Faction War, my master was secretly eating meat and drinking alcohol. After Great Hero Jeok caught him, they became drinking buddies.”
- **Defect:** Unnamed addresses Jeok Cheongang as 적 시주, a Buddhist form meaning “Benefactor Jeok,” not “Great Hero Jeok.” The draft changes his monk-specific deferential register.
- **Replacement:** “What brought the two of them together. During the Great Faction War, my master was secretly eating meat and drinking alcohol. After Benefactor Jeok caught him, they became drinking buddies.”
- **Rationale:** 시주 is the Buddhist address “benefactor”; preserving it distinguishes Unnamed’s monastic voice from ordinary martial-world honorifics.

## F03 — Major

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 삼백여 장 밖. 먼지구름.
- **Current:** “Dust cloud. About three hundred yards out.”
- **Defect:** Three hundred jang is roughly a thousand yards, not three hundred.
- **Replacement:** “Dust cloud. About a thousand yards out.”
- **Rationale:** One jang is approximately ten feet; the draft places the approaching force about three times too close.

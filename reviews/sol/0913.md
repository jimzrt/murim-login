# Review

Two scene-fact errors and several losses of voice and meaning.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 떠돌이 생활을 하던 동천마군의 자질을 알아본 어느 도사가 자신의 제자로 삼아 문파에 데려갔을 때도.
- **Current:** Even when a wandering Daoist recognized the boy’s aptitude, took him in as a Disciple, and brought him to the sect.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Daoist the wanderer; the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord was the one living as a wanderer.
- **Replacement:** Even when a Daoist recognized the wandering boy’s aptitude, took him as a Disciple, and brought him to the sect.
- **Rationale:** 떠돌이 생활을 하던 modifies 동천마군, not 도사.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그리고 수백 개의 계단 위에서 자신을 굽어보는, 원수의 핏줄을 향해 물었다.
- **Current:** Then he asked the descendant of his enemy’s bloodline, looking down at him from the top of hundreds of steps:
- **Defect:** The dangling phrase can make the Demon Lord the one looking down. His enemy’s descendant is above him, looking down at him.
- **Replacement:** Then he asked his enemy’s descendant, who was looking down at him from hundreds of steps above:
- **Rationale:** 자신을 굽어보는 describes 원수의 핏줄; the Demon Lord is below the steps.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “끝없이 불태워라. 죽지 않는다면 죽을 때까지 잿더미로 만들어 쓰러트려라. 본문의 선조들이 그리했던 것처럼.”
- **Current:** “Burn them without end. If they won’t die, keep turning them to ash until they fall. Just as our sect’s ancestors did.”
- **Defect:** “Until they fall” drops the deliberate paradox of killing creatures that will not die.
- **Replacement:** “Keep burning them. If they won’t die, burn them to ash until they do. Just as our sect’s ancestors did.”
- **Rationale:** 죽지 않는다면 죽을 때까지 repeats 죽다, making their death the stated aim.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “존나 많군.”
- **Current:** “Damn, that’s a lot.”
- **Defect:** “Damn” softens Jeok Cheongang’s blunt, unusually strong profanity.
- **Replacement:** “Fuck, that’s a lot.”
- **Rationale:** 존나 is strong profanity, and the abrupt reaction is the punchline.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** “예. 진짜 존나 많습니다. 어느 정신 나간 새끼가 취향 차이까지 고려했는지 종류도 다양해요.”
- **Current:** “Yeah. A whole damn lot. Some lunatic must’ve even taken different tastes into account, because there are all kinds.”
- **Defect:** “A whole damn lot” softens Taekyung’s emphatic echo of Jeok’s profanity.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. A fucking lot. Some lunatic must’ve even taken different tastes into account, because there are all kinds.”
- **Rationale:** 진짜 존나 많습니다 repeats and intensifies the preceding strong expression.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 느슨하게 움직인 회색빛 눈동자에, 황제의 곁에서 몸을 떨고 있는 어린 왕의 모습이 비쳤다.
- **Current:** His loose, gray eyes settled on the young king trembling beside the Emperor.
- **Defect:** “Loose eyes” is an unnatural literal rendering of his unhurried eye movement.
- **Replacement:** His gray gaze drifted to the young king trembling beside the Emperor.
- **Rationale:** 느슨하게 움직인 describes how his eyes move, not a physical quality of the eyes.

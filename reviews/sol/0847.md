# Review

Several passages alter kinship, turn people into martial arts, or supply a threat the source leaves unfinished.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그렇다고 구족(九族)을 멸할 만큼 큰 죄는 아니지.
- **Current:** It’s not a crime so great that it warrants wiping out nine generations of a family.
- **Defect:** 구족 refers to branches or degrees of kinship, not nine successive generations.
- **Replacement:** It’s not a crime so great that it warrants wiping out all nine branches of a family.
- **Rationale:** The source describes the scope of relatives subject to extermination, not a span of generations.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 하지만 사마외도(邪魔外道)만큼은 믿고 걸러야 한다. 백중 구십구가 병신이니.
- **Current:** But you should steer clear of the demonic, heterodox arts. Ninety-nine out of a hundred are idiots.
- **Defect:** The warning is about people associated with those arts; the next sentence counts people, not arts.
- **Replacement:** But steer clear of those who follow the demonic, heterodox arts. Ninety-nine out of a hundred are idiots.
- **Rationale:** The master judges a group of martial artists and says ninety-nine in a hundred of them are idiots.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 그렇다면 정파에도, 사마외도에도 속하지 않은 정사지간(正邪之間)의 무림인은 어떻습니까?
- **Current:** Then what about martial artists who belong to neither the orthodox faction nor the demonic, heterodox arts?
- **Defect:** The question contrasts affiliations of martial artists, but the draft makes one side a set of arts to which they cannot belong.
- **Replacement:** Then what about martial artists who belong to neither the orthodox faction nor the ranks of those who follow the demonic, heterodox arts?
- **Rationale:** 정파에도, 사마외도에도 속하지 않은 describes people outside both groups.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 화왕은, 아니 열화문은 대대로 정파와 사마외도를 가리지 않고 싸워 대는 미친놈들이다.
- **Current:** The Fire King—or rather, the Fire Gate Clan—are lunatics who’ve spent generations fighting both the orthodox faction and the demonic, heterodox arts.
- **Defect:** The clan fights people regardless of faction, not martial arts themselves.
- **Replacement:** The Fire King—or rather, the Fire Gate Clan—are lunatics who’ve spent generations fighting orthodox martial artists and followers of the demonic, heterodox arts alike.
- **Rationale:** 가리지 않고 싸워 대는 identifies the opposing groups without distinguishing between them.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 있다. 자그마치 삼백여 년 동안이나 미치도록 강한 미친놈들을 대대로 싸질러, 아니 배출해 낸 미친 문파가.
- **Current:** They do. There’s a mad sect that’s spent more than three hundred years breeding—no, producing—lunatics strong enough to be called insane.
- **Defect:** The draft turns their extraordinary strength into a degree of insanity.
- **Replacement:** They do. There’s a mad sect that’s spent more than three hundred years breeding—no, producing—one generation of insanely strong lunatics after another.
- **Rationale:** 미치도록 강한 means insanely strong; the repeated 미친 separately calls the people and sect mad.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 번만 더 목소리를 떨었다간 네놈의 몸뚱어리에 존재하는 털이란 털은 싹 다……!
- **Current:** “If your voice shakes one more time, I’ll rip out every hair on your body—”
- **Defect:** The draft specifies ripping out hair, while Jeok breaks off before saying what he will do to it.
- **Replacement:** “If your voice shakes one more time, every last hair on your body is going to…!”
- **Rationale:** The unfinished threat is intentional; the source supplies no action after ‘every hair.’

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 한 번으로는 부족하다. 이번에는 마음에 새기며 말해라.
- **Current:** “Once isn’t enough. This time, say them with feeling.”
- **Defect:** ‘With feeling’ loses the deliberate escalation from engraving the names in his mind to engraving them in his heart.
- **Replacement:** “Once isn’t enough. This time, say them and engrave them on your heart.”
- **Rationale:** 마음에 새기며 repeats the earlier command’s image but changes its location from mind to heart.

## F08 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 미, 미쳤습니까? 귀하가 누구인지는 모르지만 어찌 감히 황상 폐하께……!
- **Current:** “A-are you insane? I don’t know who you are, but how dare you say that about His Imperial Majesty…!”
- **Defect:** The established title ‘Emperor’ is absent from the guard’s deferential address.
- **Replacement:** “A-are you insane? I don’t know who you are, but how dare you say that about His Majesty the Emperor…!”
- **Rationale:** 황상 폐하 names the reigning Emperor while expressing the guard’s alarm and deference.

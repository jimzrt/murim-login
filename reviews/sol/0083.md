- **Source:** 안 그래도 막 부하들을 물리려던 임창수가 입을 다물며 귀를 기울였다.
- **Current:** Im Changsoo, who had just been about to send his men forward, shut his mouth and listened.
- **Defect:** The draft reverses Changsoo’s intended order: he was about to pull his men back, not send them forward.

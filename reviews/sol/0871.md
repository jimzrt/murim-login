# Review

Several action, subject, and register changes need correction; the remaining terminology warnings do not establish defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 정호군은 이름 모를 쌍둥이 중년인에게 나와 상산왕을 인도하며 짧은 전음을 남겼다.
- **Current:** Jeong Hogun led Prince Shangshan and me along behind one of the middle-aged twins whose names I didn’t know, and sent us a brief Sound Transmission.
- **Defect:** Jeong leads them to the twins, not along behind one twin.
- **Replacement:** Jeong Hogun led Prince Shangshan and me over to the middle-aged twins, whose names I didn’t know, and sent us a brief Sound Transmission.
- **Rationale:** 쌍둥이 중년인에게 나와 상산왕을 인도하며 makes the twins the people to whom Jeong brings the pair.

## F02 — Major

- **Current:** What the hell was he trying to scare us for?

- **Replacement:** Who the fuck did that bastard think he was kidding?

# Review

Two consequential fidelity errors alter Chulwoo’s relative strength and the gate’s distance from the training ground. Several additional passages lose hierarchy, wordplay, register, or the source’s intended emphasis.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 사람들은 맥이 탁 풀렸다. 빨랐다니, 그렇게 단순하고 쉬운 대답을 원한 것이 아니었다.
- **Current:** Everyone's excitement drained away. Fast? That was not the simple, obvious answer they had wanted.
- **Defect:** The second sentence misplaces the negation and implies that they wanted a simple, obvious answer, whereas the source says they did not want such a simplistic answer.
- **Replacement:** Everyone's excitement drained away. Fast? They hadn't been looking for such a simple, obvious answer.
- **Rationale:** 그렇게 단순하고 쉬운 대답을 원한 것이 아니었다 explicitly rejects that kind of simple answer.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “가능해요. 은인은.”
- **Current:** “It is possible. My Benefactor can do it.”
- **Defect:** The draft smooths away the source's emphatic inversion, which isolates Benefactor as the exception and contributes to Cheongpung's distinctive voice.
- **Replacement:** “It's possible. For my Benefactor.”
- **Rationale:** The delayed 은인은 emphasizes that what is impossible for others is possible specifically for his Benefactor.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘둘째가 질 수밖에 없었군.’
- **Current:** *Second was bound to lose.*
- **Defect:** The bare noun “Second” is not natural English and obscures that Baek Museong is referring to his second junior disciple.
- **Replacement:** *My second junior brother was bound to lose.*
- **Rationale:** 둘째 is Baek Museong's hierarchical shorthand for Chulwoo, his second junior disciple.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 다른 건 몰라도 딱 하나, 힘 하나는 신력(神力)을 타고난 놈이다. 청풍도 이놈 앞에서는 한 수 접어 줘야 한다.
- **Current:** Whatever else he lacked, he had been born with one thing: tremendous strength. Even Cheongpung had to yield a point to him in that regard.
- **Defect:** “Whatever else he lacked” invents deficiencies not stated in the source, “tremendous strength” weakens the exceptional innate 신력, and “yield a point” is an unnatural rendering of conceding superiority.
- **Replacement:** Whatever else you could say about him, he'd been born with one extraordinary gift: superhuman strength. Even Cheongpung couldn't match him in that regard.
- **Rationale:** 다른 건 몰라도 does not assert that Chulwoo lacks other qualities, while 신력을 타고난 and 한 수 접어 줘야 한다 establish innate exceptional strength surpassing even Cheongpung's.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 내가 방심한 것도 있지만 이놈 매너가 아주 똥인데?
- **Current:** I had let my guard down, sure, but this guy had terrible manners.
- **Defect:** The draft sanitizes the deliberately crude description 아주 똥인데 and removes the rhetorical, incredulous tone.
- **Replacement:** Sure, I'd let my guard down, but weren't this bastard's manners absolute shit?
- **Rationale:** 매너가 아주 똥인데 literally and comically characterizes his manners as shit, matching Taekyung's profane narration.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “철하다. 추우야.”

“그게 유언이냐?”

“아니. 띵언.”
- **Current:** “Steel yourself, Chulwoo.”

“Are those your last words?”

“No. A wise saying.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents a coherent command and loses the source's deliberate syllable-swapping joke based on “Pathetic, Chulwoo.”
- **Replacement:** “Chulthetic, Pawoo.”[^1]

“Are those your last words?”

“No. Words of wisdom.”

[^1]: Taekyung mangles “Pathetic, Chulwoo” by swapping their opening sounds.
- **Rationale:** 철하다. 추우야 swaps the opening syllables of the normal insult 추하다, 철우야; the malformed line is intentional and motivates the response about last words and wisdom.

## F08 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 일시적으로 상승한 [승부사]의 칭호 효과와 새롭게 부여한 포인트까지. 현재 내 몸에 깃든 힘은 인간의 한계를 아득히 뛰어넘는다.

거대한 체격과 신력을 타고난 철우마저도.
- **Current:** Between the temporary boost from the Title **Gambler** and the points I had just allocated, the strength inhabiting my body had far surpassed the limits of a human being.

Even Chulwoo, with his massive frame and tremendous strength, was no exception.
- **Defect:** “Was no exception” grammatically says Chulwoo's strength likewise surpassed human limits. The source instead presents even the exceptionally strong Chulwoo as unable to contend with Taekyung's current strength.
- **Replacement:** With the temporary boost from the **Gambler** Title and the points I'd just allocated, the strength coursing through my body now far surpassed human limits.

Not even Chulwoo, with his massive frame and innate superhuman strength, could overpower me.
- **Rationale:** 철우마저도 is a concessive fragment continuing the fact that Taekyung does not budge: even Chulwoo cannot overcome his strength.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “이 정도면 근력 스탯 200은 가뿐히 넘겠는데.”
- **Current:** “This should put your Strength stat comfortably over 200.”
- **Defect:** “Put” implies that the present action or some boost has raised Chulwoo's stat. Taekyung is estimating Chulwoo's existing Strength from the force he displays.

- **Rationale:** 이 정도면 … 넘겠는데 expresses Taekyung's inference about Chulwoo's stat, not a stat increase.

“줄을 서긴 무슨.”

## F12 — Minor

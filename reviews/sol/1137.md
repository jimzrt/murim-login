# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, with one changed subject and several localized additions or wording defects.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그리고 무언가를 느낀 듯, 말없이 자신을 응시하는 매종학을 향해 신중히 입술을 뗐다.
- **Current:** Then, sensing something, he noticed Mae Jonghak silently watching him. He carefully parted his lips.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Slaughter Saint the one who senses something; the source describes Mae Jonghak as watching him as though Mae had sensed something.
- **Replacement:** Then he carefully opened his mouth to speak to Mae Jonghak, who was watching him in silence as though he had sensed something.
- **Rationale:** 무언가를 느낀 듯 modifies 매종학, not the Slaughter Saint.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 특정한 장소에 담긴 상징성이란, 바로 사람에 의해 만들어지는 것이니까.
- **Current:** The symbolism attached to a place was created by the people who lived there.
- **Defect:** The draft restricts the people who give a place meaning to its residents.
- **Replacement:** The symbolism attached to a place was created by people.
- **Rationale:** 사람에 의해 identifies people as the source of the symbolism but does not say they lived there.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 구파일방과 오대세가를 필두로 한 무림인들은 말할 것도 없었다.
- **Current:** And the martial artists of the Nine Sects and One Gang and the Five Great Families were not to be outdone.
- **Defect:** “Not to be outdone” adds a competitive motive absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** And it went without saying that martial artists were gathering too, led by the Nine Sects and One Gang and the Five Great Families.
- **Rationale:** 말할 것도 없었다 means their participation went without saying; 필두로 한 identifies the groups leading the martial artists.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 다음 순간 육중하면서도 다급한 발걸음과 함께, 엄청난 덩치의 거한이 집무실로 난입하기 전까지는.
- **Current:** The next moment, before a massive brute could burst into the study with heavy, hurried footsteps—
- **Defect:** “Before ... could burst” implies the entrance was prevented, though the source says he burst in and interrupted the Slaughter Saint.
- **Replacement:** Then heavy, hurried footsteps sounded, and a massive man burst into the study.
- **Rationale:** The source's 전까지는 completes the preceding “Or tried to”: the attempt to speak lasted only until the man entered.

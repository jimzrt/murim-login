# Review

The draft has two reversed comic beats, a misidentified master, and several losses of source-specific meaning.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 억지로 입꼬리에 힘을 주며 진중한 목소리로 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** I forced the corners of my mouth up and spoke in a solemn voice.
- **Defect:** The draft makes him force a smile rather than struggle to suppress one.
- **Replacement:** I fought to keep the corners of my mouth from rising and spoke in a solemn voice.
- **Rationale:** After enjoying Hwaran’s embrace, he tries to maintain a serious expression.

## F02 — Critical

- **Current:** But unlike my Master, his Disciple had stayed in Sichuan, and there was no question he was a master physician worthy of the title.

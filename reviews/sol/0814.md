# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it adds an engagement, misdescribes a facial feature, and loses a few mechanical and comic details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이 여자, 방금 네가 죽인 그 인간의 연인이었어. 올해 안에 결혼할 생각이었나 봐.
- **Current:** This woman was engaged to the man you just killed. I guess they were planning to get married before the end of the year.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the couple engaged, while the source says they were lovers and only suggests they planned to marry.
- **Replacement:** This woman was the lover of the man you just killed. Looks like they were planning to get married this year.
- **Rationale:** 연인 identifies their relationship; 결혼할 생각이었나 봐 expresses an apparent plan, not an engagement.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 낮은 콧대와 입안으로 보이는 덧니.
- **Current:** The low bridge of its nose and the buck teeth visible inside its mouth.
- **Defect:** Buck teeth describes protruding front teeth, not 덧니.
- **Replacement:** The low bridge of its nose and the crooked teeth visible in its mouth.
- **Rationale:** 덧니 refers to teeth growing out of alignment.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 단전에서 솟구친 열기가 혈도를 타고 단검으로 내달렸다.
- **Current:** Heat surged from my dantian, raced along my acupoints, and flowed into the blade.
- **Defect:** “Along my acupoints” treats points as a continuous route and obscures the movement into the dagger.
- **Replacement:** Heat surged from my dantian, raced through my acupoints, and flowed into the dagger.
- **Rationale:** The heat travels via 혈도 from the dantian into the dagger.

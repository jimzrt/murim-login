# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes one affiliation into a title, pluralizes a single fighter, and flattens two pointed forms of address.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 그날, 청년은 마군(魔軍)이 되었다.
- **Current:** And that day, the young man became a Demon Lord.
- **Defect:** 魔軍 refers to a demonic army or force, not the Demon Lord title. The draft gives him a title at this point rather than saying he joined that force.
- **Replacement:** And that day, the young man became part of the demonic army.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly writes 魔軍, whereas Demon Lord would require a different character for 군.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** 도대체, 네년은 누구냐.
- **Current:** “Who the hell are you?”
- **Defect:** The translation drops the gendered insult in 네년.
- **Replacement:** “Who the hell are you, woman?”
- **Rationale:** His address is contemptuous and specifically directed at a woman, not merely a generic expletive.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제자인 마삼보와 죽음에서 부활한 황도십이궁의 고수를 좌우로 거느린 채
- **Current:** His Disciple Ma Sanbao and the masters of the Twelve Palaces of the Zodiac, returned from the dead, flanked him on either side
- **Defect:** The draft turns one resurrected master into multiple masters.
- **Replacement:** His Disciple Ma Sanbao and a master of the Twelve Palaces of the Zodiac, returned from the dead, flanked him on either side
- **Rationale:** 고수를 is singular here; Ma Sanbao and that master occupy the two flanking positions.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 거기 존나 못생긴 오빠. 많이 안 바쁘면 시간 좀 내주라.
- **Current:** “Hey, you ugly-ass bastard. If you’re not too busy, spare us a little time.”
- **Defect:** “Bastard” replaces the deliberately familiar, mocking 오빠, while “us” adds an addressee-side plural absent from the request.
- **Replacement:** “Hey, ugly-ass big bro. If you’re not too busy, make some time for me.”
- **Rationale:** The speaker uses a provocative older-brother address alongside strong profanity and asks him to make time.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** 지금이라도 칼 물고 자결하면, 그럼 한 번 정도는 고민해 볼게.
- **Current:** “If you bite down on a sword and kill yourself right now, I might consider telling you once.”
- **Defect:** “Once” attaches to telling him, rather than to her willingness to consider telling him.
- **Replacement:** “If you bite down on a sword and kill yourself right now, I might think about telling you.”
- **Rationale:** 한 번 정도는 고민해 볼게 qualifies the consideration, not the number of times she would answer.

# Review

Several scene facts and relationships are altered, and the mistranslated greeting breaks a central joke.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 뒤늦게 말실수를 깨달은 나는 한숨을 푹 내쉬었다.
- **Current:** “Anyway, yes. I hope both of you are in excellent health.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung repeats the mistaken wish that they be split in two; the draft gives the correct greeting, leaving no reason for the shocked reaction or his realization that he misspoke.
- **Replacement:** “Anyway, yes. I hope you’ve both been split in two.”
- **Rationale:** He says 기체후일도양단, carrying Hyuk Mujin’s erroneous 일도양단 into the formal greeting.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 바로 초상집의 주인공이 될 뻔한 그는
- **Current:** He’d very nearly become the guest of honor at a funeral instead.
- **Defect:** A guest of honor is not necessarily the dead person; Song Il nearly became the subject of his own funeral.
- **Replacement:** He’d very nearly ended up being the one everyone mourned at a funeral instead.
- **Rationale:** 초상집의 주인공 refers to the deceased, not an honored attendee.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 갑자기 나타난 새파란 놈의 정체가 바로 그 열화신룡 진태경이라는 것에 또 다시 한 번.
- **Current:** Then they realized that the blue-eyed young man who’d suddenly appeared was none other than the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, Jin Taekyung.
- **Defect:** The draft invents blue eyes. 새파란 놈 describes a very young upstart, not eye color.
- **Replacement:** Then they realized that the young upstart who’d suddenly appeared was none other than the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, Jin Taekyung.
- **Rationale:** The shock is at the newcomer’s identity despite his youth; no physical eye detail is supplied.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “종남파의 공 모(某)가, 적 대협을 뵙습니다.”
- **Current:** “Gong Iljung of the Zhongnan Sect pays his respects to Great Hero Jeok.”
- **Defect:** Gong does not introduce himself by his full name here. He uses the self-effacing 공 모, withholding his given name.
- **Replacement:** “This humble Gong of the Zhongnan Sect pays his respects to Great Hero Jeok.”
- **Rationale:** The deliberately modest introduction precedes Jeok’s question about his name.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그, 적 대협. 부디 저와 제 스승님의 얼굴을 봐서라도…….”
- **Current:** “G-Great Hero Jeok, please, for my sake and my Masters’…”
- **Defect:** The draft changes Gong’s singular Master into multiple Masters.
- **Replacement:** “G-Great Hero Jeok, please, for my sake and my Master’s…”
- **Rationale:** 제 스승님 is singular and identifies a specific relationship in his appeal.

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “응이요.”
- **Current:** “Yeah, I am.”
- **Defect:** Taekyung’s deliberately absurd attempt to tack politeness onto an informal yes disappears.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah, sir.”
- **Rationale:** 응이요 mixes casual 응 with the polite ending 요 in direct response to the complaint about his speech level.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “그래도 될걸. 나이만 새파랗지 배분은 노인네라서.”
- **Current:** “I suppose I can. I might be young, but my seniority’s that of an old man.”
- **Defect:** “I suppose I can” answers the Daoist’s rhetorical accusation as though it were permission or ability, obscuring Taekyung’s claim that his seniority entitles him to speak this way.
- **Replacement:** “I can get away with it. I might be young, but in martial seniority I’m an old man.”
- **Rationale:** 그래도 될걸 asserts that his behavior is permissible because his 배분 outranks his apparent age.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Source:** “오. 역시 주 소저. 그리핀용봉표국에 십 점.”
- **Current:** “Oh! As expected of Young Lady Ju. Ten points to the Griffin-and-Phoenix Escort Bureau.”
- **Defect:** The joke alters the established Yongbong Escort Bureau’s name by dropping its dragon half.
- **Replacement:** “Oh! As expected of Young Lady Ju. Ten points to the Griffin Yongbong Escort Bureau.”
- **Rationale:** 그리핀 is added to 용봉표국; it does not replace 용 with a griffin.

## F09 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.88
- **Source:** 적천강이 다시 한 번 위대한 경지를 개척하며 젊음을 되찾았다는 소문은 이미 퍼질 대로 퍼졌고
- **Current:** The rumor that Jeok Cheongang had broken through into a new, transcendent realm and regained his youth had spread far and wide.
- **Defect:** “Transcendent” assigns a specific quality to the new realm that the source does not establish.
- **Replacement:** The rumor that Jeok Cheongang had reached another extraordinary realm and regained his youth had spread far and wide.
- **Rationale:** 위대한 경지 calls the achievement great; it does not label the realm transcendent.

## F10 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** ‘배 째고 있었구만.’
- **Current:** *They were dragging their feet.*
- **Defect:** The draft reduces brazen refusal to mere slowness.
- **Replacement:** *They’d dug in their heels and dared anyone to do something about it.*
- **Rationale:** 배 째고 있었구만 conveys a defiant, take-it-or-leave-it stance, consistent with Taekyung’s conclusion that the two elders held the sect back.

## F11 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 내가 막 사회 초년생으로서 헌터 생활을 시작했을 때
- **Current:** When I’d just started out as a Hunter, fresh from school and new to the job,
- **Defect:** “Fresh from school” adds an educational history not stated in the source.
- **Replacement:** When I’d just entered the workforce and started out as a Hunter,
- **Rationale:** 사회 초년생 means someone new to working life, not necessarily a recent graduate.

# Review

The draft misassigns who must survive, specifies an unidentified person's gender, and changes several physical details.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 결코 죽어서는 안 되는 제자가 혈주와 싸우고 있다.
- **Current:** And his Disciple was fighting the Blood Lord, someone who absolutely could not die.
- **Defect:** The sentence makes the Blood Lord, rather than Jeok Cheongang’s Disciple, the person who must not die.
- **Replacement:** And his Disciple, who absolutely could not be allowed to die, was fighting the Blood Lord.
- **Rationale:** 결코 죽어서는 안 되는 modifies 제자.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 무려 삼 장에 달하는 거구의 괴물도
- **Current:** the monstrous figure standing three yards tall
- **Defect:** Three jang is approximately thirty feet, not three yards; the draft reduces the monster’s height to less than a third of its stated size.
- **Replacement:** the monstrous figure standing nearly thirty feet tall
- **Rationale:** 삼 장 is about nine to ten meters.

## F03 — Major

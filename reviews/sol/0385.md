# Review

One critical action reversal and five additional fidelity or register issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 5년 전에 버닝문인가 뭔가 하는 클럽에서 약 타서 성폭행 저지르다가 걸렸던 거.
- **Current:** Five years ago, you drugged someone at a club called Burning Moon or something and got caught trying to sexually assault her.
- **Defect:** The draft changes a sexual assault being committed into merely an attempted assault and adds a specifically female victim absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** Five years ago, you got caught sexually assaulting someone after drugging them at some club called Burning Moon.
- **Rationale:** 저지르다가 걸렸던 states that he was caught while committing the sexual assault, not before completing it; the victim's gender is unstated.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 잦은 미디어 노출로는 S급 헌터 중에서도 1, 2위를 다투는 놈이니 모를 수가 없다.
- **Current:** With his frequent media exposure, he was one of the most prominent S-rank Hunters in the world. There was no way I wouldn’t know him.
- **Defect:** The draft replaces the specific claim that he ranks first or second among S-rank Hunters in frequency of media exposure with the broader and unsupported claim that he is globally prominent.
- **Replacement:** He ranked first or second even among S-rank Hunters when it came to media exposure, so there was no way I wouldn’t know him.
- **Rationale:** 1, 2위를 다투는 explicitly supplies the first-or-second quantity, and 잦은 미디어 노출 is the criterion being ranked.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 「A급 헌터 주제에 주석 동지의 기대를 받는다고 하늘 높은 줄 모르고 나대……!」
- **Current:** “You’re an A-rank Hunter, but you’re getting carried away because the Chairman expects something from you and thinks highly of you—!”
- **Defect:** The draft omits the politically marked address “Comrade Chairman,” adds the unsupported claim that the Chairman thinks highly of Taekyung, and weakens the accusation that Taekyung has forgotten his place.
- **Replacement:** “You’re just an A-rank Hunter, but because Comrade Chairman expects great things from you, you’ve forgotten your place and started running wild—!”
- **Rationale:** 주석 동지 is “Comrade Chairman,” while 하늘 높은 줄 모르고 나대 describes presumptuous behavior without knowing one’s place; it does not separately state that the Chairman thinks highly of him.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 수면 아래에서는 신분을 세탁한 뒤 새로운 삶을 살고 있다는 소식도 심심찮게 들려올 정도였다.
- **Current:** There were even occasional rumors that she had washed her identity clean and was living a new life somewhere beneath the surface.
- **Defect:** “Somewhere beneath the surface” incorrectly reads the idiom as the physical location of her new life, and “washed her identity clean” is unnatural English.
- **Replacement:** Behind the scenes, it wasn’t uncommon to hear that she had assumed a new identity and begun a new life.
- **Rationale:** 수면 아래에서는 refers to information circulating out of public view, while 신분을 세탁하다 means to erase or replace one’s prior identity.

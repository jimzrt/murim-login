# Review

The draft changes a singular reference to the Blood-Sword Demon Lord into a plural one and loses the repeated collar insult. Two smaller physical descriptions also need correction.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 하지만 장장 일천여 년에 걸쳐 천하 무림과 어깨를 나란히 했던, 미친 광신도 집단에서도 유독 미쳐 있던 광견과는 비교 자체가 불가능했다.

물론, 그 미친개는 이제 다른 주인을 섬기는 것 같지만.
- **Current:** Still, there was no comparing them to the mad dogs of that insane cult of fanatics, a group that had stood shoulder to shoulder with the Murim world for more than a thousand years.

Of course, it seemed those mad dogs now served a different master.
- **Defect:** The source singles out one exceptionally rabid dog among the cultists, then says that dog serves a new master. The draft makes both references plural.
- **Replacement:** Still, there was no comparing them to the one mad dog who stood out even among that insane cult of fanatics, a group that had stood shoulder to shoulder with the Murim world for more than a thousand years.

Of course, that mad dog seemed to serve a different master now.
- **Rationale:** 유독 미쳐 있던 광견 and 그 미친개 are singular references to the man Taekyung is confronting.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그래서, 목줄 갈아 끼우니까 밥은 제때 잘 나오고?

- **Defect:** The draft drops the collar image that makes Taekyung's question an insult directed at a dog.
- **Replacement:** “So, with a new collar on, do they feed you on time?”
- **Rationale:** 목줄 갈아 끼우니까 explicitly describes changing collars; the food question continues the dog metaphor.

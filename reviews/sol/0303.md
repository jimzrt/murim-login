# Review

Two scene actions and one kinship subject are mistranslated; several additional lines lose profanity, causal precision, combat mechanics, or natural terminology.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘……너 진짜 미친 새끼니?’
- **Current:** “……Are you actually crazy?”
- **Defect:** The translation omits the strong profanity in 새끼, weakening Song Song’s blunt, hostile register.
- **Replacement:** “……Are you actually fucking crazy?”
- **Rationale:** 미친 새끼 is substantially more profane than merely calling someone crazy.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그 누구도 상상할 수 없는 일이니 당연했다. 거기에 정황을 뒷받침할 만한 증거들도 있었다.
- **Current:** That was only natural. No one could have imagined such a thing. Besides, there was evidence to support the official circumstances.
- **Defect:** “Official circumstances” is an unnatural rendering of 정황 and adds an official designation absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** That was only natural. No one could have imagined such a thing. Besides, there was evidence to back up that version of events.
- **Rationale:** 정황 here refers to the apparent account or version of events supported by the physical evidence.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 달칵, 하는 소리와 함께 사무실 문이 열린 것은 바로 그때였다.

예술적인 각선미를 자랑하는 다리가 들어서려다 말고 멈칫한다.
- **Current:** The office door opened with a click.

A pair of legs boasting an artistic silhouette stepped inside, then froze.
- **Defect:** The draft says Song Song stepped inside before stopping, while the source says she started to enter but stopped short. It also omits the explicit “just then” timing cue.
- **Replacement:** Just then, the office door opened with a click.

- **Rationale:** 들어서려다 말고 means the attempted entrance was interrupted before completion.

## F04 — Minor

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** “워로드몬, 전광.”

“아까 그거 한 번만 더 하면 죽여 버릴 거야. 진심이야.”

“……석화.”
- **Current:** “Warlordmon, Quick Attack.”

“If you do that one more time, I’ll kill you. I mean it.”

“……Petrification.”
- **Defect:** The draft turns the first half of a deliberately interrupted command into a complete Quick Attack order, then mistranslates the second half as a separate Petrification command. The source splits 전광석화 across Song Song’s threat for the joke.
- **Replacement:** “Warlordmon, Quick—”

“If you do that one more time, I’ll kill you. I mean it.”

“……Attack.”
- **Rationale:** 전광 and 석화 are the two halves of 전광석화, the established Quick Attack command; Taekyung pauses and then finishes it despite the warning.

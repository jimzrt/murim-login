# Review

Two critical subject errors alter who is going to Shaolin and who furrows their brow. The draft also mistranslates a causal joke, leaves a shout untranslated, and has several localized terminology and fidelity issues.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 석고상처럼 굳은 그의 뒷모습을 향해 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** I opened my mouth toward his back, which had gone rigid as a stone statue.
- **Defect:** 석고상 is a plaster statue, not a stone statue.
- **Replacement:** I spoke toward his back, which had gone rigid as a plaster statue.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the material specified by the source and avoids the awkward collocation “opened my mouth toward.”

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “소림으로 간다.”
- **Current:** “We’re going to Shaolin.”
- **Defect:** The draft changes Jeok Cheongang’s singular declaration into a plural one, making him explicitly include Taekyung before Taekyung decides to follow.
- **Replacement:** “I’m going to Shaolin.”
- **Rationale:** The omitted Korean subject is established by the later explicit 나는, and Jeok Cheongang is declaring his own destination.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “갈(喝)!”
- **Current:** “喝!”
- **Defect:** The shout has been left as an untranslated Chinese character, violating the English-only reading-copy requirement.
- **Replacement:** “Hah!”
- **Rationale:** 갈 is a forceful shouted exclamation intended to shock the listeners back to their senses.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “종리추, 암천, 무명. 불장.”
- **Current:** “Jongni Chu. Dark Heaven. Unnamed. Buddha Staff.”
- **Defect:** 불장 is a Buddhist or monk’s staff, not a staff belonging to the Buddha; “Buddha Staff” is unnatural outside the established compound Green Jade Buddha Staff.
- **Replacement:** “Jongni Chu. Dark Heaven. Unnamed. Buddhist Staff.”
- **Rationale:** The source uses the generic object term here, while 녹옥불장 is the proper established name introduced afterward.

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Minor

- **Source:** “그런데 불장은 뭡니까? 혹시 스님들이 들고 다니는 그 지팡이 같은 거 말씀하신 건가요?”

## F07 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “맞다. 다만 굉도가 말한 불장은 소림의 신물인 녹옥불장(綠玉佛杖)을 뜻한다.”
- **Current:** “That is correct. But the Buddha Staff Hong Dao mentioned is the Green Jade Buddha Staff, the sacred treasure of Shaolin.”
- **Defect:** The generic term remains unnatural, and “is” weakens the source’s explicit statement that Hong Dao meant the specific sacred treasure.
- **Replacement:** “That’s right. But the Buddhist staff Hong Dao mentioned was the Green Jade Buddha Staff, Shaolin’s sacred treasure.”
- **Rationale:** The source distinguishes the generic category 불장 from the named artifact 녹옥불장 and explicitly identifies the latter as what Hong Dao meant.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘제 놈들이 별수 있나. 괜히 안 한다는 사람한테 방장 직을 맡긴 게 누군데?’
- **Current:** *“What can those bastards do, anyway? Who was it that made someone who said he wouldn’t do it take the Abbot’s seat?”*
- **Defect:** 별수 있나 means they had no other choice, not “What can those bastards do?” The second rhetorical question also identifies those same people as the ones who forced the unwilling post on Hong Dao.
- **Replacement:** *“What choice did they have? They’re the ones who forced the Abbot’s seat on someone who said he didn’t want it.”*
- **Rationale:** The joke is that Shaolin had no alternative after imposing the position on an unwilling Hong Dao; the draft instead sounds like a challenge to their capabilities.

## F09 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98

- **Current:** Jeok Cheongang slightly narrowed his brow before continuing.
- **Defect:** The draft assigns the brow-furrowing to Jeok Cheongang, but the explicit subject introduced after the temporal clause shows that Taekyung is the one reacting before Jeok Cheongang continues.
- **Replacement:** As I furrowed my brow slightly, Jeok Cheongang continued.
- **Rationale:** The Korean changes to the explicit subject 적천강 for the continuing speech, so the preceding omitted subject remains the first-person narrator.

## F10 — Minor

## F11 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “개방의 거지들은 대부분 모르더군. 무공이 쓸 만한 걸 봐서는 하오문 나부랭이 같지는 않고…… 그렇지. 은영각이었어.”
- **Current:** “Most of the beggars from the Beggars’ Sect didn’t know. Judging by your martial arts, you don’t seem like some nobody from the Lower District Sect… That’s it. You’re from the Hidden Shadow Pavilion.”
- **Defect:** The draft weakens the identifying clue that the man’s martial arts are notably competent and changes the contemptuous organizational label 하오문 나부랭이 into “some nobody,” which implies an individual lack of status instead.
- **Replacement:** “Most of those Beggars’ Sect beggars didn’t know. Your martial arts are too decent for you to be Lower District Sect riffraff… That’s it. You’re from the Hidden Shadow Pavilion.”
- **Rationale:** Han Su explicitly uses the man’s capable martial arts to rule out the Lower District Sect while disparaging that organization’s members.

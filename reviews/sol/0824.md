# Review

The draft is largely faithful, but it changes a death count and misidentifies where the absorbed souls go. Several smaller choices alter the followers’ beliefs or the scene’s imagery.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 진태경에 의해 수백 번도 넘는 죽음을 겪은 도플갱어는
- **Current:** Having died at Jin Taekyung’s hands more than a hundred times
- **Defect:** Reduces the number of deaths from more than several hundred to more than one hundred.
- **Replacement:** Having died at Jin Taekyung’s hands hundreds of times
- **Rationale:** 수백 번도 넘는 describes deaths numbering in the hundreds, not merely exceeding one hundred.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 신의 뜻을 바로 세우기 위한 성전(聖戰)이라 생각했으니까.
- **Current:** They had believed it was a holy war to set God’s will right.
- **Defect:** Implies that God’s will itself needed correcting.
- **Replacement:** They had believed it was a holy war to uphold God’s will.
- **Rationale:** 바로 세우기 refers to establishing or upholding God's will through their war.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.85
- **Source:** 가파르게 쏘아지는 신형들을 따라 휘몰아치는 바람.
- **Current:** Wind roared around the figures shooting forward at a steep angle.
- **Defect:** Introduces a steep trajectory where the source describes rapid movement.
- **Replacement:** Wind roared around the figures hurtling forward.
- **Rationale:** The passage conveys the speed of their flight, not a change in elevation.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.92
- **Source:** 죽음도 무엇도 아닌, 끔찍한 심연 속 무저갱으로 인도하고 있었다.
- **Current:** It was leading them into a dreadful abyss, deeper than death or anything else.
- **Defect:** Turns the contrast with death into a comparison of depth.
- **Replacement:** It was leading them not to death, or anything else, but into a bottomless pit within a dreadful abyss.
- **Rationale:** 죽음도 무엇도 아닌 rules out death as their destination; it does not say the abyss is deeper than death.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 희끄무레한 수십의 영혼이 홀린 듯이 어둠 속 자신을 향해 흘러 들어갔다.
- **Current:** Dozens of pale souls flowed as if entranced toward the darkness that was them.
- **Defect:** Makes the darkness identical to the victims and directs the souls toward them rather than toward the Doppelganger.
- **Replacement:** Dozens of pale souls flowed as if entranced toward the Doppelganger within the darkness.
- **Rationale:** 자신 refers to the Doppelganger: the souls flow toward it inside the darkness as it absorbs their lives.

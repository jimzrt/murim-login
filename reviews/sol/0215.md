# Review

Six actionable findings: one quantity error, two meaningful fidelity issues, and three localized terminology/Markdown problems.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 3년 전 내가 맞닥트린 그놈은 E급 게이트에서 나올 수도, 나와서도 안 되는 존재였다.
- **Current:** The one I had encountered three years ago was something that could never have emerged from an E-Rank Gate, much less should have.
- **Defect:** “Much less should have” creates a malformed logical relationship between impossibility and prohibition instead of preserving both parallel negations.
- **Replacement:** The creature I had encountered three years ago was something that could not—and should not—have emerged from an E-Rank Gate.
- **Rationale:** The source states separately that the creature could not emerge from an E-Rank Gate and must not be allowed to emerge from one.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 드레이크와 함께 이른바 용족(龍族)으로 분류되는 상위 몬스터 중 하나다.
- **Current:** One of the higher monsters classified as dragonkin, along with drakes.
- **Defect:** “Higher monsters” is an unnatural and imprecise rendering of a monster’s tier.
- **Replacement:** One of the high-tier monsters classified as dragonkin, along with drakes.
- **Rationale:** 상위 몬스터 denotes an upper-tier or high-tier monster, not a physically or comparatively “higher” monster.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “1팀장 이 새끼가.”
- **Current:** “That bastard, Team Leader One.”
- **Defect:** “Team Leader One” misidentifies an ordinal team title as though “One” were a name.
- **Replacement:** “That bastard, the leader of Team One.”
- **Rationale:** 1팀장 refers to the leader of Team One; the source does not introduce a person named or titled “Team Leader One.”

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 두꺼운 통짜 갑옷을 걸친 임꺽정의 입에서 단내가 뿜어져 나왔다.
- **Current:** Steam poured from Im Kkeokjeong’s mouth as he wore his thick, one-piece armor.
- **Defect:** The draft invents visible steam in a hot jungle and renders the exertion idiom 단내가 나다 literally incorrectly; “as he wore” also makes wearing the armor sound like a simultaneous action.
- **Replacement:** Im Kkeokjeong, clad in thick, solid plate armor, panted raggedly.
- **Rationale:** 단내가 뿜어져 나왔다 conveys severe exertion and labored breathing, while 통짜 갑옷 describes heavy solid armor rather than generic “one-piece armor.”

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** - [예티의 목걸이]를 장착하셨습니다.
- **Current:** > - You have equipped **[Yeti’s Necklace]**.
- **Defect:** The System notice retains square brackets around an Item, contrary to the required System-panel formatting.
- **Replacement:** > - You have equipped **Yeti’s Necklace**.
- **Rationale:** The source brackets identify the Item, but the binding Markdown rules require System notices to omit such square brackets.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 모골이 송연해지는 울음소리와 함께 거대한 그늘이 이십여 명의 사람들을 감싸 안는다.
- **Current:** Along with a cry that raised goose bumps, a massive shadow enveloped the more than twenty people.
- **Defect:** “More than twenty” changes the approximate quantity 이십여 명 into a strict lower bound.
- **Replacement:** Along with a cry that raised goose bumps, a massive shadow enveloped the roughly twenty people.
- **Rationale:** 이십여 명 means approximately twenty or twenty-odd people, unlike the earlier explicit 이십 명이 넘는, which does mean more than twenty.

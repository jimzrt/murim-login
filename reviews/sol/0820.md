# Review

The draft has three scene-changing errors: it reverses Yahya’s survival claim, turns a thrown spear into a thrust, and obscures why Jin realizes he is unarmed. It also loses a quantity and two jokes.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 신의 선택을 받기 위해 수십, 수백이 죽었지. 하지만 나만은 아니었다.
- **Current:** Dozens, hundreds died in the attempt to be chosen by God. But I was not the only one.
- **Defect:** The last sentence says Yahya was not the only one chosen; the source contrasts those who died with Yahya, who did not.
- **Replacement:** Dozens, hundreds died trying to be chosen by God. But I alone survived.
- **Rationale:** 나만은 아니었다 excludes Yahya from the preceding deaths, not from the group of chosen warriors.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 이를 악물며 고통을 참아낸 나는 정면에서 달려드는 광신도들을 향해 역수(逆手)로 쥔 창을 내쏘았다.

투창을 시도하기에는 좁은 공간이었지만, 내게는 단 한 걸음이면 충분했다.
- **Current:** I clenched my teeth against the pain and thrust the spear in my reverse grip at the fanatics charging from the front.

The space was too tight to throw the spear, but I only needed a single step.
- **Defect:** Jin throws the spear; the draft makes him thrust it while saying there is no room to throw it.
- **Replacement:** I clenched my teeth against the pain and hurled the spear I held in a reverse grip at the fanatics charging straight at me.

There was barely room to throw a spear, but one step was all I needed.
- **Rationale:** 투창 identifies the action as a spear throw, which also explains Jin’s realization that his weapon is gone.

# Review

Three critical reversals or identity changes, plus several localized action, characterization, register, and terminology defects.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이게 배반이 아니면 내가 한족이 아니라 남만인이지. 그러니까 아니라고 하진 마시고.
- **Current:** —If this isn’t betrayal, then I’m Han Chinese in name only. So don’t bother denying it.
- **Defect:** The conditional identity joke is mistranslated. Jin says that if this does not count as betrayal, then he is not Han Chinese but a Nanman native; he does not call himself Han Chinese in name only.
- **Replacement:** —If this isn’t betrayal, then I’m not Han Chinese—I’m a Nanman native. So don’t try to deny it.
- **Rationale:** The source explicitly contrasts 한족이 아니라 with 남만인이지, making Jin’s hypothetical change of ethnicity the punchline.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정곡을 찔린 듯, 입술을 질끈 깨문 흑웅이 작게 고개를 끄덕였다.
- **Current:** As if I had struck the exact spot, Heugung clenched his lips and gave a small nod.
- **Defect:** Heugung bites his lip rather than merely clenching his lips.
- **Replacement:** As if I had hit the mark, Heugung bit his lip hard and gave a small nod.
- **Rationale:** 입술을 질끈 깨문 describes the physical action of biting down on one’s lip.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 내가 기억하는 백상 대족장은 언제나 냉철하고 뛰어난 사람이었지.
- **Current:** —The Great Chieftain Baeksang I remember was always cold and outstanding.

- **Replacement:** —The Great Chieftain Baeksang I remember was always coolheaded and exceptional.
- **Rationale:** The source characterizes Baeksang’s judgment and ability, not merely an emotionally cold disposition.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 여간 힘에 부치는지, 그가 잘게 떨리는 손으로 내려놓은 대접에서 국물이 흘러넘친다.
- **Current:** Perhaps the bowl was too heavy for him, because soup spilled over the rim as he set it down with a trembling hand.
- **Defect:** The draft invents the bowl’s weight as the cause. The source only indicates that the task appears physically taxing for the old man.
- **Replacement:** The effort seemed almost too much for him; broth spilled over the rim as he set the bowl down with a trembling hand.
- **Rationale:** 힘에 부치다 means that something exceeds or strains one’s strength, without specifying that the bowl itself is unusually heavy.

## F05 — Minor

## F06 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이중 무엇이 진실이고, 무엇이 거짓일까.
- **Current:** Which of them was telling the truth, and which was lying?
- **Defect:** The draft turns an inquiry about which elements are true or false into an accusation that one of the people is intentionally lying.
- **Replacement:** Which of these was true, and which was false?
- **Rationale:** 무엇이 진실이고, 무엇이 거짓 asks about the truth or falsity of the possibilities just listed, without explicitly assigning deliberate deception to a person.

## F07 — Critical

- **Current:** And another person who had yet to reveal himself.

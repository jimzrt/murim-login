## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “소피보러 가는 거야. 됐지?”
- **Current:** “I’m going to take a piss. Happy?”
- **Defect:** The draft makes the initial euphemism explicitly vulgar, erasing the register shift when Taekyung subsequently resorts to the more direct 노상 방뇨.
- **Replacement:** “I’m going to relieve myself. Happy?”
- **Rationale:** 소피 is a euphemistic reference to urination; the comedy depends on this restrained excuse failing before Taekyung bluntly claims he will urinate outdoors.

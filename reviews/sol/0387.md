## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 「응. 우리끼리.」
- **Current:** “Yeah. Just the two of us.”
- **Defect:** The translation changes “among ourselves” into exactly two people, even though the following sentence establishes that two other people are already part of the group.
- **Replacement:** “Yeah. Just us.”
- **Rationale:** 우리끼리 restricts the gathering to the speaker's group; it does not specify a party of two.

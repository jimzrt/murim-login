# Review

Four actionable findings: two altered meanings, one characterization error, and one lost pragmatic cue.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 특유의 일차원적인 행동으로 시비부터 걸고 봤는데, 이게 영 반응도 안 좋고 저 빵즈 놈도 생각 외로 만만치가 않네?
- **Current:** You picked a fight first with your usual one-dimensional behavior, but then neither the reaction nor that bangzi bastard turned out to be as easy as you expected, huh?
- **Defect:** The draft treats both “the reaction” and Taekyung as things that were not easy. The source instead says Wu's provocation was poorly received and Taekyung proved unexpectedly difficult.
- **Replacement:** You picked a fight with your usual one-dimensional behavior, but it didn’t go over well, and that *bangzi* bastard turned out to be tougher than you expected, huh?
- **Rationale:** 반응도 안 좋고 describes the bad response to Wu's behavior, while 만만치가 않네 applies specifically to Taekyung.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 「얘도 만만치 않네.」
- **Current:** “He’s not exactly harmless either.”
- **Defect:** “Not harmless” implies danger or malicious intent, while Pai Chen is reacting to Choi's unexpectedly eccentric fixation on her Equipment.
- **Replacement:** “This one’s quite a handful too.”
- **Rationale:** 만만치 않네 here characterizes Choi as unexpectedly formidable or difficult in a comic sense, not as physically dangerous.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그렇게 몰래 빼돌린 금송아지를 들켰으니, 놈의 반응이 좋지 않은 것은 당연했다.
- **Current:** They had discovered the golden calf his family had secretly smuggled away. Naturally, he wasn’t reacting well.
- **Defect:** The draft invents an unspecified plural discoverer and explicitly assigns the secret appropriation to Wu's family. The source only says that the secretly appropriated treasure has now been exposed.
- **Replacement:** Now that he’d been caught with the golden calf that had been secretly spirited away, it was only natural that he’d react badly.
- **Rationale:** 들켰으니 means the secret has been found out in the present confrontation; neither “they” nor “his family” is stated.

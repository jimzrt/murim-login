# Review

The draft has three substantive action and subject errors, plus a loss of meaning in the contrast between kinds of generals.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 이미 한 번 죽은 적들이, 동료들이 되살아나는 이 혼란스러운 상황 속에서 약점을 간파해 냈다.
- **Current:** In the chaos of enemies who’d already died returning as their comrades, they’d seen through his weakness.
- **Defect:** The draft turns dead enemies into comrades; the source says enemies and comrades alike are rising again.
- **Replacement:** Amid the chaos of fallen enemies and comrades alike rising again, they’d seen through his weakness.
- **Rationale:** 적들이, 동료들이 names both groups as subjects of 되살아나는.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 마치 섬광처럼 살과 뼈를 부수며 틀어박힌 십여 개의 핏줄기.
- **Current:** More than ten streams of blood burst through flesh and bone like flashes of light.
- **Defect:** Blood streams replace the greatsword fragments that lodge in Golden Ox Palace’s body.
- **Replacement:** More than ten blood-streaked shards shot into him like flashes of light, shattering flesh and bone as they lodged inside.
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentence sends the shattered blade’s fragments toward him; 틀어박힌 says they lodge in his flesh.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 자신을 방패막이로 삼아 그 거인을 쓰러트리려 하는 주인의 명령을 완수하기 위해.
- **Current:** And to fulfill his master’s order—to use himself as a shield to bring that giant down.
- **Defect:** The draft makes Golden Ox Palace choose to use himself as a shield. His master intends to use him as one.
- **Replacement:** And to carry out the order of his master, who meant to use him as a shield to bring that giant down.
- **Rationale:** 방패막이로 삼아 그 거인을 쓰러트리려 하는 modifies 주인, identifying the master as the one using him.

## F04 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 하지만 명장(名將)은 드물다.
- **Current:** But famous generals were rare.
- **Defect:** “Famous” loses the contrast between fierce battlefield fighters and exceptional commanders.
- **Replacement:** But truly great commanders were rare.
- **Rationale:** 명장 contrasts a commander distinguished by ability with the 맹장 mentioned immediately before, not merely someone widely known.

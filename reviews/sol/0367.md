# Review

Two critical subject/action errors, one major change to who faces a suicidal order, one major characterization shift, and several localized register and natural-English issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 노부는 누구인가.
- **Current:** Who was this old man?
- **Defect:** The subject is reversed. 노부 is Jeok Cheongang’s formal self-reference, not the unidentified Divine Physician.
- **Replacement:** Who was I?
- **Rationale:** The line is part of Jeok Cheongang’s comically existential reaction: where was he, who was he, and why were boulders falling on him.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 당최 상황이 어찌 되어 가는지는 모르나 우선은 몸부터 피하고 봐야 했다.
- **Current:** He had no idea what was happening, but he had to get his body out of there first.
- **Defect:** “Get his body out” is an unnatural calque that can sound as though he is moving a separate body.
- **Replacement:** He had no idea what was happening, but first he had to get himself to safety.
- **Rationale:** 몸부터 피하다 means to remove oneself from danger before doing anything else.

## F03 — Minor

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 짧지만 억겁과도 같은 시간 속, 마침내 적천강이 뇌옥의 입구를 향해 마지막 한 걸음을 떼려던 그때였다.
- **Current:** Within a short span of time that felt like an eternity, Jeok Cheongang finally took the last step toward the entrance of the prison.
- **Defect:** The draft says he completed the final step, but the source says the collapse began just as he was about to take it.
- **Replacement:** Within a short span of time that felt like an eternity, Jeok Cheongang was finally about to take the last step toward the prison entrance.
- **Rationale:** 떼려던 그때 marks an interrupted, not completed, action; that timing is what leaves the entrance blocked by the collapse.

## F05 — Major

## F11 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저 하늘 위, 어느새 물러간 먹구름 사이로 비추는 햇살을 받으며 한 소년이 우뚝 서 있었다.

## F12 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 소년의 그것처럼 맑고 반짝이는 듯하면서도, 아무런 감정도 느껴지지 않는 노회한 눈빛.
- **Current:** The boy’s eyes were clear and sparkling like a child’s, yet they held the cunning, seasoned gaze of someone who felt no emotion whatsoever.
- **Defect:** “Cunning” adds deceit absent from 노회한, while “someone who felt no emotion” changes an unreadable gaze into an assertion that the person is incapable of feeling emotion.

- **Rationale:** The source contrasts youthful-looking eyes with an aged, experienced gaze from which Jeok Cheongang can discern no emotion.

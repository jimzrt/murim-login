# Review

The draft preserves the fight’s main sequence, but changes several physical details and misorders the System notices.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 제아무리 잘 훈련받은 사냥개라고 해도 홀로 맹수를 쓰러트릴 수는 없다.

타고난 태생의 차이는 어쩔 수 없는 법이니까.

그러나 맹수로 태어나, 원하는 목적을 위해 사냥개가 된 자무카는 달랐다.
- **Current:** No matter how well-trained a hunting dog was, it couldn’t take down a beast of prey on its own.

The difference in their innate nature was simply unavoidable.

But Jamukha was different. Born a beast of prey, he had become a hunting dog for the sake of his own ambitions.
- **Defect:** “Beast of prey” makes the stronger animal sound like quarry rather than the predator contrasted with a hunting dog.
- **Replacement:** No matter how well trained a hunting dog was, it couldn’t take down a predator on its own.

The difference in what they were born to be was unavoidable.

But Jamukha was different. Born a predator, he had become a hunting dog to achieve his own ends.
- **Rationale:** The contrast is between a hunting dog and a naturally powerful 맹수; Jamukha was born the latter.

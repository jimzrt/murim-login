# Review

Two scene facts are altered, and several localized fidelity and terminology issues remain.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지난 밤사이 수십 척의 선박과 생명을 집어삼킨 동정호의 강물은 거칠었고, 저 멀리서부터 불어오는 바람은 난폭했다.
- **Current:** The waters of Dongting Lake, which had swallowed dozens of vessels and countless lives overnight, were rough, and the wind blowing in from the distance was violent.
- **Defect:** The draft adds the unsupported quantity “countless” to the lives lost; 수십 modifies the vessels, while the source gives no number for the lives.
- **Replacement:** The waters of Dongting Lake, which had swallowed dozens of vessels and claimed lives overnight, were rough, and the wind blowing in from the distance was violent.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the explicit count for the vessels without inventing a separate quantity for the fatalities.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 청풍에 질세라 홍란 역시 고개를 끄덕인다.
- **Current:** Cheongpung nodded, unwilling to be outdone by Honglan.
- **Defect:** The subject and comparison are reversed: Honglan nods so as not to be outdone by Cheongpung.
- **Replacement:** Not to be outdone by Cheongpung, Honglan nodded as well.
- **Rationale:** 청풍에 질세라 identifies Cheongpung as the person Honglan refuses to be outdone by, and 홍란 is the subject of 끄덕인다.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 그러고 보니 죽은 사공들이 사라진 지점이 전부 엇비슷합니다.
- **Current:** Come to think of it, the places where the dead boatmen disappeared were all fairly similar.
- **Defect:** The draft suggests that the locations physically resembled one another, rather than that the disappearances occurred around the same area.
- **Replacement:** Come to think of it, all the dead boatmen disappeared in roughly the same area.
- **Rationale:** 지점이 전부 엇비슷합니다 describes the locations as clustered or approximately coincident, furnishing a geographic clue about the attacks.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 생각을 끝마친 나는 불쑥 입을 열었다.
- **Current:** After finishing my preparations, I suddenly spoke.
- **Defect:** The draft changes Taekyung finishing his thoughts into finishing unspecified physical preparations.
- **Replacement:** Having finished thinking it through, I abruptly spoke.
- **Rationale:** 생각을 끝마친 refers to the conclusion of the preceding internal deliberation, not to preparations.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 나도 마찬가지다. 대 개방의 후개가 고작 이 정도로 겁을 먹을 수는 없지.
- **Current:** I feel the same. The Successor Beggar of the Great Beggars’ Sect can’t be scared off by something like this.
- **Defect:** “Great Beggars’ Sect” reads as a changed formal organization name rather than 대 functioning as a boastful modifier.
- **Replacement:** I feel the same. The Successor Beggar of the mighty Beggars’ Sect can’t be scared off by something like this.
- **Rationale:** The established organization name is Beggars’ Sect; 대 emphasizes its stature without becoming part of that name.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 이 빌어먹을 놈이 왜 잘 나가다가 시비를 걸지? 간이 배 밖으로 나왔냐?
- **Current:** This damn bastard. Why pick a fight when things were going so well? Did your guts fall out?
- **Defect:** “Did your guts fall out?” is an unnatural literal rendering of an idiom meaning that someone has become recklessly bold or lost his sense.
- **Replacement:** You damned bastard. Why pick a fight when things were going so well? Have you lost your mind?
- **Rationale:** 간이 배 밖으로 나왔냐 challenges Hyuk Mujin’s audacity; it does not describe his entrails physically falling out.

## F07 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 가까운 사람을 잃는다는 것이. 다시 한번 그날의 과거가 반복될 것 같아서 두렵다.
- **Current:** I was afraid of losing people close to me. Afraid that the past would repeat itself once again.
- **Defect:** The draft generalizes 그날의 과거 and loses Taekyung’s reference to the specific traumatic events of “that day.”
- **Replacement:** I was afraid of losing people close to me. Afraid that what happened that day would repeat itself.
- **Rationale:** 그날 points to a particular remembered event, making his fear personal and specific rather than a generic fear of history repeating.

## F08 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 하루아침에 가문과 부모를 잃은 여아가 지금의 모습으로 있기까지는…… 참으로 많은 일이 있었지요.
- **Current:** Many things happened before a little girl who lost her family and parents in a single morning could become the person she is now…
- **Defect:** “Family and parents” blurs 가문 into a redundant synonym for her parents, losing the distinct loss of her clan or household.
- **Replacement:** A great many things happened before a little girl who lost her clan and her parents overnight could become the woman she is today…
- **Rationale:** 가문 denotes her wider clan or family house, separately from 부모, while 하루아침에 idiomatically conveys a sudden, overnight loss.

- **Rationale:** 무운 is specifically fortune in war or combat, fitting Honglan’s formal farewell before the impending confrontation.

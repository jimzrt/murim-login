# Review

The draft contains two scene-fact errors and several meaningful losses of characterization or pragmatic meaning.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 한 단어로 정리하자면, 준비된 인재라는 거다.
- **Current:** In one word, he was a born talent.
- **Defect:** “Born talent” reduces Choi’s advantage to innate ability, while 준비된 인재 summarizes the combination of his natural martial talent and the substantial energy already accumulated inside him.
- **Replacement:** In short, he was a talent with all the groundwork already laid.
- **Rationale:** The preceding sentences identify two separate advantages—innate martial talent and preexisting internal energy—which 준비된 captures as preparation or readiness.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그건 아니고, 길드원 중 하나가 위험에 빠지는 것 같아서 걱정이 되네요.”
- **Current:** “No. I’m worried because it seems one of our Guild members is about to put himself in danger.”
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Guild member deliberately place himself in danger, whereas 위험에 빠지다 only says that he appears to be heading into or falling into danger.
- **Replacement:** “No. I’m worried because it looks like one of our Guild members is heading into danger.”
- **Rationale:** The source expresses Choi’s concern about Jin’s peril without explicitly assigning deliberate agency to Jin.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “그래요. 멀리 안 나갑니다.”
- **Current:** “Sure. I won’t be going far.”
- **Defect:** The draft reads 멀리 안 나갑니다 literally as a statement about Jin’s future location. In this departure exchange, it is the conventional remark that he will not accompany the visitor farther or see him out.
- **Replacement:** “All right. I won’t see you out.”
- **Rationale:** Choi has just risen to leave while Jin remains behind to cultivate; the line dismisses him without escorting him out.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** “신경 안 씁니다. 미스터 존슨 나름대로의 친밀감 표현이라는 걸 알고 있으니까요. 그보다는 길드 업무 관련 내용입니다.”
- **Current:** “I’m not concerned about that. I understand that it is Mr. Johnson’s way of expressing familiarity. This concerns Guild business.”
- **Defect:** “Expressing familiarity” is an unnatural and overly literal rendering of 친밀감 표현, obscuring Choi’s dry acknowledgment that Johnson behaves that way to show closeness.
- **Replacement:** “I’m not concerned about that. I know that’s simply Mr. Johnson’s way of showing affection. More importantly, this concerns Guild business.”
- **Rationale:** 친밀감 here means affectionate closeness or fondness, and 그보다는 explicitly pivots to the more pertinent Guild matter.

## F05 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 흐느적거리며 추락한 깃털을 짓밟은 것은 수많은 걸음과 금속으로 이루어진 전차의 바퀴였다.
- **Current:** The wheels of a tank, made of countless footsteps and metal, crushed the feathers as they drifted back down.
- **Defect:** The draft grammatically makes a tank consist of “countless footsteps and metal,” eliminating the marching feet as a separate agent that tramples the feathers.
- **Replacement:** Countless marching feet and the metal wheels of tanks crushed the feathers as they fluttered down.
- **Rationale:** The source names two things trampling the falling feathers: countless footsteps and the wheels of metal tanks.

## F06 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “마지막까지 포기하지 않았으면 살 수 있었을 텐데. 어떻게든 죽을힘을 다해 싸웠어야지. 뭐 그런 생각.”
- **Current:** “He could have lived if he hadn’t given up until the very end. He should have fought with everything he had somehow. That sort of thing.”
- **Defect:** The draft invents and repeatedly specifies a singular male subject, narrowing the source’s omitted subject and changing the movie reference’s survival consequence.
- **Replacement:** “They could have lived if they hadn’t given up until the very end. They should have fought with everything they had, no matter what. That sort of thing.”
- **Rationale:** The Korean leaves the subject unstated, while the surrounding speech turns the movie’s collective lesson into encouragement for the assembled troops; the explicit singular “he” is unsupported.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 오늘처럼 죽음의 문턱을 오고 간 뒤라면 훨씬 더하다.
- **Current:** And after coming and going from the threshold of death as many times as I had today, it was even worse.
- **Defect:** The draft adds multiple trips to the brink of death; the source describes having skirted death today without specifying a count.
- **Replacement:** And after skirting the brink of death like I had today, it was even worse.
- **Rationale:** The added phrase “as many times” introduces an unsupported quantity and changes the account of the battle.

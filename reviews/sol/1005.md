# Review

The draft has two substantive meaning changes and several localized fidelity issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** ‘그렇지 않았다면, 이미 몇 번쯤 죽고도 남았을 테니까.’
- **Current:** *If it didn’t, I’d have been killed a few times over by now.*
- **Defect:** Changes the person who would have died from Jeok Cheongang to Song Ilseom.
- **Replacement:** *If it didn’t, Jeok Cheongang would have been dead several times over by now.*
- **Rationale:** Song is explaining why Jeok can beat anyone he dislikes without suffering the consequences: his strength keeps him alive.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.91
- **Source:** “대담해! 맘도 암 대!”
- **Current:** “Dunno! Can’t tell!”
- **Defect:** Turns Mujin’s food-muffled astonishment that the situation is unbelievable into a claim that he lacks information.
- **Replacement:** “Incredibuh! Makes no shensh!”
- **Rationale:** The muffled line conveys ‘대단해! 말도 안 돼!’—astonishment and disbelief—not another answer about what happened.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 하지만 도중에 합류한 낭인 나부랭이, 물론 나부랭이치고는 그 바닥에서 엄청 유명한 데다 알고 보면 대단한 무가(武家)의 후예지만 아무튼 그런 송일섬한테까지 이런 취급을 받다니.
- **Current:** But now he was being treated this way even by Song Ilseom—a no-name wandering martial artist who’d joined them along the way. Sure, for a no-name, he was famous as hell in those circles, and, as it turned out, he came from an impressive great family. But still!
- **Defect:** “No-name” falsely suggests Song is obscure and makes the immediate acknowledgment of his fame contradictory.
- **Replacement:** But now he was being treated this way even by Song Ilseom—a lowly wandering martial artist who’d joined them along the way. Sure, for a mere wanderer, he was famous as hell in those circles, and, as it turned out, he came from an impressive martial family. But still!
- **Rationale:** Mujin disparages Song’s status as a wanderer while conceding that Song is famous and comes from a distinguished martial family.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 저 세 글자가 정확히 무슨 의미인지는 송일섬도 몰랐지만, 결코 좋은 의미가 아니라는 것쯤은 짐작할 수 있었다.
- **Current:** Song Ilseom didn’t know exactly what those three words meant, but he could guess they weren’t a compliment.
- **Defect:** Calls the rendered two-word phrase three words; the source counts Korean written characters.
- **Replacement:** Song Ilseom didn’t know exactly what the expression meant, but he could guess it wasn’t a compliment.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves Song’s uncertainty without introducing a false English word count.

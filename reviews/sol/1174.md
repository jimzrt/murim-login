# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it reverses a sacrifice, misreads a mountain metaphor as a character reference, and leaves two System windows unformatted.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그가 말하는 ‘사람들’이 정확히 누구를 칭하는 것인지, 최민우는 이미 충분히 짐작하고 있었다.

대부분의 인간보다도 더욱 인간 같은, 어느 한 존재 역시 저 세 글자에 포함되어 있다는 사실 역시도.
- **Current:** He also knew that those three words included one being who was more human than most humans.
- **Defect:** “Everyone” is one English word, not three; the count refers to the three Korean syllables of ‘사람들.’
- **Replacement:** He also knew that Jin’s request included one being who was more human than most humans.
- **Rationale:** The replacement preserves the inclusion without carrying over an inapplicable Korean syllable count.

## F02 — Major

- **Source:** 이 모든 것의 시작이라 할 수 있는 그 낡아빠진 캡슐 이용 설명서에는, 플레이어의 사망 전까지 영구 귀속된다고 적혀 있었으니까.
- **Current:** The instruction manual for that ancient capsule, the starting point of all this, said a Player remained bound to it permanently, until death.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Player bound to the capsule; the manual says the capsule is permanently bound to its Player until the Player dies.
- **Replacement:** The instruction manual for that beat-up old capsule, the starting point of all this, said it remained permanently bound to its Player until the Player died.
- **Rationale:** The attachment rule concerns the capsule and its owner.

## F03 — Major

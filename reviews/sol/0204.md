# Review

The draft is broadly faithful, but it introduces a false plural subject during the forced opening, weakens Jeok Cheongang’s exertion, completes an intentionally unfinished thought, and contains two localized characterization issues.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 강제 개통이라니!
- **Current:** They were forcing it open!
- **Defect:** The draft invents an unspecified plural actor and presents the line as external narration rather than Taekyung’s shocked realization that this is a forced opening.
- **Replacement:** *A forced opening?!*
- **Rationale:** The Korean is a subjectless internal exclamation about the procedure itself; it does not identify multiple people as performing the action.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 이렇게까지 힘껏 도와줄 줄이야.
- **Current:** I had never expected him to help me this much.
- **Defect:** The translation preserves the extent of the help but omits 힘껏, which emphasizes how much effort and strength Jeok Cheongang expended.
- **Replacement:** To think he had gone all out to help me like this.
- **Rationale:** Jeok Cheongang’s exertion is meaningful characterization and directly supports the later revelation that the procedure consumed considerable internal energy and mental strength.

## F03 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** ‘적천강, 당신은 도대체.’
- **Current:** *Jeok Cheongang, what are you, anyway…*
- **Defect:** The draft turns an intentionally unfinished, emotional thought into a question about Jeok Cheongang’s identity or nature, creating a skeptical note absent from the source.
- **Replacement:** *Jeok Cheongang, you really…*
- **Rationale:** The Korean trails off after addressing him, leaving Taekyung’s gratitude and emotion unspoken as his eyes grow moist.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.94
- **Source:** 수련동 무인인 칠득이에 의하면 들어간 이래 단 한 번도 밖으로 나오지 않았다고 했다.
- **Current:** According to Childeuk, one of the martial artists at the training hall, Mukyung hadn’t come outside even once since entering.
- **Defect:** The phrase “one of the martial artists” invents a group affiliation and blurs Childeuk’s specific role as the martial artist stationed at the training hall.
- **Replacement:** According to Childeuk, the martial artist stationed at the training hall, Mukyung hadn’t come outside even once since entering.
- **Rationale:** The Korean identifies Childeuk by his particular post rather than as one member of an unspecified group.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.93
- **Source:** “소가주만큼이나 관주께서도 안타까워하실 겁니다.”
- **Current:** “The Temple Master will feel just as sorry as you do, Lesser Family Head.”
- **Defect:** “Feel sorry” suggests pity or apology, whereas 안타까워하다 here conveys regret or distress over Jin Mukyung’s decision.
- **Replacement:** “The Temple Master will regret it just as much as you do, Lesser Family Head.”
- **Rationale:** Ak Bulgun is saying that the Temple Master will share Jin Wikyung’s regret at Mukyung’s refusal to return, not that either man pities someone.

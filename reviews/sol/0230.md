# Review

Two quantity errors alter scene facts, and several localized issues affect terminology, idiom, and character voice.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 객잔의 문이 산산조각 남과 동시에, 대여섯 명의 칼잡이들이 쏟아졌다.
- **Current:** The inn’s door exploded into splinters, and half a dozen swordsmen poured inside.
- **Defect:** The source gives an approximate count of five or six, while “half a dozen” fixes the number at six.
- **Replacement:** The inn’s door exploded into splinters, and five or six swordsmen poured inside.
- **Rationale:** 대여섯 명 explicitly means five or six people, so the uncertainty in the quantity must be preserved.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 정파와 사파, 흑도가 공존하고 수천 리 밖에는 흉신악살들이 가득하다는 마교가 있다.
- **Current:** The orthodox faction, the unorthodox faction, and the dark-path figures coexisted, while thousands of miles away stood the Demonic Cult, filled with fiends and monsters.
- **Defect:** The draft converts 수천 리 into thousands of miles without accounting for the much shorter traditional unit, greatly increasing the stated distance.
- **Replacement:** The orthodox faction, the unorthodox faction, and dark-path figures coexisted, while several thousand li away[^2] stood the Demonic Cult, filled with fiends and monsters.

[^2]: A *li* is a traditional East Asian unit of distance, commonly approximated as one-third of a mile.
- **Rationale:** The source specifies several thousand li, not several thousand miles; retaining and defining the traditional unit preserves the quantity.

## F03 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “그러게, 누울 자리를 보고 다리를 뻗었어야지.”
- **Current:** “You should’ve looked where you were lying down before stretching out your legs.”
- **Defect:** The Korean idiom has been rendered word for word, producing unnatural English and obscuring the warning that the victim should have known whom he was challenging.
- **Replacement:** “You should’ve known who you were messing with.”
- **Rationale:** 누울 자리를 보고 다리를 뻗다 means to assess the situation or know one's place before acting, rather than literally inspect a place to lie down.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.90
- **Source:** “나는 대 동천방의 고독한 맹수, 흑걸이라 한…….”
- **Current:** “I am the lone beast of the mighty Dongcheon Gang, Heukgeol—”
- **Defect:** The unlisted sobriquet 흑걸 is romanized without translation or explanation.
- **Replacement:** “I am the lone beast of the mighty Dongcheon Gang, Heukgeol[^3]—”

[^3]: *Heukgeol* is a sobriquet meaning “Black Hero.”
- **Rationale:** The introduction treats 흑걸 as a meaningful sobriquet; the required note preserves the name while making its sense available to the reader.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** 가장 먼저 달려든 민머리 사내가 벼락같은 고함과 함께 투박한 박도(朴刀)를 휘둘렀다.
- **Current:** The bald man at the front let out a thunderous shout and swung a crude dao.
- **Defect:** The draft mistakes 박도, a weapon type, for an ordinary dao described as crude.
- **Replacement:** The bald man at the front let out a thunderous shout and swung a rough-looking broad-bladed saber.
- **Rationale:** 박도(朴刀) denotes a broad-bladed saber; 투박한 supplies the separate rough or ungainly description.

## F06 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 혹여 붙잡힐세라 서둘러 객잔을 빠져나가는 그들의 등 뒤로 화살 같은 말이 따라붙었다.
- **Current:** Afraid he might change his mind, they hurried out of the inn. Words like arrows followed them from behind.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a fear that the young man will change his mind; the source says they hurry for fear of being stopped or detained.
- **Replacement:** Afraid they might be stopped, they hurried out of the inn. Words like arrows followed them from behind.
- **Rationale:** 붙잡힐세라 describes their fear of being caught or held back, without attributing a change of mind to the young man.

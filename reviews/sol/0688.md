# Review

The draft contains two critical reversals of meaning, several altered subjects, quantities, and levels of certainty, plus localized hierarchy and wording issues.

## F01 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 그 안에서 자신을 쏙 빼닮은 아들의 얼굴을 발견할 수 있었기 때문이었다.
- **Current:** He could see the face of a son who looked exactly like him.
- **Defect:** The indefinite “a son” obscures that Baeksang is seeing the face of his own specific son.
- **Replacement:** He could see the face of his son, who looked exactly like him.
- **Rationale:** 아들 is Baeksang’s son in this context, not an unspecified or hypothetical son.

## F02 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** 불을 지른 자는 진태경일 테고.
- **Current:** The one who started the fire was Jin Taekyung.
- **Defect:** The draft turns Baeksang’s initial supposition into a statement of established fact, preempting the guard captain’s caveat and Baeksang’s subsequent insistence.
- **Replacement:** The one who started the fire would be Jin Taekyung.
- **Rationale:** 일 테고 expresses a confident inference rather than confirmed knowledge.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 1.00
- **Source:** 중과부적(衆寡不敵)이라는 네 글자도 상대가 누구냐에 따라 달라진다.
- **Current:** The four characters *the many cannot defeat the few* meant different things depending on who the opponent was.
- **Defect:** The idiom has been reversed: it means that the few cannot overcome the many, not that the many cannot defeat the few.
- **Replacement:** The saying *the few cannot overcome the many* meant different things depending on who the opponent was.
- **Rationale:** 衆寡不敵 conventionally states that an outnumbered force cannot prevail; the surrounding point is that Jin Taekyung is an exception.

## F04 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 대회의의 뜻을 거스르면서까지 흉수를 구하고, 남만야수궁을 배반한 흉수 야율척에 관해서라면. 맞소.
- **Current:** If you are referring to the criminal Yayul Cheok—the man you rescued despite defying the will of the Tribal Grand Council, even though he betrayed the Nanman Beast Palace—then yes.
- **Defect:** The draft makes the Head Elder the person who rescued Yayul Cheok. In the source, Yayul Cheok is accused of defying the council to save a culprit and of betraying the Palace.
- **Replacement:** If you mean Yayul Cheok—the criminal who defied the will of the Tribal Grand Council to save a murderer and then betrayed the Nanman Beast Palace—then yes.
- **Rationale:** The coordinated verbs 구하고 and 배반한 both modify 흉수 야율척; they describe Yayul Cheok’s alleged actions.

## F05 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 묘족의 존망은 먼 옛날 과실주를 훔쳐먹다 걸려 당신에게 호된 꾸중을 듣던 한 어린아이에게 달렸소.
- **Current:** Yayul Cheok betrayed the Palace and fled, while the fate of the Miao people rests on a child who once stole some old fruit wine and received a harsh scolding from you.
- **Defect:** “Old” incorrectly modifies the fruit wine, and the detail that the child was caught stealing it is omitted.
- **Replacement:** Yayul Cheok betrayed the Palace and fled, while the fate of the Miao people rests on a child who long ago was caught stealing fruit wine and received a harsh scolding from you.
- **Rationale:** 먼 옛날 locates the childhood incident in the distant past, while 걸려 explicitly says he was caught.

## F10 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 지금까지 연락이 닿지 않는 걸 보면 확실해.
- **Current:** Judging by the fact that we have not been able to contact him, it is certain.
- **Defect:** The draft invents a singular male object for the failed contact, misleadingly suggesting that they were trying to contact Jin Taekyung. The Korean leaves the object unstated, and the subsequent context concerns the agents assigned to the trap.
- **Replacement:** The fact that there has been no contact yet makes it certain.
- **Rationale:** 연락이 닿지 않는 has no expressed object, so the translation should preserve that ambiguity rather than add “him.”

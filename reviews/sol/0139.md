# Review

The draft contains one critical subject reversal, several altered causal or tactical explanations, and meaningful losses or distortions of the source’s register and wordplay.

## F01 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** “전 빼도 상관없긴 한데. 괜찮으시겠어요?”
- **Current:** “I don’t mind being left out. Are you sure you’ll be all right?”
- **Defect:** The draft makes Taekyung volunteer to be excluded, but he is saying he does not mind leaving Cheongpung out. The following narration explicitly considers taking the other five without Cheongpung.
- **Replacement:** “I don’t mind leaving him out. But are you sure you’ll be all right?”
- **Rationale:** The omitted object is Cheongpung, the person the official has just asked whether they must bring; changing it to Taekyung reverses who would be excluded.

## F02 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 수십 대 수십의 싸움이라면 몰라도 수백 대 수백, 수천 대 수천의 전투가 벌어진다면 어지간한 절정 고수로는 전세를 뒤엎을 수 없다.
- **Current:** That might not matter in a battle of dozens against dozens. But if hundreds fought hundreds, or thousands fought thousands, even an ordinary Peak master couldn’t turn the tide by himself.
- **Defect:** The first sentence reverses the tactical qualification. The source says a small battle might be another matter, whereas the draft says inferior individual skill might not matter in a small battle.
- **Replacement:** A battle of dozens against dozens might be another story, but if hundreds fought hundreds, or thousands fought thousands, even an ordinary Peak master couldn’t turn the tide by himself.
- **Rationale:** The contrast explains that exceptional individual strength can matter at smaller scales but cannot readily overturn a mass battle.

## F04 — Major

- **Current:** *Well, there’s no reason people can’t learn martial arts just because they already have a profession.*

- **Current:** “You could just say you’re worried he’ll cause an accident. Why do you have to put it like that…?”

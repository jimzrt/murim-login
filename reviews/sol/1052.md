# Review

The draft changes a time quantity, makes the Grand Mage’s forgotten threats singular, and misstates whether her wish has already come true.

## F01 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** “또 보네?”
- **Current:** “See you again?”
- **Defect:** The English reads like a question about a future farewell rather than a greeting on meeting her again.
- **Replacement:** “Fancy seeing you again.”
- **Rationale:** 또 보네 refers to seeing her again now.

## F02 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.99
- **Source:** 자신이 무언가를 잊고 있었음을.

그것도 아주 중요하면서도, 위험한 존재들을.
- **Current:** She’d forgotten someone.

Someone very important—and very dangerous.
- **Defect:** The draft changes the forgotten threats from plural beings to one person.
- **Replacement:** She’d forgotten something.

Or rather, some very important—and very dangerous—people.
- **Rationale:** 무언가 introduces the thought, but 존재들을 resolves it as plural; she then sees two figures.

## F03 — Critical

- **Confidence:** 0.97
- **Source:** “반 각. 한참 전에 지났잖아요.”
- **Current:** “That quarter hour. It’s been a long time since it passed.”
- **Defect:** 반 각 is half a gak, not a full quarter hour.
- **Replacement:** “Seven and a half minutes. That ran out ages ago.”
- **Rationale:** A gak is fifteen minutes; the stated interval is half of one.

## F04 — Minor

- **Confidence:** 0.96
- **Source:** “좋다. 무엇이든…… 무엇이든 말해 주마.”
- **Current:** “Fine. I’ll tell you… Anything. Everything.”
- **Defect:** The draft replaces his repeated offer of ‘anything’ with an escalating promise of ‘everything.’
- **Replacement:** “Fine. Anything… I’ll tell you anything.”
- **Rationale:** 무엇이든 is repeated; the repetition conveys his desperation without changing the scope of his offer.

## F05 — Major

- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Source:** 그리고 그보다 한발 앞서, 몸뚱어리조차 제대로 건사하지 못할 만큼 처참한 죽음을 맞이한 누군가의 뜻대로.
- **Current:** And unlike the person whose wishes had come true a moment earlier, after dying so horribly she couldn’t even properly take care of her own body.
- **Defect:** The draft says the Grand Mage’s wishes had already come true and repeats ‘unlike’ from the preceding comparison. The source says Jin’s renewed strength is in accordance with what she wanted.
- **Replacement:** And just as someone who’d died a little earlier—so horribly she couldn’t even keep her body in one piece—had wanted.
- **Rationale:** 뜻대로 connects her wish to Jin growing stronger; it does not declare that all her wishes have been fulfilled.

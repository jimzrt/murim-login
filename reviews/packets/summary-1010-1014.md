## Previous block summary

# Chapters 1005–1009

## Plot

After four days of travel, the Fire Dragon Pavilion rests over a feast. Taishan sits motionless instead of eating, and Namho recognizes a scent but says nothing. Meanwhile, Sima Gong explains that Gansu has assembled thirty thousand martial artists along three defensive lines and sent scouts beyond the desert. He expects Dark Heaven to target Gansu, though he also urges Jeok Cheongang to go to Qinghai.

The scouts return early and signal suspicious intruders at the wall. Jin Taekyung mistakenly orders the guards to fire on about fifty riders led by Ma Junggeol. Jeok Cheongang persuades the leaders to admit them. The riders identify themselves as Baekma Bang, reformed Ningxia mounted-bandit leaders who came to help and bring intelligence. They report seeing at least a thousand Dark Heaven tents in the western desert, closer to Gansu than Qinghai. Taekyung concludes that the forces must fight there.

## Continuity

- Gansu Murim has assembled 30,000 martial artists in three defensive lines across Dunhuang, the Great Snow Mountain, and the Qilian Mountains. The Kongtong Sect is already deployed.
- Sima Gong sent thirty scouts beyond the desert after ominous signs. They returned early and signaled the approach of suspicious riders.
- Dark Heaven’s camp in the western desert contains at least a thousand tents. Ma Junggeol’s group followed the force for a day and a half from a distance of one hundred li; its total strength and objective remain unknown.
- Baekma Bang, Ningxia’s largest group, is opening a trade route west toward Xinjiang. Its members are reformed mounted-bandit leaders guided by an unknown master more than ten years ago.
- Ma Junggeol leads Baekma Bang and arrived with six associates, collectively known as the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang. They offer help and say they have important information about Dark Heaven.
- Taishan remains unusually motionless and uneating at the feast. Namho recognized a scent connected to a recent memory; what it means remains unresolved.
- Sima Gong expects Dark Heaven to target Gansu but has urged Jeok Cheongang to go to Qinghai. The reported camp lies on a route closer to Gansu than Qinghai.

## Translation Decisions

- Render **백마방** as “Baekma Bang” and **백마칠종** as “the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang.”
- Render **곡차** in the drinking scene as “grain liquor,” not “grain tea.”
- Keep “dark-path figures” distinct from “unorthodox faction”; render **관무불가침** as the principle of government–Murim noninterference.

### Chapter 1010

# Chapter 1010

## Plot

Taekyung decides to remain in Gansu after Ma Junggeol estimates that Dark Heaven could reach the region in about twenty days—or sooner by advancing directly. Under a worst-case estimate, the first battle could begin in one or two days.

The assembled force of nearly three thousand martial artists sets out. Taekyung’s new linked Quest, “Road of Blood,” tasks him with annihilating hostile forces in Gansu; failure means Dark Heaven wins and Gansu’s control is lost. On the march, Taekyung and Ma Junggeol trade barbed remarks while some leaders continue to treat Ma’s group as hostages.

## Continuity

- Taekyung stays in Gansu and departs with a force of nearly three thousand toward the threatened front.
- Ma Junggeol estimates Dark Heaven could reach Gansu in about twenty days, possibly sooner; Taekyung estimates a worst-case battle could begin in one or two days.
- Gansu has three defensive lines and about thirty thousand troops; Dunhuang is the foremost line and can be reached by the allied force in three days at full speed.
- Taekyung’s linked Quest, “Road of Blood,” requires annihilating hostile forces in Gansu; failure means Dark Heaven wins and Gansu’s control is lost.
- Some Gansu leaders still suspect Ma Junggeol and his men, who are effectively being held hostage while accompanying the force.

## Translation Decisions

- Render the new Quest title 피의 길 as “Road of Blood.”
### Chapter 1011

# Chapter 1011

## Plot

Taekyung’s group assigns Ma Junggeol and the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang to the Fire Dragon Pavilion. Taekyung discovers he can detect and eavesdrop on nearby Sound Transmissions, overhears the brothers’ complaints and their worries about their benefactor—whom they call the Lord—and is confronted by Ma Junggeol when he realizes someone has been listening.

## Continuity

- Ma Junggeol and his six sworn brothers are assigned to Taekyung’s Fire Dragon Pavilion while accompanying the force toward the front.
- Taekyung has newly gained the ability to detect and eavesdrop on nearby Sound Transmissions; what he can hear depends on the relative levels of the participants.
- Ma Junggeol and his brothers trust the benefactor they call the Lord, but worry that the Lord may be mentally unstable; they sent messengers toward Ningxia and are considering sending one of their own to check on him.
- Ma Junggeol and his brothers were formerly mounted bandits in Ningxia, but say they avoided robbing or killing civilians and were led onto a better path by the Lord.
- The allied force is traveling toward the threatened Gansu front, where Dark Heaven’s advance remains an unresolved danger.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “Sound Transmission” for 전음.
- Render 대인 as “the Lord” when referring to the benefactor Ma Junggeol and his brothers trust.
### Chapter 1012

# Chapter 1012

## Plot

After Ma Junggeol recounts more than a decade of events, Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang conclude that Ma and his six sworn brothers are not lying and likely are not Dark Heaven spies. Their account makes the mysterious master they call the Lord seem deeply unpredictable: he appeared in Ningxia, subdued the area, and has lived there for nearly ten years, but they know little about his identity. Taekyung asks how long it would take to bring the Lord to their destination.

## Continuity

- Ma Junggeol and the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang have recounted their history and are traveling with Taekyung’s group; Taekyung asks them to bring their mysterious benefactor, the Lord, to the group’s destination.
- Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang judge that Ma Junggeol and his brothers are telling the truth and do not appear to be Dark Heaven spies; the Lord’s identity and motives remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대인 as “the Lord” for Ma Junggeol and his brothers’ mysterious benefactor; distinguish this from 천주, “Lord of Heaven.”
### Chapter 1013

# Chapter 1013

## Plot

Taekyung challenges Ma Junggeol and the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang over their unexplained knowledge and the Lord’s possible connection to the crisis. Ma admits the Lord has advised them for more than ten years, helped them form Baekma Bang, encouraged their search for a western trade route, and urged them to seek Taekyung’s group. Taekyung sends six of the Seven Masters to fetch the Lord within five days, while Ma stays behind. Namho then says he has something important he can only tell Taekyung now.

## Continuity

- The Lord has advised Ma Junggeol and his sworn brothers for more than ten years, including encouraging them to form Baekma Bang and explore a western trade route.
- At Taekyung’s order, six of the Seven Masters leave to bring the Lord back within five days of the march’s halt; Ma Junggeol remains with Taekyung’s group.
- Namho says he has an important matter he can only disclose to Taekyung now.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 칠주야 as “seven days and nights”; Taekyung sets a five-day deadline for the Lord’s return.
### Chapter 1014

# Chapter 1014

## Plot

As the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s forces ride toward the coming battle, two of its martial artists discuss Sama Pyo’s rumored past and his succession. One makes a taboo remark about Pyo surpassing his older half-siblings; a senior martial artist silences him and warns him of the danger.

Sima Gong questions Sama Pyo about failing to return to Gansu after receiving his order. Pyo admits he disobeyed and accepts punishment without excuse. Sima Gong says sending him to the Central Plains was the right decision and acknowledges his growth, then orders that the two martial artists be dealt with. Pyo rides on with Taishan, now bound for the Black Dragon Demon Gate rather than the Fire Dragon Pavilion.

## Continuity

- Sama Pyo received and understood Sima Gong’s order to return to Gansu, but chose not to carry it out; he accepts responsibility without offering an excuse.
- Sama Pyo is returning to the Black Dragon Demon Gate, not the Fire Dragon Pavilion.
- Sima Gong considers sending Sama Pyo to the Central Plains the right decision and says his son has grown.
- Sima Gong orders that the two martial artists who made the taboo remark be dealt with; their eventual fate is not shown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 단혈방 as Danhyeol Bang and 일도단애 as One Saber Cuts the Cliff.
- Render 사마공’s address 표야 as “Pyo” when addressing Sama Pyo.

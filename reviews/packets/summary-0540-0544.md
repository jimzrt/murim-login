# Chapters 535–539

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung are summoned to the restored Murim Alliance’s Alliance Leader’s Hall, where Mae Jonghak formally recognizes their actions against Dark Heaven as humanity and chivalry. With Tang Sadok’s public support, Mae appoints them as the two pavilion masters of the Alliance Leader’s direct Two Dragons Pavilion, granting them authority to select personnel. Hwangbo Gun objects to Taekyung’s appointment because he protected the Black Dragon Demon Gate, but Mae privately voids the proposed personnel action and retains Taekyung in the post. Taekyung is instead disciplined by being ordered to skip dinner.

The System then assigns Taekyung a Two Dragons Pavilion Quest: recruit at least five companions and name the organization, or receive the Title Loner. Mungyeong refuses Taekyung’s recruitment attempt because he has already accepted Cheongpung’s offer. Mungyeong, formerly the Slaughter Saint and now known as the Divine Physician, treats patients throughout the area and recognizes Cheongpung’s snake-inspired Mimi Step as a genuine martial creation. He judges Cheongpung to have the makings of a Grandmaster and agrees to let him learn through observation. Taekyung orders Hyuk Mujin to write and post public notices seeking other recruits.

## Continuity

- The Mount Song Resolution restored the Murim Alliance. Mae Jonghak is Alliance Leader, and Song Ho commands the Hidden Shadow Pavilion.
- Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung are the two pavilion masters of the Alliance Leader’s direct Two Dragons Pavilion, with regiments and subordinate squads beneath them. They may select personnel and answer directly to the Alliance Leader.
- Tang Sadok and the Sichuan Tang Clan publicly support their appointments and acknowledge an unrepayable debt to them.
- Hwangbo Gun opposes Taekyung’s appointment; Mae Jonghak secretly rescinded the personnel action he appeared to promise Hwangbo.
- Taekyung’s first Two Dragons Pavilion Quest requires at least five companions and an organization name. Failure awards the Title Loner.
- Mungyeong is the former Slaughter Saint who buried his assassin identity after the war and became the Divine Physician. He has accepted Cheongpung’s offer, so Taekyung’s first recruitment attempt failed.
- Cheongpung created Mimi Step from Mimi’s movements. Mungyeong recognizes it as a genuine recreation of martial principles and considers Cheongpung a potential Grandmaster.
- Mimi is a large horned snake who eats dumplings, sweets, and Blood Fish.
- Taekyung is now seeking additional recruits through public notices, with Hyuk Mujin responsible for preparing them.
- The Lord of Heaven, Dark Heaven’s Gate-opening method, the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s plans in Yunnan, Jeok Cheongang’s duel with Nangong Cheon, and the cause of Ju Hwaran and Sama Pyo’s broken engagement remain unresolved.

### Chapter 540

# Chapter 540

## Plot

News of Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung's appointment as the Two Dragons Pavilion masters spreads throughout Henan. Hwangbo Gun's demand for punishment backfires: Taekyung is ordered to fast temporarily, Taishan must pay ten times the damages caused by his actions, and Hwangbo Ak receives a temporary disciplinary confinement order.

Taekyung launches an unprecedented recruitment campaign for the Two Dragons Pavilion, requiring passion, upright character, and at least the level of injuring others with Sword Energy while accepting applicants regardless of gender, sect, or age. A Honghakru band and singer turn his slogan into a catchy song, causing mass excitement and an overwhelming flood of applications.

Taekyung enjoys the song's success until Jeok Cheongang breaks into the room, nearly suffering qi deviation from the song and angrily blaming Taekyung for it.

## Continuity

- The Two Dragons Pavilion's appointment and recruitment campaign are now public throughout Henan, and the campaign has attracted far more applicants than Taekyung anticipated.
- Taekyung, Taishan, and Hwangbo Ak are subject to temporary disciplinary orders from the Murim Alliance; Taekyung must fast, Taishan must pay tenfold damages, and Hwangbo Ak must remain in disciplinary confinement.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 호거아 as Tiger Giant Child, Taishan's epithet; retain Two Dragons Pavilion, Pavilion Master, and the established rendering of 검기상인 as the level of injuring others with Sword Energy.
- Keep the recruitment song's recurring refrain as “Join the Murim Alliance’s Two Dragons Pavilion, and my era of success begins~” and render 홍학루 as Honghakru and 홍매 as Hongmae.
### Chapter 541

# Chapter 541

## Plot

The Two Dragons Pavilion's recruitment draws a vast crowd of martial artists and spectators to the inn. Hyuk Mujin begins the interviews and immediately rejects the Black Blood Saber, No Guisan, for his rudeness. An eighty-five-year-old Supreme Peak master known as Old Man Ilyang presents himself as an applicant while secretly plotting to seize the Fire Gate Clan's divine technique and exploit the Pavilion's eventual departure from Henan. Jeok Cheongang recognizes his intent, beats him unconscious, and causes many other applicants to flee; he then remarks that new arrivals will replace those who leave.

## Continuity

- Old Man Ilyang is Won Cheol, an eighty-five-year-old Supreme Peak master who seeks the Fire Gate Clan's divine technique and intends to use the Two Dragons Pavilion for his own ambitions; Jeok Cheongang has knocked him unconscious.
- Taekyung ordered first-meeting rudeness to be an automatic rejection, and Hyuk Mujin is enforcing that rule during the Two Dragons Pavilion interviews; Jeok's violent handling of Ilyang has driven many applicants away.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일양노 and the same-character variant 열양노 as Old Man Ilyang, 원철 as Won Cheol, 흑혈도 as Black Blood Saber, 노귀산 as No Guisan, and 원썬 as One Sun.
- Preserve Hyuk Mujin's deadpan interview humor, Jeok Cheongang's blunt violence, and the narration's audition, hip-hop, and forest-conservation jokes.
### Chapter 542

# Chapter 542

## Plot

Sama Pyo, Young Sect Leader of the Black Dragon Demon Gate, arrives with his giant subordinate Taishan. Jeok Cheongang recognizes Sama Pyo as the son of Black Night King Sima Gong, while Taishan's childlike greeting and voracious destruction of the inn's refreshments reveal both his unusual strength and appetite.

Sama Pyo applies to join the Two Dragons Pavilion. He claims he may want to shed the label of the demonic, heterodox path, but candidly admits that he intends to use Jin Taekyung as a useful card. Taekyung leaves him under consideration with immediate execution as the consequence for treachery and makes him responsible for Taishan's food bill.

## Continuity

- Sama Pyo, the Young Sect Leader of the Black Dragon Demon Gate and son of Sima Gong, has applied to join the Two Dragons Pavilion and is under consideration.
- Sama Pyo openly intends to use Jin Taekyung for his own benefit as a useful card, while Taekyung distrusts him but finds his candidness interesting.
- Taishan is a giant, childlike, food-obsessed subordinate of Sama Pyo whose strength allows him to destroy the remaining doorway and consume the inn's refreshments without hesitation.
- Jeok Cheongang recognizes Sima Gong, the Black Night King and founder of the modern Black Dragon Demon Gate, as sly, cunning, and inscrutable.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 흑야왕 as Black Night King and 녹림투왕 as Green Forest Battle King.
- Preserve Taishan's clipped, childlike speech and his literal expressions of respect, strength, hunger, and gratitude.
- Preserve Sama Pyo's polite but candid voice, including his explicit admission that he intends to use Taekyung as a useful card.

# Chapter 543

## Plot

Sama Pyo and Taishan travel openly through Henan despite surveillance from the Beggars' Sect and Lower District Sect. Sama Pyo explains that although he disobeyed Sima Gong's order, his chosen path still advances the destination his father wants, so he expects no punishment; he then recognizes the significance of an unidentified passerby.

Ju Hwaran submits applications for herself and Song Ilseom to join the Two Dragons Pavilion. She argues that her Peak-level swordsmanship, escort experience, knowledge of the martial world, and operational expertise make them useful despite the pavilion's danger, and Taekyung accepts them.

- Sama Pyo has disobeyed Sima Gong's orders while pursuing a route he believes still serves Sima Gong's desired goal, and he expects his position as the best available successor to protect him from punishment.

### Chapter 544

# Chapter 544

## Plot

Ju Hwaran leaves with Song Ilseom, and Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Jeok Cheongang conclude that she came to seek the fame and skill needed to protect and lead the Yongbong Escort Bureau. Jeok Cheongang also recognizes Song Ilseom as a formidable master and Taekyung accepts him as a valuable recruit.

Taekyung decides to assemble a team to help contain the spreading war against Dark Heaven. Hyuk Mujin immediately commits to following him, and their joking exchange ends with Taekyung asking Mujin to stay beside him and not die.

Mujin delivers bamboo slips bearing the names of Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, Sama Pyo, and Taishan. Despite doubts about accepting Sama Pyo and Taishan, Taekyung receives Jeok Cheongang's encouragement to move forward.

Taekyung approves Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, Sama Pyo, Taishan, and Hyuk Mujin as members of the Two Dragons Pavilion. The System confirms that the minimum companion requirement for the Quest is complete, then asks Taekyung to name his pavilion; he chooses Fire Dragon Pavilion.

## Continuity

- Ju Hwaran has left with Song Ilseom after joining Jin Taekyung's pavilion.
- Ju Hwaran seeks the fame and martial strength needed to protect and lead the Yongbong Escort Bureau.
- Song Ilseom is recognized by Jeok Cheongang as a formidable martial master and is accepted as a major asset.
- Hyuk Mujin has committed to following Jin Taekyung and remaining at his side.
- Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, Sama Pyo, Taishan, and Hyuk Mujin have been accepted as members of Taekyung's pavilion.
- The pavilion formerly called the Two Dragons Pavilion has been named the Fire Dragon Pavilion.
- The System's requirement to secure at least five companions for the Quest has been completed.

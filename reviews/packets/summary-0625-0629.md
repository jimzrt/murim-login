# Chapters 620–624

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion confirms that the Poison Flower Pavilion owner is Namho, a Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent who has spent over fifty years surveying Nanman. Namho stages the destruction of his inn to conceal the contact, gives the group a wooden map of Nanman, and joins them after receiving the Pavilion Master’s warning about the rift. He guides them toward the Nanman Beast Palace, activating and advancing the related Chain Quest.

After a dangerous journey through Nanman, including repeated poisonings, lethal fungi, and hostile terrain, the party reaches the Beast Palace’s vast directly administered territory. Taishan’s attempt to eat a protected calf causes a confrontation, while Namho’s emergency signal accidentally starts a pasture fire. Jin Taekyung extinguishes it, and the Beast Palace’s Young Palace Lord, Yayul Mok, arrives on a white tiger and escorts the group inside.

The Nanman Beast Palace’s Outer Hall is effectively a city populated by Miao people and beasts. Its residents distrust the Han Chinese because the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau massacred a Miao village about a month earlier. Sama Pyo suspects the massacre was timed as part of a larger scheme. Taekyung recognizes the powerful aura of Yayul Mok’s father, Yayul Cheok—the Beast Miao King—who is waiting in the Outer Hall.

## Continuity

- Namho is the Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent formerly posing as the Poison Flower Pavilion owner. He is an eighty-year-old Miao elder who has lived in Nanman for over fifty years.
- Namho gave the Fire Dragon Pavilion a wooden map recording Nanman’s terrain and tribal locations and is guiding the party through the Beast Palace’s territory.
- The Nanman Beast Palace Chain Quest is active; the party has arrived at the palace and reached its Outer Hall.
- Yayul Mok is the Nanman Beast Palace’s Young Palace Lord. He rides a long-bonded white tiger, understands spoken Han Chinese, and ordered the Fire Dragon Pavilion to follow him.
- Yayul Cheok is the Beast Miao King, ruler of the Nanman Beast Palace, leader of the Miao people, and the lowest-ranked of the Ten Kings. Taekyung has identified him by his aura, but their conversation has not begun.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion trespassed into protected Beast Palace territory and caused a serious pasture fire, though Taekyung ultimately extinguished it.
- Taishan remains a powerful but unpredictable asset whose appetite creates immediate diplomatic and operational problems.
- Nanman residents distrust Han Chinese because the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau recently massacred a Miao village.
- Sama Pyo suspects the massacre’s timing is connected to a larger scheme, while Song Ilseom remains hostile toward him and avoids discussing it.
- The identity of the woman traveling with the earlier Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group, the reason for that massacre, and the poisoner who killed the group remain unresolved.
- Dark Heaven is targeting Nanman, and the approaching disaster associated with the rift remains an active concern.

### Chapter 625

# Chapter 625

## Plot

The Beast Miao King, Yayul Cheok, arrives at the pasture and questions the Fire Dragon Pavilion about the fire. Jin Taekyung deflects responsibility to Namho, then withstands Yayul Cheok's punch and jokes that Jeok Cheongang described him as warmhearted and fond of pranks. Recognizing Taekyung as Jeok's Disciple, Yayul Cheok welcomes him and leads the group inside.

In the Nanman Beast Palace's main hall, Yayul Cheok recounts his violent but friendly first meeting with Jeok Cheongang and learns that Taekyung came as the Murim Alliance's Pavilion Master to address Dark Heaven. He explains that the Nanman Beast Palace has not rejected the alliance but that, as great chieftain of the Miao people, he cannot decide for the thirty-two tribes and four great tribal groups alone. Before Taekyung can ask whether Cheok supports joining, someone outside requests an audience.

## Continuity

- Yayul Cheok is the over-eighty Beast Miao King, lord of the Nanman Beast Palace, a Supreme Peak master among the Ten Kings, and great chieftain representing the Miao people.
- The Nanman Beast Palace encompasses thirty-two tribes across Nanman, including four great tribal groups, so its Palace Lord cannot decide major matters alone.
- Yayul Cheok received a delayed message about Dark Heaven more than a month ago, before the Mount Song Resolution, and understands the broad situation in the Central Plains.
- The Nanman Beast Palace has not formally rejected joining the Murim Alliance, but its decision remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Beast Miao King for 야수묘왕, Yayul Cheok for 야율척, and Palace Lord for 궁주.
- Use great chieftain for 대족장 and Miao people for 묘족.
- Retain Old Master Jeok for 적 노 when Yayul Cheok refers to Jeok Cheongang.
### Chapter 626

# Chapter 626

## Plot

A cold middle-aged man named Baeksang, the great chieftain of the Bai people, arrives to meet Yayul Cheok. Taekyung learns that Baeksang is Yayul Cheok's sworn younger brother and childhood companion, as well as Yayul Mok's sworn uncle. By baiting the irritable Yayul Mok with deliberately absurd questions, Taekyung confirms that Baeksang opposes the Nanman Beast Palace joining the Murim Alliance.

Baeksang privately refuses Yayul Cheok's fruit wine, as he has apparently done for decades, then declares that the Nanman Beast Palace will never join the Murim Alliance. The reason for his estrangement from Yayul Cheok and his cold scrutiny of Taekyung remains unknown.

## Continuity

- Baeksang is the great chieftain of the Bai people, one of Nanman's four most powerful great tribes.
- Baeksang is Yayul Cheok's sworn younger brother and childhood companion; they fought together during the Great Faction War.
- Yayul Mok is Yayul Cheok's only son for three generations, making Baeksang his father's sworn-brother uncle.
- Baeksang and Yayul Cheok are deeply estranged; Baeksang has refused Yayul Cheok's fruit wine for decades.
- Baeksang states that the Nanman Beast Palace will never join the Murim Alliance, citing the result of the previous tribal council.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion has been sent to its lodging to rest while Yayul Cheok and Baeksang discuss the alliance privately.

## Translation Decisions

- Use sworn younger brother for the relationship between 야수묘왕 and 백상.

- Retain Great Chieftain for 대족장 and Murim Alliance for 무림맹.
### Chapter 627

# Chapter 627

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion receives a deliberately hostile welcome at its temporary lodging, including insect-filled food and bathwater mixed with filth. Jin Taekyung decides to make a scene, while Yayul Mok later apologizes, promises to replace everyone responsible, and asks that the incident not reach his father's ears.

Yayul Mok explains that Nanman's hostility is rooted in the Great Faction War: Nanman sent roughly ten thousand warriors to fight in the Central Plains, fewer than a quarter returned, and Yayul Cheok lost two sons and a daughter while Baeksang lost a beloved son. Yayul Mok's three older siblings were among the dead, leaving him the only surviving son and Young Palace Lord.

## Continuity

- The Nanman Beast Palace's hostile treatment of the Fire Dragon Pavilion was carried out by internal non-Han staff and was driven by resentment over the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau incident and older Great Faction War losses.
- Yayul Mok sincerely accepts responsibility for the mistreatment, promises to replace Hwang Gae and the other responsible staff, and says he will not punish them because their anger has understandable roots.
- Nanman sent approximately ten thousand warriors to the Great Faction War, and fewer than a quarter returned alive.
- Yayul Cheok lost two sons and a daughter in the Great Faction War, while Baeksang lost a beloved son.
- Yayul Mok's three older siblings died in the Great Faction War, leaving him Yayul Cheok's only surviving son and the Young Palace Lord.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 황개 as Hwang Gae and retain 똥개 as Ddong Gae when Taekyung deliberately misremembers or mocks his name.
- Keep Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau as the established proper name, while preserving Taekyung's deliberately crude Dick Demon Escort Bureau wording in profanity.
### Chapter 628

# Chapter 628

## Plot

Yayul Mok angrily rejects the Murim Alliance's request for Nanman's help, citing the massacre of more than two hundred Miao civilians and Nanman's past sacrifices for the Han Chinese. Jin Taekyung argues that the Great Faction War was not pointless and that he came not as a diplomat but as a firefighter responding to a spreading crisis, promising to try persuasion without demanding anything in return. Yayul Mok explains that the first tribal council already opposed joining the alliance and that, in three days, all thirty-two tribes will meet; Baeksang and two other great chieftains have already united against the proposal, leaving Yayul Cheok as the only major holdout. Jin remains calm about the uncertain outcome and ends by demanding that Nanman feed him properly.

## Continuity

- Yayul Mok states that the first tribal council, held fifteen days earlier, concluded against Nanman joining the Murim Alliance.
- In three days, all thirty-two Nanman tribes will gather for a second council; Baeksang and two other great chieftains oppose joining, while Yayul Cheok has not accepted their position.
- The recent massacre killed more than two hundred people, most of them Miao elders, women, and children, intensifying Nanman's resentment toward the Han Chinese.
- Jin Taekyung has come to Nanman primarily to contain the spreading crisis rather than to serve as a diplomat, though he will still attempt to persuade the tribes.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 입맹 as joining the alliance and 대족장 as great chieftain.
- Retain Jin Taekyung's casual profanity and teasing digressions, including the firefighter metaphor and the demand to be fed.
### Chapter 629

# Chapter 629

## Plot

Jin Taekyung gets lost while returning to the Fire Dragon Pavilion's lodging, blends into the Nanman Beast Palace's Outer Hall using Mungyeong's concealment technique, and steals a crudely made tiger mask to hide his Han Chinese appearance. He learns that the Yi and Yao great chieftains have returned from a beast-subjugation campaign, then watches their victory procession and sees the Yao great chieftain Yohi, whose eyes light up when she notices him.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is currently in the Nanman Beast Palace's Outer Hall after becoming separated from the route to the Fire Dragon Pavilion's temporary lodging.
- Jin Taekyung is using a crudely made tiger mask to blend into Nanman's crowded market and conceal his Han Chinese appearance.
- The Yi and Yao peoples are two of Nanman's four great tribes; their great chieftains have returned with roughly five hundred warriors after a campaign against man-eating beasts.
- Yohi is the female great chieftain of the Yao people and is exceptionally intelligent, beautiful, and popular among her people.
- Yohi has noticed Jin Taekyung in the crowd and reacted with a sudden light in her eyes.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Yohi for 요희.
- Use Yi people for 이족 and Yao people for 요족.
- Use Crudely Made Tiger Mask for 대충 만든 호랑이 가면.

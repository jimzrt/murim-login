# Chapters 975–979

## Plot

Jin Taekyung kills Jamukha, then joins Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint to defeat Murong Baek, the North Heaven Demon Lord. Before dying, Murong reveals that the Lord of Heaven has awakened and warns that a war has begun, with at most half a year remaining. Elsewhere, an unnamed being awakens, sensing the fall of the fourth heaven and the loss of another faithful servant.

The Jin Family of Taiyuan arrives to reinforce the battered Hebei Peng warriors. Taekyung displays Murong and Jamukha’s heads, and Jin Wikyung offers the nomads surrender. Temur submits to Wikyung, but the battle against the Murong Family resumes. In a memory, Jin Mukyung recalls his childhood and his father apologizing after Mukyung’s mother died in childbirth. As the memory fades, a familiar voice asks if he is awake.

## Continuity

- Murong Baek and Jamukha are dead. The battle against the Murong Family resumed after Jin Family reinforcements arrived; its outcome is unknown.
- Temur submitted to Jin Wikyung as his lord.
- Murong Baek said the Lord of Heaven had awakened, the war had begun, and at most half a year remained.
- An unnamed being in the darkness sensed the fall of the fourth heaven and the disappearance of another faithful servant; its identity and awaited day remain unknown.
- Jin Mukyung recognized his father in a childhood memory; a familiar voice asks if he is awake, but the speaker is unidentified.
- The conditions of Cheol Mubaek, Wipeng, and Peng Cheolhu remain unknown.
- The Emperor remains gravely ill with Blood Soul Gu; the treatment said to require him to die once remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Taekyung’s mocking nickname 뽀삐 as “Poppy”; it is not an established name.
- Render 이룡신창 as “Divine Spear of the Imugi” and 모용위진 as “Murong Wijin”.
- Record Temur’s deferential address to Jin Wikyung as “my lord,” and Wikyung’s formal address to Temur as “you.”

### Chapter 980

# Chapter 980

## Plot

Jin Mukyung wakes at the Jin Family of Taiyuan, injured but recovering. Taekyung tells him the battle is over and the Murong Family has been killed or captured, but withholds the casualties. Mukyung recognizes that Taekyung’s forced cheerfulness is masking grief and demands to know who died and how many were sacrificed.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family’s surviving nomads surrendered, and the Murong Family members were killed or captured; Jin Mukyung has awakened with three broken bones and severe muscle pain, though his internal injury is comparatively mild.
- Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint helped treat Jin Mukyung’s internal injury.
- Jin Mukyung suspects Taekyung is hiding the extent of the casualties and directly asks who was sacrificed and how many; Taekyung has not answered.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Taekyung’s “crippled old servant” misunderstanding as a joke about the Bow Saint’s transforming bow; Mukyung is trying to say “transforming robot.”
### Chapter 981

# Chapter 981

## Plot

Three days after the battle at Eight Spring Gorge, Jin Mukyung learns that more than five thousand people were killed or wounded, including over three thousand dead. Reinforcements from Huashan, the Murim Alliance, and the Embroidered Uniform Guard helped end the fighting; Jin Taekyung killed Murong Wijin. Cheol Mubaek died buying Mukyung time against the Demon Bird, leaving Mukyung the Shura Annihilating Fist manual. As Taekyung speaks outside Mukyung’s door about grief, responsibility, and continuing to try, Mukyung silently thanks him.

## Continuity

- The battle at Eight Spring Gorge ended in a victory over an army of more than thirty thousand, but Shanxi and its allies suffered over five thousand casualties, including more than three thousand dead.
- Cheol Mubaek died after risking his life to give Jin Mukyung an opening against the Demon Bird; he left the Shura Annihilating Fist manual to Mukyung.
- Peng Cheolhu remains unconscious from severe injuries, and the Emperor remains gravely ill with Blood Soul Gu.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “casualties” inclusive of both dead and wounded; the chapter specifies over three thousand dead among more than five thousand casualties.
### Chapter 982

# Chapter 982

## Plot

Jin Taekyung reflects on comforting Jin Mukyung after his grief over recent losses, then shares a lighter exchange with Hyuk Mujin. Three days after the victory in Shanxi, Jin Wikyung is made to rest while others handle the aftermath. News arrives that the Murong Family attacked the Hebei Peng Family while pretending to support Shanxi. Peng Cheolyeong and the Bow Saint returned in time to help defeat them, and the Murong Family was annihilated; Murong Yeonghwi and several dozen others escaped. Jin Wikyung orders Temur’s tribespeople to hunt the fugitives, then commands Jeong Hogun to lead the Embroidered Uniform Guard to Liaoning.

Jin Wikyung keeps Temur under control by making clear that his own tribespeople would kill him if they learned the full truth of his betrayal. Jeong Hogun refuses Taekyung’s request as a personal favor but accepts it when Taekyung issues it as an order under his authority as Marquis of Shangshan.

## Continuity

- The Murong Family has been annihilated, but Murong Yeonghwi and several dozen survivors escaped; Jeong Hogun is ordered to lead the Embroidered Uniform Guard to Liaoning, and Temur’s tribespeople are ordered to pursue the fugitives.
- Jin Wikyung has been forced to rest while recovering from the battle, though he continues to handle documents and direct the response.
- Temur remains submitted to Jin Wikyung and is being compelled to help pursue the Murong survivors; exposure of his betrayal would put him at risk from his own tribespeople.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 천호 as “Thousand Captain,” the rank held by Jeong Hogun.
- Use “Marquis of Shangshan” for 상산후, the authority Taekyung invokes when commanding Jeong Hogun.
### Chapter 983

# Chapter 983

## Plot

Jeong Hogun’s forces surround the Murong estate in Shenyang. Murong Su, Murong Baek’s seventh son, risks his life to keep the remaining household members from intervening, but Jeong orders them executed until Namho intervenes. Jeong agrees to take the survivors for interrogation, and Namho declares the Murong Family dissolved while offering those proven innocent a chance to rebuild as the Murong household under a new Family Head.

## Continuity

- The Murong Family has been dissolved; about a hundred survivors, including Murong Su, are to be taken for interrogation, with their future dependent on whether their innocence is established.
- Murong Su stayed at the estate and risked his life to protect the survivors; Namho has offered them a chance to rebuild as the Murong household under a new Family Head if their innocence is proven.

## Translation Decisions

- Distinguish 모용세가 (the dissolved Murong Family) from 모용가 (the surviving Murong household/lineage).
### Chapter 984

# Chapter 984

## Plot

News that the Murong Family joined Dark Heaven spreads across the Central Plains. An imperial proclamation brands Dark Heaven traitors, calls on martial artists to join the government in punishing them, and declares that war has begun. At Eight Spring Gorge, tens of thousands honor the fallen with chrysanthemums and cornelian berries; Jin Taekyung sees Jin Wikyung and Lee Seowol grieving their uncles as a mysterious chime rings out.

## Continuity

- The imperial proclamation declares Dark Heaven traitors and calls on the martial world to help the government punish them; the war has begun.
- At Eight Spring Gorge, people honor the fallen with chrysanthemums and cornelian berries on the Double Ninth Festival.
- Lee Seowol is mourning the death of her uncle, Cheol Mubaek.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 황도 as “Imperial Capital.”

# Chapters 1180–1184

## Plot

As the allied forces approach the Tianshan Mountains, Jin Wikyung and Jin Mukyung face a vast monster horde with the Jin Family of Taiyuan. Mae Jonghak and other Murim leaders join the battle. Elsewhere, the Son of Heaven fights alongside the Imperial Guards, Baek Yeon, and Jeong Hogun; he summons the Twelve Palaces against at least twenty Black Ghosts. # Chapter 1185

## Plot

With the Imperial Army and Murim Alliance missing the agreed deadline, Jeok Cheongang’s group follows the contingency plan to head for Tianshan while the other forces draw Dark Heaven’s attention away from the desert. The Slaughter Saint says the unconscious Taekyung is unharmed and should wake within two days, though Taekyung’s earlier strength leaves him wondering whether the young martial artist held back.

After two days of rest in an abandoned town, the group uses the exhausted horses for provisions and enters Tianshan on foot. The oppressive atmosphere confirms the danger ahead, but Jeok Cheongang leads the group onward, carrying Taekyung.

- The group is following Mae Jonghak’s contingency plan: proceed to Tianshan if the allied forces miss the deadline, while the Murim Alliance and Imperial Army misdirect Dark Heaven away from the desert.
- The allied forces’ fate and whereabouts remain unknown; the group hopes they will arrive late and follow to Tianshan.

# Chapter 1186

## Plot

As the group pushes through Tianshan’s strange, lightless mist and climbs its peaks, Jeok Cheongang and the Slaughter Saint acknowledge that they have no way to explain or overcome the phenomena. They suspect the events are no longer coincidences, and Jeok worries that fate may be cruel to his unconscious Disciple. During a brief rest, Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang joke about their hardships, then share their grief over the uncertain fate of the Jin Family and Beggars’ Sect. The mist suddenly moves as if alive, catching Mujin’s attention.

- The group has climbed five peaks and has not yet found the Demonic Cult’s headquarters; they do not know how much farther they must travel.
- The mist and darkness across Tianshan are accompanied by unexplained phenomena; the mist appears to move as if alive.
- The fates of the Jin Family and Beggars’ Sect members who headed west remain unknown.

# Chapter 1188

## Plot

Separated from the others in the sinister mist, Jeok Cheongang encounters a figure appearing to be Jangcheon, his former Disciple whom he believed dead and who later became Jopil. The figure claims to have survived and begs Jeok for punishment and forgiveness. Overcome by grief, Jeok nearly accepts the apparition, but recognizes the deception when it attacks him. He catches the dagger, then burns the figure—whatever has taken on Jangcheon’s appearance—with a punch, saying he can finally let him go.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang encountered and attacked a being wearing Jangcheon’s appearance in the mist; its identity and connection to the real Jangcheon remain unconfirmed.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Jangcheon (장천) distinct from the later name Jopil (조필); the chapter identifies Jopil as the name Jangcheon used after leaving Jeok Cheongang.

# Chapter 1189

Jeok Cheongang recognized and destroyed a Black Ghost wearing Jangcheon’s appearance. Facing four more coordinated Black Ghosts and waves of monsters, he formally cast out the illusion of his former Disciple and fought on while protecting Jin Taekyung, who was unharmed on his back.

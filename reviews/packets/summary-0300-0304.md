# Chapters 295–299

## Plot

The leading Skeleton Knight evolves into a speaking Level 105 Skeleton Warlord after defeating the Black Forest’s former ruler. Its Summon the Dead and Commander’s Rally skills create and strengthen a massive undead army, but Jin Taekyung tricks the Warlord into concentrating the force and destroys most of it with One Annihilation. After surviving as a damaged skull, the Warlord bargains for its life and agrees to supply Skeletons as the Peace Guild’s contracted EXP factory manager while retaining control of the Black Forest and its undead.

The Warlord reveals that a black wizard summoned and controlled it, but an unexplained surge in magical power recently freed it. Taekyung begins harvesting resurrected Skeletons for EXP, Magic Gems, and Equipment. When the Warlord detects intruders, Taekyung approaches the Gate with its skull.

Meanwhile, the Hunter Association delays the rescue for one hour and eight minutes despite the arrival of more than thirty A-rank Hunters and an Ares Guild contingent. Choi Minwoo leads more than fifty Peace Guild members into the Black Forest, where they find a scorched trail and no life within three hundred meters. Believing Taekyung and the Named Monster dead, the Guild mourns while the Seoul Branch President and Go Jun treat the outcome as a successful avoidance of further casualties. Go Jun taunts Choi and blames the Peace Guild, only for Taekyung to return alive, mock the premature forty-ninth-day memorial rite, and challenge Go Jun to fight.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton Warlord is a Level 105 Named Monster, former ruler of the Black Forest, and commander of its undead.
- The Warlord can speak, use Summon the Dead and Commander’s Rally, and raise Skeletons on demand.
- Taekyung and the Warlord have an agreement: the Warlord retains nominal authority and supplies Skeletons as the Peace Guild’s EXP factory manager.
- The Warlord’s sudden magical-power surge freed it from the black wizard’s control despite its lack of Magic Gems; the cause remains unknown.
- Taekyung has repeatedly harvested the Warlord’s resurrected Skeletons for EXP, Magic Gems, and Equipment. He has not yet tested whether humans can absorb Magic Gems.
- The Hunter Association delayed the rescue response for one hour and eight minutes. The Seoul Branch President deferred to Go Jun and initially blocked the Peace Guild’s entry.
- Choi Minwoo, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong left their secret cultivation training to join the rescue. Choi led more than fifty Peace Guild members into the Gate.
- Taekyung survived the Black Forest devastation and is now directly confronting Go Jun; the Seoul Branch President is also present.
- Choi Minwoo remains at Four Stars in the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique and possesses hidden internal energy equivalent to one jiazi. Its origin and his grandfather’s involvement remain unresolved.
- Taekyung’s failed breakthrough into the Supreme Peak realm, the Warlord’s anomalous evolution, and the black wizard’s identity and intentions remain unresolved.

### Chapter 300

# Chapter 300

## Plot

Jin Taekyung follows the Skeleton Warlord's directions back through the Black Wizard's Black Forest and discovers that the Peace Guild believes he died. He stores the Warlord's skull in his Inventory, where the Warlord remains able to communicate through their connection.

Go Jun and Seoul Branch President Lee Woojoong arrive with hundreds of Hunters after the delayed rescue response. Taekyung returns alive, mocks Go Jun, condemns Woojoong for prioritizing Lee Jungryong's favor over Hunter safety, and confronts Go Jun after Go Jun invokes Lee's special order and threatens him. The chapter ends as Go Jun prepares to fight.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton Warlord can navigate the Black Forest by perceiving the Gate's interior from above.
- The Skeleton Warlord can be stored in Taekyung's Inventory because it is an undead monster without living vitality, and it can still communicate with Taekyung from inside.
- The Peace Guild and the returning trainees believed Taekyung had died and began mourning him after finding his equipment and the Named Monster's armor.
- Lee Jungryong sent Go Jun to eliminate Taekyung as a perceived danger during the delayed rescue operation.
- Seoul Branch President Lee Woojoong delayed the rescue response for one hour to curry favor with Lee Jungryong and the Ares Guild.
- Taekyung returned alive and publicly confronted both Go Jun and Lee Woojoong; the confrontation with Go Jun had not yet begun by the chapter's end.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이우중 as “Lee Woojoong.”
- Render 서울 중앙지부 헌터 협회장 as “Seoul Branch President,” consistent with the established Seoul Association title variants.
- Retain “forty-ninth-day memorial rite” for 49재 with the established explanatory footnote.
### Chapter 301

# Chapter 301

## Plot

Go Jun recalls the thirty years since Lee Jungryong took him in, trained him, and made him Head of Security. His absolute loyalty to Lee overrides the order to avoid conflict with the Peace Guild after Taekyung insults Lee. Go Jun attacks Taekyung at full strength, but Taekyung defeats him with a single blow, causing catastrophic internal injuries and rendering him unconscious. Taekyung orders him to tell Lee that no one can touch the Peace Guild while Taekyung is alive, then identifies himself as a descendant of the Fire King and successor to the Blazing Flame.

Taekyung intimidates Lee Woojoong and more than two hundred Association Hunters into claiming they have short-term memory loss and agreeing to keep silent about what happened. He then tells an Ares Guild team leader to report the incident to Lee Jungryong. Taekyung realizes that Ares will keep attacking whenever an opening appears and that this conflict has no true truce.

After returning to the Peace Guild members, Taekyung gives the excuse that he went to urinate. Song Song responds in an unusually gentle voice by asking whether she may kill him, so Taekyung promises to explain everything outside the Gate.

## Continuity

- Go Jun's blind loyalty to Lee Jungryong led him to defy Lee's order to avoid a clash with the Peace Guild.
- Go Jun attacked Taekyung with his full strength and was defeated by a single counterblow, suffering severe internal injuries and losing consciousness.
- Taekyung warned Lee Jungryong through Go Jun that no one can touch the Peace Guild while Taekyung is alive.
- Lee Woojoong and more than two hundred Association Hunters witnessed the defeat and agreed to suppress or deny their memories of the incident.
- An Ares Guild team leader is responsible for reporting Go Jun's defeat and Taekyung's warning to Lee Jungryong.
- Taekyung now understands the conflict with Ares as a war without a lasting truce and intends to remain vigilant.
- Song Song and the other Peace Guild members demand an explanation for Taekyung's disappearance and return.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 열화 in Taekyung's lineage declaration as “the Blazing Flame,” while retaining 화왕 as “Fire King.”
- Render Go Jun's internal address 애송아 as “brat.”
### Chapter 302

# Chapter 302

## Plot

The public and media assume Jin Taekyung died inside the Gate, but the deployed Hunters emerge with an injured Hunter on a stretcher and Taekyung walks out alive. He confirms that the Named Monster existed but has now been killed, creating a legendary live-broadcast moment.

A week later, Taekyung's interview has surpassed 200 million views and international broadcasters are requesting interviews, though he has accepted none. In Team Leader Choi's office, Taekyung privately reveals that the public story surrounding him is false, summons the Skeleton Warlord from his Inventory, calls it Warlordmon, and torments it with commands.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung survived the Named Monster raid and publicly returned alive after being presumed dead.
- Taekyung killed the Named Monster; Team Leader Choi states that Taekyung has now hunted two Named Monsters alone.
- Taekyung's live interview reached 200 million views within twenty-four hours, generating worldwide memes and international interview requests.
- Taekyung has not accepted any of the international interview offers despite recognizing their potential benefit to the Peace Guild.
- Taekyung privately told Team Leader Choi that the public story surrounding him is false and concealed the System's existence.
- The Skeleton Warlord remains alive, stored in Taekyung's Inventory, and was brought to Team Leader Choi's office for Taekyung's amusement.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 워로드몬 as “Warlordmon,” a deliberately childish nickname for the Skeleton Warlord.
- Render 뚱인데요 as “This is Patrick” to preserve the unexpected live-broadcast dumb joke.
### Chapter 303

# Chapter 303

## Plot

Taekyung’s revelation that he controls the captured Skeleton Warlord is dismissed as an impossible joke, while the government audit accepts the Warlord’s bones and armor fragments as proof of a defeated Named Monster. Taekyung continues using Warlordmon for comic commands and forces it to provide Skeleton troops as both an EXP source and staged combat practice for the Peace Guild’s new members, though the severe level gap now yields only a few level-ups.

On the day of Hayeon’s college entrance exam, Taekyung visits her afterward and learns that she missed a perfect score by one English question. Afterward, the family moves into their remodeled former home. Concerned that Lee Jungryong might target his family, Taekyung uses his growing wealth from selective high-paid advertisements, interviews, and television appearances to hire a security company, secretly purchase neighboring houses, and station covert guards around the family.

One week after Hayeon’s exam, Team Leader Choi arrives with a tired expression, says he must step away for an uncertain matter, and shows the Peace Guild a video from China on the hologram television. The chapter ends as the video begins playing.

## Continuity

- The government audit did not discover that Taekyung controls the Skeleton Warlord; the Warlord’s bones and armor fragments were accepted as evidence of a Named Monster’s defeat.
- Taekyung uses the Skeleton Warlord’s troops as a controlled training resource for Peace Guild members, while threatening the Warlord if any Guild member dies.
- Hayeon completed the college entrance exam and believes she missed a perfect score by one English question.
- Taekyung’s family moved into their remodeled former home after Hayeon’s exam.
- Taekyung accepted only a small selection of high-paying advertisements, interviews, and television appearances, substantially increasing his wealth and the Peace Guild’s stock price.
- Taekyung secretly arranged extensive security around his family by purchasing nearby houses and placing covert guards there; his mother and Hayeon remain unaware of the arrangement.
- Team Leader Choi received a matter requiring him to step away and showed the Peace Guild a video from China; the video’s contents are not yet known.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Quick Attack for 전광석화 and its shortened command 전광 when Warlordmon performs the Pokémon-style movement command.
- Use Body Slam for 몸통박치기 as Warlordmon’s comic attack command.
- Render 수능 as “college entrance exam” and 내신 7등급 as “seventh-tier school grades.”
### Chapter 304

# Chapter 304

## Plot

Team Leader Choi shows Taekyung and the Peace Guild holographic footage of a Monster Wave in a small city near Nanchong, Sichuan Province. More than three hundred thousand casualties are estimated during the first twenty-four hours, while Chinese Hunters and monsters clash in overwhelming numbers.

The Monster Wave was caused by a Named Monster Lich, an immensely powerful undead archmage capable of casting Fire Rain and claiming territory. Choi reveals that China contacted him one week earlier to request Taekyung's participation because Xiao Yang, the General Secretary of China's Central Committee, specifically wanted him.

## Continuity

- A Monster Wave occurred in a small city near Nanchong, Sichuan Province, causing approximately three hundred thousand estimated casualties during its first twenty-four hours.
- The Named Monster responsible is a Lich, an apex undead monster and fallen archmage whose fire magic is far beyond the Liches Butler Kim witnessed during the Great Cataclysm.
- The Lich declared that it would kill and take everything from anyone entering its territory, indicating that it is unlikely to be satisfied with one small city.
- China's Public Security Armed Forces deployed more than one thousand Hunters against the Monster Wave, while the assembled monsters also numbered more than one thousand.
- Team Leader Choi received a request from China one week earlier for Taekyung to participate in responding to the crisis.
- Choi identifies the person who specifically requested Taekyung as Xiao Yang, the General Secretary of China's Central Committee.
- The Sichuan catastrophe occurred ten days earlier but has not appeared as ordinary worldwide news, and the reason for that concealment remains unexplained.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Monster Wave for 몬스터 웨이브 and Lich for 리치.
- Use Public Security Armed Forces Division for 공안 무력부 and Five-Starred Red Flag for 오성홍기.
- Use Sichuan Province for 쓰촨성 and Nanchong City for 난충시.
- Use Xiao Yang for 샤오 양 and preserve the General Secretary/chongseogi-to-Jongseok mishearing gag with its explanatory footnote.
- Use Bingbing for 빙빙 and Mommy for the mother's urgent address.

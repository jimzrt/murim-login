# Chapters 60–64

## Plot

At Eight Spring Gorge, Taekyung and the reconnaissance squad surround the Head Elder, but his Finger-Flicking Technique kills numerous allied martial artists. Jin Wikyung arrives and joins the assault. Taekyung uses One Flash to destroy the Head Elder’s arm, while the elder cuts apart Wikyung’s ancestral sword and reveals that his betrayal grew from revenge for an old Great Faction War disaster.

The wounded Head Elder forms Sword Force for a final attack. Lee Cheonbaek ambushes him with a dagger, giving Taekyung the opening to finish him with One Flash. Lee dies from the effort. The System records the defeats of Lee Cheonbaek and Jin Baekyang, completes the Traitor Chain Quest, grants Taekyung multiple level-ups and increased Fame, and awards him the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi title. The First Elder dies after warning Wipeng about Dark Heaven, and the surviving black-clad forces surrender.

Dark Heaven then appears on the cliff. Its agent kills Jin Chung and the Gunggwimun disciples who stayed behind, explaining that Dark Heaven rescued the conspirators from the Demonic Cult decades earlier and implanted gu in their heads. The agent deliberately spares Taekyung.

Five days later, rumors credit the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi with the Jin Family’s victory. Mount Heng Sword Sect is collapsing after Lee Seogeun and Lee Cheonbaek’s deaths, and its First Young Master is killed by bandits. Jin Mukyung, the Heaven Shaking Sword and Taekyung’s lookalike second older brother, returns after three years at Heaven’s Gate Temple. Taekyung reaches Level 50, advances the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique to the Eighth Stage, and gains 100 unassigned points.

Mukyung immediately tests Taekyung with the Reformation Fist and grappling techniques. Despite using no internal energy, Mukyung overwhelms him. Taekyung spends ten of his points on Agility, briefly reads Mukyung’s movements, and is struck again. Their spar remains unresolved.

## Continuity

- Jin Baekyang, the former Head Elder, is dead. Lee Cheonbaek died helping Taekyung defeat him.
- The Eight Spring Gorge battle is over; the black-clad conspirators surrendered after the Head Elder’s death.
- The First Elder is dead after naming Dark Heaven. The Second and Third Elders are dead. Wipeng and the surviving senior members’ subsequent fate is unresolved.
- Dark Heaven rescued the conspirators from the Demonic Cult, implanted gu in them, and is pursuing a larger plan. Its agents, purpose, and reason for sparing Taekyung remain unresolved.
- Jin Chung and the Gunggwimun disciples who stayed with him were killed by Dark Heaven.
- Taekyung is Level 50 with Fame 1,180, fifteen years of Internal Energy, and 90 unassigned points after spending 10 on Agility. His four Titles include Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Scion of a Prestigious Family, Novice Trainee, and Gambler.
- Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi is a Peak-grade Title granting All Stats +10 and Fame +100.
- Taekyung’s Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique is at the Eighth Stage, but his Internal Energy remains fifteen years because accumulation is slow.
- Jin Mukyung is twenty-three, the Heaven Shaking Sword, a Peak-level martial genius, and Taekyung’s second older brother. He studied at Heaven’s Gate Temple in Henan for three years and is far stronger than Taekyung.
- Taekyung’s spar with Mukyung remains unresolved.
- Mount Heng Sword Sect is near collapse; Lee Seogeun and Lee Cheonbaek are dead, and the First Young Master was killed by mounted bandits.

### Chapter 65

# Chapter 65

## Plot

Jin Mukyung is shocked by Taekyung's transformation over the last three years. He recognizes Taekyung as a First Rate martial artist standing before the Peak realm, and is increasingly pressured as Taekyung's speed continues to rise.

Mukyung escalates the spar by drawing on his internal energy, defeats Taekyung, and destroys the pavilion. Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin survive the collapse and are taken to the Medicine King Hall, while the family mistakes the incident for an attack by an unknown assassin.

Socheon explains that the family suspects the assassin may be the Head Elder's hidden disciple and has formed a pursuit team. Jin Wikyung has ordered the attacker's immediate execution, while Hyuk Mujin is publicly credited with fighting bravely and protecting Taekyung, prompting rumors that he will become the next Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung judges Taekyung to be First Rate and close to the Peak realm, with growth and speed that astonish him.
- Mukyung defeats Taekyung in their spar; the pavilion is destroyed, Taekyung survives with bruises, and Hyuk Mujin is badly injured.
- The attacker is believed by the Jin Family to be an unidentified assassin, possibly the Head Elder's hidden disciple; a pursuit team is tracking the assassin.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 삼재검법 as Three Calamities Sword Technique.
- Use Medicine King Hall, Second Young Master, and Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion for 약왕당, 이공자, and 수문각주.
- Retain gongcheong seokyu for 공청석유 and explain the rare-elixir and petroleum wordplay in a footnote.
- Render 군자 as junzi with a footnote explaining the Confucian ideal of a morally upright gentleman.
- Retain Hyung-nim for 형님 in Taekyung's deferential warning, distinct from his established casual hyung.
### Chapter 66

# Chapter 66

## Plot

Jin Mukyung reunites with his older brother, Jin Wikyung, after three years but remains emotionally detached and quickly leaves to train. He acknowledges that Jin Taekyung is no longer the youngest brother he remembered after their recent fifty-exchange duel.

Wipeng leads thirty elites south under the official mission of finding the nonexistent assassin. Jin Wikyung has secretly ordered him to prepare for a larger political move: summoning every sect in Shanxi Province on New Year's Day and, if necessary, pursuing the position of Alliance Leader. Jin Wikyung's search through five days of family records finds no information about Dark Heaven.

Taekyung's Status Window shows him at Level 50 with fifty remaining points after investing heavily in Agility during the duel. Gong Yacheong is recovering and will take charge of the rebuilt Sakju Branch; Socheon and Soyul will leave with him in six months. Taekyung promises Socheon that he will visit them, though Soyul still does not know that their parents are dead.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung and Jin Wikyung are brothers who reunite after three years; Mukyung remains blunt, emotionally restrained, and focused on sword training.
- Jin Wikyung plans to summon every sect in Shanxi Province to the Jin Family on the coming New Year's Day and may seek the Alliance Leader position.
- Wipeng has been sent south with thirty elites under the cover of pursuing the nonexistent assassin.
- Jin Wikyung searched five days of family records for Dark Heaven but found no information.
- Gong Yacheong is recovering and will oversee the rebuilt Sakju Branch; Socheon and Soyul intend to accompany him in six months.
- Soyul is five years old and still does not know that her parents died in the Mount Heng Sword Sect's attack.
- Taekyung has Level 50 status, fifty unspent points, and fifteen years of internal energy after spending fifty points on Agility during his duel with Mukyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 삼문협 as Three Questions Gorge.
- Render 섬서 as Shaanxi.
- Render 삼공자 as Third Young Master.
- Render 맹주 as Alliance Leader.
- Retain Great Hero for Taekyung's respectful address to Gong Yacheong as 공 대협.
### Chapter 67

# Chapter 67

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin remains badly bruised and undergoes painful acupuncture from the Medicine King Hall Master, while Taekyung's bruises nearly vanish overnight after circulating his qi and sleeping. Taekyung is discharged, but Mujin must remain for treatment. With his pavilion destroyed, Taekyung is temporarily taken by Jin Wikyung to a large three-story residence containing two training halls. Wikyung arranges for Jin Mukyung to share the residence with Taekyung for fifteen days while Taekyung's residence is rebuilt. Mukyung accepts but requests a physician instead of servants or a cook, openly threatening to beat Taekyung again. Wikyung stops their quarrel, reprimands both brothers, and compels them to agree to the temporary cohabitation despite their mutual reluctance.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's pavilion was destroyed in his fight with Jin Mukyung, leaving him temporarily without a residence.
- Taekyung's bruises largely disappeared overnight after circulating his qi and sleeping; he suspects the recovery is due to Sleep Mode.
- The Medicine King Hall Master discharges Taekyung but keeps Hyuk Mujin under treatment after the incident.
- Jin Wikyung arranges for Taekyung and Jin Mukyung to share a large residence with two training halls for fifteen days while Taekyung's residence is rebuilt.
- Jin Wikyung's intense protectiveness toward Taekyung is already widely known within the Jin Family despite his public, formal image.
- Jin Mukyung remains hostile toward Taekyung and appears eager to fight him again, but accepts the temporary shared residence.
- Jin Wikyung intervenes when the brothers begin to fight and orders them to live together peacefully.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Medicine King Hall Master for 약왕당주.
- Use Sleep Mode for 수면 모드.
- Retain the established terms three-pyeong goshiwon, go-stop, and Red Hare with explanatory footnotes.
### Chapter 68

# Chapter 68

## Plot

Jin Wikyung arranges ten days of cohabitation between Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung. Mukyung imposes three rules requiring polite speech, silence, and immediate obedience when he claims the training hall. Taekyung leaves after witnessing Mukyung destroy steel dummies, then decides to catch up to his brother's strength.

Taekyung discovers that no quest is currently active and that the Logout Quest has vanished. The Return achievement grants him the Returnee title, activates the Login and Logout functions, and increases all his stats by ten.

While Mukyung attempts to break through the Ren and Du meridians after three years of repeated effort, Taekyung's shouting disrupts his training. Later, Wipeng arrives at Song Sword Sect with thirty Jin Family retainers, delivers Jin Wikyung's New Year's Day summons, and implies that the visit is both a warning and part of the pursuit of the assassin who attacked Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung requires Taekyung to use polite speech, remain quiet, and yield the underground training hall on command during their ten-day cohabitation.
- Taekyung wants to become stronger and hopes to learn directly from the Peak-level Mukyung during their time together.
- Taekyung has no active quest immediately before receiving the Return achievement and Returnee title.
- The Returnee title grants All Stats +10 and activates the Login and Logout functions.
- Mukyung has spent the past three years attempting to open the Ren and Du meridians and is interrupted by Taekyung before completing the attempt.
- Wipeng visits Song Sword Sect at night with thirty retainers, delivers Wikyung's New Year's Day summons, and presents the summons as a formal invitation while its political meaning is a warning.
- Song Sword Sect previously received assistance because of its relationship with the Jin Family but withdrew when the war began.
- Wipeng says the assassin who targeted Taekyung is believed to have been sent by someone hostile to the Jin Family, but he does not identify the assassin or confirm Song Sword Sect's involvement.

## Translation Decisions

- None.
### Chapter 69

# Chapter 69

## Plot

Jin Wikyung wakes from a nightmare and hears steel clashing near Jin Mukyung's residence, where Taekyung has been staying. He discovers Mukyung relentlessly striking Taekyung with his scabbard and realizes that the beating is a harsh form of training.

After Taekyung is knocked unconscious, Wikyung observes that Mukyung is impressed by Taekyung's stamina and grit despite denying that he praised him. Wikyung decides that the difficult cohabitation will benefit both brothers, while Mukyung agrees to continue it.

Taekyung wakes the next morning and adopts formal speech to avoid further punishment. Mukyung begins formally training him, offering to rebuild his inadequate martial arts from the basics after Taekyung sincerely declares that he wants to become stronger.

The System creates the Peak-Grade Quest '[Trial? Training?],' requiring Taekyung to train under Mukyung until he earns Mukyung's recognition within the remaining ten-day period. Mukyung orders Taekyung to bring his spear for real-combat training, and the System restricts Logout for the duration of the Quest.

## Continuity

- During the temporary cohabitation, Jin Mukyung begins training Jin Taekyung through harsh practical instruction and intends to rebuild Taekyung's martial arts from the fundamentals.
- Taekyung's genuine desire to become stronger earns Mukyung's agreement to teach him despite Mukyung's reluctance.
- The System Quest '[Trial? Training?]' has Grade Peak, Restriction Jin Taekyung, Mission Jin Mukyung's recognition (Incomplete), unknown Reward and Failure conditions, and 9 days 23 hours 51 minutes 10 seconds remaining when generated.
- Taekyung must bring his spear to Mukyung's training, which will be conducted as though it were real combat; Logout is restricted during this Quest.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 두 시진 as two hours.
- Use fist-and-kicking technique for 권각술 and recognition for 인정 in the Quest mission.
- Retain Hyung-nim for 형님 in Taekyung's deferential speech and preserve established System terminology, including Logout, Quest, Grade, and Peak.
- Render 아스모데우스 as Asmodeus; 마왕 아스모데우스 is the Demon King Asmodeus referenced in Taekyung's sarcastic comparison.

# Chapters 255–259

## Plot

Jongni Chu reveals himself as a Supreme Peak master who has Returned to Simplicity and overwhelms Cheongpung in the Star-Array Grand Banquet semifinal. He wounds Cheongpung and is about to kill him when Jin Taekyung intervenes. Jeok Cheongang forces the final to begin immediately, and Taekyung discovers that his eighth-stage Qi Sense Skill cannot identify Jongni because an unknown force rejects it.

Taekyung and Jongni exchange dozens of attacks. Taekyung reaches the seventh stage of Fire Dragon Divine Spear and fights through an Internal Injury, but Jongni suddenly abandons the platform after noticing something in the distance. The System awards Taekyung the official victory, restoring him and granting EXP, Fame, Points, and several level-ups. Pursuers cannot determine Jongni’s martial arts or realm.

The pursuit ends at a huge pit, where Hong Dao is found with both legs severed and fatal internal injuries. Before dying, he gives Jeok Cheongang the cryptic words “Jongni Chu, Dark Heaven, Unnamed, Buddhist Staff.” Jeok and Taekyung infer that Jongni Chu is connected to or represents Dark Heaven, and that Dark Heaven seeks the Green Jade Buddha Staff entrusted to Unnamed—the sacred treasure and symbol of Shaolin’s Abbot. Jeok orders the Murim leaders to gather at Shaolin and travels there with Taekyung.

Meanwhile, Dark Heaven operatives kill messengers, martial artists, and civilians along the route. Han Su kills a Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent and joins his longtime friend Flame Tiger; after learning that Unnamed possesses the staff, they also proceed toward Shaolin.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is the official Star-Array Grand Banquet winner because Jongni Chu left the dueling platform first.
- Taekyung’s Internal Injury was completely healed by the System’s victory rewards; he gained substantial EXP, Fame, Points, and at least three visible level-ups.
- Jongni Chu is a concealed Supreme Peak master who has Returned to Simplicity, wields Sword Force through a rusted sword, and possesses immense internal energy.
- Jongni Chu escaped using Stepping on Empty Air and Jeok Cheongang’s sword force. His destination, purpose, true identity, sect, and martial-arts origin remain unresolved.
- An unknown force rejected Taekyung’s Qi Sense Skill; its nature and connection to Jongni Chu are unknown.
- Jeok Cheongang’s best friend Hong Dao is dead. His final words implicated Jongni Chu, Dark Heaven, Unnamed, and the Buddhist Staff.
- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung are traveling to Shaolin, where Jeok expects a major crisis or “blood wind” may already have begun. The Murim leaders have been ordered to gather there.
- Hong Dao entrusted Unnamed with the Green Jade Buddha Staff. Dark Heaven is actively searching for Unnamed and the staff.
- Myriad-Mile Pursuit is tracking Jongni Chu, but Jeok expects the pursuit to fail.
- Han Su and Flame Tiger are longtime friends and Dark Heaven operatives heading toward Shaolin.
- The attacker who crippled Hong Dao, the meaning of his final words, the fate and location of Unnamed and the staff, and the situation at Shaolin remain unresolved.
- Shadow Killer’s fate, the Gold-Faced Young Master’s identity, and Dark Heaven’s relationship with Tianshan, the Demonic Cult, or the Demonic Path remain unknown.

# Chapter 260

## Plot

Cheongpung awakens after Hyuk Mujin revives him and learns that Hong Dao has been killed and Jongni Chu is blamed. Although he remembers Jongni Chu's overwhelming attack and the fear it caused him, Cheongpung insists that Jongni Chu is his friend and immediately leaves for Mount Song.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung remembers Jongni Chu's attack, including his own helplessness and fear, but still believes Jongni Chu is his friend.
- Cheongpung has left ahead of the others for Mount Song and Shaolin after learning that Hong Dao was killed and Shaolin is in danger.

- Hongcheon is Hong Dao's youngest Junior Brother and a Supreme Peak Shaolin master whose martial prowess has surpassed Hong Dao's; he remains at Shaolin to defend it.

- Flame Tiger engages Hongcheon while Han Su proceeds toward the Face-Wall Cave, where Unnamed is located and the Green Jade Buddha Staff is believed to be.

### Chapter 263

# Chapter 263

## Plot

The Blood Lord reveals that he claims to have killed Hong Dao by luring him away with a false threat to detonate a Heaven-Shaking Thunder in the spectator stands. He says Jin Taekyung's sudden intervention advanced the plan by one day and identifies Jongni Chu as its greatest variable, confirming that Jongni Chu was neither the culprit nor an accomplice. After Han Su delivers the Green Jade Buddha Staff and asks the Blood Lord to kill Jeok Cheongang and Taekyung, the Blood Lord beheads Han Su in a single strike and destroys his corpse. He then declares that he is far older than he appears, boasts that the Fire King's lineage will end that day, and confronts Jeok and Taekyung. When he senses someone hiding nearby, Cheongpung emerges from the grass, apologizing because he has never been so frightened before.

## Continuity

- The Blood Lord claims to have killed Hong Dao after luring him away from the Star-Array Grand Banquet with a fabricated threat involving a Heaven-Shaking Thunder.
- The Blood Lord states that Taekyung's intervention forced him to advance his plan by one day and that Jongni Chu was the plan's greatest variable; Jongni Chu was not the culprit or an accomplice.
- Han Su delivered the Green Jade Buddha Staff to the Blood Lord and requested the deaths of Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung.
- The Blood Lord killed Han Su, the Yin Ghost, by beheading him in a single strike and then crushed his corpse.
- The Blood Lord claims to have lived at least twice as long as Taekyung despite appearing to be in his early or mid-twenties.
- The Blood Lord declares that he can kill Jeok Cheongang within one ildagyeong, and Jeok recognizes that the threat is credible.
- Jeok Cheongang shields Taekyung and uses Sound Transmission to tell him to flee if the situation becomes unfavorable; Taekyung refuses because Jeok would otherwise face two Supreme Peak masters alone.
- The Blood Lord declares that the Fire Gate Clan's lineage may end that day; Cheongpung emerges from hiding and admits that fear kept him concealed.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Heaven-Shaking Thunder for 진천뢰, the name of the explosive invoked in the Blood Lord's false threat.
- Use Four Saints for 사성, contrasting it with the established Three Saints for 삼성.
- Retain ildagyeong for 일다경 and explain it as the short time required to drink a cup of tea.
### Chapter 264

# Chapter 264

## Plot

Cheongpung joins Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang against the Blood Lord, who recognizes him as Huashan's Invincible Divine Sword and knows that he is Sword Saint Mae Jonghak's Disciple and grandson. The Blood Lord challenges them to prove their strength through martial arts, then effortlessly splits Jeok's Flame Divine Palm at Great Completion and repels Taekyung and Cheongpung while fighting with overwhelming power comparable to or greater than the Three Saints.

After revealing that he had yielded three forms in imitation of orthodox courtesy, the Blood Lord launches his true attack. Taekyung uses the System to resummon White Flame after the Blood Lord catches it, piercing his Body-Protecting Qi and chest. Taekyung calls for Jeok Cheongang, who unleashes the Dance of the Fire God and Demon, a technique he had previously used only once to kill a thousand Demonic Cultists.

Jeok's final strike leaves the Blood Lord kneeling and apparently motionless, but the exertion causes Jeok to stagger. After Taekyung and Cheongpung move him to safety, the Blood Lord rises as if unharmed and comments admiringly on the name of Jeok's technique.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung has joined Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang in their confrontation with the Blood Lord at Mount Song.
- The Blood Lord knows Cheongpung's identity as Huashan's Invincible Divine Sword and as Sword Saint Mae Jonghak's Disciple and grandson.
- The Blood Lord's martial power is sufficient to split Jeok Cheongang's Flame Divine Palm at Great Completion and may equal or exceed the Three Saints.
- Taekyung used an Inventory resummoning trick to return White Flame to his hand and pierce the Blood Lord's Body-Protecting Qi and chest.
- Jeok Cheongang used the Dance of the Fire God and Demon after Taekyung created an opening; the technique had previously been used only once, to kill a thousand Demonic Cult members.
- The Blood Lord rose apparently unharmed after receiving Jeok Cheongang's final attack, so the confrontation's outcome remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 염라 as Yama, with an explanatory note identifying him as the Buddhist lord of the underworld.
- Render 절체절명 as Life-or-Death Crisis for the forcibly accepted System Quest.
- Preserve the Blood Lord's light, mocking tone even while he demonstrates overwhelming power and apparently survives Jeok Cheongang's finishing attack.

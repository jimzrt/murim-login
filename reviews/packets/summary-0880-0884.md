# Chapters 875–879

## Plot

After Prince Shangshan is taken into Qianqing Palace, Jin Taekyung is escorted out through its guarded corridors. He hears Aehyang, the City Lord of Sichuan Province’s former concubine, in a restricted area. Taekyung and Hong Jin later suspect she is pregnant and that the Emperor may intend her child to replace Shangshan, but neither her pregnancy nor the Emperor’s plan is confirmed. Taekyung gave Shangshan the Myriad-Poison Ring for protection.

Hong Jin plans to contact Ma Sanbao about the prince’s danger. He recounts that the late Emperor entrusted Shangshan to him shortly before dying in a confused state; Taekyung suspects Blood Soul Gu may have been involved, but this remains unconfirmed. Taekyung has also made an undisclosed personal request of Ma.

In Hangzhou, Jeok Cheongang receives two letters—one from Taekyung and another from someone close to Hong Jin—reads them, and burns them. Their contents remain unknown. He says the group has been formally invited to the imperial palace. Meanwhile, rumors circulate that Shangshan has returned to the capital and that the Emperor has an heir. An undercover Embroidered Uniform Guard captain arrests people at an inn where the rumors were discussed. The Emperor and a hidden adviser suspect the East Depot, Taekyung, or both of spreading them. The Emperor says they will make the heir rumor true and begins a great celebration; no pregnancy or birth is confirmed. He also says a long-standing target must be removed for his great undertaking, without revealing the target’s identity.

## Continuity

- The Emperor has confined Shangshan in Qianqing Palace. Taekyung left without him and gave him the Myriad-Poison Ring; whether it can protect against Blood Soul Gu is unknown.
- Aehyang is in Qianqing Palace. Hong Jin and Taekyung suspect she may be pregnant, but this and the Emperor’s intentions remain uncertain.
- Hong Jin plans to contact Ma Sanbao about Shangshan. Taekyung’s additional request to Ma is undisclosed.
- The late Emperor died after a period of mental confusion while confined. Taekyung suspects Blood Soul Gu may have been involved. The City Lord of Sichuan Province showed strange symptoms before his death, and Blood Soul Gu was found in his corpse; any connection remains unconfirmed.
- Jeok Cheongang burned the two letters he received. Their contents and the purpose of the group’s imperial invitation are unknown.
- Rumors claim Shangshan returned to the capital and the Emperor has an heir. Their truth and who spread them are unresolved. The Emperor’s long-standing target and great undertaking are also unidentified.
- Taekyung suspects the Emperor is connected to Dark Heaven, but this is unconfirmed.

### Chapter 880

# Chapter 880

## Plot

As rumors about Prince Shangshan’s return and the Emperor’s heir spread through the capital, the Embroidered Uniform Guard begins arresting and interrogating ordinary people. Jeong Hogun arrives with an imperial order to stop the interrogations and release the prisoners, then tells another Thousand Captain that the rumors are true and that the court will soon announce them publicly. He orders the Guard to secure the palace and its surroundings.

Jin Taekyung learns that Ma Sanbao spread the rumors using information Taekyung had given him. The Emperor’s swift public confirmation may buy Shangshan time, but Taekyung, Hong Jin, and Hyuk Mujin consider the possibility that the upcoming imperial banquet could be used to eliminate dissidents.

## Continuity

- The Emperor has confirmed the rumors about Prince Shangshan’s return and the existence of an heir; the rumored pregnancy and the Emperor’s intentions remain unclear.
- Ma Sanbao spread the rumors across the capital using information Jin Taekyung gave him; the Emperor ordered the arrested rumor-spreaders released.
- The imperial court plans to announce the confirmed news at a banquet attended by Prince Shangshan and the civil and military officials; Hong Jin fears it could become a trap.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “Hongmen Banquet” for 鴻門宴, retaining its implication of a banquet that may conceal an assassination attempt.
### Chapter 881

# Chapter 881

## Plot

Ma Sanbao meets secretly with a dozen allies, using his disguise technique and Bone-Shrinking Technique to enter unnoticed. The group, made up of survivors of the earlier bloodshed and the faction they built in secret, signs a pact to overthrow the Emperor. Ma warns that the Emperor may be planning another purge and agrees with the Grand Secretary that the Emperor’s banquet is likely to bring the factions into open conflict.

Ma reassures the allies that they have sufficient grounds and strength for their undertaking, while withholding details of their preparations. He says the person they asked about is safe and predicts the young martial artist will help them; the martial artist’s master, he says, could change the balance.

## Continuity

- Ma Sanbao is a central figure in a covert faction formed by survivors of the imperial capital’s earlier bloodshed; they have signed a pact to overthrow the Emperor.
- Ma Sanbao and the Grand Secretary expect the Emperor’s announced banquet to become the point of confrontation between the factions.
- Ma Sanbao says the person his allies asked about is safe and that the young martial artist will help their faction; he believes the martial artist’s master could be decisive.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 연판장 as “a pact bearing their signatures” in this chapter.
- Retain “Hongmen Banquet” for 홍문연.

# Chapter 882

## Plot

As crowds and rival scholars gather outside the imperial palace, Taishan wanders off and startles everyone before apologizing and asking to leave. His companions catch up, present an official letter identifying them as the Blazing Flame Troupe invited to perform at the imperial banquet, and are admitted through the gate.

### Chapter 883

# Chapter 883

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang’s group enters the imperial palace’s Outer Palace under false identities as a circus troupe, with the East Depot having arranged their papers and disguises. The Divine Physician gives the group Energy-Dispersing Poison to conceal their martial skill during the Embroidered Uniform Guard’s screening; Jeok can conceal his aura without it.

Jeong Hogun discovers poison on a courtesan, who stabs herself with her hairpin and dies after confessing that she sought revenge for her family’s deaths twelve years earlier. Her companions are taken to prison. Jeong then turns to question Taishan, but a familiar young man approaches and calls him Hogun.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang’s group is inside the imperial palace’s Outer Palace, disguised as a circus troupe with identities arranged by the East Depot.
- The Divine Physician gave the group Energy-Dispersing Poison to disrupt their internal energy during screening; Jeok Cheongang can conceal his aura without it.
- Jeong Hogun is screening banquet attendees for possible threats. A courtesan who confessed to seeking revenge against the Emperor is dead, and her companions have been taken to prison.
- A young man familiar to Jeok Cheongang arrives and addresses Jeong Hogun as Hogun; the young man is not identified in this chapter.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 마희단 as “circus troupe.”
- Treat 거산 as Jeok Cheongang’s uncertain name variant for Taishan, not a confirmed separate person.
### Chapter 884

# Chapter 884

## Plot

Jin Taekyung rushes to the Embroidered Uniform Guard’s screening after hearing that his companions drew attention. He persuades Jeong Hogun that he is helping protect Prince Shangshan and the Emperor from a possible attack at the banquet, then publicly presents Taishan and the circus troupe as harmless performers from north of the Yangtze. A potbellied official—an ally Ma Sanbao had placed there—orders the troupe passed through, and Hogun lets them proceed. Jeok Cheongang privately claims he trusted Taekyung’s plan all along, despite having just called him crazy.

## Continuity

- Ma Sanbao placed an unobtrusive ally among the officials overseeing the palace screening; the ally helped clear Taekyung’s disguised companions.
- Taekyung persuaded Jeong Hogun to let the circus troupe pass by framing his intervention as protection for Prince Shangshan and the Emperor.
- Taekyung cannot currently use Qi Sense.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Geosan as Taishan’s uncertain name variant, not a confirmed separate person.

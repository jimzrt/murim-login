# Chapters 100–104

## Plot

Im Chunsoo learns that Sangdong Guild’s investigation of the Peace Guild failed, Hong Woojin disappeared, and the Security Team was defeated by Jin Taekyung. Taekyung interrogates the captured Hunters, confirms that Woojin was hired by Team Leader 1 and that Im Chunsoo directed the operation, then faces Chunsoo in person. Chunsoo tests him with ice spikes, but Taekyung counters with Fire Wall before Level 80 mage Kim Hwajong arrives. Hwajong’s former-instructor relationship with Chunsoo is revealed, along with Chunsoo’s deep fear and obedience toward him. Team Leader 1 conceals the encounter and formally warns the Security Team over its unauthorized escalation.

Taekyung buys a two-story house in Goyang for his family and plans to live there alone until Hayeon finishes her entrance exam. After moving out of Hope Goshiwon and logging into Murim, he discovers Seong Jinho emerging from the capsule inside the new house.

In Murim, Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin travel through a blizzard to Honju. They stay at the Phoenix Inn, where Mujin’s travel funds rapidly dwindle under Taekyung’s appetite. When martial artists ruin Taekyung’s chicken-and-corn soup and provoke him, he punches the first aggressor. The inn’s proprietress hears that the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi subdued six men, recognizes the name, and goes to meet him.

## Continuity

- Im Chunsoo is Sangdong Guild’s Level 75 A-rank ice-mage Guild Master; the failed Security Team operation against Taekyung was conducted under his special order.
- The Security Team’s six Hunters were defeated and captured, then released by Team Leader 1. Choi Byungil’s team faces formal discipline; the final punishment remains unknown.
- Team Leader 1 assesses Taekyung as a top-tier B-rank or possibly A-rank Hunter.
- Hong Woojin is a B-rank Familiar mage hired by Team Leader 1. The relationship between Woojin’s investigation and Sangdong’s operation remains unresolved.
- Kim Hwajong is a Level 80 mage, former Hunter Training Center instructor, and the trainer who traumatized Class 25 trainee Im Chunsoo. Why he arrived at the confrontation—and why he now works as a butler—remains unknown.
- Taekyung owns a two-story detached house in Goyang and intends it as his family’s home. Logout remains active, so he no longer needs the capsule for travel between worlds.
- Seong Jinho unexpectedly emerged from Taekyung’s capsule inside the new house. How and why he entered remains unknown.
- Taekyung’s current Quest requires him to deliver the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s Lunar New Year invitation to the Mount Heng Sword Sect, now led by Lee Seowol, his former accuser.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin are staying at the Phoenix Inn’s private residence in Honju. Wikyung gave Mujin fifty silver nyang for the journey; Mujin has five silver nyang left after paying half the lodging fee.
- Mukyung is a Peak master who uses a superficially learned heat-yang technique to warm Hyuk Mujin and considers enduring hunger martial training.
- The unnamed Phoenix Inn proprietress knows the name Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi and has gone to meet Taekyung after hearing about the fight.
- The three possible surveillance properties near Taekyung’s former home remain unidentified, as do the black Familiar’s immediate instructions and the final consequences of the Phoenix Inn fight.

# Chapter 105

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin subdue six armed men who attacked them at the Phoenix Inn. The attackers include two First Rate martial artists, bear crude running-horse tattoos, and appear to belong to an organized wandering-martial-artist group. Taekyung suspects they are mounted bandits.

Wolhwa appears at the Phoenix Inn and explains that dozens of mounted-bandit groups operate on the northern plateau and that Lee Cheonbaek made a serious mistake by hiring them. In a private room, she confirms that she is both the Phoenix Inn's proprietress and a courtesan, as well as the Lower District Sect's Shanxi Branch Leader.

Wolhwa learns that Taekyung is traveling to the Mount Heng Sword Sect and proposes that she accompany him. Taekyung has not yet accepted the proposal.

## Continuity

- The six armed men who attacked Taekyung and Mujin at the Phoenix Inn were subdued; all bore crude tattoos depicting a running horse, indicating membership in an organized group.
- Wolhwa stated that dozens of mounted-bandit groups operate on the northern plateau and that Lee Cheonbaek hired them during the conflict with the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- Wolhwa is the proprietress of the Phoenix Inn, a courtesan by profession, and the Lower District Sect's Shanxi Branch Leader.
- Wolhwa proposed accompanying Taekyung to the Mount Heng Sword Sect; Taekyung's response remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve Wolhwa's playful addresses to Taekyung as our Young Master, Young Master Jin, and Young Master Sleeping Dragon.
### Chapter 106

# Chapter 106

## Plot

Wolhwa revealed that the Lower District Sect had a secret pact with the Jin Family of Taiyuan to receive compensation for its wartime assistance against the Mount Heng Sword Sect. She wanted to collect that compensation before the Jin Family peacefully absorbed the sect and hoped to expand the Lower District Sect's influence into northern Shanxi. Taekyung initially refused to accompany her because he did not want to hinder Jin Wikyung's plans.

After Jin Mukyung cut down five expensive old pine trees while training, the private residence's bill rose from eighty silver nyang to one hundred and five nyang plus twenty-three iron coins when Wolhwa corrected the manager's undercharge. Unable to pay or obtain credit, Taekyung invited Wolhwa to travel with them to the Mount Heng Sword Sect, and she accepted.

After Taekyung left, Wolhwa's subordinate reported that the Lower District Sect had found no evidence explaining his transformation from a Third Rate wastrel into the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi. Wolhwa canceled the investigation, ordered a gag order concerning Taekyung, and resolved to leave for Mount Heng with no other subordinate.

## Continuity

- The Lower District Sect assisted the Jin Family of Taiyuan during the war with the Mount Heng Sword Sect under a secret pact promising compensation connected to Mount Heng's properties or other equivalent assets.
- Jin Wikyung is pursuing a quiet and amicable merger with the weakened Mount Heng Sword Sect rather than openly seizing its spoils.
- Wolhwa is Shanxi's foremost information merchant as well as the Lower District Sect's Shanxi Branch Leader and the Phoenix Inn's proprietress.
- Wolhwa accepted Taekyung's invitation to accompany Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin to the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- Wolhwa ordered the Lower District Sect to stop investigating Taekyung and to prevent its members from discussing the investigation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 초일류 as master beyond First Rate, while retaining First Rate for 일류.
- Render 진 소협 when Wolhwa addresses Jin Mukyung as Young Hero Jin; preserve Wolhwa's polite, lightly playful Young Master forms when addressing Taekyung.
- Render 외상 as put it on credit and 철전 as iron coins.
### Chapter 107

# Chapter 107

## Plot

Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa travel by carriage toward Mount Heng with a replacement coachman who is secretly a Level 50 First Rate martial artist and bodyguard. Mujin and Taekyung tease Mukyung about his unusually awkward and shy behavior around Wolhwa, while Mukyung formally thanks her for helping the Jin Family and Wolhwa reminds him that she has not yet received proper compensation.

Around midnight, the group takes shelter in an abandoned shrine. Wolhwa explains that the Red Wind Band, a powerful mounted-bandit group from the northern plateau, avoided the battle at Eight Spring Gorge, then attacked the weakened Mount Heng Sword Sect's main base after Lee Cheonbaek's forces withdrew. With unrest spreading through the region, Taekyung notices torches approaching through a thin snowstorm.

## Continuity

- Wolhwa's replacement coachman is a Level 50 First Rate martial artist who serves as both coachman and bodyguard and is likely affiliated with the Lower District Sect.
- Mukyung behaves unusually awkwardly and shyly around Wolhwa but formally thanks her for aiding the Jin Family; Wolhwa states that she still has not received proper compensation.
- The Red Wind Band is a rising power from the northern plateau led by a formidable martial artist who commands two hundred followers.
- The Red Wind Band watched the battle at Eight Spring Gorge from roughly four hours away, avoided joining it, then headed north to target the weakened Mount Heng Sword Sect's main base.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect won the subsequent two-day battle but lost its Young Sect Leader, and the sect's weakened condition has allowed wandering martial artists, bandits, mounted bandits, and dark-path figures to cause unrest.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa are sheltering overnight in an abandoned shrine after traveling by carriage, and unidentified torch-bearing people are approaching through the snow.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 적풍단 as Red Wind Band and 적풍단주 as Red Wind Band Leader.
- Render 관제묘 as Guandi Temple.
- Render 흑도 as dark-path figures.
### Chapter 108

# Chapter 108

## Plot

At an abandoned shrine, Jin Taekyung and Wolhwa encounter ten armed human traffickers led by Lee Sam, who are transporting the Five-Colored Ghosts as captives. The Five-Colored Ghosts are former subordinates of Jang Sam who claim to have quit banditry, but they were caught trying to steal the traffickers’ money pouches. Taekyung decides to free them despite their criminal past.

Jin Mukyung arrives and ends the confrontation almost instantly, crippling Lee Sam by breaking his limbs and destroying his dantian while the other men surrender. The rescued Five-Colored Ghosts reveal that the group they followed to a pleasure house was the Red Wind Band, which plans to leave at dawn and travel nonstop to Saneum. A messenger hawk carrying a small cylinder then arrives at the shrine.

## Continuity

- Ten armed human traffickers arrived at the abandoned shrine with the Five-Colored Ghosts bound as prisoners.
- The Five-Colored Ghosts are former subordinates of Jang Sam who had quit banditry but recently turned to theft and were caught stealing money pouches.
- Jin Taekyung freed the Five-Colored Ghosts after deciding former bandits were preferable to active human traffickers.
- Jin Mukyung overwhelmed the traffickers in seconds; Lee Sam survived with all four limbs broken and his dantian destroyed, while the rest surrendered.
- Lee Sam was previously a Level 25 martial artist who had once been close to First Rate; after Mukyung’s attack, his Level window displayed Level 2.
- The traffickers identified the mounted-bandit group they had followed as the Red Wind Band and said it planned to leave at dawn for Saneum, near Eung-hyeon and the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s headquarters.
- A messenger hawk carrying a small cylinder arrived at the shrine; the sender and message remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 오색귀 as Five-Colored Ghosts, with a footnote explaining the nickname.
- Render 이삼 as Lee Sam.
- Render 전서응 as messenger hawk and 대형 as Boss in the Five-Colored Ghosts’ address to Taekyung.
- Retain established renderings of 마적단 as mounted-bandit group, 적풍단 as Red Wind Band, and 산음 as Saneum.
### Chapter 109

# Chapter 109

## Plot

A messenger hawk from the Lower District Sect's Sakju Branch reports that approximately two hundred Red Wind Band members are moving south across the plateau. Wolhwa uses lethal interrogation to extract all available information from the captured mounted bandits.

Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Wolhwa abandon the carriage and ride toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect without resting, while a Lower District Sect member takes the Five-Colored Ghosts and surviving mounted bandits to a nearby branch.

The Red Wind Band Leader, Pung Yang, has already led the band through Datong and destroyed the Mount Heng Sword Sect's Datong Branch. He orders that no prisoners be taken and that the manor be burned, rejecting plateau traditions whenever they conflict with his commands.

The Mount Heng Sword Sect learns that the Red Wind Band may attack within one or two days. Having already lost nearly eighty percent of its strength in the war with the Jin Family of Taiyuan, the sect's senior figures despair until the main hall doors suddenly explode.

## Continuity

- The messenger hawk was sent by the Lower District Sect's Sakju Branch to Wolhwa with intelligence about the Red Wind Band.
- The Red Wind Band numbers at least approximately two hundred and is moving toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect after crossing Datong.
- Pung Yang is the Red Wind Band Leader and enforces his own orders above the mounted band's inherited plateau customs.
- Wolhwa is capable of ruthless, decisive violence when extracting information from hostile martial artists.
- Chunsam is a First Rate Lower District Sect martial artist who served as the group's carriage driver and obeyed Wolhwa's orders.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa are riding day and night toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect; the Lower District Sect member is transporting the Five-Colored Ghosts and surviving mounted bandits to a branch.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect's Datong Branch was destroyed with no survivors, and the sect has lost nearly eighty percent of its strength in the war with the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- The Quest difficulty has changed to Peak.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 추종향 as tracking scent, 대동 as Datong, 토호단 as Earth Tiger Band, 풍양 as Pung Yang, and 춘삼 as Chunsam.
- Render 철검대주 as Iron Sword Squad Leader, 대항산검문 as great Mount Heng Sword Sect, and 대동지부 as Datong Branch.
- Render 단주 and 단주님 as Leader and retain Peak for 절정, First Rate for 일류, and One Strike for 일격.
- Retain Young Master Jin for Wolhwa's 진 공자 and Young Hero Jin for her 진 소협 address to Mukyung.

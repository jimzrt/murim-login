# Chapters 115–119

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung enter the Mount Heng fortress after finding Cheol Mubaek critically wounded by Pung Yang’s Temporary Strength Pill. Wolhwa and Hyuk Mujin remain behind to protect Cheol while the Jin brothers attack the Red Wind Band. Although Mukyung initially dominates, Pung Yang consumes another pill, gains enough power to produce imperfect Sword Force and Body-Protecting Qi, and reverses the fight. He reveals that he obtained the Crimson Blood Twelve Swords, the Crimson Blood Cultivation Technique, and five Temporary Strength Pills from a hidden plateau tomb, killing his companions to keep the legacy.

Mukyung appears to break Pung Yang’s defensive qi, but concealed throwing knives leave him unconscious. Taekyung consumes Jopil’s Blazing Flame Divine Pill, gaining thirty years of Scorching Yang Qi and temporarily raising his internal energy to forty-five years. He outmaneuvers Pung Yang and wounds him with a dagger before his Body-Protecting Qi fully forms, but the divine pill’s energy runs wild and severely damages Taekyung. As Pung Yang’s Temporary Strength Pill begins to wear off, Lee Seowol and nine surviving Mount Heng martial artists make a last stand so Taekyung can escape with Mukyung.

Taekyung refuses to flee and attacks with One Annihilation, but Pung Yang destroys it, severely injures him, and captures him. When Pung Yang attempts to mutilate him, Taekyung summons the Unnamed Sword. Its Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron destroys Pung Yang’s Body-Protecting Qi, leaving the confrontation unresolved.

## Continuity

- Cheol Mubaek is alive but critically injured, with broken limbs and severe internal injuries; Wolhwa’s medicine provides only temporary support.
- Jin Mukyung is unconscious after Pung Yang’s five concealed throwing knives; his recovery is unresolved.
- Pung Yang reached the Peak realm through the Crimson Blood martial arts and heterodox cultivation in two years.
- Pung Yang has approximately seventy percent mastery of the Crimson Blood Twelve Sabers and can temporarily manifest imperfect Sword Force and powerful Body-Protecting Qi with a Temporary Strength Pill.
- Pung Yang’s Temporary Strength Pill began losing effect after roughly half a shichen. He retained one final pill but refused to take it because using three consecutively could endanger his life.
- Pung Yang still intends to capture both Jin brothers and obtain the Taiyuan Jin Family’s martial arts formulas.
- Taekyung consumed the Blazing Flame Divine Pill and gained the Scorching Yang Qi attribute, but its uncontrolled energy caused severe internal injury and a major drop in his stats. The Divine Pill Absorption Quest remains active, and his survival is uncertain.
- Taekyung’s iron spear was cut to less than half its length; he learned Pung Yang’s attack pattern before the final exchange.
- Lee Seowol and nine other surviving Mount Heng Sword Sect martial artists began a sacrificial last stand to protect Taekyung and Mukyung; their fate is unresolved.
- Taekyung’s Unnamed Sword destroyed Pung Yang’s Body-Protecting Qi at the endpoint. Taekyung’s and Pung Yang’s final conditions, and whether either survives, remain unresolved.
- The Peak Quest’s Lunar New Year invitation to the Jin Family remains the governing objective.

### Chapter 120

# Chapter 120

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's Unnamed Sword, empowered by Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, destroys Pung Yang's Body-Protecting Qi and pierces his chest. Pung Yang begs for his last Temporary Strength Pill, but Taekyung refuses, and Pung Yang dies.

Pung Yang's defeat completes the Temporary Strength Pill Quest, granting Taekyung five level-ups, massive EXP and Fame, and Full Recovery. Taekyung's Blazing Flame Divine Pill is fully absorbed, raising his internal energy to forty-five years and adding the Scorching Yang Qi attribute; he uses the reward medicines to treat the wounded Mount Heng martial artists.

The two-hour search finds twenty-five survivors including Lee Seowol, though five are unlikely to survive the day. The Mount Heng Sword Sect is effectively destroyed, but Seowol vows that it will survive. Taekyung delivers Jin Wikyung's invitation to Seowol, completing the Yesterday's Enemy, Today's Ally Quest.

## Continuity

- Pung Yang is dead; Jin Taekyung killed him after the Unnamed Sword's Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron destroyed his Body-Protecting Qi and pierced his chest.
- Pung Yang's death completed the Temporary Strength Pill Quest and granted Taekyung five level-ups, massive EXP and Fame, and the Full Recovery reward.
- Full Recovery healed Taekyung's external and internal injuries and stabilized his body.
- Taekyung fully absorbed the Blazing Flame Divine Pill; his internal energy is now forty-five years and has the Scorching Yang Qi attribute.
- The Quest rewards included thirty Superior Wound Medicines and thirty Ten-Year He Shouwu, which Taekyung used to treat the wounded.
- Jin Mukyung survived his fight with Pung Yang and is recovering.
- Cheol Mubaek and the other wounded Mount Heng martial artists are recovering after receiving treatment.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect, once a power that divided Shanxi with the Jin Family of Taiyuan, no longer exists in its former state.
- Twenty-five Mount Heng survivors remain, including Lee Seowol; five are unlikely to survive the day.
- Lee Seowol remains Sect Leader and vows to preserve the Mount Heng Sword Sect for those who died defending it.
- An unnamed woman from the Jin Family of Taiyuan with substantial medical knowledge is treating the wounded.
- Taekyung delivered an invitation from Jin Wikyung, the Lesser Family Head, to Lee Seowol.
- The Yesterday's Enemy, Today's Ally Quest was completed when the invitation was delivered.

# Chapter 121

After the battle, Jin Taekyung rests in a Mount Heng Sword Sect pavilion while Hyuk Mujin massages his shoulders and reports that Jin Mukyung and the other wounded are recovering. ### Chapter 122

# Chapter 122

## Plot

Wolhwa directs the Lower District Sect's Jeongyang and Honju branches in the aftermath of the battle. Forty-seven mounted bandits survived, but the available medicine can save only about thirty; the rest will die or later be sold into slavery. The Lower District Sect begins spreading a favorable rumor that the two Jin brothers defeated Pung Yang and rescued the Mount Heng Sword Sect, expecting the Jin Family of Taiyuan to force the remaining bandits back to the plateau within five days.

Wolhwa investigates Pung Yang's sudden increase in strength and reflects on her Master's advice to observe people who defy prediction. She then visits Jin Taekyung's pavilion, where Hyuk Mujin reports that Taekyung has been muttering incomprehensible modern-world terms. Hearing Taekyung repeat “school lunch, high schooler, clank, clank,” Wolhwa withdraws, once again judging him completely unpredictable.

Two days later, Taekyung has spent most of his time learning to control his newly acquired Scorching Yang Qi. Lee Seowol has not returned after proposing marriage, and Taekyung decides to reject her because she is seventeen, the proposal is political, and he loves Song Song. Jin Mukyung has recovered enough to move independently; when Taekyung reveals that the proposal included the Blood Wolf Sword Technique, Blood Wolf Footwork, and Shura Annihilating Fist, Mukyung immediately orders him to marry her.

## Continuity

- Wolhwa can issue mobilization orders to more than thirty Lower District Sect branches across Shanxi Province.
- Forty-seven mounted bandits survived the battle; the Lower District Sect can save only about thirty with its current medicine, and the survivors will ultimately be sold as labor or arena slaves.
- The Lower District Sect is spreading the claim that Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung defeated Pung Yang and rescued the Mount Heng Sword Sect so the remaining mounted bandits will withdraw.
- Two days have passed since Lee Seowol proposed marriage, and she has not returned; Taekyung has decided to reject the proposal.
- Taekyung loves Song Song and considers marriage to Lee Seowol impossible despite the offered martial arts.
- Jin Mukyung recovered sufficiently after his injuries to move around without assistance.
- Taekyung is spending most of his time learning to control his newly acquired Scorching Yang Qi.

- Render 급식 as school lunch, 고딩 as high schooler, and 철컹, 철컹 as clank, clank; preserve the implication that this talk about a high schooler could lead to imprisonment in a footnote.

### Chapter 123

# Chapter 123

## Plot

Cheol Mubaek lies severely injured while Lee Seowol cares for him. He recounts how he and her father, Lee Cheonbaek, first met after Cheonbaek searched the mountains for three months; they fought before becoming lifelong friends. Cheol urges Seowol to pursue happiness and marriage, but she insists that rebuilding the Mount Heng Sword Sect is what she wants. He reveals that he entrusted her with the Shura Annihilating Fist manual despite its single-successor and worthy-only transmission traditions, believing the Heaven Shaking Sword or Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi could protect her.

Jin Mukyung violently urges Taekyung to marry Seowol so he can obtain the Shura Annihilating Fist, Blood Wolf Sword Technique, and Blood Wolf Footwork. Taekyung argues that the Shura Annihilating Fist, while still an outstanding Peak martial art, is an old technique whose former status does not make it one of the world's current ten greatest fist techniques, and rejects the marriage. He then reveals that Jopil left behind the Supreme Peak martial art Flame Divine Palm, leaving Mukyung stunned.

## Continuity

- Cheol Mubaek has broken limbs and serious internal injuries and requires at least four months of recuperation.
- Cheol Mubaek is the ninth-generation successor of the Shura Annihilating Fist and broke its single-successor, worthy-only transmission tradition by giving the manual to Lee Seowol.
- Cheol Mubaek and Lee Cheonbaek first met more than thirty years ago after Cheonbaek searched the mountain range for three months; they fought before becoming close friends.
- The Shura Annihilating Fist was one of the world's ten greatest fist techniques two hundred years ago, but its martial lineage was believed to have ended and it is no longer regarded as a current top-ten technique.
- Jopil is dead and left behind the Supreme Peak martial art Flame Divine Palm.
- Cheol Mubaek believes the Heaven Shaking Sword and Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi could protect Seowol if she enters a political marriage; he favors the Heaven Shaking Sword because of Taekyung's old reputation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일인전승 as “single successor” and 비인부전 as “transmission only to the worthy.”
- Render 구 대 계승자 as “ninth-generation successor” and 천하십대권법 as “the ten greatest fist techniques in the world.”
- Retain “Uncle Cheol,” “Seowol,” and “hyung” for the established familial and address conventions.
### Chapter 124

# Chapter 124

## Plot

Jin Taekyung considers Lee Seowol's offer of three Supreme Peak martial arts and reveals that the Flame Divine Palm manual came from the dead Jopil, who claimed to be its nineteenth-generation successor. Jin Mukyung warns that possessing another sect's secret art could make Taekyung and the Jin Family thieves in the eyes of the Murim. He explains that the Flame Divine Palm belonged to the Fire King, a Supreme Peak master among the world's twenty greatest experts, whose last known appearance was forty years ago after he killed a thousand Demonic Cult members at Mount Jiuhua. The Fire King's survival is unknown, and the Fire Gate Clan has only a single successor.

Jin Mukyung recovers enough from his Internal Injuries to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan, though he is not fully healed. Hyuk Mujin accompanies the group and complains about leaving home. Wolhwa sees them off, explains that she plans to tour northern Shanxi now that it has become an open market, and says she intends to maintain a close relationship with the Jin Family. After her carriage departs, Lee Seowol appears and addresses Taekyung as Benefactor.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung possesses the Flame Divine Palm manual, a Supreme Peak secret technique of the Fire Gate Clan restricted to owners of Scorching Yang Qi.
- Jopil claimed to be the nineteenth-generation successor of the Flame Divine Palm; the truth of his claim and how he obtained the manual remain uncertain.
- The Fire King was already an old man forty years before this chapter, last appeared after killing a thousand Demonic Cult members over four days and nights at Mount Jiuhua, and may still be alive.
- The Fire Gate Clan follows a single-successor tradition.
- Jin Mukyung has recovered enough from Pung Yang's Internal Injuries to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan but is not yet fully recovered.
- Wolhwa plans to tour northern Shanxi and intends to maintain a close relationship between the Lower District Sect and the Jin Family.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 진무보법 as Jin Family's Manoeuvre Technique.
- Render 열화문 as Fire Gate Clan, 안휘성 as Anhui Province, 구화산 as Mount Jiuhua, and 십구 대 계승자 as nineteenth-generation successor.
- Preserve the Samsung/Samseong pun by distinguishing Samsung from Three Saints in a footnote.

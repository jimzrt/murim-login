# Chapters 265–269

## Plot

The Blood Lord regenerates from the wounds inflicted by Jeok Cheongang’s Dance of the Fire God and Demon, revealing that he was responsible for the war that followed Taekyung’s killing of Jin Baekyang. With Jeok unconscious, Taekyung and Cheongpung fight him. Taekyung exhausts his final One Annihilation, while Cheongpung overcomes his fear and chooses to fight rather than retreat. As the Blood Lord is about to kill them, Jongni Chu reveals himself as Sword Saint Mae Jonghak, Cheongpung’s grandfather.

Mae, who has attained higher enlightenment and Returned to Youth, attacks with a divine Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords technique. Taekyung restrains the Blood Lord long enough for Mae to sever his remaining arm, but the Blood Lord escapes through black radiance while carrying the Green Jade Buddha Staff. The Life-or-Death Crisis completes, fully healing Taekyung and granting him two levels.

After the Shaolin bloodbath, Henan Province falls into turmoil as the orthodox factions mobilize. Mae treats Taekyung’s allies and publicly confirms his identity. Jeok remains unconscious, while Cheongpung begins recovering. Mae and Taekyung discuss whether Dark Heaven is an independent force, a smokescreen, or a subordinate organization of the Demonic Cult. The Blood Lord, the Temporary Strength Pill, and the Exploding Blood Demonic Art bear the Cult’s scent, but his sorcery is darker and unfamiliar. Hong Dao’s letter identifies Taekyung as the Morning Star who will drive away the darkness. Taekyung returns to Jeok’s room, orders the guards to admit no one until morning, and logs out to rest.

## Continuity

- Sword Saint Mae Jonghak is publicly revealed as Jongni Chu, Cheongpung’s grandfather. He reached Great Completion, attained higher enlightenment, and unexpectedly Returned to Youth.
- Mae’s restored sword can perform a divine version of the Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords.
- The Blood Lord escaped through black radiance with the Green Jade Buddha Staff after losing both arms and sustaining severe sword wounds. He possesses extraordinary regeneration and now vows to personally kill Mae, Jeok, Cheongpung, and Taekyung.
- The Blood Lord is the cherished Disciple of an unidentified “that person.” His identity, rank, destination, and connection to the Green Jade Buddha Staff remain unknown.
- The Life-or-Death Crisis completed after the Blood Lord’s escape, restoring Taekyung’s body and granting him enormous EXP and two level-ups.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious after exhausting himself with the Dance of the Fire God and Demon. The Luoyang Strange Physician confirmed that his life is not currently in danger but found no treatment.
- Cheongpung suffered internal injuries but is recovering and can move; he wanted to visit Taekyung as soon as he awoke.
- Song Ho and hundreds of martial artists arrived after the Shaolin massacre, while Peng Cheolhu handled the Shaolin response. Henan’s orthodox leaders are gathering as a larger war appears imminent.
- Dark Heaven’s relationship with the Demonic Cult is unresolved. The Blood Lord’s arts resemble the Cult’s, but his final sorcery is darker and unlike anything Mae recognizes.
- Hong Dao’s letter names Taekyung the Morning Star who will drive away the darkness.
- Taekyung regards Jeok as both his Master and the equivalent of a close grandfather. He has logged out to rest until morning.

- Retain **Blood Lord**, **Green Jade Buddha Staff**, **Sword Saint**, **Returned to Youth**, **Great Completion**, **Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords**, **Dance of the Fire God and Demon**, **Life-or-Death Crisis**, **Temporary Strength Pill**, and **Exploding Blood Demonic Art**.
- Render **술사** as **sorcerer** and **술법** as **sorcery**, distinct from movement techniques and lightness skills.
- Use **Luoyang Strange Physician** for 낙양괴의 and **Morning Star** for 신성.
- Preserve Mae’s calm, warm manner toward Cheongpung and Taekyung, and the Blood Lord’s confident, lightly mocking combat tone.

# Chapter 270

## Plot

After completing Logout, Jin Taekyung wakes in the modern world after sleeping for forty-two hours. His mother and Hayeon panic, scold him, and beat his back, while Hayeon reveals that she already bought the most expensive massage chair with his card. Taekyung lies about waking during the night and invents a history of sleeping through high school to discourage further questions.

Team Leader Choi visits to take Taekyung to the Peace Guild's new-member workshop, which is actually a six-night, seven-day operation at an A-rank Gate. More than twenty rookie B-rank Hunters face a Stone Golem. Song Song greatly strengthens them with buff magic, Butler Kim prevents them from relying on outside help, Team Leader Choi and Song Song are lifted into the air, and Im Kkeokjeong leads the tank formation. After a five-hour raid, the new Hunters defeat the Stone Golem.

More than ten Iron Golems then approach the exhausted group. When the new Guild members beg for help, Team Leader Choi refuses, and Jin Taekyung appears in a red flash to split one Iron Golem cleanly in half. He grins and remarks that mob farming is extremely profitable.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has returned to the modern world after more than a year in Murim and slept for forty-two hours after Logout.
- The Peace Guild is conducting a six-night, seven-day new-member workshop at an A-rank Gate.
- Song Song's buff magic is powerful enough to be mistaken for an A-rank healer's work.
- Butler Kim forces the rookie Guild members to fight without relying entirely on assistance.
- Im Kkeokjeong leads the rookie Hunters' tank formation during the Stone Golem raid.
- The rookie Hunters defeated a Stone Golem after a five-hour raid before a group of more than ten Iron Golems appeared.
- Jin Taekyung killed one of the approaching Iron Golems with a red-flashing attack.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 골렘 as Golem, 스톤 골렘 as Stone Golem, and 아이언 골렘 as Iron Golem.
- Retain Haste, Strength, Healing, and Fire Wall as the named spell renderings.
- Render 6박 7일 as six nights and seven days.
- Render 몰이 사냥 개꿀 as “Mob farming is fucking sweet,” preserving Taekyung's profane gamer voice.
### Chapter 271

# Chapter 271

## Plot

Jin Taekyung effortlessly kills more than ten Level 95 Iron Golems in a few minutes, using the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, Flame Divine Palm, and seventh-stage Fire Dragon Divine Spear. Team Leader Choi becomes interested in Taekyung's bound White Flame spear and admits that he and Butler Kim suspect Taekyung's unprecedented second awakening but will not press him about his secrets; Choi says he intends to collect Taekyung's debt with interest. The Peace Guild's six-night, seven-day workshop raid ends after only three days because Taekyung has eliminated every available Golem, including the boss monster. Im Kkeokjeong has abruptly left the raid in his gear because of an unspecified urgent matter, prompting Taekyung to message him about having drinks together. Afterward, Taekyung receives an unexpected call from his former Garam Middle School classmate Park Jihoon.

## Continuity

- Taekyung can destroy Level 95 Iron Golems with extreme ease and has soloed the workshop Gate's boss monster.
- White Flame is a bound spear made from Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron; only Taekyung can use it, and it is the only item that crosses between his separate Murim and modern-world inventories.
- Team Leader Choi believes Taekyung can be described as an unprecedented second-awakened Hunter and will not investigate the deeper secret behind his strength; Butler Kim shares this view.
- Team Leader Choi intends to collect the debt Taekyung owes him with interest, but his reason for helping Taekyung remains unstated.
- The Peace Guild workshop raid ended after three days instead of the planned six nights and seven days because Taekyung cleared the available monsters.
- Im Kkeokjeong left the raid abruptly without changing out of his equipment because of an unspecified urgent matter.
- Park Jihoon unexpectedly called Taekyung after months without contact following their chance meeting when Taekyung was buying a house.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 국정원 as NIS, South Korea's National Intelligence Service.
- Render 귀속 아이템 as bound item.
- Retain White Flame for 백염.
- Render Choi's debt remark as collecting the debt in full—with interest.

- Jihoon has been informed that higher-ups find Jin Taekyung irritating and has begun arranging an A-Rank raid at the earliest available date in connection with the Taekyung matter.

### Chapter 273

# Chapter 273

## Plot

The Peace Guild has rapidly expanded to twenty B-rank Hunters and thirty C-rank-or-lower Hunters. Team Leader Choi asks Taekyung to take charge of training the talented but inexperienced rookies, while Taekyung would rather clear A-rank Gates or spend the time with his family. Choi reveals that all A-rank Gates are booked for at least a week and offers a 200 percent monthly performance bonus for raiding and teaching.

The System generates the Quest It Was Too Much Money to Say “I’m Not Doing It!”, and Taekyung tacitly accepts it. Choi reserves several B-rank Gates, scheduling three raids in one day. During the rookies’ second raid, the Lycanthropes overwhelm their formation until Kim Jinsoo kills one, restores the formation, and directs the others. Taekyung then intervenes and kills the remaining monsters, praises Jinsoo, and disciplines three rookies for their failures.

A newspaper in Choi’s office reports the death of Hunter Jung Hyunwoo and expresses Myeongdong Guild’s regret. After Jinsoo leaves to rest before the next raid, Choi urgently calls Taekyung and orders him to come to Ilsan Hospital; the reason is not yet given.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has revealed its fangs in Murim, while Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious.
- Thirty days in the modern world correspond to only three hours in Murim, giving Taekyung time to remain in Korea while Jeok recovers.
- Peace Guild rookies are now being trained through a rotation involving Taekyung, Butler Kim, and Song-i; Im Kkeokjeong has not yet been reached.
- Kim Jinsoo graduated first in the training camp’s B-rank course and was the first person to destroy a Golem core during the preceding raid.
- Taekyung has accepted the System Quest requiring him to train the rookies and is scheduled to continue with B-rank raids.
- Team Leader Choi has urgently summoned Taekyung to Ilsan Hospital for an unexplained reason.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 선배님 as Senior in Kim Jinsoo’s address to Taekyung.
- Render 피리 부는 사나이들 as the Pied Pipers, preserving Taekyung’s mocking nickname for the two rookies who scream and attract more monsters.
- Render 안 해! 라고 하기에는 너무나 큰돈이었다 as It Was Too Much Money to Say “I’m Not Doing It!”
### Chapter 274

# Chapter 274

## Plot

Team Leader Choi urgently summons Jin Taekyung to Ilsan Hospital, where Taekyung learns that Im Kkeokjeong was badly injured after getting into a drunken fight with three unidentified Black Hunters; his arm was severed below the elbow, and all three attackers fled. Butler Kim explains that Black Hunters are unregistered Awakened people who received systematic Hunter training, while Team Leader Choi identifies Guilds as the source of such hidden weapons and warns Taekyung that a bothersome person or Guild can simply be erased.

## Continuity

- Im Kkeokjeong was attacked after drinking alone and is hospitalized in a Special Treatment Ward with severe injuries, including the loss of his arm below the elbow.
- The three attackers are Korean but have no identities matching any Awakened person or Hunter registered in Korea; they fled before being caught.
- Black Hunters are unregistered Awakened people who have received systematic training comparable to a Hunter’s training.
- Team Leader Choi states that Guilds cultivate or employ Black Hunters as concealed weapons and can eliminate troublesome people or rival Guilds.
- Jin Taekyung appoints Kim Jinsoo acting team leader for two hours and orders the rookies not to begin the next raid until he returns.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 블랙 헌터 as Black Hunter.
- Render 미등록자 as unregistered Awakened person.
- Render 특수 치료 병동 as Special Treatment Ward.

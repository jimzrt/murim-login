## Previous block summary

# Chapters 575–579

## Plot

Jin Taekyung kills the Lv. 140 King of the Black Sea Kraken, saving Busan survivors but failing to identify the humans who empowered it. As rescue efforts continue, President Baek Hanseong seeks to bring Taekyung and Choi Minwoo into his political camp. Choi instead leaves to meet Song Cheonwoo, who warns that Go Jun has prepared a trap involving Choi’s maternal grandfather, Cheon Taemin.

Inside the Peace Guild’s Yeti’s Winter Range Gate, Song reveals that Taemin suddenly collapsed more than twenty years ago and was secretly kept alive while Lee Jungryong and Song concealed his condition, conducted experiments, and purged informed aides. Song claims Taemin is still alive and may be in Ares Guild’s restricted Area A, though he cannot confirm the location. Before Choi can verify the story, Song’s Yeti’s Necklace draws hundreds of yetis into an ambush. Kim Hwajong diverts the monsters while Choi fights Song.

Choi defeats Song with Hero’s Soul and keeps him alive as a witness. Song explains that Go Jun seized his children, forcing him to attack Choi, and confirms that Taemin remains alive but unconscious. After receiving treatment, Song uses a potion-enhanced burst of strength to escape into a crevasse, leaving his whereabouts unknown.

## Continuity

- The Kraken is dead, but the humans who empowered and released it—and whether they deliberately caused the Monster Wave—remain unidentified.
- More than one thousand Mermen remain around Haeundae and Gwangalli while the Peace Guild and other forces contain Busan’s disaster.
- Cheon Taemin collapsed more than twenty years ago and has remained unconscious; the cause and the events surrounding his condition are unknown.
- Song Cheonwoo and Lee Jungryong concealed Taemin’s condition, experimented on him, and purged aides who knew the truth. Song survived by accepting the European regional director position.
- Song claims Taemin is alive and may be held in Ares Guild headquarters’ Area A, but Area A remains an unconfirmed location.
- Go Jun has seized Song’s children and is preparing a separate trap involving Choi’s maternal grandfather.
- Choi defeated Song and preserved him as a witness, but Song escaped into a crevasse after being treated.
- Kim Hwajong remains Choi’s loyal butler and considers himself Choi’s only family.

### Chapter 580

# Chapter 580

## Plot

Go Se-won learns that Go Jun secretly sent Kim Ho-jung to Busan and discovers that Go Jun used an S-grade Magic Gem to trigger the Monster Wave artificially. Go Jun intends to kill Choi Minwoo by forcing Song Cheonwoo, whose family remains hostage, into attacking him. Song chooses that course rather than risk his family's death, falls into an abyss, and is killed when an unidentified monster crushes his body and drinks his blood; the object he had kept unused in his pocket then releases darkness that becomes light.

## Continuity

- Go Jun used an S-grade Magic Gem to artificially cause the Busan Monster Wave and dispatched Kim Ho-jung to Busan on a separate assignment.
- Go Jun intends to kill Choi Minwoo by exploiting Song Cheonwoo's hostage-bound family and may also be relying on another unidentified being.
- Song Cheonwoo chose to attack Choi Minwoo because Go Jun threatened to eliminate his family if Song survived and escaped.
- Song Cheonwoo fell into an abyss and was killed by an unidentified monster that crushed his body and drank his blood.
- The object Song Cheonwoo had kept unused in his pocket was destroyed or activated at the end of his fall, causing darkness to burst forth as light.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Named Monster for 네임드 몬스터 and S-grade Magic Gem for S급 마정석.
- Use Will for 의지 when referring to Song Cheonwoo's final exertion.
### Chapter 581

# Chapter 581

## Plot

Choi Minwoo and Kim Hwajong recognize that Song Cheonwoo's attack was a trap. Choi orders an attempt to recover Song's body so the evidence can be used against Go Jun, then realizes that the Yeti's Winter Range Gate has opened a crevasse into the modern world.

The resulting Monster Wave connects the snowy Gate to the city beyond it. Kim Hwajong steps in front of Choi Minwoo as the mana-blackened snowstorm and abyss emerge.

At a Pyeongchang restaurant, an unnamed freelance Hunter team discusses its failed raid and the danger of the nearby Gate. A female Hunter recognizes Choi Minwoo in a news photograph, but the Yeti's Winter Range collapses before they can investigate, forcing the Team Leader to order an immediate report to the Hunter Association, Peace Guild, or Jin Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo orders an attempt to recover Song Cheonwoo's body and intends to use the evidence to unseat Go Jun from Ares Guild leadership.
- Choi Minwoo identifies Song Cheonwoo's assassination attempt as a trap before the Yeti's Winter Range Gate tears open into the modern world.
- The B-grade Yeti's Winter Range Gate in Pyeongchang has begun a Monster Wave that connects the Gate to the modern world; Choi Minwoo and Kim Hwajong are present at its emergence.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 582

# Chapter 582

## Plot

A gigantic Behemoth emerges from the Pyeongchang Yeti's Winter Range Gate, destroys the Gate control station, and devours more than two hundred yetis. Its Fear paralyzes the witnesses until Choi Minwoo's Hero's Soul breaks its influence. Choi, Kim Hwajong, and twenty Peace Guild Hunters form an attack against the still-hungry monster to protect more than a thousand civilians below the mountain.

In Busan, the narrator exterminates more than five hundred fleeing Mermen and discovers a family killed inside a car, intensifying his rage. After confirming the area is clear, he continues toward survivors and sees a broadcast reporting that Peace Guild Hunter Choi Minwoo has been caught in the Pyeongchang Monster Wave.

## Continuity

- Behemoth has emerged from the Pyeongchang Yeti's Winter Range Gate, emits Fear, speaks Demon Realm language, and remains hungry after devouring more than two hundred yetis.
- Choi Minwoo, Kim Hwajong, and twenty elite Peace Guild Hunters have confronted Behemoth to protect more than one thousand civilians; the battle's outcome is unresolved.
- The narrator has eliminated the Mermen gathered in Busan and continues searching for people still awaiting rescue after finding a family dead inside a car.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Behemoth for 베히모스 and Behemos for the alternate spelling 베헤모스.
- Use Hero's Soul for 영웅의 혼, White Flame for 백염, Fire Dragon's Single Tail for 화룡일미, and Flame Divine Palm for 화염신장.
- Keep the narrator's brutal, grief-stricken first-person register when describing the Mermen massacre and civilian deaths.
### Chapter 583

# Chapter 583

## Plot

Choi Minwoo learns that a Monster Wave has opened at Pyeongchang and tries to reach the battle from Busan by Teleport, but the exhausted local mages cannot safely cross the distance. While he restores his strength, Behemoth overwhelms the Peace Guild force: more than half of the twenty-two Hunters die, and Choi severs two of Behemoth's four forelegs before collapsing from accumulated injuries and exhaustion.

Kim Hwajong follows Choi into the fog, loses his left arm while rescuing him, and returns Choi to the surviving Hunters. He orders them to evacuate with the unconscious Choi while he remains behind alone to confront Behemoth with hellfire.

## Continuity

- Behemoth has killed more than half of the twenty-two Peace Guild Hunters at Pyeongchang, while Choi Minwoo is unconscious after severely injuring the monster.
- Kim Hwajong lost his left arm rescuing Choi Minwoo and stayed behind to face Behemoth after ordering the surviving Hunters to withdraw with Choi.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Guild Master for 길드장님 when the Peace Guild Hunters address Kim Hwajong, and Hell Fire for 헬 파이어.
### Chapter 584

# Chapter 584

## Plot

Kim Hwajong withstands Behemoth's attacks, dispels its black mist, and uses Flame Cannon to destroy the monster's already-wounded forelegs, forcing it to kneel and preventing it from pursuing Choi Minwoo. Behemoth then launches Hwajong into solid rock, gravely injuring him. As he faces death, memories reveal how Cheon Taemin recruited him, how he became Butler Kim and raised Minwoo, and how Minwoo recently thanked him for becoming his only family. Behemoth prepares to consume him, but a sudden flash erupts from midair before the attack lands.

## Continuity

- Kim Hwajong used Hell Fire and Flame Cannon to render Behemoth's two damaged forelegs unusable, forcing the monster to kneel, but Behemoth gravely injured him afterward.
- Kim Hwajong was recruited by Cheon Taemin into the Peace Guild alongside Jungryong and Cheonwoo, later became Butler Kim, raised Choi Minwoo after his parents died, and served as Guild Master of the Peace Guild.
- Choi Minwoo considers Kim Hwajong his only family and thanked him for taking that role before the Pyeongchang confrontation.
- Kim Hwajong is alive at the end of the chapter but faces imminent death, and an unexplained flash interrupts Behemoth's attack.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Hellfire Mage for 겁화의 마법사, distinct from Hell Fire for 헬 파이어.
- Retain Young Master for 도련님 and Butler Kim for 김 집사.
- Render 형님 as hyung in Cheon Taemin and Kim Hwajong's exchange.
- Use Magic Staff, Fire Wave, Blaze, Fire Shield, and Flame Cannon for the named spells and weapon in this chapter.

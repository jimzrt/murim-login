## Previous block summary

# Chapters 475–479

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang, Mungyeong, and Cheongpung coordinate an all-out raid against the Mutated Water God Dragon. The dragon repeatedly fires Water Breath, grows stronger while its pupils are black, and devastates the battlefield. Taekyung uses White Flame, Heavenly Strike, and summoned spears to exploit gaps in its scales while the others tear through its body. He disappears just before the dragon’s final Water Breath and kills it by driving his spear through the back of its neck.

As it dies, the dragon regains its reason and transfers a fragmented Memory Fragment covering five hundred years. It had been Dongting Lake’s benevolent Two-Horned Beast, ruling the lake and the Yangtze while pursuing ascension. When a Gate or enormous rift began emitting mana and ominous energy, demonic qi mutated the lake’s fish. The dragon fought the creatures, then sealed the rift for seven days and nights by absorbing most of the demonic qi, sacrificing its mind and becoming an evil beast.

Taekyung recognizes Honglan at the corruption site and finds her faintly scented silver hairpin in his own hair. The dragon thanks him, gives him its purified Origin Essence, and dies peacefully. Taekyung identifies Honglan as the “flower snake” responsible for corrupting the dragon and using it to kill many people. Meanwhile, Honglan enthralls Officer Song aboard a military ship and orders him to change its destination. Taekyung completes the Corrupted Spirit Beast Surprise Quest, gaining substantial EXP, Fame, and two Levels.

## Continuity

- The Mutated Water God Dragon is dead. Its restored consciousness revealed that it was originally Dongting Lake’s benevolent Two-Horned Beast.
- The dragon sacrificed its intelligence to contain the Gate or rift’s demonic qi, becoming an evil beast in the process.
- Taekyung received the dragon’s purified Origin Essence, known to humans as an inner core.
- Honglan corrupted the dragon and used it to cause the Dongting Lake and Hubei tragedies. Her motives and full involvement remain unknown.
- Honglan’s silver hairpin carries a faint scent and links her to the corruption site.
- Honglan can seize a person’s emotions and soul, enthralling and controlling them. Officer Song, commander of the military ship carrying her, is currently under her command.
- The Corrupted Spirit Beast Surprise Quest is complete; Taekyung received EXP, Fame, and two Levels.
- The Gate’s creator, purpose, and connection to demonic qi and Dark Heaven remain unresolved.
- The Dongting Fisherman’s exact role in Dark Heaven, the shared Arch Lich/Dark Heaven symbols, the destruction of Donghu Stronghold and the Yangtze River Channel League strongholds, and the missing Moving Formation traces remain unresolved.

### Chapter 480

# Chapter 480

## Plot

Mungyeong treats Gwak Bongchul, an elderly boatman who was unconscious for two days after witnessing the Dongting Lake spirit's rampage. As Gwak's memories return, he recalls the thunder, roar, and massive being that appeared over the lake, along with the deaths and destruction that followed.

Mungyeong calms Gwak with his internal energy and orders him to erase everything he saw, heard, and experienced. He instructs Gwak to accept a cover story in which he was summoned to Dongting Lake with government troops, collapsed from illness, and saw neither passengers nor the lake spirit. Mungyeong warns that the Hubei City Lord may silence him to prevent panic, then advises him to sell his boat and remain quiet before sending him away with a decoction.

## Continuity

- Gwak Bongchul is an elderly boatman from the Wuhan and Dongting Lake area who survived the Dongting Lake spirit's rampage and awakened after two days in Mungyeong's clinic.
- Gwak's recovered memories include the lake spirit's enraged roar, the deaths of thousands, blood and corpses in the Yangtze and Dongting Lake, and darkness falling over the surrounding streets.
- Mungyeong has instructed Gwak to forget the incident and adopt a false account that he collapsed after being summoned to Dongting Lake with government troops; the success of this memory alteration is not established.
- Mungyeong believes disclosure could lead the Hubei City Lord to eliminate witnesses and advises Gwak to sell his boat and keep silent.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain medical apprentice for 의생 and 의원, and render the Dongting Lake 신령 as divine spirit to distinguish it from the established Mountain Spirit rendering.
- Preserve Gwak Bongchul's rustic, deferential speech and Mungyeong's calm, courteous, quietly coercive medical voice.

# Chapter 481

## Plot

Mungyeong reflects on the boatman's protection and compensation, then overhears clinic staff repeating the authorities' fabricated account that the captured dark-path figures caused the Hubei incidents. Rumors instead suggest the arrests conceal a greater truth, but Physician Song pressures the young medical apprentices to stop discussing them and praise the Emperor, the City Lord, and Jin Taekyung.

At the honored estate, Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung's filthy master-and-Disciple quarrel continues until Mungyeong joins in, cuts Taekyung's clothes with a sword, and discusses the boatman's recovery and the Dongting Fisherman's severe injuries. Taekyung realizes he forgot to tell Mungyeong that Honglan corrupted the imugi, and Gung Gibang arrives claiming to have found a trace of her.

## Continuity

- The boatman has regained consciousness without unusual symptoms, understands what is said to him, and is being protected through Mungyeong's arrangement with the Hubei City Lord; a money pouch has been left for him as compensation.
- The Dongting Fisherman survived his encounter with the Water God Dragon at Donghu Stronghold but has crushed limbs, substantial Internal Injury, and serious Fear exposure; the City Lord's elixir is expected to aid his recovery.
- Gung Gibang has found a trace of Honglan after her whereabouts remained unknown.

# Chapter 482

## Plot

Gung Gibang reports that the military vessel on which Honglan was last seen has been found at Red Cliffs. Ninety-four of the ninety-six people aboard, excluding Honglan, stabbed themselves in the throat and died smiling, while one survivor remains.

The sole survivor is Song Ho, a Hubei military officer whose soul has been thoroughly seized. Although Mungyeong declares him effectively beyond hope, Honglan suddenly speaks through his body, confirming that she can remotely control enthralled people.

Honglan reveals that Dark Heaven has many eyes and ears, says Blood Lord reported on Taekyung, and identifies herself as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress before refusing to disclose her location.

## Continuity

- Honglan escaped from the military vessel after using the Soul-Seizing Technique to force ninety-four passengers and crew members to commit suicide.
- The surviving officer Song Ho remains physically present but mentally incapacitated, and Honglan can speak through his body from an unknown distant location.
- Honglan identifies herself as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and confirms her connection to Dark Heaven's intelligence network; her exact motives and location remain unknown.

### Chapter 484

# Chapter 484

## Plot

Taekyung's group travels by boat to the site remembered by the Water God Dragon. Cheongpung and Perfected Being Hyeongong had cut through a cliff blocking the route, and the party passes heavy security from Wudang, the Zhuge Clan, and the Beggars' Sect. Hubei's City Lord is concealing the imugi disaster while cooperating with the Murim to suppress local dark-path forces and redirect public anger toward Dark Heaven. Zhuge Feng welcomes them, reveals a device he designed as a child, and shows that the site has been enclosed and drained for investigation. Taekyung discovers a massive fissure in the cliff and triggers an ominous System notification when he touches it.

## Continuity

- The Hubei authorities are suppressing public knowledge of the Water God Dragon's rampage and have used local dark-path figures as scapegoats, increasing public anger and wariness toward Dark Heaven.
- Zhuge Feng's team has located the Water God Dragon's remembered site, enclosed the area with stone, drained the water, and exposed a large fissure running through the ancient cliff.
- Taekyung touched the exposed fissure at the Water God Dragon's site and received an ominous System notification.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Taekyung's profane contemporary comic voice, including Fire Pokémon, Origami Man, and Great Government War.
- Render 대장군 as Great General and 정관대전 as Great Government War.
- Continue rendering 노 선배님 as Senior and preserve Jeok Cheongang's blunt, threatening speech toward Zhuge Feng.
- Retain established renderings for Water God Dragon, Dark Heaven, Hubei Province, the Great Faction War, Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water, and the System.

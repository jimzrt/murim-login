## Plot

### Chapter 977

# Chapter 977

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang taunt the cornered North Heaven Demon Lord, Murong Baek, as the Bow Saint closes in. Murong says defeating him will change nothing and reveals that the Lord of Heaven awakened long ago; he believes a war against that overwhelming power has already begun and says at most half a year remains. He explains that the Murong Family’s Xianbei ancestry and long-standing contempt from other orthodox powers shaped his belief that only the strong survive. Rejecting the charge of hypocrisy, Murong burns his innate qi despite knowing it will kill him and unleashes a devastating roar. Taekyung charges first with his spear wreathed in blue-white flames, followed by Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint.

## Continuity

- Murong Baek reveals his identity as the North Heaven Demon Lord and says the Lord of Heaven has awakened, the war has begun, and at most half a year remains.
- Murong Baek burns his innate qi despite knowing it will lead to his death; the confrontation resumes as Taekyung charges him, with Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint following.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 979

# Chapter 979

## Plot

The battered Hebei Peng warriors rally when the Jin Family of Taiyuan arrives with reinforcements. Jin Taekyung throws the heads of Murong Baek and Jamukha before the opposing army, while Jin Wikyung offers Temur a chance to surrender, promising to spare lives but vowing to drive the nomads from the steppe if they refuse. Temur submits to Jin Wikyung as his lord; the fighting against the Murong Family resumes.

## Translation Decisions

## Previous block summary

# Chapters 630–634

## Plot

Yohi identifies Jin Taekyung beneath his tiger mask and reveals that the four great chieftains generally oppose Nanman joining the Murim Alliance. She seeks to place the Yao people at the center of Nanman’s power structure, while Heugung, the Yi great chieftain, remains under her and Baeksang’s influence. Baeksang recognizes Jin as well and confronts him, arguing that Nanman has already sacrificed enough for the Central Plains. His anger at Jin’s mention of someone’s son leads Jin to suspect a connection to Head Elder Jin Baekyang.

After two days of fruitless investigation for Dark Heaven traces, Yayul Cheok secretly summons Jin and Namho. Their discussion of Baeksang’s loyalty is interrupted by an urgent warning from Ailao Mountain and the System quest **Unknown Omen**. Cheok mobilizes the Nanman Beast Palace, while Yayul Mok organizes the Miao warriors and palace defenses. Jin and Cheok race to Ailao Mountain, the former headquarters of the destroyed Five Poisons Sect and a forbidden land filled with proliferating poisons and beasts.

At the mountain, they find a poisoned sentry and more than a hundred elite Nanman guards massacred. The victims suffered poison, crushing force, and dismemberment, while nearly all their accompanying beasts have vanished. A colossal Black Tiger then emerges, identified by the System as **Ailao Mountain’s Wraith**.

## Continuity

- The second Nanman tribal council is scheduled for the following day, though the Ailao Mountain crisis may disrupt it.
- Yohi wants Yao influence to dominate Nanman. She and Baeksang control Heugung, making him a possible but unreliable persuasion target.
- Baeksang remains firmly opposed to joining the Murim Alliance. He is Yayul Cheok’s lifelong friend and sworn younger brother, but Jin and Namho question whether he can still be trusted.
- Baeksang bears a burn scar from Jeok Cheongang. His reaction to Jin’s mention of someone’s son may connect him to Head Elder Jin Baekyang, but this remains unconfirmed.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion found no Dark Heaven traces around the Nanman Beast Palace, but betrayal has not been ruled out.
- Yayul Cheok and Jin Taekyung are investigating Ailao Mountain under **Unknown Omen**, a time-limited quest with five hours remaining when accepted.
- Yayul Mok has taken the Seven Miao Tigers to mobilize the Miao warriors and prepare the palace’s defenses. Namho returned to warn the Fire Dragon Pavilion. White Tiger is temporarily carrying Jin.
- Ailao Mountain has been forbidden for over a century. Three hundred elite Nanman warriors rotate through its guard posts, and the four great chieftains conduct annual extermination campaigns there.
- More than a hundred guards have been killed, while most of their beasts are missing. The Black Tiger appearing at the site is identified as Ailao Mountain’s Wraith.
- Unresolved: whether Dark Heaven caused the massacre, whether the mountain’s beasts acted independently, whether they are being controlled, and why the beasts disappeared.

### Chapter 635

# Chapter 635

## Plot

The System identifies the colossal Black Tiger as Ailao Mountain's Wraith. It emits Fear comparable to the Water God Dragon, evades the Beast Miao King's Supreme Peak attack, survives Jin Taekyung's spear strike after biting through the Force-wreathed spearhead, and escapes their Qi Sense. The Wraith then outruns Jin, the Beast Miao King, and White Tiger, while the chase reveals poisoned ravines and the corpses of Nanman warriors. It disappears into a deep valley near the heart of Ailao Mountain, prompting the enraged Beast Miao King to charge inside despite Jin's warning; Jin follows after White Tiger signals that the valley is dangerous.

## Continuity

- Ailao Mountain's Wraith is a colossal Black Tiger whose Fear resembles the Water God Dragon's power and can briefly overwhelm even the Beast Miao King.
- The Wraith possesses extraordinary strength, stamina, speed, and elusive movement; it can evade Jin Taekyung's Qi Sense and outrun all three pursuers.
- The pursuit reveals additional dead Nanman warriors and ravines blackened by poison, while the whereabouts of the remaining warriors and their beasts remain unknown.
- The Wraith has entered a deep valley in the suspected center of Ailao Mountain, with the Beast Miao King and Jin Taekyung following it despite White Tiger's warning.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress may be connected to the Wraith and the Ailao Mountain massacre, but this remains unconfirmed.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Black Tiger for 흑호.
- Use Ailao Mountain's Wraith for 애뇌산의 망령.
- Use Transcendent for 초일류.
- Retain mountain lord for 산군 and explain its tiger and mountain-spirit senses in a footnote on first use.
### Chapter 636

# Chapter 636

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King pursued the Black Tiger into the hidden Poisonblood Grounds, a deadly region created by the Five Poisons Sect inside Ailao Mountain. After learning that every elite Nanman expedition sent there had perished, they entered the poisonous swamp together. The Poison Mist afflicted them with Mildly Poisoned, but Jin removed his status abnormality with the Myriad-Poison Ring and gave the Beast Miao King a High-Grade Poison-Warding Pearl. The System completed Jin's investigation and omen Quests, awarded him items and an Achievement, and generated the chain Quest My Good Sir, Do Not Cross That Swamp, which he accepted.

## Continuity

- The Poisonblood Grounds is a hidden, forbidden region in Ailao Mountain created by the former Five Poisons Sect to raise venomous creatures and produce deadly poisons.
- The Poisonblood Grounds outer region contains aggressive venomous creatures and a swamp covered by deep green Poison Mist.
- More than a hundred years ago, a Nanman expedition of elite warriors entered the Poisonblood Grounds with lamp oil and poison-warding pearls, and not one returned.
- Baeksang's grandfather, once Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace and reportedly the strongest warrior in Bai people history, died inside the Poisonblood Grounds.
- The Beast Miao King entered the Poisonblood Grounds to avenge the Nanman Beast Palace warriors killed there and is continuing inward with Jin Taekyung.
- Jin's Myriad-Poison Ring removes the Poisoned status abnormality caused by the Poison Mist; the Beast Miao King remains affected after entering the mist and receives Jin's High-Grade Poison-Warding Pearl.
- Jin accepted the chain Quest My Good Sir, Do Not Cross That Swamp after entering the Poisonblood Grounds.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Poisonblood Grounds for 독혈지.
- Use Beast Bai King for 야수백왕.
- Use Myriad-Poison Immunity for 천독불침.
- Use Poison Mist for 독무 and Mildly Poisoned for 미약한 중독.
- Retain the deliberately colloquial quest title My Good Sir, Do Not Cross That Swamp for 님아, 그 늪을 건너지 마오.
### Chapter 637

# Chapter 637

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King cross the Poisonblood Grounds swamp under Poison Mist, fighting deadly creatures while relying on Jin's Myriad-Poison Ring and the Beast Miao King's poison-warding pearl. After the swamp, Jin learns that the Nanman Beast Palace's rumored sacred treasure, the Beast King Stone, supposedly commands every ferocious beast, though its existence is uncertain and its disappearance is tied to the founding conflict with the Five Poisons Sect. In the dense Poison Mist, they investigate a human scream, find a dead Bai warrior wrapped in sticky thread, and are surrounded by creatures descending from a gigantic tree: Thousand-Year Spiders.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King have crossed the Poisonblood Grounds swamp and entered an even denser region of Poison Mist.
- The Poisonblood Grounds contains unknown life-forms, the spirits of countless dead, and dangers capable of killing even powerful martial artists.
- The Nanman Beast Palace's legendary sacred treasure is the Beast King Stone, whose existence is uncertain but which is said to command every ferocious beast under heaven.
- The Beast King Stone supposedly disappeared after the First Palace Lord and the founding Sect Leader of the Five Poisons Sect grievously wounded each other in battle.
- Jin considers Dark Heaven's plan to create a rift in Nanman more credible than a confirmed pursuit of the uncertain Beast King Stone.
- A Bai warrior was found dead inside the Poisonblood Grounds, wrapped tightly in sticky, thread-like material.
- Thousand-Year Spiders have appeared from the trees and surrounded Jin and the Beast Miao King.
- The Quest My Good Sir, Do Not Cross That Swamp gives no stated mission or Reward and declares failure to mean death.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Beast King Stone for 수왕석.
- Use Thousand-Year Spider for 천년지주.
- Use Mr. Yayul for 야율 씨 as Taekyung's colloquial address.
- Retain My Good Sir, Do Not Cross That Swamp for the Quest title.
### Chapter 638

# Chapter 638

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King are surrounded by five Thousand-Year Spiders in the Poisonblood Grounds. The spiders attack with highly acidic slime, but Jin summons the White Flame spear and the two fighters break through the assault together. Jin kills two spiders, the Beast Miao King kills two more, and Jin levels up after defeating the second one. The final spider flees by stepping on an unseen web, so Jin and the Beast Miao King pursue it.

## Continuity

- Five Thousand-Year Spiders surrounded Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King in the Poisonblood Grounds; four were killed and one escaped.
- Thousand-Year Spiders are cunning Nanman venomous beasts regarded as kings among venomous creatures and capable of living for up to a thousand years.
- Thousand-Year Spiders can spray acidic slime that melts flesh, vegetation, rock, and corpses.
- Jin Taekyung can summon the White Flame spear from his Inventory and empower it with Scorching Yang Qi.
- Jin killed Level 117 and Level 109 Thousand-Year Spiders with White Flame and Force, then gained a level.
- The System awarded Jin the rare achievement Indiscriminate Hunting Bug, which grants additional stats against insect-form enemies.
- The last Thousand-Year Spider escaped by stepping on an unseen spiderweb, and Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King are pursuing it.

### Chapter 639

# Chapter 639

## Plot

The last surviving Thousand-Year Spider uses its speed and prepared webs to lure Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King deeper into the Poisonblood Grounds, where it summons thousands of venomous beasts to form a living wall.

Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King are nearly overwhelmed, but Jin unleashes One Annihilation through White Flame, combining three jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi into hellfire that destroys the venomous-beast army.

With the guards gone, the Beast Miao King attacks the exposed Thousand-Year Spider, catches one of its legs, and tears it free. The spider screams and falls toward the ground, but its survival is unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Thousand-Year Spider possesses intelligence comparable to that of an ordinary human and deliberately chose a battlefield covered in webs.
- The Thousand-Year Spider can summon and command the venomous beasts throughout the Poisonblood Grounds.
- Jin Taekyung's One Annihilation can destroy thousands of venomous beasts in a single attack.
- The Beast Miao King is the Bai people's great chieftain as well as Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Sword Demon for 검마.
- Use two-headed horn snake for 쌍두각사, black frog for 흑와, and golden bee for 금봉.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's vulgar humor and the Beast Miao King's blunt, furious voice.

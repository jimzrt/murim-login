# Chapters 515–519

## Plot

At Xixia, Sama Pyo confronts Shaolin’s young Martial Uncle, the scarred and hoarse-voiced Unnamed, over the Black Dragon Saber. Unnamed reveals his identity, orders Sama Pyo to return the sword, and warns that Henan will not tolerate disorder. After three months in Repentance Cave, treatment with Shaolin’s Great Restoration Pill, and enlightenment driven by regret over Hong Dao’s death, Unnamed has advanced to the Supreme Peak realm and resolved to carry on his Master’s will.

Jin Taekyung reaches Shaolin after nearly drowning during his journey toward Henan. He completes Stage 2 of Fake Murim Practitioner, earns the achievement **Single Reed Crossing the River**, improves his internal-energy control and attributes, receives bonus points and substantial EXP, and levels up. He later reunites with Unnamed, recognizes Jung Ho as his former Garam Middle School classmate Park Jung Ho, and learns that Shaolin’s new Abbot—Unnamed’s eldest Senior Brother—wants to meet him.

With the New Murim Alliance’s founding three days away, orthodox and unorthodox factions, distant great families, and eccentric experts gather near Mount Song. Jeok Cheongang, restored to middle age through Returned to Youth, finally begins his long-delayed duel with Nangong Cheon, the Azure Sky Sword King, using chopsticks as swords. Their clash coincides with a Dark Heaven assault, sending the gathered Murim forces into battle.

Jeok emerges from a burning inn and is mistaken for a Dark Heaven fiend. Unnamed’s attempt to identify him as Hong Dao’s master worsens the misunderstanding, until the Thunderbolt Saber King recognizes Jeok as the Returned-to-Youth Fire King. After arguing over the destruction and injuries caused by their clash, Jeok, Taekyung, Unnamed, and Peng Cheolhu proceed toward the Murim Alliance. Nangong Cheon intervenes after exchanging blows with Jeok and sustains a minor Internal Injury.

## Continuity

- Unnamed is now a scarred Supreme Peak master, Jung Ho’s young Martial Uncle, and Hong Dao’s practical Disciple. He spent three months in Repentance Cave, received Shaolin’s Great Restoration Pill, and carries on Hong Dao’s will.
- Shaolin’s new Abbot has held the position for less than a month, is Unnamed’s eldest Senior Brother, and wants to meet Taekyung.
- Jung Ho is Park Jung Ho, Taekyung’s former Garam Middle School classmate, and Shaolin’s Discipline Hall Master.
- Taekyung completed Stage 2 of Fake Murim Practitioner, achieved **Single Reed Crossing the River**, improved his internal-energy control and attributes, gained 50 bonus points and substantial EXP, and leveled up.
- The New Murim Alliance’s founding is imminent near Mount Song. Major orthodox and unorthodox factions, great families, and independent experts have gathered there.
- Jeok Cheongang has expelled his Heart Demon, entered a new realm, achieved Returned to Youth, and begun his promised duel with Nangong Cheon. The duel’s outcome remains unresolved.
- Peng Cheolhu, the Thunderbolt Saber King, is Jeok’s longtime rival and friend, Hong Dao’s close friend, and protective toward Unnamed. He recognizes Jeok despite his rejuvenated appearance.
- Dark Heaven attacked near the Alliance gathering. Jeok’s emergence from the burning inn caused a chaotic misidentification; the assault’s broader consequences remain to be seen.
- Nangong Cheon intervened after fighting Jeok and sustained a minor Internal Injury.
- The Black Dragon Demon Gate is an ancient unorthodox faction formerly belonging to the Demonic Cult’s Twelve Branches and remains one of the Central Plains’ strongest unorthodox powers.
- The source and significance of the familiar red gaze and gigantic snake from Taekyung’s nightmare remain unknown.
- The Lord of Heaven’s identity and connection to the dangerous force associated with Taekyung’s original world remain unresolved.
- The permanence and repeatability of Zhuge Feng’s Demon-Sealing Formation remain unresolved, as does the Southern Heaven Demon Empress’s ultimate destination and intent in Yunnan.

# Chapter 520

### Chapter 521

# Chapter 521

## Plot

Mae Jonghak asks Jeok Cheongang to become the New Murim Alliance's Alliance Leader, but Jeok refuses because the position is troublesome; Mae continues accepting the role because someone must do it.

Song Ho guides the group through the Murim Alliance's secured interior, where Taekyung detects five concealed Hidden Shadow Pavilion agents and demonstrates how his Yangtze training refined his qi control and Qi Sense. The Alliance receives an urgent Heaven Grade report from Wudang in Hubei, whose bamboo slip depicts a monster connected to but distinct from the Water God Dragon.

## Continuity

- Mae Jonghak remains the New Murim Alliance's administrator because Jeok Cheongang refused to replace him as Alliance Leader; Mae's willingness to serve is grounded in responsibility rather than ambition.
- Song Ho is the reinstated Chief of the Hidden Shadow Pavilion and commands a vetted intelligence network that includes highly trained assassins.
- Jin Taekyung's ten-day Yangtze training greatly improved his precision in handling qi, control of internal energy, and Qi Sense; he can detect five concealed agents inside the Alliance.
- A Heaven Grade emergency report from Wudang in Hubei depicts a monster suggesting that the Water God Dragon is not the only relevant threat.

- Render 지(地)급 as Earth Grade and 천(天)급 as Heaven Grade; retain Grade for the Murim Alliance's report classification.
- Retain Tess hyung for 테스 형 and use Peng Dojin, So Pyeong, and Jang Il-pal for the newly named figures.
### Chapter 522

# Chapter 522

## Plot

Wudang's second report reveals that the Killing Ghost was Jang Sam, an ordinary fisherman from near Mount Wudang who disappeared after going out alone to fish. Taekyung suspects the Blood Fish, which may have been eaten by Jang Sam or may have eaten him, caused his transformation into a monster.

Taekyung concludes that a second Gate has not yet erupted and that Dark Heaven cannot open Gates casually, but warns that future Gates could ignite the Murim's existing powder keg. He believes the Blood Fish and Jang Sam may indicate similar incidents elsewhere.

The Hidden Shadow Pavilion resumes active operations, and the Murim Alliance begins mobilizing. Nangong Cheon and Peng Cheolhu are permitted to share the secret with their families, while Mae Jonghak again asks Jeok Cheongang whether he will become Alliance Leader; Jeok's answer remains unstated.

## Continuity

- The Killing Ghost's identity is established as Jang Sam, a fisherman who became a monster after a likely interaction with the Blood Fish.
- Taekyung assesses that another Gate has not opened yet and that Dark Heaven's ability to open Gates is limited, but warns that additional Gates could trigger a catastrophe across the Murim.
- The Hidden Shadow Pavilion has resumed operations, and the Murim Alliance is actively responding to the threat.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 화약고 as powder keg and preserve the chapter's blunt profanity and monster-comparison humor.
- Retain Jang Sam as the English rendering of 장삼.

### Chapter 524

# Chapter 524

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin wakes after sleeping through his guard duty and reports that Jin Mukyung is meeting faction leaders, the Three Fiend has been transferred to the Murim Alliance for interrogation, Gung Gibang is staying at the Henan branch of the Beggars' Sect, and Cheongpung is away buying sweetmeats and dumplings. Taekyung realizes that the absence of his companions leaves an unexpected emptiness.

Mungyeong has ended Taekyung's direct training, leaving him a custom pill and a final task: incorporate the martial principles he taught into the martial arts Taekyung already knows. Mungyeong explains that Taekyung does not need another martial art and learns that Taekyung seeks strength sufficient to protect everyone around him merely through his existence.

Hyuk Mujin summons Taekyung because the time has come for the Murim world to stand beneath a single banner at the Murim Alliance.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong has ended Taekyung's direct training, given him a custom fire-qi pill, taught him martial principles rather than another martial art, and assigned him the final task of incorporating those principles into his existing martial arts.
- Taekyung wants to become an invincible protector whose existence alone keeps the people around him safe.
- Jin Mukyung is meeting the leaders of other factions, while the Murim Alliance has taken custody of the Three Fiend for further interrogation.
- Gung Gibang is temporarily staying at the Henan branch of the Beggars' Sect, and Cheongpung is temporarily away from Taekyung's group.
- The Murim world is gathering under a single banner at the Murim Alliance today.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 무적자 as The Invincible and 무리 as martial principles, distinguishing principles from martial arts.
- Render 구운몽 as The Dream of the Nine Clouds and retain the explanatory footnote for the literary reference.
- Render 문특진맞단 as Mungyeong Special Jin Custom Pill and preserve the chapter's blunt profanity and teasing humor.

## Previous block summary

# Chapters 1150–1154

## Plot

Vladimir Furin rejects Morgoth’s demand for surrender and triggers his secret Magic Gem-powered weapon, Vladimir. The blast destroys Moscow, but Morgoth survives and uses the released energy to build a Dragon Lair over the ruins. Furin’s final warning—that Jin Taekyung’s return will mean Morgoth’s end—only makes Morgoth look forward to facing him.

As footage of the destruction spreads, the United States President announces that the returning savior has come back. Jin watches the devastation and learns that Pie Chen died fighting Morgoth a week earlier, saving Chuck Hagel. Jin vows to prevent further sacrifices. At the Pentagon, he and President Donald Doramp Jr. discuss the crisis: twenty-three countries have surrendered, and U.S. preparations depended on the still-comatose Cheon Taemin. Jin struggles with guilt over arriving too late.

A recording shows Morgoth opening a Gate from the Demon Realm above Moscow. A vast monster army pours through and advances across Russia. Morgoth offers survival under his rule in exchange for surrender, demands Cheon Taemin and Jin as tribute, and gives humanity three days. The UN’s announcement of Jin’s return briefly raises hopes, but footage of the advancing army leaves people shaken.

## Continuity

- Morgoth destroyed Moscow with Furin’s weapon and opened a Gate from the Demon Realm above the ruins. A vast monster army is advancing across Russia.
- Morgoth offers survival to those who surrender and threatens destruction to those who resist. He demands Cheon Taemin and Jin Taekyung as tribute within three days.
- Cheon Taemin remains unconscious. Jin has returned and is widely regarded as a new-age savior.
- Furin’s weapon, Vladimir, was a Tsar Bomba transformed through decades of Magic Gem experiments and magical engineering. Morgoth is the sole survivor shown.
- Pie Chen died a week earlier after refusing to follow Morgoth and making a last stand. Chuck Hagel survived because of her sacrifice and has only his right arm left.
- Twenty-three countries have surrendered to Morgoth. The Arab League and some major South American countries are among them.
- The System notification shown to Jin remains unexplained.

# Chapter 1155

## Plot

News reports that Russia has surrendered to Morgoth, while monster attacks and Gate mutations drive magical power levels toward those of the Great Cataclysm. On a live broadcast, a doctor argues that humanity still has a 5.2 percent chance of defeating Morgoth; a commentator argues that Sky and Jin should choose a noble sacrifice. The doctor shoots him, and the broadcast cuts away. Discussing the incident, the Skeleton King and Chuck Hagel conclude that fear is making people more receptive to sacrificing the heroes, and that Jin may choose to give himself up. Hagel says Morgoth is overwhelmingly likely to break his promise and kill them. The Skeleton King has a sudden, unspoken thought.

## Continuity

- Morgoth accepted Russia’s surrender; Russia will disarm and retain its current government, like more than twenty countries that surrendered before it.
- Over the past eleven days, the world averaged more than four Monster Waves and around fifty Gate mutation events per day; magical power distribution rose by 2.8 points today.
- The Skeleton King fears that public support for a so-called noble sacrifice may grow and that Jin Taekyung may willingly sacrifice himself; Chuck Hagel believes Morgoth is overwhelmingly likely to break his promise and kill Jin and Sky.
- The Skeleton King has a sudden thought at the chapter’s end; its content is not revealed.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the established renderings “Silver Mountains,” “Archduke of the Demon Realm,” and “Answer the call” for the prior temporary terms.
- Render 건법 as “gun-fu” to preserve the joke about an American martial art.
### Chapter 1156

# Chapter 1156

## Plot

At the Pentagon, Jin Taekyung visits the unconscious Cheon Taemin and confronts his suspicion that Taemin was the Martial God and The Helper, and that Asmodeus may not have been completely erased. Choi Minwoo admits that he and most of their allies have secretly arranged to keep Jin confined until Morgoth’s three-day deadline expires, fearing Jin will confront Morgoth alone. Jin recognizes that Choi’s grandfather may have kept Choi at a distance because of a danger he sensed, and reveals that he has avoided his own family and guildmates to protect them from the coming battle. When Jin attempts to leave, he discovers the exit has vanished. Choi warns that breaking the protective spells could destroy the Pentagon; Jin uses his ability to see a seam in the mana binding the spells and splits it.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo and most of Jin Taekyung’s allies are secretly trying to keep him in Cheon Taemin’s Pentagon recovery chamber until Morgoth’s three-day deadline expires; Chuck Hagel and the Skeleton King were not told.
- Jin Taekyung has avoided visiting his mother, younger sister, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong because he fears their involvement in the coming battle would get them killed.
- Jin believes Cheon Taemin was the Martial God and The Helper, and that Asmodeus may not have been completely erased; these remain Jin’s conclusions, not confirmed facts.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Jin’s identification of Cheon Taemin as the Martial God and The Helper framed as his belief; the chapter supplies no independent confirmation.
- Render the chapter’s use of 결 as “seam” for the visible line in the mana binding the protective spells.
### Chapter 1157

# Chapter 1157

## Plot

Jin Taekyung breaks the magic surrounding the secret Pentagon chamber without the feared backlash, easing Magic Johnson’s and Choi Minwoo’s concern about his growing strength. He orders full mobilization to Moscow against Morgoth, but says he will go alone and that they must not act without further orders unless he cannot command.

A Code Red reports that Code Name Blue escaped through Area 52. Johnson reveals that Code Name Blue is Jin, and suspects the escapee is someone who looks like him. The group hurries to the surface and finds Chuck Hagel alone.

## Continuity

- Jin orders World Hunter Federation forces to mobilize for Moscow against Morgoth and his monsters, but plans to confront Morgoth alone; others are to await further orders unless Jin cannot command.
- Code Name Blue is Jin Taekyung; someone who looks like him has apparently escaped through Area 52, and their identity is unknown.
- Jin now recognizes that his deepest fear is the danger his death would leave for others; he has found some peace with that fear.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 코드 네임 블루 as “Code Name Blue”; the chapter identifies it as Jin’s codename and does not identify the apparent double.
### Chapter 1158

# Chapter 1158

## Plot

The Skeleton King, wearing Jin Taekyung’s appearance through a newly discovered ability to absorb, travels through a land of death toward Morgoth. He listens to the dead spirits and regards them as his people, then crosses into a pitch-black region, intending to sacrifice himself in Jin’s form. He believes humanity will learn that surrendering Jin will not stop the monsters’ destruction and slaughter.

Morgoth recalls his lifelong curiosity, his first entry into the Demon Realm, and his unanswered questions about why Asmodeus summoned him and prepared the current situation. He sets those questions aside to welcome a hero who has arrived at his palace.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton King has borrowed Jin Taekyung’s appearance through absorption and is heading toward Morgoth; he intends to sacrifice himself in that form, believing the monsters’ promise to stop their destruction and slaughter if Jin is surrendered is false.
- The Skeleton King can hear and sense the dead spirits, whom he considers his people; he suspects he may once have been human, but remembers nothing and sometimes experiences déjà vu since traveling with a strange fellow.
- Morgoth was summoned from beyond distant stars and space by Asmodeus, but does not know why; he says he is not devoted to Asmodeus and does not intend to die for him.
- Morgoth welcomes a hero to his palace; the chapter does not explicitly identify the arriving guest.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the Skeleton King distinct from Jin Taekyung: the Skeleton King has borrowed Jin’s appearance; Jin is not established as physically present in this scene.
- Render 魔力 as “magical power,” distinct from mana, and render 九泉 as “Nine Springs.”

# Chapter 1159

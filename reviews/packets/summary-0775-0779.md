# Chapters 770–774

## Plot

The Skeleton King asks Jin Taekyung to erase him publicly so Michael Silbert cannot exploit his identity. Jin refuses to sacrifice his friend and instead considers killing Michael, then placing Team Leader Choi and their allies in control of the reestablished World Hunter Federation. Before Jin and Choi can fight over this plan, Magic Johnson sends documents that reveal a possible fourth path: defeat their enemies while keeping themselves and the Skeleton King alive.

Michael uses the UN's approval of the Federation to transform worldwide fear into public hope. He presents Cheon Taemin as the rightful representative and announces the inaugural ceremony in Seoul, while secretly communicating with The Prophet and ordering surveillance of the Skeleton King. Jin, Choi, and their allies return to Korea and organize a covert operation. Choi gives instructions to fewer than ten trusted senior members of the Peace and Ares Guilds, while Jin withholds the new clue from Baek Hanseong because Michael may have spies near the President.

On the eve of the ceremony, Jin refuses to visit his mother and Hayeon, fearing that emotion will compromise him. Felix, Magic Johnson, Chuck Hagel, Faye Chen, and Choi gather for a secret meeting. Jin's Broken Body continues draining his strength, but he proceeds with the plan alongside the Peace and Ares Hunters. At the First National Assembly Hall, Michael prepares to claim Cheon Taemin's former position and ascend as humanity's leader when an unidentified voice interrupts him.

## Continuity

- The World Hunter Federation has been reestablished, with its inaugural ceremony taking place at the First National Assembly Hall in Seoul.
- Michael Silbert intends to occupy Cheon Taemin's former central position and become the Federation's leader.
- Cheon Taemin remains absent, and the Federation's representative has not yet been determined.
- Michael has converted public panic into support for the Federation and is secretly communicating with The Prophet.
- Michael ordered heightened security and surveillance of the Skeleton King, whom he believes is Jin's emotional weakness.
- Jin has rejected sacrificing the Skeleton King and is pursuing a fourth path that may eliminate Michael and their other enemies without killing the Skeleton King or themselves.
- The clue supporting the fourth path remains unverified and is being withheld from Baek Hanseong because Michael may have surveillance or a mole near him.
- Jin and the Skeleton King remain together inside a concealed, imperceptible space; the Skeleton King has been completely silent.
- Team Leader Choi has completed preparations and covertly briefed fewer than ten trusted senior Peace and Ares Guild members.
- Jin's Broken Body still consumes part of his strength, but he is committed to acting.
- Jin has not seen his mother or Hayeon before the ceremony.
- Felix, Magic Johnson, Chuck Hagel, Faye Chen, and Team Leader Choi have coordinated privately before the ceremony.
- The ceremony is not being broadcast live, and its attendee list remains secret.
- Michael is interrupted just as he prepares to claim leadership; the identity and intent of the arriving speaker are unresolved.

### Chapter 775

# Chapter 775

## Plot

Jin confronts Michael Silbert at the inaugural ceremony and challenges him to explain the atrocities he has committed. Michael detects the concealed companion accompanying Jin, reveals his brutal childhood in Paris's Tenth Arrondissement, his criminal past, and his survival of the Great Battle of Paris, but treats that history as part of his heroic image and demands submission from those who resist him. Jin answers with pity and disgust rather than fear, rejects Michael's intimidation, opens the conference hall, and forces the inaugural ceremony to begin as hundreds of attendees enter.

## Continuity

- Michael Silbert's pre-awakening life included abandonment by his father, an alcoholic and drug-addicted mother who died in 2012, several criminal convictions, and records he later erased.
- Michael awakened in 2020 during the Great Cataclysm and was the lone survivor found amid the dead after the week-long Great Battle of Paris; he later became a public hero who saved Paris twice.
- Michael's true strength remains concealed, but his qi control is practiced enough to resemble a Supreme Peak master of the Murim rather than an ordinary Hunter.
- Jin now regards Michael with pity and disgust instead of fear and openly refuses to submit to his coercion.
- The inaugural ceremony of the new World Hunter Federation has begun, with hundreds of attendees entering the conference hall.
- Jin's Broken Body continues to cause severe internal pain while he confronts Michael.
- Michael can sense Jin's concealed companion, though the companion remains imperceptible in form.
- Jin continues withholding the discovered clue from Baek because Michael Silbert may have surveillance or a mole near the President.
- The Skeleton King remains concealed with Jin and silent.
- Michael is positioning himself at the center of the World Hunter Federation while Cheon Taemin remains absent.

### Chapter 777

# Chapter 777

## Plot

At the World Hunter Federation’s inaugural ceremony, Michael Silbert uses staged news about two visitors to pressure Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi. Choi announces that his maternal grandfather, Cheon Taemin, is in a coma and too ill to join the coming war; Magic Johnson confirms the truth and says he could find no way to awaken him. As the vote for a new leader begins, Michael privately resolves to eliminate Jin. Jin holds a fragment of a tower shield used as a ballot and, through Sound Transmission, tells the Skeleton King he intends to take a near-even chance on a dangerous plan. He asks the Skeleton King to trust him and their friends, saying he will take the risk because the Skeleton King is his friend.

## Continuity

- Cheon Taemin is in a coma and too ill to participate in the coming war; Team Leader Choi publicly revealed this at the Federation’s inaugural ceremony, and Magic Johnson confirmed it.
- Michael Silbert is positioning himself to lead the World Hunter Federation and intends to eliminate Jin Taekyung as soon as possible.
- The Federation’s leadership vote has begun; Jin remains silent during it, with the Skeleton King beside him.
- Jin intends to attempt a dangerous plan with odds close to fifty-fifty, trusting his friends and the Skeleton King.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 외조부 as “maternal grandfather.”
- Keep Jin’s uncertainty between 도편추방제 and 도편추첨제 as “ostracism by lot” and “ostracism by ballot.”

### Chapter 779

# Chapter 779

## Plot

Jin Taekyung repeatedly declares that the Stone King is a monster until the gathered Hunters accept it as true and turn their weapons on him. Jin steps between them, saying the Stone King is his friend and a comrade-in-arms who saved his life. When the Kronos Guild Master, Fabian, threatens to revoke Jin’s Hunter status and execute him, Magic Johnson and several allies—including Choi Minwoo, Faye Chen, and Felix—stand with Jin. Jin confronts Michael Silbert, who threatens a Federation vote to expel Jin permanently and promises legal proceedings or summary execution. Jin asks whether Michael himself is already no different from a monster, then asks whether the wound on Michael’s neck has healed.

## Continuity

- The Stone King’s identity as a monster is publicly revealed at the Federation gathering; Jin stands between him and the Hunters who draw their weapons.
- Jin calls the Stone King his friend and comrade-in-arms, saying the Stone King saved his life.
- Magic Johnson, Chuck Hagel, Faye Chen, Felix, and Choi Minwoo openly side with Jin when Fabian threatens him.
- Fabian is the Guild Master of Kronos, an S-rank Hunter who has been active since the Great Cataclysm; he threatens Jin with immediate execution.
- Michael threatens to put Jin’s permanent expulsion to a Federation vote and claims those involved will face trial or summary execution if they resist.
- Jin questions whether Michael is already no different from a monster and refers to a wound on Michael’s neck; Michael’s nature and the wound’s status remain unconfirmed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 파비안 as Fabian, 크로노스 as Kronos, 척 헤이글 as Chuck Hagel, and 파이 첸 as Faye Chen.
- Keep the distinction between Jin’s explicit claim that the Stone King is a monster and the unresolved implication in his accusation toward Michael.

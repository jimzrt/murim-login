# Chapters 1145–1149

## Plot

While Jin Taekyung’s body sleeps in Murim, his consciousness investigates a suspected crisis in the realm of immortals. Jeok Cheongang stays beside him as the coalition army crosses the Taklamakan Desert toward Tianshan. Elsewhere, the Grand Mage awakens after being reborn through the Lord of Heaven’s grace; the Lord connects a wave she felt to the heavens opening again.

In a buried desert shelter, Choi Minwoo leads the Hunters out to fight thousands of approaching enemies. Taekyung awakens in the Skeleton King’s backpack and joins the battle. He and the Skeleton King fight exceptionally durable, rapidly regenerating monsters, while the Grand Mage—now identified as Magic Johnson—attacks from above. The Hunters defeat the monsters.

Johnson says his last contact with the group was twenty-four hours earlier. Team Leader Choi tells Taekyung he has been unconscious for ten days. The System reports that the Main Quest Cataclysm failed despite the Doppelganger’s defeat: the Demon Realm’s boundaries have partially opened, and a rift is in progress. Black Dragon Duke Morgoth has answered a summoning. Taekyung recognizes his wings and roar from his last night in Murim. In Russia, Morgoth destroys Red Square and enters Vladimir Furin’s office.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has awakened and reunited with Choi Minwoo, the Skeleton King, the Hunters, and Magic Johnson. He and the Skeleton King are friends and allies.
- Taekyung was unconscious for ten days, while Johnson’s last contact with the group was twenty-four hours earlier. The difference remains unexplained.
- The Main Quest Cataclysm failed despite the Doppelganger’s defeat. The Demon Realm’s boundaries have partially opened, and a rift is in progress.
- Black Dragon Duke Morgoth answered a summoning. He destroyed Red Square and entered Vladimir Furin’s office in Russia; his intentions are unknown.
- The Grand Mage was reborn through the Lord of Heaven’s grace and felt a wave the Lord associated with the heavens opening again. The wave’s cause and significance remain unexplained.
- The coalition army of more than two hundred thousand is crossing the Taklamakan Desert toward Tianshan. Jeok Cheongang is keeping watch over Taekyung’s sleeping body.

# Chapter 1150

## Plot

Morgoth tells Vladimir Furin that he once stayed in another world before leaving for the Demon Realm, and frames his destruction and subjugation of humanity as punishment for rejecting his offer. Furin rejects surrender, challenges Morgoth’s reason for negotiating, and suggests he fears someone he has not found. Furin presses a concealed button, triggering a deep rumble beneath the Kremlin.

## Continuity

- Morgoth says he once stayed in another world, later left for the Demon Realm, and entered another’s service there.
- Morgoth calls the catastrophe and massacre he inflicted punishment for humanity’s refusal to obey his offer; he demands Furin surrender and become a citizen in name, while Furin rejects the terms.
- Furin estimates 50 million casualties over the past ten days; North Africa and West Asia have become a land of death, with resisters rotting or turned undead and survivors enslaved.
- Furin suggests Morgoth fears someone he has not yet found; Morgoth does not confirm or deny this.
- Furin presses a concealed button, and a tremendous rumble rises from deep beneath the Kremlin; its cause is not revealed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 크렘린 궁 as “the Kremlin.”
- Render 사상자 as “casualties”; the text gives an estimate of 50 million without separating deaths from injuries.
### Chapter 1151

# Chapter 1151

## Plot

Vladimir Furin’s secret Magic Gem-powered weapon, renamed Vladimir, detonates and destroys Moscow; Morgoth survives, shapes the released energy, and builds a Dragon Lair over the ruins. Before the blast, Furin warns Morgoth that Jin’s return will mean his end, and Morgoth says he looks forward to it.

Morgoth reveals the new lair to the world through a drone camera, announcing the arrival of the calamity.

## Continuity

- Furin’s decades of secret experiments transformed the Tsar Bomba using Magic Gems and magical engineering; he renamed the weapon Vladimir after himself.
- The detonation destroys Moscow and leaves it a lifeless wasteland; Morgoth is the sole survivor shown.
- Morgoth uses the released Magic Gem energy to create a Dragon Lair over Moscow’s ruins and reveals it to the world.
- Furin’s final warning to Morgoth is that Jin’s return will end him; Morgoth welcomes the prospect.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 차르 봄바 as “Tsar Bomba” and the weapon’s new name 블라디미르 as “Vladimir.”
- Render 드래곤 레어 as “Dragon Lair.”

# Chapter 1152

## Plot

A livestream captures Morgoth’s destruction of Moscow, caused by the hydrogen bomb hidden in the Kremlin. After the United States President announces that the returning savior has come back, the narrator watches footage of the devastation and Pie Chen’s death a week earlier. - Chuck Hagel survived because of Pie Chen’s sacrifice; he has only his right arm left and says he wishes he had died in her place.
- The United States President announces that the returning savior has come back; the narrator vows to prevent further sacrifices and intends to face Morgoth.

### Chapter 1153

# Chapter 1153

## Plot

At the Pentagon, Jin Taekyung discusses the global crisis with U.S. President Donald Doramp Jr.: Morgoth’s destruction has caused catastrophic casualties and displacement, and twenty-three countries have surrendered because Morgoth promises survival to those who submit. The President says humanity’s preparations depended on the comatose Cheon Taemin. As a video reveals a Dragon Lair over Moscow’s ruins, Taekyung wrestles with guilt over arriving too late.

## Continuity

- Twenty-three countries have surrendered to Morgoth; the Arab League and some major South American countries are among them.
- Morgoth offers survival to those who surrender and destruction to those who resist, deepening divisions among humans.
- The United States’ preparations against monsters depended on Cheon Taemin, who remains comatose.
- A video shows a Dragon Lair standing over the blackened ruins of Moscow.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 외교 as “Diplomacy” when identifying Morgoth’s distinctive ability to secure surrenders through genuine promises.
- Render 도널드 as “Donald” in Donald Doramp Jr.’s name.

# Chapter 1154

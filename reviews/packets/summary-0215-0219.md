# Chapters 210–214

## Plot

Jin Taekyung becomes nationally famous as the Tollgate Hero, but invasive media expose his family’s private information. Jinho reminds him that he does not need to save everyone and should focus on becoming strong enough to protect his family and precious people.

Taekyung meets Won Myunghoon, an A-Rank Hunter, former celebrity ranker, and CEO of the Star Guild. Taekyung’s admiration leads to a friendly hyung-and-younger-brother relationship, but he declines Won’s offers to join the Star Guild or an entertainment agency. At a live ceremony, Taekyung receives his A-Rank Hunter card and achieves the A-Rank Hunter Achievement. His accidental profanity becomes a viral iTube sensation, earning him the nicknames Taekyung the Lord and Lord Fuck.

Taekyung and Team Leader Choi perfectly clear the B-Rank Gate The Lycanthrope’s Black Forest. Won’s history is revealed: he survived the Myeongdong Station Mutated Gate Catastrophe, where Do Minsu and around thirty others died, then endured a trial before retiring from entertainment. After apologizing for the false transfer report, Won arranges a joint raid between the Peace and Star Guilds, guaranteeing the Peace Guild the Gate’s drops.

The combined party enters The Black Wyvern’s Nest, an A-Rank Gate. Taekyung recognizes it as the site of a traumatic encounter three years earlier, when a Black Wyvern slaughtered his teammates.

# Chapter 215

## Plot

A mistake by the Star Guild's Team 1 Leader changed the joint raid location from Manticore's Jungle to The Black Wyvern's Nest. Team Leader Choi proposed canceling the raid because the Peace Guild was unprepared, but Taekyung insisted on proceeding despite his trauma from the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident.

The Star Guild and Peace Guild entered the A-Rank Gate, where more than twenty members crossed a vast, hot, humid primeval jungle. Won Myunghoon lent Taekyung a Yeti's Necklace, but Taekyung removed it because opening his Conception and Governor Vessels had left him unaffected by the heat, then lent it to Im Kkeokjeong.

One hour into the raid, a wyvern appeared above the party, much sooner than Won Myunghoon expected.

### Chapter 218

# Chapter 218

## Plot

Team Leader Choi examines Yeti's Necklace and complains that such a beautiful, sophisticated item is in limited supply. Won Myunghoon refuses to take it back, explaining that he values human bonds more than possessions, then reveals that the necklace is a gift for Taekyung and tells him not to give it to anyone else. After Taekyung equips it, the Item Window reveals that it marks its possessor as a target of dragonkin monsters regardless of species.

After half a day of travel, the group leaves Manticore's Jungle and enters a wasteland. The Star Guild members fight Trolls, exploiting their vulnerability to attacks on the head and heart and to fire or ice before regeneration can occur. Taekyung notices the contrast between Won's polished warmth and his private irritation at the smell of burning Troll flesh, then teases him about reports that he visits a department store five times a week as a VVIP. Won denies the report and becomes visibly uncomfortable.

Team Leader 1 returns from scouting for the missing Wyvern, deathly pale and covered in blood. Seven people had gone out, but only he returned: a single Wyvern killed the other six despite their use of concealment magic. He identifies it as a Named Monster and reveals that the group is inside an A-Rank Mutated Gate, leaving everyone in suffocating silence.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon gave Taekyung Yeti's Necklace as a personal gift and told him not to give it to anyone else.
- Yeti's Necklace activates the Wind of the Snowfield effect and causes its possessor to become a target of dragonkin monsters.
- The raid party has left Manticore's Jungle and entered a wasteland after half a day of travel.
- The Star Guild defeated several Trolls by preventing their regeneration with fire and ice after attacking their heads and hearts.
- A Wyvern identified as a Named Monster killed six of the seven scouts, confirming that the raid is taking place inside an A-Rank Mutated Gate.
- Taekyung increasingly suspects that Won's polished, friendly public manner conceals a disturbing or unfamiliar side.

### Chapter 219

# Chapter 219

## Plot

The joint raid in The Black Wyvern’s Nest is revealed to have encountered a Named Monster, a creature beyond ordinary A-Rank strength. Team Leader 1, the only member of his team to return, was overwhelmed by the monster’s Fear and abandoned the others.

Taekyung realizes that the Named Monster is the same Black Wyvern he fought at the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate three years earlier. His spear strike damaged one of its eyes, and the transformed creature now approaches the raid party across the wasteland.

Taekyung orders Won Myunghoon to take the Star Guild members and leave while he retains Yeti’s Necklace. He confronts Won over his conduct, withdraws the familiar hyung address, and warns him never to bare his teeth again. Won and the Star Guild flee as the Named Monster’s roar echoes across the wasteland.

## Continuity

- A Named Monster is present in The Black Wyvern’s Nest and is several times larger and stronger than an ordinary Wyvern.
- The Named Monster is the same Black Wyvern Taekyung fought at the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate three years earlier; Taekyung’s spear damaged one of its eyes.
- Team Leader 1 was afflicted by the monster’s Fear and survived by abandoning his team.
- Taekyung has confronted Won Myunghoon and ordered him to leave with the Star Guild while retaining Yeti’s Necklace; their previously friendly relationship is now sharply strained.

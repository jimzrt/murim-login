# Chapters 490–494

## Plot

Mungyeong begins testing Jin Taekyung without warning, using poisoned water, hidden weapons, candy, and concealed attacks. Taekyung survives Potent Paralysis Powder, Potent Soul-Bewitching Powder, Potent Energy-Dispersing Poison, and Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder through the Myriad-Poison Ring and Scorching Yang Qi, but the final detoxification permanently reduces his Strength and Agility by five points through Sinews and Meridians damage. Mungyeong reveals that these trials are part of Taekyung’s training and intends to correct his complacency before teaching him secret martial arts, without forming a formal Master-Disciple relationship.

Meanwhile, Jeok Cheongang withdraws from the group and orders them not to search for him until the Water God Dragon’s affairs are complete. Taekyung, Hyeongong, Zhuge Feng, Mungyeong, Gung Gibang, Cheongpung, and others travel to the corpse. They honor the divine creature’s sacrifice, then negotiate over its priceless scales and bones. Taekyung prevents Zhuge Feng from claiming an excessive share and persuades Wudang to help dismantle the body. Cheongpung and Hyeongong cut apart the enormous corpse, while Mungyeong watches Murim’s reverence turn into avarice.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is the Slaughter Saint, formerly Killing Ghost and later the Divine Physician. He recognizes Taekyung’s innate Heavenly Martial Physique and is testing him through escalating poisons and ambushes.
- Taekyung detoxified Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder, but permanent Sinews and Meridians damage reduced Strength and Agility by 5 each. Severe Stomachache was temporary.
- Mungyeong will teach Taekyung secret martial arts without becoming his formal master; the Fake Murim Martial Artist Quest remains accepted and in progress.
- Jeok Cheongang’s innate qi is damaged and steadily diminishing despite treatment. He has disappeared from the expedition and instructed the others not to find him until the Water God Dragon’s matters are finished.
- The Water God Dragon’s spirit has departed, but its enormous corpse is being dismantled and distributed under Taekyung’s direction. Its sacrifice prevented a greater catastrophe in Hubei Province.
- The functionally crippled Gate still leaks faint mana and demonic qi. Its residual mana mutated local life, while the Water God Dragon absorbed the Gate’s mana.
- About one hundred Level 5 Mutated Minnows, locally called Blood Fish, have been captured; the remaining population and the full spread of the mutation are unknown.
- Taekyung believes Dark Heaven’s regeneration, Teleport, and Moving Formation are forms of Magic connected to the Gate. He has told Jeok that he came from another world and that a dangerous force from it is linked to Dark Heaven.
- Honglan, the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, corrupted the Dongting Lake imugi and is traveling toward Yunnan. Her objective remains unknown.
- The severely injured Dongting Fisherman’s exact connection to Dark Heaven and the destruction of the secret refuge remains unresolved.
- Open hooks: the Gate’s destination and lost functions; the Lord of Heaven’s identity; Honglan’s plans in Yunnan; the Dongting Fisherman’s role; and Mungyeong’s remaining trials and martial arts instruction.

# Chapter 495

## Plot

Taekyung suffers severe humiliation after Mungyeong's poison causes him to soil himself, then learns that Hyeongong suspects someone stole a large quantity of the Water God Dragon's bones and scales. Taekyung secretly admits he placed the best materials in his inventory and orders Hyuk Mujin to prepare for the party's return, with most of the remaining corpse destined for the Jin Family of Taiyuan.

The System identifies the Water God Dragon's bones and scales as Supreme Peak materials and reveals that its Origin Essence is an extraordinarily pure and immense inner core damaged by mana but extracted according to the dragon's will. Taekyung considers using it for Jeok Cheongang because he is uncertain whether Jeok fully recovered from the Formless Ultimate Poison. The chapter ends with Jeok encountering and taming an old tiger while reflecting that he has already grown old.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has placed the Water God Dragon's best bones, scales, flesh, and Origin Essence in his inventory; most of the sorted remains will be moved discreetly to the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- The Water God Dragon's Origin Essence has no System type, grade, or restriction; it contains immense, exceptionally pure qi, was damaged by mana, and may be dangerous to anyone unable to withstand its power.
- Taekyung's status abnormality from Mungyeong's poison remains active, and he is worried about the aftereffects of Jeok Cheongang's Formless Ultimate Poison despite the completed treatment.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 원정 as Origin Essence and 내단 as inner core; retain the established System labels Type, Grade, Restriction, and Description.
- Render 대호 as great tiger, preserve geun as the weight unit with a footnote, and retain Taekyung's profane, humiliating banter with Hyuk Mujin and Jeok's rough speech.

# Chapter 496

## Plot

- Mungyeong continues accompanying Taekyung during his concealed martial training and lethal testing without a formal Master-Disciple relationship.

### Chapter 497

# Chapter 497

## Plot

Taekyung survives twelve simulated deaths during Mungyeong’s successive tests, realizes that imitating Mungyeong instead of fighting in his own way caused his failures, and learns that Mungyeong once taught several assassins from the vanished Salcheonmun.

For the final condition, Mungyeong blinds Taekyung with Blindness Powder and attacks with one killing sword stroke; Taekyung evades it as all his senses awaken. Mungyeong concludes that Taekyung has acquired the basics after only seven days and nights, is impressed by his exceptional speed, and privately understands Fire King Jeok Cheongang’s feelings.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong once belonged to Salcheonmun, a vanished assassin sect, and taught several assassins there; he says none of them could still be alive.
- Taekyung identifies imitating Mungyeong’s movements instead of using his own fighting style as the cause of his repeated failures.
- After seven days and nights of poisoned tests, Taekyung evades Mungyeong’s blinding final sword stroke and reaches the basic level Mungyeong was testing for.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 살천문 as Salcheonmun and 실명산 as Blindness Powder; retain Teacher’s Day as the holiday name and explain it in a footnote.

# Chapter 498

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens after Mungyeong's poisoned training and learns that he completed the Fake Murim Martial Artist Quest, earned EXP and points, greatly increased his Poison Resistance, sharpened his Qi Sense, and gained a clue to enlightenment. ## Continuity

- Taekyung completed Mungyeong's poisoned tests, gained a clue to enlightenment, and received the Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2 Follow-up Quest; its objective will depend on Mungyeong's future plans.
- The Jin Family of Taiyuan has arrived at Taekyung's location with more than fifty Jin Dragon Squad martial artists, including Wipeng and Jang Taebo; the assembled squad members are at least First Rate.
- Jang Taebo's White Flame spear has saved Taekyung's life several times, and Jang was specially summoned to accompany the Jin Family by Jin Wikyung.

### Chapter 499

# Chapter 499

## Plot

At Jin Wikyung's camp beside Dongting Lake, Wipeng reports that Lee Seowol's Mount Heng Sword Sect has been rebuilt and is growing rapidly under her leadership, with Cheol Mubaek out of seclusion and helping its affairs. Jin Mukyung has remained secluded in the training hall for more than a year after losing to Cheongpung, eating only fasting pills while pursuing a breakthrough. Jin Wikyung reveals that he established Ironcraft Hall to bring Jang Taebo back into service, but Jang refuses until Taekyung produces one of several legendary materials; Taekyung immediately provides an imugi's claw, completing the Sudden Quest and causing the Jin Family of Taiyuan to recruit Jang as Master of Ironcraft Hall.

## Continuity

- The Mount Heng Sword Sect has completed its reconstruction and is growing under Lee Seowol, while Cheol Mubaek has ended his seclusion and is actively helping the sect.
- Jin Mukyung remains secluded in the training hall more than a year after losing his duel with Cheongpung; the Star-Array Grand Banquet has occurred during his seclusion, and he refuses to emerge until he achieves a great accomplishment.
- Jin Wikyung has established Ironcraft Hall within the Jin Family of Taiyuan and recruited Jang Taebo as its Master after Taekyung supplied an imugi's claw.
- Taekyung has not contacted Lee Seowol since leaving for Mount Jiuhua more than a year ago, despite her having recently sought Jin Wikyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 장자방 as Zhang Liang, 한신 as Han Xin, and 소하 as Xiao He.
- Render 철기당 as Ironcraft Hall, 철기당주 as Master of Ironcraft Hall, 돌발 퀘스트 as Sudden Quest, and 전속 도비/노비 as personal Dobby/slave to preserve the closing joke.

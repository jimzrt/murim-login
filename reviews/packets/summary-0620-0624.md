# Chapters 615–619

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion’s Nanman expedition travels from Mount Daebyeol through Hubei and Sichuan using secret horse-caravan routes preserved in Ju Hwaran’s map and the Escort King’s records. Water Dragon Stronghold ships then carry the party down the Yangtze toward Yunnan. Along the way, Jin Taekyung restrains Song Ilseom and Sama Pyo’s hostility, while Song warns him that Hwaran has suffered greatly and must not be hurt or abandoned.

After entering Nanman, Taekyung completes the Journey to Nanman Quest and receives its rewards, including the achievement **Thousands of Li in Search of Nanman**. The resulting Peak-grade Chain Quest, **Seeds Planted in Nanman**, directs the expedition to contact a Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent in Yeongin before proceeding against the Nanman Beast Palace.

Yeongin’s inhabitants react violently to the Han Chinese because a Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group from Sichuan allegedly massacred about two hundred villagers. The group’s nearly thirty men and one woman were later found dead from deadly venom. At Poison Flower Pavilion, the elderly innkeeper reveals himself as the Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent and identifies Taekyung as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon. The woman may have been the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, but her identity remains uncertain.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion is staying at Poison Flower Pavilion in Yeongin, Nanman.
- Jin Taekyung, Ju Hwaran, Hyuk Mujin, Song Ilseom, Sama Pyo, and Taishan are part of the expedition.
- The Nanman Beast Palace remains the expedition’s primary objective.
- The Chain Quest **Seeds Planted in Nanman** has reached its contact stage with the Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent.
- The Poison Flower Pavilion owner is the elderly Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent planted in Yeongin. He speaks both the local language and Chinese and knows Taekyung’s identity.
- Water Dragon Stronghold provided the expedition’s ships. Its Deputy Stronghold Lord promised to leave one or two ships nearby until the party returns, while Mu Song remains away under Seafaring King Pa Ryun’s summons.
- Ju Hwaran’s inherited horse-caravan map and the Escort King’s records enabled the rapid overland journey through Hubei and Sichuan.
- Song Ilseom remains hostile toward the unorthodox faction but recognizes Hwaran’s suffering and is concerned for her safety.
- Yeongin’s hostility toward Han Chinese outsiders stems from the alleged Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau massacre.
- The Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group came from Sichuan, consisted of one woman and nearly thirty men, and all members later died from deadly venom.
- The woman may have been the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, possibly disguised, but the agent’s account does not confirm her likeness.
- The identity of the poisoner, the reason for the massacre, and the fate or purpose of the suspected Southern Heaven Demon Empress remain unresolved.
- The Lord of Heaven has awakened, empowered Dark Heaven’s servants, and declared that the Great War has begun.

### Chapter 620

# Chapter 620

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion confirms that the Poison Flower Pavilion owner is Namho, a non-Han Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent. To conceal their meeting, Namho stages a violent expulsion that destroys the inn, then reunites with the group at a lakeside rendezvous. He gives them a wooden map documenting Nanman’s terrain and tribes over more than fifty years, confirms that he has received warnings about the rift, and agrees to guide them toward their destination. The contact mission and Seeds Planted in Nanman Quest are completed, a new linked Quest is generated, and Namho leaves his home with the group because he believes Dark Heaven will soon bring catastrophe to Nanman.

## Continuity

- Namho is the Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent operating under the cover of the Poison Flower Pavilion in Nanman; he confirms his identity through Thousand-Faced Fox’s code exchange.
- Namho has spent more than fifty years surveying Nanman’s terrain and tribal locations and gives the Fire Dragon Pavilion his wooden map.
- Namho agrees to guide the Fire Dragon Pavilion wherever its Nanman mission requires and leaves his longtime residence with them.
- Namho has received the Pavilion Master’s warning about the rift through a messenger eagle and accepts that Dark Heaven is likely targeting Nanman.
- The Contact with a Hidden Shadow Pavilion Agent mission and Seeds Planted in Nanman Quest are complete, and a new linked Quest has appeared.
- The identity of the woman associated with the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group and the party responsible for poisoning that group remain unresolved.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion’s dangers and objectives beyond the Hidden Shadow Pavilion contact, including the Nanman Beast Palace mission, remain unresolved.

### Chapter 621

# Chapter 621

## Plot

Namho agrees to lead the Fire Dragon Pavilion toward the Nanman Beast Palace, activating the Nanman Beast Palace Chain Quest. The party travels covertly through Nanman's hostile jungles, where Hyuk Mujin is repeatedly poisoned and Taishan survives eating lethal blood-feeding fungus. They avoid the non-Han peoples and conceal their identities because Dark Heaven is believed to be targeting Nanman. After crossing Ailao Mountain and traveling for four days, they finally see the Nanman Beast Palace through the clouds and mist.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion is traveling through Nanman under Namho's guidance toward the Nanman Beast Palace.
- The Nanman Beast Palace Quest is Grade Supreme Peak, restricted to Jin Taekyung and Fire Dragon Pavilion members, with the mission to arrive at the palace still incomplete at chapter's end.
- The party must conceal its identities until reaching the Nanman Beast Palace because Nanman's non-Han peoples are hostile toward Han Chinese and Dark Heaven may accelerate its plans if alerted.
- The Beast Miao King, lord of the Nanman Beast Palace, participated in the Great Faction War and is believed to favor Jeok Cheongang and the Fire Gate Clan.
- Dark Heaven is targeting Nanman, and Taekyung believes the coming disaster and rift are only a matter of time.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 애뇌산 as Ailao Mountain.
- Render 혈생균 as blood-feeding fungus.
- Preserve the dry, teasing banter between Jin Taekyung and Namho during the journey.
### Chapter 622

# Chapter 622

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion reaches the outer territory of the Nanman Beast Palace, a vast pastureland directly governed by the Palace Lord rather than a conventional palace compound. Namho explains that the palace rules a kingdom-like domain spanning five counties and forbids outsiders from entering beyond its fences.

Taishan attempts to catch and eat a calf, forcing Jin Taekyung and the others to restrain him before he damages their relationship with the Nanman Beast Palace. Namho rages over the violation, then reveals that he is an eighty-year-old Miao elder who has spent more than fifty years in Nanman.

Namho launches an emergency signal firework to summon the palace's people, but it falls into the pasture and starts a fire. As the livestock flee, a figure appears beyond the burning hill with the roar of a wild beast.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion has reached the directly administered outer territory of the Nanman Beast Palace, a vast pastureland that serves as the palace's capital-like domain.
- The Nanman Beast Palace governs a kingdom-like territory spanning five counties, and outsiders are forbidden to enter beyond its fences.
- Livestock and other living things within the Nanman Beast Palace's territory are valuable protected property; the party must not touch them without permission.
- Namho is an eighty-year-old Miao elder who has spent more than fifty years in Nanman and is guiding the Fire Dragon Pavilion.
- Namho's emergency signal firework ignited the pasture, and a wild-beast-like figure emerged beyond the flames.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 624

# Chapter 624

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion travels through Nanman's wildly shifting climates under Yayul Mok's escort. Taekyung's careless remarks are understood by Yayul Mok, who studied Han Chinese briefly but can follow it well enough to reprimand him. Taishan bites the ear of the bear carrying him, forcing the group to restrain him just before they reach the Nanman Beast Palace.

The palace's Outer Hall is vast enough to resemble a city, with markets, homes, commerce, manufacturing, and children playing with young beasts. Its residents distrust the visiting Han Chinese because the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau recently massacred a Miao village. Sama Pyo suspects the massacre's timing is connected to a larger scheme, while Song Ilseom refuses to discuss it with him. Taekyung then senses a powerful aura and recognizes the elderly, bare-chested man seated on vine-covered steps as the Beast Miao King.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion has entered the Nanman Beast Palace's enormous Outer Hall under Yayul Mok's escort.
- Yayul Mok understands spoken Han Chinese despite having studied it only briefly and directly reprimands Taekyung for calling Nanman cursed land.
- Taishan's appetite remains a serious liability; he bites the ear of the bear carrying him and must be ordered to stop eating.
- Nanman residents are wary of Han Chinese because the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau massacred a Miao village approximately one month earlier.
- The Beast Miao King is present in the Nanman Beast Palace's Outer Hall and is recognized by Taekyung from the force of his aura before speaking.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 一山有四季, 十里不同天 as “One mountain holds four seasons, and ten li bring a different sky.”
- Retain Beast Miao King for 야수묘왕 and Nanman Beast Palace for 남만야수궁.
- Use Young Palace Lord for 소궁주 and Pavilion Master for 각주인.

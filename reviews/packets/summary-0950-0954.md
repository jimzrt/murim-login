# Chapters 945–949

## Plot

Taekyung subdues Jang Sam after the bandit chief suddenly gains twenty Levels and attacks him in an apparently irrational state. Jang Il says Jang Sam had recently relocated to Anhui and taken over two small strongholds. A silk pouch given to Jang Sam by an unknown traveler in Hubei likely contained a modified Temporary Strength Pill. Taekyung and his companions suspect Dark Heaven is distributing the pills to give ordinary fighters temporary power, at the cost of dangerous side effects and possible addiction. Taekyung sends guides to alert the Nangong Family and Anhui’s City Lord, investigate the pills, and tighten security.

In Hebei, Peng Cheolhu learns that at least ten thousand grassland troops are gathering and heading toward Shanxi, possibly in alliance with Dark Heaven. In Shanxi, Jin Wikyung weighs evacuating Datong and Saneum and retreating to Henan as the approaching force may grow to thirty thousand. Allied leaders gather, including Lee Seowol with three hundred Mount Heng martial artists; Taekyung’s younger brother also emerges from seclusion. Wikyung walks toward the gathered allies and the coming battle.

## Continuity

- Jang Sam is unconscious and being taken to the Nangong Family. His sudden rise from Level 40 to Level 60, apparent loss of reason, and connection to the modified Temporary Strength Pill remain unexplained.
- The unknown traveler who gave Jang Sam the silk pouch, the pill’s exact effects, and the distribution network remain unknown. Taekyung has ordered an investigation and tighter security in Anhui.
- Grassland forces are approaching Shanxi and may number thirty thousand by arrival. The Peng and Jin Families suspect Dark Heaven is involved, but this is unconfirmed.
- Jin Wikyung ordered evacuations around Datong and Saneum and considered retreating to Henan; after allies gathered, he walked toward them rather than immediately carrying out the retreat.
- Lee Seowol has brought three hundred Mount Heng martial artists, and allied leaders have gathered to fight. Taekyung’s younger brother has emerged from seclusion; his name is not given.
- Taekyung said the Murim Alliance’s Fire Dragon Pavilion and a thousand Embroidered Uniform Guards would reach Anhui within a day or two.

- Render 잠력단 as “Temporary Strength Pill” and 강기 as “Force.”
- Render 선비족 as “Xianbei,” 중양절 as “Double Ninth Festival,” and 이동진 as “Moving Formation.”
- Render 장퀴네스 as “Jangquines,” Taekyung’s teasing nickname for Jang Il.

### Chapter 950

# Chapter 950

## Plot

As famine, social unrest, and Dark Heaven’s growing threat unsettle the Great Nation, Song Ho reports that the Jin Family of Taiyuan has rescinded its retreat and that Shanxi is preparing to defend Eight Spring Gorge with roughly fifteen thousand troops against an estimated thirty to forty thousand enemies. Mae Jonghak recognizes that the Jin Family will not be turned back and challenges Song Ho’s purely strategic calculus: the people of Shanxi are fighting to protect more than their homes. News arrives that the Hebei Peng Family, Murong Family, Huashan, and Zhongnan Sect are each sending two thousand reinforcements. Song Ho understands the overlooked principle as chivalry; with the Double Ninth Festival four days away, two thousand martial artists depart the Murim Alliance headquarters for the north.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family of Taiyuan has rescinded its retreat. Shanxi is gathering roughly five thousand martial artists and ten thousand government troops for a stand at Eight Spring Gorge against an estimated thirty to forty thousand advancing enemy forces.
- Mae Jonghak accepts that the Jin Family’s decision cannot be reversed and holds that martial artists must not abandon civilians; the Hebei Peng Family, Murong Family, Huashan, and Zhongnan each send two thousand reinforcements, and two thousand martial artists leave the Murim Alliance headquarters for Shanxi.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 협 as “chivalry” in Mae Jonghak and Song Ho’s exchange; it names the ethical principle of defending others through martial arts.
### Chapter 951

# Chapter 951

## Plot

As a large steppe army rides through the rain toward Shanxi, Temur and the Chinggen riding beside him appear to lead it. Jamukha joins the vanguard with more than twenty thousand tribespeople and commits a thousand Keshik to the lead. In private, Jamukha and the false Chinggen reveal that the real Chinggen was killed in the earlier attack and that the impostor kept Temur alive because his submission made him easy to control. The army expects to cross the border in about a day, before the Fire King and Jin Taekyung can arrive.

## Continuity

- The person currently wearing Chinggen’s face is an impostor; the real Chinggen was killed in the attack on the khans’ gathering.
- Temur is being manipulated by the impostor, who says he kept Temur alive because he was thoroughly submissive and useful in gathering tribespeople.
- Jamukha has joined the advancing steppe army with more than twenty thousand tribespeople and a thousand Keshik; he has ordered some Keshik to lead the vanguard.
- The steppe army expects to cross the Shanxi border in about a day; Jamukha says the Fire King and Jin Taekyung will not arrive for several days.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 자무카 as Jamukha, 텡그리 as Tengri, and 케식 as Keshik.
- The character presented as Chinggen in this chapter is an impostor; do not attribute his private statements or actions to the real Chinggen.
### Chapter 952

# Chapter 952

## Plot

After more than two years in secluded training, Jin Mukyung reunites with Jin Wikyung and offers to serve the Jin Family as its Second Young Master. Wikyung appoints him Commander of the Heaven Shaking Squad and orders him to lead two hundred men against an enemy vanguard detouring around Jeongyang.

The next day, Jamukha learns that the thousand-man vanguard was annihilated, including a hundred Keshik. He kills the sole survivor before hearing the end of the message, and reflects that the invasion may not be as one-sided as expected.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung appointed Jin Mukyung Commander of the Heaven Shaking Squad and ordered him to lead two hundred men against enemy troops detouring around Jeongyang.
- A thousand-man steppe vanguard, including one hundred Keshik, was annihilated before the main army advanced; Jamukha killed its sole survivor.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 진천대 as “Heaven Shaking Squad” and 진천대주 as “Commander of the Heaven Shaking Squad.”
### Chapter 953

# Chapter 953

## Plot

Jin Mukyung returns from a successful strike: his slightly more than two hundred Heaven Shaking Squad members annihilated a thousand enemy vanguard troops, including one hundred elite soldiers, at the cost of twenty-three dead and thirty-seven wounded. Jin Wikyung’s scorched-earth preparations, defenses at Eight Spring Gorge, and the support of thousands of commoners ready to help protect Shanxi are revealed; their loyalty stems in part from the Jin Family’s past aid during famine. As a rider arrives carrying a dead Jin Family martial artist, Jin Wikyung orders everyone to prepare for battle: the enemy has arrived.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung has returned after annihilating the enemy vanguard; the Heaven Shaking Squad suffered twenty-three deaths and thirty-seven wounded.
- Jin Wikyung has evacuated people and troops from northern Shanxi, ordered the near-harvest fields burned, and prepared traps and iron caltrops along detours other than Jeongyang.
- Five earthen forts are being built at Eight Spring Gorge; thousands of commoners have joined the defensive preparations, many out of gratitude for the Jin Family’s past aid.
- The enemy has arrived at Eight Spring Gorge; Jin Wikyung has ordered the defenders to prepare for battle.
- The Emperor remains gravely ill from Blood Soul Gu, and the Divine Physician says the only path to saving him requires him to die once; Taekyung’s quest to remove the Gu and treat him remains unresolved.
- Jang Sam remains unconscious after his sudden rise in level and attack on Taekyung; the source and effects of the improved Temporary Strength Pill remain unknown.
- The Martial God’s identity and connection to the chosen one and the Bow Saint remain unknown.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s papers and silk pouch remain unexplained; the real Chinggen was killed, and an impostor wearing his face continues to accompany Jamukha.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 토성 as “earthen fort” and 철질려 as “iron caltrops.” The carpenter’s remarks in this chapter address Jin Wikyung, not Jin Taekyung.
### Chapter 954

# Chapter 954

## Plot

The steppe army reaches Eight Spring Gorge after enduring scorched fields, poisoned water, and traps. Jamukha rallies his tens of thousands of riders and leads their charge toward the gorge. Temur regrets ignoring the real Chinggen’s warnings, while concealing from the impostor wearing Chinggen’s face that he knows the truth. Facing the oncoming attack, Jin Wikyung steadies his defenders by proposing they celebrate the next Double Ninth Festival together on Mount Heng. Jin Mukyung joins the defense as the first riders burst from the darkness.

## Continuity

- The steppe army has begun its assault on Eight Spring Gorge; the battle’s outcome is unresolved.
- Temur remembers that the real Chinggen warned him of danger and knows the man beside him is Chinggen’s killer and impostor, but pretends not to know.
- Jin Wikyung has promised his defenders they will celebrate the next Double Ninth Festival together on Mount Heng.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 안다의 맹세 as “the anda oath,” a sworn-brother bond.
- Render 한 고조 as Emperor Gaozu of Han, 항우 as Xiang Yu, 유방 as Liu Bang, and 장량 as Zhang Liang.

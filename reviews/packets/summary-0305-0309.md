# Chapters 300–304

## Plot

Jin Taekyung returns alive from the Black Wizard’s Black Forest after the Peace Guild and rescue teams have presumed him dead. Go Jun, acting on Lee Jungryong’s orders, attacks him despite instructions to avoid conflict, but Taekyung defeats him with one blow. Taekyung warns Lee Jungryong that no one can touch the Peace Guild while he is alive, declares himself the Fire King’s successor, and forces Seoul Branch President Lee Woojoong and more than two hundred Hunters to conceal what they witnessed. He later reveals privately that the defeated Named Monster is still alive: the Skeleton Warlord remains in his Inventory, supplies Skeletons for Guild training, and is mockingly called Warlordmon.

The public celebrates Taekyung’s survival and the defeat of a second Named Monster, while his interview becomes an international sensation. He accepts a limited number of lucrative media and advertising offers, secretly uses his increased wealth to secure his family, and moves them into their remodeled former home. The Peace Guild trains against controlled Skeleton forces, though the level difference now produces few gains.

A week after Hayeon’s college entrance exam, Team Leader Choi shows the Guild footage of a Monster Wave near Nanchong in Sichuan Province. A Named Monster Lich, a powerful undead archmage, has devastated the city with overwhelming forces and claimed the surrounding territory. China requests Taekyung’s help specifically at the behest of Xiao Yang, the General Secretary of China’s Central Committee.

## Continuity

- Taekyung survived the Named Monster raid and has hunted two Named Monsters alone.
- Go Jun suffered catastrophic internal injuries and lost consciousness after Taekyung’s single-blow counterattack.
- Taekyung warned Lee Jungryong that the Peace Guild is untouchable while Taekyung lives; Lee’s response remains unknown.
- Lee Woojoong and over two hundred Association Hunters agreed to deny or suppress their memories of Taekyung’s confrontation.
- The Skeleton Warlord is alive, stored in Taekyung’s Inventory when not deployed, and secretly controlled by him. The government accepted its bones and armor fragments as proof of its defeat.
- Warlordmon provides Skeletons for controlled Peace Guild training, under threat that Guild deaths will bring consequences.
- Taekyung’s family lives in the remodeled former home, surrounded by covert security that his mother and Hayeon do not know about.
- Hayeon completed the college entrance exam and believes she missed a perfect score by one English question.
- A Monster Wave near Nanchong has caused approximately three hundred thousand estimated casualties in its first twenty-four hours.
- The responsible Named Monster is a Lich whose power far exceeds the ordinary Liches known during the Great Cataclysm. It claims territory and threatens anyone who enters.
- China deployed more than one thousand Hunters, and Choi has received a request for Taekyung to join the response.
- The Sichuan catastrophe occurred ten days earlier but has been concealed from ordinary worldwide news; the reason remains unresolved.
- The exact role China wants Taekyung to perform, the Lich’s full strength, and the possible spread of its Monster Wave remain unknown.
- International interview requests remain pending; Taekyung has not accepted them despite their potential benefit to the Peace Guild.

# Chapter 305

## Plot

Lee Jungryong visits Go Jun during his recovery and reframes his defeat as merely falling. He promises to give Go Jun greater strength so that Go Jun can defeat Jin Taekyung, reaffirming their Master-and-Disciple bond.

President Baek Hanseong delivers Xiao Yang's unofficial diplomatic letter and China's mercenary contract for the Sichuan Catastrophe. Lee accepts Ares Guild's participation after assessing the Lich's power and the proposed rewards, then learns that the Peace Guild also received an offer.

Baek reveals that Xiao Yang personally required Lee Jungryong, the Ares Guild, and Jin Taekyung to participate. ### Chapter 306

# Chapter 306

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi travel toward China aboard a private Boeing 747-8 VIP jet. Taekyung speaks fluent Chinese, drinks soju, warns Choi to assume the worst, and then sleeps through the flight while Choi explains that increasing mana density may be causing the recent surge in Gate accidents and that the Lich may have undergone the same unexplained evolution as the Skeleton Warlord. After a month away, Taekyung logs back into the Murim and awakens in a Murim Alliance pavilion beside the sleeping Jeok Cheongang. He wakes Hyuk Mujin from an embarrassing dream and throws a flower vase at him, but the door opens at that moment and the vase strikes the arriving Luoyang Strange Physician in the forehead, knocking him unconscious with a concussion.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi have departed for China aboard a Chinese private jet to respond to the Sichuan catastrophe.
- The International Gate Research Institute has found rapidly increasing mana density in many Gates, coinciding with a sharp rise in Gate accidents.
- The Skeleton Warlord evolved for an unknown reason, and the newly appeared Lich may have undergone a similar increase in power; Taekyung expects the confrontation to be extremely difficult.
- Taekyung spent one month away from the Murim and returned by Login; fewer than two shichen passed in the Murim during his absence.
- Jeok Cheongang was asleep in the Murim Alliance pavilion when Taekyung returned.
- Hyuk Mujin had been assigned guard duty but fell asleep while having an erotic dream; Taekyung woke him and mocked him before throwing the vase.
- The Luoyang Strange Physician arrived at dawn to examine Jeok Cheongang and was struck by Taekyung's vase, suffering a concussion.

### Chapter 307

# Chapter 307

## Plot

The Luoyang Strange Physician awakens from his concussion with an unnervingly gentle demeanor, examines the unconscious Jeok Cheongang, and determines that Jeok's qi acupoints are slowly becoming blocked. Jeok has roughly six months left, perhaps a few months more with continued qi treatment, but no known cure. The physician tells Jin Taekyung to find the legendary Divine Physician, then reveals a porcelain shard left by that physician as a clue pointing to Sichuan. The System identifies it as the Trace of the Divine Physician and generates the Quest Find Mr. Shin in Sichuan.

## Continuity

- The Luoyang Strange Physician awakens one shichen after Jin Taekyung's thrown vase caused his concussion; his outwardly gentle behavior still conceals violent threats.
- Jeok Cheongang is not suffering from demonic qi or poisoning, but his qi acupoints will slowly become blocked while he remains unconscious.
- The Luoyang Strange Physician estimates that Jeok Cheongang has six months left, or perhaps a few additional months if his qi continues to be guided through his acupoints.
- The Luoyang Strange Physician does not know Jeok Cheongang's disease or its treatment and directs Jin Taekyung to seek the Divine Physician.
- The Divine Physician has practiced for more than forty years, treated thousands during an epidemic, refuses payment, conceals his identity behind a white veil, and is protected by common people from the imperial palace.
- After spending five years on a medical tour following the Divine Physician's trail, the Luoyang Strange Physician received a porcelain shard bearing a clue that the Divine Physician was in Sichuan.
- The System identifies the porcelain clue as Trace of the Divine Physician and generates the Quest Find Mr. Shin in Sichuan.

### Chapter 308

# Chapter 308

## Plot

The Luoyang Strange Physician tells Jin Taekyung to go to Sichuan and find the Divine Physician, whose medical skill may be the only way to wake Jeok Cheongang. Taekyung accepts the Supreme Peak Quest Find Mr. Shin in Sichuan, which will fail if Jeok dies, and decides to take the unconscious Jeok with him rather than return for him.

Mae Jonghak agrees to write letters to the Sichuan Tang Clan, Emei, and Qingcheng and to provide several people Taekyung requests. The Luoyang Strange Physician reveals that he abandoned a promising official career during a plague, falsified records to turn diverted relief into aid for the sick, and became a physician after being inspired by the Divine Physician. Mae and the physician also discuss Mae's repeated rejection of proposals that he become Murim Alliance Leader after Dark Heaven's appearance.

Taekyung prepares to leave with Jeok Cheongang, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung. Gung Gibang becomes the final member of the small search party despite having learned the Myriad-Li Chasing Wind Movement Technique only to its fifth stage, and the group leaves the pavilion for Sichuan.

## Continuity

- The Divine Physician is currently in Sichuan and is the only known hope of waking Jeok Cheongang.
- The Quest Find Mr. Shin in Sichuan is Supreme Peak Grade; its mission is to find the Divine Physician and receive treatment, and its failure condition is Jeok Cheongang's death.
- Jeok Cheongang has roughly six months remaining; continued qi treatment may extend that by one or two months.
- Jin Taekyung chooses to take the unconscious Jeok Cheongang to Sichuan and search for the Divine Physician before the remaining time expires.
- Mae Jonghak will send letters to the Sichuan Tang Clan, Emei, and Qingcheng and will provide several helpers requested by Taekyung.
- The Sichuan search party consists of Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, Hyuk Mujin, Cheongpung, and Gung Gibang.
- The Luoyang Strange Physician formerly served as a promising court official, left the government during a plague after falsifying relief records to aid the sick, and became a physician inspired by the Divine Physician.
- Mae Jonghak is being considered as a candidate to lead the re-formed Murim Alliance but rejects the position.

### Chapter 309

# Chapter 309

## Plot

Three days after leaving Luoyang, Jin Taekyung uses a Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron chain to bind Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang to himself, then uses ninety years of internal energy to drag them toward Sichuan at extreme speed. Cheongpung follows while eating dumplings, and the party reaches the border between Henan and Shaanxi in two days.

The Yongbong Escort Bureau, led by twenty-one-year-old Ju Hwaran, struggles onward after a four-month trade journey that has already claimed thirty-three lives, including Chief Escort Seok. Hwaran and Chief Escort Heo Jun prepare to camp near Black Stone Mountain, but Heavenly Axe and hundreds of Green Forest bandits emerge from the darkness and demand to inspect the goods before discussing the toll.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's small Sichuan search party consists of Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, Cheongpung, and Gung Gibang, with the unconscious Jeok Cheongang being carried by Taekyung.
- Taekyung uses the Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron chain to connect himself, Hyuk Mujin, and Gung Gibang, and reaches the Henan-Shaanxi border through forced travel powered by ninety years of internal energy.
- Ju Hwaran is twenty-one years old and has led the Yongbong Escort Bureau for two years while her father, Ju Hogun, remains near death after falling into qi deviation.
- Chief Escort Seok is the thirty-third casualty of Ju Hwaran's current escort journey; poison reached his marrow, and Hwaran blames her own judgment for his death and the preceding thirty-two deaths.
- Heo Jun is Ju Hwaran's uncle and the Yongbong Escort Bureau's Chief Escort, supporting her while she leads the exhausted caravan.
- Black Stone Mountain lies on the route to Mount Zhongnan, and Black Stone Stronghold is one of the Green Forest Alliance's Eighteen Strongholds.
- Heavenly Axe is the Peak master and leader of Black Stone Stronghold; he appears with hundreds of Green Forest bandits and confronts the Yongbong Escort Bureau over its goods and toll.

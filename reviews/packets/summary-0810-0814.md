# Chapters 805–809

## Plot

Jin and the Skeleton King battle the monster commanders as the army breaks through the Hunters’ defensive line. Jin kills the Manticore Lord, while Yamamoto kills the Lycanthrope Champion and an ally whose identity is not yet revealed. The Skeleton King defeats the Death Knight Legion Commander. With the flying monsters brought down and the enemy formation broken, the Hunters win the battle and pursue the fleeing army.

The victory costs 185 Hunter lives, and roughly five thousand monsters remain. Jin calls for the exhausted Hunters to regroup before continuing the mission to kill the Prophet. He suspects the Prophet planned the chaos and wants to face him, but the Prophet remains missing. Jin’s injuries worsen into the Broken Body debuff. After less than an hour of recovery, the Hunters prepare to continue; a memory of Michael Silbert prompts an unspoken question in Jin’s mind.

### Chapter 810

# Chapter 810

## Plot

Jin becomes absorbed in questions sparked by his thoughts of Michael Silbert and loses track of nearly thirty minutes. Magic Johnson reminds him that the regrouped Hunters are waiting and urges him to resume the pursuit. Jin decides there is only one path forward, though the truth behind his questions remains unclear.

The Hunters march through the desert after burying their 185 dead and vow to return for the bodies and reunite them with their families. They encounter Ant Lions. Jin learns from one that most fleeing monsters headed west and that their leaders are dead, then kills it, knowing it caused Hunter deaths. When Johnson asks where the monsters went, Jin says east; the Skeleton King watches him wide-eyed.

## Continuity

- The 185 Hunters killed in the battle have been buried in the desert; the survivors vowed to return for their bodies and bring them to their families.
- The Ant Lion Jin interrogated said most fleeing monsters went west and that their leaders were all dead; Jin told Magic Johnson they went east, and the Skeleton King witnessed the lie.
- Jin remains preoccupied with questions about Michael Silbert, but has not shared them; the truth he suspects and his single clue remain unspecified.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magical power distinct from mana.
- Keep the Demon Realm language distinct from other languages.

# Chapter 811

- Jin privately tells the Skeleton King to listen without responding or reacting; the Skeleton King gives no spoken reply.
- Jin spots an eagle near the canyon and blocks its entrance.

### Chapter 812

# Chapter 812

## Plot

Jin Taekyung blocks the canyon exit with the Skeleton King and confronts Magic Johnson, having reviewed the evidence about The Prophet and Michael Silbert. Jin concludes that The Prophet was not the fifth Muninn: one individual had been Muninn for more than thirty years and taught Michael to use magical power.

Jin questions why Johnson helped him and admits he still doubts that a monster could use mana and potions. Johnson answers that he helped because they are friends, then raises his staff as the Skeleton King’s forces fill the canyon.

Jin’s flash blinds the group, letting him strike Yamamoto Genji. As Genji staggers, Jin asks what he is; Genji is revealed to be The Prophet.

## Continuity

- The Prophet is revealed to be Yamamoto Genji, and Jin concludes that The Prophet has been the same Muninn for more than thirty years.
- Jin believes The Prophet taught Michael Silbert to use magical power and cooperated with him toward their respective goals.
- Jin has attacked The Prophet in the canyon; the Skeleton King and hundreds of monsters are present, while the other forces were sent east to avoid involving them.
- Jin remains uncertain whether Magic Johnson is human, despite Johnson's mana use and reference to potions.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magical power distinct from mana.
- Render 지크프리트 바스만 as Siegfried Bassman.
- Preserve Jin’s distinction between the fifth Muninn and the single Muninn he believes has existed for over thirty years.
### Chapter 813

# Chapter 813

## Plot

Jin uses the Truthful Eye on The Prophet and discovers he is a Level 170 Doppelganger titled “The Final Abyss.” The creature reveals that it met Michael Silbert during the 2020 Battle of Paris, made a pact with him, and had him kill the surviving humans in exchange for his own survival. When Jin asks why the Doppelganger wanted to live among humans, a distant tremor interrupts.

## Continuity

- The Prophet is a Level 170 Doppelganger called “The Final Abyss.” It has devoured countless lives and concealed itself for more than thirty years, including under the name Muninn.
- The Doppelganger says it met Michael Silbert on December 25, 2020, during the Battle of Paris. Michael agreed to serve it and personally killed the remaining humans in exchange for being spared.
- Jin applied the single-target Truthful Eye to The Prophet, revealing its identity. The Prophet remains alive, pinned to the cliff and gravely injured; a tremor interrupts the confrontation.
- The Doppelganger has not revealed why it wanted to live among humans. The source of the tremor is unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 도플갱어 as “Doppelganger,” 진실의 눈 as “Truthful Eye,” and 최후의 심연 as “The Final Abyss.”

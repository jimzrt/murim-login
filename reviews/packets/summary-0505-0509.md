## Previous block summary

# Chapters 500–504

## Plot

Jang Taebo accepts his appointment as Master of Ironcraft Hall and takes charge of processing the Water God Dragon’s remains, recruiting renowned artisans and arranging transport under his and Wipeng’s supervision. Zhuge Feng completes the Demon-Sealing Formation, which blocks the mana leaking from the exposed Gate by drawing on the qi of the natural world. Taekyung warns that Dark Heaven caused the incident deliberately and may repeat it; Jin Wikyung identifies Henan and the New Murim Alliance as the next priorities.

The New Murim Alliance is scheduled to be founded at Mount Song in Henan Province in one month. Jin Wikyung explains that its orthodox-centered coalition must also attract the fractured unorthodox factions before Dark Heaven does. He reveals that he proposed the political arrangement that enabled the government-backed purge of Hubei’s dark-path forces, using Hongcheon, the new Provincial Administration Commissioner and Prince Shangshan’s hidden loyal retainer. Taekyung decides to leave Hubei with those close to him and those he hopes will remain beside him.

Mungyeong finds Jeok Cheongang secluded in a cave after seven days and nights without food or water. Jeok’s leaking innate qi has caused progressive lapses in memory and time, and he fears becoming a burden to Taekyung. He asks Mungyeong to protect Taekyung and become his new Master if Jeok is no longer present. Mungyeong instead tells him of a ninja who abandoned his elderly mother, leading Jeok to recognize that leaving Taekyung would be proud self-denial rather than selfless protection.

Jeok accepts Taekyung’s support and remembers that Master and Disciple have always walked side by side. His Heart Demon and the memories binding him break apart, and he rises into a new realm as powerful qi erupts around him. Mungyeong leaves the cave, Taekyung reunites with Jeok after more than a year, and Jeok is persuaded to travel with Jin Wikyung’s party to the New Murim Alliance. Zhuge Feng also recounts how his grandfather’s gamble redirected the Black Wind Corps through Mount Jiuhua, enabling Jeok to annihilate it and earn the title of Fire King.

## Continuity

- The Demon-Sealing Formation currently blocks all mana from the exposed Gate by drawing on natural qi. Its permanent durability and whether equivalent formations can be installed at future Gates remain unresolved.
- Taekyung believes Dark Heaven deliberately planned the Gate incident and that similar incidents will continue. The Lord of Heaven’s identity and connection to the dangerous force Taekyung associates with his original world remain unknown.
- Jang Taebo is Master of Ironcraft Hall and is recruiting renowned artisans to process the Water God Dragon’s remains.
- The New Murim Alliance will be founded at Mount Song in about one month, primarily under the banner of the orthodox Central Plains Murim. Its leaders intend to draw in the fractured unorthodox factions before Dark Heaven can recruit them.
- Hongcheon, Hubei’s new Provincial Administration Commissioner, secretly serves Prince Shangshan. Jin Wikyung proposed the arrangement behind the Hubei dark-path purge.
- Jeok Cheongang’s infirmities began after he left Sichuan. His leaking innate qi caused worsening memory and time loss, but his Heart Demon and the memories binding him have now been expelled.
- Jeok Cheongang has entered a new realm and is expected to travel with Jin Wikyung’s party after his secluded meditation. The lasting abilities and physical effects of his breakthrough remain unresolved.
- Mungyeong remains the Slaughter Saint and former Divine Physician. He has agreed in principle to protect Taekyung, but the precise nature of his future Master-Disciple relationship with Taekyung is not settled.
- Around one hundred Level 5 Mutated Minnows, locally called Blood Fish, were captured near the Gate; the remaining population is unknown, and the fish kill one another.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is traveling toward Yunnan and expects further deaths. Jin Mukyung remains secluded after losing to Cheongpung.
- Zhuge Gonghu’s historical gamble sent the thousand-man Black Wind Corps through Mount Jiuhua, where Jeok destroyed it and became known as the Fire King.

# Chapter 505

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang reveals that he has achieved Returned to Youth, appearing as a tall, muscular, red-haired man. He confronts Zhuge Feng over the Demonic Cult's attack on Mount Jiuhua and demands that the Zhuge Clan perform at least as well in the approaching war as it did during the Great Faction War. Taekyung's group then departs for Henan and the New Murim Alliance.

At Wudang, Sect Leader Hyeoncheon learns that the group has left and receives a letter concerning the Murim Alliance, recognizing it as evidence of an approaching war comparable to the Great Faction War. A pursuit party reports capturing the Killing Ghost, but the remains they bring are revealed to be something neither beast nor human, giving Hyeoncheon another reason to travel to Henan.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang has entered a new realm and achieved Returned to Youth; he now appears as a tall, muscular, red-haired man while retaining his gruff and threatening personality.
- Jeok Cheongang has demanded that the Zhuge Clan support the approaching war at least as effectively as it did during the Great Faction War.
- Jin Taekyung's party, now accompanied by Jeok Cheongang, has departed for Henan and the New Murim Alliance.
- Hyeoncheon is the current Sect Leader of Wudang and Hyeongong's Senior Brother, seven years older than him.
- The Murim Alliance's formation is tied to an approaching war that Hyeoncheon considers comparable to the Great Faction War.
- A Wudang pursuit party claims to have captured the Killing Ghost, but the remains attributed to the Killing Ghost are neither beast nor human and their nature is unresolved.

# Chapter 506

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's party continues toward Henan by ship along the Yangtze. Mu Song estimates they will reach Xixia in southwestern Henan within ten days if the current weather holds, after which they must continue overland.

Mu Song says he wants to avenge Uncle Hwang and the other dead and aid the Murim Alliance, but he is only a Stronghold Lord under the Yangtze River Channel League and cannot decide its major policies; that authority belongs to his Master and Alliance Leader, the Seafaring King.

Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang consider the Yangtze River Channel League and Green Forest Alliance potential threats from behind if they choose against the Murim Alliance. Mungyeong then appears and corrects Taekyung's count of two to four.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's party is traveling by ship along the Yangtze toward Xixia in southwestern Henan; after reaching the end of the tributary, they must continue overland.
- Mu Song personally wants to help the Murim Alliance, but the Seafaring King alone decides the Yangtze River Channel League's major matters.
- The Yangtze River Channel League and Green Forest Alliance originated as bandit organizations, were recognized after supporting orthodox Murim during the Great Faction War, and may become a rear threat if they oppose the Murim Alliance.
- Mungyeong has appeared with a composed expression and says there are four rather than two relevant parties or choices, without explaining what he means.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 서협 as Xixia, the place in southwestern Henan.
- Render 삼강오륜 as Three Bonds and Five Relationships, with a footnote explaining the Confucian framework.

# Chapter 507

## Plot

Jin Taekyung identifies the four powers Mungyeong implied: the Yangtze River Channel League, the Green Forest Alliance, the North Sea Ice Palace, and the Nanman Beast Palace. Jeok Cheongang explains that the latter two Outer Murim factions remained neutral or supported orthodox Murim during the Great Faction War, with the Nanman Beast Palace owing goodwill to the Fire Gate Clan after its Fifth Sect Leader destroyed the Five Poisons Sect and enabled the Beast Palace to dominate Nanman.

Jeok Cheongang believes the Nanman Beast Palace may support the New Murim Alliance but doubts the North Sea Ice Palace will participate. Mu Song must determine how the Yangtze River Channel League will respond, and Mungyeong ends by asking whether Mu Song knows his true identity.

## Continuity

- The four powers potentially affecting the New Murim Alliance are the Yangtze River Channel League, Green Forest Alliance, North Sea Ice Palace, and Nanman Beast Palace.
- Jeok Cheongang has met the Beast Miao King several times and believes the Beast Miao King will probably support orthodox Murim because of the Fire Gate Clan's historical goodwill toward Nanman.
- The Fire Gate Clan's Fifth Sect Leader destroyed the Five Poisons Sect more than two hundred years ago; the Nanman Beast Palace then absorbed Poison Valley and conquered Nanman.
- The North Sea Ice Palace remains isolationist, and Jeok Cheongang expects little from its response to the New Murim Alliance.
- Mungyeong knows or suspects Jeok Cheongang, Mu Song, and Taekyung understand more about his identity than his medical-apprentice persona reveals.

### Chapter 509

# Chapter 509

## Plot

The Linked Quest Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2 begins and is forcibly accepted. Mungyeong reveals that Taekyung’s rough movement across the Yangtze stems from the Fire Gate Clan’s chronic problem of excessively violent internal energy, then demonstrates that Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water measures internal-energy stability and precision.

Mungyeong sets Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water as Taekyung’s next training method. Mastering finer control should reduce internal-energy consumption, strengthen amplification, and allow further technical growth without new enlightenment. The training begins immediately and must continue for ten days, or until the swift ship reaches Henan; the System starts the countdown at 9 days, 23 hours, 59 minutes, and 59 seconds.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is training Taekyung to stabilize and refine the violent internal energy produced by the Fire Gate Divine Technique.
- Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2 requires Taekyung to practice Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water behind the swift ship for ten days, with the duration dependent on the journey to Henan.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain the established title Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2 and render the System’s forced acceptance and countdown literally.
- Use oar for 노, throwing blade for 비도, stern for 선미, fire qi for 화기, and stagnation for 답보.

# Chapters 320–324

## Plot

At Tengwang Pavilion, Jin Taekyung confronts Hwangbo Eom, the Taeeul Merciless Sword, and accuses the Zhongnan Sect of using a substituted Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng shipment to destroy the Yongbong Escort Bureau. Hwangbo threatens Taekyung and orders him to withdraw, but Taekyung refuses and protects Ju Hwaran.

Taekyung’s preparations bring Xi’an Beggars’ Sect records and Lower District Sect intelligence to the pavilion. Wolhwa confirms that she accepted his commission, while Heukgeol and Gung Gibang mobilize their organizations. The records show that Zhongnan covertly manipulated the Yongbong Escort Bureau’s failures for two years, including the failed Geumo Merchant Guild transaction and the disappearance of the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng.

Hwangbo responds by attacking Taekyung and ordering the Taeeul Sword Unit to seal the pavilion. Hyuk Sopyung obeys after Hwangbo produces the Zhongnan Sect Leader’s Command Token. Hwangbo also threatens the seriously ill Jeok Cheongang and the Fire Gate Clan. Taekyung orders the others to prevent anyone from leaving and fights Hwangbo inside the pavilion.

Although Taekyung remains below the Supreme Peak realm, his extraordinary physical abilities and Fire Gate techniques allow him to counter Hwangbo’s Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords, Taeeul Light-Dividing Sword, and Taeeul Formless Sword. During the duel, Taekyung’s Flamefire Path advances to seven stars. Applying Jeok Cheongang’s instruction to see with his heart, he senses Hwangbo’s invisible attacks, then destroys Hwangbo’s treasured sword with Heavenly Strike and gravely wounds him. Taekyung condemns Hwangbo for insulting Jeok Cheongang, but the confrontation’s immediate consequences remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- Hwangbo Eom is gravely injured, and his treasured sword was destroyed by Heavenly Strike. He remains alive; his next action and Zhongnan’s response are unknown.
- Taekyung’s Flamefire Path is now seven stars. He is still below Supreme Peak but can defeat Supreme Peak masters through his physical ability, martial skill, and Fire Gate techniques.
- Taekyung has publicly identified himself as the Fire Gate Clan’s nineteenth successor.
- Hwangbo knows Jeok Cheongang’s illness is serious and has threatened Taekyung and the Fire Gate Clan.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious and concealed beneath leather on Taekyung’s pack frame in a suspended-animation-like state.
- Hyuk Sopyung and the thirty-member Taeeul Sword Unit are sealing Tengwang Pavilion’s entrance under the Zhongnan Sect Leader’s Command Token.
- Taekyung has ordered Baek Museong, Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, Heukgeol, Wolhwa, Hyuk Mujin, Ju Hwaran, and the other attendees to prevent anyone from leaving.
- Wolhwa is the Shaanxi Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect and is providing intelligence and personnel under Taekyung’s commission.
- Heukgeol leads the Xi’an Beggars’ Sect branch as a three-knot disciple. Gung Gibang is the sect’s unique eight-knot Successor Beggar; they have been close friends since childhood.
- Beggars’ Sect and Lower District Sect records establish that Zhongnan manipulated the Yongbong Escort Bureau’s failures for two years. The specific participants, the timing of the ginseng substitution, and the full scope of the scheme remain unknown.
- Ju Hwaran suspected Zhongnan’s interference but could not oppose a member of the Nine Sects and One Gang. She intends to postpone accepting Taekyung’s praise until the escort mission is complete.
- The Yongbong Escort Bureau must still complete its current escort mission despite the exposed conspiracy, lost escorts, and Hwangbo’s attack.

# Chapter 325

## Plot

Jin Taekyung publicly defeats and severely injures Hwangbo Eom after the Taeeul Merciless Sword launches the first attack. When the Taeeul Sword Unit moves to intervene, Baek Museong, Ju Hwaran, Gung Gibang, Heo Jun, Song Ilseom, Heukgeol, Wolhwa, Cheongpung, and Hyuk Mujin prevent the confrontation from escalating.

Hyuk Sopyung orders his junior disciples to stand down and openly acknowledges Zhongnan's conspiracy against the Yongbong Escort Bureau, its refusal to admit wrongdoing, and its attempt to kill Taekyung. He apologizes on behalf of the sect and asks Taekyung to spare Hwangbo Eom; Taekyung agrees after striking Hwangbo once more.

At the ruined Tengwang Pavilion, Ju Hwaran demands that Zhongnan void the contract and pay the contractual penalty. Hwangbo Eom initially refuses all compensation, but Taekyung's repeated slaps force him to agree to pay.

- Tengwang Pavilion has been substantially destroyed by the duel, and Zhongnan disciples are attempting to conceal the incident from the gathered Xi'an commoners.
- Taekyung's allies from Huashan, the Beggars' Sect, the Lower District Sect, the Yongbong Escort Bureau, and the Jin Family stood with him against Zhongnan's intervention.

- The Zhongnan Sect's manipulation of the Yongbong Escort Bureau's failures is now admitted by Hyuk Sopyung, but the individual conspirators and the shipment substitution remain unidentified.
- Jeok Cheongang remains concealed beneath leather at Tengwang Pavilion in a suspended-animation-like state.
- Taekyung remains publicly identified as the Fire Gate Clan's nineteenth successor and has advanced the Flamefire Path to seven stars.
- Taekyung remains below the Supreme Peak realm but can defeat Supreme Peak masters through his extraordinary physical strength, martial ability, and Fire Gate techniques.

### Chapter 327

# Chapter 327

## Plot

Ju Hwaran confronts Song Ilseom over records showing that ten of the fifteen escort missions under his responsibility failed during the past two years. Song recognizes the evidence, acknowledges that she has exceeded his expectations, and reveals that he deliberately concealed his true ability; Hwaran then identifies Chief Escort Heo Jun as the real traitor and attacks him.

Heo Jun admits that he accepted bribes and secretly colluded with the Zhongnan Sect for two years. He drugged the bureau's food with 몽혼제, stole the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng, and helped sabotage the Yongbong Escort Bureau while concealing his actions behind fabricated mistakes.

Heo Jun begs for his life and calls his betrayal a mistake, but Ju Hwaran rejects that excuse, declaring that his actions were choices carrying responsibility. She separates herself from her father's identity as Junzi Sword Ju Hogun, claims the identity Dagger Hidden Flower Ju Hwaran, and strikes at Heo Jun again.

## Continuity

- Heo Jun's betrayal of the Yongbong Escort Bureau and two-year collusion with the Zhongnan Sect are exposed; he accepted bribes, stole thousands of silver nyang, drugged the escorts, and replaced the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng.
- Song Ilseom was targeted for execution and false blame by Heo Jun, but he is not the traitor; he concealed exceptional strength for ten years and is assessed by Jin Taekyung as stronger than the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes.
- Ju Hwaran has stabbed Heo Jun and rejected their former uncle-and-niece bond, but his fate after her final sword strike remains unresolved.

### Chapter 329

# Chapter 329

## Plot

Song Ilseom reveals that his grandmother was the surviving child of the Guangdong Chen Family rescued by Ju Gongsan. She fled with infant Song Pyosan, later told Song Ilseom the family history through the jade hairpin, and died when he was ten. After her death, Song Ilseom survived as a sword boy and developed his martial ability on battlefields, eventually becoming the Soul-Chasing Guest, who won 102 life-and-death duels.

After sharing his harsh past, Song Ilseom explains that the Guangdong Chen Family’s martial arts were lost when the family was destroyed. Ju Hwaran, Gung Gibang, and the others react to his identity while Taekyung and Cheongpung tease him about his reputation and strength.

Taekyung’s party leaves the Yongbong Escort Bureau after a meal and a drunken farewell. Ju Hwaran gives Taekyung a Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng and admits that she fears she may not lead well; Taekyung tells her that she need not succeed perfectly or force herself to stay strong. After departing, he reveals that he possesses the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng, shocking Hyuk Mujin and Cheongpung.

## Continuity

- Song Ilseom is Song Pyosan’s son and was known ten years earlier as the Soul-Chasing Guest; he has won 102 life-and-death duels.
- Song Ilseom’s grandmother was the surviving child of the Guangdong Chen Family rescued by Ju Gongsan; she fled with infant Song Pyosan, gave Song Ilseom the jade hairpin and family history, and died of illness when he was ten.
- The Guangdong Chen Family’s martial arts were lost when the family was burned, and Song Ilseom developed his own ability through battlefield experience after working as a sword boy.
- Ju Hwaran remains the Yongbong Escort Bureau’s Young Bureau Head and gave Taekyung a Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng as compensation and reassurance.
- Taekyung’s party has departed the Yongbong Escort Bureau for Sichuan with Jeok Cheongang unconscious on a pack frame; Hyuk Mujin carries the drunken Cheongpung.
- Taekyung possesses the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng and had forgotten to place it in his inventory.

## Plot

He draws White Flame through Seizing an Object Through Empty Space, releasing a fire dragon from its spearhead as the confrontation continues.

# Chapter 369

## Plot

Five days after the Three-Gate Bloodbath, Sichuan martial forces search for the surviving Third Fiend. Hwangso of the Gongdao Sect resents the mission, while his Senior Brother insists that every assigned area be searched repeatedly.

The Third Fiend, who led the attack on Emei, escaped after the intervention of the Beggars' Sect, Emei's Seven Fairies, and the presumed-dead Extinction Divine Nun. He doubled back toward Chengdu in disguise, but Mungyeong impersonated First Fiend to lure him to a cliff, severed his wrist, and revealed himself as the Third Fiend's enemy.

## Continuity

- The Three-Gate Bloodbath devastated the Tang Clan, Qingcheng, and Emei five days earlier, but their defenders and allied forces repelled Dark Heaven's assault.
- The Third Fiend is the Qilian brother who attacked Emei; he escaped the battle and is now intercepted by Mungyeong after being pursued through Sichuan.
- Extinction Divine Nun, Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun's only Senior Aunt, was believed dead after withdrawing from the world thirty years ago but is alive and remains a Supreme Peak master.
- Mungyeong used a disguise of First Fiend to approach the fleeing Third Fiend and severed his wrist; the Third Fiend's fate after the confrontation is unresolved.

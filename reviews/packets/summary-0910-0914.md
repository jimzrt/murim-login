# Chapters 905–909

## Plot

The imperial banquet battle intensifies. Jeok Cheongang clashes with Cang Gong, revealed as the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, while Jin Taekyung kills Ma Sanbao and the Golden Ox Palace commander, who served the Lord of Heaven. The Emperor cannot intervene directly, and So Gyo says she is watching Taekyung; her identity and allegiance remain unclear. Jeong Hogun and the Embroidered Uniform Guard protect Taekyung and declare for the Emperor.

Taekyung uses the unknown power of his Middle Dantian to stop and redirect arrows, then turns to rescue Hyuk Mujin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion members from a large force of black-clad pursuers. He reunites with the group and unleashes Fire Dragon’s Single Tail, a devastating blue-white fire attack. The strike leaves him nearly out of internal energy and struggling to stand. As the smoke clears, the group sees something that may explain the pursuers’ urgency and Ma Sanbao’s confidence in the rebellion, but the chapter ends before it is identified.

- More than a thousand black-clad pursuers appeared to remain after Taekyung’s attack. Their identity, capabilities, and the scene revealed in the smoke remain unknown.
- Taekyung has killed Ma Sanbao and the Golden Ox Palace commander, a servant of the Lord of Heaven.
- Taekyung can use the unknown power of his Middle Dantian to halt and redirect arrows.
- Jeok Cheongang is fighting Cang Gong, the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord; the outcome is unknown.
- Jeong Hogun and the Embroidered Uniform Guard have declared for the Emperor and protected Taekyung.
- So Gyo’s identity and allegiance remain unknown. The Emperor and Cang Gong have not revealed all their forces.

# Chapter 910

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung’s Scorching Yang Qi attack, roughly half of the first hundred black-clad pursuers rise again despite catastrophic burns, showing no pain or signs of life. Taekyung recognizes them as undead and orders the group to run as an army of the dead advances.

During his fight with the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, Jeok Cheongang recalls Hong Dao’s wager that he would surpass the Three Saints. Realizing his current realm is not his final limit, Jeok resolves to keep striving and strikes the Demon Lord with a blaze greater than any he has shown before.

## Continuity

- The black-clad pursuers include undead who can keep moving after devastating Scorching Yang Qi injuries; they are advancing on Taekyung’s group.
- Jeok Cheongang is fighting the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord and has realized he can strive beyond his current realm; he has unleashed a dramatically greater blaze.

# Chapter 911

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang’s breakthrough gives him the strength to overwhelm and kill the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord. He says he vowed to kill every member of Dark Heaven after Hong Dao’s death; as he turns from the fallen Demon Lord, an inexplicable cold pain suddenly sweeps through his body.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang has advanced beyond the Three Saints, becoming the fourth star, and has killed the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord in their duel.
- Jeok Cheongang remains committed to killing every member of Dark Heaven in vengeance for Hong Dao.
- Immediately after killing the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, Jeok Cheongang experiences sudden, unexplained cold pain throughout his body.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain “fourth star” for the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s description of Jeok Cheongang’s place beyond the Three Saints.
- Render 극염 as “Extreme Flame,” distinguishing it from the earlier “Extreme Yang.”
### Chapter 912

# Chapter 912

## Plot

The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, believed dead, returns and grievously wounds Jeok Cheongang before they clash in a devastating exchange. Jin Taekyung shields the Fire Dragon Pavilion members from the shock wave while Jeok protects them from the blast. The Demon Lord reveals that he was once part of the Maoshan Sect, whose members died resisting the forced relocation of the capital, and Ma Sanbao returns alive as his disciple. As the undead army breaches the banquet hall, Jeok and the Demon Lord face each other again.

## Continuity

- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord survived a seemingly fatal death and can no longer be described as human; he was formerly of the Maoshan Sect, whose members were killed resisting the capital’s relocation.
- Ma Sanbao is alive and is the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s disciple.
- Jeok Cheongang is gravely injured after the Demon Lord’s surprise attack but continues fighting; Jin Taekyung and Jeok each shielded the Fire Dragon Pavilion members from danger.
- The undead army breached the stone wall and entered the grand banquet hall.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain “Maoshan Sect” for 모산파 and “undead” for 언데드.
### Chapter 913

# Chapter 913

## Plot

As the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord rings a bell that raises thousands of powerful, agile corpses, Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang fight to protect the survivors. Jeok’s flames can burn the risen dead to ash, but the human formation is collapsing as the Demon Lord advances toward the Emperor.

The Demon Lord recalls being born into a time of war, losing his family to conscription and violence, and finding happiness among the Maoshan Sect before it was destroyed. He asks the Emperor what his grandfather, Taizu, did to the sect.

## Continuity

- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s bell sends a sound wave that raises the dead; the risen corpses move with their living agility and do not fear injury or death.
- Ma Sanbao is among the dead following the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord; he is no longer alive as previously recorded.
- Jeok Cheongang remains internally injured, but his light-flames are still powerful enough to burn the risen corpses to ash; he and Jin Taekyung fight together against the encroaching dead.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord says he was born during the age of chaos, lost his father and two brothers to conscription and his mother to soldiers’ violence, and later found happiness after joining the Maoshan Sect as a disciple.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord confronts the Emperor about what Taizu did to the Maoshan Sect; the battle in the banquet hall continues.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “undead” for Jin Taekyung’s general description and “jiangshi” for Jeok Cheongang’s Maoshan-related term; keep the terms distinct.
- Render the bell’s mind-reaching effect as a sound wave that raises the dead.
### Chapter 914

# Chapter 914

## Plot

The Emperor recalls the Maoshan Rebellion: Taizu ordered the sect destroyed after it resisted the relocation of the capital, but one survivor endured. The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord reveals that his family and the Maoshan Sect were taken from him, declares that the rulers and the world betrayed him, and explains how he used the Cang Gong identity to gain power within the Great Nation.

The Demon Lord raises more than a thousand dead Imperial Guards and advances on the Emperor. So Gyo reveals immense power, halts his attack, and proves dangerous enough to make him hesitate. As the undead press forward, Baek Yeon and So Gyo defend the Emperor; then Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung return to face the Demon Lord.

## Continuity

- Taizu ordered the Maoshan Sect destroyed after it resisted the relocation of the capital to Nanjing; the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord survived the massacre and holds the rulers and the world responsible for the loss of his family, Master, and fellow disciples.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord became a eunuch to infiltrate the Great Nation, accepted the Lord of Heaven's agent's offer, and used the Cang Gong identity to become a power second only to the Emperor.
- So Gyo revealed her immense power, comparable to Jeok Cheongang's, and stopped the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord from attacking the Emperor; her identity remains unknown.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord raised more than a thousand dead Imperial Guards and advanced on the Emperor; Baek Yeon and So Gyo are defending him.
- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung have returned to confront the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord; Ma Sanbao remains among the risen dead following his Master.
- Jeok Cheongang still suffers unexplained cold pain.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “undead” as Jin Taekyung’s general term distinct from “jiangshi,” Jeok Cheongang’s Maoshan-related term.

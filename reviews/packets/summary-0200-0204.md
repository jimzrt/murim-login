## Previous block summary

# Chapters 195–199

## Plot

Rumors spread that Jin Taekyung is the Fire King’s Disciple and heir to the Fire Gate Clan. Jeok Cheongang reveals that the Unnamed Sword is actually the Fire Heaven Sword and that his public claim about it being the clan’s sacred treasure was a calculated lie to deter the Zhongnan Sect. He also tells Taekyung that he found no remains at Jeongyang and has finally accepted his grief over Jangcheon.

After Taekyung insults him over the partly burned Flame Divine Palm manual, Jeok beats him severely. While treating Taekyung, Jeok performs an abbreviated cleansing of the sinews and washing of the marrow, discovers Taekyung’s extraordinarily balanced physique, and suspects he may possess the legendary Heavenly Martial Physique. Jin Wikyung confirms that Taekyung advanced from Third Rate to the beginning of the Peak realm in only three months.

At the extended Jin Family banquet, Ak Bulgun of the Shandong Yue Family offers Taekyung special admission to Heaven’s Gate Temple. Jeok interrupts, publicly declares himself the eighteenth Sect Leader of the Fire Gate Clan, accepts Taekyung as his Disciple, and names him heir to the clan’s orthodox lineage. The declaration protects Taekyung but ends his opportunity to attend Heaven’s Gate Temple or take another master. Later, Taekyung confronts Jeok over the decision, and Jeok asks whether he objects to having him as a master.

## Continuity

- The former Unnamed Sword is the Fire Heaven Sword, the beloved weapon of the Fire Gate Clan’s tenth Sect Leader, forged from Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.
- Jeok Cheongang’s claim that the sword was the Fire Gate Clan’s sacred treasure was a deliberate public deception intended to deter the Zhongnan Sect and protect Taekyung and the Jin Family.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly declared himself the eighteenth Sect Leader of the Fire Gate Clan, accepted Jin Taekyung as his Disciple, and named him heir to the clan’s orthodox lineage.
- Jeok said the declaration was meant to ensure that no one would dare approach or harm Taekyung. Whether he will genuinely teach Taekyung remains unresolved.
- Taekyung’s public status as Jeok’s Disciple prevents him from entering Heaven’s Gate Temple or becoming another master’s Disciple.
- Ak Bulgun is a spear Instructor at Heaven’s Gate Temple and a member of the Shandong Yue Family; he had offered Taekyung special admission before Jeok’s declaration.
- Jeok’s abbreviated treatment increased Taekyung’s Muscles and Bones and Sinews and Meridians by 5 each, and Strength, Stamina, and Agility by 1 each.
- Jeok suspects Taekyung possesses the Heavenly Martial Physique, but this is unconfirmed. Only someone who inherits the Fire Gate Clan’s legacy can draw out Fire Heaven Sword’s full power, and Taekyung’s ability to do so is unknown.
- Taekyung remains a Level 73 Peak Master with seventy allocated stat points and only three months of martial-arts training.
- Jang Taebo’s commissioned Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron spear is unfinished, and the Treasured Jade remains missing.
- Dark Heaven’s objective, the reason for targeting Shanxi, and the information held by the three surviving remnants remain unresolved. Jin Wikyung suspects the attack was only the beginning.
- The consequences of Woo Hwangtae’s conflict with Chulwoo and the Jin Family, and whether Song Il will honor his pledge after returning to Zhongnan, remain unresolved.
- Lee Seowol and the Mount Heng Sword Sect remain vassals of the Jin Family; Chulwoo remains in love with Seowol.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 화천검 as “Fire Heaven Sword,” replacing the earlier provisional “Unnamed Sword” once its true name is revealed.
- Render 열화문 as “Fire Gate Clan,” 적통 as “orthodox lineage,” 천무지체 as “Heavenly Martial Physique,” and 천무학관 as “Heaven’s Gate Temple.”
- Preserve the distinction between Jeok’s deliberate public lie about the “Fire Gate Clan’s sacred treasure” and the sword’s actual identity.
- Treat Jeok’s public acceptance of Taekyung as binding from Chapter 199 onward, while preserving the unresolved question of whether he will genuinely train him.
- Retain “cleansing the sinews and washing the marrow” for the constitution-improving treatment.

### Chapter 200

# Chapter 200

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang identifies Jin Taekyung as possessing the Heavenly Martial Physique, a rare martial constitution that enables exceptionally rapid mastery without the short-lived weakness of the Nine Yin Severed Meridians. Taekyung privately attributes his balanced constitution and progress to the System, conceals that fact, and allows Jeok to mistake him for an acquired version of the Heavenly Martial Physique.

Jeok repeatedly hints that he wants Taekyung to learn martial arts from him, then agrees to personally oversee Taekyung’s training until he reaches a certain level while continuing to deny that they are truly master and Disciple. For appearances, Taekyung begins addressing him as Master, and Jeok instructs him to use Old Master when they are alone. After Taekyung leaves, Jeok recalls that no one has called him Master for ten years and is reminded of his dead Disciple.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang believes Jin Taekyung possesses the Heavenly Martial Physique and regards his rapid progress as evidence of an extraordinary constitution.
- Jeok Cheongang agrees to oversee Taekyung’s martial-arts training and plans to remain until Taekyung reaches a certain level of progress, although he continues to deny that they are truly master and Disciple.
- Taekyung’s System-generated physical balance and stat growth resemble an acquired Heavenly Martial Physique; he continues to conceal the System.
- Taekyung addresses Jeok Cheongang as Master, and as Old Master when they are alone.
- Jeok Cheongang has not been called Master for ten years, and Taekyung’s appearance reminds him of his dead Disciple.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 구음절맥 as “Nine Yin Severed Meridians.”
- Render 사부님 as “Master” and 노야 as “Old Master,” preserving the private distinction.
- Preserve Jeok Cheongang’s denial that they are truly master and Disciple despite his agreement to oversee Taekyung’s training.
### Chapter 201

# Chapter 201

## Plot

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng report that the gathering is proceeding smoothly: smaller and mid-sized sects have pledged loyalty, the authorities and merchant world are cooperating, and the Jin Family's warehouses are overflowing with wealth. Seok Family Manor, one of the world's greatest merchant houses, offers generous cooperation in Shanxi, which Wikyung accepts pending review of the details. Chief Ha also asks to meet Jeok Cheongang, but Jeok has refused every private audience and nearly crippled a subordinate sent to request one.

Before dawn, Jeok Cheongang wakes Jin Taekyung with cold water and takes him to a nearby mountain to begin training. He creates the Peak-Grade Quest One Step Back, requiring Taekyung to make him retreat one step within thirty minutes. Taekyung launches a sustained spear assault that astonishes Jeok with its speed, strength, and battle-hardened technique, but Jeok ultimately catches, crushes, and melts the spearhead with his flame-covered hands; the challenge remains incomplete.

## Continuity

- The current gathering has secured the loyalty of smaller and mid-sized sects, while imperial authorities and the Shanxi merchant world actively cooperate with the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- Seok Family Manor has proposed entering the Shanxi merchant world with the Jin Family's cooperation; Jin Wikyung accepted the generous proposal in principle, with detailed terms still to be reviewed.
- Chief Ha, one of Seok Family Manor's five Outer Stewards, has been unable to obtain a private audience with Jeok Cheongang.
- Jeok Cheongang has begun personally training and evaluating Jin Taekyung at a nearby mountain.
- The Peak-Grade One Step Back Quest requires Jin Taekyung to make Jeok Cheongang retreat one step within thirty minutes; its Reward and Failure conditions remain unknown.
- Jeok Cheongang considers Taekyung's combat ability extraordinary for someone who has practiced martial arts for only three months, while Taekyung has not yet forced him to retreat.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일보 후퇴 as “One Step Back” and preserve it as the Peak-Grade Quest title.
- Render 하 총관 as “Chief Ha” and 외총관 as “Outer Steward.”
- Render 스승님 as “Master” in Jeok Cheongang's deliberately performative master-Disciple language.
### Chapter 202

# Chapter 202

## Plot

Taekyung's attacks fail to move Jeok Cheongang until he tricks Jeok into agreeing to defend without attacking. Taekyung then channels all forty-five years of his internal energy into One Annihilation, devastating the clearing and forcing Jeok exactly five steps backward.

Taekyung collapses after exhausting his strength and internal energy. Jeok catches him, channels warm internal energy into him, admits that he lost, and watches over him as he falls asleep. Jeok privately compares Taekyung's potential with the young Martial God and wishes to help the sleeping dragon obtain its dragon pearl and become a Heavenly Dragon.

## Continuity

- The Peak-Grade One Step Back Quest is complete: after Jeok Cheongang agreed to defend only, Taekyung's One Annihilation forced him back five steps.
- Taekyung exhausted all his strength and internal energy, collapsed, and received internal energy from Jeok Cheongang; Jeok admitted defeat.
- Jeok Cheongang now desires to guide Taekyung toward becoming a Heavenly Dragon, though the chapter establishes no specific future training plan beyond his current oversight.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 탄지공 as “finger-flicking technique,” 천마 as “Heavenly Demon,” and 천룡 as “Heavenly Dragon.”
### Chapter 203

# Chapter 203

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens after completing One Step Back and receives two level-ups plus 20 Bonus Points. Jeok Cheongang begins Taekyung's next training by immobilizing him against a boulder, feeding him three Scorching Yang Qi elixirs, and using his internal energy to open and cleanse Taekyung's acupoints.

The elixirs rapidly expel Taekyung's turbid qi, and Jeok realizes that Taekyung may be capable of opening the Conception and Governor Vessels. Jeok warns that the attempt could cause unbearable pain or death and asks Taekyung to blink twice if he consents. Taekyung appears to blink twice, so Jeok begins the dangerous procedure; the System then updates Acupoint Opening and creates the Conception and Governor Vessels Quest, but the result is not yet shown.

## Continuity

- Completing One Step Back grants Taekyung a large EXP reward, two level-ups, and 20 Bonus Points.
- Jeok Cheongang deliberately selects three elixirs containing pure Scorching Yang Qi so Taekyung can begin learning the Fire Gate Clan's martial arts.
- Jeok immobilizes Taekyung with acupoint sealing and rope before feeding him the elixirs and using his internal energy to open hundreds of acupoints.
- Taekyung's Heavenly Martial Physique allows him to absorb and circulate the elixirs' more-than-thirty years of energy with extraordinary speed while expelling large amounts of turbid qi.
- Jeok decides to attempt opening Taekyung's Conception and Governor Vessels after warning that the procedure carries a risk of death; Taekyung signals consent by blinking twice.
- The Conception and Governor Vessels procedure has begun, but its outcome remains unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 혈도 타통 as “Acupoint Opening” for the Quest title.
- Render 화령초 as “Fire Spirit Grass,” 홍화초 as “Red Flower Grass,” and 염적초 as “Flame Red Grass.”
- Render 탁기 as “turbid qi” and retain “Scorching Yang Qi” for 열양지기.
### Chapter 204

# Chapter 204

## Plot

Jin Taekyung endures Jeok Cheongang's forced opening of the Conception Vessel, then the Governor Vessel. The System confirms that both vessels have been opened, the Conception and Governor Vessels Quest is complete, and Taekyung collapses after exhausting his mental strength.

Jeok Cheongang carries the unconscious Taekyung and reflects that Taekyung's perseverance proves his mental strength matches his physique. Jeok concludes that the Heavenly Martial Physique chose its master well and suggests that an anticipated event may occur sooner than expected, though he says he needs to endure for several more years.

Ak Bulgun arrives at Jin Wikyung's office around noon. He explains that Heaven's Gate Temple has strict rules and that even the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes may be expelled for failing to return on time. Jin Wikyung reports that Jin Mukyung refuses to leave his secluded training and will not return until he achieves complete mastery.

Jin Wikyung gives Ak Bulgun a letter from Heaven's Gate Temple that arrived half a shichen earlier. After reading it several times, Ak Bulgun asks Jin Wikyung for his help, but the letter's contents remain undisclosed.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung successfully opens both the Conception Vessel and Governor Vessel through Jeok Cheongang's forced treatment and completes the Conception and Governor Vessels Quest.
- The System reports that opening the Conception Vessel greatly improves qi handling and dramatically increases the speed of circulating and accumulating internal energy; the achievement rewards are not yet revealed.
- Taekyung loses consciousness after the second vessel opens, while Jeok Cheongang carries him away to rest.
- Jeok Cheongang recognizes Taekyung's exceptional mental strength and believes the Heavenly Martial Physique chose its master well.
- Jin Mukyung remains secluded in the training hall, living on fasting pills and refusing to return to Heaven's Gate Temple until he achieves complete mastery.
- Ak Bulgun receives an undisclosed letter from Heaven's Gate Temple and asks Jin Wikyung for help after reading it.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 회음혈 as “Huiyin Acupoint.”
- Render 임맥 타통 as “Conception Vessel Opening.”
- Retain “Acupoint Opening” for 혈도 타통.
- Retain “Conception and Governor Vessels” for 임독양맥.
- Retain “turbid qi” for 탁기.
- Retain “Fire Spirit Grass,” “Red Flower Grass,” and “Flame Red Grass” for 화령초, 홍화초, and 염적초.

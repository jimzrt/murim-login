## Previous block summary

# Chapters 275–279

## Plot

After Im Kkeokjeong’s attack, Jin Taekyung discovers a coordinated campaign linking the Black Hunters, a media smear operation blaming him for Won Myunghoon’s death, and apparent interference with the investigation. Butler Kim traces the attackers’ “tail” to Im Yeongjun, a Level 95 A-rank Hunter hiding a Black Hunter training base in the countryside.

Taekyung attacks the mansion alone. He kills many of the twenty-eight assembled Black Hunters, defeats Im Yeongjun, destroys the mansion’s defenses, and tortures Yeongjun for information. Yeongjun identifies Myeongdong Guild as his only significant connection. Taekyung spares ten surviving Black Hunters, who are removed before Butler Kim burns and conceals the site.

Myeongdong Guild Guild Master Park Tae Seop confronts Park Jihoon over Jihoon’s unauthorized killing of Team Leader Jung Hyunwoo and his involvement in the operation targeting Taekyung. Tae Seop reveals that the Guild secretly operates Team 11, roughly thirty trained Black Hunters. After Team 11 reports an intruder at its remote base and then loses contact, Taekyung visits Myeongdong Guild headquarters when Jihoon stops answering his calls. He forces his way inside, hears Jihoon receive a report that twenty-eight people were wiped out, and seizes the unnamed Team 1 Leader while confronting Jihoon through the phone.

## Continuity

- Im Kkeokjeong lost both arms below the elbows. Healing magic and advanced potions completed his treatment, but his return as a Hunter remains uncertain.
- The Black Hunter attack, media campaign, and investigation interference are part of an organized operation intended to isolate Taekyung and the Peace Guild.
- Im Yeongjun was a Level 95 A-rank Hunter who concealed a Black Hunter base in his countryside mansion. He survived Taekyung’s assault and was removed with nine other surviving Black Hunters.
- Taekyung learned from Im Yeongjun that Myeongdong Guild was his significant organizational connection. The mansion and remaining evidence were destroyed and concealed.
- Yeongjun confirmed that the attack on Kkeokjeong was ordered by an unnamed authority above him.
- Park Jihoon is a Myeongdong Guild Team 1 Hunter and covert enforcer. He killed Team Leader Jung Hyunwoo after detecting wrongdoing, without Tae Seop’s permission.
- Park Tae Seop is Myeongdong Guild’s Guild Master, a Great Cataclysm hero, and one of Korea’s top rankers. He accepted a proposal from an unidentified person who had personally contacted him.
- Myeongdong Guild publicly has ten teams but secretly operates Team 11, containing roughly thirty Black Hunters, including three A-rank and twenty-five B-rank Hunters.
- Team 11 reported “Intruder detected” from a remote unsafe-zone location, then lost contact. Taekyung is the apparent intruder and reported killer of twenty-eight members.
- Taekyung entered Myeongdong Guild headquarters after failing to reach Jihoon, overpowered two C-rank Security Team Hunters, and bluffed entry by proposing a cooperative raid.
- Myeongdong Guild’s twenty-story headquarters houses Team 1 on the nineteenth floor and the Guild Master and board on the twentieth.
- Kim Cheol Soo is a newly hired, regulation-bound C-rank Security Team Hunter.
- Taekyung has seized the unnamed Team 1 Leader and is speaking to Jihoon through the leader’s phone.
- Unresolved: the identity of the person directing the operation; the exact roles of Myeongdong Guild and Jihoon; what wrongdoing Jung Hyunwoo uncovered; who authorized the attack on Peace Guild’s D-rank Hunter; and whether Jihoon is acting against Taekyung or pursuing a separate objective.

# Chapter 280

### Chapter 282

# Chapter 282

## Plot

Jin Taekyung confronts Park Jihoon and Myeongdong Guild Master Park Tae Seop in Jihoon's office. Jihoon explains that the Black Hunter operation and the blockade of high-level Gates were intended to suppress the Peace Guild, then offers money, land, buildings, exclusive Gate rights, and other benefits in exchange for the captured Myeongdong personnel and silence. Taekyung rejects the offer without a sincere apology to the injured D-rank Hunter and his family, identifies the unknown person who gave Jihoon his orders, and demands that person be brought to apologize. Tae Seop agrees to apologize personally, but Jihoon refuses to bring the unknown commander. Taekyung exposes Jihoon's hidden strength and subordinate status, breaks his wrist during a brief exchange, and slams his head into the table.

## Continuity

- Park Tae Seop is the Guild Master of Myeongdong Guild, a Great Cataclysm war hero, and one of Korea's top rankers; he admits personal shame over the Black Hunter operation and agrees to apologize directly to the injured D-rank Hunter.
- Park Jihoon states that Myeongdong Guild sent the Black Hunters to keep Taekyung and the Peace Guild in check, and that surrounding Guilds have blocked the Peace Guild from accessing high-level Gates to make it wither away.
- Park Jihoon offers money, power, buildings, land, exclusive rights to high-level Gates, and substantial cash in exchange for the Peace Guild handing over its prisoners and remaining silent, with the Peace Guild expected to stay within an imposed limit.
- Taekyung insists that the injured D-rank Hunter and his family must receive a sincere apology and substantial compensation, not merely payment; Jihoon gives Taekyung a reluctant personal apology.
- Jihoon was acting under orders from an unidentified person whom he calls 'that person'; Taekyung's demand to bring that person for an apology causes unmistakable agitation, and Jihoon refuses to do so.
- Jihoon is far stronger than Myeongdong Guild's nominal Team 1 Leader, rarely attends work, holds no deputy position despite acting as the Team 1 Leader's superior, and is secretly a powerful Hunter.
- Taekyung breaks Jihoon's wrist after a five-exchange hand-to-hand clash and slams Jihoon's head into the table.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain 'that person' for 그분 and preserve the deliberate ambiguity surrounding the unknown commander.
- Render 양패구상 as 'we'll both come out wounded,' preserving the negotiation's warning that both sides would suffer.
- Retain Taekyung's taunting paradox 'cleaner piece of shit' for 깨끗한 똥.
### Chapter 283

# Chapter 283

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continued assaulting Park Jihoon after overpowering him and discovered that Jihoon knew hand-to-hand combat comparable to a Peak master's grappling technique. Jihoon carried a separate phone protected by fingerprint and iris recognition, recovered from Taekyung's attack by drinking a potion, and invoked the coercive rewards and punishments associated with 'that person' to obtain Park Tae Seop's help. Taekyung released a jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi and demonstrated the overwhelming difference between their classes.

Park Tae Seop reluctantly armed himself with a greatsword and shield and joined Jihoon against Taekyung. Taekyung evaded Tae Seop's attacks, broke his shield, and used the Flow principle he had learned from Mae Jonghak to entangle and disarm both men. He shattered Jihoon's knee and was about to destroy the other one when an unidentified, impossibly deep voice interrupted the fight, leading Taekyung to wonder whether its owner was a Supreme Peak master.

## Continuity

- Park Jihoon's hand-to-hand movement is comparable to a Peak master's grappling technique, though Taekyung considers it somewhat clumsy.
- Park Jihoon carries a separate phone for secret communications; it requires fingerprint and iris recognition.
- Park Jihoon used a potion to heal the injuries Taekyung had inflicted during their confrontation.
- Jihoon says 'that person' enforces choices through definite rewards and punishments, and Park Tae Seop is unwilling to oppose that person's demands.
- Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi formed a fire dragon, dispersed through hundreds of streams, and was then recombined for release.
- Park Tae Seop fights with a greatsword and shield and possesses an aura powerful enough to confront Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi.
- Taekyung overpowered Park Tae Seop, split his shield, and used Mae Jonghak's Flow principle to disarm both opponents.
- Taekyung shattered Park Jihoon's knee and was interrupted while preparing to destroy his remaining knee.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 행신동 불닭볶음손 as 'Haengsin-dong Fire-Chicken Stir-Fried Hand,' retaining the buldak-bokkeum-myeon/son pun in a footnote.
- Render 흐름 as 'Flow' when naming Taekyung's application of Mae Jonghak's movement principle.
### Chapter 284

# Chapter 284

## Plot

Lee Jungryong arrives at Park Tae Seop's ruined office without being detected, revealing himself as Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master, one of Korea's two S-rank Hunters, and a Supreme Peak master. He claims Park Tae Seop is merely an acquaintance and Park Jihoon a stranger, while offering to resolve the situation. Taekyung refuses to release them, demands an apology, compensation, and one person's life, and implies that Jihoon must answer for the injuries inflicted on Kkeokjeong. Lee attacks with a red-aura palm, but Taekyung counters with Flame Divine Palm; Taekyung is injured while Lee remains standing. Taekyung then repeatedly stabs and mutilates Jihoon, severing both arms and seriously injuring his leg and shoulder, while Lee is restrained by the dagger at Jihoon's throat. Jihoon cries out to Lee as his Master, revealing their master-Disciple relationship. Taekyung leaves Jihoon alive but warns that his life as a Hunter may be over, and threatens to collapse the building and expose the incident publicly.

## Continuity

- Lee Jungryong is Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master, one of Korea's two S-rank Hunters, and the effective wielder of Ares Guild's authority in place of its Guild Master.
- Lee Jungryong is a Supreme Peak-level martial artist whose palm technique and qi control are comparable to the great demon Yin-Yang Twin Devils encountered at Shaolin.
- Lee Jungryong has known Park Tae Seop since the Great Cataclysm and is Park Jihoon's master; Jihoon addresses him as Master.
- Lee Jungryong gave Park Jihoon his initial orders, while the chapter does not explicitly confirm whether he is the unidentified 'that person' behind the entire operation.
- Taekyung demanded an apology, compensation, and one person's life from Lee Jungryong, then left Park Jihoon alive after severing both arms and inflicting severe injuries to his leg and shoulder.
- Lee Jungryong attacked Taekyung after their negotiation failed, but stopped advancing while Taekyung held Jihoon at knifepoint.
- Taekyung threatened to bring down the building and make half of Korea aware of the confrontation if Lee continued.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이정룡 as Lee Jungryong.
- Render 부길드장 as Vice Guild Master and 스승님/제자 as Master/Disciple, retaining Disciple capitalization for the established martial relationship.
- Retain the established renderings Ares Guild, S-rank Hunter, Supreme Peak, and Flame Divine Palm.

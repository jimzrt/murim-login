# Chapters 345–349

## Plot

Jin Taekyung obtains the Sichuan Tang Clan’s Myriad-Poison Ring from Tang Sadok after offering to help pursue Tang Taesang’s killer. The ring completes the treatment requirements for Jeok Cheongang, and the Divine Physician begins a fifteen-day treatment in the deepest cell of the Tang Clan’s underground prison. Taekyung recognizes Jeok as his Master and guards the treatment room.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord, a one-armed middle-aged man, massacres Tang Clan personnel, reveals that he killed Poison King Tang Taesang and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, and summons hundreds of black-robed hunters through a concealed Mystic Gate Formation. The Qilian Three Fiends submit to him as he prepares to hunt Emei, Qingcheng, and the Sichuan Tang Clan.

On the fourth day of treatment, Taekyung encounters the Heavenly Power Demon, an elderly prisoner who has spent more than forty years in the Tang Clan’s underground prison. The Heavenly Power Demon was formerly an Elder and commander of the Great Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, led the conquest of Qinghai, and opened the first front of its holy war. He explains that the Great Faction War was morally complicated, that the Divine Cult survived in diminished form in the Tianshan Mountains, and that its former Cult Leader became corrupt. Taekyung begins clarifying that the Yin-Yang Twin Ghosts later served Dark Heaven, but Cheongpung interrupts.

## Continuity

- The Divine Physician and Mungyeong are on day four of a fifteen-day treatment of Jeok Cheongang using the Myriad-Poison Ring and Cold-Ice Stone.
- Jeok remains unconscious and critically ill with Formless Ultimate Poison. Taekyung recognizes him as his Master and is guarding the treatment room.
- The Myriad-Poison Ring is in Taekyung’s possession, but Item Appraisal still requires an unknown special condition.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord killed Poison King Tang Taesang and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, losing one arm in the process.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord commands hundreds of black-robed hunters and intends to attack Emei, Qingcheng, and the Sichuan Tang Clan. The purpose and identity of the Lord of Heaven he invokes remain unresolved.
- The Qilian Three Fiends have submitted to the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- The Heavenly Power Demon remains imprisoned with a ruined dantian, severed limb sinews, and heavy restraints.
- The Heavenly Power Demon’s account establishes that the Great Heavenly Demon Divine Cult survived the Great Faction War in a diminished state and was driven into the Tianshan Mountains; its former Cult Leader became corrupt.
- The Yin-Yang Twin Ghosts later served Dark Heaven rather than participating in the Demonic Cult’s attack on Shaolin.
- Venerable Myoryeong remains under treatment for the Black Hand Seal.

- Use **Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun**, **Mystic Gate Formation**, **Emei Sect**, and **Sichuan Tang Clan**.
- Preserve the Heavenly Power Demon’s gruff formal self-reference as **this old man**.

### Chapter 350

# Chapter 350

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continues distributing food in the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison and tries for three days to coax the Heavenly Power Demon into eating and revealing what he knows about Dark Heaven. The Heavenly Power Demon refuses every comfort and says that torture or death would leave him without regret.

Cheongpung returns with food and a Beggars' Sect letter reporting that the search has shifted from finding the Divine Physician to finding the culprit who murdered the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun. The Beggars' Sect, the Tang Clan's Green Shadow Squad, and government troops are searching across Sichuan, with the government troops expanding toward Qingcheng and Emei while preparing for another possible incident; Jeok Cheongang's treatment remains ongoing.

Jang Il, a twenty-five-year-old two-knot Beggars' Sect Disciple, is killed in a secluded forest two days from Emei by a smiling middle-aged military official who orders his subordinates to find the other nearby Beggars' Sect members.

## Continuity

- The Heavenly Power Demon has refused food, clothing, and other conveniences for three days and will not discuss Dark Heaven despite visibly reacting to its name.
- Taekyung suspects that Dark Heaven is deeply connected to, or possibly identical with, the Demonic Cult because of the Heavenly Power Demon's former Cult allegiance and silence.
- The Beggars' Sect, Green Shadow Squad, and government troops are actively searching for the murderer of the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, and the government search has reached the areas toward Qingcheng and Emei.
- A middle-aged military official and his group killed Jang Il while searching for other Beggars' Sect members near Emei.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 원균 as Won Gyun, not Heo Gyun, and render 검남춘 as Jiannan Chun.
- Render 삼결 제자, 이결 제자, and 일결 제자 as Three-knot Disciple, Two-knot Disciple, and One-knot Disciple; render 부 분타주 as Vice Branch Leader.
- Retain Taekyung's abrupt, sarcastic voice while rendering his exaggerated courtesy toward the Heavenly Power Demon as deliberate coaxing.
### Chapter 351

# Chapter 351

## Plot

After ten days in the underground prison, Jin Taekyung completes his qi circulation and discovers that Cheongpung possesses more internal energy than he does, having accumulated it naturally and undergone repeated cleansing of the sinews and washing of the marrow as a child. The Divine Physician and Mungyeong then emerge from the corridor, exhausted but smiling.

The Divine Physician announces that Jeok Cheongang's treatment succeeded ahead of schedule. Jeok's vitality has returned, his body has visibly rejuvenated, the Yin and Yang are balanced, and no Formless Ultimate Poison remains; he remains unconscious but should awaken within three days. The Divine Physician stays to watch over him, while Mungyeong prepares to leave and check on Venerable Myoryeong.

Tang Sadok permits Jin Taekyung to keep using the Myriad-Poison Ring and remain in the underground prison for several more days, while ordering continued surveillance. Tang Sadok vows to pursue the one-armed murderer of his father and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, but a bloodied Green Shadow Squad martial artist suddenly arrives and warns that someone is coming.

## Continuity

- The fifteen-day treatment of Jeok Cheongang succeeded ahead of schedule: his Yin and Yang are balanced, Formless Ultimate Poison is gone, and his vitality has returned.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious after treatment but is expected to regain consciousness within three days; he must avoid exhausting himself after waking.
- The Divine Physician remains at the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison to watch over Jeok Cheongang's recovery.
- Mungyeong is leaving to check on Venerable Myoryeong, whose recovery and current consciousness remain unconfirmed.
- Jin Taekyung retains the Myriad-Poison Ring and has Tang Sadok's permission to use it and remain in the underground prison for several more days.
- Tang Sadok continues to have Jin Taekyung watched despite lending him the clan's sacred heirloom.
- Tang Sadok is pursuing the one-armed murderer responsible for Tang Taesang and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun's deaths.
- A bloodied Green Shadow Squad martial artist has warned Tang Sadok that someone is approaching the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord commands black-robed hunters summoned through a concealed Mystic Gate Formation and intends to hunt Emei, Qingcheng, and the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- The Qilian Three Fiends have submitted to the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- The Heavenly Power Demon remains imprisoned in the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison and has described the Great Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's complicated history and its former Cult Leader's corruption.
- The Beggars' Sect, the Tang Clan's Green Shadow Squad, and government troops continue searching for the killer of the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun.

### Chapter 352

# Chapter 352

## Plot

A bloodied Green Shadow Squad martial artist warns Tang Sadok that at least three hundred enemies have entered Chengdu disguised as government troops and are approaching the Sichuan Tang Clan.

Tang Sadok orders the entire Tang Clan to mobilize, including retired elders, untrained craftsmen, and women, and commands that everyone receive poison and hidden weapons.

Remembering the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath, Tang Sadok rejects his nephew's confidence, sounds the Tang War Drum, and summons the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders as the clan faces possible destruction.

Tang Sadok realizes that the messenger is the one-armed enemy, who transforms from a young Green Shadow Squad martial artist into the middle-aged Western Heaven Demon Lord and claims he uses demonic martial arts.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord admits that he lost his left arm while fighting Poison King Tang Taesang, while Tang Sadok vows to kill him; Tang Sadok and the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders confront him as the chapter ends.

## Continuity

- At least three hundred attackers entered Chengdu in government-troop disguises, divided into several groups to avoid suspicion.
- Tang Sadok has ordered a total Tang Clan mobilization and distributed poison and hidden weapons to every member regardless of age, sex, occupation, or martial training.
- The Tang Clan's Ten Wonders have assembled at Tang Sadok's command.
- The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion is Tang Sadok's only nephew; his father, Tang Sadok's only older brother, died during the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord infiltrated the Tang Clan disguised as a Green Shadow Squad martial artist and can alter his body through an unexplained demonic martial art.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 경월년 혈사 as Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath.
- Render 당전고 as Tang War Drum and 당문고 as Tang Clan drum, preserving the source distinction.
- Render 당문십기 as Tang Clan's Ten Wonders.
- Render 일원, 삼당, 오대 as One Origin, Three Divisions, and Five Squads.
### Chapter 353

# Chapter 353

## Plot

The First Fiend leads three hundred black-clad Dark Heaven attackers in an assault on the Sichuan Tang Clan after raising Dark Heaven's flag and invoking the Lord of Heaven's command. The Second and Third Fiends have departed to take charge of operations at Qingcheng and Emei, while the First Fiend reaches the Tang Clan's gate and begins the battle.

Underground, Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung hear the Tang War Drum and receive the automatically accepted Uninvited Guest Quest, whose only revealed failure condition is death. The Divine Physician warns that Jeok Cheongang's qi remains unstable and that external shock could be fatal, so Taekyung chooses to remain with Jeok while sending Cheongpung to aid the besieged Tang Clan and return.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has begun its assault on the Sichuan Tang Clan with three hundred black-clad attackers led by the First Fiend.
- The First Fiend is one of the Qilian Three Fiends; the Second Fiend is operating at Qingcheng and the Third Fiend at Emei.
- The Sichuan Tang Clan is under siege, and the First Fiend has reached its gate after Tang Clan defenders began firing arrows and ordered the Myriad-Poison Formation activated.
- The Uninvited Guest Quest has been automatically accepted; its Grade, Limit, Mission, and Reward are unknown, and failure means death.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious with unstable qi, making external impact dangerous while he recovers from treatment.
- Taekyung remains with Jeok Cheongang while Cheongpung leaves to strike Dark Heaven's attackers and return.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일괴, 이괴, and 삼괴 as First Fiend, Second Fiend, and Third Fiend.
- Render 악귀 as Fiend when used as the First Fiend's descriptive epithet.
- Render 만독진 as Myriad-Poison Formation.
- Render 초대받지 않은 손님 as Uninvited Guest.
- Render 마군 as Demon Lord when used as the Western Heaven Demon Lord's shortened title.
### Chapter 354

# Chapter 354

## Plot

Dark Heaven's three-hundred-man assault overwhelms the Sichuan Tang Clan's outer defenses. Their black-clad fighters carry sinister poison-neutralizing artifacts and piercing leather armor, rendering the Tang Clan's poisons and hidden weapons largely ineffective while more than a hundred clansmen die and half the Outer Hall falls.

Tang Jinhu, the retired Supreme Peak Head Elder, joins the battle and uses the Soul-Severing Thread to kill numerous attackers, but the Council of Elders and the Tang Clan's Peak masters cannot reverse the slaughter. First Fiend then appears, recognizes Tang Jinhu, and reveals that his two younger brothers are attacking Emei and Qingcheng.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord defeats Tang Sadok and most of the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders, leaving only three of them alive while Tang Sadok is gravely wounded. Before the Demon Lord can weaken him further, Cheongpung rushes into the scene, prompting the Demon Lord to wonder what kind of lunatic has arrived.

## Continuity

- The black-clad attackers possess artifacts that neutralize the Sichuan Tang Clan's deadliest poisons and wear armor resistant to ordinary weapons.
- Tang Jinhu, the Tang Clan's retired Supreme Peak Head Elder, is exhausted but fighting First Fiend after the Outer Hall suffers catastrophic losses.
- First Fiend is leading the assault on the Tang Clan while his two younger brothers attack Emei and Qingcheng.
- Tang Sadok is gravely wounded, and only three members of the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders remain after the Western Heaven Demon Lord's attack.
- Cheongpung has reached the confrontation between Tang Sadok and the Western Heaven Demon Lord.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 단혼사 as Soul-Severing Thread.
- Render 천독객 as Heaven-Poison Wanderer.
- Render 당문삼기 as Tang Clan's Three Skills and retain the footnote explaining its pun on Tang Clan's Ten Wonders.
- Render 피독주 as poison-warding pearl.
- Render 창룡후 as azure dragon's roar.

# Chapters 215–219

## Plot

A Star Guild scheduling mistake redirects the joint Peace–Star Guild raid from Manticore’s Jungle to The Black Wyvern’s Nest, an A-Rank Gate. Despite Taekyung’s trauma from the previous Black Wyvern encounter, the raid proceeds. Won Myunghoon lends Taekyung a Yeti’s Necklace, which Taekyung later equips after Item Appraisal reveals that it attracts dragonkin monsters.

Taekyung kills forty-seven B-Rank Orc Warriors alone, alarming the Star Guild. Won kills a Lv. 97 Green Wyvern, while privately arranging for a stronger monster to appear. After the party crosses the jungle and enters a wasteland, seven scouts encounter a Wyvern identified as a Named Monster. Only Team Leader 1 returns, having abandoned the others under the monster’s Fear. The Gate is confirmed to be an A-Rank Mutated Gate.

Taekyung recognizes the approaching Named Monster as the Black Wyvern that slaughtered his former teammates three years earlier at the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate. The spear wound he inflicted then damaged one of its eyes. Taekyung orders Won and the Star Guild to flee while he keeps the necklace and confronts the monster. He also withdraws his familiar “hyung” address and warns Won not to bare his teeth again, sharply straining their relationship.

## Continuity

- The Peace and Star Guild raid was accidentally redirected to The Black Wyvern’s Nest.
- The raid party is inside an A-Rank Mutated Gate, where monsters can greatly exceed the expected Gate Grade.
- Won killed the Lv. 97 Green Wyvern; Taekyung received no EXP because he did not contribute.
- Taekyung killed forty-seven B-Rank Orc Warriors using an ordinary iron spear.
- Won gave Taekyung Yeti’s Necklace as a personal gift; its Wind of the Snowfield effect attracts dragonkin monsters and marks its possessor as a target.
- A Named Monster Wyvern killed six scouts. Team Leader 1 survived by abandoning them after succumbing to Fear.
- The Named Monster is the same Black Wyvern that killed Taekyung’s former teammates three years earlier; one eye bears Taekyung’s old spear wound.
- Taekyung has ordered Won and the Star Guild to escape and remains alone to face the approaching Black Wyvern.
- Taekyung and Won’s previously friendly hyung-and-younger-brother relationship is now openly hostile or severely strained.
- The immediate unresolved crisis is whether Taekyung can survive the Named Monster and why the Black Wyvern became one.
- The Conception Vessel and Governor Vessel Achievement rewards, Gate Suppression Quest conditions, Star-Array Grand Banquet, Jin Mukyung’s return, Jeok Cheongang’s warning, and the Martial God’s condition remain unresolved.

# Chapter 220

## Plot

After Won Myunghoon leaves, Jin Taekyung confirms that the approaching Named Monster is the Black Wyvern from the incident three years earlier and chooses to face it alone. Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Song Song remain behind; Butler Kim layers magical barriers to protect them. Song Song rejects Taekyung's confession, saying she will not change her attitude for money or fame, but accepts treating him as a same-age friend and guildmate, and they agree to drink soju after the fight. The Black Wyvern arrives, having grown to more than fifty meters—at least two or three times its former size—with one cloudy eye. Taekyung recalls the comrades killed in the earlier incident, including Kim Hyunsu, Lee Hyerim, Song Donghyeok, Park Sangho, Kim Haneung, Park Gwanghyeon, and Hong Cheonsu. Hong Cheonsu had been a ten-year veteran who saved Taekyung from goblins and was called hyung; he died leaving behind a spouse and three children. Taekyung advances toward the Black Wyvern after three years and ninety-two days, channels more than one jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi and accumulated rage into his spear, and prepares to kill it with blue-burning Aura and Spear Energy.

- Song Song rejects Taekyung's confession but accepts a same-age friendship and guildmate relationship; they agree to drink soju after the battle.
- The Black Wyvern from the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident returns as a Named Monster, now more than fifty meters long and at least two or three times larger than three years earlier, with one cloudy eye.
- Taekyung remembers the comrades killed in the earlier incident, including Kim Hyunsu and Hong Cheonsu, and resolves to end his grudge with the Black Wyvern.
- Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi has surpassed one jiazi, and his accumulated rage transforms with his internal energy into blue-burning Aura and Spear Energy.

# Chapter 223

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung kills Carus, the Peace Guild members are overwhelmed by shock, relief, congratulations, and pride. They struggle to determine how to process the more-than-fifty-meter corpse of the Named Monster without revealing Taekyung's Inventory and System, eventually deciding to bring in outside Hunters.

In the jungle, Taekyung discovers Won Myunghoon at Level 80 leading the hostile group he mockingly calls hyenas.

## Continuity

- Carus's corpse is too large and difficult to process normally; its hide, bones, flesh, and scales require at least B-Rank Hunter-level assistance, preferably an A-Rank Hunter.
- Taekyung considers storing Carus's corpse in the Inventory but fears that removing it past the reporters stationed at the Gate entrance would expose him as a System user.
- The exit Gate remains closed because the Gate's separate boss monster survived, fled from Taekyung, and is hiding somewhere in the vast wilderness.
- Taekyung and Butler Kim travel back toward the original Gate entrance to recruit outside personnel, with Taekyung carrying Butler Kim on his back.
- Butler Kim uses the A-Rank spell Fire Rain during the journey, killing approximately one-third of a wandering Orc group and scattering the rest.
- Taekyung encounters Won Myunghoon at Level 80 in the jungle; Won responds with open hostility after Taekyung previously let him and his group go.

### Chapter 224

# Chapter 224

## Plot

Won Myunghoon stopped the returning Star Guild members and ordered them to surround the only route to the Gate entrance, intending to eliminate any surviving Peace Guild member before the Star Guild's involvement could be exposed. He dismissed Fear-stricken Kim Jonghun as useless but ordered him bound as bait because Jonghun knew Won's secrets and had personally acquired Yeti's Necklace.

Taekyung and Butler Kim reached the ambush and called the Star Guild out. Butler Kim healed his injured knee with Heal; after Won declared that they had come to kill them all, Taekyung broke Won's arm, disarmed his spear, and punched him while repeating his warning not to bare his teeth again.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon considers the Star Guild members compromised accomplices: they accepted his money and are bound to the consequences of the eight-year-old incident.
- Won Myunghoon ordered Kim Jonghun bound rather than killed, intending to use him as prey for the Named Monster because Jonghun knows Won's secrets and is no longer useful as a Hunter after Fear.
- Taekyung and Butler Kim reached the Star Guild's ambush; Butler Kim healed his knee with Heal, and Taekyung broke Won's arm, took his spear, and punched him after Won declared his intent to kill the Peace Guild survivors.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Won's address to Taekyung hostile rather than restoring the previously withdrawn hyung relationship; render 원명훈 씨 as Mr. Won Myunghoon before the following profanity.
- Render 개보린 as Dogvorin and explain in a footnote that it is Taekyung's dog-themed pun on Gevorin, a Korean painkiller.
- Preserve Won Myunghoon's coercive and murderous register, rendering 떨거지 as losers, 폐품 as junk, and 먹잇감 as prey.

## Previous block summary

# Chapters 85–89

## Plot

In the Boss Zone of The Minotaur’s Labyrinth, Im Changsoo plans to exploit Taekyung’s apparent interest in Song Song and use the Level 70 Minotaur Warrior to humiliate him. Taekyung instead kills the boss with a single spear technique, completes the B-rank Gate Clear Quest, and receives a Level Up and its undisclosed reward. Song Song declines his attempts to invite her to dinner.

After the raid, Changsoo transfers the wagered four billion won to Taekyung. His father, Im Chunsoo—the A-rank Guild Master of Sangdong Guild, known as Frozen—learns that Changsoo transferred eight billion won in total, fires him, and begins beating him with an ice club.

Taekyung visits his sick sister Hayeon and discovers that their mother, Kim Jeonghee, has been secretly working in a restaurant. When the restaurant owner insults Jeonghee and attacks Taekyung, he reveals his C-rank Hunter status and has Changsoo confirm both his identity and payment. Jeonghee quits and leaves with him.

At home, Taekyung uses Circulate Qi for Healing on Hayeon and Jeonghee, curing Hayeon’s fever and headache and greatly improving his mother’s condition. He gives each of them a Lesser Potion from his reality Inventory. After learning that Taekyung earned four billion won, Hayeon asks whether she can drop out of school.

## Continuity

- Taekyung killed the Level 70 Minotaur Warrior in one blow, completed the B-rank Gate Clear Quest, leveled up, and received its reward in his reality Inventory.
- Im Changsoo paid Taekyung the promised four billion won.
- Im Chunsoo is Sangdong Guild’s founder and A-rank Guild Master, known as Frozen and for his exceptional ice magic. He fired Changsoo and violently confronted him after discovering Changsoo’s eight-billion-won transfer.
- Kim Jeonghee quit her restaurant job after the owner insulted and attacked Taekyung.
- Kim Minsu is the owner’s son, a D-rank Hunter in Sangdong Guild, but Changsoo does not know him personally.
- Taekyung can safely use the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique to perform Circulate Qi for Healing on others.
- Hayeon and Jeonghee recovered substantially after receiving the treatment; Taekyung also gave each a Lesser Potion.
- Taekyung’s reality and Murim Inventories remain separate.
- Hayeon has asked about dropping out of school, but no decision has been made.
- Retaliation by Im Chunsoo or Sangdong Guild, Jeonghee’s next circumstances, and Hayeon’s schooling remain unresolved.

# Chapter 90

## Plot

Team Leader Choi gives Jin Taekyung a week of paid vacation because Peace Guild's Guild house remodeling will finish in a week. At home, Hayeon indirectly asks for allowance, and Taekyung gives her two 50,000-won bills before joining his mother's dinner.

Choi and Butler Kim discuss Taekyung's rapid growth and the possibility that he is a third-awakening Hunter. Butler Kim agrees to investigate such cases. Sangdong Guild has begun monitoring Peace Guild, with Taekyung marked as a major target, but Choi deliberately chooses not to warn him because he wants Sangdong's investigation to reveal more about Taekyung.

During his vacation, Taekyung visits a real estate office and seeks to buy a house in the safe zone where he grew up. The area was redeveloped after his family moved away following his father's death. He views the former family home, walks around the neighborhood, finds the supermarket from his childhood, and is approached by an unfamiliar man who asks whether Taekyung knows him.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo and Butler Kim suspect that Jin Taekyung may be a Hunter who awakened a third time; Butler Kim will investigate whether such cases exist.
- Sangdong Guild has deployed surveillance agents to analyze Peace Guild, and Jin Taekyung is considered a major target. Choi has chosen not to warn Taekyung because he wants Sangdong's investigation to approach Taekyung's true identity.
- Jin Taekyung intends to purchase the detached house that was his family's former home in his redeveloped childhood neighborhood.
- An unidentified man approached Jin Taekyung outside the childhood supermarket and asked whether Taekyung knew him; his identity and connection to Taekyung are unresolved.

### Chapter 91

# Chapter 91

## Plot

Jin Taekyung reunites with his former Garam Middle School classmate Park Jihwang, who has since changed his name to Park Jihoon. Jihoon is now a Hunter in Team 1 of Myeongdong Guild, and Taekyung senses that he is roughly as strong as Im Changsoo or possibly stronger. They exchange contact information and make plans to meet again.

Jihoon's girlfriend privately dismisses Taekyung as an unemployed-looking man, while Jihoon admits that he and Taekyung were not actually close despite their friendly reunion.

Taekyung agrees to buy a two-story detached house with a lawn for 3.38 billion won and pays a ten-percent deposit. He plans to complete the formal purchase, remodel the house to resemble his family's former home, and surprise his family with the news after Hayeon's college entrance examination before moving.

Taekyung senses someone watching him in an alley and briefly summons a dagger, but finds only a cat. The System reports that Qi Sense has no detectable targets. The watcher is Hong Woojin, a B-rank mage and information broker investigating Taekyung through a Link with the cat; Woojin nearly gets discovered and is left anxious by the incident.

## Continuity

- Park Jihwang, now called Park Jihoon, is Jin Taekyung's former Garam Middle School classmate and a Hunter in Team 1 of Myeongdong Guild.
- Taekyung assessed Jihoon's strength through Qi Sense as comparable to Im Changsoo's, possibly greater.
- Taekyung agreed to purchase the two-story house with a lawn for 3.38 billion won, paid a ten-percent deposit, and intends to remodel it and move after Hayeon's college entrance examination.
- Hong Woojin is a B-rank mage and information broker specializing in tracking and surveillance magic; he is investigating Taekyung and used a cat's Link to follow him.
- Taekyung's Qi Sense failed to detect Woojin's surveillance in the alley, although Woojin recognized that Taekyung nearly noticed it.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 박지훈 as Park Jihoon and 박지황 as Park Jihwang; Park Jihwang is Jihoon's former name.
- Render 명동 길드 as Myeongdong Guild.
- Retain Link as the capitalized name of Woojin's surveillance connection with the cat.
- Render 삼계탕 as samgyetang with an explanatory footnote.

# Chapter 92

### Chapter 93

# Chapter 93

## Plot

Hong Woojin controls a rice weevil Familiar while monitoring Jin Taekyung, but Taekyung's Qi Sense briefly disrupts the connection. Woojin dismisses the sensation as coincidence, severs the Link, and decides to monitor Taekyung more boldly in another form.

After sensing the Link disappear, Taekyung confirms that no Familiars remain in his home. He suspects Sangdong Guild is behind the surveillance and regards the intrusion into his family's home as an unforgivable provocation.

Taekyung visits the Ilsan Lafesta Store, where his C-rank Hunter certification grants him access through the third floor. He chooses the second floor to look for low-rank weapons useful for filling his Inventory, ultimately ordering boxes of daggers instead of a single dagger.

Assistant Manager Kim Seonhee's disappointment over the small sale vanishes when Taekyung begins ordering multiple storage boxes of weapons.

## Continuity

- Hong Woojin's rice weevil Familiar was released when he severed its Link; he plans to continue investigating Taekyung with a more conspicuous Familiar form.
- Taekyung confirmed that no Familiars remained in his home after the Link ended and now believes Sangdong Guild may be responsible for the surveillance.
- Taekyung considers the intrusion into the home shared by his family a violation of his inviolable boundary and intends to retaliate.
- Taekyung visited the Ilsan Lafesta Store and began stocking his Inventory with boxes of low-rank Hunter weapons.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 김선희 as Kim Seonhee, 대리 as Assistant Manager, 일산 as Ilsan, and 라페스타 as Lafesta.
- Render 스토어 as Store when referring to the restricted luxury magical-goods retailer.
- Render 쌀벌레 as Rice Weevil and 링크 as Link; retain Familiar for 패밀리어.
### Chapter 94

# Chapter 94

## Plot

Taekyung spends 350 million won at the Ilsan Store, stores the purchases in his Inventory, and researches Familiar magic while riding to his family’s apartment. He learns that a B-rank mage’s Familiar connection reaches up to 500 meters, with 300 meters considered a safe working distance; forced Link severance causes physical distress and may cause mana backflow, while tiny Familiars evade most detection magic.

Taekyung concludes that the people who controlled the Familiars around his home yesterday must have been nearby and decides he has another reason to catch them himself. At the apartment entrance, Hayeon reveals that she found and received permission to temporarily foster an abandoned kitten, but Taekyung identifies it through the System as a level-two Cat—Familiar.

## Continuity

- Familiar magic is mental magic: for a B-rank mage, its maximum connection distance is 500 meters and its safer operating distance is about 300 meters.
- A forcibly severed Familiar Link can cause nausea, vomiting, and a risk of mana backflow; a Familiar’s death forcibly severs the Link and causes the caster a slight physical backlash.
- Tiny Familiars such as flies and rice weevils evade most detection magic, while equipment with detection capable of finding them costs 550 million won even on a purported summer special.
- Hayeon found an abandoned level-two Cat Familiar and received Kim Jeonghee’s permission to foster it temporarily; Taekyung recognized that the kitten was not an ordinary cat.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Superior Potion for 상급 포션 and retain Familiar magic and Lv. 2 Cat—Familiar for the System terminology.
- Preserve the forum author’s exaggerated baldness jokes, profanity, and comic internet-commentary voice.
- Explain 백조 as unemployed woman with a footnote, matching the established reading copy.

# Chapters 245–249

## Plot

The Star-Array Grand Banquet opens at Mount Song. Hong Dao warns that darkness is coming, while Jeok Cheongang urges Jin Taekyung to win and defeat anyone from the Peng Family. During the preliminaries, Taekyung’s restrained strike is eclipsed by the Iron-Water Divine Dragon’s overwhelming Ship-Breaking Fist, but Taekyung responds with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist and wins every assessment.

Taekyung takes unanimous first place, defeating the Iron-Water Divine Dragon, Kunlun Cloud Dragon, Divine Marvel Dragon, Successor Beggar, and Hunroe Leg. During the free day, Jeok conceals his pride beneath stern advice. At Shaolin, Hong Dao reveals that heavenly patterns identify Taekyung as the Morning Star that rose while Jeok’s star was fading. Hong plans to step down as Abbot after the banquet and may have roughly one year left to live.

More than five hundred finalists travel to Luoyang for the main event. Taekyung meets the three eccentric finalists Baek Woo, Gung Gibang, and Zhuge Gyun, forcing the first two to behave respectfully while Zhuge chooses a cryptic third option. Song Ho, the Thousand-Faced Fox, arrives and identifies himself to the finalists. He recognizes Taekyung as Jeok’s Disciple and says Taekyung’s destruction of the mechanism core allowed about one hundred additional applicants to qualify.

## Continuity

- The preliminaries lasted three days and reduced more than fifty thousand applicants to approximately five hundred finalists.
- Jin Taekyung won unanimous first place with perfect scores in all five assessments.
- Taekyung defeated the Iron-Water Divine Dragon, Kunlun Cloud Dragon, Divine Marvel Dragon, Successor Beggar, and Hunroe Leg.
- The Iron-Water Divine Dragon abandoned the banquet and returned to the Yangtze.
- The Star-Array Grand Banquet main event will use a different format, with spectator stands for the duels, and is about to begin in Luoyang.
- Jeok Cheongang is publicly Taekyung’s Master and is proud of Taekyung’s victory, despite hiding it behind harsh advice.
- Hong Dao identifies Taekyung as the Morning Star that rose in the northern lands while Jeok’s star was fading.
- Hong Dao entrusted the Green Jade Buddha Staff to Unnamed, appointed an interim successor, and intends to step down as Abbot after the banquet. Unnamed remains in Arhat Cave.
- Hong Dao may have approximately one year of life remaining.
- The Ten Kings were the ten Supreme Peak masters of the Great Faction War; the Spear King and Wave King are dead, leaving eight effective survivors.
- Baek Woo, Gung Gibang, and Zhuge Gyun are finalists from Kunlun, the Beggars’ Sect, and the Zhuge Clan respectively.
- Song Ho is an elderly Peak master with a genial public manner and predatory attentiveness beneath it. He recognizes Taekyung and knows about the mechanism-core incident.
- Open hooks include Taekyung’s performance in the main event, the identity of the Gold-Faced Young Master who bet on him, the concealed strength of other finalists, Hong Dao’s chosen successor, and whom Song Ho was studying.

### Chapter 250

# Chapter 250

## Plot

The Demonic Cult's conquest of Qinghai, Gansu, and Sichuan in four months forced the orthodox factions, unorthodox forces, and dark-path powers to unite beneath the Martial God's banner, creating the Murim Alliance. The Alliance dissolved after the ten-year Great War and the Martial God's disappearance, but its former site remains active and now hosts the Star-Array Grand Banquet.

On the third day of the main event, Jin Taekyung defeated Black Water Dart with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, earning his fourth victory; each of his duels has lasted less than fifteen minutes. The five hundred finalists are being reduced to roughly thirty, with injuries and post-victory withdrawals becoming common. Baek Woo brings Taekyung a towel using the Cloud-Dragon Eight Forms, while Gung Gibang brings him washing water and confirms that he has mastered the Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms only to the fifth level.

Song Ho has attended every day and is revealed to have formerly headed the Murim Alliance's Hidden Shadow Pavilion, an intelligence organization once comparable to or superior to the Beggars' Sect. He leaves during the third day. Jongni Chu narrowly wins his fourth duel and earns the nickname Life-Sustaining Sword. Meanwhile, Zhuge Gyun faces the unknown Invincible Divine Sword, whose swordsmanship exhausts him; the opponent eagerly asks to see more fan techniques, prompting Zhuge to throw away his fan.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has four Star-Array Grand Banquet main-event victories, all completed in under fifteen minutes.
- The Star-Array Grand Banquet's five hundred finalists are expected to diminish to approximately thirty after the third day's scheduled duels.
- Song Ho was formerly the head of the Murim Alliance's Hidden Shadow Pavilion, whose information network once rivaled or surpassed the Beggars' Sect's.
- Jongni Chu has won four main-event duels and is now nicknamed the Life-Sustaining Sword after repeatedly winning by a narrow margin.
- Zhuge Gyun is currently dueling the unidentified martial artist known as the Invincible Divine Sword.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 251

# Chapter 251

## Plot

Jin Taekyung learns that Zhuge Gyun was completely outclassed and defeated by the Invincible Divine Sword. He recognizes the disguised opponent as Cheongpung after baiting him with the false identity of Park Taekyung of the Taiyuan Park Family; Cheongpung has been using the false name Kang Pung.

Cheongpung explains that he fled from the Three Plum Blossom Elites while returning to Huashan and spent the year traveling, eating dumplings, and barely training. He intends to remain disguised at the Star-Array Grand Banquet and face Taekyung in the finals, confidently claiming he will win.

Jongni Chu meets Taekyung and Cheongpung, immediately befriends the disguised Cheongpung, then walks past the inn and calls out about the Invincible Divine Sword. A masked Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent attacks him, but Jongni Chu overwhelms and incapacitates the agent before he can bite his poison pellet.

## Continuity

- The Invincible Divine Sword is Cheongpung, who has been hiding under the false name Kang Pung after escaping the journey back to Huashan.
- Cheongpung and Jin Taekyung intend to settle their renewed rivalry in the Star-Array Grand Banquet finals.
- The Hidden Shadow Pavilion's finest agent, Shadow Killer, served Song Ho for decades and was incapacitated by Jongni Chu after attacking him.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Kang Pung for 강풍 as Cheongpung's false name, Shadow Killer for 암중살, and Taiyuan Park Family for 태원박가.
- Keep Cheongpung's exposed voice bright, clear, innocent, and casually enthusiastic; his deep voice is an artificial disguise.
### Chapter 252

# Chapter 252

## Plot

The Star-Array Grand Banquet’s field has shrunk from more than five hundred competitors to roughly ten. Jeok Cheongang boasts that the Fire Gate Clan’s martial arts are the greatest under heaven and taunts the Thunderbolt Saber King over Jin Taekyung’s fifteen-minute defeat of the Hebei Peng Family’s Hunyuan Saber. Hong Dao stops their quarrel, then reminds Jeok that he intends to step down after this banquet and may have only a year left to live.

The Sect Leaders discuss the unexpected advancement of the Always-Victorious Sword and the Invincible Divine Sword. The Heavenly Sword True Person reveals that the Invincible Divine Sword is Cheongpung, his junior brother through their shared master, Mae Jonghak, and privately resolves to capture him and bring him back to Huashan. Hong Dao and Jeok Cheongang then reveal that the Hidden Shadow Pavilion verified every main-event finalist except Jongni Chu, whose unfamiliar martial arts and unexplained identity raise the possibility of the Demonic Path, Dark Heaven, the Demonic Cult, or another unknown affiliation. Shadow Killer’s contact was lost three days earlier. Cheongpung enters the quarterfinals against Wudang’s Willow-Cloud Divine Dragon, while Taekyung watches after advancing to the semifinals by defeating the Hebei Peng Family’s Lesser Family Head; the chapter ends as the duel begins.

## Continuity

- Hong Dao plans to retire after the current Star-Array Grand Banquet and believes he has at most about one year remaining.
- The current Huashan Sect Leader, the Heavenly Sword True Person, is Cheongpung’s eldest junior brother by generation through Mae Jonghak; he last saw Cheongpung more than ten years ago and has repeatedly tried to bring him back to Huashan.
- Song Ho recalled his former Hidden Shadow Pavilion subordinates after Hong Dao sought him out over Dark Heaven, restoring the organization for intelligence work.
- The Hidden Shadow Pavilion could verify every main-event finalist except Jongni Chu; Shadow Killer’s contact was lost three days before this chapter after his encounter with Jongni Chu.
- Jeok Cheongang and Hong Dao consider Jongni Chu’s unfamiliar martial arts potentially connected to the Demonic Path, Dark Heaven, the Demonic Cult, or another unknown source, but have no conclusion.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 혼원도 as Hunyun Saber and 유운신룡 as Willow-Cloud Divine Dragon.
- Render 마도 as Demonic Path, keeping it distinct from 마교, Demonic Cult.
- Render 승패병가지상사 as “victory and defeat are simply part of war.”

# Chapter 253

## Plot

Cheongpung defeated Wudang’s Willow-Cloud Divine Dragon in the quarterfinals. He evaded the Divine Dragon’s Sword Energy, reproduced the principle of the Taiji Wisdom Sword, and finally used the Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords to shatter the Pine-Pattern Ancient Sword. - The Willow-Cloud Divine Dragon conceded after Cheongpung shattered his Pine-Pattern Ancient Sword with the Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords.
- Jin Taekyung defeated Gung Gibang in the semifinals and advanced to the Star-Array Grand Banquet finals.
- Gung Gibang has learned only five stages each of the Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms and Dog-Beating Staff Technique.

### Chapter 254

# Chapter 254

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung defeats Gung Gibang, Jongni Chu observes that Taekyung effortlessly countered the Dog-Beating Staff Technique, broke an internal-energy-infused staff with his bare fist, endured the Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms, and deliberately accepted attacks. Jongni Chu suspects Taekyung may possess the Heavenly Martial Physique and notes that he is the Fire King's Disciple.

Hidden Shadow Pavilion agents track Jongni Chu, while separate agents watch Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung. Song Ho disguises himself as an old beggar and follows Jongni Chu, recognizing that he may have encountered him before. Song Ho's recurring pain recalls the final battle against the Demonic Cult decades earlier, when he lost his leg and heard an unknown man threaten to tear it off.

While preparing for the final, Jin Taekyung imagines Cheongpung's violet Sword Energy and considers which martial art he will need to use against him. Hyuk Mujin visits to keep him company before the final, reveals that rumors identify Cheongpung as the Invincible Divine Sword Kang Pung, and insists that Cheongpung must win the semifinal.

Jongni Chu and Cheongpung begin their semifinal. Jongni Chu's sword is filthy and heavily rusted, so Cheongpung offers to lend him his own sword. Jongni Chu refuses, points his rusted blade at Cheongpung, and says he has never had a friend like him; the result of the duel is not shown.

## Continuity

- Jongni Chu has personally judged Jin Taekyung to be an unusual martial artist who may possess the Heavenly Martial Physique.
- The Hidden Shadow Pavilion is actively surveilling Jongni Chu and has separately ordered agents to monitor Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung.
- Song Ho is an intelligence operative and master of disguise who suspects he has encountered Jongni Chu before.
- Song Ho lost one leg in the final battle against the Demonic Cult decades ago, and the old injury now causes recurring pain.
- The semifinal between Jongni Chu and Cheongpung has begun, but its outcome remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Use master of disguise for 역용술 and retain the established titles Always-Victorious Sword, Thousand-Faced Fox, and Hidden Shadow Pavilion.
- Render 청풍 소협 as Young Hero Cheongpung when Hyuk Mujin addresses him.
- Retain the abrupt tonal shift in Jongni Chu's final line: I’ve never had a friend like you.

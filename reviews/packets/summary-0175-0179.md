# Chapters 170–174

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang, the previously unnamed old man, reaches Jang Family Village with Jang-pal and is sheltered by Jang-pal’s family. After learning that tall, handsome swordsmen came looking for him, he prevents Jang-pal from pursuing them and asks where they went. He then arrives at Jang Taebo’s home, identifies himself as a passing traveler, and cryptically directs Taebo toward the worried child nearby. Jeok intends to reach Taiyuan and visit the Lower District Sect while concealing his identity.

Taekyung shows Jang Taebo his huge supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron. The *Find the Master Artisan* Quest is completed, and Taebo commits to forging the material into what he calls the greatest divine weapon under heaven. Jeok detects Taekyung’s Qi Sense despite its invisible nature; the System falsely displays him as Lv. 3. After interrogating Taekyung about his identity, sect, and abilities, Jeok releases overwhelming Extreme Yang Qi into his body.

Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin intervene, but Jeok defeats them effortlessly, recognizing Cheongpung’s Huashan origins and Zaha Divine Technique. Cheongpung’s Plum Blossom Sword Technique prompts Jeok to recall his seven-day, seven-night draw with Mae Jonghak at Mount Jiuhua more than forty years earlier. After Demonic Cultists burned the mountain, Jeok emerged from seclusion and killed all one thousand attackers, becoming known as the Fire King.

Jeok recognizes Cheongpung as Mae Jonghak’s grandson and confirms that he is the Fire King. His internal energy resonates with and strengthens Taekyung’s Scorching Yang Qi. Taekyung conceals his possession of the Flame Divine Palm manual, consumption of the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, and killing of Jopil, but Cheongpung accidentally reveals that Taekyung killed Jin Baekyang, the Blade of Flowers and Taekyung’s great-uncle. Jeok attacks Taekyung with the Flame Divine Palm. Cheongpung briefly slows the strike, but Taekyung’s defense fails, leaving him unconscious with an Internal Injury.

The clash destroys Jang Taebo’s home, leaving only its foundation stones and ending his retirement. Jeok orders Taebo not to seek help and goes ahead to the inn, saying he will decide Taekyung’s fate after hearing the full story. Cheongpung carries the unconscious Taekyung and Mujin away.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang is the nearly one-hundred-year-old Fire King of the Fire Gate Clan, a Supreme Peak master counted among the Ten Kings. Qi Sense’s Lv. 3 reading is false.
- Jeok once fought Mae Jonghak to a draw for seven days and seven nights at Mount Jiuhua. He later killed one thousand Demonic Cultists who burned the mountain.
- Jeok is heading toward Taiyuan and the Lower District Sect in disguise. He seeks an unidentified Peak master skilled mainly in sword and palm techniques.
- Cheongpung is Mae Jonghak’s grandson and disciple. He and Hyuk Mujin defended Taekyung but were defeated without being killed.
- Jin Taekyung is a Level 71 Peak Master with forty-five years of internal energy, Scorching Yang Qi, and seventy unassigned Stat Points. He killed Jin Baekyang and is unconscious with an Internal Injury after Jeok’s Flame Divine Palm.
- Taekyung possesses the Flame Divine Palm manual, consumed the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, and killed Jopil, but Jeok does not yet know these facts.
- Jang Taebo is the retired former Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild. He agreed to forge Taekyung’s Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, but his home was destroyed and his retirement has ended.
- The linked Quest generated after *Find the Master Artisan* remains unresolved.
- Jang-pal’s family, including his daughter Hanga, previously sheltered and fed Jeok. Hanga gave the visiting swordsmen information about Jeok.
- Jeok has many questions about Taekyung and has not yet decided whether to kill him.
- The identities and motives of the tall, handsome martial artists seeking Jeok remain unknown.

### Chapter 175

# Chapter 175

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang watches the unconscious Jin Taekyung and deduces that Taekyung’s Scorching Yang Qi came from the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, since the Fire Gate Clan’s Fire Gate Divine Technique has been passed down through a strict one-person succession with no leakage. He wonders what to do with Taekyung and briefly suspects dark arts after hearing Taekyung mutter unfamiliar words in his sleep.

Taekyung awakens after roughly half a day unconscious with only a minor Internal Injury from Jeok Cheongang’s Flame Divine Palm. He learns that Jeok and Cheongpung have been drinking downstairs for more than three shichen and that Jeok ordered Hyuk Mujin to report as soon as he woke.

Taekyung reveals that he possesses Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm manual and the Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron sword, while the Blazing Flame Divine Pill is inside his body after he consumed it during the battle with Pung Yang. Fearing that Jeok may be Jopil’s master and demand the items back, Taekyung and Mujin attempt to flee through the window. Jeok knocks Mujin down with a bone, then confronts Taekyung, whose excuse that he was going to the privy fails.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang concludes that Jin Taekyung’s Scorching Yang Qi is the result of consuming the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, not instruction in the Fire Gate Clan’s secret Fire Gate Divine Technique.
- Jin Taekyung wakes after at least half a day unconscious with only a minor Internal Injury from Jeok Cheongang’s Flame Divine Palm.
- Jin Taekyung has Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm manual and Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron sword, while the consumed Blazing Flame Divine Pill remains inside his body.
- Jeok Cheongang confronts Taekyung after Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin try to escape the inn; the consequences remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain 기모찌 and 야메떼 as “kimochi” and “yamete” in Taekyung’s dream utterances.

### Chapter 176

# Chapter 176

## Plot

Jin Taekyung is detained by Jeok Cheongang at Honghwa Inn after attempting to escape. Jeok interrogates him about Jopil, and Taekyung admits that he met the wandering martial artist about two months earlier.

Jeok reveals Jopil's past: during an epidemic in Anhui Province, he rescued an orphan named Jangcheon, eventually accepted him as his Disciple, and trained him at a hut deep in Mount Jiuhua. Jangcheon later stagnated in his martial progress and secretly visited a pleasure house while claiming to be in closed-door cultivation. The Azure Sky Sword King, one of the Ten Kings and Grand Family Head of the Nangong Family, then visited Jeok to report that Jangcheon was killing innocent women and mediocre wandering martial artists. After the visitor left, Jeok detected the scent of blood on the wind.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang rescued an orphan named Jangcheon during an epidemic in Anhui Province and eventually accepted him as his Disciple.
- Jangcheon trained under Jeok Cheongang for years, showing extreme resolve and obedience; Jeok improved his constitution through cleansing his sinews and washing his marrow.
- Jangcheon later stopped progressing in martial arts, secretly visited a pleasure house, and became the killer known as Jopil.
- The Azure Sky Sword King, one of the Ten Kings and the Grand Family Head of the Nangong Family, came to Jeok Cheongang because Jangcheon was killing innocent women and mediocre wandering martial artists; Jeok then smelled blood after the visitor departed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 장천 as “Jangcheon,” with the name meaning “Vast Sky.”
- Render 창천검왕 as “Azure Sky Sword King,” 태상가주 as “Grand Family Head,” 벌모세수 as “cleansing the sinews and washing the marrow,” 상단전 as “upper dantian,” and 무극 as “the Martial Extremity realm.”
### Chapter 177

# Chapter 177

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang recounts how he discovered that his Disciple Jangcheon had become Jopil, a remorseless killer. After learning of Jangcheon's murders from the Azure Sky Sword King, Jeok secretly followed him for four months before finding him torturing and killing a bound man in a red-light district. Jangcheon explained that he had never changed, only learned to embrace the rule that Might Makes Right, and admitted that killing was the only way to fill his emptiness. Although Jeok declared that he would sever Jangcheon's Sinews and Meridians, cripple his dantian, and imprison him, he could not bring himself to kill the Disciple he regarded as a son and grandson. Jangcheon used that weakness to prove Jeok could not execute him, held a vial of Bone-Melting Powder between his lips, gave his Master a final bow, and declared that he would leave and never return. In the present, Jeok breaks down in tears while claiming that rain is leaking through the old inn's ceiling.

## Continuity

- The Azure Sky Sword King brought Jeok Cheongang sufficient circumstantial and physical evidence of Jangcheon's murders for Jeok to begin secretly following his Disciple.
- Jeok followed Jangcheon for four months and initially found no proof because Jangcheon visited pleasure houses, drank, and then returned to his hut to train diligently.
- Jangcheon was torturing a bound middle-aged man in a red-light district when Jeok confronted him; he killed the man without hesitation after Jeok ordered him to stop.
- Jangcheon claims he never changed, and that childhood deprivation taught him that the weak are trampled while the strong do the trampling.
- Jangcheon identifies Wangpal, Hong Sochil, and So U-pyeong as three of the fifteen youths who once tried to take his dirt-covered dumpling; he says the others are dead.
- Jangcheon takes pleasure in killing and says the emptiness after a victim dies cannot be relieved by drinking or visiting women.
- Jeok regards Jangcheon as an only son and grandson despite their lack of blood relation, because both had been alone and Jangcheon was the only person Jeok had raised as family.
- Jeok decides that severing Jangcheon's Sinews and Meridians, crippling his dantian, and imprisoning him for life is the most he can do because he cannot kill him.
- Jangcheon knew Jeok had been watching him and deliberately committed murder before him to confirm that Jeok could not execute him, even after hundreds of victims.
- Jangcheon held a vial of Bone-Melting Powder between his lips, gave Jeok a final bow, and declared that he would leave and never return.
- Jeok is crying in the present while speaking with Jin Taekyung and claims that the old inn's ceiling is leaking rain.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 화골분 as “Bone-Melting Powder.”
- Retain 강자지존 as “Might Makes Right.”
- Render 살성 as “Slaughter Saint” and 살귀 as “killing fiend.”
- Render 면벽 수련 as “face-the-wall meditation.”
### Chapter 178

# Chapter 178

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang reveals that he asked the Azure Sky Sword King to help Jopil escape, considering it the last duty he could perform for his Disciple. He admits that even if he could return to the past, he would save Jopil again, though the following ten years would still be filled with regret.

Jeok learns that Jopil died fighting Jin Taekyung after drawing on his innate qi. He acknowledges that Taekyung did what Jeok should have done ten years earlier, declares that he owes Taekyung a debt, and sincerely apologizes to Taekyung and the others for his earlier rash actions. After dismissing them, Jeok mourns Jangcheon alone and recalls the Disciple's claim that he had never changed.

The next morning, Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin awaken at the inn. Cheongpung is excited because it is New Year's Day, prompting Taekyung to realize that he is no longer at the Jin Family of Taiyuan and to remember Wipeng's warning from before his departure.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang asked the Azure Sky Sword King to help Jopil escape from the Nangong Family's pursuit.
- Jeok Cheongang says he would have saved Jopil again if given the chance, despite regretting the consequences for the following ten years.
- Jopil died fighting Jin Taekyung after drawing on his innate qi; half of his upper body was destroyed in the battle.
- Jeok Cheongang accepts that Taekyung did what Jeok should have done ten years earlier and acknowledges a debt to him.
- Jeok Cheongang sincerely apologized to Taekyung and his companions for his rash actions.
- Jeok Cheongang continues to grieve Jangcheon, remembering him as the child and Disciple he once believed he had raised like a son.
- Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Hyuk Mujin remain at the inn after Jeok Cheongang dismisses them.
- The current day is New Year's Day, and Taekyung realizes that he is away from the Jin Family of Taiyuan despite Wipeng's warning.

- None.
### Chapter 179

# Chapter 179

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung realize it is New Year's Day and prepare to leave the inn for the Jin Family of Taiyuan, already late because Taekyung forgot Wipeng's warning. Jeok Cheongang and Jang Taebo are drinking together at the inn, while Cheongpung eats voraciously.

Jang Taebo accepts Taekyung's commission to forge the Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron into a weapon capable of making the world tremble, refuses monetary payment because the opportunity itself is sufficient, and asks Taekyung to have his fire-destroyed home rebuilt.

Taekyung returns the Flame Divine Palm martial arts manual and the Unnamed Sword to Jeok Cheongang, who reveals that a Fire Gate Clan treasure called the Treasured Jade is missing. Jeok says someone may have deceived Taekyung or Jopil may have lost it, then orders Taekyung to keep the manual and sword, warning that anything originating from the Fire Gate Clan will eventually be reclaimed. Jeok leaves with Cheongpung after threatening to kill Taekyung if the items are stolen or learned by him, and says they will meet again soon.

## Continuity

- Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Hyuk Mujin spent the night at an inn and depart on New Year's Day to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- Jang Taebo has accepted the Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron commission without requiring payment and asks Taekyung to rebuild his home, which Jeok Cheongang's Flame Divine Palm destroyed.
- Jeok Cheongang is searching for the missing Treasured Jade, a Fire Gate Clan treasure that was not among the items Gong Yacheong and Socheon gave Taekyung.
- Jeok Cheongang requires Taekyung to retain the Flame Divine Palm martial arts manual and Unnamed Sword for now and intends to reclaim Fire Gate Clan items.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 보옥 as “Treasured Jade.”

# Chapters 505–509

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang achieves Returned to Youth, becoming a tall, muscular, red-haired man while retaining his gruff personality. He confronts Zhuge Feng over the Demonic Cult’s attack on Mount Jiuhua and demands that the Zhuge Clan support the approaching war as effectively as it did during the Great Faction War. Taekyung’s party departs for Henan and the New Murim Alliance.

At Wudang, Sect Leader Hyeoncheon learns of the approaching war and decides to travel to Henan. A pursuit party claims to have captured the Killing Ghost, but the remains they bring back are neither beast nor human, leaving the identity and nature of the captive unresolved.

Taekyung’s group travels by swift ship along the Yangtze. Mu Song explains that he wants to aid the New Murim Alliance but lacks authority over the Yangtze River Channel League, whose major decisions belong to the Seafaring King. The League, the Green Forest Alliance, the North Sea Ice Palace, and the Nanman Beast Palace may all influence the alliance’s prospects. Jeok expects the Nanman Beast Palace to favor orthodox Murim because of the Fire Gate Clan’s historical aid, but doubts the isolationist North Sea Ice Palace will participate.

Mungyeong reveals that Mu Song and five Water Dragon Stronghold subordinates recognize him as an extraordinary Supreme Peak master. He forces them to keep his identity secret, even from the Seafaring King, then sends Taekyung into the Yangtze. The Linked Quest **Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2** begins.

Mungyeong explains that Taekyung’s unstable movement across the water results from the Fire Gate Clan’s excessively violent internal energy. He assigns Taekyung to practice **Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water** behind the ship for ten days or until they reach Henan. The training is intended to improve his internal-energy control, reduce energy consumption, strengthen amplification, and enable further technical growth.

# Chapter 510

## Plot

Mu Song, a lifelong sailor who prefers water to land, becomes miserable after Jeok Cheongang forces the travelers to split between two swift ships. He orders his subordinates to stay away from the stern and keep Mungyeong's identity secret, but Mungyeong catches him insulting him and relays Jeok's command that Mu Song personally handle all necessary ship operations and meal deliveries. Mungyeong also orders only two meal portions until Henan because Taekyung is expected to have no appetite.

Taekyung's internal energy is nearly depleted while he practices on the Yangtze, but instead of sinking into panic he notices a large, stealthily approaching body whose fin looks familiar: a shark appears to be swimming in the Yangtze.

## Continuity

- Mu Song and the Water Dragon Stronghold subordinates who know Mungyeong's power are forbidden to discuss Mungyeong's identity.
- Mungyeong is enforcing Jeok Cheongang's orders aboard Mu Song's ship, including keeping Mu Song away from the stern except when necessary and making him deliver meals personally.
- Taekyung's Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water practice has nearly depleted his internal energy, and a shark-like creature is approaching him in the Yangtze.

### Chapter 511

# Chapter 511

## Plot

Two swift ships of the Yangtze River Channel League pass a pleasure boat on the Yangtze. Nearby fishermen recognize the ships as raiders but are surprised when they do not attack.

Taekyung catches up to the ships by directing five sharks with the Water God Dragon's sinew. He asks fishermen for directions, releases one shark to them after receiving food, and continues onward; the fisherman realizes the white sturgeon has given him a new advantage over ordinary swift ships.

After alternating between rest and movement for half a day, Taekyung partially replenishes his depleted internal energy and releases the remaining four sharks. A person then approaches him by walking across the surface of the water and calls him a lunatic.

## Continuity

- Taekyung pursued the Yangtze River Channel League's two swift ships by training five sharks as a water-ski team with the Water God Dragon's sinew.
- Taekyung's internal energy, previously nearly depleted by training, was partially replenished through alternating rest and movement during the pursuit.
- Taekyung released the trained sharks after catching up to the swift ships; one white sturgeon remained with a fisherman, while the other four were released immediately before a water-walking stranger approached.

# Chapter 512

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang unexpectedly appears after disembarking from the swift ship because of seasickness, carrying spiced beef that Taekyung eats. The sharks and the distant swift ship disappear while they talk.

Taekyung admits that using the sharks to replenish his internal energy and chase the swift ship had become meaningless. He decides to follow by his own strength instead, while Jeok approves and leaves ahead toward Henan.

Jeok explains that the Fire Gate Clan's martial arts have a particularly violent flow and that it took him years to calm his own. He acknowledges Taekyung's exceptional martial talent and the advantage of having the Fire King and Slaughter Saint as advisors.

A proclamation from Mount Song announces the New Murim Alliance. Shaolin, Huashan, the Nine Sects and One Gang, and the Five Great Families begin joining, while the Murim debates the threat posed by demonic and heterodox practitioners and the Outer Lands; ten days pass amid the spreading upheaval.

## Continuity

# Chapter 514

## Plot

A group of twenty Shaolin monks, led by the middle-aged martial monk Jung Ho, travels toward the ferry at Xixia with a mysterious, hoarse-voiced figure in a conical hat. Hearing cries of murder, they hurry and encounter Sama Pyo, the Young Sect Leader of the Black Dragon Demon Gate, with his towering subordinate.

The subordinate has killed Blood Cudgel Do Sangho on the main road. Jung Ho identifies the corpse and explains that Do Sangho recently killed five innocent merchants in Shaanxi. Sama Pyo agrees to explain the incident, apologize to nearby commoners, and offer compensation, while shifting responsibility to his subordinate.

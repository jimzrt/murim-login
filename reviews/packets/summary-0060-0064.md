# Chapters 55–59

## Plot

Taekyung wakes in Murim after roughly two hours of deathlike sleep and learns that the reconnaissance squad has sent messengers to the Jin Family’s main force. He orders the exhausted squad to reach Eight Spring Gorge before the battle is lost. There, Mount Heng’s larger army is trapped in the Jin Family’s terrain-controlled gorge while hidden cliff archers fire on them. Lee Cheonbaek leads Mount Heng’s core forces in a desperate assault, but the Head Elder overwhelms him with Sword Energy and prepares to kill him.

Taekyung arrives, splits a spear aimed at the Head Elder, and publicly brands him a traitor. The Jin Family’s First, Second, and Third Elders reveal concealed Peak-level abilities and turn against their own side. A signal flare prompts the embedded Three Paths Sect, Byeokdo Sect, Gunggwimun, and other Five Gates of Shanxi forces to attack their supposed allies, exposing a decades-old conspiracy. The Head Elder confirms that “they” killed Lee Seogeun but does not identify the accomplice or explain the complete plan.

The Discipline Hall Master dies defending the Jin Family’s laws, rallying the Jin and Mount Heng fighters against the black-clad conspirators. The Second and Third Elders are killed, while the First Elder admits greed and glory as his stated motives and points Taekyung toward the Head Elder when Taekyung identifies revenge as the deeper cause. Jin Wikyung expels the First Elder and leaves him surrounded by Wipeng and ten senior members before leading guards toward Taekyung.

Taekyung attacks the Head Elder with the Gambler Title active but is decisively overpowered. The elder cuts his spear down to an iron rod. While trying to rescue Jin Wikyung, Taekyung grabs the wrong person and escapes with the gravely wounded, silenced, and paralyzed Lee Cheonbaek. With Hyuk Mujin and the reconnaissance squad, he forms an encirclement against the Head Elder, belatedly realizing that his raid-party tactics are useless without a tank, healer, or ranged damage dealer. The battle remains unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Eight Spring Gorge battle continues. The Head Elder remains the dominant combatant and can overpower Taekyung’s Gambler-boosted attacks with Sword Energy.
- The Head Elder’s betrayal and the Five Gates conspiracy are exposed, but the accomplice who killed Lee Seogeun, the meaning of the signal, the revenge motive, and the full conspiracy remain unresolved.
- The Second and Third Elders are dead. The First Elder is alive, expelled from the Jin Family, and surrounded by Wipeng and ten senior members; his fate is unresolved.
- Jin Wikyung is alive and leading guards toward the Head Elder. Taekyung mistakenly rescued Lee Cheonbaek instead of Wikyung.
- Lee Cheonbaek remains alive but unable to speak or move. Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and the reconnaissance squad are confronting the Head Elder.
- Taekyung knows that logging in or logging out leaves the other side in a deathlike sleep. The Ark - 2020 capsule’s safe operation, ultimate purpose, and route between realities remain unresolved.
- Taekyung’s Traitor Chain Quest remains active and requires punishing the traitor and leading the battle to victory; failure means death.

### Chapter 60

# Chapter 60

## Plot

The Head Elder is appalled by the reconnaissance squad’s dirt-throwing tactic. When Hyuk Mujin orders them to mix in rocks, the Head Elder charges; Taekyung intercepts the Sword Energy aimed at Hyuk, is cut in the side, and stalls the elder with taunting banter. Taekyung orders an encircling formation, and the squad closes in from every side.

Jin Wikyung arrives with several dozen guards and confronts the Head Elder. After confirming that the Second and Third Elders are dead and the First Elder is nearly defeated, Wikyung orders the Jin Family and Mount Heng forces to strike the rebels. The Head Elder answers with the Finger-Flicking Technique, firing stone fragments that kill several martial artists, then tears through the scattered fighters while evading Wikyung’s Sword Energy. Nearly twenty people die in the opening exchange, and Taekyung recognizes that he has been using their deaths as openings.

Taekyung uses a fallen martial artist’s body as cover and attacks with One Flash. The result of the attack and the battle remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is wounded in the side while saving Hyuk Mujin from the Head Elder, then remains in the fight and uses One Flash against him.
- Jin Wikyung reaches Taekyung with several dozen guards and leads the combined assault against the Head Elder.
- The Head Elder’s Finger-Flicking Technique uses stone fragments powered by his internal energy and kills many Jin Family and allied martial artists; he remains alive and fighting.
- The reconnaissance squad remains engaged with the Head Elder, and the outcome of Taekyung’s One Flash is unresolved.
- Lee Cheonbaek remains alive but unable to speak or move after Taekyung rescued him in the previous chapter.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the raid-party “melee damage dealers” framing and Taekyung’s dirt-and-rock tactic.
- Keep “Finger-Flicking Technique,” “Sword Energy,” “One Flash,” “Head Elder,” and the established Jin Family and Mount Heng terminology.
- Preserve the Head Elder’s contemptuous “The likes of you, stopping me?” and the pepper euphemism in his exchange with Taekyung.
### Chapter 61

# Chapter 61

## Plot

Taekyung’s One Flash destroys the Head Elder’s arm, but the elder survives and admits that Taekyung’s body can now withstand the technique’s side effects. Jin Wikyung’s ancestral treasure sword is cut apart by the Head Elder’s incomplete Sword Force, leaving Wikyung’s internal energy scattered and his insides wrenched.

The Head Elder reveals the hidden history behind his betrayal: during the Great Faction War, an ambush killed almost all of the three hundred volunteers under his command while his elder brother became Family Head and turned the disaster into a victory. The surviving eight elders and sect leaders waited decades for revenge. Taekyung accuses the Head Elder of using revenge to seize Shanxi, and the Head Elder resumes the fight.

The Jin Family forces overwhelm the one-armed Head Elder, who draws on his innate qi and forms Sword Force for a final attack. He compresses fifty feet in a single step and brings his sword down on Taekyung. Taekyung answers with another One Flash; the result and the battle remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Head Elder has lost an arm and is badly wounded, but he remains a superhuman master using incomplete Sword Force and innate qi.
- Jin Wikyung survives the destruction of his ancestral sword and continues fighting despite scattered internal energy and wrenched insides.
- Taekyung remains conscious after using One Flash and has enough physical growth to endure its side effects, though his internal energy is exhausted after the second use.
- The Head Elder’s Great Faction War history and revenge motive are revealed, but the identity of his Sound Transmission accomplice and the full conspiracy remain unresolved.
- The outcome of Taekyung’s second One Flash and the Eight Spring Gorge battle remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve “Sword Force,” “Hand Force,” “innate qi,” “One Flash,” “Head Elder,” and “Sect Leaders of the Five Gates of Shanxi.”
- Keep the Head Elder’s succession motive and Taekyung’s blunt revenge accusation explicit.
- Render the measurements as thirty centimeters for nearly a foot of Sword Energy and fifty feet for five zhang.
### Chapter 62

# Chapter 62

## Plot

Lee Cheonbaek ambushes the Head Elder with a dagger, allowing Taekyung to finish the wounded master with One Flash. Lee dies in the process. The System records Lee's defeat, grants Taekyung multiple level-ups, then records the defeat of Jin Baekyang, completes the Traitor Chain Quest, greatly increases his Level and Fame, and awards him the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi title.

The First Elder dies after warning Wipeng to beware Dark Heaven, and the black-clad forces surrender. On the cliff, Gunggwimun Sect Leader Jin Chung orders his fifty disciples to scatter and stays behind to protect them. A concealed Dark Heaven agent kills Jin Chung and massacres the disciples, revealing that Dark Heaven rescued the conspirators from the Demonic Cult forty years earlier, implanted gu in their heads, and used them for a larger plan. The agent deliberately spares Taekyung for the present.

## Continuity

- The Head Elder's name is Jin Baekyang. He is dead, killed by Taekyung after Lee Cheonbaek's ambush.
- Lee Cheonbaek is dead after helping Taekyung defeat Jin Baekyang.
- Taekyung's exact post-battle Level is unknown, but he received multiple level-ups, greatly increased Fame, completed the Traitor Chain Quest, and acquired the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi title.
- Jin Wikyung survives and does not execute the Head Elder himself. The Eight Spring Gorge battle ends with the black-clad forces surrendering.
- The First Elder dies after naming Dark Heaven; the cause of his sudden death remains unknown.
- Dark Heaven is identified as the force that rescued the conspirators, bound them with gu, and directed the larger scheme. Its full purpose, agents, and intentions toward Taekyung remain unresolved.
- Jin Chung and the Gunggwimun disciples who remained with him are killed by Dark Heaven agents. One agent spares Taekyung for the present.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve “Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi,” “Dark Heaven,” “One Flash,” “Sword Force,” “innate qi,” and “Head Elder.”
- Render the System notifications as “You have defeated Lv. 75 Lee Cheonbaek!”, “You have defeated Lv. 95 Jin Baekyang!”, “Quest **Traitor** completed!”, and “Your Level has increased greatly!”
- Keep “gu” in italics at first use with a footnote explaining the poison associated with venomous creatures.
- Preserve the title delivery and the distinction between Jin Baekyang's revealed personal name and the title Head Elder.
### Chapter 63

# Chapter 63

## Plot

Five days after the Eight Spring Gorge battle, rumors spread that the Jin Family of Taiyuan defeated Mount Heng and that the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi—Jin Taekyung—played the decisive role. Mount Heng Sword Sect is collapsing after Lee Seogeun and Blood Wolf Sword Lee Cheonbaek die, while the First Young Master is killed by mounted bandits.

Jin Mukyung, the Jin Family's Second Young Master and Heaven Shaking Sword, returns after several years away. Disguised beneath a bamboo hat and covered in grime, he hears the rumors on the road and reaches the family estate, where Hyuk Mujin is now Captain of the Gatekeepers.

Taekyung completes Qi Circulation and advances the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique to the Eighth Stage. His Status Window shows Level 50, Fame 1,180, 100 Remaining Points, fifteen years of Internal Energy, and four Titles, including the new Peak-grade Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi title. Before returning to reality, he resolves to grow as strong as possible. Jin Mukyung then bursts into his room; after Taekyung wipes away the grime, the two brothers recognize their identical faces, and Mukyung calls him hyung.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is Level 50 with Fame 1,180, fifteen years of Internal Energy, 100 Remaining Points, and four active Titles: Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Scion of a Prestigious Family, Novice Trainee, and Gambler.
- Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi is a Peak-grade Title granting All Stats +10 and Fame +100; Taekyung's fame has spread throughout Shanxi.
- Taekyung's Jin Family's Cultivation Technique reaches the Eighth Stage, but his Internal Energy remains at fifteen years because its accumulation speed is poor.
- Jin Mukyung returns to the Jin Family after several years away. He is the Heaven Shaking Sword and Jin Family Second Young Master, and he resembles Taekyung closely enough to be mistaken for him.
- Hyuk Mujin is serving as Captain of the Gatekeepers and recognizes Jin Mukyung when he returns.
- Mount Heng Sword Sect is on the verge of closing its gates; Lee Seogeun and Lee Cheonbaek are dead, and the First Young Master is killed by mounted bandits.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve “Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi,” “Heaven Shaking Sword,” “Eighth Stage,” “Qi Circulation,” “Internal Energy,” and “Captain of the Gatekeepers.”
- Keep the title “Heavenly Axe” for Jang Sam and the staged title delivery “Yama. Whip. Great Hero!” from prior chapters.
- Retain the Aoji Coal Mine footnote for Taekyung's comparison of the grime-covered visitor.
### Chapter 64

# Chapter 64

## Plot

Jin Wikyung spends two hours painting the recent battle and Taekyung as a heroic figure, but Wipeng interrupts and tears up the unfinished painting. Wipeng then reveals that Jin Mukyung, Wikyung's second younger brother, has returned after three years and gone to Taekyung's residence; a crash soon announces that the residence has collapsed.

Jin Mukyung is twenty-three, bears the epithet Heaven Shaking Sword, and reached the Peak realm at a young age. He greets Taekyung warmly but immediately punishes his casual speech by throwing him into a wall and attacking him with the Reformation Fist. Taekyung gradually reads Mukyung's movements, partially evades a strike aimed at his temple, and accepts a second round motivated by martial pride.

Mukyung remains far stronger than Taekyung while fighting without internal energy. Taekyung uses ten of the one hundred points accumulated through his ten level-ups to raise Agility, becomes confident that Mukyung's punch is slow enough to evade, and is struck anyway.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung is Taekyung's second older brother, twenty-three years old, the Heaven Shaking Sword, and a Peak-level martial genius who has spent three years at Heaven's Gate Temple in Henan.
- Jin Mukyung is substantially stronger and more skilled than Taekyung; he uses the Reformation Fist and a grappling technique while sparring with Taekyung without internal energy.
- Taekyung has one hundred unassigned points from ten level-ups and spends ten of them on Agility during his spar with Mukyung.
- The outcome of Taekyung's ongoing spar with Jin Mukyung remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Reformation Fist for 갱생권, Jin Mukyung's named fist technique.
- Render 금나수 as grappling technique rather than inventing a proper-name translation.
- Retain hyung for 형 in Taekyung's greeting to his older brother.

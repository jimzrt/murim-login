## Previous block summary

# Chapters 120–124

## Plot

Jin Taekyung kills Pung Yang with the Unnamed Sword after its Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron destroys Pung Yang’s Body-Protecting Qi. The victory completes the Temporary Strength Pill Quest, restoring Taekyung’s health and granting five level-ups, substantial EXP and Fame, and medicines that save many wounded Mount Heng Sword Sect survivors. Taekyung also fully absorbs the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, reaching forty-five years of internal energy with the Scorching Yang Qi attribute.

The Mount Heng Sword Sect is devastated, but Lee Seowol remains Sect Leader and vows to rebuild it. She accepts Jin Wikyung’s invitation to the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s New Year gathering and offers the Jin Family the sect’s territorial rights as an apology. She also offers the Blood Wolf Sword Technique, Blood Wolf Footwork, and Shura Annihilating Fist in exchange for marrying Taekyung. Taekyung initially rejects the proposal because Seowol is seventeen and because he loves Song Song, while Jin Mukyung insists that the three Peak martial arts make the marriage worthwhile.

Taekyung reveals that Jopil left behind the Flame Divine Palm, a Supreme Peak secret art of the Fire Gate Clan restricted to practitioners with Scorching Yang Qi. Mukyung warns that possessing another sect’s secret technique could make the Jin Family thieves in the eyes of the Murim. The art belonged to the Fire King, one of the world’s twenty greatest experts, whose survival after a legendary battle at Mount Jiuhua remains unknown. Mukyung returns to the Jin Family while still recovering, accompanied by Hyuk Mujin. Wolhwa departs to tour northern Shanxi and promises to maintain close ties with the Jin Family. After the carriage leaves, Lee Seowol appears and addresses Taekyung as Benefactor.

## Continuity

- Pung Yang is dead, and the Red Wind Band’s remaining forces and wider response to his death remain unresolved.
- Taekyung has fully absorbed the Blazing Flame Divine Pill and possesses forty-five years of internal energy with the Scorching Yang Qi attribute.
- The Temporary Strength Pill Quest granted Taekyung five level-ups, major EXP and Fame, and Full Recovery; the pill’s origin and long-term effects remain unknown.
- Jin Mukyung survived his Internal Injuries and can travel, but he is not fully recovered.
- Cheol Mubaek has broken limbs and serious Internal Injuries and requires at least four months of recuperation.
- Twenty-five Mount Heng survivors remain, including Lee Seowol; five were initially unlikely to survive. The sect’s reconstruction remains uncertain.
- Lee Seowol is seventeen, remains Sect Leader, accepted the New Year invitation, and has proposed marriage to Taekyung in exchange for three Peak martial arts.
- Taekyung intends to reject Seowol’s political marriage because he loves Song Song, but the proposal and territorial transaction have not been resolved.
- Cheol Mubaek gave Lee Seowol the Shura Annihilating Fist manual despite its single-successor, transmission-only-to-the-worthy tradition.
- Taekyung possesses Jopil’s Flame Divine Palm manual. Jopil claimed to be its nineteenth-generation successor, but how he obtained it and whether the claim is true remain uncertain.
- The Flame Divine Palm is a Fire Gate Clan secret art restricted to owners of Scorching Yang Qi. The Fire Gate Clan follows a single-successor tradition.
- The Fire King, the art’s legendary master, may still be alive.
- Wolhwa is Eun Sowol, the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi Branch Leader, and plans to tour northern Shanxi while preserving the alliance with the Jin Family.

# Chapter 125

## Plot

Cheol Mubaek, Lee Seowol, and the surviving Mount Heng martial artists thank Jin Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin for protecting Seowol and preserving the Mount Heng Sword Sect, while apologizing for Lee Cheonbaek's crimes. Taekyung refuses to accept their apology, saying the Jin Family is the party entitled to receive it. Seowol will visit Taiyuan on New Year's Day and expects Taekyung's answer to her marriage proposal.

On the journey back to the Jin Family of Taiyuan, Jin Mukyung remains injured after only four days of recovery. Taekyung examines Pung Yang's Temporary Strength Pill and learns that it has an unknown maker, an unknown Grade, a Peak-or-higher restriction, and powerful temporary effects including +100 combat-related stats, fifteen years of internal energy, and access to Body-Protecting Qi, with an unspecified price. A distant voice calls out Mukyung's and Taekyung's names, but its speaker is not identified.

## Continuity

- Cheol Mubaek sincerely considers Jin Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin Benefactors for protecting Lee Seowol and enabling the Mount Heng Sword Sect's survival, and vows to repay them even at the cost of his life.
- The surviving Mount Heng Sword Sect members formally apologize for Lee Cheonbaek's wrongdoing; Taekyung refuses the apology because he lacks the right to receive or grant forgiveness and says the Jin Family should receive it.
- Lee Seowol is due to visit the Jin Family of Taiyuan on New Year's Day, which is fewer than fifteen days away, and continues to await Taekyung's answer to her marriage proposal.
- The Temporary Strength Pill is made by an unknown person, has an unknown Grade, is restricted to Peak martial artists or higher, raises latent power for about one shichen, grants +100 combat-related stats, adds fifteen years of internal energy, enables Body-Protecting Qi, and carries an unspecified price or aftereffect.
- A previously unidentified person calls out to Jin Mukyung and Jin Taekyung from a distance at the end of the chapter.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 은원 as gratitude and grudges, 원단 as New Year's Day, 갑자 as jiazi with a sixty-year-cycle footnote, and 시진 as shichen.
- Render 아이템창 as Item Window and preserve the Temporary Strength Pill's unknown maker, unknown Grade, Peak-or-higher restriction, stated effects, and unspecified price.
- Preserve Cheol Mubaek's gruff teasing toward Taekyung and affectionate grandfatherly warmth toward Mukyung.

# Chapter 126

## Plot

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng rushed from Taiyuan with fifty elite guards after learning that Pung Yang, the Red Wind Band, the Mount Heng Sword Sect, and the Jin brothers were in danger. They learned en route that Pung Yang had died, the Red Wind Band had been annihilated, Taekyung had defeated Pung Yang, and Mukyung had suffered severe but nonfatal injuries. At Sakju, Wikyung welcomed Mukyung, Taekyung, and Hyuk Mujin with an enormous banner and an embrace that worsened Mukyung's injuries. During the reunion, Mukyung explained that Pung Yang had become terrifyingly powerful after taking a crimson pill, prompting speculation that it was a Demonic Cult relic with catastrophic side effects. Taekyung, realizing the danger of the pill, accidentally revealed that he had kept it in his Inventory.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung and Wipeng traveled from Taiyuan to Sakju with fifty elite guards after receiving an emergency report about Pung Yang and the Mount Heng Sword Sect.

- The Jin Family of Taiyuan erected a huge Sakju banner celebrating the safe return of Jin Mukyung, Jin Taekyung, and Hyuk Mujin.
- Taekyung possesses one Temporary Strength Pill in his Inventory and admitted this to Jin Wikyung, Jin Mukyung, and Wipeng at the chapter's end.

# Chapter 127

## Plot

Jin Taekyung defuses Jin Mukyung's anger over the Temporary Strength Pill, but Mukyung's chest wound reopens when he rises in fury and a physician must treat him.

The System updates the Temporary Strength Pill's description after Wipeng unconsciously names Dark Heaven. The item is identified as a Dark Heaven product, while Jin Wikyung and Wipeng refuse to explain what Dark Heaven is.

- The updated Temporary Strength Pill description states that it is an elixir restricted to Peak martial artists or higher; for approximately one shichen it greatly raises latent power at a following price, grants +100 combat-related stats and fifteen years of internal energy, and enables Body-Protecting Qi. Its Grade remains unknown.
- Jin Mukyung's chest wound reopened during the drinking gathering and was treated by a physician; he remains incompletely recovered.
- Wipeng is known among the escort force as the God of Drinking, while the Night King is a rumor-based epithet for Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

# Chapter 128

## Plot

The Jin Family of Taiyuan arrives in a village with fifty mounted riders and receives an enthusiastic public welcome. Jin Taekyung draws the Unnamed Sword as the crowd chants his name, the Jin Family's name, and the Heaven Shaking Sword.

The System raises Taekyung's Fame repeatedly and strengthens the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi Title. His Status Window shows Level 61, Fame 2,100 (+250), 60 remaining points, and a strengthened Title effect of all stats +15 and Fame +200.

A county magistrate praises Taekyung and Jin Mukyung for allegedly defeating more than five hundred mounted bandits. Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin deliberately inflate the account further, claiming nearly six hundred enemies and 285 kills each, which leaves the crowd and officials awestruck. The county magistrate then delivers an invitation from the City Lord to a gathering with several young prodigies, and the System creates a Quest.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi Title effect has strengthened from all stats +10 and Fame +100 to all stats +15 and Fame +200.
- Jin Taekyung is now Level 61 with Fame 2,100 (+250), 60 remaining stat points, and forty-five years of internal energy.
- Hyuk Mujin knowingly helps Taekyung present an exaggerated account of the Red Wind Band battle, turning the mounted bandits into First Rate masters and portraying Pung Yang as an invincible swordsman.
- A county magistrate gives Jin Taekyung an invitation from the City Lord for a gathering with several young prodigies; the System creates a Quest in response.

### Chapter 129

# Chapter 129

## Plot

The System creates the Third Rate quest The City Lord's Invitation after the ten-year-old City Lord of Shanxi Province invites Jin Taekyung to a luncheon with young prodigies. Taekyung intends to refuse, but Hyuk Mujin seizes the invitation and accepts on his behalf; Taekyung supports the deception, so the Quest becomes accepted and cannot be canceled.

The Jin group takes over the Phoenix Inn for the night and begins recovering from three days of continuous drinking. Jin Wikyung explains that the government and the Murim recognize each other's domains, cooperate when useful, and should not be needlessly antagonized.

Jin Mukyung reveals that Shanxi's current City Lord is the Emperor's ten-year-old youngest brother, a direct imperial relative holding the title of Prince. He was appointed City Lord at age five, and the current Emperor is rumored to have assassinated the Crown Prince before taking the throne. Mukyung was summoned to meet the City Lord three years earlier and remembers the encounter as a nightmare.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Mujin accepted the City Lord's Invitation Quest for Jin Taekyung, and Taekyung confirmed the acceptance before the county magistrate.
- The City Lord of Shanxi Province is a ten-year-old direct member of the imperial family, the Emperor's youngest brother, and a Prince; he has held the City Lord position since age five.
- The current imperial family uses the Zhu surname; the current Emperor is rumored to have assassinated the Crown Prince, his older brother, to take the throne.
- Jin Mukyung was summoned to meet the City Lord three years ago and described the encounter as a nightmare.
- The government and the Murim maintain a long-standing noninterference relationship, recognize each other's authority, and provide mutual assistance.
- The Jin Family of Taiyuan has not yet become one of the Nine Sects and One Gang or one of the Five Great Families, so its members must avoid statements that could create trouble with the government.
- The Jin group reserved the Phoenix Inn's private annex for one night at a price of fifty nyang of silver, and Hyuk Mujin declared that the inn was under the Jin Family of Taiyuan's control.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 성주의 초청 as The City Lord's Invitation, 친왕 as Prince, 주씨 as Zhu, 천자 as Son of Heaven, 황상 and 황제 as Emperor, and 태자 as Crown Prince.
- Render 구파일방 as Nine Sects and One Gang and 오대세가 as Five Great Families.
- Render 삐지다 as sulk and retain the established forms Lesser Family Head, Heaven Shaking Sword, Young Hero, and my lord for the relevant addresses and titles.

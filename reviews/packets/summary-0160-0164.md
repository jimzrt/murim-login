## Previous block summary

# Chapters 155–159

## Plot

Jin Wikyung manages the Jin Family’s rapid postwar expansion and secretly coordinates with the Shanxi Provincial Office against roughly five hundred mounted bandits near Datong. Wipeng offers to lead useful reinforcements. Huashan sends the Three Plum Blossom Elites and reports that Mae Jonghak has disappeared, presumably to find Cheongpung.

Cheongpung continues training Jin Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin through brutal sparring. Taekyung loses dozens of times but gradually reads and imitates Cheongpung’s Huashan techniques, while Cheongpung recognizes the rough, fierce Wildness underlying Taekyung’s martial arts. Mujin reaches Level 50 and resolves to become Taekyung’s right arm or heart while earning recognition under his own name.

With three days remaining before New Year’s Day, Taekyung must defeat Cheongpung to complete *Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2*. He spends 50 Stat Points each on Agility and Strength, causing his spear to resonate with his energy and emit a Spear Cry. Cheongpung responds with the Zaha Divine Technique, Sword Energy, and numerous Huashan arts. During the exchange, Cheongpung explains Mae Jonghak’s teaching that martial arts are empty space to be accepted and filled. Taekyung understands the lesson, completes the System Quest *Beyond the Wall*, and changes class to Peak Master. Their duel continues, and no victory over Cheongpung has yet been confirmed.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is now a Peak Master after completing *Beyond the Wall*; he spent 50 points on Agility and 50 on Strength, and his weapon resonated with his energy.
- *Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2* still requires Taekyung to defeat Cheongpung before New Year’s Day. The duel’s final result remains unresolved.
- Cheongpung is a twenty-year-old Peak master raised and trained by Mae Jonghak. He knows numerous Huashan martial arts, can use the Zaha Divine Technique and Sword Energy, and still has no martial title.
- Cheongpung identifies Taekyung’s rough, unrefined martial arts and naturally emerging aura as Wildness. Taekyung can increasingly read and imitate Cheongpung’s forms.
- Hyuk Mujin has reached Level 50 after repeated defeats by Cheongpung and is determined to become a notable martial artist in his own right.
- Mae Jonghak taught Cheongpung that martial arts are empty space: one must accept them as they are and fill them in. This teaching triggered Taekyung’s breakthrough.
- Huashan’s Three Plum Blossom Elites—Baek Museong, Chulwoo, and Eunhyang—are traveling to meet Cheongpung after Huashan reported Mae Jonghak missing.
- Jin Wikyung is directing the Jin Family’s branch expansion and pursuing government support against the mounted-bandit alliance near Datong. Wipeng has offered to lead martial reinforcements.
- The meaning of the crane that supposedly delivered Cheongpung to Mae Jonghak, Mae’s destination, Taecho Village, Cheongpung’s future title, the other members of the Three Hands of Zhongnan, and the novel beginning with *군림……* remain unresolved.

### Chapter 160

# Chapter 160

## Plot

Jin Taekyung accepts that reaching the Peak realm has only revealed how much farther he must progress. He thanks Cheongpung for nearly ten days of sincere training and Mae Jonghak's teaching, but Cheongpung refuses to continue their duel because Taekyung needs to rest and digest his enlightenment. Cheongpung cancels Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2 without penalty, leaving their duel unfinished.

After completing Beyond the Wall, Taekyung changes class to Peak Master, gains seven Levels from the accumulated notifications, raises Jin Family's Cultivation Technique to the ninth stage and Qi Sense to the seventh stage, unlocks the trainable Sound Transmission Skill, receives Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, and triggers the Find the Craftsman Quest.

In Northern Gaoyuan, the chieftains Temur and Chinggen, each commanding one hundred tribespeople, attend a gathering with two other powerful men. One is an unnamed middle-aged mounted-bandit figure, while the other is the mysterious Han Chinese leader known as the Human Butcher, who commands fifty subordinates and intimidates Temur into silence.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's breakthrough into the Peak realm came from accepting Cheongpung's teaching that martial arts are empty space to be accepted and filled; he now understands that he cannot defeat Cheongpung while Cheongpung fights at full strength.
- Cheongpung canceled the second Sword Saint-training Quest by his own decision, so Taekyung received no penalty; their unfinished duel is postponed until a later time.
- Taekyung's Peak breakthrough completed Beyond the Wall, changed his class to Peak Master, produced seven total Level increases in the displayed rewards, raised Jin Family's Cultivation Technique to the ninth stage and Qi Sense to the seventh stage, unlocked trainable Sound Transmission, and awarded Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron plus the Find the Craftsman Quest.
- Temur and Chinggen are chieftains in Northern Gaoyuan who each command one hundred tribespeople and claim descent from the khans.
- The Human Butcher is a mysterious Han Chinese mounted-bandit power in Northern Gaoyuan who commands fifty subordinates and earned his epithet by killing people as though slaughtering livestock.

### Chapter 161

# Chapter 161

## Plot

The eyepatched middle-aged man reveals himself as Black Sand, leader of the one-hundred-member Black Sand Band. The Heavenly Wind Band Leader, who sent Temur and Chinggen the letter promising support for their ambition to become khans, is Black Sand's subordinate, and the five-hundred-member Heavenly Wind Band is merely a subsidiary force.

Black Sand proposes that Human Butcher, Temur, and Chinggen join him in exploiting the power vacuum left by the Mount Heng Sword Sect's collapse. On New Year's Day, while Shanxi's sect leaders gather at the Jin Family of Taiyuan, the Heavenly Wind Band will draw the northern branches toward Datong; Black Sand's main force and the chieftains will destroy the Hequ Branch and charge south toward Taiyuan.

Temur, Chinggen, and the Human Butcher accept the alliance. Black Sand promises to divide all gains into four equal shares, while the Human Butcher claims Ghost Sword Wipeng as his personal target. Black Sand becomes the alliance's de facto presiding leader, and the gathering erupts into celebration before an unknown person enters the inn.

## Continuity

- Black Sand is the frivolous but powerful leader of the one-hundred-member, Han-Chinese Black Sand Band.
- The five-hundred-member Heavenly Wind Band is subordinate to Black Sand through its leader and is being used to draw the Jin Family's northern forces toward Datong.
- Black Sand, the Human Butcher, Temur, and Chinggen form an alliance to attack the Jin Family of Taiyuan on New Year's Day, using Hequ as the entry point while Shanxi's sect leaders gather at the Jin Family's main estate.
- Temur and Chinggen accepted Black Sand's proposal to support their steppe ambitions; the Human Butcher specifically wants Ghost Sword Wipeng left to him.
- Black Sand is the de facto presiding leader of the alliance, whose gains are to be divided into four equal shares.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 흑사 as “Black Sand,” 흑사대 as “Black Sand Band,” 천풍단 as “Heavenly Wind Band,” 천풍단주 as “Heavenly Wind Band Leader,” and 하곡 as “Hequ.”
- Render 성 as “stronghold” in Black Sand's proposal to seize Shanxi, while retaining the contextual identification of that stronghold with Shanxi.
### Chapter 162

# Chapter 162

## Plot

An apparently senile old man enters the Northern Gaoyuan inn where Black Sand, the Human Butcher, Temur, and Chinggen are gathered. After provoking the Human Butcher by insulting him and eating from the leaders' feast, the old man survives the Human Butcher's furious Sword Energy attack, effortlessly defeats him with a chicken bone, reveals himself as a Supreme Peak master, and kills the Human Butcher with a single strike before asking where he is.

## Continuity

- An unnamed old man entered the Northern Gaoyuan gathering and concealed Supreme Peak mastery beneath an apparently feeble, senile demeanor.
- The old man defeated the Human Butcher's Sword Energy with a chicken bone and killed him with a single painless strike; the Human Butcher is dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 참마검 as “horse-chopping sword” and preserve the established renderings of 태산압정, 검기, 전음, 절정 고수, and 초절정 고수.
- Render the old man's speech as abrasive, vulgar, abrupt, and teasing; preserve the 하오체–하오문 wordplay with a brief explanation.
### Chapter 163

# Chapter 163

## Plot

An unnamed Supreme Peak master, nearly a hundred years old and suffering from worsening bouts of senility, arrives at the Northern Gaoyuan and forces Black Sand, Temur, Chinggen, and more than two hundred followers to serve him. He demonstrates overwhelming strength, Samadhi True Fire, and Scorching Yang Qi while asking about their influence and searching for someone depicted in a portrait: a Peak master who mainly uses a sword and palm techniques.

Black Sand attempts to manipulate Chinggen through Sound Transmission, planning to find the sought-after master and extort the old man's martial arts while his mind is impaired. The old man hears the scheme, kills Black Sand with a single palm, and says he will return in about a month. He then leaves the Northern Gaoyuan and heads toward Datong on the Shanxi–Gaoyuan border to continue the search.

## Continuity

- The unnamed old man is a Supreme Peak master nearing one hundred years old; his old age has caused increasingly long periods of unconsciousness, but he has delayed its effects for roughly twenty years with two jiazi of internal energy.
- The old man is searching for an unidentified Peak master who mainly uses a sword and palm techniques, carrying a portrait and asking the Northern Gaoyuan's local powers for information.
- Black Sand was killed by the unnamed old man after attempting to exploit the old man's senility and obtain his martial arts; Chinggen and Temur survived the encounter.
- The old man left the Northern Gaoyuan for Datong and said he would return in about a month.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 삼매진화 as “Samadhi True Fire.”
- Render 정기신 as “essence, qi, and spirit,” 백염 as “white flames,” and 입신지경 as “a transcendent realm.”
- Render 노인's rejected forms of address 어르신 and 형님 as “elder” and “big brother,” respectively; preserve the old man's abrasive vulgar voice.
### Chapter 164

# Chapter 164

## Plot

Jin Taekyung celebrates reaching Level 71 and the Peak realm, then examines the Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron he obtained and receives a Quest to find a Master Artisan capable of forging it into a weapon. He hopes to make his own spear and plans to ask the Jin Family or Wolhwa for help locating the artisan.

Jin Wikyung and Hyuk Mujin arrive to celebrate Taekyung's breakthrough, producing an absurd exchange in which Mujin takes credit for standing guard and Wikyung jokingly promises him the position of Family Head. After the commotion, Taekyung asks Wikyung to investigate both the master artisan and the Fire King.

Wikyung promises to consult Taekyung before learning the Fire King's Flame Divine Palm and reveals that the Jin Family recently discovered a reclusive master artisan nearby, whose skill places him among the world's top ten. Taekyung then reminds Mujin that he once saw and refused the Flame Divine Palm martial arts manual during the Eight Spring Gorge operation.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is now a Level 71 Peak Master with forty-five years of internal energy and seventy unassigned stat points.
- Taekyung received the Quest Find the Master Artisan, requiring him to commission a Master Artisan to forge his Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron into a weapon.
- The Jin Family recently learned of a reclusive master artisan living nearby; Jin Wikyung will verify the information and send word.
- Taekyung wants information about the Fire King and will not decide whether to learn the Flame Divine Palm until he confirms whether the Fire King is alive or dead and consults Jin Wikyung.
- Jin Wikyung already knows about the Flame Divine Palm, presumably because Jin Mukyung told him; Hyuk Mujin learns that Taekyung obtained its manual after the Eight Spring Gorge battle and once offered it to him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 명장 as “Master Artisan.”
- Render 장인을 찾아라 as “Find the Master Artisan.”
- Render 가공되지 않은 만년한철 as “Unprocessed Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.”
- Render 귀환자 as “Returnee,” 명가의 자제 as “Scion of a Prestigious Family,” and 승부사 as “Gambler.”

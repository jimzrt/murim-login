# Chapters 850–854

## Plot

The Divine Physician captures the Blood Soul Gu found in the deceased City Lord of Sichuan Province. Namho explains its origin in the Five Poisons Sect’s war with the Nanman Beast Palace and its effects: it weakens its host, causes episodes of madness, and eventually kills them. Jin suspects Dark Heaven brought it to the Central Plains as part of a scheme against the Great Nation, possibly involving the imperial family. He orders an investigation and considers Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao’s trembling token a key clue.

In Shanxi, Hong Jin asks a Lower District Sect contact to find Jin Taekyung, whom he trusts to protect Zhu Bao. An imperial party takes Hong Jin away, and he heads toward the unharmed prince. Meanwhile, Jin and Jeok Cheongang convene senior allies in Sichuan to discuss the Blood Soul Gu in secret. Reports reveal that disguised Embroidered Uniform Guard agents have moved through several provinces and gathered in Shanxi with Zhu Bao and Hong Jin. After a tracking team is wiped out, the Hidden Shadow Pavilion orders Jeok, Jin, and the entire Fire Dragon Pavilion to escort Zhu Bao. The group leaves to intercept him in Jiangsu.

At Yichang’s West Gate, survivors report that more than a thousand bandits and river pirates attacked a three-company caravan at Yuhua Mountain. After a three-day pursuit and the burning of their ships, fewer than a hundred of the roughly five hundred travelers survived. Two company masters were killed, and survivors say the attackers fought with unnatural ferocity. A middle-aged man with about ten companions arrives and asks who set the ships on fire.

## Continuity

- The Divine Physician secured the Blood Soul Gu found in the deceased City Lord of Sichuan Province. It weakens hosts, causes episodes of madness, and eventually kills them. The official account of the City Lord’s death is illness.
- Jin suspects Dark Heaven brought the Blood Soul Gu from Nanman and that the City Lord’s death may be part of a scheme targeting the Great Nation or its imperial family. The motive remains unknown.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao is traveling with fifty Embroidered Uniform Guard members; Hong Jin joined him in Shanxi. Their expected route passes through Shandong and Jiangsu toward Zhejiang.
- A Shanxi tracking team was wiped out. The Hidden Shadow Pavilion issued an Alliance Leader-approved order for Jeok Cheongang, Jin Taekyung, and the entire Fire Dragon Pavilion to escort Zhu Bao; they have departed to intercept him in Jiangsu.
- At Yuhua Mountain, a caravan of three trading companies was attacked by more than a thousand bandits and river pirates. Fewer than a hundred of about five hundred survived; two of the three company masters were killed, and the attackers fought with unnatural ferocity.
- An unnamed middle-aged man with about ten companions has asked the survivors who set their ships on fire.

# Chapter 855

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang’s party continues east after Jeok killed roughly three hundred bandits and river pirates who had set fire to a caravan. - Jin Taekyung says he is currently all right but may hit a wall again; the Divine Physician is traveling with the group to treat him.

### Chapter 856

# Chapter 856

## Plot

After eleven days of grueling travel from the Sichuan Tang Clan, Jin’s exhausted party reaches Jiangsu. Jin refuses to split the group, insisting they must endure and grow stronger together; during a brief rest, he carries Hyuk Mujin while Jeok Cheongang carries Ju Hwaran.

On a guarded carriage bound for the imperial capital, Hong Jin remembers his impoverished childhood and the late Emperor’s charge to protect Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao. Zhu Bao remains confident in his elder brother, but Hong Jin fears the Emperor’s intentions. When the Embroidered Uniform Guard investigates an unidentified corpse ahead, Hong Jin hears a familiar voice and laughs, hoping it belongs to Jin’s party.

## Continuity

- Jin’s party reaches Jiangsu after eleven days on the road; the group is exhausted, and Jin insists they continue together rather than travel separately.
- Prince Shangshan and Hong Jin are traveling toward the imperial capital in a carriage surrounded by the Embroidered Uniform Guard. Hong Jin fears the Emperor may harm Zhu Bao, whom the late Emperor entrusted to his care.
- An unidentified corpse lies ahead of the procession, and Hong Jin hears a familiar voice nearby, prompting hope that Jin’s party has caught up.

### Chapter 857

# Chapter 857

## Plot

The man mistaken for a corpse wakes and turns out to be Hyuk Mujin. When an Embroidered Uniform Guard subordinate tries to attack him, Hyuk disarms him with Empty-Hand Seizes the Blade, knocks him out, and takes his water. Commander Jeong dismisses the subordinate for his earlier mistake, then questions Hyuk, who refuses to leave because he serves someone expected to return. Hyuk reveals that the flask came from a stolen Guard badge. As Jeong’s men surround him, Hyuk gives his name and stands his ground. A young man appears in a tree, calls himself the mastermind, and greets Jeong as if they have met before; when Jeong asks whether he knows him, the young man denies it with an insult.

## Continuity

- The unidentified body ahead of the Embroidered Uniform Guard procession was Hyuk Mujin, who had been sleeping; he has now reunited with the Guard while separated from the person he serves.
- Hyuk Mujin is holding a stolen Embroidered Uniform Guard badge after taking it from the unconscious subordinate; Commander Jeong’s men have surrounded him.
- An unidentified young man appeared above the procession, claimed the role of mastermind, and addressed Commander Jeong as though they had met; he then denied knowing Jeong.

### Chapter 858

# Chapter 858

## Plot

After more than ten days of travel, Jin Taekyung wakes from a brief nap in a tree and finds Hyuk Mujin holding off the Embroidered Uniform Guard. Jin praises Mujin for enduring the danger but warns him to retreat and find another way when he cannot win. Their banter fails to defuse the standoff with Commander Jeong, whose force includes dozens of Peak-level martial artists. When an archer shoots at Jin, Jin redirects the arrow, killing the horse beneath him. Commander Jeong identifies himself as Jeong Hogun and insists that his men serve the Emperor's command, not their own martial skill. Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao arrives, greets Jin, hands him a secret letter, and asks for his autograph.

## Continuity

- Jin's party has reached its destination ahead of the Embroidered Uniform Guard; exhausted from the journey, Jin slept briefly in a tree while Mujin kept watch.
- Mujin stood his ground against the Guard until Jin awoke; Jin praised his resolve but advised him to retreat and seek another approach when outmatched.
- Commander Jeong is Jeong Hogun, a formidable general with pure internal energy and a force containing dozens of Peak masters; he says their loyalty to the Emperor's command is their reason for standing against Jin.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao has reunited with Jin, has grown considerably since they last met, and handed Jin a secret letter while asking for an autograph.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 정호군 as “Jeong Hogun” and 정 천호 as “Commander Jeong.”
- Retain “Stepping on Empty Air” for 허공답보, “Embroidered Uniform Guard” for 금의위, and “Great Nation” for 대국.
### Chapter 859

# Chapter 859

## Plot

Jeong Hogun restrains his Embroidered Uniform Guard subordinate from attacking Jin Taekyung’s party, citing their orders to escort Prince Shangshan quietly and the prince’s command to lower their swords. He secretly sends the subordinate to report to the Guard’s Commander-in-Chief that, as predicted, uninvited guests have interfered.

Jin realizes the Guard’s message is bound for the imperial capital and worries that entering it could put the party at the Emperor’s mercy, especially if his suspicion of a connection between the Emperor and Dark Heaven is right. Meanwhile, Zhu Bao eagerly accepts the Divine Physician’s offer of a checkup and, amused by Hyuk Mujin’s boast, grants him the title “Tenfold Man,” which Mujin inscribes on a token.

## Continuity

- Jeong Hogun sent a subordinate to the Embroidered Uniform Guard’s Commander-in-Chief with the message that uninvited guests had interfered, as predicted; the subordinate’s report may reach the Son of Heaven.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao granted Hyuk Mujin the joking title “Tenfold Man,” which Mujin inscribed on a bronze token.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 금위군 as “Imperial Guards,” distinct from 금의위, “Embroidered Uniform Guard.”
- Render 십상남자 as “Tenfold Man.”

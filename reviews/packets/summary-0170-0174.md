# Chapters 165–169

## Plot

Jin Taekyung travels with Hyuk Mujin and Cheongpung to meet Jang Taebo, the retired former Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild and one of the world's greatest smiths. Jang initially refuses to forge Taekyung's Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron and demands the Herb of Eternal Youth, then adds gongcheong seokyu, a dragon's claw, and a dragon pearl. Taekyung rejects the resulting *In Search of the Herb of Eternal Youth* Quest and nearly gives up when Jang threatens to pressure the Nine Sects and One Gang against the Jin Family. However, after seeing Taekyung's enormous supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, Jang agrees to personally forge his spear. The *Find the Master Artisan* Quest is completed, and a linked Quest is generated.

Meanwhile, more than four hundred Heavenly Wind Band mounted bandits head south from Datong to attack and plunder the Jin Family of Taiyuan. An unidentified old man annihilates them with Scorching Yang Qi. The Datong Branches of the Jin Family and the Lower District Sect identify the attacker as a merciless Supreme Peak master. The old man then meets a woodcutter named Jang-pal, accepts his rice ball, and travels toward Jang Family Village on a wooden carrying frame.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is a Level 71 Peak Master with forty-five years of internal energy and seventy unassigned Stat Points.
- Jang Taebo is over eighty, has lived anonymously near Jeongyang for more than ten years, and spent a full jiazi as a smith. He is the retired former Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild; his disciple is the current Guild Leader.
- Jang Taebo has agreed to personally forge Taekyung's Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron into a spear. The linked Quest generated after *Find the Master Artisan* remains unresolved.
- Jang Taebo receives a Fifty-Year-Old He Shou Wu from the Nine-Room Escort Bureau every four months, and its leader owes him a favor.
- Taekyung promised to keep Jang Taebo's residence secret and will need to explain the matter to Jin Wikyung.
- Taekyung rejected *In Search of the Herb of Eternal Youth*. Jang's additional demands are gongcheong seokyu, a dragon's claw, and a dragon pearl.
- The Datong Branches of the Jin Family of Taiyuan and the Lower District Sect were established about one month ago and had tracked the Heavenly Wind Band for nearly half a month.
- The unnamed old man killed more than four hundred Heavenly Wind Band bandits with Scorching Yang Qi. The surviving Band Leader described him as a demonic old man rising from a pit of fire before dying nearly two days later.
- The unnamed old man is a senile Supreme Peak master nearing one hundred years old, searching for an unidentified Peak master skilled mainly in sword and palm techniques. His periods of unconsciousness are growing longer.
- The old man met the woodcutter Jang-pal on an unnamed mountain, accepted his rice ball, and is being carried toward Jang Family Village.
- Taekyung still seeks information about the Fire King before deciding whether to learn the Flame Divine Palm. Wipeng has recognized that Taekyung crossed the wall and reported unusual bandit activity near Datong.

# Chapter 170

## Plot

The old man reaches Jang Family Village with Jang-pal, is welcomed by Jang-pal’s family, and briefly enjoys being treated as an ordinary elder. Hanga reveals that tall, handsome martial artists carrying swords came looking for him; after recognizing the danger, the old man stops Jang-pal from going and asks where they are.

The old man demonstrates that he can chew through and break a carefully sharpened axe blade, confirming to Jang-pal that he is an extraordinary martial artist. Meanwhile, Taekyung shows Jang Taebo his enormous supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, completing Find the Master Artisan and generating a linked Quest; Jang Taebo promises to forge what he calls the greatest divine weapon under heaven.

## Continuity

- Hanga is a young girl, Jang-pal’s daughter, who lives near Jang Taebo and visits him regularly; she calls both Jang Taebo and the unnamed old man Grandpa.
- Tall, handsome martial artists carrying swords came looking for the unnamed old man after Hanga met them, gave them information, and led them to him; their identities remain unknown.
- The unnamed old man can bite through and break a carefully sharpened axe blade, and he stops Jang-pal from going after the martial artists before asking where they are.
- Taekyung possesses a huge supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, which causes Jang Taebo to complete Find the Master Artisan and commit to forging Taekyung’s material into a lifelong masterpiece.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 171

# Chapter 171

## Plot

A short, filthy old man arrives at Jang Taebo’s house, identifies himself only as a passing traveler, confirms Jang’s identity, and cryptically tells him to visit the worried child living nearby. He intends to reach Taiyuan that day and visit the Lower District Sect while concealing his identity.

Cheongpung senses that the old man is strange, so Jin Taekyung activates Qi Sense. The System displays the old man’s name as Jeok Cheongang and falsely identifies him as Level 3, but Jeok Cheongang detects the probe, crosses more than ten jang in an instant, and whispers into Taekyung’s ear.

Jeok Cheongang recognizes the exceptional martial ability of Cheongpung and Taekyung, regards Taekyung as an interesting young man from a prestigious orthodox faction, and interrogates him about his identity, sect, and Qi Sense. Taekyung admits that he uses the System. Jeok Cheongang threatens him, then pokes his chest and releases overwhelming heat that sweeps through Taekyung’s body.

## Continuity

- The previously unnamed old man is named Jeok Cheongang.
- Jeok Cheongang can detect Jin Taekyung’s Qi Sense despite its being invisible and inaudible to ordinary perception.
- Qi Sense falsely displays Jeok Cheongang as Level 3 despite his extraordinary speed, perception, and martial ability.
- Jeok Cheongang plans to reach Taiyuan and visit the Lower District Sect while keeping his identity concealed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 적천강 as “Jeok Cheongang,” 유지 the System’s displayed “Lv. 3” as a false or misleading reading rather than Jeok Cheongang’s actual strength.
- Render 사술 as “dark arts,” 무신 as “Martial God,” and 초절정 as “Supreme Peak.”
- Render Jeok Cheongang’s rough addresses 너, 네놈, and 이놈 as hostile forms such as “you” and “you bastard,” while retaining his gruff, threatening voice.
### Chapter 172

# Chapter 172

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang punishes Jin Taekyung with controlled Extreme Yang Qi, but Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin intervene to defend their Benefactor. Jeok evades their coordinated sword attack and decides to teach both young martial artists a lesson.

Jeok Cheongang overwhelms them effortlessly, lifting Jang Taebo over the fence with Seizing an Object Through Empty Space, melting Hyuk Mujin's sword with white flames, and knocking Mujin down with a palm strike. He recognizes Cheongpung's Zaha Divine Technique and Huashan origins.

Cheongpung continues fighting despite Jeok's overwhelming strength, using the Crouching Tiger Fist and the Plum Blossom Sword Technique. His twenty-four plum blossoms narrowly miss Jeok and destroy the surrounding area, prompting Jeok to ask about Cheongpung's relationship with Mae Jonghak.

Jeok recalls meeting Mae Jonghak more than forty years earlier, when the Sword Saint challenged him at Mount Jiuhua for refusing to aid the orthodox Murim against the Demonic Cult. They fought for seven days and seven nights and drew. After Mae left, Demonic Cultists burned Mount Jiuhua, causing Jeok to emerge from seclusion and kill all one thousand attackers; people thereafter called him the Fire King.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang is the Fire King and belongs to the Fire Gate Clan, whose martial arts follow one-man succession and secret transmission.
- More than forty years ago, Jeok Cheongang and Mae Jonghak fought for seven days and seven nights at Mount Jiuhua and ended in a draw.
- Jeok Cheongang emerged from seclusion after Demonic Cultists burned Mount Jiuhua and killed all one thousand attackers.
- Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin risked their lives to defend Jin Taekyung from Jeok Cheongang; Jeok defeated them but did not intend to kill them.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 반박귀진 as “Returning to Simplicity” and 이형환위 as “Shifting Form and Position.”
- Render 허공섭물 as “Seizing an Object Through Empty Space.”
- Render 백련정강 as “Baekryeon Jeonggang.”
- Render 강자지존 as “Might Makes Right.”
### Chapter 173

# Chapter 173

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang confirms that he is the Fire King and reacts with amusement when Cheongpung relays Mae Jonghak's mixed praise of him. Jeok recognizes Cheongpung as Mae's grandson and regards him as a dependable grandson-like successor, while Cheongpung innocently labels him a hidebound old man using modern slang.

Jin Taekyung recognizes Jeok Cheongang as the Fire King from his history with Mae Jonghak and the destruction of Mount Jiuhua. Jeok's internal energy resonates with Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi, assimilating into it and slightly increasing Taekyung's internal energy. Taekyung then conceals the fact that he possesses the Flame Divine Palm manual, consumed the Blazing Flame Divine Pill, and killed Jopil.

Jeok identifies Taekyung as a member of the Jin Family of Taiyuan and learns that the Blade of Flowers, Jin Baekyang, was Taekyung's great-uncle. Taekyung tells him that Baekyang died in battle about two months earlier and that he was buried in the Jin Family cemetery. Jeok asks whether Taekyung avenged him, but Cheongpung then accidentally reveals that Taekyung was the person who killed Baekyang.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang is confirmed as the Fire King, a Supreme Peak master counted among the twenty strongest under heaven, and a kindred spirit of Mae Jonghak after their encounters during the Great Faction War.
- Jeok Cheongang's internal energy is compatible with Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi; when it enters Taekyung's body, it is assimilated and slightly raises his internal energy.
- Jin Baekyang, the Jin Family of Taiyuan's Head Elder and Taekyung's great-uncle, died in battle about two months before this chapter. Jin Wikyung forced his burial in the family cemetery despite opposition from the family's senior members.
- Cheongpung accidentally tells Jeok Cheongang that Taekyung killed Jin Baekyang.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 꼰대 as “boomer” and 꼰머 as “boomer-brain” in Cheongpung's naive explanation of hidebound older people.
- Retain 국밥 as “gukbap” and explain it in a footnote as rice served in hot soup.
- Retain 작은 조부님 as “great-uncle” for Taekyung's relationship to Jin Baekyang.
### Chapter 174

# Chapter 174

## Plot

Cheongpung reveals that Jin Taekyung killed Blade of Flowers Jin Baekyang, prompting Jeok Cheongang to attack Taekyung with the Flame Divine Palm. Cheongpung's Sword Energy briefly slows the attack, but Taekyung's desperate bare-handed defense fails, leaving him unconscious with an Internal Injury.

The clash destroys Jang Taebo's home, leaving only its foundation stones. Jeok Cheongang says he has many questions for Taekyung, orders Jang not to seek help, and goes ahead to the inn. Cheongpung carries the unconscious Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin away, while Jang realizes his retirement has ended.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung killed Jin Baekyang, the Blade of Flowers.
- Jeok Cheongang attacked Taekyung with the Flame Divine Palm after learning this; Cheongpung's Sword Energy only delayed the strike, and Taekyung suffered an Internal Injury and lost consciousness.
- Jeok Cheongang says he will decide Taekyung's fate after hearing the full story because he has many questions for him; he did not initially intend to kill Taekyung.
- The clash between Jeok Cheongang and Taekyung destroyed Jang Taebo's home, including its stone wall and trees, leaving only the foundation stones and ending Jang's retirement.
- Jeok Cheongang orders Jang Taebo not to ask anyone for help, threatening to burn down Shanxi's Murim if necessary; Jang agrees.
- Jeok Cheongang heads to the inn where Taekyung, Mujin, and Cheongpung had been staying, while Cheongpung carries Taekyung and Mujin away.
- Jang Taebo recognizes that Jeok Cheongang's martial arts have reached the Supreme Peak realm and that he has no need for a weapon.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 적 할아버지 as “Grandpa Jeok” when Cheongpung addresses Jeok Cheongang.
- Render 부마도위 as “Imperial Son-in-Law” and 천하오대세가 as “Five Great Families.”
- Retain the comic comparison of Jopil's Flame Divine Palm to beef-bone soup and Jeok Cheongang's to spicy fish stew loaded with hot green chilies.

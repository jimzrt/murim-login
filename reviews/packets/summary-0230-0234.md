# Chapters 225–229

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Butler Kim defeat the Star Guild ambush. Butler Kim uses Earthquake to incapacitate the attackers, while Taekyung tests Won Myunghoon’s surrender and kills him when Won launches a concealed attack. Kim Jonghun is found bound and gagged, and the Gate opens unexpectedly as the survivors emerge before waiting reporters.

Taekyung gives HunterTV an exclusive live statement revealing that The Black Wyvern’s Nest was a Mutated Gate containing the Named Monster Carus, whom he killed. The investigation confirms the deaths of seven raid participants and Won’s role in numerous crimes and cover-ups. Hidden footage and Jonghun’s confession expose Won’s wider wrongdoing, while prosecutors resolve the legal dispute over Taekyung’s killing in his favor.

Carus’s processed remains sell secretly at Christie’s to Qatar’s Prince Cheonsur for approximately 500 billion won, leaving Taekyung about 530 billion won after fees. He establishes a Peace Guild support foundation for the families of fallen comrades, Great Cataclysm veterans, and single-parent families. After discussing the money with his mother and Hayeon, he prepares to move them into their former family home.

Before dawn, Taekyung asks Jeok Cheongang to teach him martial arts. Jeok accepts and departs with him for a year of training in Anhui. Taekyung tells Jin Mukyung that he and Cheongpung will attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet in one year. Cheongpung claims Mae Jonghak permitted him to become Jeok’s Disciple and follow Taekyung, while Baek Museong remains uncertain whether to escort him to Huashan or search for Mae. Jin Wikyung entrusts Taekyung to Jeok and reserves an important private discussion with him before departure.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon is dead. Taekyung killed him after Won violated his surrender with a concealed Aura-infused dagger attack; the System recorded Won as Level 80 and awarded EXP.
- Butler Kim is an A-Rank mage whose Earthquake can incapacitate roughly a dozen veteran B-Rank Hunters.
- Kim Jonghun survived the ambush and confessed the Star Guild’s crimes while mentally shaken.
- The investigation confirmed that seven raid participants died in The Black Wyvern’s Nest; Won’s death made eight.
- Leaked footage from a concealed PSV-96K camera exposed Won’s crimes, cover-ups, and connections to political and business figures. Prosecutors resolved the self-defense dispute favorably for Taekyung.
- Carus’s remains sold for approximately 500 billion won. Taekyung is directing hundreds of billions through a Peace Guild support foundation and plans to support his family financially.
- Taekyung has left immediately with Jeok Cheongang for a year of martial-arts training in Anhui.
- Jin Wikyung has an important private matter to discuss with Jeok before or during the departure.
- Taekyung and Cheongpung intend to attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet in one year; whether Jin Mukyung will attend remains unresolved.
- Cheongpung claims Mae Jonghak authorized him to become Jeok’s Disciple, but Mae has been missing for over a month. Baek Museong’s decision to escort Cheongpung to Huashan or search for Mae remains unresolved.
- Jin Mukyung continues isolated training: ten thousand sword strikes without internal energy, plus one hundred strikes for every mistake.
- Lee Seowol asked Taekyung to address her as Young Lady rather than Sect Leader. Chulwoo agreed not to pursue Eunhyang.

# Chapter 230

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung reach Luoyang after traveling from Taiyuan for seven days and nights. At a Luoyang inn, Jeok effortlessly defeats Heukgeol, a lower-ranking officer of the dark-path Dongcheon Sect, while Taekyung subdues another swordsman and forces the remaining gang members to remove their dead and leave. After Jeok and Taekyung begin eating, a scarred giant arrives with dozens of dark-path swordsmen seeking revenge; when they draw their weapons, an unidentified speaker invokes Amitabha and asks to pass through.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung have reached Luoyang during their journey to Anhui for a year of training.
- The Dongcheon Sect is a dark-path faction that has controlled Luoyang's nights for more than fifty years and has connections within the government.
- Jeok Cheongang can overwhelm Luoyang's dark-path fighters without killing them, while Taekyung can subdue lower-level martial artists and command them under threat.
- Heukgeol, the Dongcheon Sect's lower-ranking officer known as its lone beast, survives Jeok's attack and withdraws with his subordinates.
- A larger group of dark-path swordsmen has confronted Jeok and Taekyung at the Luoyang inn, but the identity and purpose of the person asking to pass remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Luoyang for 낙양 and Henan Province for 하남성.
- Use Dongcheon Sect for 동천파 and Dongcheon Gang for the source variant 동천방 when it appears in Heukgeol's epithet.
- Retain Heukgeol as the name for 흑걸 and explain its Black Hero meaning in a footnote.
- Use huimian noodles for 회면, baijiu for 백주, and Amitabha for 아미타불.
### Chapter 231

# Chapter 231

## Plot

A young monk from Shaolin arrives at the Luoyang inn and fearlessly pushes through the Dongcheon Sect swordsmen. When their hulking officer attacks him with a spiked mace, the monk's Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron prayer beads strip away the spikes before he destroys the weapon and brutally defeats the officer without killing him, using Shaolin's Arhat Fist.

The monk introduces himself as Unnamed, a disciple of Dharma King Hong Dao, and explains that he was ordered to bring the Master of Morning Star. Jeok Cheongang recognizes Hong Dao as the Abbot of Shaolin and an old friend. Unnamed leads Taekyung and Jeok from Luoyang to Mount Song, where Hong Dao descends from Shaoshi Peak and reunites casually with Jeok.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung continues traveling with Jeok Cheongang during their year of martial-arts training and accompanies him from Luoyang toward Shaolin.
- Unnamed is a young, naturally timid and introverted monk who becomes uncontrollably violent when angered; he is a Peak master who uses oversized Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron prayer beads and Arhat Fist.
- Unnamed is Hong Dao's Disciple in practice, but Jeok Cheongang observes that Hong Dao has not formally accepted him; Unnamed accepts this as Heaven's will.
- Hong Dao is the Abbot of Shaolin and is known in the Murim as the Dharma King.
- Jeok Cheongang and Hong Dao are old friends who speak to each other with casual familiarity.
- Hong Dao sent Unnamed to bring the Master of Morning Star to Shaolin.
- The young monk defeats the Dongcheon Sect's hulking officer and leaves him alive despite inflicting severe injuries.

### Chapter 232

# Chapter 232

## Plot

Hong Dao receives Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung at his secluded Shaolin residence, where he reveals the Green Jade Buddha Staff and jokes with Jeok about their past. Unnamed explains that Hong Dao and Jeok became drinking companions during the Great Faction War after Jeok caught Hong secretly drinking alcohol and eating meat.

Hong Dao identifies Taekyung as the Master of Morning Star. He explains that a new Morning Star rose in the north four months earlier, around the time Taekyung first awakened in the Murim, and that its light has continued to grow. Hong has studied the heavenly patterns for decades but has gradually lost the ability to read them as the heavens become increasingly distorted.

Hong Dao foresees an unknowable calamity in which dark clouds will cover the sun and stars. Jeok tells him that the hidden force manipulating the Blade of Flowers and controlling the surviving remnants of the Head Elder's faction is Dark Heaven. Hong orders Jeok to leave immediately and remain beside Taekyung so the Morning Star will not fade, while Hong prepares Shaolin for the approaching calamity.

## Continuity

- Hong Dao remains the Abbot of Shaolin and the Murim's Dharma King despite residing in a secluded, unmaintained quarters against the Discipline Hall's wishes.
- Hong Dao carries the Green Jade Buddha Staff, a thousand-year-old Shaolin sacred treasure with immense authority.
- Hong Dao has studied the heavenly patterns for decades and has been gradually losing his ability to read them for approximately thirty years; he attributes this to distortion in the heavens rather than a decline in his mind.
- A Morning Star rose in the north four months earlier, around the time Jin Taekyung awakened in the Murim, and Hong Dao identifies Taekyung as that star because its light continues to strengthen.
- Hong Dao sees an approaching, unknowable calamity involving dark clouds that will cover the sun and stars.
- Jeok Cheongang informs Hong Dao that Dark Heaven is the hidden force that manipulated the Blade of Flowers for decades and placed complex restrictions on the Head Elder's surviving faction.
- Hong Dao tells Jeok Cheongang to stay beside Taekyung and protect and strengthen the Morning Star, while Hong prepares for the calamity with all his strength.
- Hong Dao and Jeok Cheongang became drinking companions during the Great Faction War after Jeok discovered Hong secretly drinking alcohol and eating meat.

# Chapter 233

## Plot

Namgung Ryong, Family Head of the Namgung family, learns that Fire King Jeok Cheongang has returned to Mount Jiuhua with a new Disciple, Jin Taekyung. He orders the Hidden Thread to mobilize every Lesser Thread and uncover everything about Taekyung within a month.

After a fifteen-day forced march from Shanxi through Henan to Anhui, Taekyung and Jeok reach the base of Mount Jiuhua. Taekyung begs for one proper meal and a chance to wash before six months of extreme training, but Jeok binds him with Seizing an Object Through Empty Space, strikes his Sleep Acupoint, and forces him to sleep. Afterward, Jeok suffers a sudden bout of weakness, realizes he has little time, and carries Taekyung toward Mount Jiuhua.

## Continuity

- Namgung Ryong is the Family Head of the Namgung family and has committed its intelligence network to investigating Jin Taekyung.
- The Hidden Thread is a centuries-old intelligence network loyal only to the Family Head; the Lesser Threads operate beneath it as informants.
- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung have reached Mount Jiuhua in Anhui after a fifteen-day forced march from Shanxi through Henan.
- Jeok plans to subject Taekyung to six months of daily training at the greatest intensity a human can endure.
- Jeok forced Taekyung to sleep for two shichen by using Pressure-Point Strike on his Sleep Acupoint.
- Jeok experienced sudden weakness after using substantial strength and acknowledged that he does not have much time.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Hidden Thread for 비선 and Lesser Threads for 소선.
- Use Huangshan Sect for 황산파 and Mountain Lord for 산주.
- Use Pressure-Point Strike for 점혈 and Sleep Acupoint for 수혈.
- Use concealment technique for 은형술 and Heaven-grade for 천급.
### Chapter 234

# Chapter 234

## Plot

After Pressure-Point Strike wears off, Jin Taekyung awakens on Mount Jiuhua wearing four iron balls weighing two hundred geun each, for a total of roughly five hundred kilograms. The balls are connected by chains made from Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron and are Fire Gate Clan training tools meant to remain on him while he trains, sleeps, and eats.

Jeok Cheongang gives Taekyung a Fire King's Special Restriction Pill. It makes him feel full, reduces Strength, Stamina, and Agility by 100 each, prevents him from using internal energy, and lasts for one week. Taekyung accepts the training despite the restriction and the iron weights.

Jeok immediately blasts Taekyung from the mountain summit with compressed fist wind, creating the nonrefusable Fire King's Inferno Training-1 Quest. Taekyung must return to the summit within one shichen or fail, while carrying the iron weights without internal energy.

After ten days of enduring the climb, Taekyung reaches the summit with approximately fifteen minutes remaining and completes the Quest. He gains substantial EXP, levels up, and receives 30 Bonus Points. Jeok has prepared an enormous iron vest for the next stage, reduces the time limit to half a shichen, and the System changes the Quest into a repeat Quest.

Jeok recalls his own merciless training under his Master and decides to push Taekyung even harder because the time available to teach him is limited. He briefly regrets using the Fire Gate Clan's criminal-restraint equipment as training gear.

## Continuity

- Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang have reached Mount Jiuhua in Anhui, where Jeok is conducting the planned six-month extreme training.
- Taekyung must wear four two-hundred-geun iron balls continuously; their chains are made from Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.
- Fire King's Special Restriction Pill suppresses Taekyung's internal energy and reduces Strength, Stamina, and Agility by 100 each for one week.
- Taekyung accepted Jeok's training, completed the first mountain-climbing Quest after ten days, gained a level, and received 30 Bonus Points.
- Jeok has begun the next training stage with an iron vest and a half-shichen time limit; the training Quest is now repeatable.
- Jeok believes he has limited time to teach Taekyung and intends to train him more harshly than he himself was trained.
- Jin Mukyung remains isolated in an unlit training cave, practicing ten thousand sword strikes without internal energy.
- Hong Dao remains the Abbot of Shaolin and the Murim's Dharma King, master of Unnamed, and an old friend of Jeok Cheongang.
- Hong Dao has identified Taekyung as the Morning Star and foreseen an unknowable calamity as the heavenly patterns become distorted.
- Dark Heaven manipulated the Blade of Flowers for decades and placed complex restrictions on the Head Elder's surviving faction.
- Namgung Ryong has ordered the Namgung family's intelligence network to investigate Taekyung.
- Taekyung and Cheongpung intend to attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet in one year.
- Jeok Cheongang previously suffered sudden weakness after forcing Taekyung to sleep and believed he had little time.

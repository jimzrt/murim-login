# Chapters 855–859

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang’s party pushes east toward Jiangsu after he kills roughly three hundred bandits and river pirates who attacked a caravan. Jin Taekyung rejoins them and, despite his uncertain health and the group’s exhaustion, insists they travel together and reach Jiangsu quickly.

On the road to the imperial capital, Hong Jin travels with Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao and the Embroidered Uniform Guard. The Guard encounters Hyuk Mujin, who had been mistaken for a corpse. Mujin disarms a subordinate and refuses to leave while waiting for the person he serves. Jin arrives and faces off with Commander Jeong, later identified as Jeong Hogun. Zhu Bao intervenes, greets Jin, gives him a secret letter, and asks for his autograph.

Jeong restrains his men, citing the prince’s order to lower their swords, but secretly sends a subordinate to report the interference to the Guard’s Commander-in-Chief. Jin worries that traveling to the capital could leave his party at the Emperor’s mercy. Zhu Bao accepts the Divine Physician’s offer of a checkup and, amused by Mujin’s boast, gives him the joking title “Tenfold Man,” which Mujin inscribes on a bronze token.

## Continuity

- The Divine Physician has the Blood Soul Gu recovered from the deceased City Lord of Sichuan Province. It weakens its host, causes madness, and eventually kills them. Jin suspects Dark Heaven’s covert killing of the City Lord may be part of a scheme targeting the Great Nation or its imperial family.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao is traveling toward the imperial capital with Hong Jin and the Embroidered Uniform Guard. The late Emperor entrusted Hong Jin with Zhu Bao’s care; Zhu Bao trusts his elder brother, while Hong Jin fears the Emperor’s intentions.
- Jin Taekyung’s party is accompanying Zhu Bao toward the capital. Jin is concerned the Emperor could turn the Imperial Guards against them.
- Jeong Hogun leads the Embroidered Uniform Guard escort. He secretly sent a subordinate to report to the Guard’s Commander-in-Chief that uninvited guests had interfered, as predicted.
- Zhu Bao gave Hyuk Mujin the joking title “Tenfold Man,” which Mujin inscribed on a bronze token.
- Zhu Bao handed Jin a secret letter; its contents remain unknown.

### Chapter 860

# Chapter 860

## Plot

Hong Jin explains that the imperial court’s long-standing suppression of Murim sects around the imperial capital began when the Great Nation’s founder ordered Jiangsu’s sects to relocate. The Maoshan Sect and a few others refused; the Imperial Guards destroyed them, leaving Jiangsu without Murim sects.

As the party approaches Suzhou, Jin Taekyung realizes they cannot rely on local Murim forces for help and understands why Hong Jin asked him to protect Prince Shangshan. Jeong Hogun announces that the prince is to pay his respects to the Emperor that day.

## Continuity

- The party is approaching Suzhou in Jiangsu Province; Prince Shangshan is expected to meet the Emperor today.
- Jiangsu has no Murim sects: the Great Nation’s founding emperor ordered the Maoshan Sect and other holdouts destroyed by the Imperial Guards.
- Hong Jin chose Jin Taekyung to protect Prince Shangshan because he needs a trusted martial artist powerful enough to save the prince from any threat.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 茅山派 as “Maoshan Sect,” 太祖 as “Taizu,” 南京 as “Nanjing,” and 蘇州 (소주) as “Suzhou.”
### Chapter 861

# Chapter 861

## Plot

Jeong Hogun’s force of nearly a thousand Embroidered Uniform Guards escorts Prince Shangshan into the imperial capital. Hong Jin remembers the purge that once threatened the prince and vows that events will not unfold as the Emperor wishes. In Hangzhou, Jin Taekyung’s party finds the city under unusually heavy guard and reaches the imperial palace, where Taekyung presses Hong Jin about his knowledge and hidden allies. Hong Jin recounts how poverty drove him to become a eunuch, how the late Emperor recognized his abilities, and asks Taekyung whether he has heard of the East Depot.

## Continuity

- Prince Shangshan and his party have reached the imperial palace under an unusually large Embroidered Uniform Guard escort; the Emperor’s intentions toward the prince remain uncertain.
- Hong Jin became a eunuch to escape poverty and save his family; the late Emperor later recognized his abilities and told him they could be put to use.
- Hong Jin has asked Taekyung whether he knows of the East Depot; the chapter does not explain Hong Jin’s connection to it.
- Taekyung’s other helpers have not yet appeared, and Hong Jin wants to know where they are.

### Chapter 862

# Chapter 862

## Plot

Hong Jin reveals that he was part of the East Depot, and an old eunuch confronts him over bringing outsiders into the palace. When a Guard draws his sword, Taekyung stops him and urges everyone not to escalate. Baek Yeon, Commander of the Embroidered Uniform Guard, arrives and seeks to end the confrontation under the Emperor’s authority. Prince Shangshan asks Taekyung what he would do in his place; Taekyung answers that he would have beaten them all.

## Continuity

- Hong Jin was formerly a member of the East Depot; he and an old eunuch share a fraught history, including an absence of eleven years and nine days.
- Baek Yeon commands the Embroidered Uniform Guard and is trusted by the Emperor; his arrival raises the stakes of the confrontation at the palace entrance.
- Prince Shangshan asks Taekyung for candid advice as a royal command and appears more mature than when they last met.

## Translation Decisions

# Chapter 864

## Plot

Baek Yeon defends the execution of a man who drew his sword before Prince Shangshan, asserting that the Embroidered Uniform Guard answers only to the Emperor. When Taekyung challenges him, Baek says he can be punished only by the Emperor or himself, then warns that no one who enters the palace will be allowed to leave. He orders Jeong Hogun to make the party’s surveillance appear tight while leaving openings for someone to approach, and recalls an earlier assignment that has not succeeded. Shangshan tells Baek that he will not seek punishment because Baek serves the Emperor, not him. Baek privately recalls that Shangshan is the Emperor’s only younger brother and the sole direct imperial relative to survive a brutal power struggle; he considers the now-grown prince more dangerous. The chapter ends with Baek’s order that nobody leave the palace, including Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Prince Shangshan and his party are inside the imperial palace under Baek Yeon’s escort; Baek orders that nobody be allowed to leave.
- Baek Yeon orders Jeong Hogun to surveil the party while leaving apparent gaps so someone can approach them.
- Baek Yeon says an earlier assignment has not succeeded, but its nature remains unstated.
- Prince Shangshan tells Baek Yeon he will not seek his punishment because Baek is the Emperor’s subject, not his.

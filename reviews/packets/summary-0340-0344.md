# Chapters 335–339

## Plot

Jin Taekyung’s party enters the fortified Sichuan Tang Clan, where Tang Sadok reveals that his father, Poison King Tang Taesang, was tortured and murdered. The clan refuses direct aid in finding the Divine Physician while independently pursuing the unidentified killer, who survived severe poison exposure by amputating his own left forearm.

After leaving the Tang Clan, Taekyung divides his forces. Mungyeong returns to his Master with medicinal prescriptions and herbs; Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin seek help from the Emei Sect; and Taekyung and Cheongpung recruit the Qingcheng Sect and the Sichuan authorities. Qingcheng’s leader mobilizes hundreds of disciples, two Elders, and the sect’s lay network. Using Prince Shangshan’s one-use Token, Taekyung secures the cooperation of Sichuan’s City Lord, Won Gyun, who deploys several thousand troops, local constables, official notices, and a thousand silver nyang reward.

Government and Murim conduct a joint search across Sichuan for three days, but the Divine Physician remains unfound. Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin return from Emei with only one reinforcement: Venerable Myoryeong, a severely injured nun coughing blood from an internal wound. Taekyung demands an explanation for her condition.

# Chapter 340

## Plot

Venerable Myoryeong reported that the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, the Emei Sect Leader and its sole Supreme Peak master, had been murdered along with three Elders by a one-armed middle-aged man. Taekyung connected this killer to the culprit behind Tang Taesang's murder, though the connection remained unconfirmed.

Taekyung carried the critically injured Myoryeong while the Qingcheng Elders divided their forces between aiding Emei and returning to their main sect, promising to warn the Sichuan Tang Clan. Myoryeong had survived the attack only long enough to identify the killer and seek help.

Mungyeong reunited with the group and treated Myoryeong, then diagnosed the injury as the evil technique Black Hand Seal and warned that it would become irreversible after seven days and lead to death within roughly a year. When questioned about his knowledge, he said his Master had taught him, prompting Taekyung to suspect that the Master might be the Divine Physician.

## Continuity

- The Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, the Emei Sect Leader and its sole Supreme Peak master, and three Emei Elders were killed on Mount Emei three days before this chapter by a one-armed middle-aged man.
- Venerable Myoryeong survived the attack but suffers from the Black Hand Seal and requires urgent treatment before seven days have passed.
- Mungyeong knows the Black Hand Seal and its progression because his Master taught him; Taekyung now suspects that Mungyeong's Master may be the Divine Physician.
- The Qingcheng Sect has divided its available forces: one Elder is leading Disciples to aid Emei, the other is returning to the main sect, and Qingcheng will send someone to warn the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Tang Sadok is pursuing the culprit behind Tang Taesang's murder with members of the Tang Family while the Tang Clan remains partially absent from its base.
- Jin Taekyung and his companions remain in Sichuan searching for the unidentified Divine Physician, while Jeok Cheongang remains critically ill and in need of treatment.
- Ju Hwaran remains the Yongbong Escort Bureau's Young Bureau Head while investigating its traitors; Song Ilseom remains for one month to help her before seeking Ju Hogun's elixir in Xianyang.

# Chapter 342

## Plot

The Divine Physician explains that Jeok Cheongang’s unconsciousness results from a broken bodily balance, especially between Yin and Yang, caused by uncontrolled Scorching Yang Qi. The Divine Physician leads Taekyung into Cold-Ice Land within the Mystic Gate Formation to gather medicinal herbs needed for treatment, while Mungyeong remains behind to care for the patients. A new nonrefusable Chain Quest requires one hundred roots each of twenty-four kinds of herbs.

### Chapter 343

# Chapter 343

## Plot

After five days of labor in Cold-Ice Land, Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, Gung Gibang, and Cheongpung complete the collection of the required medicinal herbs. The Herb Slave of Cold-Ice Land Chain Quest and the Mountain Herb Gatherer Who Returned Alive from Hell Achievement are completed, granting Taekyung ten bonus points.

The Divine Physician initially plans to prepare the Life-Restoring Great Technique over seven days, but after three days he discovers that Jeok Cheongang's body contains Formless Ultimate Poison. The poison is being contained by his Scorching Yang Qi, so performing the technique without an antidote could leave the poison unchecked. Mungyeong proposes seeking the Sichuan Tang Clan's legendary antidote treasure, generating the Myriad-Poison Ring Quest.

## Continuity

- The required one hundred roots of each of twenty-four medicinal herb varieties have been collected in Cold-Ice Land.
- The Divine Physician and Mungyeong are preparing the collected herbs and other ingredients for the Life-Restoring Great Technique, but treatment of Jeok Cheongang has not begun.
- Jeok Cheongang has Formless Ultimate Poison inside his body; his Scorching Yang Qi currently contains the poison, while his Yin-Cold Qi remains unchanged.
- The Life-Restoring Great Technique can regulate qi and untangle disrupted qi and blood but cannot detoxify Formless Ultimate Poison on its own.
- Mungyeong believes the Sichuan Tang Clan may possess a legendary sacred artifact capable of detoxifying any poison, and the Myriad-Poison Ring Quest has been generated.

# Chapter 344

## Plot

The System generates the mandatory Peak-grade Chain Quest Myriad-Poison Ring, requiring Jin Taekyung to obtain the legendary Sichuan Tang Clan treasure or Jeok Cheongang will die. Taekyung concludes that the ring must exist because the System created the Quest.

Taekyung leaves Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang at Dong Feng's hidden clinic to guard Venerable Myoryeong, while he, Cheongpung, Dong Feng, and Mungyeong travel to the Sichuan Tang Clan to seek the ring.

Tang Sadok returns to the Sichuan Tang Clan after receiving Qingcheng's report that a single attacker killed the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun and three Emei Elders. He orders the Green Shadow Squad to investigate and tightens security around the Gate Guard Pavilion.

Tang Sadok allows Taekyung's group to enter after learning that Dong Feng may be the Divine Physician. Taekyung immediately asks Tang Sadok to lend him the Myriad-Poison Ring.

## Continuity

- The Myriad-Poison Ring is the only currently known potential treatment for Jeok Cheongang's Formless Ultimate Poison, and obtaining it is a mandatory Quest.
- Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang remain at Dong Feng's clinic to guard Venerable Myoryeong while Taekyung, Cheongpung, Dong Feng, and Mungyeong approach the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Tang Sadok suspects that the one-armed killer who murdered the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun and three Emei Elders also murdered Tang Taesang.

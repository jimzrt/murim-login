# Chapters 90–94

## Plot

During a week of paid vacation, Jin Taekyung begins arranging to buy and remodel his family’s former home. He reunites with former classmate Park Jihwang, now Park Jihoon, a Hunter in Myeongdong Guild whose strength appears comparable to or greater than Im Changsoo’s.

Sangdong Guild investigates Taekyung after Im Chunsoo receives reports of his impossible recent feats. Chunsoo orders expanded surveillance while Sangdong’s Team 1 Leader remains skeptical. Hong Woojin, a B-rank mage and information broker, monitors Taekyung through tiny Familiars. Taekyung detects and drives away the Familiars around his home, concludes the controllers must have been nearby, and decides to catch them himself.

Taekyung purchases and stores 350 million won worth of low-rank weapons at the Ilsan Store. On returning home, he learns that Hayeon has found an abandoned kitten and received permission to foster it. Taekyung identifies the kitten through the System as a level-two Cat—Familiar, raising the possibility that it is connected to the surveillance.

## Continuity

- Peace Guild’s Guild house will finish remodeling in one week; Taekyung is on paid vacation until then.
- Choi Minwoo and Butler Kim suspect Taekyung may be a third-awakening Hunter, but this remains unconfirmed.
- Taekyung agreed to buy his former family home for 3.38 billion won, paid the ten-percent deposit, and plans to remodel it and move after Hayeon’s college entrance examination.
- Park Jihwang changed his name to Park Jihoon and works in Team 1 of Myeongdong Guild.
- Im Chunsoo ordered Sangdong Guild’s Audit Team to expand surveillance of Taekyung. Sangdong’s Team 1 Leader, Chunsoo’s loyal right hand and the Guild’s only other A-rank Hunter, doubts the reports of Taekyung’s feats.
- Peace Guild’s Guild Master and Team Leader remain protected by an unexplained security Lock that hides their personal and account information.
- Hong Woojin is investigating Taekyung with Familiar magic. He severed the Link to his rice-weevil Familiar and intends to continue with more conspicuous surveillance.
- Taekyung’s Qi Sense reaches seventy meters and detected the Familiars in his home, but tiny Familiars generally evade ordinary detection magic.
- A B-rank mage’s Familiar connection reaches up to 500 meters, with approximately 300 meters considered a safe operating distance. Forced Link severance causes physical distress and may cause mana backflow.
- Taekyung spent 350 million won at the Ilsan Store and stored the weapons in his Inventory.
- Hayeon is temporarily fostering an abandoned level-two Cat Familiar with Kim Jeonghee’s permission. Its owner and connection to the surveillance are unknown.
- Retaliation by Im Chunsoo or Sangdong Guild, Woojin’s motive, Hayeon’s schooling, and the existence of third-awakening Hunters remain unresolved.

### Chapter 95

# Chapter 95

## Plot

Hong Woojin infiltrates the Level 2 kitten living with Jin Taekyung's family through his Familiar magic and uses Jin Hayeon's love of cats to observe the household. When Taekyung tries to inspect the kitten's sex, Woojin is forced to sever the Link, suffering nausea, a headache, and severe distress before recovering with a prepared potion.

Taekyung already knows the kitten is a Familiar and deliberately allowed it into the house so he could identify the people behind the surveillance. He believes the controlling mage is within 500 meters, intends to find and interrogate him, and treats the surveillance as a personal intrusion. Hayeon names the kitten Yeoreum, bargains with Taekyung for spending money, and takes food money as well.

Sangdong Guild's Security Team is conducting a Guild Master-ordered operation involving six people: a B-rank Security Team Leader, the Guild's only Familiar mage, and four close-combat Hunters specializing in tracking and stealth. After learning that Woojin nearly exposed the operation, the Security Team Leader orders the Familiar mage to use a cat as well; the mage begins searching for cat adoption listings in Ilsan.

## Continuity

- Hong Woojin used a Level 2 Cat Familiar named Yeoreum to surveil Taekyung's household, then severed the Link when Taekyung attempted to examine the kitten.
- Taekyung knowingly permits Familiar surveillance for now and plans to locate the controlling mage within 500 meters of his home and question him.
- Sangdong Guild is running a six-person surveillance operation ordered directly by its Guild Master and plans to deploy another Cat Familiar.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Yeoreum for 여름이, and render the rejected 한여름 naming pun as Midsummer.
- Retain Familiar and Link as the established terms for 패밀리어 and 링크.
### Chapter 96

# Chapter 96

## Plot

Kim Junsu, Sangdong Guild Security Team’s sole Familiar mage, and several C-rank surveillance Hunters continue monitoring Jin Taekyung. The exhausted Junsu links a black kitten as a Familiar when Taekyung leaves home, while the Security Team Leader coordinates surveillance and tries to prove his team’s superiority to Hong Woojin and impress the Guild Master.

Taekyung encounters the black Lv. 2 Cat Familiar and a disguised surveillance Hunter posing as a friendly neighbor. After the System identifies the neighbor as Lv. 42 Kim Gwondong and Taekyung confirms that no Hunter lives in the adjacent building, he concludes that the two actors are not working together and heads toward the nearby real-estate office.

The Security Team has installed eavesdropping-magic Equipment in two nearby commercial buildings and maintains multiple veteran C-rank Hunters specializing in stealth and tracking. The Security Team Leader dismisses the claim that Taekyung cleared a B-rank Gate alone as an exaggeration from Im Changsoo, but the team goes on full alert when Taekyung enters the real-estate office.

## Continuity

- Sangdong Guild’s Security Team is monitoring Jin Taekyung with its sole Familiar mage, Kim Junsu, and multiple C-rank stealth and tracking Hunters.
- Kim Junsu is a C-rank mental mage who uses Familiars and must repeatedly stay in temporary hideouts because of his work.
- Kim Gwondong is a C-rank Security Team Hunter assigned to surveillance and disguises himself as a friendly neighbor to approach Taekyung.
- The Security Team uses a black Lv. 2 Cat Familiar and has installed eavesdropping-magic Equipment in both nearby real-estate offices.
- Taekyung recognizes the black kitten and the disguised neighbor as actors, identifies Gwondong through the System, and concludes that they are not part of the same group.
- The Security Team Leader believes Taekyung is an ordinary C-rank Hunter and attributes the report of his solo B-rank Gate clear to Im Changsoo’s exaggeration.
- Taekyung enters the real-estate office while Sangdong Guild’s Security Team remains on full alert.

- Use Kim Junsu, Kim Gwondong, Nabi, and Goyang for 김준수, 김권동, 나비, and 고양시.
- Render 개냥이 as dog-cat and retain an explanatory footnote for the Korean term.
- Render 표적 as target, 패밀리어 as Familiar, and 링크 as Link.
### Chapter 97

# Chapter 97

## Plot

Jin Taekyung visits a nearby real-estate office and detects eavesdropping magic installed by the people surveilling him. He deliberately acts like a boastful, extravagant C-rank Hunter so the surveillance team will underestimate him, while the Security Team listens through the magic and relaxes after judging his behavior childish and harmless.

Through the real-estate agent's conversation, Taekyung learns that three properties near his home changed hands within the past five days: Building 5, Unit 901; Building 4, Unit 302; and Building 3, Unit 202. He concludes that the watchers are somewhere within five hundred meters of his home and plans to search the area and parking lot with Qi Sense while appearing to take a walk, intending to capture them and learn who is behind the operation.

The Security Team Leader takes offense at being described as an ugly old bachelor, leaves for a sauna, and orders Kim Junsu and another team member to prepare a transcript and full-page opinion statements. After he leaves, the two subordinates mock and curse him, while Junsu worries that stress is worsening his hair loss. Taekyung later encounters the disguised Level 42 Hunter Kim Gwondong, maintains his civilian-facing cover, and refrains from attacking him. Gwondong tells him that the black cat Familiar is still waiting in its previous location.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has confirmed that eavesdropping magic is active in the nearby real-estate office and is intentionally presenting himself as a vain, ordinary C-rank Hunter to mislead the surveillance team.
- Taekyung has identified three recently traded properties within five hundred meters of his home as possible surveillance locations: Building 5, Unit 901; Building 4, Unit 302; and Building 3, Unit 202.
- Taekyung intends to locate and capture the surveillance personnel rather than merely drive them away, but has not yet confronted them.
- Taekyung recognizes Kim Gwondong's civilian disguise as a Level 42 Hunter, probably C-rank and specialized in stealth or pursuit, and continues treating him as an undiscovered surveillance operative.
- The black kitten Familiar remains at the location where Taekyung previously left it, and Kim Gwondong knows enough about its presence to remind Taekyung that it is still there.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 도청 마법 as wiretapping magic and retain eavesdropping magic for the related surveillance transmission or equipment when context requires.
- Render 월세 as monthly rent, 전세 as jeonse lease, and 홀아비 냄새 as old-bachelor smell.
- Use a chatty, flirtatious voice for the unnamed real-estate ajumma and a friendly, ordinary neighbor voice for Kim Gwondong's disguise.
### Chapter 98

# Chapter 98

## Plot

Hong Woojin infiltrates Jin Taekyung’s home in the form of a kitten Familiar, but Jin Hayeon’s intense fondness for cats leaves him trapped in her room for more than a day and a half. After his attempts to escape and disrupt her studying fail, he soils her duvet and himself to force her to take him outside.

Jin Taekyung returns home while Hayeon is carrying the filthy kitten, and he and Hong Woojin recognize each other. Hayeon washes Yeoreum and later leaves for the library, while Taekyung privately reflects on his mother’s recent unexplained outings.

With the house empty, Taekyung circles the apartment complex while carrying a Familiar and checks the parking lot, finding no suspicious vehicles. He concludes that the watchers are hiding in one of the recently traded apartments: Building 5, Unit 901; Building 4, Unit 302; or Building 3, Unit 202.

Taekyung draws all the curtains, searches the house with mana-detection Equipment, confirms that no mana is present inside, and calls someone. Kim Junsu reports the suspicious call to the Security Team, quoting statements about a plan, Sangdong Guild not noticing anything, and an item in Taekyung’s possession. The team concludes that Taekyung has a USB and treats the development as a major breakthrough.

## Continuity

- Hong Woojin infiltrated Taekyung’s home as a kitten Familiar and was recognized by Taekyung when they met at the front door.
- Hayeon’s cat obsession kept Hong Woojin confined to her room until he deliberately soiled her bedding and himself to engineer an escape.
- Taekyung cleared the apartment parking lot of suspicious vehicles and confirmed that the surveillance base must be one of three recently traded apartments surrounding his building.
- Taekyung searched his home with mana-detection Equipment and detected no mana inside before making a suspicious phone call as bait.
- The Security Team now believes Taekyung possesses a USB and is involved in a secret plan, though whether the USB exists and who Taekyung called remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Hong Woojin’s internal narration profane, self-deprecating, and professionally deadpan during his humiliating Familiar infiltration.
- Render 현자 타임 as post-nut clarity in the comic description of Yeoreum’s emotional collapse.
- Render Hayeon’s kitten address 여름아 as Yeoreum and her self-reference 언니 as Sis or big sis according to sentence flow.
### Chapter 99

# Chapter 99

## Plot

After learning that Jin Taekyung appeared to possess a USB containing valuable intelligence, the Security Team Leader and Kim Gwondong decide to seize it by force. Despite knowing the operation would make them criminals if discovered, they proceed under the Guild Master's special orders, driven by hopes of promotion.

Taekyung realizes the watchers have taken his bait when the two kitten Familiars are released. He leads them to a deserted mountain clearing, where he exposes their surveillance, reveals that the USB contains his porn collection rather than sensitive information, and confirms that they were sent by Sangdong Guild.

Hong Woojin observes six Sangdong Guild watchers leaving the apartment after Taekyung and quits the assignment when he realizes they intend to use violence. In the clearing, Choi Byungil orders the attack; Taekyung identifies the participants and shatters the first attacker's dagger with an equipped blade, beginning the confrontation.

## Continuity

- The Sangdong Guild Security Team's operation is under a special order from Guild Master Im Chunsoo, and its leader authorizes an illegal attempt to subdue Taekyung and take the USB.
- Choi Byungil is the B-rank Security Team leader leading the field group; his Level is in the mid-sixties, while the other five watchers are C-rank Hunters around Levels 30 to 40.
- Taekyung's USB is deliberate bait containing his lifetime porn collection, and he has led the watchers into a deserted mountain clearing for the confrontation.
- Hong Woojin's rooftop hideout is on Taekyung's apartment building; after seeing the six Sangdong watchers pursue Taekyung for a violent operation, Woojin quits his assignment by texting the Team 1 Leader.
- Taekyung identifies Kim Gwondong, Choi Byungil, Park Hyungjin, Oh Gyuhyeon, Lee Mincheol, and Kim Junsu as the watchers and confirms their Sangdong Guild affiliation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 역마살 as yeokmasal with a footnote explaining the wandering-fate meaning and its age-related pun.
- Render 공(公)만 뺏기다 as merely taking the credit.
- Render 쇠뿔도 단김에 빼라 as the idiom about pulling the ox's horn while it is hot.

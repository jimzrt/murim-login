# Chapters 230–234

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung reach Luoyang on their journey to Anhui. At an inn, Jeok defeats Heukgeol, a Dongcheon Sect officer, while Taekyung subdues the other dark-path swordsmen. A young Shaolin monk named Unnamed then arrives, defeats Heukgeol without killing him, and leads them to Mount Song.

At Shaolin, the monk’s master, Hong Dao—the Abbot and Dharma King—reveals that Taekyung is the Master of Morning Star. A new Morning Star appeared in the north when Taekyung awakened in the Murim, and its growing light contrasts with the dark, unknowable calamity Hong foresees. Jeok identifies Dark Heaven as the force behind the Blade of Flowers and the Head Elder’s restricted faction. Hong orders Jeok to remain beside Taekyung while Shaolin prepares for the coming disaster.

Meanwhile, Namgung Ryong orders the Hidden Thread to investigate Taekyung. After a fifteen-day forced march, Jeok and Taekyung reach Mount Jiuhua. Jeok begins six months of extreme training, binding Taekyung to four enormous iron balls and suppressing his abilities with the Fire King’s Special Restriction Pill. Taekyung is repeatedly blasted from the summit and must climb back without internal energy. After ten days, he completes the first quest, levels up, and receives 30 Bonus Points. Jeok begins the next stage with an iron vest and a shorter time limit, intending to train him even more harshly because his own time is limited.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung are training at Mount Jiuhua in Anhui for six months.
- Unnamed is Hong Dao’s young Shaolin disciple in practice: naturally timid but violently powerful when angered. He uses Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron prayer beads and Arhat Fist.
- Hong Dao is Shaolin’s Abbot and the Murim’s Dharma King, Jeok’s old friend, and Unnamed’s master.
- Hong Dao identifies Taekyung as the Morning Star that rose when he awakened in the Murim. He foresees an unknowable calamity as the heavenly patterns become distorted.
- Dark Heaven manipulated the Blade of Flowers for decades and placed restrictions on the surviving faction of the Head Elder.
- Namgung Ryong has ordered the Namgung family’s Hidden Thread and Lesser Threads to investigate Taekyung.
- Taekyung continuously wears four two-hundred-geun iron balls linked by Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron chains.
- The Fire King’s Special Restriction Pill suppresses internal energy and reduces Strength, Stamina, and Agility by 100 each for one week.
- Taekyung completed the first mountain-climbing quest after ten days, gained a level, and received 30 Bonus Points. The repeat quest now includes an iron vest and a half-shichen time limit.
- Jeok Cheongang has suffered sudden weakness and believes he has little time to teach Taekyung.
- Jin Mukyung remains isolated, practicing ten thousand sword strikes without internal energy.
- Taekyung and Cheongpung still intend to attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet in one year.

### Chapter 235

# Chapter 235

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang subjects Taekyung to increasingly brutal training on Mount Jiuhua, including waterfall horse stances, weighted push-ups, cliff climbing, and retrieving a swallow's nest. Taekyung completes Fire King's Hellfire Training-2, earns the Unmoving achievement, converts Toughness into Endurance, gains two levels and 50 Bonus Points, and continues training for more than two months.

Jeok's increasing sleep and memory lapses become undeniable when he forgets ordering Taekyung's fist-and-foot training. Taekyung asks whether he is ill, and Jeok leads him to a hidden place instead of answering directly.

Jeok brings Taekyung into the Fire Gate Cavern, explains the Fire Gate Clan's centuries-long history, and reveals its seventeen ancestral urns. The cavern releases the restriction on Taekyung's internal energy, and Jeok offers to pass on the clan's martial arts. Taekyung accepts because he trusts Jeok, generating the Heir of the Fire Gate Quest, but Jeok's confusion interrupts the first lesson. The two then vanish from Mount Jiuhua as the seasons change three times.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has spent more than two months undergoing increasingly severe training from Jeok Cheongang on Mount Jiuhua.
- Taekyung completed Fire King's Hellfire Training-2, earned the Unmoving achievement, changed Toughness into Endurance, gained two levels, and received 50 Bonus Points.
- Taekyung completed the first Fist-and-Foot Training repeat Quest; Strength, Stamina, and Agility each increased by 1, with additional rewards deferred until he learns related martial arts.
- Jeok Cheongang's sleep has increased and he has displayed repeated forgetfulness; Taekyung recognizes these symptoms as dementia, while Jeok acknowledges suffering from the ailments of old age.
- The Fire Gate Cavern is the place where the Fire Gate Clan began and where its legacy continues; seventeen urns of former Sect Leaders are arranged around a blue-white flame.
- Entering the Fire Gate Cavern lifted the restriction on Taekyung's internal energy, which reacts strongly because it originated from the Blazing Flame Divine Pill.
- Jeok Cheongang has chosen to transmit the Fire Gate Clan's martial arts to Taekyung, and Taekyung agreed because he trusts Jeok despite the danger posed by Jeok's memory loss.
- The Heir of the Fire Gate Quest was created, and Jeok brought out the Fire Gate Divine Technique to begin Taekyung's instruction.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 치매 as dementia.
- Use Fire Gate Cavern for 열화동 and Heir of the Fire Gate for 열화의 계승자.
- Preserve Fire King's Hellfire Training-2 as the source's distinct quest-title variant.
- Use Fist-and-Foot Training for 권각 수련 and fist-and-foot martial arts for 권각술.

# Chapter 236

## Plot

After four months away, Wipeng returned to Jin Wikyung and reported that the Seven-Route Army had stabilized Northern Gaoyuan. Seven battles killed or captured two thousand mounted bandits, annihilated five major groups, and forced more than ten others to surrender. The Lower District Sect provided decisive intelligence support, while Temur and Chinggen avoided conflict after learning of Jeok Cheongang's reputation; having absorbed the remnants of Black Sand, the Human Butcher, and the Heavenly Wind Band, they now sought friendship with the Jin Family and offered furs and plundered treasure.

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng also discussed the Seongun Escort Bureau's collapse, the Jin Family's expanding trade with Shaolin and Namgung through Mount Song and Hefei, and the approaching Star-Array Grand Banquet where the brothers were expected to reunite. At Mount Jiuhua, one year into Taekyung's absence, the shaggy-haired young man joked about grilling meat over the Fire Gate Clan's sacred flame, provoking Jeok Cheongang to strike and scold him as peaceful Mount Jiuhua became noisy again.

## Continuity

- Wipeng returned to the Jin Family of Taiyuan after four months commanding the campaign to stabilize Northern Gaoyuan.
- The Jin Dragon Squad is the Jin Family's foremost armed force; Wipeng is its Commander, and Hyuk Mujin served as acting Deputy Commander during Wipeng's absence.
- Hyuk Mujin completed his first mission as acting Deputy Commander; Wipeng considers him serviceable, quick to learn, loyal, and possessed of considerable martial talent despite his incessant chatter.
- The Seven-Route Army attacked from seven directions, killed or captured two thousand mounted bandits, annihilated five major groups, and received the surrender of more than ten others.
- The Lower District Sect supported the Northern Gaoyuan campaign with surveillance, false information, and traps; Jin Wikyung considers Wolhwa's strategic ability more frightening than her beauty.
- Temur and Chinggen each command two thousand riders, absorbed the remnants of the defeated mounted-bandit powers, and now seek friendship with the Jin Family.
- Jeok Cheongang's killing of Black Sand and the Human Butcher, followed by his destruction of the Heavenly Wind Band, created the power vacuum that enabled Temur and Chinggen to rise.
- The Seongun Escort Bureau is effectively now the Jin Family Escort Bureau after Woo Hwangtae's family fled following the discovery that they had diverted military supplies through bribed officials.
- The Jin Family is conducting favorable trade cooperation with Shaolin and Namgung through a caravan route passing Mount Song and Hefei.
- Jin Wikyung has selected the title The Sleeping Dragon Enters Service for a painting he plans to complete.
- A year has passed since Jin Taekyung left for training with Jeok Cheongang; the brothers are expected to meet at the Star-Array Grand Banquet in Henan after the ground thaws.
- At Mount Jiuhua, Taekyung remains under Jeok Cheongang's harsh training and has grown long-haired, bearded, and accustomed to iron balls hanging from all four limbs.

# Chapter 237

## Plot

After a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern, Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang leave the cavern. Taekyung can open its stone gate with the internal energy of the Scorching Sun Divine Arts and departs while still wearing iron balls chained to all four limbs.

At the foot of Mount Jiuhua, they encounter Hong, a herbalist from Sichuan who has found no spiritual herbs. Jeok has already harvested the mountain's herbs, but Taekyung gives Hong a remaining ginseng root and sells it to a group of martial artists for one hundred silver nyang. Namgung Ryong then raises the price to three hundred silver nyang to compensate Jeok for offending him, identifies himself as the Family Head of the Nangong Family, and is described as the richest man in Anhui.

## Continuity

- Taekyung completed one year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern and left it with Jeok Cheongang, formally referring to himself as Jeok's unworthy Disciple.
- Taekyung can freely enter and exit the Fire Gate Cavern using the internal energy of the Scorching Sun Divine Arts.
- Taekyung still wears numerous iron balls chained to all four limbs after completing the year of training.
- Jeok Cheongang had harvested all the spiritual herbs on Mount Jiuhua before descending with Taekyung.
- Taekyung gave a remaining ginseng root to Hong, a struggling herbalist from Sichuan, and arranged its sale to passing martial artists.
- Namgung Ryong appeared at Mount Jiuhua with dozens of highly trained martial artists, paid three hundred silver nyang for the ginseng, and identified himself as the Family Head of the Nangong Family.
- Jeok Cheongang's description establishes Namgung Ryong as the richest man in Anhui.

### Chapter 238

# Chapter 238

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang arrive at the Nangong Family estate in Anhui for the Azure Sky Sword King's birthday celebration. Jeok explains that the Azure Sky Sword King is an unpredictable swordsman obsessed with martial arts who once spared Jangcheon in exchange for a spar with Jeok; Jeok won by half a form, after which the Sword King entered seclusion.

Taekyung crosses the estate while still wearing the chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls from his year of training, leaving deep tracks and attracting comments that he looks like a prisoner or an Elder of the Beggars' Sect. Ten Peak guards stop him at a pavilion and demand that he disarm, but Taekyung explains that only Jeok has the key and that removing the chains would interrupt his training.

When the youngest guard threatens to cut off the chains in accordance with Nangong law, Namgung Ryong projects his internal-energy voice across the area and orders the guards to let Taekyung inside.

## Continuity

- The Nangong Family is one of the Five Great Families, the hegemon of Anhui, and possesses more than one hundred blood relatives and thousands of subordinate martial artists.
- The Azure Sky Sword King is the Grand Family Head of the Nangong Family. Jeok Cheongang has met him twice, owes him a great debt, and considers him unpredictable and completely devoted to the sword.
- The Azure Sky Sword King wanted to spare Jangcheon in exchange for a spar with Jeok Cheongang; Jeok won by half a form, and the Azure Sky Sword King entered seclusion the following day.
- Jeok Cheongang's age-related illness has eased but continues to progress, and Taekyung recognizes that even a Supreme Peak master cannot completely halt the effects of old age.
- Taekyung remains publicly recognized as Jeok Cheongang's Disciple and heir to the Fire Gate Clan's orthodox lineage after completing a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern.
- Taekyung is still bound to chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls; the key is held by Jeok Cheongang, and the chains cannot be removed during his current training.
- Ten Peak martial artists guard the pavilion where the Nangong Family Head receives Taekyung.
- Namgung Ryong personally orders the guards to admit Taekyung after they attempt to enforce the Nangong Family's disarmament rule.

### Chapter 239

# Chapter 239

## Plot

Namgung Ryong apologizes for his son's discourtesy and asks Jin Taekyung to prevent Great Hero Jeok from dueling his father, the Azure Sky Sword King. Taekyung explains that Jeok will not back down and agrees only to try speaking with him.

Namgung Ryong's son, Nangong Ok—the Sword Dragon and Lesser Family Head—confronts Taekyung after overhearing the conversation. When Nangong Ok attempts to draw his sword, Taekyung stops the blade and then deliberately summons the Azure Sky Sword King to a secluded mountain.

Taekyung begs Namgung Cheon to postpone the duel for one year but refuses to explain Jeok's condition. Namgung Cheon offers to grant the request if Taekyung withstands three moves, generating the Sudden Quest Three-Move Kill. Taekyung accepts, removes his year-long shackles, and faces the Azure Sky Sword King's opening Emperor's Sword Form.

## Continuity

- Namgung Ryong has one child, Nangong Ok, whom he raised with great care; he acknowledges that Ok remains immature despite being the Sword Dragon and the Nangong Family's Lesser Family Head.
- Namgung Ryong believes the duel between Jeok Cheongang and Namgung Cheon could cause disastrous upheaval if either master is seriously harmed, and he will attempt to stop it himself.
- Jin Taekyung deliberately summoned the Azure Sky Sword King to a secluded mountain and asked him to delay the duel for one year without revealing that Jeok's condition is poor.
- Namgung Cheon will grant Taekyung's request if Taekyung withstands three of his moves; Taekyung accepted the resulting Quest and removed his chained iron balls before the first attack.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 검룡 as Sword Dragon.
- Render 남궁옥 as Nangong Ok, matching the reading-copy rendering of the Nangong Family's formal names.
- Render 제왕검형 as Emperor's Sword Form.
- Render 삼초살 as Three-Move Kill.
- Render 남만 as Nanman and retain the explanatory footnote for the historical regional term.

## Previous block summary

# Chapters 885–889

## Plot

Taekyung discovers that laborers carrying banquet supplies are assassins working with Eunuch Ma. Told to return and wait, he instead encounters So Gyo in an abandoned forbidden ground where the late Emperor and his relatives were once confined. So Gyo reveals that she is searching for someone and can read the instability in Taekyung’s internal energy. When he refuses to yield, she attacks; Taekyung withstands her pressure and strikes her, but appears badly weakened as a streak of light approaches.

Baek Yeon arrives with So Gyo. Taekyung stops fighting when So Gyo says Prince Shangshan is still under the Emperor’s control and that she has a mission to keep Taekyung alive. He infers that the Lord of Heaven wants him alive, returns Baek Yeon’s weapon, and withdraws. Afterward, So Gyo tells Baek Yeon that Taekyung may be the person she seeks—the one foretold by “that person”—but that she must confirm it. She retrieves two curved sabers from a flower garden and says the coming battle may surpass the one fought more than a decade ago. With the banquet three days away and Shangshan in enemy hands, Taekyung slips into the rain to find Jeok Cheongang, but Jeong Hogun blocks his way at the Outer Palace.

## Continuity

- The imperial banquet is three days away. So Gyo expects the coming battle to be fiercer than the one over a decade ago.
- Assassins disguised as laborers are inside the palace, working with Eunuch Ma; their mission is unknown.
- So Gyo is searching for someone and suspects Taekyung may be that person. The identity of the person and the foreteller she calls “that person” remain unknown.
- So Gyo says only she and the Emperor know the secret she withheld from Baek Yeon. She suggests Jeok Cheongang joining Taekyung’s side might be preferable, but does not explain why.
- So Gyo’s identity and allegiance remain unconfirmed. Taekyung suspects a connection to Dark Heaven; her mission to keep him alive and his inference that the Lord of Heaven wants him alive do not establish who she serves.
- So Gyo recovered two matching curved saber-like objects buried in a flower garden and wears one at each hip; their purpose is unknown.
- Prince Shangshan remains in the Emperor’s control. Taekyung believes the confrontation is unavoidable and is trying to reach Jeok Cheongang.
- Taekyung encounters Jeong Hogun while trying to pass the Outer Palace guards. His condition is poor after the fight with So Gyo.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain “that person” for 그분; the foreteller is not identified.
- Render 곡도 descriptively as “curved saber,” not as a proper name.
- Retain “Golden Dragon” for Baek Yeon’s weapon and “Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron” for 만년한철.
- Continue to use “Hongmen Banquet” for 홍문연.

### Chapter 890

# Chapter 890

## Plot

While Jin Taekyung hides near the Inner Palace, Jeong Hogun orders two Imperial Guard soldiers dismissed for abandoning their posts. Ma Sanbao intervenes, insists on proper procedure, and draws Jeong Hogun away; he has already noticed Taekyung and warns him through Sound Transmission to be careful, promising to contact him later.

Taekyung follows a food delivery to find the group’s lodging in the Outer Palace, learning that Taishan has eaten more than twenty plates of five-spice pork in less than half a shichen. He slips into Jeok Cheongang’s pavilion and announces his arrival.

## Continuity

- Ma Sanbao knows Taekyung was hiding nearby and helps him avoid attention; he says he will contact Taekyung later.
- Taekyung reaches Jeok Cheongang’s pavilion in the Outer Palace. Taishan is staying with the group and is eating an extraordinary amount of five-spice pork.

## Translation Decisions

- Distinguish the Imperial Guard (금위군) from the Embroidered Uniform Guard (금의위).
- Render Ma Sanbao’s repeated 절차대로 as “Follow procedure.”
### Chapter 891

# Chapter 891

## Plot

Jin Taekyung reunites with the Fire Dragon Pavilion members and briefs them on the imperial crisis. The group discusses So Gyo’s unknown identity and why she released Jin, then considers the strength of the imperial forces and the possibility that the Emperor expects the coming conflict to end in the enemy’s favor.

## Continuity

- Jin has rejoined the Fire Dragon Pavilion group in the imperial palace and briefed them on recent events; he reports seeing at least six Supreme Peak masters and powerful Embroidered Uniform Guards.
- So Gyo is a highly skilled flexible-sword user serving Dark Heaven; Jeok Cheongang and Namho cannot identify her, and her age and explanation for releasing Jin remain unknown.
- The grand banquet is approaching. Ju Hwaran doubts the enemy would benefit from letting information about the confrontation spread before a decisive battle.
- The restoration army led by Ma Sanbao has spent more than a decade preparing for the coup; Jin believes the Emperor has kept the East Depot in check and may be confident the outcome cannot be changed.

### Chapter 892

# Chapter 892

## Plot

Jin, Jeok Cheongang, and Namho question why their enemies are pursuing a decisive battle despite the risks and Jeok’s strength. Jin suspects Ma Sanbao may know more and asks Namho about the Hidden Shadow Pavilion’s history with the imperial household.

Namho recounts that the Pavilion sought an alliance with the imperial household during the Great Faction War, but the late Emperor refused and later ordered closer surveillance of martial artists, preventing the Pavilion from sending agents to the imperial capital. Jin then asks whether Ma Sanbao might personally recruit martial artists; when he reveals they are assassins, the group looks toward Sama Pyo and the Divine Physician, who both protest.

## Continuity

- The enemy regards a decisive battle as the fastest route to controlling the Great Nation, yet appears undeterred by Jin’s interference or Jeok Cheongang’s strength; the source of that confidence remains unknown.
- Jin plans to seek information from Ma Sanbao and suspects he may have personally recruited assassins.
- During the Great Faction War, the Hidden Shadow Pavilion sought the imperial household’s aid against the Demonic Cult, but the late Emperor refused; Namho says the court subsequently increased surveillance of martial artists and barred the Pavilion from sending agents to the imperial capital.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 캐삭빵 as “all-or-nothing match” in this context.
### Chapter 893

# Chapter 893

## Plot

Jin asks Sama Pyo and the Divine Physician about their ties to assassins. The Divine Physician recalls his Master, the former Slaughter Saint, and Sama Pyo and Namho connect the old assassin Jin encountered to the feared One-Legged Ghost Killer, though Namho says the identification is uncertain and the man is currently an ally. Jin struggles with the moral cost of joining forces with a killer who takes lives for money, then sets out to ask Ma Sanbao why he brought the assassins into the cause.

The Divine Physician recounts that his Master destroyed Salcheonmun because its members felt no regret or remorse for their deeds.

## Continuity

- The old assassin Jin encountered may be the One-Legged Ghost Killer, a famed Qinghai assassin reputed to have killed a Supreme Peak Kunlun Elder; Namho says the identification is uncertain and the assassin had a prosthetic leg.
- The old assassin is currently an ally expected to fight alongside the group, but Jin is troubled by that alliance and intends to ask Ma Sanbao why he brought the assassins into the cause.
- Jin reflects that he has killed people without seeking alternatives and is trying to become better; he does not consider himself a good person.
- The grand banquet and a possible decisive battle remain ahead; the enemy's confidence in victory remains unexplained.
- Ma Sanbao's possible role in recruiting assassins remains unresolved; the old assassin himself disclosed a connection to Ma Sanbao.

## Translation Decisions

- Render both 독각귀살 and 독각살귀 as “One-Legged Ghost Killer”; they are used for the same possibly identified assassin.
### Chapter 894

# Chapter 894

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung leaves, Jeok Cheongang tells the others that Hong Dao foresaw an approaching calamity and identified Jin as the Morning Star who would continue to shine through the darkness. Jeok believes there is no absolute justice in the Murim; his own cause is to protect those he still has and to try to make good choices. He trusts Jin to choose his own path and vows to remain at his side.

Jin goes to meet Ma Sanbao in a hidden palace refuge. Ma calmly offers to explain either the assassins he hired or So Gyo, leaving Jin startled.

## Continuity

- Hong Dao foresaw an unknown calamity and identified Jin Taekyung as the Morning Star, whose light would persist through the coming darkness.
- Jeok Cheongang regards protecting those he still has as his cause, hopes to make good choices, and will support Jin whatever path he chooses.
- Ma Sanbao confirms that he hired the assassins; his reasons for hiring them remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “Morning Star” for 신성 and “master of the Morning Star” for 신성의 주인, referring to Jin Taekyung.

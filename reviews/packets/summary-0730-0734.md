# Chapters 725–729

## Plot

Jin Taekyung tells Jeok Cheongang that the Lord of Heaven has taken a personal interest in him and that Dark Heaven’s fanatical servants may treat their leader as a living god. Jeok accepts Jin’s account of traveling between Murim and another world resembling the realm of immortals, recognizes the coming war’s danger, and asks Jin to reveal everything he has hidden. Jin later returns to the modern world relieved that Jeok accepts him as Jin Taekyung rather than as a mysterious Little Immortal.

At the Peace Guild, Jin reveals that he created the beginner-friendly Smiling Mana Cultivation Method and intends to distribute it worldwide. Choi Minwoo warns that major Hunters and Guilds will resist a technique that undermines their control, while Jin argues that rising mana, mutation Gates, and monster waves make widespread cultivation necessary. Jin’s recent campaign against rebels and terrorists was partly intended to reduce the danger of the method being misused. He proposes using Choi’s status as Cheon Taemin’s sole blood relative and heir to promote the release.

The Peace Guild commits to an ambitious international expansion despite the risks. Choi’s resolve is strengthened by a message left at a deceased loved one’s grave, and he brings President Baek Hanseong into the plan. Meanwhile, a ghostlike infiltrator exploits a brief blackout at a government building, steals an unknown item from underground evidence storage, bypasses the alarms, and escapes unseen.

Jin and Choi rename the method the Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique and secure government backing. Baek announces a major, government-supported release as a Korean national project, provoking worldwide media upheaval and broad public praise. Ares Guild executives confront Choi for acting without notice, but Cheon Taemin’s name and Choi’s legitimacy restrain their opposition. After Choi apologizes, Jin challenges the executives’ silence, leaving their response unresolved.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang accepts Jin Taekyung’s cross-world origin and asks him to disclose his remaining secrets.
- Dark Heaven’s full strength, the Lord of Heaven’s intentions, and the coming war remain major threats.
- Jin created the stable, beginner-accessible Smiling Mana Cultivation Method.
- The method has been renamed the Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique and is scheduled for public release through government cooperation in two days.
- Jin and Choi Minwoo are concealing both Cheon Taemin’s unconscious condition and Jin’s authorship of the technique.
- Jin believes broad cultivation is necessary because increasing mana is producing stronger monsters, mutation Gates, and monster waves.
- Jin’s campaign against rebel groups and terrorist organizations was intended partly to reduce threats before the technique’s release.
- Choi is Peace Guild Master, controls the Ares Guild, and is Cheon Taemin’s sole blood relative and heir.
- Jin, Choi, Im Kkeokjeong, Song Song, and the Skeleton King have committed to the dangerous international expansion together.
- President Baek Hanseong has publicly committed the Blue House to the release and framed it as a Korean national project.
- The announcement has triggered intense international media attention and mostly favorable public reaction.
- Ares Guild executives remain uneasy about Choi’s unilateral authority; Jin has confronted them after Choi’s apology.
- An unidentified ghostlike entity stole an unknown item from a government evidence storage room during a blackout. A Security Team employee named Sehwan unknowingly passed near its shadow.
- Open hooks: the contents of the upcoming announcement, the technique’s distribution and regulation, the Ares executives’ response, the exposure of Cheon Taemin’s condition and Jin’s authorship, and the identity of the infiltrator and possible media leaker.

# Chapter 731

### Chapter 732

# Chapter 732

## Plot

Jin Taekyung follows Team Leader Choi's request and publicly posts the prepared file through his enormous personal social-media account in front of Huginn, then needles the Odin Guild messenger with dinner jokes and insults. Huginn threatens Jin and briefly releases an aura powerful enough to rival an S-rank Hunter, but Team Leader Choi's warning prevents the confrontation from escalating; Huginn apologizes and withdraws.

After Huginn leaves, Team Leader Choi explains that the messenger may have come to confirm Cheon Taemin's absence rather than merely oppose the Mana Cultivation Method. They discuss the possibility that the secret leaked through Lee Jungryong's Grand Mage-level assistant who constructed Area A. Choi admits that Jin is his Joker card, and the two leave to eat kimchi stew while Jin's phone continues buzzing with sharing notifications.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has publicly posted the prepared file through his personal social-media account, whose follower count exceeds five hundred million, making the Mana Cultivation Method's release an immediate public event.
- Huginn delivered Odin Guild's opposition, threatened Jin and Team Leader Choi with severe consequences, demonstrated S-rank-level power, and withdrew after Choi warned him not to act rashly.
- Team Leader Choi suspects Huginn came to confirm Cheon Taemin's absence and that Odin may consider Cheon Taemin's condition at least as important as the Mana Cultivation Method.
- Only six people currently know that Cheon Taemin is in a vegetative state: Jin Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, Song Song, Im Kkeokjeong, the Skeleton King, and Magic Johnson; the other known holders of the secret are dead.
- A Grand Mage-level mage constructed Ares Guild's Area A, and Choi and Jin recognize that this mage may have been connected to Lee Jungryong and may have enabled the secret to leak.
- Team Leader Choi has become more willing to bluff and confront Odin because he regards Jin Taekyung as a decisive Joker card.
- Odin Guild is a massive power with potentially hidden S-rank Hunters, and Jin judges that the other major Guilds may side with it.

### Chapter 733

# Chapter 733

## Plot

After finally getting a full night's sleep at Cheon Taemin's mansion, Jin Taekyung learns that Wu Shaiming and more than fifty members of the Wu family, including children, were found dead after being imprisoned in Beijing. Xiao Yang had been trying to persuade Wu Shaiming to surrender the family Mana Cultivation Method that made Wu Heixing an S-rank Hunter, but the massacre destroyed that possibility. Jin and Team Leader Choi conclude that Odin Guild most likely ordered the extermination to prevent the method's spread—or as a response to the previous night's events—and Jin decides to visit Odin Guild in Paris to confront its master.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung, his family, Team Leader Choi, and the Skeleton King are currently staying at Cheon Taemin's heavily protected mansion, which remains publicly known as Taemin's residence.
- Wu Shaiming, former Premier of the State Council of the People's Republic of China and leader of the Crown Prince Party, was imprisoned after corruption allegations and remained Xiao Yang's greatest political rival.
- Wu Shaiming and more than fifty Wu family members, including children, died simultaneously from apparent sudden cardiac arrest while in custody, amounting to the annihilation of the Wu household.
- Xiao Yang had been trying to obtain the Wu family's Mana Cultivation Method, which had enabled Wu Heixing to become an S-rank Hunter.
- Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi consider Odin Guild the most likely perpetrator and suspect the massacre was intended to prevent the Mana Cultivation Method from being transferred or to answer the previous night's move.
- Jin Taekyung intends to travel to Paris with Team Leader Choi to meet Odin Guild's master.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 우 쉐이밍 as Wu Shaiming and 우 헤이싱 as Wu Heixing.
- Render 멸문지화 as “the annihilation of an entire household.”
- Retain Jin Taekyung's dry, profane, confrontational voice in “the boss's ugly mug.”
### Chapter 734

# Chapter 734

## Plot

Jin Taekyung considers confronting Odin Guild's master in Paris but accepts Team Leader Choi's argument that acting without a defensible pretext would only earn them an Interpol wanted notice. They review Odin Guild's earlier demand that China lease ten Sichuan cities for ninety-nine years and authorize a Beijing branch, which China rejected.

The Skeleton King interrupts Jin's discussion of public support with breaking news: Odin Guild has declared its support for Ares, revealed a new Mana Cultivation Method, and claimed the project had been prepared for years. Jin is stunned by the sudden reversal.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi will not immediately attack or confront Odin Guild; they recognize public sentiment and legal justification as essential strategic protections.
- During the Sichuan Province monster wave, Odin Guild demanded a ninety-nine-year lease of ten cities, including Chengdu, Meishan, Ziyang, and Suining, plus permission to establish an official branch in Beijing; China rejected the demand.
- Odin Guild had contacted the French government before negotiations ended and benefits from the protection and political cover of its Paris base.
- Odin Guild publicly declares support for Ares and presents a new Mana Cultivation Method as a project prepared for several years.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 최 팀장 as Team Leader Choi.
- Render 샤오 양 주석 as Chairman Xiao Yang.
- Render 마나 연공법 as Mana Cultivation Method.
- Render 민심 as public sentiment.

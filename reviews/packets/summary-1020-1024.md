## Previous block summary

# Chapters 1015–1019

## Plot

Taekyung’s force of roughly three thousand marches west from Tianshui, through Lanzhou and the Hexi Corridor, reaching the Qilian Mountains. Along the way, Taekyung is flustered by Wolhwa’s unusually affectionate missive and Ju Hwaran’s questions about their relationship. At the Qilian Mountains, Gansu’s leaders defend their strategy of leaving the rear exposed to lure Dark Heaven into a trap if it uses the Moving Formation. Taekyung lets their decision stand for now, though he suspects Sima Gong is hiding something.

Namho reports that Sama Pyo burned a secret letter shortly before the group left the Jin Family of Taiyuan. The scent of camel-oil paper suggests it came from Gansu or Qinghai, and Sima Gong is suspected as its sender, but the letter’s contents and purpose remain unknown. Jeok Cheongang asks whether Taekyung would kill Pyo and Taishan if they were acting on orders tied to Dark Heaven; Taekyung cannot answer.

Sima Gong reinforces the westbound force with three thousand troops from the Qilian Mountains. As the army moves west through falling snow, Hyuk Mujin tells Taekyung that trust in him keeps him from fearing danger. Pyo questions Sima Gong’s decision-making and reflects on the ruthless calculations that shaped his rise as heir. A red flare bursts above a distant hill.

## Continuity

- The westbound force has reached the Qilian Mountains and now includes three thousand troops from the forces stationed there. It is moving west through the mountains; Dark Heaven’s location and objective remain unknown.
- Gansu’s leaders chose to leave forces on the rear front to delay Dark Heaven if it uses the Moving Formation. Taekyung allowed the decision to stand for now.
- Sama Pyo burned a secret letter shortly before the group left the Jin Family of Taiyuan. Camel-oil paper suggests it came from Gansu or Qinghai; Sima Gong is only a suspected sender. Its contents, sender, and purpose are unconfirmed.
- Taishan appeared unusually gloomy and ate less as the group approached Gansu; whether he knows about the letter remains unknown.
- Taekyung remains unsure whether he would kill Sama Pyo and Taishan if they were acting on orders connected to Dark Heaven.
- Sama Pyo recognizes that Sima Gong’s ruthless calculations, including sacrificing family, shaped the Black Dragon Demon Gate and Pyo’s rise as heir. Sima interprets Pyo’s recent deference as a return to his former self.
- A red flare burst above a distant hill as the force moved west; what it signaled is unknown.
- Wolhwa’s latest missive includes affectionate personal language and a wish that she and Taekyung meet again. Ju Hwaran’s response to it remains unresolved.
- Namho’s important disclosure to Taekyung was the report about Pyo burning the letter.
- Six Baekma Bang men left to fetch the Lord; Ma Junggeol remains with Taekyung’s group. The Lord’s identity and motives remain unknown.
- Sama Pyo previously disobeyed Sima Gong’s order to return to Gansu. Sima Gong also ordered two martial artists dealt with for a taboo remark about Pyo’s succession; their fates remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 하서주랑 as “Hexi Corridor.”
- Retain “Moving Formation” for 이동진.
- Render 밀서 as “secret letter,” distinct from 전서, “missive.”
- Render 군자도 as “Junzi Saber,” preserving 군자 as “junzi” and 刀 as “Saber.”
- Retain “iron ball” as the image for the weight of Sama Pyo’s inherited constraints; it is not a literal restraint.

### Chapter 1020

# Chapter 1020

## Plot

A red emergency flare from the scouts prompts the allied force to prepare for battle. Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang rush ahead and learn that Dark Heaven has taken Dunhuang. A messenger reports that the Jade Gate Pass was breached, at least half of the ten-thousand-strong defensive force was killed, wounded, or missing, and the Kongtong Sect lost about thirty percent of its strength, including two Elders and the entire Demon-Subduing Squad. The Kongtong Sect Leader escaped, but his whereabouts and the fate of the other survivors are unknown. Believing Dark Heaven is advancing rapidly toward the Great Snow Mountain, Taekyung orders all forces in the Qilian Mountains mobilized, overruling Sima Gong’s earlier decision by invoking his authority as Marquis of Shangshan.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has captured Dunhuang after breaching the Jade Gate Pass; the fall occurred about three days before the report reached the leadership.
- The Dunhuang defense suffered catastrophic losses: at least two thousand dead and three thousand seriously wounded or missing; the Kongtong Sect lost about thirty percent of its strength, including two Elders and all one hundred members of the Demon-Subduing Squad.
- The Kongtong Sect Leader escaped Dunhuang with surviving troops, but his location and the fate of the other survivors remain unknown.
- Jin Taekyung orders all forces stationed in the Qilian Mountains mobilized, overruling Sima Gong’s prior conclusion that they should not be fully deployed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 玉門關 as “Jade Gate Pass,” 武威劍門 as “Martial Might Sword Sect,” 伏魔隊 as “Demon-Subduing Squad,” and 真人 as “Perfected One.”
### Chapter 1021

# Chapter 1021

## Plot

Invoking his imperial title as Marquis of Shangshan, Jin Taekyung orders the Gansu leaders to gather every force from the Qilian Mountains at the Great Snow Mountain. Sima Gong agrees to comply for now, and the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord agrees on Zhongnan’s behalf. Sima Gong sends Sama Pyo back to Taekyung, but Taekyung realizes Pyo will be spying on them for his father.

As the five thousand troops set out, Sima Gong confirms that he ordered Pyo to watch Taekyung’s group. He tells Pyo that the plan is to secure the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s resurgence and make Pyo ruler of Gansu, adding that there cannot be two tigers on one mountain. Their parting is not the affectionate farewell Sima Gong had described to Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Sima Gong agrees to Taekyung’s order to bring all forces from the Qilian Mountains to the Great Snow Mountain; the Gansu leaders and Zhongnan prepare to move within half a day.
- Sama Pyo returns to Taekyung’s group under orders from Sima Gong to observe their every move; Taekyung suspects Pyo is acting as a spy, not a comrade.
- Sima Gong says he intends the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s resurgence and Pyo’s eventual rule over Gansu, and insists their goal remains unchanged despite setbacks.
- Sama Pyo accepts his father’s spying order without questioning it; Sima Gong expresses satisfaction at his obedience.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 상산후 as “Marquis of Shangshan,” 열후 as “marquis,” and 흑야왕 as “Black Night King.”
### Chapter 1022

# Chapter 1022

## Plot

Jin Taekyung’s force of five thousand reaches the Great Snow Mountain after a brutal forced march. Signals from allied forces on the mountain bring relief, but Taekyung remains troubled by Sama Pyo’s silence and Jeok Cheongang’s question about whether Pyo and Taishan are carrying out Sima Gong’s secret orders—and whether those orders involve Dark Heaven. The scale of Dark Heaven’s victory at Dunhuang leaves Taekyung wondering what hidden strength made them confident of invading Gansu.

A flashback shows a badly wounded Kongtong disciple gain what he believes is a breakthrough, only for a blood-covered middle-aged killer to catch his sword and reveal that the disciple alone had seemed to slow. The killer says the sect brought its fate on itself by refusing to surrender, kills the disciple—identified as the Kongtong Sword Dragon—and looks toward the Great Snow Mountain as a tremendous rumble begins.

## Continuity

- Signals from allied forces confirm that the Great Snow Mountain is occupied by Taekyung’s side as his five-thousand-strong force arrives.
- A middle-aged Dark Heaven killer has slaughtered more than a hundred Kongtong Sect members, including the Kongtong Sword Dragon; his identity and strength remain unknown.
- Taekyung suspects Dark Heaven has at least three Supreme Peak masters or one overwhelming superhuman, and wonders whether the Lord of Heaven is involved; these remain suspicions.
- Sama Pyo remains under Sima Gong’s order to watch Taekyung’s group. Taekyung still does not know whether Sima Gong’s secret orders connect Pyo and Taishan to Dark Heaven.
- The secret letter received by Sama Pyo remains unexplained, and the Kongtong Sect Leader’s whereabouts remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 공동검룡 as “Kongtong Sword Dragon.”
- Render 복마검 as “Demon-Subduing Sword” and 무아 as “No-self.”
### Chapter 1023

# Chapter 1023

## Plot

As Taekyung’s thirty-thousand-strong force struggles up the Great Snow Mountain, he worries that its numbers conceal a lack of strength and that Sima Gong may have his own designs. A Gansu military officer, Hong Pyo, recognizes Taekyung as the Marquis of Shangshan and salutes him, then insists on checking Sima Gong’s identity tablet despite his status. Jeok Cheongang approves of Hong Pyo’s strict devotion to duty.

Before the group can respond to the urgent war drums echoing from beyond the ridge, Hyuk Mujin jokes about losing control from excitement at the troops’ salute, and Taekyung tells him to keep his dignity as a marquis.

## Continuity

- Taekyung’s force has gathered thirty thousand people for the advance up the Great Snow Mountain; more than half are from unorthodox factions, and Taekyung is concerned that the force’s numbers overstate its strength.
- Hong Pyo, a Deputy Thousand Captain of the Gansu Regional Military Commission, strictly enforces identity checks at a military checkpoint and recognizes Taekyung as the Marquis of Shangshan.
- Urgent war drums sound from beyond the ridge; their source and what prompted them are unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 홍표 as Hong Pyo, 위지휘사사 as Regional Military Commission, and 부천호 as Deputy Thousand Captain.
- Render 호패 as identity tablet.
### Chapter 1024

# Chapter 1024

## Plot

The Blood-Sword Demon Lord overpowers one of the Three Elders of Tianshan and reveals his contempt for the former Heavenly Demon, whom he once followed. He now serves the Lord of Heaven and is certain the hidden strength in his army will defeat the Fire King. He orders a messenger sent to the Great Snow Mountain, while privately anticipating a meeting with Jin Taekyung, whom he has been ordered not to kill.

At the Great Snow Mountain, the scouting party’s hundred members return dead, each killed with a single, identical sword strike. Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang face an approaching army of tens of thousands as the defenders prepare for battle. A group carrying a white banner approaches through the enemy forces; its purpose is not yet known.

## Continuity

- The Blood-Sword Demon Lord once served the Heavenly Demon but now serves the Lord of Heaven; he believes the Lord of Heaven’s army will defeat the Fire King and bring the Great Snow Mountain’s defenders under its control.
- The Blood-Sword Demon Lord has been ordered not to kill Jin Taekyung and wants to meet him before battle, believing his master has taken an unexplained interest in Taekyung.
- All one hundred Great Snow Mountain scouts were killed with a single sword strike each; Jeok Cheongang recognizes the similar wounds but cannot place them.
- An army of tens of thousands has reached the Great Snow Mountain. A group carrying a white banner is approaching through its ranks, and its purpose is unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 혈검마군 as “Blood-Sword Demon Lord” and 천산삼노 as “Three Elders of Tianshan.”

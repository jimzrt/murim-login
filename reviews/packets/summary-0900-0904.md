# Chapters 895–899

## Plot

Ma Sanbao explains how So Gyo helped the coup succeed and asks Jin Taekyung to join the restoration effort. Taekyung signs the pledge and arranges for Murim Alliance reinforcements to be summoned for the grand banquet. After the flood, the Emperor announces a three-day birthday banquet for the imperial prince, while a mysterious bamboo-hat man stirs public hopes around Prince Shangshan.

Taekyung reunites with Hyuk Mujin and sends the Fire Dragon Pavilion members to await the reinforcements, keeping himself and Jeok Cheongang behind. On the way to the banquet hall, Taekyung recognizes opium’s scent on Hong Jin’s pipe and wonders whether the Emperor smokes it. The Emperor prepares to leave Qianqing Palace; So Gyo, having stopped his agents from following the departed martial artists, waits on the roof with her weapons. At the banquet hall, Taekyung sees a vast golden procession approaching five hours late.

## Continuity

- Taekyung joined the restoration effort, signing the pledge in his name alone. He gave Ma Sanbao a cipher to summon Murim Alliance reinforcements for the banquet.
- So Gyo helped Baek Yeon defeat Cang Gong during the coup more than ten years ago. Her identity and connection to Dark Heaven remain unconfirmed.
- The Emperor announced a three-day birthday banquet for the imperial prince, beginning the day after the flood. Public anger is growing, and a mysterious bamboo-hat man’s reference to shelter at Shangshan led the crowd to imagine Prince Shangshan as a successor.
- Murim Alliance reinforcements were expected within half a day to a day. Hyuk Mujin is leading the Fire Dragon Pavilion members to await them; Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang stayed behind. Mujin is to check Taekyung’s note only after ensuring they are not followed.
- Taekyung suspects the Emperor smokes opium, having recognized its scent from his earlier audience at Qianqing Palace. The reason is unknown.
- The Emperor is preparing to leave Qianqing Palace for the banquet. So Gyo prevented his agents from pursuing the martial artists who left the palace and has reclaimed her weapons.
- The banquet is underway, and the Emperor’s five-hours-late golden procession is approaching the Grand Banquet Hall.

- Render 黃道十二宮 as “Twelve Palaces of the Zodiac,” the title for the twelve Supreme Peak masters protecting the capital.
- Render 東廠掌印太監 as “Seal-Holding Eunuch of the East Depot.”
- Keep “Shangshan” in the crowd’s mountain imagery, where it alludes to Prince Shangshan.
- Retain “Tenfold Man” as Hyuk Mujin’s boastful title.
- Translate 앵속 as “poppy” in the explanation and use “opium” when Taekyung identifies the substance.

# Chapter 900

### Chapter 901

# Chapter 901

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung openly mocks the Emperor, the Emperor forgives him for his disrespect, prompting Taekyung to suspect the response reveals something important. Wei Zhong, the East Depot’s Seal-Holding Eunuch known as Cang Gong, arrives after a long recovery and trades courteous but pointed remarks with the Emperor. Their exchange exposes their opposing views of the imperial succession, while Taekyung judges Wei Zhong an exceptionally powerful master. The Emperor says Prince Shangshan and the pregnant Consort are on their way; Taekyung then asks to move seats beside Wei Zhong, ostensibly because the raised seats offer more side dishes.

## Continuity

- The Emperor forgives Jin Taekyung for openly disrespecting him; Taekyung suspects this response has a significant explanation that remains unknown.
- Wei Zhong, addressed as Cang Gong, is the East Depot’s Seal-Holding Eunuch and has recovered enough from a prolonged illness to move about and attend the banquet.
- Wei Zhong and the Emperor exchange courteous, pointed remarks and have opposing views of who should inherit the throne; neither states his preferred heir.
- The Emperor says Prince Shangshan and the pregnant Consort are on their way to the banquet.
- Taekyung judges Wei Zhong more powerful than anyone else present, even Baek Yeon, and believes Wei Zhong and So Gyo are roughly evenly matched.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 902

# Chapter 902

## Plot

At the Emperor’s banquet, Jin Taekyung eats and speaks privately with Cang Gong, who says he will signal when they should act and that the Fire King is needed to defeat So Gyo. Cang Gong reveals that the Murim Alliance has sent reinforcements. The Emperor publicly declares an heir; Aehyang arrives under Embroidered Uniform Guard protection, and Prince Shangshan follows her. Taekyung confirms Shangshan still has the Myriad-Poison Ring. After Cang Gong questions why the Emperor forgave Taekyung, Taekyung concludes that the Emperor does not want Shangshan put in danger.

## Continuity

- The Emperor publicly declares Prince Shangshan his heir at the imperial birthday banquet; Aehyang arrives under Embroidered Uniform Guard protection, visibly uneasy and touching her belly.
- Cang Gong and Taekyung are waiting for Cang Gong’s signal to act; Cang Gong says So Gyo must be dealt with and the Fire King is essential, while the Fire King remains outside the banquet hall.
- Prince Shangshan still carries the Myriad-Poison Ring entrusted to him by Taekyung.
- Taekyung concludes that the Emperor forgave him because the Emperor does not want Prince Shangshan put in danger.
- Cang Gong says the Murim Alliance has sent reinforcements despite having deployed most of its forces against Dark Heaven.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Cang Gong’s confidential dialogue distinct from the public banquet through the sound-blocking internal-energy barrier.
- Render the Emperor’s heir declaration as a public designation of Prince Shangshan.
### Chapter 903

# Chapter 903

## Plot

Taekyung realizes the Emperor does not want Prince Shangshan endangered and identifies So Gyo’s inexplicable decision to let him go as the clue that exposed the scheme. Cang Gong confirms So Gyo was the plan’s only flaw but admits he does not know her identity or allegiance. He reveals the Lord of Heaven’s interest in Taekyung and displays a chilling power unlike Yin-Cold Qi. Taekyung provokes Cang Gong and attacks as a signal; someone blocks his punch, and the Fire King bursts into the banquet hall. As Taekyung follows with the Flame Divine Palm, the confrontation continues.

Hyuk Mujin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion party leave the Imperial Capital for the Jiangsu–Zhejiang border on Taekyung’s mission. Mujin senses something is wrong and learns that the others were warned by Jeok Cheongang that Taekyung would send him away to keep him safe. The group then spots countless silent figures moving through the forest and reacts on instinct.

## Continuity

- The Emperor does not want Prince Shangshan put in danger; Cang Gong confirms it.
- So Gyo remains unidentified and unaffiliated as far as Cang Gong and Taekyung can determine; Cang Gong considers her the only flaw in the scheme.
- The Lord of Heaven is searching for Taekyung and has shown interest in his unprecedented achievements or another quality he alone can recognize.
- Cang Gong wields an unfamiliar, chilling power distinct from Yin-Cold Qi; an unknown figure blocks Taekyung’s attack, and the Fire King enters the banquet hall.
- Hyuk Mujin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion party are traveling to the Jiangsu–Zhejiang border on Taekyung’s mission; Jeok Cheongang had warned them Taekyung would send Mujin away for his safety.
- Countless silent figures have appeared in the forest near Mujin’s group.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the incantation 아씨오 as “Accio,” preserving Taekyung’s magical reference.
### Chapter 904

# Chapter 904

## Plot

Cang Gong repels Jin Taekyung with an unfamiliar chilling power, but Jeok Cheongang arrives as the banquet hall erupts into a battle between loyalists and hidden attackers. A rain of arrows falls on the combatants and the Emperor; Taekyung burns it away and fights through the attackers. Ma Sanbao appears, kills a group of Embroidered Uniform Guards, and confronts Taekyung, who answers by striking at him.

## Continuity

- Cang Gong's chilling power is unfamiliar to Taekyung and cannot be explained as merely Yin-Cold Qi; Cang Gong wants to take Taekyung to the Lord of Heaven, believing he can be recruited.
- The banquet-hall battle is underway: attackers have infiltrated the Embroidered Uniform Guard, and a rain of arrows has targeted the hall and the Emperor.
- Jeok Cheongang has joined Taekyung in the banquet-hall battle.
- Ma Sanbao has killed several Embroidered Uniform Guards and confronted Taekyung; Taekyung attacks him, and Ma's present allegiance and motives remain unclear.
- So Gyo's identity and allegiance remain unknown; she is beside the Emperor with Baek Yeon.
- The Emperor does not want Prince Shangshan endangered.
- Hyuk Mujin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion party are traveling to the Jiangsu–Zhejiang border on Taekyung's mission; silent figures have appeared in the nearby forest.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 鴻門宴 as “Hongmen Banquet,” retaining the allusion to a treacherous feast.

## Previous block summary

# Chapters 1140–1144

## Plot

The Son of Heaven enfeoffs Jin Taekyung as Prince Shangshan and offers him the title Prince of Ye, which he declines. Murim leaders gather in Xining to honor him. After avoiding the Bow Saint for three days, Taekyung meets her by the river. She recounts how the Martial God united Murim and vanished after defeating the Heavenly Demon, then says he wanted to find Taekyung for the sake of the realm. Taekyung suspects she is withholding part of the truth.

Jeok Cheongang wears the pocket watch Taekyung had kept in his Inventory. Jeok says he found it where Taekyung collapsed ten days earlier. Holding it recalls that The Helper gave it to Taekyung as a final gift; its System classification changes from Common to Special, and its Item information says it holds a secret. Hyuk Mujin remembers that its hand has moved, and Taekyung realizes it has moved backward and may have circled one or more times. Taekyung suspects The Helper was the Martial God, and that the Martial God was Cheon Taemin, whose consciousness the System may have preserved in the Inventory. He tells Jeok he has something to discuss.

After Mae Jonghak declares the campaign a fight to protect the realm, more than two hundred thousand troops leave Xining for a cursed forbidden land beyond the desert to fight the Lord of Heaven. Before dawn, Taekyung leaves in a prepared carriage to pursue an unspecified plan and falls into a deep sleep; Jeok waits with the others.

## Continuity

- More than two hundred thousand Murim forces are marching from Xining toward a cursed forbidden land beyond the desert to fight the Lord of Heaven.
- Taekyung left Xining asleep in a prepared carriage to pursue an unspecified plan; Jeok Cheongang is waiting for him.
- Taekyung suspects The Helper was the Martial God and that the Martial God was Cheon Taemin; he theorizes the System preserved Cheon’s consciousness in the Inventory. None of this is confirmed.
- The pocket watch, The Helper’s final gift to Taekyung, returned from the Inventory and was found where Taekyung collapsed. Its classification changed from Common to Special, and its Item information says it holds a secret. Its hand moved backward from the position Hyuk Mujin remembers.
- The Bow Saint says the Martial God sought Taekyung for the sake of the realm; Taekyung suspects she has withheld part of the truth.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 上山王 as “Prince Shangshan” and 寧王 as “Prince of Ye.”
- Render 合從軍 (합종군) as “coalition army” and 팔황 as “Eight Directions.”
- Render 회중시계 as “pocket watch” and 누가 만들었는지 모를 회중시계 as “Pocket Watch of Unknown Make.”

### Chapter 1145

# Chapter 1145

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang keeps watch over the sleeping Jin Taekyung, whose consciousness has gone to the realm of immortals. Taekyung has promised to return after investigating a suspected crisis there. As the coalition army marches across the Taklamakan Desert toward Tianshan, Jeok resolves to stay beside him and protect him; the Slaughter Saint and others remain nearby, and Jeok takes comfort in seeing how many people care for his Disciple. Dark clouds gather over the desert.

Elsewhere, a shadow awakens in a sealed chamber after briefly losing consciousness, kills a subordinate for questioning the truth, and seeks answers from the Lord of Heaven. The Lord says the shadow—now revealed to be the Grand Mage—must have felt a wave because root and branch are one, and the Grand Mage was reborn through the Lord’s grace. A faint green light rises; the Lord caresses jade and declares that the heavens have opened once again.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is asleep in the Murim world while his consciousness is in the realm of immortals; Jeok Cheongang stays beside him until he wakes.
- The coalition army of more than two hundred thousand is crossing the Taklamakan Desert toward Tianshan, and Jeok intends to protect Taekyung there.
- The Grand Mage has been reborn through the Lord of Heaven’s grace and felt a wave that the Lord connects to the heavens opening again; its cause and significance remain unexplained.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 仙界 as “realm of immortals,” 塔克拉玛干 as “Taklamakan Desert,” and 汗血寶馬 as “sweat-blood horse.”
- Render 時辰 as “shichen.”
### Chapter 1146

# Chapter 1146

## Plot

In a buried Gulf War shelter beneath the desert, Choi Minwoo learns that thousands of enemies are approaching. Though waiting could keep his group safe, he struggles with the prospect of leaving others to face the attackers. The Skeleton King reveals that the Hunters are already ready to fight, trusting Minwoo to make the same choice. Minwoo draws his sword and cuts through the shelter wall to lead them out. As the Skeleton King charges toward the enemy, an unexpected voice says, “Dad’s not sleeping.”

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo is commanding a group of Hunters in a buried desert shelter; thousands of enemies are approaching, and he chooses to lead the Hunters into the fight rather than wait safely underground.
- The Skeleton King is with Choi Minwoo’s group, and the Hunters trust Minwoo to lead them into battle.
- Choi Minwoo regards Jin Taekyung as someone who takes responsibility for his actions, even in a crisis.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 스켈레톤 킹 as “Skeleton King.”
- Render 방공호 as “bomb shelter.”

### Chapter 1148

# Chapter 1148

## Plot

Taekyung and the Skeleton King fight the remaining named monsters at the bomb shelter. Their bodies are exceptionally dense and regenerate quickly, but Taekyung kills several by exploiting openings; the Skeleton King has also grown much stronger. The two exchange a quiet, affectionate greeting before resuming the fight. A tremendous light then gathers overhead. Taekyung recognizes the attack raining down on the monsters as the work of his friend, the Grand Mage.

## Continuity

- Taekyung and the Skeleton King are reunited allies who consider each other friends.
- The named monsters have exceptionally durable bodies and rapid regeneration; Taekyung and the Skeleton King continue fighting them at the bomb shelter.
- The Grand Mage sends a destructive attack from overhead, aimed at the monsters; Taekyung recognizes her as his friend.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 스켈레톤 킹 as “Skeleton King.”
- Render 스톤 킹 as “Stone King” in the Skeleton King’s joking reference to himself.
### Chapter 1149

# Chapter 1149

## Plot

The Hunters finish off the monsters in the desert with help from the Grand Mage, Magic Johnson. Johnson reunites with Taekyung and reveals that his last contact with the group was twenty-four hours ago. Team Leader Choi then tells Taekyung that he has been unconscious for ten days.

The System reports that the Main Quest Cataclysm failed despite the Doppelganger's defeat, partially opening the Demon Realm's boundaries and allowing unknown beings to invade. A rift is in progress, and Black Dragon Duke Morgoth has answered a summoning. Taekyung recognizes Morgoth's wings and roar from his last night in Murim.

In Russia, Morgoth enters Vladimir Furin's office after destroying Red Square. Furin confronts him while clasping his hand.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has been unconscious for ten days; Magic Johnson's last contact with the group was twenty-four hours ago, and Choi says the first contact was three hours before that.
- The Main Quest Cataclysm failed despite the Doppelganger's defeat; the Demon Realm's boundaries have partially opened and a rift is in progress.
- Black Dragon Duke Morgoth has answered a summoning; Taekyung recognizes his wings and roar from his last night in Murim.
- Morgoth destroyed Red Square and entered Vladimir Furin's office in Russia.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 흑룡공 as “Black Dragon Duke” and 모르고스 as “Morgoth.”

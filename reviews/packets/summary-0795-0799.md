## Previous block summary

# Chapters 790–794

## Plot

The Main Quest [Cataclysm] reveals its true target: eliminate The Prophet within an unspecified time limit. Jin Taekyung leads the World Hunter Federation’s multinational force into the Middle East, where The Prophet is hiding and may be preparing a catastrophic attack with a large supply of unrefined Magic Gems.

During the search, the Skeleton King and Xiao Shen find twenty members of a missing squad dead in the desert. Their bodies are desiccated like Siegfried Wassmann’s, and the Skeleton King reports that an unidentified presence appeared and vanished without a trace. After the Skeleton King defeats an attacking Monster Wave, Jin connects the deaths to Siegfried’s still-incomplete [An Unknown Death] Quest and realizes Michael Silbert was not Siegfried’s killer.

Jin interrogates the captive Huginn, who identifies The Prophet as Muninn and says Muninn killed Siegfried. Jin recognizes that Huginn and Muninn are Odin’s two ravens: there was never only one Prophet.

## Continuity

- Jin is the World Hunter Federation’s Alliance Leader, pursuing [Cataclysm], which requires him to eliminate The Prophet before an unspecified deadline. At least 100,000 Hunters have been deployed to search the Middle East.
- The Prophet is Muninn, hiding somewhere in the Middle East. Jin suspects a major attack may be imminent; Muninn has a large supply of unrefined Magic Gems.
- Huginn says Muninn killed Siegfried Wassmann. Jin believes Huginn is telling the truth. Huginn remains captive and has begun disclosing what he knows; Jin has not promised to release him.
- Jin’s Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] remains incomplete, despite learning that Muninn killed Siegfried.
- The Skeleton King and Xiao Shen remain in the Middle East. The Skeleton King defeated the desert Monster Wave without casualties; more than a thousand undead remain with him.
- Twenty people found in the desert died in the same desiccated manner as Siegfried. The cause of their deaths is unknown. The unidentified presence the Skeleton King saw disappeared without leaving a trace.
- Muninn’s relationship to Michael Silbert is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep **magical power** distinct from **mana**.
- Render **무닌** as **Muninn**; The Prophet is Muninn.
- Keep the System Quest title as **An Unknown Death** and its Grade as **Supreme Peak**.
- Do not identify the cause of the desert deaths or the vanished presence until revealed.

### Chapter 795

# Chapter 795

## Plot

Jin completes the Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] after Huginn confirms that The Prophet killed Siegfried Wassmann, and receives the special Item Eyes of Truth. Pressed for more information, Huginn reveals that Muninn is an identity passed between five people; the current Prophet is the fifth, and Huginn saw him once about three years ago. He describes the man as white, mostly concealed, and seemingly unsuited to close combat, though he admits this is only an impression.

Huginn says the Muninn from three years ago brought Michael Silbert information about Cheon Taemin, Leviathan, and Magic Gems, including Leviathan’s presence and the plan to draw it to Tokyo. Jin concludes that the Muninns served as hidden operatives who gathered intelligence, eliminated rivals, and moved unrefined Magic Gems from the Middle East and Africa. He orders the search teams recalled and the leadership team to the strategy room, then leaves Huginn unconscious in the torturers’ custody for further questioning.

## Continuity

- The Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] is complete; Jin received the special Item Eyes of Truth, and the quest confirmed that The Prophet killed Siegfried Wassmann.
- Muninn is a succession of identities: Huginn says the current Prophet is the fifth. The first disappeared during the Great Cataclysm; Huginn met the second after the Cataclysm ended and saw the current Prophet once, about three years ago.
- Huginn describes the current Prophet as a white man whose face was mostly covered and whose build seemed unsuited to close combat; this is Huginn’s uncertain personal impression, not a confirmed Hunter type.
- The Muninn from about three years ago supplied Michael Silbert with information about Cheon Taemin, Leviathan, and Magic Gems, including Leviathan’s presence and the plan to lure it to Tokyo.
- Jin believes the Muninns secretly moved unrefined Magic Gems from the Middle East and Africa, stored them, eliminated rivals, and gathered intelligence. He orders the Middle East search teams recalled and calls the leadership team to the strategy room.
- Huginn remains captive; Jin leaves him unconscious in the torturers’ custody and orders them to continue questioning him.

## Translation Decisions

- Treat Muninn as a succession of identities, not as proof that one person has held the role throughout.
- Keep Magic Gem distinct from mana and magical power.
### Chapter 796

# Chapter 796

## Plot

As a blood moon hangs over the desert, rising magical power has altered the atmosphere and brought torrential rain. The Prophet, crossing the desert behind an invisible barrier, realizes the magical eagles pursuing him have disappeared. He suspects the Skeleton King sent them and recognizes that Jin Taekyung’s rise and the Federation’s swift recovery have disrupted his plans. With essential components still missing and the search closing in, he decides to expand his hunt and spots evacuation vehicles crossing the dunes.

Yamamoto Genji is furious after Jin Taekyung orders him to return from the Middle East. Humiliated by public mockery for avoiding the Leviathan incident and the launch ceremony, he suspects Jin is trying to deny him credit. He stops the unmanned evacuation vehicle, and the surrounding rain suddenly goes silent. The Hunters realize they are under attack just before a tremendous impact hits them.

## Continuity

- The Prophet is fleeing across the desert under concealment; the magical eagles pursuing him have stopped, and he suspects the Skeleton King sent them. His search window is closing, key components for his plan remain uncollected, and he has chosen to expand his hunt.
- The Prophet has identified Jin Taekyung as the source of the unexpected setbacks to his plans and believes Jin’s influence is growing beyond prediction.
- Yamamoto Genji is with Japanese Self-Defense Forces Hunters in an unmanned evacuation vehicle in the Middle East; the vehicle is attacked after he orders it to stop. The attackers and outcome are unknown.
- The main quest [Cataclysm] still requires Jin Taekyung to eliminate The Prophet before an unspecified time limit expires.
- The cause of Siegfried Wassmann’s death and the deaths of twenty people found in the desert remains unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 질풍불참 as “Swift No-Show,” preserving the online joke that contrasts Yamamoto’s repeated absences with Genji’s Swift Strike.
- Keep magical power distinct from mana.

### Chapter 798

# Chapter 798

## Plot

Jin reaches the J1 battlefield and finds hundreds dead, but The Prophet has already escaped. After six hours of searching a 700-kilometer radius with no result, Magic Johnson concludes that The Prophet is a mage capable of evading detection; Johnson is certain The Prophet acted alone. Jin learns that Yamamoto Genji is J1’s sole survivor. Yamamoto was critically injured but has awakened and is recovering. Jin treats him with internal energy, and Yamamoto responds by calling Jin “Chōsenjin.”

Jin blames himself for sending the Hunters into danger and feels he has no right to condemn Yamamoto for being the lone survivor. He still resents Yamamoto for arriving late during the battle against Leviathan.

## Continuity

- The Prophet escaped the J1 battlefield before reinforcements arrived; a six-hour search across a 700-kilometer radius found no trace of him.
- Magic Johnson believes The Prophet was alone and that magic explains his disappearance; The Prophet’s location and means of escape remain unknown.
- Yamamoto Genji is J1’s sole survivor. He was critically injured, has awakened, and is recovering under care after Jin treated him.
- Jin feels responsible for the deaths because he sent the Hunters to the battlefield; he resents Yamamoto for his past conduct but recognizes he has no right to blame him for surviving.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magic distinct from mana; retain “Chōsenjin” as the Japanese ethnic slur used by Yamamoto.
### Chapter 799

# Chapter 799

## Plot

Yamamoto apologizes to Jin and recounts the J1 attack: The Prophet stopped all ten transport vehicles, killed the team, and drained blood and a pale mist from the corpses. Yamamoto admits by implication that he tried to flee and says The Prophet left when reinforcements arrived. Magic Johnson concludes the corpse-draining was not human magic; Jin recognizes The Prophet as a monster. The Prophet left a message intended to draw Jin in.

Elsewhere, Amir’s hidden group watches a convoy of more than five hundred people approach. Protected by an unseen veil, they remain undetected; Amir orders Hamid to wait for The Prophet and the coming holy war.

## Continuity

- The Prophet is a monster, not a human mage; he stopped the J1 vehicles and absorbed blood and a pale mist from the dead, but the nature of that power is unknown.
- Yamamoto is J1’s sole survivor. He says The Prophet left after hearing reinforcements arrive; Jin suspects Yamamoto tried to flee, and the reason The Prophet spared him remains unclear.
- The Prophet left a message meant to lure Jin; its contents are not yet known.
- Amir and his subordinate Hamid lead a group concealed by an unseen veil near a convoy of more than five hundred people. Amir orders them to wait for The Prophet; they intend to wage a holy war.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magic distinct from mana; The Prophet’s revealed monstrous nature does not establish that his corpse-draining ability is magic.
- Render 조센징 as “Chōsenjin,” an ethnic slur, and 인샬라 as “Inshallah” (“God willing”).

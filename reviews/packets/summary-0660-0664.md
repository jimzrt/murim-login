# Chapters 655–659

## Plot

A Supreme Peak master devastates Yohi’s Western Yao Estate, killing more than a hundred Yao warriors and beasts. Jin and Yayul Mok conclude that Dark Heaven likely deployed the attacker. Heugung’s guards are found dead, and a severed wrist believed to be his is recovered, but footprints indicate that a third party intervened and abducted Yohi and Heugung rather than staging their deaths.

Baeksang publicly accuses Jin of the massacre. Utu-ri testifies that he saw Jin, masked, dining near the West Gate with an unfamiliar middle-aged man shortly before the attack. Baeksang uses the testimony and Jin’s unexplained movements to order his capture dead or alive, but the Beast Miao King blocks the arrest and defends Jin. Baeksang proposes that the Tribal Grand Council decide Jin’s fate on its second day.

The System generates the Either-Or Quest, requiring Jin to choose between resistance and surrender while warning that the choice may kill someone. Unable to escape with his companions, Jin obtains the Beast Miao King’s promise to protect them and clear his name. He punches Baeksang, then declares that he surrenders.

Meanwhile, a reconnaissance squad racing south through Guangxi to stop the Blood Monk receives a sealed order from the Nanman Beast Palace. The squad turns against Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin; Ju Hwaran is alive but subdued with a Pressure-Point Strike, while Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin surrender their weapons and are bound.

## Continuity

- Jin remains the Third Young Master of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, head of the Fire Dragon Pavilion, and an ally of the Nanman Beast Palace and Fire Gate Clan.
- Yohi’s Western Yao Estate was destroyed by an unidentified Supreme Peak master. Dark Heaven is suspected, but the attacker’s identity and motive remain unknown.
- Heugung and Yohi are missing. Heugung’s apparent death is unconfirmed, and a third party was present at the estate.
- Baeksang has accused Jin of the Inner Palace massacre and arranged circumstantial evidence against him, including Utu-ri’s testimony.
- The Beast Miao King prevented Jin’s immediate arrest and promised to protect Jin’s companions and clear his name.
- Jin chose surrender under the System’s Either-Or Quest after punching Baeksang. The consequences of his surrender and the second-day Council judgment remain unresolved.
- The reconnaissance squad is traveling south through Guangxi to intercept the Blood Monk, expected to arrive within two or three days.
- A palace-sealed order caused two tribal chieftains to turn the squad against Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin. The reason for the order is unknown.
- Ju Hwaran is alive but captured separately after being subdued with a Pressure-Point Strike.
- Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin are disarmed, bound, and held by the reconnaissance squad. Song Ilseom suspects that Jin caused a major incident in the Inner Palace.

# Chapter 660

## Plot

Chief Jang and Chief Go secretly follow the Beast Miao King's order to keep the Han Chinese members of the reconnaissance squad away from the Inner Palace, subduing and retaining the three captives so they can protect them from becoming hostages. Chief Jang believes Jin Taekyung was framed, but tightens Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin's bindings after an embarrassing attempt to loosen them.

Jin is imprisoned in the deepest part of the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison, bound with enormous iron balls and rendered unable to use internal energy by a Force-Sealing Pill. Baeksang visits, dismisses Jin's final punch as a frightened child's outburst, and announces that Jin will be executed publicly at noon in two days.

### Chapter 661

# Chapter 661

## Plot

Baeksang reveals that twenty tribal chieftains, including himself, have united to place Jin Taekyung on the Tribal Grand Council's final agenda and execute him at noon in two days. He explains that the coalition was bought with promises of tribal revival, influence, and wealth, then recounts the war more than fifty years earlier, when he and the Beast Miao King led ten thousand warriors alongside the Zhongnan Sect against the Demonic Cult. Fewer than one-tenth survived, and after the Martial God defeated the Heavenly Demon, Baeksang's forces joined the pursuit of the Demonic Cult's remnants. Jin identifies Baeksang's deceased only child as Baekhwi, provoking Baeksang into revealing that their history began at Gansu's Great Snow Mountain while leading the Southern Army with the Zhongnan Sect.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains imprisoned in the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison, bound by iron balls and unable to use internal energy because of the Force-Sealing Pill; his public execution is scheduled for noon in two days.
- Baeksang has secured the support of twenty tribal chieftains and is using Jin's execution as the final-day agenda of the Tribal Grand Council.
- Baeksang's only child was named Baekhwi; Baekhwi is deceased and would have become the Beast Miao King's son-in-law if he had lived.
- Baeksang and the Beast Miao King once led ten thousand Nanman warriors alongside the Zhongnan Sect in the Southern Army during the war against the Demonic Cult; fewer than one-tenth survived.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion was attacked after Jin left, Dark Heaven is suspected of organizing or enabling the assault, and Heugung and Yohi remain missing after the destruction of Yohi's Western Yao Estate.
- Chief Jang and Chief Go are secretly keeping the three Han Chinese reconnaissance-squad captives away from the Inner Palace, while Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin remain bound and unconscious and Ju Hwaran remains among the captives.
- The reconnaissance squad is traveling through Nanman while guarding against the Blood Monk, and Jin remains committed to protecting his people.

## Translation Decisions

### Chapter 662

# Chapter 662

## Plot

Baeksang recounts the battle at Great Snow Mountain during the Great Faction War. The Southern Army, led alongside the Zhongnan Sect under Venerable Wusang, was ambushed by an elite Demonic Cult strike force. Baekhwi disappeared while pursuing the enemy with several warriors, and Baeksang later learned that the Great Snow Fiend had killed him and Venerable Wusang. Baeksang further claims that allied forces deliberately withheld support, killed the messenger, pursued the retreating enemy for military credit, and later erased the messenger's corpse and the evidence implicating the Zhongnan Sect. His old hatred of the Central Plains and Han Chinese erupts against Jin in the underground prison, where he attacks and declares that Jin's people taught him what he had to do.

## Continuity

- Baekhwi was killed without leaving a corpse during the Great Snow Mountain battle; Baeksang identifies the Great Snow Fiend as the killer.
- Venerable Wusang, former Sect Leader of the Zhongnan Sect and master of the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord, died while fighting with a recurring Internal Injury, ultimately killed by the Great Snow Fiend.
- Baeksang claims that the Zhongnan Sect and other Western Army allies deliberately ignored his request for support, killed the messenger, pursued retreating Demonic Cult forces for military credit, and later removed the messenger's corpse and other evidence.
- Baeksang's hatred of the Central Plains and Han Chinese stems from his belief that Nanman allies were betrayed after fighting in the Great Faction War.
- Baeksang attacked Jin in the underground prison with tangible internal energy, cutting Jin's forehead, but Jin remained still and confronted him.

### Chapter 663

# Chapter 663

## Plot

Jin Taekyung acknowledges the legitimacy of Baeksang's anger while condemning the innocent Nanman victims created by Baeksang's alliance with Dark Heaven. He compares Baeksang to the Head Elder, arguing that revenge has trapped them both on a path of guilt and destruction, and demands to know how much more blood Baeksang intends to spill.

Baeksang leaves convinced that Jin cannot escape his execution, but Jin's words and memories of his dead child Hwi cause him to waver for the first time in decades. Jin refuses to accept his death and discovers Taishan above the broken ceiling, hungry and imprisoned for eating too much.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains bound in the underground prison with sealed internal energy and heavy iron balls, with his public execution scheduled in two days.
- Jin intends to escape and stop Baeksang rather than submit to the scheduled execution.
- Baeksang believes Jin's compassion for the Han Chinese under his command caused him to surrender instead of fleeing.
- Baeksang's only child Hwi was killed during the Great Snow Mountain battle, and the memory continues to drive his grief and revenge.
- Baeksang accepted an offer from an unidentified beautiful woman decades ago in pursuit of revenge and believes he has crossed a river from which he cannot return.
- Jin identifies the Miao village massacre, the Thousand-Year Spiders' escape from the Poisonblood Grounds, and the Western Yao Estate incident as a connected plot that has killed more than three hundred people.
- Baeksang leaves the prison while privately wavering over the path of revenge and the consequences of completing it.
- Taishan is imprisoned above Jin's cell after being punished for eating too much and discovers the broken ceiling.

# Chapter 664

## Plot

Jin Taekyung discovers that the damaged underground prison connects his cell with Taishan's cell one floor above. Taishan reports that Namho and Sama Pyo are being held separately and are safe, while Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin were captured but kept elsewhere. Jin receives a new linked Quest, Escape from Namshank, and resolves to escape before his execution.

Sudal, the Deputy Stronghold Lord of the Water Dragon Stronghold, abandons his post near Yunnan and leads three swift ships toward Guizhou to raid a target. They find a crewless Yangtze River Channel League ship occupied only by the Blood Monk, who asks whether it travels to Nanman.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains imprisoned in the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison with sealed internal energy and iron balls; his execution is scheduled for noon in two days.
- Taishan is imprisoned in the cell above Jin and can communicate through the broken ceiling; he was confined after breaking both wrists of a Nanman attendant who underfed him.
- Namho and Sama Pyo are detained in separate locations and are reportedly safe, while Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin have been captured and are being held elsewhere.
- Jin suspects the Blood Monk may serve the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and may threaten the reconnaissance squad during its movement.
- Sudal is leading three swift ships toward Guizhou, where the Blood Monk has taken control of a crewless ship and appears interested in traveling to Nanman.
- Jin's immediate objective is to escape the underground prison before the execution.

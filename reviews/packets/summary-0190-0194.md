# Chapters 185–189

## Plot

Cheongpung is recognized as Mae Jonghak’s Disciple and Baek Museong’s Martial Uncle, but because he never underwent Huashan’s initiation ceremony, he is technically outside the sect. He refuses the Heavenly Sword True Person’s order to return to Huashan and chooses to remain with Jin Taekyung.

Taekyung reveals that the Fire King entrusted him with the Flame Divine Palm manual and the Unnamed Sword forged from Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron. Jin Wikyung orders Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung to keep the items and their connection to the Fire King secret. He also halts the interrogation of three surviving Dark Heaven remnants, preserving them as evidence while suspecting Shanxi was only the beginning of Dark Heaven’s plans.

At the Jin Family’s grand New Year’s banquet, Jin Wikyung publicly reaffirms the family’s place in Shanxi and accepts the Mount Heng Sword Sect as a vassal after Lee Seowol and First Elder Cheol Mubaek swear loyalty. He also wins support and gifts from former neutrals. Chulwoo falls intensely in love with Seowol and challenges Taekyung after seeing them interact. Chulwoo defeats numerous challengers, but Taekyung accepts the System Quest *There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much* and faces him.

Chulwoo’s incomplete Fist Qi cuts Taekyung’s spear down to a short rod. Taekyung spends his remaining stat points—twenty on Strength and fifty on Agility—and gains the advantage, ending the block by stabbing the back of Chulwoo’s neck while perched on his shoulder. The duel’s outcome remains unresolved.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang, the Fire King, entrusted Taekyung with the Flame Divine Palm manual and the Unnamed Sword forged from Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.
- Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung must keep the Fire King’s items and related information completely secret.
- Three Dark Heaven remnants remain alive under Wipeng’s custody; their restrictions prevented useful interrogation. Jin Wikyung suspects Dark Heaven’s Shanxi attack was only an opening move.
- Lee Seowol is eighteen and remains Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect, now a vassal of the Jin Family. Cheol Mubaek is its First Elder.
- Chulwoo is twenty-five, Level 95, in love with Seowol, and capable of manifesting incomplete Fist Qi through his gauntlets.
- Choo Dohwan of the Iron Blood Sect is Level 65 and known as the Iron Fist; Chulwoo defeated him and at least fourteen other challengers.
- Taekyung accepted the quest *There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much*; refusing it would grant the System Title “Weak Male.”
- Taekyung has unlocked Sound Transmission but cannot yet use it reliably without practice.
- The identities of the figures behind the split human curtain at the Grand Training Ground remain unknown.
- Open hooks include Dark Heaven’s objective, the three remnants’ identities, Jeok Cheongang’s intentions toward Taekyung, completion of Taekyung’s commissioned weapon, the Treasured Jade’s whereabouts, consequences of Woo Hwangtae’s conflict, and the final result of the Taekyung–Chulwoo duel.

# Chapter 190

### Chapter 191

# Chapter 191

## Plot

After Chulwoo is carried away unconscious, Jin Taekyung is declared the final winner of the Jin Family of Taiyuan Duel Tournament Sudden Event and receives Fame, EXP, a Level Up, and 10 bonus points. The celebration becomes chaotic until an elderly master disperses the crowd with a powerful lion's roar. The old man is Song Il, the Roaring Fury Swordsman and an Elder of the Zhongnan Sect, who arrived with Gong Ilhyuk, Hyuk Mujin, and two unidentified men. Song Il claims Gong Ilhyuk was badly injured by Cheongpung at Prince Shangshan's residence and demands compensation for mocking the Zhongnan Sect and injuring its Disciple. He humiliates Baek Museong, recognizes Cheongpung as the Sword Saint's heir, threatens to formally complain to Huashan, and orders the Jin Family to seal its gates until Zhongnan sends someone. Jin Wikyung rejects the order, and Taekyung openly curses the unfair treatment; Wikyung admits he feels the same. Song Il draws a sword covered in exceptionally powerful Sword Energy, but an unidentified clear voice warns that killing Taekyung will result in Song Il's death, causing him to freeze.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung won the Jin Family of Taiyuan Duel Tournament Sudden Event and received Fame, EXP, a Level Up, and 10 bonus points.
- Song Il, the Roaring Fury Swordsman, is an Elder of the Zhongnan Sect, the senior brother of Sect Leader Gong Iljung, and of the same seniority as Huashan's Sect Leader, the Heavenly Sword True Person.
- Song Il claims Gong Ilhyuk was injured by Cheongpung and demands redress for the injury and the alleged insult to Zhongnan.
- Song Il ordered the Jin Family of Taiyuan to seal its gates until Zhongnan sends someone, but Jin Wikyung rejected the order.
- An unidentified person with a clear, ringing voice warned Song Il that killing Taekyung would result in Song Il's death.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 연쇄고백마 as “Serial Confession Man,” Taekyung's mocking description of Chulwoo.
- Render 사자후 as “lion's roar” for the crowd-shattering cry.
- Render 봉문 as “seal its gates.”
- Preserve Taekyung and Jin Wikyung's blunt profanity as a deliberate rejection of Song Il's abuse of authority.

# Chapter 192

## Plot

Song Il loses control after Jin Taekyung and Jin Wikyung reject his domineering conduct, attacks Taekyung with the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords, and is stopped when the mysterious speaker reveals himself as Jeok Cheongang, the Fire King.

Jeok Cheongang uses Six-Harmonies Voice Transmission to threaten Song Il, catches his wrist after Taekyung narrowly evades the attack, publicly humiliates Song with slaps and insults, and reveals that he entrusted the Fire Gate Clan's sacred treasure to Taekyung.

## Continuity

- The unidentified voice that stopped Song Il is Jeok Cheongang, who has reached the Supreme Peak realm and can use Six-Harmonies Voice Transmission.
- Jeok Cheongang and Song Il met when Song was barely twenty; Jeok once saved him at his Master's request, and Song later accused Jeok of stealing his battle credit.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly intervened when Song Il attacked Taekyung, then humiliated Song for threatening commoners and ordering the Jin Family to seal its gates.
- Song Il's Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords had reached nine-tenths mastery; Taekyung narrowly evaded the attack with torn clothing and minor bleeding.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly stated that he had entrusted the Fire Gate Clan's sacred treasure to Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 육합전성 as “Six-Harmonies Voice Transmission” and 천하삼십육검 as “Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords.”

### Chapter 193

# Chapter 193

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang reveals that the Unnamed Sword is the Fire Gate Clan’s sacred treasure and publicly confirms that he entrusted it to Jin Taekyung. Taekyung realizes that Jopil stole the sword from Jeok Cheongang and that Cheongpung’s earlier speculation about Jeok taking him as a Disciple may not have been nonsense.

After forcing Song Il to swear that he will not pursue the Jin Family over the incident, Jeok Cheongang follows the departing Zhongnan group. Song Il incapacitates Gong Ilhyuk and the other two members of the Three Hands in anger, but Jeok breaks Song’s wrist, strikes him with the Flame Divine Palm, and threatens to destroy the Zhongnan Sect and Mount Zhongnan if Taekyung or the Jin Family is harmed. Song Il abandons his threat and orders the Three Hands to return to Zhongnan.

## Continuity

- The Unnamed Sword is the Fire Gate Clan’s sacred treasure, and Jeok Cheongang entrusted it to Jin Taekyung.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly threatens to destroy the Zhongnan Sect and Mount Zhongnan if Song Il or the Zhongnan Sect harms Jin Taekyung or the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- Song Il leaves the Jin Family humiliated and injured, then orders the Three Hands of Zhongnan to return to Zhongnan after Jeok’s warning.
- Gong Ilhyuk incited Song Il to confront the Jin Family; Song Il later renders Gong Ilhyuk and the other two members of the Three Hands unconscious.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 열화문의 신물 as “Fire Gate Clan’s sacred treasure”; this refers to the Unnamed Sword.
- Render 종남산 as “Mount Zhongnan.”
- Retain “Tongue King” for 혀왕, Taekyung’s joking nickname for Jeok Cheongang’s verbal intimidation.
### Chapter 194

# Chapter 194

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang returns after an unusually long absence and trades insults with Jin Wikyung, initially mistaking Taekyung’s eldest brother for his father before learning that Wikyung is thirty-six and unmarried. The Fire King becomes the center of attention at the Jin Family banquet, accepts a Poison-Averting Ring and bank drafts from the owner of the Yangcheon Merchant Association in exchange for a vague promise to help with the Hebei Peng Family, then reveals that his past with the Thunderbolt Saber King consisted of one drink followed by a fight rather than friendship. After accepting numerous gifts from supplicants, Jeok offers all the accumulated treasures, elixirs, and bank drafts to Taekyung before leaving to rest, leaving Taekyung wondering whether the offer is connected to becoming his Disciple.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung states that he is thirty-six years old and unmarried.
- Jeok Cheongang's past with the Thunderbolt Saber King was one drinking encounter followed by a fight; Jeok denies friendship and says the later story of mutual recognition was fabricated by the Hebei Peng Family.
- At the Jin Family banquet, Jeok Cheongang accepts the gifts and valuables offered by visitors and then offers the entire collection to Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 피독지환 as “Poison-Averting Ring,” 철혈도 as “Iron Blood Saber,” 양천상회 as “Yangcheon Merchant Association,” and 마이클 천강 as “Michael Cheongang.”
- Render 우화왕 좌화왕 as “Right Fire King, Left Fire King,” preserving the 좌우왕 wordplay in a footnote.
- Retain Jeok Cheongang’s gruff, shameless, and teasing voice when he accepts gifts and dismisses the merchants’ requests.

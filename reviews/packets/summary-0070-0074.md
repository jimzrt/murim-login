# Chapters 65–69

## Plot

Jin Mukyung is astonished that Jin Taekyung has become a First Rate martial artist approaching the Peak realm. When Mukyung uses internal energy, he defeats Taekyung and destroys the pavilion. Taekyung and the badly injured Hyuk Mujin are taken to the Medicine King Hall, while the Jin Family mistakes the incident for an assassin’s attack and begins a pursuit for the Head Elder’s possible hidden disciple. Mujin receives public credit for protecting Taekyung, leading to speculation that he may become the next Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion.

Mukyung reunites with Jin Wikyung after three years but remains detached and focused on training. Wikyung sends Wipeng and thirty elites south under the cover of pursuing the nonexistent assassin, while secretly preparing to summon every sect in Shanxi Province on New Year’s Day and potentially seek the Alliance Leader position. Wikyung finds no mention of Dark Heaven in the family records. Gong Yacheong continues recovering and will oversee the rebuilt Sakju Branch, with Socheon and Soyul planning to join him in six months.

While Taekyung’s residence is rebuilt, Wikyung places him and Mukyung together temporarily. Mukyung imposes rules of polite speech, silence, and obedience regarding the training hall. After Mukyung harshly beats Taekyung with his scabbard, Taekyung sincerely asks to become stronger. Mukyung agrees to rebuild his martial arts from the fundamentals through practical, real-combat training.

The System grants Taekyung the Return achievement and Returnee title, activating Login and Logout and increasing all stats by ten. It then creates the Peak-Grade Quest “[Trial? Training?],” requiring Mukyung’s recognition before the remaining cohabitation period ends. Logout is restricted, and Mukyung orders Taekyung to bring his spear for training.

- Jin Wikyung plans a New Year’s Day summons for all Shanxi sects and may pursue the Alliance Leader position; whether the sects will attend and whether he will become Alliance Leader remain unresolved.
- Wipeng is traveling south with thirty Jin Family elites under the pretext of pursuing the assassin.

# Chapter 70

## Plot

Jin Taekyung endures five spars with Jin Mukyung over two hours and is knocked unconscious five times, yet lasts two hundred exchanges in the final spar. Mukyung is astonished by Taekyung's spear instincts, which resemble those of a seasoned wandering martial artist, and is unable to reconcile them with Taekyung's short training period. # Chapter 71

# Chapter 73

### Chapter 74

# Chapter 74

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung meet Jin Wikyung in his newly organized office. Exhausted from managing the Jin Family's immense workload, Wikyung embraces both brothers despite Mukyung's resistance and jokingly considers putting Taekyung to work.

Jin Wikyung orders Taekyung and Mukyung to visit the Mount Heng Sword Sect at the personal request of its new Sect Leader, Lee Seowol. Taekyung receives the forcibly created Quest [Yesterday's Enemy, Today's Ally], which requires him to deliver an invitation to the Jin Family for the coming New Year's Day; its Grade is First Rate, its Reward is unknown, and it has no failure penalty.

Lee Seowol is revealed to be Lee Cheonbaek's third child and the younger sister of the deceased Young Sect Leader and Lee Seogeun. Mukyung agrees to go after learning that Seowol may reveal some of the Mount Heng Sword Sect's Peak martial arts. They depart immediately for Eung-hyeon in a four-horse carriage, with Hyuk Mujin driving after Mukyung dismissed the attendants. Taekyung pretends to circulate his qi while preparing to log out, and the System displays another logout prompt.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung's office has been restored to an orderly state, but he remains severely overworked and wants at least one day of rest.
- The new Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect is Lee Seowol, a woman and Lee Cheonbaek's third child; she is the younger sister of the deceased Young Sect Leader and Lee Seogeun.
- Lee Seowol personally requested that Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung be sent to the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- Taekyung has been given the forced Quest [Yesterday's Enemy, Today's Ally]: invite the Mount Heng Sword Sect to the Jin Family of Taiyuan for the coming New Year's Day. The mission remains incomplete, the Reward is unknown, and Failure is listed as None.
- Jin Mukyung accepted the journey after learning that the Mount Heng Sword Sect would consider revealing some of its Peak martial arts to him.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin have departed for Eung-hyeon, where the Mount Heng Sword Sect is located; the journey is expected to take approximately three days.
- Hyuk Mujin remained with the group because he obeys Taekyung as his squad leader. The regular attendants and coachman were dismissed before departure.
- Taekyung intends to log out and wants to test his newly refined martial arts in a Gate after returning.

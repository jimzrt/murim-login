# Seokchil (석칠)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 310
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Middle-aged porter for the Seongun Escort Bureau with nearly twenty years of experience.
- **Personality:** Gruff, practical, responsible toward his family, and unexpectedly caring toward the inexperienced Cheongpung.
- **Voice:** Blunt, colloquial, and teasing; gives practical advice in a gruff tone.
- **Relationships:** Senior colleague and informal mentor to Cheongpung; says he has five mouths to feed.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 130

# Im Yeongjun (임영준)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 281
- **Aliases:** the “tail”
- **Role:** Level 95 A-rank Black Hunter; one of the three men who attacked Im Kkeokjeong and the leader of the hidden countryside base containing twenty-eight Black Hunters; was tortured by Jin Taekyung for information, survived through repeated potion use, and was removed alive but incapacitated with the other surviving Black Hunters
- **Personality:** Ruthless, contemptuous, self-assured, and confident in the protection provided by his organization
- **Voice:** Threatening, mocking, and condescending toward opponents he considers inexperienced
- **Relationships:** Works with the Black Hunters operating from the countryside mansion; recognizes Jin Taekyung as the Hunter retaliating for the attack on Im Kkeokjeong
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 276

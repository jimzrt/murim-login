# Kim Hwajong (김화종)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 663
- **Aliases:** Hwajong (화종); Butler Kim
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** The deceased old butler and personal escort who served Choi Minwoo's family, was Choi's grandfather, and sacrificed his life to restrain Behemoth before Jin Taekyung killed it.
- **Personality:** Loyal, vigilant, and uncompromising toward perceived threats to Choi Minwoo, with enduring care shown in the farewell message he left him.
- **Voice:** Formal and deferential toward Choi Minwoo, cold and openly hostile toward Song Cheonwoo; his written farewell is affectionate and encouraging.
- **Relationships:** Kim Hwajong was Choi Minwoo's grandfather and butler, formerly close to Song Cheonwoo until loyalty and ambition divided them; Choi mourns him at his grave.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 100 and 576 (merged former Hwa-jong profile)

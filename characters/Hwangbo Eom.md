# Hwangbo Eom (황보엄)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 319
- **Aliases:** Taeeul Merciless Sword
- **Role:** Supreme Peak master of the Zhongnan Sect known as the Taeeul Merciless Sword and its Second Martial Uncle, Hwangbo Eom is at Tengwang Pavilion where he has rejected compromise after Ju Hwaran's shipment proved to be Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng rather than the requested Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng.
- **Personality:** Ruthless, severe, proud, and deeply invested in restoring Zhongnan's standing.
- **Voice:** Calmly courteous when offering tea, then cold, commanding, and cutting when reprimanding others.
- **Relationships:** Song Il is his only Senior Brother, the current Sect Leader is his martial brother, and Hyuk Sopyung is his junior.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 318

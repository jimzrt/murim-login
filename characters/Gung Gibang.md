# Gung Gibang (궁기방)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 337
- **Aliases:** Successor Beggar, Beggar Prince, pure-blooded beggar, ultimate beggar
- **Role:** Beggars' Sect Successor Beggar and unique eight-knot disciple, with authority to order the Xi'an branch and mobilize Beggars' Sect support for Taekyung's investigation.
- **Personality:** Vulgar, aggressive, and quick-tempered.
- **Voice:** Blunt, profane, and vividly threatening.
- **Relationships:** Rival finalist alongside Baek Woo and Zhuge Gyun; trades insults with Taekyung and is helping investigate Tang Taesang’s murder through Beggars’ Sect intelligence.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 248

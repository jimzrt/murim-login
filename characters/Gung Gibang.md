# Gung Gibang (궁기방)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1191
- **Aliases:** Successor Beggar, Beggar Prince, pure-blooded beggar, ultimate beggar
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** Gung Gibang is the Beggars' Sect Successor Beggar and a unique eight-knot disciple.
- **Personality:** Vulgar, aggressive, and quick-tempered, but grieves deeply for fellow Beggars’ Sect disciples and defends those who risk their lives for others.
- **Voice:** Blunt, profane, and vividly threatening.
- **Relationships:** Gung Gibang is a rival finalist alongside Baek Woo and Zhuge Gyun, and shares blunt, teasing friendships with Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin.
- **Appearance (ch 248–851):** Gung Gibang is a male beggar with matted, greasy, unkempt hair and a grimy, dirt-smeared, ugly face. He has yellowed teeth. Tags: male, beggar, matted greasy hair, grimy face, dirty, ugly face, yellowed teeth
- **Appearance (from ch 852):** Gung Gibang is a male beggar who has returned to youth (banro-hwandong) and looks about a hundred years younger. He has matted, greasy, unkempt hair, a grimy, dirt-smeared, ugly face and yellowed teeth. Tags: male, beggar, rejuvenated, much younger, matted greasy hair, grimy face, ugly face, yellowed teeth
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 248

# Senior Colonel Wang (왕 상교)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 391
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Senior officer commanding the diverted operation under Senior General Liao's authority.
- **Personality:** Arrogant, contemptuous, coercive, and confident in his tactical authority.
- **Voice:** Condescending and authoritarian.
- **Relationships:** Operates under Senior General Liao's command; mocks Shao Shen and is regarded by a subordinate as connected to the Princelings and as Liao's lapdog.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 391

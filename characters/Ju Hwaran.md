# Ju Hwaran (주화란)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1191
- **Aliases:** Hwaran
- **Gender:** female
- **Role:** Ju Hwaran is a Level 88 Young Bureau Head, leader of the Yongbong Escort Bureau, an experienced Nanman guide, and an active member of the Fire Dragon Pavilion.
- **Personality:** Intelligent, capable, responsible, and filial; remains controlled under pressure but has grown more assertive and openly impatient after the hardships she has endured.
- **Voice:** Clear, polite, restrained, and determined.
- **Relationships:** Escort King Ju Gongsan was her paternal grandfather, Ju Hogun is her father, Heo Jun was her uncle, Sama Pyo was her former fiancé in a political engagement she accepted for her father's sake, Song Ilseom is her direct escort who accompanied her previous journey to Yeongin, and Jin Taekyung is a trusted ally; the Yongbong Escort Bureau has longstanding ties with Yeongin’s inhabitants.
- **Appearance (ch 309–921):** Ju Hwaran is an exceptionally beautiful young woman with a cold, elegant face, beautiful brush-drawn eyebrows, large black eyes, and a bare face without makeup. She wears a face veil and a plain, single-colored martial outfit, and carries a flexible sword (yeongeom) in a scabbard at her waist. - **Appearance (from ch 922):** Ju Hwaran appears rejuvenated, with a youthful look restored, after returning to youth. She has an exceptionally beautiful, cold face, brush-drawn eyebrows, large black eyes that curve like half-moons when smiling, and a bare face without makeup. She wears a face veil and a plain, single-colored martial outfit, and carries a flexible sword in a scabbard at her waist.

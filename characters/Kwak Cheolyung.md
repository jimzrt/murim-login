# Kwak Cheolyung (곽철융)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 245
- **Aliases:** Dogon
- **Role:** Middle-aged Peak martial artist and one of the three legendary Dogons, known for gambling based on meticulous research and calculation.
- **Personality:** Relaxed, supremely confident, analytical, and secretive about his wagers.
- **Voice:** Calm, self-assured, lightly teasing, and casually authoritative.
- **Relationships:** Places a large wager on Cheol Soo taking first place in the Star-Array Grand Banquet preliminaries and maintains a confidential betting relationship with a bald underworld bookmaker.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 245

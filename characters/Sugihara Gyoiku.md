# Sugihara Gyoiku (스기하라 교이쿠)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 745
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Sugihara Gyoiku is a longtime Tokyo cargo-shipping laborer and Tokyo native.
- **Personality:** He is practical, unpretentious, and takes pride in being a necessary worker.
- **Voice:** He speaks casually and bluntly, with occasional rough Japanese exclamations.
- **Relationships:** He works alongside fellow cargo laborers at Tokyo Bay.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 745

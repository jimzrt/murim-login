# Tang Horyong (당호룡)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 375
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Pavilion Master of the Poison Dragon Pavilion and one of the few remaining direct-line members of the Sichuan Tang Clan
- **Personality:** Responsible and candid; burdened and anxious about leading the devastated clan without Tang Sadok
- **Voice:** Polite and formal, with frank expressions of concern
- **Relationships:** Tang Sadok's younger cousin; one of the few remaining direct-line members of the Sichuan Tang Clan
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 374

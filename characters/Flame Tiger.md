# Flame Tiger (염호)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 261
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Dark Heaven operative known as the Yin Ghost's longtime partner; stocky old man with a distinctive red beard; one half of the Yin-Yang Twin Freaks, a pair of former demonic fiends who served the Demonic Cult during the Great Faction War; attacks Shaolin and severely wounds Hongcheon in a Supreme Peak battle.
- **Personality:** Rough, violent, and casually contemptuous of Han Su's victims.
- **Voice:** Rough, blunt, and familiar.
- **Relationships:** Longtime friend of Han Su; reports that matters above are active and joins him in proceeding toward Shaolin.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 259

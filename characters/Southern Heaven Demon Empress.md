# Southern Heaven Demon Empress (남천마후)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1025
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** female
- **Role:** The Southern Heaven Demon Empress was Honglan, the creator of the rift behind the Inner Palace, and was killed after the rift closed.
- **Personality:** Playful, cruel, confident, and casually dismissive of mass death and the suffering of others.
- **Voice:** Light, taunting, amused, and delighted even when discussing murder or imminent catastrophe.
- **Relationships:** She commands and advises Baeksang, treats Jin Taekyung and Yayul Cheok as expendable to the grand plan, and keeps a masked hunting dog whom she trained carefully.
- **Appearance (ch 482–702):** Southern Heaven Demon Empress is a strikingly, bewitchingly beautiful woman with a body described as beautiful and slender wrists and arms. She has upturned, cat-like eyes and lustrous, abundant hair. She wears a gungjang (court dress). Tags: female, strikingly beautiful, painting-like beauty, bewitching charm, cat-like eyes, upturned eyes, lustrous abundant hair, slender arms, beautiful body, gungjang court dress
- **Appearance (from ch 703):** Southern Heaven Demon Empress, after the curse of returned aging, is an aged crone who has lost her once world-beautiful appearance. She has cat-like, upturned eyes with a hollow gaze, only one arm (the other blown off entirely), and flesh torn from her side as if bitten off. She wears a gungjang (court dress). Tags: female, aged crone, old woman, one-armed, torn flank, cat-like eyes, hollow gaze, gungjang court dress
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 688

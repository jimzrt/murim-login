# Tang Jinhu (당진후)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 354
- **Aliases:** Heaven-Poison Wanderer
- **Role:** Retired Supreme Peak master of the Sichuan Tang Clan, Head Elder, and head of the Council of Elders.
- **Personality:** Experienced, fierce, decisive, and fiercely protective of his Tang Clan kin.
- **Voice:** Blunt and battle-commanding, with furious protective outbursts.
- **Relationships:** A direct descendant of the Sichuan Tang Clan, he leads its Council of Elders and recognizes First Fiend as an old enemy.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 354

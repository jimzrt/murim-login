# Heuk Jongpil (흑종필)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 241
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Former leader of Black Mountain Stronghold; an illiterate former butcher, natural-born plunderer, and Peak master of axe techniques who commanded more than three hundred bandits before an unidentified hidden master killed him and left him headless.
- **Personality:** Greedy, opportunistic, boastful, and quick to panic when confronted by overwhelming danger.
- **Voice:** Gruff, blunt, profane, and commanding toward his underlings.
- **Relationships:** Commands Black Mountain Stronghold and pays four-tenths of its monthly profits to the Green Forest Alliance in exchange for support and information.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 240

# Ae-hyang (애향)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 377
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Favorite concubine of the Sichuan Governor who covertly manipulates him on behalf of an unidentified superior.
- **Personality:** Seductive, calculating, flattering, and manipulative.
- **Voice:** Coquettish, affectionate, and persuasive, with concealed menace.
- **Relationships:** Favorite concubine and trusted confidante of the Sichuan Governor; appears to serve an unidentified superior.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 377

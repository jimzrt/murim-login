# Grand Sorceress (대술사)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1175
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** female
- **Role:** The veiled woman who leads the white-robed sorcerers in service of the Lord of Heaven; a supreme mage, so she is also called a Grand Mage (대마도사).
- **Personality:** Devoted and reverent toward her master; she welcomes his awakening with intense joy.
- **Voice:** Highly deferential and formal, using self-abasing language when addressing her master.
- **Relationships:** A loyal servant of the Lord of Heaven, whom she awaits and obeys.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 1041

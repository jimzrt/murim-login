# Yao Wei (야오위)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 379
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** A former A-rank Hunter of the Public Security Armed Forces Department who became a Dullahan after being reanimated on the battlefield.
- **Personality:** Competitive, playful, friendly, and prone to teasing complaints toward Shao Shen.
- **Voice:** Uses familiar, joking complaints with his friend Shao Shen.
- **Relationships:** Shao Shen was his close friend, colleague, and regular sparring partner.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 379

# Yao Wei (야오위)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 379
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** A-rank Hunter of the Public Security Armed Forces who is killed and reanimated as a Dullahan.
- **Personality:** Friendly and teasing toward Shao Shen before his death.
- **Voice:** Playful, complaining banter in Shao Shen's memories of their sparring.
- **Relationships:** Friend, comrade, and recurring sparring partner of Shao Shen.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 379

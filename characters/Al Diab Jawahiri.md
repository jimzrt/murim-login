# Al Diab Jawahiri (알 디아브 자와히리)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 610
- **Aliases:** Al Diab
- **Role:** Al-Diab Jawahiri is the elderly leader of Al-Qaeda and a major terrorist ideologue in the Middle East.
- **Personality:** He is calm, manipulative, fanatically committed to religious unity, and convinced that his ideology justifies mass suffering.
- **Voice:** He speaks in a composed, persuasive, and self-righteous manner that collapses into panic when threatened.
- **Relationships:** He commands Al-Qaeda and opposes the masked young man who attacked his hideout.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 610

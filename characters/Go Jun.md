# Go Jun (고준)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 795
- **Aliases:** Team Leader Seok
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** Go Jun was Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master, Lee Jungryong's Disciple and former Head of Security, the de facto successor to Lee's Ares legacy, and a mutated monster who was killed by Jin Taekyung in Area A.
- **Personality:** Disciplined and controlled under ordinary pressure, fiercely loyal to Lee Jungryong, but consumed by humiliation and rage when Ares Guild's authority is challenged.
- **Voice:** Dry, controlled, and formal with subordinates; deferential when addressing his Master.
- **Relationships:** Go Jun inherited Lee Jungryong's Ares legacy after becoming his Disciple and regarded Jin Taekyung and Choi Minwoo as enemies before his death.
- **Appearance (ch 285–593):** Go Jun is a man who looks to be in his 30s, with a stiff, expressionless, hard-looking face and a square, angular jaw that is extremely hard. His red eyes are sunken and dark, giving him a gloomy look. He wears the faintly red-glowing Fire Dragon Armor (Hwaryongap), an old worn necklace around his neck, and emits a red aura. Tags: male, 30s look, expressionless, stiff face, square jaw, sunken red eyes, gloomy, red aura, faint red-glowing armor, Fire Dragon Armor, worn necklace
- **Appearance (from ch 594):** Go Jun is now a grotesque hybrid monster, mutated after absorbing multiple S-grade Magic Gems. His body has Troll-like regeneration, so flesh and bone regrow quickly at severed stumps. He keeps the stiff face with square, angular jaw, the sunken red eyes, the red aura, the faintly red-glowing Fire Dragon Armor and the old worn necklace. Tags: male, grotesque hybrid monster, mutated, Troll-like regeneration, regrowing flesh and bone, square jaw, sunken red eyes, red aura, faint red-glowing armor, worn necklace
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 285

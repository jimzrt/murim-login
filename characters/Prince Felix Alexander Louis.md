# Prince Felix Alexander Louis (필릭스 알렉산더 루이)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 385
- **Aliases:** Prince Felix, His Royal Highness Prince Felix
- **Role:** British prince, third in line to the throne, Duke of Cambridge, Earl of Strathearn, Baron Carrickfergus, Knight of the Garter, and Knight of the Thistle
- **Personality:** Arrogant, condescending, ceremonially minded, and hypocritically self-righteous
- **Voice:** Formal, imperious, and polished
- **Relationships:** William serves as his formal attendant; he treats Jin Taekyung as a commoner while claiming to oppose class divisions.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 385

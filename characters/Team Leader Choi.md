# Team Leader Choi

- **Safe through:** Chapter 768
- **Aliases:** Choi Minwoo (최민우)
- **Role:** Team Leader Choi is Jin Taekyung's meticulous intelligence and operations lead who tried to delay the UN's World Hunter Federation decision to buy time because war was imminent.
- **Personality:** Calm, pragmatic, meticulous, and emotionally steady under pressure.
- **Voice:** Measured, professional, and reassuring without minimizing responsibility.
- **Relationships:** A trusted ally and operational adviser to Jin Taekyung.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 42–54

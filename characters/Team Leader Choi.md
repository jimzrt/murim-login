# Team Leader Choi (최 팀장)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1175
- **Aliases:** Choi Minwoo (최민우)
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** Team Leader Choi is Jin Taekyung's meticulous intelligence and operations lead, a trusted ally and natural leader capable of guiding the reestablished World Hunter Federation.
- **Personality:** Calm, pragmatic, meticulous, and resolute under pressure.
- **Voice:** He speaks in measured, concise declaratives, using calm, resolute phrasing to rally others without overstating the danger.
- **Relationships:** A trusted ally and operational adviser to Jin Taekyung, whom he regards as a model of taking responsibility in a crisis; he is the maternal grandson of Cheon Taemin.
- **Appearance (ch 41–829):** Team Leader Choi is a man in his 20s with a slender build that hides great muscle strength. He has strikingly handsome, celebrity-level looks. He wears a thin tailored suit over a luxury designer shirt, a polished, photo-shoot style. Tags: male, 20s, slender, hidden muscle, very handsome, thin tailored suit, casual suit, designer shirt
- **Appearance (from ch 830):** Team Leader Choi is a man in his 20s with a slender build, in a later timeline after battle injuries. His face is covered in scars and he has lost one arm. He still wears a thin suit over a luxury designer shirt. Tags: male, 20s, slender, face covered in scars, one arm missing, thin suit, designer shirt
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 42–54

# Jonghun (종훈)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 226
- **Aliases:** Team Leader 1
- **Role:** A-rank Hunter and Team 1 Leader of the Star Guild under CEO Won Myunghoon; the only member of his team to return alive after encountering the Named Monster in The Black Wyvern’s Nest, he was afflicted by Fear and fled without his team; Won now considers him unusable as a Hunter and ordered him bound as bait because Jonghun knows Won's secrets and personally acquired Yeti's Necklace; while still mentally shaken after the raid, he confessed everything to the investigators.
- **Personality:** Obedient and deferential toward Won Myunghoon; casually dismissive of the Peace Guild's weaker members
- **Voice:** Formal and deferential toward his CEO
- **Relationships:** Was taken in and raised by Won Myunghoon when he had nothing; has worked with Won for more than ten years, reports directly to him, and selects the Star Guild members for the joint raid.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 214

# Taishan (태산)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1183
- **Aliases:** Tiger Giant Child
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** Taishan is a giant subordinate of Sama Pyo in the Black Dragon Demon Gate and a member of the Fire Dragon Pavilion.
- **Personality:** Childlike, obedient, food-obsessed, and dim-witted, with intense wariness toward strangers and absolute trust in Sama Pyo; becomes explosively violent when his meat is threatened.
- **Voice:** Clipped, simple, and childlike.
- **Relationships:** He serves Sama Pyo, whom he calls Lord, trusts Jin Taekyung as Pavilion Master, and has grown attached to the Fire Dragon Pavilion members.
- **Appearance (from ch 37):** Taishan is a male figure with a huge, bulky, mountain-like giant frame, roughly 2.7 m tall. He has very large, round, bulging eyes, as big as a calf's. Tags: male, giant, huge bulky build, mountain-sized frame, about 2.7 m tall, big round bulging eyes, calf-like eyes
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 533

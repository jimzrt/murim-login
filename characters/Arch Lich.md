# Arch Lich (아크 리치)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 381
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Superior undead being who sent three incomplete Liches to kill humans and create more undead.
- **Personality:** Not established in Chapter 381.
- **Voice:** Not established in Chapter 381.
- **Relationships:** The three former necromancers fear its disappointment and the loss of the power it granted them; they previously encountered it as a ferryman across the sea of death.

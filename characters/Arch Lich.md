# Arch Lich (아크 리치)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 412
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** The Arch Lich is an undead ruler who commands a monster army, empowers elite retainers with its own mana, and observes the battlefield through Familiars.
- **Personality:** Calculating, arrogant, vindictive, and wary of humans because of its painful defeat in the distant past.
- **Voice:** Cold and contemptuous in thought, with terse telepathic commands and judgmental internal commentary.
- **Relationships:** The Arch Lich empowered Lei Fei as a Death Knight Lord, commands the battlefield's elite undead, and now suspects Jin Taekyung may be the ancient Adversary who defeated it.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 392

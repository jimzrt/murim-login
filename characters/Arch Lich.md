# Arch Lich (아크 리치)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 422
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** The Arch Lich is an apex undead monster and master of dark magic who was resurrected after falling into the River of Death.
- **Personality:** Arrogant, prideful, calculating, and haunted by the human it once feared.
- **Voice:** Archaic, taunting, and increasingly resolute.
- **Relationships:** The Arch Lich once served a king and now regards Jin Taekyung as a possible Adversary, the king's eternal nemesis.
- **Appearance (from ch 381):** Arch Lich has a strikingly distinctive, unforgettable face that stays in memory after a single glance. No other visual traits are described. Tags: male, distinctive face, memorable features
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 392

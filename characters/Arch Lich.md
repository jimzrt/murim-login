# Arch Lich (아크 리치)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 420
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** The Arch Lich is the lord of all undead monsters and the force behind the city's transformation into a Gate.
- **Personality:** Cruel, predatory, and confident, it takes pleasure in human suffering and treats noble souls as material.
- **Voice:** Formal, archaic, and amused, using calm taunts and grand claims of superiority.
- **Relationships:** Master of the undead and the being who corrupted Lei Fei into the Death Knight Lord; seeks to corrupt Jin Taekyung.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 392

# Muhammad Saladir ad-Din (무함마드 살라디르 앗 딘)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 609
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Muhammad Saladir ad-Din is the leader of a massive Islamic terrorist organization with numerous subordinate branches and a rival armed terrorist organization opposing him.
- **Personality:** Ruthless, authoritarian, self-important, and convinced that he is a new prophet and divinely protected; becomes terrified when faced with death.
- **Voice:** Formal and commanding at first, then pleading and fearful under Jin Taekyung's coercion.
- **Relationships:** Muhammad commands numerous terrorist branches, relies on elite assassins and bodyguards, and is forced by Jin Taekyung to order war against a rival organization.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 608

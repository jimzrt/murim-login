# Bow Saint (궁성)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1178
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** female
- **Role:** The Bow Saint is one of the Three Saints, a legendary archer who vanished from the Murim for decades; the source first marks her as a woman in Chapter 921.
- **Personality:** Not established.
- **Voice:** Not established.
- **Relationships:** Named with the Sword Saint and the Slaughter Saint as the Three Saints; the Martial God left her a letter.
- **Appearance (from ch 308):** Bow Saint is a woman with an imposing, giant-like presence, pale transparent skin, and fine, silky lustrous hair that flutters in the wind. Her eyes curve like half-moons with deep-set pupils. Her fingers are steel-hard and calloused, and she wields two curved sabers (gokdo) that join into a giant bow. Tags: female, imposing build, pale skin, silky hair, half-moon eyes, deep-set pupils, calloused hands, two curved sabers, giant bow
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 337

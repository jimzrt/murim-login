# Bow Saint (궁성)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1178
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** female
- **Role:** The Bow Saint is one of the Three Saints, a legendary archer who vanished from the Murim for decades; the source first marks her as a woman in Chapter 921.
- **Personality:** Not established.
- **Voice:** Not established.
- **Relationships:** Named with the Sword Saint and the Slaughter Saint as the Three Saints; the Martial God left her a letter.
- **Appearance (from ch 308):** Bow Saint is a giant-like, imposing woman with pale, transparent-looking skin. Fine, silky hair flutters in the wind, and clear, brightly shining eyes curve like half-moons, narrowing and curving like a full moon when she smiles. Her fingers are hard as steel and calloused, and she carries two curved sabers that connect into one giant bow. Tags: woman, giant-like build, pale transparent skin, fine silky hair, half-moon eyes, bright clear eyes, calloused hands, two curved sabers, giant bow
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 337

# Bow Saint (궁성)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1178
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** female
- **Role:** The Bow Saint is one of the Three Saints, a legendary archer who vanished from the Murim for decades; the source first marks her as a woman in Chapter 921.
- **Personality:** Not established.
- **Voice:** Not established.
- **Relationships:** Named with the Sword Saint and the Slaughter Saint as the Three Saints; the Martial God left her a letter.
- **Appearance (from ch 922):** Bow Saint is a giant-like, imposing woman with a youthful body, restored after returning to youth. She has fine, silky, lustrous hair that flutters in the wind, transparent pale skin, and half-moon curved, narrow eyes with deep-set, clear, brightly shining pupils. Her eyes curve like a full moon when she smiles. Her fingers are steel-hard and calloused, and she grips two curved sabers (gokdo) that join into one giant bow. Tags: female, youthful body, giant-like build, silky lustrous hair, pale transparent skin, half-moon narrow eyes, deep-set bright pupils, calloused steel-hard fingers, two curved sabers, giant bow
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 337

# Taecheong Jin-in (태청진인)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1114
- **Aliases:** Perfected One Taecheong
- **Role:** Former leader of the Kunlun Five Immortals and an elder of the Kunlun Sect; he died fighting the Blood Lord after using a Temporary Strength Pill.
- **Personality:** Calm and resolute, willing to sacrifice himself and his fellow elders to protect others.
- **Voice:** Measured and formal, using restrained Daoist courtesies even under threat.
- **Relationships:** He was a Martial Uncle of Kunlun Sect Leader Cheongheoja and led the Kunlun Five Immortals; he helped Jeok Cheongang escape the Blood Lord.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 1114

# General Liao (랴오 상장)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 390
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Commander in chief of the 13th Group Army of the Chengdu Military Region, which includes seven divisions and brigades.
- **Personality:** Ambitious, evasive, and willing to risk an unauthorized operation to prove himself while implicated in military procurement corruption.
- **Voice:** Blustery and formally deferential, becoming nervous when confronted by Jin.
- **Relationships:** Commands the military forces following Jin's Hunters and serves as Jin's allied Chinese counterpart on the western front.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 386

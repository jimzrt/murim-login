# General Liao (랴오 상장)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 386
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** A senior Chinese general and pure-blooded member of the Crown Prince Party who participates in the Sichuan war council.
- **Personality:** Obnoxious, impatient, factional, and willing to accept catastrophic civilian losses for a quick victory.
- **Voice:** Blunt, demanding, and confrontational toward rival faction officials.
- **Relationships:** A Crown Prince Party rival of Wei Fenghu and the Shanghai Gang; proposes a nuclear strike against the Monster Wave.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 386

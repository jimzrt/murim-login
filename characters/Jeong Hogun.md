# Jeong Hogun (정호군)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1182
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** Jeong Hogun is a Thousand Captain of the Embroidered Uniform Guard who guards the Son of Heaven and leads his thousand guards in battle.
- **Personality:** Disciplined and resolute, Jeong Hogun is willing to defy an order to protect the Son of Heaven, then obeys when entrusted to remain at his side.
- **Voice:** Formal and rigid, emphasizing duty to imperial authority.
- **Relationships:** Jeong Hogun serves the Son of Heaven as a trusted guard and commands a thousand Embroidered Uniform Guards.
- **Appearance (from ch 858):** Jeong Hogun is a young man whose face looks about twenty, early adult. He wears splendid brocade imperial robes with armor as leader of the Imperial Guard (Geumuiwi). Tags: male, early twenties look, youthful face, brocade imperial robes, armor, Imperial Guard, Geumuiwi
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 858

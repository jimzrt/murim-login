# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 595
- **Aliases:** Stone-King, Mr. King
- **Role:** A blond foreigner called the Skeleton King who waits with Kim Hwajong's body and guides Jin Taekyung into the morgue.
- **Personality:** Not established beyond his solemn concern for the dead.
- **Voice:** Archaic, formal, and admonishing.
- **Relationships:** Stands with Jin Taekyung's companions and helps Jin say farewell to Kim Hwajong.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

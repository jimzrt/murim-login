# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 740
- **Aliases:** Stone-King, Mr. King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is an undead monster allied with Jin Taekyung who assists him and looks after him during his recovery.
- **Personality:** Sensitive to insults, awkwardly caring, and comic beneath his proud demeanor.
- **Voice:** Formal and self-important, with the recurring self-reference this body.
- **Relationships:** An undead ally and companion of Jin Taekyung.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 750
- **Aliases:** Stone-King, Mr. King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is a monster from the Demon Realm who accompanies Jin Taekyung at the Japanese emergency command meeting.
- **Personality:** Literal, blunt, combative, and quick to mock perceived stupidity.
- **Voice:** Direct, colloquial, and abrasive, with literal questions and provocative insults.
- **Relationships:** He follows Jin Taekyung's account of the failed encirclement and confronts the Japanese Defense Minister during the meeting.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

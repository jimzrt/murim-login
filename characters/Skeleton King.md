# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 425
- **Aliases:** Skeleton Warlord, Bones
- **Role:** The former Skeleton Warlord, revived and transformed into a Lv.160 Skeleton King through Hero’s Soul, who fights beside Jin Taekyung.
- **Personality:** Loyal, self-sacrificing, and wryly humorous.
- **Voice:** Quiet, understated, and familiar.
- **Relationships:** Friend and ally of Jin Taekyung; enemy of the Arch Lich.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 753
- **Aliases:** Stone King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is an undead monster allied with Jin Taekyung and Choi Minwoo who has adapted to the modern world.
- **Personality:** Sarcastic, theatrical, anxious, and increasingly fond of the living world despite his fear of being eaten.
- **Voice:** Exasperated and dramatic, mixing profanity, literal objections, and darkly comic self-preservation.
- **Relationships:** He is an ally of Jin Taekyung and Choi Minwoo, though they recently coerced him into serving as bait for Leviathan.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

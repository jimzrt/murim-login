# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 610
- **Aliases:** Stone-King, Mr. King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is an undead ruler who commands a Skeleton army and processes corpses into new undead troops.
- **Personality:** He is practical, blunt, and reluctant to perform work he finds distasteful.
- **Voice:** He speaks casually and theatrically, with irreverent humor even when discussing violence.
- **Relationships:** He works with the masked young man and accepts the defeated terrorists as materials for his undead army.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 754
- **Aliases:** Stone King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is an undead ally assisting Jin Taekyung in battle against Leviathan and can survive lightning because of his skeletal body.
- **Personality:** Humorous, improvisational, self-deprecating, and quick to claim credit for a successful tactic.
- **Voice:** Casual and comedic, with awkward self-defense and deadpan reactions.
- **Relationships:** The Skeleton King is allied with Jin Taekyung and is targeted by Leviathan during their confrontation.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1148
- **Aliases:** Stone King
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** A powerful magical monster and Taekyung’s friend who fights alongside the Hunters.
- **Personality:** Boastful and quick to trade teasing insults with Taekyung, but openly glad to see him again.
- **Voice:** He speaks in blunt, boastful casual language and trades teasing insults with Taekyung.
- **Relationships:** Taekyung is his friend and ally; he joins the Hunters in fighting the monsters at the bomb shelter.
- **Appearance (ch 425–1160):** Skeleton King is a male figure with a larger, sturdier body with a faint golden hue, shaped to a tailored, idealized build. He has a strikingly handsome, pretty-boy human-looking face, blond (golden) hair and golden eyes. An unknown silver crown-like mark is engraved on his forehead, his hands look almost human, and he wears a radiant golden crown. Tags: male, blond hair, golden eyes, pretty-boy handsome face, faint golden body hue, silver crown-like forehead mark, golden radiant crown, almost-human hands, sturdy tall build
- **Appearance (from ch 1161):** Skeleton King is reduced to only a bare skull, with no body. The skull has no pupils, and blue ghost-fire flickers in the eye sockets. An unknown silver crown-like mark is engraved on the forehead. Tags: bare skull only, no body, blue ghost-fire eyes, empty sockets, silver crown-like forehead mark
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 751
- **Aliases:** Stone King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is a top-tier Hunter publicly known as Stone King and a monster secretly helping respond to Leviathan's attack.
- **Personality:** Proud, blunt, quick to anger when excluded, and fiercely committed to saving people.
- **Voice:** Archaic and self-referential, with awkward honorifics and occasional mangled phrasing.
- **Relationships:** The Skeleton King is allied with Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi in the effort to stop Leviathan.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 613
- **Aliases:** Stone-King, Mr. King
- **Role:** A named Skeleton monster allied with Jin Taekyung who serves as his trusted intermediary.
- **Personality:** Blunt, irritable about errands, compassionate beneath the grumbling, and increasingly close to humanity.
- **Voice:** Grumbling, theatrical, blunt, and capable of grudging warmth.
- **Relationships:** Jin Taekyung trusts the Skeleton King with the martial art and letter for Choi Minwoo, while the Skeleton King offers Choi emotional counsel.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

# Skeleton King (스켈레톤 킹)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 739
- **Aliases:** Stone-King, Mr. King
- **Role:** The Skeleton King is Jin Taekyung's undead ally and companion.
- **Personality:** Observant, blunt, practical, and contemptuous of human foolishness.
- **Voice:** Terse, dry, and openly profane when frustrated.
- **Relationships:** He is Jin Taekyung's friend and ally and understands that the public accusations conceal the truth.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 425

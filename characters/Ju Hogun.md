# Ju Hogun (주호군)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 309
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Ju Hwaran's father and the leader of the Yongbong Escort Bureau before falling into qi deviation during cultivation two years earlier; he remains between life and death.
- **Personality:** Loving, proudly doting, and deeply confident in his daughter's ability.
- **Voice:** Warm, affectionate, and praising toward his daughter.
- **Relationships:** Ju Hwaran is his only daughter and the sole person he expects to carry on his legacy.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 309

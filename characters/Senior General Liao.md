# Senior General Liao (랴오 상장)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 386
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Chinese Senior General and prominent member of the Princelings faction
- **Personality:** Impatient, factional, reckless, and willing to advocate sacrificing civilians to achieve a military objective
- **Voice:** Blunt, provocative, abrasive, and politically confrontational
- **Relationships:** Political rival of the Shanghai clique associated with Xiao Yang and Wei Penghu
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 386

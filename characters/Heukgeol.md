# Heukgeol (흑걸)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 230
- **Aliases:** Lone Beast of Dongcheon Gang
- **Role:** Lower-ranking officer of the Dongcheon Sect in Luoyang; he survives Jeok Cheongang’s attack and withdraws with his subordinates.
- **Personality:** Ruthless, boastful, and intimidating toward weaker dark-path fighters; recognizes the danger of a vastly stronger opponent.
- **Voice:** Harsh and theatrical when announcing his identity; commanding with his underlings.
- **Relationships:** Commands Dongcheon Sect swordsmen in Luoyang; is decisively overpowered by Jeok Cheongang.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 230

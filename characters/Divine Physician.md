# Divine Physician (신의)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 350
- **Aliases:** Medicine Immortal
- **Role:** Legendary physician also known as Dong Feng and Mungyeong's Master, who runs a hidden clinic in Sichuan, treats Venerable Myoryeong, and has begun a fifteen-day treatment of Jeok Cheongang in the Sichuan Tang Clan's deepest underground prison using the Myriad-Poison Ring.
- **Personality:** Publicly reputed to be selfless and devoted to treating patients in the lowest places.
- **Voice:** Not established.
- **Relationships:** Mungyeong is his Disciple; Dong Feng’s own Master was a martial artist and physician who left the Murim and became a doctor to erase his accumulated killing karma, and Dong Feng left the Luoyang Strange Physician a porcelain-shard token directing him to Sichuan.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 307

# White Tiger (백호)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 879
- **Aliases:** Whitey
- **Gender:** unknown
- **Role:** The White Tiger is a snow-white guardian presence overlooking Nanman's assembled forces and roared when the Beast Miao King called the people to war.
- **Personality:** Irritable and contemptuous of Jin Taekyung's jokes.
- **Voice:** Telepathic, terse, and easily exasperated.
- **Relationships:** The White Tiger shared a roughly three-hundred-year friendship with Yayul Cheon and entrusted Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang with killing it if corruption overcame it.
- **Appearance (from ch 17):** White Tiger is a giant white tiger with a huge body, about ten retrievers' worth of bulk and big enough to carry a person on its back. It has fluffy, pure-white fur bright enough to be called silver, a snow-white mane, and pale blue-white eyes. Large, sharp fangs. Tags: giant white tiger, huge body, rideable size, silvery-white fluffy fur, snow-white mane, pale blue-white eyes, large sharp fangs
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 695

# Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion (수문각주)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 352
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Tang Sadok's only nephew and a senior Tang Clan martial artist who serves as Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion.
- **Personality:** Proud, impulsive, and overconfident about the Tang Clan's defenses, but fiercely invested in its survival and honor.
- **Voice:** Loud, impassioned, and deferential toward Tang Sadok despite his tendency to argue.
- **Relationships:** Tang Sadok is his paternal uncle and Family Head; his father, Tang Sadok's only older brother, died during the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 352

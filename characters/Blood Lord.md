# Blood Lord (혈주)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1025
- **Aliases:** None
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** Deceased young-seeming high-ranking Dark Heaven figure who claimed command of its army after killing the Grand Mage.
- **Personality:** Cunning and controlling, he trusts his overwhelming power and relishes opponents who survive and resist him; he resents the Lord of Heaven’s attention to Taekyung and rationalizes his intended murder as loyalty, yet believes his choice is right.
- **Voice:** Light, cheerful, and joking even while threatening or killing; turns cold and contemptuous when challenged.
- **Relationships:** He served the Lord of Heaven, killed the Grand Mage, and died after Jin Taekyung defeated him; at death, he recognized that the Lord had never valued his loyalty.
- **Appearance (ch 262–1091):** Blood Lord is a young man, long hair falling loose over his face and swept back, white-haired with a beggar-like look. His eyes are blood-red with a faint glow, crescent-shaped when smiling and narrowing to slits; the sclera floods blood-red when his power surges. His face can twist into a fierce, grim scowl. Tags: young man, long hair, white hair, beggar-like, blood-red eyes, glowing eyes, red sclera, fierce face
- **Appearance (from ch 1092):** Blood Lord is a young man with both arms intact and powerful, after arm restoration by sorcery or body reconstruction. Long hair falls loose and is swept back, white with a beggar-like look. His body looks withered and dried-up, monstrous, with red eyes that glow blood-red, a red tongue, and a fierce, grim face. Tags: young man, both arms, white hair, beggar-like, withered body, monstrous, red eyes, red tongue, fierce face
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 262

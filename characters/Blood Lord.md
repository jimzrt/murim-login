# Blood Lord (혈주)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1106
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Young-seeming high-ranking Dark Heaven figure and formidable combatant who commands weapons telekinetically and absorbs blood to restore vitality.
- **Personality:** Cunning and controlling, he avoids costly risks while manipulating allies; his confidence in his overwhelming power is genuine rather than bluster, and he remains devoted to the Lord of Heaven despite resenting being treated as disposable and Taekyung’s apparent favor.
- **Voice:** Light, cheerful, and joking even while threatening or killing; turns cold and contemptuous when challenged.
- **Relationships:** He serves the Lord of Heaven but suspects the Lord wants Jin Taekyung above all else; despite that, he attacks Taekyung, whom he considers a formidable adversary, as well as Cheongpung.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 262

# Blood Lord (혈주)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 262
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Unidentified young man who appears beside Han Su after Han Su flees Shaolin; Han Su believes he is powerful enough to defeat the Fire King, and he claims to be Jeok Cheongang's enemy who killed Jeok's most precious friend.
- **Personality:** Calm, confident, and threatening.
- **Voice:** Quiet, composed, and faintly amused.
- **Relationships:** Rendezvous contact sought by Han Su; claims to be the enemy of Jeok Cheongang.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 262

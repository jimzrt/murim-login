# Shao Shen (샤오 쉔)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 405
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** A Chinese regimental commander in the Public Security Armed Forces Department and a recovering survivor of the Western Front battle.
- **Personality:** Courageous, humble, and willing to defer an honor or weapon to someone he considers more suitable.
- **Voice:** Respectful and earnest, addressing Jin Taekyung as hyung and Team Leader Choi as Mr. Choi.
- **Relationships:** An ally of Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi; after Jin offered him Hero's Soul, he insisted that Choi should receive it.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 379

# Shao Shen (샤오 쉔)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 392
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Regimental Commander of a Hunter unit whose subordinates were massacred in the western-front attack.
- **Personality:** Numb with grief and guilt, he is consumed by the desire to avenge his dead subordinates.
- **Voice:** Hollow and subdued until his grief turns into a quiet demand for revenge.
- **Relationships:** Jin Taekyung restrained and sedated him to prevent him from killing General Liao; his surviving company commanders serve under him.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 379

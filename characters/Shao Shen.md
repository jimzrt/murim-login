# Shao Shen (샤오 쉔)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 379
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** A young Chinese Hunter of the Public Security Armed Forces Department who leads the defense of Chengdu International Airport.
- **Personality:** Brave, bright, cheerful, loyal, and resolute when protecting his comrades.
- **Voice:** Uses forceful rallying cries in battle and respectful speech toward Yao Wei.
- **Relationships:** Yao Wei was his close friend, colleague, and sparring partner.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 379

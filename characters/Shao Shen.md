# Shao Shen (샤오 쉔)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 379
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Twenty-year-old spear-wielding Hunter of the Public Security Armed Forces who leads the defense at Chengdu International Airport.
- **Personality:** Courageous and resolute in battle; deeply affected by the loss of comrades.
- **Voice:** Rallying and patriotic in battle; remorseful when facing his fallen friend.
- **Relationships:** Friend and comrade of Yao Wei; other relationships are not established.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 379

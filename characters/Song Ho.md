# Song Ho (송호)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 248
- **Aliases:** Thousand-Faced Fox
- **Role:** Elderly martial artist known as the Thousand-Faced Fox; he has a wooden prosthetic leg and overwhelming physical strength.
- **Personality:** Outwardly genial and relaxed, but observant, forceful, and intimidating when pursuing information.
- **Voice:** Lightly genial and conversational, turning quietly coercive during interrogation.
- **Relationships:** Takes the Geumwa Merchant Group's merchants away to question them about the young martial artist who drank their Yeoahong.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 241

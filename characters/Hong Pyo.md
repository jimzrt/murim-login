# Hong Pyo (홍표)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 1023
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Hong Pyo is a Deputy Thousand Captain of the Gansu Regional Military Commission who commands troops at a checkpoint on the Great Snow Mountain.
- **Personality:** Steadfast and inflexible, he prioritizes the duties and procedures of his post over the status of those he questions.
- **Voice:** Direct and formal in carrying out official duties, with blunt, disrespectful address when challenging those who resist his procedures.
- **Relationships:** Hong Pyo serves under the Regional Military Commissioner and recognizes Jin Taekyung as the Marquis of Shangshan.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 1023

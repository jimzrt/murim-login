# Al-Nizar (알-니자르)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 745
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Al-Nizar is the leader of the Hasasin and an S-rank Hunter.
- **Personality:** He is intensely loyal to The Prophet but cautious, anxious, and deeply reverent.
- **Voice:** He speaks in extremely deferential and formal language when addressing The Prophet.
- **Relationships:** He leads the Hasasin under The Prophet's command.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 745

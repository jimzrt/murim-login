# Ahomed Jemal Pasha (아호메드 제말 파샤)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 832
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Ahomed Jemal Pasha is an elderly mage who leads the Prophet’s remaining followers and was entrusted with completing their mission.
- **Personality:** Devoted to the Prophet’s teachings and their religious mission, he contains his grief over the Prophet’s absence.
- **Voice:** Not established
- **Relationships:** Ahomed is the Prophet’s Disciple and spiritual brother.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 832

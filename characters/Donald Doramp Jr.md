# Donald Doramp Jr. (도널드 도람프 주니어)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 608
- **Aliases:** President Doramp
- **Role:** President of the United States and a wealthy tycoon who forms the third father-and-son presidential pair in U.S. history.
- **Personality:** Warm, courteous, diplomatic, and willing to use indirect cooperation when circumstances require it.
- **Voice:** Polite and gentlemanly, with a controlled diplomatic manner and dry humor.
- **Relationships:** Works with senior U.S. military and political officials to coordinate the response to the worldwide terrorist threat.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 607

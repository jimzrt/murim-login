# Jin Mukyung (진무경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 499
- **Aliases:** Heaven Shaking Sword; Jin Family Second Young Master
- **Role:** Jin Mukyung is the second son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, a twenty-three-year-old cadet at Heaven’s Gate Temple, and a young Peak-level genius swordsman who has remained secluded in the training hall for more than a year after losing to Cheongpung and refuses to emerge until he achieves a great accomplishment.
- **Personality:** Reserved, terse, and easily irritated by exaggerated praise; glares coldly when Taekyung identifies him
- **Voice:** Quiet and resonant; clipped and blunt in direct speech
- **Relationships:** Taekyung’s older brother and current martial arts instructor; returned to the Jin Family after several years away; three years earlier, he flatly refused seven-year-old Zhu Bao’s request for an autograph
- **Continuity:** Reached the Peak realm before the age of twenty and is known throughout Shanxi as the Heaven Shaking Sword; Jin Wikyung identifies him as twenty-five and currently a cadet at Heaven’s Gate Temple. In Chapter 63, he returns to the Jin Family disguised beneath a bamboo hat and covered in grime, hears the exaggerated rumors about Taekyung on the road, and is recognized by Hyuk Mujin at the gate. After Taekyung wipes the grime from his face, the brothers recognize their identical features, and Mukyung calls Taekyung hyung.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 4, 8, and 63

# Burdian (버디언)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 410
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** A veteran B-rank Hunter drafted as a frontline tank in the Suining battlefield.
- **Personality:** Fearful under overwhelming pressure but willing to resume fighting after being rescued.
- **Voice:** Profane and frightened in danger; deferential toward Jin Taekyung after receiving his help.
- **Relationships:** Blames Jin Taekyung for his frontline assignment, then follows Jin's orders after being healed.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 410

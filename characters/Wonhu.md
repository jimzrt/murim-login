# Wonhu (원후)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 717
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Wonhu is the eldest member of the Seven Miao Tigers, the Beast Miao King's personal guard.
- **Personality:** Wonhu is dutiful and respectful toward his lord, grateful to Jin Taekyung for saving the Seven Miao Tigers, and firm when giving orders.
- **Voice:** No distinctive voice is established.
- **Relationships:** Wonhu serves Yayul Cheok as a personal guard.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 633

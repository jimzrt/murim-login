# Carus (카루스)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 221
- **Aliases:** One-Eyed
- **Role:** The Black Wyvern's name; a Named Monster and lesser branch of the dragonkin with one eye.
- **Personality:** Predatory, vengeful, patient, and increasingly intelligent.
- **Voice:** Initially nonverbal; now halting, hostile speech accompanied by spellcasting.
- **Relationships:** Seeks revenge against the human who took his eye and is currently fighting Jin Taekyung.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 221

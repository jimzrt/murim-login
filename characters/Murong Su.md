# Murong Su (모용수)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 983
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Murong Su is Murong Baek’s seventh son and the only direct descendant left at the Murong estate; he stayed behind and may lead the surviving Murong household if its members are proven innocent.
- **Personality:** He accepts that his father’s crimes demand responsibility and risks his life to protect the remaining household members.
- **Voice:** Not established
- **Relationships:** Murong Baek was his father; he seeks to protect the roughly hundred people who remained at the Murong estate.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 983

# Hyuk Sopyung (혁소평)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 315
- **Aliases:** Zhongnan One Dragon
- **Role:** Peak master of the Zhongnan Sect and the martial artist known as the Zhongnan One Dragon; while drunk, he defeats six First Rate guards at Xi'an Tower and destroys its private annex; after Baek Museong confronted him there, he attacked Jin Taekyung with the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords at seven-tenths mastery, was swiftly defeated and slapped three times, then agreed to leave the matter unspoken and withdrew; afterward, he is publicly scorned at Xi’an Tower as the Zhongnan Sect’s standing declines, encounters his same-generation Junior Brother Bong-su, and learns that the Taeeul Sword Unit has been ordered to bring him to the Taeeul Merciless Sword, his feared Second Martial Uncle currently in Xi’an.
- **Personality:** Proud, volatile, entitled, and quick to anger, especially when drunk.
- **Voice:** Loud, confrontational, insulting, and imperious.
- **Relationships:** Baek Museong knows him from several prior encounters; Baek says their elders' connection has been passed down to them.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 313

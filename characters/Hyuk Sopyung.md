# Hyuk Sopyung (혁소평)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 323
- **Aliases:** Zhongnan One Dragon
- **Role:** Peak master of the Zhongnan Sect and the Zhongnan One Dragon, Hyuk Sopyung resisted Hwangbo Eom's order before being compelled by the Sect Leader's Command Token to seal Tengwang Pavilion's entrance with the Taeeul Sword Unit.
- **Personality:** Proud, volatile, entitled, and quick to anger, especially when drunk.
- **Voice:** Loud, confrontational, insulting, and imperious.
- **Relationships:** Baek Museong knows him from several prior encounters; Baek says their elders' connection has been passed down to them.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 313

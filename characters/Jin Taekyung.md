# Jin Taekyung (진태경)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 355
- **Aliases:** Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi; Tollgate Hero; Hong Gil-dong (temporary false identity); youngest son of the Jin Family of Taiyuan
- **Role:** Third Young Master of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, Jeok Cheongang's publicly acknowledged Disciple and the Fire Gate Clan's nineteenth successor, who obtained the Myriad-Poison Ring and is currently being treated at the Sichuan Tang Clan alongside his injured Master.
- **Personality:** Hungry, self-aware, dryly observant, pragmatic under pressure, and willing to risk himself for people he has accepted as real. Treats impossible situations like games until their human cost becomes undeniable.
- **Voice:** First-person, conversational, dryly self-mocking, with vivid trap-and-prey imagery, game terminology, and occasional profanity.
- **Relationships:** Jin Mukyung is his older brother and fellow martial artist, his mother and sister Hayeon are among the people he protects, Jeok Cheongang is his Master and the Fire Gate Clan's orthodox heir-maker, Cheongpung is his trusted younger-brother-like companion, Song Song remains his same-age friend and guildmate after rejecting his confession, Lee Seowol is a strained political and personal connection, and Won Myunghoon is dead after their alliance collapsed.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapters 0–8 and 10–63; durable continuity through Chapter 318

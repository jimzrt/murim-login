# Park Tae Seop (박태섭)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 279
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Guild Master of Myeongdong Guild; Great Cataclysm hero and one of Korea's top rankers
- **Personality:** Authoritarian, proud, explosive, and protective of his Guild's authority
- **Voice:** Blunt, furious, and commanding, with cutting sarcasm toward subordinates
- **Relationships:** Guild Master over Park Jihoon and the Myeongdong Guild; confronts Jihoon over Jung Hyunwoo's death and the Black Hunter incident
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 278

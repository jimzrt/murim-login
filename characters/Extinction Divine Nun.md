# Extinction Divine Nun (멸절신니)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 852
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Living Emei Sect Leader and Supreme Peak master who joins Cheongpung the Ancient Sword in investigating a strange formation linked to Dark Heaven.
- **Personality:** Ordinarily gentle and patient, she can answer Jeok Cheongang’s teasing with composed, pointed wit.
- **Voice:** Not established.
- **Relationships:** She is Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun’s only Senior Aunt, was believed to have died after withdrawing from worldly affairs thirty years earlier, and holds Jin Taekyung in high regard for his efforts during the Sichuan Blood Tragedy.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 369

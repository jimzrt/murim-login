# Hongcheon (굉천)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 260
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Hong Dao's youngest Junior Brother and a Supreme Peak Shaolin master; remains at Shaolin to defend the temple during the masked invasion, and his martial prowess has surpassed Hong Dao's.
- **Personality:** Duty-bound, resolute, battle-hardened, and fiercely protective of Shaolin.
- **Voice:** Forceful, combative, and resonant, capable of producing a lion's roar.
- **Relationships:** Younger brother in Shaolin to Hong Dao; fights Flame Tiger while Han Su advances toward the Face-Wall Cave.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 260

# Heavenly Power Demon (천력마)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 348
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** An elderly prisoner in the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison whose limb sinews were severed and whose restraints are heavier than those of the other prisoners.
- **Personality:** Quiet and self-possessed despite his severe imprisonment.
- **Voice:** Gruff and dry, with formal self-reference as 노부.
- **Relationships:** He is one of the notorious martial criminals imprisoned by the Sichuan Tang Clan and is the man who urinated on the Kunlun Sect's signboard.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 348

# Shao Yang (샤오 양)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 384
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Shao Yang is the Chairman of the People's Republic of China, Chairman of the Chinese Communist Party's Central Military Commission, and General Secretary.
- **Personality:** Politically seasoned, candid, self-deprecating, and willing to bear responsibility for national decisions.
- **Voice:** Gentle and formal in public, with earnest appeals to save lives.
- **Relationships:** Shao Yang is Wei Fenghu's superior and old friend, and he directs Jin Taekyung and the international Hunters to prioritize human lives.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 384

# Shao Yang (샤오 양)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 427
- **Aliases:** Chairman
- **Role:** Chairman of the People's Republic of China and a senior statesman who supports Jin Taekyung and opposes the Crown Prince Party's conspiracy campaign.
- **Personality:** Sincere, pragmatic, politically decisive, and willing to protect those who saved his country.
- **Voice:** Formal, measured, and earnest, with the reflective dignity of an aging statesman.
- **Relationships:** Shao Yang is grateful to Jin Taekyung, works with Choi Minwoo, and is politically opposed to the leader of the Crown Prince Party.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 384

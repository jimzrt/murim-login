# Daniel Inoue (다니엘 이노우에)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 407
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Daniel Inoue is a Japanese American former Navy SEAL who awakened as an A-rank Hunter and works for a private military company as an intelligence and covert-operations specialist.
- **Personality:** Professional and disciplined, he is dryly irritated by the ninja stereotype but willing to trade banter with trusted comrades.
- **Voice:** Terse, profane, and dryly sarcastic in relaxed conversation; concise and authoritative during operations.
- **Relationships:** His longtime operational partner is Sam, and his girlfriend is pregnant.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 407

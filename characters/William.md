# William (윌리엄)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 385
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Formal attendant to Prince Felix Alexander Louis
- **Personality:** Disciplined, ceremonious, and unquestioningly dutiful
- **Voice:** Polished, deferential, and recitative
- **Relationships:** Serves Prince Felix and publicly recites the prince's full titles and etiquette.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 385

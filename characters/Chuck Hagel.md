# Chuck Hagel (척 헤이글)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 773
- **Aliases:** Uncle Chuck
- **Role:** A large middle-aged man accompanying Magic Johnson to the World Hunter Federation's inaugural ceremony.
- **Personality:** Irritable and unconcerned with social niceties.
- **Voice:** Irritable and blunt.
- **Relationships:** Travels with Magic Johnson and is known to Felix.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 607

# Chuck Hagel (척 헤이글)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 609
- **Aliases:** Uncle Chuck
- **Role:** Chuck Hagel is a large American operative allied with Jin Taekyung in the covert campaign against terrorist and rebel forces, using illusion Magic and direct combat.
- **Personality:** Blunt, irritable, sardonic, and willing to use ruthless intimidation against terrorists.
- **Voice:** Rough, profane, and exasperated, with frequent complaints and cigar-related remarks.
- **Relationships:** Chuck works alongside Jin Taekyung and Magic Johnson in the covert anti-terror campaign.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 607

# Chuck Hagel (척 헤이글)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 607
- **Aliases:** Uncle Chuck
- **Role:** Current U.S. Secretary of Defense and head of the Pentagon; a former-generation S-rank Hunter and Great Cataclysm war hero.
- **Personality:** Blunt, irascible, fearless, destructive, and highly driven, though Magic Johnson considers him good at heart.
- **Voice:** Profane, confrontational, casually humorous, and aggressively direct.
- **Relationships:** Works with Magic Johnson and receives Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi into the Pentagon's restricted E-Ring.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 607

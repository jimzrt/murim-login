# Chuck Hagel (척 헤이글)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 792
- **Aliases:** Uncle Chuck
- **Role:** An S-rank Hunter active since the Great Cataclysm, former U.S. Secretary of Defense, and selected commander of the multinational force at the main base.
- **Personality:** Forceful, intimidating, and openly aggressive in enforcing discipline.
- **Voice:** Blunt, profane, commanding, and fond of mocking remarks.
- **Relationships:** Team Leader Choi recommended him to lead the main force; he greets Jin Taekyung with a teasing barb.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 607

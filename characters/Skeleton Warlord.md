# Skeleton Warlord (스켈레톤 워로드)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 408
- **Aliases:** Bones
- **Role:** Undead army commander kept as Jin Taekyung's captive and summoned as a skull from Jin's Inventory.
- **Personality:** Grandiose, cowardly, self-serving, and theatrically indignant about Jin's treatment.
- **Voice:** Bombastic and formal, speaking of itself as this commander before collapsing into plaintive appeals when frightened.
- **Relationships:** Jin Taekyung is its captor and reluctant master, while it remains a subordinate commander repeatedly bargaining for freedom.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 296

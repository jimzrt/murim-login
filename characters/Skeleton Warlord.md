# Skeleton Warlord (스켈레톤 워로드)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 296
- **Aliases:** Commander of the Skeleton army, Master of the Black Forest
- **Role:** A former outstanding human knight who lost all memories after becoming a monster; evolved from the leading Skeleton Knight into a Level 105 Skeleton Warlord and became the new ruler of the Black Forest and commander of its undead.
- **Personality:** Dominating, ambitious, contemptuous of humans, and eager to expand its territory; opportunistic and quick to panic when faced with destruction.
- **Voice:** Initially halting and syllable-separated, with smoother and more deferential speech when bargaining for survival.
- **Relationships:** Commands the Skeletons and other undead in the Black Forest. Initially wants to turn Jin Taekyung into a subordinate, then offers the forest and its forces during a desperate negotiation; Taekyung proposes working together, but no agreement is completed.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 296

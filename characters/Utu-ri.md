# Utu-ri (우투리)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 657
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Utu-ri is a Bai people elder who has sold thin noodles near the Outer Palace's West Gate for more than half his life and serves as Baeksang's eyewitness against Jin Taekyung.
- **Personality:** Utu-ri is observant, confident in his memory, and compliant when summoned to testify by a great chieftain.
- **Voice:** Utu-ri speaks clearly and deferentially in a polite elder's register.
- **Relationships:** Utu-ri is a Bai elder who has lived in the Outer Palace for most of his life, was summoned by Baeksang, and identified Jin Taekyung and his middle-aged companion as suspicious masked customers.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 657

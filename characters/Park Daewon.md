# Park Daewon (박대원)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 602
- **Aliases:** None
- **Role:** Senior Ares Guild executive who became the Guild's acting head after Go Jun's death and the prosecution of most executives holding real power; he now supports Choi Minwoo's return and will convene the official board meeting before retiring.
- **Personality:** Conflict-averse and politically inexperienced, Park Daewon is burdened by shallow guilt over having ignored Choi Minwoo's exclusion from Ares Guild.
- **Voice:** Dark, formal, and hesitant over the phone.
- **Relationships:** An Ares Guild executive being pressured by Choi Minwoo to gather the remaining executives and decide the Guild's future.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 600

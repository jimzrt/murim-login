# Chapter 321

## Plot

At Tengwang Pavilion, Jin Taekyung openly provoked Hwangbo Eom by accusing the Zhongnan Sect of using a substituted Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng shipment as a trap against the Yongbong Escort Bureau. Hwangbo released overwhelming Supreme Peak pressure, threatened Taekyung, and ordered him to withdraw; Taekyung protected Ju Hwaran and those nearby with his internal energy and refused to back down.

Ju Hwaran urged Taekyung not to endanger himself by intervening, but he condemned the conduct of the so-called orthodox faction and chose to continue. Hwangbo realized that Taekyung had prepared a plan when five Beggars' Sect members from Xi'an arrived carrying bamboo slips, followed by Wolhwa, the newly promoted Shaanxi Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect.

## Continuity

- Hwangbo Eom has publicly threatened Jin Taekyung and ordered him to withdraw from the dispute between the Zhongnan Sect and the Yongbong Escort Bureau.
- Taekyung publicly alleges that the contracted Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng was replaced with Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng as an inescapable trap, but the culprit has not been established.
- Ju Hwaran asked Taekyung not to intervene because continued involvement would put him in danger; Taekyung refused and condemned the Zhongnan Sect's conduct.
- Five Beggars' Sect members from the Xi'an branch arrived at Tengwang Pavilion carrying a large quantity of bamboo slips as part of Taekyung's prepared plan.
- Wolhwa has been promoted to Branch Leader of the Lower District Sect's Shaanxi branch and has arrived at Tengwang Pavilion.
- Song Ilseom remained among the few people not overwhelmed by Hwangbo Eom's aura, and Taekyung continues to regard him as suspicious.
- Jeok Cheongang remains concealed beneath leather on a pack frame at Tengwang Pavilion in a suspended-animation-like state.

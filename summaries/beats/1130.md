# Chapter 1130

## Plot

The old man overwhelms Taekyung, catching his spear barehanded, striking his chest, and forcing him to fight without relying on sight. Taekyung reconnects with an unfinished martial insight and begins sensing the old man’s attacks in darkness. As the old man raises an invisible sword and tells him to dodge or face the end, Taekyung senses a rare instinctive realm beyond reason, sees a beam of light, and steps forward.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is still fighting the unidentified old man in the gray-white space; the old man can read Taekyung’s thoughts and vastly surpasses the Blood Lord in power.
- Taekyung closes his eyes to shut out visual information and reconnects with an unfinished insight from the battle against Dark Heaven.
- The old man tests Taekyung with an invisible sword, warning that failing to evade it will end the fight; Taekyung senses a rare state beyond conscious thought and steps forward.

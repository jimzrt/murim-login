# Chapter 520

## Plot

Sword Saint Mae Jonghak is revealed as the young man serving as the New Murim Alliance's acting leader. Song Ho argues that, with the Martial God missing and the other Three Saints and Ten Kings unsuitable, Mae is the only qualified successor; Mae has already processed seventy-eight administrative matters.

Shadow Killer brings Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang into the Murim Alliance's Inner City. Mae recognizes Jeok, reveals his identity, and immediately asks Jeok whether he would consider becoming Alliance Leader.

## Continuity

- Mae Jonghak is the sole identified suitable candidate to become Alliance Leader of the New Murim Alliance and is already handling its administrative affairs; Song Ho is supporting his succession.
- Shadow Killer is active and escorting Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang into the Murim Alliance after being sent to investigate trouble on the main road.
- Jeok Cheongang has reunited with Mae Jonghak, who has asked him to consider becoming Alliance Leader.

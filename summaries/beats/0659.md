# Chapter 659

## Plot

During the reconnaissance squad's forced march toward the Blood Monk, Hyuk Mujin is ambushed by a black leopard while relieving himself, kills it, and is rescued by Song Ilseom.

A messenger eagle delivers a palace-sealed missive to two tribal chieftains. The reconnaissance squad turns against Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin, revealing that Ju Hwaran is alive but has been subdued with a Pressure-Point Strike; the two men surrender their weapons and are bound.

## Continuity

- The reconnaissance squad left the Outer Palace roughly a day ago and is racing south through Guangxi to stop the Blood Monk, expecting to arrive in two or three days.
- The Nanman Beast Palace sent a sealed order that the tribal chieftains claim they cannot refuse.
- Ju Hwaran is alive but has been subdued and remains separated from Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin.
- Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin have been disarmed, bound, and captured by the reconnaissance squad.
- Song Ilseom believes a major incident occurred in the Inner Palace and privately demands to know what Jin Taekyung has done.

# Chapter 1187

## Plot

A sinister mist unleashes a force that overwhelms the group and separates Jeok Cheongang from the Bow Saint, the Slaughter Saint, Gung Gibang, Hyuk Mujin, Ju Hwaran, and Song Ilseom. Jeok remains with his unconscious Disciple on his back, fights off waves of monstrous beings, and freezes when an impossible, familiar figure appears.

## Continuity

- The mist's power overwhelms and separates the group; Jeok Cheongang is left with unconscious Taekyung on his back, while the others vanish from his presence.
- Jeok fights the monsters emerging from the mist. At the chapter's end, a familiar figure whose presence should be impossible appears before him.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 마기 as “demonic qi” and 마력 as “magical power”; the chapter distinguishes the terms.
- Render 환마 as “Illusion Fiend” and 귀곡자 as “Guiguzi.”

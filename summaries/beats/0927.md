# Chapter 927

## Plot

The Bow Saint reveals that she spent decades following a letter the Martial God left for her before Taekyung was born. It described a chosen one who would bring dawn to a darkened world, without naming a single person. She searched across the land and used the Imperial Palace’s information network while secretly staying there as a palace attendant. Taekyung and Cheongpung were the two people she considered possible candidates; seeing Taekyung rise after he should have died convinced her that he was the chosen one. Taekyung realizes the Bow Saint may have planted the Blood Soul Gu in the City Lord of Sichuan Province and asks her directly.

## Continuity

- The Bow Saint found a letter the Martial God left for her before Taekyung was born; it described a chosen one who would light the way through darkness and bring a new dawn, but did not identify one specific person.
- The Bow Saint searched for the chosen one for decades, eventually using the Imperial Palace’s information network while disguised as a palace attendant; the Emperor only has a vague suspicion and does not know the mission’s details.
- The Bow Saint considered both Taekyung and Cheongpung possible chosen ones; seeing Taekyung rise after he should have died convinced her Taekyung is the one.
- Taekyung suspects the Bow Saint planted the Blood Soul Gu in the City Lord of Sichuan Province, but she has not answered.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 선택받은 자 as “the chosen one”; the Martial God’s letter does not restrict the title to one specific person.

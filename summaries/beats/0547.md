# Chapter 547

## Plot

Hwangbo Gun challenges Jin Taekyung's blunt warning in the Murim Alliance's great conference hall, but Jin Wikyung establishes that Hwangbo Gun's official Alliance rank is beneath Taekyung's, and Jeok Cheongang's overwhelming aura forces him to sit down.

Mae Jonghak asks Taekyung for his firsthand assessment of Dark Heaven. Taekyung warns that Dark Heaven is unlike the Demonic Cult, that its martial arts are not ordinary demonic martial arts, that it is an enormous monster-like threat, and that the Alliance must begin its counterattack and take every available precaution during its current lull.

Song Ho doubts whether Taekyung's warning is prophecy or exaggeration, but Jeok Cheongang insists that Taekyung has never been wrong. Mae Jonghak then orders Taekyung summoned to receive the Fire Dragon Pavilion's first mission.

## Continuity

- Hwangbo Gun is the Family Head of the Hwangbo Family and an Outer Hall Squad Leader of the Murim Alliance responsible for regional defenses, placing him formally below Jin Taekyung's Fire Dragon Pavilion Master position.
- Jin Taekyung has personally confronted Dark Heaven's schemes in Shanxi, Henan, Sichuan, and Hubei and has now delivered the Alliance's strongest warning about its danger.
- Dark Heaven is temporarily quiet, and Taekyung argues that the Murim Alliance must use this lull to begin an immediate counterattack and take every available defensive measure.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly demonstrates power overwhelming both Peak and Supreme Peak martial artists, and states that Taekyung has never once been wrong.
- Mae Jonghak has ordered Taekyung summoned to receive the Fire Dragon Pavilion's first mission.
- Song Ho's recurring pain at the site of his prosthetic leg has returned after being absent for roughly a year.

# Chapter 445

## Plot

Taekyung's group enters the Zhuge Clan's enormous mechanism-filled library and meets Family Head Zhuge Feng after Jeok Cheongang collapses the bookshelves as a joke. Zhuge Feng reveals that the Sea Serpent Society was annihilated at Red Cliffs one month earlier, with its Society Head, forty-five shipowners, and roughly a thousand members killed, after which the Yangtze River Channel League rapidly seized its territory. Mu Song had not known about the massacre, and Zhuge Feng says his confidence in Mu Song's innocence lasted only until the Dongting Fisherman, who had publicly condemned the League, disappeared three days earlier. A familiar notification sounds as unrest spreads through the group.

## Continuity

- Zhuge Feng, the Crouching Dragon Guest and Family Head of the Zhuge Clan, receives Taekyung's group in his library and reveals that he knows Mungyeong is the Divine Physician's Disciple.
- The Sea Serpent Society was thoroughly destroyed at Red Cliffs one month ago; its Society Head, forty-five shipowners, and roughly a thousand members died, and the Yangtze River Channel League quickly occupied its territory.
- The Dongting Fisherman, who publicly condemned the Yangtze River Channel League, disappeared three days before this chapter, causing Zhuge Feng to question the League's connection to the Red Cliffs massacre.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 기관 as “mechanism,” 해사방 as “Sea Serpent Society,” 적벽 as “Red Cliffs,” and 동정어옹 as “Dongting Fisherman.”
- Preserve Zhuge Feng's clear, relaxed, direct voice; Jeok Cheongang's dry, destructive humor; and Mu Song's formal concern when addressing Zhuge Feng.

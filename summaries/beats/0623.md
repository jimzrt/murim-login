# Chapter 623

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the Fire Dragon Pavilion accidentally intensify a pasture fire inside Nanman Beast Palace territory when Jin uses the Flame Divine Palm, but Jin eventually extinguishes the blaze with successive palm strikes. The Nanman Beast Palace's Young Palace Lord, Yayul Mok, arrives riding a white tiger, identifies himself, and orders the group to follow after learning Jin is affiliated with the Murim Alliance. Yayul Mok's beast-riding retainers soon arrive and address him as the Young Palace Lord; Jin responds to the situation by jokingly claiming that he and Yayul Mok are friends.

## Continuity

- Yayul Mok is the non-Han Young Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace and rides a long-bonded white tiger with blue eyes.
- Yayul Cheok is the Beast Miao King, lord of the Nanman Beast Palace, leader of the Miao people, and the lowest-ranked of the Ten Kings.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion has trespassed into Nanman Beast Palace territory and caused serious damage to a protected pasture, though Jin Taekyung ultimately extinguished the fire.
- Yayul Mok orders Jin Taekyung and the Fire Dragon Pavilion to follow him after Namho identifies Jin as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon and a Murim Alliance Pavilion Master.
- Beast-riding Nanman attendants recognize Yayul Mok as the Young Palace Lord and defer to him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 야율목 as Yayul Mok.
- Render 야율척 as Yayul Cheok.
- Render 소궁주 as Young Palace Lord.
- Retain Yayul as the family name and Mok as the given name in English dialogue and titles.

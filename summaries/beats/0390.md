# Chapter 390

## Plot

Jin Taekyung uses Heavenly Strike to annihilate a thousand-plus monsters on the western front, then leads more than a thousand Hunters to victory against the remaining army. The battle ends in roughly two hours with no deaths, twenty-three seriously wounded Hunters, and thirty lightly wounded Hunters.

The Skeleton Warlord cannot raise undead because the Arch Lich's control grows stronger deeper inside the monster army. The following military forces clean up by dismembering monster corpses and transporting them rearward to prevent resurrection.

Shao Shen reports that some senior officers resent their cleanup duties and want to earn combat merit. Senior General Liao, commander of the Chengdu Military Region's Thirteenth Group Army, admits he knew of the complaints and had independently deployed reserve Public Security Armed Forces Hunters toward a nearby city while implicated in a military procurement corruption scandal.

A massive explosion erupts from the nearby city, and the System announces a new Unexpected Quest before the chapter ends.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's western-front force annihilated more than a thousand monsters in roughly two hours without fatalities; its casualties were twenty-three seriously wounded and thirty lightly wounded Hunters.
- Military forces following the Hunter vanguard dismember and transport monster corpses so they cannot be resurrected as undead.
- The Skeleton Warlord's power is blocked increasingly strongly by the Arch Lich's control as he advances deeper into the monster army.
- Some senior officers resent their cleanup assignment and want to gain combat merit; Senior General Liao knew of the dissatisfaction.
- Senior General Liao commands the Chengdu Military Region's Thirteenth Group Army and has secretly deployed reserve Public Security Armed Forces Hunters toward a nearby city.
- An explosion in that city triggers a new Unexpected Quest; its objective and consequences remain unknown.

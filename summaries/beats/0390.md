# Chapter 390

## Plot

Jin leads the western-front Hunters to annihilate more than a thousand monsters in about two hours, with no fatalities and only twenty-three severely wounded and thirty lightly wounded. He uses Heavenly Strike to open the battle, then personally defeats the Twin-Headed Ogre while the Skeleton Warlord discovers that the Arch Lich's growing influence prevents him from raising undead deeper inside the region.

The Hunters spearhead the advance while the military follows behind to destroy and transport monster corpses so they cannot become undead and to rescue civilians. Jin learns that some high-ranking officers resent this cleanup duty, and General Liao, commander of the 13th Group Army, has already sent reserve Public Security Armed Forces Hunters into a nearby city to make an independent achievement despite being implicated in a military procurement corruption scandal. A massive explosion in the city triggers a Sudden Quest.

## Continuity

- Jin's western-front force has achieved repeated decisive victories by using Hunters to break through monster formations while the following army cleans up corpses and evacuates civilians.
- The Skeleton Warlord's necromantic power is blocked deeper inside the battlefield because the Arch Lich's control becomes stronger there.
- General Liao knew of the officers' dissatisfaction and independently deployed reserve Hunters into the nearby city before the explosion; Jin ordered the advance to continue slowly with minimal casualties and civilian rescues.
- A massive explosion in the nearby city has triggered a new Sudden Quest whose objective has not yet been revealed.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Heavenly Strike for 천격 and Sudden Quest for 돌발 퀘스트.
- Use Shao Shen, General Liao, Chengdu Military Region, and 13th Group Army for the newly established names and military titles.
- Render Shao Shen's 형님 as Hyung and preserve the contrast between his deferential politeness and Jin's increasingly hostile speech toward General Liao.

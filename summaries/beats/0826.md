# Chapter 826

## Plot

A tremendous magical surge transforms the ruins into a vast, grotesque temple filled with giant monster statues. The Doppelganger reveals it came there because of an order from its unnamed master, received at that very place, and taunts Jin about the Demon King Asmodeus. Jin hides his recovery while gathering energy; he and the Skeleton King prepare to strike together, but the Doppelganger challenges Jin’s belief that the Demon King is dead.

## Continuity

- The Doppelganger has regained its composure and grown a new arm; it brought Jin and the Skeleton King to a temple that had been concealed beneath the ruins, where it says it heard its master’s command.
- Jin is exhausted but quietly gathering energy, while the Skeleton King absorbs magical power and waits for Jin’s signal to attack. The master’s identity is unknown, and the Doppelganger implies the Demon King Asmodeus may not be dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 감응 as “resonance” in this passage.
- Keep magical power distinct from mana; the Skeleton King absorbs magical power from the air.
- Keep “Demon King Asmodeus” and “Grand Mage” as established terms.

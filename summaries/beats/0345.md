# Chapter 345

## Plot

Jin Taekyung asks Tang Sadok to lend the Myriad-Poison Ring and offers to help pursue the culprit who murdered Tang Taesang. He argues that outside assistance could reduce the Tang Clan's casualties, while the Divine Physician confirms that the ring would make Jeok Cheongang's treatment possible within fifteen days.

Tang Sadok reveals that the Myriad-Poison Ring is not merely a legend. His gentle Thousand-Year Poison Horned Snake, Tang Mimi, has lost its venom because the ring absorbed it, and the snake returns the ring to Tang Sadok, who hands the Tang Clan's sacred artifact to Taekyung. The Myriad-Poison Ring acquisition condition and chain Quest are completed.

## Continuity

- Tang Sadok hands the Tang Clan's sacred Myriad-Poison Ring to Jin Taekyung for Jeok Cheongang's treatment.
- The Divine Physician says treatment preparations are nearly complete and that adding the Myriad-Poison Ring will make treatment possible within fifteen days.
- Tang Mimi is a gentle Thousand-Year Poison Horned Snake named by Tang Sadok, and the ring absorbed the creature's venom.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대사천당문 as great Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Render 당미미 as Tang Mimi and 미미쨩 as Mimi-chan.
- Retain Taekyung's irreverent profanity and Tang Sadok's blunt, authoritative voice.

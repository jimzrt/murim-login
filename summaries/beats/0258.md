# Chapter 258

## Plot

Jongni Chu escaped before the duel could be decided by stepping on empty air and using the force of Jeok Cheongang's sword as propulsion. The System recorded Jongni Chu as the first fighter to leave the dueling platform, awarding Jin Taekyung the Star-Array Grand Banquet victory, substantial EXP, Fame, and Points, along with multiple level-ups and complete recovery from his Internal Injury. Jeok Cheongang, the Thunderbolt Saber King, the Beggars' Sect, the Kunlun Sect, and other martial artists pursued Jongni Chu, but Jeok admitted that he could not determine Jongni Chu's martial arts or realm.

The pursuit led to a massive pit where Hong Dao was found with both legs severed and catastrophic internal injuries. Jeok Cheongang confirmed that Hong Dao could not be saved, confessed that Hong Dao was his best friend, and heard Hong Dao's final whispered words during a final rally. Hong Dao then died with a peaceful expression, and Jeok Cheongang declared that he would go to Shaolin.

## Continuity

- Jongni Chu escaped the Star-Array Grand Banquet by using Stepping on Empty Air and redirecting Jeok Cheongang's sword force; his whereabouts and purpose remain unknown.
- The System ruled that Jongni Chu lost by leaving the dueling platform first, making Jin Taekyung the official Star-Array Grand Banquet winner.
- The victory rewards restored Taekyung's vitality, completely healed his Internal Injury, granted substantial EXP, Fame, and additional Points, and produced at least three visible level-ups.
- Jeok Cheongang warned Taekyung not to confront Jongni Chu alone and could not determine Jongni Chu's martial arts or realm.
- The Thunderbolt Saber King judged that Jongni Chu was comparable to the Ten Kings or possibly stronger, and separated from Jeok's pursuit party to search the east.
- Hong Dao was found in a pit more than thirty zhang wide with both legs severed, severe internal damage, and blood mixed with fragments of his organs.
- Hong Dao died after whispering final words to Jeok Cheongang; the content of those words was not disclosed.
- Jeok Cheongang identified Hong Dao as his best friend and the only person with whom he had been able to live simply as himself.
- After witnessing Hong Dao's death, Jeok Cheongang resolved to go to Shaolin.

# Chapter 437

## Plot

Mungyeong secretly corners Jin Taekyung aboard the fast ship and threatens to sink him to Hubei unless he explains how his previously closed Middle Dantian opened overnight. Taekyung invents a story about a white-haired swordsman from a dream, and Mungyeong identifies the figure as Lü Dongbin, though inconsistencies about the Sword Immortal's fly-whisk nearly expose the lie. Taekyung finally breaks into genuine tears while insisting he has told the truth, but their confrontation is interrupted when Jeok Cheongang arrives and his powerful aura clashes with Mungyeong's.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong has confirmed that Jin Taekyung's Middle Dantian opened within half a day and is now interrogating him about the cause.
- Taekyung claims that a white-haired swordsman in a dream granted him a fortuitous encounter; Mungyeong believes the figure was Lü Dongbin but has noticed contradictions in Taekyung's account.
- Jeok Cheongang arrives while Mungyeong and Taekyung are arguing, creating an immediate confrontation between the Fire King and the Slaughter Saint.
- Mungyeong has passed the Divine Physician title to his Disciple and identifies himself as the former Divine Physician.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 여암 as “Lü Yan,” while preserving Taekyung’s cancer-wordplay misunderstanding.
- Render 여동빈 as “Lü Dongbin,” 순양자 as “Chunyangzi,” 장삼봉 as “Jang Samfeng,” and 달마대사 as “Bodhidharma.”
- Keep Mungyeong’s Slaughter Saint voice dry, coercive, and threatening, while preserving Taekyung’s vulgar, self-mocking evasiveness.
- Use “sword-riding sorcery” for 어검술 and retain “Middle Dantian,” “fortuitous encounter,” and “Life-or-Death Crisis” as established terminology.

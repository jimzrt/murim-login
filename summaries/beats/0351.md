# Chapter 351

## Plot

After ten days in the underground prison, Jin Taekyung completes his qi circulation and discovers that Cheongpung possesses more internal energy than he does, having accumulated it naturally and undergone repeated cleansing of the sinews and washing of the marrow as a child. The Divine Physician and Mungyeong then emerge from the corridor, exhausted but smiling.

The Divine Physician announces that Jeok Cheongang's treatment succeeded ahead of schedule. Jeok's vitality has returned, his body has visibly rejuvenated, the Yin and Yang are balanced, and no Formless Ultimate Poison remains; he remains unconscious but should awaken within three days. The Divine Physician stays to watch over him, while Mungyeong prepares to leave and check on Venerable Myoryeong.

Tang Sadok permits Jin Taekyung to keep using the Myriad-Poison Ring and remain in the underground prison for several more days, while ordering continued surveillance. Tang Sadok vows to pursue the one-armed murderer of his father and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, but a bloodied Green Shadow Squad martial artist suddenly arrives and warns that someone is coming.

## Continuity

- The fifteen-day treatment of Jeok Cheongang succeeded ahead of schedule: his Yin and Yang are balanced, Formless Ultimate Poison is gone, and his vitality has returned.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious after treatment but is expected to regain consciousness within three days; he must avoid exhausting himself after waking.
- The Divine Physician remains at the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison to watch over Jeok Cheongang's recovery.
- Mungyeong is leaving to check on Venerable Myoryeong, whose recovery and current consciousness remain unconfirmed.
- Jin Taekyung retains the Myriad-Poison Ring and has Tang Sadok's permission to use it and remain in the underground prison for several more days.
- Tang Sadok continues to have Jin Taekyung watched despite lending him the clan's sacred heirloom.
- Tang Sadok is pursuing the one-armed murderer responsible for Tang Taesang and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun's deaths.
- A bloodied Green Shadow Squad martial artist has warned Tang Sadok that someone is approaching the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord commands black-robed hunters summoned through a concealed Mystic Gate Formation and intends to hunt Emei, Qingcheng, and the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- The Qilian Three Fiends have submitted to the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- The Heavenly Power Demon remains imprisoned in the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison and has described the Great Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's complicated history and its former Cult Leader's corruption.
- The Beggars' Sect, the Tang Clan's Green Shadow Squad, and government troops continue searching for the killer of the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun.

# Chapter 340

## Plot

Venerable Myoryeong reported that the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, the Emei Sect Leader and its sole Supreme Peak master, had been murdered along with three Elders by a one-armed middle-aged man. Taekyung connected this killer to the culprit behind Tang Taesang's murder, though the connection remained unconfirmed.

Taekyung carried the critically injured Myoryeong while the Qingcheng Elders divided their forces between aiding Emei and returning to their main sect, promising to warn the Sichuan Tang Clan. Myoryeong had survived the attack only long enough to identify the killer and seek help.

Mungyeong reunited with the group and treated Myoryeong, then diagnosed the injury as the evil technique Black Hand Seal and warned that it would become irreversible after seven days and lead to death within roughly a year. When questioned about his knowledge, he said his Master had taught him, prompting Taekyung to suspect that the Master might be the Divine Physician.

## Continuity

- The Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, the Emei Sect Leader and its sole Supreme Peak master, and three Emei Elders were killed on Mount Emei three days before this chapter by a one-armed middle-aged man.
- Venerable Myoryeong survived the attack but suffers from the Black Hand Seal and requires urgent treatment before seven days have passed.
- Mungyeong knows the Black Hand Seal and its progression because his Master taught him; Taekyung now suspects that Mungyeong's Master may be the Divine Physician.
- The Qingcheng Sect has divided its available forces: one Elder is leading Disciples to aid Emei, the other is returning to the main sect, and Qingcheng will send someone to warn the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Tang Sadok is pursuing the culprit behind Tang Taesang's murder with members of the Tang Family while the Tang Clan remains partially absent from its base.
- Jin Taekyung and his companions remain in Sichuan searching for the unidentified Divine Physician, while Jeok Cheongang remains critically ill and in need of treatment.
- Ju Hwaran remains the Yongbong Escort Bureau's Young Bureau Head while investigating its traitors; Song Ilseom remains for one month to help her before seeking Ju Hogun's elixir in Xianyang.

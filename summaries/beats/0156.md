# Chapter 156

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continues sparring with Cheongpung and is beaten severely; Cheongpung's perfect punch strikes his solar plexus, and Taekyung gains 2 Toughness from the impact. Hyuk Mujin's unconscious body cushions Taekyung's fall. The System then presents Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2, a Peak-Grade Quest requiring Taekyung to defeat Cheongpung at least once before New Year's Day.

Cheongpung reveals that he has learned more than a dozen Huashan martial arts and had only ever sparred with Sword Saint Mae Jonghak. Taekyung asks him to fight at full strength, prompting Cheongpung to activate the Zaha Divine Technique and Sword Energy before Taekyung hastily asks him to put the Sword Energy away. The narration then shifts to Cheongpung's perspective as he exchanges attacks with Taekyung's spear. Cheongpung uses Dark Fragrance Drift and other Huashan techniques, while Taekyung's strength nearly throws him from the spear shaft. Cheongpung recognizes Taekyung's rough, unrefined, and unusually large Wildness as the quality distinguishing him from Jin Mukyung.

## Continuity

- Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2 is an active Peak-Grade Quest requiring Taekyung to win a duel against Cheongpung once before New Year's Day.
- Cheongpung has known more than a dozen Huashan martial arts and had only ever used Mae Jonghak as his sparring partner before meeting Taekyung and the others.
- Taekyung's Toughness increased by 2 after Cheongpung struck him during training.
- Taekyung asked Cheongpung to use his full strength; Cheongpung activated the Zaha Divine Technique and Sword Energy, but Taekyung asked him to suppress the Sword Energy.
- Cheongpung considers Jin Mukyung and Jin Taekyung the strongest people he has met since leaving Huashan.
- Cheongpung perceives Taekyung's martial arts as rough and unrefined but identifies his naturally emerging aura as Wildness.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 암향표 as “Dark Fragrance Drift,” 복호권 as “Crouching Tiger Fist,” and 천근추 as “Thousand-Catty Drop.”
- Render 야성 as “Wildness” when Cheongpung identifies the defining quality of Taekyung's martial arts.
- Retain the established Cheongpung-to-Taekyung address “Benefactor” for 은인 and the established Jin Family's Manoeuvre Technique rendering for 진무보법.

# Chapter 872

## Plot

Taekyung realizes the gaunt guide is the Emperor, a Supreme Peak master who has concealed his strength and identity even from Ma Sanbao. The Emperor probes what Hong Jin told Taekyung, briefly surrounds him with hidden guards, then dismisses them and corrects his manner of address. Prince Shangshan obeys the Emperor’s summons and prostrates himself; the Emperor questions him about his age and martial training, recalls their family’s martial tradition, and says he will take care of him, leaving Taekyung wary of the Emperor’s intentions.

## Continuity

- The gaunt Supreme Peak guide is the Emperor, who has hidden his martial strength and other information from Ma Sanbao; he knows Taekyung is connected to the Slaughter Saint.
- The Emperor’s intentions toward Prince Shangshan remain suspect: he regards the twelve-year-old as a potential threat but says he will take care of him.
- Ma Sanbao is leading a group seeking to enthrone Prince Shangshan; Taekyung has not accepted Ma Sanbao’s offer to help.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 황공 as “stand in fear and awe” in the Emperor’s demanded form of deference.

# Chapter 149

## Plot

Two Confucian scholars visiting Xi’an plan to climb Huashan and Lotus Peak, but one nearly falls from exhaustion and is saved by an ordinary-looking young man waiting for companions.

The young man is Baek Museong, Huashan’s Lone Crane and the first of its Three Plum Blossom Elites. His junior disciples Chulwoo and Eunhyang arrive after clashing with the Black Serpent Sect, a Xi’an gambling-den gang, and taking its money and a jade hairpin. Baek forces them to return the property and apologizes to the innkeeper on their behalf.

Huashan has been sealed after Sword Saint Mae Jonghak entered the sleeping Sect Leader’s quarters, left a dagger and a handwritten note, and disappeared. Following a messenger pigeon from Shanxi Province, Huashan’s leaders dispatched the Three Plum Blossom Elites. Baek had met Cheongpung ten years earlier and looks forward to seeing how he has grown.

## Continuity

- Baek Museong is a first-generation Huashan disciple known as Huashan’s Lone Crane and the first of the Three Plum Blossom Elites.
- Chulwoo and Eunhyang are Baek Museong’s junior disciples and fellow members of the Three Plum Blossom Elites; both are notorious troublemakers who caused trouble with the Black Serpent Sect while traveling.
- Huashan was sealed after Mae Jonghak secretly entered the Sect Leader’s quarters, left a dagger and handwritten letter, and went into seclusion; the search for his residence remains ongoing.
- Baek Museong met Cheongpung ten years ago and is now traveling with the other Plum Blossom Elites to meet him again.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 서안 as “Xi’an” and 서악 as “Western Peak.”
- Render 흑사파 as “Black Serpent Sect,” 화산일학 as “Huashan’s Lone Crane,” 매화삼절 as “Three Plum Blossom Elites,” and 매화검수 as “Plum Blossom Swordsmen.”
- Retain “Senior Brother” for 대사형; render 큰 오라버니 as “Big Brother” when Eunhyang deliberately uses the familiar alternative.

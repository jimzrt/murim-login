# Chapter 723

## Plot

Under the Sacred Rain, Nanman rapidly restores the devastated Nanman Beast Palace and abolishes the Great Chieftain system, leaving the Palace Lord as its sole authority while retaining the Tribal Grand Council. Yohi is pardoned for helping evacuate the Outer Palace and becomes an early advocate of the reform. Yayul Mok gives Jin Taekyung the newly written Mother Goddess Scripture, which records Nanman's history around the Earth Mother Goddess, grants Jin the Title Apostle of the Earth Mother Goddess, and marks his Religious Reformation achievement. As Jin prepares to leave Nanman, Yayul Cheok gathers the Nanman people at the North Gate and formally petitions the Fire Dragon Pavilion Master to admit Nanman into the Murim Alliance.

## Continuity

- The Sacred Rain continues to grant Nanman's people vitality and enhanced physical ability while restoring life to the land, though its effects will end when the rain stops.
- The Nanman Beast Palace's Inner Palace was devastated and hundreds of Outer Palace houses and pavilions collapsed, but restoration is nearing completion.
- The Great Chieftain system has been abolished with the agreement of the Miao people and the other four great tribes.
- The Nanman Beast Palace now operates under the sole authority of the Palace Lord, while the Tribal Grand Council remains to handle unforeseen crises.
- Yohi was pardoned after helping evacuate Outer Palace tribespeople and was the first to call for abolishing the Great Chieftain system.
- The Earth Mother Goddess is Nanman's sole publicly recognized deity, and the Beast Miao King serves as both Palace Lord and priest.
- The Mother Goddess Scripture presents the Earth Mother Goddess as Nanman's ancient mother goddess and records her apostles, including Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, the Fire Dragon Pavilion members, and Namho.
- Yayul Cheok formally petitioned Jin Taekyung, as Fire Dragon Pavilion Master, for Nanman's admission into the Murim Alliance.

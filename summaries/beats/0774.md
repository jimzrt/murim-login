# Chapter 774

## Plot

Jin Taekyung dreams of his early Hunter days, remembering how Hong Cheonsu saved him and became like an older brother before sacrificing himself for Jin years later. After waking, Jin confirms with Team Leader Choi that preparations are complete, resolves to act despite the lingering effects of Broken Body, and leaves with the Peace and Ares Guild Hunters while his concealed companion remains silent.

At the First National Assembly Hall, Michael Silbert revisits the founding of the original World Hunter Federation and the empty central space once occupied by Sky. Convinced that Cheon Taemin is gone, Michael declares that he will take Sky's place and ascend to the throne, but an arriving voice interrupts him with contempt.

## Continuity

- Jin's unresolved Broken Body continues to consume part of his strength, but he has committed to carrying out today's plan regardless.
- Team Leader Choi has completed preparations, and Peace and Ares Guild Hunters are moving with Jin toward the World Hunter Federation's inaugural ceremony.
- Jin's concealed companion remains completely silent, and Jin refuses to let him leave despite that silence.
- The new World Hunter Federation's inaugural ceremony is taking place at the First National Assembly Hall.
- Michael intends to occupy Cheon Taemin's former central position in the World Hunter Federation and present himself as humanity's next leader.

# Chapter 1155

## Plot

News reports that Russia has surrendered to Morgoth, while monster attacks and Gate mutations drive magical power levels toward those of the Great Cataclysm. On a live broadcast, a doctor argues that humanity still has a 5.2 percent chance of defeating Morgoth; a commentator argues that Sky and Jin should choose a noble sacrifice. The doctor shoots him, and the broadcast cuts away. Discussing the incident, the Skeleton King and Chuck Hagel conclude that fear is making people more receptive to sacrificing the heroes, and that Jin may choose to give himself up. Hagel says Morgoth is overwhelmingly likely to break his promise and kill them. The Skeleton King has a sudden, unspoken thought.

## Continuity

- Morgoth accepted Russia’s surrender; Russia will disarm and retain its current government, like more than twenty countries that surrendered before it.
- Over the past eleven days, the world averaged more than four Monster Waves and around fifty Gate mutation events per day; magical power distribution rose by 2.8 points today.
- The Skeleton King fears that public support for a so-called noble sacrifice may grow and that Jin Taekyung may willingly sacrifice himself; Chuck Hagel believes Morgoth is overwhelmingly likely to break his promise and kill Jin and Sky.
- The Skeleton King has a sudden thought at the chapter’s end; its content is not revealed.

## Translation Decisions

# Chapter 698

## Plot

The radiance of the Beast King Stone blocks the demonic qi pouring from the rift. The Southern Heaven Demon Empress recognizes the sacred treasure she searched for over ten years and demands that Jin Taekyung surrender it to the Lord of Heaven. Jin and the guardian spirit resist her overwhelming pressure, while Jin orders Yohi to evacuate the tribespeople from the Outer Palace.

Jin and the White Tiger launch an attack on the Southern Heaven Demon Empress with White Flame and the flame-wreathed azure dragon's roar, causing light and darkness to collide across the Nanman Beast Palace. The battle devastates the Outer Palace and sends civilians fleeing. Baeksang saves an old palace attendant he once expelled, heals her, orders her son to escape through the East Gate, and continues toward the Inner Palace to face the moment he has envisioned for decades.

## Continuity

- The Beast King Stone has manifested its radiance and is currently blocking the demonic qi emerging from the rift.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress searched Nanman for the Beast King Stone for more than ten years and now seeks to take it for the Lord of Heaven.
- Jin Taekyung has ordered Yohi to lead the Outer Palace tribespeople to safety while he and the guardian spirit confront the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- Jin Taekyung and the White Tiger have begun a direct attack on the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, but the outcome remains unresolved.
- The clash of light and darkness has devastated the Outer Palace, causing civilian deaths, injuries, and mass evacuation.
- Baeksang has helped a former palace attendant and directed her family toward the East Gate before proceeding toward the Inner Palace.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Beast King Stone for 수왕석 and sacred stone for 신석.
- Use divine artifact for 신물, distinguishing it from the named Beast King Stone.
- Use East Gate for 동문 and palace attendant for 궁인.

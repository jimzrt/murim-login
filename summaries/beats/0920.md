# Chapter 920

## Plot

So Gyo’s single strike kills or incapacitates more than fifty rebel fighters. As the rebels hesitate, she joins her two curved swords into their original form—a bow—and is recognized as the Bow Saint.

The exhausted Emperor asks Baek Yeon to sound the war drum. Drums answer from all directions as a vast imperial force surrounds the rebels, who charge toward the Emperor and his family in an attempt to capture them.

Across the blood-soaked hall, So Gyo recognizes Jin Taekyung as the person she has long sought. The chapter ends as she fires a brilliant shaft of light across the banquet hall.

## Continuity

- So Gyo is the Bow Saint; her two curved swords can join into the bow’s original form.
- So Gyo recognizes Jin Taekyung as the long-sought answer and key she had been searching for.
- A large imperial force has surrounded the rebels, who are charging the Emperor and his family to capture them.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord lies captured at Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang’s feet; Ma Sanbao is missing.

# Chapter 110

## Plot

Cheol Mubaek, the Peak-level Tiger of Mount Heng and close friend and peer of Lee Cheonbaek, storms into the Mount Heng Sword Sect's main hall. He rebukes the senior figures for considering rebellion against Cheonbaek's final wishes and nearly suffocates them with his terrifying Scorching Yang Qi. Lee Seowol calms him, then orders the Iron Sword Squad Leader and Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion to address her as Sect Leader rather than Young Lady, asserting the authority she inherited from her father.

While riding toward Mount Heng, Taekyung reviews the Quest requiring him to invite the Mount Heng Sword Sect to the Jin Family during the upcoming Lunar New Year. He confirms that he is now Level 55 and discovers that Mujin has reached Level 38, nearly double his Level from their first meeting. When Taekyung verbally attempts to open Mujin's Status Window, the Quest instead gains a 22-hour time limit: they must reach Mount Heng before it expires or face an irreversible consequence. The exhausted horses force the party to stop at a stream for a one-hour rest, despite their projected arrival being after the deadline.

## Continuity

- Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa are riding toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- The Five-Colored Ghosts and surviving mounted bandits are being taken to a nearby Lower District Sect branch.
- A messenger hawk from the Lower District Sect's Sakju Branch delivered intelligence to Wolhwa.
- The Red Wind Band is moving south with approximately two hundred members.
- The Red Wind Band has crossed Datong and destroyed the Mount Heng Sword Sect's Datong Branch with no survivors.
- Pung Yang is the Red Wind Band Leader and commands his force with ruthless authority.
- Chunsam is a First Rate Lower District Sect martial artist who served as the group's carriage driver.
- Wolhwa uses lethal interrogation to obtain information from hostile mounted bandits.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect has lost nearly eighty percent of its strength in the war with the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect's main hall doors have exploded as the attack begins.
- The Quest concerning the Mount Heng Sword Sect is graded Peak and now requires Taekyung to deliver an invitation to the Jin Family during the upcoming Lunar New Year.
- Cheol Mubaek is a Peak master known as the Tiger of Mount Heng and a close friend and peer of Lee Cheonbaek.
- Cheol Mubaek's powerful Scorching Yang Qi can heat the main hall until nearby people struggle to breathe, but he can suppress it at Lee Seowol's request.
- Lee Seowol is the current Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect and publicly rejects being addressed as Young Lady.
- Cheol Mubaek is Lee Seowol's paternal uncle and feels responsible for protecting her.
- The Quest requires Taekyung to reach the Mount Heng Sword Sect within 22 hours; being late is stated to be irreversible.
- Taekyung is Level 55, while Hyuk Mujin is Level 38 after rising from Level 20 over roughly two months.
- The party's horses are exhausted after a forced march and require a one-hour rest.

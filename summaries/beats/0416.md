# Chapter 416

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Lee Jungryong, and Wu Heixing break through the monster army and enter the Arch Lich's city stronghold, where they discover that the city is incompletely transforming into a massive Gate. The System creates the Quest One Who Returned from Death and disables Login until it ends; the three Hunters move stealthily through the fog toward the Arch Lich, while Jin reveals that he is convinced Lee and Wu are more interested in attacking him from behind than fighting the Arch Lich.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung, Lee Jungryong, and Wu Heixing have crossed the battlefield and entered the city that serves as the Arch Lich's stronghold.
- The city is being transformed into one enormous Gate by the Arch Lich's anchored mana, but the transformation is not yet complete.
- The Quest One Who Returned from Death is active, and Jin cannot use the Login function until it ends.
- The three Hunters are conserving their strength and avoiding monsters while searching the city for the Arch Lich.
- Based on Minwoo's warning, Jin is convinced that Lee and Wu intend to betray him; Lee stops after Jin voices the accusation.
- The Skeleton Warlord claims Korea as his homeland and urges Jin to turn back, reporting dizziness and nausea as they approach the city.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 게이트화 as Gate transformation.
- Render 어둠에 잠식된 도시 as City Consumed by Darkness and 죽음에서 돌아온 자 as One Who Returned from Death.
- Keep the established renderings Arch Lich, suicide squad, Force, and Formation J, with Formation J's joke rendered as “Just fucking fight.”

# Chapter 851

## Plot

As Hong Jin rides through Taiyuan, he reflects on his impoverished past and the Jin Family’s rise. He tells his coachman to alert the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi branch and use its information network to find Jin Taekyung, whom he trusts to protect Prince Shangshan. An imperial party arrives to take Hong Jin away; the prince is unharmed for now, and Hong Jin goes to him.

## Continuity

- Hong Jin knows the coachman is connected to the Lower District Sect’s Shanxi branch and asks him to report the situation and find Jin Taekyung.
- Hong Jin identifies Jin Taekyung as the most trustworthy person in Murim to protect Prince Shangshan.
- An imperial party presents a decree and takes Hong Jin; Prince Shangshan is unharmed at that moment, and Hong Jin heads toward him.
- Taiyuan’s Jin Family has become a dominant power in the city; local residents voice concern about ominous Murim rumors and activity in the northern plateau.

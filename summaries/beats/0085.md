# Chapter 85

## Plot

Im Changsoo, humiliated by Jin Taekyung and resentful over the money and byproducts he owes, plots revenge in the Boss Zone. He intends to exploit Taekyung's apparent feelings for Song Song, seize the camera, and use the Level 70 Minotaur Warrior to exhaust Taekyung before staging a lasting humiliation.

Taekyung kills the Minotaur Warrior with a single spear technique, receives a Level Up, and completes the B-rank Gate Clear Quest. Afterward, he asks Song Song for a heal and invites her to dinner, but she turns down both steak and a salad bar with a deadpan vegetarian-and-carnivore exchange.

## Continuity

- Im Changsoo's planned Boss Zone ambush and humiliation scheme is rendered moot when Taekyung kills the Level 70 Minotaur Warrior in one blow.
- Taekyung completes the B-rank Gate Clear Quest, receives a Level Up, and has the Quest Success Reward deposited into his Inventory.
- Song Song is identified as a C-rank healer and declines Taekyung's dinner invitations.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일섬 as One Annihilation and 미노타우로스 대전사 as Minotaur Warrior.
- Render 관심법 as mind-reading technique and preserve the barbarian-against-barbarian wording for 이이제이.

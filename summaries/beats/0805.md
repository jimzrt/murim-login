# Chapter 805

## Plot

Jin Taekyung charges the three S-rank commanders, tests their strength, and wounds the Manticore Lord, but their coordinated counterattack injures his thigh. The Skeleton King joins him, and the two choose opponents as the monster army advances. They discover Yamamoto has retreated to the front ranks beside Choi Minwoo. While the Skeleton King raises a thousand undead, the monster army breaks through their small defensive line. Jin confronts the Manticore Lord, which speaks of The Prophet as being everywhere and nowhere; Jin prepares to attack it and the others.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is fighting in the Rub’ al Khali against a monster force exceeding ten thousand; he is wounded in the thigh by the Manticore Lord.
- Jin identifies the Manticore Lord as exceptionally strong and cunning, and a coordinated attack by it, the Lycanthrope Champion, and the Death Knight Legion Commander can overwhelm his defense.
- The Skeleton King can maintain a force of one thousand undead while fighting and directing them; he chooses to fight the Death Knight while Jin faces the Manticore Lord.
- The monster army obeys an unseen authority and bypasses Jin and the Skeleton King to attack the Hunters; the Prophet’s location remains unknown.
- Jin trusts Magic Johnson and the others to handle the aerial monsters; Yamamoto has withdrawn to the front ranks beside Choi Minwoo.

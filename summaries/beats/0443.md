# Chapter 443

## Plot

After ten days of sailing, Jin Taekyung's group reaches Hubei aboard Mu Song's ships, where locals and officials show unusual hostility toward the Yangtze River Channel League. Mu Song learns that nearby League strongholds have sent no word despite knowing of his arrival and orders an investigation. A Hubei official attempts to arrest the group, but Jin Wikyung uses his connection to the newly appointed Provincial Administration Commissioner Yi Hongcheon to force an apology and reveal Jeok Cheongang's identity as the Fire King. Before the group can leave the harbor, martial artists from the Zhuge Clan arrive.

## Continuity

- The group has reached Hubei after ten days of travel with Mu Song and the Water Dragon Stronghold fleet.
- The Yangtze River Channel League's nearby Hubei strongholds have failed to contact Mu Song, prompting him to send men to investigate.
- Jin Wikyung has established political ties with Yi Hongcheon, the newly appointed Hubei Provincial Administration Commissioner, and can invoke that connection when dealing with local officials.
- A group of Zhuge Clan martial artists has arrived at the Hubei harbor.

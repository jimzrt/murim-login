# Chapter 122

## Plot

Wolhwa directs the Lower District Sect's Jeongyang and Honju branches in the aftermath of the battle. Forty-seven mounted bandits survived, but the available medicine can save only about thirty; the rest will die or later be sold into slavery. The Lower District Sect begins spreading a favorable rumor that the two Jin brothers defeated Pung Yang and rescued the Mount Heng Sword Sect, expecting the Jin Family of Taiyuan to force the remaining bandits back to the plateau within five days.

Wolhwa investigates Pung Yang's sudden increase in strength and reflects on her Master's advice to observe people who defy prediction. She then visits Jin Taekyung's pavilion, where Hyuk Mujin reports that Taekyung has been muttering incomprehensible modern-world terms. Hearing Taekyung repeat “school lunch, high schooler, clank, clank,” Wolhwa withdraws, once again judging him completely unpredictable.

Two days later, Taekyung has spent most of his time learning to control his newly acquired Scorching Yang Qi. Lee Seowol has not returned after proposing marriage, and Taekyung decides to reject her because she is seventeen, the proposal is political, and he loves Song Song. Jin Mukyung has recovered enough to move independently; when Taekyung reveals that the proposal included the Blood Wolf Sword Technique, Blood Wolf Footwork, and Shura Annihilating Fist, Mukyung immediately orders him to marry her.

## Continuity

- Wolhwa can issue mobilization orders to more than thirty Lower District Sect branches across Shanxi Province.
- Forty-seven mounted bandits survived the battle; the Lower District Sect can save only about thirty with its current medicine, and the survivors will ultimately be sold as labor or arena slaves.
- The Lower District Sect is spreading the claim that Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung defeated Pung Yang and rescued the Mount Heng Sword Sect so the remaining mounted bandits will withdraw.
- Two days have passed since Lee Seowol proposed marriage, and she has not returned; Taekyung has decided to reject the proposal.
- Taekyung loves Song Song and considers marriage to Lee Seowol impossible despite the offered martial arts.
- Jin Mukyung recovered sufficiently after his injuries to move around without assistance.
- Taekyung is spending most of his time learning to control his newly acquired Scorching Yang Qi.

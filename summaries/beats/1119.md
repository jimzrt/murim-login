# Chapter 1119

## Plot

At the East Gate, Hyuk Mujin fights through fear and exhaustion, remembering Jin Taekyung’s example and refusing to retreat. After killing a Dark Heaven fanatic, he presses toward the jiangshi sorcerer while Song Ilseom, Ju Hwaran, Sama Pyo, and Taishan help clear his way. Taishan throws Mujin toward the sorcerer, and Mujin beheads him with the last of his strength.

The ritual bell falls from the sorcerer’s hand, and roughly three hundred surviving monsters stop moving. A tremendous surge of qi then blasts open the East Gate. A shadow with overwhelming power confronts Mujin, asks his name, and strikes him in the chest; Mujin’s vision goes dark.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Mujin beheaded the jiangshi sorcerer at the East Gate; the ritual bell fell, and roughly three hundred surviving monsters stopped moving.
- A powerful, unidentified figure opened the East Gate with a surge of qi and struck Hyuk Mujin in the chest. Mujin’s condition is unknown.

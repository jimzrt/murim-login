# Chapter 732

## Plot

Jin Taekyung follows Team Leader Choi's request and publicly posts the prepared file through his enormous personal social-media account in front of Huginn, then needles the Odin Guild messenger with dinner jokes and insults. Huginn threatens Jin and briefly releases an aura powerful enough to rival an S-rank Hunter, but Team Leader Choi's warning prevents the confrontation from escalating; Huginn apologizes and withdraws.

After Huginn leaves, Team Leader Choi explains that the messenger may have come to confirm Cheon Taemin's absence rather than merely oppose the Mana Cultivation Method. They discuss the possibility that the secret leaked through Lee Jungryong's Grand Mage-level assistant who constructed Area A. Choi admits that Jin is his Joker card, and the two leave to eat kimchi stew while Jin's phone continues buzzing with sharing notifications.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has publicly posted the prepared file through his personal social-media account, whose follower count exceeds five hundred million, making the Mana Cultivation Method's release an immediate public event.
- Huginn delivered Odin Guild's opposition, threatened Jin and Team Leader Choi with severe consequences, demonstrated S-rank-level power, and withdrew after Choi warned him not to act rashly.
- Team Leader Choi suspects Huginn came to confirm Cheon Taemin's absence and that Odin may consider Cheon Taemin's condition at least as important as the Mana Cultivation Method.
- Only six people currently know that Cheon Taemin is in a vegetative state: Jin Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, Song Song, Im Kkeokjeong, the Skeleton King, and Magic Johnson; the other known holders of the secret are dead.
- A Grand Mage-level mage constructed Ares Guild's Area A, and Choi and Jin recognize that this mage may have been connected to Lee Jungryong and may have enabled the secret to leak.
- Team Leader Choi has become more willing to bluff and confront Odin because he regards Jin Taekyung as a decisive Joker card.
- Odin Guild is a massive power with potentially hidden S-rank Hunters, and Jin judges that the other major Guilds may side with it.

# Chapter 236

## Plot

After four months away, Wipeng returned to Jin Wikyung and reported that the Seven-Route Army had stabilized Northern Gaoyuan. Seven battles killed or captured two thousand mounted bandits, annihilated five major groups, and forced more than ten others to surrender. The Lower District Sect provided decisive intelligence support, while Temur and Chinggen avoided conflict after learning of Jeok Cheongang's reputation; having absorbed the remnants of Black Sand, the Human Butcher, and the Heavenly Wind Band, they now sought friendship with the Jin Family and offered furs and plundered treasure.

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng also discussed the Seongun Escort Bureau's collapse, the Jin Family's expanding trade with Shaolin and Namgung through Mount Song and Hefei, and the approaching Star-Array Grand Banquet where the brothers were expected to reunite. At Mount Jiuhua, one year into Taekyung's absence, the shaggy-haired young man joked about grilling meat over the Fire Gate Clan's sacred flame, provoking Jeok Cheongang to strike and scold him as peaceful Mount Jiuhua became noisy again.

## Continuity

- Wipeng returned to the Jin Family of Taiyuan after four months commanding the campaign to stabilize Northern Gaoyuan.
- The Jin Dragon Squad is the Jin Family's foremost armed force; Wipeng is its Commander, and Hyuk Mujin served as acting Deputy Commander during Wipeng's absence.
- Hyuk Mujin completed his first mission as acting Deputy Commander; Wipeng considers him serviceable, quick to learn, loyal, and possessed of considerable martial talent despite his incessant chatter.
- The Seven-Route Army attacked from seven directions, killed or captured two thousand mounted bandits, annihilated five major groups, and received the surrender of more than ten others.
- The Lower District Sect supported the Northern Gaoyuan campaign with surveillance, false information, and traps; Jin Wikyung considers Wolhwa's strategic ability more frightening than her beauty.
- Temur and Chinggen each command two thousand riders, absorbed the remnants of the defeated mounted-bandit powers, and now seek friendship with the Jin Family.
- Jeok Cheongang's killing of Black Sand and the Human Butcher, followed by his destruction of the Heavenly Wind Band, created the power vacuum that enabled Temur and Chinggen to rise.
- The Seongun Escort Bureau is effectively now the Jin Family Escort Bureau after Woo Hwangtae's family fled following the discovery that they had diverted military supplies through bribed officials.
- The Jin Family is conducting favorable trade cooperation with Shaolin and Namgung through a caravan route passing Mount Song and Hefei.
- Jin Wikyung has selected the title The Sleeping Dragon Enters Service for a painting he plans to complete.
- A year has passed since Jin Taekyung left for training with Jeok Cheongang; the brothers are expected to meet at the Star-Array Grand Banquet in Henan after the ground thaws.
- At Mount Jiuhua, Taekyung remains under Jeok Cheongang's harsh training and has grown long-haired, bearded, and accustomed to iron balls hanging from all four limbs.

# Chapter 1055

## Plot

Sama Pyo returns to the battlefield and finds his dying father, Sima Gong. Sima Gong admits that he betrayed the Gansu sects and the Kongtong Sect for practical gain and survival, then rebukes Pyo for asking about the past instead of the burdens awaiting him as heir. Pyo proposes saving the Black Dragon Demon Gate by sacrificing its leaders and offering compensation to those seeking revenge. Sima Gong approves of his heir’s resolve and tells him to strike; a flash of light crosses his eyes, but the chapter does not confirm the outcome.

## Continuity

- Sima Gong admits that he manipulated the defense of Dunhuang and passed information to the Blood-Sword Demon Lord; Sama Pyo proposes taking responsibility by sacrificing the Gate’s leaders and compensating those seeking revenge.
- Sima Gong recognizes Sama Pyo as his rightful heir and tells him to strike; the result is not shown.
- The battle is effectively won by the allied forces; the Blood-Sword Demon Lord and the mages are dead, the Grand Mage has disappeared, and the Black Ghosts are gone.
- The Grand Mage departed for Qinghai on a new mission; the identity of the other servant, the Lord of Heaven’s identity and connection to Asmodeus, the missing Kongtong survivors, and the mysterious green light remain unresolved.

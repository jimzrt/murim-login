# Chapter 229

## Plot

Jin Taekyung decides to leave immediately with Jeok Cheongang for a year of martial-arts training in Anhui. Jin Wikyung entrusts Taekyung to Jeok and privately reserves an important discussion with him before departure.

Jin Mukyung continues an exacting training regimen in isolation, performing ten thousand sword strikes without internal energy and adding one hundred strikes for every mistake. Taekyung tells him that he and Cheongpung will attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet in one year, prompting Mukyung to regain his composure and resume a perfect strike.

Hyuk Mujin, Cheongpung, Wolhwa, Lee Seowol, and the Three Plum Blossom Elites gather at the main gate to see Taekyung off. Cheongpung claims Mae Jonghak has permitted him to become Jeok’s Disciple and follow Taekyung; Baek Museong plans either to escort Cheongpung back to Huashan or search the Central Plains for Mae’s whereabouts. Taekyung reconciles with Chulwoo while warning him not to make advances toward Eunhyang, and Lee Seowol asks Taekyung to address her as Young Lady rather than Sect Leader. Jeok then seizes Taekyung and departs with him while Jin Wikyung tearfully wishes him a safe return.

## Continuity

- Taekyung leaves immediately with Jeok Cheongang for a year of martial-arts training in Anhui.
- Jin Wikyung entrusts Taekyung to Jeok and has an important private matter to discuss with Jeok before their departure.
- Taekyung tells Jin Mukyung that he and Cheongpung will attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet in one year.
- Jin Mukyung trains in an unlit cave with ten thousand daily sword strikes, no internal energy, and one hundred additional strikes for each mistake; his defeat by Cheongpung remains the impetus for his training.
- Cheongpung claims Mae Jonghak has given permission for him to become Jeok Cheongang’s Disciple and follow Taekyung, though Baek Museong questions how he obtained permission while Mae has been missing for over a month.
- Baek Museong plans either to escort Cheongpung back to Huashan or search the Central Plains for Mae Jonghak, and will leave when Huashan sends a message.
- Hyuk Mujin packed Taekyung’s travel luggage at Jin Wikyung’s order; Wolhwa learned of the departure through the Lower District Sect.
- Taekyung warned Chulwoo not to make advances toward Eunhyang, and Chulwoo agreed.
- Lee Seowol asked Taekyung to call her Young Lady rather than Sect Leader during their farewell.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일만격 as ten thousand strikes and 추가 백 회 as one hundred additional strikes.
- Render 안휘성 as Anhui, 행낭 as travel bag or luggage, and 반 시진 as half a shichen.
- Render 소저 as Young Lady in Lee Seowol’s requested form of address; retain Young Lady Lee for Taekyung’s address to her.

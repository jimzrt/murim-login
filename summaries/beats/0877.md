# Chapter 877

## Plot

Jin Taekyung explains that Aehyang’s medicinal decoction and the Emperor’s secrecy make it highly likely she is pregnant, and Hong Jin and Hyuk Mujin recognize that a new heir could put Prince Shangshan in danger.

Hong Jin recounts how the Fourth Prince spared him after the purge of the late Emperor’s family and how the late Emperor entrusted Shangshan to his care. Taekyung suspects the late Emperor may have suffered from Blood Soul Gu, as the City Lord of Sichuan Province did, but the cause remains unconfirmed.

Hong Jin plans to contact Ma Sanbao about Shangshan’s danger, and Taekyung says the Myriad-Poison Ring should protect the prince. Taekyung also makes a personal request of Ma Sanbao. In Hangzhou, Jeok Cheongang receives a message from someone claiming to bring word from the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.

## Continuity

- Hong Jin believes Aehyang is very likely pregnant, but her pregnancy and the Emperor’s intentions remain unconfirmed.
- Hong Jin says the late Emperor entrusted Prince Shangshan to him shortly before dying while mentally confused; Hong Jin suspects this may resemble the City Lord of Sichuan Province’s symptoms, but the cause is unknown.
- Hong Jin plans to contact Ma Sanbao about Shangshan’s possible danger, and Taekyung has made an additional personal request of Ma whose contents are not revealed.
- Taekyung believes the Myriad-Poison Ring may protect Shangshan from Blood Soul Gu, but its effectiveness against that poison has not been established.
- Jeok Cheongang is working under cover at the Hyuk Family Textile Shop in Hangzhou and has received a message from a visitor claiming to carry word from the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain “Blood Soul Gu,” “Myriad-Poison Ring,” “Formless Ultimate Poison,” and “Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.”
- Keep the suspected pregnancy and any connection between Blood Soul Gu and the late Emperor explicitly uncertain.

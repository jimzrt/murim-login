# Chapter 434

## Plot

Jin reunites with his mother, Hayeon, and his allies before departing China. Chairman Shao Yang confirms that Jin's fifty-trillion bounty is being processed and reveals that Xiao Shen is his grandson, while Faye Chen, Felix, Magic Johnson, and the others offer their farewells. Jin boards the aircraft with his family and companions, then uses Login to depart toward the other world awaiting him.

## Continuity

- Jin's mother and Hayeon are aboard the chartered aircraft with him as he leaves China.
- Chairman Shao Yang has confirmed that Jin's fifty-trillion bounty will be paid officially soon.
- Xiao Shen is Chairman Shao Yang's grandson, and Shao Yang is grateful to Jin for saving him.
- Jin has logged in aboard the departing aircraft and begun returning toward the other world.
- Wei Fenghu continues to grieve Lei Fei's death while maintaining a respectful relationship with Jin.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 최 팀장 as “Team Leader Choi,” 샤오 양 주석 as “Chairman Shao Yang,” 샤오 쉔 as “Xiao Shen,” and 파이 첸 as “Faye Chen.”
- Render 형님 as “hyung” when Xiao Shen addresses Jin.
- Retain Jin's dry, profane humor when discussing the bounty, Felix's insult, and Magic Johnson's attraction to Felix.

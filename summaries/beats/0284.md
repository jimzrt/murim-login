# Chapter 284

## Plot

Lee Jungryong arrives at Park Tae Seop's ruined office without being detected, revealing himself as Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master, one of Korea's two S-rank Hunters, and a Supreme Peak master. He claims Park Tae Seop is merely an acquaintance and Park Jihoon a stranger, while offering to resolve the situation. Taekyung refuses to release them, demands an apology, compensation, and one person's life, and implies that Jihoon must answer for the injuries inflicted on Kkeokjeong. Lee attacks with a red-aura palm, but Taekyung counters with Flame Divine Palm; Taekyung is injured while Lee remains standing. Taekyung then repeatedly stabs and mutilates Jihoon, severing both arms and seriously injuring his leg and shoulder, while Lee is restrained by the dagger at Jihoon's throat. Jihoon cries out to Lee as his Master, revealing their master-Disciple relationship. Taekyung leaves Jihoon alive but warns that his life as a Hunter may be over, and threatens to collapse the building and expose the incident publicly.

## Continuity

- Lee Jungryong is Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master, one of Korea's two S-rank Hunters, and the effective wielder of Ares Guild's authority in place of its Guild Master.
- Lee Jungryong is a Supreme Peak-level martial artist whose palm technique and qi control are comparable to the great demon Yin-Yang Twin Devils encountered at Shaolin.
- Lee Jungryong has known Park Tae Seop since the Great Cataclysm and is Park Jihoon's master; Jihoon addresses him as Master.
- Lee Jungryong gave Park Jihoon his initial orders, while the chapter does not explicitly confirm whether he is the unidentified 'that person' behind the entire operation.
- Taekyung demanded an apology, compensation, and one person's life from Lee Jungryong, then left Park Jihoon alive after severing both arms and inflicting severe injuries to his leg and shoulder.
- Lee Jungryong attacked Taekyung after their negotiation failed, but stopped advancing while Taekyung held Jihoon at knifepoint.
- Taekyung threatened to bring down the building and make half of Korea aware of the confrontation if Lee continued.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이정룡 as Lee Jungryong.
- Render 부길드장 as Vice Guild Master and 스승님/제자 as Master/Disciple, retaining Disciple capitalization for the established martial relationship.
- Retain the established renderings Ares Guild, S-rank Hunter, Supreme Peak, and Flame Divine Palm.

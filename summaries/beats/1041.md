# Chapter 1041

## Plot

Jin Taekyung breaks through the white-robed mages’ varied spells and binding magic, with the System reporting improved Qi Sense and freer control of qi. He reaches the Grand Mage, revealed to be a veiled woman; her barrier makes a single failed strike enough to let her escape. She says she prepared more, then unleashes an enormous surge of energy.

## Continuity

- The white-robed mages can use varied Magic beyond body enhancement and Gravity Magic; Jin’s repeated dispellings make their energy reverse and incapacitate them.
- Nineteen of the twenty white-robed mages have fallen. The Grand Mage remains, separated from Jin by a powerful barrier; Jin believes he has only one chance to break through before she escapes.
- The Grand Mage is a veiled woman whose name and face Jin does not know. She leads the white-robed mages and has just released a vast amount of energy.
- Jin’s Qi Sense improves slightly, and he gains somewhat freer control of qi after dispelling Magic.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대마도사 as Grand Mage.
- Render the named spells 파이어 볼, 스톤 월, 매직 애로우 as Fire Ball, Stone Wall, and Magic Arrow.

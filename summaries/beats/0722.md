# Chapter 722

## Plot

The Earth Mother Goddess's sudden appearance transforms Nanman's religious landscape into a mass movement bordering on fanaticism. Jin Taekyung teaches the Beast Miao King to use his new position as the goddess's sole priest to unify Nanman, reframe the traitors' punishment as divine judgment, and convene the Tribal Grand Council. The council absorbs the old local faiths beneath the Earth Mother Goddess and begins planning a scripture. After ten days in the underground prison, the traitorous tribal chieftains are publicly executed while the Sacred Rain washes away their blood. The thirty-two divided tribes finally unite as one people of Nanman, openly preparing for the coming Great War.

## Continuity

- The Earth Mother Goddess has become Nanman's public supreme deity and the focus of a rapidly spreading, fanatical faith.
- The Beast Miao King has embraced his role as the Earth Mother Goddess's sole priest and is using religion to unify and reform Nanman.
- The Tribal Grand Council accepted the Earth Mother Goddess while incorporating the old local gods beneath her authority; a scripture recording Nanman's history and the gods' words is being developed.
- More than twenty traitorous tribal chieftains were executed after ten days in the underground prison, and their bodies were thrown to beasts.
- The formerly divided thirty-two tribes now publicly regard themselves as one united Nanman people.
- The new sacred stone remains in Nanman under Muyaho's guardianship, while the Nanman Beast Palace continues rebuilding after the rift disaster.

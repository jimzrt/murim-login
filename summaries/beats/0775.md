# Chapter 775

## Plot

Jin confronts Michael Silbert at the inaugural ceremony and challenges him to explain the atrocities he has committed. Michael detects the concealed companion accompanying Jin, reveals his brutal childhood in Paris's Tenth Arrondissement, his criminal past, and his survival of the Great Battle of Paris, but treats that history as part of his heroic image and demands submission from those who resist him. Jin answers with pity and disgust rather than fear, rejects Michael's intimidation, opens the conference hall, and forces the inaugural ceremony to begin as hundreds of attendees enter.

## Continuity

- Michael Silbert's pre-awakening life included abandonment by his father, an alcoholic and drug-addicted mother who died in 2012, several criminal convictions, and records he later erased.
- Michael awakened in 2020 during the Great Cataclysm and was the lone survivor found amid the dead after the week-long Great Battle of Paris; he later became a public hero who saved Paris twice.
- Michael's true strength remains concealed, but his qi control is practiced enough to resemble a Supreme Peak master of the Murim rather than an ordinary Hunter.
- Jin now regards Michael with pity and disgust instead of fear and openly refuses to submit to his coercion.
- The inaugural ceremony of the new World Hunter Federation has begun, with hundreds of attendees entering the conference hall.
- Jin's Broken Body continues to cause severe internal pain while he confronts Michael.
- Michael can sense Jin's concealed companion, though the companion remains imperceptible in form.
- Jin continues withholding the discovered clue from Baek because Michael Silbert may have surveillance or a mole near the President.
- The Skeleton King remains concealed with Jin and silent.
- Michael is positioning himself at the center of the World Hunter Federation while Cheon Taemin remains absent.

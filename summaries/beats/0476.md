# Chapter 476

## Plot

Jin Taekyung realizes that the Mutated Water God Dragon must be hunted rather than fought honorably, suppresses its Fear with qi, and rallies Jeok Cheongang, Mungyeong, and Cheongpung into an all-DPS raid. He orders them to spread out, coordinates their movements while the dragon is lured onto land, argues with Jeok and Mungyeong over his slang-heavy commands, and finally gives the signal for all four to strike.

## Continuity

- Taekyung consciously reclaims both his Hunter and Murim identities and takes command of the battle as a coordinated monster hunt.
- Taekyung can use a powerful wave of qi to push back the Mutated Water God Dragon's Fear and free the others from its immediate pressure.
- The battle remains active on land: Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, Cheongpung, and Mungyeong have surrounded the dragon and launched their coordinated attack.
- The dragon's Berserk Status increases all abilities but clouds combat judgment, and its rampage continues.
- Taekyung possesses the Heavenly Martial Physique, giving him superhuman physical strength independent of his accumulated internal energy and martial enlightenment.
- The dragon's scales are so durable that early Peak Sword Energy cannot properly cut them; Force is normally required to split them and damage what lies beneath.
- Cheongpung remains resistant to Fear, while Mungyeong and Jeok Cheongang remain at least partially affected but continue fighting.
- The dragon possesses extreme speed, immense durability, black scales, and centuries of accumulated qi, and its physical power has sharply increased.
- The dragon's anger manifests as violent storms, lightning, swollen river whirlpools, and other responses from the surrounding water and weather.
- The dragon's black pupil accompanies Breath: it forms massive water spheres, can fire them repeatedly in rapid succession, and has now shown a corresponding increase in physical strength.
- Zhuge Feng remains under cover protecting the others, and the severely injured Dongting Fisherman remains alive for interrogation about Dark Heaven.
- An unidentified figure remains on the dragon's head and can tear out its whiskers barehanded.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 광폭화 as Berserk and preserve the System Status distinction.
- Render 레이드, 힐러, 탱커, 딜러, and 원딜충 with consistent raid-game terminology, while preserving Taekyung's profane, improvisational combat humor and Jeok Cheongang's confusion over modern slang.
- Render 장수 돌침대 as Jangsu stone bed and retain the explanatory footnote for the brand reference.
- Continue rendering 수염 as whiskers and distinguish the dragon's anomalous qi from Force and Sword Energy.
- Retain jang and geun measurements, and preserve established food renderings such as live-fish sashimi and bone-in sashimi with its explanatory footnote.

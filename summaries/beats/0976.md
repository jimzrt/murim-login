# Chapter 976

## Plot

The Bow Saint’s arrows interrupt the North Heaven Demon Lord’s retreat, and Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang attack him together with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist. The Demon Lord is badly wounded: his armor is shattered, and he tears his impaled shoulder free to escape. He heads for Jin Wikyung, hoping to take him hostage, but finds the Bow Saint blocking his way. Taekyung then emerges from the dust and calls the Demon Lord “Poppy,” leaving him stunned; the confrontation’s outcome is not shown.

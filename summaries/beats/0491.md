# Chapter 491

## Plot

Jin Taekyung survives Mungyeong's first night's poison trial, though hidden weapons left him exhausted, wounded, and poisoned. The Myriad-Poison Ring and Scorching Yang Qi prevented the traps' potent poisons from killing him. Mungyeong then sends Taekyung another poisoned candy through Cheongpung, inflicting Potent Energy-Dispersing Poison and causing his internal energy to dissipate.

A small secret group travels by swift ship to inspect the Water God Dragon's corpse. Perfected Being Hyeongong, Zhuge Feng, Mungyeong, Gung Gibang, and Cheongpung accompany Taekyung, while Jeok Cheongang declines to attend and asks the others not to seek him until everything is finished. The Water God Dragon's spirit has departed, but its enormous body remains in silver-scaled, wounded form after ruling Dongting Lake and the Yangtze for five hundred years. Taekyung recognizes that the raid is over and that the corpse now needs to be disposed of.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is testing Taekyung through successive poison traps; Taekyung survived the first night's hidden weapons and has now been poisoned by candy containing Potent Energy-Dispersing Poison.
- Jeok Cheongang has withdrawn from the Water God Dragon expedition and instructed the group not to look for him until all matters are finished.
- The Water God Dragon's spirit has left its body, while its massive corpse remains at the place where it last lay; its once-black scales are now silver and its body is covered in wounds.

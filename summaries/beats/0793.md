# Chapter 793

## Plot

Jin arrives at the desert site and reunites with Xiao Shen and the Skeleton King, who has defeated the Monster Wave with more than a thousand undead and no casualties. The Skeleton King says something he could not identify appeared and vanished without a trace. He shows Jin and the others twenty desiccated bodies; their deaths resemble Siegfried Wassmann’s death a month earlier.

Jin realizes that Michael Silbert was not the one who visited Siegfried on the day he died: the System’s still-incomplete [An Unknown Death] Quest contradicts the assumption that Michael killed him. Back at the main force, Jin confronts Huginn and asks whether The Prophet killed Siegfried; the chapter ends before Huginn answers.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton King and his undead army defeated the desert Monster Wave in under thirty minutes without casualties; more than a thousand undead remain with him.
- Twenty people were found dead in the desert, their bodies dried out in the same manner as Siegfried Wassmann’s body a month earlier. The Skeleton King could not identify what caused the deaths or find its trace.
- Jin’s Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] remains incomplete and requires him to discover how and by whom Siegfried Wassmann died. Jin now knows Michael Silbert was not the visitor who killed Siegfried on the day of his death.
- Jin asks Huginn whether The Prophet killed Siegfried; no answer is given in this chapter.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the System Quest title as “An Unknown Death” and its Grade as “Supreme Peak.”
- Keep “magical power” distinct from mana; the Skeleton King is described as an expert on magical power.

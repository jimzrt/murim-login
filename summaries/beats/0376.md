# Chapter 376

## Plot

Mungyeong and his Disciple Dong Feng revisit their first meeting and reveal that Mungyeong is the Divine Physician and former Slaughter Saint, while Dong Feng became his physician Disciple after losing his family to an epidemic. Dong Feng urges Mungyeong to use his medical skills to prevent the great war he foresees, but Mungyeong insists that he has left Murim and will only treat the wounded. At Chengdu's western harbor, Jin Taekyung's party prepares to depart aboard Mu Song's ship, and an unidentified boy arrives asking to come aboard.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is the legendary Divine Physician and former Slaughter Saint; he has sworn never to kill again and intends to remain focused on treating patients.
- Dong Feng is Mungyeong's long-serving Disciple and a physician; he lost his wife and two children to an epidemic before Mungyeong saved and accepted him.
- Dong Feng foresees a great war and asks Mungyeong to help prevent it, but Mungyeong refuses to return to Murim and leaves him to care for the patients.
- Jin Taekyung's party is departing Chengdu by fast ship after the Hour of the Dog, and an unidentified boy has just arrived seeking passage.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 신의 as Divine Physician and establish Mungyeong as the identity behind the title; render 동봉 as Dong Feng.
- Render 홍무 as Hongwu and 술시 as the Hour of the Dog.
- Retain Slaughter Saint for 살성 and Sichuan Tang Clan for 사천당문.

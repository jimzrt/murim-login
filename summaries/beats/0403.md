# Chapter 403

## Plot

Wei Fenghu leads more than twenty military transports and their fighter escorts to the devastated Western Front, where four thousand Hunters land amid the ruins of the temporary headquarters. They find Jin Taekyung alive after the battle, and Jin tells Wei that no monsters remain and delivers Lei Fei's final expressions of love and apology.

Wei recognizes Lei Fei's death and entrusts Lei Fei's sword, Hero's Soul, to Jin. The sword grants Jin Hero's Power after recognizing his upright character, then rejects him and removes the effect when he imagines using it to cut down anyone it deems unworthy.

Jin watches the unconscious Team Leader Choi and Shao Shen recover from accumulated fatigue, then questions the gloomy Skeleton Warlord about Lei Fei's soul. The Skeleton Warlord confirms that only a faint trace remains, admits that it has no memories of its own past, and is heartened by Jin's teasing reassurance before Jin is summoned by a minister of state.

## Continuity

- Wei Fenghu and more than four thousand Hunters have reached the Western Front, where Jin Taekyung annihilated the monster army and survived the ten-percent teleportation.
- Wei Fenghu understands that Lei Fei died after completing his final mission; Jin delivered Lei Fei's love, apology, and praise, and Wei entrusted Lei Fei's sword to Jin.
- Lei Fei's wife and daughter were moved to safety while he was missing.
- Team Leader Choi and Shao Shen remain unconscious because of accumulated physical and mental fatigue, although their injuries were healed by top-grade potions.
- Hero's Soul is a Supreme Peak sword that can grant Hero's Power to someone it recognizes as having an upright character.
- Hero's Soul rejected Jin and removed Hero's Power when he formed an evil intention, even though the intention was only a joke.
- The Skeleton Warlord absorbed some of the mana released when Lei Fei disappeared and confirms that only a faint trace of Lei Fei's soul remains in Hero's Soul.
- The Skeleton Warlord has no memories of its own past and has begun questioning what kind of being it once was.
- Jin has been summoned by a minister of state after the Western Front battle.
- The wider war against the Arch Lich remains unresolved despite the Western Front victory.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 영웅의 혼 as Hero's Soul and 영웅의 힘 as Hero's Power.
- Keep Supreme Peak as the system Grade for 초절정 and preserve the established System and Item Window terminology.
- Render 최 팀장 as Team Leader Choi and 샤오 쉔 as Shao Shen.
- Render 국무부장 동지 as Comrade Minister of State.
- Preserve Jin's dry, self-mocking voice and the Skeleton Warlord's formal first-person self-reference as this commander.

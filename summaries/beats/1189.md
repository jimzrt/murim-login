# Chapter 1189

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang recognized and destroyed a Black Ghost wearing Jangcheon’s appearance. Facing four more coordinated Black Ghosts and waves of monsters, he formally cast out the illusion of his former Disciple and fought on while protecting Jin Taekyung, who was unharmed on his back.

The four Black Ghosts struck Jeok Cheongang from his blind spots. He twisted aside but was wounded; with the monsters’ numbers and the Black Ghosts’ coordinated attacks turning the fight against him, he prepared to unleash his full power before Taekyung startled him with a sudden remark.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang destroyed a Black Ghost wearing Jangcheon’s appearance; he is now facing four coordinated Black Ghosts and waves of monsters in the mist.
- Jeok Cheongang was wounded while shielding Jin Taekyung, who remains on his back and unharmed.
- The other separated companions’ status remains unknown.

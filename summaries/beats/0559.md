# Chapter 559

## Plot

Magic Johnson arrives by Teleportation and reunites with Jin Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, Im Kkeokjeong, and the Skeleton King, enduring jokes about his visit to an Itaewon gay bar with the Skeleton King after Lee Jungryong’s funeral.

Johnson gives Team Leader Choi a memory chip containing thirty-two videos of recent Gate disasters: two Monster Waves and thirty Mutated Gates. One incident occurred four days earlier near Arizona in the Mojave Desert, killing twenty-two people and injuring thirty-five.

The United States Ministry of National Defense concealed the Mojave incident because it occurred at a restricted military base, despite the cover-up violating the post-Great Cataclysm constitution. Taekyung recognizes the same dilemma surrounding Dark Heaven’s existence.

Johnson reveals that all thirty-two incidents occurred within the past week, confirming that the Gate disasters are accelerating far beyond the known Korean crisis and leaving the room in stunned silence.

## Continuity

- Magic Johnson is one of only three Grand Mages among humanity and has supplied Team Leader Choi with intelligence on concealed US Gate disasters.
- Johnson’s obtained materials contain thirty-two incidents from the past week: two Monster Waves and thirty Mutated Gates; a Mojave Desert Monster Wave four days earlier killed twenty-two and injured thirty-five before being covered up by the US Ministry of National Defense.
- Taekyung now knows that major Gate disasters are being concealed from the public and sees the situation as paralleling the decision to keep Dark Heaven’s existence secret until people are prepared.

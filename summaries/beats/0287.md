# Chapter 287

## Plot

Lee Jungryong visits Im Kkeokjeong's hospital room and reveals a long, tense history with Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim. Choi once worked under Ares Guild and was sent overseas by Lee, while Kim saved Lee's life at the collapse of Mapo Bridge eighteen years earlier and now openly despises him. Go Jun attacks Taekyung after he insults Lee, but Taekyung defeats him with ease. Lee then recognizes that Taekyung learned significant martial arts and asks about the older brother who taught him. His questions about Soyeong, Choi's deceased mother, provoke Choi and Kim before Lee leaves, demanding that the captives be handed over after negotiations. Choi reveals that Soyeong was Cheon Taemin's daughter, making him Cheon Taemin's maternal grandson; Taekyung realizes that Cheon Taemin is the Ares Guild Master, the world's greatest Hunter, and the Slayer who killed the Demon King.

## Continuity

- Team Leader Choi worked for Ares Guild for roughly three or four years, was immediately transferred overseas by Lee Jungryong so he could not enter headquarters, and resigned the previous year.
- Lee Jungryong has known Team Leader Choi since childhood; Choi called him Grandpa when he was young.
- Kim Hwajong met Lee Jungryong eighteen years earlier and saved his life during the collapse of Mapo Bridge; Kim deeply regrets having saved him and openly hates him.
- Soyeong, Team Leader Choi's mother, died long ago and was the daughter of Cheon Taemin.
- Team Leader Choi is Cheon Taemin's maternal grandson.
- Cheon Taemin is described as humanity's great hero, the world's greatest Hunter, the Ares Guild Master, and the Slayer who killed the Demon King.
- Lee Jungryong's hospital visit was not an apology. He used it to demonstrate his power, brought compensation money and lilies, and demanded that the captives be handed over after negotiations.
- Go Jun attacked Taekyung in the hospital room and was defeated; Lee Jungryong's previously controlled composure visibly broke afterward.
- Lee Jungryong believes Taekyung learned his martial arts from an older brother, but the identity of that person and the connection to Lee remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 소영 as Soyeong and 천태민 as Cheon Taemin.

- Render 도련님 as Young Master, 선배 and 선배님 as Senior, and 형님 in Lee Jungryong's question as older brother.

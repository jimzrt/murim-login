# Chapter 522

## Plot

Wudang's second report reveals that the Killing Ghost was Jang Sam, an ordinary fisherman from near Mount Wudang who disappeared after going out alone to fish. Taekyung suspects the Blood Fish, which may have been eaten by Jang Sam or may have eaten him, caused his transformation into a monster.

Taekyung concludes that a second Gate has not yet erupted and that Dark Heaven cannot open Gates casually, but warns that future Gates could ignite the Murim's existing powder keg. He believes the Blood Fish and Jang Sam may indicate similar incidents elsewhere.

The Hidden Shadow Pavilion resumes active operations, and the Murim Alliance begins mobilizing. Nangong Cheon and Peng Cheolhu are permitted to share the secret with their families, while Mae Jonghak again asks Jeok Cheongang whether he will become Alliance Leader; Jeok's answer remains unstated.

## Continuity

- The Killing Ghost's identity is established as Jang Sam, a fisherman who became a monster after a likely interaction with the Blood Fish.
- Taekyung assesses that another Gate has not opened yet and that Dark Heaven's ability to open Gates is limited, but warns that additional Gates could trigger a catastrophe across the Murim.
- The Hidden Shadow Pavilion has resumed operations, and the Murim Alliance is actively responding to the threat.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 화약고 as powder keg and preserve the chapter's blunt profanity and monster-comparison humor.
- Retain Jang Sam as the English rendering of 장삼.

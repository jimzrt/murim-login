# Chapter 189

## Plot

Jin Taekyung accepted the System Quest There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much and entered a duel with Chulwoo after Chulwoo challenged him over Lee Seowol.

Chulwoo used incomplete Fist Qi through his gauntlets to cut Taekyung's spear down to a short metal rod, but Taekyung allocated twenty remaining points to Strength and fifty to Agility, then gained the upper hand.

The chapter ends with Taekyung stabbing the back of Chulwoo's neck with the shortened steel weapon while sitting on his shoulder; the duel's final outcome is not yet established.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung accepted the Quest There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much; refusing it would grant the Title Weak Male.
- Chulwoo is Level 95 and can manifest incomplete Fist Qi through his dull-black gauntlets.
- Jin Taekyung allocated all seventy remaining stat points: twenty to Strength and fifty to Agility.
- The duel between Jin Taekyung and Chulwoo remains unresolved at the chapter's end; Taekyung has momentarily gained the dominant position.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 한 남자가 있어, 널 너무 사랑한 as “There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much.”
- Render 나약한 수컷 as the System Title “Weak Male.”
- Render 권기 as “Fist Qi,” retaining the distinction between incomplete manifested qi and ordinary internal energy.

# Chapter 257

## Plot

Song Ho detects a clash between Supreme Peak masters at the dueling platform. After receiving a report that Shadow Killer has been found, he deploys Hidden Shadow Pavilion agents to secure the area and heads toward the platform.

Jin Taekyung and Jongni Chu exchange dozens of attacks during the Star-Array Grand Banquet final. Jongni Chu demonstrates Returning to Simplicity, immense internal energy, and Sword Force, while Taekyung uses his year of training and System gains to reach the seventh stage of Fire Dragon Divine Spear. Taekyung suffers an Internal Injury, but Jongni Chu abruptly leaves after staring toward a distant point, leaving the duel unresolved.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and Jongni Chu's final duel remains unresolved after Jongni Chu suddenly leaves the battlefield.
- Jongni Chu has reached Returning to Simplicity within the Supreme Peak realm, can wield Sword Force through his rusted sword, and possesses enough internal energy to withstand Taekyung's full-strength attacks.
- Jin Taekyung suffered an Internal Injury during the duel but continued fighting.
- After a year of training under Jeok Cheongang, Taekyung sleeps only half a shichen per day with System-assisted recovery and has advanced the Fire Dragon Divine Spear to its seventh stage.
- Taekyung's Status Window displayed 500 Remaining Points; he invoked System commands that advanced his Strength, Agility, Stamina, Muscles and Bones, and Sinews and Meridians by one step.
- Song Ho received a report that Shadow Killer had been found and went toward the dueling platform with Hidden Shadow Pavilion agents.
- Hong Dao had already left Song Ho's location, and Song Ho chose to trust Hong Dao's judgment.
- The identities, affiliations, and motives behind Jongni Chu and Dark Heaven remain unknown.

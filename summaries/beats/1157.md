# Chapter 1157

## Plot

Jin Taekyung breaks the magic surrounding the secret Pentagon chamber without the feared backlash, easing Magic Johnson’s and Choi Minwoo’s concern about his growing strength. He orders full mobilization to Moscow against Morgoth, but says he will go alone and that they must not act without further orders unless he cannot command.

A Code Red reports that Code Name Blue escaped through Area 52. Johnson reveals that Code Name Blue is Jin, and suspects the escapee is someone who looks like him. The group hurries to the surface and finds Chuck Hagel alone.

## Continuity

- Jin orders World Hunter Federation forces to mobilize for Moscow against Morgoth and his monsters, but plans to confront Morgoth alone; others are to await further orders unless Jin cannot command.
- Code Name Blue is Jin Taekyung; someone who looks like him has apparently escaped through Area 52, and their identity is unknown.
- Jin now recognizes that his deepest fear is the danger his death would leave for others; he has found some peace with that fear.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 코드 네임 블루 as “Code Name Blue”; the chapter identifies it as Jin’s codename and does not identify the apparent double.

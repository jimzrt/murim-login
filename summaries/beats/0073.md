# Chapter 73

## Plot

After ten days of hellish training, Jin Taekyung masters the Jin Family’s Spear Technique and Jin Family’s Manoeuvre Technique. The System grants him the First Stage Skill Martial Arts Manual Creation, which can create manuals for those two mastered arts.

Jin Mukyung recognizes that Taekyung has already made the techniques his own and feels both jealousy and fighting spirit. Their spar ends when Mukyung uses Sword Energy to cut through Taekyung’s uniform without injuring him. Mukyung declares training complete, and the System confirms that the Training? Trial! Quest has been completed, granting a Level Up, moving its Reward to the Inventory, and promising an additional Reward.

Childeuk arrives injured, with bruises and several broken teeth, and explains that he recently became a formal martial artist directly under Jin Wikyung. His Level is 12. Wikyung embraces and praises him as Jang Childeuk, then privately admits to Taekyung that he and Childeuk had a minor misunderstanding.

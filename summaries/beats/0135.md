# Chapter 135

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continues tormenting Woo Jintae until the Young Bureau Head passes out, then confronts the four remaining Five Gates heirs and orders them to accept ten sword-case blows each after they plead for leniency.

The crowd recognizes Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi and largely sides with the Jin Family of Taiyuan, while condemning the Five Gates and the Seongun Escort Bureau as corrupt and self-serving.

One passionate public defender of the Jin Family is revealed to be Jang Childeuk, now Level 15. After Taekyung begins beating the heirs, Cheongpung asks to strike the final remaining heir himself because he has never done anything like it before.

## Continuity

- Woo Jintae is left unconscious after Taekyung's prolonged beating and verbal humiliation.
- The public reputation of the Jin Family of Taiyuan and the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi is strong enough that local guests openly support the family against the Five Gates of Shanxi.
- Jang Childeuk is present at Honghwa Inn as a Level 15 martial artist and openly advocates for the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- Cheongpung shows an unsettling curiosity about violence by requesting to hit the final remaining Five Gates heir.

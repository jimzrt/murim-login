# Chapter 1180

## Plot

Jin Wikyung and Song Ho travel with the martial forces across a barren plateau under torrential rain. Wikyung worries over his younger brothers and recalls that Great Sir joined Taekyung’s group, though he never managed to speak with him. Song Ho says the Imperial Army crossed Lop Nur fifteen days earlier and should reach the Tianshan Mountains by the day after tomorrow. The government-Murim army has split into three forces at the Qinghai-Xinjiang border; as Wikyung and Mukyung approach the Tianshan Mountains, lightning reveals the roars of countless monsters.

## Continuity

- The government-Murim army has divided into three forces at the Qinghai-Xinjiang border; the Imperial Army is expected to rendezvous with them near the Tianshan Mountains before sunset in two days.
- The land along the route remains barren and devoid of visible life; the cause is still unknown.
- Great Sir joined Taekyung’s group, but Jin Wikyung has not yet had a proper conversation with him.
- Countless monsters roar beyond the Tianshan Mountains as the forces approach.

## Translation Decisions

- Use the established glossary rendering Great Sir for 대인; the reading copy’s Big Man is inconsistent with that entry.

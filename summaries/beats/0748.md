# Chapter 748

## Plot

Michael Silbert learns that Jin Taekyung has accepted Japan's request for assistance and that Ares Guild, Peace Guild, and the Korean Air Force are sending five teams to Tokyo. He implies that Shanghai will be the next terrorist target because China sided with Jin, then personally enters the Japanese battlefield with Huginn, determined to surpass Cheon Taemin and Jin Taekyung. Meanwhile, Leviathan devastates Japan with waves, lightning, and water spears, having awakened because the world's increased magical power and an untouched Magic Gem drew it from its long sleep. As Leviathan attempts to consume the Magic Gem and regain its original strength, a blue-white burst of light-flames strikes the waves.

## Continuity

- Michael has begun the Japanese operation in silver armor and intends to make Michael Silbert the undisputed best, distinct from Cheon Taemin and Jin Taekyung.
- Michael identifies Shanghai as a likely next terrorist target because China supported Jin Taekyung.
- Leviathan is attacking Japan and overwhelming B-rank-and-higher Hunters, defensive Magic, Tower Shields, and unmanned fighter jets with its control over waves, storms, lightning, and water.
- Leviathan awakened in response to the world's increased magical power and an untouched Magic Gem, which it seeks to consume to restore its original strength.
- A blue-white burst of light-flames strikes Leviathan at the chapter's endpoint; its source and effect remain unresolved.

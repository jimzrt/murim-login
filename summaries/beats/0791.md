# Chapter 791

## Plot

On the flight to the Middle East, Jin Taekyung reviews intelligence on The Prophet and learns the search could take more than sixty days. With The Prophet still holding a huge supply of unrefined Magic Gems, Jin fears a catastrophic attack may be imminent and insists they must find them before they are ready.

Meanwhile, the Skeleton King and Xiao Shen investigate the location where a search squad lost contact. As they arrive, the Skeleton King spots something unexpected.

## Continuity

- The World Hunter Federation and allied forces are searching the Middle East for The Prophet; 100,000 Hunters have been deployed, but the search has found no notable leads after its first day, and could take more than sixty days in the worst case.
- The Prophet has a large quantity of unrefined Magic Gems and may be preparing a major attack, but Jin suspects they are not ready to carry it out yet.
- The Skeleton King and Xiao Shen are investigating the site where a search squad lost contact; the Skeleton King has just spotted something unidentified.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magical power distinct from mana.
- Preserve the Skeleton King's shift from “Mr. S” to “Mr. King” as a running honorific joke.

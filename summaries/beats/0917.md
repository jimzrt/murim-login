# Chapter 917

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang overwhelms the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, destroying both arms and a leg and driving him into the ground. As the Demon Lord faces defeat, he summons the assassin Heaven's Slaughter; just as the assassin launches a surprise attack, a flaming spear cuts through the air.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang has pushed beyond his prior limits and is close to defeating the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord has lost both arms and a leg, but summons Heaven's Slaughter as a last resort.
- A flaming spear interrupts Heaven's Slaughter's attack on Jeok Cheongang; its wielder is not yet identified.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 천살 as “Heaven's Slaughter,” the name or title used when the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord summons the assassin.
- Keep “Force” for 강기 distinct from “death energy” for 사기.

# Chapter 794

## Plot

Jin Taekyung interrogates the captive Huginn, who had resisted earlier interrogators. Jin’s threats and violent, potion-fueled torment finally get Huginn to speak; Huginn says The Prophet killed Siegfried Wassmann.

When pressed about The Prophet, Huginn reveals that the person called The Prophet is Muninn. Jin recalls that Huginn and Muninn are Odin’s two ravens, realizing there was never only one.

## Continuity

- Huginn says The Prophet, also known as Muninn, killed Siegfried Wassmann; Jin is certain Huginn is telling the truth.
- Huginn remains a captive and has begun disclosing what he knows about The Prophet. Jin promises to kill him if that is what Huginn wants, but does not promise to release him.
- The Prophet’s identity as Muninn is revealed; the relationship between Muninn and Michael Silbert remains unclear.
- Jin’s Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] remains incomplete despite learning who killed Siegfried Wassmann.
- Jin is pursuing the Main Quest [Cataclysm], which requires him to eliminate The Prophet within an unspecified time limit; The Prophet is hiding somewhere in the Middle East.

# Chapter 1148

## Plot

Taekyung and the Skeleton King fight the remaining named monsters at the bomb shelter. Their bodies are exceptionally dense and regenerate quickly, but Taekyung kills several by exploiting openings; the Skeleton King has also grown much stronger. The two exchange a quiet, affectionate greeting before resuming the fight. A tremendous light then gathers overhead. Taekyung recognizes the attack raining down on the monsters as the work of his friend, the Grand Mage.

## Continuity

- Taekyung and the Skeleton King are reunited allies who consider each other friends.
- The named monsters have exceptionally durable bodies and rapid regeneration; Taekyung and the Skeleton King continue fighting them at the bomb shelter.
- The Grand Mage sends a destructive attack from overhead, aimed at the monsters; Taekyung recognizes her as his friend.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 스켈레톤 킹 as “Skeleton King.”
- Render 스톤 킹 as “Stone King” in the Skeleton King’s joking reference to himself.

# Chapter 586

## Plot

Jin Taekyung accepts Kim Hwajong's death and places the unconscious Choi Minwoo beside him, then dismembers Behemoth to recover its surviving Supreme Peak Magic Gem, Behemoth's Turbid Abyss. The item reveals that Behemoth absorbed another source of mana, allowing Taekyung to connect the Monster Waves and the deaths of Hwajong and Choi's allies to Go Jun. Taekyung withdraws from the Peace Guild, leaves Skeleton King to protect the Guild and its people, and takes Peace Guild members to Ares Guild by Teleport.

## Continuity

- Kim Hwajong died after sacrificing himself to restrain Behemoth, and Jin Taekyung could not save him; Choi Minwoo remains unconscious and was placed beside Hwajong with their hands joined.
- Behemoth's Turbid Abyss is a Supreme Peak Magic Gem containing Behemoth's abyss and source of mana; it absorbed mana from another source, became turbid and stronger, and requires difficult purification before use.
- Jin Taekyung identifies Go Jun as the person behind the day's events, including the Monster Wave and the deaths of Hwajong and Choi Minwoo's allies.
- Jin Taekyung has withdrawn from the Peace Guild and is traveling to Ares Guild to confront the consequences of Go Jun's actions; Skeleton King remains to protect the people and the Guild.
- Cheon Taemin suddenly collapsed more than twenty years ago and has remained unconscious, but the cause is unknown.
- Song Cheonwoo and Lee Jungryong concealed Taemin's condition, waited two years, conducted experiments, and purged aides who knew the truth.
- Song claims Taemin is still alive, but his location is unknown and Area A is only suspected; Choi Minwoo treats the account as genuine while investigating.
- Busan's Kraken has been eliminated, but more than one thousand Mermen remain across Haeundae and Gwangalli while the Peace Guild and other forces contain the disaster.
- Go Jun seized Song Cheonwoo's children as leverage, used an S-grade Magic Gem to cause the Busan Monster Wave, and targeted Choi Minwoo, possibly with help from another unidentified being.
- Song Cheonwoo was killed by an unidentified monster after falling into an abyss, and the unused object in his pocket released darkness that became light after his death.
- Jin Taekyung killed Behemoth with One Annihilation and received a level-up that removed Exhaustion and Internal Energy Depletion.

# Chapter 295

## Plot

The Skeleton Knight that had absorbed two others evolved into a Level 105 Skeleton Warlord, a Named Monster capable of speech. Its Summon the Dead skill raised the undead army to more than three hundred and added two Skeleton Knights; its Commander’s Rally then increased every monster’s Level by five and strengthened their physical and magical abilities. Jin Taekyung ordered the Peace Guild members to retreat through the Gate while he stayed behind to delay the army, allowing Kim Jinsoo and the others to escape. After confirming that he was alone with the undead army, Taekyung revealed that his apparent sacrifice had been a ploy to secure the entire army as EXP and launched One Annihilation against them.

## Continuity

- The leading Skeleton Knight evolved into a Level 105 Skeleton Warlord after two other Skeleton Knights submitted to it.
- The Skeleton Warlord is a Named Monster capable of halting speech and possesses the special skills Summon the Dead and Commander’s Rally.
- Summon the Dead increased the undead force to more than three hundred and added two Skeleton Knights; Commander’s Rally raised every subordinate monster’s Level by five and enhanced its strength, Agility, and magic.
- Only fifty Peace Guild members entered the Gate, and more than half were veteran third-week Tosa mastiffs; Kim Jinsoo effectively serves as deputy team leader.
- Jin Taekyung deliberately stayed behind after ordering the Peace Guild members through the Gate, intending to kill the concentrated Skeleton army and gain its EXP.

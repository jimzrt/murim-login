# Chapter 86

## Plot

After clearing The Minotaur's Labyrinth, the party sells the collected Minotaur byproducts and Magic Gems to the Administration. Im Changsoo promises to send Taekyung the agreed-upon 4 billion won by the following day and receives Taekyung's account information.

Im Kkeokjeong warns Taekyung that Sangdong Guild is powerful because its Guild Master, Im Chunsoo, is an A-rank Hunter known as Frozen, a renowned ice mage who built the Guild by crushing its competitors. Im Chunsoo learns that Changsoo transferred 8 billion won to two accounts, summons him, fires him, and prepares to beat him with an ice club after having him dragged into the office.

## Continuity

- Im Changsoo promises to transfer the agreed-upon 4 billion won to Taekyung by tomorrow, but whether he fulfills that promise remains unresolved.
- Im Chunsoo is the A-rank Guild Master and founder of Sangdong Guild. He is known as Frozen, ranks among Korea's top five ice mages, and reportedly has an extremely bad temper despite trying to mellow with age.
- Im Chunsoo learns that Im Changsoo transferred 8 billion won total to two accounts, summons him to the Guild office, dismisses him, and violently confronts him.
- The Sangdong Guild Team One Leader brings Im Changsoo to Im Chunsoo's office and closes the room to visitors for roughly half a day.
- Im Kkeokjeong believes Sangdong Guild could use its local influence against the newly established Peace Guild, while Taekyung refuses to let his Guild members be harmed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 프로즌 as Frozen, Im Chunsoo's established ice-mage epithet.
- Render 호부견자 as “a tiger father and a dog son,” preserving the literal contrast and the insult directed at Im Changsoo.
- Render 떡고물 as “a little something off the top” and 개망신 as “utterly humiliated,” preserving the colloquial tone.

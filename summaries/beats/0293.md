# Chapter 293

## Plot

Jin Taekyung successfully guides Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong through the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique. All four complete a complete circulation before sunset and soon learn to perform the Small Circulation independently. Im Kkeokjeong adapts most unexpectedly as a Second Awakener, suggesting exceptional room for growth and a possible early return to Guild work if he resolves his trauma.

The System recognizes that several trainees have reached One Star in the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique and changes Taekyung's Title from Trainee to Training Instructor. During the following week, Lee Jungryong and the Peace Guild secretly complete their negotiations, two more Mutated Gate accidents occur, and a Hunter-community post reveals that a C-rank Security Team Hunter was hired for the Peace Guild at Taekyung's direction.

Taekyung treats the Peace Guild's new-hire workshop as a Gate raid. Veteran recruits accept the ordeal and equip themselves, while first-day hires protest the unexpected field exercise. The Training Instructor effect makes the trainees tense, and Taekyung leads the recruits into the Gate in formation.

## Continuity

- Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong have begun using the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique; all four completed a complete circulation and can now perform the Small Circulation independently.
- Im Kkeokjeong, still a D-rank Hunter, adapted most quickly among the four trainees; his Second Awakening may explain his potential, and repeated qi circulation is expected to improve his physical foundations.
- Several Peace Guild trainees reached One Star in the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique, changing Taekyung's System Title from Trainee to Training Instructor.
- Lee Jungryong and the Peace Guild secretly completed their negotiations during the week after the training, while two additional Mutated Gate accidents occurred.
- A C-rank Hunter formerly assigned to a major Guild's Security Team was hired into the Peace Guild at Taekyung's direction and assigned to its Security Team.
- Taekyung has begun using Gate raids as practical workshops for new Peace Guild hires, while earlier recruits have been conditioned to accept the Guild's unusually severe training.

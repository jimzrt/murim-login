# Chapter 789

## Plot

After a nightmare filled with familiar dead and an apparently world-ending blaze, Jin Taekyung wakes from his long sleep to find Hayeon and his mother at his side. He accepts the World Hunter Federation’s appointment as Alliance Leader, then meets Team Leader Choi, who reports arrests of major figures tied to Michael Silbert. Jin asks where The Prophet is.

## Continuity

- Jin’s nightmare showed a world consumed by fire and then a rift splitting the sky and space; whether it foretells an actual catastrophe remains unknown.
- Jin has awakened, accepted the World Hunter Federation’s appointment as Alliance Leader, and returned to contact with Team Leader Choi.
- Major political and business figures tied to Michael Silbert were arrested after the National Assembly battle; The Prophet’s whereabouts remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Rift” as the rendering of 균열.
- Treat the fire and rift in Jin’s nightmare as an ominous vision, not confirmed future events.

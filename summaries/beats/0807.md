# Chapter 807

## Plot

Jin’s planned level-up is lost when Yamamoto kills the Lycanthrope Champion, but Jin uses the Myriad-Poison Ring to absorb the Manticore Lord’s venom and end his Poisoned and Convulsions status abnormalities. The Skeleton King kills the Death Knight and joins Jin. Jin attacks the monster army with White Flame and Heavenly Strike, while the Skeleton King and a Grand Mage bring down the Griffin leader and the other flying monsters. With the monster army’s formation broken, Jin orders the Hunters to advance, declaring the battle won.

## Continuity

- The Myriad-Poison Ring absorbed the Manticore Lord’s venom, curing Jin’s Poisoned and Convulsions status abnormalities; Jin remains physically worn down with depleted internal energy.
- Yamamoto Genji killed the Lycanthrope Champion, taking the last hit Jin had expected to earn a level-up from.
- The Skeleton King killed the Death Knight and absorbed its departing soul; it later struck the Griffin leader with a bone spear.
- A Grand Mage used Reverse Gravity to slam the remaining flying monsters, including roughly a hundred Griffins, into the ground; Jin’s forces advanced after the monster army’s formation collapsed.
- Jin leads roughly one thousand Hunters against a monster force exceeding ten thousand in the Rub’ al Khali; the Prophet’s location and means of directing the army remain unknown.
- Jin pursues Main Quest [Cataclysm], which requires him to eliminate the Prophet within an unspecified time limit.

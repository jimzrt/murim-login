# Chapter 1051

## Plot

The Grand Mage realizes Jin Taekyung’s resolve and the limits of her authority, but he severs his own heart meridians rather than submit. Desperate to save him, she unleashes healing power that engulfs the hill.

Jin survives as the System removes his injuries and grants him the “Go Ahead, Gut Me!” achievement. He recalls the White Flame spear, breaks free of the vines, and attacks the Grand Mage. She evades with Blink, but Jin anticipates her movement and closes in.

## Continuity

- Jin’s heart-meridian injury is healed, and his other listed injuries and status effects are removed; the fight with the Grand Mage continues.
- The Grand Mage says she lacks authority to kill Jin and acts to save him because her master has waited a long time for him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render the achievement 배 째 as “Go Ahead, Gut Me!”
- Render 필활 as “will to save” in contrast with 필살, “will to kill.”

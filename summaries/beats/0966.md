# Chapter 966

## Plot

Jin Mukyung and Peng Cheolhu fight Jamukha in the gorge. Jamukha injures Mukyung while parrying Cheolhu’s full-powered strike, but Cheolhu resumes his attack and the two continue their duel. Jin Wikyung leads the Shanxi fighters forward, judging that their advantage is temporary while the Hebei Peng Family remains outnumbered in the basin. Jamukha’s Keshik centurions hold back an unnamed weapon at his order. As the battle outside the gorge remains evenly matched, an unidentified group emerges from the darkness and rains thousands of arrows onto the battlefield.

## Continuity

- Mukyung is alive but grievously internally injured; he has risen again and remains near the battle at the gorge’s center.
- Cheolhu and Jamukha are still fighting in the gorge; Cheolhu has recognized that Jamukha is stronger and more perceptive than he expected.
- Wikyung is alive, leading Shanxi fighters toward the gorge’s center, and believes their current momentum will not last if the steppe army regains its composure.
- The Hebei Peng Family is fighting the steppe army in the basin while outnumbered by more than ten to one; Cheolhu has left the basin with some of its elite to fight in the gorge.
- Jamukha ordered his Keshik centurions not to use an unnamed weapon without his permission, saying more battles lie ahead.
- Jamukha anticipated before reaching Shanxi that other forces would come to aid the Jin Family; an unidentified group has now appeared beyond the basin and fired thousands of arrows.

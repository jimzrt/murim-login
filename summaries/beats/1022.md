# Chapter 1022

## Plot

Jin Taekyung’s force of five thousand reaches the Great Snow Mountain after a brutal forced march. Signals from allied forces on the mountain bring relief, but Taekyung remains troubled by Sama Pyo’s silence and Jeok Cheongang’s question about whether Pyo and Taishan are carrying out Sima Gong’s secret orders—and whether those orders involve Dark Heaven. The scale of Dark Heaven’s victory at Dunhuang leaves Taekyung wondering what hidden strength made them confident of invading Gansu.

A flashback shows a badly wounded Kongtong disciple gain what he believes is a breakthrough, only for a blood-covered middle-aged killer to catch his sword and reveal that the disciple alone had seemed to slow. The killer says the sect brought its fate on itself by refusing to surrender, kills the disciple—identified as the Kongtong Sword Dragon—and looks toward the Great Snow Mountain as a tremendous rumble begins.

## Continuity

- Signals from allied forces confirm that the Great Snow Mountain is occupied by Taekyung’s side as his five-thousand-strong force arrives.
- A middle-aged Dark Heaven killer has slaughtered more than a hundred Kongtong Sect members, including the Kongtong Sword Dragon; his identity and strength remain unknown.
- Taekyung suspects Dark Heaven has at least three Supreme Peak masters or one overwhelming superhuman, and wonders whether the Lord of Heaven is involved; these remain suspicions.
- Sama Pyo remains under Sima Gong’s order to watch Taekyung’s group. Taekyung still does not know whether Sima Gong’s secret orders connect Pyo and Taishan to Dark Heaven.
- The secret letter received by Sama Pyo remains unexplained, and the Kongtong Sect Leader’s whereabouts remain unknown.

# Chapter 585

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's One Annihilation pierces Behemoth and kills the Level 150 ancient monster. The resulting level-up removes his Exhaustion and Internal Energy Depletion, though severe fatigue remains.

Kim Hwajong's innate qi has nearly burned away after he restrained Behemoth, leaving him beyond recovery. He accepts his death, asks after Choi Minwoo, comforts Taekyung, and dies peacefully after Taekyung lies that Minwoo is approaching.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung killed Behemoth with One Annihilation.
- Kim Hwajong sacrificed his life to restrain Behemoth and died beside Taekyung and Skeleton King.
- Choi Minwoo was seen unharmed on the mountainside before Kim Hwajong died.
- Skeleton King used a top-grade potion and absorbed the magic in Kim Hwajong's bones, but could not save him.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Skeleton King for 스켈레톤 킹.
- Use Young Master for 도련님 when Kim Hwajong addresses Jin Taekyung.
- Use final rally for 회광반조 and Internal Energy Depletion for 공력 소진.

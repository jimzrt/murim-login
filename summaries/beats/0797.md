# Chapter 797

## Plot

At the strategy headquarters, Jin Taekyung and the command staff assess Huginn’s confession and conclude The Prophet may be powerful enough to defeat a Grand Mage alone—or may have known Siegfried Wassmann personally. As search teams withdraw, the Skeleton King’s undead army alarms one team before J1 reports multiple dead and injured in an attack. Jin rushes toward the reported fighting. At the attack site, The Prophet has slaughtered nearly all of Yamamoto Genji’s group; Yamamoto is the only one still alive when reinforcements arrive.

## Continuity

- The Prophet is revealed as the attacker responsible for the massacre of J1 team members; Yamamoto Genji is alive at the scene but facing The Prophet as reinforcements arrive.
- The withdrawal of the dispersed search teams is underway; more than 70 percent have returned, but teams remain in transit or temporarily out of contact.
- The Skeleton King’s undead force is moving among the search teams and has alarmed F6; headquarters requests his support after J1 reports casualties.

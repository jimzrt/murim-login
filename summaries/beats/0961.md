# Chapter 961

## Plot

Cheol Mubaek recognizes the Demon Bird as the Blood Soul Fat Demon, who reveals that he once fought Cheol’s master, the Fist Hero, and cut off both his arms. The Demon Bird attacks Cheol, Wipeng, and Jin Mukyung.

During the battle, the Demon Bird’s Sword Force overwhelms Cheol and Wipeng, but their weapons withstand it. He mistakes them for Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron; they are actually made from the bones of the Water God Dragon. Wipeng redirects the force while Cheol grips the Demon Bird’s blade despite severe injuries, creating an opening for another swordsman’s attack.

## Continuity

- The Demon Bird is also known as the Blood Soul Fat Demon; he severed both arms of Cheol Mubaek’s master, the Fist Hero.
- Cheol Mubaek and Wipeng’s weapons are made from the Water God Dragon’s bones, not Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.
- Cheol Mubaek and Wipeng are severely injured but still fighting the Demon Bird at Eight Spring Gorge; the outcome of the swordsman’s attack is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 혈혼비마 as “Blood Soul Fat Demon” and 권호 as “Fist Hero.”
- The Demon Bird mistakes the Water God Dragon bone weapons for Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron; preserve the distinction.

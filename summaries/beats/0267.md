# Chapter 267

## Plot

The Blood Lord escaped through black radiance, leaving behind blood, his severed wrist, and the Green Jade Buddha Staff. The Life-or-Death Crisis then completed, restoring Jin Taekyung's body and granting him enormous EXP and two level-ups. Jongni Chu treated Cheongpung and Jeok Cheongang; Jeok's injuries were not life-threatening but left him unconscious, while Cheongpung suffered internal injuries and was expected to recover.

Song Ho arrived with hundreds of martial artists after the Shaolin bloodbath. He recognized Jongni Chu as Sword Saint Mae Jonghak, the man who had saved his life, and Mae confirmed his identity to Taekyung. At an unknown cavern, the severely wounded Blood Lord received treatment from sorcerers after delivering the Green Jade Buddha Staff; he killed subordinates who displeased him and swore to kill Mae, Jeok, Cheongpung, and Taekyung with his own hands.

## Continuity

- Jongni Chu is revealed to be Sword Saint Mae Jonghak, Cheongpung's grandfather, and Taekyung now knows his identity.
- Mae Jonghak concealed his identity as Jongni Chu through the Star-Array Grand Banquet and had intended to remain hidden until the banquet ended.
- Song Ho recognizes Mae Jonghak as the Great Hero who saved his life in the past.
- Jin Taekyung completed the Life-or-Death Crisis, fully recovered from his injuries, received enormous EXP, and gained two levels.
- Mae Jonghak treated Cheongpung and Jeok Cheongang after the Blood Lord escaped; Jeok remains unconscious after expending too much energy, while Cheongpung has internal injuries but is expected to recover.
- Song Ho and hundreds of martial artists arrived after the Shaolin attack; Peng Cheolhu divided the forces and was handling the Shaolin response.
- The Blood Lord escaped through black radiance to an unidentified cavern or base with the Green Jade Buddha Staff; both arms were severed and his body was covered in severe sword wounds.
- The Blood Lord is described as the Disciple cherished by an unidentified 'that person.' Sorcerers began restoring his broken bones and flesh.
- The Blood Lord now personally vows to kill Mae Jonghak, Jeok Cheongang, Cheongpung, and Jin Taekyung.

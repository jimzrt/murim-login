# Chapter 847

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang mocks the Son of Heaven and dismisses the idea that imperial authority is sacred, then overwhelms the Captain of the Guards with his Scorching Yang Qi. The Divine Physician arrives and the Captain realizes Jeok is the Fire King. Jeok’s teasing and threats frighten the Captain, while the Divine Physician calmly intervenes. The physician says he cannot leave his current patients to treat the City Lord’s lovesickness, though he may visit if they improve. An armored subordinate then arrives with an urgent, unfinished report: “C-City Lord…!”

## Continuity

- The Divine Physician declines to leave his current patients to see the City Lord, but may visit if they improve; an armored subordinate arrives with an urgent report whose contents are not yet revealed.
- The Captain of the Guards recognizes Jeok Cheongang as the Fire King after witnessing his overwhelming Scorching Yang Qi.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 동방삭 as “Dongfang Shuo,” the long-lived figure invoked in the reference to 삼천갑자.

# Chapter 537

## Plot

Hwangbo Gun, Family Head of the Hwangbo Family, privately complains to Mae Jonghak that Jin Taekyung protected the Black Dragon Demon Gate and deserves punishment and personnel action. Mae appears to agree, then privately voids the personnel action and keeps Taekyung's pavilion-master position; Song Ho reports opposition to the recent appointments, pending replies from the Nanman Beast Palace and North Sea Ice Palace, and favorable cooperation from the Green Forest Alliance and Yangtze River Channel League.

Mae orders Taekyung to receive discipline while retaining his post. The punishment is to skip dinner, after which Taekyung leaves with Hyuk Mujin to recruit teammates.

## Continuity

- Hwangbo Gun is the Family Head of the Hwangbo Family, an old battlefield comrade of Mae Jonghak, and an opponent of Jin Taekyung's appointment as a pavilion master.
- Mae Jonghak privately rescinded the personnel action he appeared to promise Hwangbo Gun and intends for Jin Taekyung to retain his pavilion-master position despite disciplinary action.
- Song Ho has a prosthetic leg and remains the Chief of the Hidden Shadow Pavilion.
- The Green Forest Alliance and Yangtze River Channel League intend to send their leaders personally to Henan, while replies from the Nanman Beast Palace and North Sea Ice Palace remain pending.
- Jin Taekyung has been ordered to skip dinner as discipline and has begun recruiting teammates with Hyuk Mujin.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 독대 as private audience and 인사 처분 as personnel measures.
- Preserve Mae Jonghak's carefree repeated response, 'That can happen,' as a marker of his unshakable temperament.

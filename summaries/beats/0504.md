# Chapter 504

## Plot

Mungyeong’s intervention helps Jeok Cheongang break free of his Heart Demon; Jeok’s qi erupts as he reaches a new realm. Mungyeong leaves the cave, Taekyung arrives, and the Master and Disciple reunite after a year and several months. At the departure point, Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin trade vulgar insults while Jin Wikyung waits with Mungyeong and prepares to travel with Jeok to the New Murim Alliance. Zhuge Feng recounts that his grandfather’s remembered record and gamble redirected the Black Wind Corps through Mount Jiuhua, enabling Jeok’s first public feat and title as Fire King, before Jeok and Taekyung appear together.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang’s Heart Demon and the dark memories binding him have been expelled; his qi surged outward as he broke free of those chains and entered a new realm.
- Mungyeong’s intervention helped Jeok Cheongang overcome his Heart Demon despite Mungyeong not initially intending to cause a breakthrough.
- Jin Wikyung expects Jeok Cheongang to travel with them to the New Murim Alliance after seven days and nights of seclusion, apparently persuaded by Taekyung.
- Zhuge Gonghu’s historical gamble used a record of a one-person sect to direct the Demonic Cult’s thousand-man Black Wind Corps through Mount Jiuhua, where Jeok Cheongang annihilated it and became known as the Fire King.

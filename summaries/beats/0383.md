# Chapter 383

## Plot

Wei Fenghu transports Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi in a state-guest business jet escorted by five fighter aircraft because Sichuan Province is effectively at war due to magical communications interference and flying-monster attacks. During the flight, Wei explains that the disaster began in Gaoping District of Nanchong City and reveals that Lei Fei, a concealed Chinese S-rank Hunter and head of the Sichuan Public Security Armed Forces Department, disappeared with his Hunters when the first Monster Wave began. Wei, Lei Fei's maternal uncle and adoptive father, believes Lei Fei is alive and asks Jin to bring him back if they meet; Jin agrees without making a guarantee. The aircraft arrives at a temporary operations headquarters on Mount Qingcheng.

## Continuity

- Lei Fei is one of China's concealed S-rank Hunters and led the Public Security Armed Forces Department stationed in Sichuan Province.
- Lei Fei disappeared with the Hunters under his command when the first Monster Wave began one week earlier; his death remains unconfirmed because the Arch Lich blocked communications and surveillance.
- Wei Fenghu is Lei Fei's maternal uncle and raised him as his own son.
- Jin Taekyung promised Wei Fenghu that he would bring Lei Fei back if he encountered him, while warning that he could not guarantee Lei Fei's survival.
- The temporary operations headquarters is located on Mount Qingcheng in Sichuan Province.

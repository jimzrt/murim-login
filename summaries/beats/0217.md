# Chapter 217

## Plot

Jin Taekyung detects forty-seven B-Rank Orc Warriors before the others and kills them all in about fifteen minutes with an ordinary iron spear. Won Myunghoon and Team Leader 1 are alarmed by his strength, while Won privately resents Taekyung's rejection of his offers and orders Jonghun to find a boss monster or lure strong Wyverns.

Im Kkeokjeong reveals that Taekyung once protected him during an E-Rank Gate attack, and Taekyung lends him Won's Yeti's Necklace. Team Leader Choi recognizes the item, prompting Taekyung to use Item Appraisal for the first time.

## Continuity

- Taekyung killed forty-seven B-Rank Orc Warriors alone in roughly fifteen minutes using an ordinary iron spear.
- Won Myunghoon privately resents Taekyung's rejection and regards the Peace Guild as inferior; he ordered Kim Jonghun to find a boss monster or lure strong Wyverns.
- Im Kkeokjeong was among the injured people Taekyung protected during an E-Rank Gate attack two years earlier and remains deeply grateful.

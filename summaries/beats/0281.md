# Chapter 281

## Plot

Park Tae Seop worries that Jin Taekyung is powerful enough to crack Myeongdong Guild, but Park Jihoon dismisses the threat. Choi Minwoo then calls Jihoon using Manager Kim’s phone, revealing that the Peace Guild has captured Myeongdong personnel. Jihoon receives a photograph of Taekyung holding the incapacitated Team 1 Leader in Jihoon’s office, followed by the message “Bring Melona when you come,” and crushes the phone in shock. After burning the Black Hunters’ secret house, Taekyung and Choi split up: Choi leads an ambush to secure evidence while Taekyung acts alone. Taekyung defeats roughly fifty Myeongdong Hunters, occupies Jihoon’s office, and keeps an unpaid Security Team Hunter as his cameraman. At the end of the chapter, two massive presences approach and the office door is blasted apart.

## Continuity

- Park Tae Seop’s intuition identifies Jin Taekyung as a siege-cannon-level threat capable of cracking even Myeongdong Guild’s defenses, while Park Jihoon continues to underestimate him.
- After the Black Hunters’ secret house was burned, Choi Minwoo led an ambush to secure evidence and captured personnel dispatched by Myeongdong Guild; Taekyung acted independently.
- Choi Minwoo called Park Jihoon using Manager Kim’s phone after the Peace Guild captured Myeongdong personnel.
- Taekyung captured and held Myeongdong Guild Team 1’s leader in Park Jihoon’s office, then defeated approximately fifty additional Myeongdong Hunters and made the office forbidden territory.
- At chapter’s end, two massive presences approached Taekyung’s position and the office door was blown apart; the chapter does not explicitly identify them.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 최민우 as Choi Minwoo and 김 실장 as Manager Kim.
- Render 그분 as “that person” without identifying the figure behind the Black Hunter operation.
- Render 어른폰 as AdultPhone and retain the footnote explaining the Korean child/adult pun.
- Render 공성포 as “siege cannon” in Park Tae Seop’s metaphor.
- Render 올 때 메로나 as “Bring Melona when you come,” preserving the threatening joke and its footnote.

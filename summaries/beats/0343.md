# Chapter 343

## Plot

After five days of labor in Cold-Ice Land, Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, Gung Gibang, and Cheongpung complete the collection of the required medicinal herbs. The Herb Slave of Cold-Ice Land Chain Quest and the Mountain Herb Gatherer Who Returned Alive from Hell Achievement are completed, granting Taekyung ten bonus points.

The Divine Physician initially plans to prepare the Life-Restoring Great Technique over seven days, but after three days he discovers that Jeok Cheongang's body contains Formless Ultimate Poison. The poison is being contained by his Scorching Yang Qi, so performing the technique without an antidote could leave the poison unchecked. Mungyeong proposes seeking the Sichuan Tang Clan's legendary antidote treasure, generating the Myriad-Poison Ring Quest.

## Continuity

- The required one hundred roots of each of twenty-four medicinal herb varieties have been collected in Cold-Ice Land.
- The Divine Physician and Mungyeong are preparing the collected herbs and other ingredients for the Life-Restoring Great Technique, but treatment of Jeok Cheongang has not begun.
- Jeok Cheongang has Formless Ultimate Poison inside his body; his Scorching Yang Qi currently contains the poison, while his Yin-Cold Qi remains unchanged.
- The Life-Restoring Great Technique can regulate qi and untangle disrupted qi and blood but cannot detoxify Formless Ultimate Poison on its own.
- Mungyeong believes the Sichuan Tang Clan may possess a legendary sacred artifact capable of detoxifying any poison, and the Myriad-Poison Ring Quest has been generated.

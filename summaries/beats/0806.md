# Chapter 806

## Plot

Jin fights the Manticore Lord and Lycanthrope Champion while the Skeleton King battles the Death Knight Legion Commander. After the Manticore Lord damages his Fire Dragon Armor and poisons him, Jin uses the Lycanthrope Champion’s claw to hold it in place, then kills the Manticore Lord with a dagger and hellfire. Just as Jin is about to finish the Lycanthrope Champion, an unidentified ally arrives and cuts off its head.

## Continuity

- Jin is poisoned by the Manticore Lord; his Fire Dragon Armor is damaged and at risk of being automatically returned to his Inventory if damaged again.
- Jin’s special debuff has drastically reduced all his stats, but his strength still lets him overpower the Lycanthrope Champion.
- Jin kills the Manticore Lord with a dagger and hellfire; an unidentified ally kills the Lycanthrope Champion before Jin can finish it.
- The Skeleton King is fighting the Death Knight Legion Commander.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magical power distinct from mana.
- Render the source’s 만티코아 로드 as Manticore Lord, consistent with the established Manticore terminology.

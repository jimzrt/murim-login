# Chapter 404

## Plot

Search teams find no survivors among the dead in the small city except the three known survivors, while Hunters debate the scale of Jin Taekyung's victory. Jin overhears the discussion, rebukes a cynical young Hunter, insists that everyone fought and that the battle was nearly over when he arrived, and asks a casualty clerk to list Lei Fei among the dead despite the missing body.

Wei Fenghu and five S-rank Hunters wait for Jin in a conference room. Jin declares that they should end the war and eliminate the culprit behind the catastrophe.

## Continuity

- The Western Front search confirms that the city has no recoverable survivors among those who remained to fight; the only known survivors are the Public Security Armed Forces Department regimental commander, Jin Taekyung, and one other person identified by the searchers as the final survivor.
- Jin Taekyung asks that Lei Fei be included on the official list of the dead because Lei Fei's body will not be found.
- Jin publicly downplays his own contribution, saying that everyone fought and that the battle was almost over when he arrived.
- Wei Fenghu and five S-rank Hunters have assembled with Jin for an operation intended to end the war by killing the force responsible.

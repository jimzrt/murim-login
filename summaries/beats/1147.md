# Chapter 1147

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens in the Skeleton King’s backpack and returns to the fight, leading Choi Minwoo and more than two hundred Hunters against thousands of monsters. The Hunters wield Auror; seven powerful, humanlike creatures then appear, and Taekyung kills one before anyone can see or sense him move.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has awakened and rejoined Choi Minwoo, the Skeleton King, and the Hunters at the bomb shelter battlefield; seven humanlike enemies appeared, and one is dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 리자드맨 as “Lizardman,” distinct from 리자드, which is glossed as “Charmeleon.”
- Render 오러 as “Auror” and 검기 as “Sword Energy.”

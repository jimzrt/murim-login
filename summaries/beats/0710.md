# Chapter 710

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang arrives at the battlefield, revealing that the Blood Monk is the Fire King, and uses his Flame Divine Palm against the already crippled Southern Heaven Demon Empress. Jin Taekyung realizes that her death will kill him as well, shields her from the attack, and bites through her throat before Jeok's flames strike him. Jeok believes Jin has died, but a clear bell and warm recovery envelop Jin; Jeok then discovers that Jin is alive and apparently restored enough to speak.

## Continuity

- The Blood Monk is Jeok Cheongang, who arrived carrying a Zen staff and intervened against the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- Jeok Cheongang's twelve-tenths Flame Divine Palm struck the Southern Heaven Demon Empress after her remaining innate qi had been stripped away.
- Jin Taekyung shielded the Southern Heaven Demon Empress from Jeok's attack and tore into her throat because her death would also kill him.
- Jeok's Flame Divine Palm caused catastrophic injuries to Jin Taekyung, but a bell and warmth followed, and Jin later appeared conscious with his hand restored.
- Jeok Cheongang regarded Jin Taekyung as his sole Disciple and the ember that gave him a reason to live; he believed Jin had died and grieved before discovering that he survived.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 혈승 as Blood Monk when identifying Jeok Cheongang's revealed sobriquet.
- Retain Flame Divine Palm, Zen staff, innate qi, and Great Firmament Immortal.
- Render 십이성 as twelve-tenths of its normal limit.
- Preserve Jeok Cheongang's gruff, profane voice and Jin Taekyung's dry first-person voice.

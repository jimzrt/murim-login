# Chapter 901

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung openly mocks the Emperor, the Emperor forgives him for his disrespect, prompting Taekyung to suspect the response reveals something important. Wei Zhong, the East Depot’s Seal-Holding Eunuch known as Cang Gong, arrives after a long recovery and trades courteous but pointed remarks with the Emperor. Their exchange exposes their opposing views of the imperial succession, while Taekyung judges Wei Zhong an exceptionally powerful master. The Emperor says Prince Shangshan and the pregnant Consort are on their way; Taekyung then asks to move seats beside Wei Zhong, ostensibly because the raised seats offer more side dishes.

## Continuity

- The Emperor forgives Jin Taekyung for openly disrespecting him; Taekyung suspects this response has a significant explanation that remains unknown.
- Wei Zhong, addressed as Cang Gong, is the East Depot’s Seal-Holding Eunuch and has recovered enough from a prolonged illness to move about and attend the banquet.
- Wei Zhong and the Emperor exchange courteous, pointed remarks and have opposing views of who should inherit the throne; neither states his preferred heir.
- The Emperor says Prince Shangshan and the pregnant Consort are on their way to the banquet.
- Taekyung judges Wei Zhong more powerful than anyone else present, even Baek Yeon, and believes Wei Zhong and So Gyo are roughly evenly matched.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 東廠掌印太監 as “Seal-Holding Eunuch of the East Depot”; retain “Cang Gong” for 창공.

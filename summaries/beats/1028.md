# Chapter 1028

## Plot

The Blood-Sword Demon Lord watches Jin Taekyung with eager fascination as Taekyung reveals White Flame and an immense dark-blue Force. Jeok Cheongang recognizes that Taekyung has reached the realm of the Ten Kings, making him the eleventh giant. Taekyung wounds the First Elder and kills the Third; Sama Pyo stabs and kills the Third Elder with hidden daggers, then Taekyung kills the Second Elder. The First Elder remains alive, watching in horror.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has reached the realm of the Ten Kings and is recognized by Jeok Cheongang as the eleventh giant.
- In the fight with the Three Elders of Tianshan, Taekyung wounded the First Elder and killed the Second; Sama Pyo killed the Third with daggers. The First Elder survived the chapter, wounded and horrified.
- The Blood-Sword Demon Lord remained apart from the fight while watching Taekyung; he showed no guilt over the thousand lives he had taken.

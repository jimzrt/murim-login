# Chapter 361

## Plot

The Western Heaven Demon Lord reaches the Tang Clan's treatment room, identifies the Divine Physician, and declares himself one of the Lord of Heaven's four servants. He overpowers the physician, takes the Myriad-Poison Ring, and crushes the physician's lower dantian before attempting to remove the unconscious Jeok Cheongang.

Jin Taekyung arrives and attacks with White Flame. The Heavenly Power Demon has transferred three jiazi of internal energy to Taekyung in exchange for a request to kill the Western Heaven Demon Lord, allowing Taekyung to resume the confrontation. The Divine Physician confirms that Jeok Cheongang is safe, and Taekyung declares that the Western Heaven Demon Lord is going to die.

## Continuity

- The Western Heaven Demon Lord has obtained and equipped the Myriad-Poison Ring.
- The Divine Physician was overpowered, had his hand broken and lower dantian crushed, and remains alive but severely injured in the treatment room.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious and is safe in the treatment room at the end of the chapter.
- The Heavenly Power Demon escaped after the collapsing ceiling broke his restraints and transferred three jiazi of internal energy to Jin Taekyung.
- The Heavenly Power Demon asked Jin Taekyung to kill the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- Jin Taekyung has resumed his confrontation with the Western Heaven Demon Lord while wielding White Flame and greatly increased internal energy.
- The underground prison continues to collapse around the treatment room.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord claims that the Tang Clan, Qingcheng, and Emei have been erased, but the wider assault's actual outcome remains unconfirmed.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord openly serves an unidentified Lord of Heaven and describes himself as one of four loyal servants.

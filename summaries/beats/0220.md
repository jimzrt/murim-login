# Chapter 220

## Plot

After Won Myunghoon leaves, Jin Taekyung confirms that the approaching Named Monster is the Black Wyvern from the incident three years earlier and chooses to face it alone. Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Song Song remain behind; Butler Kim layers magical barriers to protect them. Song Song rejects Taekyung's confession, saying she will not change her attitude for money or fame, but accepts treating him as a same-age friend and guildmate, and they agree to drink soju after the fight. The Black Wyvern arrives, having grown to more than fifty meters—at least two or three times its former size—with one cloudy eye. Taekyung recalls the comrades killed in the earlier incident, including Kim Hyunsu, Lee Hyerim, Song Donghyeok, Park Sangho, Kim Haneung, Park Gwanghyeon, and Hong Cheonsu. Hong Cheonsu had been a ten-year veteran who saved Taekyung from goblins and was called hyung; he died leaving behind a spouse and three children. Taekyung advances toward the Black Wyvern after three years and ninety-two days, channels more than one jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi and accumulated rage into his spear, and prepares to kill it with blue-burning Aura and Spear Energy.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon leaves the Mutated Gate after Taekyung orders him and the Star Guild away.
- Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Song Song remain with Taekyung; Butler Kim creates layered magical barriers while Taekyung fights alone.
- Song Song rejects Taekyung's confession but accepts a same-age friendship and guildmate relationship; they agree to drink soju after the battle.
- The Black Wyvern from the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident returns as a Named Monster, now more than fifty meters long and at least two or three times larger than three years earlier, with one cloudy eye.
- Taekyung remembers the comrades killed in the earlier incident, including Kim Hyunsu and Hong Cheonsu, and resolves to end his grudge with the Black Wyvern.
- Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi has surpassed one jiazi, and his accumulated rage transforms with his internal energy into blue-burning Aura and Spear Energy.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 굵고 길게 as “long and broad” in Taekyung's revised life motto.
- Render 남녀칠세부동석 as “boys and girls over seven shouldn’t sit together.”
- Retain “hyung” for Taekyung's address to Hong Cheonsu.
- Keep Song Song's rejection blunt and practical, followed by the shift into casual same-age-friend speech.

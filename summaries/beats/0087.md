# Chapter 87

## Plot

Im Changsoo keeps his promise and deposits four billion won into Jin Taekyung's account. Taekyung is dazed by the sudden fortune and visits his family home, where Hayeon is studying despite a 39-degree fever. Hayeon reveals that their mother is secretly working at a restaurant and asks Taekyung not to look for her, but he already knows where she is.

Kim Jeonghee, Taekyung and Hayeon's fifty-year-old mother, is mistreated by the restaurant owner and insulted over Taekyung's F-rank status. She finally breaks from her usual submission, defends her son, and curses the owner. Taekyung arrives at the restaurant, pushes aside an employee who tries to stop him, and calls Jeonghee Mom.

## Continuity

- Im Changsoo fulfills his promise by transferring four billion won to Jin Taekyung.
- Hayeon has a 39-degree fever, left school after second period, and continues studying before summer vacation.
- Hayeon knows that Kim Jeonghee is secretly working in a restaurant and has been asked to keep it from Taekyung.
- Kim Jeonghee has worked in the restaurant kitchen for over a year while supporting her family.
- Taekyung's father died when a Gate opened in the middle of downtown during the Great Cataclysm.
- Kim Jeonghee openly defends Taekyung and rejects the idea that his Hunter Grade determines his worth.
- Kim Jeonghee confronts and verbally abuses the restaurant owner after the owner insults her and Taekyung.
- Taekyung arrives at the restaurant and identifies Kim Jeonghee as his mother.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain oppa for Hayeon's direct address to Taekyung, especially when making an important request.
- Use ajumma for the generic address 아줌마 and retain its explanatory footnote.
- Render 사장님 contextually as Boss or ma'am when Kim Jeonghee addresses the restaurant owner.
- Retain goshiwon with an explanatory footnote.

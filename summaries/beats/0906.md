# Chapter 906

## Plot

As traitors attack the banquet, Ma Sanbao reveals that he is the disciple of Cang Gong, now known as the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, and claims the Emperor has used Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang as expendable weapons. Taekyung defeats Ma despite his injuries and depleted internal energy, killing him with a short spear.

## Continuity

- Ma Sanbao revealed his allegiance to Cang Gong, the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, before Jin Taekyung killed him during the banquet-hall battle.
- Taekyung is badly injured, with his hands severely damaged and his internal energy depleted.
- The banquet-hall battle continues; Jeok Cheongang is fighting Cang Gong, now called the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, while the Emperor and So Gyo remain on the sidelines.
- Ma claimed the Emperor had used Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang to eliminate So Gyo; the claim remains unconfirmed.
- So Gyo's identity and allegiance remain unknown.
- Silent figures have appeared near Hyuk Mujin's group as they travel to the Jiangsu–Zhejiang border.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Eastern Heaven Demon Lord for 동천마군, Cang Gong's bestowed title; keep it distinct from Lord of Heaven.

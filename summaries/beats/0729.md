# Chapter 729

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Choi Minwoo seek government support for publicly releasing Cheon Taemin's Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique, a safe and broadly applicable method renamed from the Smiling Mana Cultivation Method. They persuade President Baek Hanseong to announce government cooperation and an upcoming major announcement, framing the release as a national project. Baek's televised statement triggers worldwide media upheaval and widespread public praise, while Ares Guild executives challenge Choi for withholding advance notice. Choi apologizes but retains their reluctant respect because of Cheon Taemin's name and his own legitimacy. Jin then confronts the executives' silence after Choi's apology, forcing the meeting toward its next response.

## Continuity

- The Heaven and Earth Cultivation Technique will be publicly released through a cooperative government-backed announcement scheduled for two days later.
- Baek Hanseong has publicly committed the Blue House to cooperation and framed the cultivation technique's release as a Korean national project.
- Jin and Choi are concealing the truth about Cheon Taemin's unconscious condition and Jin's authorship of the technique.
- The public and international media have reacted explosively but predominantly positively to the planned release.
- Ares Guild executives remain wary of Choi Minwoo's unilateral authority, though Cheon Taemin's apparent intervention gives Choi formidable legitimacy.
- Jin has directly challenged the Ares executives after Choi's apology, and their response remains pending.

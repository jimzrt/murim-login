# Chapter 289

## Plot

Jin Taekyung convinces Team Leader Choi not to abandon the Peace Guild despite Ares Guild's overwhelming strength. Choi reveals that Ares officially has more than one hundred A-rank Hunters besides Lee Jungryong, along with countless mid- and low-rank Hunters and additional undisclosed forces.

Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong each choose to remain with the Peace Guild. Song Song demands double pay and hazard compensation, while Im Kkeokjeong decides to continue his Hunter work after recovering from his injuries. Taekyung then discovers the Martial Arts Manual Creation Skill, which requires at least 300 sheets of A4 paper.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong commit to continuing with the Peace Guild despite its coming conflict with Ares Guild.
- Taekyung promises to help make the Peace Guild strong enough to confront Ares Guild and reveals that he possesses more secrets and power than Choi has calculated.
- Ares Guild officially has more than one hundred A-rank Hunters besides Lee Jungryong, as well as countless mid- and low-rank Hunters; the emphasis on officially indicates that additional undisclosed forces exist.
- Song Song remains with the Peace Guild after requesting double her current salary and hazard pay; she intends to retire at thirty and has two years remaining toward that goal.
- Im Kkeokjeong chooses to remain a Hunter and continue with the Peace Guild after recovering, despite still having both arms bandaged and his actual return to field work not yet confirmed.
- Jin Taekyung discovers the System Skill 비급제작, which requires at least 300 sheets of A4 paper to create a martial arts manual; no manual has yet been created.

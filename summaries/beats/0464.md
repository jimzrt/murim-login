# Chapter 464

## Plot

Taekyung discovers the Dongting Fisherman’s underwater secret refuge, follows its passage into a cavern, and confirms from a recently extinguished torch that the Fisherman is inside. He finds unexplained damage throughout the passage, then reaches the cavern’s central chamber and launches a surprise attack from dry ground after summoning Fire Dragon Armor, the Myriad-Poison Ring, and White Flame. Just as his Force-covered spearhead reaches the slowly turning Dongting Fisherman, a snow-white streak of light strikes it, leaving the attack’s outcome unresolved.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has entered the Dongting Fisherman’s secret refuge and confirmed that the Dongting Fisherman is inside; the refuge’s half-burned torch suggests he was present within the last one or two shichen.
- Freshly broken sections throughout the cave passage bear the traces of one person deliberately applying considerable force; the cause and identity remain unknown.
- Taekyung launched a surprise attack against the Dongting Fisherman while fighting on solid ground, avoiding the System’s underwater twenty-percent power reduction; a white streak of light has just interrupted the attack.

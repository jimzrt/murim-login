# Chapter 1103

## Plot

Jin Taekyung cuts through the fanatics attacking Xining, entering a No-self Trance and nearing another insight. A blood-red attack breaks his trance; Cheongpung intercepts it, and the blast breaches the city wall.

Taekyung survives with no apparent serious injuries and returns to the fight, but Cheongpung is missing. Taekyung blames himself for pursuing enlightenment as the Blood Lord approaches.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung intercepted the blood-red attack aimed at Jin Taekyung; after the blast breached Xining’s wall, Cheongpung could not be found.
- The attackers breached Xining’s wall, and fighting continues at the breach.
- The Blood Lord is approaching Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep No-self distinct from Trance: No-self is the self-forgetting state; Trance describes the intense dreamlike state Taekyung experiences.
- Render 일기당천 as “One Against a Thousand.”

# Chapter 310

## Plot

Heavenly Axe and the five hundred bandits of Black Stone Stronghold refuse the customary toll and reveal that they know the Yongbong Escort Bureau is transporting a Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng secretly commissioned by the Zhongnan Sect. After four months of attacks and heavy losses, Ju Hwaran suspects that one of the three senior escort captains who knew the secret may have leaked it. Song Ilseom abruptly kills a bandit and declares that everyone must fight, ending any chance of negotiation. As Heavenly Axe attacks, Ju Hwaran shields a strange young man who has arrived with three companions and a pack frame. At his request, she shouts for help; he accepts the resulting Quest, and a wave of heat surrounds her.

## Continuity

- Heavenly Axe and five hundred Black Stone Stronghold bandits intend to massacre the Yongbong Escort Bureau and silence witnesses rather than accept the usual toll.
- The Yongbong Escort Bureau has endured dozens of battles during the past four months and now has slightly more than seventy people remaining, including roughly forty escorts and the caravan porters.
- A Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng secretly commissioned by the Zhongnan Sect is known only to a handful of Yongbong Escort Bureau personnel; if fully digested, it can grant a full jiazi of internal energy.
- Ju Hwaran suspects an insider among Seokchil, Noh Piljung, and Song Ilseom, but the leak's source is not established.
- Song Ilseom initiates the battle by killing a Black Stone Stronghold bandit and ordering everyone to fight; his motive is unresolved.
- Taekyung's four-person party arrives during the standoff, and Ju Hwaran accepts the young man's request for help, triggering a Quest and a wave of heat.

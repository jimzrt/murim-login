# Chapter 572

## Plot

An intelligent Kraken recalls how Dongseok fed it magic power gifted by “that person,” transforming the black-sea creature into a vastly stronger being that killed Dongseok and tore open a rift to the surface world. Leading Mermen and Sirens in a Monster Wave, it attacks Busan, destroys Gwangan Bridge, and kills civilians before Taekyung confronts it. Taekyung relocates the battle away from survivors, severs multiple tentacles with White Flame and Force infused with Scorching Yang Qi, and drives his spear toward the Kraken’s face; the fight remains unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Busan Monster Wave is led by an intelligent Kraken that originated in the black sea and gained immense magic power after absorbing a gift from Dongseok on behalf of “that person.”
- The Kraken killed Dongseok after being empowered, tore open a rift into the surface world, and led nearly a thousand Mermen and Sirens onto the coast.
- The Kraken’s wave destroyed the middle of Gwangan Bridge and caused extensive civilian deaths; Taekyung is now fighting it away from the remaining survivors, and the confrontation is unresolved.
- Dongseok slaughtered the raid party before empowering the Kraken and said he would accomplish something; his objective and the identity of “that person” remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 크라켄 as Kraken and 그것 as italicized the thing until the narration identifies it as the Kraken.
- Render 마력 as magic power, 동석 씨 as Mr. Dongseok, and 김 팀아 as Team Leader Kim.
- Retain Merman, Siren, Monster Wave, Gwangan Bridge, White Flame, Force, Scorching Yang Qi, and Stepping on Empty Air as established renderings.

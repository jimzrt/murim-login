# Chapter 590

## Plot

Jin Taekyung erupts through Ares Guild's successive ceilings with Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, then stores White Flame and uses Flame Divine Palm to burn through the healers and defenders who attack him. He condemns them for obeying Go Jun's order to kill him and warns that the Busan and Pyeongchang Monster Waves may have been artificially caused by Go Jun.

In Ares's indoor garden, Jin recognizes a middle-aged Guild executive as part of Song Cheonwoo's faction but continues past him when the man chooses to fight under Go Jun's orders. Jin exposes Go Jun's claim that Song returned to the United Kingdom as a lie, destroys a lifelike statue of Lee Jungryong with Force, and orders the remaining defenders to either lower their weapons and clear his path or guard his back.

## Continuity

- Jin has fought through Ares Guild headquarters, incapacitating roughly two hundred elite Ares Hunters while continuing toward Go Jun; the Skeleton King remains behind to protect the Peace Guild and its people.
- The middle-aged greatsword-wielding Guild executive belongs to Song Cheonwoo's faction but still describes himself as a hunting dog forced to obey Go Jun's orders.
- Jin concludes that Go Jun used a stand-in to make it appear that Song Cheonwoo returned to the United Kingdom.
- Jin has destroyed the lifelike statue of Lee Jungryong in Ares's garden and demanded that the remaining defenders choose between yielding and supporting his advance.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Jin's openly profane, accusatory voice when he condemns Ares personnel for obeying orders and justifying their choices.
- Use eight-tenths mastery for 팔 성, preserving the established Flame Divine Palm terminology.
- Preserve the hunting-dog metaphor for 사냥개, stand-in for 대역, and ambitious warlord for 효웅.

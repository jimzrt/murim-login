# Chapter 262

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung arrive at Shaolin to find civilians, monks, and masked attackers massacred. Jeok kills several dozen attackers, rescues Hongcheon, and together with Taekyung defeats Flame Tiger, who is killed by Jeok after attempting to bargain with information about Dark Heaven. Han Su escapes with the Green Jade Buddha Staff and reaches a rendezvous with an unidentified young man called the Blood Lord, who claims to be Jeok Cheongang's enemy and to have killed his most precious friend.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung arrive at Shaolin during the massacre and find Hongcheon alive but severely injured.
- Jeok Cheongang kills Flame Tiger after Flame Tiger attacks him while using Hongcheon as a hostage; Taekyung helps rescue Hongcheon and contributes to the fight.
- Han Su escapes Shaolin with the Green Jade Buddha Staff and seeks a designated rendezvous with the Blood Lord.
- The unidentified young man called the Blood Lord cannot be sensed by Taekyung and claims to have killed Jeok Cheongang's most precious friend.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Blood Lord for 혈주.
- Use Sanzu River for 삼도천.
- Render 권강 as fist force, distinct from 권기 as Fist Energy.
- Use Old Master for 노야 when Taekyung addresses Jeok Cheongang privately.

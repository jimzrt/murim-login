# Chapter 730

## Plot

An important guest arrives at Ares Guild during a leadership meeting. Choi Minwoo ends the meeting and leads Jin Taekyung to his private office, where a blank platinum business card engraved with the ravens Huginn and Muninn identifies the visitor's connection to Odin, the world's greatest Guild. A person with a massive, carefully contained aura waits by the fireplace, but their identity is not revealed.

In an evidence storage room, the youngest employee discovers that a necklace from Go Jun's personal effects has vanished despite the room remaining locked and its anti-theft and alarm Magic never activating. Manager Kim and the Security Team Leader confirm the disappearance, leaving the manager terrified of the consequences.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo is Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master and its new City Lord.
- Jin Taekyung proposed using Cheon Taemin's reputation in connection with making the current public project public.
- A blank platinum business card engraved with Huginn and Muninn is associated with an important visitor to Ares Guild.
- Odin is recognized as the world's greatest Guild.
- A necklace from Go Jun's personal effects has disappeared from secured evidence storage without triggering its protective Magic.
- The person waiting in Choi Minwoo's private office possesses a massive, carefully contained aura.

# Chapter 277

## Plot

Jin Taekyung slaughtered the Black Hunters surrounding Im Yeongjun, quickly killing several of them while confirming that his first target was not one of Im Kkeokjeong's three attackers. Im Yeongjun revealed that the attack on Kkeokjeong had been an order from above and offered to negotiate Taekyung's retirement as an A-rank Hunter, threatening his mother and younger sister when Taekyung refused. Taekyung rejected the offer and chose the way of Murim over compromise.

Taekyung continued cutting through the Black Hunters, including their tanks, mages, melee fighters, and archers. He defeated Im Yeongjun in three exchanges, destroying his shoulder with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, then pursued him into the mansion and shattered its layered defensive barriers with the Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist. With Im Yeongjun and the surviving Black Hunters trapped inside the ruined mansion, Taekyung declared himself a monster like them and asked whether Im knew the Tendon-Splitting and Bone-Twisting technique.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung killed numerous Black Hunters at Im Yeongjun's countryside mansion and drove the survivors inside.
- Im Yeongjun confirmed that the assault on Im Kkeokjeong was an order from an unnamed authority above him.
- Im Yeongjun threatened Taekyung's mother and younger sister and offered to arrange his life as a compensated civilian instead of an A-rank Hunter.
- Taekyung rejected all compromise, deciding to answer the Black Hunters according to the way of Murim.
- Taekyung defeated Im Yeongjun in three exchanges and destroyed one of his shoulders with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist.
- Taekyung destroyed the mansion's powerful layered defensive barriers and severely damaged the mansion with the Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist.
- Im Yeongjun survived the initial assault and remained inside the ruined mansion with surviving Black Hunters when Taekyung confronted him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 멸천신권 as Heaven-Destroying Divine Fist, distinct from 멸염신권, rendered Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist.
- Render 삼성의 공력 in this context as three-tenths of internal energy, not Three Saints.
- Retain the stepping-stone metaphor in Im Yeongjun's proposed negotiation and Taekyung's rejection.
- Preserve Taekyung's graphic, dryly mocking first-person voice during the massacre.

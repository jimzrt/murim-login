# Chapter 662

## Plot

Baeksang recounts the battle at Great Snow Mountain during the Great Faction War. The Southern Army, led alongside the Zhongnan Sect under Venerable Wusang, was ambushed by an elite Demonic Cult strike force. Baekhwi disappeared while pursuing the enemy with several warriors, and Baeksang later learned that the Great Snow Fiend had killed him and Venerable Wusang. Baeksang further claims that allied forces deliberately withheld support, killed the messenger, pursued the retreating enemy for military credit, and later erased the messenger's corpse and the evidence implicating the Zhongnan Sect. His old hatred of the Central Plains and Han Chinese erupts against Jin in the underground prison, where he attacks and declares that Jin's people taught him what he had to do.

## Continuity

- Baekhwi was killed without leaving a corpse during the Great Snow Mountain battle; Baeksang identifies the Great Snow Fiend as the killer.
- Venerable Wusang, former Sect Leader of the Zhongnan Sect and master of the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord, died while fighting with a recurring Internal Injury, ultimately killed by the Great Snow Fiend.
- Baeksang claims that the Zhongnan Sect and other Western Army allies deliberately ignored his request for support, killed the messenger, pursued retreating Demonic Cult forces for military credit, and later removed the messenger's corpse and other evidence.
- Baeksang's hatred of the Central Plains and Han Chinese stems from his belief that Nanman allies were betrayed after fighting in the Great Faction War.
- Baeksang attacked Jin in the underground prison with tangible internal energy, cutting Jin's forehead, but Jin remained still and confronted him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 무상진인 as Venerable Wusang.
- Render 대설귀 as Great Snow Fiend.
- Distinguish the Southern Army (남군) from the Western Army (서군) in the Great Faction War account.
- Retain Outer Lands for 새외 and Han Chinese for 한족.

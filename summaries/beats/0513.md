# Chapter 513

## Plot

In Xixia, Henan, a merchant recognizes a young martial artist as the unnamed Young Sect Leader of the Black Dragon Demon Gate and sells him a famed sword for twenty silver nyang, supposedly after deducting the merchant's life. Blood Cudgel Do Sangho challenges and insults the young leader, who provokes him in return; when Blood Cudgel threatens to take revenge, an eight-foot subordinate crushes his head with a massive two-section staff. As the crowd scatters, monks from Shaolin Temple approach.

## Continuity

- The Black Dragon Demon Gate, an unorthodox faction from Gansu, has sent its unnamed Young Sect Leader to Xixia in Henan; the young leader is arrogant and fearless, while an eight-foot subordinate obeys and protects him by killing Blood Cudgel Do Sangho.
- The New Murim Alliance has been publicly announced from Mount Song; Shaolin, Huashan, the Nine Sects and One Gang, and the Five Great Families are moving to join it, while observers suspect the Black Dragon Demon Gate may be heading there to enter the alliance.

# Chapter 565

## Plot

After Magic Johnson leaves, Taekyung reviews the chip's holographic footage and discovers more than twenty videos documenting simultaneous Gate and monster crises worldwide, alongside a seven-percent year-over-year rise in mana levels. He suspects the beginning of a second Great Cataclysm and realizes the strengthened monsters are increasingly overwhelming normal defenses. Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim arrive, confirm that national concealment is no longer viable, and decide that Peace Guild must reinforce Gate defenses without postponing action against Ares Guild; Choi reveals that their errand involved a major Ares figure who leads the faction threatening Go Jun.

## Continuity

- Magic Johnson's chip contains more than twenty videos and attached materials showing simultaneous Gate and monster crises across multiple regions, while worldwide mana levels have risen seven percent over the previous year.
- Taekyung suspects the crisis may be the beginning of a second Great Cataclysm and intends to accelerate the plan he has developed since Murim to prevent Korea from becoming a hellish peninsula.
- Team Leader Choi considers the national concealment limit already exceeded and says delayed disclosure would only increase confusion.
- Choi's immediate priority is reinforcing Gate defenses, even though doing so reduces Peace Guild's raid personnel and its ability to fully use every Guild Gate.
- Choi will not postpone action against Ares Guild; he describes a major Ares figure as a former war hero who helped his maternal grandfather suppress the Great Cataclysm, was once Lee Jungryong's most trusted friend, and now leads the largest internal faction threatening Go Jun.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Mutated Gate, Giant Mantis, Fire Rain, and Patrache as established renderings.
- Render 폭등 as skyrocketing increase or surge according to sentence rhythm, 헬반도화 as Korea becoming a hellish peninsula, and 폭몬 as monsterfall.
- Preserve Taekyung's profanity and game-like comparisons in the discussion of monster strength and type advantages.
- Render 최 팀장님 as Team Leader Choi and 도련님 as Young Master.

# Chapter 969

## Plot

Murong Baek reveals that he spared Jamukha decades ago, then attacks his longtime friend and former rival Peng Cheolhu, accusing no one aloud but demonstrating his allegiance to Dark Heaven. Peng fights back and is buried beneath the collapsing cliff, but Murong senses that he is still alive.

Murong retrieves his spear and rebukes Jamukha for disobeying orders and nearly taking an improved Temporary Strength Pill, whose severe addictive effects make it too dangerous for the commander. Jamukha has lost nearly half his shoulder but expects to recover after regulating his qi.

Murong finds Jin Mukyung sheltering the bloodied Cheol Mubaek and Wipeng beneath the rubble and moves to attack them. A blue-white-flamed spear smashes through the cliff before he can strike.

## Continuity

- Murong Baek spared Jamukha during the pursuit decades ago, recruited him into Dark Heaven, and is now directing his military role.
- Murong Baek betrayed Peng Cheolhu, his longtime friend and former rival, and intends to kill him; Peng remains alive beneath the rubble.
- Murong plans to ambush the arriving Huashan and Zhongnan reinforcements after preparing the gorge, while eliminating survivors who could reveal Dark Heaven's actions.
- Jamukha disobeyed Murong's order to delay Peng and did not take the improved Temporary Strength Pill; his shoulder is nearly half severed.
- Jin Mukyung, Cheol Mubaek, and Wipeng are beneath the collapsed cliff; their condition is unresolved. An unidentified spear with blue-white flames has interrupted Murong's attack.

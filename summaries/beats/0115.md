# Chapter 115

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, Wolhwa, and Hyuk Mujin find Cheol Mubaek critically wounded after Pung Yang defeated him alone with the aid of a red pill. Wolhwa gives Cheol a temporary medicine, and Jin Mukyung orders Wolhwa and Hyuk Mujin to remain behind while he and Taekyung enter the fortress.

Jin Mukyung openly challenges Pung Yang, causing the System time limit to disappear, while Taekyung and Mukyung attack the Red Wind Band. Pung Yang recognizes that Mukyung is stronger than himself and that Taekyung is slaughtering his subordinates, so he consumes another Temporary Strength Pill. Before the battle's outcome is determined, Lee Seowol has already agreed to marry Pung Yang under threat that he will mutilate the Mount Heng Sword Sect's surviving martial artists and destroy their dantians.

## Continuity

- Cheol Mubaek survived Pung Yang's attack but remains critically wounded with broken limbs and severe internal injuries; Wolhwa's medicine is only a temporary measure.
- Cheol Mubaek warned Jin Taekyung's group to beware Pung Yang's red pill, which is identified as the Temporary Strength Pill.
- Wolhwa and Hyuk Mujin stay behind to protect Cheol Mubaek while Jin Mukyung and Jin Taekyung enter the fortress.
- Jin Mukyung is stronger than Pung Yang and comparable to Cheol Mubaek at this level; Jin Taekyung is defeating the Red Wind Band's mounted bandits.
- Pung Yang coerced Lee Seowol into agreeing to marry him by threatening the surviving Mount Heng Sword Sect martial artists and Cheol Mubaek.
- Pung Yang intends to use the Mount Heng Sword Sect's name to recruit martial artists and expand his power.
- Pung Yang consumed another Temporary Strength Pill after Jin Mukyung and Jin Taekyung began overwhelming him; his eyes turned blood-red.

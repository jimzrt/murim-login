# Chapter 747

## Plot

A colossal tsunami destroys Tokyo Bay, its port, and nearby industrial areas before lightning from the awakened deep-sea monster annihilates rescue helicopters, Japanese aircraft, and hundreds of Hunters. Japan identifies the monster as Susanoo, while Jin recognizes it as Leviathan, an ancient S-rank sea demon once commanded by Asmodeus.

As the Prophet announces a second wave of terrorist attacks and monster waves begin in Manhattan and elsewhere, Japan requests emergency assistance from Korea. Ares Guild and Peace Guild prepare five volunteer teams of B-rank Hunters or higher, while the Air Force readies an approximately thirty-minute flight because Teleport Magic is unsafe amid Tokyo's sharply unstable magical-power distribution.

Jin recalls that Leviathan vanished after the Demon King's death and wonders whether its return after more than thirty years is a coincidence or an inevitability deliberately arranged by someone.

## Continuity

- The Prophet has announced a second series of terrorist attacks, and monster waves beginning in Manhattan have placed the United States and other affected countries under emergency entry and exit restrictions.
- Leviathan, an ancient and extremely powerful S-rank monster once commanded by Asmodeus, has reappeared in Japan after more than thirty years and is attacking with enormous waves and widespread lightning; Japan calls it Susanoo.
- Tokyo Bay, its port, nearby industrial areas, and surrounding coastal defenses have been overwhelmed, while the tsunami has already swallowed nearly ten cities and threatens Tokyo.
- Japan has formally requested emergency assistance through diplomatic channels, and Korea is currently the only nearby Asian country able to provide support.
- Ares Guild and Peace Guild are sending five volunteer teams of B-rank Hunters or higher as an advance force, supported by the Korean Air Force.
- Teleport Magic cannot currently be used safely because Tokyo's magical-power distribution has risen to more than three times its previous level and is highly unstable.
- Jin suspects that Leviathan's return may have been intentionally caused rather than coincidental.

# Chapter 235

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang subjects Taekyung to increasingly brutal training on Mount Jiuhua, including waterfall horse stances, weighted push-ups, cliff climbing, and retrieving a swallow's nest. Taekyung completes Fire King's Hellfire Training-2, earns the Unmoving achievement, converts Toughness into Endurance, gains two levels and 50 Bonus Points, and continues training for more than two months.

Jeok's increasing sleep and memory lapses become undeniable when he forgets ordering Taekyung's fist-and-foot training. Taekyung asks whether he is ill, and Jeok leads him to a hidden place instead of answering directly.

Jeok brings Taekyung into the Fire Gate Cavern, explains the Fire Gate Clan's centuries-long history, and reveals its seventeen ancestral urns. The cavern releases the restriction on Taekyung's internal energy, and Jeok offers to pass on the clan's martial arts. Taekyung accepts because he trusts Jeok, generating the Heir of the Fire Gate Quest, but Jeok's confusion interrupts the first lesson. The two then vanish from Mount Jiuhua as the seasons change three times.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has spent more than two months undergoing increasingly severe training from Jeok Cheongang on Mount Jiuhua.
- Taekyung completed Fire King's Hellfire Training-2, earned the Unmoving achievement, changed Toughness into Endurance, gained two levels, and received 50 Bonus Points.
- Taekyung completed the first Fist-and-Foot Training repeat Quest; Strength, Stamina, and Agility each increased by 1, with additional rewards deferred until he learns related martial arts.
- Jeok Cheongang's sleep has increased and he has displayed repeated forgetfulness; Taekyung recognizes these symptoms as dementia, while Jeok acknowledges suffering from the ailments of old age.
- The Fire Gate Cavern is the place where the Fire Gate Clan began and where its legacy continues; seventeen urns of former Sect Leaders are arranged around a blue-white flame.
- Entering the Fire Gate Cavern lifted the restriction on Taekyung's internal energy, which reacts strongly because it originated from the Blazing Flame Divine Pill.
- Jeok Cheongang has chosen to transmit the Fire Gate Clan's martial arts to Taekyung, and Taekyung agreed because he trusts Jeok despite the danger posed by Jeok's memory loss.
- The Heir of the Fire Gate Quest was created, and Jeok brought out the Fire Gate Divine Technique to begin Taekyung's instruction.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 치매 as dementia.
- Use Fire Gate Cavern for 열화동 and Heir of the Fire Gate for 열화의 계승자.
- Preserve Fire King's Hellfire Training-2 as the source's distinct quest-title variant.
- Use Fist-and-Foot Training for 권각 수련 and fist-and-foot martial arts for 권각술.

# Chapter 655

## Plot

Yohi's Western Yao Estate was attacked by an unidentified Supreme Peak master who used Force to swiftly slaughter more than a hundred Yao warriors and beasts; no survivors had been found. Jin and Yayul Mok concluded that Dark Heaven had directly intervened and likely mobilized a Supreme Peak master, while faint footprints indicated that a third party had abducted Yohi and Heugung rather than the attack being their staged conflict.

Heugung had visited Yohi shortly after parting from Jin with four guards, all of whom were killed. A severed wrist believed to be Heugung's was found beside Yohi's nearly scentless pouch, but his death remained unconfirmed. Baeksang arrived before the Beast Miao King and declared that the guilty party was coming out on their own, revealing that the only known Supreme Peak masters in Nanman were not limited to Baeksang and the Beast Miao King.

## Continuity

- Yohi's Western Yao Estate was devastated, with more than a hundred Yao warriors and beasts dead and no survivors located.
- The attacker was a Supreme Peak master whose swift, precise use of Force indicated extensive killing experience.
- Jin and Yayul Mok concluded that Dark Heaven had directly intervened, probably by deploying at least one of its Supreme Peak masters, but the motive remains unknown.
- Heugung visited Yohi with four guards shortly after parting from Jin; the guards were killed, and a severed wrist believed to be Heugung's was found, but his death is unconfirmed.
- Faint footprints showed that a third party intervened and that the incident was not a staged attack by Heugung or Yohi; at least three people had been present.
- Yohi disappeared from her estate, leaving behind a nearly scentless pouch as a possible clue.
- Baeksang arrived before the Beast Miao King and said that the guilty party was coming out on their own; the person he meant remains unidentified.

# Chapter 449

## Plot

Mu Song's group reaches Donghu Stronghold after crossing Tianling Falls, but receives no response. Jeok Cheongang retrieves the corpse of Wang Pil, the Little Tide Demon and Hwang Chung's right-hand man, revealing that something terrible has happened. The group reaches the stronghold to find its ferry landing, settlement, ships, and inhabitants—including civilians and children—completely destroyed. Hwang Chung is also found dead. Taekyung and Jeok suspect that Dark Heaven may have used a hidden Moving Formation to attack without being detected, but the culprit and motive remain unknown.

## Continuity

- Donghu Stronghold was destroyed and every person found there, including its civilian families and children, was killed; Wang Pil and Hwang Chung were among the dead.
- Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang suspect that Dark Heaven could have used a hidden Moving Formation to bypass Tianling Falls, but this remains unconfirmed.

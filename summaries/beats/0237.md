# Chapter 237

## Plot

After a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern, Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang leave the cavern. Taekyung can open its stone gate with the internal energy of the Scorching Sun Divine Arts and departs while still wearing iron balls chained to all four limbs.

At the foot of Mount Jiuhua, they encounter Hong, a herbalist from Sichuan who has found no spiritual herbs. Jeok has already harvested the mountain's herbs, but Taekyung gives Hong a remaining ginseng root and sells it to a group of martial artists for one hundred silver nyang. Namgung Ryong then raises the price to three hundred silver nyang to compensate Jeok for offending him, identifies himself as the Family Head of the Nangong Family, and is described as the richest man in Anhui.

## Continuity

- Taekyung completed one year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern and left it with Jeok Cheongang, formally referring to himself as Jeok's unworthy Disciple.
- Taekyung can freely enter and exit the Fire Gate Cavern using the internal energy of the Scorching Sun Divine Arts.
- Taekyung still wears numerous iron balls chained to all four limbs after completing the year of training.
- Jeok Cheongang had harvested all the spiritual herbs on Mount Jiuhua before descending with Taekyung.
- Taekyung gave a remaining ginseng root to Hong, a struggling herbalist from Sichuan, and arranged its sale to passing martial artists.
- Namgung Ryong appeared at Mount Jiuhua with dozens of highly trained martial artists, paid three hundred silver nyang for the ginseng, and identified himself as the Family Head of the Nangong Family.
- Jeok Cheongang's description establishes Namgung Ryong as the richest man in Anhui.

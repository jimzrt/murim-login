# Chapter 709

## Plot

Jin Taekyung returns to the battlefield instead of abandoning the Baekcheon Unit, urging Wang Ho and the remaining warriors to survive together. Inspired by his resolve, Wang Ho changes their objective from delaying the Southern Heaven Demon Empress to killing her. Jin endures the Empress's internal attack and drives White Flame through her chest, but his heart meridian is torn and he reaches the brink of death. As the Empress attempts to finish him with her hand blade, an unidentified attacker arrives and destroys her only arm while issuing a cold insult.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has returned to fight beside the Baekcheon Unit and is determined that they survive together.
- Fewer than twenty Baekcheon Unit warriors remain when they launch their final assault on the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- Jin Taekyung drives White Flame through the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's chest after enduring another internal attack from her.
- Jin Taekyung's heart meridian is torn, his strength fails, and death is approaching as he releases White Flame.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is critically wounded, her innate qi is being consumed by her remaining life, and she attempts to kill Jin before dying.
- An unidentified attacker destroys the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's only arm while she is about to strike Jin.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 심맥 as heart meridian.
- Render 수도 as hand blade.
- Retain innate qi, Force, Sword Energy, White Flame, Baekcheon Unit, and Fiend.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's first-person conversational voice and profanity in moments of extreme resolve.

# Chapter 1004

## Plot

Taekyung’s party reaches a eerily empty city whose gates open to reveal Sima Gong and roughly a thousand Black Dragon Demon Gate disciples. Sima Gong explains that the civilians left of their own accord and welcomes the group; he and Jeok Cheongang trade familiar barbs. After Sima Gong recognizes Taekyung, Taekyung tries to conceal that he knocked Hyuk Mujin from his horse, then has Namho drop jerky on Mujin to knock him out again before the group enters.

## Continuity

- Sima Gong and roughly a thousand Black Dragon Demon Gate disciples are gathered at the gates of an occupied city; the streets are empty, with no visible signs of battle or blood.
- Sima Gong is Sama Pyo’s father and is personally familiar with Jeok Cheongang, who openly says he dislikes him.
- Taekyung notices Sama Pyo’s eyes sink as his father approaches; the reason is not established.

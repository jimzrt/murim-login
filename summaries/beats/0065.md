# Chapter 65

## Plot

Jin Mukyung is shocked by Taekyung's transformation over the last three years. He recognizes Taekyung as a First Rate martial artist standing before the Peak realm, and is increasingly pressured as Taekyung's speed continues to rise.

Mukyung escalates the spar by drawing on his internal energy, defeats Taekyung, and destroys the pavilion. Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin survive the collapse and are taken to the Medicine King Hall, while the family mistakes the incident for an attack by an unknown assassin.

Socheon explains that the family suspects the assassin may be the Head Elder's hidden disciple and has formed a pursuit team. Jin Wikyung has ordered the attacker's immediate execution, while Hyuk Mujin is publicly credited with fighting bravely and protecting Taekyung, prompting rumors that he will become the next Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung judges Taekyung to be First Rate and close to the Peak realm, with growth and speed that astonish him.
- Mukyung defeats Taekyung in their spar; the pavilion is destroyed, Taekyung survives with bruises, and Hyuk Mujin is badly injured.
- The attacker is believed by the Jin Family to be an unidentified assassin, possibly the Head Elder's hidden disciple; a pursuit team is tracking the assassin.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 삼재검법 as Three Calamities Sword Technique.
- Use Medicine King Hall, Second Young Master, and Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion for 약왕당, 이공자, and 수문각주.
- Retain gongcheong seokyu for 공청석유 and explain the rare-elixir and petroleum wordplay in a footnote.
- Render 군자 as junzi with a footnote explaining the Confucian ideal of a morally upright gentleman.
- Retain Hyung-nim for 형님 in Taekyung's deferential warning, distinct from his established casual hyung.

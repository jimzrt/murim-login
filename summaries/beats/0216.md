# Chapter 216

## Plot

A Lv. 97 Green Wyvern attacked the joint raid team, but the Hunters survived its acidic poison and dive attack after receiving magical buffs.

Won Myunghoon killed the Green Wyvern alone by piercing its neck with an Aura-coated spear. Taekyung received no EXP because he made no contribution and privately judged Won's combat ability far weaker than expected.

Won and Team Leader 1 privately discussed the Yeti's Necklace, whose effect had apparently brought the Wyvern to them. Won wanted an even stronger monster to appear so surviving witnesses could testify.

## Continuity

- The joint raid was accidentally redirected to The Black Wyvern's Nest; Taekyung insisted on proceeding despite his trauma, and a Lv. 97 Green Wyvern appeared before the group left the jungle area.
- Won Myunghoon killed the Green Wyvern alone with an Aura-coated spear; Taekyung received no EXP because he made no contribution.
- Won and Team Leader 1 discussed the Yeti's Necklace, which Team Leader 1 acquired and magical scans cannot detect; Won wants a stronger monster to appear so surviving witnesses can testify.

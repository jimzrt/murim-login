# Chapter 373

## Plot

On the return to the Sichuan Tang Clan, the Slaughter Saint tells the two Sect Leaders, Jeok Cheongang, and Jin Taekyung that he intends to resume living as Mungyeong, the young physician, and never kill again. He demands that his identity remain secret; Taekyung agrees, then deliberately addresses him as Mungyeong, causing the person walking ahead to stagger. Back at the pavilion, Hyuk Mujin and the beggar remain unaware of Mungyeong's identity.

Taekyung reviews a flood of System notifications reporting his arrival at the Supreme Peak realm, attainment of Lv. 120, defeat of Lv. 170 No Gunbaek, completion of the Uninvited Guest Quest, massive EXP and Fame gains, a new Title, enlightenment, and major martial-arts advancement, including the Blazing Flame Divine Technique reaching the Eighth Stage. A new unnamed Item is bound alongside White Flame and can be summoned through the Inventory once named. Before Taekyung can inspect it, Hyuk Mujin announces that an investigation team from Henan has arrived looking for him.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is the Slaughter Saint. He briefly resumed his assassin identity during the recent crisis but intends to return to life as a young physician and has sworn never to kill again.
- The people present, Cheongpung, and Mungyeong's Disciple know his identity; he states that everyone else who saw him in that form that day is dead. Taekyung agrees to keep the secret and address him as Mungyeong.
- Taekyung has reached the Supreme Peak realm and Lv. 120, defeated Lv. 170 No Gunbaek, completed the Uninvited Guest Quest, gained substantial EXP and Fame, acquired an unspecified new Title, and advanced his martial arts through enlightenment.
- A new unnamed bound Item is listed with White Flame. It will be fully bound and summonable through the Inventory from anywhere after Taekyung gives it a name.
- An investigation team from Henan has arrived at the Sichuan Tang Clan and is looking for Taekyung.
- A hidden cavern near Chengdu concealed Dark Heaven forces and contained supplies, weapons, and a vast unidentified formation.
- The formation called 이동진 by Samgoe transported hundreds of people across hundreds of li, but it has completely lost its power and cannot currently be activated.
- Taekyung can determine through the Integrated Language Pack that the formation's patterns are not writing, but he cannot explain how he knows.

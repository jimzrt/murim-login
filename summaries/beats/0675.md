# Chapter 675

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Muyaho return to Ailao Mountain while following the tracking scent leading to Yohi. Jin intends to rescue Yohi and Heugung, whose testimony could overturn Nanman's current crisis, but hundreds of Nanman forces ambush them. Jin and Muyaho overwhelm the beasts and warriors, capture a tribal chieftain, and learn that Baeksang has issued a general mobilization, become temporary Palace Lord, and stationed nearly ten thousand troops in the Inner Palace while the Miao people remain under surveillance. Suspecting Dark Heaven's plan is nearing completion, Jin charges deeper into the flames spreading across Ailao Mountain.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and Muyaho have reached Ailao Mountain while tracking Yohi's tracking scent through a Supreme Peak-grade Quest.
- Jin believes rescuing Yohi and Heugung is essential because their testimony could prove his innocence and reveal the truth behind the tragedy in Nanman.
- Baeksang has issued a general mobilization; nearly ten thousand troops are stationed in the Inner Palace, three tribes led by Baeksang are moving, and the Miao people are under strict surveillance.
- Jin and Muyaho defeated a large ambush on Ailao Mountain, and Jin captured a tribal chieftain who provided information about Nanman's mobilization.
- Jin suspects Dark Heaven's great undertaking is larger than previously believed and may be nearing completion.

## Translation Decisions

- Use I Can See Your Tracking Scent for 너의 추종향이 보여.
- Use Find Yohi for 요희 발견 and preserve Supreme Peak as the Quest Grade.
- Use temporary Palace Lord for 임시 궁주 and Inner Palace for 내궁.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's crude, irreverent first-person narration and game terminology.

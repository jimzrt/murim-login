# Chapter 363

## Plot

Jin Taekyung shields the treatment room from the Western Heaven Demon Lord's attack but suffers severe injuries. The Divine Physician survives while protecting Jeok Cheongang, though his dantian and martial arts are destroyed and his wrist is broken. Taekyung attempts to bargain with the Myriad-Poison Ring, but the Demon Lord reveals that it is an indestructible sacred treasure that cannot even be appraised by the System.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord attacks Taekyung to reclaim the ring. Taekyung breaks the Demon Lord's ankle and gains a sudden martial insight that allows him to sense and evade the Demon Lord's qi-controlled sword, but he is still badly wounded. Just as the Demon Lord is about to kill him, Jeok Cheongang awakens and orders the Demon Lord not to touch Taekyung.

## Continuity

- The Myriad-Poison Ring is in Jin Taekyung's possession, cannot be appraised by the System, and is a sacred treasure that resists his immense physical strength.
- The Divine Physician is alive but has lost his dantian and martial arts and suffered a broken wrist after shielding Jeok Cheongang.
- Jeok Cheongang awakened in the treatment room and confronted the Western Heaven Demon Lord when Taekyung was about to be killed.
- Jin Taekyung is severely injured but gained a martial insight that let him sense and evade the Western Heaven Demon Lord's qi-controlled sword.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord's ankle was broken by Taekyung, but he remains alive and hostile while pursuing the Myriad-Poison Ring.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord prioritizes the Myriad-Poison Ring above the destruction of the Sichuan Tang Clan, Qingcheng, and Emei.
- Cheongpung remains engaged in his confrontation with First Fiend.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord serves an unidentified Lord of Heaven as one of four loyal servants.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord's claim that the Tang Clan, Qingcheng, and Emei were destroyed remains unconfirmed.

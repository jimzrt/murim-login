# Chapter 931

## Plot

After waking, Jin Taekyung sends away imperial physicians and a stream of officials before welcoming Hong Jin. Hong says he has been promoted to Eunuch Hong and put in charge of the East Depot, though the Cang Gong post will likely remain vacant. He reports that Ma Sanbao has evaded a three-day search. Taekyung reflects on the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s final instruction to find an unspecified object at a particular place, then opens the System window and is met by a torrent of messages.

## Continuity

- Hong Jin has been promoted to Eunuch Hong and is responsible for the East Depot; the Cang Gong position is expected to remain vacant.
- Ma Sanbao’s whereabouts are unknown after he escaped and evaded the Imperial Army’s search.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s final instruction was to find an unspecified object at a particular place; Taekyung does not yet know the object or its significance.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Cang Gong as the title; Hong Jin is responsible for the East Depot, but does not take the vacant Cang Gong position.

# Chapter 681

## Plot

Jin Taekyung survives the Great Snow Fiend's devastating Yin-Cold Qi attack despite severe internal injuries, temporarily seals his wounds with internal energy, and rises with White Flame while White Tiger watches over him. The Great Snow Fiend reveals that the Southern Heaven Demon Empress ordered him to capture Jin alive if possible and offers to spare Jin and preserve the Fire Gate Clan and Jin Family of Taiyuan if he surrenders, but Jin rejects the offer and provokes him by mocking his past retreat during the Great Faction War. The Great Snow Fiend declares Jin's last words will be delivered to Jeok Cheongang, and Jin throws himself into a fight to kill rather than merely survive.

## Continuity

- Jin survived the Great Snow Fiend's chest strike but is suffering severe internal injuries and extreme Yin-Cold Qi; he has temporarily stabilized himself and remains able to fight.
- The Great Snow Fiend and Black Hand Fist Demon continue attacking Jin in the Poisonblood Grounds, and Jin launches another attack at the chapter's end.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress personally ordered the Great Snow Fiend to capture Jin alive if possible because Jin could become a major future threat.
- The Great Snow Fiend is senior to the Black Hand Fist Demon and can overrule him; Black Hand protested the order to capture Jin but obeyed the threat of punishment.
- The Great Snow Fiend killed the former Sect Leader of the Zhongnan Sect during the Great Faction War, then escaped from Great Snow Mountain and remained hidden.
- White Tiger remains beside Jin and alerts him while he is incapacitated.
- The Yangtze River Channel League's swift ship has arrived at the reconnaissance squad's position with an unidentified person aboard.
- Namho plans to escort the detained Han Chinese reconnaissance members to the Central Plains so the Murim Alliance and Sichuan's major sects can aid Nanman.
- Yohi and Heugung remain captive and alive, awaiting the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's return.

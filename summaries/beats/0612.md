# Chapter 612

## Plot

Jin Taekyung returns to his modern-world home and reunites with his older friend Jin-ho, now a civil servant in the Hunter and Gate Management Department. Over ramyeon and soju, Jin-ho reveals that he has correctly guessed Taekyung's connection to the five masked figures' anti-terror campaign, but agrees to keep silent because exposing it would put him in danger.

Jin-ho's companionship helps Taekyung recognize that he came home not only to retrieve the game capsule that first brought him to the Murim, but also to find comfort. Although his training remains unfinished because of the heart demon and his grief over Kim Hwajong, Taekyung accepts that he must return to the Murim and leaves the capsule behind once more.

## Continuity

- Jin-ho is a civil servant in the Hunter and Gate Management Department and has inferred that Jin Taekyung is involved with the five masked figures' campaign.
- Jin-ho agrees to keep Taekyung's identity secret, understanding that disclosure would expose him to danger.
- Middle Eastern terrorist groups and Afghan rebel forces have issued a joint statement promising to restrain terrorism, but Taekyung believes their submission will be temporary.
- Taekyung's training to complete a new martial result has reached its final stage but remains incomplete because of the heart demon and unresolved grief over Kim Hwajong.
- Taekyung realizes that he needs to return to the Murim after finding temporary emotional clarity through his reunion with Jin-ho.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Jin-ho hyung as Jin Taekyung's familiar address for his older friend.
- Render 심마 as heart demon.
- Retain Hunter and Gate Management Department for 헌터 및 게이트 관리 부서.
- Use Section Chief Kim for 김 계장 and preserve the irreverent tone of Jin-ho's speech.
- Retain gongbu as a romanized term when Taekyung reflects on study as the work of mastering something.

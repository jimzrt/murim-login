# Chapter 820

## Plot

Jin Taekyung fights through the fanatics and confronts Yahya Muhammad Ahmad Bedouin, who reveals that he can wield both mana and magical power and was trained by the Doppelganger. As the battle threatens to delay Jin’s pursuit of the fleeing Doppelganger, he awakens a new application of his Middle Dantian’s power: he stops a volley of arrows in midair, turns them toward the enemy, and fires them back at Yahya and the fanatics.

## Continuity

- Yahya Muhammad Ahmad Bedouin is a fanatical, experienced fighter who wields both mana and magical power; the Doppelganger selected and taught him.
- Jin’s Middle Dantian advances during the fight, allowing him to suspend incoming arrows in space and redirect them toward his enemies.
- Jin is still trying to reach the fleeing Doppelganger, but Yahya and the fanatics remain between them.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep mana distinct from magical power.
- Keep Middle Dantian and the demonstrated spatial pressure, arrow suspension, and redirection associated with its newly advanced power.

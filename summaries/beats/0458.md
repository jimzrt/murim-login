# Chapter 458

## Plot

Jin Taekyung rescues two survivors from the wreckage in Dongting Lake just before the Tragedy of Dongting Lake Quest expires, completing the Quest and earning the Water Rescue Worker Title. The aftermath reveals that the two rescued people are the only survivors of the massacre; one is Honglan, who later regains consciousness. Taekyung and Cheongpung grieve with the mourners, and Taekyung vows to kill those responsible.

## Continuity

- The Tragedy of Dongting Lake Quest is complete; Taekyung rescued two people, the only survivors found among the disaster's victims, and earned the Water Rescue Worker Title.
- Honglan survived the Dongting Lake disaster and regained consciousness; the other rescued survivor remained unconscious when Taekyung was informed.
- Cheongpung is troubled by the deaths in Henan, Sichuan, Donghu Stronghold, and Dongting Lake, and seeks Taekyung's guidance; Taekyung responds that he contains his grief by cursing the perpetrators, blaming his own helplessness, and vowing revenge.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 무릉도원 as Wuling Peach Blossom Spring and explain it in a footnote as an idyllic utopia.

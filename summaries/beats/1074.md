# Chapter 1074

## Plot

Cheongpung and the Slaughter Saint reunite with Taekyung’s group. The Slaughter Saint forces the group to move, subdues Jeong Hogun, and is recognized by Bow Saint; Great Sir calls herself Soonja and befriends Cheongpung. The Slaughter Saint repeatedly leaves to stop Dark Heaven’s pursuers. After nearly two days, the group escapes the wilderness with a captured black-robed man and reaches a lakeside camp, where Gung Gibang and other beggars are waiting.

## Continuity

- Taekyung reunites with Cheongpung and the Slaughter Saint after several months; Cheongpung continues to address Taekyung as Benefactor.
- Bow Saint recognizes the Slaughter Saint, whose identity is revealed to the group; he is a peer from her past and acts to set the changed world right.
- The Slaughter Saint and Cheongpung, not Great Sir, defeated the ten thousand monsters; the Slaughter Saint spent nearly two days stopping Dark Heaven’s pursuit.
- The group escapes the wilderness with a black-robed captive bound in rope and reaches a large lake, where Gung Gibang’s group awaits.
- Great Sir gives her name as Soonja and calls Cheongpung a spirited young man.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 순자 as Soonja, the name Great Sir gives herself in this chapter.
- Retain Cheongpung’s established address “Benefactor” for Taekyung and translate 아주머니 as “Auntie” when Cheongpung addresses Soonja.

# Chapter 824

## Plot

The Doppelganger flees west with the Prophet’s black-clad personal guard, lamenting its losses and confirming that Jin Taekyung is the Chosen One. At a ruined oil field where its master once stayed during the Great Cataclysm, it exploits the guards’ doubts with false sympathy, then massacres twenty of them and absorbs their life force and souls. As it believes it has replenished itself, blue-white flames erupt from above and engulf it.

## Continuity

- The Doppelganger reached a ruined former oil field where its master stayed during the Great Cataclysm, killed twenty members of its personal guard, and absorbed their vitality and souls.
- The Doppelganger confirmed Jin Taekyung is the Chosen One; its master may be pleased by this confirmation.
- Blue-white flames erupted above the ruins and engulfed the Doppelganger; their source and effect are unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the Doppelganger’s performed religious devotion distinct from its actual motives; it manipulates its followers’ faith.

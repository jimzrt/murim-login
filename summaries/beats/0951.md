# Chapter 951

## Plot

As a large steppe army rides through the rain toward Shanxi, Temur and the Chinggen riding beside him appear to lead it. Jamukha joins the vanguard with more than twenty thousand tribespeople and commits a thousand Keshik to the lead. In private, Jamukha and the false Chinggen reveal that the real Chinggen was killed in the earlier attack and that the impostor kept Temur alive because his submission made him easy to control. The army expects to cross the border in about a day, before the Fire King and Jin Taekyung can arrive.

## Continuity

- The person currently wearing Chinggen’s face is an impostor; the real Chinggen was killed in the attack on the khans’ gathering.
- Temur is being manipulated by the impostor, who says he kept Temur alive because he was thoroughly submissive and useful in gathering tribespeople.
- Jamukha has joined the advancing steppe army with more than twenty thousand tribespeople and a thousand Keshik; he has ordered some Keshik to lead the vanguard.
- The steppe army expects to cross the Shanxi border in about a day; Jamukha says the Fire King and Jin Taekyung will not arrive for several days.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 자무카 as Jamukha, 텡그리 as Tengri, and 케식 as Keshik.
- The character presented as Chinggen in this chapter is an impostor; do not attribute his private statements or actions to the real Chinggen.

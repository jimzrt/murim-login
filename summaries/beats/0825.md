# Chapter 825

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the Skeleton King arrive by long-range Teleport at the Doppelganger’s location. Despite severe physical aftereffects from the spell, Jin strikes with White Flame, and the Skeleton King attacks in coordination with him. The Doppelganger survives by using Blink and shields, but the Skeleton King severs one of its arms. It says it needs the Grand Mage’s soul for what comes next, then a tremendous force pulses through the ruins.

## Continuity

- Jin and the Skeleton King have engaged the Doppelganger; the Doppelganger is injured but alive and still possesses the Grand Mage’s soul and power. Jin cannot move because his mental strength is depleted, and the ruins are now pulsing with an unknown force.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 에어 슬래시 as “Air Slash” and 실드 마법 as “Shield magic.”
- The blue-white flames are produced by Jin’s White Flame spear attack.

# Chapter 888

## Plot

Baek Yeon arrives to confront Jin Taekyung alongside So Gyo. Taekyung prepares to fight but stops when So Gyo reveals that Prince Shangshan remains in the Emperor’s power and says she has a mission to keep Taekyung alive. Taekyung infers that the Lord of Heaven still wants him alive, returns Baek Yeon’s weapon, and leaves rather than risk Shangshan’s life. He intends to face the coming imperial banquet as the decisive battleground.

## Continuity

- So Gyo says she has a mission requiring Jin Taekyung to remain alive; Taekyung infers that the Lord of Heaven wants him alive, but the reason and So Gyo’s allegiance remain unconfirmed.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao remains in Qianqing Palace under the Emperor’s control, restraining Taekyung from fighting So Gyo and Baek Yeon.
- The imperial grand banquet is approaching and is expected to become the decisive confrontation.
- Baek Yeon’s weapon is named Golden Dragon and is made of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron; Taekyung returns it rather than fight.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 금룡 as “Golden Dragon,” the name of Baek Yeon’s weapon.
- Retain “Hongmen Banquet” for 홍문연 and “Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron” for 만년한철.

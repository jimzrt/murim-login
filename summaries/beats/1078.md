# Chapter 1078

## Plot

Aboard the ships crossing Qinghai Lake, Jin Taekyung meets Hak Su, Cheongheoja’s Senior Disciple and Hak Woo’s senior brother. Hak Su expects the Alliance Leader and righteous warriors to join them, but Taekyung doubts the Central Plains can commit its full strength while Dark Heaven can use the Moving Formation. Taekyung reflects that the Lord of Heaven may want him alive, though he still cannot understand the reason. He and Hyuk Mujin haul the bound black-robed captive from the lake; the captive promises to talk, but reveals no information in this chapter.

## Continuity

- The ships are carrying the allies across Qinghai Lake; they expect to reach their destination in about half a day.
- Hak Su is Cheongheoja’s Senior Disciple, Hak Woo’s senior brother, and a possible successor to the Kunlun Sect leadership.
- Taekyung believes the Lord of Heaven does not want him killed, based on the Grand Mage’s choice to spare him in Gansu; the reason remains unknown.
- The black-robed captive has been submerged in Qinghai Lake for a day and night and now promises to talk; no information has yet been obtained.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Hak Su for 학수; Taekyung’s mistaken 학소 (Hakso) is a name-confusion joke, not an alternate name.
- Keep Taekyung’s conclusion that the Lord of Heaven wants him alive framed as his belief, not confirmed fact.

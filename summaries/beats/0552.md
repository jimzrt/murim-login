# Chapter 552

## Plot

Taekyung completes Logout and Synchronization after spending more than two months in Murim, returning to the modern world on January 1, 2047—his twenty-eighth birthday. Team Leader Choi welcomes him with strong instant coffee and birthday wishes at the enormous mansion where Taekyung now resides. Hayeon and the human-disguised Skeleton King interrupt the reunion with insults, escalating into a chaotic fight until Kim Jeonghee arrives and projects an aura comparable to one of the Ten Kings.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has returned to the modern world after more than two months in Murim; Synchronization applies increased stats, does not carry over scars or injuries, and restricts certain Titles.
- January 1, 2047, is Taekyung's twenty-eighth birthday.
- Taekyung's modern-world residence is an enormous mansion where Team Leader Choi, Hayeon, the Skeleton King, and Kim Jeonghee are present.
- The Skeleton King is currently using a human form created through Magic Johnson's illusion magic.
- Time passes at a ratio of roughly ten days in Murim to one hour in the modern world.
- Kim Jeonghee possesses an aura at least comparable to that of one of the Ten Kings when angered.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve Synchronization, Logout, Title, Grade, and Seizing an Object Through Empty Space as established system and technique renderings.
- Render 최 팀장 as Team Leader Choi, 매직 존슨 as Magic Johnson, and 스켈레톤 킹 as Skeleton King.
- Render 아저씨 as old man, 인간 계집 as human girl, and 양키 as Yankee in Hayeon's insults.
- Preserve Taekyung's dry self-mockery, Hayeon's profane sibling banter, and the Skeleton King's archaic formal diction.

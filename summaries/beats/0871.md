# Chapter 871

## Plot

Jeong Hogun warns Jin Taekyung and Prince Shangshan to watch every word and move as the twin Supreme Peak masters escort them deeper into Qianqing Palace. Taekyung notices Shangshan trembling, recognizes his own childhood grief in the boy’s fear, and comforts him with a pat on the head. After the twins leave them at an iron door, a gaunt Supreme Peak master guides them onward, trades jokes with Taekyung, and claims the Emperor is enjoying a diversion. At the bedchamber, he announces that he has delivered the two people summoned by imperial command, but no one else is present; he turns to them with a smile and says he enjoyed the brief diversion.

## Continuity

- Taekyung and Prince Shangshan have reached the Emperor’s bedchamber, where their gaunt Supreme Peak guide is the only person present; the guide’s identity and the meaning of his claimed diversion are unknown.
- Taekyung recognizes Shangshan’s fear and feels uncertain whether he could abandon the boy if forced to choose between rebellion and submission; he comforts Shangshan by patting his head.
- The twin Supreme Peak masters escorted Taekyung and Shangshan through Qianqing Palace, then stopped at an iron door and told them to enter.
- Taekyung’s System and Inventory remain unavailable during the update, and his condition worsens without the Divine Physician’s pills.
- Ma Sanbao secretly remained in the palace, leads a group seeking to enthrone Prince Shangshan, and offered Taekyung a reward for helping; Taekyung has not accepted.

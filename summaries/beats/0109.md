# Chapter 109

## Plot

A messenger hawk from the Lower District Sect's Sakju Branch reports that approximately two hundred Red Wind Band members are moving south across the plateau. Wolhwa uses lethal interrogation to extract all available information from the captured mounted bandits.

Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Wolhwa abandon the carriage and ride toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect without resting, while a Lower District Sect member takes the Five-Colored Ghosts and surviving mounted bandits to a nearby branch.

The Red Wind Band Leader, Pung Yang, has already led the band through Datong and destroyed the Mount Heng Sword Sect's Datong Branch. He orders that no prisoners be taken and that the manor be burned, rejecting plateau traditions whenever they conflict with his commands.

The Mount Heng Sword Sect learns that the Red Wind Band may attack within one or two days. Having already lost nearly eighty percent of its strength in the war with the Jin Family of Taiyuan, the sect's senior figures despair until the main hall doors suddenly explode.

## Continuity

- The messenger hawk was sent by the Lower District Sect's Sakju Branch to Wolhwa with intelligence about the Red Wind Band.
- The Red Wind Band numbers at least approximately two hundred and is moving toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect after crossing Datong.
- Pung Yang is the Red Wind Band Leader and enforces his own orders above the mounted band's inherited plateau customs.
- Wolhwa is capable of ruthless, decisive violence when extracting information from hostile martial artists.
- Chunsam is a First Rate Lower District Sect martial artist who served as the group's carriage driver and obeyed Wolhwa's orders.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa are riding day and night toward the Mount Heng Sword Sect; the Lower District Sect member is transporting the Five-Colored Ghosts and surviving mounted bandits to a branch.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect's Datong Branch was destroyed with no survivors, and the sect has lost nearly eighty percent of its strength in the war with the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- The Quest difficulty has changed to Peak.

# Chapter 296

## Plot

The leading Skeleton Knight evolves into a Level 105 Skeleton Warlord after defeating the Black Forest’s previous ruler, becoming the forest’s new master and commander of its undead. Once an outstanding human knight, the Warlord has lost all memories of its former life and now seeks to expand its territory by raising ever-larger undead armies.

Jin Taekyung lures the Skeleton Warlord and hundreds of Skeletons into a narrow path before unleashing One Annihilation. The blue-flame attack destroys hundreds of undead, leaves Taekyung Exhausted, and triggers a level-up that restores all status abnormalities. He then finishes off the surviving Skeletons and discovers that the Warlord’s damaged skull is still alive.

The Warlord reveals that it can speak, attempts to bargain by offering the Black Forest, and claims it can gather more than a thousand subordinates to find whatever Taekyung wants. Taekyung rejects the forest as compensation, considers destroying the Warlord for its skull, then proposes working together after realizing that the Warlord’s command over the undead could be useful. The Warlord’s skull trembles at Taekyung’s sinister smile.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains committed to the Peace Guild alongside Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong despite the Guild’s conflict with Ares Guild.
- The Peace Guild continues to possess more than twenty Gate monopolies, real estate, and enormous negotiated compensation from Lee Jungryong’s side.
- The Peace Guild continues recruiting and training Hunters through selective intake, generous allowances, and exclusive Gate rights.
- Taekyung continues personally leading harsh accelerated training raids for Peace Guild recruits, using monster luring and formation drills.
- Kim Jinsoo remains the informal leader and operational right hand of the third-week trainees during raids.
- The A-rank Gate Black Wizard’s Black Forest is ruled by a black wizard or necromancer and contains powerful undead monsters.
- The leading Skeleton Knight has evolved into a speaking Level 105 Skeleton Warlord, the new master of the Black Forest and commander of its undead.
- The Skeleton Warlord claims it can gather more than a thousand subordinate undead by searching the Black Forest, but no working arrangement with Taekyung has yet been established.
- Taekyung used One Annihilation in a confined path to destroy hundreds of the Warlord’s Skeleton army, received one level-up, and had Exhaustion and all other status abnormalities removed by the System.
- The Skeleton Warlord survived the attack as a damaged skull and is now negotiating with Taekyung after Taekyung proposed working together.
- Taekyung completed this raid without any trainee deaths; he had previously ordered all fifty Peace Guild members out of the Gate and stayed behind alone.
- Team Leader Choi and three other senior Guild members remain focused on training the Jin Family’s Cultivation Technique rather than joining this raid.

# Chapter 878

## Plot

At the textile shop, Jeok Cheongang identifies a disguised visitor as an East Depot eunuch sent by Jin Taekyung, using their shared code word “Monster” as proof. The messenger also delivers a sealed letter from someone closely connected to Hong Jin. Jeok reads both letters and burns them; their contents are not revealed. The group prepares to go to the imperial palace, where Jeok says they have been formally invited.

## Continuity

- An East Depot eunuch delivered two letters to Jeok Cheongang: one from Jin Taekyung and one from someone closely connected to Hong Jin; Jeok burned both after reading them, and their contents remain unrevealed.
- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung share the code word “Monster,” which the eunuch used to prove Taekyung sent him.
- Jeok Cheongang says the group is formally invited to the imperial palace and plans to go there soon.

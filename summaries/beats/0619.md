# Chapter 619

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion reaches Poison Flower Pavilion in Yeongin, where the locals’ hostility toward Han Chinese has intensified because a group from the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau allegedly slaughtered roughly two hundred villagers before all of its members died from venom. After paying an exploitative innkeeper for food and information, Taekyung learns that the group came from Sichuan and included a woman who might have been the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, though the innkeeper does not recognize her likeness. The innkeeper then reveals himself as the elderly Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent they came to contact and identifies Taekyung as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.

## Continuity

- The elderly owner of Poison Flower Pavilion is the Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent planted in Yeongin; he speaks both the local language and Chinese and has recognized Jin Taekyung.
- A Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group from Sichuan, consisting of one woman and nearly thirty men, arrived in Yeongin more than fifteen days earlier and was associated with the overnight massacre of approximately two hundred nearby villagers.
- Every member of the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group was found dead after being poisoned by deadly venom; Taekyung cannot pursue them.
- The innkeeper did not recognize the likeness of the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, so whether she was the woman in the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group remains unresolved.
- Yeongin’s hostility toward Han Chinese is a consequence of the massacre attributed to that group.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 天魔镖局 as Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau.
- Render 蛮族 as Man people.

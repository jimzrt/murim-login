# Chapter 1093

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang confirms that the Bow Saint is helping Jin Taekyung, then joins the Slaughter Saint against the Black Ghosts. After a Black Ghost rises again from what seemed a fatal wound, they identify the enemies as former fiends of the Demonic Cult who defy death. Jeok clears a vast area with a full-strength Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, but the Grand Mage survives behind an immense ice wall. She vows to kill him for that person; Jeok, pressed for time while the Slaughter Saint and Taekyung remain in separate fights, orders her to clear the way and then turns away.

## Continuity

- The Bow Saint is helping Jin Taekyung, who is fighting the Blood Lord; Jeok Cheongang considers Taekyung no match for the Blood Lord yet.
- The Slaughter Saint is fighting Black Ghosts alongside Jeok Cheongang; Black Ghosts can rise again after apparently fatal injuries.
- Jeok Cheongang's full-strength Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist turns hundreds of enemies to ash, but the Grand Mage survives behind an ice wall that is still melting from the attack.
- The Grand Mage vows to kill Jeok Cheongang for that person; Jeok turns away after their standoff, with the fight's outcome unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 생강시 as “living jiangshi”; retain the established term “Black Ghosts” for 흑귀.

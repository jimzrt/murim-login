# Chapter 1050

## Plot

The Grand Mage reveals that the Lord of Heaven has watched Jin Taekyung and identified him as the Chosen One. She says her master wants Jin to survive and grow stronger, but refuses to explain why. The Blood-Sword Demon Lord cannot understand why his disposal is tied to Jin’s growth.

Jin connects the Chosen One title to the Bow Saint and the Martial God’s letter. When the Grand Mage binds and chokes him, he challenges her to kill him, then severs his heart meridians using the faint healing power she gave him.

## Continuity

- The Grand Mage says the Lord of Heaven ordered her faction to watch Jin and wants him to survive and grow stronger; his reasons remain unknown.
- Jin severs his heart meridians while bound by the Grand Mage’s plant magic, using the faint healing power she gave him. His condition afterward is unresolved.
- The Grand Mage identifies Jin as the Chosen One, matching the title the Bow Saint used based on the Martial God’s letter.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대술사 as Grand Mage, consistent with 대마도사; both refer to the veiled woman.
- Keep 심맥 as heart meridian, consistent with the established glossary.

# Chapter 412

## Plot

Jin Taekyung uses One Annihilation to erase ten Darkened Death Knights and Liches, countless elite monsters, and the Arch Lich's observing Familiars. The attack grants him four Level Ups, massive EXP and Fame, the Title One Against a Thousand, and the new Intimidation stat. Its effects terrify the surviving monsters and greatly raise the suicide squad's morale, allowing more than two hundred fighters to charge through the breach. The Arch Lich's Familiar Link is severed and its ten empowered guards are destroyed, leaving it injured and wondering whether Jin may be the ancient Adversary who defeated it long ago.

## Continuity

- One Annihilation can annihilate multiple Level 100-plus elite undead at once, though Jin regards it as a double-edged spear.
- Jin now possesses the Title One Against a Thousand and the Intimidation stat; the Title enhances his battlefield performance, reduces fatigue consumption, frightens enemies, and boosts allied morale.
- The Arch Lich's Familiar Link has been forcibly severed, its ten empowered guards have been destroyed, and it has suffered backlash from their loss.
- The surviving monster army is intimidated and disorganized while the more than two hundred members of Jin's suicide squad advance as a unified force.
- The Arch Lich suspects that Jin may be the ancient human Adversary who defeated it in the past.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일기당천 as One Against a Thousand.
- Render 위압 as Intimidation.
- Render 어둠에 물든 as Darkened.
- Render 마계어 as Demon Realm language.
- Preserve Jin's dry, profane, game-like voice in the Jin-shelin joke and his battlefield commands.

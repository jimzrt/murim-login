# Chapter 855

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang’s party continues east after Jeok killed roughly three hundred bandits and river pirates who had set fire to a caravan. Jeok praises Ju Hwaran’s competence and invites her to call him Old Master, while forbidding Hyuk Mujin from doing the same. Jin Taekyung reunites with the group and says he feels well for now, though he may hit a wall again. With Jiangsu about three days away at their current pace, Jin says they should make it in two.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang killed roughly three hundred bandits and river pirates three days earlier after they set fire to a caravan; his party took the surviving merchant ship as payment for rescuing the traders.
- Ju Hwaran has a map passed down from her grandfather and estimates Jiangsu is about three days away at their current pace; Jin Taekyung intends to reach it in two days.
- Jin Taekyung says he is currently all right but may hit a wall again; the Divine Physician is traveling with the group to treat him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 노야 as “Old Master”; Jeok Cheongang offers this form of address to Ju Hwaran but explicitly refuses it to Hyuk Mujin.

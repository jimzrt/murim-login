# Chapter 550

## Plot

Mae Jonghak's first mission for the Fire Dragon Pavilion creates the Journey to Nanman Quest, requiring Jin Taekyung and the pavilion to enter Nanman without a time limit or useful hints. As Taekyung prepares to leave Henan, Cheongpung says goodbye and reveals that he is learning martial arts from Mungyeong by observation to become stronger and adapt to this world. Mungyeong agrees that Nanman is a plausible site for Dark Heaven's second rift, warning that a successful attack could spread chaos from Yunnan through Guizhou and Guangxi to Sichuan. Song Ilseom arrives in disguise with the discreet transport arranged by Ju Hwaran, while Jeok Cheongang remains in Henan; before Taekyung departs, Mungyeong sends him a grim warning through Sound Transmission and Cheongpung gives him dumplings.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion's first mission is the Journey to Nanman: Jin Taekyung and the pavilion must enter Nanman, with a linked quest as the reward and the Title Can't Go to Nanman as the failure penalty.
- The six-member Fire Dragon Pavilion is preparing to leave Henan for Nanman; Song Ilseom has arrived as a disguised Peak master with the transport arranged by Ju Hwaran within half a shichen.
- Jeok Cheongang remains in Henan while Taekyung and the other pavilion members depart because the current crisis requires more people in more places.
- Cheongpung is learning Mungyeong's martial arts through observation because he wants to become stronger and adapt himself to this world.
- Mungyeong considers Nanman a plausible target for Dark Heaven's second rift; if the plan succeeds, the resulting chaos could spread from Yunnan through neighboring Guizhou and Guangxi and onward to Sichuan.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 남만행 as Journey to Nanman and 남만을 못 가 as Can't Go to Nanman.
- Render 반 시진 as half a shichen, 의생 as medical apprentice, and 폰의생 as fake physician.
- Preserve Taekyung's blunt profanity and self-mocking humor, Cheongpung's innocent voice, and Mungyeong's dry, threatening voice.
- Retain the hill metaphor for Jeok Cheongang and the grimly affectionate tone of his warning through Sound Transmission.

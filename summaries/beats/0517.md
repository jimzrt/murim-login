# Chapter 517

## Plot

Unnamed reunites with Taekyung after spending three months in Repentance Cave, explaining that regret over failing to protect Hong Dao and the Green Jade Buddha Staff drove him there. Shaolin treated him with the Great Restoration Pill, allowing him to advance into the Supreme Peak realm. Shaolin's new Abbot, Unnamed's eldest Senior Brother, wants to meet Taekyung and says Hong Dao had apparently anticipated this need. At an inn near Mount Song rented by the Murim Alliance, Taekyung observes the enormous gathering for the Alliance's founding in three days, including the Murong Family from Liaoning, before a tremendous roar interrupts his thoughts.

## Continuity

- Unnamed has advanced from Peak to Supreme Peak after three months of treatment, training, and enlightenment in Repentance Cave, aided by Shaolin's Great Restoration Pill.
- Shaolin's new Abbot has held the position for less than a month, is Unnamed's eldest Senior Brother in their lineage, and wants to meet Jin Taekyung.
- The Murim Alliance's founding is three days away, with orthodox and unorthodox factions, eccentric experts, and distant great families gathering near Mount Song.

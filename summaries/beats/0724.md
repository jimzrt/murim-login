# Chapter 724

## Plot

Nanman remains unified after its reforms, and the Beast Miao King and Yayul Mok commit its forces to aid the Central Plains against Dark Heaven. As the Sacred Rain fades, the Beast Miao King memorializes his lost sworn brother, orders the war drums sounded, and leads Nanman's beasts and people into mobilization as the prelude to the Great War begins.

While traveling from Nanman, Jin Taekyung and his companions trade absurd banter before Jin reflects on their recovery, Nanman Beast Palace's formal admission to the Murim Alliance, and Dark Heaven's still-hidden full strength. He becomes increasingly uneasy about the Lord of Heaven's unusual interest in him, then receives a private request to talk from Jeok Cheongang.

## Continuity

- Nanman is unified under the Beast Miao King's authority, and the Nanman Beast Palace has formally joined the Murim Alliance as an ally against Dark Heaven.
- Yayul Mok has learned humanity from Jin Taekyung and vows to fight with his life for Nanman's people and their allies.
- The Beast Miao King promises to send more troops against Dark Heaven than Nanman sent during the Great Faction War.
- The Sacred Rain is fading but has healed Jin Taekyung's companions from their injuries.
- The Great War's prelude has begun with Nanman's beasts and people mobilizing.
- Dark Heaven's full strength remains unrevealed, while the Lord of Heaven has shown an unexplained personal interest in Jin Taekyung.
- The White Tiger is present above Nanman's assembled forces and roars in response to the Beast Miao King's war cry.
- Jeok Cheongang requests a private conversation with Jin Taekyung during the journey from Nanman.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain God wills it! for 신께서 원하신다!
- Render 전고 as war drums.
- Render 신강 as Xinjiang.
- Preserve the established Lord of Heaven rendering for 천주 and the comic, profane tone of Jin Taekyung's narration.

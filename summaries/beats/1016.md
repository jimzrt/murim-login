# Chapter 1016

## Plot

At the Qilian Mountains, Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang challenge the Gansu leaders’ deployment of more than thirty thousand troops across three fronts. Sima Gong admits they knowingly chose a strategy that could leave the rear exposed: if Dark Heaven uses the Moving Formation to attack there, the invaders would be trapped behind the defensive lines while the allied forces concentrate on the front. The plan accepts enormous sacrifices, including the people and martial artists left in the rear, prompting the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord’s furious objection.

## Continuity

- The allied leaders have reached the Qilian Mountains, where the Gansu forces are deployed across three fronts.
- Sima Gong’s strategy knowingly risks the rear being ravaged if Dark Heaven uses the Moving Formation to bypass the front; he expects that move to trap Dark Heaven behind the lines while the allied forces focus on the frontal attack.
- Wolhwa’s affectionate missive still risks being misunderstood by Ju Hwaran.
- Namho has an important matter he says he can only disclose to Taekyung now.
- Six Baekma Bang men left to fetch the Lord within five days of the march’s halt; Ma Junggeol remains with Taekyung’s group.
- Sama Pyo disobeyed Sima Gong’s order to return to Gansu; the consequences remain unknown.
- Sima Gong ordered two martial artists dealt with for making a taboo remark about Sama Pyo’s succession.

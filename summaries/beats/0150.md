# Chapter 150

## Plot

Four days after losing to Cheongpung, Jin Mukyung isolates himself, trains relentlessly, and realizes that his reputation as the Heaven Shaking Sword and one of the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes had made him a frog in a well. His duel with Cheongpung gives him clear motivation, and his Sword Energy becomes denser and sharper than ever.

Taekyung attempts to force open his Conception and Governor Vessels using forty-five years of internal energy and the Scorching Yang Qi gained from the Blazing Flame Divine Pill. The attempt fails, slightly damages his acupoints, lowers his Sinews and Meridians by one, and leaves him in severe pain.

Hyuk Mujin and Cheongpung interrupt Taekyung during the aftermath, leading Mujin to misunderstand the cause of his condition and receive a slap that knocks him unconscious. Cheongpung explains that Mae Jonghak warned against forcing open the vessels and that his own Governor Vessel opened naturally two years earlier through enlightenment. Recognizing Cheongpung's overwhelming talent, Taekyung asks him for training help; Cheongpung agrees to teach him several things, excluding martial arts forbidden by Mae Jonghak.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung lost his duel with Cheongpung four days before this chapter after roughly three hundred exchanges.
- Mukyung secluded himself to train after the defeat; the duel gave him clearer ambition and his Sword Energy became denser and sharper.
- Taekyung's attempt to force open the Conception and Governor Vessels failed, slightly damaged his acupoints, and reduced his Sinews and Meridians by one.
- Cheongpung's Governor Vessel opened naturally two years earlier through enlightenment; Mae Jonghak warned him not to force either vessel open.
- Taekyung asked Cheongpung to help with his training, and Cheongpung agreed to teach him except for martial arts Mae Jonghak had forbidden.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 검신 as “Sword God” and 검성 as “Sword Saint,” preserving the distinction used for Mae Jonghak's titles.
- Render 임독양맥 as “Conception and Governor Vessels,” 기해 as “qi sea,” 근맥 as “Sinews and Meridians,” 사해오호 as “Four Seas and Five Lakes,” and 환골탈태 as “Bone Transformation.”

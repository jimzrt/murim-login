# Chapter 76

## Plot

Taekyung officially joined the Peace Guild, completed the Guild Membership achievement, and received 10 points. Team Leader Choi explained that Taekyung's contract provided a 500 million won signing bonus, a fixed monthly salary of 50 million won, a seventy-percent settlement share, and housing, a car, and other benefits.

Taekyung travelled by limousine with Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim to the Guild district in Bucheon. The Guild house was the dilapidated Sooni's Super, purchased from the surviving family of its stubborn former owner. There he learned that Im Kkeokjeong and Miss Song were also Guild members, making five members in total; Miss Song returned from shopping at the end of the chapter.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is now a member of the Peace Guild and has completed the Guild Membership achievement for 10 points.
- Taekyung's Peace Guild contract provides a 500 million won signing bonus, a fixed monthly salary of 50 million won, a seventy-percent settlement share, and Guild-provided housing, a car, and other benefits.
- The Peace Guild's Guild house is Sooni's Super, a small, dilapidated corner store in Bucheon's Gate-dense district; the property cost slightly more than 2 billion won per pyeong.
- Im Kkeokjeong joined the Peace Guild after Team Leader Choi recruited him while he was hospitalized; he remains an E-rank Hunter.
- The Peace Guild has five members: Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Miss Song. Miss Song is a founding member and was shopping for Taekyung's welcome party.
- Taekyung consumed Essence of the Himalayas, which increased his Intelligence by 1 for one hour.

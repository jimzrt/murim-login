# Chapter 1036

## Plot

Jin Taekyung recognizes the Moving Formation, the corrupted Water God Dragon and Blood Fish, the rift, and the Black Ghosts as evidence of Magic. Jeok Cheongang pulls him clear of the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s attack, and a veiled white-robed woman’s gravity magic delays his attempt to rejoin Jeok. Fighting through the Dark Heaven followers, Jin realizes the System can answer a mass summon from his Inventory: he awakens his Middle Dantian and Will, summons one hundred blades, and prepares to unleash them.

## Continuity

- Jin identifies Magic as the explanation for the strange phenomena he has encountered, including the Moving Formation, corrupted creatures, the rift, and the Black Ghosts.
- A veiled white-robed woman uses gravity magic to force Jin down among the enemy ranks and delay his advance toward Jeok Cheongang.
- Jin discovers he can summon a large number of weapons from his Inventory at once; he awakens his Middle Dantian and manifests one hundred blades aimed at the enemy.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 마법 as “Magic” and 중단전 as “Middle Dantian,” consistent with the established glossary.
- The chapter ends as Jin prepares to release the one hundred summoned blades; do not imply their impact or outcome.

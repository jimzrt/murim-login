# Chapter 441

## Plot

In China, Go Jun violently searches for Lee Jungryong's holographic recorder at the former Arch Lich stronghold, bribing the investigation team and preparing to use whatever evidence it contains against Jin Taekyung. Though he plans to inherit control of Ares Guild, his hatred of Jin is overshadowed by the fear and helplessness left by their confrontation.

Meanwhile, Jin Taekyung trains aboard the Water Dragon Stronghold on the Yangtze, dodging Jeok Cheongang's finger flicks while adapting to his Middle Dantian. Cheongpung is forced to participate, but falls asleep while maintaining perfect meditation posture, frustrating Jeok's attempt to use him as an example.

## Continuity

- Go Jun is in China seeking Lee Jungryong's holographic recorder from the former Arch Lich stronghold; he believes it contains evidence that could be used against Jin Taekyung.
- Go Jun is expected to become Ares Guild's new captain after Lee Jungryong's death, but Jin Taekyung's defeat left him consumed by fear and a sense of helplessness.
- Jin Taekyung is training aboard the Water Dragon Stronghold to become accustomed to his opened Middle Dantian by maintaining a clear mind while dodging Jeok Cheongang's attacks.
- Cheongpung has been compelled to join Taekyung's training and can maintain flawless posture and breathing even while asleep.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 홀로그램 녹화기 as “holographic recorder.”
- Render 명경지수 as “clear as a mirror and still as water,” 탄지 as “finger flick,” and 노야 as “Old Master.”
- Preserve Go Jun's controlled but increasingly fearful voice and Taekyung's dry, profane first-person humor.

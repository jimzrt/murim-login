# Chapter 647

## Plot

At a banquet in the Inner Palace, the Beast Miao King gives Jin Taekyung the seat beside him while the other Fire Dragon Pavilion members receive less prominent places. Taekyung worries about Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin, who have gone north-east to investigate the Blood Monk, while Baeksang, Yohi, and Heugung remain absent from the banquet. Baeksang eventually arrives with the great chieftains and greets the Beast Miao King, but his freezing gaze confirms that the confrontation between him and Taekyung is not over.

The Beast Miao King explains that Nanman's celebratory festival honors the warriors who died at Ailao Mountain by believing they have returned to their gods as divine warriors. He then recounts meeting the Martial God twice more than fifty years earlier: first as a white-bearded immortal-like figure who defeated five Supreme Peak fiends and five hundred Blood Ghost Squad members alone, and later as a young boy. Taekyung realizes that the Martial God's history and disappearance resemble Cheon Taemin's, though he cannot accept the possibility that they are connected.

While the banquet begins and Inner Palace warriors move through the North Gate, a shadow receives a report that the Blood Monk is the scouts' target and that Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin are accompanying the mission on Taekyung's orders. The shadow turns toward a dark reddish-brown flame, while the Southern Heaven Demon Empress watches it flicker and smiles.

## Continuity

- The Beast Miao King personally witnessed the Martial God defeat five Supreme Peak fiends and five hundred Blood Ghost Squad members more than fifty years ago, then met him once more in the form of a young boy.
- Jin Taekyung suspects that the Martial God and Cheon Taemin share more than coincidental similarities, but their connection remains unconfirmed.
- Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin are traveling north-east to investigate the Blood Monk and engage in combat if necessary.
- Baeksang has returned to the Nanman Beast Palace banquet with the great chieftains, and his cold hostility toward Jin Taekyung remains active.
- A shadow and its subordinates are monitoring the Blood Monk investigation for the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Blood Ghost Squad for 혈귀대.
- Use divine man for 신인 when describing the Martial God's transformation beyond humanity.
- Use Water Style for 수둔 and Outer Palace for 외궁.
- Render 혁퀴벌레 as Hyukroach for Taekyung's joking nickname for Hyuk Mujin.

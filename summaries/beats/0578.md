# Chapter 578

## Plot

Song Cheonwoo launches his planned surprise attack on Choi Minwoo, but Choi blocks the strike and proves he has become a formidable warrior. Kim Hwajong arrives to protect Choi, and Song reveals that he is wearing the Yeti's Necklace, which marks him as a yeti's kin and draws hundreds of yetis across the crevasse. Hwajong lures the yetis away while Choi fights Song alone. Wounded but refusing to yield, Choi draws on the power of Hero's Soul and his resolve as Cheon Taemin's blood relative, ending the chapter with a full-strength strike cutting across Song's chest.

## Continuity

- Song Cheonwoo wears the Yeti's Necklace to exploit the yetis' recognition of its wearer as one of their own and to draw hundreds of yetis into the confrontation.
- Kim Hwajong arrives to defend Choi Minwoo, responds to Song Cheonwoo with unprecedented fury, and draws the yetis away so Choi can fight Song alone.
- Choi Minwoo can wield Hero's Soul and has developed enough aura and combat ability to match Song Cheonwoo, despite sustaining multiple wounds.
- Choi Minwoo's resolve not to die is tied to the blood of his maternal grandfather, Cheon Taemin, and to the enlightenment he gained while advancing against thousands of monsters.
- Song Cheonwoo attacks Choi because Go Jun is holding Song's blood relatives hostage, and the chapter ends immediately after Choi's strike crosses Song's chest.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Hero's Soul for 영웅의 혼 as the named sword.
- Use Yeti's Necklace for 예티의 목걸이; distinguish its dragonkin-attracting function from its marking the wearer as a yeti's kin.
- Retain dragonkin for 용족 and yeti for 예티.

# Chapter 221

## Plot

Jin Taekyung achieves Manifestation of Qi, unlocking Spear Energy, gaining Unaffected by a Hundred Poisons, two level-ups, and 100 Bonus Points. Using blue Spear Energy and Scorching Yang Qi exceeding one jiazi, he pierces the Black Wyvern's knee, climbs its body with daggers, and splits one of its wings.

The Black Wyvern reveals that it can speak and identifies itself as Carus, the One-Eyed. Carus explains through his memories that he grew stronger by consuming monsters and absorbing Magic Gems, then learned magic. He heals himself and casts five overlapping Slow spells, but Taekyung counters by allocating 100 points to Agility and attacks again.

## Continuity

- Taekyung completed the Manifestation of Qi Achievement and received Spear Energy, Unaffected by a Hundred Poisons, two levels, and 100 Bonus Points.
- Taekyung's blue Spear Energy can cut through the Black Wyvern's hide and scales, which withstand ordinary magic and weapons.
- The Black Wyvern has been severely wounded: its knee is pierced, dozens of blades are embedded in its body, and one wing has been split apart, but it remains alive.
- The Black Wyvern's name is Carus, also called the One-Eyed; it can speak and recognizes Taekyung as the human who took its eye three years earlier.
- Carus became stronger by eating monsters and absorbing Magic Gems, developed high Intelligence, established a nest, and learned magic.
- Carus can cast Heal and Slow; five Slow spells were stacked on Taekyung, who countered them by assigning 100 points to Agility.
- The battle between Taekyung and Carus is still underway.

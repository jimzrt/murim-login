# Chapter 1009

## Plot

Ma Junggeol explains that Baekma Bang is opening a trade route west from Ningxia through the Great Steppe and the desert toward Xinjiang. During their journey, his men spotted Dark Heaven forces in the western desert: after following them for a day and a half from a distance of one hundred li, they saw at least a thousand tents among the dunes. The group believes the forces may number in the thousands or tens of thousands and be targeting Gansu. Jin Taekyung concludes that this is where their forces must fight.

## Continuity

- Baekma Bang crossed the Great Steppe and entered the desert while exploring a trade route to Xinjiang; its scouts saw at least a thousand Dark Heaven tents in the western desert, on a route closer to Gansu than Qinghai.
- Ma Junggeol says Baekma Bang followed the Dark Heaven force for a day and a half, keeping a distance of one hundred li, before returning with the information.

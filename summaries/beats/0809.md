# Chapter 809

## Plot

The fleeing monster army routs after the Hunters’ victory, but roughly five thousand monsters remain and 185 Hunters have died. Jin recognizes that pursuing without regrouping would leave the exhausted forces vulnerable. Neither the Prophet nor his purpose is clear; Jin concludes the battle’s chaos is part of the Prophet’s calculations and suspects the Prophet wants to face him. Jin’s accumulated injuries worsen: the System adds the Broken Body debuff, weakening Muscles and Bones and increasing injury risk while accelerating internal energy consumption. After less than an hour of recovery, the Hunters finish preparations to continue their mission to kill the Prophet. A memory of Michael Silbert sparks a sudden question in Jin’s mind.

## Continuity

- The battle in the Rub’ al Khali ends in a Hunter victory, with 185 dead and an estimated five thousand monsters still fleeing; the surviving Hunters regroup before resuming the pursuit.
- The Prophet remains unlocated. Jin suspects the battlefield situation was part of the Prophet’s plan and believes the Prophet wants to meet him, but the Prophet’s identity and objective remain unknown.
- Jin has the new Broken Body debuff in addition to the existing Damaged Body effects: Muscles and Bones are weakened, injury risk is considerably higher, and internal energy is consumed faster. His physical condition improves somewhat after less than an hour of energy circulation.
- Jin’s mission remains to kill the Prophet and prevent the disaster; he continues despite the debuff and mounting exhaustion.
- A memory of Michael Silbert prompts a sudden, as-yet-unspoken question in Jin’s mind.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magical power distinct from mana.
- Render the new special debuff as Broken Body and retain Damaged Body as the separate existing debuff.

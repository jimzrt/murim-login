# Chapter 182

## Plot

Baek Museong meets Jin Wikyung at the Jin Family of Taiyuan's gate. The gathered martial artists recognize Baek as Huashan's Lone Crane, Chulwoo as the Defeated Flower Fist, and Eunhyang as the Flower Sword Phoenix, identifying the three as Huashan's Three Plum Blossom Elites. Baek and Wikyung proceed inside for a private conversation, while Chulwoo and Eunhyang remain behind to rest.

Chulwoo uses an excuse about needing a privy to stay behind, then jokes with the crowd and singles out Woo Hwangtae, dragging the middle-aged escort chief toward the mountainside. Meanwhile, Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung approach the Jin Family. Mujin explains the fame of Baek and the other Three Plum Blossom Elites, while Cheongpung identifies their titles and says that Taekyung is already strong enough to catch up to them. Chulwoo sees Taekyung and addresses him as Brother over there.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung and Baek Museong meet at the Jin Family of Taiyuan's gate and begin a private conversation.
- Eunhyang is publicly known by the epithet Flower Sword Phoenix; Chulwoo remains known as the Defeated Flower Fist.
- Chulwoo deliberately stays behind with Eunhyang, singles out Woo Hwangtae, and takes him toward the mountainside.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 화검봉 as “Flower Sword Phoenix,” Eunhyang’s epithet.
- Treat 화산말학 and 매화손절 as Taekyung’s mistaken hearings of 화산일학 and 매화삼절, not as genuine Huashan titles.
- Render 형장 as “Brother” or “Brother over there” in Chulwoo’s informal address.

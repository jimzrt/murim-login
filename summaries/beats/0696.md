# Chapter 696

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Yohi, the guardian spirit, Muyaho, and a vast beast army enter the Outer Palace and destroy five watchtowers before confronting Baeksang at the Inner Palace gate. Nearly a thousand archers aim at them, but their resolve falters when they recognize Yohi alive and unrestrained. Baeksang publicly acknowledges Yohi and admits that he betrayed everyone and became an unforgivable turncoat to pursue a purpose maintained for decades. As Jin realizes that Baeksang's despair may signal a larger danger and orders everyone to retreat, thunder erupts, dark clouds cover the sky, and an unknown voice declares that it is already too late.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and the beast army have entered the Outer Palace and destroyed five watchtowers.
- Yohi has returned alive and stands beside Jin at the head of the beast army, without restraints.
- The Inner Palace has nearly one thousand archers positioned on its stone walls, but their resolve weakens after recognizing Yohi.
- Baeksang has opened the Inner Palace gate and confronted Jin and Yohi in front of his warriors.
- Baeksang has publicly admitted betraying everyone and becoming a turncoat to pursue a purpose maintained for decades.
- Thunder and dark clouds have suddenly covered the battlefield, followed by an unidentified voice saying that it is already too late.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain guardian spirit for 수호령 and Great Chieftain for 대족장.
- Render 흉수 as monster in the repeated exchange between Jin, Yohi, and Baeksang.
- Preserve Jin's blunt, profane response to Baeksang's confession.

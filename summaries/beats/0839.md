# Chapter 839

## Plot

Jin borrows Ju Wongong’s carriage for the Fire Dragon Pavilion and arrives at the rebuilt Sichuan Tang Clan, where members of the Tang Clan and other Sichuan sects welcome him. He sees that Sichuan Murim has begun to recover and reunite after Dark Heaven’s attack.

Tang Sadok greets Jin warmly as the Sichuan Tang Clan’s benefactor and leads him to the Old Master.

## Continuity

- Jin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion have arrived at the Sichuan Tang Clan; Tang Sadok is escorting Jin to the Old Master.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 균열 as a social fracture or division in this passage, not the supernatural rift.

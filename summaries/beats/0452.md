# Chapter 452

## Plot

Taekyung's party escapes Tianling Falls and reaches Zaoyang, where they must continue by land toward the Zhuge Clan on Mount Longzhong. At the ferry landing they encounter Qingxia Hall, an influential social club formed by the children of powerful Hubei families, whose lavish procession scatters silver nyang among the commoners. While Taekyung condemns the spectacle, Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin rush to collect the money, and Cheongpung abandons the group to buy sweets, jeonbyeong, and dumplings, prompting Taekyung's furious outburst.

## Continuity

- The party has safely passed Tianling Falls, reached Zaoyang, and is preparing to travel by carriage through Hubei toward the Zhuge Clan on Mount Longzhong.
- A Zhuge Clan retainer is arranging carriages through the Zaoyang branch and the relay stations on the main roads.
- Qingxia Hall is an unofficial Hubei social club made up of influential families' children; its members possess powerful backing despite being weak martial artists.
- At Zaoyang, Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin are distracted by Qingxia Hall's scattered silver nyang, while Cheongpung has gone to buy food and sweets.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 조양 as Zaoyang and 청협방 as Qingxia Hall.
- Render 청협방탄무인단 as Qingxia Bulletproof Martial Artist Corps when Taekyung jokingly imagines a performance group name.
- Preserve Taekyung's modern, profane sarcasm about idols, K-pop, rich-kid clubs, and his companions' shameless behavior.

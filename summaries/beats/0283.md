# Chapter 283

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continued assaulting Park Jihoon after overpowering him and discovered that Jihoon knew hand-to-hand combat comparable to a Peak master's grappling technique. Jihoon carried a separate phone protected by fingerprint and iris recognition, recovered from Taekyung's attack by drinking a potion, and invoked the coercive rewards and punishments associated with 'that person' to obtain Park Tae Seop's help. Taekyung released a jiazi of Scorching Yang Qi and demonstrated the overwhelming difference between their classes.

Park Tae Seop reluctantly armed himself with a greatsword and shield and joined Jihoon against Taekyung. Taekyung evaded Tae Seop's attacks, broke his shield, and used the Flow principle he had learned from Mae Jonghak to entangle and disarm both men. He shattered Jihoon's knee and was about to destroy the other one when an unidentified, impossibly deep voice interrupted the fight, leading Taekyung to wonder whether its owner was a Supreme Peak master.

## Continuity

- Park Jihoon's hand-to-hand movement is comparable to a Peak master's grappling technique, though Taekyung considers it somewhat clumsy.
- Park Jihoon carries a separate phone for secret communications; it requires fingerprint and iris recognition.
- Park Jihoon used a potion to heal the injuries Taekyung had inflicted during their confrontation.
- Jihoon says 'that person' enforces choices through definite rewards and punishments, and Park Tae Seop is unwilling to oppose that person's demands.
- Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi formed a fire dragon, dispersed through hundreds of streams, and was then recombined for release.
- Park Tae Seop fights with a greatsword and shield and possesses an aura powerful enough to confront Taekyung's Scorching Yang Qi.
- Taekyung overpowered Park Tae Seop, split his shield, and used Mae Jonghak's Flow principle to disarm both opponents.
- Taekyung shattered Park Jihoon's knee and was interrupted while preparing to destroy his remaining knee.

# Chapter 1123

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens under Final Rally, which suppresses his pain, heightens his Qi Sense, and lets Jeok Cheongang’s Scorching Yang Qi heal some of his injuries. With a nine-minute-59-second quest timer, Taekyung fights forward alongside Jeok Cheongang, Cheongpung, the Saints, and civilians who charge to protect him. He remembers fallen companions and loved ones, including Hyuk Mujin, whom he believes is dead, then shatters two Black Ghosts’ Force attacks. The Slaughter Saint and Bow Saint engage the Black Ghosts, whose wounds and severed limbs keep regenerating under overlapping magic. Taekyung advances with Jeok Cheongang and Cheongpung and confronts the Blood Lord; the quest timer shows five minutes remaining.

## Continuity

- Final Rally is active: it greatly reduces Taekyung’s pain, temporarily greatly increases his Qi Sense, and lets Scorching Yang Qi heal some of his Internal Injuries; the sudden quest has a nine-minute-59-second limit, with five minutes remaining at chapter’s end.
- Taekyung has rejoined the battle and is advancing with Jeok Cheongang and Cheongpung toward the Blood Lord.
- Civilians and the remaining defenders are charging to protect Taekyung; they call for the Marquis of Shangshan to be protected.
- The Slaughter Saint and Bow Saint are fighting two Black Ghosts; the Black Ghosts regenerate severe wounds and severed limbs, aided by overlapping magic.
- Taekyung believes Hyuk Mujin died, but did not witness where or how he died; their last promise remains unfulfilled.
- Taekyung remembers Ju Hwaran and their past conversation in the Fire Courtyard, including her asking whether she could succeed and his telling her she need not force herself.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 회광반조 as “Final Rally,” consistent with the established glossary; retain “Black Ghost” for 흑귀 and “Force” for 강기.
- Render the sudden quest title 바람 앞의 촛불, 혹은 불꽃 as “A Candle in the Wind, or a Flame.”

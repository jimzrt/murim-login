# Chapter 819

## Plot

The Hunters and fanatics fought a brutal, attritional battle around the canyon. Magic Johnson’s Fire Storm was nearly canceled by Aqua Storms cast by thirty A-rank War Mages, while fanatics focused their attacks on Johnson and the Skeleton King to keep them from pursuing the fleeing Doppelganger. The Skeleton King caught the badly wounded Choi Minwoo, who soon passed out; a furious blond man arrived and joined the fight. The Skeleton King drew the Hero’s Sword from Choi’s hand and urged the newcomer to hurry before someone more dangerous arrived.

## Continuity

- The fanatics number roughly ten thousand and include thirty A-rank War Mages trained to counter Magic Johnson’s Fire Storm with Aqua Storm.
- The fanatics use their numerical advantage and concentrated attacks to pin down Magic Johnson and the Skeleton King while the Doppelganger escapes with a small escort.
- Choi Minwoo fought until he was severely wounded and passed out; the Skeleton King caught him and took the Hero’s Sword from his hand.
- A furious blond man has arrived at the Skeleton King’s position; this chapter does not name him.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Fire Storm and Aqua Storm as distinct named spells.
- Keep magical power distinct from mana.

# Chapter 986

## Plot

After regaining consciousness, the dying Thunderbolt Saber King asks Jin Taekyung to inherit all his remaining internal energy through the dangerous forbidden technique Transmitting Internal Energy Across the Body. Taekyung accepts the System’s emergency Quest, trusting that the transfer is for the sake of the world. Jeok Cheongang believes Taekyung has a chance of surviving because of his extraordinary physique, divine healing power, and prior successful transfer; the transfer begins.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung accepted the emergency Quest Transmitting Internal Energy Across the Body and is now receiving the dying Thunderbolt Saber King’s remaining internal energy; the process is extremely dangerous.
- The Thunderbolt Saber King chose Taekyung because he trusts him to use the transfer for the sake of the world; Jeok Cheongang believes Taekyung can survive it.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “Transmitting Internal Energy Across the Body” for 격체전공.
- Render 뇌반업 as “Prison of Karma,” as in the English reading copy.

# Chapter 405

## Plot

Lee Jungryong views the monster wave and China's losses as an opportunity for Ares Guild, while recognizing Jin Taekyung's growing fame and strength as a threat. He orders Go Jun to protect Lieutenant General Wang Ochun, the new northern-front commander, and identifies Suining City in Sichuan Province as the operation's final destination because the Arch Lich is there.

The next morning, Jin leaves for the mobile-warfare operation while Team Leader Choi and Shao Shen remain behind to recover. Jin gives Hero's Soul to Shao Shen because of his courage and sacrifice, but Shao Shen decides that Team Leader Choi is more suited to wield it and insists that Choi accept the sword before Jin joins the assembled troops.

## Continuity

- The five fronts will begin mobile warfare at 06:30 the following morning, then cut off the monster army's retreat and establish a dense encirclement.
- Suining City in Sichuan Province is the operation's final destination and the place where the Arch Lich is located.
- Lee Jungryong sees China's devastation as an opening for Ares Guild and considers Jin Taekyung's growing heroic reputation dangerous.
- Go Jun has gained strength, defeated two Death Knights at the northern front, and remains Lee Jungryong's disciple and Head of Security.
- Lieutenant General Wang Ochun has become the northern-front commander after his predecessor's death; he has maintained covert meetings with Go Jun at hidden safe houses.
- Ares Guild suffered eleven severely injured and forty-six lightly injured personnel on the northern front, with no deaths.
- Team Leader Choi and Shao Shen remain behind to recover because neither is currently fit for the departing operation.
- Hero's Soul did not reject Shao Shen when he gripped its hilt; Shao Shen then judged Team Leader Choi more suitable and pressed the sword on him.
- Jin Taekyung has departed with the assembled forces for the operation intended to reach the Arch Lich.

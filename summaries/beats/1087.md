# Chapter 1087

## Plot

The Blood Lord and Grand Mage argue over his unilateral actions in Qinghai. He admits privately that he lied to the Lord of Heaven about fighting Mae Jonghak, then reveals his real plan: the advancing Green Forest Alliance and Yangtze River Channel League were bait and cover for testing Dark Heaven’s hidden magic formations. Ten thousand Dark Heaven faithful crossed into the Central Plains through the formations and joined the two alliances; most formations retain one use, though two or three used at Shaolin may be spent. The Blood Lord intends to march on Xining, while keeping the option to use the remaining formations for a larger invasion.

## Continuity

- Ten thousand Dark Heaven faithful crossed into the Central Plains through hidden magic formations and joined the Green Forest Alliance and Yangtze River Channel League.
- Most of Dark Heaven’s hidden magic formations retain one use; two or three used in the Shaolin attack may have lost their power.
- The Blood Lord’s orders are to take Qinghai and, if possible, secure Jin Taekyung; he now orders an army to march on Xining.
- The Blood Lord lied to the Lord of Heaven about his final fight with Mae Jonghak, claiming hundreds of exchanges; he privately admits he would not have lasted three exchanges had he known Mae Jonghak’s identity and conserved his strength.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “magic formations” for 마법진 and “Moving Formation(s)” for 이동진; keep the two terms distinct.

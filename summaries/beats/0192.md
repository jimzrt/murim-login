# Chapter 192

## Plot

Song Il loses control after Jin Taekyung and Jin Wikyung reject his domineering conduct, attacks Taekyung with the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords, and is stopped when the mysterious speaker reveals himself as Jeok Cheongang, the Fire King.

Jeok Cheongang uses Six-Harmonies Voice Transmission to threaten Song Il, catches his wrist after Taekyung narrowly evades the attack, publicly humiliates Song with slaps and insults, and reveals that he entrusted the Fire Gate Clan's sacred treasure to Taekyung.

## Continuity

- The unidentified voice that stopped Song Il is Jeok Cheongang, who has reached the Supreme Peak realm and can use Six-Harmonies Voice Transmission.
- Jeok Cheongang and Song Il met when Song was barely twenty; Jeok once saved him at his Master's request, and Song later accused Jeok of stealing his battle credit.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly intervened when Song Il attacked Taekyung, then humiliated Song for threatening commoners and ordering the Jin Family to seal its gates.
- Song Il's Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords had reached nine-tenths mastery; Taekyung narrowly evaded the attack with torn clothing and minor bleeding.
- Jeok Cheongang publicly stated that he had entrusted the Fire Gate Clan's sacred treasure to Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 육합전성 as “Six-Harmonies Voice Transmission” and 천하삼십육검 as “Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords.”
- Render 신물 in Jeok Cheongang's revelation as “sacred treasure.”

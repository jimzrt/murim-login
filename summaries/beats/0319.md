# Chapter 319

## Plot

Ju Hwaran completes the Yongbong Escort Bureau's delivery to Hwangbo Eom at Tengwang Pavilion. The casket bears the seal of the Shandong Seongsu Jang Family, and its current Family Head, the Pill Physician, personally placed and signed the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng inside.

When Hwangbo Eom orders Ju Hwaran to open the casket, it contains Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng instead. Hwangbo declares that Zhongnan will accept no compromise and demands two hundred thousand silver nyang in compensation, a sum that could reduce the Yongbong Escort Bureau to an empty shell. Song Ilseom shows almost no reaction to the crisis.

Jin Taekyung's party enters the teahouse, including Cheongpung, Hyuk Mujin, and Baek Museong. Hwangbo Eom recognizes Taekyung and senses that the approaching group possesses power comparable to an Elder of the Nine Sects and One Gang.

## Continuity

- The casket delivered by Ju Hwaran at Tengwang Pavilion contains Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng rather than the contracted Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng.
- Hwangbo Eom demands two hundred thousand silver nyang from the Yongbong Escort Bureau and refuses any compromise.
- Ju Hwaran and Heo Jun face the possible collapse of the Yongbong Escort Bureau if its lands and assets must be sold to pay the claim.
- The casket bears the seal of the Shandong Seongsu Jang Family, whose current Family Head, the Pill Physician, was said to have placed and signed the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng inside.
- Song Ilseom is present at Tengwang Pavilion and shows no outward reaction to the shipment discrepancy beyond a slight twitch of his mouth.
- Jin Taekyung's party has entered Tengwang Pavilion, with Baek Museong accompanying Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang remaining unconscious.
- Hwangbo Eom senses approaching qi comparable to that of an Elder of the Nine Sects and One Gang before recognizing Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 환의 as Pill Physician and 성수장가 as Seongsu Jang Family.
- Retain Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng for 백년설삼 and the established snow ginseng rendering for 설삼.
- Keep Hwangbo Eom's coercive use of 자네 as you in his dialogue with Ju Hwaran.

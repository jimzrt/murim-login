# Chapter 298

## Plot

Taekyung turns the Skeleton Warlord's resurrection ability into an EXP harvest. He makes the Warlord activate Commander's Rally, gathers the newly raised Skeletons, destroys them with Spear Energy, and collects the resulting EXP, Magic Gems, and Equipment.

After arguing over the terms of a contract, the Skeleton Warlord agrees to retain his position as master of the Black Forest and commander of the undead army while serving as the Peace Guild's EXP factory manager. He later reveals that the black wizard directly summoned him, yet a sudden unexplained surge in magical power recently freed him from the summoner's control. Near the end of the harvest, he senses intruders entering the forest, and Taekyung heads toward the Gate entrance with the Warlord's skull.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton Warlord's Commander's Rally strengthens the Strength, Agility, and Magic of monsters under his command and raises their Levels by five.
- The Skeleton Warlord accepts a contract with Taekyung to supply as many Skeletons as requested while retaining his titles and nominal authority; he rationalizes the arrangement as protecting his soldiers rather than submitting.
- The Warlord had no Magic Gems, and his sudden increase in magical power somehow allowed him to escape the black wizard's previously absolute control; neither he nor Taekyung knows the cause.
- Taekyung considers testing whether a human can absorb a Magic Gem, contrary to established academic consensus, but the attempt does not occur before the Warlord detects intruders.
- The Warlord senses other humans entering the Black Forest from the south near the Gate entrance, and Taekyung identifies them as his friends.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 스켈레톤 아처 as “Skeleton Archer.”
- Render 경험치 공장장 as “EXP factory manager” and 친인파 as “pro-human”; retain the Warlord's archaic, indignant voice against Taekyung's sarcastic colloquial voice.
- Render 우렁각시 as “snail bride” and explain the Korean folktale reference in a footnote.
- Render the 남아일언 중천금 pun as “A man's word is worth a thousand pieces of gold,” followed by the Warlord's “A man who lost his mama?” misinterpretation.

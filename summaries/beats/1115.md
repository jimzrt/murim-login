# Chapter 1115

## Plot

At the South Gate, the exhausted defenders are close to collapse despite the Slaughter Saint and Bow Saint’s efforts. The Slaughter Saint orders a retreat, with elite troops covering the rear; the Bow Saint agrees to go with him to the Inner City and Jin Taekyung.

The Bow Saint destroys a rain of fireballs with a shot of light. The Slaughter Saint admits he wants to believe in Taekyung and in her, and reflects that Mungyeong considers Taekyung worthy of risking his life for.

## Continuity

- The South Gate defenders were ordered to retreat in an orderly fashion while the elites at each gate cover the rear; the outcome of the retreat is not established.
- The Slaughter Saint and Bow Saint set out for the Inner City to join Jin Taekyung.
- The Bow Saint destroyed the incoming fireballs with an arrow of light.

## Translation Decisions

- Use she/her for the Bow Saint; the chapter identifies her with feminine pronouns.
- Render 大宗師 as “Great Master.”

# Chapter 302

## Plot

The public and media assume Jin Taekyung died inside the Gate, but the deployed Hunters emerge with an injured Hunter on a stretcher and Taekyung walks out alive. He confirms that the Named Monster existed but has now been killed, creating a legendary live-broadcast moment.

A week later, Taekyung's interview has surpassed 200 million views and international broadcasters are requesting interviews, though he has accepted none. In Team Leader Choi's office, Taekyung privately reveals that the public story surrounding him is false, summons the Skeleton Warlord from his Inventory, calls it Warlordmon, and torments it with commands.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung survived the Named Monster raid and publicly returned alive after being presumed dead.
- Taekyung killed the Named Monster; Team Leader Choi states that Taekyung has now hunted two Named Monsters alone.
- Taekyung's live interview reached 200 million views within twenty-four hours, generating worldwide memes and international interview requests.
- Taekyung has not accepted any of the international interview offers despite recognizing their potential benefit to the Peace Guild.
- Taekyung privately told Team Leader Choi that the public story surrounding him is false and concealed the System's existence.
- The Skeleton Warlord remains alive, stored in Taekyung's Inventory, and was brought to Team Leader Choi's office for Taekyung's amusement.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 워로드몬 as “Warlordmon,” a deliberately childish nickname for the Skeleton Warlord.
- Render 뚱인데요 as “This is Patrick” to preserve the unexpected live-broadcast dumb joke.

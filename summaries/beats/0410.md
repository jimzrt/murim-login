# Chapter 410

## Plot

Lee Jungryong recalls the battlefield of the Great Cataclysm, tells Go Jun that Jin Taekyung is the crack threatening everything he has built, and leads the Ares Guild elites and Chinese Hunters into the fog to attack.

Jin Taekyung rescues the frontline Hunter Burdian from an undead ogre, heals him with a potion, and clears a path for the suicide squad. He discovers that the Arch Lich's fog strengthens and Darkens the monsters, levels up while fighting, and orders Team Leader Choi and Shao Shen to withdraw beyond its range before a tremendous roar splits the sky.

## Continuity

- The Arch Lich's fog is a magic that strengthens monsters within its range by Darkening them.
- Monsters across the battlefield, from goblins to high-rank species, are stronger than their ordinary counterparts and may have unusually high Levels.
- Jin Taekyung is clearing a path and advancing with a suicide squad toward the Arch Lich.
- Lee Jungryong regards Jin Taekyung as the crack that could bring down everything Lee has built and intends to eliminate the threat.
- Lee Jungryong and Go Jun are commanding Ares Guild elites and Chinese Hunters in the assault.
- Jin Taekyung rescued the veteran B-rank Hunter Burdian, healed his injuries, and ordered him to survive and fight weaker monsters.
- Jin Taekyung's Level Up restored his fatigue and some injuries and returned his physical condition to its peak.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 어둠에 물든 as Darkened in System monster names and related prose.
- Render 강기 as Force.
- Render 결사대 as suicide squad.
- Render 빵즈 as bangzi and retain a brief explanatory footnote for the derogatory term.

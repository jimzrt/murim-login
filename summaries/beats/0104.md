# Chapter 104

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin arrive in Honju at night and stay at the lavish Phoenix Inn after Jin Wikyung orders them to use the best lodging and meals available.

Hyuk Mujin pays twenty-five of the fifty silver nyang entrusted to him for the private residence and worries over his remaining funds, while Mukyung treats enduring hunger as training and leaves for the garden. Taekyung’s enormous appetite quickly threatens Mujin’s emergency savings as he orders and eats dish after dish.

A group of martial artists ruins Taekyung’s chicken-and-corn soup with a shattered liquor bottle and mocks him. Taekyung responds by punching the first aggressor in the face. The Phoenix Inn’s unnamed proprietress is later told that one man subdued six others, recognizes the name Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, and rises to see him.

## Continuity

- Taekyung, Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin have reached Honju and are lodging at the Phoenix Inn’s private residence.
- Jin Wikyung instructed the travelers to use excellent lodging and meals because they were traveling without attendants; he gave Mujin fifty silver nyang for expenses.
- Hyuk Mujin has five silver nyang in personal emergency savings after paying half the private residence fee, and Taekyung’s appetite places those funds under immediate strain.
- Mukyung remains intensely devoted to martial-arts training and considers enduring hunger a form of training.
- A Phoenix Inn proprietress has heard that the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi subdued six martial artists and is going to meet him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 별채 as private residence and 냥 as nyang; render 은자 오십 냥 as fifty silver nyang and 철전 as iron coins.
- Render 봉황객잔 as Phoenix Inn.
- Render 계용옥미갱 and the source variant 계용옥미앵 as chicken-and-corn soup; preserve the latter as a spelling variant of the same dish.
- Render 형장 as Brother in the martial artists’ address to Taekyung.

# Chapter 1169

## Plot

Humanity rallies as Jin returns from the sky after defeating Morgoth. System notices register a new Fire Dragon Divine Spear form, Open Heaven, improvements to his martial arts, and level-ups that heal his injuries. His Fire Dragon Armor is destroyed, and he is left mentally exhausted. Morgoth, gravely wounded with his Dragon Heart exposed, tells Jin that he spent millennia seeking God and now believes Jin is chosen by God. The Skeleton King’s head appears in Jin’s arms as Morgoth repeats that claim.

## Continuity

- Jin’s level-ups healed his physical injuries, but he remains severely mentally exhausted; the Fire Dragon Armor was destroyed.
- Jin created the third Fire Dragon Divine Spear form, Open Heaven; Fire Gate Divine Technique and Qi Sense reached the ninth star, and he gained insight into Mind’s Eye, which activates only under certain conditions and causes extreme fatigue.
- Morgoth is gravely wounded and nearing death. He says he spent his life seeking God and calls Jin chosen by God.
- The Skeleton King’s head appeared and spoke to Jin.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 심안 as Mind’s Eye and 드래곤 하트 as Dragon Heart.

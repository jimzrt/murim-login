# Chapter 706

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the guardian spirit charge into the mutant army while the Beast Miao King engages the exhausted Southern Heaven Demon Empress. The Baekcheon Unit suffers severe losses in the ensuing close-quarters battle, and Jin, despite his depleted internal energy and injuries from One Annihilation, fights from the guardian spirit's back with White Flame. As he kills mutants who were once human, the guardian spirit helps him confront his guilt and insists that the dead do not resent him. After Wang Ho declares the battle over, Jin rejects this and advances with the guardian spirit toward the bleeding Southern Heaven Demon Empress.

## Continuity

- The Beast Miao King fights the Southern Heaven Demon Empress directly and has the advantage because she is already exhausted.
- The Baekcheon Unit loses more than half its members while battling the enlarged, strengthened mutants, who retain the martial arts they possessed as humans.
- Jin Taekyung remains severely depleted after One Annihilation but continues fighting from the guardian spirit's back with White Flame.
- The guardian spirit and Jin take the lead after the Beast Miao King separates to fight the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.
- The guardian spirit tells Jin that the mutants do not resent him and urges him to stop blaming himself for ending their suffering.
- Jin and the guardian spirit continue toward the Southern Heaven Demon Empress after the mutant battle, because Jin considers the disaster unfinished while she remains alive.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain One Annihilation, White Flame, Fist Force, Force, Sword Energy, Internal Injury, demonic qi, and Baekcheon Unit as established terminology.
- Render 초일류 as Supreme First Rate, 백천대주 as Commander of the Baekcheon Unit, 신수 as divine beast, and 신병이기 as divine weapon.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's profane, self-deprecating humor and the guardian spirit's terse, teasing, and compassionate telepathic voice.

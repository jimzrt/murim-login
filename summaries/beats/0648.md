# Chapter 648

## Plot

The Beast Miao King stops Baeksang's confrontation with Jin Taekyung and attempts to reconcile them over fruit wine. Baeksang orders Jin and the other Central Plains guests to leave, refuses to explain his hatred of the Han Chinese, and declares that the past cannot be undone. Jin repairs Baeksang's shattered cup with internal energy and uses it to express a plea to restore trust, while the Beast Miao King watches knowingly.

In an unnamed gorge in Guizhou, an unidentified strange man kills approximately two hundred attackers who seek revenge for fallen martial brothers and friends. He carries a Zen staff, walks through the resulting sea of corpses and blood, and heads south.

## Continuity

- The Beast Miao King is Baeksang's sworn elder brother and childhood companion and asks Jin Taekyung and Baeksang to set aside their conflict.
- Baeksang remains deeply distrustful of the Central Plains, demands that Jin and the other Central Plains guests leave Nanman, and refuses to explain the source of that hostility.
- Jin Taekyung has opened his Middle Dantian and can now control his internal energy precisely enough to reconstruct a shattered porcelain cup.
- An unidentified Zen-staff wielder killed roughly two hundred attackers in a Guizhou gorge and is traveling south.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Little brother for the Beast Miao King's 아우 and Palace Lord for Baeksang's 궁주.
- Preserve the explicit metaphor of the cup as trust and the spilled wine as people.
- Retain Jin Taekyung's blunt, irreverent comic voice in his reply that he is going to take a piss.

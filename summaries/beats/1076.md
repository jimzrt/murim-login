# Chapter 1076

## Plot

As Taekyung’s group gathers at Qinghai Lake, the Kunlun Sect Leader Cheongheoja arrives by ship and reveals that more vessels are coming to evacuate their roughly three thousand allies. Cheongheoja confirms that his disciple Hak Woo is safe and will meet Taekyung after they leave. Cheongheoja’s conversation with his unnamed disciple hints at a hidden ember that may bring disaster, while the campfires on the shore represent hope of resisting it.

## Continuity

- Cheongheoja is the Kunlun Sect Leader and Hak Woo’s master; he says Hak Woo is safe and Taekyung will see him after the group leaves Qinghai Lake.
- The group’s roughly three thousand allies are preparing to leave Qinghai Lake aboard dozens of ships; Dark Heaven’s threat has not fully disappeared.
- Cheongheoja says Kunlun’s headquarters was surrendered to protect lives and warns of a hidden ember that could turn everything to ash.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 능공허도 as “Gliding Across the Void,” distinct from 허공답보 (“Stepping on Empty Air”).

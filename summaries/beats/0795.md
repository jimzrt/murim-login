# Chapter 795

## Plot

Jin completes the Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] after Huginn confirms that The Prophet killed Siegfried Wassmann, and receives the special Item Eyes of Truth. Pressed for more information, Huginn reveals that Muninn is an identity passed between five people; the current Prophet is the fifth, and Huginn saw him once about three years ago. He describes the man as white, mostly concealed, and seemingly unsuited to close combat, though he admits this is only an impression.

Huginn says the Muninn from three years ago brought Michael Silbert information about Cheon Taemin, Leviathan, and Magic Gems, including Leviathan’s presence and the plan to draw it to Tokyo. Jin concludes that the Muninns served as hidden operatives who gathered intelligence, eliminated rivals, and moved unrefined Magic Gems from the Middle East and Africa. He orders the search teams recalled and the leadership team to the strategy room, then leaves Huginn unconscious in the torturers’ custody for further questioning.

## Continuity

- The Supreme Peak Quest [An Unknown Death] is complete; Jin received the special Item Eyes of Truth, and the quest confirmed that The Prophet killed Siegfried Wassmann.
- Muninn is a succession of identities: Huginn says the current Prophet is the fifth. The first disappeared during the Great Cataclysm; Huginn met the second after the Cataclysm ended and saw the current Prophet once, about three years ago.
- Huginn describes the current Prophet as a white man whose face was mostly covered and whose build seemed unsuited to close combat; this is Huginn’s uncertain personal impression, not a confirmed Hunter type.
- The Muninn from about three years ago supplied Michael Silbert with information about Cheon Taemin, Leviathan, and Magic Gems, including Leviathan’s presence and the plan to lure it to Tokyo.
- Jin believes the Muninns secretly moved unrefined Magic Gems from the Middle East and Africa, stored them, eliminated rivals, and gathered intelligence. He orders the Middle East search teams recalled and calls the leadership team to the strategy room.
- Huginn remains captive; Jin leaves him unconscious in the torturers’ custody and orders them to continue questioning him.

## Translation Decisions

- Treat Muninn as a succession of identities, not as proof that one person has held the role throughout.
- Keep Magic Gem distinct from mana and magical power.

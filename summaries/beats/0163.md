# Chapter 163

## Plot

An unnamed Supreme Peak master, nearly a hundred years old and suffering from worsening bouts of senility, arrives at the Northern Gaoyuan and forces Black Sand, Temur, Chinggen, and more than two hundred followers to serve him. He demonstrates overwhelming strength, Samadhi True Fire, and Scorching Yang Qi while asking about their influence and searching for someone depicted in a portrait: a Peak master who mainly uses a sword and palm techniques.

Black Sand attempts to manipulate Chinggen through Sound Transmission, planning to find the sought-after master and extort the old man's martial arts while his mind is impaired. The old man hears the scheme, kills Black Sand with a single palm, and says he will return in about a month. He then leaves the Northern Gaoyuan and heads toward Datong on the Shanxi–Gaoyuan border to continue the search.

## Continuity

- The unnamed old man is a Supreme Peak master nearing one hundred years old; his old age has caused increasingly long periods of unconsciousness, but he has delayed its effects for roughly twenty years with two jiazi of internal energy.
- The old man is searching for an unidentified Peak master who mainly uses a sword and palm techniques, carrying a portrait and asking the Northern Gaoyuan's local powers for information.
- Black Sand was killed by the unnamed old man after attempting to exploit the old man's senility and obtain his martial arts; Chinggen and Temur survived the encounter.
- The old man left the Northern Gaoyuan for Datong and said he would return in about a month.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 삼매진화 as “Samadhi True Fire.”
- Render 정기신 as “essence, qi, and spirit,” 백염 as “white flames,” and 입신지경 as “a transcendent realm.”
- Render 노인's rejected forms of address 어르신 and 형님 as “elder” and “big brother,” respectively; preserve the old man's abrasive vulgar voice.

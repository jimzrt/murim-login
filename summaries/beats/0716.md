# Chapter 716

## Plot

Ju Hwaran forcefully reprimands Taishan, Sama Pyo, Namho, and Hyuk Mujin for disturbing Jin Taekyung while he recovers, revealing how deeply worried she had been about him. The reunited Fire Dragon Pavilion members share their accounts of the crisis, while Jeok Cheongang and the Beast Miao King conceal Jin's true condition by claiming he collapsed from exhaustion. Yohi has voluntarily entered the underground prison despite Yayul Mok's willingness to forgive her, Nanman is rebuilding the Inner and Outer Palaces, and Yayul Mok is overwhelmed with restoration duties. The Beast Miao King has been missing for two days, throwing Nanman's leadership into an uproar.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion members are alive and reunited after the Nanman crisis, and Jin Taekyung is recovering among them.
- Ju Hwaran is intensely protective of Jin and now forcefully polices the Pavilion's behavior around him.
- Jeok Cheongang and the Beast Miao King concealed Jin's true condition by reporting that he collapsed from exhaustion.
- Jin's System remains concealed from the Fire Dragon Pavilion; the surviving Baekcheon Unit members are expected to keep silent.
- Yohi voluntarily entered the underground prison after siding with Baeksang and later repenting.
- The Nanman Beast Palace is rebuilding its ruined Inner Palace and damaged Outer Palace.
- Yayul Mok is occupied with restoration work and has become difficult to locate.
- The Beast Miao King has been missing for two days, and Nanman's leadership is in an uproar.

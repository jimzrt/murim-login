# Chapter 632

## Plot

Yayul Cheok secretly summons Jin Taekyung and Namho before dawn to a ruined shrine, where he reflects on his lifelong bond with Baeksang. Jin and Namho warn that even trusted allies may serve Dark Heaven, while Jin insists that the Southern Heaven Demon Empress will not leave Nanman alone. An urgent warning and System alert interrupt the meeting.

## Continuity

- Yayul Cheok is under pressure from Nanman's tribal chiefs and secretly meets Jin Taekyung and Namho away from the Inner Palace.
- Yayul Cheok and Baeksang were lifelong childhood friends and sworn brothers who fought together, but Jin and Namho now openly question whether Baeksang can still be trusted.
- Namho's past includes a cherished Hidden Shadow Pavilion subordinate whose betrayal caused more than five thousand Alliance members to die.
- Jin is certain that the Southern Heaven Demon Empress intends to interfere with Nanman, and the meeting is interrupted by an unidentified urgent warning accompanied by a System alert.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 인시 as Insi, the shichen from three to five in the morning.
- Retain Muyaho for 무야호 as the White Tiger's personal name and preserve its mighty-wilds tiger meaning.
- Retain seori for 서리 with an explanatory note for its colloquial sense of stealing produce from a field.

# Chapter 1127

## Plot

The Blood Lord, mortally wounded, questions what he fought for and realizes the Lord of Heaven never valued his loyalty. As he makes one last attack on Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang blocks it; the surrounding allies shield Taekyung from the rain. The Blood Lord dies, and the Dark Heaven army collapses.

The System grants Taekyung multiple Level Ups and completes several Quests, extending his Final Rally to 5 minutes 35 seconds. His injuries from One Annihilation have damaged his innate qi beyond recovery, and he believes he is going to die. He shares a brief farewell with Cheongpung and prepares to say goodbye to the others.

## Continuity

- The Blood Lord is dead; the Dark Heaven army has collapsed, and the Murim Alliance’s reinforcements have surrounded the battlefield.
- Jeok Cheongang returned to Taekyung’s side and blocked the Blood Lord’s final attack.
- Taekyung’s innate qi is damaged beyond what Level Up recovery can heal. His Final Rally has 5 minutes 35 seconds remaining after the Level Ups; he believes his death is inevitable and is preparing to leave.
- Taekyung and Cheongpung exchanged a brief farewell; Taekyung still intends to say goodbye to others.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 선천지기 as “innate qi” and 진원진기 as “original true qi” when both terms are stated.
- Keep Taekyung’s coarse, irreverent speech in his farewell to Cheongpung and his terse response to the Blood Lord.

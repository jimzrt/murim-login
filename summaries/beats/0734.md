# Chapter 734

## Plot

Jin Taekyung considers confronting Odin Guild's master in Paris but accepts Team Leader Choi's argument that acting without a defensible pretext would only earn them an Interpol wanted notice. They review Odin Guild's earlier demand that China lease ten Sichuan cities for ninety-nine years and authorize a Beijing branch, which China rejected.

The Skeleton King interrupts Jin's discussion of public support with breaking news: Odin Guild has declared its support for Ares, revealed a new Mana Cultivation Method, and claimed the project had been prepared for years. Jin is stunned by the sudden reversal.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi will not immediately attack or confront Odin Guild; they recognize public sentiment and legal justification as essential strategic protections.
- During the Sichuan Province monster wave, Odin Guild demanded a ninety-nine-year lease of ten cities, including Chengdu, Meishan, Ziyang, and Suining, plus permission to establish an official branch in Beijing; China rejected the demand.
- Odin Guild had contacted the French government before negotiations ended and benefits from the protection and political cover of its Paris base.
- Odin Guild publicly declares support for Ares and presents a new Mana Cultivation Method as a project prepared for several years.

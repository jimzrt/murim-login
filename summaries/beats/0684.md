# Chapter 684

## Plot

Jin uses Inventory to bombard the dust-shrouded Great Snow Fiend with summoned spears, then pursues him after detecting blood. Realizing that the Great Snow Fiend's apparent vulnerability is a trap, Jin twists aside, but the Great Snow Fiend redirects White Flame through the Fire Dragon Armor and drives the spear into Jin's chest. The system reports that the armor is partially destroyed, Jin is bleeding and suffering massive internal injuries, and qi deviation is imminent.

The Great Snow Fiend, who lost his right arm during Muyaho's ambush and Jin's counterattack, believes Jin is beyond saving. Instead, Jin grips the spear shaft and drives it deeper into his own chest to force himself forward, then unleashes the last of his blue-white light-flames into the Great Snow Fiend as the chapter ends.

## Continuity

- Muyaho ignored Jin's order to avoid the battle, ambushed the Great Snow Fiend, and disappeared into the distance afterward.
- The Great Snow Fiend lost his right arm during the ambush and Jin's subsequent spear attack.
- White Flame pierced the Fire Dragon Armor and remains driven into Jin's chest.
- Part of the Fire Dragon Armor was destroyed; its automatic repair will take three days.
- Jin is suffering bleeding, massive internal injury, and a risk of qi deviation while remaining conscious and able to fight.
- Jin forced himself forward through the spear wound and struck the Great Snow Fiend with his final blue-white light-flame attack.
- Black Hand Fist Demon is dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 막대한 내상 as "Massive Internal Injury" in the system status.
- Keep 광염 as "light-flames" and 화염 as "flame" according to context.

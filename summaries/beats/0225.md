# Chapter 225

## Plot

Jin Taekyung confronted Won Myunghoon and the Star Guild ambush. Butler Kim revealed himself as an A-Rank mage and used Earthquake to incapacitate around a dozen veteran B-Rank Hunters, causing the hostage situation to fail. Won surrendered, but Taekyung deliberately tested his trustworthiness; Won attempted a surprise attack with an Aura-infused dagger, so Taekyung severed his wrist, cut his calf tendons, and killed him by cutting his throat. The System identified Won as Level 80 and awarded Taekyung a considerable amount of EXP.

Taekyung and Butler Kim restrained the defeated Star Guild members and discovered that the Star Guild's Team One Leader, Kim Jonghun, had been gagged and bound to a tree. Outside the Gate, reporters waiting for the raid party witnessed the Gate open unexpectedly and around a dozen people emerge, prompting widespread shock.

## Continuity

- Butler Kim is an A-Rank mage whose Earthquake spell can incapacitate around a dozen veteran B-Rank Hunters at once.
- Taekyung never trusted Won Myunghoon after receiving Yeti's Necklace and regarded Won's renewed attack as proof that he would remain a threat.
- Won Myunghoon surrendered, betrayed that surrender by attacking Taekyung, and was killed by Taekyung after Taekyung disarmed and disabled him.
- The System recorded Won Myunghoon as Lv. 80 and awarded Taekyung a considerable amount of EXP for killing him.
- Kim Jonghun, the Star Guild's Team One Leader, was found gagged and bound to a tree after the ambush failed.
- The Gate opened unexpectedly after the raid, and around a dozen people emerged before the waiting reporters.

# Chapter 1092

## Plot

Jin Taekyung faces the Blood Lord and recognizes that his enemy is still stronger, though no longer impossibly beyond his reach. Their first exchange reveals the Blood Lord’s monstrous strength and an abnormally enlarged, stitched-on arm; the Blood Lord attributes it to “his grace.” He knocks White Flame from Taekyung’s hands and wounds him, but Taekyung keeps taunting him. The Fire King and Slaughter Saint attack from behind, and a streak of lightning descends from the nearby city wall.

## Continuity

- The fight against the Blood Lord is underway; Taekyung is wounded and disarmed, while the Fire King and Slaughter Saint have joined the battle.
- The Blood Lord has an abnormally enlarged arm with stitched-looking marks and strength beyond human limits; he credits “his grace” for it.
- An unidentified streak of lightning descends from the city wall at the end of the chapter.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 나려타곤 as “Narye tagon,” retaining the established term and its humiliating implication of rolling on the ground like a lazy donkey.

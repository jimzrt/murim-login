# Chapter 456

## Plot

Taekyung and Zhuge Gyun conclude that the Dongting Fisherman may still be hiding in Hubei Province and may be one of Dark Heaven's culprits. They suspect the massacres of the Sea Serpent Society and three Yangtze River Channel League strongholds were a diversion before a larger attack targeting a sacred treasure or a major orthodox sect or family. Zhuge Gyun agrees to coordinate the Zhuge Clan's intelligence with the Beggars' Sect and Lower District Sect, while Taekyung orders warnings sent to Wudang and Donghu Stronghold. The Zhuge Clan's own sacred treasures have all been lost, destroyed, or reduced to a broken four-wheeled cart. Taekyung leaves for Dongting Lake with Cheongpung carrying the chained and acupoint-sealed Three Fiends, intending to investigate whether the prisoner bears Dark Heaven codes or markings. After traveling through the night and carrying the exhausted Hyuk Mujin, the party reaches Wuhan, where they hear that a boat has sunk in Dongting Lake.

## Continuity

- Taekyung believes the Dongting Fisherman may still be in Hubei Province and may be a Dark Heaven subordinate involved in the Donghu Stronghold massacre.
- Taekyung suspects the Hubei massacres were a diversion and that Dark Heaven is preparing a larger attack against a sacred treasure or a major orthodox sect or family.
- Zhuge Gyun will mobilize the Zhuge Clan's intelligence resources and cooperate with the Beggars' Sect and Lower District Sect in searching Hubei Province outside Dongting Lake.
- Taekyung's party has left the Zhuge Clan for Dongting Lake, reached Wuhan, and heard that a boat has sunk in Dongting Lake.
- The Three Fiends is being transported alive in chains with all acupoints sealed so Taekyung can investigate possible Dark Heaven codes or markings.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 무한 as Wuhan, 사륜차 as Four-Wheeled Cart, 병법이십사편 as Twenty-Four Chapters on the Art of War, and 백우선 as White Feather Fan.
- Render 등하불명 as unable to see what is right beneath one's nose while preserving Taekyung's comparison to Cheongpung walking past a dumpling shop.
- Preserve Taekyung's dry, profane humor, Cheongpung's deferential Benefactor address, and Zhuge Gyun's formal analytical voice.

# Chapter 166

## Plot

Jang Taebo, formerly the Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild and one of the world’s most renowned smiths, has retired to an anonymous village life as Old Man Jang. Over eighty and cantankerous, he spends his days eating, sleeping, and caring for the neighboring boy Hanga, his only conversational companion.

Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung arrive in the village seeking the retired Master Artisan. After learning that Old Man Jang is an outsider with no family, they enlist Hanga to identify him. Hanga leads them to Jang Taebo, who recognizes Taekyung as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi but flatly refuses to accept any commission before hearing the request.

## Continuity

- Jang Taebo is over eighty, a renowned former Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild who spent a full jiazi working as a smith before retiring.
- Jang Taebo has lived anonymously in a village near Jeongyang for more than ten years, avoiding attention and refusing commissions; he is blunt, cantankerous, and values his quiet life.
- Hanga is a local village boy who lives near Jang Taebo, visits him regularly, and is Jang Taebo’s only conversational companion.
- Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung located Jang Taebo after searching the village; Jang Taebo immediately refused Taekyung’s commission.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 장 노인 as “Old Man Jang,” 장태보 as “Jang Taebo,” 야장 as “smith,” 집성촌 as “clan village,” and 항아 as “Hanga.”
- Keep Jang Taebo’s voice curt and cantankerous, including his flat rejection of the commission.

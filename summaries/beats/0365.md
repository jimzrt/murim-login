# Chapter 365

## Plot

Jin Taekyung breaks through to the Supreme Peak realm, advances the Fire Gate Divine Technique and Fire Dragon Divine Spear to the eighth stage, and manifests Force for the first time. He and Jeok Cheongang combine their attacks to severely injure the Western Heaven Demon Lord.

The Lord of Heaven temporarily possesses the Western Heaven Demon Lord's body, but Taekyung uses One Annihilation to destroy the borrowed body. The possessing entity escapes after promising to return. Taekyung collapses from exhaustion, Jeok catches him, and the underground prison begins to collapse.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has reached the Supreme Peak realm and manifested Force.
- The Fire Gate Divine Technique and Fire Dragon Divine Spear have both reached the eighth stage.
- The Lord of Heaven can temporarily possess the Western Heaven Demon Lord's body; the borrowed body crumbled after One Annihilation, but the possessing entity escaped and promised to return.
- Jeok Cheongang survived the confrontation and caught Taekyung as he collapsed from exhaustion.
- The underground prison is collapsing.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 강기 as Force and retain Heavenly Strike, Flame Divine Palm, Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, and One Annihilation.
- Render the Lord of Heaven's use of the Western Heaven Demon Lord's body as temporary possession, distinguishing the possessing entity from the Demon Lord.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's profane, self-mocking voice and Jeok Cheongang's rough, protective voice.

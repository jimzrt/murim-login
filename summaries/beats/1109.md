# Chapter 1109

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung’s shout, Cheongpung and Jeong Hogun’s Embroidered Uniform Guards force their way through the fanatics toward the Blood Lord. The Blood Lord rapidly regenerates by absorbing blood from the dead, regains his strength and memories, and recognizes Cheongpung and his debt to Mae Jonghak.

Cheongpung wounds the Blood Lord and presses the attack, but the Blood Lord calls for reinforcements. Countless flying beasts descend on the battlefield, with another being at their center; the chapter ends as a tearing impact fills Cheongpung’s vision with blood.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung, still weakened after using One Annihilation, manages to shout an order to attack.
- The Blood Lord regenerates by absorbing blood, regains his strength and memories, recognizes Cheongpung, and remembers a debt to Mae Jonghak.
- Countless flying beasts descend on the battlefield after the Blood Lord calls for them; the being at their center is unidentified.
- Cheongpung attacks the Blood Lord, but the chapter ends with a violent impact and blood obscuring his vision; the outcome is unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain the established renderings “Blood Lord,” “Lord of Heaven,” and “Embroidered Uniform Guard.”

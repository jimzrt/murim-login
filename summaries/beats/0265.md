# Chapter 265

## Plot

The Blood Lord regenerates from the catastrophic injuries inflicted by Jeok Cheongang's Dance of the Fire God and Demon, claiming that demonic martial arts are responsible. Jeok is exhausted and unconscious, leaving Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung to face him. Cheongpung initially freezes in fear, but chooses to remain and fight after Taekyung gives him the choice between fleeing with Jeok and fighting to the death.

The Blood Lord reveals that he witnessed Taekyung kill Jin Baekyang at Eight Spring Gorge and was the one responsible for the war that followed. Enraged, Taekyung uses One Annihilation and then fights the Blood Lord in a prolonged exchange. The Blood Lord eventually stops regenerating but remains strong enough to overpower Taekyung and Cheongpung. Cheongpung refuses to retreat because he would regret it for the rest of his life, smiles through his fear, and is about to be killed when a familiar clear voice declares that he has broken free of his shell.

## Continuity

- The Blood Lord possesses extraordinary regeneration that restores even catastrophic bodily damage, which he attributes to demonic martial arts.
- Jeok Cheongang exhausted himself with the Dance of the Fire God and Demon and is unconscious and unable to continue fighting.
- The Blood Lord confirms that he witnessed the Eight Spring Gorge battle and was responsible for the war that caused the deaths and devastation remembered by Taekyung.
- Taekyung uses his final One Annihilation against the Blood Lord, then fights until he collapses.
- Cheongpung experiences genuine fear for the first time, but chooses to fight despite it and refuses to retreat because doing so would leave him with lifelong regret.
- After sustaining severe injuries, Cheongpung smiles at the Blood Lord without fear and hears a familiar voice say that he has broken free of his shell.
- The Blood Lord is eventually wounded badly enough that he can no longer immediately regenerate, but he still overpowers the incapacitated Taekyung and injured Cheongpung.

# Chapter 978

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, and the Bow Saint combine their attacks to overwhelm Murong Baek, the North Heaven Demon Lord. Taekyung rejects Murong’s choices and kills him with White Flame.

In the darkness, an unnamed being awakens after sensing that the fourth heaven has fallen and another faithful servant has disappeared. It realizes the day it had long awaited is near, and smiles.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung killed Murong Baek, the North Heaven Demon Lord, after fighting alongside Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint.
- An unnamed being in the darkness awakened upon sensing that the fourth heaven had fallen and another faithful servant had disappeared; it believes the day it has awaited is near.
- Murong Baek said the Lord of Heaven had awakened, the war had begun, and at most half a year remained.
- The conditions of Jin Mukyung, Cheol Mubaek, Wipeng, and Peng Cheolhu remain unknown.
- The Hebei Peng Family’s fate against the pill-enhanced Keshiks remains unknown.
- The Emperor remains gravely ill with Blood Soul Gu; the treatment said to require him to die once remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이룡신창 as “Divine Spear of the Imugi,” Murong Baek’s sobriquet.
- Do not identify the unnamed being in the darkness beyond what this chapter establishes.

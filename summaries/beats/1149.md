# Chapter 1149

## Plot

The Hunters finish off the monsters in the desert with help from the Grand Mage, Magic Johnson. Johnson reunites with Taekyung and reveals that his last contact with the group was twenty-four hours ago. Team Leader Choi then tells Taekyung that he has been unconscious for ten days.

The System reports that the Main Quest Cataclysm failed despite the Doppelganger's defeat, partially opening the Demon Realm's boundaries and allowing unknown beings to invade. A rift is in progress, and Black Dragon Duke Morgoth has answered a summoning. Taekyung recognizes Morgoth's wings and roar from his last night in Murim.

In Russia, Morgoth enters Vladimir Furin's office after destroying Red Square. Furin confronts him while clasping his hand.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has been unconscious for ten days; Magic Johnson's last contact with the group was twenty-four hours ago, and Choi says the first contact was three hours before that.
- The Main Quest Cataclysm failed despite the Doppelganger's defeat; the Demon Realm's boundaries have partially opened and a rift is in progress.
- Black Dragon Duke Morgoth has answered a summoning; Taekyung recognizes his wings and roar from his last night in Murim.
- Morgoth destroyed Red Square and entered Vladimir Furin's office in Russia.

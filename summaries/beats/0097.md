# Chapter 97

## Plot

Jin Taekyung visits a nearby real-estate office and detects eavesdropping magic installed by the people surveilling him. He deliberately acts like a boastful, extravagant C-rank Hunter so the surveillance team will underestimate him, while the Security Team listens through the magic and relaxes after judging his behavior childish and harmless.

Through the real-estate agent's conversation, Taekyung learns that three properties near his home changed hands within the past five days: Building 5, Unit 901; Building 4, Unit 302; and Building 3, Unit 202. He concludes that the watchers are somewhere within five hundred meters of his home and plans to search the area and parking lot with Qi Sense while appearing to take a walk, intending to capture them and learn who is behind the operation.

The Security Team Leader takes offense at being described as an ugly old bachelor, leaves for a sauna, and orders Kim Junsu and another team member to prepare a transcript and full-page opinion statements. After he leaves, the two subordinates mock and curse him, while Junsu worries that stress is worsening his hair loss. Taekyung later encounters the disguised Level 42 Hunter Kim Gwondong, maintains his civilian-facing cover, and refrains from attacking him. Gwondong tells him that the black cat Familiar is still waiting in its previous location.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has confirmed that eavesdropping magic is active in the nearby real-estate office and is intentionally presenting himself as a vain, ordinary C-rank Hunter to mislead the surveillance team.
- Taekyung has identified three recently traded properties within five hundred meters of his home as possible surveillance locations: Building 5, Unit 901; Building 4, Unit 302; and Building 3, Unit 202.
- Taekyung intends to locate and capture the surveillance personnel rather than merely drive them away, but has not yet confronted them.
- Taekyung recognizes Kim Gwondong's civilian disguise as a Level 42 Hunter, probably C-rank and specialized in stealth or pursuit, and continues treating him as an undiscovered surveillance operative.
- The black kitten Familiar remains at the location where Taekyung previously left it, and Kim Gwondong knows enough about its presence to remind Taekyung that it is still there.

# Chapter 371

## Plot

Jin Taekyung is publicly hailed as the Blazing Fire Divine Dragon, and Jeok Cheongang takes pride in seeing his disciple recognized as a true hero. Their celebration turns into a chaotic spectacle before Jeok privately admits that Taekyung did well.

The Sect Leaders of Qingcheng and Emei ask Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang to accompany them to investigate a strange formation connected to Samgoe. Outside the Sichuan Tang Clan, Taekyung reunites with Mungyeong and makes embarrassing jokes before discovering that Mungyeong is actually the Slaughter Saint. The group travels toward a cliff-lined area while awaiting a reply from Henan.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is now publicly recognized by the epithet Blazing Fire Divine Dragon, and Jeok Cheongang is proud of his rise.
- The Qingcheng Sect Leader Clear Wind Ancient Sword and the Emei Sect Leader Extinction Divine Nun ask Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang to investigate a strange formation associated with information obtained from Samgoe.
- Mungyeong is revealed to be the martial artist known as the Slaughter Saint.
- A message hawk has been sent to Henan for further information, and the formation's nature and purpose remain unresolved.

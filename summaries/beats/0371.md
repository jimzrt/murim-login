# Chapter 371

## Plot

Jin Taekyung is publicly hailed as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, and Jeok Cheongang privately takes pride in seeing his Disciple recognized as a new Great Hero. After the celebration, Jeok reunites with Jin, Cheongpung, and Hyuk Mujin, while Cheongpung and Mimi entertain the room.

Cheongpung the Ancient Sword and the living Extinction Divine Nun, now acting as Emei's Sect Leader, ask Jin and Jeok to investigate a strange formation connected to the captured Third Fiend; a messenger eagle sent to Henan is still awaited. Jin then discovers that the quiet Mungyeong is actually the Slaughter Saint and is horrified that he had jokingly asked him about wet dreams.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's public sobriquet is Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, and Jeok Cheongang recognizes him as a new Great Hero who has crossed the Supreme Peak threshold.
- Extinction Divine Nun is alive and serves as Emei Sect Leader; she is older than Jeok Cheongang and the Senior Aunt of the late Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun.
- Cheongpung the Ancient Sword and Extinction Divine Nun are investigating a bizarre formation connected to Dark Heaven and the captured Third Fiend, with Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang accompanying them.
- Mungyeong's identity as the Slaughter Saint is now known to Jin Taekyung, who remains deeply embarrassed by his earlier vulgar teasing.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 진인 as Perfected One, 도우 as Fellow Daoist, and 신니 as Venerable Nun when used as forms of address.
- Render 진 시주 as Benefactor Jin and preserve the Teacher Jin Seong-ae and Teacher Gu Seong-ae parody with a footnote explaining the Korean sex-educator reference.

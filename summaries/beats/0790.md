# Chapter 790

## Plot

Jin learns that the Main Quest’s true target was never Michael Silbert: its mission updates to require eliminating The Prophet within a time limit. The Prophet is in a cave in the Middle East as the World Hunter Federation mobilizes against them.

Footage from a surviving camera at the National Assembly exposes the truth behind the Federation’s arrests and wins broad public support. Jin orders the Federation to concentrate all available forces in the Middle East.

The Prophet’s followers report that the desert is under surveillance and estimate that at least 100,000 Hunters are approaching. The Prophet promises to punish them in the name of God, believing the long wait is about to bear fruit.

## Continuity

- The Main Quest [Cataclysm] is incomplete; its mission is to eliminate The Prophet within an unspecified time limit.
- The Prophet is in a cave in the Middle East. The World Hunter Federation is mobilizing against them at Jin’s order; their followers estimate at least 100,000 Hunters are approaching.
- Surviving National Assembly camera footage has been released publicly, revealing the Federation’s operation and gaining it broad support.

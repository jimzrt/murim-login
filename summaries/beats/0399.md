# Chapter 399

## Plot

Jin Taekyung annihilates the monsters before him and confronts the Level 135 Death Knight Lord. Their clash destroys the hospital and surrounding ground; Jin kills an attacking Death Knight, is cut across the chest, then uses the Fire Dragon Armor and relentless strikes to break the Lord's armor, wrist, sword, and helmet. As Jin prepares the final blow, he recognizes the exposed undead face as resembling Lei Fei.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has overwhelmed the monster army and is fighting the Level 135 Death Knight Lord as an equal or superior combatant.
- Jin killed an interfering Death Knight and used the opening to damage the Death Knight Lord, whose armor and helmet are shattered and whose sword has been dropped.
- The Death Knight Lord's sword cut Jin across the chest, but Jin remains combat-capable and continues attacking.
- The Fire Dragon Armor is approximately fifty percent restored and protected Jin from part of the Death Knight Lord's attack.
- Jin recognizes the exposed face beneath the Death Knight Lord's broken helmet as resembling the missing Lei Fei, but the identity is not confirmed.
- The battle between Jin and the Death Knight Lord destroyed the hospital and devastated the surrounding ground.
- The Skeleton Warlord remains behind the battle guarding Choi and Shao at Jin's command.

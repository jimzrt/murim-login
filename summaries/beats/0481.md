# Chapter 481

## Plot

Mungyeong reflects on the boatman's protection and compensation, then overhears clinic staff repeating the authorities' fabricated account that the captured dark-path figures caused the Hubei incidents. Rumors instead suggest the arrests conceal a greater truth, but Physician Song pressures the young medical apprentices to stop discussing them and praise the Emperor, the City Lord, and Jin Taekyung.

At the honored estate, Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung's filthy master-and-Disciple quarrel continues until Mungyeong joins in, cuts Taekyung's clothes with a sword, and discusses the boatman's recovery and the Dongting Fisherman's severe injuries. Taekyung realizes he forgot to tell Mungyeong that Honglan corrupted the imugi, and Gung Gibang arrives claiming to have found a trace of her.

## Continuity

- The boatman has regained consciousness without unusual symptoms, understands what is said to him, and is being protected through Mungyeong's arrangement with the Hubei City Lord; a money pouch has been left for him as compensation.
- The Dongting Fisherman survived his encounter with the Water God Dragon at Donghu Stronghold but has crushed limbs, substantial Internal Injury, and serious Fear exposure; the City Lord's elixir is expected to aid his recovery.
- Gung Gibang has found a trace of Honglan after her whereabouts remained unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 산재처리 as “workers’ compensation” to preserve Taekyung’s modern comic diction.
- Render 거열형 as being torn apart by chariots, and retain the established gukbap footnote.
- Use “Hyang,” “Physician Song,” and “Medical Apprentice Hwang” for the newly introduced clinic personnel.

# Chapter 438

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang interrupts Mungyeong's pressure on Taekyung, learns that Mungyeong was investigating Taekyung's newly opened Middle Dantian, and dismisses the possibility that Taekyung learned demonic martial arts. He begins Taekyung's training and orders him to stay away from Mungyeong.

That night, Jeok meets Mungyeong aboard the fast ship, drinks the Seafaring King's prized Jiannan Chun, and argues that Taekyung's rapid advancement cannot be judged by ordinary experience. Jeok says Taekyung broke through the Supreme Peak wall and opened his Middle Dantian in only two years, calls Taekyung and the Sword Saint's Disciple divine dragons, and tells Mungyeong to accept what he cannot explain.

With the journey to Hubei beginning, Taekyung logs out to the modern world aboard a private jet bound for Korea. The Skeleton King observes his sleep and is struck after calling him strange.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang has confirmed Taekyung's Middle Dantian and begun supervising his training.
- Taekyung's account of meeting a sword-wielding old man in a dream remains a fabricated explanation for how his Middle Dantian opened.
- Mungyeong suspects Taekyung's rapid advancement could only result from demonic, heterodox arts, while Jeok rejects that conclusion and urges him to accept the unexplained.
- Jeok says Taekyung broke through the Supreme Peak wall and opened his Middle Dantian in only two years.
- Jeok describes Taekyung and the Sword Saint's Disciple as divine dragons beyond ordinary understanding.
- Jeok orders Taekyung to avoid Mungyeong after the confrontation.
- The journey to Hubei will take a little over a week, during which Taekyung will alternate between the modern world and Murim.
- Taekyung has logged out aboard a private jet bound for Korea, with the Skeleton King accompanying him.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Middle Dantian, Supreme Peak, demonic, heterodox arts, demonic martial arts, Sound Transmission, Jiannan Chun, and divine dragons as established renderings.
- Render 검선 여동빈 as Sword Immortal Lü Dongbin and 하계 as lower world.
- Keep Jeok Cheongang's voice blunt, gruff, threatening, and dryly teasing, contrasted with Mungyeong's restrained and sardonic voice.
- Preserve Taekyung's conversational profanity and comic modern references.

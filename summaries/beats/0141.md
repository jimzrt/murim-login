# Chapter 141

## Plot

Li Feng reveals that Sword Saint Mae Jonghak remained hidden at Huashan and that, ten years earlier, he saw a ten-year-old Cheongpung performing the Plum Blossom Sword Technique at Mae Jonghak's residence. Li Feng explains that witnessing Cheongpung's talent, rather than losing to Gong Ilhyuk, was the reason he eventually left Huashan. Cheongpung demonstrates Huashan's Zaha Divine Technique and cleaves the luncheon table with a single Sword Energy strike, prompting Li Feng to recognize him as his Martial Uncle.

## Continuity

- Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint, remained in deep seclusion at a hidden residence on Huashan; reaching it required breaking through ten formations.
- Li Feng visited Mae Jonghak's residence with Huashan's leaders about ten years ago and saw Cheongpung there as a ten-year-old child performing the Plum Blossom Sword Technique.
- Li Feng left Huashan after realizing that Cheongpung's extraordinary talent made remaining there pointless; his departure was not caused by his defeat to Gong Ilhyuk.
- Cheongpung knows the Six Harmonies Sword, Plum Blossom Sword Technique, Supreme Clarity Sword, Taeeul Miri Palm, Falling Flower Chasing Shadow Palm, and Scattering Flowers Shadowless Hand.
- Cheongpung can use the Huashan technique Zaha Divine Technique and project exceptionally potent Extreme Yang internal energy.
- Li Feng recognizes Cheongpung as his Martial Uncle because Cheongpung is Mae Jonghak's grandson and martial heir.

# Chapter 263

## Plot

The Blood Lord reveals that he claims to have killed Hong Dao by luring him away with a false threat to detonate a Heaven-Shaking Thunder in the spectator stands. He says Jin Taekyung's sudden intervention advanced the plan by one day and identifies Jongni Chu as its greatest variable, confirming that Jongni Chu was neither the culprit nor an accomplice. After Han Su delivers the Green Jade Buddha Staff and asks the Blood Lord to kill Jeok Cheongang and Taekyung, the Blood Lord beheads Han Su in a single strike and destroys his corpse. He then declares that he is far older than he appears, boasts that the Fire King's lineage will end that day, and confronts Jeok and Taekyung. When he senses someone hiding nearby, Cheongpung emerges from the grass, apologizing because he has never been so frightened before.

## Continuity

- The Blood Lord claims to have killed Hong Dao after luring him away from the Star-Array Grand Banquet with a fabricated threat involving a Heaven-Shaking Thunder.
- The Blood Lord states that Taekyung's intervention forced him to advance his plan by one day and that Jongni Chu was the plan's greatest variable; Jongni Chu was not the culprit or an accomplice.
- Han Su delivered the Green Jade Buddha Staff to the Blood Lord and requested the deaths of Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung.
- The Blood Lord killed Han Su, the Yin Ghost, by beheading him in a single strike and then crushed his corpse.
- The Blood Lord claims to have lived at least twice as long as Taekyung despite appearing to be in his early or mid-twenties.
- The Blood Lord declares that he can kill Jeok Cheongang within one ildagyeong, and Jeok recognizes that the threat is credible.
- Jeok Cheongang shields Taekyung and uses Sound Transmission to tell him to flee if the situation becomes unfavorable; Taekyung refuses because Jeok would otherwise face two Supreme Peak masters alone.
- The Blood Lord declares that the Fire Gate Clan's lineage may end that day; Cheongpung emerges from hiding and admits that fear kept him concealed.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Heaven-Shaking Thunder for 진천뢰, the name of the explosive invoked in the Blood Lord's false threat.
- Use Four Saints for 사성, contrasting it with the established Three Saints for 삼성.
- Retain ildagyeong for 일다경 and explain it as the short time required to drink a cup of tea.

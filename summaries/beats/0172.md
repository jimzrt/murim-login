# Chapter 172

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang punishes Jin Taekyung with controlled Extreme Yang Qi, but Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin intervene to defend their Benefactor. Jeok evades their coordinated sword attack and decides to teach both young martial artists a lesson.

Jeok Cheongang overwhelms them effortlessly, lifting Jang Taebo over the fence with Seizing an Object Through Empty Space, melting Hyuk Mujin's sword with white flames, and knocking Mujin down with a palm strike. He recognizes Cheongpung's Zaha Divine Technique and Huashan origins.

Cheongpung continues fighting despite Jeok's overwhelming strength, using the Crouching Tiger Fist and the Plum Blossom Sword Technique. His twenty-four plum blossoms narrowly miss Jeok and destroy the surrounding area, prompting Jeok to ask about Cheongpung's relationship with Mae Jonghak.

Jeok recalls meeting Mae Jonghak more than forty years earlier, when the Sword Saint challenged him at Mount Jiuhua for refusing to aid the orthodox Murim against the Demonic Cult. They fought for seven days and seven nights and drew. After Mae left, Demonic Cultists burned Mount Jiuhua, causing Jeok to emerge from seclusion and kill all one thousand attackers; people thereafter called him the Fire King.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang is the Fire King and belongs to the Fire Gate Clan, whose martial arts follow one-man succession and secret transmission.
- More than forty years ago, Jeok Cheongang and Mae Jonghak fought for seven days and seven nights at Mount Jiuhua and ended in a draw.
- Jeok Cheongang emerged from seclusion after Demonic Cultists burned Mount Jiuhua and killed all one thousand attackers.
- Cheongpung and Hyuk Mujin risked their lives to defend Jin Taekyung from Jeok Cheongang; Jeok defeated them but did not intend to kill them.

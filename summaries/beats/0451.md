# Chapter 451

## Plot

Mungyeong privately analyzes the Donghu Stronghold corpses and determines that at least two, possibly three, Supreme Peak masters attacked using Force or an equally sharp weapon, with at least one wielding an unusual crooked blade and external arts capable of crushing bodies and collapsing buildings. The evidence points to the missing Dongting Fisherman as the leading suspect for the Dark Heaven massacre, though his motive remains unknown.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong concludes that at least two and possibly three Supreme Peak attackers destroyed Donghu Stronghold; at least one used an unusual weapon resembling a Snake Sword, while another or the same attacker used devastating external arts.
- The Dongting Fisherman is now the leading suspect in the Donghu Stronghold massacre because his Supreme Peak realm, unusual black-wood fishing rod, missing corpse, and disappearance after crossing Tianling Falls match the evidence; his possible connection to Dark Heaven remains unproven.
- The bodies of Hwang Chung, Hwang Cheol, and Do Ripgun shared severe upper-body separation, while commoners were crushed rather than cut, revealing different methods of attack.

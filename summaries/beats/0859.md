# Chapter 859

## Plot

Jeong Hogun restrains his Embroidered Uniform Guard subordinate from attacking Jin Taekyung’s party, citing their orders to escort Prince Shangshan quietly and the prince’s command to lower their swords. He secretly sends the subordinate to report to the Guard’s Commander-in-Chief that, as predicted, uninvited guests have interfered.

Jin realizes the Guard’s message is bound for the imperial capital and worries that entering it could put the party at the Emperor’s mercy, especially if his suspicion of a connection between the Emperor and Dark Heaven is right. Meanwhile, Zhu Bao eagerly accepts the Divine Physician’s offer of a checkup and, amused by Hyuk Mujin’s boast, grants him the title “Tenfold Man,” which Mujin inscribes on a token.

## Continuity

- Jeong Hogun sent a subordinate to the Embroidered Uniform Guard’s Commander-in-Chief with the message that uninvited guests had interfered, as predicted; the subordinate’s report may reach the Son of Heaven.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao granted Hyuk Mujin the joking title “Tenfold Man,” which Mujin inscribed on a bronze token.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 금위군 as “Imperial Guards,” distinct from 금의위, “Embroidered Uniform Guard.”
- Render 십상남자 as “Tenfold Man.”

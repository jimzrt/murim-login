# Chapter 671

## Plot

Jin identifies Yohi's damaged scent pouch as a high-grade tracking scent and realizes that Muyaho, an exceptionally intelligent spiritual creature, can detect it. The System generates the Supreme Peak-grade Sudden Quest Yohi's Tracking Scent, requiring Jin to choose between two directions: northwest toward the reconnaissance squad or southeast along Yohi's scent.

Jin hesitates because pursuing Yohi may mean abandoning the people who stayed behind to protect his escape. He recognizes that returning after escaping Baeksang's net over heaven and earth could take seven days and nights, by which time the Beast Miao King and the rescuers may already be dead.

Namho awakens bound to the back of a large beast and discovers that Yayul Mok's plan succeeded, but Jin is no longer with him and Taishan. He urgently asks where Jin Taekyung has gone.

## Continuity

- The System has generated the Sudden Quest Yohi's Tracking Scent, whose incomplete mission requires Jin to choose a direction; its reward and failure conditions remain unknown.
- Yohi left behind a nearly odorless high-grade tracking scent in her damaged scent pouch, and Muyaho can detect it.
- Muyaho can distinguish two tracking directions: northwest toward the reconnaissance squad and southeast toward Yohi.
- Jin understands that leaving Nanman and returning later could take seven days and nights, potentially leaving the Beast Miao King and the rescuers dead before he can help them.
- Jin is emotionally conflicted between escaping Nanman and pursuing the consequences of Yohi's tracking scent.
- Namho has awakened on a large beast after Yayul Mok's plan succeeded and has discovered that Jin is absent from the traveling group.
- Baeksang's net over heaven and earth remains an imminent threat, while the Beast Miao King's fate and the fate of the people who stayed behind remain unresolved.

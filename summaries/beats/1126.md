# Chapter 1126

## Plot

Mae Jonghak arrives and severs the Blood Lord’s arm with a strike of purple Force. The Blood Lord survives the ensuing attack, but the power granted by the Lord of Heaven disappears, leaving him badly wounded.

Though critically injured, Taekyung grabs the Blood Lord’s ankle to hold him in place, taunts him, and buys time. Reinforcements arrive: the Yangtze River Channel League, Green Forest Alliance, and Murim Alliance.

Taekyung bites the Blood Lord’s neck. The powerless Blood Lord falls into the pool of blood; his fate and the battle’s outcome remain unresolved.

## Continuity

- Mae Jonghak has joined the battle, and reinforcements from the Yangtze River Channel League, Green Forest Alliance, and Murim Alliance have arrived.
- The Blood Lord is gravely wounded and has lost the power granted by the Lord of Heaven; he falls into a pool of blood after Taekyung bites his neck, but his death is not confirmed.
- Taekyung is critically injured but remains conscious enough to hold and bite the Blood Lord; his survival is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 하청지회 as “an auspicious meeting after the Yellow River runs clear,” preserving Wolhwa’s wordplay and the letter’s promise to meet again.

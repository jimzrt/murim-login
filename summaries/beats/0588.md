# Chapter 588

## Plot

Korea reels after the Busan and Pyeongchang Monster Waves, while media speculation grows over the disappearance of Jin Taekyung, the Hunter who defeated both Named Monsters. Jin suddenly appears in Jongno, covered in human and monster blood, and leads a growing crowd to Ares Guild headquarters.

Jin enters the Ares lobby, spares a low-ranking Security Team member, demands that Go Jun be brought to him, and warns the assembled Hunters not to interfere. When Ares issues a Code Red naming Jin as the target, the Hunters attack him with a massive combined barrage.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has reached Ares Guild headquarters and openly begun his confrontation with Go Jun.
- Jin suppresses his murderous intent toward uninvolved Ares personnel because he refuses to become a monster, but the Ares Code Red places the Guild's forces against him.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Rodin's The Thinker for 로댕의 생각 난 사람, preserving Jin's art-reference joke.
- Render the Code Red announcement as designating all Ares Guild members and naming Jin Taekyung as the target.
- Keep Jin's height at approximately 190 centimeters, or nearly six-foot-three, rather than six-foot-four.
- Preserve Jin's dry, profane register in 조지기 딱 좋은 날씨다 and 나 왔다. 이 씨벌놈아.

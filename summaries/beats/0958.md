# Chapter 958

## Plot

Jamukha orders his Keshik to press the assault at Eight Spring Gorge. When a clan chieftain orders a retreat, the Keshik execute him and slaughter his followers; Temur realizes his own choice to survive has helped lead his people to their deaths. Three of Jamukha’s commanders are killed, and he orders the remaining Keshik to wear down the defenders while limiting further losses to his personal guard.

Jin Mukyung fights through the attackers and faces the Chinggen impostor, who throws aside the blood-soaked black-wood fishing rod and raises two swords.

## Continuity

- The battle at Eight Spring Gorge remains unresolved. Jin Mukyung is now fighting the Chinggen impostor; three of Jamukha’s commanders of a hundred have died, and Jamukha orders the Keshik still in the gorge to continue the assault.
- Temur admits to himself that he chose survival over loyalty and led his followers into danger. Jamukha has made clear that Temur’s safety depends on obedience.
- Jamukha’s forces have killed the chieftain and many followers of the Black Sheep Clan for disobeying his order and attempting to retreat.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Sword Demon” for 검귀 and “life-and-death duel” for 생사결.
- Render the impostor wearing Chinggen’s face as Chinggen when the scene uses his name, without implying the real Chinggen has returned.

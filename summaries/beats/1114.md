# Chapter 1114

## Plot

A flash of fire reveals Jeok Cheongang, who has returned from the North Gate to confront the Blood Lord. Jeok is exhausted and badly injured from his earlier battle; the Blood Lord exploits his concern for Taekyung and overpowers him.

Five Kunlun elders intervene, help Jeok, and send him toward the Inner City. They take Temporary Strength Pills by breaking their innate qi and hold off the Blood Lord for fifteen minutes, dying in the fight. The Blood Lord resumes his advance toward the Inner City.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang left the North Gate and confronted the Blood Lord; exhausted and badly injured, he was sent toward the Inner City by Taecheong Jin-in.
- The Kunlun Five Immortals used Temporary Strength Pills by breaking their innate qi to hold off the Blood Lord; all five died after buying fifteen minutes.
- The Blood Lord killed a wounded follower and resumed advancing toward the Inner City after the Kunlun elders fell.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 곤륜오선 as “Kunlun Five Immortals” and 태청진인 as “Perfected One Taecheong.”
- Retain “Xizang” for 西藏 as the Murim region and “Tibet” when Taekyung identifies it from his modern-world perspective.

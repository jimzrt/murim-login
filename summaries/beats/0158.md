# Chapter 158

## Plot

Seven days and nights of sparring with Cheongpung have passed, leaving Jin Taekyung with at least sixty consecutive losses and only three days remaining before New Year's Day, when he must complete his Quest by defeating Cheongpung. After circulating his qi, Taekyung observes that Hyuk Mujin has reached Level 50 and has suffered his ninetieth defeat to Cheongpung.

Taekyung challenges Cheongpung to fight seriously, spends fifty of his one hundred remaining stat points on Agility, and attacks at unprecedented speed. Cheongpung combines Dark Fragrance Drift with Five-Element Plum Blossom Steps, then draws his sword and uses Sword Energy and the Zaha Divine Technique. Their collision produces a powerful backlash, while Taekyung's spear begins emitting a Spear Cry, indicating that his internal energy and physique have reached the conditions associated with approaching the Peak realm without an actual breakthrough.

## Continuity

- Seven days and nights of continuous sparring have passed between Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung; Taekyung has lost at least sixty times, and three days remain before New Year's Day.
- Hyuk Mujin is now Level 50, up two Levels from the Level 48 recorded ten days earlier, and has lost ninety sparring matches to Cheongpung.
- Taekyung's Status Window shows Level 64, Fame 2,400 (+250), forty-five years of internal energy, and 100 remaining stat points; he assigns 50 points to Agility during the spar.
- Cheongpung regards Taekyung as an interesting opponent and reveals his competitive pride as a martial artist; he uses Sword Energy and the Zaha Divine Technique in their current exchange.
- Taekyung's spear emits a Spear Cry during the collision, while Cheongpung recognizes internal energy and a physique fulfilling the conditions associated with preparation for the Peak realm; Taekyung does not break through in this chapter.

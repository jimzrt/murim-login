# Chapter 432

## Plot

Magic Johnson learns that Jin knew the truth from the beginning and that Lee Jungryong and Go Jun were his enemies, then chooses to trust Jin as a friend and agrees to help protect Jin's people and support his growth. They clean the bloodied scene, reject the risky and illegal option of altering Go Jun's memories, and leave Go Jun to be carried away by three immobilized A-rank Ares Hunters after Jin threatens them and urges them to reconsider their allegiance. Jin plans to weaken Ares Guild's influence while strengthening Peace Guild, evades Magic Johnson's questions about the magic circle by changing the subject to a club outing with the Skeleton King, and senses that it is nearly time to return to Murim.

## Continuity

- Magic Johnson now knows the truth about Jin's confrontation with Lee Jungryong and Wu Heixing, accepts Jin as a friend, and agrees to help protect Jin's people and aid his growth.
- Jin refuses to attempt memory manipulation on Go Jun because failure or detectable traces would create severe legal consequences; Go Jun remains alive and is removed from the scene by three A-rank Ares Hunters.
- Jin has threatened the three A-rank Ares Hunters and planted the idea that they should choose a safer allegiance before Ares Guild's leadership vacuum worsens.
- Jin intends to let Peace Guild's growth and Ares Guild's loss of Lee Jungryong expose and deepen the latter's instability rather than openly begin a war.
- Jin believes the time to return to Murim is approaching.

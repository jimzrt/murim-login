# Chapter 658

## Plot

The System generates the Either-Or Quest, forcing Jin Taekyung to choose between resisting and surrendering while warning that his choice may change surrounding circumstances and kill someone. Pro-Baeksang chieftains demand his arrest, while a smaller faction supporting the Beast Miao King defends him. Knowing that escape with his companions is nearly impossible, Jin secures the Beast Miao King's promise to protect them and clear his name, then chooses to surrender rather than abandon them. Before submitting, however, he punches Baeksang and immediately declares, "I surrender!"

## Continuity

- The System's Either-Or Quest requires Jin to choose exactly one of surrender or resistance and warns that the choice may cause a person's death.
- The Tribal Grand Council's chieftains are divided between Baeksang's demand for Jin's arrest and the Beast Miao King's defense of Jin, while many chieftains remain silent or uncertain.
- Jin believes Baeksang's proposed detention and investigation are a carefully prepared trap that would treat him as guilty from the outset.
- Jin promises to surrender because escaping through the Nanman Beast Palace's net over heaven and earth with all of his companions would be nearly impossible.
- The Beast Miao King promises to protect Jin's subordinates and clear Jin's name regardless of what happens.
- Jin has declared surrender after punching Baeksang, leaving the immediate consequences of his chosen path unresolved.
- Heugung and Yohi remain missing after the attack on the Western Yao Estate, and Heugung's apparent death remains unconfirmed.
- The identity of the Supreme Peak attacker and Dark Heaven's motive remain unknown.
- Baeksang's alleged collusion with Dark Heaven remains unresolved.
- Yohi's nearly scentless pouch remains a possible clue.

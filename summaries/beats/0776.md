# Chapter 776

## Plot

At the World Hunter Federation's inaugural ceremony in the National Assembly Hall, surviving veterans and younger heroes gather around the round table and divide their support between Michael Silbert and Jin Taekyung. Michael grows uneasy that Jin may have recognized the truth behind his past, sends a signal through a pager, and regards the Skeleton King beside Jin as essential to maintaining his own position. When the ceremony announces that all attendees have arrived despite Cheon Taemin's absence, the gathering erupts in shock and confusion, and an unidentified attendee rises and walks toward the center of the table.

## Continuity

- The World Hunter Federation's inaugural ceremony is underway at the National Assembly Hall, with roughly three hundred old- and new-generation heroes forming factions around Michael Silbert and Jin Taekyung.
- Cheon Taemin does not attend the ceremony, and the attendees realize that humanity's savior may remain absent through the coming crisis.
- Michael fears that Jin Taekyung has genuinely recognized something previously sensed only by Cheon Taemin, rather than merely bluffing.
- Michael believes fear-backed power and rule are superior to leadership and intends to become the central representative of the Federation; he signals an unseen associate through a pager as a contingency.
- Michael experiences searing pain in his neck when Jin's heated gaze meets his, but Jin looks away before Michael can determine whether Jin noticed.
- Michael considers the Stone King beside Jin to be important to his own control of the gathering.
- An unidentified attendee rises and walks toward the center of the round table.

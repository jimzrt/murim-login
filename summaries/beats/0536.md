# Chapter 536

## Plot

Mae Jonghak formally recognizes Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung's actions against Dark Heaven as acts of humanity and chivalry, and the assembled leaders affirm them despite their youth. Tang Sadok publicly supports them, stating that the Sichuan Tang Clan owes them an unrepayable debt. Mae then offers them membership in the Alliance Leader's direct Two Dragons Pavilion; both accept, and he appoints them as its two pavilion masters with authority to select personnel, though the remaining details are drowned out by repeated System bells.

# Chapter 384

## Plot

At the Mount Qingcheng operations headquarters, Jin Taekyung meets Wei Penghu and Team Leader Choi after arriving by business jet. Wei assumes Taekyung is secretly an S-rank Hunter cultivated with extensive Korean government support, and Taekyung and Choi silently decide not to correct the misunderstanding. Wei then reveals that Xiao Yang, the leader of the People's Republic of China, is waiting in the underground bunker.

Xiao Yang introduces himself as China's state chairman, asks the assembled Hunters to save as many people as possible, and promises appropriate gratitude while prioritizing human life. He rejects Wei Penghu's request for full authority over the Central Military Commission and instead takes personal responsibility for every matter. The bunker also contains several international S-rank Hunters, including the War Mage Magic Johnson. A young Chinese Hunter insults Taekyung by calling him a peninsula bangzi, and Taekyung immediately answers with an equally offensive insult.

## Continuity

- Xiao Yang is the Chairman of the Central Military Commission of the Chinese Communist Party, the General Secretary, and the state chairman of the People's Republic of China.
- Xiao Yang personally asks the assembled Hunters to minimize casualties and stop the disaster, while retaining overall authority and responsibility instead of granting Wei Penghu full authority.
- Magic Johnson is an S-rank Hunter, one of the world's three Archmages, and a combat-specialized War Mage; he already knows Jin Taekyung's Sibeol-jwa nickname.
- Several additional international S-rank Hunters are gathered in the Mount Qingcheng bunker, including an unnamed Chinese man and woman and two unnamed Western men.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대마법사 as Archmage and 워 메이지 as War Mage.
- Render 국가 주석 as state chairman or Chairman of the People's Republic of China, and 총서기 as General Secretary.
- Preserve the 종석이 joke as Jongseok, a mistaken personal-name reading used in place of General Secretary.
- Render 반도의 빵즈 as peninsula bangzi and retain a footnote identifying bangzi as a derogatory Chinese slur for Koreans.
- Preserve Taekyung's self-mocking embarrassment, including 죽는다면 사인은 수치사 as death by humiliation.

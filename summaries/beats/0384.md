# Chapter 384

## Plot

Jin Taekyung arrives at Mount Qingcheng and is taken to an underground bunker, where he meets Shao Yang, Chairman of the People's Republic of China. Shao asks the assembled Hunters to save as many people as possible, retains full authority and responsibility for the crisis, and personally urges Jin to prioritize human life. Jin then meets several international S-rank Hunters, including the Archmage and War Mage Magic Johnson, before trading insults with a young Chinese Hunter who mocks him as Lord Fuck and an A-rank Hunter.

## Continuity

- Shao Yang is the Chairman of the People's Republic of China, Chairman of the Chinese Communist Party's Central Military Commission, and General Secretary.
- Shao Yang is a politically seasoned leader who publicly asks the assembled Hunters to prioritize human lives and personally accepts full authority and responsibility for the crisis.
- Wei Fenghu requested full authority over the Central Military Commission, but Shao Yang refused and chose to retain responsibility himself.
- International S-rank Hunters have gathered at the underground operations headquarters, including the Archmage and War Mage Magic Johnson.
- Magic Johnson knows Jin Taekyung as Lord Fuck because his youngest daughter knows the nickname.

- Use Shao Yang for 샤오 양 and Magic Johnson for 매직 존슨; preserve the distinction between Archmage and War Mage.
- Retain the deliberate awkwardness of Jin's 'Welcome' to the Chairman and the chapter's Lord Fuck, peninsula bangzi, and gukbap wordplay.

# Chapter 326

## Plot

Taekyung, Ju Hwaran, Wolhwa, and representatives of the Beggars' Sect and Lower District Sect reconcile the evidence and calculate Yongbong Escort Bureau's losses. Ju Hwaran boldly adds disputed damages until the total reaches four hundred thousand silver nyang, while Hyuk Sopyung apologizes on Zhongnan's behalf and promises to inform its Sect Leader and Elders.

Hwangbo Eom is forced to sign an agreement voiding all contracts between Zhongnan and the Yongbong Escort Bureau except the compensation payment, then leaves severely injured after questioning whether Taekyung has crossed the wall. As the Zhongnan party departs, Ju Hwaran draws her flexible sword and declares that only one final matter remains.

## Continuity

- Ju Hwaran has formally ended all contracts between the Yongbong Escort Bureau and Zhongnan except for Zhongnan's four-hundred-thousand-silver-nyang compensation payment.
- Hwangbo Eom survived Taekyung's defeat but was left severely injured, requires at least a year of recuperation, and signed the compensation agreement under duress.
- Ju Hwaran identifies thirty-three Yongbong Escort Bureau personnel who died during the current escort mission as members of her family and refuses to make further concessions.
- The Beggars' Sect and Lower District Sect records show that some suspected incidents were not caused by Zhongnan, but their consolidated evidence supports the bureau's remaining claim.
- Ju Hwaran has drawn her flexible sword against one final person connected to the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng substitution, but that person's identity has not yet been revealed.
- Hyuk Sopyung publicly apologized on Zhongnan's behalf and said he would immediately report the matter to the Sect Leader and Elders.

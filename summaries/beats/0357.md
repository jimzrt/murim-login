# Chapter 357

## Plot

Dark Heaven overwhelms the Sichuan Tang Clan. The Western Heaven Demon Lord leaves Tang Sadok alive on a hill to watch the clan's destruction, then enters the underground prison after finding his infiltrators dead and breaks through its walls.

Jin Taekyung confronts the Western Heaven Demon Lord, who intends to take Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Jeok Cheongang alive to the Blood Lord and seize the Myriad-Poison Ring. Taekyung evades the eighth-stage Ghost Prison Finger and launches Heavenly Strike, while Cheongpung recognizes the clash, manifests Sword Force, and challenges First Fiend.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has effectively won the assault on the Sichuan Tang Clan, with the Tang Clan's green-clad defenders largely dead.
- Tang Sadok remains alive but gravely wounded and captive on a hill overlooking the Tang Clan, forced to watch its destruction.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord has entered the underground prison after at least twenty infiltrators were killed there, and he is now fighting Jin Taekyung.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord intends to capture Jin Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Jeok Cheongang alive for the Blood Lord and take the Myriad-Poison Ring.
- Jin Taekyung can sense invisible qi after developing a new sensitivity while fighting Hwangbo Eom; he evaded the Western Heaven Demon Lord's eighth-stage Ghost Prison Finger.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord has reattached another person's arm to his body; after ten days it moves almost naturally, though the left arm remains a stated weakness.
- Cheongpung is alive and fighting First Fiend at the Tang Clan.
- Cheongpung has manifested Sword Force, establishing that he has reached the Supreme Peak realm.
- Cheongpung regards Jin Taekyung as his friend and only true martial rival.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 권강 as Fist Force.

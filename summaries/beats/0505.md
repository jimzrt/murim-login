# Chapter 505

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang reveals that he has achieved Returned to Youth, appearing as a tall, muscular, red-haired man. He confronts Zhuge Feng over the Demonic Cult's attack on Mount Jiuhua and demands that the Zhuge Clan perform at least as well in the approaching war as it did during the Great Faction War. Taekyung's group then departs for Henan and the New Murim Alliance.

At Wudang, Sect Leader Hyeoncheon learns that the group has left and receives a letter concerning the Murim Alliance, recognizing it as evidence of an approaching war comparable to the Great Faction War. A pursuit party reports capturing the Killing Ghost, but the remains they bring are revealed to be something neither beast nor human, giving Hyeoncheon another reason to travel to Henan.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang has entered a new realm and achieved Returned to Youth; he now appears as a tall, muscular, red-haired man while retaining his gruff and threatening personality.
- Jeok Cheongang has demanded that the Zhuge Clan support the approaching war at least as effectively as it did during the Great Faction War.
- Jin Taekyung's party, now accompanied by Jeok Cheongang, has departed for Henan and the New Murim Alliance.
- Hyeoncheon is the current Sect Leader of Wudang and Hyeongong's Senior Brother, seven years older than him.
- The Murim Alliance's formation is tied to an approaching war that Hyeoncheon considers comparable to the Great Faction War.
- A Wudang pursuit party claims to have captured the Killing Ghost, but the remains attributed to the Killing Ghost are neither beast nor human and their nature is unresolved.

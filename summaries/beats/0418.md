# Chapter 418

## Plot

Jin Taekyung kills Wu Heixing, then learns that Lee Jungryong recorded an edited hologram designed to make Wu's death appear to be Jin's deliberate murder and turn the public against him and those he protects.

Lee reveals that Wu was only a disposable tool, unleashes his full strength, and attacks Jin after declaring that no one will destroy the empire he built, not even Jin, Choi Minwoo, or his unnamed hyung.

Lee's swordsmanship and Force initially overwhelm Jin and wound him, but Jin recognizes and corrects his own arrogance, withstands the assault, and counters with the Fire Dragon Divine Spear before declaring that Lee will die there.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung killed Wu Heixing by breaking his neck; the System recorded Wu as Level 135, awarded substantial EXP, and warned that public knowledge of the killing would cause serious repercussions.
- Lee Jungryong possesses a holographic recording of Wu begging for his life and Jin killing him, and intends to exploit an edited version to provoke public condemnation of Jin and harm the Peace Guild, Jin's mother, and Hayeon.
- Lee regarded Wu Heixing as a disposable tool and deliberately preserved the recording as part of his plan.
- Lee Jungryong has revealed power and combat skill far beyond what he previously displayed and is now fighting Jin Taekyung directly.
- Lee Jungryong claims that his own strength built an empire no one can destroy, naming Jin, Choi Minwoo, and an unidentified person he calls hyung as potential threats.
- Jin Taekyung was cut by Lee's Force and had his internal energy disrupted, but remains capable of matching Lee and has resumed the offensive.
- Jin recognized that his assumption that modern S-rank Hunters could not efficiently manipulate qi was arrogant and that Lee's mastery of qi and martial technique is exceptional.
- Jin has killed Wu Heixing but still must survive and defeat Lee Jungryong while the city's Gate transformation remains active.
- The Quest One Who Returned from Death remains active, keeping Login unavailable until the Quest ends.
- The Skeleton Warlord continues to suffer unexplained dizziness and nausea while urging Jin to turn back.

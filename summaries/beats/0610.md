# Chapter 610

## Plot

The masked protagonist confronts Al Diab Jawahiri, the leader of Al-Qaeda, in an underground hideout. Al Diab attempts to justify terrorism through religious unity, but the protagonist rejects him, severs his wrist, and defeats the assassins protecting him with the Fire Dragon Armor, Force, and his short sword. The Skeleton King then arrives and prepares to turn the dead terrorists into undead soldiers, which the protagonist accepts because he considers them enemies rather than his own kind.

## Continuity

- Al Diab Jawahiri is the elderly leader of Al-Qaeda and a committed terrorist ideologue who claims Muslims will eventually unite beneath Allah's banner.
- Al Diab's underground hideout and its trained defenders have been defeated by the masked protagonist; Al Diab remains in the Skeleton King's custody after losing his wrist.
- The Fire Dragon Armor can repel ordinary aura-based attacks and weapons, while Force remains capable of harming opponents protected by lesser defenses.
- The Skeleton King can use the corpses of defeated terrorists as materials for his undead army, and the protagonist has accepted this disposition of the dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Al Diab Jawahiri and Al-Qaeda for 알 디아브 자와히리 and 알 카에다.
- Retain Fire Dragon Armor, Force, Finger Qi, and grappling technique for the established Murim terminology.
- Render 이이제이 as “Fight barbarians with barbarians” and preserve the Line 1 subway reference as a cultural joke.

# Chapter 640

## Plot

Jin Taekyung kills the injured Level 119 Thousand-Year Spider after the Beast Miao King brings it to him, and the resulting level-up substantially restores his battered body while leaving him in need of treatment and rest. Jin and the Beast Miao King proceed deeper into the Poisonblood Grounds, wary of Ailao Mountain’s Wraith, which still has not attacked them. They discover a vast moonlit swamp filled with countless objects wrapped in transparent spiderwebs and numerous pure-white eggs.

## Continuity

- The injured Level 119 Thousand-Year Spider was killed by Jin Taekyung, and its corpse was left behind.
- One Annihilation inflicted Internal Injury, Exhaustion, Anemia, and Paralyzed on Jin; the level-up removed those status abnormalities and substantially restored his damaged body, but he still needs treatment and rest.
- Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King are moving deeper into the Poisonblood Grounds together.
- Ailao Mountain’s Wraith remains inside the Poisonblood Grounds and is considered the primary threat, but it did not join the previous battles.
- Jin feels guilty about failing to prevent the massacre of the Nanman warriors and suspects that this burden may also be a Heart Demon.
- Jin Taekyung and the Beast Miao King have found a vast moonlit swamp containing many transparent web-wrapped objects and pure-white eggs.

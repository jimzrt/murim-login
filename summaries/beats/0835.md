# Chapter 835

## Plot

On the swift ship bound for Sichuan, Sudal struggles to keep Taishan supplied with food and grows exasperated with his unruly passengers. He meets Namho, whose swollen forehead came from a Black Gold Bee sting—not Iron Head Technique—and whose grand claims about his wartime exploits turn out to be boasts about sending messages from the rear. Jin Taekyung emerges after five days of rest, and the ship reaches Sichuan that night; Sudal decides to retire.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has rested for five days and feels better when he emerges; the swift ship reaches Sichuan that night.
- Sudal decides to retire upon reaching Sichuan.
- Namho's swollen, blackened forehead is from a Black Gold Bee sting; he has not learned Iron Head Technique.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 철두공 as “Iron Head Technique” and 흑금봉 as “Black Gold Bee.” Namho's boasts about his wartime exploits are comic exaggerations, not evidence that he is a martial artist.

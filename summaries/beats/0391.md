# Chapter 391

## Plot

Zhang Wei, commander of the 2nd Company of the Sichuan Province Public Security Armed Forces, was uneasy when Senior Colonel Wang ordered his unit to bypass the front, advance with the army, and later split up to search for survivors. Wang claimed the operation came directly from Senior General Liao and threatened Zhang with disciplinary action if he refused.

The devastated town near Suining City seemed to contain only scattered monster stragglers until an invisible barrage destroyed the senior officer, dozens of soldiers, the armored vehicles and tanks, and the three combat helicopters. Ten Death Knights then appeared and began slaughtering the surviving forces, including Zhang Wei.

A black knight arrived and ordered the Death Knights to kill everyone. However, when the black knight found a family and their child hiding beneath a collapsed building, it could not bring itself to attack them. After hearing the child cry, it abruptly ordered the Death Knights to withdraw and led them away, leaving the three humans alive.

## Continuity

- Zhang Wei commanded the 2nd Company of the Sichuan Province Public Security Armed Forces and was trusted by his subordinates; he died during the Death Knight assault near Suining City.
- Senior Colonel Wang holds the same rank as Shao Shen, is awaiting promotion to major general, and is regarded by a subordinate as connected to the Princelings and as Senior General Liao's lapdog; he led the diverted operation under Liao's command.
- Ten Death Knights appeared in the ruined town and obeyed a superior black knight who could project black energy and speak the demon-realm language.
- The black knight's order was to annihilate the humans, but it was unable to kill a hidden family and child and instead ordered an immediate retreat; the reason remains unknown.

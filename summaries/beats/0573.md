# Chapter 573

## Plot

A normal Grade C Gate in Busan rapidly transforms into a Mutated Gate and then a Monster Wave, unleashing Mermen and a Siren on civilians. The Siren's song paralyzes the crowd and Hunters until the Skeleton King kills her and devastates the Mermen with a rain of bones, though he cannot raise an undead army openly because of his restriction.

Taekyung continues fighting the Kraken. After cutting its tentacles down from nearly thirty to eight, he dodges an incoming attack, steps through the air, and drives the Third Form of Fire Dragon Divine Spear, Takoyaki, toward the Kraken's eye.

## Continuity

- The Busan Monster Wave rapidly escalated from a normal Grade C Gate into a Mutated Gate and then a Monster Wave, producing Mermen and a Siren amid a civilian disaster.
- The Siren's song can enthrall civilians and suppress Hunters, while the Skeleton King's bone barrage targets monsters without harming the humans in the intersection.
- The Skeleton King cannot raise an undead army publicly because of an imposed restriction; he instead fights with his bone-based power and faces more than five hundred additional Mermen.
- Taekyung's unresolved battle with the Kraken has reduced the creature's nearly thirty tentacles to eight, and Taekyung is now attacking its eye with Fire Dragon Divine Spear's Third Form, Takoyaki.
- The Kraken is an intelligent sea monster transformed by absorbing magic power from a gift supplied by Dongseok on behalf of that person.
- Dongseok's objective and the identity of that person remain unknown.
- The worldwide Gate and monster crisis may mark the beginning of a second Great Cataclysm, with rising magic power strengthening monsters and worsening Hunter shortages.
- Team Leader Choi is prioritizing Gate defenses while working with Song Cheonwoo against Go Jun's control of Ares Guild.
- Taekyung is a Supreme Peak master publicly recognized as S-rank-level while retaining an A-rank license, leads the Fire Dragon Pavilion's first Nanman mission, and patrons Peace Guild.
- The six-member Fire Dragon Pavilion mission is entering Nanman through the Journey to Nanman Quest, whose reward is a linked quest and whose failure penalty is Can't Go to Nanman.
- Mungyeong assigned Taekyung the final task of incorporating martial principles into his learned martial arts, while Cheongpung accompanies Mungyeong.
- Dark Heaven remains a monster-like threat capable of causing rifts and creating mutants, while the Mount Song Resolution restored the Murim Alliance and Go Jun continues leveraging Song Cheonwoo's children.

# Chapter 382

## Plot

Jin Taekyung captures the three incomplete Liches commanding the undead army and interrogates them about the Arch Lich's week-long attack. After confirming their testimony through the Skeleton Warlord's oath on the River of Death, Jin rejects their attempted loyalty and destroys them, while the Skeleton Warlord devours their death energy and grows far stronger. Jin completes the Unexpected Attack Quest, gains the Undead Hunter Title, EXP, Fame, and one level, then meets Team Leader Choi, Shao Shen, and Wei Fenghu, who says that everyone is waiting for Jin at the operations headquarters.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung destroyed the three incomplete Liches who commanded the undead army, causing the monster army to collapse.
- The three incomplete Liches identified themselves as servants of an Arch Lich that first appeared one week earlier and swore that their account was true on the River of Death.
- The Skeleton Warlord absorbed the three Liches' death energy, became substantially stronger, and remains stored in Jin Taekyung's Inventory under the mocking nickname Bones.
- Jin Taekyung completed the Unexpected Attack Quest, acquired the Undead Hunter Title, gained EXP and Fame, and reached Level 121 after one level-up.
- Wei Fenghu is the Minister of National Defense under China's Central Military Commission and a four-star general.
- Wei Fenghu is taking Jin Taekyung to the operations headquarters, where a jet is waiting and unidentified people are expecting him.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the Skeleton Warlord's bickering pet dynamic; render 골골 as Bones and retain Warlordmon when it uses its former name.
- Keep Jin Taekyung's casual, dry, and profane voice, including the deliberate Lord Fuck gag when Shao Shen nearly says 시벌좌.
- Render 웨이펑후 as Wei Fenghu and his title as Minister of National Defense under the Central Military Commission.
- Render 사기 as death energy and 죽음의 강 as River of Death.
- Retain Arch Lich, Undead Hunter, and Bayern as established terminology.

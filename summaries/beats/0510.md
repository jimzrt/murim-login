# Chapter 510

## Plot

Mu Song, a lifelong sailor who prefers water to land, becomes miserable after Jeok Cheongang forces the travelers to split between two swift ships. He orders his subordinates to stay away from the stern and keep Mungyeong's identity secret, but Mungyeong catches him insulting him and relays Jeok's command that Mu Song personally handle all necessary ship operations and meal deliveries. Mungyeong also orders only two meal portions until Henan because Taekyung is expected to have no appetite.

Taekyung's internal energy is nearly depleted while he practices on the Yangtze, but instead of sinking into panic he notices a large, stealthily approaching body whose fin looks familiar: a shark appears to be swimming in the Yangtze.

## Continuity

- Mu Song and the Water Dragon Stronghold subordinates who know Mungyeong's power are forbidden to discuss Mungyeong's identity.
- Mungyeong is enforcing Jeok Cheongang's orders aboard Mu Song's ship, including keeping Mu Song away from the stern except when necessary and making him deliver meals personally.
- Taekyung's Rising on Duckweed, Crossing Water practice has nearly depleted his internal energy, and a shark-like creature is approaching him in the Yangtze.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 수적 as river bandit, 부채주 as deputy Stronghold Lord, 후미 as stern, and retain dip-and-taste punishment for 찍먹형.
- Preserve Mu Song's profane, exasperated voice against Mungyeong's deceptively cheerful and threatening politeness.

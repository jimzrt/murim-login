# Chapter 459

## Plot

Honglan regains consciousness and confirms that she is a First Rate martial artist. Ju Wongong survived the attack but remains unconscious under guard, and Taekyung concludes that Dark Heaven was not specifically targeting him.

Honglan recounts that a single unseen attacker used Force to sink the escort ships and pleasure boat, then escaped through Dongting Lake. She identifies the attacker as a water-arts master capable of matching the Seafaring King, leading Taekyung to identify the culprit as the Dongting Fisherman, who has hidden refuges throughout the lake.

## Continuity

- Honglan is the sole surviving eyewitness to the Dongting Lake attack; she saw one attacker use Force and knows several possible locations of the Dongting Fisherman's hidden refuges.
- Ju Wongong survived the Dongting Lake attack and has passed the most dangerous stage of his injuries, but remains unconscious under heavy guard.
- The Dongting Fisherman is a previous-generation Supreme Peak water-arts master comparable to the Seafaring King, remains in Hubei Province, and is identified as Dark Heaven's tail and the suspected perpetrator of the Dongting Lake attack.
- Taekyung plans to follow Honglan immediately to search the Dongting Fisherman's hidden refuges and uncover Dark Heaven's movements and plans.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 水功 as water arts and preserve the distinction between Force and other martial techniques.
- Preserve Honglan's humble, formally respectful voice and her address of Taekyung as Benefactor.
- Preserve Taekyung's dry, profane humor and the established rendering of the Dongting Fisherman as Dark Heaven's tail.

# Chapter 429

## Plot

After the press conference, Jin Taekyung is escorted by Xiao Shen to a hotel suite, where Magic Johnson reveals that he has given the Skeleton King a nearly perfect human body by carving magic circles into the bones of the Named Monster. Jin can perceive the texture of qi after opening his Middle Dantian and cuts through Johnson's concealment magic with Force.

The Skeleton King adopts the name Stone-King, practices Korean and English learned from the Internet, and immediately clashes with Jin over his appearance and behavior. Their fight destroys the suite's desk and scatters Johnson's papers, revealing a pattern Jin recognizes from Sichuan.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung can perceive the texture of qi and sever layered magic with Force after opening his Middle Dantian.
- Magic Johnson has created a near-perfect human appearance for the Skeleton King using magic circles carved into the Skeleton King's bones.
- The Skeleton King can now appear human outside his Inventory and is attempting to establish a human-world identity as Stone-King.
- Jin recognized a pattern on Magic Johnson's scattered papers as the same pattern he previously saw in Sichuan.
- Chairman Shao is preparing a political reckoning against the Crown Prince Party after its persecution and forced labor campaign.
- Jin's mother and Hayeon remain in China under Chairman Shao's protection.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 샤오 쉔 as “Xiao Shen” and preserve his deferential address to Jin as “hyung.”
- Render 매직 존슨 as “Magic Johnson,” 스켈레톤 킹 as “Skeleton King,” and 스톤-킹 as “Stone-King.”
- Preserve the dry, profane rivalry between Jin and the Skeleton King, including the Skeleton King's grandiose insults and Internet slang.

# Chapter 342

## Plot

The Divine Physician explains that Jeok Cheongang’s unconsciousness results from a broken bodily balance, especially between Yin and Yang, caused by uncontrolled Scorching Yang Qi. Treatment requires an elixir containing at least one jiazi of energy and possessing Yin-Cold Qi, obtained within four months. Jin Taekyung provides a Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng given to him by Ju Hwaran under the guise of Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng, satisfying the core requirement and completing the related Quest and achievement.

The Divine Physician leads Taekyung into Cold-Ice Land within the Mystic Gate Formation to gather medicinal herbs needed for treatment, while Mungyeong remains behind to care for the patients. A new nonrefusable Chain Quest requires one hundred roots each of twenty-four kinds of herbs.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang’s treatment requires a Yin-Cold elixir containing at least one jiazi of energy within four months; the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng supplied by Ju Hwaran satisfies the core elixir requirement.
- Dong Feng has taken Jin Taekyung into Cold-Ice Land to gather one hundred roots each of twenty-four medicinal herb varieties, while Mungyeong cares for the patients at the clinic.
- Ju Hwaran secretly gave Taekyung the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng while calling it Hundred-Year-Old Snow Ginseng, and Taekyung plans to stop in Shaanxi to thank her.

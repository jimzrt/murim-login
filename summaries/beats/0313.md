# Chapter 313

## Plot

Baek Museong is staying at Xi'an Tower after completing a year of secluded training that deepened his strength and enlightenment. Five days earlier, Heavenly Sword True Person's messenger eagle brought news of Shaolin's blood calamity, Hong Dao's death, and Mae Jonghak's possible appointment as Alliance Leader of the revived Murim Alliance. Baek foresees a bloody storm over the Murim and resolves that he and Huashan must prevent it. Meanwhile, the drunk Zhongnan One Dragon, Hyuk Sopyung, defeats six First Rate guards and destroys the private annex while demanding better treatment. Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Gung Gibang reach Xi'an after nearly seven days of forced travel, and the guards admit them when Taekyung names Baek. They find Baek waiting in the devastated annex with his sword drawn.

## Continuity

- Baek Museong has completed a year of secluded training and gained enlightenment that made him stronger and more profound.
- Heavenly Sword True Person's five-day-old message reports Shaolin's blood calamity, Hong Dao's death, and Mae Jonghak's consideration as Alliance Leader of the revived Murim Alliance.
- Baek Museong believes a bloody storm will soon engulf the Murim and intends for himself and Huashan to prevent it.
- Hyuk Sopyung, known as the Zhongnan One Dragon, is a Peak master who defeated six First Rate Xi'an Tower guards while drunk and destroyed the private annex.
- Baek Museong and Hyuk Sopyung know each other from several prior encounters, and Baek says their elders' connection has been passed down to them.
- Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Gung Gibang have reached Xi'an while traveling toward Sichuan to find the Divine Physician.

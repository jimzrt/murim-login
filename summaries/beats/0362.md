# Chapter 362

## Plot

Jin Taekyung resumes his battle with the Western Heaven Demon Lord after recovering from his previous injuries and receiving three jiazi of internal energy from the Heavenly Power Demon. He uses retained Stat Points to increase his Strength and Agility, allowing him to withstand and physically pressure the Western Heaven Demon Lord, though the Demon Lord remains vastly superior in martial skill.

Jin notices the Myriad-Poison Ring on the Western Heaven Demon Lord's hand and steals it through the Inventory before storing it again. Enraged, the Western Heaven Demon Lord launches his Sword Force toward the treatment room instead of Jin, and Jin rushes to intercept the attack; the result is unresolved.

## Continuity

- The Myriad-Poison Ring is now in Jin Taekyung's Inventory after he took it from the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord is enraged by losing the Myriad-Poison Ring and has redirected his attack toward the treatment room.
- Jin Taekyung can now contend physically with the Western Heaven Demon Lord because of the Heavenly Power Demon's transferred internal energy and Jin's increased Strength and Agility, but the Demon Lord's martial skill remains far superior.
- Jin Taekyung is attempting to protect the treatment room containing the unconscious Jeok Cheongang; the outcome is unresolved.
- The Divine Physician remains alive but severely injured after resisting the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- The underground prison remains in the process of collapsing.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord's claim that the Tang Clan, Qingcheng, and Emei were destroyed remains unconfirmed.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord serves the unidentified Lord of Heaven as one of four loyal servants.
- Cheongpung remains engaged in his confrontation with First Fiend.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 법화 as Lotus Sutra and retain a footnote explaining the Buddhist scripture.
- Render 이어타정 as carp-leaping maneuver and 지풍 as Finger Qi.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's vulgar, self-mocking voice and the crude wordplay in his battle dialogue.

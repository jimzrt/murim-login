# Chapter 844

## Plot

The Blood Lord inspects Dark Heaven’s experiments on Peak masters and rejects the results as too weak and short-lived, though he acknowledges that the sorcerers’ research is progressing. He orders them to preserve their remaining strength and prepare selected people as seeds to be buried and spread when the time comes.

The Blood Lord recalls how Jin Taekyung obstructed him and how both Jin and Cheongpung became far stronger than he expected. He resolves not to underestimate Jin again and to gather greater strength for the Lord of Heaven’s great cause. The Lord of Heaven recently ordered him to bring down the heavens; the Blood Lord sends out missives carried by dozens of black-feathered hawks.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has spent years and substantial resources developing experimental seeds; some were scattered across the Central Plains, and some have already blossomed.
- The experiments consume Peak masters and attempt to increase the potency of two effects, but the current results remain weak and their duration has not improved.
- The Blood Lord orders sorcerers to select suitable people as seeds, bury them deeply, and prepare to spread them later; he sends out missives by hawk.
- The Blood Lord knows Jin Taekyung obstructed him during the confrontation in which Mae Jonghak’s Force severed his arms; his arms have since regenerated stronger.
- The Lord of Heaven awoke from a long slumber ten days earlier, ordered the Blood Lord to bring down the heavens, and then fell asleep again.
- The Blood Lord regards the deaths of the Western Heaven Demon Lord and Southern Heaven Demon Empress as losses to Dark Heaven and resolves to gather more strength for the Lord of Heaven’s cause.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “seed” as the term for Dark Heaven’s experimental agents or material; the chapter does not specify their precise nature.
- Render 천하를 뒤틀다 as “the world is twisting” and 하늘을 무너트려라 as “bring down the heavens.”
- Keep Force distinct from martial arts and magical power.

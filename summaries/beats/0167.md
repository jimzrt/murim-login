# Chapter 167

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung visit Jang Taebo at night, but the retired smith angrily rejects Taekyung's request to forge the Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron and orders them never to return. Jang Taebo says the current Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild learned smithing by watching him and could handle the material.

After spending the night at a shabby village inn, Taekyung learns that Jang Taebo rarely leaves his home, has money saved from his successful career, and receives a fifty-year-old He Shou Wu through the Nine-Room Escort Bureau every four months. Taekyung offers Jang Taebo a thirty-year-old He Shou Wu, but Jang spits it out because it is bitter and reveals his superior specimen instead.

Jang Taebo says he will consider accepting the commission only if Taekyung brings him the Herb of Eternal Youth. The System creates the In Search of the Herb of Eternal Youth Quest, which Taekyung rejects by shouting, 'No!'

## Continuity

- Jang Taebo refuses Taekyung's weapon commission and says he will only consider it if Taekyung brings him the Herb of Eternal Youth.
- Jang Taebo receives a fifty-year-old He Shou Wu through the Nine-Room Escort Bureau every four months; the bureau leader owes him a favor.
- Taekyung rejects the System Quest In Search of the Herb of Eternal Youth.

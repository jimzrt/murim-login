# Chapter 995

## Plot

After speaking privately with Jin Wikyung, Taekyung gathers the Fire Dragon Pavilion and reveals that unprecedented activity is underway in Xinjiang, now Dark Heaven’s stronghold. The Hidden Shadow Pavilion lost more than thirty elite agents, and their last messenger eagle carried the final contact; the enemy may number more than a hundred thousand and is said to be far more powerful than the Demonic Cult’s forces of fifty years ago.

Taekyung recalls Jeok Cheongang’s account that the Demonic Cult arose around the same time as Shaolin, then decides that the group will head west. A new Quest alert sounds.

## Continuity

- Taekyung and the Fire Dragon Pavilion are heading west toward Xinjiang in response to unprecedented Dark Heaven activity.
- More than thirty Hidden Shadow Pavilion agents are reported dead, and contact ended after their first and last messenger eagle; enemy numbers may exceed a hundred thousand.
- Taekyung judges Dark Heaven’s followers to be far stronger and more dangerous than the Demonic Cult’s forces of fifty years ago.
- Taekyung’s decision to head west triggers a new Quest alert; its objective is not established in this chapter.
- Taekyung still cannot Logout and wonders whether the restriction is connected to the events in Xinjiang, but has no confirmation.

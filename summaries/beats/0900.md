# Chapter 900

## Plot

The Emperor arrives at the Grand Banquet Hall after years of seclusion, looking aged but watching the assembled officials with a sharp gaze. Baek Yeon announces him, and Ma Sanbao leads the hall in a cheer of welcome.

During the banquet, the Emperor approaches Jin Taekyung and asks what should be done with two officials. Taekyung asks permission to drink first, then tells the Emperor to kill them or spare them as he pleases.

## Continuity

- The imperial birthday banquet is underway, and the Emperor has arrived in person after years in Qianqing Palace.
- The Emperor publicly addresses Jin Taekyung and asks him to decide the fate of two officials; Taekyung drinks and answers that the Emperor should choose whether to kill or spare them.
- Ma Sanbao is present at the banquet, and the Emperor regards him as a major political adversary.

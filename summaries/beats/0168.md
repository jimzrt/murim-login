# Chapter 168

## Plot

Jin Taekyung rejects the impossible Quest to find the Herb of Eternal Youth and refuses Jang Taebo's additional demands for gongcheong seokyu, a dragon's claw, and a dragon pearl. After Jang threatens to return to the Ironcraft Guild and pressure the Nine Sects and One Gang against the Jin Family, Taekyung admits defeat and promises to keep Jang's residence secret. Jang then offers to recommend his disciple for Taekyung's spear commission, but after seeing Taekyung's enormous supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, he becomes enthusiastic and agrees to personally forge the weapon, completing the Find the Master Artisan Quest and triggering a linked Quest.

## Continuity

- Taekyung rejects the In Search of the Herb of Eternal Youth Quest.
- Jang Taebo demands gongcheong seokyu, a dragon's claw, and a dragon pearl as further conditions for helping with Taekyung's weapon.
- Taekyung promises Jang Taebo that he will not reveal Jang's residence and will explain the matter to Jin Wikyung.
- After seeing Taekyung's massive supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, Jang Taebo agrees to personally forge Taekyung's spear.
- The Find the Master Artisan Quest is completed and a linked Quest is generated.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 천검진인 as “Heavenly Sword True Person.”
- Render 여의주 as “dragon pearl,” 용의 발톱 as “dragon's claw,” and 신병이기 as “divine weapon.”
- Render 공청석유 as “gongcheong seokyu” and the generated Quest 공청석유, 용의 발톱, 여의주 구하기 as “Get Gongcheong Seokyu, a Dragon’s Claw, and a Dragon Pearl.”

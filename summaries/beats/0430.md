# Chapter 430

## Plot

Jin discovers that the magic-circle fragments Magic Johnson received from the Arch Lich's base contain the same patterns and symbols as the Moving Formation he saw in Murim. The Skeleton King infers that the circle absorbed life force to accumulate mana, but cannot decipher its symbols. Jin visits the inactive circle at the ruined city, senses only the lingering traces of countless deaths, and receives no answer from the System. As Jin reflects on the connection between the two worlds and the capsule that began his journey, Go Jun appears in the ruins and confronts him.

## Continuity

- The magic-circle fragments from the Arch Lich's base can be assembled into a massive circle whose patterns and symbols match the Moving Formation seen in Murim, despite differences in arrangement and orientation.
- The Skeleton King's identification of the circle as a life-force absorption magic circle is an uncertain inference based on the scent of countless deaths at the site, not an understanding of its symbols.
- Jin personally examines the inactive circle at the ruined city but cannot decipher it; the System remains silent.
- Jin now recognizes an unresolved connection among the Murim formation, the Arch Lich's magic circle, the phenomena seen during battles with the Blood Lord and Western Heaven Demon Lord, and the junk capsule.
- Go Jun, Lee Jungryong's Disciple and Head of Security, arrives in the ruins and addresses Jin with an unusually cold demeanor.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이동진 as “Moving Formation.”
- Render 아크 리치 as “Arch Lich,” 매직 존슨 as “Magic Johnson,” and 스켈레톤 킹 as “Skeleton King.”
- Render 스톤-킹 as “Stone-King” and preserve the Skeleton King's grandiose, Internet-influenced insults.
- Preserve Jin's dry, profane voice and polite but familiar address toward Magic Johnson.

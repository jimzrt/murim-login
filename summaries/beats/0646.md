# Chapter 646

## Plot

Jin Taekyung explains the tribal grand council to the Fire Dragon Pavilion. Ju Hwaran and Song Ilseom provide no certain identification for the Blood Monk, while Namho says the Blood Monk may be backed by Dark Heaven. The group discusses whether Baeksang's hostility toward the Central Plains comes from grief over losing his only child or from a grudge against the orthodox Murim, with the possibility of a dangerous alliance with Dark Heaven. Jin plans to send scouts and Fire Dragon Pavilion members toward Guizhou, but Ju Hwaran volunteers to accompany them. After initially refusing, Jin accepts her request.

## Continuity

- The Blood Monk is an unidentified bald, beardless, apparently middle-aged martial artist using a steel Zen staff who has killed several hundred people in Guizhou.
- Namho considers it highly likely that Dark Heaven is behind the Blood Monk, though this remains unconfirmed.
- Nanman will send selected scouts and warriors toward Guizhou, and Jin Taekyung will include some Fire Dragon Pavilion members in the mission.
- Ju Hwaran volunteered for the Guizhou scouting mission, and Jin Taekyung accepted her participation despite his concern for her safety.
- Baeksang's opposition to the Central Plains may be driven by grief over losing his only child or by a grudge against the orthodox Murim; his possible alliance with Dark Heaven remains unresolved.
- The Nanman trade routes were closed after opposition from several tribes led by the Bai people.
- The hidden entity that recognizes Jin Taekyung, the missing ferocious beasts, Ailao Mountain's Wraith, and the pure-white eggs remain unexplained.

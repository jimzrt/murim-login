# Chapter 875

## Plot

After Prince Shangshan is escorted away, So Gyo leads Jin Taekyung through Qianqing Palace’s guarded, maze-like corridors. Taekyung learns the palace uses mechanisms and formations, then hears a woman’s angry voice from a restricted passage and recognizes it as Aehyang, the concubine the City Lord of Sichuan Province lost to the Emperor. When Taekyung tries to investigate, So Gyo and palace assassins stop him; he surrenders and continues toward the exit.

## Continuity

- Prince Shangshan remains in the imperial capital under the care of palace attendants led by So Gyo; Taekyung gave him the Myriad-Poison Ring for protection against poisoning.
- Taekyung believes the Emperor and Dark Heaven are connected, but their relationship remains unconfirmed.
- Qianqing Palace’s defenses include mechanisms and formations, at least three Supreme Peak masters, and more than a hundred elite assassins; So Gyo escorts Taekyung out and can summon palace assassins.
- Taekyung hears Aehyang’s voice in a restricted part of Qianqing Palace and recognizes her as the concubine the City Lord of Sichuan Province lost to the Emperor; her situation there is unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 기관진식 as “mechanisms and formations.”
- Retain “Third Shadow,” “First Shadow,” “No Shadow,” and “Marquis Within the Passes.”

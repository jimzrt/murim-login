# Chapter 134

## Plot

Jin Taekyung publicly slaps Woo Jintae repeatedly after Jintae refuses to apologize for insulting him and Cheongpung. Taekyung invents the Dodong Sect and the identity Tien Shinhan when Jintae demands to know his affiliation, then overwhelms Jintae with a manic barrage of slaps while mocking his threats and declaring his love for Song-i.

The other young heirs of the Five Gates of Shanxi refuse to intervene despite Woo Jintae's desperate pleas. Hyuk Mujin warns them that Taekyung will attack them next, persuades them to apologize to Cheongpung, and then orders them to plant their heads on the floor as the more important second step.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung publicly humiliated Woo Jintae with a prolonged barrage of slaps after Jintae refused to apologize; Taekyung claimed the fabricated Dodong Sect identity Tien Shinhan.
- The other four current Five Gates of Shanxi scions would not fight Taekyung, apologized to Cheongpung after Hyuk Mujin's warning, and were then ordered by Mujin to prostrate themselves.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 도동파 as Dodong Sect and 천진반 as Tien Shinhan; both are Taekyung's fabricated identity and affiliation.
- Render 대가리 박으십쇼 as “bend over and plant your heads on the floor,” preserving Mujin's blunt, comic coercion.
- Keep Taekyung's slap-battle dialogue manic and teasing, including the Song-i punchline.

# Chapter 848

## Plot

Jin Taekyung wakes after Jeok Cheongang treated him with Pushing the Meridians and Passing Through the Acupoints. His Scorching Yang Qi and physical condition have improved, but pain in his lower dantian confirms that his injuries are not fully healed. Jeok Cheongang and the Divine Physician have already left, telling Hyuk Mujin to bring Jin after he wakes; their letter reports that the City Lord of Sichuan Province is dead.

The Divine Physician examines the City Lord’s corpse and rejects the local physician’s conclusion of sudden death. He finds a small, writhing organism on a needle drawn from an acupoint and identifies it as gu poison, leaving the cause and circumstances of the death unresolved.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang performed Pushing the Meridians and Passing Through the Acupoints on Jin while he was unconscious; Jin’s condition improved, but his lower-dantian pain and incomplete recovery remain unresolved.
- The City Lord of Sichuan Province is dead. The Divine Physician found gu poison in his body and is investigating the cause of death.

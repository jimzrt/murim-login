# Chapter 949

## Plot

Jin Wikyung weighs evacuating Shanxi and retreating to Henan rather than risk the lives entrusted to him, as a grassland force expected to reach thirty thousand approaches and he fears the undisclosed strength of Dark Heaven. Wipeng reminds him that Shanxi has grown stronger; Lee Seowol arrives with three hundred Mount Heng martial artists and other allied leaders, and Taekyung’s younger brother emerges from seclusion. Wikyung walks toward the gathered allies and the coming fight.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung has ordered the evacuation of people around Datong and Saneum and considered withdrawing to Henan, but after allied leaders gather at his hall, he walks toward them rather than immediately carrying out that retreat.
- Jin Wikyung believes Dark Heaven is behind the grassland mobilization and fears the enemy’s still-hidden strength; the approaching force is expected to number thirty thousand by the time it reaches Shanxi.
- Lee Seowol brings three hundred Mount Heng Sword Sect martial artists to fight the foreign enemy; allied sect and family leaders who pledged themselves to the Jin Family also gather.
- Taekyung’s younger brother emerges from a long seclusion, but this chapter does not state his name.

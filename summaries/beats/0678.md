# Chapter 678

## Plot

Jin Taekyung confronts two Supreme Peak masters in the Poisonblood Grounds. The disheveled master identifies himself as the Black Hand Fist Demon, confirms that he carried out the Western Yao Estate killings, and reveals that Yohi and Heugung are being held until the Southern Heaven Demon Empress returns. The slender master warns that the Demon Empress considers Jin a future obstacle. Jin awakens the fire dragon within his dantian and prepares to fight both enemies as the slender master launches twin wheels. Meanwhile, Namho and the remaining companions catch up with the Nanman reconnaissance squad advancing toward the northern border.

## Continuity

- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is absent from the Poisonblood Grounds for now.
- Black Hand confirms that Yohi and Heugung are alive but captive and claims responsibility for the Western Yao Estate killings.
- Black Hand and an unnamed slender Supreme Peak master are cooperating against Jin Taekyung.
- The slender master knows Jin's identity and regards him as a major future obstacle, while Jin knows nothing about the man's identity.
- Jin has committed to fighting rather than retreating, despite believing the two-master battle may be extremely difficult.
- Muyaho remains beside Jin and refuses to retreat, though Jin fears he may be unable to protect the White Tiger.
- Jin awakens the fire dragon in his lower dantian and coats White Flame in blue-white fire as the battle begins.
- Namho and three surviving companions catch up with the Nanman reconnaissance squad near the northern border.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 흑수권마 as Black Hand Fist Demon while retaining Black Hand as 흑수's established name.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's deliberate shifts between formal, archaic, and profane casual speech as part of his provocation.
- Render 쌍륜 as twin wheels for the slender master's paired weapons.

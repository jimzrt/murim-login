# Chapter 392

## Plot

Jin arrives at a massacred city after the target of the Unexpected Assault disappears, causing the Quest to be canceled and reducing his Strength by 10. Shao Shen tries to pursue revenge against the attackers and General Liao, but Jin incapacitates and puts him to sleep before his subordinates agree to wait for a better time. When General Liao reveals that the failed operation was not supposed to fail, Jin breaks his arms and crushes his kneecaps. Elsewhere, the weakened Arch Lich orders the black knight to withdraw the undead legions and lure the human army inward, reaffirming that the Arch Lich serves a separate true king.

## Continuity

- The Sudden Quest Unexpected Assault failed because its target completed the objective and disappeared; Jin lost 10 Strength as the failure penalty.
- Zhang Wei's force and many of Shao Shen's Hunters were massacred, while Jin and the Public Security Armed Forces arrived too late to pursue the attackers.
- Shao Shen survived, but Jin struck his Paralysis, Mute, and Sleep Acupoints to prevent him from killing General Liao; Shao Shen's surviving subordinates accepted that revenge must wait.
- General Liao's order caused the deaths of approximately one hundred Hunters and hundreds of soldiers; after revealing that the operation was meant to succeed, he was severely injured by Jin.
- The Arch Lich is still weaker than its former self and ordered the black knight to pull back every undead legion within its range and draw the human army into the battlefield.
- The Arch Lich serves a true king whose identity remains unknown.

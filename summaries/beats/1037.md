# Chapter 1037

## Plot

Sama Pyo realizes he can dodge a powerful enemy’s Sword Energy only by exposing an ally to it, so he stays and is nearly killed. A young Black Dragon Demon Gate martial artist he had saved earlier intervenes and dies saving him. Sima Gong arrives with the Gate’s elite, and Sama Pyo confronts him for delaying his arrival and choosing a different path from his father’s. Sima Gong recognizes the dead young man as one of two people he had ordered placed in the front line, where both died. Elsewhere, Jin Taekyung cuts through the enemy ranks with controlled weapons and launches White Flame at the Blood-Sword Demon Lord as he charges Jeok Cheongang; the result is unresolved.

## Continuity

- Sama Pyo risks his life rather than dodge an enemy attack into an ally; a young Black Dragon Demon Gate martial artist he had saved dies intercepting the attack for him.
- Sama Pyo confronts Sima Gong over his delayed arrival and suspected other intentions, and says the Black Dragon Demon Gate will survive regardless of the battle’s outcome.
- Sima Gong’s earlier order placed two men who spoke of the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s and Sama Pyo’s hidden history in the front line; both died there.
- Sima Gong arrives at the front with the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s elite martial artists.
- Jin Taekyung launches White Flame at the Blood-Sword Demon Lord, who was charging the already wounded Jeok Cheongang; the outcome is unresolved.

# Chapter 133

## Plot

Woo Jintae hosts four scions of the current Five Gates of Shanxi at Honghwa Inn, using the wealth of the Seongun Escort Bureau to flatter them, distribute gifts, and cultivate their dependence. The luncheon with Shanxi's ten-year-old Prince City Lord is scheduled for the following day, and Woo has prepared to win the child over.

Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung are mocked by the five First Rate scions after Cheongpung wonders who is stronger between himself and the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes. Taekyung challenges them from below, identifies their names through their Level Windows, and slaps the Level 45 spokesman Jintae when he asks how Taekyung knew them.

## Continuity

- Woo Jintae is the heir of the Seongun Escort Bureau and uses its wealth, gifts, and bribes to control the current Five Gates of Shanxi's scions.
- The current Five Gates of Shanxi represent an alliance of more than twenty small and medium-sized sects; the former Five Gates, including the Samdo Sect and Gunggui Sect, were annihilated at the Battle of Eight Spring Gorge after being exposed as agents of the Head Elder.
- Woo Jintae and the Five Gates scions are due to attend the City Lord of Shanxi's luncheon tomorrow; Woo has prepared to win over the ten-year-old imperial Prince.
- The five young prodigies at Honghwa Inn are named Seongryong, Cheonwoo, Myeonghwa, Sohye, and Jintae. They are pampered scions of the current Five Gates and First Rate martial artists; Jintae is Level 45 and acted as their spokesman.
- Taekyung's confrontation with the five scions has begun after they refused to apologize for mocking Cheongpung and spitting on Taekyung from the second-floor railing.

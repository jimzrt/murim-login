# Chapter 672

## Plot

After choosing to follow Yohi's Tracking Scent, Jin retraces the southeastern route toward the Nanman Beast Palace while riding Muyaho. Near a stream, he rests and cooks wild boar, then detects roughly fifty approaching Nanman warriors. Jin ambushes and interrogates them, learns credible information about the Nanman Beast Palace's internal situation, and sends the survivors back with a warning that he is watching them and will kill anyone who harms the surviving Miao warriors. To protect the people who stayed behind and draw Baeksang's forces away from them, Jin burns an entire forest as a beacon before continuing onward with Muyaho.

## Continuity

- Jin has chosen to follow Yohi's Tracking Scent and is moving southeast after retracing the route toward the Nanman Beast Palace.
- Jin has escaped the underground prison, but the Logout function remains blocked and Nanman's forces are searching for him.
- Muyaho has carried Jin for roughly three shichen through Nanman's rugged terrain and continues traveling with him despite exhaustion.
- Taishan stayed behind with the others after Jin ordered him to flee through the reconnaissance chieftains and the Water Dragon Stronghold's swift ships; Taishan promised to wait five nights for Jin's return.
- Jin has deliberately spread fear among the Nanman people aligned with Baeksang by releasing interrogated warriors with warnings of future retaliation.
- The surviving Miao warriors attacked during the underground-prison escape remain under Jin's protection, while Baeksang's net over heaven and earth remains an active threat.
- Jin has lit a forest beacon near the Outer Palace to draw Baeksang's pursuit toward himself.
- The Beast Miao King's fate, the fate of the remaining rescuers, and the outcome of Taishan's escape plan remain unresolved.

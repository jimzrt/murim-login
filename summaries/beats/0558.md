# Chapter 558

## Plot

Team Leader Lee Seungyeop awakens in an ambulance after the F-Rank Mutated Gate incident and learns that his entire team survived. Song Song, the Peace Guild's Healer Team Leader and temporary head of its emergency rescue team, explains that the team is piloting free rescue, transport, treatment, and trauma counseling for Hunters in the capital region.

At the Peace Guild House, Taekyung learns that Mutated Gates have appeared more than five times in Korea during the past month and twice already this month. Team Leader Choi has requested comparative data from a reliable source, who arrives in person: Magic Johnson, the American Grand Mage.

## Continuity

- The Peace Guild is piloting an emergency rescue team for medium- and low-grade Gates in the capital region in cooperation with government agencies; Song Song is its temporary leader, and the service provides free transport, treatment, and psychological counseling to Hunters.
- Jin Taekyung is the wealthy patron funding the Peace Guild's public-safety rescue operation and recruited Song Song to help run it.
- Mutated Gates are increasing sharply: Korea recorded more than five incidents during the previous month and two during the current month, including the Gate Taekyung just handled; Team Leader Choi has requested North American and European data from Magic Johnson, who has arrived at the Peace Guild.

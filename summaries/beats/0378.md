# Chapter 378

## Plot

After logging out of the Murim, Jin Taekyung awakens aboard a private jet flying toward Chengdu International Airport at twenty-five thousand feet, only to find the airport under attack and more than ten Wyverns pursuing the aircraft. With Team Leader Choi protecting the cabin using a Barrier, Jin cuts open the plane door, throws a spear charged with flame Force, kills the lead Wyvern known as Black Star and two more, and sends the remaining Wyverns fleeing.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has returned to the modern world and is aboard a private jet sent by China's Central Committee toward Chengdu International Airport.
- Chengdu International Airport is under a large-scale battle between humans and monsters, and its control tower is not responding.
- More than ten Wyverns pursued the jet; Jin killed their leader, Black Star, and at least two additional Wyverns with a spear enhanced by flame Force.
- Team Leader Choi can cast a Barrier capable of sealing the aircraft opening and preventing the cabin from losing pressure.
- Jin Taekyung concludes that the Lich behind the recent monster wave has extended its attack to Chengdu.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 강기(劍罡) as Force in the general term and as Aura Blade when the modern-world equivalence is explicitly stated.
- Render 브레스 as Breath and 에어 브레스 as Air Breath; retain Barrier for 배리어.

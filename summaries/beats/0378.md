# Chapter 378

## Plot

Jin Taekyung successfully completes Logout and awakens aboard the private jet returning him to the modern world, only to learn that the plane is under attack by monsters.

Chengdu International Airport is engulfed in a battle between humans and monsters, while around a dozen A-rank wyverns pursue the jet. Team Leader Choi trusts Taekyung to resolve the crisis and uses barrier magic to seal the opening against the pressure.

Taekyung cuts a door into the aircraft with an Aura Blade, withstands the high-altitude wind, and kills the lead wyvern and multiple companions by throwing a spear. The remaining wyverns panic and postpone their revenge.

## Continuity

- Logout has completed successfully; Taekyung has returned to the modern world and is aboard a private jet sent by China's Central Committee toward Chengdu International Airport.
- Chengdu International Airport is under attack, with humans and monsters engaged in a deadly battle and the control tower unresponsive.
- Taekyung identifies the recent monster crisis as the likely work of the Lich extending its reach, but the Lich's exact involvement remains unresolved.
- Team Leader Choi is traveling with Taekyung and can use ring-based barrier magic strong enough to block the aircraft's pressure loss.
- Taekyung can use sword qi, called an Aura Blade in the modern world, to cut through the aircraft's alloy hull.
- Taekyung kills the wyvern leader known as Black Star and several other wyverns with a discounted spear from the Hunter Market.
- The remaining wyverns survive the encounter and vow to seek revenge later.

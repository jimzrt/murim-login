# Chapter 170

## Plot

The old man reaches Jang Family Village with Jang-pal, is welcomed by Jang-pal’s family, and briefly enjoys being treated as an ordinary elder. Hanga reveals that tall, handsome martial artists carrying swords came looking for him; after recognizing the danger, the old man stops Jang-pal from going and asks where they are.

The old man demonstrates that he can chew through and break a carefully sharpened axe blade, confirming to Jang-pal that he is an extraordinary martial artist. Meanwhile, Taekyung shows Jang Taebo his enormous supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, completing Find the Master Artisan and generating a linked Quest; Jang Taebo promises to forge what he calls the greatest divine weapon under heaven.

## Continuity

- Hanga is a young girl, Jang-pal’s daughter, who lives near Jang Taebo and visits him regularly; she calls both Jang Taebo and the unnamed old man Grandpa.
- Tall, handsome martial artists carrying swords came looking for the unnamed old man after Hanga met them, gave them information, and led them to him; their identities remain unknown.
- The unnamed old man can bite through and break a carefully sharpened axe blade, and he stops Jang-pal from going after the martial artists before asking where they are.
- Taekyung possesses a huge supply of Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, which causes Jang Taebo to complete Find the Master Artisan and commit to forging Taekyung’s material into a lifelong masterpiece.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 신병이기 as “divine weapon,” 노부 as “this old man,” and 할부지 as “Grandpa.”

# Chapter 124

## Plot

Jin Taekyung considers Lee Seowol's offer of three Supreme Peak martial arts and reveals that the Flame Divine Palm manual came from the dead Jopil, who claimed to be its nineteenth-generation successor. Jin Mukyung warns that possessing another sect's secret art could make Taekyung and the Jin Family thieves in the eyes of the Murim. He explains that the Flame Divine Palm belonged to the Fire King, a Supreme Peak master among the world's twenty greatest experts, whose last known appearance was forty years ago after he killed a thousand Demonic Cult members at Mount Jiuhua. The Fire King's survival is unknown, and the Fire Gate Clan has only a single successor.

Jin Mukyung recovers enough from his Internal Injuries to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan, though he is not fully healed. Hyuk Mujin accompanies the group and complains about leaving home. Wolhwa sees them off, explains that she plans to tour northern Shanxi now that it has become an open market, and says she intends to maintain a close relationship with the Jin Family. After her carriage departs, Lee Seowol appears and addresses Taekyung as Benefactor.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung possesses the Flame Divine Palm manual, a Supreme Peak secret technique of the Fire Gate Clan restricted to owners of Scorching Yang Qi.
- Jopil claimed to be the nineteenth-generation successor of the Flame Divine Palm; the truth of his claim and how he obtained the manual remain uncertain.
- The Fire King was already an old man forty years before this chapter, last appeared after killing a thousand Demonic Cult members over four days and nights at Mount Jiuhua, and may still be alive.
- The Fire Gate Clan follows a single-successor tradition.
- Jin Mukyung has recovered enough from Pung Yang's Internal Injuries to return to the Jin Family of Taiyuan but is not yet fully recovered.
- Wolhwa plans to tour northern Shanxi and intends to maintain a close relationship between the Lower District Sect and the Jin Family.

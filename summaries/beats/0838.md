# Chapter 838

## Plot

Jin Taekyung reunites with Ju Wongong, who has recovered since nearly drowning at Dongting Lake. Wongong reveals that he has been temporarily appointed acting City Lord of Sichuan Province while still under exile. Taekyung sees an opportunity to use the office’s authority and government manpower to guard against Dark Heaven, and heads for the Sichuan Tang Clan to meet someone he has been separated from.

## Continuity

- Ju Wongong is serving as the acting City Lord of Sichuan Province by imperial order while his punishment and exile remain in effect.
- Jin intends to use Wongong’s authority and the government’s manpower to prepare for possible Dark Heaven activity in Sichuan.
- Jin is heading to the Sichuan Tang Clan to meet someone; the person’s identity is not established here.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 주원공 as Ju Wongong, distinguishing the name from the joke “Young Master Protagonist.”
- Render 진 공자 as “Young Master Jin” when Ju Wongong addresses Taekyung.

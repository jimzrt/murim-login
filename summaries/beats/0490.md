# Chapter 490

## Plot

Mungyeong dismisses Taekyung without explaining his training, and the Fake Murim Martial Artist Quest reveals that Mungyeong will test him by various methods before teaching his secret martial arts. Taekyung reflects on his relationship with Jeok Cheongang and goes looking for him, but Jeok has disappeared from the camp.

After unsuccessfully searching with Gung Gibang and others, Taekyung returns to his tent after midnight and drinks water laced with Potent Paralysis Powder. He neutralizes it with the Myriad-Poison Ring and Scorching Yang Qi, only to trigger a second poisoning by Potent Soul-Bewitching Powder.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong has begun Taekyung's training by testing him without warning through a poison trap.
- Taekyung neutralized Potent Paralysis Powder with the Myriad-Poison Ring and Scorching Yang Qi, but a second poison has just affected him.
- Jeok Cheongang's whereabouts remain unknown after Taekyung searched the camp; Taekyung believes Jeok deliberately hid from him.

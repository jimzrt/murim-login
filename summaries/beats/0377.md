# Chapter 377

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung's party leaves Chengdu, the corrupt City Lord of Sichuan Province retreats to his favorite concubine Aehyang, who manipulates him into concealing the government's role in the recent bloodshed and exaggerating his accomplishments. An ominous red light appears in Aehyang's eyes, and events proceed according to her—or her master's—wishes.

Aboard three Water Dragon Stronghold ships, Jin Taekyung discovers that a fragment of the Western Heaven Demon Lord's Black Dragon Armor became a bound Item after its owner's death. Mungyeong explains that the armor changes with its owner's energy; Taekyung imbues it with the Fire Gate Divine Technique, names it Fire Dragon Armor, and learns that it can repair itself by consuming his internal energy before logging out for a long sleep.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's party has departed Chengdu aboard three Water Dragon Stronghold fast ships, with Mungyeong traveling under his medical-apprentice identity.
- A fragment of the Western Heaven Demon Lord's Black Dragon Armor is now Jin Taekyung's bound Item, renamed Fire Dragon Armor after resonating with his Fire Gate Divine Technique; it can repair its damaged sections by consuming his internal energy.
- Aehyang is exerting supernatural influence over the Sichuan City Lord, whose eyes and judgment are affected by an ominous red force; she has directed him to conceal the government's misconduct and falsely claim credit for restoring order.
- Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang remain heavily injured, allowing Jin Taekyung to have river pirates dunk their heads in the Yangtze as punishment for joking about attractive women.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 화룡갑 as Fire Dragon Armor, retaining Black Dragon Armor for 흑룡갑.
- Render 신병이기 as Divine Weapon and 종속 아이템 as bound Item.
- Retain the comic metaphor 찍먹 as dunking someone's head repeatedly in water rather than literal dipping.

# Chapter 1021

## Plot

Invoking his imperial title as Marquis of Shangshan, Jin Taekyung orders the Gansu leaders to gather every force from the Qilian Mountains at the Great Snow Mountain. Sima Gong agrees to comply for now, and the Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord agrees on Zhongnan’s behalf. Sima Gong sends Sama Pyo back to Taekyung, but Taekyung realizes Pyo will be spying on them for his father.

As the five thousand troops set out, Sima Gong confirms that he ordered Pyo to watch Taekyung’s group. He tells Pyo that the plan is to secure the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s resurgence and make Pyo ruler of Gansu, adding that there cannot be two tigers on one mountain. Their parting is not the affectionate farewell Sima Gong had described to Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Sima Gong agrees to Taekyung’s order to bring all forces from the Qilian Mountains to the Great Snow Mountain; the Gansu leaders and Zhongnan prepare to move within half a day.
- Sama Pyo returns to Taekyung’s group under orders from Sima Gong to observe their every move; Taekyung suspects Pyo is acting as a spy, not a comrade.
- Sima Gong says he intends the Black Dragon Demon Gate’s resurgence and Pyo’s eventual rule over Gansu, and insists their goal remains unchanged despite setbacks.
- Sama Pyo accepts his father’s spying order without questioning it; Sima Gong expresses satisfaction at his obedience.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 상산후 as “Marquis of Shangshan,” 열후 as “marquis,” and 흑야왕 as “Black Night King.”

# Chapter 496

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin records a profane diary covering the aftermath of the imugi's death: Gung Gibang's repeated humiliation, the corpse's secret transport and partial disappearance, Cheongpung's uselessly simple advice, and Mimi consuming the Blood Fish. He notes that Jeok Cheongang has vanished, Taekyung has become sharper and nearly impossible to sense, and Mungyeong is often with him.

Zhuge Feng announces that the Zhuge Clan has successfully sealed the Gate's gap with a formation, though Gung Gibang observes that this may not be a fundamental solution. Mujin and Gung speculate that Mungyeong is learning martial arts from Taekyung and mourn the imagined corruption of the innocent apprentice.

Taekyung undergoes another concealed and nearly fatal trial, exchanging crude banter through Sound Transmission with an unseen opponent before a blinding sword strike is launched at him.

## Continuity

- The Zhuge Clan has successfully sealed the exposed Gate gap with a formation, but whether the seal is permanent or stops the Gate's underlying problem remains unresolved.
- Mungyeong continues accompanying Taekyung during his concealed martial training and lethal testing without a formal Master-Disciple relationship.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 취팔선권 as Drunken Eight Immortals Fist; preserve the diary's crude shit-and-piss humor and the concealed identity of Taekyung's unseen trainer.

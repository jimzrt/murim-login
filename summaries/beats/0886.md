# Chapter 886

## Plot

Taekyung finds himself in an abandoned imperial forbidden ground where the late Emperor and direct imperial relatives were once confined after the rebellion. So Gyo explains that she is there to find someone and asks how long Taekyung has practiced martial arts. He lies that it has been fifteen years; So Gyo sees through him, reveals movement skill beyond First Rate, and says the imperial family’s intelligence network is why she remains there.

## Continuity

- The forbidden ground in the Outer Palace is the abandoned place where the late Emperor and direct imperial relatives were confined after the rebellion more than ten years ago; no one guards it.
- So Gyo is searching for someone she says she must find. She has continued receiving news from Murim and can move with skill beyond First Rate.
- Taekyung told So Gyo he had practiced martial arts for fifteen years; she identified the claim as a lie.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 禁地 as “forbidden ground” in this location; retain established terms including “Outer Palace,” “Inner Palace,” “Qianqing Palace,” “Embroidered Uniform Guard,” and “Scorching Yang Qi.”

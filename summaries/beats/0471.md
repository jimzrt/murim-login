# Chapter 471

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang reflects on his long life, the Great Faction War, and the limits of his experience when confronted by the enormous Mutated Water God Dragon. The dragon's Fear briefly freezes Jeok, Mungyeong, and Zhuge Feng, but Jeok breaks free through rage and Scorching Yang Qi while Mungyeong overcomes it through reason and enlightenment. Jeok and Mungyeong charge the dragon using their signature techniques.

Taekyung and Cheongpung coordinate their assault, cutting through the dragon's whisker attacks while Cheongpung protects Taekyung's back with the Zaha Divine Technique. Mungyeong cuts one of the dragon's black horns, Jeok strikes its waist with a fire-infused fist, and Taekyung charges the falling maw with White Flame aimed at its eye.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang, Mungyeong, and Zhuge Feng have reached Taekyung's location and joined the battle against the Mutated Water God Dragon.
- The dragon's Fear affects even Jeok Cheongang and Mungyeong; both partially overcome it, while Zhuge Feng remains affected to some degree.
- Taekyung and Cheongpung are fighting the dragon together, with Cheongpung using the Zaha Divine Technique to intercept attacks aimed at Taekyung.
- The Mutated Water God Dragon is highly intelligent, uses hundreds of three-jang whiskers as weapons, recognizes the Fire Dragon Armor's strength, and targets Taekyung's unarmored flesh.
- Mungyeong has resumed his Slaughter Saint identity in battle and is using the Ghost Illusory Slaughter Step and a small sword.
- Jeok Cheongang's fist has damaged the dragon's waist, and Taekyung is attacking its eye with White Flame as the dragon falls.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 홍건 as Red Turbans.
- Render 천마 신교 as Heavenly Demon Divine Cult when used as the full sect name.
- Render 십이경맥 as Twelve Regular Meridians.
- Render 시 서펜트 as Sea Serpent and 몬스터 대백과 as Monster Encyclopedia.
- Preserve Taekyung's dry, profane combat voice and Cheongpung's dreamy voice while rendering Jeok's curses as forceful direct insults.

# Chapter 215

## Plot

A mistake by the Star Guild's Team 1 Leader changed the joint raid location from Manticore's Jungle to The Black Wyvern's Nest. Team Leader Choi proposed canceling the raid because the Peace Guild was unprepared, but Taekyung insisted on proceeding despite his trauma from the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident.

The Star Guild and Peace Guild entered the A-Rank Gate, where more than twenty members crossed a vast, hot, humid primeval jungle. Won Myunghoon lent Taekyung a Yeti's Necklace, but Taekyung removed it because opening his Conception and Governor Vessels had left him unaffected by the heat, then lent it to Im Kkeokjeong.

One hour into the raid, a wyvern appeared above the party, much sooner than Won Myunghoon expected.

## Continuity

- The joint raid location was accidentally changed from Manticore's Jungle to The Black Wyvern's Nest by the Star Guild's Team 1 Leader; Team Leader Choi initially wanted to cancel, but Taekyung persuaded both Guilds to proceed.
- Taekyung entered The Black Wyvern's Nest despite his lingering trauma, asserting that he is no longer the powerless F-Rank Hunter he was three years earlier.
- Won Myunghoon lent Taekyung a Yeti's Necklace for the jungle heat; Taekyung gave it to Im Kkeokjeong after removing it because his opened Conception and Governor Vessels left him unaffected by the heat.
- A wyvern appeared above the raid party after only one hour inside The Black Wyvern's Nest.

# Chapter 1084

## Plot

Mu Song leads the Yangtze River Channel League’s swift ships against the Great Nation’s military fleet under Pa Ryun’s command. Though the League gains the upper hand, Mu Song is troubled by the alliance with Dark Heaven and the attack on government soldiers; he had opposed the decision with his Junior Brother but obeys Pa Ryun. When he sees the soldiers’ fear, he turns his saber aside and orders his men to spare them. After sinking more than a hundred military vessels carrying thousands of troops in two shichen, the League turns west.

## Continuity

- The Yangtze River Channel League attacks the Great Nation’s fleet under Pa Ryun’s command; Mu Song spares the soldiers aboard the ships he boards.

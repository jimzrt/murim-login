# Chapter 1043

## Plot

The descending Hell Fire sphere freezes the battlefield in terror. Though exhausted and badly injured, Jin Taekyung uses White Flame to strike it; his spear bends part of the sphere’s course and opens a rift in its flames. Other streaks of light then rise from the ground and strike toward the sphere, but the chapter ends before its fate is known.

## Continuity

- The Grand Mage’s Hell Fire descends as a vast sphere capable of killing thousands; Jin’s spear changes its course and opens a rift but does not stop it.
- Jin is left with an empty dantian, damaged acupoints, and severe exhaustion after his incomplete One Annihilation and final spear throw.
- Other fighters launch dazzling attacks from the ground toward the Hell Fire sphere; their identities and the result are not yet shown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 헬 파이어 as Hell Fire when named; use lowercase hellfire descriptively for 겁화.
- Continue rendering 일섬 as One Annihilation and 백염 as White Flame.

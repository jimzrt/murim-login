# Chapter 758

## Plot

Germany has requested assistance from both Jin Taekyung and Michael Silbert for the impending Monster Wave centered on Munich, which may occur within five hours. Jin, Team Leader Choi, and the Skeleton King hurry toward Germany while learning that Michael's core forces are still suppressing a Monster Wave in South Africa.

Jin realizes that Monster Waves may now arise naturally and with increasing frequency rather than only through engineered terrorism. The System presents Cataclysm as Jin's first Main Quest, but leaves its mission, reward, and failure conditions unknown; Jin nevertheless resolves to stop the disaster threatening Munich and the world.

In Cape Town, South Africa, an empowered Troll horde massacres civilians and Hunters. An unidentified figure effortlessly kills the Troll leader and advances with overwhelming force, stating that a friend is waiting.

## Continuity

- Germany has requested Jin Taekyung's assistance with the imminent Munich Monster Wave, and Jin is traveling there with Team Leader Choi and the Skeleton King.
- Michael Silbert has also been asked to assist in Munich, but his core forces are currently suppressing a Monster Wave in South Africa before heading to Germany.
- Monster Waves may now occur naturally with increasing frequency, suggesting the beginning of a new age of disaster beyond the Great Cataclysm.
- The System has issued Cataclysm as Jin's first Main Quest; its mission, reward, and failure conditions remain unknown.
- An unidentified powerful figure killed the Troll leader during the Cape Town Monster Wave and said that a friend was waiting.

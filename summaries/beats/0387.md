# Chapter 387

## Plot

After leaving the underground bunker, Faye Chen invites Jin Taekyung to drink before the possible deployment to the front. Prince Felix declines when she cannot provide a 1945 Romanée-Conti, while Magic Johnson joins after Team Leader Choi accepts the invitation. Jin then follows Wu Heixing into a secluded forest, discovers that Wu secretly uses Sound Transmission and martial arts including an internal-energy cultivation technique, and defeats him after Wu attacks. Jin takes Wu's top-grade potion as a settlement, heals him with a high-grade potion, and warns him not to reveal the incident. Team Leader Choi subsequently sends Jin an urgent, garbled text asking him to come quickly.

## Continuity

- Faye Chen treats drinking before a dangerous deployment as an eve-of-battle ritual; Magic Johnson and Team Leader Choi accompany her, while Prince Felix leaves after his preferred wine is unavailable.
- Wu Heixing secretly knows Sound Transmission and martial arts, including an internal-energy cultivation technique and fist-and-foot martial arts; Jin defeats him after he draws his sword and attacks.
- Jin acquires Wu Heixing's top-grade potion, gives Wu a high-grade potion to recover, and considers the matter settled under threat of silence.
- Team Leader Choi sends Jin an urgent and garbled message after leaving with Faye Chen and Magic Johnson.

# Chapter 674

## Plot

The Southern Heaven Demon Empress visits Baeksang in the Palace Lord's Hall and reveals that Dark Heaven's decades-long undertaking is nearing completion. She identifies the Beast Miao King and Jin Taekyung as the remaining major obstacles, accepts Baeksang's admission that his sworn-brotherly feelings helped the Beast Miao King escape, and orders him to find and eliminate the Beast Miao King within three days while Dark Heaven handles Jin Taekyung.

Baeksang, now Nanman's temporary Palace Lord, kneels before the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and accepts her command despite recognizing himself as her controlled puppet. Meanwhile, Jin Taekyung and Muyaho continue fleeing south, having repelled multiple pursuit squads and set fire to several mountains and a pasture; on the second day, Muyaho leads Jin to a dark forest.

## Continuity

- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is the visible representative of Dark Heaven and has controlled Baeksang through a decades-long arrangement that gave him hope after the death of his child.
- Baeksang's left arm was severed by Force during the previous night's escape attempt; the wound has stopped bleeding, but the severed end remains painful.
- Baeksang has been ordered to find and eliminate the Beast Miao King within three days, while the Southern Heaven Demon Empress states that Dark Heaven will deal with Jin Taekyung.
- Dark Heaven's undertaking is entering its final stage, and its intended route from Nanman is meant to become a bridgehead toward the Central Plains for the return of the Lord of Heaven.
- Jin Taekyung and Muyaho are fleeing south together after defeating approximately six pursuit squads; Muyaho's white fur is stained with blood and soot, and they have reached a mountain covered in darkness beside a black forest.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Great undertaking for 대사 in this chapter's Dark Heaven plot context, distinguishing it from the glossary sense of 대사 as a senior Buddhist monk's title.
- Use Palace Lord's Hall for 궁주전.
- Retain the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's playful, taunting address toward Baeksang alongside the underlying threat and terror.
- Retain Jin Taekyung's irreverent first-person narration, including his crude wager involving Hyuk Mujin.

# Chapter 857

## Plot

The man mistaken for a corpse wakes and turns out to be Hyuk Mujin. When an Embroidered Uniform Guard subordinate tries to attack him, Hyuk disarms him with Empty-Hand Seizes the Blade, knocks him out, and takes his water. Commander Jeong dismisses the subordinate for his earlier mistake, then questions Hyuk, who refuses to leave because he serves someone expected to return. Hyuk reveals that the flask came from a stolen Guard badge. As Jeong’s men surround him, Hyuk gives his name and stands his ground. A young man appears in a tree, calls himself the mastermind, and greets Jeong as if they have met before; when Jeong asks whether he knows him, the young man denies it with an insult.

## Continuity

- The unidentified body ahead of the Embroidered Uniform Guard procession was Hyuk Mujin, who had been sleeping; he has now reunited with the Guard while separated from the person he serves.
- Hyuk Mujin is holding a stolen Embroidered Uniform Guard badge after taking it from the unconscious subordinate; Commander Jeong’s men have surrounded him.
- An unidentified young man appeared above the procession, claimed the role of mastermind, and addressed Commander Jeong as though they had met; he then denied knowing Jeong.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 정천호 as “Commander Jeong” and 호패 as “identity tag,” following the reading copy.
- Keep Hyuk Mujin’s irreverent, profane speech distinct from Commander Jeong’s calm, controlled register.

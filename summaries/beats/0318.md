# Chapter 318

## Plot

At Tengwang Pavilion, Hwangbo Eom, the Taeeul Merciless Sword, tests Hyuk Sopyung by pouring tea with overwhelming internal energy, then reprimands him for disgracing Zhongnan and criticizes both Song Il and the current Sect Leader. Hyuk Sopyung finally defends his Master and demands that Hwangbo show proper respect to the Sect Leader of the Great Zhongnan Sect; Hwangbo reveals that he is working to restore Zhongnan's standing.

Ju Hwaran arrives at the teahouse and formally presents herself to Hwangbo Eom as the Young Bureau Head of the Yongbong Escort Bureau. Hwangbo asks whether she brought the item he requested, but the nature of the item remains unrevealed.

Jin Taekyung's party travels to Tengwang Pavilion while Gung Gibang warns against confronting Hwangbo Eom. Taekyung insists they are only going for tea, reaches the door with Baek Museong and the others, senses Hwangbo's Supreme Peak aura and the presence of the Taeeul Sword Unit inside, assures Jeok Cheongang that he will protect him, and opens the door.

## Continuity

- The Taeeul Merciless Sword is Hwangbo Eom, a Supreme Peak master of the Zhongnan Sect and a Senior Martial Uncle to Hyuk Sopyung.
- Hwangbo Eom believes Zhongnan has declined into a hollow institution and is working to restore the sect's standing.
- Hyuk Sopyung defended the authority of his Master, the current Zhongnan Sect Leader, against Hwangbo Eom's criticism.
- Ju Hwaran has arrived at Tengwang Pavilion with an item requested by Hwangbo Eom, but the item has not yet been identified.
- The Yongbong Escort Bureau has reached Xi'an after losing thirty-three escorts, whose bone urns are being carried with the survivors.
- The Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng remains sealed in Ju Hwaran's possession, and three days remain before the Zhongnan Sect's delivery deadline.
- The Zhongnan Sect is waiting in Xi'an at Tengwang Pavilion, and the Taeeul Merciless Sword has personally come to receive the requested shipment.
- Jin Taekyung's party has reached Tengwang Pavilion with Jeok Cheongang still unconscious and being transported on a pack frame.
- Baek Museong is accompanying Jin Taekyung's party to Tengwang Pavilion rather than traveling separately.
- Ju Hwaran still suspects that the Zhongnan Sect leaked information about the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng and wanted the Yongbong Escort Bureau's mission to fail.
- Song Il's current movements remain unconfirmed, while his unclear origins and poor reputation continue to make him suspicious.

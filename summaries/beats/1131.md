# Chapter 1131

## Plot

The old man guides Taekyung to recognize that he perceived qi through the Mind’s Eye. As their meeting ends, Taekyung recognizes the old man as the Helper, who once taught him to circulate qi. The Helper gives him a final gift and sends him back, urging him to save everyone and himself. The Helper remains alone in the gray-white space, which persists; he says he chose to remain there and must wait again.

Jeok Cheongang grieves over Taekyung’s apparently lifeless body. As a tear lands on Taekyung’s hand, a finger submerged in blood moves.

## Continuity

- The old man is the Helper who first taught Taekyung to circulate qi; his identity beyond that remains unknown.
- Taekyung recognized that he perceived the invisible attack through the Mind’s Eye, and the Helper gave him an unidentified final gift before sending him back.
- The Helper says the gray-white space will endure and that he remains there by his own choice, awaiting an unknown end.
- Taekyung returns to his body, which Jeok Cheongang believes lifeless; a finger moves at the chapter’s end, leaving his condition unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 도우미 as “the Helper” and 심안 as “the Mind’s Eye.”

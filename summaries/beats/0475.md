# Chapter 475

## Plot

The Mutated Water God Dragon unleashes an extraordinarily powerful Water Breath that devastates the riverbank, then begins charging a second one almost immediately. Its black pupils accompany a sudden increase in physical strength, forcing Jin Taekyung to change tactics.

Taekyung recalls White Flame through the System's Inventory and uses Heavenly Strike to split the dragon's scales, flesh, and bone across its nose. The strike collides with the nearly completed Water Breath and the fire dragon's claw in a burst of red and blue light.

The clash is heard throughout the area around Dongting Lake. The injured dragon thrashes into the lake, and Jeok Cheongang, Mungyeong, and Cheongpung move to support Taekyung, who they fear may drown. Jeok reveals that Mount Jiuhua was his first home and that his actions during the Great Faction War established his reputation as the Fire King. Taekyung then emerges from the water and orders everyone into an attack formation like a seasoned Hunter.

## Continuity

- The Mutated Water God Dragon can fire Water Breath repeatedly at extreme speed, and its body becomes substantially stronger while its pupils are black; the exact cause remains uncertain.
- Taekyung's Heavenly Strike, using White Flame, clashes directly with the dragon's Water Breath and the fire dragon's claw, leaving the battle unresolved.
- Jeok Cheongang considers Mount Jiuhua his first true home and killed those who burned it during the Great Faction War, leading to his Fire King reputation.
- Jeok Cheongang, Mungyeong, and Cheongpung intervene because a battle underwater gives the Water God Dragon an overwhelming advantage and Taekyung may drown.
- Cheongpung calls Taekyung Aguaman after Taekyung jokingly asked him to use that name; Taekyung emerges from the lake and commands an attack formation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 워터 브레스 as Water Breath while retaining Breath as the fixed dragonkin power term.
- Render 아구아맨 as Aguaman as Cheongpung's deliberately mistaken superhero nickname.
- Retain Jeok Cheongang's established this-old-man voice and the profane, absurd humor of his banter.
- Render 쩍벌충 as wide-mouth bastard to preserve Taekyung's insulting combat humor.

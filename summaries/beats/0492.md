# Chapter 492

## Plot

The gathered martial artists honor the Water God Dragon as a self-sacrificing divine creature, but their gratitude quickly gives way to greed over its priceless scales and bones. Taekyung rebukes Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin for planning weapons from the corpse, while Perfected Being Hyeongong reveals that he is equally eager to dismantle it.

The group begins negotiating the distribution of the Water God Dragon's corpse. Zhuge Feng attempts to claim ten percent for the Zhuge Clan, but Taekyung and Jin Wikyung use a deliberately exaggerated apology to pressure him into accepting a smaller but fair share.

## Continuity

- The Water God Dragon's sacrifice prevented a greater catastrophe in Hubei Province, and its enormous corpse is now being treated as valuable spoils to be dismantled and distributed.
- Taekyung and Jin Wikyung successfully negotiate against Zhuge Feng's attempted ten-percent claim and secure a fair share for the Zhuge Clan.

# Chapter 211

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi encounter Won Myunghoon, a flashy A-rank Hunter and former celebrity ranker who now runs the Star Guild in Incheon. Taekyung recognizes Won as his longtime favorite Hunter, and the two quickly establish a hyung-and-younger-brother relationship. Won explains that an incident eight years earlier ended his public career, that he acquired the Star Guild the previous year, and that Taekyung's recent fame helped revive interest in him. After hearing Taekyung enthusiastically praise his spear manual and career, Won seriously offers to recruit Taekyung and make him a star Hunter.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon is a thirty-nine-year-old A-rank Hunter, former top-one-hundred ranker, entertainer, and current CEO of the Star Guild in Incheon.
- Won Myunghoon's public career declined after an unexplained incident eight years ago; he began returning to the Hunter world after acquiring the Star Guild the previous year.
- Taekyung and Won Myunghoon now address each other as hyung and younger brother in a friendly, informal relationship.
- Won Myunghoon has offered to recruit Taekyung into the Star Guild and personally turn him into a star Hunter.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 원명훈 as “Won Myunghoon” and 스타 길드 as “Star Guild.”
- Retain “hyung” for Taekyung's address to Won Myunghoon and render Won's 동생 framing as “younger brother.”
- Render 주간 헌터즈 as “Weekly Hunters,” and preserve the established English titles for Won Myunghoon's songs.

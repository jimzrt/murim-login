# Chapter 679

## Plot

The reconnaissance squad of more than two hundred people reaches Nanman's northeastern border after three days, where Yayul Mok confirms that the Beast Miao King chose to stake his fate on keeping faith with Jin Taekyung and Baeksang. The two chieftains learn that Baeksang ordered the Han Chinese members detained and suspects that Baeksang and Dark Heaven may have withheld the crisis in the Inner Palace so the Blood Monk could eliminate the Beast Miao King's loyalists.

Namho decides that the Han Chinese should either be released to escape Baeksang or escorted to the Central Plains so the Murim Alliance can aid Nanman. Sama Pyo reveals that the Yangtze River Channel League's swift ship is nearby, but it unexpectedly arrives at their position instead of remaining half a day west. Namho orders everyone to prepare for battle, and an unidentified person steps ashore.

Jin Taekyung continues fighting the Black Hand Fist Demon and the unidentified Supreme Peak master wielding twin wheels. After the twin wheels cut Muyaho's fur and delay his response, Jin blocks the Black Hand Fist Demon's punch with one palm, releases White Flame, and launches the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist through blue-white flames.

## Continuity

- The Beast Miao King deliberately sent Yayul Mok and the others to the underground prison after deciding that keeping faith with Jin Taekyung and Baeksang was worth staking Nanman's fate.
- Namho believes Baeksang and Dark Heaven have likely seized the Inner Palace and may have intended the Blood Monk to deal with the Beast Miao King's loyalists.
- The Han Chinese members of the reconnaissance squad are detained but unharmed, with their Sleep Acupoints struck.
- Namho proposes taking the Han Chinese to the Central Plains so the Murim Alliance and the Sichuan sects can bring aid to Nanman; he estimates the return journey at seven to ten days.
- The Yangtze River Channel League's swift ship has arrived unexpectedly at the reconnaissance squad's position, and an unidentified person has stepped ashore.
- Jin Taekyung is still fighting the Black Hand Fist Demon and the unidentified twin-wheel Supreme Peak master in the Poisonblood Grounds.
- Muyaho's fur was cut by one of the twin wheels while Jin protected him during the battle.
- Jin has discarded White Flame temporarily and is using the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist with blue-white flames.
- Yohi and Heugung remain captive and alive, awaiting the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's return.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress remains absent from the battlefield, while her agents continue to threaten Jin's allies.

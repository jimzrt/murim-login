# Chapter 335

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's party reaches the fortified Sichuan Tang Clan, whose gate guards admit them only after Taekyung presents a handwritten letter from Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint.

Inside the Tang Clan, the party finds craftsmen and martial artists preparing weapons amid unexplained wartime tension and poison mist. Mungyeong is escorted away to seek a medicinal prescription, while Taekyung, Gung Gibang, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung surrender their weapons, undergo a search, and enter the Family Head's Hall after being summoned inside.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's party has entered the Sichuan Tang Clan's fortified compound, and the Family Head has permitted Taekyung's group to enter the Family Head's Hall after receiving Mae Jonghak's letter.
- The Sichuan Tang Clan is in unexplained heightened defensive readiness, with crossbows, armed personnel, weapon preparation, and poison mist throughout the compound.
- Mungyeong has been separated from the party and escorted to seek a medicinal prescription from the Sichuan Tang Clan.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 당가타 as Tang Family Hill and 당가 as Tang Family when the source distinguishes it from 사천당문, rendered Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Retain Oseong and Haneum as the paired historical names in Taekyung's joke and explain the pairing in a footnote.
- Render 의생님 as Medical Apprentice and 약방문 as prescription.
- Render 독무 as poison mist.

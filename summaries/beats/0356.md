# Chapter 356

## Plot

Jin Taekyung defends the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison alone, ambushing and killing two waves of Dark Heaven infiltrators before concealing their corpses. He then questions the restrained Heavenly Power Demon, who reveals that the Western Heaven Demon Lord was once one of the Divine Cult's four Protectors and closest aides to its Cult Leader, but led the Cult Leader astray before adopting that title. A distant explosion shakes the prison as the battle above continues.

## Continuity

- The Dark Heaven assault has breached the Tang Clan's Outer Hall, and a fierce battle continues in the Inner Hall.
- Taekyung is alone defending the underground prison and is using its labyrinthine structure and concealed corpses to preserve the advantage of surprise.
- The Heavenly Power Demon remains alive and restrained, while the other prisoners have been moved elsewhere.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord was one of the Divine Cult's four Protectors, served closest to the Cult Leader, and led him astray before calling himself the Western Heaven Demon Lord.

# Chapter 533

## Plot

Jin Taekyung meets Sama Pyo, the Black Dragon Demon Gate's Young Sect Leader and Black Dragon Saber, after Sama Pyo helps Hyuk Mujin and arrives to retrieve Taishan, a giant subordinate with a childlike voice and enormous appetite. Sama Pyo and Taekyung trade insults before Sama Pyo departs; afterward, Ju Hwaran reveals that Sama Pyo was once her fiancé, shocking everyone at the table.

## Continuity

- The previously unfamiliar martial artist is identified as Sama Pyo, Young Sect Leader of the Black Dragon Demon Gate and wielder of the Black Dragon Saber; Song Ilseom judges him stronger and more dangerous than before.
- The previously unidentified giant is identified as Taishan, Sama Pyo's enormous subordinate; Taishan is food-obsessed, obedient to Sama Pyo, and speaks in clipped, childlike sentences.
- Ju Hwaran and Sama Pyo were formerly engaged, but the circumstances and reason for the ended engagement remain unexplained.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일기천룡 as One-Ride Heavenly Dragon, 태산 as Taishan, and preserve Taishan's clipped, literal speech.
- Preserve Taekyung's blunt insults, the chapter's explicit profanity, and the abrupt comedic shock of Hwaran's former-fiancé revelation.

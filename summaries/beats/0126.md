# Chapter 126

## Plot

Jin Wikyung and Wipeng rushed from Taiyuan with fifty elite guards after learning that Pung Yang, the Red Wind Band, the Mount Heng Sword Sect, and the Jin brothers were in danger. They learned en route that Pung Yang had died, the Red Wind Band had been annihilated, Taekyung had defeated Pung Yang, and Mukyung had suffered severe but nonfatal injuries. At Sakju, Wikyung welcomed Mukyung, Taekyung, and Hyuk Mujin with an enormous banner and an embrace that worsened Mukyung's injuries. During the reunion, Mukyung explained that Pung Yang had become terrifyingly powerful after taking a crimson pill, prompting speculation that it was a Demonic Cult relic with catastrophic side effects. Taekyung, realizing the danger of the pill, accidentally revealed that he had kept it in his Inventory.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung and Wipeng traveled from Taiyuan to Sakju with fifty elite guards after receiving an emergency report about Pung Yang and the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- Jin Wikyung learned that Pung Yang was dead, the Red Wind Band had been annihilated, Jin Taekyung had defeated Pung Yang, and Jin Mukyung had been severely injured but was recovering.
- The Jin Family of Taiyuan erected a huge Sakju banner celebrating the safe return of Jin Mukyung, Jin Taekyung, and Hyuk Mujin.
- Taekyung possesses one Temporary Strength Pill in his Inventory and admitted this to Jin Wikyung, Jin Mukyung, and Wipeng at the chapter's end.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 전서응 as messenger eagle to distinguish it from the established messenger pigeon.
- Render 폭혈단 as Blood-Exploding Pill, 구주 as Nine Provinces, 고원 as Gaoyuan, and 산서제일인 as Shanxi's Number One.
- Render 사술 as dark arts, 마기 as demonic qi, 선천지기 as innate qi, 소음인 as Soeumin, and 태양인 as Taeyangin; retain explanatory footnotes for the cultural references.

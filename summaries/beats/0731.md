# Chapter 731

## Plot

Huginn, a powerful Odin Guild messenger dressed like a nineteenth-century European gentleman, visits Ares Guild to deliver a veiled warning about the impending release of the Mana Cultivation Method. Jin Taekyung exposes the theatrical intimidation and forces Huginn to state the demand plainly: stop the release or face repercussions. Huginn assumes Choi Minwoo will comply, but Choi pretends to cancel the release while secretly arranging for the Mana Cultivation Method to be uploaded and released that same day.

## Continuity

- Huginn is an Odin Guild operative who serves its Guild Master with absolute loyalty and has confronted Jin Taekyung and Choi Minwoo over the Mana Cultivation Method.
- Odin Guild demands that Ares Guild cancel the Mana Cultivation Method's release and threatens unspecified repercussions if refused.
- Choi Minwoo falsely tells Huginn that the release is canceled, then reveals that the Mana Cultivation Method will instead be released that day.
- Chronos Guild is another member of the world's Ten Great Guilds and is among the major Guilds connected to Odin's current concerns.
- Go Jun's necklace remains missing from secured evidence storage despite the storage room's protective Magic.

# Chapter 695

## Plot

The Nanman Beast Palace braces for civil war before any campaign against the Central Plains. Nearly ten thousand warriors have gathered in the Inner Palace, while five tribal chieftains and their people have left the Outer Palace in support of the former Palace Lord, Yayul Cheok.

Jin Taekyung and Yohi arrive at the North Gate riding with the guardian spirit of the sacred stone and an immense beast army. Jin confirms that Dark Heaven's scheme has not yet begun, then splits the iron gate with Force. The guardian spirit releases the sacred stone's pillar of light, and the beasts charge into the Nanman Beast Palace, beginning the Great War that will decide Nanman's fate.

## Continuity

- Nanman Beast Palace is on the verge of a civil war, with nearly ten thousand warriors awaiting the new Palace Lord in the Inner Palace.
- Five tribal chieftains have left the Outer Palace in support of former Palace Lord Yayul Cheok, who has been branded a traitor by the current regime.
- Jin Taekyung and Yohi have reached the Nanman Beast Palace's North Gate with the guardian spirit and a vast army of beasts before Dark Heaven's Rift or other scheme has begun.
- The White Tiger is the sacred stone's guardian spirit, restored to its former silver-white form, carrying the sacred stone and communicating telepathically with Jin.
- Jin used blue-white Force to split the iron gate, after which the sacred stone emitted a pillar of light visible across a vast distance.
- The beast army has begun its assault, marking the start of the Great War that will determine Nanman's fate.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 동문, 서문, 북문, and 남문 as East Gate, West Gate, North Gate, and South Gate.
- Render 내전 as civil war and 전의 as fighting spirit.
- Render 흰둥아 as Whitey, Jin Taekyung's teasing nickname for the White Tiger.
- Render 진인사대천명 as “Do everything in your power, then leave the result to Heaven and wait.”

# Chapter 198

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang tells Jin Wikyung that cleansing the sinews and washing the marrow was unnecessary for Jin Taekyung and suggests that Taekyung may possess the Heavenly Martial Physique. Wikyung reveals that Taekyung advanced from a Third Rate wastrel to the beginning of the Peak realm in only three months, leading Jeok to reconsider the scale of Taekyung's talent and wonder why Heaven brought them together.

The next day, Hyuk Mujin forces Taekyung to attend the extended Jin Family banquet, where Jeok Cheongang makes him drink heavily. Ak Bulgun of the Shandong Yue Family introduces himself as a spear Instructor from Heaven's Gate Temple, explains that he came to retrieve Jin Mukyung, and offers Taekyung special permission to enroll as a cadet. Jeok interrupts when Ak Bulgun places a hand on Taekyung's shoulder, claiming Taekyung as his own.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has practiced martial arts for only three months, advancing from Third Rate to the beginning of the Peak realm during that period.
- Jeok Cheongang suspects that Jin Taekyung may possess the Heavenly Martial Physique, although he is not yet certain because Taekyung's current progress seems insufficient compared with the legends of that physique.
- Ak Bulgun, a spear Instructor at Heaven's Gate Temple, offers Taekyung special permission to enroll after the normal admission period; Taekyung has not decided whether to accept.
- Jeok Cheongang remains at the Jin Family while the extended gathering continues, drinks through the night with Jin Wikyung, and forcibly brings Taekyung to the banquet the following day.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 악불군 as “Ak Bulgun” and 산동악가 as “Shandong Yue Family.”
- Retain “Heavenly Martial Physique” for 천무지체 and “Heaven’s Gate Temple” for 천무학관.
- Render Jeok Cheongang’s possessive final address as “Take your hand off what’s mine,” without treating it as confirmation that Taekyung is his Disciple.

# Chapter 341

## Plot

Mungyeong reveals that he is the Disciple of the Divine Physician and guides Jin Taekyung's party through a Mystic Gate Formation to a hidden clinic in Sichuan. They find the Divine Physician, whose personal name is Dong Feng, living in a secluded seasonal landscape.

Dong Feng treats Venerable Myoryeong and says she can fully recover after completing treatment and resting. He then examines Jeok Cheongang and says he believes he can treat him, giving Taekyung hope and completing the related Quests.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is established as Dong Feng's Disciple, and Dong Feng is the Divine Physician sought by Taekyung's party.
- Dong Feng has begun treating Venerable Myoryeong and Jeok Cheongang; Myoryeong is expected to recover, while Jeok's treatment outcome remains unresolved.

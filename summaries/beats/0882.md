# Chapter 882

## Plot

As crowds and rival scholars gather outside the imperial palace, Taishan wanders off and startles everyone before apologizing and asking to leave. His companions catch up, present an official letter identifying them as the Blazing Flame Troupe invited to perform at the imperial banquet, and are admitted through the gate.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung’s group has an official invitation to perform as the Blazing Flame Troupe at the imperial banquet; the palace gate commander admits them after checking the sealed letter.

## Translation Decisions

- Taishan briefly calls himself Geosan, then uses that name; treat Geosan as an uncertain name variant for Taishan, not a confirmed separate person.
- Render 열화단 as “Blazing Flame Troupe.”

# Chapter 370

## Plot

Seven days after the Three-Gate Bloodbath, the Sichuan Tang Clan is rebuilding while Emei nuns conduct funerals for the many dead. Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang are recovering from their fight with the Third Fiend, while Cheongpung entertains himself with Mimi. Jin Taekyung awakens, discovers that everyone survived, reaches the Supreme Peak realm, and receives the new sobriquet Blazing Flame Divine Dragon as the people of Sichuan honor him and Jeok Cheongang silently approves.

## Continuity

- The Sichuan Tang Clan suffered catastrophic losses in the Three-Gate Bloodbath and is now being rebuilt with aid from Emei, Qingcheng, the Beggars' Sect, other martial organizations, and ordinary workers.
- Jin Taekyung is awake after exhausting himself, has reached the Supreme Peak realm and manifested Force, and has received the sobriquet Blazing Flame Divine Dragon.
- The Third Fiend was captured by an unidentified figure, while the Second Fiend's fate remains unknown.
- Mungyeong told Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang that Jin Taekyung ordered them to assist Emei while Taekyung was unconscious, leaving the companions confused when Taekyung denies giving that order.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 열화신룡 as Blazing Flame Divine Dragon, distinct from 화산신룡 as Huashan Divine Dragon.
- Render 참이슬 as Chamisul and preserve the Korean soju-brand joke in a footnote.
- Use Mimi for 미미 and Mimi-chan for 미미쨩.
- Use Third Fiend for singular 삼괴 references and Three Fiends for collective references.

# Chapter 740

## Plot

After more than three days of isolating himself in qi circulation and contemplation, Jin Taekyung accepts a meal from the Skeleton King, resumes eating, and decides to leave after bathing.

Jin acknowledges that his actions contributed to the terrorist attacks but resolves to pursue Michael Silbert and the Prophet regardless of public opinion. Team Leader Choi reports that Odin Guild controls more than two hundred effectively owned Gates through a vast asset network, while the Prophet remains unidentified and unlocated. Magic Johnson then reports that a Grand Mage likely connected to Michael has been found, only for a call to reveal that the person is dead.

## Continuity

- Jin accepts responsibility for contributing to the attacks without allowing guilt to prevent him from pursuing the perpetrators.
- Odin Guild controls more than two hundred Gates through permanent leases and possesses an enormous network of assets and businesses, many held through nominally unrelated entities.
- Available documents show no direct evidence that Odin Guild supplied the unrefined Magic Gems used in the attacks; the likely source remains terrorist- or rebel-controlled Gates in the Middle East or Africa.
- The Prophet's identity and location remain unknown despite a worldwide search, including efforts by the United States.
- A Grand Mage believed to have contacted Michael Silbert has been identified but is already dead.

# Chapter 276

## Plot

Butler Kim's investigation identifies the Black Hunter known as the “tail” as Im Yeongjun, a Level 95 A-rank Hunter living as a wealthy rural transplant. The group finds evidence that his village feasts may conceal the procurement of supplies for a hidden Black Hunter training operation, and Taekyung senses twenty-eight people inside the mansion, including the three attackers who severed Im Kkeokjeong's arms.

Taekyung leaves the minivan alone despite warnings that the base may contain dozens of A-rank Hunters. He subdues the guard dogs with killing intent, cuts through the iron fence with hand qi, and triggers Alarm Magic, Sound-Blocking Magic, and Vision-Blocking Magic. Im Yeongjun recognizes him and orders the assembled Black Hunters to attack after Taekyung offers to spare everyone except the three attackers if they disarm and confess. Taekyung equips White Flame and prepares to fight the human-hunting Black Hunters as a martial artist of Murim.

## Continuity

- Butler Kim traced the Black Hunter “tail” to Im Yeongjun, a Level 95 A-rank Hunter disguised as an ordinary middle-aged rural resident.
- Taekyung sensed twenty-eight Black Hunters at Im Yeongjun's mansion; three, including Im Yeongjun, participated in the attack on Im Kkeokjeong two days earlier.
- Taekyung entered the Black Hunter base alone, offered terms for surrender, and began fighting after Im Yeongjun rejected them and ordered an attack.
- The mansion is protected by Alarm Magic, Sound-Blocking Magic, and Vision-Blocking Magic that prevent the battle from being detected outside.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 임영준 as Im Yeongjun.
- Render 꼬리 as the “tail,” the investigation codename for the traced Black Hunter.
- Render 세 얼간이 as the Three Idiots, Taekyung's mocking collective nickname.
- Render 도사견 as Tosa dogs and 보신탕 as dog-meat soup.
- Render 뚱인데요 as “This is Patrick” to preserve Taekyung's evasive joke.
- Render 수강 as qi condensed around the hand, distinct from a weapon or named martial technique.

# Chapter 1173

## Plot

The Collapse begins as magical power runs out of control, Gates open around the world, and monster waves emerge. Governments impose martial law and evacuate civilians while Hunters deploy to defend humanity.

At the front, Ares Guild Team 32 Leader Im Kkeokjeong steadies a frightened rookie by recalling a Hunter who trained persistently despite being F-rank and unable to find work. The rookie says he hopes to become like Kkeokjeong; as a monster approaches, Kkeokjeong calls for formation. A message then reports that Alpha has awakened.

## Continuity

- The Collapse has begun, with Gates opening and monster waves emerging around the world.
- Im Kkeokjeong leads Ares Guild Team 32 on the battlefield.
- A message to all Hunters reports that Alpha has awakened.

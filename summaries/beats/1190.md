# Chapter 1190

## Plot

Jin Taekyung wakes while being carried by Jeok Cheongang and admits he stayed quiet because it was comfortable. He breaks the chains binding them, and the two fight the Black Ghosts and monsters in the mutated Gate Forest of Death-I in the Tianshan Mountains. Taekyung recognizes the place as familiar, but not the Black Ghosts’ faces.

Taekyung kills the last Black Ghost, which says that “that person” is waiting for him. The Gate is cleared; Taekyung levels up and recovers from status ailments and fatigue. Jeok turns away from the Black Ghost’s empty shell, seeming relieved, and the two leave together.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung cleared the mutated Gate Forest of Death-I in the Tianshan Mountains; the Black Ghosts and monsters there were defeated, and Taekyung leveled up and recovered from status ailments and fatigue.
- Taekyung was conscious while Jeok carried him and deliberately stayed quiet after realizing he was comfortable.
- The last Black Ghost said that “that person” was waiting for Taekyung; that person’s identity and purpose remain unknown.
- Jeok regards the Black Ghost’s remains as an empty shell of a boy who had once been someone’s joy and reason for living, and now feels able to let him go.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep rendering 그분 as “that person”; the figure remains unidentified.
- Render 겁화 as “hellfire.”

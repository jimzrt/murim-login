# Chapter 823

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung defeats Hamid Shah Masoud and forces the fanatics to surrender, his exhausted Middle Dantian and depleted mental strength leave him barely able to stand. Still intent on pursuing the Doppelganger, he persuades Magic Johnson to teleport him to the place the Doppelganger first revealed; the Skeleton King joins him, while Johnson agrees to send them and asks them to return alive.

## Continuity

- Jin's Middle Dantian is at its limit and his mental strength is depleted after the battle; the System cannot heal either condition.
- Jin and the Skeleton King are about to use Magic Johnson's Teleport magic to pursue the Doppelganger; Johnson remains behind to guard the battlefield.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Teleport distinct from Blink and Warp; the unusually high magical-power concentration makes this Teleport attempt extremely dangerous.
- Render 등가교환 as “Equivalent Exchange.”

# Chapter 1107

## Plot

As Jin Taekyung unleashes One Annihilation against the Blood Lord, instinct warns him of a hidden Black Ghost moving between them. Taekyung commits to the attack anyway; a streak of light cuts the Black Ghost with the Zaha Divine Technique just before Taekyung’s blue-black flames engulf it and the Blood Lord.

## Continuity

- Taekyung’s One Annihilation has become powerful enough to threaten his own life; he uses it against the Blood Lord, but the outcome is unresolved.
- The Blood Lord concealed one Black Ghost as a shield for Taekyung’s attack; an unidentified attacker cuts it with the Zaha Divine Technique.
- The battle at Xining’s breached western wall continues; Jeok Cheongang remained at the North Gate as of chapter 1105.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain “Black Ghost” for 흑귀 and “Zaha Divine Technique” for 자하신공.

# Chapter 248

## Plot

At Shaolin, Hong Dao tells Jeok Cheongang that the heavenly patterns once showed Jeok's star fading and predicted that he should already have died, but the flow changed when a Morning Star rose in the northern lands. Hong identifies Jin Taekyung as that Morning Star and says the Fire King's star has grown brighter. Jeok reflects that meeting Taekyung rekindled his will to live and that his own martial arts advanced through the past year's training. Hong then reveals that he entrusted the Green Jade Buddha Staff to Unnamed, chose someone to serve as Abbot until Unnamed returns from Arhat Cave, and intends to step down after this Star-Array Grand Banquet, though he may live for about another year.

The Ten Kings were the ten Supreme Peak masters who dominated the Murim during the Great Faction War, but the Spear King and Wave King have already died, others have grown old, and only eight effectively remain. After the three-day preliminaries, more than fifty thousand applicants were reduced to roughly five hundred finalists. Jin Taekyung is recognized as the Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi and is discussed alongside his achievements under the Fire King. He encounters three eccentric finalists: Baek Woo of Kunlun, Gung Gibang of the Beggars' Sect, and Zhuge Gyun of the Zhuge Clan. Taekyung calls them the three idiots, orders them to stop chattering, and threatens to treat them as dead if they meet in a duel. Song Ho then appears on the dais with a wooden prosthetic leg and introduces himself as the Thousand-Faced Fox.

## Continuity

- Hong Dao intends to relinquish the Shaolin Abbot position after the current Star-Array Grand Banquet and may have approximately one year of life remaining.
- Hong Dao entrusted the Green Jade Buddha Staff to Unnamed and selected someone to serve as Abbot until Unnamed returns from Arhat Cave.
- Hong Dao's heavenly-pattern reading identifies Jin Taekyung as the Morning Star that rose while Jeok Cheongang's star was fading; Jeok's star is now brighter than before.
- Jeok Cheongang's will to live was rekindled by meeting Taekyung, and he has advanced through a year of teaching, training, and contemplation.
- The Ten Kings originally numbered ten Supreme Peak masters of the Great Faction War; the Spear King and Wave King are dead, and eight effectively remain.
- More than fifty thousand Star-Array Grand Banquet applicants were reduced to approximately five hundred finalists, or about one percent.
- Baek Woo is the Kunlun Cloud Dragon, Gung Gibang is the Beggars' Sect's Successor Beggar, and Zhuge Gyun is the Zhuge Clan's Divine Marvel Dragon; all three advanced to the main event.
- Song Ho has arrived at the Star-Array Grand Banquet and publicly identified himself as the Thousand-Faced Fox.

# Chapter 690

## Plot

Yohi encounters an overwhelming Black Tiger in the bright enclosed realm, but it vanishes without a trace after a strange wind passes through the area. The pond heals a wound on Yohi's hand, and Muyaho confirms through gestures that the Black Tiger saved and treated them.

Yohi watches over the unconscious Jin Taekyung while fearing that Nanman's crisis is worsening beyond their reach. Jin dreams of his final childhood memory with his father, but his adult consciousness disrupts the memory; he awakens in the pond and answers Yohi's shocked question with, "I don't know. I just woke up."

## Continuity

- Yohi, Muyaho, and Jin Taekyung are inside a bright, warm realm containing a pond with healing properties.
- The pond rapidly heals wounds and has been restoring Jin Taekyung and Muyaho.
- A colossal Black Tiger with blue-white eyes appeared in the realm, radiated an overwhelming aura, and vanished behind the great tree without leaving a trace.
- Muyaho confirms that the Black Tiger saved and treated Yohi, Jin Taekyung, and itself, but neither Yohi nor Muyaho knows the creature's identity, owner, or nature.
- Jin Taekyung was unconscious in the pond but awakens at the end of the chapter.
- Jin Taekyung's awakening follows a dream of his final childhood morning with his father, whose departure was followed by the event that made it their last memory together.
- Yohi deeply trusts Jin Taekyung and believes his strength and moral conviction may be necessary to save Nanman.
- The Beast Miao King has been branded a traitor, fled the Nanman Beast Palace, and disappeared while Nanman mobilizes toward the Outer Palace.
- The Miao leadership is imprisoned, and the Southern Heaven Demon Empress is nearing completion of her grand plan in an unknown refuge.

# Chapter 897

## Plot

As the capital reels from the flood, the Emperor announces a three-day birthday banquet for the imperial prince, beginning the following day. Public anger grows, and a mysterious man in a bamboo hat urges the crowd to seek shelter at Shangshan. An elderly scholar interprets this as a reference to Prince Shangshan, prompting the crowd to imagine him ascending the throne; the bamboo-hat man vanishes before they can question him.

## Continuity

- The Emperor has announced a three-day imperial prince’s birthday banquet, beginning the following day, despite the flood’s damage and public suffering.
- A mysterious bamboo-hat man publicly criticized the Emperor and spoke of shelter at Shangshan; an elderly scholar connected his words to Prince Shangshan, and the crowd began imagining the prince as a possible successor.
- The bamboo-hat man disappeared before the crowd could ask him for answers.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Shangshan” in the crowd’s mountain imagery; the passage turns the place-name into an allusion to Prince Shangshan.

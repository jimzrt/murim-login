# Chapter 831

## Plot

Jin escapes the Doppelganger’s abyssal illusion when the System activates a firewall and blocks it. He tears apart the Doppelganger’s real shadow body with Scorching Yang Qi, then destroys it by stomping on it. The Doppelganger warns that Asmodeus will return and the future Jin saw will come to pass; Jin resolves to change that future. After the Doppelganger is erased, the System reports that Main Quest [Cataclysm] has failed.

## Continuity

- The Doppelganger is erased by Jin, but Main Quest [Cataclysm] fails immediately afterward.
- The System calls Jin “the Chosen One” and “the Master of the Ark,” activates a firewall, and blocks the Doppelganger as a newly detected virus; the meaning of these designations and the Ark remains unknown.
- The Doppelganger’s abyssal power drew Jin into an illusion in which he saw a possible future involving Asmodeus and a devastated world; the vision’s reliability is unknown.
- The Doppelganger claims Asmodeus will return, but Jin does not know whether that warning is true.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 선택받은 자 as “the Chosen One” and 방주의 주인 as “the Master of the Ark.”
- Render [격변] as [Cataclysm], consistent with the established Main Quest terminology.

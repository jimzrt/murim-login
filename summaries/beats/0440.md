# Chapter 440

## Plot

Jin Taekyung receives a massive global welcome at Incheon, where hundreds of thousands cheer during a televised car parade that ultimately ends at Peace Guild's guild house. Surrounded by his family and Team Leader Choi, Taekyung resolves to protect those close to him, while he forces the sulking Skeleton King to join Peace Guild under a prepared contract. After Magic Johnson announces negotiations with Peace Guild, the public celebration continues into Christmas Eve, tormenting Go Jun, who rages at Taekyung's name before being interrupted by a cautious report.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has returned to Korea and is now the focus of a globally broadcast welcome and car parade attended by hundreds of thousands.
- Taekyung's family was present for his return, and he resolved to protect them from the dangers he has faced.
- Taekyung and Team Leader Choi reaffirmed their ongoing cooperation and mutual support.
- The Skeleton King remained concealed in Inventory during the public events, and Taekyung has ordered him to join Peace Guild under an already prepared contract.
- Magic Johnson publicly announced that he is pursuing an agreement with Peace Guild.
- Go Jun remains alive and is overwhelmed by hatred and fear after seeing Taekyung's name; a cautious speaker interrupts his outburst with an unresolved report.

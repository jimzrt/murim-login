# Chapter 562

## Plot

Inside the Yeokgok Mutated Gate, an Ares raid team is surrounded by more than a thousand orcs under an Orc Lord and expects to die. Jin Taekyung kills the Orc Lord by throwing a spear through its head and orders the Hunters to get behind him.

Head of Security Go Se-won enters Ares Guild's skyscraper headquarters, uses privileged access to teleport into restricted Section A, and proceeds to report to the VIP despite the Guild's increasingly harsh internal discipline.

The media praises the Peace Guild, Jin Taekyung, and Choi Minwoo after successive Mutated Gates produce no fatalities, while Go Jun—now Ares Guild's Vice Guild Master—reads the coverage in an office wrecked by his anger.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has entered the Yeokgok Mutated Gate, killed its Orc Lord, and rescued the trapped Ares raid team.
- Go Se-won is Ares Guild's Head of Security and a Team Leader with access to the restricted Section A; he reports to Go Jun.
- Go Jun's control of Ares Guild is increasingly iron-fisted, and he is angered by the public praise directed toward the Peace Guild, Jin Taekyung, and Choi Minwoo.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 오크 로드 as Orc Lord, 국회의사당 as National Assembly, and A구역 as Section A.
- Render 고세원 as Go Se-won and retain Head of Security for 경호팀장.
- Retain Mutated Gate, Demon Realm language, Magic Trap, Tower Shield, and Teleport as established terminology.
- Keep Go Jun's dialogue curt and informal, and Go Se-won's speech deferential toward his superior.

# Chapter 397

## Plot

Choi Minwoo leads the Public Security Armed Forces Department's withdrawal during the monster assault, then turns back with Shao Shen and 325 Hunters to make a final stand protecting the People’s Liberation Army. After the undead army wins and brings two human survivors before the black knight, the black knight hesitates to reap their souls because of the child’s shoe he found. Jin survives the dangerous teleportation, reaches the hospital rooftop, and confronts the black knight.

## Continuity

- The monster army overwhelms the human defense, while Shao Shen orders surviving Hunters and rear units to withdraw through the open North Gate.
- Choi Minwoo and Shao Shen instead lead 325 Hunters in a final stand against the monsters to protect the People’s Liberation Army.
- The black knight has recovered a dirt-covered infant shoe from the previous day’s location, experiences unexplained chest pain, and has begun questioning his own hesitation and obedience.
- The black knight’s forces win the battle and bring two human survivors to him as potential new servants.
- Jin Taekyung survives teleportation and reaches the hospital rooftop, where he directly confronts the black knight.

# Chapter 485

## Plot

Jin Taekyung touches the fissure at the Water God Dragon site and the System identifies it as a mostly nonfunctional Gate: entry is impossible and the Gate Conquest Quest cannot be generated. The Gate leaks faint demonic qi, confirming Taekyung's fears.

Taekyung connects Dark Heaven's regeneration, teleportation, and Moving Formation to magic and wonders whether the Lord of Heaven is linked to the dangerous force from his original world. He tells Jeok Cheongang that he is from another world and that a fully opened Gate could release monsters; Jeok claims to understand, misinterprets Taekyung's confession as a death wish, and beats him. Jeok later approaches Mungyeong, who refuses to talk.

## Continuity

- The fissure at the Water God Dragon site is a mostly nonfunctional Gate that cannot be entered and cannot generate the Gate Conquest Quest.
- Taekyung now suspects that Dark Heaven's regeneration, teleportation, and Moving Formation are magical phenomena connected to the Gate.
- Taekyung disclosed his otherworldly origin and his suspicion that an evil force from his original world is linked to Dark Heaven.
- Jeok Cheongang trusts Taekyung more deeply than anyone else, even while responding to Taekyung's impossible claims with mockery and violence.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 기억의 파편 as Memory Fragment.
- Render 게이트 공략 as Gate Conquest and retain the established System terminology.
- Render 텔레포트 as Teleport and 마법 as Magic.
- Render 시산혈해 as sea of corpses and blood; retain the established Old Master address for 노야 and the rough, profane banter between Taekyung and Jeok.

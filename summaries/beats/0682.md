# Chapter 682

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continues fighting the Great Snow Fiend and Black Hand Fist Demon despite his internal injuries. He deliberately presses forward, allowing a twin wheel to slash his shoulder while he clashes with Black Hand. When the Great Snow Fiend moves in for the killing blow, he instead drives an ice sword into Black Hand's back at the same moment Jin's hand pierces Black Hand's chest and reaches the Great Snow Fiend. Blue-white hellfire burns from the blood-covered strike, Black Hand's body collapses, and an unidentified red armor appears across Jin's upper body.

## Continuity

- Jin deliberately accepted a shoulder wound to create an opening while fighting Black Hand Fist Demon.
- The Great Snow Fiend abandoned the capture order's restraint and attacked Jin with intent to kill.
- The Great Snow Fiend's ice sword pierced Black Hand's back, while Jin's hand pierced through Black Hand's chest and reached the Great Snow Fiend.
- Black Hand Fist Demon's body is collapsing after being pierced by both attacks.
- Blue-white hellfire is burning at the point of Jin's strike, and unidentified red armor covers Jin's upper body.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain the hunter-and-wounded-beast imagery throughout the Great Snow Fiend's internal narration.
- Keep Jin's calm, game-like reference to giving him EXP and render the newly revealed armor as unidentified until its nature is established.

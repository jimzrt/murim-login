# Chapter 144

## Plot

Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao asks Jin Taekyung for an autograph. Taekyung carves Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi, Jin Taekyung into a wooden tablet, adds words encouraging Zhu Bao to persevere, and gives it to him. Zhu Bao plans to enlarge the tablet and hang it on the Shanxi Provincial Office's signboard. Taekyung then tells Zhu Bao about defeating Jopil, Jin Baekyang, and Pung Yang, introduces Cheongpung as Mae Jonghak's disciple, and leaves Zhu Bao enthusiastically questioning Cheongpung.

The City Lord's luncheon quest condition is fulfilled, with its reward deferred until the luncheon ends. Hong Jin and Li Feng ask the Jin Family of Taiyuan to support an Escort Bureau capable of expanding from Shaanxi into the Central Plains, offering half the funding and every possible convenience. They changed their intended partner from Zhongnan Sect to Huashan after learning that Mae Jonghak had remained there and raised successors. Taekyung agrees only to pass the proposal to Jin Wikyung, then suggests taking over the Seongun Escort Bureau by changing its sign.

## Continuity

- Zhu Bao is an earnest young prince and enthusiastic admirer of Jin Taekyung; he wants to emulate Taekyung and plans to display Taekyung's autograph at the Shanxi Provincial Office.
- Jin Taekyung is still below the Peak realm and cannot use Sword Energy, despite having defeated the Peak masters Jopil, Jin Baekyang, and Pung Yang.
- The City Lord's Invitation quest condition, attending the City Lord's luncheon, is fulfilled; its reward is due after the luncheon ends.
- Hong Jin and Li Feng seek the Jin Family of Taiyuan's name and resources for an Escort Bureau expanding from Shaanxi toward the Central Plains; they offer half the funding and maximum official convenience.
- The proposed project shifted from the Zhongnan Sect to Huashan because Hong Jin learned that Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint, had remained at Huashan and raised successors.
- Taekyung will relay the Escort Bureau proposal to Jin Wikyung without endorsing or deciding it himself.
- Taekyung proposes using the Seongun Escort Bureau as the project's base, believing the Jin Family could absorb it with Hong Jin and Li Feng's support.
- Cheongpung is introduced to Zhu Bao as Mae Jonghak's disciple and a Peak master; Zhu Bao becomes eager to learn Sword Energy from him.
- Cheongpung is a twenty-year-old Peak master raised by Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint, and recognized by Li Feng as his Martial Uncle.
- Mae Jonghak remained in deep seclusion at a hidden Huashan residence and had been there at least ten years before the luncheon.
- Li Feng left Huashan after becoming discouraged by Cheongpung's extraordinary talent and later acted as Hong Jin's intermediary with Huashan.
- Hong Jin is a eunuch who has served Prince Shangshan since infancy and is the power behind the Shanxi Provincial Office as Deputy Military Commissioner.
- Gong Ilhyuk left the luncheon humiliated and vowed revenge against those who rejected and insulted him.
- Taekyung encouraged Cheongpung to insult the Zhongnan disciples, and Cheongpung swore for the first time.
- Cheongpung wants to join the royal guard and agreed to call Li Feng Martial Nephew in exchange for royal-guard armor and weapons.
- The identities of the other two members of the Three Hands of Zhongnan remain unknown.
- Taekyung's exact parentage and the meaning of Mae Jonghak's claim that a crane delivered him to Mae remain unclear.

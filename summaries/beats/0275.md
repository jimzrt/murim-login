# Chapter 275

## Plot

After seeing Im Kkeokjeong injured, Jin Taekyung rejects the idea that he was merely naive about the modern world and recognizes that organized power, money, and hidden violence can produce monsters outside the Gates.

At the Guild House, Team Leader Choi and Song Song show Taekyung a coordinated media campaign blaming Won Myunghoon's death on Taekyung and questioning the legal outcome. A preview interview with former Sopung Guild Team Leader Kim Sangshik portrays Taekyung as an ugly, treacherous person who sacrificed his teammates during the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident. Choi and Taekyung conclude that the Black Hunter attack, the smear campaign, and apparent interference with the investigation are connected and intended to isolate Taekyung and the Peace Guild. Butler Kim then reports by phone that he has found the 'tail.'

## Continuity

- Im Kkeokjeong's both arms were severed below the elbows; high-ranking healer recovery magic and advanced potions have completed treatment, but his return as a Hunter remains uncertain.
- Team Leader Choi and Taekyung conclude that the Black Hunters were sent as part of an organized operation, with the hostile public-opinion campaign serving the same effort to isolate Taekyung and the Peace Guild.
- Kim Sangshik, the former Sopung Guild Team Leader who previously caused Taekyung's dismissal, has given a major-media interview accusing Taekyung of abandoning teammates during the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident.
- The investigation into Im Kkeokjeong's attack appears subject to outside pressure: the opposing side possesses the CCTV footage, and all three witnesses have become unreachable.
- Butler Kim reports that he has found 'the tail'; its identity and significance are not yet known.

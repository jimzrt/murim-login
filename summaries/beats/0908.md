# Chapter 908

## Plot

Taekyung reaches the stone wall and uses the power of his Middle Dantian to stop and redirect a volley of arrows. He kills the commander, revealed as the Golden Ox Palace of the Twelve Palaces of the Zodiac and a servant of the Lord of Heaven, then offers the Imperial Guards a chance to return to the imperial court and urges them to eliminate the most committed traitors among themselves. He leaves to rescue the Fire Dragon Pavilion members, who are fleeing a large force of black-clad attackers.

## Continuity

- Taekyung remains injured and in the banquet-hall battle; he has killed Ma Sanbao and now kills the Golden Ox Palace commander.
- Taekyung can use his Middle Dantian’s unknown power to halt and redirect arrows; the commander identifies this power as the Middle Dantian.
- Taekyung offers the Imperial Guards a chance to side with the imperial court and avoid being branded traitors; he tells them to kill the most culpable traitors themselves.
- Fire Dragon Pavilion members are fleeing more than a thousand black-clad attackers, and Taekyung is moving to protect them.
- Jeok Cheongang is fighting Cang Gong, the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord; the outcome remains unknown.
- So Gyo’s identity and allegiance remain unknown.
- The Emperor and Cang Gong have not revealed all their forces; the identity of the black-clad attackers remains unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “Twelve Palaces of the Zodiac” for 황도십이궁 and “Golden Ox Palace” for 금우궁.
- Render 금위군 as “Imperial Guards,” distinct from the Embroidered Uniform Guard.

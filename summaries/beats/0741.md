# Chapter 741

## Plot

Jin, Team Leader Choi, and the Skeleton King travel to the Swiss Alps after Magic Johnson reports that a man connected to A Area has died. They pass through a concealed mana barrier into a cave where Magic explains the Wizard Guild's losses, the fallout from the vigilante operation, and the pressure facing Chuck Hagel. At the end of the cave, they discover the shriveled corpse of the retired Grand Mage who designed A Area, triggering the System to generate a new Quest.

## Continuity

- Magic Johnson is investigating the death of the retired Grand Mage who designed A Area and has brought Jin, Team Leader Choi, and the Skeleton King to the deceased mage's hidden cave in the Alps.
- The deceased A Area designer had been Magic Johnson's friend and had secretly prepared the cave as a future hideout.
- Magic Johnson's Wizard Guild lost its L.A. branch in the coordinated terrorist attacks and suffered civilian casualties.
- The vigilante operation against Middle Eastern terrorists and African rebel groups was carried out with the tacit approval of the United States President but was later exposed by the Prophet.
- Chuck Hagel, the United States Secretary of Defense and a vigilante participant, is under severe internal and external pressure and may be removed from office.
- The ten terrorist attacks and resulting fear have accelerated the rise of mana levels, with mutation Gate phenomena now occurring dozens of times each day.
- Jin believes the thread connecting him to one of the perpetrators has been severed, while he remains prepared to hunt the Prophet and the other attackers.
- The System generated a new Quest immediately after the group discovered the A Area designer's corpse.

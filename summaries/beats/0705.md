# Chapter 705

## Plot

The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is shaken when the three-hundred-warrior Baekcheon Unit arrives under Wang Ho. Baeksang secretly trained the Bai warriors for decades, entrusted their mobilization token to the Beast Miao King, and left instructions that they follow Yayul Cheok and oppose Dark Heaven.

Yayul Cheok rallies roughly thirteen thousand Nanman and Bai warriors after the Beast King Stone awakens another three-thousand-strong force. Wang Ho reports that the Empress's five hundred elites have been annihilated, while the mutants retreat before the sacred stone's power. Yayul orders the united army forward, but the Empress raises a dragon tornado as Jin Taekyung rides the guardian spirit into the confrontation.

## Continuity

- Baeksang secretly trained the three-hundred-warrior Bai Baekcheon Unit in Wenshan and left its jade mobilization token with Yayul Cheok.
- Wang Ho commands the Baekcheon Unit, which has joined Yayul Cheok and the allied Nanman forces.
- The Beast King Stone awakened approximately three thousand Bai warriors, who surrendered and joined Yayul Cheok after hearing Jin Taekyung's warning.
- Yayul Cheok now leads roughly thirteen thousand allied warriors against the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and the mutants.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress's approximately five hundred elite subordinates were annihilated or captured, eliminating her final reserve.
- The mutants instinctively retreat before the light of the sacred stone and recognize that they cannot defeat the approaching coalition.
- The guardian spirit protects the advancing warriors with the sacred stone's light while carrying Jin Taekyung.
- Yayul Cheok orders the allied forces to advance, and the Southern Heaven Demon Empress responds by raising a dragon tornado.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 문산 as Wenshan and 태족 as Dai people.
- Render 주군 as my lord when used by Wang Ho for Yayul Cheok.
- Retain Baekcheon Unit, Beast King Stone, sacred stone, guardian spirit, mutant, and Southern Heaven Demon Empress as established terminology.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's exhausted conversational profanity and the guardian spirit's terse telepathic voice.

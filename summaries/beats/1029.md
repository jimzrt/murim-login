# Chapter 1029

## Plot

After Sama Pyo kills the Third Elder, Jin Taekyung brutally disables the First Elder, who remains alive. The Blood-Sword Demon Lord admires Taekyung’s cruelty and reveals that he has long admired Jeok Cheongang for burning his attackers alive at Mount Jiuhua. As the armies approach, Taekyung realizes the battle has begun; a ghostly force of riders arrives, and he recognizes them as Death Knights.

## Continuity

- Sama Pyo killed the Third Elder; Jin Taekyung killed the Second Elder and has left the First Elder gravely injured but still breathing.
- The Blood-Sword Demon Lord says he has admired Jeok Cheongang since hearing that Jeok’s attackers at Mount Jiuhua were burned alive.
- As the armies converge at the Great Snow Mountain, a force identified by Taekyung as Death Knights arrives.

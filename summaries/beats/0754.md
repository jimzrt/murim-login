# Chapter 754

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continues his aerial battle against Leviathan despite the Broken Body debuff, using Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist and the second form of Fire Dragon Divine Spear, Heavenly Strike, to destroy its water attacks and pierce its brow. Leviathan retaliates with a lightning strike, but the Skeleton King draws the attack and survives because he is made of bones.

Badly wounded and terrified after mistaking Jin for the human who defeated Asmodeus, Leviathan flees toward the deep sea with Jin clinging to its body and attacking it. Jin declares that they can finally fight together, but countless bones gather in the abyss and block Leviathan's path.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's Broken Body debuff has drastically reduced his physical attributes, making his body heavy, slowing his movement, and accelerating his internal-energy consumption.
- Jin Taekyung has driven his flaming spear into Leviathan's brow and remains mounted on its body while continuing the attack.
- The Skeleton King can survive Leviathan's lightning because his skeletal body functions as a lightning rod.
- Leviathan now fears Jin Taekyung as the possible human who defeated its former master, Asmodeus, and is fleeing toward the deep sea.
- An unknown mass of bones has formed in the deep sea and is blocking Leviathan's path as Jin and the Skeleton King prepare to fight together.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 망가진 신체 as Broken Body.
- Render 스켈레톤 킹 as Skeleton King and retain it as a title.

# Chapter 762

## Plot

Michael Silbert arrives in Munich aboard Odin Guild's private aircraft and orders Huginn to leave the gathered reporters in place because he intends to meet Jin Taekyung publicly. Huginn recognizes that public opinion has swung decisively toward Jin after the Leviathan incident and Munich Monster Wave, granting him an invisible pardon that Michael has never obtained.

Michael meets Jin before the cameras, and their apparently cordial handshake becomes a silent contest of strength that reveals Michael possesses unexpectedly immense physical power. During their private exchange, Michael declares that he came to Munich to make an announcement capable of turning the entire world upside down, then faces the cameras to deliver the unexpected bombshell.

## Continuity

- Michael Silbert has arrived in Munich and publicly met Jin Taekyung before a large international press gathering.
- Public opinion now strongly favors Jin Taekyung; even media outlets aligned with Odin Guild have become cautious, while the public's longstanding affection for Jin has survived the recent propaganda campaign.
- Michael has seen the video Huginn obtained in Japan and came to Munich intending to make a world-changing public announcement.
- Michael's physical strength is unexpectedly superhuman, allowing him to match Jin Taekyung's grip despite Jin's System-enhanced body.
- Michael and Jin maintain a hostile private conversation beneath their public performance of friendship, while Michael presents himself as calm, cordial, and confident before the cameras.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 케이프타운 as Cape Town.
- Preserve Michael's private exchanges with Jin as silent, inaccessible communication distinct from their spoken public dialogue.
- Render 면죄부 as pardon in the metaphorical sense of public forgiveness or immunity.

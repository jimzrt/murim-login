# Chapter 719

## Plot

Jin Taekyung accepts the Quest [Corrupted Divine Artifact] after realizing that the demonic qi within the corrupted sacred stone cannot be suppressed indefinitely. Jeok Cheongang proposes destroying it, but Jin stops him because a failed destruction could release the demonic qi and cause another catastrophe. Jin then recalls the guardian spirit and takes Jeok and the Beast Miao King to Ailao Mountain. At the Sacred Land, distinct from the Poisonblood Grounds, he places the artifact on the ground, revealing a hidden radiant space and a brilliant pond. Jin realizes that purification may create a new sacred stone.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has accepted the Quest [Corrupted Divine Artifact].
- The corrupted divine artifact is the former sacred stone, now consumed by demonic qi and still being suppressed by the Beast Miao King.
- Jeok Cheongang proposed destroying the corrupted divine artifact, but Jin Taekyung stopped him because a failed attempt could release the demonic qi.
- The Poisonblood Grounds and the Sacred Land are separate places; the Poisonblood Grounds are a human-created land of death, while the Sacred Land is Nanman's heart and a land of life.
- Placing the corrupted divine artifact at the Sacred Land revealed a hidden radiant space containing a brilliant pond.
- Jin Taekyung believes purification at the Sacred Land may produce a new sacred stone.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 타락한 신물 as Corrupted Divine Artifact and keep it distinct from 신석, sacred stone.
- Render 정화 as Purification.
- Preserve the distinction between 독혈지, Poisonblood Grounds, and 성지, Sacred Land.
- Retain 적 노 as Old Master Jeok and 화왕 as Fire King.

# Chapter 103

## Plot

Jin Taekyung, Jin Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin travel by carriage toward Honju through a blizzard. Mukyung uses a heat-yang technique to warm the frozen Hyuk, then repeatedly strikes the sleeping Taekyung’s forehead until he wakes, prompting a quarrel that Taekyung avoids by ordering Hyuk to keep driving.

After returning to Murim, Taekyung reviews the Quest “Yesterday’s Enemy, Today’s Ally,” which requires him to deliver an invitation to the Mount Heng Sword Sect for the upcoming Lunar New Year. The weakened sect is now led by Lee Seowol, with whom Taekyung has an uncomfortable history stemming from the former false accusation against him.

## Continuity

- Taekyung, Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin are traveling by carriage to Honju, with Hyuk serving as coachman.
- Jin Mukyung is a Peak master who has only superficially learned the heat-yang technique used to warm Hyuk Mujin.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect has nearly collapsed after Lee Seogeun’s poisoning, Lee Cheonbaek’s fall, the battle at Eight Spring Gorge, and raids by roughly two hundred wandering martial artists and mounted bandits; Lee Seogwang was killed defending the sect.
- Lee Seowol is Lee Cheonbaek’s last surviving descendant and the current Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- Taekyung’s current Quest requires him to deliver the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s invitation to the Mount Heng Sword Sect for the upcoming Lunar New Year; it is Grade First Rate, has no failure penalty, and its reward is unknown.
- Taekyung and Lee Seowol’s relationship is thoroughly uncomfortable because their earlier circumstances were connected to the false accusation that Taekyung tried to force himself on her.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 열양공 as heat-yang technique and the related 화기 as fire qi.
- Render 원단 as Lunar New Year.
- Render 초일류 as top-tier First Rate.
- Render 항산검문주 as Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect.

# Chapter 727

## Plot

Choi Minwoo realizes that Jin Taekyung created the stable, beginner-accessible Smiling Mana Cultivation Method and intends to release it to the entire world. He warns that the method's publication will provoke the powerful Hunters and Guilds who guard their own cultivation techniques, while invisible pressure is already delaying Peace Guild's overseas expansion. Jin argues that rising mana, mutation Gates, and monster waves make it necessary to raise Hunters' overall strength, explaining that his recent campaign against rebel groups and terrorists was meant to reduce the danger of the method being misused. With public opinion still favoring them, Jin proposes using Choi's status as Cheon Taemin's sole blood relative and heir to promote the release, joking that they should ride the Savior coin.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung created the stable, beginner-accessible Smiling Mana Cultivation Method and intends to make it publicly available.
- Jin believes the world's increasing mana is strengthening monsters and causing mutation Gates and monster waves, making broader Hunter training necessary.
- Jin recently suppressed rebel groups and terrorist organizations, using information obtained through Magic Johnson and the Pentagon, to reduce potential threats before releasing the method.
- Major Guilds around the world are watching Jin and Choi Minwoo, and invisible pressure has temporarily halted the establishment of additional overseas branches.
- Choi Minwoo is the Peace Guild Master and has taken control of the Ares Guild while benefiting from his status as Cheon Taemin's sole blood relative and heir.
- If conditions worsen substantially, Jin is prepared to reveal the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique to people who have first learned the fundamentals of mana cultivation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 싱글벙글 마나 연공법 as The Smiling Mana Cultivation Method.
- Render 심판의 일주일 as The Week of Judgment.
- Render 구세주 코인 as the Savior coin, preserving Jin's sarcastic promotional metaphor.
- Preserve Jin's profane, self-mocking voice and Choi Minwoo's formal, controlled voice in their debate.

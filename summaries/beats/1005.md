# Chapter 1005

## Plot

After four days of hard travel, the Fire Dragon Pavilion members rest over a feast. Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin bicker over seniority, while Song reflects on the Pavilion’s formidable eccentrics. He then notices that Taishan has stopped eating; Namho, who had been lost in thought, says he recognizes what he smells and asks the others to be quiet.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion members have finished a four-day journey and are resting over a feast.
- Song Ilseom insists on speaking casually to the younger Hyuk Mujin, who objects but continues addressing Song politely.
- Taishan sits motionless before a feast instead of eating, and Namho reacts to a scent that seems to recall a recent memory.

# Chapter 264

## Plot

Cheongpung joins Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang against the Blood Lord, who recognizes him as Huashan's Invincible Divine Sword and knows that he is Sword Saint Mae Jonghak's Disciple and grandson. The Blood Lord challenges them to prove their strength through martial arts, then effortlessly splits Jeok's Flame Divine Palm at Great Completion and repels Taekyung and Cheongpung while fighting with overwhelming power comparable to or greater than the Three Saints.

After revealing that he had yielded three forms in imitation of orthodox courtesy, the Blood Lord launches his true attack. Taekyung uses the System to resummon White Flame after the Blood Lord catches it, piercing his Body-Protecting Qi and chest. Taekyung calls for Jeok Cheongang, who unleashes the Dance of the Fire God and Demon, a technique he had previously used only once to kill a thousand Demonic Cultists.

Jeok's final strike leaves the Blood Lord kneeling and apparently motionless, but the exertion causes Jeok to stagger. After Taekyung and Cheongpung move him to safety, the Blood Lord rises as if unharmed and comments admiringly on the name of Jeok's technique.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung has joined Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang in their confrontation with the Blood Lord at Mount Song.
- The Blood Lord knows Cheongpung's identity as Huashan's Invincible Divine Sword and as Sword Saint Mae Jonghak's Disciple and grandson.
- The Blood Lord's martial power is sufficient to split Jeok Cheongang's Flame Divine Palm at Great Completion and may equal or exceed the Three Saints.
- Taekyung used an Inventory resummoning trick to return White Flame to his hand and pierce the Blood Lord's Body-Protecting Qi and chest.
- Jeok Cheongang used the Dance of the Fire God and Demon after Taekyung created an opening; the technique had previously been used only once, to kill a thousand Demonic Cult members.
- The Blood Lord rose apparently unharmed after receiving Jeok Cheongang's final attack, so the confrontation's outcome remains unresolved.

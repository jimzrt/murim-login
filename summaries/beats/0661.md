# Chapter 661

## Plot

Baeksang reveals that twenty tribal chieftains, including himself, have united to place Jin Taekyung on the Tribal Grand Council's final agenda and execute him at noon in two days. He explains that the coalition was bought with promises of tribal revival, influence, and wealth, then recounts the war more than fifty years earlier, when he and the Beast Miao King led ten thousand warriors alongside the Zhongnan Sect against the Demonic Cult. Fewer than one-tenth survived, and after the Martial God defeated the Heavenly Demon, Baeksang's forces joined the pursuit of the Demonic Cult's remnants. Jin identifies Baeksang's deceased only child as Baekhwi, provoking Baeksang into revealing that their history began at Gansu's Great Snow Mountain while leading the Southern Army with the Zhongnan Sect.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains imprisoned in the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison, bound by iron balls and unable to use internal energy because of the Force-Sealing Pill; his public execution is scheduled for noon in two days.
- Baeksang has secured the support of twenty tribal chieftains and is using Jin's execution as the final-day agenda of the Tribal Grand Council.
- Baeksang's only child was named Baekhwi; Baekhwi is deceased and would have become the Beast Miao King's son-in-law if he had lived.
- Baeksang and the Beast Miao King once led ten thousand Nanman warriors alongside the Zhongnan Sect in the Southern Army during the war against the Demonic Cult; fewer than one-tenth survived.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion was attacked after Jin left, Dark Heaven is suspected of organizing or enabling the assault, and Heugung and Yohi remain missing after the destruction of Yohi's Western Yao Estate.
- Chief Jang and Chief Go are secretly keeping the three Han Chinese reconnaissance-squad captives away from the Inner Palace, while Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin remain bound and unconscious and Ju Hwaran remains among the captives.
- The reconnaissance squad is traveling through Nanman while guarding against the Blood Monk, and Jin remains committed to protecting his people.

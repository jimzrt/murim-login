# Chapter 770

## Plot

The Skeleton King accepts that humanity will reject him as a monster and asks Jin Taekyung to erase him publicly, leaving Jin trapped between sacrificing his friend and allowing Michael Silbert to seize everything. Jin instead chooses a third path: he will kill Michael, then have Team Leader Choi use his allies and Cheon Taemin's legacy to take control of the reestablished World Hunter Federation, but Choi draws the Hero's Sword to stop him from leaving and their staged confrontation begins before a mysterious vibration interrupts them.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton King acknowledges that he is a monster and proposes that Jin Taekyung erase him in front of everyone to prevent Michael Silbert from exploiting his identity.
- Jin Taekyung regards the Skeleton King as both a comrade and a friend and cannot accept sacrificing him merely for the greater good.
- Team Leader Choi insists that Jin must choose, then refuses to let Jin confront Michael alone by drawing the Hero's Sword.
- Jin chooses a third path: kill Michael Silbert, then have Team Leader Choi take control of the reestablished World Hunter Federation with support from Magic Johnson, Chuck Hagel, Pai Chen, Prince Felix, allied S-rank Hunters, and friendly nations.
- A faint vibration interrupts the confrontation between Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi before they can fight.

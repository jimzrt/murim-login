# Chapter 327

## Plot

Ju Hwaran confronts Song Ilseom over records showing that ten of the fifteen escort missions under his responsibility failed during the past two years. Song recognizes the evidence, acknowledges that she has exceeded his expectations, and reveals that he deliberately concealed his true ability; Hwaran then identifies Chief Escort Heo Jun as the real traitor and attacks him.

Heo Jun admits that he accepted bribes and secretly colluded with the Zhongnan Sect for two years. He drugged the bureau's food with 몽혼제, stole the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng, and helped sabotage the Yongbong Escort Bureau while concealing his actions behind fabricated mistakes.

Heo Jun begs for his life and calls his betrayal a mistake, but Ju Hwaran rejects that excuse, declaring that his actions were choices carrying responsibility. She separates herself from her father's identity as Junzi Sword Ju Hogun, claims the identity Dagger Hidden Flower Ju Hwaran, and strikes at Heo Jun again.

## Continuity

- Heo Jun's betrayal of the Yongbong Escort Bureau and two-year collusion with the Zhongnan Sect are exposed; he accepted bribes, stole thousands of silver nyang, drugged the escorts, and replaced the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng.
- Song Ilseom was targeted for execution and false blame by Heo Jun, but he is not the traitor; he concealed exceptional strength for ten years and is assessed by Jin Taekyung as stronger than the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes.
- Ju Hwaran has stabbed Heo Jun and rejected their former uncle-and-niece bond, but his fate after her final sword strike remains unresolved.

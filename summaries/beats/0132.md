# Chapter 132

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin meet the exceptionally young Peak master Cheongpung, who begs them for candied hawthorn skewers because he has not eaten all day. Taekyung feeds him a full meal at Honghwa Inn, where Cheongpung reveals that he was raised by his grandfather in the mountains from age five and came down to test himself against the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes. Five silk-clad men and women on the inn's second floor then look down at the group and laugh mockingly.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung is an exceptionally young Peak master whose Level Jin Taekyung cannot determine through Qi Sense.
- Cheongpung lived with his grandfather in the mountains from age five; his grandfather relocates once or twice a year because strange people keep finding him.
- Cheongpung descended from the mountains to discover whether he or the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes were stronger.
- Five silk-clad men and women on Honghwa Inn's second floor mocked Taekyung, Mujin, and Cheongpung; their identities are unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 은인 as Benefactor and 공자 as Young Master in Cheongpung and Mujin's forms of address.
- Render 개방 as Beggars' Sect.
- Render 산니백육 as Garlic Pork, 어향육사 as Fish-Fragrant Shredded Pork, 경장육사 as Beijing Sauce Shredded Pork, and 규화계 as Beggar's Chicken.
- Render 매구 as Maegu and 매채구육 as Maechae Guyuk, with a footnote explaining the waiter's abbreviation and the dish.

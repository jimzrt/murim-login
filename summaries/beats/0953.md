# Chapter 953

## Plot

Jin Mukyung returns from a successful strike: his slightly more than two hundred Heaven Shaking Squad members annihilated a thousand enemy vanguard troops, including one hundred elite soldiers, at the cost of twenty-three dead and thirty-seven wounded. Jin Wikyung’s scorched-earth preparations, defenses at Eight Spring Gorge, and the support of thousands of commoners ready to help protect Shanxi are revealed; their loyalty stems in part from the Jin Family’s past aid during famine. As a rider arrives carrying a dead Jin Family martial artist, Jin Wikyung orders everyone to prepare for battle: the enemy has arrived.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung has returned after annihilating the enemy vanguard; the Heaven Shaking Squad suffered twenty-three deaths and thirty-seven wounded.
- Jin Wikyung has evacuated people and troops from northern Shanxi, ordered the near-harvest fields burned, and prepared traps and iron caltrops along detours other than Jeongyang.
- Five earthen forts are being built at Eight Spring Gorge; thousands of commoners have joined the defensive preparations, many out of gratitude for the Jin Family’s past aid.
- The enemy has arrived at Eight Spring Gorge; Jin Wikyung has ordered the defenders to prepare for battle.
- The Emperor remains gravely ill from Blood Soul Gu, and the Divine Physician says the only path to saving him requires him to die once; Taekyung’s quest to remove the Gu and treat him remains unresolved.
- Jang Sam remains unconscious after his sudden rise in level and attack on Taekyung; the source and effects of the improved Temporary Strength Pill remain unknown.
- The Martial God’s identity and connection to the chosen one and the Bow Saint remain unknown.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord’s papers and silk pouch remain unexplained; the real Chinggen was killed, and an impostor wearing his face continues to accompany Jamukha.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 토성 as “earthen fort” and 철질려 as “iron caltrops.” The carpenter’s remarks in this chapter address Jin Wikyung, not Jin Taekyung.

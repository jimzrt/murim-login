# Chapter 656

## Plot

Baeksang publicly identifies Jin as the culprit behind the Western Yao Estate massacre and turns the gathering into a trial, while Jin refuses to yield the initiative and asserts his innocence.

The Beast Miao King arrives and questions Jin about the tragedy; Jin denies any connection, and the Beast Miao King and Yayul Mok confirm that Jin's quarters were attacked by unidentified assassins. Baeksang counters by emphasizing the apparent contradiction that such assassins were strong enough to threaten a Supreme Peak master.

Baeksang establishes an alibi supported by fifteen chieftains and one hundred warriors, then asks Jin where he was. Namho secretly signals Jin to say nothing, since revealing his meeting with Heugung cannot be proved. Baeksang then summons Utu-ri, the old owner of the noodle shop, who arrives at the Western Yao Estate gate.

## Continuity

- Baeksang has publicly accused Jin Taekyung of being the culprit behind the Inner Palace tragedy and has presented a broad faction-backed alibi for himself.
- The Beast Miao King and Yayul Mok publicly confirm that Jin's residence was attacked by unidentified assassins shortly after the banquet.
- Namho warns Jin through concealed finger-writing not to disclose his meeting with Heugung because the meeting cannot currently be verified.
- Baeksang has summoned Utu-ri, the old noodle-shop owner, as a witness or source of evidence before the assembled chieftains.

# Chapter 564

## Plot

The Peace Guild's rapid growth has made its Guild House too cramped, so Team Leader Choi has bought the surrounding properties and begun a massive expansion using hundreds of Awakened workers.

Magic Johnson visits and discusses the Blue House press conference's attack on Go Jun's legitimacy, the roughly ten Ares Guild members who have transferred to Peace, and the secret training room reserved for Jin Taekyung and the Guild's founding members. He gives Jin a chip for Team Leader Choi and reports that mana levels worldwide have risen seven percent over the previous year.

## Continuity

- The Peace Guild is expanding its Guild House after its membership outgrew the existing facility.
- Go Jun's legitimacy as Lee Jungryong's successor has been publicly undermined, and roughly ten Ares Guild members have transferred to Peace Guild, visibly exposing cracks in Ares's authority.
- The secured training room is restricted to Jin Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong for training in the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique.
- Magic Johnson gives Jin a chip to deliver to Team Leader Choi and reports that worldwide mana levels are seven percent higher than the previous year.

# Chapter 645

## Plot

Baeksang attacked Jin Taekyung during the tribal grand council, but Jin defeated him while remaining seated and forced him to withdraw after the Beast Miao King intervened. Baeksang's public humiliation deepened the political divide, with Yohi, Heugung, and nearly half the chieftains leaving with the pro-Baeksang faction.

The Beast Miao King ordered Chief Jang and Chief Go to organize scouts toward Guizhou because of the Blood Monk threat. Back at the quarters, Namho correctly guessed that Jin had caused a disturbance, while Ju Hwaran and the others humorously defended him before Jin admitted that a minor problem had occurred.

## Continuity

- Baeksang attacked Jin Taekyung with Finger Qi and direct physical force, but Jin overpowered him with Flame Divine Palm and superior strength while remaining seated.
- The Beast Miao King stopped the confrontation and ordered both men to stand down; Baeksang apologized and withdrew with Yohi, Heugung, and nearly half of the thirty-two chieftains.
- Jin's actions have made more than ten thousand Bai people hostile to him, and the pro-Baeksang faction now represents roughly half of the Nanman chieftains.
- The tribal grand council continues for three days alongside the festival; a memorial stele and commemorative banquet for the dead will be held tonight before the council resumes.
- The Beast Miao King ordered Chief Jang and Chief Go to form a fast scouting party and monitor the route toward Guizhou because of the Blood Monk and the possibility of Dark Heaven involvement.
- Approximately two hundred elite warriors of Ailao Mountain remain trapped in the Thousand-Year Spider webs, which appear to shield them from the Poison Mist.
- The missing ferocious beasts have not been found in the Poisonblood Grounds, the purpose of Ailao Mountain's Wraith remains unknown, and the nature of the pure-white eggs remains unknown.
- An unidentified entity who recognizes Jin Taekyung has killed two informants, while the Blood Monk's destination and possible connection to Dark Heaven remain unknown.

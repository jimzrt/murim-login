# Chapter 447

## Plot

Taekyung's group travels toward Donghu Stronghold aboard four fast ships with elite Zhuge Clan and Wudang disciples, including the Supreme Peak master Perfected Being Hyeongong. The ships reach Tianling Falls, a massive whirlpool guarding the route, and Taekyung learns that Mu Song has crossed it only three or four times and that Zhuge Feng deliberately concealed this. As the fast ship enters the whirlpool, Mu Song orders everyone to hold on and Tianling Falls slams into the vessel.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's group is traveling toward Donghu Stronghold aboard four Water Dragon Stronghold fast ships accompanied by elite Zhuge Clan and Wudang disciples.
- Perfected Being Hyeongong is a veteran Wudang Supreme Peak master, the current Sect Leader's Junior Brother, and a master of the Taiji Wisdom Sword's ultimate stage.
- Tianling Falls is a deadly whirlpool and narrow passage that serves as Donghu Stronghold's natural defense; the group has reached it and the fast ship has been struck by its current.
- Mu Song has crossed Tianling Falls only three or four times and is relying on the League's fast ships and his crew's skill to navigate it.
- Zhuge Feng intentionally concealed Mu Song's limited experience with Tianling Falls from the other passengers.

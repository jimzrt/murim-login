# Chapter 1133

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens after his heart and innate qi recover, and the allied forces celebrate their victory over the Blood Lord and Grand Mage. Seeing bone birds fly west, he realizes Ma Sanbao has remained at Mount Kunlun to prepare a strike through the Moving Formation against the Central Plains. Before Taekyung can respond, Mae Jonghak uses a Pressure-Point Strike to make him rest. At Kunlun, Ma Sanbao receives the birds’ report and sets out for the Central Plains with his subordinates.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has recovered and awakened after another Bone Transformation; Mae Jonghak disables him with a Pressure-Point Strike so he can rest.
- The Blood Lord and Grand Mage died in the battle at Qinghai; the allied forces won, but Ma Sanbao remained at Mount Kunlun.
- Ma Sanbao intends to use a Moving Formation at Mount Kunlun to attack the Central Plains, and departs with his subordinates after receiving the bone birds’ report.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이동진 as “Moving Formation,” 선천진기 as “innate qi,” and 점혈 as “Pressure-Point Strike.”

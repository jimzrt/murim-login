# Chapter 425

## Plot

The Arch Lich tortures Jin with controlled healing until Hero’s Soul pierces its chest and reconstructs the destroyed Skeleton Warlord as a Lv.160 Skeleton King. Hero’s Soul then fully heals Jin and restores his attributes, allowing him to recover White Flame and launch One Annihilation at the Arch Lich and the enormous Gate together; the chapter ends with the blue fire dragon engulfing both.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton Warlord was not erased; Hero’s Soul reconstructed it as a larger, stronger Lv.160 Skeleton King with a crown-like silver mark.
- The Skeleton King regards Jin as a friend and ally, and Jin accepts it as his friend.
- Hero’s Soul’s radiance is consuming the Arch Lich’s mana and body after piercing its chest, but the Arch Lich’s final fate is unresolved.
- Hero’s Soul removed all of Jin’s status effects, healed all injuries, and restored all attributes.
- Jin recovered White Flame and launched One Annihilation against both the Arch Lich and the enormous Gate.
- The Gate was nearing completion and was strengthening the Arch Lich before One Annihilation struck it.
- The Quest One Who Returned from Death remains active, keeping Login unavailable until the Quest ends.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 스켈레톤 킹 as Skeleton King and 골골이 as Bones.
- Render 영웅의 혼 as Hero’s Soul and 치유의 빛 as the light of healing.
- Preserve the Arch Lich’s archaic, contemptuous register and Jin’s profanity.

# Chapter 92

## Plot

Im Chunsoo receives a report on Jin Taekyung and orders the Sangdong Guild's Team 1 Leader to verify it, suspecting either inaccurate information, coordinated lies, an unconfirmed A-rank Hunter, or a third-awakening Hunter. He orders the Guild Audit Team to expand Taekyung's surveillance while leaving Peace Guild's protected Guild Master and Team Leader alone. At home, Taekyung and Hayeon discover that several unusually inactive flies are Level 1 familiars when Taekyung extends his Qi Sense through the house.

## Continuity

- Sangdong Guild's Team 1 Leader is its only A-rank Hunter besides Im Chunsoo and serves as Chunsoo's loyal right hand.
- The Team 1 Leader doubts the report that Taekyung, recently reawakened as a C-rank Hunter, defeated a group of B-rank Minotaurs and killed the Minotaur Warrior boss with one strike.
- Im Chunsoo orders additional surveillance of Taekyung through the Sangdong Guild Audit Team, despite Hong Woojin's warning not to interfere with his investigation.
- Peace Guild's Guild Master and Team Leader remain unidentified to Sangdong Guild; their personal and account information is protected by a security Lock reportedly imposed by an upper agency.
- Taekyung's Qi Sense reaches a radius of seventy meters and identifies three Level 1 fly familiars in the house.

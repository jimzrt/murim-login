# Chapter 717

## Plot

Jin Taekyung learns that the Nanman leadership abandoned its search for the missing Beast Miao King after Jeok Cheongang ordered them to calm the people and promised to intervene if the Palace Lord did not return by midnight. Jin, having fully recovered after seven days and nights, secretly leaves the pavilion and locates the Beast Miao King in a dilapidated shrine within the Outer Palace.

The Seven Miao Tigers recognize Jin as Nanman's benefactor, and Wonhu allows him through despite the Beast Miao King's order to admit no one. Jin confronts the Beast Miao King about his disappearance and learns that he is suffering unbearable grief over failing to protect the Nanman tribespeople and his sworn brother, while questioning whether he is qualified to remain Palace Lord.

## Continuity

- The Nanman Beast Palace is still being rebuilt after the rift disaster; the demonic qi is gone, but the deaths, displacement, and fear remain.
- The Beast Miao King is hiding in a dilapidated shrine in the Outer Palace rather than being missing from Nanman entirely.
- Jeok Cheongang halted the search for the Beast Miao King and ordered the Nanman leadership to stabilize public sentiment, promising to act after midnight if the Beast Miao King had not appeared.
- Jin Taekyung has recovered fully after seven days and nights of rest and a level-up.
- The Beast Miao King is grieving his failure to protect the Nanman tribespeople and Baeksang, and he doubts his qualifications as Palace Lord.
- Wonhu and the Seven Miao Tigers remember Jin as the benefactor who saved them in the underground prison.

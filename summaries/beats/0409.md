# Chapter 409

## Plot

Before dawn, Team Leader Choi visits Jin Taekyung to warn him that the operation against the Arch Lich is dangerous because Lee Jungryong and Wu Heixing will be involved. Jin jokes about Choi's martial talent, dismisses the concern, and tells him to prepare for the battle that will begin before the day ends. After Choi leaves, Jin argues with the reluctant Skeleton Warlord, whose skull receives a small crack when Jin flicks it.

At Suining City, the Western Front advances toward the fog-covered city while the other allied fronts surround it. Jin uses humor to ease the soldiers' tension, then delivers an indirect exhortation to keep fighting without giving up. As corpses rise, Skeletons emerge, and monsters move within the fog, Jin announces that the battle has begun.

## Continuity

- The Western Front's thousands of Hunters and soldiers are advancing on fog-shrouded Suining City while the other allied fronts surround it.
- The battle for Suining City begins as corpses rise, Skeletons emerge, and monsters move within the encroaching fog.
- Jin Taekyung expects defeating the Arch Lich to be the key to stopping the undead threat.
- Jin Taekyung has three jiazi of internal energy and can recover from ordinary fatigue by circulating his qi, reducing his need for sleep.
- Team Leader Choi deeply trusts Jin Taekyung but fears the operation because Lee Jungryong and Wu Heixing are involved as dangerous, untrustworthy comrades.
- Team Leader Choi is a rapidly advancing martial artist with exceptional martial talent and substantial internal energy accumulated in his body.
- Jin Taekyung uses humor and a speech infused with internal energy to ease the Western Front's tension and urge the soldiers not to give up.
- The Skeleton Warlord remains Jin Taekyung's captive and reluctant companion, and its skull now has a small crack after Jin flicked it.

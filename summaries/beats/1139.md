# Chapter 1139

## Plot

The Son of Heaven survived by accepting the White Illusion Jiangshi Art and arrives in Xining with a hundred thousand Imperial Guards as the Murim forces gather. Jin Taekyung and Mae Jonghak agree that the fallen heroes’ blood debt must be repaid to the Lord of Heaven. Three days after waking, Taekyung welcomes the Emperor, who says he should now be called Prince Shangshan.

## Continuity

- The Son of Heaven used the White Illusion Jiangshi Art to survive and has arrived in Xining with a hundred thousand Imperial Guards.
- Murim forces from across the realm have gathered in Xining; their shared goal is to repay the blood debt to the Lord of Heaven.
- Jin Taekyung identifies Cheon Taemin as the Martial God.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 上山王 as “Prince Shangshan,” the title the Emperor says Jin Taekyung should now bear.

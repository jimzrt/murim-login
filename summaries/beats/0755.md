# Chapter 755

## Plot

The newly enthroned Skeleton King reveals his crown and commands the bones and wraiths of countless dead marine creatures, forming an undead legion that blocks Leviathan and attacks it with Raise Skeleton while Jin Taekyung continues inflicting severe injuries.

Leviathan breaks free and charges toward a seabed cliff to escape, but Jin's Aquatic Rescue Worker Title expires during the pursuit, removing his underwater adaptations. After Leviathan crashes into the cliff and escapes Bone Binding, Jin launches a final hellfire-infused spear and hears Leviathan scream.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton King commands a vast undead marine legion and possesses power distinct from ordinary magical power.
- Jin Taekyung and the Skeleton King are jointly pursuing and attacking Leviathan in the deep sea.
- The Aquatic Rescue Worker Title, Webbed Feet, and Gills have expired; reactivation is unavailable for 6 days, 23 hours, and 59 seconds.
- Jin is seeking repayment for the more than one hundred thousand casualties caused by the disaster and has launched a final spear attack against Leviathan.
- Leviathan has been struck by the final spear and screamed, but its survival is unresolved.

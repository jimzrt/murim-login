# Chapter 348

## Plot

The Western Heaven Demon Lord gathers hundreds of black-robed hunters and receives the submission of the Qilian Three Fiends. He reveals that the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun are dead, their lives costing him one arm, and declares that the time has come to begin his plans.

On the fourth day of Jeok Cheongang's treatment, Jin Taekyung mentally spars with Cheongpung before luring him away with rumors of an imperial chef. Taekyung then feeds the prisoners in the underground prison and learns that the quietest prisoner, the Heavenly Power Demon, is the man who urinated on the Kunlun Sect's signboard.

## Continuity

- The fifteen-day treatment of Jeok Cheongang is on its fourth day, and Jin Taekyung continues guarding him in the underground prison.
- The Qilian Three Fiends have submitted to the Western Heaven Demon Lord, who has killed the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun at the cost of one arm.
- The Heavenly Power Demon is imprisoned in the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison with severed limb sinews and unusually heavy restraints.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 기련산 as Qilian Mountains, 기련삼괴 as Qilian Three Fiends, 삼괴 as Three Fiends, and 삼노 as Three Old Men.
- Render 난화수 as Chaotic Flower Hand, 천하일단 as One Cut Under Heaven, and 매화삼릉검 as Plum Blossom Three-Ridge Sword.
- Render 채양보음 descriptively as harvesting yang to replenish yin.

# Chapter 1108

## Plot

Cheongpung survives being buried in the blast and stealthily crosses the battlefield using the Slaughter Saint’s Ghost Illusory Slaughter Step, which he has mastered to nine-tenths and blended with his Dark Fragrance Drift. He strikes the Black Ghost as Jin Taekyung unleashes One Annihilation, but Taekyung collapses, and the System gives no death confirmation. The Blood Lord survives the flames with his senses and memories gone; driven by thirst, he absorbs blood and bites a subordinate.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung survived the blast, reached the Black Ghost, and struck it as Taekyung unleashed One Annihilation.
- Jin Taekyung collapsed after unleashing One Annihilation; the System did not confirm the enemy’s death.
- The Blood Lord survived but woke without his memories or senses, driven by an overwhelming thirst for blood; he began feeding on a subordinate.
- Cheongpung has learned the Slaughter Saint’s Ghost Illusory Slaughter Step and adapted it alongside Dark Fragrance Drift.

# Chapter 609

## Plot

Jin Taekyung infiltrates the bedroom of Muhammad Saladir ad-Din, leader of a massive Islamic terrorist organization, disguised as a red-yarn-masked assassin calling himself a back scratcher. He kills Muhammad's elite bodyguards, reveals his identity, and uses the threat of execution to force Muhammad to order an immediate all-out war against a rival armed terrorist organization.

Jin, Chuck Hagel, Magic Johnson, and their allies continue their covert campaign across the desert, fighting or secretly dismantling terrorist and rebel groups, capturing their leaders, and controlling some with Magic Johnson's custom brainwashing Magic. Their bounty rises from ten million to thirty million and then fifty million dollars, and one week passes as the campaign proceeds.

## Continuity

- Muhammad Saladir ad-Din is under Jin Taekyung's control and has been forced to order his subordinate branches into total war against a rival armed terrorist organization.
- Chuck Hagel is a large American operative allied with Jin Taekyung who uses illusion Magic, carries a cigar, and participates directly in the covert anti-terror campaign.
- Jin Taekyung's team is conducting a fast-moving campaign against terrorist groups and rebel forces, capturing or controlling their leaders across the desert.
- Kasim, Muhammad's trusted S-rank Hunter assassin and bodyguard, and bodyguards Omari, Sadat, and Nasser have been killed.
- Omar al-Hussein, leader of a Sunni faction, has been captured during the campaign.
- The campaign has continued for one week, with the team's bounty reaching fifty million dollars.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 효자손 as “back scratcher” and retain the explanatory footnote for the literal Korean wordplay.
- Use Muhammad Saladir ad-Din for 무함마드 살라디르 앗 딘.
- Use Chuck Hagel for 척 헤이글.
- Render 수니파 as “Sunni,” not “Sooni.”
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's taunting profanity and switch to a cold, condemning tone when he denounces Muhammad.

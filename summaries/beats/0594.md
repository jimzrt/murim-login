# Chapter 594

## Plot

Jin Taekyung believes he has killed Go Jun, but discovers that Go Jun regenerated after absorbing multiple S-grade Magic Gems and transformed into a grotesque hybrid monster with Troll-like recovery. Jin brutally exhausts the mutation's regenerative power, decapitates Go Jun, and finally receives the System's kill chime, yet his revenge leaves him hollow rather than relieved.

## Continuity

- Go Jun's apparent death was temporary because the multiple S-grade Magic Gems he absorbed triggered a mutation combining Troll-like regeneration with traits from another monster.
- Jin Taekyung finally killed and decapitated the mutated Go Jun in Area A; the System confirmed the final death.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Troll for 트롤, Mutation for 변이, and Named Monster for 네임드 몬스터.

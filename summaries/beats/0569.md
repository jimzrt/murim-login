# Chapter 569

## Plot

Go Se-won escorts Song Cheonwoo from Go Jun's office after the abduction of Song's children. Song warns that Go Jun has become a monster and begs Se-won to prevent the children from dying pointlessly before teleporting away; Se-won is left convinced that Go Jun intends to eliminate Song naturally.

Jin Taekyung has spent up to three days isolated in the training room developing an unfinished new martial endeavor. The Skeleton King arrives in a Captain America-inspired disguise and asks for Taekyung's help with an abnormal Mutated Gate registering enormous magic power.

## Continuity

- Go Se-won personally ordered and cleaned up the abduction of Song Cheonwoo's family, though he is increasingly troubled by Go Jun's methods.
- Song Cheonwoo has been teleported away after asking Go Se-won to save his children; Go Jun's plan appears to be Song's quiet, natural-looking elimination.
- Taekyung's isolated training project is nearly complete but remains unfinished.
- The Skeleton King reports an abnormal Mutated Gate with an enormous magic power reading and requests Taekyung's assistance.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 세종 기지 as King Sejong Station.
- Render 정도 in this moral context as “a line a person should not cross,” and 마력 수치 as “magic power reading.”

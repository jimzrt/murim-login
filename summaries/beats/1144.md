# Chapter 1144

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin recalls that the pocket watch’s hand once pointed to a different position. Taekyung realizes it has moved backward and may have circled one or more times; the watch’s updated Item information says it holds a secret. He suspects The Helper knew about the watch because he was the Martial God, and further suspects that the Martial God was Cheon Taemin, whose consciousness may have been preserved by the System in the Inventory. Taekyung tells Jeok Cheongang he has something to discuss.

After the banquet, Mae Jonghak addresses the gathered forces in Xining and declares their war against the Lord of Heaven a fight to protect the realm. More than two hundred thousand troops begin marching toward a cursed forbidden land beyond the desert. Taekyung decides this is his only chance to resolve his doubts, exchanges a brief Sound Transmission with Jeok Cheongang, and leaves in a prepared carriage before dawn. He falls into a deep sleep while Jeok Cheongang waits with the others.

## Continuity

- More than two hundred thousand Murim forces have left Xining and are marching toward a cursed forbidden land beyond the desert to fight the Lord of Heaven.
- Taekyung suspects The Helper was the Martial God and that the Martial God was Cheon Taemin; he theorizes the System preserved Cheon’s consciousness in the Inventory.
- The pocket watch’s hour hand has moved backward from the position Hyuk Mujin remembers; its updated Item information says it holds a secret.
- Taekyung has left Xining asleep in a prepared carriage to pursue an unspecified plan; Jeok Cheongang is waiting for him with the others.

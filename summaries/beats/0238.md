# Chapter 238

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang arrive at the Nangong Family estate in Anhui for the Azure Sky Sword King's birthday celebration. Jeok explains that the Azure Sky Sword King is an unpredictable swordsman obsessed with martial arts who once spared Jangcheon in exchange for a spar with Jeok; Jeok won by half a form, after which the Sword King entered seclusion.

Taekyung crosses the estate while still wearing the chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls from his year of training, leaving deep tracks and attracting comments that he looks like a prisoner or an Elder of the Beggars' Sect. Ten Peak guards stop him at a pavilion and demand that he disarm, but Taekyung explains that only Jeok has the key and that removing the chains would interrupt his training.

When the youngest guard threatens to cut off the chains in accordance with Nangong law, Namgung Ryong projects his internal-energy voice across the area and orders the guards to let Taekyung inside.

## Continuity

- The Nangong Family is one of the Five Great Families, the hegemon of Anhui, and possesses more than one hundred blood relatives and thousands of subordinate martial artists.
- The Azure Sky Sword King is the Grand Family Head of the Nangong Family. Jeok Cheongang has met him twice, owes him a great debt, and considers him unpredictable and completely devoted to the sword.
- The Azure Sky Sword King wanted to spare Jangcheon in exchange for a spar with Jeok Cheongang; Jeok won by half a form, and the Azure Sky Sword King entered seclusion the following day.
- Jeok Cheongang's age-related illness has eased but continues to progress, and Taekyung recognizes that even a Supreme Peak master cannot completely halt the effects of old age.
- Taekyung remains publicly recognized as Jeok Cheongang's Disciple and heir to the Fire Gate Clan's orthodox lineage after completing a year of training in the Fire Gate Cavern.
- Taekyung is still bound to chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls; the key is held by Jeok Cheongang, and the chains cannot be removed during his current training.
- Ten Peak martial artists guard the pavilion where the Nangong Family Head receives Taekyung.
- Namgung Ryong personally orders the guards to admit Taekyung after they attempt to enforce the Nangong Family's disarmament rule.

# Chapter 352

## Plot

A bloodied Green Shadow Squad martial artist warns Tang Sadok that at least three hundred enemies have entered Chengdu disguised as government troops and are approaching the Sichuan Tang Clan.

Tang Sadok orders the entire Tang Clan to mobilize, including retired elders, untrained craftsmen, and women, and commands that everyone receive poison and hidden weapons.

Remembering the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath, Tang Sadok rejects his nephew's confidence, sounds the Tang War Drum, and summons the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders as the clan faces possible destruction.

Tang Sadok realizes that the messenger is the one-armed enemy, who transforms from a young Green Shadow Squad martial artist into the middle-aged Western Heaven Demon Lord and claims he uses demonic martial arts.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord admits that he lost his left arm while fighting Poison King Tang Taesang, while Tang Sadok vows to kill him; Tang Sadok and the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders confront him as the chapter ends.

## Continuity

- At least three hundred attackers entered Chengdu in government-troop disguises, divided into several groups to avoid suspicion.
- Tang Sadok has ordered a total Tang Clan mobilization and distributed poison and hidden weapons to every member regardless of age, sex, occupation, or martial training.
- The Tang Clan's Ten Wonders have assembled at Tang Sadok's command.
- The Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion is Tang Sadok's only nephew; his father, Tang Sadok's only older brother, died during the Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord infiltrated the Tang Clan disguised as a Green Shadow Squad martial artist and can alter his body through an unexplained demonic martial art.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 경월년 혈사 as Gyeongwol Year Bloodbath.
- Render 당전고 as Tang War Drum and 당문고 as Tang Clan drum, preserving the source distinction.
- Render 당문십기 as Tang Clan's Ten Wonders.
- Render 일원, 삼당, 오대 as One Origin, Three Divisions, and Five Squads.

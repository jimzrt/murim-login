# Chapter 933

## Plot

As Jin Taekyung is escorted to Qianqing Palace, the Embroidered Uniform Guards honor him for fighting alongside them and bid him farewell. He finds the Emperor gravely weakened and realizes he has been using poppy. The Emperor reveals that a trusted loyalist poisoned him with the Blood Soul Gu shortly after the coup more than ten years ago; the poison has reached his marrow, and it killed the imperial family members who preceded him. Baek Yeon and So Gyo knew the secret, and Taekyung is now the third person besides them to learn it. The Emperor refuses a physician’s help and says he has no regrets after enduring more than ten years.

## Continuity

- The Emperor says a trusted loyalist poisoned him with the Blood Soul Gu shortly after the coup; it has reached his marrow, and he believes no one can escape its effects.
- Before Taekyung learned the secret, only Baek Yeon and So Gyo knew of the Emperor’s Blood Soul Gu poisoning; Taekyung is now also privy to it.
- The Emperor has used poppy; Taekyung identifies its scent, but the chapter does not establish that poppy caused the Blood Soul Gu poisoning.
- Jeong Hogun and the Embroidered Uniform Guards honor Taekyung as a comrade-in-arms and bid him farewell before he enters Qianqing Palace.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 血魂蠱 as “Blood Soul Gu” and 罌粟 as “poppy”; do not imply that poppy caused the Emperor’s poisoning.

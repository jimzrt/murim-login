# Chapter 355

## Plot

Cheongpung arrives during the Western Heaven Demon Lord's confrontation with Tang Sadok, dodges the Demon Lord's eighth-stage Ghost Prison Finger, deflects his follow-up attack, and is recognized as Sword Saint Mae Jonghak's Disciple. He learns that the Blood Lord seeks to kill him, Jeok Cheongang, and Jin Taekyung personally, and unmistakable hostility appears in his normally clear eyes.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord defeats the counterattack by Tang Sadok and the three surviving members of the Tang Clan's Ten Wonders, killing one of them and capturing Tang Sadok. He orders First Fiend to handle Cheongpung, permitting his death if he cannot be subdued; First Fiend engages Cheongpung while the Demon Lord advances into the Inner Hall.

The Western Heaven Demon Lord walks through the blood-soaked Inner Hall with Tang Sadok restrained and tells him that the Tang Clan is collapsing before demanding the location of the Myriad-Poison Ring. First Fiend's assault has killed nearly a hundred Tang Clan members, including Tang Jinhu, and only two of the former Ten Wonders remain alive.

## Continuity

- The Dark Heaven assault has crossed from the Outer Hall into the Inner Hall; nearly one hundred Tang Clan members, including Tang Jinhu, are dead, and the attack remains in progress.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord has defeated Tang Sadok's last counterattack, captured him, and is demanding the location of the Myriad-Poison Ring.
- Only two members of the Tang Clan's former Ten Wonders remain alive after the Western Heaven Demon Lord kills one of the three survivors.
- First Fiend has been ordered to fight Cheongpung and may kill him if he cannot be subdued; Cheongpung is actively resisting him.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord and the Blood Lord serve the same master, and the Blood Lord wants to personally kill Cheongpung, Jeok Cheongang, and Jin Taekyung.
- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung are being treated at the Tang Clan, and the Western Heaven Demon Lord intends to take them and Cheongpung alive if possible.
- The Uninvited Guest Quest remains accepted with unknown parameters and death as its failure condition.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 귀옥지 as Ghost Prison Finger.
- Render 유성추월검 as Meteor Chasing the Moon Sword.
- Render 당 할아버지 as Grandpa Tang for Cheongpung's address to Tang Sadok.
- Preserve the 당문십기/당문삼기 homophone as Tang Clan's Ten Wonders/Tang Clan's Three Skills with an explanatory footnote.

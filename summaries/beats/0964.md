# Chapter 964

## Plot

Jamukha arrives at Eight Spring Gorge with thousands of reinforcements, turning the battle against Shanxi’s exhausted defenders. Jin Wikyung entrusts the battle’s aftermath to Lee Seowol and stays to fight, inspiring the defenders to charge with him.

Jamukha offers to spare Jin Mukyung and the others if Mukyung submits and becomes his hunting dog. Mukyung refuses, despite the hope that submission could save everyone. The confrontation is interrupted when horns announce an enemy attack, revealing that the tremor came from another approaching force.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung entrusts Lee Seowol with everything that follows and remains in the battle rather than retreating; his fate is unresolved.
- Jamukha offers to spare Jin Mukyung and the others if Mukyung submits to him; Mukyung refuses.
- Horns signal an enemy attack as Jamukha realizes the approaching force caused the tremor; the force’s identity and direction remain unknown.

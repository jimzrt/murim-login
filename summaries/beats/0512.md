# Chapter 512

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang unexpectedly appears after disembarking from the swift ship because of seasickness, carrying spiced beef that Taekyung eats. The sharks and the distant swift ship disappear while they talk.

Taekyung admits that using the sharks to replenish his internal energy and chase the swift ship had become meaningless. He decides to follow by his own strength instead, while Jeok approves and leaves ahead toward Henan.

Jeok explains that the Fire Gate Clan's martial arts have a particularly violent flow and that it took him years to calm his own. He acknowledges Taekyung's exceptional martial talent and the advantage of having the Fire King and Slaughter Saint as advisors.

A proclamation from Mount Song announces the New Murim Alliance. Shaolin, Huashan, the Nine Sects and One Gang, and the Five Great Families begin joining, while the Murim debates the threat posed by demonic and heterodox practitioners and the Outer Lands; ten days pass amid the spreading upheaval.

## Continuity

- Taekyung abandons the shark shortcut and intends to reach Henan by refining his own ability to cross the river.
- Jeok Cheongang has left ahead toward Henan after accepting Taekyung's decision and encouraging him to catch up using his own strength.
- The New Murim Alliance has been publicly announced from Mount Song, with Shaolin, Huashan, and the major orthodox groupings moving to join it.
- The orthodox Murim regards Dark Heaven as the successor to the Demonic Cult, while the stance of demonic and heterodox factions and the Outer Lands remains unresolved.

# Chapter 769

## Plot

Michael Silbert learns that the UN has approved the reestablishment of the World Hunter Federation and, after receiving congratulations directly from the President of France, believes his victory and eventual coronation are assured. He privately reflects on Jin Taekyung, whose heat left the first wound he has suffered in more than a decade.

Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi acknowledge that they failed to stop the decision. More than twenty countries are nearing civil war, fear is destroying the rule of law, and the three-day investigation into Siegfried Wassman's materials and Michael's history has produced no new way to expose or stop him.

The Skeleton King proposes that Jin publicly reveal his identity and erase him, thereby removing Michael's leverage. Team Leader Choi argues that this could work because the public would never trust the Skeleton King once they knew he was a monster, despite the lives he had saved. Jin is horrified by the proposal and has not accepted it.

## Continuity

- The UN approved the reestablishment of the World Hunter Federation despite opposition from Jin's side and several major powers.
- Michael Silbert has a fresh heat-inflicted wound around his neck, his first injury in more than a decade, and believes he has won.
- Jin Taekyung, Team Leader Choi, and the Skeleton King spent three days investigating Siegfried Wassman's hideout materials and Michael Silbert's past but found no actionable evidence; Magic Johnson had already reviewed the materials repeatedly.
- The Skeleton King has proposed publicly exposing himself as a monster and having Jin Taekyung erase him to eliminate Michael Silbert's leverage.
- Team Leader Choi believes the public would reject the Skeleton King because of his monster identity, even though he helped save people in Sichuan, Busan, Japan, and Munich.

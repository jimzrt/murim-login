# Chapter 219

## Plot

The joint raid in The Black Wyvern’s Nest is revealed to have encountered a Named Monster, a creature beyond ordinary A-Rank strength. Team Leader 1, the only member of his team to return, was overwhelmed by the monster’s Fear and abandoned the others.

Taekyung realizes that the Named Monster is the same Black Wyvern he fought at the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate three years earlier. His spear strike damaged one of its eyes, and the transformed creature now approaches the raid party across the wasteland.

Taekyung orders Won Myunghoon to take the Star Guild members and leave while he retains Yeti’s Necklace. He confronts Won over his conduct, withdraws the familiar hyung address, and warns him never to bare his teeth again. Won and the Star Guild flee as the Named Monster’s roar echoes across the wasteland.

## Continuity

- A Named Monster is present in The Black Wyvern’s Nest and is several times larger and stronger than an ordinary Wyvern.
- The Named Monster is the same Black Wyvern Taekyung fought at the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate three years earlier; Taekyung’s spear damaged one of its eyes.
- Team Leader 1 was afflicted by the monster’s Fear and survived by abandoning his team.
- Taekyung has confronted Won Myunghoon and ordered him to leave with the Star Guild while retaining Yeti’s Necklace; their previously friendly relationship is now sharply strained.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 레어 몬스터 as “Rare Monster,” 피어 as “Fear,” and 힘껏 찌르기 as “Stab with All My Strength.”
- Retain “Named Monster” for 네임드 몬스터 and “Black Wyvern” for 블랙 와이번.
- Render Taekyung’s cold correction from 명훈이 형 to 원명훈 씨 as “Myunghoon hyung. No, Mr. Won Myunghoon.”

# Chapter 507

## Plot

Jin Taekyung identifies the four powers Mungyeong implied: the Yangtze River Channel League, the Green Forest Alliance, the North Sea Ice Palace, and the Nanman Beast Palace. Jeok Cheongang explains that the latter two Outer Murim factions remained neutral or supported orthodox Murim during the Great Faction War, with the Nanman Beast Palace owing goodwill to the Fire Gate Clan after its Fifth Sect Leader destroyed the Five Poisons Sect and enabled the Beast Palace to dominate Nanman.

Jeok Cheongang believes the Nanman Beast Palace may support the New Murim Alliance but doubts the North Sea Ice Palace will participate. Mu Song must determine how the Yangtze River Channel League will respond, and Mungyeong ends by asking whether Mu Song knows his true identity.

## Continuity

- The four powers potentially affecting the New Murim Alliance are the Yangtze River Channel League, Green Forest Alliance, North Sea Ice Palace, and Nanman Beast Palace.
- Jeok Cheongang has met the Beast Miao King several times and believes the Beast Miao King will probably support orthodox Murim because of the Fire Gate Clan's historical goodwill toward Nanman.
- The Fire Gate Clan's Fifth Sect Leader destroyed the Five Poisons Sect more than two hundred years ago; the Nanman Beast Palace then absorbed Poison Valley and conquered Nanman.
- The North Sea Ice Palace remains isolationist, and Jeok Cheongang expects little from its response to the New Murim Alliance.
- Mungyeong knows or suspects Jeok Cheongang, Mu Song, and Taekyung understand more about his identity than his medical-apprentice persona reveals.

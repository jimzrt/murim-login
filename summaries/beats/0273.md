# Chapter 273

## Plot

The Peace Guild has rapidly expanded to twenty B-rank Hunters and thirty C-rank-or-lower Hunters. Team Leader Choi asks Taekyung to take charge of training the talented but inexperienced rookies, while Taekyung would rather clear A-rank Gates or spend the time with his family. Choi reveals that all A-rank Gates are booked for at least a week and offers a 200 percent monthly performance bonus for raiding and teaching.

The System generates the Quest It Was Too Much Money to Say “I’m Not Doing It!”, and Taekyung tacitly accepts it. Choi reserves several B-rank Gates, scheduling three raids in one day. During the rookies’ second raid, the Lycanthropes overwhelm their formation until Kim Jinsoo kills one, restores the formation, and directs the others. Taekyung then intervenes and kills the remaining monsters, praises Jinsoo, and disciplines three rookies for their failures.

A newspaper in Choi’s office reports the death of Hunter Jung Hyunwoo and expresses Myeongdong Guild’s regret. After Jinsoo leaves to rest before the next raid, Choi urgently calls Taekyung and orders him to come to Ilsan Hospital; the reason is not yet given.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has revealed its fangs in Murim, while Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious.
- Thirty days in the modern world correspond to only three hours in Murim, giving Taekyung time to remain in Korea while Jeok recovers.
- Peace Guild rookies are now being trained through a rotation involving Taekyung, Butler Kim, and Song-i; Im Kkeokjeong has not yet been reached.
- Kim Jinsoo graduated first in the training camp’s B-rank course and was the first person to destroy a Golem core during the preceding raid.
- Taekyung has accepted the System Quest requiring him to train the rookies and is scheduled to continue with B-rank raids.
- Team Leader Choi has urgently summoned Taekyung to Ilsan Hospital for an unexplained reason.

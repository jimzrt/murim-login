# Chapter 270

## Plot

After completing Logout, Jin Taekyung wakes in the modern world after sleeping for forty-two hours. His mother and Hayeon panic, scold him, and beat his back, while Hayeon reveals that she already bought the most expensive massage chair with his card. Taekyung lies about waking during the night and invents a history of sleeping through high school to discourage further questions.

Team Leader Choi visits to take Taekyung to the Peace Guild's new-member workshop, which is actually a six-night, seven-day operation at an A-rank Gate. More than twenty rookie B-rank Hunters face a Stone Golem. Song Song greatly strengthens them with buff magic, Butler Kim prevents them from relying on outside help, Team Leader Choi and Song Song are lifted into the air, and Im Kkeokjeong leads the tank formation. After a five-hour raid, the new Hunters defeat the Stone Golem.

More than ten Iron Golems then approach the exhausted group. When the new Guild members beg for help, Team Leader Choi refuses, and Jin Taekyung appears in a red flash to split one Iron Golem cleanly in half. He grins and remarks that mob farming is extremely profitable.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has returned to the modern world after more than a year in Murim and slept for forty-two hours after Logout.
- The Peace Guild is conducting a six-night, seven-day new-member workshop at an A-rank Gate.
- Song Song's buff magic is powerful enough to be mistaken for an A-rank healer's work.
- Butler Kim forces the rookie Guild members to fight without relying entirely on assistance.
- Im Kkeokjeong leads the rookie Hunters' tank formation during the Stone Golem raid.
- The rookie Hunters defeated a Stone Golem after a five-hour raid before a group of more than ten Iron Golems appeared.
- Jin Taekyung killed one of the approaching Iron Golems with a red-flashing attack.

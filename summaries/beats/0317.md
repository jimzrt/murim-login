# Chapter 317

## Plot

The Yongbong Escort Bureau reaches Xi'an after surviving the Black Stone Stronghold, but thirty-three escorts have died and three days remain before the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng must be delivered to the Zhongnan Sect. Ju Hwaran learns that the Taeeul Merciless Sword has come to Xi'an personally and suspects the Zhongnan Sect may have leaked the ginseng's location and deliberately engineered the dangerous escort conditions, though Chief Escort Heo Jun rejects this as dangerous speculation. Heo also admits that surveillance of Song Ilseom lapsed during the night's rest, leaving his current movements unconfirmed. Meanwhile, Jin Taekyung's party leaves Xi'an Tower with the unconscious Jeok Cheongang secured beneath a ventilated pile of furs, while Baek Museong departs separately after saying goodbye to Cheongpung; rumors then confirm that the Taeeul Merciless Sword is heading to Tengwang Pavilion to meet the Yongbong Escort Bureau.

## Continuity

- The Yongbong Escort Bureau has reached Xi'an after losing thirty-three escorts, whose bone urns are being carried with the survivors.
- The Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng remains sealed in Ju Hwaran's possession, and three days remain before the Zhongnan Sect's delivery deadline.
- The Zhongnan Sect is waiting in Xi'an at Tengwang Pavilion, and the Taeeul Merciless Sword has personally come to receive the shipment.
- Ju Hwaran suspects that the Zhongnan Sect leaked information about the Thousand-Year Snow Ginseng and wanted the Yongbong Escort Bureau's mission to fail.
- Chief Escort Heo Jun considers that suspicion dangerous conjecture and has lost the latest surveillance of Song Ilseom after the escorts relaxed their guard.
- Song Ilseom's current movements remain unconfirmed, while his unclear origins, poor reputation, and Level 110 continue to make him suspicious.
- Jin Taekyung's party has departed Xi'an with Jeok Cheongang still unconscious and being transported on a pack frame.
- Taekyung has become personally curious about Ju Hwaran and increasingly suspicious of Song Ilseom.

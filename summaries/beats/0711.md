# Chapter 711

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens perfectly healed after five System level-ups, exposing the System to Jeok Cheongang, who reveals that he is the Blood Monk and had disguised himself as a monk while destroying six unorthodox faction organizations in Guangxi. Jeok explains that he rushed to Jin's aid after hearing from Jin's allies, and the two reconcile before beginning to treat the battlefield's survivors. Jin's Qi Sense detects only about thirty living people from the nearly one thousand Nanman warriors and three hundred Baekcheon Unit members. Jin stabilizes Wang Ho, while Jeok judges that the gravely injured Beast Miao King can probably survive but is uncertain about the wounded White Tiger. The guardian spirit reveals that it saved Jin and entrusted him with the sacred stone because he reminded it of the first Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace, then indicates that it intends to use the sacred stone to close the rift.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has recovered completely through five System level-ups, though his mind remains exhausted from the repeated battles.
- Jeok Cheongang is confirmed to be the Blood Monk and had disguised himself as a monk to evade Dark Heaven while fighting the unorthodox faction in Guangxi.
- Jeok Cheongang reached the battlefield after learning that Jin Taekyung was in danger and has reunited with his surviving Disciple.
- Only about thirty people remain alive at the battlefield from the nearly one thousand Nanman warriors and three hundred Baekcheon Unit members.
- Wang Ho survives his severe injuries after Jin Taekyung sends internal energy through him.
- The Beast Miao King remains unconscious with numerous sword fragments embedded in his body and severe internal injuries, but Jeok Cheongang believes he can survive treatment.
- The White Tiger remains gravely wounded but conscious and has revealed that it intends to use the sacred stone to close the rift.
- The guardian spirit saved Jin Taekyung and entrusted him with the sacred stone partly because he reminds it of the first Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace.

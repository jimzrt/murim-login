# Chapter 712

## Plot

The rift continues spreading beyond the Inner Palace toward the Outer Palace, and its demonic qi threatens to corrupt living beings and create another catastrophe. The guardian spirit explains that only the sacred stone can withstand the darkness and resolves to enter the rift's center with it, asking Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang to kill it if it becomes corrupted.

Jin offers to seal the rift himself, but Jeok refuses and offers to go in his place. After the guardian spirit asks what Jeok would do if Jin were corrupted, Jeok promises to kill Jin and then choose death himself; he also agrees to kill the guardian spirit if necessary. Jin summons the sacred stone, which the guardian spirit absorbs before advancing into the rift.

The sacred stone's light purifies the surrounding darkness, and the White Tiger enters the rift. As it approaches the center, it remembers its lonely childhood and its three-hundred-year friendship with the fearless tribal chieftain Yayul Cheon, including the time it refused to help him in war and later regretted that choice. The guardian spirit recognizes that its true mission was to protect the land rather than merely guard the sacred stone, then lunges into the rift and releases a tremendous shock wave.

## Continuity

- The guardian spirit has absorbed the sacred stone and entered the rift to seal it, accepting the risk of corruption and death.
- The sacred stone's light has the power to purify the darkness and demonic qi flowing from the rift.
- Jeok Cheongang has promised to kill Jin Taekyung if the darkness corrupts him and to kill the guardian spirit if it becomes corrupted.
- The guardian spirit's true mission is to protect everything in the land, not merely to guard the sacred stone.
- The guardian spirit and Yayul Cheon shared a friendship spanning roughly three hundred years.

## Translation Decisions

- Continue rendering the guardian spirit's 의념 as Will.
- Render 정화 as purification when describing the sacred stone's cleansing power.
- Retain Old Master for Jin Taekyung's address to Jeok Cheongang and this old man for Jeok's 노부.

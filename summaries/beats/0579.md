# Chapter 579

## Plot

Choi Minwoo defeats Song Cheonwoo, breaks his wrist, and leaves him gravely wounded. Song explains that Go Jun seized his children, making the alliance with Choi an offer he could not refuse, and confirms that Cheon Taemin is alive but has been unconscious for more than twenty years. Choi and Kim Hwajong keep Song alive as a witness, but after drinking a potion, Song uses superhuman strength to escape into a crevasse.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo defeated Song Cheonwoo with Hero's Soul and kept him alive as a decisive witness despite wanting him dead.
- Song Cheonwoo's alliance with Choi was his own decision, but Go Jun's seizure of Song's children forced him to accept the arrangement.
- Song confirms that Cheon Taemin is alive but has remained unconscious for more than twenty years.
- Song escaped into a crevasse after Choi treated his wounds enough to keep him alive.
- Choi Minwoo recognizes Kim Hwajong as his only family, and Hwajong continues to serve him as butler and escort.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Hyung for 송천우's 형님 when he addresses Cheon Taemin.
- Use crevasse for 크레바스 and mid-grade potion for 중급 포션.

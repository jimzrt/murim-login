# Chapter 742

## Plot

Jin receives the forcibly accepted Supreme Peak Quest Unknown Death, which requires him to uncover how and by whom Siegfried Wassmann, one of the world's three Grand Mages, was killed. In Siegfried's sealed Swiss hideout, Jin, Magic Johnson, and Team Leader Choi find his corpse unnaturally dried out without wounds or decomposition, suggesting an unknown form of magic. Their investigation confirms Siegfried created A Area and deeply revered Cheon Taemin, making Michael Silbert the strongest suspect because he somehow knows about A Area and Cheon Taemin's condition. The Skeleton King's observation that the hideout contains unusually dense magical power is explained by its laboratory stores of monster corpses and Magic Gems, leaving the cause of death unresolved.

## Continuity

- Siegfried Wassmann was a reclusive Swiss Grand Mage and the creator of A Area; his corpse was found unnaturally desiccated in his sealed hideout after roughly three years without contact.
- Siegfried had very few relationships, was a close friend of Magic Johnson, and held an almost worshipful respect for Cheon Taemin.
- Michael Silbert remains the leading suspect in Siegfried's death, but how he learned about A Area and whether he ordered the killing are unknown.

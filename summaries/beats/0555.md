# Chapter 555

## Plot

Go Jun confronts Prime Minister Jang Taekhwan before Lee Jungryong’s national funeral, forces him to arrange a private meeting with the President, and reveals that he has assumed the Vice Guild Master role while preparing to inherit Ares Guild’s influence. As chief mourner, Go Jun publicly positions himself as the new power behind the Guild and privately swears vengeance against Jin Taekyung and Choi Minwoo.

Five days of grand funeral ceremonies pass before the story shifts to an unidentified Hunter party fighting monsters in a dark cave. The Gate suddenly erupts with mana far beyond a low-level Gate’s capacity and is identified as a Mutated Gate, leaving the hunters facing apparent death.

## Continuity

- Go Jun is now Ares Guild’s Vice Guild Master and chief mourner for Lee Jungryong; he has dozens of A-rank Hunters serving as his new subordinates and claims responsibility for preserving Lee’s legacy.
- Go Jun carries a battered necklace recovered from the ruins of the Arch Lich’s former stronghold after bribing the investigation leader; it is not Lee Jungryong’s keepsake, but Go Jun considers it significant.
- An unidentified Hunter party is fighting monsters inside a Gate when a surge of extreme mana transforms the situation into a Mutated Gate crisis; their fate is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 국가장 and 국장 as national funeral, and 상주 as chief mourner.
- Render 변이 게이트 as Mutated Gate and 아크 리치 as Arch Lich.
- Render 유지 as dying wish in Go Jun’s argument that Lee left him more than his legacy.

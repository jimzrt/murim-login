# Chapter 992

## Plot

Taekyung tells Jeok Cheongang that memories of the original Jin Taekyung appeared in a dream. They consider whether the original’s consciousness may have remained inside Taekyung, though the truth is unknown. They agree to keep discussing the matter privately; the Bow Saint may know something but is still in Hebei. Jeok confirms that Peng Cheolhu is gone and says he died smiling. Taekyung opens the System window after Jeok leaves.

## Continuity

- Memories of the original Jin Taekyung appeared in Taekyung’s dream; whether some part of the original’s consciousness remains inside him is unresolved.
- Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang will discuss the consciousness mystery privately; revealing it could expose Taekyung to accusations of using dark arts.
- The Bow Saint may know part of the truth about the chosen one, but she remains in Hebei and Taekyung is unsure whether she would discuss it with him.
- Jeok Cheongang confirms that Peng Cheolhu is gone and says he died smiling.

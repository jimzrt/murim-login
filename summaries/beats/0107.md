# Chapter 107

## Plot

Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa travel by carriage toward Mount Heng with a replacement coachman who is secretly a Level 50 First Rate martial artist and bodyguard. Mujin and Taekyung tease Mukyung about his unusually awkward and shy behavior around Wolhwa, while Mukyung formally thanks her for helping the Jin Family and Wolhwa reminds him that she has not yet received proper compensation.

Around midnight, the group takes shelter in an abandoned shrine. Wolhwa explains that the Red Wind Band, a powerful mounted-bandit group from the northern plateau, avoided the battle at Eight Spring Gorge, then attacked the weakened Mount Heng Sword Sect's main base after Lee Cheonbaek's forces withdrew. With unrest spreading through the region, Taekyung notices torches approaching through a thin snowstorm.

## Continuity

- Wolhwa's replacement coachman is a Level 50 First Rate martial artist who serves as both coachman and bodyguard and is likely affiliated with the Lower District Sect.
- Mukyung behaves unusually awkwardly and shyly around Wolhwa but formally thanks her for aiding the Jin Family; Wolhwa states that she still has not received proper compensation.
- The Red Wind Band is a rising power from the northern plateau led by a formidable martial artist who commands two hundred followers.
- The Red Wind Band watched the battle at Eight Spring Gorge from roughly four hours away, avoided joining it, then headed north to target the weakened Mount Heng Sword Sect's main base.
- The Mount Heng Sword Sect won the subsequent two-day battle but lost its Young Sect Leader, and the sect's weakened condition has allowed wandering martial artists, bandits, mounted bandits, and dark-path figures to cause unrest.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, Mujin, and Wolhwa are sheltering overnight in an abandoned shrine after traveling by carriage, and unidentified torch-bearing people are approaching through the snow.

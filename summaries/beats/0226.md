# Chapter 226

## Plot

Jin Taekyung gives HunterTV an exclusive live broadcast outside The Black Wyvern’s Nest, publicly revealing that the Gate was Mutated and contained a Named Monster that he killed. He reports seven deaths among the raid participants, then adds Won Myunghoon to make eight and describes Won’s death as having been “disposed of.”

The emergency investigation confirms the Named Monster’s death. Kim Jonghun confesses everything while still mentally shaken, and an anonymous USB containing footage recorded with a concealed PSV-96K camera exposes Won Myunghoon’s conduct. Public anger over Won’s buried crimes and Taekyung’s killing of him intensifies.

## Continuity

- Taekyung publicly disclosed that The Black Wyvern’s Nest was a Mutated Gate containing a Named Monster and that he killed it.
- Seven raid participants died, and Won Myunghoon’s death brought the confirmed total to eight; the remaining survivors were reported safe.
- HunterTV streamed Taekyung’s statement live on iTube, quickly reaching 100,000 viewers.
- The investigation confirmed Carus’s corpse and obtained conclusive raid footage; Kim Jonghun confessed to the Star Guild’s actions while still mentally shaken.
- The leaked footage exposed additional wrongdoing and suspicions surrounding Won Myunghoon, producing intense public backlash.

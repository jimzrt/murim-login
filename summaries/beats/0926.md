# Chapter 926

## Plot

Baek Yeon orders the arrest of the treasonous officials. Taekyung worries that innocent people may be caught in the purge, and the Emperor says he will honor Zhu Bao’s request to establish each person’s guilt and spare the innocent.

The Bow Saint leads Taekyung to a secluded garden and identifies him as the chosen one mentioned by the Martial God. She says he should have possessed powers to escape even death, then asks why he is in an irrecoverable state.

## Continuity

- The Emperor has ordered that the purge distinguish the guilty from the innocent, honoring Crown Prince Zhu Bao’s request to spare those uninvolved in the treason.
- The Bow Saint identifies Taekyung as the Martial God’s chosen one and says his present irrecoverable state contradicts the powers he was supposed to possess.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 황태제 as “Crown Prince” in this succession context.
- Use “Old Master” for 노야 when Taekyung addresses Jeok Cheongang.

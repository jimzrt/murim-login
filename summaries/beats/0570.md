# Chapter 570

## Plot

On a January morning in Busan, a freelance team drives to Haeundae's C-rank Gate, Siren's Black River, with Lee Dongseok, a temporary Hunter using a changed face and assumed name. Dongseok is carrying out a life-threatening assignment for an unnamed person and keeps a valuable, dangerous object in his subspace pocket; he was abandoned at birth, raised in an orphanage secretly supported by Lee Jungryong, and regards the former Vice Guild Master as a father. About an hour after the team enters, the Gate's magic power reading rises uncontrollably, a rift opens over Haeundae's beach, and a dense breath of death spreads across the sand. Jin Taekyung arrives in Busan through three Teleportation magic circles and senses screams, explosions, and immense mana shaking the earth.

## Continuity

- Lee Dongseok is using a changed face and assumed identity while carrying out an unnamed person's dangerous mission; the valuable object entrusted to him remains undisclosed.
- Lee Dongseok regards the late Lee Jungryong as a father, and Ares Guild as the home he shared with him; Lee Jungryong secretly supported the orphanage where Dongseok grew up.
- The Haeundae C-rank Gate Siren's Black River transforms roughly an hour after the team enters: its magic power reading spikes, a rift tears open, and deadly energy spreads across the beach.
- Jin Taekyung has arrived in Busan and sensed multiple screams, explosions, and immense mana from the unfolding Haeundae crisis.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 이동석 as Lee Dongseok, 동석 씨 as Dongseok, 팀장님 as Team Leader, and 전 부길드장님 as former Vice Guild Master.
- Render 세이렌의 검은 강 as Siren's Black River and retain Busan, Haeundae, Pocheon, and Gyeonggi Province.
- Retain Mutated Gate, Monster Wave, magic power reading, and Teleportation magic circle as established terminology.
- Keep the Team Leader's and gate manager's dialogue distinctly informal and regionally colloquial without overexplaining the dialect.

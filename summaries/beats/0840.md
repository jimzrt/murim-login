# Chapter 840

## Plot

At the Sichuan Tang Clan, Tang Sadok explains that allied martial artists remain to guard the clan against another Dark Heaven attack. He escorts Jin to the Rear Court, where Jin finds Jeok Cheongang arguing with a physician who refuses to leave his patients to treat Jin. Jeok and Jin reunite awkwardly after Jin overhears the argument.

## Continuity

- Jin has awakened after being unconscious for three days and is now at the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Allied martial artists remain stationed at the Sichuan Tang Clan to help treat patients, rebuild, and guard against another Dark Heaven attack.
- Jeok Cheongang arrived in Sichuan two days earlier and is urging a physician to treat Jin; the physician refuses to leave his remaining patients, and the dispute is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 醜王 as “Disgrace King” when Jin mockingly imagines a new epithet for Jeok Cheongang.

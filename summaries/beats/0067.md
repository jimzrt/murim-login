# Chapter 67

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin remains badly bruised and undergoes painful acupuncture from the Medicine King Hall Master, while Taekyung's bruises nearly vanish overnight after circulating his qi and sleeping. Taekyung is discharged, but Mujin must remain for treatment. With his pavilion destroyed, Taekyung is temporarily taken by Jin Wikyung to a large three-story residence containing two training halls. Wikyung arranges for Jin Mukyung to share the residence with Taekyung for fifteen days while Taekyung's residence is rebuilt. Mukyung accepts but requests a physician instead of servants or a cook, openly threatening to beat Taekyung again. Wikyung stops their quarrel, reprimands both brothers, and compels them to agree to the temporary cohabitation despite their mutual reluctance.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's pavilion was destroyed in his fight with Jin Mukyung, leaving him temporarily without a residence.
- Taekyung's bruises largely disappeared overnight after circulating his qi and sleeping; he suspects the recovery is due to Sleep Mode.
- The Medicine King Hall Master discharges Taekyung but keeps Hyuk Mujin under treatment after the incident.
- Jin Wikyung arranges for Taekyung and Jin Mukyung to share a large residence with two training halls for fifteen days while Taekyung's residence is rebuilt.
- Jin Wikyung's intense protectiveness toward Taekyung is already widely known within the Jin Family despite his public, formal image.
- Jin Mukyung remains hostile toward Taekyung and appears eager to fight him again, but accepts the temporary shared residence.
- Jin Wikyung intervenes when the brothers begin to fight and orders them to live together peacefully.

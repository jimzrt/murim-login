# Chapter 68

## Plot

Jin Wikyung arranges ten days of cohabitation between Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung. Mukyung imposes three rules requiring polite speech, silence, and immediate obedience when he claims the training hall. Taekyung leaves after witnessing Mukyung destroy steel dummies, then decides to catch up to his brother's strength.

Taekyung discovers that no quest is currently active and that the Logout Quest has vanished. The Return achievement grants him the Returnee title, activates the Login and Logout functions, and increases all his stats by ten.

While Mukyung attempts to break through the Ren and Du meridians after three years of repeated effort, Taekyung's shouting disrupts his training. Later, Wipeng arrives at Song Sword Sect with thirty Jin Family retainers, delivers Jin Wikyung's New Year's Day summons, and implies that the visit is both a warning and part of the pursuit of the assassin who attacked Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung requires Taekyung to use polite speech, remain quiet, and yield the underground training hall on command during their ten-day cohabitation.
- Taekyung wants to become stronger and hopes to learn directly from the Peak-level Mukyung during their time together.
- Taekyung has no active quest immediately before receiving the Return achievement and Returnee title.
- The Returnee title grants All Stats +10 and activates the Login and Logout functions.
- Mukyung has spent the past three years attempting to open the Ren and Du meridians and is interrupted by Taekyung before completing the attempt.
- Wipeng visits Song Sword Sect at night with thirty retainers, delivers Wikyung's New Year's Day summons, and presents the summons as a formal invitation while its political meaning is a warning.
- Song Sword Sect previously received assistance because of its relationship with the Jin Family but withdrew when the war began.
- Wipeng says the assassin who targeted Taekyung is believed to have been sent by someone hostile to the Jin Family, but he does not identify the assassin or confirm Song Sword Sect's involvement.

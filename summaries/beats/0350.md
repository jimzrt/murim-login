# Chapter 350

## Plot

Jin Taekyung continues distributing food in the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison and tries for three days to coax the Heavenly Power Demon into eating and revealing what he knows about Dark Heaven. The Heavenly Power Demon refuses every comfort and says that torture or death would leave him without regret.

Cheongpung returns with food and a Beggars' Sect letter reporting that the search has shifted from finding the Divine Physician to finding the culprit who murdered the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun. The Beggars' Sect, the Tang Clan's Green Shadow Squad, and government troops are searching across Sichuan, with the government troops expanding toward Qingcheng and Emei while preparing for another possible incident; Jeok Cheongang's treatment remains ongoing.

Jang Il, a twenty-five-year-old two-knot Beggars' Sect Disciple, is killed in a secluded forest two days from Emei by a smiling middle-aged military official who orders his subordinates to find the other nearby Beggars' Sect members.

## Continuity

- The Heavenly Power Demon has refused food, clothing, and other conveniences for three days and will not discuss Dark Heaven despite visibly reacting to its name.
- Taekyung suspects that Dark Heaven is deeply connected to, or possibly identical with, the Demonic Cult because of the Heavenly Power Demon's former Cult allegiance and silence.
- The Beggars' Sect, Green Shadow Squad, and government troops are actively searching for the murderer of the Poison King and the Heaven-Shaking Venerable Nun, and the government search has reached the areas toward Qingcheng and Emei.
- A middle-aged military official and his group killed Jang Il while searching for other Beggars' Sect members near Emei.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 원균 as Won Gyun, not Heo Gyun, and render 검남춘 as Jiannan Chun.
- Render 삼결 제자, 이결 제자, and 일결 제자 as Three-knot Disciple, Two-knot Disciple, and One-knot Disciple; render 부 분타주 as Vice Branch Leader.
- Retain Taekyung's abrupt, sarcastic voice while rendering his exaggerated courtesy toward the Heavenly Power Demon as deliberate coaxing.

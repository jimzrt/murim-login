# Chapter 249

## Plot

Song Ho, the genial but imposing Thousand-Faced Fox, recognizes Jin Taekyung as Jeok Cheongang's Disciple and reveals that Taekyung's destruction of the mechanism core during the final preliminary assessment allowed roughly one hundred additional candidates to qualify. Song Ho praises Taekyung's blunt determination to win, but briefly reveals a sharp, predatory gaze while surveying the finalists.

The more than five hundred finalists travel from Mount Song to Luoyang for the Star-Array Grand Banquet's main event. During the journey, Taekyung subdues Baek Woo and forces him to use polite speech, then gives Gung Gibang and Zhuge Gyun the same three choices; Gung chooses silence and agrees to call Taekyung Jin hyung, while Zhuge chooses the third option through a Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage allusion. Jongni Chu agrees to remain quiet, and the group arrives at the Murim Alliance.

## Continuity

- Song Ho recognizes Jin Taekyung as Jeok Cheongang's Disciple and knows of Taekyung's strong preliminary performance.
- Song Ho states that Taekyung's destruction of the mechanism core allowed approximately one hundred additional candidates to qualify.
- More than five hundred finalists travel from Mount Song to Luoyang, where the Star-Array Grand Banquet's main event will be held.
- The main event will use a different format from the preliminaries and has spectator stands for the duels.
- Taekyung remains determined to win regardless of whether he faces four hundred, five hundred, or one thousand competitors.
- Taekyung subdues Baek Woo aboard the carriage and compels him to remain silent and use polite speech.
- Gung Gibang agrees to remain silent, use polite speech, and address Taekyung as Jin hyung; Zhuge Gyun chooses the third option through the Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage allusion.
- The finalists arrive at the Murim Alliance in Luoyang.

# Chapter 753

## Plot

The Skeleton King is forced onto a small motorboat carrying two S-rank Magic Gems to lure Leviathan from the deep sea. After circling near an uninhabited island for an hour, the boat is attacked by the roughly three-hundred-meter-long Leviathan. Jin Taekyung, who concealed his vital energy with the Turtle Breath Technique, impales Leviathan's mouth with a blue-white flame spear, rescues the Skeleton King, and confronts the monster in midair.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung learned the Turtle Breath Technique from the Slaughter Saint and can use it to suppress his breathing, heartbeat, body temperature, and vital energy.
- Jin Taekyung, Choi Minwoo, and their allies used the Skeleton King and two Japanese-government-provided S-rank Magic Gems as bait for Leviathan.
- Leviathan emerged near an uninhabited island in the Philippine Sea and attacked the bait boat.
- Jin Taekyung destroyed Leviathan's regenerated teeth and struck the roof of its mouth with a blue-white flame spear before rescuing the Skeleton King.
- The Skeleton King likes the modern world and its living nature, cities, technology, and people, but fears being eaten by Leviathan.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 유골사태 as boneshed to preserve the Skeleton King's bloodless pun on 혈유사태.
- Keep samgyeopsal and ssamjang, and render 파채 as scallion salad.
- Render 통통배 as little motorboat and 필리핀해 as Philippine Sea.

# Chapter 256

## Plot

Jin Taekyung catches Cheongpung as he collapses after Jongni Chu's shallow chest cut. Jongni Chu claims the attack was only a feint that injured Cheongpung's mental strength, but Taekyung realizes he was completely deceived and challenges Jongni Chu despite the risk of disqualification.

Jeok Cheongang intervenes, temporarily assumes the Star-Array Grand Banquet's presiding authority because Hong Dao is absent, and orders the final to begin immediately. He asks Jongni Chu whether he came from Tianshan, but Jongni Chu refuses to answer and instead hints that Cheongpung could be harmed. Jeok threatens to burn alive anyone who kills his Disciple, while Taekyung accepts the possibility of death and declares that nothing is known until it is tried.

Taekyung uses his eighth-stage Qi Sense Skill to examine Jongni Chu, but the Skill fails to identify him: an unknown force rejects it despite the permitted Level and distance. This unprecedented resistance strengthens Taekyung's suspicion that Jongni Chu uses demonic martial arts, though Jongni Chu's affiliation remains unconfirmed. The final then begins immediately, with Taekyung and Jongni Chu charging each other and their first attacks colliding after two steps.

# Chapter 90

## Plot

Team Leader Choi gives Jin Taekyung a week of paid vacation because Peace Guild's Guild house remodeling will finish in a week. At home, Hayeon indirectly asks for allowance, and Taekyung gives her two 50,000-won bills before joining his mother's dinner.

Choi and Butler Kim discuss Taekyung's rapid growth and the possibility that he is a third-awakening Hunter. Butler Kim agrees to investigate such cases. Sangdong Guild has begun monitoring Peace Guild, with Taekyung marked as a major target, but Choi deliberately chooses not to warn him because he wants Sangdong's investigation to reveal more about Taekyung.

During his vacation, Taekyung visits a real estate office and seeks to buy a house in the safe zone where he grew up. The area was redeveloped after his family moved away following his father's death. He views the former family home, walks around the neighborhood, finds the supermarket from his childhood, and is approached by an unfamiliar man who asks whether Taekyung knows him.

## Continuity

- Peace Guild's Guild house remodeling is scheduled to finish in one week, and Jin Taekyung is on paid vacation until then.
- Choi Minwoo and Butler Kim suspect that Jin Taekyung may be a Hunter who awakened a third time; Butler Kim will investigate whether such cases exist.
- Sangdong Guild has deployed surveillance agents to analyze Peace Guild, and Jin Taekyung is considered a major target. Choi has chosen not to warn Taekyung because he wants Sangdong's investigation to approach Taekyung's true identity.
- Jin Taekyung intends to purchase the detached house that was his family's former home in his redeveloped childhood neighborhood.
- An unidentified man approached Jin Taekyung outside the childhood supermarket and asked whether Taekyung knew him; his identity and connection to Taekyung are unresolved.

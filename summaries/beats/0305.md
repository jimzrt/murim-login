# Chapter 305

## Plot

Lee Jungryong visits Go Jun during his recovery and reframes his defeat as merely falling. He promises to give Go Jun greater strength so that Go Jun can defeat Jin Taekyung, reaffirming their Master-and-Disciple bond.

President Baek Hanseong delivers Xiao Yang's unofficial diplomatic letter and China's mercenary contract for the Sichuan Catastrophe. Lee accepts Ares Guild's participation after assessing the Lich's power and the proposed rewards, then learns that the Peace Guild also received an offer.

Baek reveals that Xiao Yang personally required Lee Jungryong, the Ares Guild, and Jin Taekyung to participate. Taekyung and Team Leader Choi depart for China on a private jet after Taekyung accepts the offer of fifty trillion won for killing the Lich, while joking about buying the aircraft and negotiating the Guild's share.

## Continuity

- Lee Jungryong has accepted China's request for Ares Guild participation in the Sichuan Catastrophe response.
- China's contract requests thirty A-rank Hunters and two hundred B-rank Hunters as mercenaries.
- China has offered ten billion won for one week of participation, with an additional ten billion won per week beyond that period, and fifty trillion won for killing the Lich.
- President Baek Hanseong is pursuing an anti-Ares political stance while cooperating with Lee Jungryong when their interests align.
- Xiao Yang personally required Lee Jungryong, the Ares Guild, and Jin Taekyung to participate in the Chinese operation.
- Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi have departed for China aboard a Chinese private jet.
- Lee Jungryong intends to strengthen Go Jun so that Go Jun can defeat Jin Taekyung.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Baek Hanseong for 백한성 and President or Mr. President for 대통령 and 대통령 각하.
- Use Chairman Xiao Yang for 샤오 양 주석 and retain Chairman Jongseok for Taekyung's mistaken 주석-to-종석 wordplay.
- Use People's Liberation Army for 인민해방군 and Central Military Commission for 중앙 군사 위원회.
- Use Focke-Wulf for 포케불프.

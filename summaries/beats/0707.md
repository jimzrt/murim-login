# Chapter 707

## Plot

The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is overwhelmed by the Beast Miao King, whose attacks expose how severely her strength has declined after Jin Taekyung's earlier assault. She crashes into the Inner Palace ruins, discovers that the masked man has been crushed beneath a boulder, and realizes that the battle and her grand plan are ending in defeat. Jin Taekyung, the guardian spirit, and the Beast Miao King surround her and launch a coordinated final attack. Facing certain death, she awakens the innate qi within her through the rift, causing space to twist, but an unidentified sword suddenly thrusts from the ruins and buries itself in her back.

## Continuity

- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is severely injured, exhausted, and surrounded by Jin Taekyung, the guardian spirit, the Beast Miao King, and Nanman forces.
- The Beast Miao King has overwhelmed the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and joins Jin Taekyung and the guardian spirit in a coordinated attack against her.
- The masked man is crushed beneath a massive boulder but continues twitching while awaiting his abnormal recovery.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress awakens the innate qi she has carried since birth through the rift, causing space to twist around her.
- An unidentified sword emerges from the ruins behind the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and stabs her in the back at the chapter's end.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress believes the Nanman battle is over and that her own fate is approaching its end, while still desiring to survive and continue her grand plan and revenge.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Fist Force, external arts, demonic qi, mental strength, Finger Qi, Baekcheon Unit, guardian spirit, Fiend, Lord of Heaven, innate qi, and rift.
- Preserve the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's playful cruelty while shifting her internal voice to desperate, reverent, and fatalistic under imminent death.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's mocking, profane conversational voice when he taunts the defeated Southern Heaven Demon Empress.

# Chapter 668

## Plot

Jin Taekyung suddenly emerges from the underground prison with Yayul Mok in his grasp, having broken free despite the iron-ball restraints and internal-energy seal. He humiliates and defeats the Bai captain by stopping his Sword Energy with an invisible Middle Dantian force, then knocks out a group of Peak squad captains with his chains. As the terrified Bai warriors retreat, Jin reveals that Taishan is approaching from the stairs, roaring with hunger.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has escaped the underground prison, broken the chains attached to his iron balls, and retained access to an invisible force despite the seal on his internal energy.
- Jin can use power associated with his Middle Dantian to stop Sword Energy without touching the blade and can fight with overwhelming strength and speed.
- Jin defeated the Bai warriors' captain and knocked out multiple Peak-level squad captains, leaving the remaining Bai warriors afraid to attack.
- Yayul Mok and the Seven Miao Tigers remain engaged in the underground-prison rescue, and Yayul still carries the antidote intended for Jin.
- Taishan is approaching the battlefield from the stairs while roaring that he is hungry.

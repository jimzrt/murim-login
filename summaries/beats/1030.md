# Chapter 1030

## Plot

Jin Taekyung recognizes seven powerful Death Knights approaching with the enemy army, and realizes the Blood-Sword Demon Lord used the Three Elders of Tianshan as bait to draw his forces out. As the army closes in, Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Sama Pyo prepare to face it; Taekyung tells Sama Pyo not to let the world’s label of “unorthodox” define him.

## Continuity

- Seven black-clad riders on ghost horses are identified by Taekyung as Death Knights; each seems at least as powerful as Lei Fei was as a Death Knight Lord.
- The Blood-Sword Demon Lord used the Three Elders of Tianshan as bait to draw Taekyung’s forces out of the Great Snow Mountain.
- Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Sama Pyo stand together against the advancing army; Taekyung regards Sama Pyo as someone worth trusting beyond his unorthodox affiliation.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the riders’ identity as Death Knights based on Taekyung’s recognition; their specific rank and allegiance are not established here.
- Use “unorthodox” for Sama Pyo’s imposed faction identity, distinct from “dark-path figures” in Taekyung’s list of categories.

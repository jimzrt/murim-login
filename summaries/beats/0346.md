# Chapter 346

## Plot

Jin Taekyung receives the Myriad-Poison Ring, but Item Appraisal fails because a special condition is required. The Divine Physician estimates that the ring raises Jeok Cheongang's treatment prospects above seventy percent and agrees to make up the remainder with his medical skill. Tang Sadok selects the secluded, sunless, freezing underground prison of the Sichuan Tang Clan as the treatment site and leads the party toward its deepest and largest cell.

While the party prepares for treatment, Cheongpung befriends Mimi-chan, who performs the Whirlwind technique at his command. In a separate scene, an unidentified middle-aged man kills four Tang Clan men, interrogates a survivor, and claims that Poison King Tang Taesang endured four days of torture before dying.

## Continuity

- The Myriad-Poison Ring is in Jin Taekyung's possession, but its appraisal requires a special condition.
- The Divine Physician estimates Jeok Cheongang's treatment has a success chance above seventy percent with the ring and his medical skill supplying the rest.
- Jeok Cheongang's treatment is planned for the deepest and largest cell of the Sichuan Tang Clan's underground prison, with a fifteen-day stay.
- The underground prison is secluded, sunless, bitterly cold, and contains brutal torture facilities overseen by Old Man Gung.
- Cheongpung has an extraordinary affinity with animals, and Mimi-chan can perform Whirlwind at his command.
- An unidentified middle-aged man has killed four Tang Clan men and is interrogating a surviving fifth; he claims Poison King Tang Taesang died after four days of torture.

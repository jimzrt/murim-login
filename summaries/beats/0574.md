# Chapter 574

## Plot

Taekyung pierces the Kraken's eye with White Flame and attacks it with his fists and Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist. The Kraken reveals that humans gave it power and led it outside, causing Taekyung to suspect that someone deliberately created the Monster Wave by having it absorb unpurified Magic Gems. Taekyung withholds the killing blow to interrogate the Kraken, but it feigns weakness, sprays him with poisonous ink, and inflicts Poisoned and Paralyzed status effects while reducing his Strength and Agility. As Taekyung begins resisting the poison with Scorching Yang Qi, the Myriad-Poison Ring, and Unaﬀected by a Hundred Poisons, the Kraken charges Gwangan Bridge and the survivors awaiting rescue, forcing Taekyung to launch a hellfire-wreathed spear toward it.

## Continuity

- The Kraken claims that humans gave it power and led it outside, imitating an unidentified person's Korean speech while making the disclosure.
- Taekyung deduces that unpurified Magic Gems could have increased a Gate's magic power enough to cause a Mutated Gate or Monster Wave; the perpetrator and motive remain unknown.
- Kraken's Ink is a deadly poison that applies Poisoned and Paralyzed, temporarily lowers Strength and Agility, and can be resisted through Unaﬀected by a Hundred Poisons and internal-energy circulation.
- Taekyung chose to preserve the Kraken as the sole witness, but the Kraken exploited his lowered guard and attacked the survivors at Gwangan Bridge.
- The outcome of Taekyung's final hellfire-coated spear attack and the Kraken's survival are unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 통합 언어팩 as Integrated Language Pack and 마계어 as Demon Realm language.
- Render 크라켄의 먹물 as Kraken's Ink, 마비 as Paralyzed, and 중독 as Poisoned for System labels.
- Preserve the Kraken's broken, stop-start Demon Realm speech while rendering the imitated human speech as smooth and natural English.
- Preserve Taekyung's blunt profanity and the distinction that a Murim warrior fights while a Hunter fights and protects others.

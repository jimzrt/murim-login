# Chapter 532

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin accidentally soils himself after eating dumplings and dozing while standing guard, then becomes trapped in Gowolru's privy. An unidentified young martial artist mocks his predicament, gives him a silk outer robe, recognizes him as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon's right arm, and promises him a chance to repay the favor.

At the damaged Gowolru, Jin Taekyung agrees to three hundred silver nyang in compensation and charges it to the Hwangbo Family's tab, securing a feast for his party. He reunites with Ju Hwaran, forces Hak Woo to leave through a threatening Sound Transmission, confirms that Song Ilseom remains Hwaran's direct escort, and learns that the unidentified giant has eaten six meals that day.

Hyuk Mujin eventually returns with the unfamiliar young man behind him; the stranger smiles at Taekyung, whose party does not yet know his identity or purpose.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's growing fame as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon lets him charge Gowolru's repair costs to the Hwangbo Family and obtain immediate hospitality.
- Ju Hwaran and Jin Taekyung have reunited at Gowolru after the Yongbong Escort Bureau and Zhongnan Sect dispute; she remembers his prior intervention and sees that his manner has not changed.
- Hak Woo is the Kunlun Sect's top young prodigy, known as the Kunlun Cloud Dragon; Taekyung can intimidate him into leaving through Sound Transmission.
- Song Ilseom remains Ju Hwaran's direct escort and stays with her despite Taekyung's attempt to clear the room.
- The unidentified giant who attacked Hwangbo Ak now travels with Taekyung's group, has an enormous appetite, and claims to have eaten only six meals that day.
- An unidentified young martial artist helped Hyuk Mujin after his accident, knows Hyuk as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon's right arm, and has now appeared beside him before Taekyung.

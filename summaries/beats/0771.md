# Chapter 771

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi's confrontation is interrupted by Magic Johnson's urgent holographic call and a packet of documents. The information sparks a previously unconsidered possibility, causing Jin to halt his immediate plan to kill Michael Silbert and declare that they must find a fourth path in which their enemies die and they survive. Jin's unleashed qi triggers building-wide alarms and evacuation, while his full-force self-slap causes a severe nosebleed that Choi treats with a potion.

A broadcast reveals that the UN has approved the reestablishment of the World Hunter Federation. Michael publicly praises the decision, redirects the representative position toward Cheon Taemin as the living savior who defeated the Demon King, and announces that the inaugural ceremony will be held in Seoul two days later. He declares that the Hunters will gather, choose their representative, and become humanity's sword and shield despite The Prophet, monsters, or even the Demon King's return.

## Continuity

- Magic Johnson sends Team Leader Choi intelligence that reveals a possible new route through the crisis, interrupting Jin's immediate plan to confront Michael Silbert.
- Jin has declared a fourth path: eliminate the forces responsible for the crisis while preserving himself and his allies, rather than sacrificing the Skeleton King or surrendering the world to Michael.
- Jin's qi outburst causes a building-wide emergency, and his self-inflicted slap produces a severe nosebleed that is stopped by a healing potion.
- The UN has approved the reestablishment of the World Hunter Federation, and Michael has announced its inaugural ceremony in Seoul two days from now.
- Michael publicly presents Cheon Taemin as the proper representative while positioning the Federation as humanity's united sword and shield.

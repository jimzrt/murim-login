# Chapter 1112

## Plot

At the North Gate, the Dalai Lama recognizes Jeok Cheongang’s techniques and explains that the Potala Palace’s grudge began over two hundred years ago, when Songhak destroyed its forces after its monks interrupted his meal. The Palace later built its strength and allied with Dark Heaven to seek revenge against the Fire Gate Clan. Jeok exposes the Dalai Lama’s use of demonic power, then kills him while declaring that he will protect the Fire Gate Clan.

Jeok Cheongang and Perfected Being Hyeoncheon had both been gravely injured defending the North Gate. Hyeoncheon is still alive but barely breathing when Jeok calls out to him during the fight.

## Continuity

- The Dalai Lama is dead: Jeok Cheongang killed him at the North Gate.
- The Potala Palace’s long-standing vendetta against the Fire Gate Clan began when Songhak, its Fifth Sect Leader, killed one hundred Potala martial monks and crippled another hundred after they tried to detain him; he later escaped pursuit and returned to the Central Plains.
- The Dalai Lama sought an alliance with Dark Heaven because the Potala Palace lacked the strength to take revenge on the Central Plains alone; Dark Heaven’s pills, martial arts, and demonic power helped him grow stronger.
- Jeok Cheongang and Perfected Being Hyeoncheon are severely injured after defending the North Gate; Hyeoncheon is alive but barely conscious.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain established renderings: Songhak, Ghost Flame Fist, Flame Divine Palm, Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, and azure dragon’s roar.
- Render 창도 as “Chamdo.”

# Chapter 689

## Plot

Yohi awakens alone in an immense sealed stone chamber with her internal energy restored but no visible exit. After fruitlessly searching and attempting to escape, she discovers Muyaho emerging from the rock wall, initially appearing incomplete before becoming whole. Muyaho carries her through the wall into a bright, verdant realm filled with peaceful animals, then stops before a pond where Jin Taekyung lies unconscious. As an unknown energy-laden wind scatters leaves from a colossal tree, a Black Tiger rises beneath its shadow.

## Continuity

- Yohi awakens in an unexplained enclosed stone realm with her sealed internal energy restored and her flexible sword missing.
- Yohi spends more than two shichen searching for an exit, striking the stone and climbing the vines with the Wall Lizard Technique, but finds no escape route.
- Muyaho appears from the rock wall, guides Yohi onto its back, and carries her through the wall into a bright realm filled with plants, flowers, and animals.
- Jin Taekyung is unconscious and half-submerged in a pond approximately ten zhang from Yohi and Muyaho.
- A cool wind carrying an unknown energy scatters leaves from a colossal tree, after which a Black Tiger rises beneath its shadow.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve Yohi's mistaken belief that she has entered the afterlife as her subjective interpretation, not an established fact.
- Render 사술 as "dark arts" and 흑호 as "Black Tiger," keeping it distinct from the White Tiger.
- Retain Yohi's incredulous, darkly comic internal voice during her encounter with Muyaho.

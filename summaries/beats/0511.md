# Chapter 511

## Plot

Two swift ships of the Yangtze River Channel League pass a pleasure boat on the Yangtze. Nearby fishermen recognize the ships as raiders but are surprised when they do not attack.

Taekyung catches up to the ships by directing five sharks with the Water God Dragon's sinew. He asks fishermen for directions, releases one shark to them after receiving food, and continues onward; the fisherman realizes the white sturgeon has given him a new advantage over ordinary swift ships.

After alternating between rest and movement for half a day, Taekyung partially replenishes his depleted internal energy and releases the remaining four sharks. A person then approaches him by walking across the surface of the water and calls him a lunatic.

## Continuity

- Taekyung pursued the Yangtze River Channel League's two swift ships by training five sharks as a water-ski team with the Water God Dragon's sinew.
- Taekyung's internal energy, previously nearly depleted by training, was partially replenished through alternating rest and movement during the pursuit.
- Taekyung released the trained sharks after catching up to the swift ships; one white sturgeon remained with a fisherman, while the other four were released immediately before a water-walking stranger approached.

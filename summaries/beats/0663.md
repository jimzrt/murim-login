# Chapter 663

## Plot

Jin Taekyung acknowledges the legitimacy of Baeksang's anger while condemning the innocent Nanman victims created by Baeksang's alliance with Dark Heaven. He compares Baeksang to the Head Elder, arguing that revenge has trapped them both on a path of guilt and destruction, and demands to know how much more blood Baeksang intends to spill.

Baeksang leaves convinced that Jin cannot escape his execution, but Jin's words and memories of his dead child Hwi cause him to waver for the first time in decades. Jin refuses to accept his death and discovers Taishan above the broken ceiling, hungry and imprisoned for eating too much.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains bound in the underground prison with sealed internal energy and heavy iron balls, with his public execution scheduled in two days.
- Jin intends to escape and stop Baeksang rather than submit to the scheduled execution.
- Baeksang believes Jin's compassion for the Han Chinese under his command caused him to surrender instead of fleeing.
- Baeksang's only child Hwi was killed during the Great Snow Mountain battle, and the memory continues to drive his grief and revenge.
- Baeksang accepted an offer from an unidentified beautiful woman decades ago in pursuit of revenge and believes he has crossed a river from which he cannot return.
- Jin identifies the Miao village massacre, the Thousand-Year Spiders' escape from the Poisonblood Grounds, and the Western Yao Estate incident as a connected plot that has killed more than three hundred people.
- Baeksang leaves the prison while privately wavering over the path of revenge and the consequences of completing it.
- Taishan is imprisoned above Jin's cell after being punished for eating too much and discovers the broken ceiling.

# Chapter 563

## Plot

Go Se-won arrives at Ares Guild headquarters and finds Vice Guild Master Go Jun watching President Baek Hanseong's New Year press conference with Jin Taekyung. Jin's sleeping, sarcastic, and profane live-broadcast behavior becomes a historic broadcasting accident, while the public's enthusiastic response makes Go Se-won realize that Jin is being elevated as a new heroic figure and possible second Cheon Taemin.

Jin publicly reveals that the third Mutated Gate was effectively controlled by a major Guild whose rescue team failed to arrive for fifteen minutes, allowing the mana level to rise until mutation. The President announces a government investigation and frames Jin as inheriting the deceased hero's legacy, prompting Go Jun to destroy the protected television and lose control while vowing to kill Jin.

## Continuity

- Three Mutated Gates have appeared within the first ten days of the new year.
- Jin Taekyung and President Baek Hanseong held an official New Year press conference together, and Jin publicly discussed suppressing the three recent Mutated Gates.
- Jin revealed that the third Mutated Gate was handled by a major Guild under a permanent lease, that its rescue team failed to arrive for fifteen minutes, and that timely intervention by even one A-rank Hunter might have prevented the full mutation.
- The Korean government intends to investigate the Guild's negligent response to the third Mutated Gate.
- Go Se-won recognized that the press conference's questioning was being steered toward exposing the major Guild's Gate monopoly and management failure.
- Go Jun's suppressed humiliation and anger over Lee Jungryong's death and Ares Guild's declining influence erupted during the broadcast; he destroyed the protected television and vowed to kill Jin Taekyung.
- Public perception is shifting toward treating Jin Taekyung as a new hero who carries forward the legacy associated with Cheon Taemin and the recently deceased Lee Jungryong.

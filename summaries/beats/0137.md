# Chapter 137

## Plot

Hyuk Mujin warns Jin Taekyung that the Murim is full of hidden, persistent grudges and possible assassins, but his knowledge comes mostly from wuxia novels and he confuses fiction with reality, earning a punch from Taekyung. At Honghwa Inn, Prince Shangshan's royal command arrives with an official and roughly one hundred soldiers to summon the young Murim prodigies. The injured Five Gates heirs repeatedly collapse during the public proclamation, while Woo Jintae remains unconscious and absent. Taekyung explains his absence as the result of a minor disagreement, then proposes Cheongpung as a more impressive replacement for the luncheon delegation.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Mujin is an avid reader of wuxia novels and sometimes mistakes fictional Murim conventions for reality; Taekyung punched him after he kept insisting that such fictional dangers were commonplace.
- Prince Shangshan's royal command summons the young Murim prodigies staying at Honghwa Inn to the luncheon, and the command is delivered with an official procession and approximately one hundred soldiers.
- The four injured Five Gates heirs accompanying Taekyung collapse during the public proclamation, while Woo Jintae remains unconscious after his beating and cannot attend.
- Taekyung presents Woo Jintae's absence as an injury from a minor disagreement and proposes Cheongpung as a more impressive replacement because Cheongpung is a Peak master.
- Taekyung and Cheongpung slept in the annex after Cheongpung fell asleep following the hot spring; Taekyung still has not identified Cheongpung's background.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 산서성부 as “Shanxi Provincial Office.”
- Render 왕명 as “royal command.”
- Render 철전 as “iron coins” and retain “shichen” for 시진.
- Render 씹덕 as “otaku” in Taekyung's mocking voice.
- Render the literary titles 점소이 검신 되다, 아파야 무인이다, and 무림의 아들 걸어서 구주팔황 세 바퀴 반 as established in the reading copy.

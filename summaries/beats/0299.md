# Chapter 299

## Plot

After delaying the rescue for one hour and eight minutes, the Seoul Branch President finally permits entry. Choi Minwoo leads more than fifty Peace Guild members into the Black Wizard's Black Forest, with Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Song Song among them. They find a completely scorched trail, no movement or life within three hundred meters, and apparent evidence that Jin Taekyung and the Named Monster were destroyed together. The Peace Guild mourns Taekyung while the Seoul Branch President and Go Jun arrive and congratulate themselves for avoiding further casualties.

Go Jun taunts Choi Minwoo, blames the Peace Guild for Taekyung's apparent death, and tells him that the dead do not return. Taekyung then appears alive, mocks Go Jun for completing the forty-ninth-day memorial rite while he was away urinating, spits out phlegm, and challenges him to fight.

## Continuity

- The Seoul Branch President delayed the rescue response despite the Peace Guild's protest, allowing more than one hour to pass after Taekyung entered the Gate.
- Choi Minwoo led more than fifty Peace Guild members into the Black Forest after declaring that the Guild would take the lead.
- The Peace Guild initially found no life in the devastated area and believed Taekyung had died alongside the Named Monster.
- Go Jun supported the Peace Guild's dangerous advance, then taunted Choi Minwoo and blamed the Guild for Taekyung's apparent death.
- Jin Taekyung survived and returned to the trail, immediately confronting Go Jun with profanity.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 49재 as “forty-ninth-day memorial rite,” with a footnote explaining the Korean Buddhist observance.
- Render 산화하다 in the destruction context as “be oxidized,” preserving the misleading evidence that Taekyung and the monsters were destroyed.
- Render 서울 중앙 협회장 as “Seoul Branch President” and 경호팀장 as “Head of Security.”

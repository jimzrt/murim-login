# Chapter 1111

## Plot

The West Gate has fallen, with more than half of its garrison reported as casualties; Cheongpung and Jin Taekyung retreated to the Inner City, both badly injured, and Taekyung may not survive. At the South Gate, the Slaughter Saint chooses to remain with the Bow Saint and defend their position rather than go to Taekyung, while the Bow Saint’s motives remain unclear. The Slaughter Saint calls on Jeok Cheongang for help, and Jeok answers with an earthquake.

The Blood Lord heads toward the Inner City to kill Taekyung. At the North Gate, Jeok Cheongang has killed all but the last of the Twelve Secret Monks and advances on the Dalai Lama.

## Continuity

- The West Gate has fallen; more than half of its garrison are confirmed casualties, and some survivors retreated to the Inner City while others continue holding off the enemy.
- Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung reached the Inner City but are both badly injured; Taekyung may not survive.
- The Slaughter Saint remains at the South Gate with the Bow Saint because the defense needs him; the Bow Saint’s reason for not trying to save Taekyung is unresolved.
- Jeok Cheongang has killed all but the last of the Twelve Secret Monks and is confronting the Dalai Lama at the North Gate; he has caused a major earthquake at the South Gate.
- The Blood Lord is heading toward the Inner City intending to kill Jin Taekyung.

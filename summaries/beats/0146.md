# Chapter 146

## Plot

At the end of the luncheon, Zhu Bao gives Jin Taekyung a golden medallion called Prince Shangshan's Token, which can be presented to request a favor; Taekyung receives it as the Quest Reward. Hong Jin boards Taekyung's carriage without an appointment and arranges gifts, a messenger, soldiers, and a delegation to visit the Jin Family of Taiyuan, while Li Feng entrusts Cheongpung to Taekyung. Cheongpung decides to stay at the Jin Family after obtaining the Royal Guard Armor Set, and the Five Gates of Shanxi's young prodigies agree to remain at Honghwa Inn until New Year's Day while supporting the Seongun Escort Bureau. During the journey, Cheongpung reveals that he came to Huashan at about age three or four and was not born there. Hong Jin reveals that he is a eunuch, explains that he once served the late Emperor and was ordered to assist Prince Shangshan, and says he came to the frontier in something like exile before the carriage reaches the Jin Family of Taiyuan.

## Continuity

- The Quest target was very satisfied with the day's meeting, and the Quest Reward, Prince Shangshan's Token, was obtained at departure.
- Prince Shangshan is ten years old and is expected to attend the Jin Family's grand banquet in roughly fifteen days, around New Year's Day.
- Hong Jin is a eunuch who formerly served the late Emperor; the late Emperor ordered him to assist Prince Shangshan, whom Hong Jin has served since infancy.
- Hong Jin came to the frontier in something like exile and remains loyal to Prince Shangshan despite openly admitting that he likes bribes.
- Hong Jin and a nearly hundred-soldier escort with a hastily assembled delegation are traveling to the Jin Family of Taiyuan without a prior appointment.
- Cheongpung received the Royal Guard Armor Set and decided to stay temporarily at the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- Cheongpung says he came to Huashan when he was about three or four years old and was not born there; before encountering civilization, he slept on grass or rocks while living with Mae Jonghak.
- The Five Gates of Shanxi's young prodigies will remain at Honghwa Inn until New Year's Day, cooperate regarding the Seongun Escort Bureau, and rely on Taekyung to smooth matters with their Sect Leaders.
- The current Military Commissioner is described as incompetent and fond of bribes; he is appointed and dismissed directly by the Emperor.
- Cheongpung has no martial title yet, and Zhu Bao will not accept his autograph until he acquires one.

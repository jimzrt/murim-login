# Chapter 123

## Plot

Cheol Mubaek lies severely injured while Lee Seowol cares for him. He recounts how he and her father, Lee Cheonbaek, first met after Cheonbaek searched the mountains for three months; they fought before becoming lifelong friends. Cheol urges Seowol to pursue happiness and marriage, but she insists that rebuilding the Mount Heng Sword Sect is what she wants. He reveals that he entrusted her with the Shura Annihilating Fist manual despite its single-successor and worthy-only transmission traditions, believing the Heaven Shaking Sword or Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi could protect her.

Jin Mukyung violently urges Taekyung to marry Seowol so he can obtain the Shura Annihilating Fist, Blood Wolf Sword Technique, and Blood Wolf Footwork. Taekyung argues that the Shura Annihilating Fist, while still an outstanding Peak martial art, is an old technique whose former status does not make it one of the world's current ten greatest fist techniques, and rejects the marriage. He then reveals that Jopil left behind the Supreme Peak martial art Flame Divine Palm, leaving Mukyung stunned.

## Continuity

- Cheol Mubaek has broken limbs and serious internal injuries and requires at least four months of recuperation.
- Cheol Mubaek is the ninth-generation successor of the Shura Annihilating Fist and broke its single-successor, worthy-only transmission tradition by giving the manual to Lee Seowol.
- Cheol Mubaek and Lee Cheonbaek first met more than thirty years ago after Cheonbaek searched the mountain range for three months; they fought before becoming close friends.
- The Shura Annihilating Fist was one of the world's ten greatest fist techniques two hundred years ago, but its martial lineage was believed to have ended and it is no longer regarded as a current top-ten technique.
- Jopil is dead and left behind the Supreme Peak martial art Flame Divine Palm.
- Cheol Mubaek believes the Heaven Shaking Sword and Sleeping Dragon of Shanxi could protect Seowol if she enters a political marriage; he favors the Heaven Shaking Sword because of Taekyung's old reputation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 일인전승 as “single successor” and 비인부전 as “transmission only to the worthy.”
- Render 구 대 계승자 as “ninth-generation successor” and 천하십대권법 as “the ten greatest fist techniques in the world.”
- Retain “Uncle Cheol,” “Seowol,” and “hyung” for the established familial and address conventions.

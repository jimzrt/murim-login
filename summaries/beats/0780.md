# Chapter 780

## Plot

Jin Taekyung confronts Michael Silbert with evidence that he can absorb monsters’ magical power while containing mana, explaining how Michael concealed his nature and grew powerful. Michael effectively confirms the revelation, recalls that Cheon Taemin once saw through him, then awakens a vast, increasingly dark power and declares that the only person he fears is absent.

## Continuity

- Michael Silbert can absorb monsters’ magical power to grow stronger while maintaining and concealing mana within himself; Jin identifies him as effectively half-human and half-demon, and Michael does not deny the revelation.
- Michael says Cheon Taemin once fixed his gaze on him and caused him fear; he avoided Lee Jungryong’s funeral in case Taemin was there.
- Michael awakens a vast power in which darkness has increasingly overwhelmed the balance of light and darkness.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep magical power distinct from mana.

# Chapter 667

## Plot

Yayul Mok and the Seven Miao Tigers infiltrate the underground prison, silently subdue its outer guards and wardens, disguise themselves as Bai warriors, and descend toward Jin Taekyung's cell. Their deception fails at the fourth-floor security checkpoint, where the guards recognize the danger and raise the alarm.

Yayul Mok and the Seven Miao Tigers fight through more than fifty Bai warriors while refusing to kill fellow Nanman people. With the rescue party overwhelmed, Wonhu opens a path for a desperate breakthrough, and Yayul Mok prepares to give Jin an antidote that will release the seal on his internal energy. Just as he reaches a dark corridor, someone emerges from the darkness, grabs his shoulder, and asks where he is going.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains imprisoned in the underground prison, with execution scheduled for noon in two days.
- The Beast Miao King and Yayul Mok are jointly risking their positions and lives to rescue Jin Taekyung and prevent a war between Nanman and the Central Plains.
- Yayul Mok and the Seven Miao Tigers have infiltrated the underground prison disguised as Bai warriors, but the rescue attempt has been discovered at the fourth-floor security checkpoint.
- Yayul Mok's group has sworn not to kill fellow Nanman people during the rescue, leaving them severely outnumbered by the prison guards.
- Gisan and Dogok remain behind near the captured wardens to preserve the infiltration's cover, while Wonhu leads the breakthrough with the other Seven Miao Tigers.
- Yayul Mok carries a wooden case containing an antidote that will release the seal on Jin Taekyung's internal energy rather than a Force-Sealing Pill.
- An unidentified person has seized Yayul Mok at the entrance to a dark corridor, and the rescue's outcome remains unresolved.
- Twenty tribal chieftains, including Baeksang, support Jin Taekyung's execution as the agenda of tomorrow's final Tribal Grand Council.
- The Beast Miao King and Baeksang are sworn brothers whose opposing responses to their children's deaths have driven them apart.
- Baeksang remains visibly conflicted and has not summoned the guards to stop the rescue.

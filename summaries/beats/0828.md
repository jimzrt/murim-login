# Chapter 828

## Plot

Jin’s white-blue flames destroy the Doppelganger’s seventy-two Golems and magic circles, causing mana backlash. As the Doppelganger tries to escape, it blinds the Skeleton King with an enhanced Light spell. Jin guides the temporarily blinded Skeleton King by Sound Transmission, helping him evade the Doppelganger’s spells and anticipate its Blink. The Skeleton King then cleaves the Doppelganger in two; its fate is not confirmed, and its final thought laments losing its EXP.

## Continuity

- Jin’s flames destroyed the temple’s Golems and magic circles, leaving the Doppelganger injured by mana backlash.
- The Doppelganger tried to escape using a Teleport magic circle; the Skeleton King interrupted it and struck it in two, but whether it survived is unknown.
- Jin used Sound Transmission to guide the temporarily blinded Skeleton King through the Doppelganger’s attacks.
- The Doppelganger’s enhanced Light magic temporarily blinded the Skeleton King.
- Jin’s Main Quest [Cataclysm] still requires him to eliminate the Doppelganger; this chapter does not confirm its death.
- The Doppelganger’s claims about Asmodeus and the temple, and the cause of the earlier radiance, remain unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep mana distinct from magical power.
- Render 라이트 마법 as “Light magic,” 텔레포트 as “Teleport,” 블링크 as “Blink,” and 바인딩 as “Binding.”
- Render [영웅의 검] as “Hero’s Sword” and 경험치 as “EXP.”

# Chapter 549

## Plot

Jin Taekyung briefs the six-member Fire Dragon Pavilion on their mission to investigate the Nanman Beast Palace, identifies the region's harsh geography and climate as a major obstacle, and orders an immediate departure. Ju Hwaran uses her prior Nanman escort experience and the Escort King's records to plan their supplies and route, while the group chooses Mount Daebyeol as its secondary meeting point. Her former engagement to Sama Pyo creates visible awkwardness, and Taekyung finds himself unsettled by it.

## Continuity

- The six-member Fire Dragon Pavilion must leave for the Nanman Beast Palace immediately, carrying minimal supplies and arranging horses and carriages within half a shichen.
- Ju Hwaran previously traveled to Nanman on an escort assignment and knows its terrain and hidden routes through the Escort King's records.
- The party will disperse and regroup at Mount Daebyeol to leave the crowded area discreetly.
- Ju Hwaran and Sama Pyo's ended political engagement remains emotionally awkward for both of them, though the reason it ended is still unknown.
- Taekyung considers the Nanman Beast Palace an especially suitable potential site for Dark Heaven's second rift because it contains many spiritual creatures and venomous beasts.

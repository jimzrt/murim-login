# Chapter 1128

## Plot

Jin Taekyung reunites with the surviving Fire Dragon Pavilion members and learns that Hyuk Mujin is alive, though unconscious and badly injured. Mae Jonghak praises Mujin’s courage and tells Taekyung that the allied forces are fighting under Taekyung’s name.

Mae Jonghak explains that he persuaded the Seafaring King and the Green Forest Battle King to resist Dark Heaven, as in the Great Faction War. He tells Taekyung that people needed someone to believe in, and calls Taekyung a light who unites them beyond rank or formality.

As Taekyung’s remaining time runs down, he and Jeok Cheongang speak about meeting again after death. Taekyung asks Jeok to pass a message to his family in the realm of immortals if they ever meet. With one second left, Taekyung apologizes to his Master, and the world around him closes.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Mujin is alive but unconscious after suffering serious internal injuries; the Seafaring King removed the lethal blood in time, and Mae Jonghak says Mujin is out of danger.
- The Seafaring King and Green Forest Battle King have joined the resistance against Dark Heaven after Mae Jonghak persuaded them.
- The allied forces are fighting in Xining and shouting Jin Taekyung’s name; the battle is still underway.
- Taekyung’s System countdown falls from 59 seconds to 1 second; as it expires, the world around him closes. His survival remains uncertain.
- Taekyung asks Jeok Cheongang to pass word to Taekyung’s family in the realm of immortals if Jeok ever meets them.
- Jeok Cheongang tells Taekyung he hopes they will meet again after death; their conversation ends before Jeok can state the favor he wanted in return.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 저승 as “afterlife” in Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang’s conversation.
- Render 우화등선 as “ascend to immortality.”

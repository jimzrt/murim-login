# Chapter 508

## Plot

Mungyeong reveals that Mu Song and five Water Dragon Stronghold subordinates recognized him as a highly ranked Supreme Peak master. He threatens all six into silence, forbidding them to reveal his identity even to the Seafaring King. Jeok Cheongang withdraws to circulate his qi and orders Taekyung to stay behind, leaving Taekyung alone with Mungyeong. Mungyeong blasts Taekyung off the ship into the Yangtze, and Linked Quest Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2 begins.

## Continuity

- Mu Song and five Water Dragon Stronghold subordinates know that Mungyeong is an exceptionally powerful Supreme Peak master but have not inferred that he is the Slaughter Saint; Mungyeong has threatened them into keeping this secret from the Seafaring King and everyone else.
- Mungyeong has begun Linked Quest Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2 by launching Taekyung into the river.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 궁예 as Gung Ye and preserve the mind-reading joke with a brief explanatory footnote.
- Render 연계 퀘스트 as Linked Quest and 가짜 무림인-2단계 as Fake Murim Martial Artist—Stage 2.
- Keep Mungyeong's threats dry and lethal, Mu Song's speech formal and fearful, and Taekyung's profanity comedic but restrained.

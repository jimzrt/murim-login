# Chapter 1061

## Plot

Taekyung uses Qi Sense on Great Sir. The System only partially succeeds, showing Level 119 but initially no name; after Great Sir calls himself “Madman,” the display changes, and later changes again to “Gaettong.” Ma Junggeol says Great Sir’s name changes between visits and recalls “Jeomsuni,” “Gaettong,” and “Sottong.” Taekyung gives up trying to make sense of it.

## Continuity

- Great Sir is Level 119; the System first showed his name as unknown, then displayed Madman and finally Gaettong after he named himself. Ma Junggeol says the name he uses changes between visits, including Jeomsuni and Sottong; his identity remains unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 미친놈 as “Madman” when Great Sir adopts it as a name; retain the established “Gaettong” rendering for 개똥이.

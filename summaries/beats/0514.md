# Chapter 514

## Plot

A group of twenty Shaolin monks, led by the middle-aged martial monk Jung Ho, travels toward the ferry at Xixia with a mysterious, hoarse-voiced figure in a conical hat. Hearing cries of murder, they hurry and encounter Sama Pyo, the Young Sect Leader of the Black Dragon Demon Gate, with his towering subordinate.

The subordinate has killed Blood Cudgel Do Sangho on the main road. Jung Ho identifies the corpse and explains that Do Sangho recently killed five innocent merchants in Shaanxi. Sama Pyo agrees to explain the incident, apologize to nearby commoners, and offer compensation, while shifting responsibility to his subordinate. The conical-hatted figure then claims Sama Pyo's sword belongs to someone else, but Sama Pyo insists it is his. A distant shout announces the arrival of the Jin Family of Taiyuan.

## Continuity

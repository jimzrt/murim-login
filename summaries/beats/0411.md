# Chapter 411

## Plot

The Arch Lich's Magic Trap devastates the battlefield, but Johnson's Barrier protects Jin Taekyung and the more than two hundred members of the suicide squad. Jin leads them through the opening instead of stopping to fight, while the main force follows behind.

Watching through countless Familiars, the Arch Lich recalls the ancient Adversary who once drove a sword into its body. Believing the Adversary may have died, it sends its twenty elite undead guards into the battle and redirects half of them toward Jin after recognizing him as the human who defeated the Death Knight Lord.

Jin and the suicide squad reach the elite rear forces, where ten undead around Level 120 block their path. While Team Leader Choi urges retreat, Jin treats the powerful Liches and Death Knights as lunchboxes and prepares to use One Annihilation.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung and the suicide squad have survived three Magic Traps and are advancing through the Arch Lich's elite rear forces while minimizing casualties.
- Johnson's Barrier protected the suicide squad from the latest trap, though some members were injured; Jin intends to thank Johnson after the battle.
- Jin's immediate objective is to break through the monsters and join Lee Jungryong and Wu Heixing rather than become bogged down in combat.
- The Arch Lich observes the battlefield through numerous Familiars and commands its undead forces remotely.
- The Arch Lich was once a powerful monster trusted by a king, fell in the final battle at the hands of the human Adversary, and now exists as an undead ruler.
- The Arch Lich has sent twenty elite undead guards, including Death Knights and Liches, into the battle and redirected half of them to eliminate Jin.
- The Arch Lich recognizes Jin as the human who defeated the Death Knight Lord created from Lei Fei and considers Jin an irritating threat that must be removed.
- Ten Darkened undead around Level 120 now stand between Jin's suicide squad and its destination.

- Render 대적자 as the Adversary.
- Render 매직 트랩 as Magic Trap and 영웅의 혼 as Hero's Soul.

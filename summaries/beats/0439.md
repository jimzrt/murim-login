# Chapter 439

## Plot

Jin Taekyung gives the Skeleton King a translation-magic ring from Magic Johnson, but the undead monster's attempt to pass as an American through his Atlanta origin story and Korean-accented English only creates more trouble. The Skeleton King reveals that he has a social-media account with 5,134 followers and sends Magic Johnson an embarrassing message, prompting Taekyung to insist that he keep his identity hidden. Team Leader Choi explains that Korea is preparing an enormous welcome for Taekyung, including a car parade and the President's appearance, while Taekyung objects to political involvement despite the tax benefits offered in connection with the fifty-trillion bounty on the Arch Lich. At Incheon Airport, Taekyung nevertheless shakes hands with the President before a wall of cameras, while the Skeleton King privately condemns the supposedly vile humans who arranged everything.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton King's undead identity remains concealed from the public, and he agrees to restrain himself rather than reveal it.
- The Skeleton King adopts Stone-King as his claimed identity and insists that Team Leader Choi call him Mr. King.
- Magic Johnson gave the Skeleton King a translation-magic ring and a Magic Gem-powered smartphone; the Skeleton King has 5,134 social-media followers.
- Jin Taekyung has returned to Korea as its publicly recognized representative S-rank Hunter, drawing tens of thousands of people to Incheon Airport and causing surrounding traffic to stop.
- A military- and police-controlled car parade was prepared for Taekyung, and the President greeted him and posed with him at the airport photo line.
- The Chinese government's fifty-trillion bounty for the Arch Lich is payable to Jin Taekyung and may create several trillion in Korean tax liability; Taekyung chooses to pay the taxes rather than seek political favor.
- Team Leader Choi handles Taekyung's media, logistics, and official arrangements, but Taekyung has warned him not to make political commitments without consultation.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 스톤 킹 as “Stone-King” and preserve the Skeleton King's preferred title “Mr. King.”
- Preserve the Skeleton King's pompous “this king” voice, formal diction, and the address “vile human.”
- Retain Taekyung's profanity, crude humor, and Korean-accented-English jokes.
- Preserve Arch Lich, S-rank Hunter, Magic Gem, Inventory, Blue House, Peace Guild, and Demon Realm as established terminology.

# Chapter 328

## Plot

Ju Hwaran's sword strike kills Heo Jun after his betrayal is exposed. She pays respects to the outsiders who helped the Yongbong Escort Bureau, accepts Taekyung's party as guests, and prepares to recover the dead and return to the bureau.

During the meal, Taekyung, Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, Hyuk Mujin, Ju Hwaran, and Song Ilseom discuss the bureau's food and Song Ilseom's concealed identity. Song Ilseom reveals that his father was Song Pyosan and that his mother was the child Ju Gongsan rescued from the Guangdong Chen Family during the Great Faction War, then presents a faded jade hairpin connected to that rescue.

## Continuity

- Heo Jun is dead after Ju Hwaran's final sword strike.
- Song Ilseom has revealed that his father was Song Pyosan and that his mother was the child Ju Gongsan rescued from the Guangdong Chen Family during the Great Faction War; the faded jade hairpin he presents is connected to that rescue.
- Taekyung's party must find the Divine Physician in Sichuan within Jeok Cheongang's six-to-eight-month treatment window, but their departure has been delayed by the Zhongnan dispute and the Yongbong Escort Bureau's hospitality.

# Chapter 620

## Plot

The Fire Dragon Pavilion confirms that the Poison Flower Pavilion owner is Namho, a non-Han Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent. To conceal their meeting, Namho stages a violent expulsion that destroys the inn, then reunites with the group at a lakeside rendezvous. He gives them a wooden map documenting Nanman’s terrain and tribes over more than fifty years, confirms that he has received warnings about the rift, and agrees to guide them toward their destination. The contact mission and Seeds Planted in Nanman Quest are completed, a new linked Quest is generated, and Namho leaves his home with the group because he believes Dark Heaven will soon bring catastrophe to Nanman.

## Continuity

- Namho is the Hidden Shadow Pavilion agent operating under the cover of the Poison Flower Pavilion in Nanman; he confirms his identity through Thousand-Faced Fox’s code exchange.
- Namho has spent more than fifty years surveying Nanman’s terrain and tribal locations and gives the Fire Dragon Pavilion his wooden map.
- Namho agrees to guide the Fire Dragon Pavilion wherever its Nanman mission requires and leaves his longtime residence with them.
- Namho has received the Pavilion Master’s warning about the rift through a messenger eagle and accepts that Dark Heaven is likely targeting Nanman.
- The Contact with a Hidden Shadow Pavilion Agent mission and Seeds Planted in Nanman Quest are complete, and a new linked Quest has appeared.
- The identity of the woman associated with the Heavenly Demon Escort Bureau group and the party responsible for poisoning that group remain unresolved.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion’s dangers and objectives beyond the Hidden Shadow Pavilion contact, including the Nanman Beast Palace mission, remain unresolved.

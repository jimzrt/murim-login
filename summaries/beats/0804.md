# Chapter 804

## Plot

The arrival of the S-rank monsters renews the battle. Jin Taekyung restores the Hunters’ morale with the effect of One Against a Thousand, keeps command, and assigns Magic Johnson to protect the force from aerial threats while Team Leader Choi holds the front. Taekyung uncovers and attacks the Scorpion King, then charges toward the S-rank monsters as their armies advance across the ground and sky.

## Continuity

- Three S-rank monsters lead the ground assault; a Griffon leads hundreds of flying monsters.
- Taekyung remains in command and assigns Magic Johnson to aerial defense and Team Leader Choi to the front line.
- Taekyung attacks the Scorpion King and, after a System alert, charges toward the S-rank monsters.
- Taekyung believes The Prophet is somewhere on the battlefield, but neither he nor the Skeleton King has located him.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 강기 as Force, distinct from Sword Force.
- Use Scorpion King, Soldier Scorpion, Worker Scorpion, and Elite Scorpion for the System’s scorpion labels.

# Chapter 523

## Plot

Jin Taekyung cultivates the Fire Gate Divine Technique, achieves Three Flowers Gather at the Crown, and attempts to break through Five Qi Returning to Origin. Mungyeong interrupts him, causing his internal energy to reverse and making him vomit, then gives him a custom-made pill containing substantial fire qi for his ongoing training.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has achieved Three Flowers Gather at the Crown but has not yet reached Five Qi Returning to Origin.
- Mungyeong is actively training Taekyung to control the rough fire qi of the Fire Gate Divine Technique and has given him a custom-made fire-qi pill.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 칠공 as seven apertures, 단환 as pill, 저승사자 as Grim Reaper, 저그 as Zerg, and 나이아가라 폭포 as Niagara Falls.
- Render Taekyung's teasing address 문 씨 as Mr. Moon and preserve the chapter's blunt profanity and monster-comparison humor.

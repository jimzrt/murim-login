# Chapter 929

## Plot

As fighting spreads across the imperial capital, a former Grand Preceptor gathers supporters of Prince Shangshan and leads them toward the mountain, inspired by a song planted among the people by a mysterious bamboo-hatted man. Jeong Hogun arrives in golden armor and reveals that his earlier words and actions in the marketplace were carried out under the Emperor’s command. He delivers the Emperor’s decree authorizing the former Grand Preceptor to raise Crown Prince Zhu Bao’s banner and join the Embroidered Uniform Guard in defeating the traitors. The united forces rout the rebels; three days later, someone awakens.

## Continuity

- The former Grand Preceptor, an elder driven from office decades ago, accepts the Emperor’s decree and leads thousands of supporters of Zhu Bao, the Crown Prince, to join Jeong Hogun’s Embroidered Uniform Guard against the traitors.
- Jeong Hogun was the bamboo-hatted man who planted the song and urged support for Prince Shangshan in the marketplace; he confirms these actions were done under the Emperor’s command.
- The fighting in the imperial capital ends with the rebel forces routed and scattered; three days later, an unidentified person wakes.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 반정 as “restoration” in this chapter’s political context.
- Use “Crown Prince” for 황태제 when referring to Zhu Bao’s succession position.

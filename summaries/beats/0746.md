# Chapter 746

## Plot

After returning from Switzerland, Jin and his allies face a rapidly worsening crisis: mutation Gates now appear several times a day, magical-power levels rise continuously, and disaster coverage dominates the media. Jin suppresses a monster wave at a protest rally in Sokcho, Gangwon Province, while reporters exploit the catastrophe to attack him, the Ares Guild, and his connection to Siegfried Wassmann's death. Song Song helps him recover from the strain, and they return to Team Leader Choi's office with the Skeleton King concealed in Jin's Inventory. Despite continued efforts, Magic Johnson has found no breakthrough, The Prophet remains untraceable, and Odin Guild's influence keeps growing. Team Leader Choi then reveals that Tokyo Bay collapsed three minutes earlier.

## Continuity

- Mutation Gates are now occurring three or four times daily in Korea, while magical-power distribution continues rising worldwide.
- Jin remains under intense media attack orchestrated through Odin Guild's influence, with public opinion being turned against him and Ares Guild.
- Three days after the Switzerland trip, Magic Johnson's investigation has produced no result and The Prophet remains undetectable despite Magic and satellite surveillance.
- Jin continues concealing the Skeleton King's identity and limiting his public exposure; the Skeleton King is currently being carried in Jin's Inventory.
- Tokyo Bay has collapsed, creating an immediate crisis whose cause and connection to the approaching ancient monster remain unknown.

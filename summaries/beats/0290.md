# Chapter 290

## Plot

Jin Taekyung uses Martial Arts Manual Creation in a hospital bathroom, spends Intelligence points to meet the 100-Intelligence requirement for a Peak-grade manual, and creates a manual for the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique. After bringing it to the Peace Guild, Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim recognize it as a Mana Cultivation Method. Taekyung conceals its true origin by claiming that his late father taught it as a family health regimen and that the original manual was lost.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has the Martial Arts Manual Creation Skill, which can create manuals only for martial arts at Great Completion and requires physical materials.
- Taekyung created a manual for the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique using A4 paper and a pen after investing Intelligence points to reach the required 100 Intelligence.
- Team Leader Choi and Butler Kim identify the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique manual as a Mana Cultivation Method.
- Taekyung claims that his late father taught him the Jin Family's Cultivation Technique as a family health regimen and that the original manual disappeared after the family moved.
- Taekyung now understands that Lee Jungryong and his subordinates' martial movements and mana use are connected to the Mana Cultivation Method.

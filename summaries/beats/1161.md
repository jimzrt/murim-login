# Chapter 1161

## Plot

The blast from the Skeleton King's strike devastates the Dragon Lair and kills tens of thousands of monsters. Morgoth survives, wounded by the Hero's Sword, and finds the Skeleton King reduced to his head but still alive. The Skeleton King rejects Morgoth's attempt to define his identity, affirming his friendship with Jin Taekyung before losing consciousness. Morgoth reflects on friendship and his new curiosity about Jin, then senses an unexpected guest. The guest asks what Morgoth is holding.

## Continuity

- Morgoth and the Skeleton King survived the Dragon Lair's devastating blast; the Skeleton King is unconscious and reduced to his head, while Morgoth has a wound through his palm from the Hero's Sword.
- The Skeleton King identifies Jin Taekyung as his friend and rejects Morgoth's claim that he belongs with Morgoth or shares his nature.
- Morgoth has become intensely curious about Jin Taekyung and the meaning of friendship; an unidentified guest arrives as Morgoth holds the Hero's Sword.
- Morgoth's three-day deadline and demand for Cheon Taemin and Jin Taekyung as tribute remain in effect.
- Cheon Taemin remains unconscious in a recovery capsule in a hidden Pentagon chamber.
- Jin believes Cheon Taemin was the Martial God and The Helper, and suspects Asmodeus was not completely erased; these identities and suspicions are unconfirmed.
- The World Hunter Federation was ordered to mobilize for Moscow against Morgoth and his monsters; Jin intended to go alone.
- Morgoth was summoned by Asmodeus from beyond distant stars and space, but says he is not devoted to Asmodeus.

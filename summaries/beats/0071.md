# Chapter 71

## Plot

Jin Mukyung reveals his extraordinary martial-arts talent by converting the Jin Family’s Spear Technique into a sword technique with only minor adjustments. He then turns Taekyung’s training into a physical lesson, overwhelming him in a spar while demonstrating the importance of caution, reading an opponent’s intent, and maintaining physical conditioning. After paralyzing and silencing Taekyung with acupoint techniques, Mukyung threatens him with a lethal technique and cuts his neck only shallowly before releasing the restraints. Taekyung survives and Mukyung tells him that he died once that day.

## Continuity

- Jin Mukyung is a naturally gifted martial-arts genius capable of adapting the Jin Family’s forms across different weapons.
- Mukyung’s current instruction of Taekyung uses repeated physical combat and practical lessons, including hidden iron protection and deceptive tactics.
- Mukyung can temporarily immobilize and silence Taekyung through acupoint techniques; after a shallow neck cut and the release of those techniques, he tells Taekyung that Taekyung died once that day.

## Translation Decisions

- Translate 마혈 as Paralysis Acupoint and 아혈 as Mute Acupoint in System notices.
- Render 분근착골 as Tendon-Splitting and Bone-Twisting.
- Retain the established Jin Family’s Spear Technique, Jin Sword Technique, and Sky-Piercing Strike terminology.

# Chapter 530

## Plot

Henan remains lively and comparatively safe despite the approaching war because thousands of Murim Alliance martial artists have gathered there. Jin Taekyung travels through the crowded streets with Hyuk Mujin and Gung Gibang toward Gowolru, where they expect to meet the Kunlun Cloud Dragon and Hwangbo Ak. Hyuk's concern about attracting attention is comically revealed to be fear of having an accident because he urgently needs a toilet. Before they reach the inn, an explosion blows open part of Gowolru and sends Hwangbo Ak into the street. A gigantic unidentified man attacks Hwangbo again, and Taekyung steps forward to stop him.

## Continuity

- Gowolru is the three-story inn where Taekyung, Hyuk Mujin, and Gung Gibang were scheduled to meet the Kunlun Cloud Dragon and Hwangbo Ak.
- Hwangbo Ak was thrown from Gowolru and attacked by an unidentified gigantic man; Taekyung stepped between them and refused to move.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 고월루 as Gowolru and 취팔선보 as Drunken Eight-Immortals Step; preserve the blunt, profane banter and the toilet-misunderstanding comedy.

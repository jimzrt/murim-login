# Chapter 829

## Plot

The Doppelganger repeatedly dies and regenerates while the Skeleton King cuts it down, but Jin’s spear and blue-white flames reduce it to a Level 10 shadow. Jin confirms its true essence with the Eye of Truth and pins it down, furious over the devastation it caused. When the Doppelganger begs for its life and blames the king—Demon King Asmodeus—Jin’s anger wavers.

## Continuity

- The Doppelganger survived its apparent destruction by regenerating in new bodies, drawing on its stored lives; after Jin’s attack, it remains as a Level 10 shadow and is at Jin’s mercy.
- Jin’s Eye of Truth revealed the Doppelganger’s true essence as Level 10, despite its stolen powers and memories.
- The Doppelganger says its crimes were carried out on Demon King Asmodeus’s orders; Jin’s reaction to the accusation is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Erasure distinct from ordinary death or destruction; the Doppelganger fears its remaining lives may be exhausted.
- Render 수마 as “sleep demon.”

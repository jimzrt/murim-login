# Chapter 701

## Plot

The Southern Heaven Demon Empress reveals that the masked man is her carefully trained hunting dog. Dark power resembling the force of defying heaven regenerates his shattered bones and wounds, while Jin realizes that the man cannot feel pain. The Demon Empress also explains nothing about the Lord of Heaven's interest in Jin, and Jin provokes her by suggesting that she does not know the reason either.

The Demon Empress's power continues to grow as demonic qi flows from the rift. Jin and the exhausted guardian spirit each engage one opponent so they can end the battle before the humans and beasts in the Inner Palace finish mutating. Jin attacks with Flamefire Path, but the Demon Empress overpowers him, drives him into the ground, and finally slices his thigh while the fight remains ongoing.

## Continuity

- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress becomes stronger as the demonic qi flowing from the rift grows denser.
- The masked man is the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's carefully trained hunting dog; he regenerates from grievous injuries and appears unable to feel pain.
- Jin Taekyung and the wounded guardian spirit are fighting the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and the masked man while trying to stop the Inner Palace's humans and beasts from completing their mutation.
- Jin has been driven into a direct fight with the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and suffers a fresh thigh wound at the chapter's end.
- Jin infers that the Lord of Heaven's interest in him is why the Southern Heaven Demon Empress watched him in Hubei Province instead of killing or abducting him.

## Translation Decisions

- Use defying heaven for 역천 as the masked man's supernatural regenerative power.
- Use Regeneration for 재생 and mutation for 변이.
- Use masked man for 복면인 and preserve his identity and relationship to the Great Snow Fiend as unresolved.
- Continue using demonic qi for 마기 and magical power for 마력.

# Chapter 708

## Plot

Baeksang emerges from the ruins and stabs the Southern Heaven Demon Empress from behind, disrupting her attempt to pour all her innate qi into a final attack. She survives the resulting explosion, while Yayul Cheok and the White Tiger shield Jin Taekyung and are left gravely wounded.

The Southern Heaven Demon Empress uses her remaining life force to attack Wang Ho and more than a hundred surviving Baekcheon warriors. The guardian spirit urges Jin to flee with the diminished sacred stone, but Jin instead runs toward the Southern Heaven Demon Empress.

## Continuity

- Baeksang has been flung away with catastrophic injuries after stabbing the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, and his fate remains unresolved.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress survived the explosion, remains severely injured, and is burning through her remaining life while retaining some innate qi.
- Yayul Cheok shielded Jin Taekyung from the explosion and is unconscious with sword fragments embedded throughout his body.
- The White Tiger shielded Jin Taekyung and is gravely wounded; the sacred stone has shrunk to roughly the size of a child's fist and emits only a faint light.
- Wang Ho and more than a hundred surviving Baekcheon warriors are confronting the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and are being overwhelmed.
- The Nanman Beast Palace suffered catastrophic shaking and damage but did not completely collapse, allowing the evacuated survivors to witness its survival.
- Jin Taekyung survived the explosion and chose to attack the Southern Heaven Demon Empress rather than flee.

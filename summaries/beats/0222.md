# Chapter 222

## Plot

Jin Taekyung pierces Carus's eye with Spear Energy, survives the Named Monster's Heal, Dark Binding, and poison magic through Unaffected by a Hundred Poisons, and spends 100 Bonus Points to raise Strength and Stamina by 50 each. He breaks free, counters Carus's remaining spells and attacks, and continues the fight while confronting the anger, guilt, and grief tied to the deaths three years earlier.

Taekyung defeats and kills the Level 115 Carus after Carus accepts his death. The System grants the Named Monster Defeated Achievement, records a perfect clear of the A-Rank Gate The Black Wyvern's Nest, and awards enormous EXP and Fame with two displayed level-ups. Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Song Song then run to him.

## Continuity

- Taekyung pierced Carus's eye with Spear Energy, then defeated the Level 115 Named Monster after resisting its Heal, Dark Binding, Magic Arrow, Slow, and Binding Magic.
- Carus's dragonkin trait greatly strengthened his magic, but Taekyung's Unaffected by a Hundred Poisons neutralized the Paralysis Poison and Nerve Poison from Carus's binding spell.
- Taekyung spent 100 Bonus Points, assigning 50 to Strength and 50 to Stamina, and used the resulting power to break Carus's thorny vines.
- Taekyung killed Carus after the wounded Wyvern resignedly asked him to do so; the revenge connected to the Sangdong Station Mutated Gate incident is complete from Taekyung's immediate perspective.
- The System granted the Named Monster Defeated Achievement, awarded an unspecified corresponding Reward, and recorded a perfect clear of The Black Wyvern's Nest.
- The clear produced an enormous amount of EXP and Fame and displayed two Level Up notifications; the resulting final Level and complete Reward details remain undisclosed.
- Team Leader Choi, Butler Kim, Im Kkeokjeong, and Song Song reached Taekyung immediately after Carus's death; Taekyung regards them as his guildmates and his people.

# Chapter 261

## Plot

At the collapsed Face-Wall Cave, Han Su, revealed as the Yin Ghost, kills the Four Great Vajras and advances toward Unnamed to seize the Green Jade Buddha Staff under the Lord of Heaven's command. Unnamed refuses to surrender it and prepares to fight.

Elsewhere, Flame Tiger overwhelms Hongcheon while masked attackers use the Exploding Blood Demonic Art to destroy part of the Hundred and Eight Arhats Formation and slaughter Shaolin monks. Hongcheon is gravely wounded and loses an arm but rises again to defend Shaolin, only for Flame Tiger to prepare the killing blow before two arriving voices interrupt him; Flame Tiger recognizes the older speaker as the Fire King.

## Continuity

- Han Su is known as the Yin Ghost and has killed Shaolin's Four Great Vajras at the entrance to the Face-Wall Cave.
- Han Su's mission is to obtain the Green Jade Buddha Staff, acting under the Lord of Heaven's command.
- Unnamed is inside the Face-Wall Cave, refuses Han Su's demand, and is preparing to fight him.
- Flame Tiger has severely wounded Hongcheon in their Supreme Peak battle; Hongcheon has lost his arm below the shoulder but remains determined to defend Shaolin.
- The masked attackers destroyed part of the Hundred and Eight Arhats Formation with the Exploding Blood Demonic Art, killing more than twenty Arhat monks and leaving the formation broken.
- Flame Tiger's attempted execution of Hongcheon is interrupted by two arriving voices, and he identifies the older speaker as the Fire King.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Yin Ghost for 음귀 as Han Su's epithet.
- Use Four Great Vajras for 사대금강, Hundred and Eight Arhats for 백팔나한, and Hundred and Eight Arhats Formation for 백팔나한진.
- Use Exploding Blood Demonic Art for 폭혈마공 and Unshakable Mind for 부동심.
- Use Benefactor for the Buddhist address 시주.

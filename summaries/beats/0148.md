# Chapter 148

## Plot

Jin Wikyung and Hong Jin immediately develop a joking rapport, addressing each other as Comrade Hong and Lesser Family Head Jin while Hong Jin humorously escalates his praise of Jin Taekyung from the greatest under heaven to the greatest of all time.

Cheongpung reveals that Sword Saint Mae Jonghak is his grandfather and martial instructor, that he secretly left Huashan, and that he intends to defeat all the Ten Dragons and Phoenixes before returning. He chose Jin Mukyung as his first opponent.

Cheongpung activates the Zaha Divine Technique, causing violet light-flames and Extreme Yang qi to surge around him. Jin Mukyung accepts the challenge, draws his sword, and the duel begins when their Sword Energy and light-flames collide.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung is the grandson and disciple of Sword Saint Mae Jonghak; he secretly left Huashan without Mae Jonghak's knowledge and did not know his grandfather's identity until descending from the mountain.
- Cheongpung has begun a dueling tour intended to defeat all Ten Dragons and Phoenixes, with Jin Mukyung as his first target.
- Jin Mukyung accepted Cheongpung's challenge despite the injuries that prevent him from using all his abilities; the duel's outcome is not established in this chapter.
- Jin Wikyung and Hong Jin form an immediate, playful rapport and use the addresses Comrade Hong and Lesser Family Head Jin with each other.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 비무행 as “dueling tour.”
- Render 청강검 as “blue-steel sword.”
- Render 광염 as “light-flames.”
- Use “Comrade Hong” for 홍 동지 and “Lesser Family Head Jin” for 진 소가주님 in the Wikyung–Hong Jin exchange.

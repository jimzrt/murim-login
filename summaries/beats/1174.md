# Chapter 1174

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens after three days and learns from Choi Minwoo that events have worsened while he was unconscious. The Dragon Heart has opened, magical power and rift progress are surging, and the Main Quest “Rift and Collapse” has failed; the System has created “The Foreordained Collapse,” a choice-sensitive quest warning of irreversible consequences. Jin visits the still-unconscious Cheon Taemin in a secret facility beneath the Pentagon, confirms his belief that Taemin is the Martial God and a former Player, then gathers his allies and reveals that he intends to leave to confront the invader from beyond their world.

## Continuity

- Three days passed while Jin Taekyung was unconscious; Choi Minwoo and the others survived.
- The Dragon Heart opened, causing magical power and rift progress to surge. The Main Quest “Rift and Collapse” failed and was replaced by “The Foreordained Collapse.”
- Cheon Taemin remains unconscious in a secret facility deep beneath the Pentagon; Jin knows Taemin is the Martial God and a former Player.
- Jin intends to leave to confront the invader from beyond this world; he knows where the invader is.

# Chapter 272

## Plot

Jin Taekyung accepts Park Jihoon's invitation to drink and learns that Jihoon transferred into their middle school in his second year, attended Hankuk University's Business Administration department, awakened before graduating, and comes from a family that has operated a Hunter-related business for more than thirty years. After an enjoyable reunion, Jihoon takes a separate call and reveals that his nominal Myeongdong Guild Team 1 Leader answers to him; Jihoon orders the earliest possible A-Rank raid and says the higher-ups find Taekyung irritating, implying that the raid is intended to remove him under cover of a Gate accident.

## Continuity

- Taekyung has moved out of the Jin Family home and into a Bucheon officetel three days before this chapter; Jinho hyung is the only person he expects to find there.
- Park Jihoon and Taekyung reconnect after eleven years without regular contact, following only a brief conversation a few months earlier.
- Park Jihoon owns a luxury officetel in Cheongdam-dong and is wealthy enough to maintain it as a private retreat.
- Park Jihoon is nominally a Hunter on Myeongdong Guild Team 1, but the nominal Team 1 Leader recognizes Jihoon's superior authority and obeys his directions.
- Jihoon has been informed that higher-ups find Jin Taekyung irritating and has begun arranging an A-Rank raid at the earliest available date in connection with the Taekyung matter.

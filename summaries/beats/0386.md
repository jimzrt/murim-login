# Chapter 386

## Plot

Lee Jungryong arrives at the underground bunker and is greeted by the assembled S-rank Hunters. He recognizes that Choi Minwoo has become much stronger and that Jin Taekyung has crossed the wall, which leaves him anxious and impatient. A long strategy meeting follows: Senior General Liao proposes launching dozens of nuclear weapons across Sichuan, but the proposal is rejected because of the danger to survivors, the land, and possible teleportation of the warheads. The six S-rank Hunters are ultimately assigned to six fronts, each supported by three Army and Air Force divisions and Hunters from the Public Security Armed Forces. As the meeting ends, an unidentified person uses Sound Transmission to summon Taekyung.

## Continuity

- Lee Jungryong is the practical leader of Ares Guild and one of the world's top three S-rank Hunters; he now recognizes Choi Minwoo's substantial growth and Jin Taekyung's crossing of the wall.
- Senior General Liao is a member of the Princelings faction and advocates using nuclear weapons to resolve the Sichuan monster wave; Wei Penghu rejects the proposal.
- The six S-rank Hunters present at the bunker are assigned to six fronts, with three Army and Air Force divisions and Public Security Armed Forces Hunters attached to each front.
- An unidentified person contacts Jin Taekyung through Sound Transmission immediately after the meeting.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 랴오 상장 as Senior General Liao, 태자당 as the Princelings, and 상하이방 as the Shanghai clique.
- Retain the source's ox-related wordplay in Liao's maxim as an English footnote.
- Render 전음 as Sound Transmission and preserve the established conversational, self-mocking first-person voice.

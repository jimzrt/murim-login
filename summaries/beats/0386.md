# Chapter 386

## Plot

Lee Jungryong arrives at the underground headquarters and immediately recognizes that Jin Taekyung has crossed the wall into true mastery while Choi Minwoo has also grown dramatically stronger. Jin's calm defiance unsettles Lee, who feels anxiety and impatience for the first time since taking control of the Ares Guild.

During the prolonged war council, General Liao proposes using dozens of nuclear weapons across Sichuan, but Wei Fenghu rejects the plan because of the civilian and territorial devastation. Magic Johnson warns that the Arch Lich could redirect the warheads with spatial-transference magic, after which Wei organizes the six S-rank Hunters and Chinese forces into six directions. As everyone prepares to move out, an unknown person uses Sound Transmission to summon Jin.

## Continuity

- Lee Jungryong has confirmed that Jin Taekyung crossed the wall into true mastery, while Choi Minwoo's qi has become much greater and more refined than before.
- The agreed response divides six S-rank Hunters among six directions, with three Army and Air Force divisions and Public Security Armed Forces Department Hunters assigned to each direction.
- General Liao belongs to the Crown Prince Party, which rivals the Shanghai Gang faction associated with Chairman Shao Yang and Wei Fenghu.
- The Monster Wave has caused at least hundreds of thousands of casualties, and the possibility that the Arch Lich has raised the dead as undead makes the coming operation exceptionally dangerous.
- An unidentified person has contacted Jin Taekyung through Sound Transmission immediately after the war council.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 랴오 상장 as General Liao, 태자당 as Crown Prince Party, 상하이방 as Shanghai Gang, 오군 as five armies, and 공안 무력부 as Public Security Armed Forces Department.
- Retain Sound Transmission for 전음 and Archmage for 대마법사; preserve the source's overtly vulgar humor and Magic Johnson's threatening gay-joke exchange.

# Chapter 435

## Plot

Mungyeong and Jeok Cheongang speak privately aboard the ship. Mungyeong's Slaughter Saint persona briefly replaces his cheerful medical-apprentice act, but he admits only that he is traveling with Jin's group and cannot explain why he continues to accompany them. Jeok praises Jin's progress and Cheongpung's greater raw talent, then thanks Mungyeong for saving Jin.

Mungyeong discovers that Jin has already opened his Middle Dantian while examining him in his sleep. Jin wakes, mistakes the examination for an assault, and calls for Jeok, whose intervention destroys the cabin and burns one of Mu Song's special fast ships. Jeok and Jin trade blame afterward while Mungyeong returns to treating the people who fell into the water.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong's Slaughter Saint identity remains concealed beneath his medical-apprentice persona, though Jeok Cheongang recognizes and addresses the older identity.
- Mungyeong continues traveling with Jin Taekyung's group for a reason he cannot explain, describing the relationship only as traveling together.
- Jin Taekyung has opened his Middle Dantian, a development Mungyeong discovers unexpectedly while examining him.
- Jeok Cheongang believes Jin is extraordinarily capable but considers Cheongpung superior in pure martial talent.
- Jeok Cheongang's intervention destroys one of Mu Song's special fast ships after Jin calls for help.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 노야 as “Old Master” when Jin Taekyung calls to Jeok Cheongang.
- Preserve the contrast between Mungyeong's cheerful medical-apprentice persona and the dry, dangerous voice of the Slaughter Saint.
- Use “Middle Dantian,” “Heavenly Martial Physique,” “Turtle Breath Technique,” “Mingmen acupoint,” and “Flame Divine Palm” consistently with the established glossary.
- Preserve Jin Taekyung's startled profanity and teasing exchange with Jeok Cheongang.

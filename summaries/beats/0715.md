# Chapter 715

## Plot

Jin Taekyung awakens after seven days and nights of unconsciousness, physically recovered but haunted by memories of the Inner Palace massacre and guilt over surviving when others died. Jeok Cheongang counsels him to be grateful for surviving and to mourn the dead, then masks his concern with their usual teasing and threats before leaving.

The Fire Dragon Pavilion members reunite around Jin, with Taishan destroying the door and crashing into the group in his enthusiasm. Hyuk Mujin and Taishan joke darkly about Jin's death while Namho and Sama Pyo react with exasperation, until Ju Hwaran destroys the remaining table and demands that everyone behave in front of a patient.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has awakened after seven days and nights and has regained his physical clarity and strength.
- Jin remembers the deaths at the Inner Palace and is struggling with survivor's guilt over failing to save everyone.
- Jeok Cheongang recognizes Jin's guilt, believes overcoming it will make him stronger, and quietly cares for him despite masking that concern with blunt mockery.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion members are reunited around Jin in Nanman.
- Taishan's enthusiastic arrival destroyed the door and damaged Jin's quarters.
- Ju Hwaran is active and present with the Fire Dragon Pavilion, enforcing order after the reunion's destructive joking.

# Chapter 772

## Plot

Michael Silbert's speech successfully turns worldwide fear into hope, and the public celebrates him as the hero who restored the World Hunter Federation. In private, he admits that his tears were sincere, though he cannot fully identify the emotions behind them.

Michael sensed the intense qi or heat released during the disturbance at Jin Taekyung's hotel and orders Huginn to increase security and surveillance until the inaugural ceremony, especially around the monster. He recognizes that Jin has come to regard the monster as a friend and intends to exploit that emotional weakness.

Michael secretly enters a conversation with The Prophet through a magic mirror. Meanwhile, public enthusiasm spreads across the world, the inaugural ceremony approaches tomorrow, and prominent Hunters and Guild Masters—including Jin—travel toward Korea.

## Continuity

- Michael Silbert has successfully redirected worldwide panic into public support for the reestablished World Hunter Federation.
- The disturbance at Jin Taekyung's hotel originated on the top floor after the monster left, then abruptly became quiet; magic could not reveal the interior or transmit its sounds.
- Michael sensed Jin's powerful qi outburst from several kilometers away and considers Jin capable of taking a dangerously reckless course.
- Michael has ordered heightened security and continuous surveillance of the monster until the inaugural ceremony.
- Michael believes Jin regards the monster as a friend and identifies that attachment as Jin's fatal weakness.
- Michael is secretly communicating with The Prophet through a magic mirror.
- Jin, Team Leader Choi, and Magic Johnson are still pursuing a possible fourth path that could defeat Michael's plan without sacrificing Jin's monster friend or the world.
- The decisive contents of Magic Johnson's documents and the viability of the fourth path remain unresolved.
- The World Hunter Federation's inaugural ceremony is tomorrow, and Jin is traveling to Korea to attend it.

# Chapter 713

## Plot

The guardian spirit's sacrifice seals the rift, disperses the demonic qi, and allows the sacred stone to purify the corrupted energy. After the guardian spirit mutates, Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang honor its final request and kill it.

Baeksang survives the collapse and admits that he knowingly followed Dark Heaven's plan because the Southern Heaven Demon Empress promised to preserve his supposedly dead child, Baekhwi. He had sacrificed Nanman's warriors and beasts while creating the Baekcheon Unit in the hope that someone would stop the catastrophe. After the Beast Miao King learns the truth, Baeksang asks his sworn elder brother to kill him.

## Continuity

- The rift has closed, the demonic qi has disappeared, and the sacred stone is purifying the corrupted energy.
- The guardian spirit became a mutated divine-beast-like tiger after sacrificing itself to seal the rift, then was killed by Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang at its own request.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is dead.
- Baeksang knowingly served Dark Heaven's plan and mobilized nearly ten thousand Nanman warriors and beasts as living sacrifices.
- Baeksang accepted the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's bargain because Dark Heaven had kept Baekhwi alive in a deep sleep and promised to restore him after the grand plan.
- Baeksang created the Baekcheon Unit in the hope that someone other than himself would stop the catastrophe.
- The Beast Miao King now knows that Baekhwi survived and that Baeksang concealed the truth to protect him.
- Baeksang has asked the Beast Miao King to kill him.

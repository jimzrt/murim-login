# Chapter 140

## Plot

Jin Taekyung needles Gong Ilhyuk of the Three Hands of Zhongnan by pretending not to recognize the Zhongnan Sect's Sect Leader, Gong Iljung, then exposes that Iljung is only Ilhyuk's father's cousin. Taekyung continues mocking Ilhyuk's pride in his connection while avoiding an outright public confrontation.

Hong Jin identifies himself as Shanxi Province's Deputy Military Commissioner and Li Feng as the Assistant Military Commissioner responsible for training the Provincial Office's soldiers. When Gong Ilhyuk confronts Cheongpung over his vague origins and attempts to attack him, Taekyung intervenes, but Cheongpung effortlessly counters and destroys Ilhyuk's arm with the Taeeul Miri Palm. Cheongpung reveals that his grandfather, Mae Jonghak, taught him the technique, causing Li Feng to recognize the Sword Saint.

## Continuity

- The Jin Family group is attending the City Lord's luncheon at the Shanxi Provincial Office, where His Highness is expected to arrive soon.
- Hong Jin is the Level 22 Deputy Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province and Li Feng's direct superior.
- Li Feng is the Assistant Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province, a former Huashan lay disciple who oversees the training of the Provincial Office's soldiers.
- Gong Ilhyuk and two unnamed martial artists are the Three Hands of Zhongnan; Ilhyuk is nearly forty and above Level 70, while the other two are at least advanced First Rate.
- Gong Iljung is the current Sect Leader of the Zhongnan Sect and bears the title Wind-and-Cloud Sword Lord; he is Gong Ilhyuk's father's cousin.
- Gong Ilhyuk relies on his relationship to Gong Iljung for status and benefits within the Zhongnan Sect.
- Cheongpung currently lives in Shanxi, previously stayed in Henan, and came there after being in Shaanxi.
- Cheongpung's grandfather is Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint, who taught him the Taeeul Miri Palm.
- Cheongpung can render a Level 70-plus martial artist completely helpless with a single counter, but the smell of blood makes him nauseated and causes him to vomit.
- Taekyung intervened to prevent a public fight involving Cheongpung, then withdrew when the Zhongnan Sect's other two Three Hands blocked Li Feng.
- Taekyung's unfinished reference beginning with 군림 remains unclear.

# Chapter 1008

## Plot

After three volleys of arrows, the Gansu leaders admit the fifty-odd riders, largely at Jeok Cheongang’s insistence. Jin Taekyung recognizes their leader as Ma Junggeol through his system window and, with Jeok, establishes that the group does not appear to be concealing exceptional martial strength. The newcomers identify themselves as Baekma Bang, a group founded by reformed Ningxia mounted-bandit leaders under an unknown master’s guidance more than ten years ago. Its chief, Ma Junggeol, says they came to offer help and have important information about Dark Heaven.

## Continuity

- Ma Junggeol leads Baekma Bang and arrived from Ningxia with six associates known collectively as the Seven Masters of Baekma Bang.
- Baekma Bang was founded by former Ningxia mounted-bandit leaders who changed their ways under the guidance of an unknown master more than ten years ago.
- Baekma Bang has fought steppe nomads and helped newly settled commoners; Ma Junggeol offers the Murim Alliance important information about Dark Heaven.

# Chapter 72

## Plot

Jin Mukyung tells Taekyung that he died once during their training and condemns him as a half-finished fighter who has mistaken luck for skill. Taekyung accepts the criticism, asks Mukyung for help as his older brother, and receives a System time limit of 9 days 20 hours 23 minutes.

Mukyung recognizes Taekyung's genuine talent and begins training him more intensely, requiring constant spear practice and sparring before returning to Heaven's Gate Temple within fifteen days. Meanwhile, Jin Wikyung recruits the servant Childeuk to deliver meals to his brothers, but dismisses him after misunderstanding Childeuk's eager responses as romantic advances.

After nine days of brutal training, Taekyung gains insight into his martial arts and achieves mastery of the Jin Family's Spear Technique and Manoeuvre Technique. The System awards the achievement Master a First Rate Martial Art, generates the Martial Arts Manual Creation Skill, greatly raises all Stats, and grants two Level Ups.

## Continuity

- Taekyung accepts that he is a half-finished martial artist and asks Jin Mukyung to help complete his training.
- The System displays a time limit of 9 days 20 hours 23 minutes after Taekyung asks Mukyung for help.
- Mukyung recognizes Taekyung's genuine martial talent and intensifies his training with continuous spear practice and sparring; Mukyung must return to Heaven's Gate Temple within fifteen days.
- Taekyung achieves mastery of the Jin Family's Spear Technique and Manoeuvre Technique, earns the Master a First Rate Martial Art achievement, gains the Martial Arts Manual Creation Skill, receives a significant increase to all Stats, and gains two Levels.
- Jin Wikyung has begun to be called the Junzi Sword for his swift recovery efforts and fair handling after the Jin Family's victory in the war.
- Childeuk is an illiterate but physically strong and diligent Jin Family servant whom Wikyung selected to deliver every meal to Mukyung and Taekyung; Wikyung removes him from that post after a misunderstanding.

# Chapter 218

## Plot

Team Leader Choi examines Yeti's Necklace and complains that such a beautiful, sophisticated item is in limited supply. Won Myunghoon refuses to take it back, explaining that he values human bonds more than possessions, then reveals that the necklace is a gift for Taekyung and tells him not to give it to anyone else. After Taekyung equips it, the Item Window reveals that it marks its possessor as a target of dragonkin monsters regardless of species.

After half a day of travel, the group leaves Manticore's Jungle and enters a wasteland. The Star Guild members fight Trolls, exploiting their vulnerability to attacks on the head and heart and to fire or ice before regeneration can occur. Taekyung notices the contrast between Won's polished warmth and his private irritation at the smell of burning Troll flesh, then teases him about reports that he visits a department store five times a week as a VVIP. Won denies the report and becomes visibly uncomfortable.

Team Leader 1 returns from scouting for the missing Wyvern, deathly pale and covered in blood. Seven people had gone out, but only he returned: a single Wyvern killed the other six despite their use of concealment magic. He identifies it as a Named Monster and reveals that the group is inside an A-Rank Mutated Gate, leaving everyone in suffocating silence.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon gave Taekyung Yeti's Necklace as a personal gift and told him not to give it to anyone else.
- Yeti's Necklace activates the Wind of the Snowfield effect and causes its possessor to become a target of dragonkin monsters.
- The raid party has left Manticore's Jungle and entered a wasteland after half a day of travel.
- The Star Guild defeated several Trolls by preventing their regeneration with fire and ice after attacking their heads and hearts.
- A Wyvern identified as a Named Monster killed six of the seven scouts, confirming that the raid is taking place inside an A-Rank Mutated Gate.
- Taekyung increasingly suspects that Won's polished, friendly public manner conceals a disturbing or unfamiliar side.

# Chapter 457

## Plot

An unavoidable Peak-grade Quest, The Tragedy of Dongting Lake, sends Taekyung and his companions to a burning lake filled with wrecked vessels and corpses. Taekyung orders Gung Gibang and Hyuk Mujin to destroy two ferries so their fragments can be scattered across the water, then uses his Inventory to create a path and crosses the lake with Cheongpung and Gung Gibang while Hyuk Mujin gathers boats and people at the shore. They find no survivors among the nearby wrecks, but Taekyung realizes the Quest is still active and splits up with the others to search farther across the lake; with only 1 minute 12 seconds remaining, he hears a faint plea for help.

## Continuity

- The unavoidable Peak-grade Quest The Tragedy of Dongting Lake requires Taekyung to rescue people before the time limit expires; at chapter's end, 1 minute 12 seconds remain.
- At Dongting Lake, all victims visible near the initial wrecks are dead, Qi Sense detects no survivor within range, and Taekyung, Cheongpung, and Gung Gibang are searching farther across the lake while Hyuk Mujin gathers boats and people at the shore; a distant survivor has just called for help.

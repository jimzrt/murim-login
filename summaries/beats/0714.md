# Chapter 714

## Plot

Baeksang asks the Beast Miao King to kill him, recalls their childhood as sworn brothers, and explains that his execution is the only way to honor their vow and atone for betraying Nanman. After publicly proclaiming Dark Heaven's future threat and declaring his loyalty to the Lord of Heaven, Baeksang is killed by the Beast Miao King's single punch at his own request.

Jin Taekyung covers Baeksang's face with the old silk called Baekcheon, closes his eyes, hears his allies arriving, and collapses. He dreams of fighting increasingly absurd Dark Heaven enemies and the people he failed to save before waking after seven days to find Jeok Cheongang violently interrogating a physician and demanding that the Beast Miao King be summoned.

## Continuity

- Baeksang is dead, having been executed by the Beast Miao King at his own request after confessing to serving Dark Heaven's plan.
- Jin Taekyung was unconscious for seven days after witnessing Baeksang's death and has now awakened under medical care.
- The Beast Miao King killed Baeksang while mourning his sworn younger brother and fulfilling their childhood vow.
- Baeksang's final public warning claimed that Dark Heaven will eventually devour Nanman.
- The rift has closed, demonic qi has disappeared, and the sacred stone is purifying the corrupted energy.
- The mutated guardian spirit was killed by Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang after sacrificing itself and requesting death.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is dead.
- Dark Heaven has kept Baekhwi alive in a deep sleep, and Baeksang accepted the Southern Heaven Demon Empress's bargain to recover him.
- Baeksang created the Baekcheon Unit as a contingency to stop the catastrophe.

## Translation Decisions

- Continue rendering the guardian spirit's 의념 as Will.
- Render the sacred stone's cleansing effect as purification.
- Retain established renderings: Old Master, Blazing Flame Divine Palm, and Sacrifice and Rest.
- Render 백천 as Baekcheon and retain the directional dream titles as Eastern Heaven Demon Lord and East-West Heaven Demon Empress.

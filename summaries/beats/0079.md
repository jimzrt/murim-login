# Chapter 79

## Plot

The Peace Guild members recover from their champagne-party hangover over breakfast, then Team Leader Choi announces that they must begin working: this will be the Guild's first raid.

Choi shows Taekyung and Im Kkeokjeong footage of the Bucheon Terminal Guild's raid against seven B-rank Minotaurs in the Minotaur's Labyrinth. The Minotaurs were defeated, but two C-rank Hunters died. Taekyung uses Qi Sense to view the Guild members' Levels—Choi 75, Butler Kim 80, Song Song 64, and Im Kkeokjeong 24—and concludes that their upcoming raid is dangerous.

## Continuity

- The Peace Guild is preparing to undertake its first raid.
- The Bucheon Terminal Guild's raid against the B-rank Minotaur's Labyrinth ended with all Minotaurs defeated but two C-rank Hunters dead.
- The Peace Guild members' displayed Levels are Choi Minwoo 75, Kim Hwajong 80, Song Song 64, and Im Hyeokjun 24.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Minotaur for 미노타우로스, Bucheon Terminal Guild for 부천터미널 길드, and The Minotaur's Labyrinth for 미노타우로스의 미로.
- Use haejangguk for 해장국 with a footnote explaining its hangover-soup meaning, and retain makgeolli with a cultural footnote.

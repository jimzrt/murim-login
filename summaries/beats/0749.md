# Chapter 749

## Plot

Jin's One Annihilation shatters Leviathan's teeth and tears away half its maw and one eye, but the technique's recoil leaves Jin with severe injuries and the permanent-risk Broken Body debuff.

A Top-Grade Potion removes Jin's ordinary status abnormalities and restores his internal energy, but Broken Body rejects the potion's effect and continues reducing his combat-related attributes.

Using the Water Rescue Worker Title, Jin and the Skeleton King pursue Leviathan underwater. Leviathan swallows the Magic Gem it sought along with wreckage and flees toward the open sea, while Jin cuts through its magical water wave and launches another spear attack; the result is unknown.

## Continuity

- Jin's One Annihilation is powerful enough to shatter Leviathan's magical teeth but severely damages his body on use.
- Broken Body cannot be removed by artificial means, keeps Jin's combat-related attributes reduced by 50, and may cause permanent attribute loss depending on his recovery.
- Leviathan has been grievously wounded but remains alive, has swallowed the Magic Gem it sought, and is fleeing underwater while Jin pursues it.
- The Water Rescue Worker Title allows Jin to move rapidly underwater for twenty-four hours, but its effectiveness is reduced by twenty percent because of its incompatibility with Scorching Yang Qi.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 공력 소진 as Internal Energy Depletion, 빈혈 as Anemia, 근육 파열 as Muscle Tear, and 망가진 신체 as Broken Body.
- Render 수상 구조대원 as Water Rescue Worker and 최상급 포션 as Top-Grade Potion.
- Retain One Annihilation, White Flame, Magic Gem, magical power, and Scorching Yang Qi as established renderings.

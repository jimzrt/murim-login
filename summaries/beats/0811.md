# Chapter 811

## Plot

The Skeleton King realizes Jin lied about the monsters fleeing east, but trusts Jin and repeats the lie to Magic Johnson. Jin divides the forces, sending the main pursuit east while taking the Skeleton King, Magic Johnson, and Yamamoto west toward the oil fields indicated by The Prophet’s message about buried black jewels.

Jin orders Team Leader Choi to avoid clashes while pursuing the remaining monsters, then leads the four-person group to a canyon. He spots an eagle overhead and blocks the canyon entrance.

## Continuity

- The Skeleton King knows Jin lied about the monsters’ direction and chooses to trust Jin, telling Magic Johnson that most of the monsters fled east.
- Jin sends the main force east to pursue the monsters and leads the Skeleton King, Magic Johnson, and Yamamoto west toward the oil fields named in The Prophet’s message.
- Jin privately tells the Skeleton King to listen without responding or reacting; the Skeleton King gives no spoken reply.
- Jin spots an eagle near the canyon and blocks its entrance.

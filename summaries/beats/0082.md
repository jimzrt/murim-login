# Chapter 82

## Plot

The fifteen-person raid team enters The Minotaur’s Labyrinth. Im Kkeokjeong takes the front as tank after equipping Peak-grade Matador’s Full-Body Armor and Matador’s Shield, whose bonuses are especially effective against bovine-type monsters. Five holes in the cavern produce eight Minotaur Warriors, overwhelming the four B-rank Sangdong Guild Hunters who initially advance. Team Leader Choi notices that Taekyung takes the threat seriously despite posing as a C-rank Hunter. Im Changsoo mocks the B-rank Hunters and deliberately calls Taekyung Jang Taekyung; Taekyung corrects him and retaliates by calling him Shit Changsoo, making Changsoo’s smile disappear.

## Continuity

- The fifteen-person raid team enters The Minotaur’s Labyrinth with Im Kkeokjeong serving as the front-line tank.
- Choi supplies Im Kkeokjeong with Peak-grade Matador’s Full-Body Armor and Matador’s Shield, which grant bonuses against bovine-type monsters and can activate Taunt and Hallucination.
- A Level 58 Minotaur Warrior appears first; further monsters emerge from the cavern’s five holes until eight Minotaurs are present.
- The four B-rank Sangdong Guild Hunters who initially advance are insufficient to handle eight Minotaurs.
- Team Leader Choi recognizes that Taekyung’s serious assessment of the Minotaurs is unusual for a purported C-rank Hunter.
- Im Changsoo mocks Taekyung and misnames him Jang Taekyung; Taekyung corrects him and answers with the insult Shit Changsoo, ending the chapter with Changsoo visibly angered.

# Chapter 957

## Plot

At Eight Spring Gorge, the steppe army suffers heavy losses trying to climb the cliffs, but the Chinggen impostor breaks through the archers’ defenses and confronts the Dongting Fisherman. The Fisherman, recovered after the Jin Family of Taiyuan treated his injuries, fights to repay his debts to them and take revenge on Dark Heaven; the chapter ends as he and the impostor clash.

Jin Wikyung assigned the Dongting Fisherman to defend the cliffs, anticipating that the enemy would send its elite troops there. The Fisherman accepts the assignment out of gratitude to the Jin Family, which saved his life twice.

## Continuity

- The Chinggen impostor has joined the assault on Eight Spring Gorge and reached the cliffs, where he is now facing the Dongting Fisherman.
- The Dongting Fisherman has recovered from his injuries and is fighting for the Jin Family of Taiyuan, which restored his mind and treated him; he also seeks revenge on Dark Heaven. Their fight is unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the Dongting Fisherman’s measured, old-fashioned speech distinct, including his fishing metaphors and self-reference as 노부 (“this old man”).

# Chapter 1059

## Plot

Hyeoncheon draws his sword and shallowly cuts Sama Pyo, but stops short of killing him. Taekyung prevents Taishan from charging the Kongtong Disciples, then Hyeoncheon renounces collective vengeance and tells his surviving Disciples to grieve.

Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint return with a foul-smelling, weeping man whom Jeok calls a madman; some mounted bandits call the man Great Sir.

## Continuity

- Hyeoncheon spared Sama Pyo after wounding him shallowly; he declares that the Kongtong Sect will pursue revenge without punishing relatives for their kinship.
- Taishan charged toward Sama Pyo, and Taekyung stopped him by knocking him unconscious.
- Hyeoncheon tells the surviving Kongtong Disciples to grieve for their dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대인 as “Great Sir” when used by the mounted bandits to address the unidentified man.

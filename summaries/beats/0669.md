# Chapter 669

## Plot

Jin, Taishan, Yayul Mok, and the Seven Miao Tigers overcome the Bai warriors in the underground prison; Taishan's hunger-driven rampage forces most of them to surrender, and Yayul Mok spares them by having them incapacitated and confined.

Wonhu opens the prison and Yayul Mok reveals that Namho and the unconscious Sama Pyo have been rescued. Jin completes the Escape from Namshank Quest, receives a Level Up and partial recovery, gives the antidote pills to Taishan and Sama Pyo, and accepts Muyaho from Yayul Mok before heading toward the reconnaissance squad. Yayul Mok and the Seven Miao Tigers remain behind to delay pursuit, and Wonhu entrusts the collapsed Young Palace Lord to Jin.

## Continuity

- Jin has escaped the underground prison and completed the Escape from Namshank Quest before the scheduled execution.
- The Quest Reward grants Jin a Level Up, removes some injuries and Status Effects, and causes the force constricting his Lower Dantian to melt away; he still has not fully understood the Middle Dantian's power.
- Taishan, Yayul Mok, and the Seven Miao Tigers enabled the escape; the surrendered Bai warriors survived and were confined in the underground prison.
- Namho and Sama Pyo were rescued from the Inner Palace; Sama Pyo received an antidote but remains unable to fight at full strength.
- Yayul Mok gave the spiritual creature Muyaho to Jin and directed him northeast from their first meeting place toward the reconnaissance squad.
- Yayul Mok and the Seven Miao Tigers are remaining behind to delay discovery, which they expect within half a shichen at most.
- Yayul Mok collapsed as Wonhu caught him and asked Jin to look after the Young Palace Lord.
- The public execution arranged by Baeksang and the Tribal Grand Council remains the unresolved political consequence of Jin's escape.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 해약 as antidote, 하단전 as Lower Dantian, and 반 시진 as half a shichen.
- Retain the established renderings of the food names in Taishan's hunger chant and preserve the informal comic references to the Hulk, Saiyans, and Kamehameha.
- Use hyung for 형 when referring to the Miao speaker's older brother.

# Chapter 1003

## Plot

The group enters Gansu and reaches Tianshui, where Sama Pyo reflects on returning home after a little over a year away. A Lower District Sect member finishes his escort and reporting mission, delivers a sealed bamboo tube from Wolhwa to Taekyung, and reports that Ningxia’s former mounted-bandit gangs disbanded long ago; many survivors became horse caravans, merchants, or escort groups. The unidentified Supreme Peak master who pacified them remains in Ningxia, and even the Lower District Sect’s informants do not know his identity. The group then encounters someone unexpected in Tianshui.

## Continuity

- The group has reached Tianshui in Gansu; an unexpected person is there to meet them.
- Dark Heaven’s forces are approaching from beyond the desert and may rival or exceed the Hundred Thousand Demonic Disciples; their destination and objective remain unknown, and multiple fronts are possible.
- The Great Nation has joined the allied forces. The Murim Alliance’s intelligence network is active and shares information with nearby sects under emergency orders.
- Wolhwa sent Taekyung a sealed bamboo tube; Ju Hwaran briefly watched it with a somber expression.
- Ningxia’s mounted-bandit gangs disbanded long ago. Their former members became horse caravans, merchant companies, or Escort Bureaus; the Lower District Sect’s review found no suspicious signs in the pacification.
- An unidentified Supreme Peak master remains in Ningxia. The Lower District Sect reports that he is not a fiend, but his identity is unknown.
- Sama Pyo and Taishan have returned to their home region after being away for a little over a year.
- The reason Sama Pyo’s father ordered him to return immediately remains unknown.
- The Demon-Sealing Formation may neutralize Moving Formations; Zhuge Feng’s clan used it to contain the rift at Dongting Lake.

# Chapter 680

## Plot

Jin Taekyung nearly kills the Black Hand Fist Demon, but a slender old man wielding twin wheels intervenes and forces him to retreat. The old man and Black Hand Fist Demon then attack Jin and Muyaho together. Jin wounds Black Hand Fist Demon with the Fire Dragon Divine Spear and exploits his rage, but the old man's Finger Qi pierces Jin's shoulder. When Jin counters with the Flame Divine Palm, the old man neutralizes it with Yin-Cold Qi and reveals himself as the Great Snow Fiend.

## Continuity

- The unidentified twin-wheel master in the Poisonblood Grounds is the Great Snow Fiend, the former ruler of Great Snow Mountain.
- The Great Snow Fiend and Black Hand Fist Demon are coordinating their attacks against Jin Taekyung and Muyaho.
- Jin is wielding White Flame and using blue-white fire techniques, including the Fire Dragon Divine Spear and Flame Divine Palm.
- Jin's attack cuts the Black Hand Fist Demon's nose and confirms that the older man's pride and anger create exploitable openings.
- The Great Snow Fiend's Finger Qi pierces Jin's shoulder, and his Yin-Cold Qi clashes directly with Jin's Flame Divine Palm.
- The battle ends this chapter with Jin and the Great Snow Fiend's palms colliding amid opposing blue-white flame and pure-white cold.
- Muyaho remains alive and fighting beside Jin, though the twin wheels have shaved away much of his fur.
- The Yangtze River Channel League's swift ship and its unidentified arrival remain at the reconnaissance squad's position.
- Namho still plans to escort the detained Han Chinese reconnaissance members to the Central Plains for reinforcements.
- Yohi and Heugung remain captive and alive while the Southern Heaven Demon Empress remains absent from the battlefield.

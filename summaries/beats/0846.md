# Chapter 846

## Plot

More than twenty riders from Seongju Prefecture arrive at the Sichuan Tang Clan seeking the Divine Physician for their ailing City Lord. Jeok Cheongang questions their Captain of the Guards and learns that the City Lord’s condition began after a summons to the Imperial Capital, where he lost his favored concubine, Aehyang, to the Son of Heaven.

## Continuity

- The City Lord’s illness began after his return from the Imperial Capital; his condition alternates between lucidity and violent episodes and is worsening despite treatment by local physicians.
- The City Lord’s Captain of the Guards seeks the Divine Physician because the City Lord’s favored concubine, Aehyang, was taken by the Son of Heaven.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 애향 as Aehyang and 천자 as “Son of Heaven.”

# Chapter 288

## Plot

Lee Jungryong restores Go Jun's strength by channeling mana into his injured arm. Go Jun reports that Jin Taekyung's strength and speed surpassed his own, that Taekyung possesses extensive combat experience, and that Taekyung has learned a mana cultivation method. Lee confirms that this explains Taekyung's extraordinary growth and orders Go Jun to intensify surveillance of the Peace Guild, assess its useful personnel, reinforce the security team, and monitor Ares executives and overseas branches.

Lee Jungryong recalls meeting Cheon Taemin at age thirty and becoming captivated by him, regarding the unrelated man as an older brother despite their lack of blood relation. Meanwhile, Team Leader Choi explains the pressure Lee placed on him and the Peace Guild, then offers to terminate the Guild's contracts to protect its members. Taekyung rejects the offer and declares that they will become stronger, promising to make them strong himself.

## Continuity

- Lee Jungryong is certain that Jin Taekyung has learned a mana cultivation method; its source remains unknown.
- Go Jun's strength and speed were inferior to Taekyung's, and Go Jun recognizes Taekyung as an experienced combatant rather than an ordinary monster-only Hunter.
- Ares Guild's security team has received instruction in mana cultivation methods and gives Lee Jungryong absolute loyalty.
- Lee Jungryong regards Cheon Taemin as an older brother despite no blood relation; he met Cheon at age thirty and has long held him in both respect and fear.
- Mentioning Cheon Taemin is an unspoken taboo for Lee Jungryong's subordinates; Go Jun apologized and knelt after doing so.
- Lee Jungryong ordered Go Jun to strengthen surveillance of the Peace Guild, assess its personnel, reinforce the security team, and monitor Ares executives and overseas branches.
- Team Leader Choi's earlier Peace Guild recruits were diverted by unusually generous offers or disappeared before signing, consistent with pressure from Ares Guild.
- Taekyung rejected Choi's offer to terminate the Peace Guild contracts and committed to making the Guild members stronger.

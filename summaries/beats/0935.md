# Chapter 935

## Plot

Baek Yeon visits the Emperor, and their teasing gives way to an earnest exchange of mutual respect. Baek reflects on the Emperor’s sacrifices and his plans to entrust Zhu Bao with his loyal retainers and power before his death. The Emperor tells Baek that Taekyung promised to save him, and admits the promise has given him hope despite his belief that it is probably impossible. After hearing Taekyung’s account, Jeok Cheongang confirms that the Emperor is poisoned with Blood Soul Gu; Taekyung acknowledges it, breaking his promise to keep the matter secret.

## Continuity

- The Emperor has prepared for his death by transferring his loyal retainers and power base to Zhu Bao, while avoiding his younger brother to spare him the grief of an impending farewell.
- Taekyung promised the Emperor he would save him; the Emperor now knows of that promise and feels hopeful despite believing his survival is probably impossible.
- Taekyung tells Jeok Cheongang that the Emperor is poisoned with Blood Soul Gu, breaking his promise to keep the matter secret.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 백연’s deferential address to the Emperor as “Your Majesty”; their private exchanges also include familiar teasing.
- Render 폐하 as “Your Majesty” and 천자 as “Son of Heaven.”

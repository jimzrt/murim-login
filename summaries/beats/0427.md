# Chapter 427

## Plot

The Arch Lich's erasure ends the thirty-four-day Winter War, causing the undead legions to collapse and celebrations to erupt worldwide. Jin Taekyung becomes an international hero, but conspiracy theories question his involvement in the deaths of Wu Heixing and Lee Jungryong. Four days later, Chairman Shao Yang confirms that Jin prevented a catastrophe across Asia, rejects the allegations, and reveals that the Crown Prince Party faces prosecution for corruption. After Shao Yang leaves, Jin finally regains consciousness and curses the suffocating hospital room.

## Continuity

- The Arch Lich has been erased, ending the thirty-four-day monster wave known as the Winter War; the remaining undead legions were annihilated and worldwide celebrations followed.
- Jin Taekyung is being celebrated internationally as the hero who prevented a catastrophe that could have engulfed China, Asia, or the world.
- Media and public speculation now questions Jin's role in the deaths of Wu Heixing and Lee Jungryong, citing Jin's prior argument with Wu and his lack of visible injuries when found.
- Jin's vital signs remained normal while he was inexplicably unconscious in the hospital for four days, and he regained consciousness at the chapter's end.
- A civilian physician known as the reincarnation of Hua Tuo and a world-class healer were unable to determine why Jin remained unconscious.
- Investigators found traces of a Gate at the Arch Lich's former base and estimated its potential scale to be catastrophic.
- Chairman Shao Yang rejects the conspiracy theories against Jin, while Wu Heixing's father, leader of the Crown Prince Party, promotes them and conducts an independent investigation.
- Shao Yang and his allies have prepared to prosecute the Crown Prince Party leadership over astronomical corruption and embezzlement revealed during the war.

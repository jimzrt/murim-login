# Chapter 359

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's full-powered Flame Divine Palm, Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, and One Annihilation fail to defeat the Western Heaven Demon Lord, whose Body-Protecting Qi and Black Dragon Armor withstand the attacks; One Annihilation wounds his shoulder, but the Demon Lord overwhelms Taekyung with superior martial arts and Sword Force. Taekyung survives more than three hundred moves while suffering severe internal injuries, a fractured left arm, and probable chest fractures, then remains standing with almost no internal energy and refuses to yield, insisting that his stubborn will to protect the Fire King and Cheongpung is itself supernatural power. The fight remains unresolved as he raises a faint Spear Energy and challenges the silent Demon Lord to attack.

## Continuity

- The Western Heaven Demon Lord possesses the Black Dragon Armor beneath his yellow robe and continues fighting with a broken sword that emits ink-black Sword Force.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord's martial arts and internal energy are far beyond Taekyung's, and he can read Taekyung's attacks and strike from thirty-six directions.
- Taekyung's One Annihilation wounds the Western Heaven Demon Lord's shoulder but cannot kill him; the Demon Lord says an earlier use might have taken his arm, though Taekyung would still have died.
- Taekyung has survived more than three hundred moves against the Western Heaven Demon Lord at full strength but now has severe internal injuries, a fractured left arm, and probable chest fractures.
- Taekyung has no Stat Points remaining and almost no internal energy, yet remains standing and continues to resist the Western Heaven Demon Lord.
- Dark Heaven has effectively won the assault on the Sichuan Tang Clan, while fighting continues in the ruined Family Head's Hall.
- Tang Sadok remains alive but gravely wounded and captive on a hill overlooking the destroyed Tang Clan.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious and the Divine Physician remains in the underground prison.
- Cheongpung remains alive at Supreme Peak and is fighting First Fiend; Cheongpung and Taekyung recognize each other as friends and true martial rivals.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord still seeks the Myriad-Poison Ring and intends to deliver Taekyung and Cheongpung alive to the Blood Lord.

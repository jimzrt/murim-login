# Chapter 108

## Plot

At an abandoned shrine, Jin Taekyung and Wolhwa encounter ten armed human traffickers led by Lee Sam, who are transporting the Five-Colored Ghosts as captives. The Five-Colored Ghosts are former subordinates of Jang Sam who claim to have quit banditry, but they were caught trying to steal the traffickers’ money pouches. Taekyung decides to free them despite their criminal past.

Jin Mukyung arrives and ends the confrontation almost instantly, crippling Lee Sam by breaking his limbs and destroying his dantian while the other men surrender. The rescued Five-Colored Ghosts reveal that the group they followed to a pleasure house was the Red Wind Band, which plans to leave at dawn and travel nonstop to Saneum. A messenger hawk carrying a small cylinder then arrives at the shrine.

## Continuity

- Ten armed human traffickers arrived at the abandoned shrine with the Five-Colored Ghosts bound as prisoners.
- The Five-Colored Ghosts are former subordinates of Jang Sam who had quit banditry but recently turned to theft and were caught stealing money pouches.
- Jin Taekyung freed the Five-Colored Ghosts after deciding former bandits were preferable to active human traffickers.
- Jin Mukyung overwhelmed the traffickers in seconds; Lee Sam survived with all four limbs broken and his dantian destroyed, while the rest surrendered.
- Lee Sam was previously a Level 25 martial artist who had once been close to First Rate; after Mukyung’s attack, his Level window displayed Level 2.
- The traffickers identified the mounted-bandit group they had followed as the Red Wind Band and said it planned to leave at dawn for Saneum, near Eung-hyeon and the Mount Heng Sword Sect’s headquarters.
- A messenger hawk carrying a small cylinder arrived at the shrine; the sender and message remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 오색귀 as Five-Colored Ghosts, with a footnote explaining the nickname.
- Render 이삼 as Lee Sam.
- Render 전서응 as messenger hawk and 대형 as Boss in the Five-Colored Ghosts’ address to Taekyung.
- Retain established renderings of 마적단 as mounted-bandit group, 적풍단 as Red Wind Band, and 산음 as Saneum.

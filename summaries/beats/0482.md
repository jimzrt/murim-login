# Chapter 482

## Plot

Gung Gibang reports that the military vessel on which Honglan was last seen has been found at Red Cliffs. Ninety-four of the ninety-six people aboard, excluding Honglan, stabbed themselves in the throat and died smiling, while one survivor remains.

The sole survivor is Song Ho, a Hubei military officer whose soul has been thoroughly seized. Although Mungyeong declares him effectively beyond hope, Honglan suddenly speaks through his body, confirming that she can remotely control enthralled people.

Honglan reveals that Dark Heaven has many eyes and ears, says Blood Lord reported on Taekyung, and identifies herself as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress before refusing to disclose her location.

## Continuity

- Honglan escaped from the military vessel after using the Soul-Seizing Technique to force ninety-four passengers and crew members to commit suicide.
- The surviving officer Song Ho remains physically present but mentally incapacitated, and Honglan can speak through his body from an unknown distant location.
- Honglan identifies herself as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and confirms her connection to Dark Heaven's intelligence network; her exact motives and location remain unknown.

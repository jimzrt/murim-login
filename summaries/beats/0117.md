# Chapter 117

## Plot

Jin Mukyung realizes that Pung Yang's use of the Temporary Strength Pill has made even the ordinary Three Calamities Sword Technique overwhelmingly powerful, though he evades the attack. Jin Taekyung then strikes Pung Yang with One Annihilation, but Pung Yang's newly revealed Body-Protecting Qi blocks it. Mukyung and Pung Yang clash directly; Mukyung appears to win after breaking the protective qi and injuring Pung Yang, but Pung Yang had already embedded five throwing knives in Mukyung, leaving him unconscious. Pung Yang resumes killing Mount Heng Sword Sect martial artists and declares that he will take the Jin brothers and obtain their family's martial arts formulas. Taekyung remembers the dangerous Blazing Flame Divine Pill left by Jopil and decides to use it against Pung Yang.

## Continuity

- Pung Yang's Temporary Strength Pill lets him use overwhelmingly powerful saber qi and maintain Body-Protecting Qi that blocks Jin Taekyung's One Annihilation and Jin Mukyung's Sword Energy.
- Jin Mukyung won the visible exchange with Pung Yang by breaking his Body-Protecting Qi with Striking the Ox Across the Mountain, but Pung Yang's concealed throwing knives had already incapacitated him.
- Pung Yang resumed his attack after the duel and killed more than ten exhausted Mount Heng Sword Sect martial artists with throwing knives.
- Pung Yang intends to take Jin Mukyung and Jin Taekyung and learn the Jin Family of Taiyuan's martial arts formulas to supplement the Crimson Blood Cultivation Technique.
- Jin Taekyung has decided to consume Jopil's Blazing Flame Divine Pill to fight Pung Yang, but he has not yet taken it.

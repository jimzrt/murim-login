# Chapter 1073

## Plot

Taekyung’s side holds against ten thousand monsters, but their allies and the Supreme Peak masters are tiring, and Taekyung prepares to break the enemy formation with the Middle Dantian’s power. The Great Sir suddenly appears at the vanguard, calls himself Gaettong, and orders the monsters to kneel. The monsters then all collapse for an unknown reason. A boy’s voice and Cheongpung’s voice reach Taekyung from afar.

## Continuity

- The monsters surrounding Taekyung’s force all collapsed after the Great Sir appeared; the cause is unknown.
- The Great Sir calls himself Gaettong and claims the monsters should obey Chunja’s command; he refers to himself as Chunja after changing his name and gender.
- A young boy and Cheongpung arrive nearby, though their locations and connection to the Great Sir are not established.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 춘자 as Chunja, the name the Great Sir says the monsters should obey.

# Chapter 239

## Plot

Namgung Ryong apologizes for his son's discourtesy and asks Jin Taekyung to prevent Great Hero Jeok from dueling his father, the Azure Sky Sword King. Taekyung explains that Jeok will not back down and agrees only to try speaking with him.

Namgung Ryong's son, Nangong Ok—the Sword Dragon and Lesser Family Head—confronts Taekyung after overhearing the conversation. When Nangong Ok attempts to draw his sword, Taekyung stops the blade and then deliberately summons the Azure Sky Sword King to a secluded mountain.

Taekyung begs Namgung Cheon to postpone the duel for one year but refuses to explain Jeok's condition. Namgung Cheon offers to grant the request if Taekyung withstands three moves, generating the Sudden Quest Three-Move Kill. Taekyung accepts, removes his year-long shackles, and faces the Azure Sky Sword King's opening Emperor's Sword Form.

## Continuity

- Namgung Ryong has one child, Nangong Ok, whom he raised with great care; he acknowledges that Ok remains immature despite being the Sword Dragon and the Nangong Family's Lesser Family Head.
- Namgung Ryong believes the duel between Jeok Cheongang and Namgung Cheon could cause disastrous upheaval if either master is seriously harmed, and he will attempt to stop it himself.
- Jin Taekyung deliberately summoned the Azure Sky Sword King to a secluded mountain and asked him to delay the duel for one year without revealing that Jeok's condition is poor.
- Namgung Cheon will grant Taekyung's request if Taekyung withstands three of his moves; Taekyung accepted the resulting Quest and removed his chained iron balls before the first attack.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 검룡 as Sword Dragon.
- Render 남궁옥 as Nangong Ok, matching the reading-copy rendering of the Nangong Family's formal names.
- Render 제왕검형 as Emperor's Sword Form.
- Render 삼초살 as Three-Move Kill.
- Render 남만 as Nanman and retain the explanatory footnote for the historical regional term.

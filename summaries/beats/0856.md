# Chapter 856

## Plot

After eleven days of grueling travel from the Sichuan Tang Clan, Jin’s exhausted party reaches Jiangsu. Jin refuses to split the group, insisting they must endure and grow stronger together; during a brief rest, he carries Hyuk Mujin while Jeok Cheongang carries Ju Hwaran.

On a guarded carriage bound for the imperial capital, Hong Jin remembers his impoverished childhood and the late Emperor’s charge to protect Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao. Zhu Bao remains confident in his elder brother, but Hong Jin fears the Emperor’s intentions. When the Embroidered Uniform Guard investigates an unidentified corpse ahead, Hong Jin hears a familiar voice and laughs, hoping it belongs to Jin’s party.

## Continuity

- Jin’s party reaches Jiangsu after eleven days on the road; the group is exhausted, and Jin insists they continue together rather than travel separately.
- Prince Shangshan and Hong Jin are traveling toward the imperial capital in a carriage surrounded by the Embroidered Uniform Guard. Hong Jin fears the Emperor may harm Zhu Bao, whom the late Emperor entrusted to his care.
- An unidentified corpse lies ahead of the procession, and Hong Jin hears a familiar voice nearby, prompting hope that Jin’s party has caught up.

# Chapter 75

## Plot

Taekyung logs out after spending roughly twenty days in Murim and finds that only 2:05:35 has passed in the modern world. He returns to his goshiwon, where Seong Jinho surprises him and demands dinner. They eat gopchang, continue drinking at a third-round makgeolli bar, and Jinho becomes completely drunk, forcing Taekyung to carry him home. Team Leader Choi then texts Taekyung to meet at the same place and time the next day.

Using Sleep Mode, Taekyung sleeps for less than three hours, circulates his qi, receives a slight increase in internal energy, and spends the morning running and doing bodyweight exercises. Jinho visits to cook ramen while watching television, reveals that he plans to move out soon, and rejects Taekyung's implied offer to live together. Taekyung leaves to meet Team Leader Choi.

Team Leader Choi meets Taekyung alone in a café that he owns, and Taekyung signs the contract after their discussion. A System alert rings immediately after he completes the final signature.

# Chapter 223

## Plot

After Jin Taekyung kills Carus, the Peace Guild members are overwhelmed by shock, relief, congratulations, and pride. They struggle to determine how to process the more-than-fifty-meter corpse of the Named Monster without revealing Taekyung's Inventory and System, eventually deciding to bring in outside Hunters.

The exit Gate does not open because the separate boss monster remains alive and fled into the wilderness. Taekyung and Butler Kim return toward the entrance, with Butler Kim using Fire Rain to destroy part of an Orc group. In the jungle, Taekyung discovers Won Myunghoon at Level 80 leading the hostile group he mockingly calls hyenas.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo, Butler Kim, Song Song, and Im Kkeokjeong witnessed Taekyung's overwhelming defeat of Carus and recognized that their previous assessment of his abilities was incomplete.
- Carus's corpse is too large and difficult to process normally; its hide, bones, flesh, and scales require at least B-Rank Hunter-level assistance, preferably an A-Rank Hunter.
- Taekyung considers storing Carus's corpse in the Inventory but fears that removing it past the reporters stationed at the Gate entrance would expose him as a System user.
- The exit Gate remains closed because the Gate's separate boss monster survived, fled from Taekyung, and is hiding somewhere in the vast wilderness.
- Taekyung and Butler Kim travel back toward the original Gate entrance to recruit outside personnel, with Taekyung carrying Butler Kim on his back.
- Butler Kim uses the A-Rank spell Fire Rain during the journey, killing approximately one-third of a wandering Orc group and scattering the rest.
- Taekyung encounters Won Myunghoon at Level 80 in the jungle; Won responds with open hostility after Taekyung previously let him and his group go.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Named Monster and boss monster distinct: a Named Monster is an uninvited intruder, while the boss monster is the Gate's original resident.
- Render 파이어 레인 as Fire Rain and 리젠 as Regen.
- Render Taekyung's 아저씨 address for Im Kkeokjeong as Uncle, preserving its familiar teasing tone.
- Render Won Myunghoon's hostile 이러ᄂ 싸가지 없는 새끼 sentiment as ill-mannered bastard.

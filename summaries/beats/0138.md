# Chapter 138

## Plot

Jin Taekyung travels to Prince Shangshan's luncheon in a luxurious six-horse carriage with Cheongpung and the injured Five Gates heirs. Cheongpung's complete lack of worldly experience and intense curiosity about the imperial family alarm everyone, and he accidentally detaches an exquisitely crafted golden dragon from the carriage. Elsewhere, Assistant Military Commissioner Li Feng enters the grand hall and confronts Eunuch Hong, his political rival. Eunuch Hong reveals that he has invited famous martial artists from Shaanxi, including the Three Hands of Zhongnan, to entertain Prince Shangshan. Gong Ilhyuk, the third of the Three Hands, taunts Li Feng over a humiliating defeat at Huashan ten years earlier, just before the young prodigies of Shanxi Murim are announced.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung has never ridden even an ordinary carriage and is fascinated by unfamiliar worldly experiences, including the imperial family.
- Hyuk Mujin cannot attend Prince Shangshan's luncheon because Taekyung bruised his face that morning; Taekyung deliberately substitutes Cheongpung as the more impressive young prodigy.
- Li Feng is Shanxi Province's Assistant Military Commissioner and a former Huashan lay disciple who left the sect nearly ten years ago.
- Eunuch Hong is the powerful Deputy Military Commissioner of Shanxi Province and a political rival of Li Feng; he arranged a gathering of martial artists for Prince Shangshan.
- Huashan and the Zhongnan Sect have been bitter rivals competing for supremacy in Shaanxi for approximately one hundred years.
- Gong Ilhyuk is the third member of the Three Hands of Zhongnan and openly taunts Li Feng about Li Feng's defeat at Huashan ten years earlier.

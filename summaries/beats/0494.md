# Chapter 494

## Plot

The Water God Dragon's corpse is rapidly dismantled under Jin Taekyung's direction. Mungyeong recognizes Taekyung's innate Heavenly Martial Physique and decides to correct his complacency while testing him through poison. He tricks Hyuk Mujin into delivering Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder; Taekyung detoxifies it with the Myriad-Poison Ring and Scorching Yang Qi, but suffers permanent Strength and Agility losses of five points each from Sinews and Meridians damage, along with a temporary Severe Stomachache. When Taekyung leaves to deal with it, Mungyeong attacks him with four concealed daggers and reveals that the poison and ambush were part of his training.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is testing Taekyung through successive poisoned traps and concealed attacks, intending to teach him secret martial arts without forming a formal Master-Disciple relationship while correcting Taekyung's complacent mindset.
- Detoxifying Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder left Taekyung with minor Sinews and Meridians damage that permanently reduced Strength and Agility by 5 each; Severe Stomachache is a temporary status abnormality.

- Render 칠보추혼산 as Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder, 강력한 칠보추혼산 as Potent Seven-Step Soul-Chasing Powder, and 심각한 복통 as Severe Stomachache.
- Retain Heavenly Martial Physique, Sinews and Meridians, and the established rough, profane Taekyung voice.

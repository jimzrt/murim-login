# Chapter 861

## Plot

Jeong Hogun’s force of nearly a thousand Embroidered Uniform Guards escorts Prince Shangshan into the imperial capital. Hong Jin remembers the purge that once threatened the prince and vows that events will not unfold as the Emperor wishes. In Hangzhou, Jin Taekyung’s party finds the city under unusually heavy guard and reaches the imperial palace, where Taekyung presses Hong Jin about his knowledge and hidden allies. Hong Jin recounts how poverty drove him to become a eunuch, how the late Emperor recognized his abilities, and asks Taekyung whether he has heard of the East Depot.

## Continuity

- Prince Shangshan and his party have reached the imperial palace under an unusually large Embroidered Uniform Guard escort; the Emperor’s intentions toward the prince remain uncertain.
- Hong Jin became a eunuch to escape poverty and save his family; the late Emperor later recognized his abilities and told him they could be put to use.
- Hong Jin has asked Taekyung whether he knows of the East Depot; the chapter does not explain Hong Jin’s connection to it.
- Taekyung’s other helpers have not yet appeared, and Hong Jin wants to know where they are.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 동창 as “East Depot.”
- Hong Jin addresses Taekyung as “Young Master Jin.”

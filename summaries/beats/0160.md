# Chapter 160

## Plot

Jin Taekyung accepts that reaching the Peak realm has only revealed how much farther he must progress. He thanks Cheongpung for nearly ten days of sincere training and Mae Jonghak's teaching, but Cheongpung refuses to continue their duel because Taekyung needs to rest and digest his enlightenment. Cheongpung cancels Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2 without penalty, leaving their duel unfinished.

After completing Beyond the Wall, Taekyung changes class to Peak Master, gains seven Levels from the accumulated notifications, raises Jin Family's Cultivation Technique to the ninth stage and Qi Sense to the seventh stage, unlocks the trainable Sound Transmission Skill, receives Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron, and triggers the Find the Craftsman Quest.

In Northern Gaoyuan, the chieftains Temur and Chinggen, each commanding one hundred tribespeople, attend a gathering with two other powerful men. One is an unnamed middle-aged mounted-bandit figure, while the other is the mysterious Han Chinese leader known as the Human Butcher, who commands fifty subordinates and intimidates Temur into silence.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's breakthrough into the Peak realm came from accepting Cheongpung's teaching that martial arts are empty space to be accepted and filled; he now understands that he cannot defeat Cheongpung while Cheongpung fights at full strength.
- Cheongpung canceled the second Sword Saint-training Quest by his own decision, so Taekyung received no penalty; their unfinished duel is postponed until a later time.
- Taekyung's Peak breakthrough completed Beyond the Wall, changed his class to Peak Master, produced seven total Level increases in the displayed rewards, raised Jin Family's Cultivation Technique to the ninth stage and Qi Sense to the seventh stage, unlocked trainable Sound Transmission, and awarded Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron plus the Find the Craftsman Quest.
- Temur and Chinggen are chieftains in Northern Gaoyuan who each command one hundred tribespeople and claim descent from the khans.
- The Human Butcher is a mysterious Han Chinese mounted-bandit power in Northern Gaoyuan who commands fifty subordinates and earned his epithet by killing people as though slaughtering livestock.

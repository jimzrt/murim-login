# Chapter 866

## Plot

Jin Taekyung meets Ma Sanbao, the East Depot’s second-in-command, who reveals that he and Hong Jin are longtime friends and allies. Ma sent Hong secret intelligence about disguised Embroidered Uniform Guard members heading north toward Shanxi, enabling Hong to prepare before asking Taekyung for help. Ma says his loyalty is to the late Emperor, not the ruler now on the throne, and urges Hong to join him in setting right the chaos caused by the traitor.

## Continuity

- Ma Sanbao, the East Depot’s Brush-Holding Eunuch and second-in-command, is a longtime friend and ally of Hong Jin; he has secretly remained in the imperial palace loyal to the late Emperor while waiting for his moment to act.
- About a month ago, Ma sent Hong Jin a secret letter reporting that some Embroidered Uniform Guard members were traveling north in disguise and might be headed to Shanxi Province.
- Hong Jin asked Jin Taekyung for help after careful consideration and with time to prepare based on Ma Sanbao’s intelligence.
- Ma Sanbao and Hong Jin now say the time has come to act against the traitor on the throne, but they have not yet explained their full plan.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 동창 병필태감 as “Brush-Holding Eunuch of the East Depot”; 병필태감 is the East Depot’s second-in-command.
- Render 첩형 as “Constable” and 태감 as “Eunuch” in forms of address.

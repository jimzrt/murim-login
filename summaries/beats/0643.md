# Chapter 643

## Plot

Jin Taekyung enters the Nanman tribal grand council with Yayul Mok and faces Baeksang's opposition. Jin challenges Baeksang to seek the other chieftains' opinions, and support gradually gathers from the Zang, Hui, Man, Bouyei, and other tribes because Jin saved their people at Ailao Mountain and because of the Fire King's and Fire Gate Clan's past contributions. Yayul Cheok arrives, recognizes the majority consensus, and formally grants Jin permission to attend the council, creating the Quest [Tribal Grand Council].

In a hidden, damp darkness, an unknown entity learns that Jin attended the council and kills two subordinates who report the news. The entity recognizes Jin's name and quietly remarks that it has been a long time.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung has been admitted to the Nanman tribal grand council with the support of a majority of the chieftains.
- Yayul Cheok formally authorized Jin's attendance as both Palace Lord of the Nanman Beast Palace and great chieftain of the Miao people.
- Baeksang opposed Jin's admission on procedural and traditional grounds but did not overturn the majority decision.
- Several tribes support Jin because he helped save their warriors at Ailao Mountain, while others support him because of historical debts owed to the Fire King or the Fire Gate Clan.
- A hostile and unidentified entity in a hidden location knows Jin Taekyung and claims that their last meeting was long ago; it has killed two informants over reports about Jin's council attendance.
- The Quest [Tribal Grand Council] has been created.

# Chapter 360

## Plot

The Western Heaven Demon Lord decisively overwhelms Jin Taekyung in the underground prison, shattering his limbs and inflicting a fatal Internal Injury while Taekyung's internal energy is exhausted. Taekyung remains conscious long enough to headbutt him, but cannot stop him from leaving.

As Taekyung believes the battle and the wider assault are lost, he recalls Jeok Cheongang's sacrifice and resolves not to collapse or flee. The Western Heaven Demon Lord leaves him alive temporarily, promising to take him if he survives and stating that Jeok Cheongang, the Divine Physician, and the defenders outside will not change the outcome.

The damaged underground prison continues to shake and collapse. As Taekyung's senses fade and he expects death, the Heavenly Power Demon appears before him with a torn chain and a gaunt, unusually tall body.

## Continuity

- The Western Heaven Demon Lord has defeated Jin Taekyung in the underground prison; Taekyung's limbs are crippled, his internal energy is exhausted, and he cannot move independently.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord leaves Taekyung alive for the moment and proceeds onward, intending to return and take him if he survives.
- The underground prison has suffered extensive structural damage from the battle and continuing tremors.
- Taekyung fails to stop the Western Heaven Demon Lord and fears for Jeok Cheongang, the Divine Physician, and Cheongpung.
- The Western Heaven Demon Lord claims that the Tang Clan, Qingcheng, and Emei will be destroyed, but this chapter does not confirm those outcomes.
- The Heavenly Power Demon appears before Taekyung after the chain restraining him has been torn away.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain Western Heaven Demon Lord, Heavenly Power Demon, White Flame, Spear Energy, Body-Protecting Qi, and underground prison as established renderings.
- Preserve Taekyung's exhausted, profane, first-person voice and his self-blaming reaction to the apparent defeat.
- Treat the Western Heaven Demon Lord's statements about the wider assault as claims or threats rather than confirmed outcomes.

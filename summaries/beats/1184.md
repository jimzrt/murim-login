# Chapter 1184

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang reveals that Taekyung was secretly designated the allied forces’ main attack against Dark Heaven, a plan everyone present accepted to keep him hidden as their last weapon. Taekyung refuses to abandon his missing companions and decides to turn back, but the Slaughter Saint incapacitates him with a specially made medicine and the Bow Saint blocks his escape; Jeok catches him as he collapses.

## Continuity

- Taekyung was kept unaware of the plan to make him the main attack because Dark Heaven’s eyes and ears may be hidden everywhere; the Sword Saint, Emperor, and gathered allies agreed.
- Taekyung chooses to turn back at least half a day to search for his missing companions, despite the urgency of continuing toward the Lord of Heaven.
- The Slaughter Saint used a medicine near the boundary of poison to incapacitate Taekyung; the Bow Saint and Jeok Cheongang intervened, and Taekyung collapsed into Jeok’s arms.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Dark Heaven for 암천, Lord of Heaven for 천주, the Adversary for 대적자, and fasting pills for 벽곡단.

# Chapter 548

## Plot

After the conference, Jin Wikyung accepts Taekyung's warning about Dark Heaven and asks to hear the secrets Taekyung has been unable to tell once the crisis is over. Song Ho then summons Taekyung to Mae Jonghak's office, where Mae, Jeok Cheongang, and Song treat his claim that monsters may overrun the world as a serious possibility despite Taekyung admitting only fifty-percent certainty.

Using a map of the realm and its sects, Taekyung identifies a likely next target for Dark Heaven and argues that the Southern Heaven Demon Empress may already be heading there. Song reports that a dispatch sent more than seven days earlier has received no reply and that trouble may already have begun; Mae Jonghak responds by giving Taekyung his first order as Fire Dragon Pavilion Master.

Taekyung immediately returns to his residence, orders Hyuk Mujin to summon the entire Fire Dragon Pavilion for its first mission, and names Nanman as their destination.

## Continuity

- Mae Jonghak has ordered Jin Taekyung's first Fire Dragon Pavilion mission to Nanman, and Taekyung has begun mobilizing the pavilion.
- Taekyung believes the Southern Heaven Demon Empress may already be moving toward Dark Heaven's suspected next target; Song Ho's dispatch to that location has received no reply for more than seven days.
- Jin Wikyung has accepted that Taekyung is keeping important secrets and wants to hear them when the current crisis is over.
- The Hidden Shadow Pavilion had already investigated the suspected target after receiving reports from Hubei Province, but the rift phenomenon was unexpected.

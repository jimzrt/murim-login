# Chapter 450

## Plot

Taekyung's group reaches Donghu Stronghold's island and finds Hwang Chung, Dangyang Stronghold Lord Hwang Cheol, and Honghu Stronghold Lord Do Ripgun dead among the devastation. No survivors or witnesses remain, and the bodies indicate that the Hubei territory of the Yangtze River Channel League was destroyed by an overwhelming force.

The group finds no trace of the Moving Formation that may have brought Dark Heaven to the island. Zhuge Feng decides to send Taekyung's party to the Zhuge Clan for formation and mechanism specialists, while Wudang cannot readily provide aid because an unidentified killer demon has murdered more than thirty people, including twenty pilgrims on Mount Wudang. Jin Wikyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Mungyeong remain behind while the others prepare to depart.

## Continuity

- Hwang Chung, Hwang Cheol, and Do Ripgun were found dead at Donghu Stronghold, and no survivor or witness was discovered.
- The Yangtze River Channel League's Hubei strongholds, including Donghu, Dangyang, and Honghu, have been devastated; Dark Heaven is suspected but not proven responsible.
- The search of Donghu Stronghold and its surrounding cliffs found no trace of the suspected Moving Formation.
- Taekyung's party will travel to the Zhuge Clan to obtain formation and mechanism specialists, with Zhuge Feng providing several retainers.
- Wudang is occupied by an unidentified skilled killer demon who has killed more than thirty people, including twenty pilgrims on Mount Wudang and prospective Wudang lay disciples.
- Jin Wikyung and Jeok Cheongang remain at Donghu Stronghold to continue the search, while Mungyeong chooses to stay behind for unstated reasons.

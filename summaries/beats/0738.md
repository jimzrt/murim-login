# Chapter 738

## Plot

Within twenty-four hours of the Paris disaster, ten coordinated Monster Waves strike major cities and landmarks around the world, leaving governments, civilians, and Hunters in chaos. Odin Guild successfully suppresses five waves, and Michael Silbert publicly presents himself as a grieving hero while declaring that the attacks were deliberate terrorism.

Jin and Team Leader Choi realize that the attacks targeted crowded landmarks, major corporations, and especially the overseas branches of major Guilds in order to isolate Ares Guild. Meanwhile, a hidden Prophet confirms that ten fanatical warriors completed their missions and died, then orders the world to learn what caused the disaster.

## Continuity

- Ten coordinated Monster Waves occurred across the world within a single day, confirming that the disaster was a planned terrorist campaign rather than a natural catastrophe.
- Michael Silbert publicly emerged as the hero who suppressed five Monster Waves and used Paris CCTV footage to frame the events as purposeful terrorism while concealing his own role.
- The attacks targeted crowded landmarks, major corporate headquarters, and the branches of major Guilds, with isolating Ares Guild identified as Michael's objective.
- Jin and Team Leader Choi believe a final strike intended to isolate Ares Guild from the world is imminent.
- A hidden Prophet commands a religiously fanatical organization whose ten warriors completed the coordinated attacks and returned to what they called God's embrace.
- The Prophet intends to reveal to the world what caused the disasters, but the Prophet's identity and the nature of the final strike remain unknown.
- Michael and Huginn engineered the broader terrorist operation through an alliance with Middle Eastern fanatics seeking weapons for revenge.
- The forced Quest Chain of Terror Attacks remains active and cannot be refused.
- Cheon Taemin remains unconscious, leaving Ares Guild vulnerable to the consequences of the attacks.
- Idle Bystander continues to reduce Jin's abilities by ten percent during its thirty-day duration.

# Chapter 315

## Plot

Hyuk Sopyung leaves Xi’an Tower under public scorn after his earlier defeat, confronting further humiliation from the chief manager and reflecting on the Zhongnan Sect’s declining standing. He encounters his Junior Brother Bong-su, who reveals that the Taeeul Sword Unit has come to Xi’an on orders to bring him back because their Second Martial Uncle, the Taeeul Merciless Sword, is in the city. Hyuk Sopyung fears this Senior Martial Uncle more than even his own Master.

Jin Taekyung successfully performs True Qi Guidance on the unconscious Jeok Cheongang. Because they share the Fire Gate Clan’s lineage, their energies merge without resistance; Jeok’s condition improves slightly and Taekyung’s Scorching Yang Qi rises slightly. The experience grants Taekyung new insight, raises the Fire Gate Divine Technique to seven stars, and causes him to level up. Afterward, Cheongpung nearly suffocates Hyuk Mujin while trying to stop his snoring, forcing Taekyung to revive Mujin with Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Fist. Gung Gibang returns from the Xi’an branch of the Beggars’ Sect with information unrelated to the Divine Physician and receives five silver nyang before sharing it.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Sopyung’s humiliation at Xi’an Tower reinforces that the Zhongnan Sect’s standing has declined after Jeok Cheongang, Mae Jonghak, and Cheongpung’s recent prominence.
- Bong-su is Hyuk Sopyung’s same-generation Junior Brother in the Zhongnan Sect. The Taeeul Sword Unit has come to Xi’an to bring Hyuk back.
- The Zhongnan Sect’s Second Martial Uncle, known as the Taeeul Merciless Sword, is currently in Xi’an and is feared throughout the sect.
- Taekyung’s True Qi Guidance can guide Jeok Cheongang’s energy because both share the Fire Gate Clan’s lineage.
- True Qi Guidance slightly improves Jeok Cheongang’s condition and slightly increases Taekyung’s Scorching Yang Qi.
- Taekyung’s Fire Gate Divine Technique reaches seven stars after he gains insight from experiencing Jeok Cheongang’s energy, and he levels up.
- Gung Gibang visited the Beggars’ Sect’s Xi’an branch and obtained information that is not about the Divine Physician.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 태을검대 as Taeeul Sword Unit and 태을무정검 as Taeeul Merciless Sword.
- Render 대사형 as Senior Brother and 사제 as Junior Brother.
- Preserve the 귀식대법/귀신대법 wordplay as Turtle Breath Technique/Ghost Technique.
- Use Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Fist for 심폐소생권 and retain the Butterfly Dream allusion with a footnote.

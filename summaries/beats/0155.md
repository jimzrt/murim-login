# Chapter 155

## Plot

Jin Wikyung manages the Jin Family's rapid postwar expansion from his office, prioritizing the establishment and stabilization of branches while secretly coordinating with the Shanxi Provincial Office for aid against five allied mounted-bandit groups gathering near Datong; Wipeng offers to lead useful reinforcements. Wikyung privately hopes the family will become a great family. News then arrives that Huashan has sent the Three Plum Blossom Elites and that Mae Jonghak has disappeared, likely to find Cheongpung. At the chapter's end, Cheongpung is beating Taekyung after their Wall Lizard Technique training ended.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung is prioritizing the establishment and stabilization of Jin Family branches across Shanxi, while seeking government assistance against approximately five hundred mounted bandits near Datong through a secret agreement with the Shanxi Provincial Office.
- Wipeng offered to personally accompany useful martial artists in the operation against the mounted bandits.
- Huashan sent the Three Plum Blossom Elites to meet Cheongpung and reported that Mae Jonghak had disappeared; Jin Wikyung and Wipeng believe Mae may have broken seclusion to seek Cheongpung.
- The Wall Lizard Technique training ended two days earlier, and Cheongpung is currently beating Taekyung.

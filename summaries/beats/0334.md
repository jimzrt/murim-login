# Chapter 334

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's party reaches Chengdu after three days aboard Mu Song's fast ship. Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious and is steadily weakening; Taekyung can only slow his decline with True Qi Guidance, leaving the Divine Physician as their sole hope.

Mu Song uses his negotiated understanding with Chengdu's authorities to land the party, then promises to send subordinates for their return journey. Taekyung gives Mungyeong travel money and learns that the medical apprentice is also headed to the Sichuan Tang Clan, allowing him to accompany the party.

## Continuity

- The fast ship carries Taekyung's party and Mungyeong from Guang'an to Chengdu, after which the group heads toward the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Jeok Cheongang is unconscious on the pack frame, visibly weakening; True Qi Guidance can only delay his decline, and treatment by the Divine Physician remains the only known hope.
- Mungyeong treats the ship's crew, has integrated naturally into Taekyung's group, and now travels with them toward the Sichuan Tang Clan.
- Taekyung gives Mungyeong several silver nyang and invites him to visit the Jin Family of Taiyuan after becoming a physician.
- Mu Song has negotiated a practical compromise with Chengdu's authorities that allows Water Dragon Stronghold vessels to approach the city under controlled conditions and will send subordinates to assist with the party's return.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 공자님 as Young Master when Mungyeong addresses Taekyung.

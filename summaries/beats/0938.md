# Chapter 938

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang and the Bow Saint recall a devastating battle more than fifty years ago, when 1,319 members of the orthodox death squad died fighting the Demonic Cult. The Bow Saint reveals that she has never forgotten the dead and counts casualties after every battle. Jeok learns that she deliberately put Jin Taekyung at risk during the recent banquet-hall battle to confirm that he was the chosen one and understand his power and character; she believes the decision was necessary for the sake of far more lives, perhaps the whole world, and says the Martial God chose her for that reason. Jeok, enraged at the danger to his Disciple, stops short when she names the 2,562 allies killed at the banquet hall. Hyuk Mujin then runs toward them.

## Continuity

- The Bow Saint says the Martial God chose her, and she tested Jin Taekyung in the banquet-hall battle to confirm whether he was the chosen one and learn his power and character.
- The Bow Saint counts the dead after every battle and has never forgotten the 1,319 orthodox fighters killed in the battle at Mount Small Hua more than fifty years ago.
- The Bow Saint says 2,562 allies died in the grand banquet hall three days earlier, and that acting sooner could have saved at least half of them.
- Jeok Cheongang is furious that the Bow Saint put Jin Taekyung in danger, but says his bond with Taekyung has been unchanged since he accepted him as a Disciple.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 소화산 as Mount Small Hua and 여산 as Mount Li; render 종남 as the Zhongnan Sect.

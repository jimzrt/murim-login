# Chapter 922

## Plot

After the battle, So Gyo tells Jin Taekyung through Sound Transmission that he is the chosen one the Martial God spoke of, promising to explain later. Taekyung reunites with his surviving allies, thanks Jeok Cheongang, and walks to confront the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord.

The Demon Lord recalls his murdered mother and Master, then claims that Dark Heaven’s agents are spread throughout the land and that civil war will engulf the Great Nation before Dark Heaven advances into the Central Plains.

## Continuity

- So Gyo identifies Jin Taekyung as the Martial God’s chosen one and says she has kept a long story about it to herself; she will explain when the situation allows.
- Jeok Cheongang and Jin Taekyung openly express gratitude for each other’s steadfast support.
- Jeok Cheongang, Hyuk Mujin, Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, Sama Pyo, Taishan, and the Divine Physician all survived and reunited with Jin Taekyung.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord recalls that his mother and Master were killed; their suffering remains vivid to him, while their happy faces have faded from his memory.
- The Eastern Heaven Demon Lord claims Dark Heaven has agents throughout the land and predicts civil war in the Great Nation followed by an advance into the Central Plains. This remains his claim, not a confirmed outcome.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Force” for 강기 distinct from “death energy” for 사기.
- Render 엄마신장 as “Mom’s Divine Palm” in Jin Taekyung’s joking explanation.

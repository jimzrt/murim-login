# Chapter 1145

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang keeps watch over the sleeping Jin Taekyung, whose consciousness has gone to the realm of immortals. Taekyung has promised to return after investigating a suspected crisis there. As the coalition army marches across the Taklamakan Desert toward Tianshan, Jeok resolves to stay beside him and protect him; the Slaughter Saint and others remain nearby, and Jeok takes comfort in seeing how many people care for his Disciple. Dark clouds gather over the desert.

Elsewhere, a shadow awakens in a sealed chamber after briefly losing consciousness, kills a subordinate for questioning the truth, and seeks answers from the Lord of Heaven. The Lord says the shadow—now revealed to be the Grand Mage—must have felt a wave because root and branch are one, and the Grand Mage was reborn through the Lord’s grace. A faint green light rises; the Lord caresses jade and declares that the heavens have opened once again.

## Continuity

- Taekyung is asleep in the Murim world while his consciousness is in the realm of immortals; Jeok Cheongang stays beside him until he wakes.
- The coalition army of more than two hundred thousand is crossing the Taklamakan Desert toward Tianshan, and Jeok intends to protect Taekyung there.
- The Grand Mage has been reborn through the Lord of Heaven’s grace and felt a wave that the Lord connects to the heavens opening again; its cause and significance remain unexplained.

## Translation Decisions

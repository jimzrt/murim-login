# Chapter 380

## Plot

Shao Shen and the Public Security Armed Forces are nearly crushed when a burning airplane tears through the rear half of the monster army. The plane stops just short of Shao Shen, revealing Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi; Choi's barrier magic saves the occupants, who are unconscious. Shao recognizes Taekyung as the Korean Hunter known as Sibeol-jwa.

Taekyung receives the Unexpected Assault Quest and joins the defense of Chengdu International Airport. He kills large numbers of monsters with Scorching Yang Qi, White Flame, Flame Divine Palm, and Flame-Annihilating Divine Fist, confirming that roughly half of the nearly two-thousand-strong monster army consists of undead. The Skeleton Warlord judges that the undead are being controlled by someone other than the Lich, whose control is probably weaker than the Skeleton Warlord's. When the Skeleton Warlord chants, “Grow, grow, skeletons, skeletons…,” the undead abruptly stop moving.

## Continuity

- Taekyung and Team Leader Choi arrive at Chengdu International Airport aboard the burning private jet; Choi's barrier magic prevents the occupants from being killed, though the occupants and flight attendants are unconscious.
- Shao Shen recognizes Jin Taekyung's nickname, Sibeol-jwa, and addresses him as Teacher Jin. Taekyung directs Shao Shen to protect his subordinates and save as many people as possible.
- The System generates the unexpected Quest Unexpected Assault; its outcome is not shown.
- A large portion of the airport's monster army is undead, with no detectable life force, and the army is under an unidentified controller's command.
- The Skeleton Warlord can exert control over the undead army; his chant causes the undead monsters to stop in the middle of battle.
- The Skeleton Warlord believes the controller's undead control is inferior to his own and says the Lich from Taekyung's holographic recording probably did not personally lead the attack.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 시벌좌 as Sibeol-jwa and retain a footnote explaining the Korean profanity in the nickname.
- Render 중앙 군사 위원회 as Central Military Commission, distinct from Central Committee of China.
- Render 화염신장 as Flame Divine Palm and 멸염신권 as Flame-Annihilating Divine Fist.
- Render 진 선생님 as Teacher Jin for Shao Shen's formal address to Taekyung.

# Chapter 1094

## Plot

As Jin Taekyung and the Blood Lord continue their close-quarters fight, the Blood Lord is astonished that Taekyung can withstand him. The Bow Saint’s arrow helps Taekyung press an attack, but the Blood Lord catches his flaming palm strike and holds him at bay.

Cheongpung, Cheongheoja, Perfected Being Hyeoncheon, the Slaughter Saint, and the Bow Saint join Taekyung, surrounding the Blood Lord. The Blood Lord recognizes that Taekyung had drawn him into this confrontation, but Taekyung senses a second encirclement: about a dozen Black Ghosts, with the Grand Mage behind them. He judges that the clash could end in mutual destruction or worse.

The Blood Lord privately questions why the Lord of Heaven wants Taekyung kept alive, though he remains loyal to his master. He breaks free from Taekyung’s grip, vows to kill him, and a distant horn sounds.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung, Cheongheoja, Perfected Being Hyeoncheon, the Slaughter Saint, and the Bow Saint have joined Jin Taekyung against the Blood Lord.
- About a dozen Black Ghosts and the Grand Mage are positioned around the allied fighters; Taekyung fears the confrontation may end in mutual destruction or worse.
- The Blood Lord believes the Lord of Heaven values Taekyung’s safety above that of his loyal servants, and wants permission to kill Taekyung; his inner plea does not reach his master.
- The Blood Lord breaks free from Taekyung and vows to kill him; a horn sounds in the distance.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the Blood Lord’s belief that the Lord of Heaven prioritizes Taekyung’s safety framed as the Blood Lord’s interpretation, not confirmed fact.

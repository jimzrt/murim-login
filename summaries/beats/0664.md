# Chapter 664

## Plot

Jin Taekyung discovers that the damaged underground prison connects his cell with Taishan's cell one floor above. Taishan reports that Namho and Sama Pyo are being held separately and are safe, while Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin were captured but kept elsewhere. Jin receives a new linked Quest, Escape from Namshank, and resolves to escape before his execution.

Sudal, the Deputy Stronghold Lord of the Water Dragon Stronghold, abandons his post near Yunnan and leads three swift ships toward Guizhou to raid a target. They find a crewless Yangtze River Channel League ship occupied only by the Blood Monk, who asks whether it travels to Nanman.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung remains imprisoned in the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison with sealed internal energy and iron balls; his execution is scheduled for noon in two days.
- Taishan is imprisoned in the cell above Jin and can communicate through the broken ceiling; he was confined after breaking both wrists of a Nanman attendant who underfed him.
- Namho and Sama Pyo are detained in separate locations and are reportedly safe, while Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, and Hyuk Mujin have been captured and are being held elsewhere.
- Jin suspects the Blood Monk may serve the Southern Heaven Demon Empress and may threaten the reconnaissance squad during its movement.
- Sudal is leading three swift ships toward Guizhou, where the Blood Monk has taken control of a crewless ship and appears interested in traveling to Nanman.
- Jin's immediate objective is to escape the underground prison before the execution.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Escape from Namshank for 남생크, preserving the Shawshank pun.
- Retain underground prison for 뇌옥 and iron balls for 철구.
- Use Deputy Stronghold Lord for 부채주 and Stronghold Lord for 채주.
- Use Sudal for 수달.

# Chapter 686

## Plot

Jin Taekyung remains alive but falls into a deep sleep after defeating the unidentified old man and Black Hand Fist Demon, two Supreme Peak masters. Awed by his victory and sacrifice, Yohi resolves to expose Baeksang's conspiracy with Dark Heaven, abandon the Inner Palace, and travel west with Heugung and Jin to Boshan, where she can rally the Yao people and warn Nanman within seven days.

Heugung gives Yohi the Black Hand Fist Demon's antidote, carries Jin onto Muyaho, and prepares to leave the Poisonblood Grounds. When Yohi notices that Heugung has concealed more than his identity, he remarks that he had to hide himself first; blood then sprays and a massive silver body collapses, leaving the attack's target and Heugung's true nature unresolved.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is alive and in a deep sleep after defeating two Supreme Peak masters; Heugung is carrying him while Muyaho transports them.
- Black Hand and the Black Hand Fist Demon are dead, and the Western Yao Estate's warriors have been massacred.
- Yohi has decided to expose Baeksang and Dark Heaven, accept responsibility for her own complicity, and avoid the Inner Palace.
- Yohi and Heugung intend to reach Boshan in three or four days, mobilize up to one thousand Yao warriors, and spread the truth across Nanman within seven days.
- Yohi has taken the Black Hand Fist Demon's antidote while her internal energy remains sealed.
- Heugung's final remark about hiding himself precedes an unexplained attack in which blood sprays and a large silver body collapses.

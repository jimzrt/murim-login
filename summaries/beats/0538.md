# Chapter 538

## Plot

Jin Taekyung begins serving as Pavilion Master of the Two Dragons Pavilion, a position commanding a regiment and its squads, while public rumors make potential recruits wary of his deadly missions and Cheongpung's snake.

The System generates a Quest requiring Taekyung to secure at least five companions and name his organization, with failure awarding the Title Loner. He approaches Mungyeong as his first recruit, but Mungyeong refuses because he has already agreed to join another group; Cheongpung then appears and apologizes to Taekyung as his Benefactor.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung's formal title is Pavilion Master of the Two Dragons Pavilion, with a regiment and subordinate squads beneath the pavilion.
- The System's first Two Dragons Pavilion Quest requires at least five companions and an organization name; failure awards the Title Loner.
- Mungyeong has already committed to another group and refuses Taekyung's initial recruitment attempt.

# Chapter 100

## Plot

Im Chunsoo learns that the investigation into the Peace Guild failed, that Hong Woojin disappeared, and that the Security Team was probably defeated by Jin Taekyung. He identifies Seong Jinho as Taekyung's goshiwon manager and phone contact, then goes to Ilsan.

Taekyung defeats the six Security Team Hunters, treats their injuries with seized supplies, and confiscates their remaining Equipment and consumables. He intimidates Kim Junsu into revealing that Hong Woojin was a B-rank Familiar mage hired by Team Leader 1 and that the operation was ultimately directed by Im Chunsoo.

Im Chunsoo calls Taekyung, releases the captured Hunters through Team Leader 1, and walks with Taekyung before attacking him with ice spikes as a test. Taekyung counters with Fire Wall, and Level 80 Kim Hwajong arrives at the hiking-trail entrance and addresses Im Chunsoo familiarly.

## Continuity

- Im Chunsoo confirms that Sangdong Guild began investigating the Peace Guild after Im Changsoo was extorted for a hundred million won.
- The Security Team's field operation against Jin Taekyung failed; Choi Byungil was defeated and all six Hunters were captured until Team Leader 1 released them.
- Im Chunsoo is a Level 75 A-rank ice mage and personally confronts Taekyung after deciding that Taekyung's existence has stirred his interest.
- Taekyung's phone contact was Seong Jinho, a thirty-year-old civilian exam candidate and the manager of Taekyung's goshiwon in Bucheon; the two are like sworn brothers.
- Kim Hwajong is a Level 80 mage identified as Butler Kim, and he arrives at the confrontation between Im Chunsoo and Taekyung.

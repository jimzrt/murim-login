# Chapter 385

## Plot

Jin Taekyung trades escalating insults with Wu Heixing after Wu provokes him with anti-Korean slurs. Faye Chen intervenes briefly, and Jin recognizes her as an older S-rank Hunter, Great Cataclysm hero, and former romance-film star.

Jin publicly ridicules Wu's drug scandal, sexual-assault acquittal, and rumored political protection. Prince Felix Alexander Louis and his attendant William then arrive; Felix humiliates Wu while patronizing Jin as lowborn and demanding that Jin kiss his hand.

Magic Johnson jokingly offers to kiss the prince's hand in Jin's place, but Felix withdraws the offer while claiming to oppose outdated etiquette. Wu, enraged by Jin's refusal to defer to him and jealous of Chairman Shao Yang's attention, reaches for his sword before Lee Jungryong arrives with Wei Fenghu.

## Continuity

- Faye Chen is an S-rank Hunter, a Great Cataclysm hero, and a former romance-film lead whose youthful appearance conceals that she is over fifty; Jin has known of her since childhood because his mother is a fan.
- Wu Heixing is an S-rank Hunter notorious for frequent media exposure, anti-Korean hostility, criminal scandals, and arrogant status-conscious behavior; Jin's confrontation leaves Wu humiliated and nearly drawing his sword.
- Prince Felix Alexander Louis is third in line to the British throne and treats Jin as lowborn while presenting himself as an advocate of equality; his attendant William enforces Felix's ceremonial etiquette.
- Lee Jungryong arrives at the underground headquarters alongside Wei Fenghu immediately after Wu Heixing begins reaching for his sword.

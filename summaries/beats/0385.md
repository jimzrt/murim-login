# Chapter 385

## Plot

Jin Taekyung trades insults with Wu Heixing, an S-rank Hunter who provokes him with anti-Korean slurs. Pai Chen de-escalates the exchange, and Taekyung identifies her as a veteran S-rank Hunter, Great Cataclysm hero, and former romance-film actress who appears much younger than her actual age. Taekyung then publicly mocks Wu Heixing's drug and sexual-assault scandals, while Team Leader Choi corrects the details of Wu's drug conviction.

Prince Felix Alexander Louis and his attendant William assert Felix's elaborate British royal titles and etiquette, while Felix condescendingly treats Taekyung as a commoner. Magic Johnson jokes about kissing Felix's hand. Wu Heixing becomes increasingly enraged over losing Chairman Xiao Yang's attention to Taekyung and reaches for his sword, but the bunker door opens before he can draw. Wei Penghu enters with Lee Jungryong, whose arrival immediately captures Taekyung's attention.

## Continuity

- Pai Chen is an S-rank Hunter, a hero of the Great Cataclysm, and a former romance-film star; despite appearing to be in her early twenties, she is over fifty and has lived under a new identity.
- Wu Heixing is an S-rank Hunter with heavy media exposure, a history of drug and sexual-assault scandals, and a rumor that he is the son of a high-ranking Chinese Communist Party official. He antagonizes Jin Taekyung and is jealous of the attention Taekyung receives from Xiao Yang.
- Prince Felix Alexander Louis is a British prince who is third in line to the British throne and holds the titles Duke of Cambridge, Earl of Strathearn, Baron Carrickfergus, Knight of the Garter, and Knight of the Thistle. He regards Taekyung as a commoner while presenting himself as an egalitarian.
- William is Prince Felix's formal attendant and recites Felix's full titles when addressing the gathered Hunters.
- Lee Jungryong has arrived at the temporary operations headquarters with Wei Penghu. Taekyung recognizes him as a man whose apparently middle-aged exterior conceals an old tiger and a cunning snake, and Lee smiles when their eyes meet.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain bangzi and chink as the explicit slurs in the exchange, with the established bangzi footnote.
- Render 파이 첸 as Pai Chen, 우헤이싱 as Wu Heixing, and 필릭스 알렉산더 루이 as Prince Felix Alexander Louis.
- Render Felix's British titles as Duke of Cambridge, Earl of Strathearn, Baron Carrickfergus, Knight of the Garter, and Knight of the Thistle.
- Preserve Taekyung's conversational, self-mocking voice, the offensive insult exchange, and the satire of Felix's ceremonial etiquette and Wu Heixing's scandals.

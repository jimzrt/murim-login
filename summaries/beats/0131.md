# Chapter 131

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin arrive in Taiyuan ahead of the City Lord's luncheon. Mujin's emotional return to his hometown turns into a comic misunderstanding when Taekyung learns that Mujin's parents are healthy, wealthy textile merchants whose enormous shop has branches in Henan and Hebei, and that a younger sibling relieved him of the need to inherit the family business. At Honghwa Inn, they encounter Cheongpung, a dreamy young man with a System Level of ??? and the aura of another Peak master, who politely asks to eat a candied hawthorn skewer.

## Continuity

- Hyuk Mujin returned to Taiyuan for the first time in five years; his parents are alive and own Hyuk Family Textile Shop, the largest textile shop in Taiyuan, with branches in Henan and Hebei.
- Mujin left home to avoid inheriting the family business, but the birth of his youngest sibling later removed that obligation.
- Taekyung recognizes that he has treated Mujin too harshly; their relationship is no longer defined by the ill-fated nature of their first meeting.
- Cheongpung has appeared at Honghwa Inn as an enigmatic young man with a ??? Level and the aura of another Peak master.

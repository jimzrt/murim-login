# Chapter 1031

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang struggle against seven soulless Death Knights, whom the Blood-Sword Demon Lord calls Black Ghosts. Sama Pyo joins the fight and helps create openings, but the Black Ghosts prove difficult to stop. The Zhongnan disciples and Sima Gong’s Gansu martial artists arrive as the opposing armies meet on the snowy plain and begin their battle.

## Continuity

- The Blood-Sword Demon Lord commands an army of tens of thousands and seven soulless Death Knights called Black Ghosts; they are stronger than before and can recover from attacks that appear fatal.
- Jin Taekyung, Jeok Cheongang, and Sama Pyo are fighting the Black Ghosts as the armies led by the Blood-Sword Demon Lord and the gathered Murim forces collide.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 흑귀 as “Black Ghosts,” the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s name for the Death Knights.

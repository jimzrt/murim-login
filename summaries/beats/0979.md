# Chapter 979

## Plot

The battered Hebei Peng warriors rally when the Jin Family of Taiyuan arrives with reinforcements. Jin Taekyung throws the heads of Murong Baek and Jamukha before the opposing army, while Jin Wikyung offers Temur a chance to surrender, promising to spare lives but vowing to drive the nomads from the steppe if they refuse. Temur submits to Jin Wikyung as his lord; the fighting against the Murong Family resumes.

In a memory, Jin Mukyung sees himself as a young child practicing swordsmanship after his mother died in childbirth. His father, the Family Head, dismisses the retainers’ self-blame and quietly apologizes to Mukyung before the memory collapses and a familiar voice asks if he is awake.

## Continuity

- Temur submits to Jin Wikyung and the Jin Family of Taiyuan; the battle against the Murong Family resumes.
- Jin Mukyung’s memory shows his father grieving his wife’s death in childbirth and apologizing to Mukyung; Mukyung recognizes him as his father.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 모용위진 as Murong Wijin.
- Record Temur’s deferential address to Jin Wikyung as “my lord,” and Jin Wikyung’s formal address to Temur as “you.”

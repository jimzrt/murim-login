# Chapter 162

## Plot

An apparently senile old man enters the Northern Gaoyuan inn where Black Sand, the Human Butcher, Temur, and Chinggen are gathered. After provoking the Human Butcher by insulting him and eating from the leaders' feast, the old man survives the Human Butcher's furious Sword Energy attack, effortlessly defeats him with a chicken bone, reveals himself as a Supreme Peak master, and kills the Human Butcher with a single strike before asking where he is.

## Continuity

- An unnamed old man entered the Northern Gaoyuan gathering and concealed Supreme Peak mastery beneath an apparently feeble, senile demeanor.
- The old man defeated the Human Butcher's Sword Energy with a chicken bone and killed him with a single painless strike; the Human Butcher is dead.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 참마검 as “horse-chopping sword” and preserve the established renderings of 태산압정, 검기, 전음, 절정 고수, and 초절정 고수.
- Render the old man's speech as abrasive, vulgar, abrupt, and teasing; preserve the 하오체–하오문 wordplay with a brief explanation.

# Chapter 74

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung meet Jin Wikyung in his newly organized office. Exhausted from managing the Jin Family's immense workload, Wikyung embraces both brothers despite Mukyung's resistance and jokingly considers putting Taekyung to work.

Jin Wikyung orders Taekyung and Mukyung to visit the Mount Heng Sword Sect at the personal request of its new Sect Leader, Lee Seowol. Taekyung receives the forcibly created Quest [Yesterday's Enemy, Today's Ally], which requires him to deliver an invitation to the Jin Family for the coming New Year's Day; its Grade is First Rate, its Reward is unknown, and it has no failure penalty.

Lee Seowol is revealed to be Lee Cheonbaek's third child and the younger sister of the deceased Young Sect Leader and Lee Seogeun. Mukyung agrees to go after learning that Seowol may reveal some of the Mount Heng Sword Sect's Peak martial arts. They depart immediately for Eung-hyeon in a four-horse carriage, with Hyuk Mujin driving after Mukyung dismissed the attendants. Taekyung pretends to circulate his qi while preparing to log out, and the System displays another logout prompt.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung's office has been restored to an orderly state, but he remains severely overworked and wants at least one day of rest.
- The new Sect Leader of the Mount Heng Sword Sect is Lee Seowol, a woman and Lee Cheonbaek's third child; she is the younger sister of the deceased Young Sect Leader and Lee Seogeun.
- Lee Seowol personally requested that Jin Taekyung and Jin Mukyung be sent to the Mount Heng Sword Sect.
- Taekyung has been given the forced Quest [Yesterday's Enemy, Today's Ally]: invite the Mount Heng Sword Sect to the Jin Family of Taiyuan for the coming New Year's Day. The mission remains incomplete, the Reward is unknown, and Failure is listed as None.
- Jin Mukyung accepted the journey after learning that the Mount Heng Sword Sect would consider revealing some of its Peak martial arts to him.
- Taekyung, Mukyung, and Hyuk Mujin have departed for Eung-hyeon, where the Mount Heng Sword Sect is located; the journey is expected to take approximately three days.
- Hyuk Mujin remained with the group because he obeys Taekyung as his squad leader. The regular attendants and coachman were dismissed before departure.
- Taekyung intends to log out and wants to test his newly refined martial arts in a Gate after returning.

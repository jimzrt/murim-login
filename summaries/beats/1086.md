# Chapter 1086

## Plot

Mae Jonghak reveals that a long-prepared opportunity to strike back against Dark Heaven has arrived. He gives Zhuge Feng Song Ho’s secret list of embedded spies, allies, and meeting locations, then leaves; Zhuge Feng prepares the Zhuge Clan to evacuate immediately and urges Jin Taekyung to hold on in the west.

## Continuity

- Mae Jonghak and the New Murim Alliance have prepared a coordinated operation against Dark Heaven; Zhuge Feng is tasked with carrying it out using Song Ho’s intelligence and selected allies.
- Zhuge Feng orders the Zhuge Clan to leave its current home immediately.
- Mae Jonghak leaves the Zhuge Clan garden after entrusting the operation to Zhuge Feng; his destination is not stated.

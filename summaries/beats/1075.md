# Chapter 1075

## Plot

Taekyung reunites warmly with Gung Gibang at the lakeside camp, where the Beggars’ Sect has prepared fires and rations for the exhausted allies. The Slaughter Saint reveals that he and Cheongpung arrived in Qinghai about a month ago and secretly investigated possible spies before Dark Heaven’s attack forced them to hide among the enemy. They found no spies they could identify, but could not reach Taiqing Hall because of a strange, chilling presence around it; Taekyung suspects it was magical power.

## Continuity

- Gung Gibang and the Beggars’ Sect have prepared a lakeside camp with fires and dry rations for Taekyung’s group.
- The Slaughter Saint and Cheongpung secretly arrived in Qinghai about a month ago to investigate possible spies; they identified none, but do not consider the matter settled.
- During Dark Heaven’s attack, the Slaughter Saint and Cheongpung hid among the enemy; Cheongpung learned to conceal his presence under the Slaughter Saint’s instruction.
- A strange, chilling presence prevented the Slaughter Saint and Cheongpung from reaching Taiqing Hall; Taekyung suspects magical power.

## Translation Decisions

- Use “magical power” for 마력.
- Use “concealment technique” for 은잠술.

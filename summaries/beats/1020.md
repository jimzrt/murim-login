# Chapter 1020

## Plot

A red emergency flare from the scouts prompts the allied force to prepare for battle. Jin Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang rush ahead and learn that Dark Heaven has taken Dunhuang. A messenger reports that the Jade Gate Pass was breached, at least half of the ten-thousand-strong defensive force was killed, wounded, or missing, and the Kongtong Sect lost about thirty percent of its strength, including two Elders and the entire Demon-Subduing Squad. The Kongtong Sect Leader escaped, but his whereabouts and the fate of the other survivors are unknown. Believing Dark Heaven is advancing rapidly toward the Great Snow Mountain, Taekyung orders all forces in the Qilian Mountains mobilized, overruling Sima Gong’s earlier decision by invoking his authority as Marquis of Shangshan.

## Continuity

- Dark Heaven has captured Dunhuang after breaching the Jade Gate Pass; the fall occurred about three days before the report reached the leadership.
- The Dunhuang defense suffered catastrophic losses: at least two thousand dead and three thousand seriously wounded or missing; the Kongtong Sect lost about thirty percent of its strength, including two Elders and all one hundred members of the Demon-Subduing Squad.
- The Kongtong Sect Leader escaped Dunhuang with surviving troops, but his location and the fate of the other survivors remain unknown.
- Jin Taekyung orders all forces stationed in the Qilian Mountains mobilized, overruling Sima Gong’s prior conclusion that they should not be fully deployed.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 玉門關 as “Jade Gate Pass,” 武威劍門 as “Martial Might Sword Sect,” 伏魔隊 as “Demon-Subduing Squad,” and 真人 as “Perfected One.”

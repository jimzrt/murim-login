# Chapter 488

## Plot

The Water God Dragon is a sacred spirit creature far more powerful than ordinary spirit beasts and absorbed all the Gate's mana; after its mutated rampage, Dongting Lake has returned to calm. Taekyung is led toward a secluded area by Mungyeong, whose hidden identity and intentions make him increasingly uneasy.

Seven Wudang Peak masters stop Taekyung and Mungyeong at a restricted area after the hour of the Dog. The middle-aged Daoist recognizes Taekyung as the Blazing Flame Divine Dragon and allows them through after Taekyung awkwardly pretends that he and Mungyeong are close friends; Mungyeong uses Sound Transmission to direct and threaten him while secretly eavesdropping on the guards.

Once the witnesses leave and a qi curtain seals off the area, Mungyeong's harmless medical-apprentice persona gives way to the true Slaughter Saint, the greatest assassin in history. He reveals that he must pass down secret martial arts to Taekyung, leaving Taekyung stunned by the revelation.

## Continuity

- The Water God Dragon is a sacred spirit creature with power beyond ordinary spirit beasts and absorbed all the Gate's mana.
- Dongting Lake is calm again after the Water God Dragon's mutated state caused thunder and waves to nearly overturn it.
- Mungyeong's young medical-apprentice persona conceals his true identity as the Slaughter Saint, the greatest assassin in history; he can conceal countless blades and eavesdrop on others' Sound Transmissions.
- Mungyeong intends to teach Jin Taekyung secret martial arts, following Jeok Cheongang's request that he look after and instruct Taekyung.

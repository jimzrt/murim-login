# Chapter 431

## Plot

Jin confronts Go Jun in the ruins and admits that he killed Lee Jungryong and Wu Heixing. After overpowering Go Jun, exposing three hidden Hunters, and severely mutilating him, Jin leaves him alive and demands that the conflict end with Lee Jungryong. Go Jun gives a faint nod, and Jin heals him with potions before Magic Johnson reveals himself and says they have much to discuss.

## Continuity

- Go Jun survives Jin's retaliation for Lee Jungryong's death but is left grievously mutilated and terrified.
- Jin deliberately spares Go Jun because another disappearance would strengthen suspicions that Jin killed Lee Jungryong and Wu Heixing.
- Go Jun accepts Jin's demand that Ares Guild's retaliation end with Lee Jungryong.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve Jin's dry, profane, and brutally threatening voice during his confrontation with Go Jun.
- Render 내가중수법 as Inner-Family Heavy Hand, 아혈 as Mute Acupoint, and 수혈 as Sleep Acupoint.
- Preserve the Skeleton King's comic interruption and render his final question as “What about the club?”

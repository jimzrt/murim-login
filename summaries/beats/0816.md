# Chapter 816

## Plot

The Doppelganger repeatedly resurrects while Jin Taekyung kills it, but its supply of absorbed lives is finite and has dwindled to less than half. It realizes Jin is the Chosen One its master warned it to avoid, tries to flee, and is cut down again; after taking on Siegfried Bassman’s face and drawing out vast mana, it prepares to fight back.

## Continuity

- The Doppelganger has died more than two hundred times in under ten minutes; it resurrects using absorbed lives, and fewer than half remain.
- The Doppelganger says it is the last survivor of its species after a purge in the Demon Realm; it is called the Final Abyss.
- The Doppelganger served a master who sent it to this world and ordered it to avoid the target until its plan was complete; the master called Jin Taekyung the Chosen One.
- The Doppelganger regarded Michael Silbert as a subordinate and disposable tool.
- The Doppelganger has taken on Siegfried Bassman’s face and gathered vast mana; Jin recognizes the face, but the chapter does not establish what happens next.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 소멸 as “Erasure,” distinct from ordinary death.

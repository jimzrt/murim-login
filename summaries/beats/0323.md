# Chapter 323

## Plot

Hwangbo Eom attacks Jin Taekyung with Sword Force inside Tengwang Pavilion, but Taekyung dodges through his physically transcendent abilities and protects Ju Hwaran. Hwangbo orders the Taeeul Sword Unit to seal the exits; Hyuk Sopyung initially resists, but obeys after Hwangbo produces the Sect Leader's Command Token. Hwangbo reveals that he knows Jeok Cheongang's illness is serious and threatens to settle his bad blood with the Fire Gate Clan. Taekyung orders everyone to block the entrance and challenges Hwangbo, beginning a fierce duel in which he counters the Heavenly River Thirty-Six Swords and Taeeul Light-Dividing Sword with the Fire Dragon Divine Spear; the clash remains unresolved as Heavenly Strike unleashes a pillar of hellfire.

## Continuity

- Taekyung's physical abilities have reached a realm beyond humanity even though he has not gained the enlightenment required for the Supreme Peak realm.
- Hwangbo Eom knows that Jeok Cheongang's illness is serious and has openly threatened Taekyung and the Fire Gate Clan.
- Hwangbo Eom attacked Taekyung inside Tengwang Pavilion and is now fighting him with Supreme Peak martial arts.
- Hyuk Sopyung and the thirty-member Taeeul Sword Unit are blocking Tengwang Pavilion's entrance under the Sect Leader's Command Token.
- Taekyung has ordered Baek Museong, Cheongpung, Gung Gibang, Heukgeol, Wolhwa, Hyuk Mujin, Ju Hwaran, and the other attendees to prevent anyone from leaving.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious beneath leather on a pack frame at Tengwang Pavilion.

## Translation Decisions

- Use Taeeul Light-Dividing Sword for 태을분광검, Fire Dragon's Single Tail for 화룡일미, and Heavenly Strike for 천격.
- Render 초절정 고수(물리) as “Supreme Peak master—physically?” to preserve Taekyung's joke about his abnormal physical power.

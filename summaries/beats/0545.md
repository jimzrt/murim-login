# Chapter 545

## Plot

Jin Taekyung names the Fire Dragon Pavilion, completing its Quest requirements and formally establishing it. The System grants him the unique Title Fire Dragon Pavilion Master, the rare achievement Murim Alliance Civil Servant with a Sword Rice Bowl, substantial Fame and EXP, and a Level Up; his Fame surpasses 10,000, strengthening some Title effects and greatly increasing Charm and Intimidation.

Jeok Cheongang has been placed in the Five Kings Hall, an organization containing five of the Ten Kings, with Jeok occupying its chief seat. He resents the appointment, but counsels Taekyung to advance while protecting his family, companions, subordinates, and Master, reminding him that he is also someone others want to protect. Later, Taekyung and Cheongpung attend a major meeting in the Alliance Leader's Hall, where the gathered masters react to their reputations and the Alliance Leader is announced.

## Continuity

- The Fire Dragon Pavilion is formally established under the Alliance Leader, with Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung as its two pavilion masters and Ju Hwaran, Song Ilseom, Sama Pyo, Taishan, and Hyuk Mujin as members.
- Jin Taekyung holds the unique Title Fire Dragon Pavilion Master; his total Fame has surpassed 10,000, some Title effects have strengthened, Charm and Intimidation have greatly increased, and he has leveled up.
- The Five Kings Hall contains Thunderbolt Saber King Peng Cheolhu, Azure Sky Sword King Nangong Cheon, Seafaring King Pa Ryun, Fist King Yan Hwapyeong, and Fire King Jeok Cheongang, with Jeok in the chief seat.
- Taekyung intends to keep advancing toward the coming war while protecting his blood relatives, companions, subordinates, and Master rather than abandoning what he has gained.
- Jeok Cheongang warns Taekyung to think carefully, return alive even against an apparently unbeatable opponent, and remember that others want to protect him.
- The Alliance Leader's Hall is hosting a major gathering of Murim's supporting figures, and the Alliance Leader is entering as Taekyung and Cheongpung take their places.

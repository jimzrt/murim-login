# Chapter 800

## Plot

Recovery Hunters find more than two hundred bodies from the J1 attack dried out like centuries-old mummies, despite having been dead for less than two hours. Rumors about the corpses spread among the gathered forces, and fear threatens morale.

The Skeleton King says he remembers only a dark, ominous place before regaining consciousness in a Gate and knows little about the Demon Realm or the Prophet. Magic Johnson confirms that the Prophet's ability is unprecedented and suggests that, if the Prophet is a monster, it may be an unidentified top-level Named monster. Taekyung orders an immediate departure to pursue the Prophet using the message sent through Yamamoto, even if the trail leads into a trap.

## Continuity

- More than two hundred J1-related bodies were found bloodless and severely desiccated less than two hours after death; rumors about them are spreading and eroding Hunter morale.
- The Skeleton King's pre-Gate memories are limited to a dark, ominous place; he cannot identify the Prophet.
- Magic Johnson has never encountered the Prophet's kind of ability and considers the Prophet a possible unidentified top-level Named monster if it is a monster.
- Taekyung is setting out immediately to pursue the Prophet by following the message sent through Yamamoto, despite the possibility of a trap.

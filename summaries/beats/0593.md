# Chapter 593

## Plot

Go Jun's absorbed S-grade Magic Gem power overwhelms Jin, but Go Jun's threats against Jin's family and allies provoke Jin's full resolve. Jin stores and resummons White Flame to land the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist, follows with a crushing hand strike, and continues fighting despite severe injuries. As Go Jun's corrupted power is identified as demonic energy, Jin uses Striking Second, Hitting First to pierce his chest with White Flame, then summons Fire Dragon Armor to block Go Jun's final counterattack and drives the spear deeper.

## Continuity

- Go Jun's absorbed S-grade Magic Gem has given him power beyond Jin's expectations, allowing him to wield enormous Aura Blades and corrupted demonic energy.
- Go Jun threatened to kill Jin's family, Peace Guild allies, and anyone connected to Jin; Jin responded by committing himself to Go Jun's death.
- Jin wounded Go Jun with the Flame-Extinguishing Divine Fist and White Flame, but Go Jun remains capable of launching lethal counterattacks.
- Fire Dragon Armor blocked Go Jun's counterattack aimed at Jin's heart, and Jin is pressing the spear embedded in Go Jun's chest.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 마력 as demonic energy, distinct from mana and 마법 rendered as Magic.
- Retain Striking Second, Hitting First for 후발선제 and Aura Blade for 오러 블레이드.

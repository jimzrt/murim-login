# Chapter 602

## Plot

Team Leader Choi pressures the remaining Ares Guild executives by implying that he knows their secrets, openly declaring his intention to take over the Guild legally, and promising sweeping personnel changes and promotions once the crisis ends.

When Director Kim resists, Jin Taekyung reinforces Choi's threat by pretending he is about to use violence, while Choi and Jin jokingly discuss the possible legal consequences. The assembled executives recognize the danger and gradually side with Choi.

Vice President Park Daewon formally supports Choi's return, apologizes for having ignored him, and announces that he will convene an official board meeting. With more than thirty retainers now supporting him, Choi is effectively recognized as Ares Guild's next City Lord.

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo has openly declared that he intends to make Ares Guild his own through a legal process with the executives' consent.
- Choi Minwoo has secured the support of the assembled Ares Guild executives by combining intimidation with promises of stability, appointments, and promotions.
- Park Daewon supports Choi Minwoo's return to Ares Guild, plans to convene the official board meeting, and intends to retire after completing that task.
- Jin Taekyung is actively supporting Choi Minwoo's campaign and uses his reputation for violent action to strengthen Choi's pressure on the executives.
- Choi Minwoo's managerial ability, political skill, and role in raising Peace Guild support his claim to lead Ares Guild, alongside his status as Cheon Taemin's only blood descendant.
- Cheon Taemin remains unconscious after more than twenty years and hidden in a second secret area within Ares Guild's Area A.
- The exact location and access method of Cheon Taemin's hidden area remain unknown to Jin Taekyung, Choi Minwoo, and Go Se-won.
- Lee Jungryong and Song Cheonwoo concealed Cheon Taemin's condition, while Choi Minwoo continues pursuing control of Ares Guild to secure him.
- Choi Minwoo has publicly identified Cheon Taemin as his maternal grandfather and is now positioned to begin a formal takeover of Ares Guild.

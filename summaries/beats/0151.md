# Chapter 151

## Plot

Jin Taekyung inspects the newly rebuilt pavilion and private training ground, then stops Hyuk Mujin from leaving and invites him to train alongside Taekyung and Cheongpung. After Taekyung persuades him to set aside his insecurity and accept the opportunity, Mujin agrees. Cheongpung begins teaching them using the training method Mae Jonghak used on him, which turns out to involve reaching the summit of a cliff hundreds of jang high after Cheongpung recalls nearly dying there twice as a child.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung now has a private training ground in a newly rebuilt pavilion at the Jin Family of Taiyuan.
- Hyuk Mujin accepted Taekyung's invitation to train with Taekyung and Cheongpung, seeking to become stronger while pursuing his goal of becoming Master of the Gatekeeper Pavilion.
- Cheongpung has begun teaching Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin with the childhood training method used by Mae Jonghak: climbing a very high cliff after running to the training hall.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 연무장 as “training ground,” 수련동 as “training hall,” 청석 as “bluestone,” and 십팔반병기 as “eighteen traditional weapons.”
- Render 찍고 땡 as “touch-and-go method.”

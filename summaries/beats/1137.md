# Chapter 1137

## Plot

Seven days after the battle, Mae Jonghak and the Slaughter Saint discuss the failed search for an unnamed target and the growing coalition preparing to march west toward Xinjiang. Mae says the search will continue with fewer people assigned to it; defeating the Lord of Heaven is the only way to stop the conflict.

The Slaughter Saint remains troubled by the Bow Saint’s conduct when Jin Taekyung was in mortal danger and by an ominous feeling tied to the Son of Heaven. Taishan bursts in with news that the Pavilion Master has awakened.

## Continuity

- Seven days after the battle, Jin Taekyung has awakened; the Slaughter Saint and Mae Jonghak had believed he was still unconscious.
- The search for an unnamed target has not succeeded; it will continue with reduced manpower.
- The Imperial House, the Murim Alliance, martial artists, and volunteer militias are beginning a westward advance toward Xinjiang, united against the Lord of Heaven.
- The Slaughter Saint suspects the Bow Saint concealed another motive when Jin Taekyung was in mortal danger; she has been difficult to find since the battle.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 滅魔正天 as “Exterminate the Demons and Set Heaven Right.”
- Retain “Great Ming” for 大明 and “Lord of Heaven” for 天主.

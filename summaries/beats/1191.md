# Chapter 1191

## Plot

Jin Taekyung recognizes the Tianshan Mountains’ magical power as resembling Morgoth’s Dragon Lair, then uses the Mind’s Eye to cut through the hidden barriers between spaces. The effort causes severe harm and status effects, but the System reports that mutated Gates Forest of Death-II through -VI have been destroyed, awards him the Door Opener title, and restores his injuries and fatigue after he levels up.

The separated companions emerge through the rifts: Hyuk Mujin, Gung Gibang, Song Ilseom, Ju Hwaran, Cheongpung, the Slaughter Saint, and the Bow Saint. The Great Sir does not appear. The Bow Saint briefly shows intense emotion, the cause of which is not established.

## Continuity

- Mutated Gates Forest of Death-II through -VI were destroyed when Taekyung cut through the hidden barriers; Taekyung gained the Door Opener title and leveled up, restoring his injuries and fatigue.
- The separated companions reappeared, but the Great Sir was absent after the final rift closed; his whereabouts remain unknown.
- Taekyung activated the Mind’s Eye to perceive and cut through hidden spaces. Its use caused severe physical harm and carries a System warning of potentially fatal consequences; his insight into the ability deepened.
- The Bow Saint briefly displayed intense emotion after emerging from her rift; its cause is unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 심안 as “Mind’s Eye,” 변이 게이트 as “mutated Gate,” and 문을 여는 자 as “Door Opener.”

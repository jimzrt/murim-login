# Chapter 336

## Plot

Jin Taekyung presents Mae Jonghak’s letter to Tang Sadok, who learns that Jin Baekyang has died and reads the Sword Saint’s account of Jeok Cheongang’s condition and the search for the Divine Physician.

Tang Sadok refuses to let Taekyung meet Poison King Tang Taesang or provide direct assistance, offering only guides to the Qingcheng Sect and Emei Sect. Cheongpung appeals to human compassion, while Taekyung warns that refusing help will establish an unpleasant debt of gratitude and grudges between him and the Sichuan Tang Clan.

Tang Sadok reveals that the Sichuan Tang Clan’s emergency mobilization is a response to Tang Taesang’s murder. An unidentified middle-aged man is shown after killing Tang Taesang; poison remains in one arm, which he amputates before leaving the cave.

## Continuity

- Tang Sadok is the current Family Head of the Sichuan Tang Clan and refuses direct aid in treating Jeok Cheongang because the clan is mobilized against those responsible for Tang Taesang’s murder.
- Poison King Tang Taesang, Tang Sadok’s father and the former Family Head, is dead; his body was found with none of his limbs intact.
- The unidentified middle-aged man who killed Tang Taesang survived severe poison exposure by amputating his left forearm, but his identity and unfinished objective remain unknown.
- The Sichuan Tang Clan will provide Taekyung’s party only several guides to the Qingcheng Sect and Emei Sect while withholding further assistance.
- Taekyung’s party remains inside the Sichuan Tang Clan’s Family Head’s Hall, and the clan’s heightened defenses and weapons preparations are responses to Tang Taesang’s death.
- Jeok Cheongang remains unconscious and has at most roughly half a year to live without treatment; the Divine Physician remains the party’s best hope.

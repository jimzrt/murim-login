# Chapter 260

## Plot

Cheongpung awakens after Hyuk Mujin revives him and learns that Hong Dao has been killed and Jongni Chu is blamed. Although he remembers Jongni Chu's overwhelming attack and the fear it caused him, Cheongpung insists that Jongni Chu is his friend and immediately leaves for Mount Song.

Shaolin Temple is under attack by hundreds of masked warriors who invoke the Lord of Heaven and slaughter monks and pilgrims. Hongcheon, Hong Dao's youngest Junior Brother and a Supreme Peak master, defends the temple until Flame Tiger and Han Su arrive. The two reveal themselves as the Yin-Yang Twin Freaks, confirm Hong Dao's death, and advance toward the Face-Wall Cave, where Unnamed and the Green Jade Buddha Staff are located.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung remembers Jongni Chu's attack, including his own helplessness and fear, but still believes Jongni Chu is his friend.
- Cheongpung has left ahead of the others for Mount Song and Shaolin after learning that Hong Dao was killed and Shaolin is in danger.
- Shaolin Temple is being invaded by hundreds of masked warriors who obey commands attributed to the Lord of Heaven and show no pain, fear, or hesitation.
- Hongcheon is Hong Dao's youngest Junior Brother and a Supreme Peak Shaolin master whose martial prowess has surpassed Hong Dao's; he remains at Shaolin to defend it.
- Flame Tiger and Han Su are the Yin-Yang Twin Freaks, notorious former demonic fiends who served the Demonic Cult during the Great Faction War.
- Flame Tiger engages Hongcheon while Han Su proceeds toward the Face-Wall Cave, where Unnamed is located and the Green Jade Buddha Staff is believed to be.

# Chapter 1143

## Plot

After waking, Jin Taekyung finds the pocket watch he had kept in his Inventory around Jeok Cheongang’s neck. Jeok says he found it where Taekyung collapsed ten days earlier. Holding it brings back the memory that the Helper gave it to him as a final gift. Its System description changes from a Common Item to a Special Item, and Hyuk Mujin drunkenly says its time differs from when he last saw it.

## Continuity

- The Helper’s final gift to Taekyung was the pocket watch of unknown make; it was absent from Taekyung’s Inventory without his knowledge and its item classification changed after he held it.
- Hyuk Mujin recognized the pocket watch and said it showed a different time from the last time he saw it.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 회중시계 as “pocket watch” and 누가 만들었는지 모를 회중시계 as “Pocket Watch of Unknown Make.”

# Chapter 1146

## Plot

In a buried Gulf War shelter beneath the desert, Choi Minwoo learns that thousands of enemies are approaching. Though waiting could keep his group safe, he struggles with the prospect of leaving others to face the attackers. The Skeleton King reveals that the Hunters are already ready to fight, trusting Minwoo to make the same choice. Minwoo draws his sword and cuts through the shelter wall to lead them out. As the Skeleton King charges toward the enemy, an unexpected voice says, “Dad’s not sleeping.”

## Continuity

- Choi Minwoo is commanding a group of Hunters in a buried desert shelter; thousands of enemies are approaching, and he chooses to lead the Hunters into the fight rather than wait safely underground.
- The Skeleton King is with Choi Minwoo’s group, and the Hunters trust Minwoo to lead them into battle.
- Choi Minwoo regards Jin Taekyung as someone who takes responsibility for his actions, even in a crisis.

# Chapter 154

## Plot

Over three days, Jin Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin complete Cheongpung's ten cliff climbs under increasingly dangerous rock-throwing training. The quest activates Wall Lizard Technique, grants a Level Up with 10 Stat Points and 10 Skill Points, and upgrades Beginner Trainee to Intermediate Trainee; Cheongpung's delight generates the linked Quest Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2. Taekyung jokingly tells Mujin that he came from another world and has a System, but Mujin dismisses the claim as unbelievable. New Year's Day is ten days away.

## Continuity

- The three-day Wall Lizard Technique training is complete: Jin Taekyung and Hyuk Mujin climbed the cliff ten times, and Taekyung acquired Wall Lizard Technique, gained 10 Stat Points and 10 Skill Points, and had Beginner Trainee upgraded to Intermediate Trainee.
- Cheongpung completed comparable childhood training in fifteen days; at age ten he routinely climbed and fell from Falling Goose Peak on Huashan, which exceeds five hundred jang, though he could not climb it fully until age eighteen.
- Cheongpung's successful training generates the linked Quest Sword Saint Training: A Secondhand Experience—2; Taekyung expects the linked quest chain to continue.
- Hyuk Mujin has demonstrated substantial persistence and martial talent during the cliff training.
- Taekyung told Hyuk Mujin, as a deliberately unbelievable joke, that he came from another world where monsters and a System exist; Mujin did not believe him.

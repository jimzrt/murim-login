# Chapter 1178

## Plot

The Slaughter Saint, Hyuk Mujin, and Cheongpung each admit how Jin Taekyung’s secret spread among the group. Taekyung reassures Ju Hwaran that he is human, around twenty-eight, and from a place where people live; she accepts his explanation. Jeok Cheongang says Great Sir has not been told and leaves that decision to Taekyung, but warns that Bow Saint may have difficulty accepting the truth.

Alone in the desert, Bow Saint reflects on Taekyung’s secret and questions whether the Martial God’s letter truly expresses what he wants. A faint sound comes from behind her.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang, the Slaughter Saint, Hyuk Mujin, Cheongpung, and Ju Hwaran know that Taekyung comes from the realm of immortals; Taekyung has explained that he is human and around twenty-eight.
- Great Sir has not been told Taekyung’s secret. Jeok Cheongang leaves the decision to Taekyung.
- Bow Saint has learned Taekyung’s secret and questions whether the Martial God’s letter is truly right; a faint sound comes from behind her in the desert.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 선계 as “the realm of immortals” and 신선 as “immortal”; distinguish these from 우화등선, “ascending to immortality.”

# Chapter 374

## Plot

Jin Wikyung visits the Sichuan Tang Clan and urges acting Family Head Tang Horyong to relocate the clan to Henan, Shaanxi, or Shanxi, promising support from orthodox Murim as a greater war approaches. Horyong does not make a final relocation decision.

Jin Wikyung reunites with Taekyung and confirms that he must escort Samgoe back to Henan. Their departure is arranged for within one shichen, but Tang Sadok awakens at the chapter's end, delaying the planned departure from half a shichen to one shichen.

## Continuity

- Jin Wikyung is the Lesser Family Head of the Jin Family of Taiyuan, the leader who unified Shanxi Murim, and a wealthy landowner who has offered the Sichuan Tang Clan land and orthodox support.
- Tang Horyong is the acting Family Head of the Sichuan Tang Clan and the Pavilion Master of the Poison Dragon Pavilion; he is burdened by the clan's devastation and uncertain about leading it without Tang Sadok.
- Tang Sadok, the Poison King and Horyong's older cousin, awakens at the end of Chapter 374 after remaining unconscious since the Dark Heaven attack.
- Mae Jonghak, the Sword Saint, has warned that a war greater than the Great Faction War will soon break out; orthodox Murim is rallying in Henan and preparing the new Murim Alliance.
- Jin Wikyung has been tasked with escorting Samgoe, one of the principal culprits behind the Three-Sect Bloodbath, back to Henan; Samgoe is bound under guard and repeatedly attempts suicide after his testicles were crushed.
- Taekyung expects to return to his original world soon; approximately six hours have passed there while he has spent about two months in Murim.
- The item previously shown in Taekyung's Inventory is identified as the Myriad Poison Ring, which Taekyung considers taking before leaving.

# Chapter 463

## Plot

In a hidden cave, Fire King Jeok Cheongang reaches Five Qi Returning to Origin and Furnace Fire Pure Blue after more than a century of cultivation, but remains unable to cross the next barrier and recognizes his desire for Returned to Youth as greed rooted in unresolved inner demons. He and Zhuge Feng discuss Dark Heaven's resurgence and the troubled era approaching.

Mungyeong reveals that he is the Slaughter Saint and has reached Returned to Youth. He warns Jeok Cheongang that his disciple, and perhaps everyone else, is in danger; Jeok immediately orders Zhuge Feng to launch the boat.

At the final suspected refuge of the Dongting Fisherman, Jin Taekyung sends the others onto land and dives into Dongting Lake using the Water Rescue Worker Title. He swims deeper through the lake and discovers a pitch-black cave.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang has achieved Five Qi Returning to Origin and Furnace Fire Pure Blue, but cannot currently advance beyond that barrier.
- Jeok Cheongang believes his unresolved inner demons and accumulated resentment caused his infirmities of old age, while his second Disciple helped preserve his sanity.
- Mungyeong is the Slaughter Saint, has reached Returned to Youth, and has now revealed his identity to Jeok Cheongang and Zhuge Feng.
- Mungyeong warned that Jin Taekyung is in danger and that everyone may be threatened; Jeok Cheongang ordered an immediate boat launch.
- Jin Taekyung reached the final suspected Dongting Fisherman site, entered the lake alone, and found a black underwater cave.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 오기조원 as Five Qi Returning to Origin, 노화순청 as Furnace Fire Pure Blue, and 반로환동 as Returned to Youth.
- Render 살성 as Slaughter Saint and 복자 as diviner.
- Render 천령폭 as Tianling Falls and preserve Jeok Cheongang's gruff, profane, mock-complaining voice alongside Taekyung's dry profanity.
- Preserve established renderings for Dark Heaven, the Great Faction War, the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, and the Water Rescue Worker Title.

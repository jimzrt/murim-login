# Chapter 333

## Plot

Mungyeong, a young Level 6 medical apprentice whose parents were killed by Hwang Tae-gu and whose life Mu Song once saved, asks Mu Song to spare Hwang and punish him under League law rather than kill him. Mu Song destroys Hwang's dantian and immobilizes him for transport to League headquarters; Mungyeong's earlier tactics saved all twenty passengers, and after learning that Mu Song is sailing toward Xi'an, he boards the ship.

Mungyeong reveals that he saved the passengers by turning the boat into an uncontrollable drifting obstacle, using deck boards as shields and breaking the mast and sails to buy time against the river-bandits.

## Continuity

- Mungyeong is a Level 6 medical apprentice from a humble military household; his parents were killed on the Yangtze more than ten years ago, and Mu Song saved him at that time.
- Mungyeong interrupted Mu Song's execution of Hwang Tae-gu, requested that Hwang live, and asked that his martial arts be crippled and that he be punished under Yangtze River Channel League law.
- Mu Song destroyed Hwang Tae-gu's dantian and martial arts, immobilized him with Pressure-Point Strike, and ordered that he be transported to the League headquarters for punishment.
- All twenty passengers attacked by Hwang Tae-gu's river-bandits survived, though some were injured; Mungyeong's improvised naval tactics were chiefly responsible for buying them time.
- Mungyeong has boarded Mu Song's ship while traveling toward Xi'an.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 문경 as Mungyeong, 의생 as medical apprentice, 인의대협 as Great Hero of Benevolence and Righteousness, 맹규 as League regulations, 본단 as League headquarters, and 기해혈 as qi-sea acupoint.
- Preserve the distinction between destroying Hwang Tae-gu's dantian and killing him; he remains alive but has lost his martial arts.
- Render Mungyeong's elaborate promise to repay the favor as a Jeong Mong-ju allusion and retain the explanatory footnote.

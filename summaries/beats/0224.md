# Chapter 224

## Plot

Won Myunghoon stopped the returning Star Guild members and ordered them to surround the only route to the Gate entrance, intending to eliminate any surviving Peace Guild member before the Star Guild's involvement could be exposed. He dismissed Fear-stricken Kim Jonghun as useless but ordered him bound as bait because Jonghun knew Won's secrets and had personally acquired Yeti's Necklace.

Taekyung and Butler Kim reached the ambush and called the Star Guild out. Butler Kim healed his injured knee with Heal; after Won declared that they had come to kill them all, Taekyung broke Won's arm, disarmed his spear, and punched him while repeating his warning not to bare his teeth again.

## Continuity

- Won Myunghoon considers the Star Guild members compromised accomplices: they accepted his money and are bound to the consequences of the eight-year-old incident.
- Won Myunghoon ordered Kim Jonghun bound rather than killed, intending to use him as prey for the Named Monster because Jonghun knows Won's secrets and is no longer useful as a Hunter after Fear.
- Taekyung and Butler Kim reached the Star Guild's ambush; Butler Kim healed his knee with Heal, and Taekyung broke Won's arm, took his spear, and punched him after Won declared his intent to kill the Peace Guild survivors.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep Won's address to Taekyung hostile rather than restoring the previously withdrawn hyung relationship; render 원명훈 씨 as Mr. Won Myunghoon before the following profanity.
- Render 개보린 as Dogvorin and explain in a footnote that it is Taekyung's dog-themed pun on Gevorin, a Korean painkiller.
- Preserve Won Myunghoon's coercive and murderous register, rendering 떨거지 as losers, 폐품 as junk, and 먹잇감 as prey.

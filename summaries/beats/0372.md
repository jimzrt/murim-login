# Chapter 372

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and the others reach a hidden cavern near Chengdu whose entrance is concealed by an Illusion Formation. Inside, they find supplies, weapons, and a vast formation covering the floor. The formation is unidentified until Samgoe's testimony reveals that Dark Heaven called it a Transportation Formation and used it to move hundreds of people across hundreds of li, though it has now lost all power. Taekyung recognizes that its patterns are not writing, awkwardly admits he guessed this, memorizes the formation's layout, and leaves the group wondering what he knows about it.

## Continuity

- A hidden cavern near Chengdu served as a base for Dark Heaven's forces and contains a vast floor formation.
- The floor formation, called a Transportation Formation by Samgoe, once enabled long-distance movement but is now an inert shell with no detectable qi.
- The Slaughter Saint concludes that Dark Heaven is unquestionably a successor of the Demonic Cult.

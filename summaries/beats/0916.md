# Chapter 916

## Plot

Jin Taekyung throws himself between Jeok Cheongang and a strike, taking a grievous wound to save his Master. Grief and rage draw out Jeok’s latent power, and he unleashes a vast white fire dragon that incinerates Golden Ox Palace and forces the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord to defend himself with death energy.

Critically injured and losing his senses, Taekyung reflects that the gamble may have saved Jeok, but fears dying before he can see his loved ones again. He begs the System for more time, and a clear chime rings out just as he does.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung is alive but critically injured after taking a strike meant for Jeok Cheongang; he appeals to the System, whose chime answers at the chapter’s end.
- Jeok Cheongang unleashes immense white fire after Taekyung saves him, incinerating Golden Ox Palace and confronting the Eastern Heaven Demon Lord, who shields himself with death energy.

## Translation Decisions

- Retain “Force” for 강기 and “death energy” for 사기; the chapter distinguishes them.

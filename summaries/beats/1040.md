# Chapter 1040

## Plot

The Grand Mage remains calm as the mages fear that Jin Taekyung will die, but So Gunak falls and Jin revives after defeating him and leveling up. His Fire Dragon Armor is severely damaged and unavailable for three days. Jin continues fighting, identifies limits on the mages’ body-enhancement Magic, and cuts Taishan with White Flame.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung defeated Level 175 So Gunak; the resulting level-up restored his body.
- Fire Dragon Armor was recalled to Inventory after severe damage and cannot be resummoned until its automatic repair completes in three days.
- Jin remains in the battle against the surviving enemies and mages; he cuts Taishan at the chapter’s end.
- The Grand Mage expects Jin to survive and reacts to his revival with anticipation.
- The mages’ body-enhancement Magic requires its target to be alive and has limits.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 대술사 as Grand Mage, including when used as a direct address.
- 태산이여 addresses Taishan by name in the final invocation.

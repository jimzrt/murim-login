# Chapter 266

## Plot

Jongni Chu arrives at Mount Song and reveals himself as Sword Saint Mae Jonghak, Cheongpung's grandfather. He explains that he reached Great Completion, attained higher enlightenment, unexpectedly Returned to Youth, and spent the past year traveling under the name Jongni Chu. After restoring his rust-covered cherished sword, Mae attacks the Blood Lord with a divine version of the Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords. Jin Taekyung, still conscious despite his injuries, restrains the Blood Lord long enough for Mae to sever his remaining arm, but the Blood Lord bites the Green Jade Buddha Staff and vanishes inside ominous black radiance.

## Continuity

- The familiar voice that told Cheongpung he had broken free of his shell belongs to Sword Saint Mae Jonghak, who has been traveling under the identity Jongni Chu.
- Mae Jonghak killed the Heaven-Poison Demon Lord after the Great Faction War and received the title Sword Saint.
- Mae Jonghak reached Great Completion, encountered a further wall, attained higher enlightenment, and unexpectedly Returned to Youth.
- Mae Jonghak's restored cherished sword can produce a divine version of the Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords.
- Cheongpung loses consciousness after the Blood Lord injures him; Taekyung remains conscious long enough to seize the Blood Lord's ankle.
- The Blood Lord escapes with the Green Jade Buddha Staff by biting it and disappearing in black radiance; his identity and destination remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 천독마군 as Heaven-Poison Demon Lord.
- Retain Sword Saint for 검성 and Thirty-Six Plum Blossom Swords for 매화삼십육검.
- Use Returned to Youth for 반로환동 and Great Completion for 대성.
- Keep Mae Jonghak's speech calm and warm toward Cheongpung, then cold and judgmental toward the Blood Lord.

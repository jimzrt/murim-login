# Chapter 907

## Plot

Despite his injuries and the System being effectively sealed, Jin Taekyung kills Ma Sanbao and reenters the banquet-hall battle, cutting through Dark Heaven’s elite while trying to reach Jeok Cheongang. Hundreds of arrows target him, but Jeong Hogun and the Embroidered Uniform Guard shield him and declare themselves allies of the Emperor. Hogun cannot vouch for So Gyo, whose allegiance remains unknown. As Taekyung prepares to face the next wave, Hyuk Mujin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion members arrive, followed by numerous black figures.

## Continuity

- Taekyung killed Ma Sanbao and continues fighting, exhausted and badly injured, with his hands damaged and internal energy depleted.
- Jeong Hogun and the Embroidered Uniform Guard intervene to protect Taekyung and declare their allegiance to the Emperor.
- So Gyo’s identity and allegiance remain unknown; even Jeong Hogun cannot confirm she is an ally.
- Jeok Cheongang and Cang Gong are still fighting, and Taekyung suspects both the Emperor and Cang Gong have withheld forces.
- Hyuk Mujin and the Fire Dragon Pavilion members arrive at the battle with numerous black figures trailing them.

# Chapter 159

## Plot

Jin Taekyung spends fifty points each on Agility and Strength during his duel with Cheongpung, causing his spear to resonate with his energy and withstand Cheongpung's Sword Energy. The System generates the Quest Beyond the Wall, which Taekyung accepts. As the duel continues, Cheongpung employs the Zaha Divine Technique, Taeeul Miri Palm, Plum Blossom Sword Technique, Crouching Tiger Fist, and Plum Blossom Five-Point Finger, while Taekyung increasingly reads and counters his forms. Cheongpung explains Mae Jonghak's teaching that martial arts are empty space to be accepted and filled; after meditating on the idea, Taekyung completes the Quest and changes class to Peak Master.

## Continuity

- Taekyung spent 50 points on Agility and 50 on Strength during the duel, and his weapon resonated with his energy.
- Taekyung accepted and completed the System Quest Beyond the Wall and changed class to Peak Master.
- Taekyung still has not been confirmed to have defeated Cheongpung; their duel and its result remain unresolved.
- Cheongpung demonstrated a broad range of Huashan martial arts during the duel and openly enjoys martial arts themselves.
- Mae Jonghak taught Cheongpung that martial arts are empty space: one should accept them as they are and fill them in; this idea triggered Taekyung's breakthrough.
- Taekyung recognizes that the Jin Family's Spear Technique and Manoeuvre Technique are straightforward First Rate arts whose subtlety is limited compared with Huashan's secret techniques.
- Taekyung's class advancement occurred after he had spent seven years surviving Gates and the Murim while pursuing survival and eventual retirement.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 매화오품지 as “Plum Blossom Five-Point Finger.”
- Render 벽을 넘어서 as “Beyond the Wall” and 절정 고수 as “Peak Master” for the System Quest and resulting class.
- Preserve the wordplay between 무공 meaning “martial arts” and 무공 meaning “empty space” in Mae Jonghak's teaching.

# Chapter 660

## Plot

Chief Jang and Chief Go secretly follow the Beast Miao King's order to keep the Han Chinese members of the reconnaissance squad away from the Inner Palace, subduing and retaining the three captives so they can protect them from becoming hostages. Chief Jang believes Jin Taekyung was framed, but tightens Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin's bindings after an embarrassing attempt to loosen them.

Jin is imprisoned in the deepest part of the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison, bound with enormous iron balls and rendered unable to use internal energy by a Force-Sealing Pill. Baeksang visits, dismisses Jin's final punch as a frightened child's outburst, and announces that Jin will be executed publicly at noon in two days.

## Continuity

- Chief Jang and Chief Go are secretly protecting the three Han Chinese reconnaissance-squad captives from being taken to the Inner Palace, following the Beast Miao King's concealed order.
- Jin Taekyung is imprisoned in the deepest part of the Nanman Beast Palace's underground prison under Baeksang's authority.
- A Force-Sealing Pill is blocking Jin's dantian, leaving him unable to use internal energy for several days.
- Baeksang has sentenced Jin Taekyung to public execution at noon two days after Chapter 660.
- Jin openly accuses Baeksang of betraying his sworn brother and Nanman to Dark Heaven while remaining imprisoned to protect his people.
- Song Ilseom and Hyuk Mujin are bound and unconscious after being struck at their Sleep Acupoints, while Ju Hwaran remains among the three captives held by the reconnaissance squad.
- The Fire Dragon Pavilion was attacked after Jin left, and Dark Heaven is suspected of organizing or enabling the assault.
- Yohi's Western Yao Estate was attacked by a Supreme Peak master, and Heugung and Yohi remain missing.
- The reconnaissance squad is traveling through Nanman while guarding against the Blood Monk.

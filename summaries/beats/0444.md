# Chapter 444

## Plot

Jin Taekyung's group is welcomed into the Zhuge Clan's vast Hubei compound by Lesser Family Head Zhuge Gyun, whose ancestor Zhuge Gonghu was Jeok Cheongang's old friend. The group notices unusual fear and covert security throughout the Zhuge Clan's territory, but Gyun postpones explaining the situation until after their arrival.

The group reaches the Inner Hall after passing through eight gates, where Zhuge Gyun's father, Family Head Zhuge Feng, is waiting to receive the honored guests.

## Continuity

- The Zhuge Clan's current Lesser Family Head is Zhuge Gyun, son of Family Head Zhuge Feng and great-grandson of Zhuge Gonghu.
- The Zhuge Clan is conducting covert security operations in its own territory, including hopae inspections and undercover martial artists among the commoners, because of an unexplained emergency.
- Jin Taekyung's group has entered the Zhuge Clan's Inner Hall as honored guests and is about to meet Family Head Zhuge Feng.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 신기제갈 as “Divine Mechanism Zhuge,” 파선지왕 as “Fan-Wisdom King,” 와룡객 as “Crouching Dragon Guest,” 복룡산 as “Mount Fulong,” and 융중산 as “Mount Longzhong.”
- Preserve Jeok Cheongang's irreverent, grandiose insults toward Zhuge Gonghu and Taekyung's dry, profane reactions.

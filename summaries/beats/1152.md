# Chapter 1152

## Plot

A livestream captures Morgoth’s destruction of Moscow, caused by the hydrogen bomb hidden in the Kremlin. After the United States President announces that the returning savior has come back, the narrator watches footage of the devastation and Pie Chen’s death a week earlier. Pie died fighting Morgoth after refusing to follow him; Chuck Hagel, who survived because of her sacrifice, regrets living. The narrator vows not to let anyone else be sacrificed.

## Continuity

- Morgoth’s attack and the bomb’s blast destroy Moscow and its surrounding metropolitan area; the narrator hears a minimum estimate of twenty million dead, with no signs of life.
- Pie Chen, an S-rank Hunter from Hong Kong and the narrator’s friend, died a week earlier after making a last stand against Morgoth and refusing to follow him.
- Chuck Hagel survived because of Pie Chen’s sacrifice; he has only his right arm left and says he wishes he had died in her place.
- The United States President announces that the returning savior has come back; the narrator vows to prevent further sacrifices and intends to face Morgoth.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 파이 첸 as “Pie Chen.”
- Retain “Erasure” for 소멸 and “Black Dragon Duke” for 흑룡공.

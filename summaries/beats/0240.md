# Chapter 240

## Plot

Jeok Cheongang displays the Five Qi Returning to Origin realm. Jin Taekyung reports that the Nangong Family’s banquet has been canceled because the Azure Sky Sword King entered seclusion after gaining a minor insight. Jeok and Taekyung leave the Nangong estate for Henan and the Star-Array Grand Banquet; before departing, Jeok silently notices Taekyung’s hastily repaired shackles and cut sleeves.

The Azure Sky Sword King tells Namgung Ryong that Jin Taekyung is a Hidden Dragon possessing the Heavenly Martial Physique, while Jeok Cheongang is the dragon pearl who became one by taking Taekyung as his Disciple. He reveals that he has reached nine-tenths mastery of the Emperor’s Sword Form but has not achieved Great Completion, and that Taekyung withstood three of his moves. He orders Namgung Ryong to ensure that Nangong Ok does not attend the Star-Array Grand Banquet.

At a mountain route between Henan and Anhui, Heuk Jongpil and the more than three hundred bandits of Black Mountain Stronghold prepare to ambush the Geumwa Merchant Group using information from the Green Forest Alliance. Jeok and Taekyung race toward the ambush, stop after noticing the bandits’ scouts, and decide to deal with them. Before Heuk Jongpil can finish warning his men, an enormous hand wraps around his shoulder.

## Continuity

- Jeok Cheongang publicly recognizes Jin Taekyung as his Disciple and heir to the Fire Gate Clan’s orthodox lineage.
- Taekyung remains bound to chained Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron balls whose key Jeok holds.
- Jeok Cheongang’s dementia and age-related weakness continue to progress.
- Taekyung and Jeok Cheongang arrived at the Nangong Family estate in Anhui for the Azure Sky Sword King’s birthday celebration.
- The Nangong Family is one of the Five Great Families and the hegemon of Anhui.
- The Azure Sky Sword King is Nangong Cheon, the Grand Family Head of the Nangong Family, and is obsessed with swords and martial training.
- The Azure Sky Sword King previously spared Jangcheon in exchange for a spar with Jeok Cheongang; Jeok won by half a form, after which the Sword King entered seclusion.
- Namgung Ryong is the Family Head of the Namgung family and commands the Hidden Thread and Lesser Threads.
- Nangong Ok is Namgung Ryong’s only child, the Nangong Family’s Lesser Family Head and the Sword Dragon.
- Namgung Ryong asked Taekyung to stop the duel between Jeok Cheongang and Namgung Cheon because of the danger to both masters and the wider martial world.
- Namgung Cheon agreed to consider Taekyung’s request only if Taekyung withstood three moves.
- Taekyung accepted the Three-Move Kill Sudden Quest and removed his chained iron balls before Namgung Cheon’s first attack.
- Jeok Cheongang has achieved the Five Qi Returning to Origin realm.
- The birthday banquet was canceled when Namgung Cheon entered seclusion, and Jeok and Taekyung left the Nangong estate for Henan.
- Namgung Cheon has reached nine-tenths mastery of the Emperor’s Sword Form but has not achieved Great Completion.
- Taekyung withstood three moves of Namgung Cheon’s Emperor’s Sword Form; Namgung Cheon regards Taekyung as a Hidden Dragon and Jeok as the dragon pearl.
- Namgung Cheon ordered Namgung Ryong to keep Nangong Ok from attending the Star-Array Grand Banquet.
- Heuk Jongpil leads Black Mountain Stronghold, commands more than three hundred bandits, and pays four-tenths of its monthly profits to the Green Forest Alliance.
- Jeok and Taekyung have reached the vicinity of Black Mountain Stronghold’s ambush of the Geumwa Merchant Group; the identity of the person who seized Heuk Jongpil remains unknown.

# Chapter 568

## Plot

The person applauding Taekyung was the Skeleton King, who appeared in a leather coat and theatrically introduced himself as King Fury, Director of Barrier. Taekyung immediately beat him for the absurd superhero performance, then acknowledged that the Skeleton King had become reliable enough to act independently and granted him greater freedom after discussing his church visit, income, and limited socialization.

Taekyung sent the Skeleton King to answer an emergency call while he remained behind in isolation, repeatedly circulating his qi and training as he pursued a new path suggested by a faint clue.

Go Jun told Song Cheonwoo that he had taken Song's children, dismissing the word kidnapping and calling it protection while eating steak. Song reacted with shock and fury.

## Continuity

- Taekyung now trusts the Skeleton King to handle suitable Gate emergencies alone and has granted him more personal freedom, though he still considers the monster dangerously under-socialized.
- The Skeleton King earns six hundred million won annually for ten years through his Peace Guild arrangement and has begun socializing through church.
- Go Jun has seized Song Cheonwoo's children and presents the abduction as protection, confirming the leverage he is using against Song.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 킹 퓨리 as King Fury and preserve it as the Skeleton King's fabricated superhero identity; render 배리어의 국장 contextually as Director of Barrier.
- Retain the Skeleton King's mock-archaic, melodramatic register against Taekyung's blunt, profane, irreverent voice.
- Preserve the running jokes around capitalism, ten years of slavery, and the Skeleton King's desire for a noble grinding session.

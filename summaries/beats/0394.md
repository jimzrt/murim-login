# Chapter 394

## Plot

Jin Taekyung and Team Leader Choi meet Michael Johnson, who escorts them to Magic Johnson and General Liao at the temporary headquarters. Choi reveals that he secretly spread favorable rumors about Jin's confrontation with Liao by mobilizing hostile Hunters and paying soldiers, preserving Jin's public standing. Magic explains that monster armies withdrew overnight from every front except the deeply penetrated western front, then announces that a military conference has been convened in Chengdu and that he must temporarily withdraw personnel from the southern front.

## Continuity

- Team Leader Choi deliberately engineered favorable rumors about Jin Taekyung's confrontation with General Liao, using money and existing anti-Liao sentiment to protect Jin from political consequences.
- Magic Johnson commands the southern front, has temporarily left it without immediate risk, and reports that monster armies withdrew overnight from every front except the western front.
- A military conference has been convened in Chengdu, and Magic Johnson must pull personnel from the southern front and deliver written orders to the commander.

# Chapter 652

## Plot

Sama Pyo is ambushed at night by roughly thirty masked attackers who surround the pavilion and attack without warning. He uses his Black Dragon Saber, concealed mechanical arrow device, hidden weapons, poison, and ruthless combat experience to kill twenty attackers, including seven Peak masters, while holding off the rest.

Namho wakes to the attack and rouses Taishan by claiming the masked men ate his meat. Taishan erupts from the damaged pavilion and attacks the intruders. Jin Taekyung then returns and asks Sama Pyo whether the masked attackers are his friends.

## Continuity

- A planned nighttime assault targets the Fire Dragon Pavilion compound while Jin Taekyung, Song Ilseom, Ju Hwaran, and Hyuk Mujin are absent.
- Sama Pyo survives the attack after killing twenty masked attackers, seven of whom are Peak masters; his combat style relies on concealed weapons, poison, and survival-focused demonic, heterodox arts.
- Namho awakens Taishan by invoking the threat to his meat, and Taishan responds with overwhelming force that destroys part of the pavilion.
- Jin Taekyung returns to the compound after the battle has begun, while the masked attackers' identities and motive remain unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Use upper First Rate for 초일류 and short spear for 단창.
- Use mechanical device for 기관장치 and retain hidden weapon for 암기.
- Retain pavilion for 전각 and Inner Palace for 내궁.

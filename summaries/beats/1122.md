# Chapter 1122

## Plot

The exhausted defenders at the Inner City make a desperate stand against tens of thousands of fanatics. Jeok Cheongang supports the critically wounded Jin Taekyung while fighting, and the Slaughter Saint and Bow Saint join them; Cheongpung also arrives. The Slaughter Saint’s words make Jeok realize Taekyung may be dying. As a powerful, unidentified figure approaches, civilians armed with crude weapons arrive to protect the people fighting for them. Taekyung briefly wakes and tells the figure to shut up.

## Continuity

- The Inner City defenders number about five thousand and are badly outnumbered and exhausted; civilians have joined the fight with crude weapons.
- Jin Taekyung is critically wounded and briefly regains consciousness; the Slaughter Saint’s warning leads Jeok Cheongang to fear Taekyung will die.
- Jeok Cheongang, the Slaughter Saint, the Bow Saint, and Cheongpung are fighting together at the Inner City.
- An unidentified man or monster approaches the defenders with overwhelming power; his identity and the outcome are unknown.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep the approaching figure unidentified; this chapter does not reveal who he is.

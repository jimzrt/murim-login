# Chapter 531

## Plot

Jin Taekyung intervenes when an unidentified giant from the demonic, heterodox side attacks Hwangbo Ak after Hwangbo insults him. Gung Gibang evacuates the civilians, while Taekyung learns that Ju Hwaran and two men who were with the giant are unharmed. Taekyung catches the giant's two-section staff and Hwangbo's leg, forces both sides to reconcile through threats and mockery, humiliates and intimidates Hwangbo by naming his identity and invoking the injured Zhongnan elders, then sends him away. A familiar voice finally calls out, “Sir Jin!”

## Continuity

- The unidentified giant says his lord instructed him to think three times before fighting; he attacked Hwangbo Ak after Hwangbo drew his sword first and insulted him.
- Jin Taekyung is vastly stronger than both the giant and Hwangbo Ak, stopping their attacks with one movement and compelling them to end the fight.
- Ju Hwaran was among the two men and woman accompanying the unidentified giant; she was unharmed and has now called out to Taekyung after a long separation.

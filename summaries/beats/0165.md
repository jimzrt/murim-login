# Chapter 165

## Plot

Wipeng delivered Jin Wikyung’s information about Jang Taebo, a former Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild who now lives in a village near Jeongyang. The Ironcraft Guild has close ties to the Nine Sects and One Gang, and its current Guild Leader is Jang Taebo’s disciple. Jin Taekyung set out to meet Jang Taebo and commission a custom weapon using his Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron.

Wipeng recognized that Taekyung had crossed the wall and warned him to return by noon the next day for the Jin Family of Taiyuan’s New Year’s Day banquet. He also reported unusual mounted-bandit activity near Datong. Hyuk Mujin prepared the journey, while Cheongpung joined them enthusiastically and brought an excessive amount of food.

After sunset, more than four hundred mounted bandits belonging to the Heavenly Wind Band gathered under their unnamed giant leader and turned south to kill, plunder, and burn. Before they could depart, an unidentified old man appeared and challenged them with an icy-white shining hand.

## Continuity

- Wipeng has recognized that Jin Taekyung crossed the wall and reached the Peak realm.
- Jang Taebo was the Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild until more than a decade ago and now lives in a village near Jeongyang; Taekyung is traveling there to meet him about forging a custom weapon.
- The current Guild Leader of the Ironcraft Guild is Jang Taebo’s disciple, and the guild has close ties to the Nine Sects and One Gang.
- Wipeng reported unusual mounted-bandit movement near Datong in the northern Gaoyuan.
- More than four hundred Heavenly Wind Band mounted bandits, led by an unnamed giant, turned south intending to attack and plunder the Jin Family of Taiyuan before an unidentified old man confronted them.

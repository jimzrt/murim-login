# Chapter 121

## Plot

After the battle, Jin Taekyung rests in a Mount Heng Sword Sect pavilion while Hyuk Mujin massages his shoulders and reports that Jin Mukyung and the other wounded are recovering. Wolhwa's Lower District Sect support force has arrived with physicians, cooks, and laborers to manage relief work. Lee Seowol visits to accept the Jin Family of Taiyuan's invitation and offers all rights to the territory occupied by the Mount Heng Sword Sect as an apology. She then presents her father's Blood Wolf Sword Technique and Blood Wolf Footwork, along with Cheol Mubaek's Shura Annihilating Fist, as Peak martial arts in a transaction: in exchange, she asks Taekyung to marry her. Afterward, Hyuk Mujin reveals that the seventeen-year-old Lee Seowol made the proposal, leaving Taekyung stunned.

## Continuity

- The Lower District Sect sent a prepared relief force to the Mount Heng Sword Sect after the battle, including physicians, cooks, and laborers.
- The wounded Jin Mukyung, Cheol Mubaek, and other survivors are reported to be recovering.
- Wolhwa's real name is Eun Sowol, and she is the Lower District Sect's Shanxi Branch Leader.
- Lee Seowol accepted Jin Wikyung's invitation for the Mount Heng Sword Sect to attend the Jin Family of Taiyuan's New Year gathering.
- Lee Seowol offered all rights to the territory occupied by the Mount Heng Sword Sect to the Jin Family of Taiyuan as an apology for the past conflict.
- Lee Seowol offered the Blood Wolf Sword Technique, Blood Wolf Footwork, and Shura Annihilating Fist as Peak martial arts in a transaction.
- Lee Seowol proposed marriage to Jin Taekyung in exchange for the three Peak martial arts.
- Lee Seowol is seventeen years old, and Taekyung is shocked to learn that fact.

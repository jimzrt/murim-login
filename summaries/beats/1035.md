# Chapter 1035

## Plot

After leveling up upon defeating four Death Knights, Jin Taekyung confronts the Blood-Sword Demon Lord, whose former Demonic Cult comrades were among the Black Ghosts. Taekyung uses Qi Sense to identify the Demon Lord as Chuk Banghyeol and watch his level rise from 170 to 180. As a white-robed figure invokes the Wind Ghost’s power and imbues him with it, Taekyung realizes the force at work is Magic, not dark arts.

## Continuity

- The four Black Ghosts Taekyung defeated were former Demonic Cult fiends who had once shared a faction with the Blood-Sword Demon Lord.
- Qi Sense reveals the Blood-Sword Demon Lord’s name as Chuk Banghyeol and shows his level rising from 170 to 180 as he approaches.
- A white-robed figure chants for the Wind Ghost’s power to dwell in the Blood-Sword Demon Lord; Taekyung identifies the power as Magic rather than dark arts.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 척방혈 as Chuk Banghyeol.
- Render 풍귀 as Wind Ghost.

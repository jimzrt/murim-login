# Chapter 975

## Plot

Jamukha attacks Jeok Cheongang, but Taekyung intercepts him, tears away his braided hair, and overwhelms him with Flame Divine Palm strikes while Fire Dragon Armor withstands Jamukha’s blade. Jamukha dies, and Taekyung levels up. The North Heaven Demon Lord retreats toward the gorge exit and attacks the trapped Shanxi fighters, but a huge arrow of light strikes toward him before his attack lands.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung killed Jamukha; Jamukha’s ambition to rule the steppe and north, and his feigned loyalty to Murong Baek, are now resolved by his death.
- Jin Taekyung leveled up after defeating Jamukha; the System announced substantial EXP and Fame gains.
- The North Heaven Demon Lord is retreating toward the gorge exit after Jamukha’s death. A huge arrow of light is flying toward him; its source and outcome are unknown.
- The conditions of Jin Mukyung, Cheol Mubaek, Wipeng, and Peng Cheolhu remain unknown.
- The Emperor remains gravely ill with Blood Soul Gu; the treatment said to require him to die once remains unresolved.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep “Shifting Form and Position,” “Flame Divine Palm,” and “Fire Dragon Armor” as established terms.

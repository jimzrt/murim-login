# Chapter 858

## Plot

After more than ten days of travel, Jin Taekyung wakes from a brief nap in a tree and finds Hyuk Mujin holding off the Embroidered Uniform Guard. Jin praises Mujin for enduring the danger but warns him to retreat and find another way when he cannot win. Their banter fails to defuse the standoff with Commander Jeong, whose force includes dozens of Peak-level martial artists. When an archer shoots at Jin, Jin redirects the arrow, killing the horse beneath him. Commander Jeong identifies himself as Jeong Hogun and insists that his men serve the Emperor's command, not their own martial skill. Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao arrives, greets Jin, hands him a secret letter, and asks for his autograph.

## Continuity

- Jin's party has reached its destination ahead of the Embroidered Uniform Guard; exhausted from the journey, Jin slept briefly in a tree while Mujin kept watch.
- Mujin stood his ground against the Guard until Jin awoke; Jin praised his resolve but advised him to retreat and seek another approach when outmatched.
- Commander Jeong is Jeong Hogun, a formidable general with pure internal energy and a force containing dozens of Peak masters; he says their loyalty to the Emperor's command is their reason for standing against Jin.
- Prince Shangshan Zhu Bao has reunited with Jin, has grown considerably since they last met, and handed Jin a secret letter while asking for an autograph.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 정호군 as “Jeong Hogun” and 정 천호 as “Commander Jeong.”
- Retain “Stepping on Empty Air” for 허공답보, “Embroidered Uniform Guard” for 금의위, and “Great Nation” for 대국.

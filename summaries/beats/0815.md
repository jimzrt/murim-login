# Chapter 815

## Plot

Choi Minwoo leads roughly eight hundred Hunters into the canyon to meet the fanatics. Their charge breaks through the undead line; an old fanatic commander cuts down Minwoo’s griffin, but Minwoo and the Hunters leap clear and join the fight. A powerful Arab fanatic recognizes Minwoo as Sky’s blood and attacks him. The Skeleton King intervenes, and the battle’s shock fractures the canyon walls.

The cracks help free the trapped Doppelganger. Jin Taekyung kills Brody Woods, whose death triggers a Level Up that removes Jin’s Fatigue and Muscle Pain and restores some internal energy, though the Broken Body debuff rejects the healing power. Jin keeps fighting the Doppelganger, which completes its 145th resurrection and continues to regenerate.

## Continuity

- Minwoo and roughly eight hundred Hunters are fighting the fanatics in the canyon; a powerful Arab fanatic has targeted Minwoo, and the Skeleton King has engaged him.
- The battle’s shock caused small cracks in the canyon, helping free the Doppelganger.
- The Doppelganger has completed its 145th resurrection and is still regenerating after Jin’s attacks.
- Jin defeated Level 78 Brody Woods and leveled up; Fatigue and Muscle Pain were removed, internal energy began recovering, but the Broken Body debuff rejected the healing power.

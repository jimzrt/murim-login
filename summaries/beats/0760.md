# Chapter 760

## Plot

Jin leaves the unconscious Joel Schumacher under the Skeleton King's protection and orders the Skeleton King to suppress his magical power and conceal his authority from the watching humans. The Minotaur Lord rallies thousands of Minotaurs against Jin and the assembled Hunters, and Jin orders everyone to kill them all.

Jin leads the Skeleton King, Team Leader Choi, and more than two hundred elite Hunters from the Ares and Peace Guilds into the counterattack. His Fire Dragon Divine Spear and overwhelming physical strength tear through the Minotaur ranks, leaving the Minotaur Lord isolated, fearful, and facing Jin's final charge.

## Continuity

- Joel Schumacher survived the ambush but is unconscious and is being guarded while the Munich battle continues.
- The Skeleton King has been instructed to suppress his magical power and conceal his supernatural abilities from humans unless their use is unavoidable.
- Jin is leading Team Leader Choi, the Skeleton King, and more than two hundred Ares and Peace Guild Hunters against the Munich Monster Wave.
- The Minotaur Lord has rallied the Minotaur army, but fear has replaced its initial wariness as Jin closes in to kill it.
- Jin's Broken Body debuff and battle fatigue remain active.
- Jin secretly retains Leviathan's corpse and the two Japanese-government S-rank Magic Gems in his Inventory while publicly claiming they were destroyed.
- The Main Quest: Cataclysm remains active, but its mission, reward, and failure conditions are unknown.
- Michael Silbert remains engaged in the Cape Town Monster Wave after his forces began suppressing the South African disaster.

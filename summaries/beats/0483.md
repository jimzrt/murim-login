# Chapter 483

## Plot

Honglan, revealed as the Southern Heaven Demon Empress, refuses to explain how Dark Heaven made the impossible Gate and demonic qi real. She declares herself a servant acting on Lord of Heaven's orders, while Jeok Cheongang, Jin Wikyung, Gung Gibang, and the others vow to protect Taekyung and destroy Dark Heaven. The possessed military officer dies after the Southern Heaven Demon Empress abandons his body.

Cheongpung reports that Zhuge Feng found the location from the Water God Dragon's memories: the Gate. Meanwhile, the Southern Heaven Demon Empress travels toward Yunnan in an Escort Bureau carriage, delighted by the deaths she expects to cause and by meeting a new female escort.

## Continuity

- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress confirms that Lord of Heaven is the figure whose orders she follows and says that no one can know his will.
- Cheongpung reports that Zhuge Feng has located the Gate site remembered by the Water God Dragon.
- Jin Wikyung pledges the Jin Family of Taiyuan's protection of Taekyung, while Gung Gibang pledges the Beggars' Sect's support.
- The Southern Heaven Demon Empress is traveling toward Yunnan and anticipates further deaths there.

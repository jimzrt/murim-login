# Chapter 157

## Plot

During the third day of sparring with Cheongpung, Jin Taekyung survives his forty-fifth duel, identifies the Crouching Tiger Fist's forms, and roughly copies Cheongpung's Plum Blossom Fist after only three days of observation. Cheongpung recognizes Taekyung's unusual martial talent, but becomes unsettled when Taekyung copies his martial arts and a dangerous aura rises from him.

After regaining consciousness, Hyuk Mujin witnesses the training ground being destroyed by the ongoing exchange. Cheongpung's Huashan martial arts remain graceful and overwhelming, while Taekyung's tenacious, fierce Wildness and rapid growth let him continue fighting despite severe attacks. Mujin resolves to become strong enough to be remembered as Taekyung's right arm or heart, and by his own name.

## Continuity

- Cheongpung learned the Crouching Tiger Fist from Mae Jonghak; its named form One Fist Subdues the Tiger has enough force to knock Taekyung down.
- On the third day of sparring and his forty-fifth duel with Cheongpung, Taekyung can increasingly read Cheongpung's forms and roughly copy the Plum Blossom Fist.
- Cheongpung considers Taekyung a martial genius because of his rapid imitation, while Taekyung attributes his ability to visual acuity, athletic talent, and the System.
- Cheongpung recalls that Mae Jonghak called him a thief who stole martial arts, and he is displeased when Taekyung imitates his techniques.
- Cheongpung uses the Plum Blossom Sword Technique and Heavenly Eagle Claw during the ongoing spar; Taekyung remains below the Peak realm but continues fighting through the attacks.
- Hyuk Mujin has resolved to grow strong enough to be recognized as Jin Taekyung's right arm or heart and to make the name Hyuk Mujin memorable.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 복호권 as “Crouching Tiger Fist,” 일권복호 as “One Fist Subdues the Tiger,” 매화권 as “Plum Blossom Fist,” 천응조 as “Heavenly Eagle Claw,” 봉미혈 as “Fengwei acupoint,” 태극 1장부터 8장까지 as “Taegeuk Forms 1 through 8,” and 대성 as “Great Attainment.”
- Keep Taekyung's first-person narration conversational, dry, self-mocking, and occasionally profane; render 투로 as “sequence of forms” and 기세 as “aura” in this chapter.
- Render the grandfather's martial-learning maxim 눈이 칠, 발이 삼 as “seventy percent eyes and thirty percent feet.”

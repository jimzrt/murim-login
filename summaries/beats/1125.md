# Chapter 1125

## Plot

White Flame pierces the Blood Lord, and Taekyung says he instinctively knew the Blood Lord’s movements and how to counter them. The Blood Lord acknowledges that Taekyung was stronger in that moment, but Taekyung’s power runs out and the spear misses his heart. The Blood Lord tears the spear free, takes White Flame, and turns toward Taekyung; Jeok Cheongang and Cheongpung intervene, but the Blood Lord knocks them aside.

Taekyung struggles to stand and attacks with a summoned dagger. The Blood Lord crushes Taekyung’s wrist, and Taekyung kicks the falling dagger into the Blood Lord’s shin. The Blood Lord breaks Taekyung’s legs and grabs his throat. As Taekyung’s vision darkens, it turns white, then red; what happens next is unresolved.

## Continuity

- Taekyung’s spear attack pierced the Blood Lord but missed his heart when Taekyung’s internal energy ran out; the Blood Lord pulled out the spear and now wields White Flame.
- Jeok Cheongang and Cheongpung intervened to protect the fallen Taekyung, but the Blood Lord knocked them aside.
- The Blood Lord has crushed Taekyung’s wrist and legs and is choking him; Taekyung’s final vision shifts to white and red, with the outcome unresolved.
- The Blood Lord intends to kill Taekyung and believes his choice is justified; he resented the Lord of Heaven’s attention to Taekyung and the apparent disregard for the servants’ welfare.

## Translation Decisions

- Keep 회광반조 as “final rally”; it briefly suppresses Taekyung’s pain during his dagger attack.
- Keep the Blood Lord’s “true Heaven” distinct from the “paltry heaven” Taekyung associates with the people he fights to protect.

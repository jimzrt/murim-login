# Chapter 190

## Plot

Jin Taekyung evaded Chulwoo's Crouching Tiger Fist through overwhelming speed, resisted Chulwoo's attempt to throw him, and knocked him unconscious with a punch to the temple. The duel ended with Taekyung's victory, completing the Quest There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much and granting large EXP and Fame rewards plus two level-ups.

Baek Museong acknowledged Taekyung's exceptional martial talent, apologized to Jin Wikyung for his earlier rudeness, and accepted that he and Chulwoo had learned from the duel. At the Jin Family gate, Hyuk Mujin and a low-ranking martial artist heard the celebration before four Zhongnan Sect visitors arrived; their leader identified himself as the Roaring Fury Swordsman and demanded to be taken to Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung.

## Continuity

- Jin Taekyung defeated Chulwoo after the duel appeared to have ended, overpowering his grip and knocking him unconscious with a punch to the temple.
- The Quest There Is a Man Who Loved You So Much was completed, granting large EXP and Fame rewards and causing two level-ups; Taekyung's Level is now 73.
- Taekyung stated that his Strength stat exceeds 300 and that he was already stronger than Chulwoo before allocating his recent stat points; his strength and speed surpassed Chulwoo's after the allocation.
- Cheongpung assessed that Taekyung's victory came from speed rather than a supreme martial art and said Taekyung had read and countered Crouching Tiger Fist after only two days.
- Baek Museong apologized to Jin Wikyung for belittling Taekyung and acknowledged that he and Chulwoo had learned a great deal from the duel.
- Four Zhongnan Sect visitors arrived at the Jin Family gate, led by the Roaring Fury Swordsman, and demanded immediate guidance to Jin Taekyung and Cheongpung.

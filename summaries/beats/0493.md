# Chapter 493

## Plot

Perfected Being Hyeongong is tempted by the imugi's exceptionally hard bones, and Jin Taekyung manipulates that desire by offering Wudang a share of the corpse in exchange for help dismantling it. Taekyung contrasts Hyeongong's old sword with his solid Ten-Thousand-Year Cold Iron spear, White Flame, and promises an additional share of imugi bones. Cheongpung cuts through the corpse's waist with the Martial Extremity Grand Unity Sword, while Hyeongong ultimately uses the Taiji Wisdom Sword to separate its scales from its bones. Mungyeong watches the scene and concludes that Murim has gone completely mad.

## Continuity

- The Water God Dragon's spirit has departed; its enormous corpse at Dongting Lake is being cut apart for distribution, with Jin Taekyung coordinating shares and Hyeongong and Cheongpung contributing sword work.

# Chapter 433

## Plot

Twenty days after the Small Cataclysm, international Hunters and rescue workers continue rebuilding Sichuan Province while Chairman Shao Yang's faction publicly purges Wu Xueming and the Crown Prince Party. Jin Taekyung secretly joins the rescue effort, saves the last survivor in one area, and is accidentally exposed during a live CCTV broadcast after denying his identity, turning the broadcast disaster into a sensational scoop with ratings exceeding thirty percent. An aircraft formation arrives to take him back to Korea.

## Continuity

- Jin secretly participated in the Sichuan rescue effort, using his martial power and internal warmth to save a severely weakened elderly survivor.
- Jin's presence in Sichuan and identity as Jin Taekyung were exposed during a live CCTV broadcast, including his irreverent remarks, which became a major public scoop.
- Chairman Shao Yang has begun a large-scale purge of Wu Xueming and leading Crown Prince Party figures, alongside a sweeping military reorganization.

## Translation Decisions

- Render 소격변 as “Small Cataclysm.”
- Render 웨이자 장 as “Zhang Weijia,” 샤오 양 as “Shao Yang,” and 우쉐이밍 as “Wu Xueming.”
- Preserve Jin's dry, profane, self-deprecating voice during the live broadcast.

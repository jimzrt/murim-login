# Chapter 304

## Plot

Team Leader Choi shows Taekyung and the Peace Guild holographic footage of a Monster Wave in a small city near Nanchong, Sichuan Province. More than three hundred thousand casualties are estimated during the first twenty-four hours, while Chinese Hunters and monsters clash in overwhelming numbers.

The Monster Wave was caused by a Named Monster Lich, an immensely powerful undead archmage capable of casting Fire Rain and claiming territory. Choi reveals that China contacted him one week earlier to request Taekyung's participation because Xiao Yang, the General Secretary of China's Central Committee, specifically wanted him.

## Continuity

- A Monster Wave occurred in a small city near Nanchong, Sichuan Province, causing approximately three hundred thousand estimated casualties during its first twenty-four hours.
- The Named Monster responsible is a Lich, an apex undead monster and fallen archmage whose fire magic is far beyond the Liches Butler Kim witnessed during the Great Cataclysm.
- The Lich declared that it would kill and take everything from anyone entering its territory, indicating that it is unlikely to be satisfied with one small city.
- China's Public Security Armed Forces deployed more than one thousand Hunters against the Monster Wave, while the assembled monsters also numbered more than one thousand.
- Team Leader Choi received a request from China one week earlier for Taekyung to participate in responding to the crisis.
- Choi identifies the person who specifically requested Taekyung as Xiao Yang, the General Secretary of China's Central Committee.
- The Sichuan catastrophe occurred ten days earlier but has not appeared as ordinary worldwide news, and the reason for that concealment remains unexplained.

# Chapters 369–373

## Plot

Chapters 369–370 are intentionally skipped in this expedition and have no accepted local English continuity. The source-only bridge begins with Taekyung publicly recognized as the Flame Divine Dragon, then follows his investigation with Jeok Cheongang and the Qingcheng and Emei Sect Leaders into a hidden Dark Heaven cave and its inactive transport formation. Slaughter Saint reveals his identity as Mungyeong, chooses to return to his physician persona, and accompanies Taekyung back toward the Sichuan Tang Clan. Chapter 373 ends with Jin Taekyung at Level 120, a new unnamed Inventory-bound item, and an investigative party from Henan seeking him.

## Continuity

- Chapters 369–370 are not translated locally and must not be treated as accepted continuity.
- Chapters 371–373 are summarized from Korean source only; see `docs/EXPEDITION_SEED.md`.
- Slaughter Saint is secretly Mungyeong and wants to leave Murim after this intervention.
- The Dark Heaven transport formation is inactive and its origin and purpose remain unresolved.
- Chapter 374 opens at the Sichuan Tang Clan with Jin Wikyung preparing to discuss relocation and escort Samgoe toward Henan.

## Translation Decisions

- Preserve the established forms Dark Heaven, Demonic Cult, Slaughter Saint, One Flash, Peak, Supreme Peak, and Internal Energy.
- Treat new terms in Chapters 374 onward as current-source terminology, not as established by the skipped range.
- Do not use this source-only bridge as a substitute for missing chapter translations.
